Lost access to everything?

Yes, a claimed profile can be re-claimed. The email is a key you can rotate. Your credits live with the record, not the address.
1
Start a re-claim from any new email on your own page ("This is me, I lost my old login").
2
We ping the old address and open a short cool-down. If someone still controls it, they can veto, which is what stops hijacking. A dead inbox simply never responds.
3
You re-prove it's you with the cheap signals we already use: peer vouches from people you shipped with, and/or a work-email domain match. Same machinery as a contested claim.
4
Ownership transfers to your new key. Credits, vouches, skills and history all stay attached to you. Nothing is lost.
Your hotmail scenario: if you'd linked a Google/GitHub sign-in or a recovery email at claim time, recovery is instant: just use the other key. If you truly lost every login, you start a re-claim from your new email; we ping the dead hotmail (it never answers), a cool-down passes, your old colleagues' vouches or your work-domain confirm you, and the profile moves to your new email. You never lose the credits. The one thing that makes this smooth is linking a second key now, so we nudge for it right after you claim.
