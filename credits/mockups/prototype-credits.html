Lost access to everything?

Yes, a claimed profile can be re-claimed. The email is a key you can rotate. Your credits live with the record, not the address.
1
Start a re-claim from any new email on your own page ("this is me, I lost my old login").
2
We ping the old address and open a short cool-down. If someone still controls it they can veto, which stops hijacking. A dead inbox never responds.
3
You re-prove it is you with peer vouches from people you shipped with, and/or a work-email domain match. Same machinery as a contested claim.
4
Ownership transfers to your new key. Credits, vouches, skills and history stay attached to you. Nothing is lost.
Your hotmail scenario: if you linked Google/GitHub or a recovery email at claim time, recovery is instant, just use the other key. If you lost every login, you re-claim from a new email, we ping the dead hotmail (no answer), a cool-down passes, your colleagues' vouches or work-domain confirm you, and the profile moves over. You never lose the credits.
