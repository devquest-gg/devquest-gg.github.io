Why we're building this

Your gameography, finally yours to own.

There are places to look up who made a game, like MobyGames and IGDB. But those are third-party catalogues, edited around you, not by you. And a game's own credits are the studio's call, so real work gets downgraded to "Special Thanks," mislabelled, cut after a layoff, or left off entirely. And a growing share of work is never credited at all: live-service games ship and update for years with no credits to point to, so the people building them have nowhere to say "I made this." No existing site lets the person who did the work own their own record, and build one where the credits never did.

DevQuest Credits flips that. The person who did the work owns the record of it. You claim the games you actually made, the teammates who were there confirm it, and it stays yours: opt-in, peer-confirmed, and openly licensed, so it outlives any studio, any layoff, and even us.

Your work deserves your name on it.

Find your credits, claim your page, and take it with you wherever you go next.
