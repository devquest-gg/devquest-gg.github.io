Why we're building this

Games have databases. They don't have a record you control.

There are places to look up who made a game, like MobyGames and IGDB. But they are third-party catalogues, edited around you rather than by you. Credits still get people cut after layoffs, mislabelled, or left off a game entirely, and no existing site lets the person who did the work own and correct their own record.

DevQuest Credits flips that. The person who did the work owns the record of it. Opt-in, peer-verified, and openly licensed, so it can outlive any one company, including us.

Your work deserves your name on it.
