Quick style guide

The essentials below, or download the full brief (PDF).

Canvas: 32 × 32 px, transparent background.
Subject: ~24 px tall, centered, a couple px of padding.
Style: chunky-friendly: keep features ≥2 px so it reads at header size.
Palette: the DevQuest set (greens, gold, teal, purple, coral) on the dark UI.
Outline: dark, near-black (#0b0e13), all the way around.
Format: PNG (indexed is fine). No animation for v1.

What we can and can't accept

A few firm rules. Submissions that break these can't be used.

Original work only. You made it yourself, by hand, for this.
No existing characters or IP. No Mario, Pokémon, Sonic, and so on, not even "your own take." Invent something new.
No AI art. The whole point is human-made. Hand-drawn only.
All-ages and kind. Nothing explicit, gory, hateful, or demeaning to any group.
No tracing or plagiarism. By submitting, you confirm it's yours and copies no one.
