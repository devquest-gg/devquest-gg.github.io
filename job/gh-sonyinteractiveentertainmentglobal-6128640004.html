Role Overview:

The Office Manager provides dedicated administrative and operational support to the DTIS Europe organisation, ensuring the effective coordination of business activities, leadership support, employee engagement initiatives, and day-to-day team operations.

This role acts as a central point of coordination for administrative processes, travel and expense support, meetings, events, communications, and office-related requirements. The Office Manager works closely with DTIS leaders, employees, and partner functions to improve operational efficiency and enhance the employee experience across the region.

While the role supports the day-to-day needs of the DTIS Europe organisation, facilities-related responsibilities, including building management, maintenance, vendor management, access control, and facilities invoice processing, remain the responsibility of the Facilities organisation.

What you ll be doing:

Administrative Executive Support

Provide administrative support to DTIS leaders and teams across Europe.

Coordinate calendars, meetings, and leadership schedules as required.

Prepare meeting materials, presentations, agendas, and supporting documentation.

Track actions and follow up on key deliverables to ensure timely completion.

Support internal communications and organisational coordination activities.

Travel Expense Management

Act as the primary point of contact for Travel Expense (T E) related queries and issue resolution.

Support employees and leaders with travel arrangements, expense submissions, and policy guidance.

Liaise with internal stakeholders to resolve travel and expense-related issues.

Monitor and track outstanding actions to ensure timely resolution.

Events Employee Engagement

Plan and coordinate team meetings, offsites, workshops, training sessions, and employee engagement activities.

Manage event logistics, including scheduling, communications, attendee coordination, catering, and meeting materials.

Support leadership visits and regional onsite activities.

Coordinate event communications and post-event follow-up actions.

Office Operational Support

Manage office supplies and team-specific workplace requirements.

Coordinate onboarding and offboarding activities in partnership with HR, IT, Facilities, and other support teams.

Act as a key point of contact for employees regarding administrative, operational, and workplace-related queries.

Support the delivery of a positive and productive employee experience across the DTIS Europe organisation.

Process Improvement Coordination

Develop, maintain, and improve administrative processes, documentation, and operational trackers.

Identify opportunities to improve efficiency and simplify ways of working.

Maintain organisational records, team documentation, and knowledge repositories.

Coordinate activities across multiple stakeholders to ensure smooth operational delivery.

What we re looking for:

Proven experience in an Office Manager, Executive Assistant, Administrative Manager, Operations Coordinator, or similar role.

Strong organisational and time management skills, with the ability to manage multiple priorities simultaneously.

Excellent written and verbal communication skills.

High attention to detail and strong follow-through.

Proficiency with Microsoft Office 365, including Outlook, Teams, PowerPoint, and Excel.

Ability to work independently, exercise sound judgement, and maintain confidentiality.

Strong stakeholder management and relationship-building skills.
