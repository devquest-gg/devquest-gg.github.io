The Challenge

Global Procurement provides strategic sourcing, category management, and procurement operations expertise to support T2 and its labels.

A key member of the Global Procurement team, the Procurement Operations Associate is responsible for managing the day-to-day transactional activities, purchase orders and other operations tasks in the IT spend category.

What You’ll Take On

Review assigned purchase requisitions and contracts to ensure appropriate purchasing method/structure, authorization, and policy compliance

Create purchase orders in compliance with contract terms and manage the purchase order through its lifecycle

Act as the primary point-of-contact for APAC IT hardware and dev-kit logistics, providing real-time support to our studios in the region.

Manage and administer regional e-commerce purchasing catalogs (e.g., Amazon Business) across key APAC markets including India, South Korea, Australia, Japan, and Singapore.

Execute routine bid and buy transactions for standard IT hardware and software requests by soliciting quotes from multiple resellers, evaluating pricing, and conducting light commercial negotiations to drive cost savings.

Be accountable for researching and resolving Receiving and Accounts Payable issues for assigned purchase order and departments

Provide assistance, guidance, and training to stakeholders throughout the business on procurement related topics

Build strong relationships with our global partners and suppliers to foster collaboration, and open communication

Support process improvement and change initiatives across the procurement to pay cycle including adoption of the ServiceNow Source-to-Pay orchestration tool – Procurement Center

Maintain vital databases and other reports

Assist in identifying opportunities to reduce spending and minimize risk

What You Bring

Bachelor’s degree in Supply Chain, Finance, Business, or relevant field of study is preferred and 1-3 years of buying, supply chain, procurement, or other related experience

Demonstrated experience in managing a high volume of requests and providing related administrative support within a large organization

Strong analytical, oral, and written communication skills across global contexts

Comfortable working in a fast-paced environment

Experience with JD Edwards, ServiceNow and other P2P platforms is a plus
