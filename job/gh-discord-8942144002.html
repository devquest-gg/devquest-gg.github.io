Data Engineer

Discord · San Francisco Bay Area

Mid Engineering $160K–$200K Posted 2026-11-26 Full time
Apply on Discord’s site →

Discord has a highly engaged community of millions of daily active users who use the platform for many different reasons, but there’s one thing that nearly everyone does: play video games.

Discord plays a uniquely important role in the future of gaming, and we are focused on making it easier and more fun for people to hang out before, during, and after playing games.

Discord is looking for a Data Engineer to join our Data Engineering team and help build the trusted data foundations that power decisions across the company. Data Engineering is the foundational layer between Discord s raw data and the teams that depend on it: Data Science, ML, Product, Engineering, and business stakeholders. You ll design, build, and maintain clean, tested, well-documented datasets that those teams can discover, trust, and self-serve from.

You ll join one of two squads, Gaming or Ads , working alongside experienced data engineers and data scientists. This is a growth role: you ll take on well-scoped problems with guidance from senior teammates, and build toward owning a data domain end to end.

If you enjoy data modeling, SQL, and making reliable data easy for other teams to use, read on.

What You Will Be Doing

Build and maintain well-modeled, tested, and documented datasets in BigQuery using dbt, with guidance from senior data engineers

Take ownership of scoped pipelines, tables, and metric improvements within your squad, from requirements through delivery and documentation

Write accurate, efficient SQL and Python to transform structured and unstructured data into curated datasets for analytics, experimentation, and ML

Improve the reliability, freshness, and quality of existing datasets by adding tests, monitoring, and documentation

Partner with data scientists, product managers, and engineers to turn clear requests into concrete data models, and ask good questions when requests are ambiguous

Follow team standards for modeling, naming, version control, and CI/CD, and contribute to reusable assets such as shared tables and macros

Grow toward independently owning a domain: scoping, building, and driving adoption of your datasets

Build foundational work on our systems to improve the lives of your fellow Data Engineers and Data Builders.

Squad Focus :

Gaming : foundational gaming datasets, including third-party game data and user-to-game signals

Ads : datasets that support Ads measurement and analytics

What You Should Have

2+ years of experience in data engineering or analytics engineering building curated datasets

Experience designing and maintaining data models from structured and, ideally, unstructured sources

Strong SQL: you write accurate, readable, efficient queries

Working proficiency in Python or another programming language, plus version control and CI/CD fundamentals

Prior data engineering experience. Data Platform-only or general software engineering backgrounds are not a fit without hands-on DE work

Ability to translate clear business and product questions into well-modeled data, and to communicate clearly with technical and non-technical partners

A track record of taking scoped work from start to finish, and the curiosity and initiative to grow into broader ownership

A desire to work with passionate people who care about making Discord s data trustworthy and easy to use

Bonus Points

Passion for Discord or online communities

Experience with dbt, BigQuery, or similar modern analytics and data engineering tools

Experience building dashboards or reports in a BI tool such as Looker or Tableau

Experience designing data models that support experimentation or product analytics

Exposure to a semantic layer, metrics store, or shared metric definitions

Experience with data quality testing, monitoring, or documentation practices

Gaming: experience with third-party gaming data (such as SensorTower) or mobile measurement and attribution data

Ads: experience with ads, measurement, or attribution datasets
