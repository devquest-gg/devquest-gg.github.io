Tel-Aviv, Israel

Full-time

Senior

Description

Come and join a rapidly expanding mobile games company!

Who are we?

SuperPlay is a leading gaming company dedicated to creating unforgettable mobile gaming experiences. Founded in 2019 by industry experts from Playtika and Rovio, the company strives to develop innovative and exciting casual games that captivate millions of players worldwide and remain enjoyable for years to come.

Dice Dreams, our flagship game, has become a global sensation and a top-100-grossing hit, while Domino Dreams, our next big success, is also making a significant impact in the industry.

Our Newest Game – A Magical Collaboration with Disney! Released in 2025, Disney Solitaire brings beloved Disney stories and characters to life in an unforgettable gaming experience.

Our team, headquartered in Tel Aviv, is made up of talented, passionate, and ambitious professionals. And the future is even more exciting! Want to be part of our journey? Check out the job description below.

What will you do at SuperPlay?

We are looking for a Full-time, 2D After Effects Motion Graphics Artist to join our Animation Team.

This role requires someone who has a strong eye for design and must be able to jump into any project, at any phase of production.

Creating and delivering high-value motion graphics videos, animated video assets and mockups for the game.

Collaborating closely in the game’s creation process by interacting with the Animation Lead, Art Director and various departments while adapting to the game’s technical and production needs.

Editing video footage and adding effects/elements to enhance motion graphics working on 3D and 2D compositing environments.

Staying up to date on the latest post-production processes and techniques

Who are you?

2+ years of motion graphics design experience Advanced knowledge of Adobe Suite: After Effects and Photoshop.

Good knowledge of Trapcode Suite or similar plugins for particle simulation and 3D effects for motion graphics Experienced with animating and compositing 2D characters Follow brand standards for external clients, as well as internal game and partner teams Motivated, collaborative, exceptionally creative, organized, and desire to make every work their best! Effectively communicate ideas to a wide variety of team personnel, such as product managers, animation team, designers and more A team player with good communication skills and a personal sense of responsibility and ownership Must be detail-oriented and have the ability to work on multiple projects.

You get bonus points for:

Experience in 2D Animation Experience with Spine2D.

Does this describe you perfectly?
