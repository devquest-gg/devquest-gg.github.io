Job Overview

We are looking for a Full Stack Developer to join the World of Tanks Console team, with a strong focus on backend systems and live-service platform integration. While the role is titled "Full Stack," the day-to-day work is heavily weighted toward backend engineering - building and maintaining the services that support core gameplay systems, player data, and platform integrations across a live, high-traffic console title.

Reports to

Solution Architect

What will you do?

Design, build, and maintain backend services supporting live-service functionality for World of Tanks Console

Develop and integrate platform-specific authentication and services (Xbox, PlayStation Network, Steam)

Design and implement REST APIs used across internal and platform-facing systems

Work with distributed messaging systems to support scalable, event-driven architecture

Collaborate with cross-functional teams to ensure reliability and performance of production systems

Contribute to infrastructure and deployment practices using containerized and orchestrated environments

What are we looking for?

Strong proficiency in Python, including async Python (asyncio)

Experience with PostgreSQL and MySQL

Experience with RabbitMQ / AMQP messaging systems

Experience with Redis

Experience with Docker and Kubernetes

Strong understanding of REST API design principles

What additional skills will help you stand out?

Experience with Django

Experience with OAuth2 / JWT, particularly for platform authentication (Xbox, PSN, Steam)

Experience with Kafka

Experience with Elasticsearch

Familiarity with Twisted

Nice to have / can be developed on the job:

Experience with gaming platform APIs (Xbox Live, PSN, Steam)

Familiarity with Python 2.7

Work mode

Hybrid (3 days of work from the office).

About Wargaming

Wargaming is an award-winning online game developer and publisher headquartered in Nicosia, Cyprus. Operating since 1998, Wargaming has become one of the leaders in the gaming industry with 15 offices worldwide, including studios in Chicago, Prague, Shanghai, Tokyo, and Vilnius. Our diverse and multicultural team works together to deliver a top-class experience to millions of players who enjoy Wargaming s titles across all major gaming platforms. Our flagship products include free-to-play hits World of Tanks, World of Warships and World of Tanks Blitz.
