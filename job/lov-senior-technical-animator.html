Senior / Lead Technical Animator

Black Banner Studios · České Budějovice · Remote

Lead Animation Remote Posted 2026-11-20 Full time

Legacy of Valor is an ambitious medieval RPG and strategy game combining large-scale real-time battles, third-person melee combat, and tactical army control in Unreal Engine 5. At Black Banner Studios, we're looking for an exceptional Senior / Lead Technical Animator who combines outstanding character animation skills with deep technical expertise. Someone who can create beautiful, convincing character animations and independently develop the systems that bring them to life in a real-time game. Our proprietary Large Army technology already supports thousands of simultaneously animated soldiers. We are now working to further improve animation quality, responsiveness, and consistency, from close-up melee combat to massive battlefield encounters. A major challenge will be refining our split-body animation system, ensuring that independent upper-body and lower-body animations blend naturally, even at the hips or lower in the skeletal hierarchy. The goal is to eliminate foot sliding, popping, twisting, stretching, and other artifacts while maintaining convincing movement during complex combat interactions. You'll independently develop and validate animation solutions in Unreal Engine, working with programmers as needed to adapt them for LoV's GPU-driven Large Army system. You'll also contribute directly to animation production and help establish the quality standards for the project. We're open to candidates at both Senior and Lead levels, with the final title and responsibilities determined by experience and the scope of technical or artistic leadership. If you believe you'd be a great fit for this role, even if you don't meet every requirement listed below, we'd still love to hear from you. Please don't hesitate to apply! Create and polish high-quality character animations through keyframing, motion capture editing, and animation refinement, particularly for locomotion and melee combat. Independently build, test, and polish character animation setups in Unreal Engine, including idle, locomotion, transitions, and attacks during movement. Use Motion Matching, Animation Blueprints, layered blending, IK, foot locking, and procedural corrections to achieve natural, responsive character movement. Develop and validate split-body animation solutions, including seamless upper / lower-body blending, pelvis control, and consistent foot placement. Define animation authoring requirements, technical standards, and workflows that ensure reliable blending and high visual quality. Work closely with programmers to translate validated animation solutions into requirements for LoV's GPU-driven animation technology. Collaborate with and guide other animators while contributing directly to ongoing animation production. Extensive professional experience in character and technical animation for games, including significant contributions to at least one shipped commercial title. Excellent artistic animation skills, demonstrated through a strong portfolio showing convincing body mechanics, weight, balance, and timing. Strong keyframe animation skills and practical experience with motion capture cleanup, editing, and retargeting. Proficiency with professional animation tools such as Maya, MotionBuilder, or Blender. Advanced hands-on experience with Unreal Engine animation systems and the ability to independently implement and refine complete character animation setups. Demonstrated ability to independently create, implement, test, and polish complete real-time animation setups without routine programmer assistance. Deep understanding of skeletal animation, joint hierarchies, root motion, pelvis movement, locomotion, foot placement, and IK. Strong problem-solving skills, including the ability to distinguish between animation asset, rigging, blending, and runtime issues. Passion for video games, particularly medieval RPGs, open-world games, and strategy titles. Experience with Lyra or comparable character animation and locomotion frameworks in Unreal Engine. Experience with split-body animation, combat locomotion, procedural animation, Control Rig, Full Body IK, or motion warping. Experience with character rigging, skinning, and animation pipeline development. Experience optimizing animation for large crowds, including LOD-based animation, GPU-driven systems, or Vertex Animation Textures (VAT). Experience with mounted combat, complex character interactions, or multiple character types. Experience working on AAA and / or open-world game projects. Scripting or automation experience for animation production workflows. Location: Remote / International, with the option to work in the Czech Republic. Engagement: Full-time, long-term collaboration. Contract: Contractor / B2B worldwide, or direct employment for candidates residing in or willing to relocate to the Czech Republic. Start Date: As soon as mutually agreed. Language: Professional working proficiency in English. Compensation: Competitive, based on experience and expertise.
