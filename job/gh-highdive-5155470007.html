Senior Gameplay Animator

Our team is growing, and we are looking for a highly motivated, talented and experienced Senior Gameplay Animator to help us lay the groundwork for our original Unreal5 project. The person we are looking for will create industry leading AAA characters/creature’s animations and help set artistic benchmark!

You will be responsible for the creation of high-quality key-frame and mocap animation assets, while also overseeing the integration and upkeep of animation within the game. Furthermore, you’ll take the responsibility for understanding the animation pipeline and the processes, spanning from the conceptual phase through implementation. and contributing ideas to support its improvement. Gameplay animators in our studio are expected to work very closely with Game Designers, Programmers and Animation TDs.

If you have a great collaborative spirit, like to get involved and look for an exceptional opportunity to leave your mark on a completely new IP, come join us!

Responsibilities:

Work closely with Animation Director, Game Designers, Programmers and Animation TDs to create high quality Stylized Animation assets that support and enhance gameplay

Assist the Animation Director in defining and realizing their creative vision

In charge of animation production, integration, maintenance, and suggesting innovative solutions to enhance animation systems in Unreal 5

Document and share your knowledge often and freely with other members of the team

Be a role model with the drive to help others succeed with a positive attitude

Qualifications:

At least 7 years of documented experience in 3D animation

Excellent knowledge of Motion Builder and/or Maya

Excellent knowledge of animation principles and strong keyframe animation skills

Experience in both realistic and stylized animation for bipedal / quadruped characters and other organic and inorganic forms

Knowledge of motion capture editing

Strong problem-solving skills

Experienced in creating animations for real-time game engines, with a solid understanding of technical constraints and optimization techniques

Proven proactivity in taking initiatives and driving projects forward

Outstanding creativity and very strong commitment to a project

Ability to mentor Mid and Junior Animators

Ability to effectively collaborate and excellent communication skill

Gameplay combat experience is preferable

Why could we be the perfect place for you:

We offer the unique experience of being part of the foundation of a new international game team right in the heart of Montreal, which benefits from the rich diversity of its members and the cultural exchange with our partners in Hangzhou.

We also offer:

- Insurance coverage and telemedicine from day one,

- Transportation and meal allowance,

- Referral bonus,

- RRSP with employer contribution.

Join us to work on an ambitious project, in a unique ‘Start-Up Atmosphere’ and with a team of passionate and energetic game developers!
