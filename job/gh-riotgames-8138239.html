Senior Illustration Artist - Riftbound

Riot Games · Los Angeles

Senior Art $150K–$210K Posted 2026-08-25 Full time
Apply on Riot Games’s site →

As part of the Illustration team, you’ll put stylus to Cintiq and create promotional art that represents the essence of a Champion, thematic, or gameplay to millions of players. You will use your artistic eye and experience with dynamic lighting, form, and color to tell concise and evocative stories. By working with a team of dedicated illustrators, visual designers, writers, animators, and game developers, you will help decide and illustrate each character’s unique place in our universe.

As a Senior Illustration Artist on the Riftbound team, you will play an important role in delivering high-quality card illustrations. Working closely with the Art Director and cross-disciplinary partners, you will help plan, brief, and deliver card art across multiple future sets, ensuring each illustration supports the creative vision, gameplay needs, and production standards. You will create clear art briefs and provide thoughtful, actionable feedback to external artists, guiding artwork from early concept through final, print-ready delivery. Drawing on a strong understanding of illustration fundamentals and visual storytelling, you will help maintain a consistent quality bar for Riftbound and contribute to creating memorable imagery within the League of Legends universe. Additionally, while working within the constraints of physical trading card production, you will apply your knowledge of print processes, color management, and readability to help ensure artwork reproduces beautifully and consistently at scale. You will collaborate closely with designers to ensure art reinforces gameplay goals, and with visual designers to ensure illustrations complement frames, typography, and other graphic elements. You will translate creative direction into clear, actionable briefs and artist-facing documentation, provide craft guidance and feedback to external partners, and collaborate with producers and other disciplines to deliver cohesive, compelling card sets. You will report to the project s Art Director.

Responsibilities:

Create clear, compelling art briefs that translate Riftbound’s artistic vision into actionable direction for external illustrators

Provide thoughtful, craft-focused feedback to external artists, helping guide illustrations from concept through final delivery

Apply strong judgment in illustration fundamentals, composition, color, and storytelling to elevate artwork quality

Collaborate closely with the Art Director to understand, uphold, and contribute to Riftbound’s illustration art direction

Help maintain consistency and quality across artwork while identifying opportunities to improve individual illustrations

Ensure card art supports gameplay clarity, mechanics, and readability in close partnership with game designers

Work with visual designers to ensure illustrations complement frames, typography, and other graphic elements

Review artwork for consistency, polish, and fidelity to the League of Legends IP

Support the planning and delivery of card art across multiple future sets, helping ensure creative goals and schedules are met

Apply knowledge of print production, color management, and physical card constraints to deliver print-ready artwork

Partner cross-functionally with producers, designers, external artists, and other disciplines to deliver cohesive, globally appealing card sets

Share craft knowledge and provide feedback to teammates and external partners, contributing to a collaborative and supportive art team

Required Qualifications:

6+ years of professional experience in illustration, or a related creative discipline

Experience working with providing clear, actionable, and constructive artistic feedback for external vendors or outsourcing partners

Strong illustration craft skills with a portfolio that demonstrates a broad range of artistic styles

Strong foundation in illustration fundamentals, including anatomy, composition, lighting, color theory, and visual storytelling

Experience creating clear creative briefs and working within established style guides and visual frameworks

Experience with production pipelines for physical goods and print, including vendor collaboration and asset delivery

Experience working within video game development pipelines, from concept through final implementation

Ability to balance creative quality, production requirements, feedback, and deadlines across multiple pieces of artwork

Desired Qualifications:

BA in illustration, fine arts, or a related field (or equivalent practical experience)

Knowledge of print processes, color management, and physical production constraints

Familiarity with the League of Legends universe and its visual language

For this role, you ll find success through craft expertise, a collaborative spirit, and decision-making that prioritizes the delight of players. We will be looking at your past studies, experience, and your personal relationship with games. If you embody player empathy and care about players experiences, this could be your role!
