Sr Accountant (Contract)

6-month contract

San Mateo, CA (Collab)

Role Summary:

We are seeking an experienced and proactive Senior Accountant (Contractor) to support our accounting team, with a primary focus on business partnering, process improvements, and accounting oversight across Fixed Assets, Leases, and Intangible Assets.

The ideal candidate will have strong technical accounting knowledge, excellent communication skills, detail oriented and demonstrated experience managing multiple priorities, meet tight deadlines and collaborate effectively across teams

What You’ll Be Doing:

Fixed Assets, Leases Intangible Assets Accounting

Ø Provide accounting oversight for Fixed Assets, Construction in Progress (CIP), Leases, and Intangible Assets.

Ø Review capitalization assessments to ensure compliance with company accounting policies and applicable IFRS requirements.

Ø Coordinate with Facilities and relevant stakeholders to identify lease additions, modifications, terminations, impairments, and sublease arrangements.

Ø Support month-end and quarter-end close activities, including reconciliations, journal entry preparation and reviews, and variance analysis.

Business Partnering Stakeholder Management

Ø Act as a primary accounting contact for business partners across Facilities, IT, Corporate Planning, Procurement, AP and other functional teams to understand upcoming projects, capital expenditures, lease changes, and technology implementations that may have accounting implications.

Ø Provide guidance to business stakeholders on capitalization policies, expense recognition, asset useful lives, and other accounting requirements.

Ø Proactively communicate accounting requirements, deadlines, and process changes to business partners.

Ø Collaborate with Technical Accounting on complex transactions requiring additional analysis or interpretation.

Process Improvement Operational Excellence

Ø Evaluate existing accounting processes for Fixed Assets, Leases, and Intangible Assets to identify inefficiencies, control gaps, and opportunities for automation.

Ø Lead process improvement initiatives to streamline workflows, reduce manual activities, and improve the accuracy and timeliness of financial reporting.

Ø Develop and maintain standardized procedures, process documentation, checklists, and internal controls.

Outsourced Accounting Team Oversight

Ø Provide day-to-day oversight and guidance to the outsourced accounting team responsible for Fixed Assets, Leases, and related accounting activities.

Ø Review deliverables prepared by the outsourced team to ensure completeness, accuracy, and adherence to established timelines.

Ø Serve as an escalation point for accounting questions, unresolved issues, and operational challenges.

Financial Reporting, Controls Compliance

Ø Support timely and accurate month-end, quarter-end, and year-end financial close activities.

Ø Ensure compliance with IFRS accounting standards, including IAS 16, IAS 38, IFRS 16, and applicable impairment guidance.

Ø Review balance sheet reconciliations and supporting schedules to ensure accuracy and completeness.

Ø Support internal and external audit requests by preparing documentation and coordinating with relevant stakeholders.

What We’re Looking For:

Ø Bachelor s degree in Accounting, Finance, or equivalent. CPA/CA or similar preferred

Ø Minimum of 5–7 years of progressive accounting experience, preferably within a multinational organization.

Ø Strong understanding of general ledger accounting, financial close processes, and balance sheet reconciliations.

Ø Experience with Fixed Assets, Lease Accounting, and Intangible Assets accounting. Working knowledge of IFRS, including IAS 16, IAS 38, IFRS 16, and IAS 36.

Ø Recent SAP/S4 HANA experience strongly preferred.

Ø Demonstrated ability to partner effectively with business stakeholders and cross-functional teams.

Ø Experience identifying, implementing, and driving accounting process improvements.

Ø Advances excel skills

Ø Ability to work in a fast-paced environment within very tight deadlines.

Ø Excellent analytical skills with ability to research independently and efficiently.
