About us:

Deep expertise. Personal and Industry evolution. Impeccable craft. These are Limit Break’s founding principles.

We combine the power of technology, and creative vision to create experiences that connect people from all corners of the globe.

About the Role: You will be responsible for VFX production on live-service titles, leveraging your high level of expertise and hands-on experience to drive improvements in visual quality.

Responsibilities:

Collaborate with art directors and concept artists to establish VFX style and quality standards appropriate to the title

Lead the production of VFX assets, including effects, simulations, textures, and materials

Manage tasks, coordinate schedules, mentor staff, and create documentation for internal and external VFX teams

Collaborate with engineers and technical artists to build and improve the VFX production pipeline

Investigate and resolve VFX-related technical issues that arise in DCC tools and game engines

Optimize assets and manage quality with the aim of balancing visual quality and performance

Qualifications:

5+ years of practical experience in VFX production for game development, or equivalent skills and experience

Experience presenting VFX expression policies and production direction, and proactively leading a team's production

Experience driving quality improvement across the whole team through instructing, reviewing, and providing feedback to members

Deep understanding of timing, motion, silhouette, weight, and volume

Proficiency in asset production using industry-standard DCC tools (Maya, Houdini, Photoshop, etc.)

Practical experience with real-time VFX using Unreal Engine, Unity, or a proprietary game engine

Solid understanding of performance budgets and technical constraints for optimized content production

High level of cross-departmental collaboration skills, and the ability to proactively drive production while grasping the team's situation

Nice to Have:

Experience profiling and optimizing assets with consideration for memory and performance

Broad interest in real-time rendering technology and asset production tools in general

Experience developing and introducing VFX production tools and automation tools

Experience with procedural content production

Experience establishing VFX quality standards and regulations
