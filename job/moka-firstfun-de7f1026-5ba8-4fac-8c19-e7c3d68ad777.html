About the Role We are seeking an AI-Native Game Developer who is passionate about integrating state-of-the-art artificial intelligence into game development. In this role, you will leverage Generative AI (AIGC & LLMs), Reinforcement Learning, Computer Vision, and other AI technologies to innovate game design, content creation, and iteration.

You will help shape an AI-native game development pipeline and drive automation across game design, asset creation, QA testing, CI/CD, production, publishing, and marketing. Your work will push the boundaries of what is possible in modern mobile game development.

Key Responsibilities: Apply state-of-the-art AI innovations to redefine mobile game development, including gameplay iteration, game design, and QA infrastructure that supports long-term game growth.

Continuously research emerging AI technologies, proactively experiment, track progress, and apply AI solutions and techniques to enhance game development workflows and productivity.

Research and apply Generative AI models to enable content generation.

Utilize machine learning techniques to optimize level design, enemy AI strategies, and automated game testing.

Design and develop AI-powered internal tools to improve development efficiency.

Collaborate closely with game designers, artists, and production teams to explore and implement AI-native innovations in game development.

Required Qualifications: Bachelor’s degree or higher in Computer Science, Artificial Intelligence, Game Development, or a related field.

Strong self-motivation and curiosity in AI, with an open mindset toward adopting AI in game development and the ability to independently explore, learn, and apply AI techniques in practical scenarios.

Familiarity with AI fundamentals and infrastructure, with hands-on experience in AI-generated content.

Solid game development experience, familiarity with Unity, and proficiency in C#, Python, and Lua.

Understanding of Generative AI applications in game development.

Knowledge of reinforcement learning algorithms and generative models for game AI design.

Excellent communication and cross-team collaboration skills, with the ability to work effectively with designers, artists, and production teams.

Passion for games and a strong desire to innovate at the intersection of AI and game development.

Preferred Qualifications: Experience developing games using AI-generated content (AIGC) and AI-assisted coding tools.

Proven experience leading or contributing to AI-native game projects or building AI-native game prototypes.

Knowledge of AI-powered 2D asset creation, 3D modeling, and animation techniques for game development.

Publications, talks, or open-source contributions related to AI, machine learning, or game development.
