Every day, tens of millions of people come to Roblox to explore, create, play, learn, and connect with friends in 3D immersive digital experiences– all created by our global community of developers and creators.

At Roblox, we’re building the tools and platform that empower our community to bring any experience that they can imagine to life. Our vision is to reimagine the way people come together, from anywhere in the world, and on any device.

We’re on a mission to connect a billion people with optimism and civility, and looking for amazing talent to help us get there.

A career at Roblox means you’ll be working to shape the future of human interaction, solving unique technical challenges at scale, and helping to create safer, more civil shared experiences for everyone.

As a Backend Engineer, you will have the independence, opportunity, and end-to-end responsibility to help us build a critical, complex set of backend systems that support the creators of games and other experiences within our Roblox ecosystem.

The Creator Success Team empowers Roblox to be the best development platform for creating 3D social experiences, allowing our creators to publish compelling content within the Roblox ecosystem. This includes services and solutions around the Creator Hub, Developer Forum, and Creator Dashboard. You will create and own mission-critical services in this cross-discipline team within our Roblox ecosystem.

As a team, we believe in fast iteration and are biased toward action while keeping the long view. We have numerous exciting projects ahead of us for the years to come, including but not limited to:

Build ML-driven services and large scale data pipeline to drive player insights and optimise monetization opportunities for creators

Build next generation communication platform providing creators with powerful tools to manage the player community, organize virtual events, and collect player feedback

Build a state-of-the-art remote configuration/experiment platform to enable the continued growth of games with the world s largest concurrent user(CCU)

You Are:

Experienced in industry: An outstanding software engineer with 8+ years of industry experience

Experienced with Distributed Systems: You are deeply familiar with and regularly work with concepts like stream processing, data pipeline, queue, RPC, retry strategies, etc

Experienced with native code : You have worked on deep and challenging problems in complex networked codebases, with 3+ years of experience in C#, Java, Go, or any other object-oriented programming language

Platforms : You can jump with ease between Windows, OS X, Linux, Android, iOS, and other platforms. You understand where platform-specific code is relevant.

Responsible: You build systems with proper monitoring, logging, operations, and automated testing in mind.

Teamwork: A team player and love collaborating with others to push the boundaries and challenge the status quo

You Will:

Lead technical decision-making and guide the development team.

Lead engineering and development for our services and systems at a massive scale

Collaborate with cross-functional and cross-team leaders to push the boundary of what is possible

Self-organize and take ownership of projects throughout their full lifecycle.
