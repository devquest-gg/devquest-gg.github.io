Every day, tens of millions of people come to Roblox to explore, create, play, learn, and connect with friends in 3D immersive digital experiences– all created by our global community of developers and creators.

At Roblox, we’re building the tools and platform that empower our community to bring any experience that they can imagine to life. Our vision is to reimagine the way people come together, from anywhere in the world, and on any device.

We’re on a mission to connect a billion people with optimism and civility, and looking for amazing talent to help us get there.

A career at Roblox means you’ll be working to shape the future of human interaction, solving unique technical challenges at scale, and helping to create safer, more civil shared experiences for everyone.

About the role:

Millions of Roblox creators depend on Studio, Roblox’s creation engine, and Creator Hub, a unification of developer tools and resources, to bring their creative vision to life. As the Senior Product Designer for Creator Analytic, you will design solutions to help creators gain a better understanding of their games. You know how to turn complex pieces of data into visualizations and actionable insights that are easy to comprehend. You’ll be able to apply your expertise from similar problem spaces to make analytics on the platform engaging, accessible, and delightful. This is a high visibility role, responsible for defining the core experiences that promote engagement on our platform.

You will design and build end-to-end UX journeys, working collaboratively with other team leads across product, research, data science, and engineering. You will bring previous relevant experience or become a domain expert through this role, understanding user needs and designing innovative design solutions. You will report to the Senior Design Manager for Creator Experiences.

You will:

Craft end-to-end experiences, define UX strategy, and bring in new perspectives and deep industry knowledge.

Deeply care about craft by creating visually compelling and intuitive user interfaces across devices for a global audience.

Partner with product and engineering to drive design discussions and proactively provide insights, in addition to design specs and partnering on implementation to deliver a high-quality, high-craft experience

Leverage analytics and user feedback to identify pain points and implement data-driven improvements.

Present design journeys and strategies at the team and group level.

You have:

Proven experience (5+ years) in product design, with a strong track record of delivery creator and game platform tooling.

A portfolio demonstrating exceptional interaction and visual design craft, paired with strong product, systems, and strategic thinking.

Strong proficiency in design tooling, such as Figma, and confidence in AI prototyping and concepting in everyday workflows in their designs.

End-to-end systems thinking that advocates for holistic and connected user experiences.

Ability to navigate ambiguity, influence cross-functional decisions, and balance user needs with business goals and technical constraints.

Skills to problem solve and think critically, and develop a principled approach towards a high quality solution.

A passion for immersive platforms, gaming, user-generated content, or tools that enable people to create and express themselves.
