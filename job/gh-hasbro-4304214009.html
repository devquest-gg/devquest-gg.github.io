We are seeking a Product Quality Assurance Engineer to supervise the quality, integrity, and consistency of printed cards, packaging, and game products. This person will collaborate consistently alongside Product Engineering, R D, Brand/Marketing, and Hasbro Far East offices.

This position is a hybrid role based out of the Renton, WA office location. On-site Tuesday-Thursday.

What You ll Do:

• Develop and implement quality assurance procedures/protocols to ensure consistent product quality throughout the manufacturing process. • Collaborate with design, engineering, manufacturing, and supply chain teams to establish quality criteria and specifications for new products. • Conduct detailed inspections and testing of raw materials, components, and finished products to verify compliance. • Analyze data and performance metrics to identify trends, patterns, and areas for improvement in manufacturing processes. • Investigate customer complaints or product returns and implementing corrective and preventive actions. • Supervise production processes and equipment to identify potential sources of defects or deviations from quality standards, proposing and implementing solutions to mitigate risks. • Work with suppliers and vendors to ensure the reliability of incoming materials used in production. • Document and maintain detailed records of quality inspections, test results, and corrective actions taken. • Provide training and mentorship to supply chain partners on procedures, standard processes, and quality control techniques. • Stay informed about industry developments, technological advancements, and regulatory changes.

What You ll Bring:

• Bachelor s degree or 3-5 years equivalent experience in engineering, quality assurance, or a related field. • Previous experience in quality assurance/control, or a similar role within a manufacturing or production environment. • Solid understanding of quality management systems, quality standards (e.g., ISO 9001), and regulatory requirements relevant to the industry. • Experience in chemical safety testing requirements and CPSC regulations. • Global regulatory labeling experience. • Proficiency in QA tools and methodologies, such as statistical process control (SPC), failure mode and effects analysis (FMEA), and root cause analysis (RCA). • Excellent analytical and problem-solving skills, with the ability to interpret data, identify trends, and implement effective solutions. • Effective communication and interpersonal skills along with ability to effectively work with multi-functional teams and communicate complex technical concepts to non-technical collaborators. • Attention to detail and a dedication to upholding high standards of quality and excellence. • Certifications such as Certified Quality Engineer (CQE) or Six Sigma Green Belt/Black Belt may be advantageous.
