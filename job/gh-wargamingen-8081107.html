Job Overview

We are looking for a passionate Product Manager to join its team working on World of Warships (WoWS).In this role, you will contribute to WoWS’s success in China by effectively implementing live operation, production, content strategy, and the 3 rd Party Publishing(3PP) brand guideline.

You will work closely with World of Warships franchise team and learn hands-on experience of partnership management as well as negotiation skills. You will also join a stable team with a healthy work-life harmony Our team motto is "Focus on impact, not just hours," where we set clear boundaries between work and life harmony.

Reports to

This position will report to the Publishing Producer, China

What will you do?

Work with World of Warships China Product Team on all aspects of the live ops and production in China, to contribute to the content roadmap for WoWS China, proposing new content formats and ideas to keep publishing assets fresh and engaging, in line with the brand guideline

Work with Publishing Producer and Product Manager, China, to Support day to day partnership management with 360, coordinating messaging and creative direction across digital, CRM, and campaign contents

Coordinating communication and initiatives with internal stakeholders and external partners to review and proofread publishing/marketing assets

Work closely with the Global content team and external partners to check and tailor the content production in the early stage

Supporting and executing live operation, patch releases

Provide review reports for content production and source to identify key issues and potential risks

Travel occasionally (approximately 1-2 times per year) to meet with partners and attend business meetings

What are we looking for?

2~5 years’ experience in relevant fields such as Product management, Contents Management or Marketing in the gaming or relevant industries such as entertainment, media and platform

Ability to work both independently and as part of a team.

Ability to work in a fast-paced , changing, multicultural environment

Ability to understand different sets of product data to make a data-driven decision and strategy

Understanding of Chinese culture and cultural sensitivity to support licensing and content strategy

Business level in Chinese and ability to communicate clearly and concisely, both verbally and in writing, in English

Excellent interpersonal and communication skills to collaborate with local partners and regional teams and global teams

Eagle eye for content review

What additional skills will help you stand out?

Bachelor’s degree in marketing, business, journalism, or relevant disciplines

Experience in 3rd Party Publishing Business model

Familiarity with Wargaming products

Hands-on Mobile app operation experience

Experience in product analytics such as tableau and google analytics

Business acumen to connect dots and synthesize information from various sources for holistic decision making

About Wargaming

Wargaming is an award-winning online game developer and publisher headquartered in Nicosia, Cyprus. Operating since 1998, Wargaming has become one of the leaders in the gaming industry with 15 offices worldwide, including studios in Chicago, Prague, Shanghai, Tokyo, and Vilnius. Our diverse and multicultural team works together to deliver a top-class experience to millions of players who enjoy Wargaming s titles across all major gaming platforms. Our flagship products include free-to-play hits World of Tanks, World of Warships and World of Tanks Blitz.
