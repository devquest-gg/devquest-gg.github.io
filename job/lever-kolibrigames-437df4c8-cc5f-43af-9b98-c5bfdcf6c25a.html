UI/UX Designer (f/m/d)

We are looking for a UI/UX Designer to work on our hit game ‘Idle Bank Tycoon’.

You will be part of our IBT Team, contributing to a game with over 60 million downloads. In this role, you will design screens, flows, and prototypes, conduct playtests, and refine user-centric designs. Working in an agile, fast-paced environment, you’ll help identify and develop future features by gaining a deep understanding of our player needs and motivations. By leveraging data, player insights, and F2P expertise, you’ll ensure the game remains intuitive, visually polished, and highly engaging - always keeping the player experience at the heart of every decision.
