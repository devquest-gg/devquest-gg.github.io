---------------------- WB Games Montreal, a division of Warner Bros. Interactive Entertainment (WBIE), seeks an Advanced Technical Animator.

Your New Role…  In this role, you review and oversee the work of external skinning and rigging partners who deliver high-quality character assets, and you are a recognized leading contributor at the studio .

Reporting to the Lead Technical Artist, you keep the quality of outsourced character skinning high and consistent with the goals set by the Lead Technical Artist and the Directors, while meeting the demands of the production schedule.

You manage the tracking and flow of a large volume of outsourced work , and you bring ideas to the team on how to improve the skinning pipeline and scale it to handle expanded scope.

While the role focuses mainly on reviewing external development, you are also an excellent technical animator with strong rigging and skeletal mesh skinning skills of your own , ready to get hands-on to finish assets on time and complete bug fixes before content ships.

You have strong experience working with external partners , strong communication skills and the ability to give clear artistic and technical feedback . You also mentor junior artists and research, develop and recommend new art tech and design that pushes the studio s quality bar and pipelines forward.

Your Role Accountabilities...

Review the work of external partners and clearly provide artistic and technical feedback on rigging and skeletal mesh skinning quality, technical requirements and build metrics.

Manage character assets and tasks, tracking the flow of outsourced work so that deliveries meet quality goals and the production schedule.

Assist outsourcing partners with technical issues.

Provide skinning support through Jira, including hands-on skinning work and bug fixes before content ships.

Research, develop and propose pipeline improvements that increase efficiency and scale the skinning pipeline to handle expanded scope.

Make day-to-day decisions that balance character skinning quality with the production schedule, using best judgement within the goals set by the Lead Technical Artist and Directors.

Research, develop, and present new methods, techniques, and tools that push our art quality to the next level.

Actively participate in peer reviews, meetings, mentor other artists on team.

Qualifications & Experience…  8 years of progressively complex related experience in the industry.

At least 3 games, at least two of them published, preferably including at least one Xbox Series X|S or PS5 title.

5 years of experience with Maya and skeletal mesh skinning and rigging.

Experience with Unreal Engine is required.

Strong ability and experience in working with outsourcing partners toward the creation of high-quality character assets that meet technical requirements.

Strong technical troubleshooting skills within Maya and Unreal.

An excellent eye for artistic quality, with the ability to help others achieve high-quality results.

Strong experience and understanding of character art and animation pipelines.

Ability to research and develop forward-thinking technology and tools, and to adapt rapidly to new or changing technology with an interest in evolving and improving the skinning pipeline.

Must have an active, passionate interest in current video games to help drive the game to the next level.

Solid organization skills with adherence to file structures, naming conventions and other established protocols.

Excellent ability to self-manage and accurately predict and adhere to task length estimates and deadlines.

Must be an excellent collaborator, coordinating and working with teams both inside and outside of the studio, with a willingness to provide and accept direction and the ability to embrace change.

Ability to clearly articulate ideas both in writing and verbally.

Other Requirements  Python and tool-building knowledge is a nice to have.

Portfolio or demo reel demonstrating rigging and skinning quality, technical problem solving and attention to detail.
