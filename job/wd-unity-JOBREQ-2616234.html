The opportunity Our Customer QA team is looking for a passionate new member to help deliver high-quality services to our users! In this role, you will be the vital connection between customers, quality assurance, development, and other teams, ensuring timely, polite, and accurate responses to bug reports. Your responsibilities will include reproducing customer-reported issues and documenting them thoroughly for further investigation. This opportunity allows you to deepen your knowledge of the Unity Engine by working with bug reports across various Editor features, contribute to improving the Engine used by millions worldwide, and build a strong foundation for your future career—whether within Unity or beyond. Additionally, you’ll join a team of like-minded colleagues who share similar hobbies and interests, making every day more enjoyable! What you’ll be doing Work with customers to reproduce their bug reports in-house Explore the scope of customer bug reports and provide more detailed advice to developers Collaborate with other testers and developers in an agile environment You will be collaborating with multiple partners What we’re looking for You must currently be enrolled in a relevant Bachelor or Master’s degree program in Lithuania Experience with an object-oriented programming language such as C# You should be self-motivated and able to work independently You should be confident in communicating with colleagues and customers and have a good level of English You might also have Experience using Unity Experience with other Game Engines, other type of Editors (video, image, 3Dmodel) Additional information Relocation support is not available for this position Work visa/immigration sponsorship is not available for this position Gross pay monthly: EUR € 1664.30  This range reflects the anticipated base salary for this position. Beyond base salary, this role may be eligible for equity awards and participation in our company incentive plans (such as annual discretionary bonuses or sales commissions).
