Who We Are

Founded in 2023, Small Axe Studios is 2K’s newest stand-alone studio, built to push the boundaries of game development and bring 2K’s next groundbreaking sports franchise to life. Headquartered in Burnaby, we are assembling a world-class team of visionaries, builders, and passionate gamers who thrive on creative collaboration, technical excellence, and bold ideas. At Small Axe Studios, we believe big things start with sharp focus—and we’re crafting an exceptional, player-first experience from the ground up. If you’re ready to tackle unique challenges, leave your mark on a brand-new IP, and help shape the future of interactive sports entertainment, we want you on our team!

What We Need

Do you see UI as more than menus and icons—as a core part of a game’s identity and experience

Do you see UI as more than menus and icons — as the connective tissue between player intent and player experience?

What You’ll Do

Design

Own and maintain the atomic design system based in Figma.

Design and refine player flows, wireframes, interaction patterns, and information hierarchies that improve clarity, usability, and comprehension.

Translate complex player needs and gameplay systems into intuitive, elegant UX solutions.

Iterate on designs and solutions that enhance usability, clarity, and emotional impact.

Guide adaptation of core gameplay, controls, and menu experiences across multiple platforms and input methods, ensuring parity and quality across all environments.

Visual Art

Help define the game s UI visual style in partnership with the Art Director and Design team.

Translate wireframes and UX systems into refined, visually cohesive interfaces.

Build final assets, iconography, motion graphics, and mockups that balance form and function.

Collaboration

Collaborate across Design, Art, and Engineering disciplines to bring ideas from concept to shippable feature.

Provide feedback in reviews and help maintain visual and UX consistency across the product.

Stay current on trends in games, apps, and visual design to bring fresh thinking to the team.

What Will Make You A Great Fit

Experience shipping at least one AAA game or equivalent project.

Strong portfolio showing a range of UI Art work and or UX Design work.

Skilled in Figma using components for Atomic Design structures.

Skilled in Photoshop, Illustrator, and After Effects (or equivalent) for UI asset creation and motion design.

Comfortable working across teams - Design, Art, Engineering -to bring ideas to life.

Strong communicator who gives and receives feedback with clarity and respect.

Deep curiosity about games and a thoughtful approach to how UI supports the player experience.

Beneficial Qualifications

Background in graphic design, layout, and gestalt principles.

Familiarity with implementing UI in Unreal Engine (UMG), including Blueprints, animations, and materials.

Experience with UI shaders/materials and 2D FX for game engines.
