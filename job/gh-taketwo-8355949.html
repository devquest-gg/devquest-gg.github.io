THE CHALLENGE

We are seeking a highly skilled and polished Executive IT Support Engineer to provide direct, white-glove technical support to our executive leadership team. This individual must be as comfortable managing critical, high-stakes support situations as they are scripting advanced solutions or solve complex infrastructure issues.

The ideal candidate thrives in fast-paced environments, maintains calm under pressure, and can translate deep technical knowledge into seamless executive experiences. We entrust you with ensuring that C-suite leaders stay securely connected and fully operational—no matter the environment, platform, or challenge.

WHAT YOU’LL TAKE ON

Executive-Facing Technical Support

Deliver elite, responsive, and personalized support to executives across devices, applications, and remote or in-office locations.

Act as a trusted technology advisor to senior leadership, ensuring minimal disruption and proactive issue prevention.

Set up and maintain executive computing environments (Mac/Windows/Linux), ensuring secure and efficient performance.

Advanced Problem Solving Root Cause Resolution

Lead diagnostics and resolution for high-impact incidents across hardware, software, network, and platforms.

Address sophisticated technical issues, advancing only when necessary, and providing quick resolution with clear communication.

Provide post-incident analysis, trends, and recommendations to prevent recurrence.

Infrastructure Networking Support

Support network access, VPN, wireless, and remote connectivity for C-suite offices and offsite engagements.

Collaborate with infrastructure teams on configurations, server access, and system dependencies impacting executive workflows.

Ensure secure access to internal and cloud-based platforms while maintaining data integrity and compliance.

Automation Scripting

Use scripting (e.g., PowerShell, Bash, Python) to automate frequent support tasks, streamline deployments, and reduce manual errors.

Build and maintain tools or scripts to monitor performance and enhance proactive support.

Mobility, AV, and Conference Technology

Manage mobile device setup, policy compliance, and synchronization (iOS/Android).

Support executive video conferencing (Zoom, Teams, Webex) and in-office AV systems with a high degree of reliability and discretion.

WHAT YOU BRING

Required Skills Experience

5+ years in a high-level desktop support or systems engineering role, with at least 2 years supporting executive leadership.

Expert-level solutioning across macOS, Windows, and mobile platforms.

Strong networking knowledge: TCP/IP, VPN, DHCP, DNS, Wi-Fi, remote access tools.

Proficiency in one or more scripting languages (e.g., PowerShell, Bash, Python).

Familiarity with infrastructure systems: servers, authentication services (Active Directory, Okta), cloud platforms (e.g., Office 365, G Suite, AWS/Azure).

Experience with endpoint management tools (Jamf, Intune, SCCM, etc.).

Soft Skills

Outstanding discretion and integrity in high-trust environments.

Strong verbal and written communication; able to explain technical issues clearly to non-technical audiences.

Calm, resourceful, and respectful under pressure.

Proactive approach with the ability to anticipate and prevent issues before they intensify.

Preferred Qualifications

Certifications such as CompTIA Network+, Microsoft Certified: Modern Desktop Administrator Associate, or Apple Certified Support Professional.

Previous experience working in a creative or fast-paced corporate environment.

Background supporting hybrid or remote-first executive teams.
