Nex is on a mission to help families rediscover the joy of movement. Created by parents for parents, Nex combines technology and play to deliver fun, social, and interactive experiences powered by natural body motion, encouraging kids and adults to move more, play more, and have fun together. Nex Playground, the company’s award-winning active play system, is purpose-built to get families moving year-round, with safety and privacy as core considerations in its intentional design. It is certified kidSAFE+ COPPA compliant and built to support healthy, active play for all ages and abilities.

Nex Playground features a growing library of 50+ experiences, including motion and dance games, fitness and educational experiences, and Nex Originals. Content includes collaborations with partners like Hasbro, Sesame Workshop, and NBCUniversal. Nex has been recognized by Fast Company’s Most Innovative Companies, TIME’s Best Inventions, and Parents’ Best Entertainment System for Families, and has earned Red Dot, IDEA, and Core77 international design awards. We encourage you to explore Have Fun and Is Motion Gaming Back? , as they offer a deeper look into our culture, values, and explain how our approach to motion gaming differs from previous generations.

To protect yourself from recruitment scams, please note that Nex communicates through official company emails ending in @nex.inc. If you receive a suspicious email or message, please do not respond and report it immediately to us.

Location: Remote US or Canada Annual salary: $95,000 - $125,000 USD (Final base salary within this range will be determined based on job-related factors, including the candidate’s experience, skills, qualifications, and geographic location) Type: 6 month contract with potential FTE conversion Reports to: VP of Design

As our Senior Graphic Designer, you ll be at the heart of our creative efforts, working closely with our marketing and product teams to craft stunning visuals that resonate with our audience. You’ll be the go-to person for everything design-related, from our website and social media to retail channel materials and product launches.

In addition to crafting design deliverables for Nex marketing channels, you will coordinate with internal and external partners, including creative agencies, to execute marketing projects and campaigns and ensure delivery of all elements of the creative package.

What You ll Do

Website, Social and Emails: Bring our digital presence to life with eye-catching designs that engage and delight our users. You’ll create everything from stunning website visuals to social media graphics and email campaigns that stand out in a crowded inbox.

Retail Channel Materials: Design impactful retail materials including PDPs (Product Detail Pages), templates and in-store displays. You will also work on digital channels such as Amazon. Your work will help our products shine in the retail environment, making them irresistible to shoppers.

Product Launch Visual Assets: Lead the visual strategy for our product launches. You’ll create cohesive and compelling visual assets that tell the story of our products, from initial teasers to full-scale launch campaigns. This includes Nex Playground, but also accessories and other merchandise as we build out the product ecosystem.

What We’re Looking For

Experience: 5+ years of design experience, preferably in a similar role within the creative, agency or marketing field. Some experience leading or producing multi-channel campaigns.

Skills: Mastery of design tools such as Adobe Creative Suite (Photoshop, Illustrator, InDesign) and Figma or Sketch. Strong understanding of typography, color theory, and layout principles.

Creativity: A keen eye for detail and a passion for pushing creative boundaries. You’re not afraid to take risks and try new things. Set the bar for creative excellence and maintain it consistently.

Collaboration: Excellent communication skills and the ability to work well in a team. You’ll be collaborating closely with various departments, so being a team player is a must.

Delivery Management: Manage project workflows, oversee the end-to-end production process of campaigns to ensure timely and high-quality deliverables and coordinate with external vendors where necessary.

Time Management: Ability to manage multiple projects simultaneously and meet deadlines without compromising on quality.

Portfolio: A strong portfolio showcasing a range of design work, particularly in digital, retail and product launch contexts.

Why Join Us?

Impact: Your work will directly influence our brand and how the world sees us.

Growth: Opportunities for professional growth and development.

Culture: A supportive and inclusive environment where your ideas matter.

Flexibility: We offer flexible working hours and remote work options to fit your lifestyle.

We Offer

Competitive compensation package.

Flexible working hours and vacation policy.

Product-driven culture that treasures talents and individual growth.

Front-row seat and hands-on experience with cutting edge technologies in the evolving gaming field

Nex is located in San Jose, California, USA and Hong Kong . Learn more about us at https://www.nexplayground.com/about .
