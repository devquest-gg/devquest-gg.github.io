Get to know the role

Job Title: Software Development Engineer in Test

Job Summary: As the SDET for Consent Management Platform, you will be responsible for establishing and delivering test frameworks for the consent and preference experiences across the Pokémon digital ecosystem in a continuous integration and delivery model. A primary focus of this role will be the Consent Management capability: bringing greater consistency, accountability and scalability to how customer choices and preferences are captured, maintained and honored across fan-facing experiences. You will develop the initial test strategy, including designing and implementing an automated framework for the continuous delivery of the platforms and services that enable this capability. This includes the Preference Center, consent-management services and APIs, relevant data and identity integrations, audit capabilities, and the technical controls required to support customer choices across content-driven websites, authenticated experiences, marketing platforms, event systems and other global Pokémon digital initiatives. Consent Management sits at the intersection of fan experience, technology, privacy and data governance. You will therefore work closely with Software Engineering, DevOps, InfoSec, Legal, Privacy, Data Governance, Marketing Technology, Product and business stakeholders to translate agreed business, fan-experience and regulatory requirements into coherent platform capabilities and experiences. Success in this role requires the ability to think critically and provide innovative solutions to continuous integration and delivery requirements. Connecting business outcomes and fan experience with strong engineering execution — balancing customer needs, regulatory requirements, operational excellence, platform scalability and long-term maintainability.

FLSA Classification (US Only): Exempt

People Manager: No

What you’ll do

Partner with all levels of the technical organization to build and enhance an automation framework focused on quality.

Develop customer centric automation test processes for all existing and new product development.

Develop and maintain automated tests in python and Selenium to ensure traceability to requirements

Build and enhance tools for internal use to ensure quality at scale.

Lead project implementation and execution through test strategies for a wide range of technically challenging products.

Take ownership of changes in our software test methodology for continuous improvement

Document, troubleshoot, and resolve bugs through reviews with development team.

Execute and report automated and manual test cases during all phases of the development life cycle.

Advocate for a quality mindset across all partners and re-enforce a “shift-left” culture in our SDLC

What you’ll bring

Five (5) to seven (7) years of relevant professional experience or a demonstrated equivalent level of expertise.

Bachelor s degree in a related field or a demonstrated equivalent level of applicable experience.

Experience operating within an Agile development environment.

Experience with continuous testing and delivery practices.

Experience with test frameworks (Selenium, nUnit, xUnit, and pytest).
