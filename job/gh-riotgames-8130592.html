Principal Software Engineer, Gameplay - Unpublished R&D Product

Riot Games · Los Angeles

Lead Engineering $229K–$320K Posted 2026-08-27 Full time

Riot engineers bring deep knowledge of specific technical areas but also value the opportunity to work in many broader domains. As a software engineer, you ll also dive into projects that focus on team cohesiveness and cross-team goals. You ll lead with a clear illustration of extraordinary engineering.

As a Principal Software Engineer and technical lead you’ll take technical ownership of multiple key areas. You will drive the technical vision and execution of gameplay features. You will collaborate with cross-disciplinary teams to build systems that bring our players dreams to life. If you have a passion for gameplay engineering and thrive in a dynamic, player-focused environment, this is the role for you.

Responsibilities:

Collaborate with external teams in other business units working in the same technical spaces (including Game Designers, Artists, Animators, Quality, Engineers, and Producers) to shape player experiences

Give feedback to designers and artists on the technical tradeoffs for improvements of their tooling

Give feedback to product leaders about product direction and benefits for creative teams

Brainstorm, design, and implement prototypes to find answers to product, development, and technical questions

Lead and deliver game content tooling to support external teams (including Game Designers, Artists, Animators, and Producers)

Collaborate with and mentor engineering teammates

Deliver exciting and meaningful improvements to gameplay tools focused on opening game design space in environments, physics, audio, and navigation

Build projects implementing complex system changes

Required Qualifications:

8-12 yrs of collective professional experience in game development

Experience shipping games across multiple platforms (PC, console, mobile)

Experience building gameplay systems and collaborating with designers

Expert with C++ in complex code bases

Experience mentoring fellow developers

Experience leading technical designs and technical decisions

Experience with building game content tools and pipelines

For this role, you ll find success through craft expertise and a collaborative spirit that prioritizes the delight of players. We will look at your past studies and experience, but for this role, we also look for dedicated people with a personal relationship with games. If you embody player empathy and care about players experiences, this is the role for you!

Our Perks:

Riot focuses on work/life balance, shown by our open paid time off policy and other perks such as flexible work schedules. We offer medical, dental, and life insurance, parental leave for you, your spouse/domestic partner, and children, and a 401k with company match. Check out our benefits pages for more information.
