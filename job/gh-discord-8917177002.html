Associate Commercial Counsel

Discord · San Francisco Bay Area or Remote (U.S.) · Remote

Entry Other Remote $200K–$225K Posted 2026-11-03 Full time
Apply on Discord’s site →

Discord has a highly engaged community of millions of daily active users who use the platform for many different reasons, but there’s one thing that nearly everyone does: play video games.

Discord plays a uniquely important role in the future of gaming, and we are focused on making it easier and more fun for people to hang out before, during, and after playing games.

Discord s Commercial Marketing Legal (CoMa) team is the legal engine behind Discord s revenue and partnership business. We partner with Global Partnerships, Developer Platform Solutions, Corporate Strategy, Ad Solutions, Data Science, AdTech, Marketing, and Procurement to structure and close the deals that bring games, creators, and brands to Discord s community. As Associate Commercial Counsel, you ll be a key advisor for integrating Discord s social features into games, enabling in-game commerce and digital collectibles, and building new monetization models alongside some of the biggest names in gaming and entertainment. You will also support our marketing and events teams on creative endeavors that promote Discord in the industry and to users. You ll handle a high volume of deals with real ownership, and you ll help shape how Discord approaches new partnership types at scale. This person will report to the Managing Counsel, Commercial Marketing Legal.

What you ll be doing:

Draft, negotiate, and close a high volume of commercial agreements. These include Advertising, API and SDK integration agreements, commerce and in-app purchase agreements, and ad tech related transactions.

Negotiate IP licensing agreements with game, anime, and entertainment licensors for digital collectibles and in-app content.

Review, advise on, and support sponsorship and marketing efforts.

Structure novel monetization and revenue models, such as virtual currency, bundled subscriptions, rewards programs, and revenue share.

Support payments and platform agreements, including payment processors, gift card distribution, and app store and developer program terms.

Build and maintain scalable templates, playbooks, and self-serve processes, including simplifying complex agreements into modular, easy-to-use forms.

What you should have:

A JD and active membership in good standing with at least one U.S. state bar. You also need to be eligible to register as in-house counsel in California.

At least 4 years of commercial transactional experience, with a combination of top law firm and/or in-house experience at a technology company preferred.

Experience drafting and negotiating complex commercial agreements, such as technology licensing, platform integrations, and revenue partnerships.

Experience with advertising, gaming, digital media, data privacy and consumer internet.

Understanding of privacy principles, especially with respect to commercial agreements involving the transfer or personal information.

Familiarity with marketing legal issues, including copyright, privacy, and consumer protection principles.

The ability to manage a high-volume docket while prioritizing effectively and meeting business timelines.

Sound business judgment and the ability to give practical, risk-calibrated advice in fast-moving, ambiguous situations.

Excellent communication skills, with the ability to translate complex legal issues for non-lawyer stakeholders and run with work independently.

Bonus points:

Familiarity with payments, virtual currency, or subscription and bundling arrangements.

Experience with international or APAC counterparties.
