Job Overview

We’re looking for an Encounter Designer to help shape the next generation of event modes for World of Tanks at Wargaming. As a senior member of the event team, you will define and champion the combat experience and encounter philosophy behind our seasonal and limited-time modes.

What will you do?

Act as the bridge that connects player combat, enemy combat, and level design teams.

Own and advocate for the full combat experience, making sure player abilities, enemy behaviour, and encounter design fit together into engaging gameplay.

Design, prototype, and refine player combat systems, enemy mechanics, encounters, and moment-to-moment gameplay.

Set up combat and encounter design frameworks, standards, and best practices so that several feature teams can build at scale.

Build combat encounters that challenge players, reward mastery, allow for different playstyles, and support progression goals.

Analyse player feedback, telemetry, and playtest results to find ways to improve combat feel, pacing, difficulty, and engagement.

Work with leadership to spot risks, agree on priorities, and keep combat work aligned with the wider goals of the game.

Hold a high bar for quality, accessibility, usability, and player experience throughout development.

What are we looking for?

Deep knowledge of combat design fundamentals, including pacing, responsiveness, game feel, encounter design, progression, and player mastery.

Substantial hands-on experience building and balancing combat systems for action games.

Strong skills in gameplay prototyping, iterative development, tuning, and using playtests to guide design decisions.

Experience working closely with engineering teams to turn design goals into solid, reliable gameplay features

Excellent written and spoken communication, with the ability to explain design intent clearly and give feedback people can act on.

Hands-on experience with current game development tools and engines

Solid grasp of what motivates and engages players, how progression systems work, and how great gameplay keeps players coming back.

Thorough knowledge of game development pipelines and of how disciplines work together in production.
