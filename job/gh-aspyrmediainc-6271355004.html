Senior Artist

Aspyr Media · Austin

Senior Art Onsite Posted 2026-11-25 Full time

**Please note that all positions posted for Aspyr Media, Inc. are in-person/in-office only; remote positions are not available.**

Aspyr is a leading game publisher and developer, bringing the world s biggest franchises and bold new projects to players everywhere.

Since 1996, we’ve published over 200 games and our team is dedicated to bringing beloved franchises like Tomb Raider and Star Wars to new and familiar audiences across all platforms.

We focus on collaboration and creativity, crafting high-quality, engaging games that leave a lasting impact on players worldwide.

Responsibilities:

Reporting to the Head of Creative, the Senior Artist will be heavily focused on concept development, visual exploration, and establishing the artistic direction across our portfolio of games. Beyond creating high-quality concept art, this role will help set and maintain the visual quality bar, lead and mentor artists, manage creative teams, collaborate with external development partners, provide art direction and feedback, and develop essential visual documentation. The ideal candidate will be a versatile artist with exceptional concept art skills, a strong creative vision, proven leadership and people management experience, and the ability to translate ideas into compelling visuals while guiding teams and projects to the highest standards of artistic excellence.

Collaborate with Designers, Artists, Technical Artists

Architect and develop animation features from the ground up

Build debugging tools

Write clear documentation on animation features that designers and other engineers can understand and put into use

Create efficient code and algorithms with known performance characteristics

Essential Functions:

External Partner Collaboration and Art Reviews: Serve as a key artistic liaison for external development studios; review artwork and milestone deliverables, providing clear, constructive, and actionable feedback through written direction, concept paint-overs, and visual examples to ensure quality standards are met

Concept Art and Visual Development: Create high-quality concept art, including character designs, environments, props, key art, mood paintings, and visual explorations that establish the artistic vision for new projects, remasters, and remakes; translate gameplay ideas, narrative themes, and creative goals into compelling visual designs

Artistic Vision and Direction: Collaborate with creative leadership to define, develop, and champion the artistic goals, style, and visual identity of each project; communicate and guide this vision across internal teams and external development partners

Visual Quality and Benchmarking: Establish and maintain the visual quality bar through benchmark concept art, paintovers, target renders, and visual references; create clear artistic targets that guide production and ensure consistency, quality, and alignment with the project s creative vision

Art Bibles and Documentation: Develop and maintain comprehensive art bibles, style guides, and visual documentation covering character design, environments, lighting, color palettes, props, and overall artistic direction

Creative Problem-Solving and Iteration: Explore multiple artistic approaches, solve visual challenges, and rapidly iterate on concepts based on creative, technical, and production feedback; balance artistic ambition with practical development requirements

Pre-Production and RFP Support: Produce high-quality concept art, mood boards, key visuals, and presentation materials to support internal greenlight pitches, project proposals, and external Requests for Proposals (RFPs); help establish visual targets and evaluate potential development partners

Cross-Disciplinary Collaboration: Work closely with Production, Game Design, Narrative, and Marketing to ensure a cohesive artistic vision across all disciplines; provide creative input on production milestones, visual targets, and scope while supporting the highest achievable quality within project schedules and resources

Mentorship and Creative Collaboration: Share artistic knowledge and expertise, mentor fellow artists, and foster a collaborative environment that encourages creative exploration, continuous improvement, and artistic excellence

Preferred Qualifications:

8+ years of professional experience as an artist in the video game industry

Bachelor’s Degree in Art, Game Design, or a related field

Portfolio of outstanding quality that demonstrates a mastery of art fundamentals (composition, color theory, anatomy, lighting, architecture) and a strong ability to create compelling characters, environments, and worlds

At least two shipped titles in an artist role, preferably concept art

Expert proficiency in Adobe Photoshop and experience with 3D software (e.g., Blender, ZBrush, Maya, Keyshot) for creating blockouts, assembling scenes, and executing paint-overs

Proven experience creating and maintaining art style guides or visual development bibles

Deep understanding of modern game development pipelines with hands-on experience using Unreal Engine or comparable game engines, and the ability to create art that respects technical and production limitations

Exceptional communication, interpersonal, and presentation skills, with the ability to provide clear, diplomatic, and constructive feedback to external partners

Self-motivated, organized, and able to thrive in a highly collaborative and dynamic environment

Experience working on remakes or remasters of classic games, a plus

Previous experience in an art direction, lead, or mentorship capacity, a plus

Deep passion for and knowledge of the games and IPs in Aspyr s catalog, a plus

Versatility across multiple styles, from photorealistic to stylized, a plus
