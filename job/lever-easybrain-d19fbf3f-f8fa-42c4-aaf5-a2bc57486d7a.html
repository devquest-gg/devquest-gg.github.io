Creative Manager

Easybrain · Warsaw

Mid Marketing Hybrid Posted 2026-10-28 Full time
Apply on Easybrain’s site →

Easybrain is looking for a Creative Manager to work on advertising creatives for our mobile games played by millions of users worldwide. You will work closely with a Creative Producer to develop creative concepts, ensure the quality of advertising materials, and explore new approaches to promoting our products.

Responsibilities:

- Generate ideas and concepts for video, static, and playable ads within the overall creative strategy for each product;

- Write clear, structured briefs for graphic and video designers and playable ad developers;

- Review video and static creatives to ensure they meet the brief and provide actionable feedback;

- Test playable ads to ensure game mechanics work correctly and the user experience is intuitive;

- Analyse competitors’ advertising creatives, monitor trends, and explore new ideas and approaches;

- Contribute to creative performance analysis and suggest improvements;

- Support the Creative Producer in organising and coordinating the production of advertising materials.

Requirements:

- 2+ years of experience in creative marketing for mobile games;

- Understanding of different advertising creative formats and their specific requirements;

- A strong eye for visual quality and composition, with close attention to detail;

- Creative thinking and the ability to generate fresh ideas;

- Ability to communicate ideas clearly, write effective briefs, and provide constructive feedback;

- Strong organisational skills, a sense of ownership, and the ability to manage multiple tasks;

- Experience using AI tools to generate creative ideas, explore visual concepts, and create advertising materials;

- Interest in mobile games and a desire to grow in performance marketing;

- Russian language is a necessity;

- English at Upper-Intermediate level (B2) or above.

Will be a plus:

- Experience working on advertising creatives for Puzzle and Board mobile games;

- Basic understanding of user acquisition metrics;

- Experience using graphic design tools;

- Experience with Confluence and Jira.

Benefits:

Besides the engaging tasks, support from experienced colleagues, challenge, and drive, we offer:

- Full support in relocating to countries where our offices are located;

- High-end market salary with performance bonuses;

- All needed equipment;

- Regular company events and monthly Friday meetings;

- Social benefits (private medical cover, sports reimbursement, etc.);

- Paid vacations, sick days;

- English, Greek, and Polish online language classes;

- Reimbursement for education and professional development.
