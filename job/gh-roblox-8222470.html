Every day, tens of millions of people come to Roblox to explore, create, play, learn, and connect with friends in 3D immersive digital experiences– all created by our global community of developers and creators.

At Roblox, we’re building the tools and platform that empower our community to bring any experience that they can imagine to life. Our vision is to reimagine the way people come together, from anywhere in the world, and on any device.

We’re on a mission to connect a billion people with optimism and civility, and looking for amazing talent to help us get there.

A career at Roblox means you’ll be working to shape the future of human interaction, solving unique technical challenges at scale, and helping to create safer, more civil shared experiences for everyone.

About the role:

Avatars are core to our mission and everyday, millions of people come to Marketplace or other avatar shopping experiences to discover and shop for creations that help them express their individuality. They dress and play with their avatar as they connect with friends across thousands of immersive digital experiences. Powering the creativity of our Marketplace is our community of creators and developers who are building businesses and establishing their brand by selling creations. These creators range from individuals to small businesses to large brands, all of which are attracted to the Roblox platform, community and offerings.

Our Marketplace is a critical component of Roblox’s economy, accounting for a third of economic activity on the platform. As a Senior Product Manager responsible for Avatar creators, you will have a front seat at shaping the future of the Marketplace and what it means to build an economy powered by our community.

You will:

Be responsible for building and shipping creator driven products that unlock more expression on the platform

Have ownership of our UGC creator ecosystem, decide which new categories we should build, manage supply health and grow core metrics that ladder up to helping people create avatars for the broader community

Get hands-on with our community, understand their needs, and partner with the engineers, designers and data scientists to build roadmaps

Balance the needs of a two-sided Marketplace, constantly striving to deliver for our community and consumers, while using a combination of data and research to inform and iterate on decisions

Be a champion of Marketplace and our Economy across the company and with the community

Build the future of the Roblox Economy!

You have:

5+ years of Product Management experience shipping high quality, delightful experiences for either developers, creators, small businesses or brands that have a high technical fluency. Bonus if you’ve done it in two-sided marketplaces.

You go deep into the needs of customers, are not afraid to have direct relationships with them and are an advocate for their needs

You understand what it takes to build a digital business, how owners think about their bottom line, tools they want to grow their business and the biggest unlocks Roblox can provide to grow creator earnings across consumer and creator products.

Interdisciplinary experience. In addition to the standard product functions of design and engineering, you have experience working within a multidimensional environment including analytics, business development, communications/PR, and marketing

Skills to convey complex ideas through written, visual, and verbal communication, always focusing on the reasons behind everything you do. Those skills transition from the detailed and technical for specs to the inspirational for strategic presentations.

Spirit of an Entrepreneur with an ability to contribute to product design, project management and technical ideas.

Skills to execute in a growing environment, with competing resources and priorities, through the product life cycle from planning and development, through launch.
