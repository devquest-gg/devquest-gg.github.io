Join us and be part of the play! Position Overview

Zynga is looking for a strategic, experienced, and intellectually curious Ad Revenue Analytics Lead to join our world-class advertising team. In this role, you will own the end-to-end analytics and optimization strategy for Zynga s advertising inventory, challenging conventional thinking and driving measurable revenue impact across our portfolio of mobile games. This is a high-visibility, high-impact role that blends technical rigor with business leadership. You will drive cross-functional initiatives, mentor analysts, and translate complex data into strategies that move the business forward.

What You ll Do

Define and own the roadmap for key ad revenue initiatives that maximize revenue across Zynga s advertising inventory

Lead complex, multi-stakeholder initiatives from ideation through rollout across our game portfolio

Stay close to daily ad revenue performance, flagging anomalies, diagnosing shifts, and surfacing optimization opportunities, while keeping ad monetization leadership informed of trends and their business implications

Lead the design and execution of complex A/B tests; translate findings into actionable strategies

Collaborate with product and central analytics teams, driving alignment and shared ownership of ads revenue outcomes

Partner with data science on ML and AI-powered models to improve ad targeting, pricing, and inventory optimization

Mentor and provide technical and strategic guidance to junior analysts on the team

What You Bring

Bachelor’s degree in a quantitative field

5+ years of experience in a data-intensive analytics role

Prior experience in mobile gaming or advertising is a big plus

Expert-level SQL skills

Proficiency in Python or R for data analysis, statistical modeling, and automation

Advanced proficiency in Tableau or equivalent BI/dashboarding tools

Familiarity with machine learning concepts and AI tools as applied to revenue analytics

A self-starter who identifies problems, builds the case, and drives solutions to completion — without waiting for direction

Proven ability to influence and align cross-functional teams

Intellectual curiosity and a challenger mindset — unafraid to question assumptions and push for deeper insight

Strong project management and prioritization skills in a fast-paced, deadline-driven environment

Excellent written, verbal, and visual communication skills
