Every day, tens of millions of people come to Roblox to explore, create, play, learn, and connect with friends in 3D immersive digital experiences– all created by our global community of developers and creators.

At Roblox, we’re building the tools and platform that empower our community to bring any experience that they can imagine to life. Our vision is to reimagine the way people come together, from anywhere in the world, and on any device.

We’re on a mission to connect a billion people with optimism and civility, and looking for amazing talent to help us get there.

A career at Roblox means you’ll be working to shape the future of human interaction, solving unique technical challenges at scale, and helping to create safer, more civil shared experiences for everyone.

Creator Hub (create.roblox.com) helps millions of Roblox creators discover the tools, resources, and opportunities they need to succeed. From core Hub experiences and search to notifications and the Developer Forum, we help creators find what matters, stay informed, and take action.

We re evolving Creator Hub from a collection of tools into a more connected, contextual workspace that helps creators launch, understand, grow, and operate their games.

As a Principal Software Engineer, you’ll be a hands-on technical lead across a broad set of creator-facing products and services. You’ll help shape the roadmap and lead engineers through major product initiatives. You’ll partner with teams across Roblox to deliver cohesive creator experiences.

You Will

Lead technical direction, architecture, and end-to-end delivery for major Creator Hub product initiatives.

Partner with Product, Design, and engineering leadership to shape the roadmap, evaluate tradeoffs, and turn product goals into actionable technical plans.

Design and evolve full-stack product solutions, including backend services, APIs, and data models that power creator-facing experiences.

Drive technical alignment across product and engineering teams, resolving cross-team dependencies, and influencing architectural decisions.

Mentor engineers, strengthen technical ownership, and raise the bar for engineering quality and execution.

You Have

8+ years of software engineering experience, with a strong track record of building and shipping user-facing products.

BS or MS in Computer Science, or equivalent practical experience.

Strong backend engineering experience, including designing production services, APIs, and data models in a language such as C#, Java, Go, or Python

Experience building full-stack products and working with modern web technologies such as TypeScript, React, or similar frameworks.

Experience as a technical lead, including setting direction, driving delivery through multiple engineers and influencing decisions with Product, Design, and partner teams.

You Are

Product-minded: You care about solving real user problems and delivering measurable value.

A hands-on technical leader: You combine technical depth with sound judgment and help engineers around you succeed.

Pragmatic and collaborative: You navigate ambiguity, make thoughtful tradeoffs, and bring people together to deliver results.
