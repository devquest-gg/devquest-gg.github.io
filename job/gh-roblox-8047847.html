Roblox is seeking an On-Site Asset Management Specialist to join Infrastructure Asset Management of our rapidly growing Foundation Resource Management team in the Phoenix Data Center region. You will be responsible for tracking and inventorying the physical data center assets throughout the assets’ lifecycle to ensure compliance to SOX and audits for Roblox.

We are looking for a team member who has experience working with Data Center Operations, Accounting, Procurement, Software Engineering and 3rd party Vendor teams and understands the importance of tracking physical assets, its related details and the impact asset movements have on the organization.

You Will:

Ensure the data center physical fixed asset inventory is tracked from Receipt (via delivery notifications) through End of Life within Roblox’s physical Asset Management Database.

The asset lifecycle tracking procedures include:

Goods Receipt

Physical moves (both inter intra)

RMAs

Disposals

Process financial goods receipt in Roblox’s financial database to complete proper 3-way matches

Oversee physical asset tagging standards, maintaining asset tag thresholds and distribution of asset tags

Establish, organize and maintain a component inventory tracking process and system

Perform annual physical asset audit as part of SOX control

Train new On-Site Asset Management team members on standards and basic best practices for On-Site Operations team members

Serve as the Point of Contact for logistics planning and coordination with vendors for On-Site receipts/deliveries across multiple sites

Liaise with vendors to convey logistical process post-mortems for Roblox order deliveries

Document runbooks, standard operating procedures and guidelines to ensure process and execution contingencies

You Have:

BA/BS (Preferred) or experienced, with minimum 5+ years’ experience in Logistics, Warehouse and/or Data Center Asset Management

Detail oriented and have excellent organizational and analytical skills

Strong customer service and communication skills

Proven ability to collaborate with cross-functional teams and vendors

Able to handle changing priorities

Computer savvy with strong working knowledge of spreadsheet software

Please note, this role does require to be onsite 5 days a week.
