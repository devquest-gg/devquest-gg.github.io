Lead VFX Artist

Turtle Rock Studios · Irvine

Lead Art Hybrid $135K–$165K Posted 2026-07-03

At Turtle Rock, we make the games we want to play. We re dedicated to providing a collaborative environment where our team members feel empowered with the creative freedom to contribute and grow. While we are based in Orange County, CA, we support flexible work arrangements ranging from in-studio, hybrid, to fully remote throughout North America. Join our award-winning game development team and help create our next hit title.

We are looking for a Lead VFX Artist to be a part of our VFX team. The Lead VFX Artist will maintain consistency across the department providing direction and feedback, ensuring all team members understand their role in the department and know what is expected of them. The right candidate will look for opportunities to enhance the player experience, embrace and provide constructive feedback while guiding the creative process; driving VFX process for game effects. This position will report to the Creative Director.

Responsibilities

Lead the VFX team: maintain consistency across the department providing direction and feedback, ensuring all team members understand their role in the department and know what is expected of them

Lead team meetings and relay important studio information when required

Work closely with department manager to provide mentorship and coaching for VFX team members including but not limited to: 1:1s, career development, performance evaluations, and goal setting

Guide the creative process; drive the concept process for game effects

Bring in-progress and prototype effects to completion

Work with the Art Directors and art teams to ensure artistic unity across the game

Create real-time visual effects to enhance environments, characters, and combat

Work with designers, engineers, and other artists in creating strong moment-to-moment gameplay

Collaborate with other team members to ensure that assets fall within set parameters in regards to performance, best practices, and art style

Playtest with both the studio and the VFX team to review and iterate VFX

Requirements

7+ years game experience in creating effects in game development with one or more shipped titles on multiple platforms

Strong portfolio demonstrating expertise in creating real-time effects

Self-driven, strong communication skills, and a positive attitude

Ability to problem-solve and prioritize issues while working with a team

Experience with creating compelling, real-time effects using Photoshop, Maya, and/or in-game effects systems

Knowledgeable of 3D graphics, particle systems, physics, etc.

Experience in UE5

Keen production sensibilities, ability to collaborate on timelines and communicate with Production and team members on task duration and prioritization

Ability to maintain high quality and consistent progress

Strong leadership, mentoring, and teaching skills

Strong ability to interpret and adapt to general and flexible direction. Can thrive in a highly collaborative, team-oriented environment

Proficient in Maya, 3ds Max, or Blender

Preferences

Shipped one or more major titles on current generation/PC platforms as a Senior VFX artist

Experience with AAA First Person action titles

Previous leadership experience preferred, or senior/principal role in games environments

Experience working with shaders and materials

Familiarity with asset tracking and issue tracking tools such as Perforce and JIRA

Passion for games
