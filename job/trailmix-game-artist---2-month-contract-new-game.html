The Opportunity Trailmix is looking for a Game Artist to join our New Games team on a two-month contract, supporting the creation of map and decoration assets for an original casual mobile game.

The main focus of the role will be concepting and creating high-quality, detailed 2D assets for our expanding isometric world. You&#x27;ll help build out new locations, decorations and map content that support narrative, progression and visual storytelling.

You&#x27;ll work closely with our art and design team, taking assets from initial concepts and references through to polished final artwork ready for implementation in Unity.

The Day to Day Creating polished 2D assets for an isometric game map.

Concepting, designing and rendering buildings, environments, decorations, props and other world content.

Developing assets that fit an established visual style and quality bar.

Taking work from rough concept through to production-ready artwork.

Preparing assets for implementation in Unity.

Working closely with the internal art team, responding quickly to feedback and iteration.

Using AI-assisted tools where appropriate to support ideation and production efficiency.

About You Excellent 2D art, concepting and rendering skills.

Strong Photoshop experience.

Experience creating environments, decorations or map assets for casual mobile games.

Strong understanding of isometric perspective and construction.

The ability to quickly understand and match an established art style.

Fast, pragmatic production skills and the ability to deliver polished work to deadlines.

Openness to using AI tools thoughtfully where they can help accelerate the production process.

Unity experience is a plus.

A strong portfolio demonstrating environment design, rendering and polished game-ready assets.

You&#x27;ll be joining a small, collaborative team during a busy period of production, so we&#x27;re looking for someone who can get up to speed quickly, work independently and produce high-quality work from day one.

Please apply with your portfolio, availability, and day rate.
