We are looking for a highly motivated UI artist to join our product art team!

Responsibilities:

Execute all visual design stages from creative concept to final hand-off to development teams

Build scalable visual solutions, templates and design systems

Leverage AI tools to accelerate ideation, exploration and production

You will be responsible for the concept, research, sketches and mood boards, to the final materials and deliveries. Our UI elements includes in-game creative campaigns, re-skined events, albums, loading screens, players communications and unique projects by demand.

Present and communicate designs and key milestone deliverables to peers and executive level stakeholders

Proactively improve workflows, quality and efficiency.

We believe in pro-activness that defines the role, and not the role that defines the tasks. You will be expected to initiate actions and take ownership on processes – to achieve the goals and beyond.

Requirements:

At least 2 years of experience as UI Designer

Eager to succeed, locked on target and uses all resources available to achieve it

Experience incorporating AI tools into creative workflows

Strong conceptual thinking and problem-solving skills

Ability to work independently and take ownership

Proven UI experience in gaming industry

Strong composition, color, typography and graphic design skills

Attention to detail

Up-to-date with the latest UI trends, techniques, and technologies

Responsible and able to manage your time independently

Self-learner and self-motivated

Able to work in a team and produce quality assets in a high-paced environment.
