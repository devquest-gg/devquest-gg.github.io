Senior Oracle Fusion Financials & Procurement Consultant (Techno-Functional)

Scopely · Bangalore

Senior Other Hybrid Posted 2026-11-25 Full time

Scopely is looking for a talented Senior Oracle Fusion Financials Procurement Consultant (Techno-Functional) to join our team based in the Bangalore office in a hybrid working mode.

At Scopely, we care deeply about what we do and want to inspire play, every day - whether in our work environments alongside our talented colleagues or through our deep connections with our communities of players. We are a global team of game lovers who are developing, publishing and innovating the games industry, connecting millions of people around the world daily.

Show us the money! As the Finance team, we’re number crunchers with top-notch analytical and reporting skills. As a shared service, we get to work with all of Scopely s talented studios.

What You’ll Do

We are expanding our Finance Systems team and seeking an experienced Oracle consultant with deep expertise in Oracle Fusion Cloud Financials and Procure-to-Pay (P2P), including Procurement. This is an additional position within the existing team.

You will partner closely with the Finance Systems lead, Accounting, Finance, Procurement, and technical teams to independently lead complex investigations, enhancements, and operational support activities. The role requires strong functional expertise, technical troubleshooting skills, sound judgment, and clear communication.

You should be comfortable receiving a business problem, researching it independently, recommending a solution, executing thorough testing, and driving delivery with limited direction. Onboarding will focus on our business, configuration, and processes; you are expected to bring established Oracle expertise and quickly assume ownership of complex assignments.

Own complex Oracle Financials and Procurement issues and enhancements from investigation through solution design, testing, deployment coordination, and post-release validation.

Support the end-to-end P2P process, including requisitions, purchasing, approvals, receiving, invoice matching, supplier management, and payments.

Translate Accounting, Finance, and Procurement requirements into functional specifications, configuration changes, and technical requirements.

Independently research unfamiliar issues using Oracle documentation, support resources, release notes, and controlled testing.

Diagnose issues across configuration, transaction processing, accounting rules, reporting, data, and integrations; identify root causes and recommend sustainable solutions.

Design and execute functional, integration, regression, and negative test scenarios. Validate accounting outcomes and downstream impacts, document evidence, and coordinate business acceptance.

Use SQL, Oracle reporting tools, and supported data access methods to investigate transactions, reconcile results, and validate solutions.

Assess quarterly Oracle updates and coordinate testing of changes affecting financial and procurement processes.

Manage Oracle support requests, providing reproducible examples and supporting evidence and driving issues toward resolution.

Communicate progress, findings, risks, dependencies, and decisions clearly. Raise blockers early and provide recommendations and next steps.

Maintain practical documentation and identify opportunities to strengthen controls, reduce manual effort, and improve system reliability.

What success looks like:

Complex work can be delegated to you with confidence that you will clarify requirements, develop an approach, and drive execution.

Solutions address the full process across Finance and Procurement, including accounting, controls, and downstream dependencies.

Recommendations clearly explain the evidence, business impact, alternatives, and risks.

Testing demonstrates that solutions work across realistic scenarios and produce the intended financial outcomes.

Stakeholders receive timely updates and early visibility into blockers.

You quickly build knowledge of our environment and expand the work you can own independently.

What We’re Looking For

Substantial hands-on experience with Oracle Fusion Cloud Financials and Procurement, including independent ownership of complex production issues and enhancements.

Strong end-to-end P2P expertise, with a clear understanding of how Procurement, Receiving, Payables, and Payments interact and affect accounting.

Deep expertise in General Ledger, Subledger Accounting, and Payables, with experience in additional modules such as Receivables, Cash Management, Fixed Assets, or Intercompany.

Strong understanding of accounting flows, procurement and invoice approval controls, invoice matching, receipt accruals, subledger-to-GL reconciliation, multicurrency processing, and period-close activities.

Technical proficiency in SQL and working knowledge of Oracle Financials and Procurement data structures, OTBI, BI Publisher, data imports, and integrations.

Demonstrated ability to investigate ambiguous problems, evaluate alternatives, and deliver well-supported recommendations with limited supervision.

Proven ability to create and execute thorough test plans, investigate defects, and validate business and accounting results.

Excellent written and verbal English communication skills, including the ability to explain complex issues clearly to nontechnical stakeholders.

Strong prioritization, follow-through, and judgment about when to proceed independently and when to escalate.

Ability to collaborate across time zones, with agreed working-hour overlap with global stakeholders.

Bonus Points

Experience with Oracle Cloud EPM, particularly Account Reconciliation Cloud Service (ARCS) and Financial Consolidation and Close Cloud Service (FCCS). EPM experience is a plus.

Understanding of data flows and integrations between Oracle Fusion Financials and EPM applications.

Experience supporting a global, multi-entity, multicurrency finance environment.

Experience with Oracle Integration Cloud, REST APIs, FBDI, and financial reporting or analytics platforms.

Experience with implementations, acquisitions, migrations, or significant finance and procurement process changes.

Relevant Oracle certifications or an accounting, finance, information systems, or related background.

About Scopely

Scopely is a leading video game and global interactive entertainment company, home to many of the world’s most beloved and enduring experiences, including two of the most successful mobile games of all-time “MONOPOLY GO!” and “Pokémon GO,” along with “Stumble Guys,” “Star Trek™ Fleet Command,” “MARVEL Strike Force,” “WWE Champions,” the Scrabble® franchise, “Yahtzee® With Buddies,” and many others. Across mobile, web, PC, and console, Scopely creates, develops, publishes, and live-operates one of the most diversified and award-winning portfolios in the games industry — bringing hundreds of millions of players together through a shared love of play.

Founded in 2011, Scopely is powered by its exceptional team — including thousands of world-class gamemakers around the globe, a distinctive tenet-driven culture, and its proprietary technology platform, Playgami. Together, these strengths have fueled Scopely’s position as the #1 mobile games company in the U.S. and #2 globally, generating more than $10 billion in lifetime revenue. Whether building global sensations like “MONOPOLY GO!” from the ground up, or expanding through strategic acquisitions, including the FoxNext, GSN, and Scopely Explore games businesses — Scopely consistently delivers experiences players love today and return to for years to come.

Recognized multiple times as one of the "100 Most Influential Companies in the World" by TIME magazine and one of Fast Company s "World s Most Innovative Companies" and “Best Workplaces for Innovators,” Scopely believes that video games can be a force for good — creating meaningful connections, vibrant communities, and making life better through play.
