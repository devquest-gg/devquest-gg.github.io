Growth & User Acquisition Intern

Wildlife Studios · São Paulo

Entry Marketing Hybrid Posted 2026-11-17 Intern
Apply on Wildlife Studios’s site →

At Wildlife, the Product Marketing team shapes the growth strategy of our games throughout their lifecycle, from the first tests to global launch. We work close to the product, helping decide which games to scale, which markets and channels to bet on, and how to invest our marketing budget efficiently.

As a Business Analyst Intern, you’ll learn by doing, alongside analysts and leaders who make these decisions every day. You’ll take on real analyses that feed into real product and investment decisions.

What you ll do

Support analyses of engagement, monetization and acquisition cost for games in different stages of their lifecycle

Help design, monitor and evaluate tests, learning to read what the data shows (and what it doesn’t)

Build and maintain dashboards and reports that help the team track game and campaign performance

Support the team in comparing markets, channels (Meta, Google, TikTok) and audiences to test

Help with early LTV and payback models, learning how to work with limited data

Collaborate with game, product and creative teams, and present your findings clearly

What you ll need

Currently pursuing a Bachelor’s degree in Engineering, Computer Science, Statistics, Economics, Business or related fields, with graduation expected between December 2027 and December 2028

Availability for a [carga horária a definir]-hour/week internship

Strong analytical thinking and curiosity to understand the “why” behind the numbers

Basic knowledge of SQL and/or Python or R (through classes, personal projects or previous experiences)

Good organization and communication skills

Comfort with ambiguity and with questions that don’t come fully defined

Intermediate or advanced English

Nice to have

Exposure to A/B testing or statistics applied to experiments

Experience with BI tools (Looker, Tableau, Power BI or similar)

Interest in growth, performance marketing, consulting or the financial market

Being a mobile games player and curious about the industry

More about you

You’re proactive, like to understand problems in depth and aren’t afraid to ask questions. You want to learn fast, receive feedback and grow in a fast-paced environment, using data to support your opinions.

About Wildlife

Wildlife is a mobile game developer and publisher that has launched more than 60 titles and reached billions of players around the world, with offices in Brazil, Argentina, Ireland and the United States.
