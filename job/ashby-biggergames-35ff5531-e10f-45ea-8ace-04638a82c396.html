About Bigger Games

At Bigger Games, we create engaging and innovative mobile games enjoyed by millions of players around the world. We are a passionate team driven by creativity, curiosity, and the ambition to build unforgettable gaming experiences.

We are looking for a curious, creative, and highly motivated Marketing Intern who shares our passion for mobile games and is eager to learn, contribute, and grow with us!

What You'll Do

- Support the Performance Marketing team in developing and executing creative concepts for our games.

- Participate in creative brainstorming sessions and contribute fresh ideas for marketing campaigns.

- Play and analyze hybrid casual games to understand gameplay mechanics, player motivations, and user experience.

- Research market trends, competitor games, and high-performing ad creatives to identify new creative opportunities.

- Collaborate with the Performance Marketing team throughout the creative production process, from ideation to execution.

- Test our games and provide feedback on gameplay, user experience, and potential improvements.

What We're Looking For

- Currently pursuing a university degree and available to work at least 3 days per week from our office.

- A genuine passion for mobile games, especially hybrid casual games, with a strong understanding of gameplay mechanics and player experience.

- Strong analytical and problem-solving skills, with the ability to think critically and approach challenges from different perspectives.

- A creative mindset and curiosity to explore new ideas, trends, and opportunities.

- Excellent communication skills and the ability to collaborate effectively within a team.

- A proactive, detail-oriented, and self-motivated approach to work.

- A strong willingness to learn, take initiative, and continuously improve.

What We Offer

- Meal and transportation allowance

- Flexible working hours around your university schedule

- Opportunity for a full-time role after graduation

- Team events and social activities

- Hands-on experience in the mobile gaming industry

- All the tools and equipment you need to do your best work

- PS5 and gaming corner to unwind and enjoy your favorite games

- Modern office in the heart of Istanbul

Previous experience in gaming or marketing is not required. What matters most to us is your passion for games, curiosity, and eagerness to learn!
