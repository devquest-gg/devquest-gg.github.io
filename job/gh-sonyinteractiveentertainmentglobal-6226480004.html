Senior Manager, Operations

The Senior Manager, Operations will be responsible for leading logistics and warehousing activities across the AMER region (US, CAN, LATAM) for Sony Interactive Entertainment, ensuring PlayStation products move efficiently, reliably, and compliantly through the end-to-end regional supply chain.

This role will oversee inbound logistics, destination activity from port to warehouse, warehouse operations, and outbound transportation across both B2B and B2C channels. Working closely with the Japan Logistics group for international transportation, regional Operations teams, Planning, Commercial, D2C, Finance, Customer Service, external warehousing (3PL), and logistics partners including 4PL Control Towers, this senior leader will ensure the AMER supply chain is resilient, cost effective, agile, and able to deliver against PlayStation’s customer and commercial commitments.

The Logistics Operations and Warehouse Operations team members will report into this role, with responsibility for managing performance, developing capability, and ensuring operational execution meets the needs of the business.

What You’ll Be Doing:

Lead all AMER logistics and warehousing operations, ensuring inbound, warehouse, and outbound activities are delivered safely, accurately, on time, and to the required service level.

Partner closely with the Japan Logistics group to manage inbound transportation from factory, creating resilience across supply routes, shipment planning, issue management, and operational execution.

Ensure destination activities from port into warehouse are completed on time and correctly, including coordination with freight partners, customs teams, port operations, and warehousing providers.

Oversee warehouse operations across AMER, ensuring service providers deliver efficient, cost effective, resilient, and agile fulfilment capability for both B2B and B2C channels.

Manage outbound transportation performance to deliver On Time In Full across retail, distributor, eCommerce, and PlayStation Direct customer requirements.

Lead logistics and warehousing partner performance management, ensuring partners meet agreed service levels, cost expectations, operational standards, and business commitments. Own MBRs and relevant processes, ensuring the annual review schedule is planned, cordinated, and delivered to provide key insights into SIE and out to our warehousing, transport, and service partners.

Work cross-functionally with Planning, Commercial, D2C Operations, Finance, Customer Service, and regional market teams to ensure operational plans are aligned to demand, customer needs, launch activity, and business priorities.

Drive continuous improvement across cost, service, resilience, agility, warehouse productivity, transport performance, and operational ways of working. This role requires close collaboration hand in hand with Operational Excellence and Transformation to drive a defined improvement plan, develop and input into MRP, and undertake regular metric reviews in a standardised and systematic manner.

Key Areas of Focus:

Inbound Logistics Supply Chain Resilience

Manage inbound transportation activity from factory into AMER.

Partner with Japan Logistics to ensure shipment plans, lead times, capacity, and risks are actively managed.

Build resilience across inbound routes, providers, processes, and escalation paths.

Ensure inbound issues are identified early and resolved quickly to protect customer and business commitments.

Port, Destination Warehouse Flow

Oversee destination activity from port into warehouse.

Ensure stock is received, transferred, and made available accurately and on time.

Coordinate with freight forwarders, customs partners, port providers, and warehouse teams to maintain flow.

Identify and remove bottlenecks across inbound handling, clearance, transport, and receipt processes.

Warehouse Operations

Lead AMER warehouse operations across B2B and B2C activity.

Manage warehousing partners to deliver high service, strong productivity, cost control, and operational flexibility.

Ensure warehouse operations support retail, distributor, D2C, launch, promotional, returns, and peak requirements.

Drive partner accountability through service reviews, performance metrics, issue resolution, and improvement plans.

Design, Deploy, and Deliver Continuous Improvement initiatives throughout the warehousing network.

Outbound Transportation

Manage outbound transport operations across AMER for B2B and B2C customers.

Ensure transport partners deliver On Time In Full against agreed customer promises.

Monitor delivery performance, cost, capacity, claims, exceptions, and service quality.

Work with Customer Service, D2C, Commercial, and Planning teams to ensure customer requirements are understood and delivered.

Leadership Operational Performance

Lead the Logistics Operations and Warehouse Operations teams.

Build a high-performing operational culture focused on service, accountability, resilience, and continuous improvement.

Establish clear governance, KPIs, reporting, and escalation routines across logistics and warehousing. Undertake regular standardised metrics reviews with the team, tracking actions to pivot, stay agile, and keep the business on track.

Deliver measurable improvements in service performance, cost efficiency, partner capability, and operational execution via a well-defined plan of action. Input into the MRP process and communicate plans effectively.

Create a Lean culture, capitalising on VSMs and undertaking waste removal activities via Continuous Improvement. Work closely with the Operational Excellence Team to gain support and take guidance on strategic and continuous improvement plans.

What We’re Looking For:

12+ years’ experience in logistics, warehousing, supply chain operations, transportation, fulfilment, or a related field.

Strong experience managing regional logistics and warehouse operations across complex supply chains.

Experience working with inbound freight, port-to-warehouse activity, 3PL warehousing partners, and outbound transport providers.

Strong understanding of B2B and B2C operational requirements, including retail fulfilment, eCommerce, and direct-to-consumer delivery.

Proven ability to manage service partners against cost, service, resilience, and performance expectations.

Strong leadership experience, with the ability to manage operational teams and influence cross-functional partners.

Excellent analytical and problem-solving skills, with a focus on continuous improvement and operational control.

Strong stakeholder management skills across Logistics, Planning, Commercial, Finance, Customer Service, D2C, and regional teams.

Excellent written, verbal, and presentation skills.

BA/BS in Supply Chain, Logistics, Operations, Business, or another related field preferred.
