At Turtle Rock, we make the games we want to play. We re dedicated to providing a collaborative environment where our team members feel empowered with the creative freedom to contribute and grow. While we are based in Orange County, CA, we support flexible work arrangements ranging from in-studio, hybrid, to fully remote throughout North America. Join our award-winning game development team and help create our next hit title.

Live operations is built into the foundation before a game ever launches.

As Senior Live Producer, you re the operational backbone of our upcoming live game, shaping the processes, pipelines, and cross-functional partnerships that will carry it from online tests through launch and beyond. You re not only the day-to-day planner, prioritizer, and advocate for a cross-functional live team spanning QA, release engineering, and production leadership, you re also the person leadership turns to for a clear read on test health, incident status, and what s coming next.

Responsibilities

Champion high-level live initiatives and coordinate across departments to hit delivery goals

Maintain live team roadmap; triage, prioritize, and delegate tasks across the live team to keep operations running smoothly

Partner with QA, Production Leadership, and Release Engineering to ensure successful first party submissions and proper issue tracking

Communicate the health and status of online services regularly to leadership

Coordinate incident reporting for online tests and live game; re-examine processes to future proof against similar issues

Work with live team and partners to ensure new content and events are delivered to the community smoothly and on time

Maintain collaborative relationships with internal departments and external groups

Identify operational and technical risks early and own mitigation strategies

Play the game

Requirements

5+ years’ experience in the games industry, with meaningful time spent in live operations

Good sense of perspective - able to switch between "details" and "big picture" as needed

Experience managing rapid-release content schedules and live operations on online multiplayer titles for Console and PC platforms

Experience shipping console game titles including dealing with first party certification processes

Experience with scheduling tools and managing a project budget

Experience in risk analysis and mitigation, as well as incident report tracking and analysis

Capable of multitasking and managing several important items at once, without allowing anything to be lost in the process

Excellent communicator at all levels — team members, leads, and senior leadership — stays composed and focused under pressure

Preferences

Experience working with remote studios and contractors

Deep understanding of online infrastructure best practices and methodologies including performance, scalability, reliability and security needs and considerations
