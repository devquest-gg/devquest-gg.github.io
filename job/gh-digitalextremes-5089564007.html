ABOUT THIS POSITION

We are seeking a Lead Data Engineer with extensive experience in data infrastructure to join our team. This role serves as a technical leader while also directing the team—ensuring projects stay on track, timelines are met, and direct reports are actively mentored and developed. We need a proactive, solutions-oriented engineer who can rapidly master our current self-hosted systems, identify performance bottlenecks, strategically guide our infrastructure into the future , and lead the team through major, large-scale projects.

Unlike many modern roles that rely entirely on cloud-managed services, this position focused on owning and managing self-hosted and on-premise data stacks.

RESPONSIBILITIES

Technical Leadership: Lead technical project initiatives from conception to delivery, managing project timelines and coordinating cross-functional team efforts.

Team Lead Culture: Mentor and guide direct reports, conducting code reviews, provide technical coaching, and foster engineering best practices. .

Infrastructure Ownership: Manage, maintain, and optimize self-hosted database solutions, focusing on performance tuning, hardware upgrades, and OS/environment monitoring for large-scale data warehousing.

Pipeline Development: Design, develop, and maintain robust ETL/ELT pipelines for data ingestion and processing from varied, complex data sources.

Data Governance Security: Ensure infrastructure and pipelines strictly comply with government policies, data privacy standards, and GDPR regulations.

Collaboration: Partner with data scientists, analysts, and business stakeholders to translate complex data requirements into scalable infrastructure.

REQUIREMENTS

Technical Infrastructure Expertise:

Deep Database Foundations: Extensive hands-on experience managing and optimizing database management systems, specifically PostgreSQL and MongoDB (both relational and NoSQL ecosystems).

Self-Hosted Mastery: Proven experience setting up, maintaining, and tuning self-hosted/on-premise databases and data warehouses.

System Environment: Strong proficiency in SQL and deep comfort working within Linux/Unix environments (command line, shell scripting, system performance tuning).

Data Orchestration: Hands-on experience with modern ETL orchestration tools such as Dagster or Airflow.

Data Modeling: In-depth knowledge of core data warehousing concepts and relational data modeling.

Leadership Project Delivery:

Demonstrated Leadership: Proven track record of taking informal or formal leadership on technical projects, setting milestones, and ensuring deliverables are met.

Mentorship Mindset: Experience guiding, coaching, or mentoring junior/mid-level engineers (e.g., leading code reviews, onboarding team members, helping with career growth).

Communication: Ability to adapt to organizational constraints and effectively communicate architectural decisions to both technical and non-technical stakeholders.
