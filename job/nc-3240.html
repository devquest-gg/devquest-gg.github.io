2D Artist/UI Designer

Nordcurrent · Vilnius

Mid Art Posted 2024-09-05 Full time
Apply on Nordcurrent’s site →

Join our Development Team as a 2D General Artist and shape the way players interact with our world-class games. Collaborating with the other members of our art and design team, you will create visually compelling art and user interfaces that enhance gameplay and provide an exceptional user experience for millions of players globally. In addition to your UI/UX expertise, you will contribute to creating production art, including characters, environments, and other artistic elements that bring our games to life.

Your Key Responsibilities:

Create production art, including characters, environments, and other assets, maintaining a consistent art style.

Create UI assets while collaborating with UX designers to create user-friendly interfaces that elevate the player experience.

A Successful Candidate Must Have:

Excellent digital drawing/painting skills with raster graphics using Adobe Photoshop or other similar software;

Understanding of color theory, composition, and human-computer interaction (HCI) principles;

Experience in creating visually engaging interfaces for casual games or mobile platforms;

Good English language skills.

Extra Great to Have:

Understanding of vector graphics, digital iconography, and typography;

Experience with 3D modeling software and a solid understanding of visual aesthetics;

Ability to create and adapt production art, including characters and environments, to suit various game styles;

A good sense of style and experience working in the casual gaming industry;

Expertise in UX;

Hands-on experience with Figma.

Please note: Applications without portfolios showcasing your work will not be considered.

Salary:  From 2630 EUR/month (gross)
