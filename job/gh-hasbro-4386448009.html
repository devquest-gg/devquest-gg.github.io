Associate Game Designer - D&D

Wizards of the Coast · Renton

Entry Design Posted 2026-08-31 Full time
Apply on Wizards of the Coast’s site →

We take play seriously. We’re looking for curious adventurers ready to find their party, fueled by imagination and drive to build what’s never been built before. At Hasbro and Wizards of the Coast, you’ll collaborate with passionate teams to reimagine our iconic brands and create experiences that spark joy, connection, and community through the magic of play. This is your chance to shape legendary play that lasts a lifetime.

The Dungeons Dragons Game Design team is a collaborative and versatile group dedicated to making D D as fun as it can be. Our team is responsible for D D game content from beginning to end, working with cross-functional teams to ensure the experiences we make resonate with players and fans. This includes mechanical and narrative design, play innovations, playtests, product exploration, and other design-related tasks.

This Associate Game Designer role draws on your D D knowledge, your curiosity and enthusiastic proclivity for learning, and an understanding of game design theory and philosophy. You ll write compelling, fun game content under the leadership of senior designers.

Location Work Arrangement

This role is based in Renton, WA and follows a hybrid schedule, with in-office collaboration Tuesday, Wednesday, and Thursday each week.

What You ll Do:

Conceptualize, design, and develop portions of D D products—especially discrete game elements such as monsters, magic items, and spells—with guidance from project leads.

Analyze and incorporate player feedback to craft outstanding game content

Assist in preparing products for publication

Contribute to design documentation and research

Help review material for game balance, clarity, consistency, and player enjoyment

Brainstorm themes, stories, encounters, and discrete game design elements to drive narrative-based gameplay experiences

Participate as an active, creative member of a cross-functional team, contributing ideas in brainstorms, playtests, and meetings alongside artists, designers, editors, and producers

What You ll Bring:

At least 1 year of experience in tabletop game design, including both narrative and rules design

Ability to consistently translate designs from ideas to final implementation

Comfort working in shared documentation systems (e.g., Confluence) and version-controlled design environments

Strong creative writing, storytelling, and technical design skills

Strong familiarity with Dungeons Dragons and proficiency in the fifth edition rules

Strong collaboration and communication skills

A deep understanding of Dungeons Dragons game mechanics, balance considerations, and modular design structures

Solid technical writing and editorial skills, including attention to detail and dedication to accuracy

Strong time/project management skills with ability to multitask and prioritize

Curiosity, enthusiasm, and genuine love of games, with a motivation to learn more

Nice to Have:

Experience crafting for or contributing to Unearthed Arcana or other public-facing playtest programs

Experience generating player-facing rules content and refining it through testing and review

Familiarity with D D Beyond, VTTs, or digital-first content pipelines
