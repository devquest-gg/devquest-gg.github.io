DESIGN

What We Do

Put your design skills to work at a cutting-edge company that challenges the norm. From character and level design to competitive gameplay and system design, help shape the future of interactive entertainment at Epic.

What You ll Do

Epic is looking for a Senior Level Designer to join our Design team working on Fortnite Battle Royale. In this role, you ll use your level design experience, knowledge of design tools, and understanding of competitive multiplayer gameplay to create and evolve memorable locations and experiences for Fortnite players. Level Designers at Epic are part of a cross-disciplinary content team working together to build compelling gameplay spaces that expand and evolve the Fortnite universe.

In this role, you will

Design and build buildings, natural landmarks, POIs, and gameplay spaces for Fortnite using modular kits while balancing visual quality, gameplay needs, and player flow

Create compelling and competitive PvP spaces that support a variety of playstyles, combat scenarios, traversal options, and player strategies

Own and drive the vision for in-game locations from early planning and blockout through iteration, polish, and ship

Use environmental and ambient storytelling to bring locations and the broader Fortnite world to life

Prototype, playtest, iterate, and make informed design decisions based on player experience and gameplay needs

Partner closely with Art, Gameplay, Production, and other disciplines to align creative vision with gameplay and production requirements

Provide thoughtful design feedback and help maintain a high-quality bar across the team Identify and solve complex level design problems while working within technical, gameplay, and production constraints

What we re looking for

7+ years of professional Level Design experience, or equivalent experience

Experience designing and shipping multiplayer PvP levels or gameplay spaces; Battle Royale experience is a plus

Experience using Unreal Engine (UE4/UE5) or comparable professional level design tools

Experience taking levels or major gameplay spaces from concept/blockout through iteration, polish, and ship

Understanding of multiplayer level design fundamentals, including player flow, combat spaces, traversal, sightlines, readability, pacing, and risk/reward

Experience working directly with environment artists on prop placement, lighting, mood/atmosphere, composition, and design markup

Ability to collaborate effectively across disciplines, communicate design intent, provide feedback, and incorporate feedback from others

Demonstrated ability to independently identify problems, develop solutions, and iterate based on playtesting and player experience
