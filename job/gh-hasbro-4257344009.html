What You ll Do:

DRIVE the rhythm of business for the Secret Lair product, program, business and digital teams.

ENSURE partners receive product information, SKU level documentation, relevant business performance metrics and KSPs accurately and on time.

PARTNER with producers and creative teams ensuring successful creation and launch of all Secret Lair products.

BUILD user-friendly dashboards, reports and communications that are audience-specific to ensure clarity and alignment. Maintain and update reporting tools and dashboards.

ANTICIPATE and adapt to new situations and/or changing demands and respond proactively to business needs.

COLLABORATE with internal operations and logistics teams to manage production and delivery timelines.

What You ll Bring:

Previous experience as a Producer, Program Manager, Marketing/Business Analyst, or similar role.

Ability to jump into fast-paced and evolving situations and make things better for your team.

An organized, meticulous approach to bookkeeping and building efficiencies to further streamline processes.

Excellent communication skills including: clear and concise written and verbal communication, editing, as well as accuracy and attention to detail.

You possess a growth mindset.

Proficiency with Microsoft Office Suite (especially Excel) and familiarity with Smartsheet.

Nice to Have:

First-hand experience in online/DTC sales or product/program management.
