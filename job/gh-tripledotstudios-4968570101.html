Who are we?

Tripledot Studios is one of the largest independent mobile games companies in the world.

We are a multi-award-winning organisation, with a global 2,500+ strong team across 12 studios.

Our expanded portfolio includes some of the biggest titles in mobile gaming, collectively reaching top chart positions around the world and engaging over 25 million daily active users.

Tripledot’s guiding principle is that when people love what they do, what they do will be loved by others.

We’re building a company we’re proud of. One filled with driven, incredibly smart and detail-orientated people, who LOVE making games.

Our ambition is to be the most successful games company in the world, and we’re just getting started.

The role is working within our studio: Puzzleplay

About Puzzleplay

Puzzleplay is Tripledot’s new player rewards platform, designed to make playing our games even more fun and engaging. By playing our casual, free-to-play mobile games, users can earn points, complete challenges, and win real prizes. It’s our way of giving back to players and rewarding them for doing what they already love. Puzzleplay is a key part of our growth strategy, combining smart engagement mechanics with high-quality game content.

Role Overview

We are looking for a Monesation Operations Associate to join our Jakarta Office. This role will work closely with our world-class Marketing team (based in London) to grow our mobile game apps. We’re looking for an extremely careful and detail-oriented person who is also able to support the Product and Marketing teams in improving our games’ monetisation.

Key Responsibilities

Optimising to grow marketing revenue in the millions, and impressions in the hundreds of millions

Constantly improving and growing the team’s operational capabilities

Managing and improving the team’s dashboards to help monitor business performance

Identifying and producing lists of competitor ads for the marketing team

Required Skills, Knowledge and Expertise

Detail-oriented and extremely organized/careful

Good communication skills in English (required to communicate in)

Ability to work in a collaborative, fast-paced environment

Analytical and metric-driven

Strong passion to learn about the mobile gaming / adtech industries
