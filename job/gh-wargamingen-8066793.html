Job Overview

Wargaming Belgrade is looking for a creative 2D Artist who thrives on variety, enjoys early-stage visual development, and sees constraints as an opportunity for innovation. In this role, creativity is your strongest asset. You will explore visual directions, develop concepts, and shape the mood and atmosphere of in-game events and marketing initiatives from the earliest stages.

Our 2D Art team is responsible for the visual identity of the World of Warships franchise, creating everything from marketing key art and in-game store graphics to client-facing content, backgrounds, animatics, and skins. While our visual style is grounded in realistic naval art, built on photobash and 3D foundations, we re looking for someone who can bring fresh ideas and a strong artistic vision. You will take creative ownership of complex tasks, develop visual solutions from scratch, collaborate closely with Art Directors, and help shape the creative direction of our projects.

Reports to

2D Team Lead

What will you do?

Illustrations: create high-quality promotional illustrations, produce polished art for in-game shops and publishing, including loading screens, banners, previews, event backgrounds, and special offer images

Visual Development: early sketches for events and marketing campaigns. Some skins concepts (in case of ships it’s a mix of hard surf and environment concept art)

Workflow Execution: utilize photobash, matte painting, and 3D renders (Blender) to build detailed, realistic scenes with ships, environments, and props. Most often, 3D models will be provided by the 3D team

Creative Ownership: take full ownership of your work from the sketch phase to final polish, with minimal guidance and strong attention to detail

In-House Collaboration: prepare sketches and concept drafts to pass to teammates for further refinement. Work closely with the Art Director, publishing teams, and other departments to propose visual solutions and ensure alignment

Problem Solving: identify design challenges quickly and propose creative, practical solutions during art reviews and collaborative meetings

What are we looking for?

3+ years of experience working as a Concept Artist, 2D Artist, or Illustrator in the GameDev industry

Expert-level skills in Adobe Photoshop

Hands-on experience with 3D software (ideally Blender) to block out scenes, set lighting, and render bases for overpaint

Excellent understanding of composition, lighting, color theory, perspective, and design principles, with the ability to clearly convey form and atmosphere

Experienced with matte painting and photobash techniques

Upper-Intermediate (B2) or higher English knowledge

What additional skills will help you stand out?

Stronger 3D skills: modeling, texturing, or sculpting (e.g., ZBrush, 3D-Coat).

Experience giving precise, structured, and constructive art feedback to in-house team members

Ability to adapt to different art styles when needed (realistic, semi-realistic, stylized)

Exceptional sketching skills for storyboards and quick proof-of-concept designs

AAA experience is a strong plus

What would we like to see in your portfolio?

A portfolio showcasing realistic or semi-realistic concept design, environments, and cinematic key art

Work mode

During the probation period: onsite

After the probation period: onsite or hybrid (3 days per week in Belgrade office)

About Wargaming

Wargaming is an award-winning online game developer and publisher headquartered in Nicosia, Cyprus. Operating since 1998, Wargaming has become one of the leaders in the gaming industry with 15 offices worldwide, including studios in Chicago, Prague, Shanghai, Tokyo, and Vilnius. Our diverse and multicultural team works together to deliver a top-class experience to millions of players who enjoy Wargaming s titles across all major gaming platforms. Our flagship products include free-to-play hits World of Tanks, World of Warships and World of Tanks Blitz.
