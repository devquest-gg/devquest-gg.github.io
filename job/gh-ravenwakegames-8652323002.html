Position Title: Senior Game Engineer

Studio/Department: Standing Stone Games

Employee Type: Full Time, Regular

Location : Remote ( within BC, Canada)

Salary Range: $120,000 - $140,000 CAD

Ravenwake Games is the newest studio within Enad Global 7, a global leader in video game development and publishing. Backed by iconic franchises such as EverQuest®, PlanetSide®, DC Universe™ Online, Dungeons Dragons Online®, The Lord of the Rings Online™, Magic: The Gathering Online, and MechWarrior®, we draw on a legacy of genre‑defining experiences. We’re committed to delivering entertainment that inspires, engages, and unites players around the world. At Ravenwake Games, our mission is to craft immersive worlds that bring players together, build vibrant communities, and deliver unforgettable gameplay at every turn.

Description

We are looking for a Senior Game Engineer to bring our favorite Dungeons and Dragons and Lord of the Rings moments off the page and into our massively multiplayer online game on PC! You ll collaborate with designers and other disciplines to build engaging, memorable gameplay experiences.

Responsibilities

Work closely with game designers to understand the overall vision for a system

Develop and test code for new systems and augment existing systems

Solve complex technical problems across game engine, tools, and live-service systems

Work with QA to help develop test plans for and debug a system

Tune and fix bugs in existing game-level systems as required to enhance the game

Provide reliable and accurate time estimates for tasks

Document systems thoroughly, both in terms of design and implementation

Participate in code reviews to help maintain code quality across the team

Other duties and responsibilities as assigned by the manager

Qualifications

Interest in working on the game user interface and game systems

4-7+ years of programming experience, gaming industry experience preferred

Strong problem-solving skills, with advanced knowledge of algorithms, data structures, and optimization

Proficient working on a variety of systems in the realms of game engine, tools, game support services and the games themselves

Debugging, including post-mortem debugging

Experience working in C++ and SQL

Able to collaborate with other engineers and other non-technical team members, including as game teams, customer service and ops

Bonuses

At least one shipped title

Microsoft WCF, C#, JSON, SOAP

Postgres

Python, Perl, or other scripting languages
