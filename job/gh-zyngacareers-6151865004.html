Join us and be part of the play! Position Overview:

Zynga’s Game of Thrones Slots team is looking for a Full Stack Engineer in the Austin, Chicago, San Mateo, or Toronto areas to join our team as Software Engineer.

We have a well-rounded, passionate and tight-knit, distributed team with a healthy mix of engineering skill levels and are looking to strengthen our team in addressing the diverse and complex technical challenges at the core of our products. We need a self-driven and motivated engineer who is a strong communicator, works well as a team player, and performs well under tight deadlines.

The candidate would have an opportunity to work on core slot machine gameplay and features (including UI / UX), engine-level systems, and have a focus on solving challenging problems towards the development of slot machines. The ideal candidate will have strong working knowledge of Unity and C#, plus experience building features on the server backend (PHP preferred).

Our mission is to “Connect the World through Games” by building a truly social experience that makes the world a better place. To be successful in this role you will have a passion for games, a high level of integrity, great technical depth and skills, and a strong desire to grow in skill and experience building and deploying successful software features for live Games that delight our players.

What You ll Do:

Architect and develop features and slot game functionality in Unity with C#, and on the server backend (PHP preferred)

Dive into the internals of slot engine systems to extend them as-needed

Develop tools for engineering and content creators

Define and scope out tasks systems

Triage and address bugs quickly

What You Bring:

B.Sc. in Computer Science, or equivalent

1–2 years of industry experience as a Software Developer. Candidates from other fields should have 3+ years of relevant software engineering experience

Experience with git

Strong working knowledge of Unity and C#

Experience building features on a server backend (PHP preferred)

Solid foundation in data structures, algorithms and software methodologies

Excellent communication skills and comfort working in a collaborative environment

Bonus Points:

Mobile game development experience

Performance profiling and optimization experience

Experience developing Slot Machines
