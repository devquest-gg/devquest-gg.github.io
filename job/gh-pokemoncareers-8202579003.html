Get to know The Pokémon Company International

The Pokémon Company International manages the Pokémon property outside of Asia and is responsible for brand management, licensing and marketing, the Pokémon Trading Card Game, the animated TV series, home entertainment, and the official Pokémon website. Pokémon was launched in Japan in 1996 and today is one of the most popular children’s entertainment properties in the world.

Learn more online at corporate.pokemon.com and pokemon.com .

Get to know the role

Job Title: Associate Product Marketing Manager, Video Games

Job Summary: The Associate Product Marketing Manager, Video Games, will be supporting the execution of various marketing programs that support key video game products and campaigns. This role will assist the Manager, Product Marketing (Video games) with the creation and sharing of product-specific marketing plans, assets, guidelines, and approvals with external and internal partners.

People Manager: No

What you’ll do

Work closely with internal stakeholders, channel support teams (e.g. PR, Social Media and Pokemon.com) and publishing partners to lead the marketing strategy for the title(s) you manage, with oversight from Manager.

Where applicable, manage effective cross-platform strategies (e.g. Nintendo hardware and mobile platforms).

Assist with creation and execution of videogame specific or brand related marketing initiatives.

Campaign ‘postmortem’ analysis.

Generation and dissemination of regular marketing and sales reports and marketing data.

Gather and manage marketing assets and maintain asset library.

Coordinate videogame marketing material approvals for EMEA region.

Maintain a thorough understanding of brand guidelines.

Oversee the execution of other ad-hoc videogame marketing projects.

Record of meeting minutes and actions.

Gather and share current videogame market landscape and competitor activity.

What you’ll bring

Two (2) to four (4) years of relevant professional marketing experience (ideally within a videogame company) or a demonstrated equivalent level of expertise.

Bachelor s degree ideally Marketing/Advertising related, or a demonstrated equivalent level of applicable experience.

Proven history of driving effective marketing strategies driving customer satisfaction and product adoption.

Keen interest in videogames - in particular, Pokémon on Nintendo consoles and mobile devices.

Experience partnering closely with Japanese and global stakeholders is a plus.

Excellent attention to detail.

Excellent listening and communication skills.

Familiarity with marketing reporting and analytical tools.

Excellent organization skills.

Ability to act with a high degree of autonomy and initiative.

Strong problem-solving skills, with a proactive approach to project management.

Conversant with MS Office packages, especially PowerPoint and Excel.

Additional fluency in European or Japanese language a plus.

Base Salary Range: For this role, new hires generally start between £42,000.00 - £54,500.00 per year. The full range is £42,000.00 - £73,400.00 per year.
