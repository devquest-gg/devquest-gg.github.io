Every day, tens of millions of people come to Roblox to explore, create, play, learn, and connect with friends in 3D immersive digital experiences– all created by our global community of developers and creators.

At Roblox, we’re building the tools and platform that empower our community to bring any experience that they can imagine to life. Our vision is to reimagine the way people come together, from anywhere in the world, and on any device.

We’re on a mission to connect a billion people with optimism and civility, and looking for amazing talent to help us get there.

A career at Roblox means you’ll be working to shape the future of human interaction, solving unique technical challenges at scale, and helping to create safer, more civil shared experiences for everyone.

Roblox is seeking a Content Designer to join our Monetization team and drive the future of our platform s economy. In this role, you will play a crucial role in defining the content strategy and information architecture of strategic initiatives across Monetization, creating seamless and compelling experiences that empower both our users and creators. You will be a key player in shaping how we grow revenue, enhance developer earnings, and evolve our core monetization offerings within the Roblox ecosystem.

You Will:

Own and deliver UX content for key monetization surfaces and flows at Roblox, from early exploration through launch

Develop a deep understanding of user and developer needs, behaviors, and motivations to inform content design decisions across payment, purchase, and monetization experiences

Bring a fresh perspective and deep industry knowledge to drive innovation in monetization design

Collaborate with product managers, designers, researchers, and engineers to ensure experiences are valuable, understandable, and easy to use

Apply UX writing and content strategy to improve clarity, usability, and engagement across player, parent, and creator experiences

Contribute to building and maintaining content guidelines, patterns, and frameworks that support consistency in voice and tone

Communicate and document content decisions clearly, using empathy, logic, research insights, and data to inform recommendations

Advocate for high-quality content design within your area of ownership

Help improve team processes and collaboration by contributing to shared ways of working

Identify and implement AI solutions to improve content quality and scale

You Have:

Bachelor s degree or equivalent experience in Product Design, User Experience Design, Graphic Design, or a related field

Extensive experience (5+ years) in content design, with a strong portfolio demonstrating expertise in designing for e-commerce, payments, or innovative consumer experiences

Strong systems thinking and a track record of designing comprehensive and connected user experiences

Solid understanding of user-centered design principles, information architecture, e-commerce best practices, and monetization strategies

Experience collaborating with and influencing cross-functional partners, including product managers, designers, researchers, and engineers

A strong drive to deeply understand product goals from both a business and user perspective, fostering a culture of learning and iteration

Excellent communication and presentation skills, with the ability to articulate content design concepts and rationale clearly and persuasively

A collaborative mindset with the ability to work effectively across teams and influence design direction

A strong interest in the gaming industry, user-generated content platforms, and the creator economy

Keen interest in user experience trends, fintech, and emerging technologies

A self-motivated approach to continuously expand design skills and explore new tools and techniques, including a proven ability to incorporate AI into your workflow
