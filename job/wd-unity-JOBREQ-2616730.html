The opportunity At Unity Technologies, we provide a growing number of services to our customers as an end-to-end solution for game development and operations, player engagement, and monetization.

We are looking for a hard-working Senior Counsel with experience advising on product development, go-to-market, and privacy-related matters for cloud, analytics, SaaS, and game operations-related products. This role will support the rapid growth and evolution of Unity s business unit that helps developers create game and app experiences in the ‘Engine’ business, including cloud services. Come work on the intersection of our ground-breaking Unity game engine, online game management, and our innovative multiplayer ecosystem. Collaborate with global teams across Unity to advise on various legal issues and help build, implement and maintain processes and tools that support our commercial operations. This is a rare opportunity to work in a dynamic, fast-paced environment with our team, where your work will have a direct impact on our business. If you are the can-do and collaborative teammate with a passion for solving problems and driving cross-functional projects over the finish line, this would be an outstanding fit for you! What you’ll be doing Advise product, engineering, and go-to-market teams on legal issues across the Create product lifecycle, including product development, compliance, intellectual property, and customer-facing terms Partner with the business on new AI products and early-stage moonshot projects, turning novel legal questions into practical guidance Draft and negotiate technology transactions, both inbound and outbound Advise on privacy and data protection from a product and deal perspective What we’re looking for Active membership in a provincial or state bar Experience in a law firm and/or in-house legal department advising on technology transactions and products, preferably for cloud-based software or SaaS Working knowledge of privacy and data protection law as applied to products and deals Ability to assess legal risk and give practical advice that balances risk and reward in support of business objectives Familiarity with, or strong interest in, the legal issues raised by AI products You might also have The confidence and ability to collaborate with senior executives, clients, and co-workers in a professional and positive manner.

A self-starter with a roll-up-your-sleeves demeanor, strong attention to detail, solution-orientation, and “keep it human” approach to communicating.
