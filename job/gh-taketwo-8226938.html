The Challenge

Design beautiful, creative, and innovative graphical assets primarily for employee facing-communications and social media. Building tools, templates, and bespoke design projects. Reporting to the Sr. Director of Internal Communications, this role will partner with business functions that communicate directly to employees, such as Events, IT and HR.

NOTE: This is a 12-month fixed-term contract position.

What You’ll Take On

Design captivating graphical assets delivered in formats such as HTML, digital signage, presentations, documents, reports, social media, web assets, and video.

Follow established design guidelines, standards and approach to drive consistent high level employee communications.

Ensure designs and content consistently and positively reflect the brand image and company ideals.

Apply and maintain the company s visual identity through creating reusable guidance and tools, and helping colleagues produce consistent, on-brand communications.

Regularly explore new ways to communicate with employees via animation, video, interactive tools etc.

Collaborate with all teams that directly communicate with employees to help create a seamless experience for employees.

Turn project briefs into visual designs within agreed timelines.

Create and maintain a library of templates and visual collateral.

What You Bring

4 years+ experience working in a design-based role in the creative industry.

Skilled in Google Suite and Microsoft Office products.

High level of proficiency with design tools such as Adobe Creative Suite and Canva, and willingness to learn new skills and tools as technology evolves.

Good knowledge of design fundamentals including colour, layout, and typography.

Highly creative.

A keen attention to detail and aesthetic.

Ability to collaborate and prioritise work.

Proactive with strong time management skills.

Strong stakeholder management, communication, and follow-through.

Flexibility, adaptability and willingness to support those around you.
