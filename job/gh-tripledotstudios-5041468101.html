Role Overview

We’re looking for a Lead Game Designer to drive the design and execution of features, events, and gameplay systems across our live mobile games. Reporting to the Product Director, you’ll lead our game design team while remaining deeply hands-on, turning product goals and player insights into engaging experiences that improve retention, engagement, and monetization.

This is a hands-on leadership role. You’ll design features from concept through launch, build prototypes, define mechanics and systems, and work closely with product, development, art, and analytics to bring ideas to life. You’ll be responsible for ensuring every feature has a clear purpose, measurable goals, and delivers meaningful results for both our players and the business.

AI is central to how we work. You’ll use it extensively across research, design ideation, prototyping, documentation, and analysis, building new workflows and exploring how AI can help us design, test, and iterate faster.

We’re a small, ambitious team, so you’ll have real ownership over the experiences we create and the opportunity to make a significant impact. We’re looking for someone who combines exceptional game design instincts with strong analytical thinking, understands what makes free-to-play mobile games successful, and is just as comfortable doing the work as they are leading others.

Responsibilities

Lead game design across our live titles , establishing design standards, guiding priorities, and ensuring features, events, and systems support our product goals.

Own feature design from concept through launch and beyond , developing mechanics, systems, documentation, and prototypes while supporting implementation, playtesting, and iteration.

Design for measurable outcomes , establishing clear KPI targets and success benchmarks for every feature, and evaluating performance against those goals after launch.

Drive engaging gameplay and progression systems , applying a deep understanding of player psychology, retention loops, reward mechanics, and monetization to create compelling experiences.

Lead and mentor the game design team , providing direction, reviewing work, developing talent, and maintaining high standards while continuing to design features yourself.

Collaborate on game economy design , working closely with the Game Economy Designer to ensure features, rewards, and progression systems support healthy and sustainable economies.

Own game design documentation and delivery , producing clear GDDs, requirements, acceptance criteria, and configuration data to support efficient development and on-time releases.

Use data to inform design decisions , analyzing player behaviour, evaluating A/B tests, and translating insights into improvements to existing and future features.

Collaborate across product development , working closely with product managers, developers, artists, QA, and analytics to align design decisions, resolve challenges, and deliver high-quality features.

Stay ahead of mobile game trends , researching successful casual and social casino games, understanding emerging mechanics and design approaches, and identifying opportunities for our products.

Champion AI-assisted game design , using AI extensively for research, ideation, prototyping, documentation, and analysis while developing workflows that improve the team’s efficiency and capabilities.

Requirements

7+ years of professional game design experience, including at least 3 years working on live free-to-play mobile games.

A proven track record of designing and launching successful features, with measurable improvements to retention, engagement, monetization, or other key product KPIs.

Exceptional understanding of free-to-play game design, including gameplay loops, progression systems, LiveOps, monetization mechanics, and player psychology.

Strong analytical and product instincts, with the ability to use data, player insights, and commercial objectives to guide design decisions.

Experience designing features end to end, from early concepts and documentation through implementation, testing, launch, and iteration.

Strong familiarity with game analytics and experimentation, including dashboards, A/B testing, and working directly with data through SQL or spreadsheets.

Experience leading or mentoring game designers, providing direction, feedback, and maintaining high standards while remaining hands-on with design work.

Extensive practical experience using AI tools, incorporating AI into research, design, prototyping, documentation, or analysis workflows.

Strong communication and organizational skills, with the ability to articulate design decisions, align cross-functional teams, and deliver high-quality work against production timelines.

A hands-on, adaptable mindset, comfortable moving between creative design, technical problem-solving, data analysis, and production needs to move the product forward.
