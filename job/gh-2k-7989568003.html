What We Need

Reporting to the Manager, Paid, this role is a key member of the "pod" team, with a primary focus on driving campaign execution and delivery with a strong sense of ownership and accountability across media plans that support overarching brand and performance strategies . The Associate Manager is responsible for managing detailed planning workflows, ensuring campaigns across platforms such as Meta, Google, and 1st party channels are executed with precision alongside agency managed media . This role requires a proactive professional who can own end-to-end performance tracking, maintain strong platform partner relationships, and drive campaign delivery across in house and agency with a high sense of accountability .

What You’ll Do

Campaign Execution Pod Delivery

Drive the execution and delivery of paid media campaigns with a strong sense of ownership and accountability within the pod team .

Translate media planning into actionable steps by managing detailed workflows and rigorous QA processes to ensure accuracy and timely delivery .

Own the "live" process for campaigns, coordinating the implementation of creative assets, tracking, and targeting parameters across multiple platforms .

Monitor daily campaign pacing and performance tracking, conducting deep-dive post-campaign analyses (PCAs) to uncover actionable insights .

Maintain clear, up-to-date documentation and ensure all timelines are met for the overarching media strategies .

Contribute to planning discussions to strengthen strategic understanding .

Platform Activation Partner Management

Manage recurring platform representative relationships and meetings (e.g., Meta, Google, TikTok), actively seeking opportunities to maximize platform value and improve deliverables .

Execute and optimize "hands-on-keyboard" campaigns within in-house media platforms, ensuring alignment with 1st party data activation goals .

Participate in agency and partner meetings with a clear understanding of campaign goals, ensuring partner outputs align with tactical objectives .

Actively build and hold relationships with key platform partners to stay informed on alpha/beta opportunities and technical troubleshooting .

Cross-Functional Collaboration Support

Communicate effectively across the team to ensure clarity and alignment on campaign beats, proactively resolving blockers where possible .

Demonstrate a growing awareness of cross-functional interdependencies (Creative, Analytics, Marketing Tech) and contribute to planning discussions to strengthen strategic understanding .

Identify and escalate resourcing gaps or scope misalignments early to enable smooth execution and cross-functional alignment.

Operational Financial Oversight

Manage the internal finance and procurement processes for the pod, including PO creation, budget tracking, and invoice reconciliation .

Support the Manager in the application of commercial context to planning decisions, flagging data inconsistencies or risks .

Contribute to a culture of curiosity and collaboration by proactively sharing knowledge and offering support to peers .

What Will Make You A Great Fit

Core Competencies

Cross-functional Collaboration Influence: Drives campaign execution and delivery; communicates effectively across the team to ensure alignment and resolve blockers .

External Partner Management: Owns execution of partner deliverables and manages recurring platform rep relationships to maximize value .

Planning, Prioritization Execution: Translates strategy into actionable steps; maintains up-to-date documentation and ensures timeline adherence .

Insight, Quality Risk Management: Owns end-to-end campaign performance tracking and QA; conducts deep-dive analyses to inform future best practices .

Strategic Communication: Communicates clearly in written updates and meetings; asks clarifying questions to ensure alignment on next steps .

Required Qualifications, Knowledge, and Job-Related Skills

2–4 years of experience in media planning or digital marketing execution .

Established professional contributor with a full understanding of Meta Ads Manager and Google Ads interfaces .

Experience in tactical campaign execution, including budget management, trafficking, and QA processes .

Proven ability to analyze multiple data sets to support performance tracking and ongoing optimization .

Strong communication skills with experience managing professional relationships with external platform partners .

Proficient in Excel/Google Sheets for data documentation and financial tracking .

Prefer red Qualifications

Experience in the gaming or entertainment industry .

Familiarity with Customer Data Platform (CDP) activation .

Experience in an agenc y or high-growth "pod" environment .
