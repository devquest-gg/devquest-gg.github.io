Senior Character Grooming & Technical Artist

Black Banner Studios · České Budějovice · Remote

Senior Art Remote Posted 2026-11-20 Full time
Apply on Black Banner Studios’s site →

Legacy of Valor is an ambitious medieval RPG and strategy game combining massive real-time battles, third-person melee combat, and tactical army control in Unreal Engine 5. At Black Banner Studios, we're looking for an experienced Senior Character Grooming & Technical Artist who combines excellent artistic judgment with strong technical expertise in realistic hair and fur creation, character integration, and real-time optimization. Our proprietary Large Army technology allows thousands of characters to appear simultaneously on vast battlefields, while our third-person gameplay and cinematics demand excellent visual quality up close. We need someone who can create beautiful, natural-looking human hairstyles, beards, and facial hair, ideally alongside realistic animal grooms, and make them work efficiently in Unreal Engine 5. You'll also help our character artists turn high-quality assets into optimized, game-ready characters, ensuring that technical constraints never unnecessarily compromise artistic quality. If you believe you'd be a great fit for this role, even if you don't meet every requirement listed below, we'd still love to hear from you. Please don't hesitate to apply! Create beautiful, realistic human hair, beards, eyebrows, eyelashes, and facial hair using professional grooming tools. Develop and optimize real-time grooming solutions in Unreal Engine 5, balancing visual fidelity with performance. Create or adapt hair-card, strand-based, and LOD-appropriate solutions for different character types and viewing distances. Collaborate with character artists to integrate finished models, textures, materials, and grooms into Unreal Engine. Optimize character assets, including geometry, textures, materials, LODs, and runtime rendering costs. Develop efficient character integration workflows and reusable technical solutions suitable for a small production team. Troubleshoot visual and technical issues involving hair, skin materials, character assets, and rendering performance. Work closely with character artists, technical artists, animators, and programmers to maintain consistent quality across character representations. Extensive professional experience in character grooming, character technical art, or related real-time character production. Strong artistic grooming skills, demonstrated through a portfolio of beautiful, realistic human hair and facial hair. Practical experience with professional grooming tools such as XGen, Ornatrix, Houdini, or equivalent software. Strong experience bringing character grooms and assets into Unreal Engine 5, with an understanding of real-time rendering and optimization. Good understanding of strand-based grooming, hair cards, hair shading, and scalable grooming solutions. Experience with game-ready character workflows, including topology, UVs, textures, materials, and LODs. Strong technical problem-solving skills and the ability to balance artistic quality, performance, and production efficiency. Ability to collaborate with character artists while independently taking responsibility for technical integration and grooming tasks. Passion for video games, particularly medieval RPGs, open-world games, and strategy titles. Experience creating realistic animal grooming, particularly horses and other large mammals. Experience optimizing hair and fur for large numbers of simultaneously visible characters. Advanced knowledge of Unreal Engine's Groom system, hair materials, and binding workflows. Experience creating realistic skin, hair, and fur shaders within Unreal Engine's material system. Experience with Substance Designer, Substance Painter, or procedural texturing workflows. Strong hands-on 3D modeling, sculpting, or character texturing skills. Experience with procedural grooming, hair simulation, or grooming pipeline automation. Experience preparing and optimizing character assets for animation, facial performance, and cinematics. Experience working on AAA and / or open-world game projects. Location: Remote / International, with the option to work in the Czech Republic. Engagement: Full-time, long-term collaboration. Contract: Contractor / B2B worldwide, or direct employment for candidates residing in or willing to relocate to the Czech Republic. Start Date: As soon as mutually agreed. Language: Professional working proficiency in English. Compensation: Competitive, based on experience and expertise.
