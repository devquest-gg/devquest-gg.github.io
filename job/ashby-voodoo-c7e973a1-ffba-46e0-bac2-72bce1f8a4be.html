Founded in 2013, Voodoo is a tech company that creates mobile games and apps with a mission to entertain the world. Gathering 800 employees, 7 billion downloads, and over 200 million active users, Voodoo is the #3 mobile publisher worldwide in terms of downloads after Google and Meta. Our portfolio includes chart-topping games like Mob Control and Block Jam, alongside popular Studios like BeachBum and apps such as BeReal and Wizz.

This position is available fully remote within a +/- 2hr time difference with Paris..

Team

Join Cortex, a small, fast-moving studio building smart mobile puzzle games — The Almanac, a daily ritual for tens of thousands of players, and our new titles like Shikaku. We're a lean, growth-driven team backed by Voodoo. You'll sit at the heart of how we find and convert new players.

Role

As Cortex's Growth Manager, you'll own the creative engine behind our user acquisition. Your job is to produce a high volume of ad creatives with AI tools, test them fast, and find what makes players click and install. You'll start on creative production and ASO, and progressively grow into monitoring and steering our UA campaigns day-to-day. This is a junior role, ideal for a first or second professional experience — we hire on your creative output and your energy, not your CV.

Responsibilities

- Produce a high volume of ad creatives (video, static, playable concepts) using AI tools like Scenario, Midjourney, Runway and CapCut

- Test creatives, read the results, and iterate fast to bring the CPI down

- Own ASO across the App Store: keywords, screenshots, and continuous iteration

- Watch the market and competitors to feed fresh creative angles

- Support and progressively take ownership of daily UA monitoring (AppLovin, Meta)

- Work hand-in-hand with the founder, and level up on UA economics through an onboarding with Voodoo's growth team

Profile

- Gen Z, marketing brain paired with creative hands

- Already creating on your own — a faceless page, UGC, AI experiments. You bring a portfolio, not a diploma

- Fluent with AI creative tools, or the kind of person who learns a new tool in an afternoon

- Analytical curiosity: you want to understand why a creative converts

- Huge energy and ownership, comfortable shipping 10 rough creatives to find the 1 that works

- Video editing is a strong plus

- Fluent English

Benefits: Competitive salary, performance bonus, and other benefits according to the country you reside in.
