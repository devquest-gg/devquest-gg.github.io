Product Specialist

Bigger Games · Istanbul

Mid Other Onsite Posted 2026-06-23 Full time
Apply on Bigger Games’s site →

We're looking for a Product Specialist who will be creating amazing games and building the best experience for our users in collaboration with a talented team. At Bigger, we are focused on player motivation and engagement and our Product team is the key player that will help us fulfill this mission.

If you are a passionate product specialist who loves games and shares our passion for enjoying and crafting games, come and join us!

ABOUT THIS ROLE:

- Take responsibility of the games from design to their main metrics

-  Help developing a strategic roadmap for improving the experience for millions of players

- Analyze user data to improve the user experience and monitor main metricsIterate with A/B tests and come up with data-driven product decisions

- Work closely with all functions to make the products that people love

- Work on every aspect of your product such as brainstorming a new feature, releasing a new update or improving an animation in the game

- Closely follow mobile game trends and gather market intelligence

- Optimize and track monetization channels to improve LTV

YOU COULD BE A GREAT FIT IF:

- Bachelor’s degree in Engineering, Mathematics, Economics or relevant fields

- Minimum 2 years of relevant business experience

- A good sense of gameplay experience and special attention to detail

- Good analytical skills to make data-driven decisions

- Passionate about startup environment

- Being a collaborative team player

- Experience with Excel and SQL is a big plus

BONUS POINT IF:

- You’re familiar with hybrid casual games and actively keep up with the latest trends. If not, now’s a great time to start!

- You’re curious about the gaming industry and eager to learn more.

- You enjoy working in fast-moving, dynamic environments.

BENEFITS:

- Be part of a top-tier team, learn from the best, and create games players love.

- Competitive compensation.

- Health insurance with unlimited coverage.

- A beautiful, centrally located office in the heart of Istanbul.

- Opportunities to attend global conferences and stay connected with the industry

- Relocation support to make your move hassle-free if needed.
