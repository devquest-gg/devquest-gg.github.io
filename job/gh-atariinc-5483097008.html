Associate Producer - Atari Location: Remote (US)

About Us

Atari is an interactive entertainment company and an iconic gaming industry brand recognized worldwide for its multi-platform games and licensed products. Atari owns and/or manages a portfolio of more than 400 games and franchises.

The Atari family of brands includes Digital Eclipse, Nightdive Studios, Infogrames, AtariAge, MobyGames, as well as Coatsink, Early Morning Studios, and Stormteller Games spanning game development, publishing, and community experiences worldwide.

Atari operates internationally with offices in the US, UK, France, Germany, Sweden, and India.our dynamic team and play a key role in shaping the financial health of our diverse business operations.

About the Role

As an Associate Producer working on Atari’s rapidly growing publishing label of projects, you will act as both a publishing producer and junior release manager supporting the launch of a large number of small titles across PC and consoles throughout the year. You’ll be responsible for tracking key project milestones, working closely with license partners for approvals, and managing the setup and eventual release of projects through first-party management systems.

What You’ll Do

Oversee multiple projects and their backlogs from start to finish, working within detailed production schedules, fast submission cycles, and tight approval deadlines.

Facilitate cross-departmental collaboration to acquire necessary assets and communicate project updates effectively.

Serve as a point of contact for all publishing-related matters while working closely with development team project leads.

Manage project timelines, roadmaps, and budgets for concurrent projects.

Establish and maintain production workbacks, milestones, and approval processes.

Ensure the quality of all projects and coordinate publishing services such as QA and localization.

Coordinate IP collaborations and approvals with external IP owners.

Set up backend systems for projects and storefronts, user onboarding, and launch.

Create first-party submission schedules and handle platform submissions, securing waivers, reporting bugs, and managing communication with first-party vendors.

Coordinate with internal departments for assets, pricing, metadata, and key management/distribution, and manage external localization teams for store pages.

What We’re Looking For

At least two years of experience in PC and console game production.

A track record of shipping at least two titles on different platforms within the last three years.

Demonstrated ability to produce, manage, and drive projects across multiple departments.

Knowledge of first-party submission platforms and forms.

Excellent communication skills, both verbal and written.

Strong presentation, reporting, and organizational abilities.

Experience working on multiple projects simultaneously.

Strong critical thinking and problem-solving skills, with the ability to bring solutions to the table and anticipate blockers.

Alignment with Atari’s core values: long-term thinking, growth mindset, passion excellence, and drive.

Bonus Points

Familiarity with legacy games and platforms.

Familiarity working with task-tracking systems like Asana.

Experience working as a release manager for PC and console games.

Experience working with first-party backend platforms such as NDP, Playstation Partners, Partner Center, and Steamworks.

Experience with emulation technology.

Proficiency in Google Workspace tools.

Experience using Miro for visual workflows.

Knowledge in FTP clients.

Target Salary: $75k - $90k
