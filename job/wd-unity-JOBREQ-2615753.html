Senior Strategic Sales Lead

Unity · Beijing

Lead Other Posted 2026-10-01 Full time

The opportunity The Senior Strategic Sales Lead, will lead Unity Grow s commercial strategy with China s top app publishers and game developers. This is a unified sales role: you ll pitch and sell across both sides of the business. You ll help partners acquire high-value users and turn their audiences into sustainable, high-yield revenue. You ll own the full Unity Grow portfolio of monetization, mediation and user acquisition as one growth solution. This role calls for a sophisticated consultant who understands how to balance user experience (UX), growth and monetization in China s unique mobile ecosystem.

What you ll be doing Build C-suite partnerships: Open doors and build strategic relationships with CEOs and monetization, UA and growth leaders at China s top publishers.

Lead the monetization pitch: Position LevelPlay, the Unity Network and Unity Offerwall as one monetization stack. Advise partners on ad formats, bidding and placements to maximize yield.

Drive user acquisition: Sell Unity Ads UA, Offerwall engagement campaigns, Aura on-device distribution and Unity CTV to help partners scale against their ROAS and CPI goals.

Connect both sides: Show publishers how Unity Grow links acquisition and monetization, and grow Unity s share of wallet across both.

Be the voice of the market: Share local trends and player insights with Global Product teams to shape features for China.

Speak tech and business: Move easily between SDK and API discussions with engineers and revenue pitches to executives (eCPM, ARPDAU, fill rate, ROAS).

Own the big moments: Plan and run campaigns during key commercial windows like Golden Week, Singles Day and Lunar New Year.

What we re looking for Monetization experience (mediation, in-app bidding, programmatic, rewarded or Offerwall formats). Candidates with this background will be prioritized.

Proven track record selling ad-tech solutions to app publishers or game developers Executive-level business development and relationship-building skills Solid understanding of user acquisition and performance marketing (CPI, ROAS, CPE). Exposure to on-device or CTV channels is a plus.
