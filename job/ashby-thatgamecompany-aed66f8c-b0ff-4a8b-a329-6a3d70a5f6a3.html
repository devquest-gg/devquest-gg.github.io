UGC & Creator Community Specialist

Role Overview

Responsible for building and operating the local UGC (User-Generated Content) ecosystem and creator community for thatgamecompany's (TGC) IPs. Drive player passion and foster a healthy, vibrant, and brand-aligned creator community by building long-term creator relationships, developing incentive frameworks, and executing co-creation initiatives.

Key Responsibilities

- Ecosystem Strategy & Execution: Contribute to the formulation and execution of local UGC and creator community strategies, continuously optimizing creator growth and incentive frameworks.

- Creator Relationship Management: Build and maintain long-term, trusted partnerships with key creators, community leaders, and fan networks to elevate brand engagement.

- UGC Campaigns & Initiatives: Plan and execute UGC marketing initiatives, contests, and co-creation projects in line with game milestones and major brand beats.

- Insights & Content Amplification: Monitor creator ecosystem trends and community sentiment, driving the cross-channel amplification of high-quality player-generated content.

- Cross-Functional Alignment: Partner closely with Global Community, local marketing, and social teams to ensure creator initiatives align with broader brand objectives.

Requirements

- Experience: 3–5 years of experience in creator operations, UGC ecosystem, or community management within gaming, ACG, or digital content industries.

- Industry Insight & Taste: Strong knowledge of creator dynamics and fan-creation culture; exceptional aesthetic judgment to evaluate content quality and potential.

- Empathy & Communication: Outstanding interpersonal skills and empathy, with a proven ability to cultivate lasting trust with diverse creators.

- Language: Fluent in both Mandarin and English to collaborate seamlessly across international teams.

- Brand Alignment: Passionate about gaming and deeply aligned with TGC’s philosophy of emotional connection and artistic design.
