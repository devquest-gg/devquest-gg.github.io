L USD (Universal Scene Description) s impose peu à peu comme la norme pour échanger des données 3D dans les secteurs de la fabrication, de la robotique et de la simulation. Vous serez responsable de la couche qui permet à Unity de lire, d écrire et de convertir en aller-retour des stages USD, y compris la synchronisation en direct entre Unity et des outils de simulation externes.

Ce rôle vous offre une réelle autonomie. projets libres (open source) de 3D ou d USD -- The opportunity We re hiring a Senior Software Engineer to join our Industry Engineering team in Montreal and become the first dedicated engineer on Unity s OpenUSD integration. USD (Universal Scene Description) is fast becoming the standard way to exchange 3D data across manufacturing, robotics and simulation. You ll own the layer that lets Unity read, write and round-trip USD stages, including live sync between Unity and external simulation tools.

This is a role with real ownership. You ll shape the integration s architecture and API, keep it dependable over the long term, and work closely with our asset management and 3D data conversion teams. Treat the list below as where you d start, not as the limits of the job. If you enjoy building a product where native C and C# meet, join us.

What you ll be doing Own and evolve a C# integration layer over native OpenUSD libraries, including the native interop, the API surface and the cross-platform builds (Windows, macOS, Linux) Build import, export and round-trip of USD stages in the Unity Editor and at runtime, covering composition arcs, variants, payloads and physics Develop live scene sync between Unity and external USD-based simulation tools Make USD import fast and reliable on large, real-world industrial scenes Fix bugs and add features, keeping long-term maintenance cost and documentation quality in mind Break down complex problems that span several components and teams, then work with their owners to solve them Work with product, partner and customer-facing teams to turn real industrial USD workflows into product features What we re looking for Strong professional experience with C# and C , including native interop (P/Invoke or similar) Hands-on experience with OpenUSD: stages, layers, composition and schemas Solid 3D fundamentals: scene graphs, transforms, meshes and materials Experience shipping and maintaining native libraries across several platforms A proven track record of owning a library or codebase end to end, from design through long-term maintenance Enthusiasm for using AI tooling in your own engineering work You might also have Experience with Unity or another real-time engine Experience with simulation, robotics or digital-twin pipelines, or with CAD and other industrial 3D data formats Contributions to open-source 3D or USD projects Additional information Relocation support is not available for this position Work visa/immigration sponsorship is not available for this position Base Salary Range: We determine the base salary range for this role based on your primary work location: Zone A: $125,300 – $175,300 CAD gross Cette fourchette correspond au salaire de base prévu pour ce poste.
