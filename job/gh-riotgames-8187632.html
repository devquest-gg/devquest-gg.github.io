Treasury and Risk Manager

Riot Games · Seoul

Mid People & Ops Posted 2026-09-14 Full time
Apply on Riot Games’s site →

Riot Games was established in 2006 by entrepreneurial gamers who believe that player-focused game development can result in great games. In 2009, Riot released its debut title League of Legends to critical and player acclaim. As the most played PC game in the world, over 100 million play every month. Players form the foundation of our community and it’s for them that we continue to evolve and improve the League of Legends experience.

We’re looking for humble but ambitious, razor-sharp professionals who can teach us a thing or two. We promise to return the favor. Like us, you take play seriously; you’re passionate about games. We embrace those who see things differently, aren’t afraid to experiment, and who have a healthy disregard for constraints.

That s where you come in.

As a Treasury and Risk Manager on the Treasury team, you will help expand the Treasury function for our Korea publishing operation, evolving the cash management and payment operations. As a Treasury professional sitting on the local Enterprise team, you will be the senior treasury professional that oversees all cash management operations, including cash investments in Korea. You will have demonstrated experience with cross-border treasury operations and handling foreign currency transactions. You will make immediate important decisions regarding funding, optimization of cash balances, and establish Riot’s investment in Korea. Your work will help develop and implement new procedures for the sustainability of Riot’s growth.

You will report to Sr. Manager, Treasury of the team.

Responsibilities :

Execute daily hands-on cash operations (cash forecasting, bank procedures, account reconciliations); Manage the capital structure across Korean entities

Modernize the integrated bank account structure across the Korea publisher; Manage Riot’s bank relationships in the region with integrity and professionalism, including asset managers and commercial bankers; Work with Riot’s local and international cash management banks to create automated payment structures for Accounts Payable and Payroll operations

Prepare regional cash forecasts, working across the Korea publisher to obtain and analyze cash trends and forecast variances

Partner with Accounting, Tax, and Legal to manage accounting, tax, and regulatory impacts of treasury activities (Mark to Market impacts, local regulations, etc.) ; Manage the bank account documentation; coordinate with Legal on bank resolutions with authorization levels, and manage the bank KYC process for legal compliance; Support Strategic Finance and Strategic Sourcing Teams with financial analysis and contract negotiations; Support Riot’s accounting group on all financial reporting matters and Riot’s annual audit locally, including reconciliation of local cash accounts and coordinating local audit requirements with local banks

Manage the Intercompany settlement process for Korea publisher; Execute FX transactions across Korea publisher and identify opportunities for strategic ST hedging of cash flows and balance sheet exposure

Initiate and align with Central team on annual Dividend program

Manage short term cash investments and ensure daily compliance with investment guidelines

Manage Riot’s insurance programs in Korea; ensure compliance with all policy subjectivities and local compulsory requirements; Implement and manage local Credit Card programs

Required Qualifications/Skills:

Minimum of 8 years of International Treasury experience

Bachelor s Degree in Finance or Accounting

Experience in a multinational company

Knowledge of payment systems, including SWIFT, GIRO, Real Time Payments (RTP)

Experience with integrated Treasury Management Systems (TMS)

Excellent communication skills; business level English required (verbal and written)

Strong analytical skills, including advanced spreadsheet and modeling capabilities

Desired Qualifications:

MBA, Finance or other related master degree.

For this role, you ll find success through craft expertise, a collaborative spirit, and decision-making that prioritizes your fellow Rioters, who are the customers of your work. Being a dedicated fan of games is not necessary for this position!
