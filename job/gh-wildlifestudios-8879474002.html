Social Media Intern — Wildlife

Are you ready to help give voice to the largest mobile gaming community in Latin America? Wildlife Studios Marketing team is looking for a Social Community Intern to support the management of social media channels and community initiatives for our Live Games. We are seeking someone with creative drive, analytical curiosity, and a strong eagerness to learn how connecting player feedback directly to product strategy fuels game growth. In this role, you will play an active part in supporting day-to-day content planning and execution, learning firsthand how to measure the business impact of social actions in a performance-driven environment.

What you ll do

Support the Social Calendar: Assist in creating, scheduling, and publishing content across key platforms including Discord, Meta (Instagram/Facebook), TikTok, YouTube, and X.

Community Engagement Moderation: Monitor official channels, support Discord server moderation, and assist in driving authentic engagement with our player community.

Player Feedback Loop: Help gather and organize community sentiment and player feedback into actionable reports for product teams.

Analytical Support: Assist in tracking and organizing core social metrics (reach, engagement, tracking links/UTMs) to evaluate publication performance.

Cross-Functional Support: Coordinate briefings and facilitate communication between internal art teams, partner agencies, and content creators.

Bot Automation Setup: Support basic setup and maintenance of bots, webhooks, and moderation tools on Discord and social platforms.

What you ll need

Currently pursuing a degree in Marketing, Communications, Advertising, Design, or a related field.

Active presence on and passion for social media platforms (TikTok, Instagram, Discord, X, YouTube).

Strong written and verbal communication skills.

Curious, proactive mindset with a strong desire to learn basic performance data analysis.

Solid organizational skills and ability to manage daily deadlines.

Nice to have

Passion for mobile games and the wider gaming/entertainment industry.

Proficiency in English (written and spoken), as our game channels reach global audiences.

Basic skills in image/video editing or content creation (Canva, CapCut, Photoshop, etc.).

Familiarity with Discord setup (server management, roles, bots).

More about you

Proactive Learner: Eager to get hands-on experience and learn quickly in a fast-paced environment.

Empathetic Communicator: Natural ability to adopt brand voice and connect authentically with gamers.

Organized: Skilled at managing tasks efficiently amid shifting priorities.

About Wildlife

Wildlife is one of the leading mobile game developers and publishers in the world. We have released more than 60 titles, reaching billions of people around the globe. Today, we have offices in Brazil and the United States. Here, we create games that will excite, intrigue, and engage our players for years to come!
