WHAT WE NEED

HB Studios is a driving force in the creation of sports games and the studio behind PGA TOUR 2K Golf – presently working on PC and Consoles. HB Studios has an opportunity for a Game Designer (Live Content ) who is knowledgeable about golf, and passionate about the art of game development to join their team.

As a Game Designer with a focus on game content and Live Ops, you will work with a team of professionals designing and developing one of the fastest growing sports game franchises in the industry!

WHAT YOU LL DO

Work with feature owners to understand existing game systems, and drive plans and implementation of content to support those features.

Design, author and maintain the playable gameplay-, event-, and objective-based content needed to fuel a thoughtful, and engaging player experience.

Design and implement supplementary level designs on top of existing courses to support gameplay needs such as Challenges gameplay.

Shepherd content through implementation, focusing on hands-on work; including playtesting, tuning, data entry, content creation, and scripting.

Ensure all tools and features are working as intended and coordinate with the QA team to report bugs and reproduction steps.

Use the provided toolset and operate on the outlined processes for handling working files, implementing levels, vetting check-ins, etc.

Generate and design new ideas and extensions that improve our Live Service features over time.

Contribute to our growing philosophy of game design and how we make games.

WHAT WILL MAKE YOU A GREAT FIT

Typically, a minimum of 2 to 4 years of experience in Game Design.

A strong grasp on the game of golf, the world of professional golf, and PGA TOUR.

Strong systems design foundation with demonstrable understanding of game loops, player motivations, and retention.

A solid understanding of technical and production aspects of game development, backed by associated experience, including at least one shipped title or project.

Excellent written and verbal communication skills, with the ability to collaborate effectively and clearly document and present ideas in a distributed team that relies on Slack and video calls.

Proficiency with common game development software and tools.

Self-motivated individual with a desire to explore, research, grow, and become a go-to subject-matter expert in areas of need.

Strong working knowledge of current design trends and mechanics across major modern sports franchises.

Experience with live service games is a plus.

We believe the ideal candidate is a creative person with a love for game design, and hunger to deliver high-quality, inviting, and engaging golf experiences through video games. We value a proven ability to breakdown game issues, mechanics or concepts into lay terms and share views on game-specific issues in a constructive manner. We are seeking both detail-minded, and big-picture persons who work well with others and consider all forms of feedback.
