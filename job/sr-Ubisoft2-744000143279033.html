Ubisoft (HQ) · Paris

As a DevOps Linux Administrator at Ubisoft Paris, you will join the Build and Orchestration team, a key group responsible for managing and evolving Ubisoft’s IT services. You will work closely with international teams, including Massive Entertainment on productions such as The Division and Avatar, to ensure the reliability, performance, and evolution of critical services.

What you’ll do Develop, optimize, and maintain CI/CD pipelines with GitLab Automate recurring tasks using Python , Bash, and C# scripts Manage cloud and on-premises infrastructures with Terraform , Kubernetes , and infrastructure as code tools Monitor IT services with Prometheus , Grafana , and OTEL to ensure stability and performance Collaborate with production teams and internal partners to align technical and operational needs Participate in incident resolution and post-incident analysis to improve service reliability Contribute to the evolution of automation, deployment and operations practices Prioritize and coordinate multiple technical topics in an international collaborative environment What you bring to the team Proficiency in Linux systems Experience in DevOps and system administration in complex environments Strong scripting skills with Python , Bash , or C# Experience with GitLab CI/CD , Terraform , and Kubernetes Knowledge of Azure and AWS environments Experience with monitoring and observability tools such as Prometheus , Grafana and OTEL Excellent communication and collaboration skills Fluency in English required to collaborate with international teams and partners What to send our way Your CV highlighting relevant skills and experiences Relevant project links, GitHub contributions, or technical portfolio Ubisoft's perks 💰 Profit Sharing, yearly company saving plan. 25 paid time off + 12 additional paid days off. 50% of your transportation pass is paid by the company, lunch vouchers (9€/day), healthcare for you and your family, and lots of Ubisoft additional perks.

👶 Maternity leaves of 20 weeks, paternity/co-parental leaves of 7 weeks.

📍 Our office is located in Saint Mandé, (Metro line 1, Saint Mandé station). Gym available in the building.
