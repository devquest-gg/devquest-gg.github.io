The Director of Information Security reports directly to the VP of Information Security and provides strategic leadership and cross-functional coordination.

Primary Responsibilities

Strategic Leadership

Drive the CISO’s strategy in strategic company projects

Communicate top-down strategic plans and KPIs to relevant stakeholder and teams

Translate security concepts into business language for mid-level management

Provide risk reporting for mid-level management

Advise on security implications of new business initiatives

Cross-Functional Coordination

Serve as liaison between security function and other business units

Coordinate activities across the different information security teams

Align security initiatives with business objectives

Security Program Governance

Drive security innovation and continuous improvement initiatives

Serve as the focal point for the security department’s efforts in the organization’s AI transformation

Act as BISO (Business Information Security Officer) for newly joined business units and sub-companies

Qualifications

8+ years in information security with 3+ years in leadership

Strong understanding of security frameworks and best practices

Excellent communication and leadership skills

Strategic thinking with business alignment capabilities

Mobile gaming security experience preferred

Reporting Structure

Reports to: Chief Information Security Officer (CISO)

Collaborates with but does not oversee: Product Security, Security Engineering and Compliance, and Security Operations managers
