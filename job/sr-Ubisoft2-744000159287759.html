We are looking for a passionate Junior Dev Tester who is eager to build a career in software quality assurance and technical testing. They will work closely with Developers, Product Owners, and QA team members to ensure product features meet quality expectations and are delivered with confidence.

This role is ideal for candidates with a background in Software Testing, Computer Science, or a related field who are interested in developing their skills in Automation Testing, API Testing, and modern QA practices.

Responsibilities : Functional Testing Analyze requirements, specifications, and design documents.

Design, maintain, and execute test cases and test scenarios.

Perform Functional Testing, Feature Testing, and Exploratory Testing.

Verify bug fixes and conduct regression testing.

Ensure assigned features meet quality standards before release.

Defect Management Identify, document, and track software defects.

Provide clear and detailed bug reports to support investigation and resolution.

Participate in defect triage and assist with severity and priority assessment.

Collaboration Actively participate in Agile ceremonies, including: Sprint Planning, Daily Stand-ups, Sprint Reviews, Retrospectives Collaborate with Developers, Designers, Product Owners, and Producers to clarify requirements and identify potential quality risks.

Technical Testing Support the creation and maintenance of automated tests under the guidance of Senior Dev Testers or QA Leads.

Assist in test environment setup and validation activities.

Hard skills Having knowledge of: Software Testing Life Cycle (STLC) Software Development Life Cycle (SDLC) Agile/Scrum methodologies Test Case Design Techniques Defect Management Process Ability to read and understand code in at least one of the following languages: C#, Java, JavaScript / TypeScript, Python Soft Skills Strong analytical and problem-solving skills.

Good communication and teamwork abilities.

Eagerness to learn and continuously improve.

Strong attention to detail and quality.

Ability to read and understand technical documentation in English, and basic verbal and written communication skills in English.

Preferred Qualifications Candidates with one or more of the following will have an advantage: Basic knowledge of Automation Testing concepts.

Experience with tools such as: Playwright, Selenium, Cypress ISTQB Foundation Certification.

Experience testing web, mobile, or game applications.

Basic knowledge of AI generation.
