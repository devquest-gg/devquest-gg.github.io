Senior Game Developer (C++)

Gameloft · Lviv

Senior Engineering Posted 2026-10-13 Full time
Apply on Gameloft’s site →

We are looking for an experienced C++ Game Developer to join the team working on Disney Magic Kingdoms . As part of the development team, you will contribute to the full development cycle of the game&#xa0; building new features and technologies, supporting existing systems, and helping us continuously improve the quality and performance of the product. You will work with different areas of the game, combining strong C++ engineering fundamentals with hands-on game development and close collaboration across disciplines.

Daily power-ups Develop game features in C++ according to design specifications, technical requirements, and existing programming standards; Integrate different game components, including engine systems, gameplay, UI, graphics, animations, and sound; Design, build, and maintain efficient, reusable, and reliable code; Optimize and troubleshoot game systems to ensure strong performance, quality, and responsiveness; Contribute to game design specifications and technical specifications; Support and improve existing game technologies and systems; Investigate technical issues and contribute to solutions throughout the development lifecycle.

Your allies You will work closely with other Game Developers, 2D and 3D Artists, Game Designers, Level Designers, and Backend Developers , contributing your technical expertise throughout the development process. You will be part of a cross-functional environment where close collaboration between engineering, art, and design is essential to bringing new game content and features to players.

Leveling up This role gives you the opportunity to deepen your expertise in C++ game development , work with complex real-time systems, and gain experience across different areas of a live mobile game. You will be able to strengthen your knowledge of game architecture, performance optimization, multithreading, and system integration while contributing to a globally recognized Disney title.

Next mission briefing Our recruitment process may include: First interview: Recruiter interview; Test: Online technical assessment; Second interview: Technical interview; Final interview: Project/Team interview, if required.

Your next level This role is a great match for an experienced C++ Developer who enjoys solving technical challenges, working across different game systems, and seeing their code directly contribute to the player experience.

YOUR TOOLKIT 3+ years of practical C++ programming experience on commercial or other real-world projects; Strong understanding of software architecture and object-oriented programming ; Solid knowledge of 3D mathematics ; Practical experience with multithreading ; Ability to design and maintain efficient, reusable, and reliable code; Understanding of performance, optimization, and application responsiveness; Strong problem-solving and debugging skills; Ability to work effectively within a cross-functional development team; Responsibility, ownership, and commitment to agreed deadlines; Good English reading and writing skills .

YOUR PLAYER PROFILE You are a developer who enjoys solving complex technical problems while keeping the bigger product picture in mind. Experience in any of the following areas will be a strong advantage: Programming for mobile devices and/or consoles ; Professional or personal experience in video game development or other real-time 3D applications ; Knowledge of client-server communication and networking technologies ; Experience with scripting languages such as Python, Lua, TypeScript , or similar; Experience working with Unity and/or Unreal Engine .

Loots & Perks Possibility to work remotely in a global product company; Competitive salary based on your experience and qualifications; 21 days of paid vacation; 5 days of paid sick leave; National holidays as non-working days; Medical insurance; Access to psychologist support; Parental leave; Access to our educational platform with courses, training programs, and certifications; Employee Referral Program; Technical support and equipment.

Ready to hit start? You will be working on Disney Magic Kingdoms &#xa0; a live mobile game where players build and expand their own Disney theme park, unlock iconic Disney characters, complete story-driven quests, and enjoy regularly updated events and content.&#xa0; To consider your application, we require a full version of your CV in English.

Qualified candidates will be invited to complete an online technical assessment as part of the selection process.
