Senior Game Systems Designer - Strategy & Simulation

Black Banner Studios · České Budějovice · Remote

Senior Design Remote Posted 2026-11-20 Full time
Apply on Black Banner Studios’s site →

We're looking for an experienced Senior Game Systems Designer with a strong background in designing complex, interconnected gameplay systems for large-scale strategy, simulation, or strategy / RPG hybrid games. Legacy of Valor combines third-person RPG gameplay, massive real-time battles, tactical army control, settlement development, kingdom management, and a persistent strategic world. We're looking for someone who can help bring these different gameplay layers together into a cohesive, engaging, and rewarding experience. Your primary focus will be on strategic gameplay systems, including settlement management, base and castle building, economy, resources, diplomacy, faction relationships, and world-level progression. This is a hands-on design role requiring both creative vision and strong technical understanding. You'll work closely with our Game Director, Gameplay Programmers, artists, and other designers to develop detailed design specifications, prototype mechanics, and refine gameplay systems through testing and iteration. If you believe you'd be a great fit for this role, even if you don't meet every requirement listed below, we'd still love to hear from you. Please don't hesitate to apply! Design and refine interconnected strategic gameplay systems, including settlement management, construction, economy, diplomacy, faction relationships, and kingdom progression. Define mechanics for resource production, consumption, trade, construction, settlement upgrades, and economic development. Design settlement and castle-building systems, including customization, progression, and their integration with the wider game world. Develop diplomacy, faction relationships, and strategic interactions supporting a persistent medieval world. Design systems connecting the strategic world with real-time battles, RPG exploration, and settlement gameplay. Create comprehensive, implementation-ready game design documents covering mechanics, rules, formulas, gameplay flows, dependencies, and balancing parameters. Prototype, test, and refine gameplay mechanics using Unreal Engine 5 Blueprints, spreadsheets, diagrams, and other appropriate tools. Collaborate closely with the Game Director, Design Director, and Gameplay Programmers to translate designs and prototypes into robust production systems. Work with UI / UX Designers to make complex mechanics intuitive, readable, and accessible. Develop balancing models, identify exploits and conflicting mechanics, and continuously improve gameplay through playtesting and iteration. A genuine passion for playing video games, particularly on PC and consoles, with broad familiarity across different genres. Familiarity with games such as Mount & Blade II: Bannerlord, Manor Lords, Crusader Kings III, Total War, or similar titles. Extensive professional experience designing complex gameplay systems for large-scale strategy, simulation, management, or strategy / RPG hybrid games. Significant design responsibility on at least one major commercially released strategy, simulation, or management game. Proven experience designing interconnected systems such as settlement management, base building, resource economies, diplomacy, faction relationships, and strategic progression. Exceptional ability to create clear, comprehensive, implementation-ready game design documents, including detailed mechanics, formulas, diagrams, balancing parameters, and system dependencies. Strong understanding of game economies, resource management, progression systems, and long-term player motivation. Excellent analytical and systems-thinking skills, including experience with spreadsheets, mathematical models, and gameplay balancing. Strong practical experience with Unreal Engine 5 Blueprints or comparable visual scripting tools, including gameplay prototyping and iteration. Ability to translate complex gameplay concepts into achievable development tasks and clear technical requirements. Strong understanding of strategy game UX, player decision-making, gameplay readability, and the balance between complexity and accessibility. Proven ability to independently design, prototype, playtest, and refine gameplay systems. Excellent communication and collaboration skills, particularly when working with programmers, designers, and artists. Experience designing games combining real-time strategy, RPG gameplay, and persistent world simulation. Experience with medieval strategy games involving feudal politics, warfare, settlement development, and economic management. Experience designing settlement and castle-building systems with extensive player customization. Experience with AI-driven factions, diplomacy, trade, or dynamic world simulation. Experience designing strategic world maps connected to playable battles, settlements, and RPG locations. Experience with cooperative or multiplayer strategy and simulation systems. Background in game art or visual design, with an understanding of how artistic presentation supports gameplay systems and player experience. Experience with Unreal Engine C++ programming or other gameplay programming languages. Experience working on AAA, large open-world, or highly systemic sandbox games. Passion for medieval history, strategy games, management simulations, RPGs, and systemic sandbox gameplay. Location: Remote / International, with the option to work in the Czech Republic. Engagement: Full-time, long-term collaboration. Contract: Contractor / B2B worldwide, or direct employment for candidates residing in or willing to relocate to the Czech Republic.
