Join Sandbox Interactive and support the vibrant Albion Online player community as a Freelance Support Agent. We're looking for proactive, empathetic, and tech-savvy individuals fluent in Italian (or any other languages) to help players experience the best of our award-winning MMO across all platforms. Operate within a passionate, international team committed to high-quality service and player satisfaction.

About Sandbox Interactive and Albion Online Sandbox Interactive is the independent studio behind Albion Online, the first truly cross-platform MMO available for Windows, Mac, Linux, iOS, and Android. With over 350,000 daily players, we’ve built a thriving, global gaming environment. You’ll become part of an international team based in Berlin, working to make Albion Online an unforgettable experience for players worldwide.

Main Responsibilities Provide fast, friendly, and effective support to players via our ticketing platform, as well as potentially through chat and email Resolve technical, account, and gameplay queries with empathy and professionalism Escalate complex issues and collaborate with internal teams to ensure thorough resolution Identify and report recurring player issues, suggesting improvements to enhance the player experience Maintain up-to-date knowledge of Albion Online and its systems, staying informed about game updates and community trends Deliver support in a clear, concise, and respectful manner, ensuring player confidentiality and satisfaction Key Requirements Fluency in written and spoken Italian Solid verbal and written English communication skills for effective internal collaboration and external communication Flexible availability to cover shifts, including evenings and weekends (remote/freelance) Demonstrated empathy, patience, and excellent problem-solving skills Comfortable using digital tools (ticketing platforms, spreadsheets, basic troubleshooting) Deep interest in online games and a genuine desire to help the player community Additional Information This is a freelance, remote, part-time position All applicants must be 18+ and able to receive international payments Applications must be submitted in English If you’re fluent in any additional languages, we’d be happy to hear from you.

Ready to Support Albion Online's Community? If you’re passionate about games, fluent in Italian or another language, and motivated to provide a great player experience, we’d be happy to hear from you.

About Sandbox Interactive

Sandbox Interactive is the ambitious game development studio behind the award-winning game Albion Online. We started as a small independent group of experienced gaming professionals in 2012, and now employ an international team of more than 100 people. Our team is passionate about making Albion Online the best game it can be, and we’re constantly on the lookout for talented individuals to join our growing team in the heart of Berlin.

Sandbox Interactive is part of Stillfront. Stillfront is a global gaming company with 1400+ employees. We develop a wide range of digital games that attract over 50 million players each month. From well-established franchises like Supremacy, Big Farm, and BitLife to niche games, we span many different genres, including strategy, simulation, RPG and action, and casual and mash-up games.
