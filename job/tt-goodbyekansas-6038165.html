What we’re looking for Strong professional experience as a Realtime TD, Technical Artist, Tools Engineer or similar technical realtime role, ideally at Senior or Lead level.

Advanced working knowledge of Unreal Engine gained through commercial AAA games, cinematics, virtual production or other high-end realtime projects.

Strong scripting or programming ability, with experience creating production tools rather than solely using existing workflows.

Experience with Python, Blueprints or other relevant scripting and programming languages.

The ability to work independently, investigate unfamiliar technical problems and develop practical solutions.

Experience developing, maintaining or improving artist-facing tools and realtime production workflows.

Experience integrating Animation and FX into Unreal Engine and troubleshooting associated pipeline issues.

Experience working with realtime assets, levels, materials, shaders and rendering workflows.

Good understanding of realtime performance, profiling and optimisation.

Experience working with version control systems such as Perforce, Git or similar.

An understanding of how DCC applications and Unreal Engine connect within a wider content-production pipeline.

Clear communication skills and the ability to work effectively with artists, supervisors, production teams and clients.

What you might add Experience developing tools or systems using C++.

Experience with HLSL or other shader languages.

Experience with Houdini, Blender, Maya or other DCC applications.

Experience with Unreal Engine s Chaos systems, including rigid-body simulation and destruction.

Experience with realtime cloth, groom or other physics-based simulation workflows.

Experience working within virtual production or on-set environments.

A background in disciplines such as FX, Animation, Lighting, Look Development, Modelling or Shaders.

Knowledge of colour management within realtime and cinematic pipelines.

Experience designing tools specifically for artists and production teams.

Previous experience working on AAA game co-development or in-game cinematic pipelines.
