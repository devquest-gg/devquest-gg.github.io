Game Producer, Live Experience - Wild Rift (Contractor)

Riot Games · Shanghai

Mid Production Posted 2026-06-10 Contractor
Apply on Riot Games’s site →

Riot Games was founded in 2006 by gamers who believe that putting players first can create truly great games. Since launching League of Legends in 2009, we’ve grown into a global leader in games, serving millions of players every day. Players are at the heart of everything we do, and we continuously evolve our experiences to better serve them.

We’re looking for passionate professionals who take play seriously and bring a strong sense of ownership to their work. You are organized, collaborative, and able to support live operations in a fast-paced, cross-functional environment.

That s where you come in.

As a Game Producer, Live Experience (Contractor) on the Wild Rift team, you will support the Live Experience team in executing day-to-day live operations for the RoW (Rest of World) regions. You will work closely with product, design, operations, and QA teams to ensure in-game operational content is configured, delivered, and launched accurately and efficiently, helping provide a stable and high-quality player experience.

What You’ll Do:

Support day-to-day live operations execution for Wild Rift RoW regions, including in-game events, rewards, and mail configuration and delivery.

Prepare and maintain operation whitepapers, execution documents, and related operational configuration documentation.

Support patch-related operational work, including event setup, reward distribution, and other live operation tasks.

Coordinate and track in-game banner/key art (“face image”) related requests and support publishing workflows.

Collaborate with cross-functional teams including product, design, QA, and operations to ensure operational content is delivered on time and with quality.

Assist in reviewing operational configurations, tracking issue resolutions, and supporting day-to-day live service stability.

Required Qualifications:

2+ years of experience in the game industry or related fields.

Strong execution skills, sense of ownership, and communication abilities.

Comfortable working in a fast-paced environment with multiple parallel tasks.

Familiar with basic game operation workflows and has an understanding of game event operations.

Passion for games.

Fluent in Mandarin Chinese.

Desired Qualifications:

Experience in game live operations execution or event configuration is a plus.

Experience supporting mobile game operations is a plus.

Basic English reading, writing, or communication skills are a plus.

Please include both your resume and a cover letter. We read every application carefully—show us your passion for games and your understanding of player experiences.

Employment Type: employment contract with FESCO Adecco, a professional personnel company in China
