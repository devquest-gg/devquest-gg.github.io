Playtika is looking for a Principal Data Scien tist to join the Data AI Department and shape the scientific direction of our work across the player experience . This position is based in Herzliya , Israel .

We are looking for people with exceptional soft skills and outstanding hard skills .

W e love to address complex intellectual challenges and make sense of high dimension al and dynamic al data that impact the game experience of millions of players.

You will join a multidisciplinary team with a variety of business use-cases and algorithmic approaches , and you will help shape its scientific direction .
