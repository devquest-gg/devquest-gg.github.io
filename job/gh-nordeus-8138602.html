Senior Product Manager

Nordeus · Belgrade

Senior Production Posted 2026-08-19 Full time
Apply on Nordeus’s site →

THE DIFFERENCE YOU’LL MAKE:

Top Goal is our next major title, a Football game built for a passionate audience that could become a defining product in mobile sports gaming. We re at the stage where ambition needs to meet execution, and we re looking for a Senior Product Manager to maximise this incredible opportunity with us.

This is a unique chance to be part of something from the ground up - not just a feature owner, but someone who shapes a franchise. If you want to own a piece of history in sports gaming, this is the role for you.

WHO YOU’LL WORK WITH:

Impact Product strategy and Product roadmap with Game Lead and Creative Director based on research data and mobile gaming knowledge

Engineering, Design, and Art to align on priorities and ship with speed

The Marketing Team in order to build impactful launch plans and detect growth opportunities (app store featuring, influencers, etc.)

In partnership with Analytics Team, dig into game or feature / events performance analysis and propose a plan to improve KPIs

WHAT YOU LL DO:

Work in multidisciplinary pods - autonomous teams with everything they need to move fast and own their outcomes. As a Senior PM, you ll lead one of those pods, acting as its strategic and operational head. That means setting direction, prioritizing focus, and being accountable for what the pod ships and the results it drives.

Drive data-informed product decisions - identify growth levers, run experiments, and iterate fast

Build the feedback loops that keep us close to players as we scale

Determine and set up KPIs for new game features and participate in high-level design

Own the game pricing and participate in the game economy creation or evolution with Game Designers

Drive Live Operations by building Live Ops Product roadmap and executing on monthly offers Events

Leverage Data Analytics, A/B testing, Push Notifications and other tools to drive KPIs

Track and develop an expert-level understanding of the mobile gaming industry, including developing ability to qualitatively and quantitatively teardown mobile games

WE ARE A MATCH, IF YOU:

Have at least 5 years of experience working on B2C digital products, ideally in Product Management, Game Design or Business Analysis or similar roles

Have a strong analytical skills and experience with data-driven decision making

Are highly user-centric

Have strong communication and writing skills, with experience in preparing and presenting reports and feedback to various stakeholders

Are passionate about mobile games

Have a preference for working in a fast-paced agile development environment

BONUS POINTS:

Experience working on free-to-play mobile games

Passion for football and an understanding of football audiences and products

WHY YOU’LL LOVE WORKING HERE:

The team behind the game: transparency and trust from day one, paired with a strong sense of teamwork- that s the essence of who we are. It s not just what you do - it’s how you do it and who you do it with. With 280+ teammates from around the world, we’re on a mission to bring the joy of winning to millions.

We make your life at Nordeus hassle-free: enjoy Nordeus provided breakfast, lunch, snacks, and beverages, a fully-equipped gym, organized sports activities (yoga, Brazilian jiu-jitsu, basketball, football), an ergonomic workstation, top-notch tech equipment (including laptop, mobile phone and bill coverage, and other tech), a kids’ playroom, a music corner, board and video games + latest consoles.

Perks? We’ve got plenty: premium private medical insurance for you and your family, flexible working hours, take-what-you-need vacation policy, offsites abroad for the whole company, fully-paid maternity and paternity leave, employee stock purchase plan, access to L D platforms and opportunities and many more.

Make your mark on games played by millions: with over 300M registered users, Top Eleven is the world’s most successful football management game. Golf Rival, with more than 60M registered users, is the #2 mobile golf game, aiming to claim the top spot. With one more game in the works, we are continuing our efforts to create powerful mobile sports gaming experiences built to last.

Meaningful career experience: work with experienced game makers and mentors who will support you from day one, helping you map a career path that s true to you.

Impact beyond the screen: It is not just about us. We are part of something bigger than a job. We create a better future for generations to come through the efforts of the Nordeus Foundation.

Not from Serbia? Wherever you’re coming from, we offer full relocation support for you and your family to make your move to Belgrade smooth and comfortable. Learn more about how we’ll help you settle in here.

GOOD TO KNOW:

This is a hybrid, on-site position based in Belgrade.

If you are not a Serbian citizen, we offer a relocation package.
