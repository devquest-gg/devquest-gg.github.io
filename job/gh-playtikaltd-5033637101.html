DevSecOps Tech Lead

Playtika · Herzliya

Lead Engineering Posted 2026-11-17 Full time

Ambition Needs a Place to Grow.

At Playtika, we create mobile games enjoyed by millions worldwide.

Combining the scale of a public company (NASDAQ: PLTK) with the speed and ownership of a startup, we are a global leader where innovation, data, and creativity shape the future of entertainment.

Here, your ideas matter, your decisions impact products, and your growth is supported across global studios.

Bring your hunger where it belongs: at the front of the pack.

As DevSecOps Tech Lead, you set technical direction and lead hands-on delivery for security across Playtika’s SDLC, CI/CD, build infrastructure, and software supply chain. You make secure delivery scalable across studios and shared-tech teams, while directing and developing the team and keeping its technical bar high.

The role requires enterprise experience across varied engineering environments, a record of initiating and leading cross-functional projects, and the ability to influence stakeholders through technical judgment. Playtika’s current scope includes repository and runner security, dependency and artifact controls, automation, provenance, and security observability.

Key Responsibilities

Set and evolve secure technical patterns for source control, CI/CD workflows and runners, credentials, artifact repositories, dependencies, and build/release integrity across cloud and on-prem environments.

Design, build, and operate delivery-integrated controls such as SAST/SCA, secrets, container and IaC scanning, dependency and package governance, provenance, and policy enforcement; ensure findings reach accountable owners.

Initiate and lead cross-functional DevSecOps projects from problem definition and architecture through implementation, rollout, adoption, and measurable operations; manage trade-offs and stakeholder alignment.

Stay hands-on in automation, services, integrations, and troubleshooting; improve security visibility, evidence, and response for delivery-platform and supply-chain risks.

Prioritize and direct team technical work; review designs and code, challenge weak approaches constructively, and coach engineers to improve quality and ownership.

Partner with CI/CD and DevEx, Infrastructure/IT, SRE/Operations, R D, and Information Security; document standards and provide practical guidance that supports adoption without unnecessary delivery friction.

Requirements

Must Have

5+ years in DevOps, platform, infrastructure, CI/CD, product security, or DevSecOps, with substantial hands-on experience securing delivery systems in a corporate or enterprise environment.

Experience across varied platforms, teams, or operating environments; a track record of initiating and leading multiple technical projects through rollout and ongoing operations.

Strong practical knowledge of CI/CD platforms and Git-based workflows, Linux, networking, and cloud and/or on-prem infrastructure.

Broad experience across core DevSecOps areas, including pipeline/repository security, secrets, dependency and artifact risk, vulnerability scanning, and container or IaC security.

Software development and automation skills in at least one language (e.g., Python, Go, Bash, PowerShell, or Groovy); able to design and review maintainable integrations and services.

Demonstrated ability to direct and mentor engineers, provide rigorous technical review, and influence stakeholders across security and engineering teams.

Experience with GitHub Enterprise/Actions and self-hosted runners, JFrog Artifactory or similar, and security tooling for SAST/SCA, secrets, IaC, or containers.

Experience with Kubernetes, Terraform or other IaC, public/private cloud, or hybrid infrastructure.

Knowledge of SBOMs, signing and provenance (e.g., SLSA), security observability, or AI/ML supply chain security.

Nice to Have

Hands-on experience using AI tools in engineering or security workflows, such as coding assistants or agents.

Experience with enterprise AI-tool evaluation and governance, including security and privacy risk reviews, approval criteria, controlled rollout, and ongoing oversight
