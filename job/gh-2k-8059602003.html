Job Title: Senior User Researcher - 2K Sports Business Unit

Location: London (UK),

Hybrid Work Model : 3 days in office, Mon, Wed, Thurs

Who We Are

2K is headquartered in Novato, California, and is a wholly owned label of Take-Two Interactive Software, Inc. (NASDAQ: TTWO). Founded in 2005, 2K is a global video game publisher powered by some of the most influential game development studios in the world—including Visual Concepts, Firaxis Games, Hangar 13, CatDaddy Games, Cloud Chamber, and HB Studios.

Our portfolio features iconic, critically acclaimed franchises across multiple platforms and genres, including NBA 2K , WWE 2K , PGA TOUR 2K , BioShock , Borderlands , Sid Meier’s Civilization , and XCOM .

At 2K, we pride ourselves on creating an inclusive, highly collaborative environment where world-class talent can Come as You Are and do their best work. We are dedicated to diversity, equity, and inclusion, and we encourage candidates of all backgrounds to explore our global opportunities.

What We Need

As part of our Central User Research organization that serves all of 2K, we are seeking a highly experienced, deeply passionate Senior User Researcher to drive end-to-end user research planning, execution, and stakeholder partnerships in service of our industry-leading Sports Business Unit.

In this role, domain user research expertise and a genuine passion for sports are core to success. Our central UR team has an incredible pipeline of high-impact, innovative work on the horizon across our flagship sports titles. We need a Senior Individual Contributor who lives and breathes sports media, interactive entertainment, sports gaming, and sports culture - someone who can translate fandom, gaming enthusiasm, and athletic mechanics into world-class player experiences.

We are looking for a top-tier user research practitioner who brings proactive communication, immaculate organizational discipline, and a consultative mindset. You excel at building trust with game developers, studio heads, and publishing leaders, managing competing studio priorities with calm composure, and leading high-impact qualitative and quantitative studies independently. Because our development studios, research labs, and vendor partners are globally distributed, regular hands-on presence is key - expect domestic and international travel roughly once per month.

What You Will Do

1. End-to-End Study Planning Hands-on Execution

Lead User Research Execution: Onboard rapidly to design, execute, and deliver high-priority user research studies for upcoming sports title milestones. Conduct hands-on study design, moderation, usability analysis, and report delivery across all phases of development.

Maintain Methodological Rigor: Serve as a subject matter expert who leads by example, applying best-in-class qualitative and quantitative user research techniques, study protocols, and actionable user experience synthesis to elevate game design quality across our portfolio.

Bridge Sports UR: Leverage your deep knowledge of sports culture, sports broadcasting/media, and interactive gaming to ask the right questions and decode how fans evaluate fun and immersion in games.

2. Proactive Stakeholder Partnership Strategic Alignment

Be a Proactive Communication Lead: Establish consistent, forward-looking communication rhythms with studio leadership, Executive Producers, and Directors across multiple titles and teams. Anticipate user research needs weeks before major production milestones rather than reacting to incoming tickets.

Bridge Communication Build Consensus: Translate complex UR methodologies and player insights into clear, high-impact business and game design recommendations for cross-functional partners. Champion the player’s voice with patience, nuance, diplomacy, and clarity.

Integrate Educate: Embed deeply within game development roadmaps to ensure user research is a seamless, friction-free input at key design, interface, production, and greenlight decisions.

3. Participant Sampling, Resourcing Prioritization

Drive Relevant Sports Sampling Strategy: Ensure all qualitative and quantitative user research studies are powered by appropriate player demographics, behavioral profiles, and sports fandom levels. Oversee candidate screening criteria, recruitment pipelines, and sample validity to represent core, casual, and prospective player segments.

Manage Project Trade-Offs: Act as a thoughtful, objective filter between incoming high-velocity studio requests and available research capacity. Balance immediate hands-on study needs against overarching team roadmaps, offering lean/scrum research alternatives when constraints demand it.

Manage Budgets Vendor Ecosystems: Oversee relationships with external research agencies, specialized sports recruitment vendors, and testing facilities. Negotiate scopes, hold vendors to high methodological standards, and scale research operations smoothly.

What Success Looks Like in 90 Days

30 Days: Onboard rapidly, audit active sports studio roadmaps, build rapport with primary studio leadership, and take direct ownership of immediate, high-priority sports user research studies.

60 Days: Deliver high-impact user research findings on critical imminent projects, refine sports participant recruitment strategies to ensure proper sample quality across fan profiles, and establish a clear roadmap for upcoming research initiatives.

90 Days: Serve as a trusted strategic partner across our Sports titles - owning end-to-end research execution, vendor operations, and study delivery while setting a high bar for sports UR excellence across 2K.

Who We Believe Would Be an Excellent Fit

The Sports Media Enthusiast: You follow sports culture, sports broadcasting, sports media/entertainment platforms, and sports gaming closely. You understand what makes a sports experience feel authentic, fun, intuitive, and thrilling to different levels of fans.

The Expert Practitioner: You are a senior individual contributor who loves the craft of user research - from writing test plans and moderating lab sessions to running surveys and crafting compelling executive presentations.

The Sampling Research Strategist: You understand that great user research depends on testing with the right target audience. You have proven instincts for participant criteria, screening, and driving representative sampling across diverse player cohorts.

The Exceptional Communicator: You default to over-communicating context early, flagging operational delays before they become blockers, and tailoring your message seamlessly to developers, designers, or executives.

Highly Organized Operator: You run on structure, systems, and clear documentation. You thrive when managing multiple moving parts across time zones, recruitment channels, active studies, and partner studios.

Qualifications Background

Hands-on User Research Expertise: 5+ years of direct, hands-on experience in User Research, Human-Computer Interaction (HCI), or User Experience design, with a proven ability to design, execute, and present end-to-end qualitative and quantitative user studies independently.

Sports Industry / Media Focus: Demonstrated passion for and professional experience or background in sports, sports media, sports gaming, or related entertainment/media industries.

Proven Capabilities in Relevant Sampling: Demonstrated experience setting participant criteria, designing screener logic, and managing participant recruitment pipelines to ensure robust, representative sample groups across diverse player segments.

Methodological Breadth: Deep proficiency in user research / UX methods (usability testing, interface analysis, survey design, diary studies, focus groups, playtesting, heuristic evaluations).

Vendor Resource Management: Demonstrated experience managing project budgets, external research agencies, recruitment vendors, and multi-project research roadmaps.

Travel Requirement: Willingness and ability to travel domestically and internationally approximately once per month (~25% travel) to visit partner development studios, oversee external lab studies, and engage with key stakeholders.

Education: Degree in Human-Computer Interaction, Cognitive Psychology, Behavioral Economics, Human Factors, Statistics, or equivalent practical experience.

Bonus Points / Preferred

Deep familiarity with AAA Console/PC sports games, live-service titles, or free-to-play ecosystems.

Hands-on experience working directly in sports broadcast production, sports digital media, or interactive sports entertainment.

Experience navigating operational systems/dashboards (e.g., Jira, Confluence, Airtable) to streamline multi-project tracking.

Hands-on experience with UX/UXR tools and analytics suites (e.g., Qualtrics, Tableau, SPSS/R, SQL, or telemetry analysis).
