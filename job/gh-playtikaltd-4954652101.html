VIP Account Manager

Playtika · Bucharest

Mid Other Posted 2026-09-03 Full time
Apply on Playtika’s site →

Ambition Needs a Place to Grow.

At Playtika, we create mobile games enjoyed by millions worldwide.

Combining the scale of a public company (NASDAQ: PLTK) with the speed and ownership of a startup, we are a global leader where innovation, data, and creativity shape the future of entertainment.

Here, your ideas matter, your decisions impact products, and your growth is supported across global studios.

Bring your hunger where it belongs: at the front of the pack.

Responsibilities :

Manage the VIP Players as a group as well as one-on-one communication.

Increase players engagement with the game and their retention.

Function as a "middle-man" between the game and the player, by manufacturing strong relationships and understanding the player s professional and personal needs.

Hold an ongoing conversation with the players, and provide an immediate answer to any questions and problems that occur on all available platforms (email, messenger, in-game VIP Chat).

Use your virtual persona and use social media as a platform of engagement with players.

Quick response and support according to target times set with independence.

Responsible for creating custom content for the players, working hand in hand with creative and monetization teams

Understand studio KPIs and Objectives in order to craft custom-made effective strategies for different segments.

Deliver insights and conclusions that contribute to the business, produce basic analytical reports.

Stay up-to-date with the product and studio updates to deliver proper service to the VIP customer

Requirements:

Proficiency in English - both written and spoken

Microsoft Office - medium level

Able to think forward and have work plans for the upcoming week/month.

Ability to understand the activity of other departments for a business vision that will help provide the best possible customer experience.

Ability to participate in high level cross-functional teams and contribute with high level professional knowledge.

Willingness to take on additional wider projects on the daily role according to abilities and strengths.

Experience in handling customers requests.

High level analytical knowledge to operate multiple CRM systems

Monetization knowledge.

Time management prioritization skills

Resourcefulness and open-mindedness

Ability to adapt to different environments and changing priorities.

Nice to have

Understanding of social media engagement strategies.
