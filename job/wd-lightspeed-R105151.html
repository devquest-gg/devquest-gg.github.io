Over 5 years of experience in game sound production, with full-time participation in high-quality, AAA-standard game sound design, and a comprehensive understanding of the entire game sound production process.

2. Strong sound design skills, including efficient audio recording, processing, and mixing capabilities. Proficiency in using at least one DAW (Digital Audio Workstation) software and practical skills in sound design tools such as effects processors and synthesizers.

3. Excellent abilities in sound design and pipeline construction, with proficiency in audio middleware such as Wwise. Familiarity with basic operations of game engines such as Unity and Unreal Engine (UE).

4. Japanese fluency is required; candidates who can communicate in English or Chinese are highly welcomed.

5. Project management skills with the ability to coordinate within and outside the team to meet needs on time.

6. A passionate gamer with a deep interest in game sound and a unique understanding of the relationship between gameplay and sound design.

7. The ability to independently lead projects and work collaboratively as part of a team, with a focus on learning and teamwork.
