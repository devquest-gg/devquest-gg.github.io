Senior Manager, Art Management - Unpublished R&D Product

Riot Games was established in 2006 by entrepreneurial gamers who believe that player-focused development leads to exceptional games. In 2009, Riot released its debut title League of Legends to critical and player acclaim. Since then, Riot has expanded its portfolio and studios globally, continuing to create games that inspire and connect players around the world.

We’re looking for humble but ambitious, razor-sharp professionals who can teach us a thing or two. We promise to return the favor. Like us, you take play seriously; you’re passionate about games. We embrace those who see things differently, aren’t afraid to experiment, and who have a healthy disregard for constraints.

That’s where you come in.

As a Senior Manager, Art Management, you supervise a team s creative results, emphasizing team potential, operational processes, and overall productivity. Your scope of responsibilities covers an entire initiative. These responsibilities include overall culture, performance, and growth of your direct reports.

Responsibilities:

Build and sustain an environment where art leads and their teams can thrive, balancing craft excellence, team health, and player-focused outcomes.

Lead, mentor, and grow a multi-disciplinary art team in Singapore by managing and enabling craft leads to build high-performing, player-focused teams.

Oversee the creation and delivery of high-quality art that upholds a creative vision and elevates the player experience.

Build, maintain and Improve art production pipelines to ensure efficiency, scalability, and sustainable long-term delivery.

Build, maintain and Improve external development , identifying opportunities for externalization, designing sustainable engagements with appropriate partners.

Translate vision into actionable goals , working with production to ensure art content production is reliable, sustainable, and on schedule.

Make clear prioritization and process decisions to ensure timely, high-standard delivery across multiple content streams.

Partner with recruiting to hire world-class talen t and support your reports in building their teams.

Manage and coach craft leads, supporting their career and personal growth through clear development strategies, mentorship, and feedback. Empower them to grow both their craft and leadership impact while building high-performing teams.

Collaborate with peers and leadership to ensure strong alignment across disciplines. Step into art direction if needed to guide decisions, resolve conflicts, and model excellence, while prioritizing team growth over hands-on execution.

Build strong relationships across teams and with global partners - including Rioters in NA and Singapore - to ensure goals, dependencies and requirements are clearly understood and effectively executed.

Required Qualifications:

10+ years in the games industry, with at least 4+ years in art management / art leadership roles . Experienced with the game art production pipeline and interdependencies between disciplines. Proven experience leading and developing multi-disciplinary art teams.

Strong background collaborating with external vendors and co-development partners.

Skilled in performance management with the ability to coach, develop, and, when needed, address underperformance effectively and empathetically to sustain a high bar for excellence.

Fluent in English (spoken and written), able to communicate abstract or complex ideas clearly.

High self-awareness, empathy, and strong stakeholder management skills.

Growth-minded and outcome-driven, able to navigate ambiguity with optimism and guide others to learn and improve through change.

Comfortable leading through others - balancing people development, team performance, and creative alignment to achieve exceptional outcomes.

Desired Qualifications:

Experienced with the entire game art production pipeline, knowing the dependencies and how every discipline coordinates with each other.

Experience collaborating with producers or development managers on planning and schedules.

Broad experience shipping stylized AAA game content.

Multilingual skills (Mandarin or other regional languages) to support external partner collaboration.

Proven ability to design or refine team frameworks that improve culture, growth, and long-term.

World-class mentorship and coaching skills.

Good to Have Qualifications:

Passion for competitive PVP games

Having experience in collaborating with development managers or producers on content planning and schedule maintenance.

Experience working on video games. Bonus points for Outsourcing experience

Broad experience, working production end to end.

Variety engine experience such as Unreal.
