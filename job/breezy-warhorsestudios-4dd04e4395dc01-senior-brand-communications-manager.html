About the Role

As part of the Warhorse Studios Marketing team, you will help shape how players, media, creators and partners experience and understand our games and brands.

Working closely with colleagues across Marketing, including PR and social media, you will develop and drive communication strategies that support our products throughout their lifecycle. You will ensure that messaging is clear, authentic and consistent across channels, helping bring our brands closer to players around the world.

This role is ideal for someone who combines strategic thinking with hands-on communication skills and enjoys turning game features, development stories and brand values into compelling narratives.

Responsibilities

Own and develop communication strategies for Warhorse Studios brands and products.

Define and maintain key messaging frameworks, ensuring consistency across marketing, PR and social media activities.

Work closely with colleagues across Marketing to align communication plans and campaign messaging.

Create, refine and approve communication materials including press releases, blog posts, presentations, talking points, media briefs and other marketing content.

Monitor media coverage, creator content and player sentiment, using insights to refine messaging and communication priorities.

Support the preparation of studio representatives for interviews, presentations and public appearances.

Collaborate on the planning and delivery of media, creator and consumer-facing activities, including game launches, showcases and industry events.

Coordinate communication initiatives with external agencies, partners, creators and platform holders where required.

Help identify communication risks and contribute to crisis communication planning and messaging when necessary.

Work with internal stakeholders to source, review and approve assets that support communication and marketing activities.

Skills & Profile

Exceptional written and verbal communication skills in English.

Strong storytelling abilities.

Strategic mindset combined with strong attention to detail.

Ability to build clear and compelling narratives around games and brands.

Excellent collaboration and stakeholder management skills.

Strong understanding of gaming culture, player communities, creators and games media.

Passion for games and the games industry.

Experience working with media, influencers, agencies or platform partners is a plus.

Experience

5+ years of experience in communications, PR, brand marketing or related fields.

Experience in the games industry is strongly preferred.

Experience working across multiple communication channels, including earned, owned and social media.

Degree-level education or equivalent professional experience.
