Role Overview

As the Program Manager, Workplace Experience Real Estate (APAC) , you will serve as a regional program resource supporting Workplace Experience (WE) Partners and regional leadership across APAC.

This role exists to provide specialized expertise, project support, and regional coordination across Corporate Real Estate (CRE), workplace programs, and operational initiatives. You will help build consistency, share best practices, support project delivery, and strengthen program maturity across APAC while enabling local teams to successfully execute within their markets.

Responsibilities:

Program Project Leadership

Provide project management expertise and execution support for regional workplace, construction, and workplace transformation initiatives where additional capacity or specialized knowledge is required.

Manage projects within established timelines, budgets, and quality expectations while balancing regional business needs.

Coordinate project teams, vendors, consultants, and cross-functional partners to ensure successful project delivery.

Identify risks, dependencies, and resource constraints and partner with stakeholders to develop mitigation strategies and escalation plans.

Step directly into projects where additional leadership, expertise, or bandwidth support is required.

Corporate Real Estate (CRE) Portfolio Management

Support regional leadership and Workplace Experience Partners by providing analysis, recommendations, and project support related to APAC real estate strategy and portfolio initiatives.

Partner with regional leadership and Workplace Experience Partners to evaluate portfolio opportunities, workplace investments, lease events, and occupancy strategies.

Assess regional real estate market conditions, trends, and opportunities, providing insights and recommendations that support informed decision-making.

Provide coordination, reporting, and subject matter expertise to support lease administration, portfolio governance, and compliance activities across APAC.

Partner with central CRE teams to coordinate regional transactions, workplace projects, and portfolio initiatives.

Develop portfolio reporting, business cases, project summaries, and executive presentations to support strategic planning and decision-making.

Regional Program Development Governance

Partner with global COEs and regional stakeholders to develop, socialize, and improve frameworks, standards, playbooks, and governance models that can be adopted across APAC.

Support WE Partners in understanding and implementing regional and global workplace standards while accounting for local market needs.

Partner with global COEs to ensure APAC perspectives and business needs are represented early in program development.

Support continuous improvement initiatives by identifying operational gaps, opportunities, and best practices across markets.

Synthesize regional priorities, roadmaps, and program performance metrics to support strategic planning and decision-making.

Workplace Operations Capability Building

Act as a trusted advisor and subject matter expert to Workplace Experience Partners across APAC.

Coach and support WE Partners in areas including real estate, workplace operations, vendor management, workplace programs, and project delivery.

Provide consultation and thought partnership on complex operational challenges and strategic initiatives.

Provide consultation, tools, and program support to WE Partners implementing workplace programs within their markets.

Foster knowledge sharing and operational excellence across the regional WE community.

Cross-Functional Partnership

Build strong relationships with stakeholders across Workplace Experience, Security, IT, Finance, Procurement, Legal, HR, and business leadership.

Partner with regional and global teams to align workplace initiatives with organizational priorities.

Support regional programs related to Safety Security, Sustainability, Food Programs, Events, and Integrated Facilities Management where additional program management expertise is needed.

Analyze regional program and vendor performance data and provide recommendations to WE Partners and leadership on opportunities for improvement.

Required Qualifications:

7+ years of experience in Corporate Real Estate, Facilities Management, Workplace Operations, Project Management, or related fields.

Experience managing workplace, real estate, construction, or operational programs across multiple locations or markets.

Strong understanding of corporate real estate principles, workplace strategy, lease administration, project delivery, and vendor management.

Experience working with cross-functional stakeholders and influencing outcomes without direct authority.

Experience using data, reporting, and emerging technologies to identify trends, surface opportunities, and support more informed planning, prioritization, and decision-making.

Demonstrated ability to lead complex projects involving multiple stakeholders, competing priorities, and changing business requirements.

Experience developing operational processes, governance frameworks, and scalable program solutions.

Strong financial, budgeting, analytical, organizational, and problem-solving skills.

Excellent written, verbal, and presentation skills, with the ability to communicate effectively with stakeholders at all levels.

Ability to operate independently while balancing strategic thinking with hands-on execution.

Experience working across diverse APAC markets and cultures.

Preferred Qualifications:

Demonstrated ability to identify and apply AI-enabled tools, automation, or systems improvements to streamline workflows, strengthen decision-making, and improve program outcomes across cross-functional teams.

Experience within technology, gaming, entertainment, or other fast-paced multinational organizations.

Knowledge of workplace transformation, hybrid work strategies, sustainability initiatives, or integrated facilities management.

Professional certifications in project management, facilities management, real estate, or related disciplines.
