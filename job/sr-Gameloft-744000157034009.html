Graphic Designer (Middle/Senior)

Gameloft · Ho Chi Minh City

Senior Art Posted 2026-10-22 Full time
Apply on Gameloft’s site →

WHAT YOU WILL BE WORKING ON​ What you will focus on The Graphic Designer in the Creative Agency Team plays a vital role in adapting and creating compelling visuals: illustrations and marketing assets to drive engagement and support marketing strategies across various channels. This includes creating promotional materials for user acquisition campaigns, social media, in-game events, branding initiatives, and merchandise. The designer collaborates with multiple stakeholders, including marketing, live operations, and user acquisition teams, to ensure visuals align with creative briefs and enhance the player experience.&#xa0; Working under the guidance of the Creative Agency Lead, the designer brings creativity, precision, and innovation to every project.

What it means on a daily basis​ Create high-quality 2D assets for marketing and promotional campaigns, including social media graphics, banners, app store images, cross-promotions, and user acquisition ads.

Design in-game visuals and branding materials, such as icons, splash screens, event banners, logos, infographics, presentations, and merchandise.

Adapt designs to diverse artistic styles, project requirements, and digital or print formats while maintaining creativity and attention to detail.

Collaborate with internal teams, leads, and licensors to incorporate feedback, contribute ideas, and support the creative direction of 2D assets.

Follow artistic, brand IP, and licensing guidelines to ensure visual consistency, high-quality deliverables, and timely delivery.

Organize and archive design files, prepare final assets for deployment across platforms, and monitor static ad performance to recommend improvements.

Stay updated on industry trends, design tools, and emerging technologies, proposing innovative solutions to enhance the quality and engagement of marketing materials.

What success will look like Respect delivery deadlines.

Ensure that creatives adhere to the high quality standards.

Maintain the highest standards of professionalism with regard to the team and clients.

Who you would be working with​ You will report to the Creative Agency Team Lead (CAT Lead) and work closely with the Project Manager, as well as other Graphic Designers, Illustration Artists, and Motion Designers on the team.

You will also coordinate with various internal and external stakeholders, including the Marketing team, Game team, and Clients (depending on the project).

Your recruitment journey​ CV and portfolio assessment Initial discussion with recruiter Test Interview with managers Where this role can take you Advance to a more senior level (e.g., Principal Graphic Designer) or pivot horizontally to other roles within the Creative Agency Team or relevant departments, provided qualifications are met.

WHAT YOU NEED TO SUCCEED​ Fluency in English with excellent communication skills.

Strong knowledge of Adobe Photoshop and Illustrator (Web Pack).

Strong composition and layout skills.

Ability to independently manage the entire creative process, from asset preparation to final delivery, while adhering to a creative brief.

An online portfolio and/or demo reel, which must be included with your application.

Bachelor’s / College Degree in Graphic Design / Art / Creative Multimedia is recommended.

WHO YOU ARE A strong sense of creativity and innovation.

Capability to juggle multiple tasks with competing deadlines in a fast-paced environment.

Strong artistic sensibilities, a keen eye for detail, and a passion for constantly innovating and exploring new techniques.

A willingness to learn about game-engine content manipulation.

Ability to meet deadlines and handle multiple projects.

Passion and experience working in video games.

WHAT’S IN IT FOR YOU​ Benefits A hybrid working model with 2 days working from home every week.

A range of well-being events and policies to support employees physically, mentally, and emotionally.

Various training packages, including internal training, training sponsorship, and e-learning platforms (iLearn, Udemy, O'Reilly,...).

Opportunities to collaborate and train with Gameloft worldwide experts and develop yourself.

An attractive monthly salary alongside Tet and other performance bonuses.

Minimum 12 days of paid annual leave, plus 5 days of paid sick leave, Christmas leave, Birthday leave, CSR leave,...

100% coverage of mandatory insurance plus private healthcare insurance.

A monthly allowance to support lunch meals and working-from-home electricity and Internet bills.

Relocation support for international candidates joining from overseas.

Life at Gameloft​ A collaborative workplace environment, with over 15+ nationalities, where hundreds of world-renowned game titles were born.

An open-space office, a canteen, a billiard table, a ping-pong table, and a foosball table.

A gaming room equipped with mutiple devices (PS, Xbox, Nintendo Switch, arcade machine,...).

A capable and encouraging team of professionals focused on creating compelling video games.

A global company where you can meet great people around the world.

A fun place to work in the heart of the city.
