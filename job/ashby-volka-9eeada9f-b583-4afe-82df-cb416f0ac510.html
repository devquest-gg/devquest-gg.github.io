ABOUT OUR GAME

Taonga: the Island Farm https://taongafarm.com/en/play/ is an immersive exploration and adventure game set on a colorful tropical archipelago.

What begins as a casual farming experience gradually evolves into a rich, multi-layered adventure. Players discover increasing depth through travel, story-driven quests, strategic battles, engaging mini-games, competitive events, and much more — transforming the game into a captivating midcore adventure with sophisticated mechanics.

We’re looking for a talented Marketing Creative Artist to develop and refine vibrant static marketing visuals for Taonga. You’ll combine AI generation with hands-on work in Adobe Photoshop to produce polished banners for advertising campaigns across various platforms.

[https://app.ashbyhq.com/api/images/user-content/5b4bb49f-81e7-43db-ab5a-6a09c4aa1deb/bd9b7882-0922-423b-9747-fe830e965c9a/image.png]

RESPONSIBILITIES

1. Generate visual content using ChatGPT, Stable Diffusion, ComfyUI, and other relevant AI tools.

2. Work confidently with different models, LoRAs, prompts, conditioning and control techniques, and generation settings to achieve the required composition, consistency, and visual style

3. Refine AI-generated, primarily farm-themed marketing banners in Adobe Photoshop using manual retouching, compositing, and paintovers, and adapt approved visuals to different sizes and formats.

4. Stay informed about advances in AI art and emerging tools and techniques.

5. Prepare organized source files and final exports according to technical requirements.

6. Reuse and adapt existing materials when appropriate.

REQUIREMENTS

1. Has at least 2 years of experience creating 2D art.

2. Has at least 6 months of experience in marketing.

3. Has a strong understanding of human anatomy.

4. Is an experienced user of Adobe Photoshop.

5. Is experienced with ChatGPT, Stable Diffusion, ComfyUI, and other AI tools.

6. A minimum English proficiency level of A2.

The hiring process includes a short unpaid test task.

We require a portfolio of your work to apply for this position.
