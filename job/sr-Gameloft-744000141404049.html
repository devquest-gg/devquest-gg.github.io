Main responsibilities: Content Creation: Contribute to the development of compelling and creative content across social media platforms, press releases, newsletters, and a wide range of communication materials to effectively strengthen our brand presence.

Event Coordination: Support the end-to-end planning and execution of both virtual and in-person events, ensuring smooth operations and engaging experiences for internal teams and external audiences.

Stakeholder Collaboration: Proactively collaborate with cross-functional departments to understand their communication needs and independently align actions to deliver clear, consistent messaging.

Key qualifications: Bachelor’s degree in Communications, Public Relations, Marketing, or related field (or equivalent experience).

Strong written and verbal communication skills, with a keen eye for detail and creativity.

Familiarity with social media platforms and best practices for community engagement.

Ability to multitask and prioritize tasks effectively in a deadline-driven environment.

Experience with content creation tools (e.g., Canva, Capcut) and office tools (PowerPoint).
