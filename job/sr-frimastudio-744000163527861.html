Senior Technical Artist

Frima Studio · Québec

Senior Art Posted 2026-11-24 Full time
Apply on Frima Studio’s site →

What your weeks are going to look like Contribute to game design by proposing creative solutions tailored to the project’s needs, while adhering to constraints (budget, technical, artistic, etc.); Establish effective work methods for artists and guide them in their approaches; Analyze memory and performance issues throughout the project; Create efficient shaders or materials for texturing characters, environments, or special effects; Optimize and automate graphics processes and production pipelines to improve real-time performance and the efficiency of the art teams’ work; Create and maintain scripts and tools that artists use in game engines or 3D software; Work closely with programmers to develop new features in games; Work proactively with producers and directors to find solutions to efficiency issues; Participate in progress meetings to discuss technical challenges and ensure rigorous tracking of deliverables; Guide and support technical artists by providing them with guidance and sharing best practices in development to ensure code quality and compliance with the team’s standards; Serve as the technical point of contact for the project; Contribute to skill development within the team through mentoring and ongoing training.

What we're looking for Bachelor’s degree in computer science or a related field, combined with experience deemed equivalent; A minimum of ten (10) years of relevant experience in the video game industry; Proven experience as a technical artist on complex projects; Advanced knowledge of video game constraints and performance management (memory, GPU, CPU); Excellent knowledge of Unreal engine; Knowledge of graphics programming in HLSL, GLSL, CG, and ShaderLab; Knowledge of scripting languages such as Python, C#, MEL, C++, etc.; Knowledge of software such as Blender, Maya, Substance Designer, and/or Houdini; Willingness to travel internationally as needed.

English language skills are required to perform the job, as we collaborate with English-speaking partners.

&#xa0; What we value in our team Ability to work in a multidisciplinary team; Good time management and adherence to production deadlines; Good artistic culture (games, multimedia, cinema, etc.); Demonstrated ability to solve complex problems; Show autonomy, resourcefulness, organization, and rigour.
