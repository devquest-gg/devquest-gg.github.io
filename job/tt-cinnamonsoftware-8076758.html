About Cinnamon Cinnamon is the most ambitious independent Roblox studio in the world.

Our games have generated billions of visits, reached millions of players worldwide and continue to grow every day. Our portfolio includes LifeTogether (3.5+ billion visits), BlockSpin (1+ billion visits) and Bayview (600+ million visits), alongside an ambitious project currently in development.

We build original games. We own our products. We invest in them for the long term.

While many games experience a brief spike in popularity before disappearing, our focus is on building products that continue to grow every single day, retain players and attract large audiences long after launch. We're building games designed to become long-term destinations for millions of players and become legendary.

The Role We are looking for talented short-form Roblox creators who know how to make content people actually want to watch.

We're after creators who consistently turn gameplay into viral moments. The kind of videos people send to their friends, watch twice, and can't scroll past.

If you're creative, understand TikTok or YouTube Shorts, and know how to make funny, engaging content, we'd love to work with you.

What You'll Do Create short-form funny and relatable clips featuring our Roblox games.

Produce funny, entertaining, and highly shareable content.

Stay on top of trends and adapt them to our games.

Work with our team on ideas, feedback, and upcoming content opportunities.

What We're Looking For An established audience on TikTok, YouTube Shorts, or similar.

A strong understanding of short-form content and what makes videos perform well.

Great editing skills and a creative mindset.

A good sense of humour and strong storytelling.

Reliability and consistent uploads.

What You'll Get Competitive paid partnership.

Direct access to the Cinnamon team.

Early access to upcoming content opportunities.

Creative freedom to make content in your own style.

The opportunity to grow alongside some of our biggest Roblox projects.
