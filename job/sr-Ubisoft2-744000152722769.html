Creator Project Manager Assistant - Internship (6 months) January 2027 (W/M/NB)

Ubisoft (HQ) · Paris

Entry Production Posted 2026-09-30 Intern
Apply on Ubisoft (HQ)’s site →

As part of Ubisoft’s Global Content Creators team within the Marketing department, you will help structure and improve our content creator operations. At the intersection of marketing, technology, and project management, this internship will give you the opportunity to work on concrete initiatives, including process optimization, tool improvements, documentation, operational analysis, and coordination of international campaigns.

Your responsibilities will include: Project Management Support cross-functional projects by preparing materials, tracking action items, coordinating with internal stakeholders, and keeping project tracking documents up to date.

Identify operational blockers, define needs, and make recommendations to improve tools, processes, or information flows.

Prepare internal communications, including newsletters, meeting recaps, presentation materials, and post-project reviews.

Enabling Internal Operations Improve briefing and coordination processes for creator campaigns by centralizing requests, tracking campaign statuses, and increasing visibility across central, local, and brand teams.

Strengthen collaboration with creator teams across 20 locations by sharing information, gathering feedback, and consolidating needs.

Conduct user research and provide operational support through surveys, workshops, testing, bug reporting, and follow-up on improvements.

Create, maintain, and structure internal documentation to support onboarding, knowledge sharing, best practices, and effective use of tools.

Analysis & Reporting Monitor trends in influencer marketing, social media, and engagement strategies to identify opportunities and best practices.

Contribute to reporting on Ubisoft Creators Program (UCP) operations, including campaign tracking, data consolidation, qualitative and quantitative analysis, and actionable insights.

Conduct benchmarks and market research on creator programs, engagement strategies, and growth opportunities for UCP.

What you will gain from this internship Guidance and support to help you grow, gain autonomy, and receive regular feedback.

Meaningful projects that contribute to the team’s goals and align with your interests.

Exposure to projects spanning operations, analysis, documentation, and continuous improvement.

You’ll join a supportive environment where you’ll be encouraged to ask questions, share ideas, and contribute to high-visibility projects.

What we’re looking for You are currently a student and ideally have some previous experience in influencer marketing, digital marketing, project management, community management, or process improvement.

You are organized, detail-oriented, analytical, curious, and adaptable, with strong communication and interpersonal skills and the ability to collaborate effectively with a wide range of stakeholders.

You are fluent in English, both written and spoken.

You are proficient in Microsoft Office, particularly PowerPoint, Excel, and Word, and are comfortable using collaboration and project management tools.

If you’re looking for a unique opportunity to gain experience in the gaming industry and explore influencer marketing in gaming, we’d love to hear from you!
