Business Unit LIGHTSPEED STUDIOS is made up of passionate players who advance the art & science of game development through great stories, great gameplay, and advanced technology. We are focused on bringing next generation experiences to gamers who want to enjoy them anywhere, anytime, across multiple genres and devices.

What the Role Entails As a Business Development Manager, your primary mission is to anchor our organization at the intersection of AI innovation and the gaming ecosystem. You will be the eyes and ears in North America, focusing heavily on Generative AI and UGC platforms to drive our next generation of growth. You will transform technical trends into strategic intelligence and bridge the gap between world-class talent and our internal product teams.

Key Responsibilities: AI & UGC Insight: Lead the identification and evaluation of emerging Generative AI and UGC technologies and partners, while assessing their potential impact on game production and player experience Market Intelligence: Monitor and analyze the North American gaming market shifts and technology trends. Deliver time-sensitive intelligence to senior leadership and studio heads to support strategic decision-making.

Project & Talent Scouting: Proactively source and engage with top-tier video-game industry talent and high-potential startups/projects in North America. In addition, build a pipeline of game-changing innovation for potential investment or collaboration.

Expert Network & Development Capability Sourcing: Develop and maintain a solid network of contacts related to subject matter experts, and high-quality con 0development/outsourcing partners to accelerate our internal project timelines.

Who We Look For Technical Acumen: A deep, demonstratable interest in game-related technology. High proficiency in AI applications is mandatory. Experience with or a strong understanding of UGC ecosystems (e.g., Roblox, Minecraft, UEFN) is a top-tier advantage.

Strategic Vision: 3-5 years of experience in tech BD, strategy with a proven track record of identifying industry-shifting trends before they go mainstream.

Sourcing Capabilities: Strong hunter instincts with the ability to navigate the NA tech landscape to find elite talent and stealth-mode projects.

Local Professional Network: Currently based in the California Bay Area with an active network among local developers, AI researchers and startup founders.

Language & Communication: Exceptional analytical output and negotiation skills in both Mandarin and English. Ability to synthesize complex technical concepts into clear business cases for cross-border stakeholders.
