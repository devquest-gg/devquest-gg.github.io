Quality Assurance Analyst

Frima Studio · Québec · Remote

Senior QA Remote Posted 2026-10-26 Full time
Apply on Frima Studio’s site →

What your weeks are going to look like Attend meetings with the partner team to gather and analyze relevant information regarding the assigned content in order to prepare test plans; Define and maintain appropriate test plans in accordance with project specifications; Coordinate and monitor the completion of test suites based on deliverables; Create, maintain, and present dashboards showing KPIs related to product quality; Regularly track the status of reported bugs and update test documentation; Identify, collect, and analyze test data to prepare progress reports; Actively participate in the continuous improvement of the player experience; Work to improve and optimize quality assurance practices on your project (tools, methods, etc.); Engage and educate team members on the importance of product quality; Influence the scope, direction, and testing methodology required for your project; Support the quality assurance lead, as needed, in project management; Participate in executing test suites, as needed.

What we're looking for College diploma or combination of relevant studies (computer science, multimedia, web, video game, etc.); Minimum of three (3) years of relevant experience as a Quality Assurance Analyst in the video game industry; Experience working on a mobile game project (a significant asset); Good knowledge of Jira; Knowledge of data analysis tools; Excellent command of French and English (spoken and written). English skills are required to perform the job, as we collaborate with English-speaking partners.

Willingness to travel internationally as needed.
