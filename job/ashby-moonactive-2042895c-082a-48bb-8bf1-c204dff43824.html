Moon Active is one of the world’s fastest-growing mobile game companies, providing entertainment to millions of active users across the universe. The company is headquartered in the heart of Tel Aviv.

- 3+ years of professional experience in software development.

- Strong knowledge of C#.

- Experience with object-oriented programming using design patterns, algorithms and data structures.

Advantages

- Experience in native mobile development on Android and IOS (Java, Objective-C).

- Experience with Unity editor scripting.
