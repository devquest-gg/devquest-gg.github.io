Technical Game Design Intern - Summer 2027

Riot Games · Los Angeles

Entry Design Posted 2026-11-11 Intern
Apply on Riot Games’s site →

All Game Designers at Riot have a base set of skills: you design, tweak, and optimize the player experience. Your analytical skills, player insight, and creativity aid in crafting in- and out-of-game experiences that engage and surprise players.

As a Technical Game Design Intern, you will work extensively in technical problem spaces, building tools and systems that unlock themselves and other game designers, and also own key gameplay designs. In your application, you will select which product you prefer to apply for.

Potential Responsibilities:

League of Legends

Work on live operations and new content for one of the core gameplay expressions of League of Legends (Summoner’s Rift or ARAM: Mayhem)

Work on champion abilities, items, augments, or supporting game systems

Collaborate with Designers and Engineers to add features and workflows to existing tools and pipelines to help the team work efficiently, or tackle unforeseen gameplay problems

Teamfight Tactics

Work on the TFT Gameplay initiative, contributing directly to a set that will be released in 2027

Work on Traits, champions, champion abilities, or supporting systems such as the set mechanic

Collaborate with Designers and Engineers to add features and workflows to existing tools and pipelines to help the team work efficiently, or tackle unforeseen gameplay problems

Required Qualifications:

Graduating in the 2026 or 2027 academic year studying Computer Science, Engineering, or Technology related field

Completed courses or related work experience in data structures and algorithms

Coding experience using C++ and Blueprints in Unreal Engine

Deep gameplay experience with League of Legends or TFT

Experience collaborating through testing, tuning, and partnering with designers, engineers, and artists to reach player experience goal

Available to work full-time hours for the 12 week internship duration

Minimum GPA of 3.0

Desired Qualifications:

Unreal or Game Engine experience, specifically with building character abilities

Experience working in data-driven setups such as spreadsheets, scripts, config tables

Experience reading, modifying, and improving code or Blueprints built by others

Demonstrated understanding of animation timing, hit impact, and incorporating player feedback

Demonstrated experience creating a prototype, mod, or school project that shows your design and implementation experience.

Experience playing Ranked League of Legends or TFT

For this role, you ll find success through craft expertise, a collaborative spirit, and decision-making that prioritizes the delight of players. We will certainly be looking at your past studies and experience, but for this role, we also look for dedicated people with a personal relationship with games. If you embody player empathy and care about the experiences of players, this could be the role for you!

Work Authorization:

Candidates must be legally authorized to work in the United States for the duration of the program.

This position is not eligible for visa sponsorship. We do not sponsor visas for this position or internship conversions.
