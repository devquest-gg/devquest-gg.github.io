Contract VFX Artist

Mob Entertainment is a startup multimedia studio that is best known for our hit indie horror game " Poppy Playtime" .

This franchise is one of the hottest new properties in gaming, and our team is naturally expanding as our ambition expands.

To give a taste of our brand s popularity, more than 100 billion YouTube views of Poppy Playtime related content have occurred since the game s launch 4 years ago.

The Role

We’re looking for a Contract VFX Artist to help bring our worlds to life through high-quality, real-time visual effects. You’ll play a key role in creating atmospheric, stylized, and gameplay-driven effects that enhance immersion, tension, and player feedback.

This is a hands-on role focused on execution and iteration, working closely with art, design, and engineering to deliver polished in-game effects on a defined timeline.

What You’ll Do

Create high-quality real-time VFX for gameplay, environments, and cinematics (particles, shaders, lighting effects, etc.)

Design and implement effects that enhance player feedback, readability, and emotional impact

Work within Unreal Engine (Niagara preferred) to build, iterate, and polish effects

Collaborate with art direction to ensure consistency with the game’s visual style and tone

Partner with designers to support gameplay clarity and moment-to-moment feedback

Optimize VFX for performance, memory, and scalability across target platforms

Iterate quickly based on feedback and production needs

Maintain clean organization and documentation of VFX assets

What We’re Looking For

3+ years of experience creating real-time VFX for games

Strong portfolio demonstrating high-quality, stylized, and/or atmospheric effects

Experience with Unreal Engine (Niagara strongly preferred) or similar real-time engines

Solid understanding of shaders, materials, and particle systems

Ability to balance visual quality with performance constraints

Strong collaboration and communication skills

Ability to work independently and deliver assets on schedule in a contract environment

Nice to Have

Experience working on horror or atmospheric games

Knowledge of Houdini or procedural VFX workflows

Basic scripting or technical art experience

Experience shipping at least one game title

Familiarity with console and multi-platform optimization
