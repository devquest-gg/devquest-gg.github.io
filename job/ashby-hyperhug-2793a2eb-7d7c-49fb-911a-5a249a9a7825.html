Senior Legal Counsel

HyperHug · Limassol · Remote

Senior Other Remote Posted 2026-09-08 Full time
Apply on HyperHug’s site →

HyperHug is an international game development studio specializing in the shooter genre. Our main office is based in Cyprus, but our talented team works remotely from around the world. We’re developing Oxide: Survival Island, an immersive multiplayer survival shooter for mobile platforms, and we’re always looking for motivated individuals who want to grow with us, contribute their creativity, and help shape the future of our games.

Are you a commercially minded leader with a passion for the video game industry? Do you aspire to shape and lead the legal function at a fast-growing gaming company?

This is a unique opportunity to establish and own the legal department at HyperHug, the creator of Oxide: Survival Island—a multiplayer survival shooter that has engaged over 100 million players worldwide. We're looking for an experienced law leader to join our Limassol office and oversee our global legal strategy.

As our Senior Legal Counsel, you will take on a broader remit than most in-house roles. You’ll build, implement, and maintain the corporate, commercial, and employment framework, protect our intellectual property across international markets, collaborate closely with product teams to help launch new features, and work directly with founders and key stakeholders. If you’re ready to move from advising on decisions to making them, this role is for you.

WHAT YOU'LL DO

- Lead Operational Excellence: Build and maintain corporate, commercial and employment framework.

- Drive Commercial & IP Strategy: Protect and commercialise the IP behind the company’s game and brand.

- Partner with Product: Work with product, design and marketing teams on new features and designs.

- Own the Platform Relationship: Develop terms, in-app purchase, subscription rules and refunds.

- Protect Players and Data: Own terms of service, privacy policies, GDPR compliance, and the requirements of the Apple App Store and Google Play. Own player safety and child protection: age rating, age assurance, parental controls, moderation and reporting.

- Negotiate Commercial Deals: Draft and negotiate publishing, distribution, platform, UA and outsourcing agreements.

WHAT YOU'LL NEED

- Experience: 7+ years of post-qualification experience (PQE) in Corporate, Commercial and IP at a Cypriot or UK law firm or Big 4 company; Candidates combining top-tier law firm or Big 4 experience with in-house experience at a leading video game, IT or tech company will also be considered.

- Education: A UK or Cypriot bachelor’s degree in law (LL.B.) is required; other jurisdictions will not be considered given Cyprus s common law framework. An LL.M. and/or legal ranking recognition is a strong advantage.

- Technical Expertise: Deep expertise in intellectual property law.

- Compliance Expertise: Applied GDPR experience beyond policy drafting, and working knowledge of app store policy in practice (Apple and Google developer terms, in-app purchase, subscription and refund rules) and player safety/content moderation obligations for a multiplayer game.

- Language Skills: Native-level Greek and fluent English (written and spoken).

WHAT WE OFFER

- Medical insurance for you and your family, including dental and optical coverage

- €500 monthly Wolt allowance

- SportBenefit subscription

- Company car

- Up to €900 per month towards school or university fees for your children

- Top-level equipment (laptop, monitor, etc.)

- Learning and development opportunities

- Discounts with partner businesses

WHO WE ARE

HyperHug is based in Limassol, building and publishing hit games enjoyed by millions. Our flagship title, Oxide: Survival Island, drops players into a persistent multiplayer world to gather, build, raid, and survive together.
