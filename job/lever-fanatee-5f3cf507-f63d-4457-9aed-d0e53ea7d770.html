About the position

We are seeking a detail-oriented and analytical Junior Marketing Performance Analyst to join our marketing team. The ideal candidate will play a crucial role in optimizing our marketing strategies by analyzing data, identifying trends, and providing actionable insights to enhance campaign performance for our games. Your main responsibility will be to gain new players for our games (user acquisition).

Responsibilities:

- Assist in the implementation of user acquisition campaigns across various digital channels.

- Monitor and analyze key performance indicators (KPIs) to assess the effectiveness of user acquisition campaigns.

- Collaborate with cross-functional teams to gather data and insights for campaign planning and optimization.

- Generate reports on campaign performance and provide insights for continuous improvement.

- Conduct A/B testing and provide recommendations based on data-driven insights.

- Conduct market research to identify trends, competitors, and opportunities for user acquisition.

- Stay updated on industry trends and best practices in user acquisition and digital marketing.

- Monitor the performance of creative assets for active campaigns

- Benchmark and research different types of creatives

Qualifications:

- Bachelor's degree in Engineering, Information Technology, Marketing, Business,  or a related field.

- Knowledge of basic marketing principles and concepts.

- Proficient in English at an advanced level - Ability to articulate ideas clearly and concisely (it will be tested along the process)

- 1 year of experience with management of online advertising campaigns, preferably for apps

- Strong analytical and quantitative skills with the ability to interpret complex data sets.

- Familiarity with data visualization tools and techniques.

- Detail-oriented with the ability to manage multiple tasks simultaneously.

- Proficient in Microsoft Office suite, especially Excel, or Google Sheets.

Preferred Skills:

- Previous work with mobile game advertising.

- Familiarity with digital advertising platforms such as Google Ads, Facebook Ads, Unity Ads, etc.

Personal Attributes:

- Proactive and self-motivated.

- Ability to adapt to a fast-paced and dynamic work environment.

- Ability to work collaboratively in a team environment.

- Eagerness to learn and adapt to new challenges.

- Strong problem-solving skills.

- Enthusiastic about the digital marketing landscape.

- Proactive mindset with the ability to work independently and collaboratively within a team.

- Strong communication and interpersonal skills.

- Must be a São Paulo resident.

What We Offer:

- Beyond a vibrant and enjoyable work atmosphere, we offer:

- Competitive compensation aligned with market standards

- Flexible benefits

- Health and dental insurance

- Profit sharing

- Career development opportunities and professional growth

While the role is primarily remote, our São Paulo office is available for those seeking a dedicated workspace. There, you’ll find a calm environment, a stocked fridge with snacks and drinks, and even a ping-pong table for a bit of fun. Come help shape the future with our amazing team!

About Fanatee

Fanatee is a mobile gaming startup founded in 2013. Since then, our goal has been to enable people to play our games anytime, anywhere. We believe that quality is the key to success.

Our creative processes are well planned and carefully detailed, and our talent lies in turning great ideas into great businesses: our games have hit over 300M downloads worldwide. We believe that a diverse work environment contributes to an increasingly responsible society that values differences. Our dream allows us to do what we love the most: work with talented, passionate people and allow them to achieve goals as big as they can dream of.

If you want to share this ride with us and join a lightweight, fun, and focused environment that breathes innovation and game culture, apply now!
