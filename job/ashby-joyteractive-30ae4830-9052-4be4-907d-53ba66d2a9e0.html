We’re looking for an experienced Level Designer to join the Picture Blast Solitaire team.

What are you working on?

- Genres: Puzzle, Casual

- Platforms: Mobile, iOS, Android

For which tasks (responsibilities)?

- Ownership of the level design direction and weekly delivery of levels to production: art review, feedback to artists, uploading levels to the build, control of deadlines and quality

- Difficulty curve tuning across levels and within a single level

- Work with the level editor: setting up generation of the content delivery queue based on defined constraints

- Analysis of level metrics (churn, playtime), replacement and improvement of underperforming levels

- Generating and testing level design hypotheses to improve key metrics: playtime, retention, monetization (e.g. interstitial vs. rewarded ad balance)

- Assembly of level sets for A/B tests and test setup together with developers and the game designer

- Automation of routine processes in the content pipelinel

What kind of professional are we looking for?

- 3+ years of level design experience in puzzle, solitaire, match-3, or merge games

- Strong experience in difficulty curve tuning

- Experience with A/B testing of levels and level sets

- Experience with analytics: behavioral and monetization metrics, including ad metrics (interstitial, rewarded, eCPM)

- Experience with level editors or internal tools

- Ownership of results and proactivity

Nice to have

- Visual taste or an art background

- Experience with procedurally generated levels
