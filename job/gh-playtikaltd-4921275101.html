Data Ops Engineer

Playtika · Warsaw

Mid Engineering Posted 2026-07-07 Full time

Ambition Needs a Place to Grow.

At Playtika, we create mobile games enjoyed by millions worldwide.

Combining the scale of a public company (NASDAQ: PLTK) with the speed and ownership of a startup, we are a global leader where innovation, data, and creativity shape the future of entertainment.

Here, your ideas matter, your decisions impact products, and your growth is supported across global studios.

Bring your hunger where it belongs: at the front of the pack.

As a Data Operations Engineer you will be responsible for managing applications and data infrastructure in production environments fully hosted in private clouds and on-premise data centers, taking overall responsibility for the quality of the data services that the company provides.

The technology stack is mainly: Linux, Docker, Kubernetes, Ansible, Jenkins, TeamCity, Aerospike, MariaDB, PostgreSQL, Vertica, Hadoop ecosystem, SingleStore, ELK stack, Kafka, Git.

Infrastructure: private clouds, on-premise data centers.

Responsibilities

Deep understanding of the company applications and IT infrastructure;

Support existing data infrastructure;

Participation in the creation of new infrastructure parts, documentation development, customer onboarding;

Being a focal point for R D teams;

Participate in investigation process (in case of any issues/incident for specific R D team);

Providing solutions and support for critical IT environment.

Requirements

At least 3 years of practical experience in Linux administration;

Experience with containerization and container orchestration systems (e.g. Kubernetes);

Understanding of TCP/IP network stack;

Good troubleshooting skills and basic application debugging;

Understanding/experience with message queue systems (e.g. Apache Kafka);

Acquaintance with MySQL, PostgreSQL, Aerospike (or other NoSQL solutions).

Nice to have

Experience with VCS (preferably Git);

Experience with Hadoop ecosystem (HDFS, YARN, Spark, Hive);

Experience with CI/CD systems such as Jenkins, TeamCity;

Experience in managing systems with configuration management (Ansible).
