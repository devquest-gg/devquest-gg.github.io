Role Overview

As a VFX/Animator, you will work closely with Art and Tech Art leadership to elevate game quality by creating stunning VFX optimized for mobile performance, alongside compelling animations for UI, environments, and character. Beyond asset creation, you will collaborate cross-functionally with Art, Design, Engineering, and QA—troubleshooting rigging, VFX, and animation issues directly in Unity while helping refine team workflows and best practices.

Key Responsibilities

Work with the Art Director, Lead Artists and Lead Technical Artists to establish visual standards and technical specifications of VFX and animation on different projects.

Improve the visual quality of our games by creating high-quality real-time VFX that are performant on mobile.

Work on UI, Gameplay, Character and Environment animation to create stunning and compelling moments.

Work closely with Tech Art to troubleshoot VFX, animation, rigging, and implementation issues directly in-engine.

Collaborate cross-functionally with Art, Design, Engineering, and QA teams throughout development.

Contribute to improving animation workflows and best practices within the team.

Required Skills, Knowledge and Expertise

Strong portfolio showing examples of VFX and Animation for casual games.

Experience with Unity particle system, animator controllers, and basic state machines is a must

Good knowledge of Photoshop.

Good skills in art fundamentals like colour composition and visual hierarchy

Passionate about games

3D experience with Blender is a plus

After Effects experience for animated mockups is a plus
