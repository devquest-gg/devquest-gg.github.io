Mid Animation Posted 2025-03-24 Full time

Responsibilities: 1. Write scripts for animated films, including storylines, dialogues, and storyboards to create engaging content.

2. Contribute ideas during project planning to ensure scripts align with overall project goals.

3. Create animation scripts that cater to specific target audiences.

4. Revise and adjust scripts based on the director’s feedback to maintain high quality and meet deadlines.

4. Experience with literary, dramatic, or game scripts is preferred.

2. Knowledge of animation industry trends and how script design affects audience experience.

3. Existing industry resources in screenwriting (please briefly describe).

Application Requirements: 1. Submit related works (scripts, storyboards, character designs, etc.).

2. Share your experience with reviewing animated works and your insights.

3. Describe your creative strengths and interests in screenwriting.
