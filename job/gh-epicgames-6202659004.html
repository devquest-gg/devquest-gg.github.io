ENGINE PROGRAMMER INTERN

What You ll Do

Epic Games is looking for an Engine Programmer Intern to join them for the 2027 season. Working on our Fortnite Online team, you ll be supporting a multitude of systems and features for our Engine and Fortnite.

If it s an online game feature, we touch it. Epic engineers wear many hats. As an intern, you are provided with a meaningful experience with high expectations for performance. If you want to build something Epic, this is the opportunity for you!

In this role, you will

Work closely with the Fortnite Online team (FNO) by architecting, developing, and maintaining shared online gameplay technology

Contribute to the online systems and toolsets used by licensees in the Unreal Engine as well as online features and services used by Fortnite and other games at Epic

Develop solutions to various technical challenges from initial concept to final shipped code and ensure that it is stable for our players once released

What we re looking for

Bachelor s degree in Computer Science or a related discipline required, with strong analytical thinking and problem-solving skills strongly desired

Demonstrable proficiency in object-oriented programming and C++ required, with familiarity with Java considered a plus

Demonstrable knowledge of networking, REST, and utilizing back-end services with resiliency in mind

Demonstrated interest in online multiplayer games, and interest in games as a service also a plus

Familiarity with Unreal Engine or UEFN is strongly desired

Technical portfolio demonstrating programming work in a student or internship environment, or game/feature development, with examples of code and experience developing online features, tools, or other relevant projects strongly desired

Strong preference for demonstrating AI usage and leveraging LLMs for code and tooling development

This internship has a flexible start date in 2027. Recruitment will be ongoing until teams find an ideal match. Applicants must be legally authorized to work in the posting location for the duration of the internship. For more information about Epic’s Early Career Program, visit epicgames.com/earlycareers .
