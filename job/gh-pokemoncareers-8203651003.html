Get to know the role

Job Title: Technical Program Manager

Job Summary: The Technical Program Manager leads the program and project portfolio that advances IT Infrastructure Services priorities, service maturity, and organizational capabilities. This role connects strategy to execution by turning business and technology priorities into an integrated roadmap, establishing the mechanisms needed to evaluate demand and sequence work, and providing leaders with clear information about business value, dependencies, risks, capacity, and decisions.This is an individual contributor role with accountability for portfolio governance, demand management, cross-functional alignment, and delivery of complex technology programs. The Technical Program Manager works through influence across technical teams and business stakeholders, partners with Operational Excellence to support broader Technology portfolio visibility, enables consistent execution across Project Managers and technical leaders, and directly leads selected high-priority or cross-departmental initiatives. Success will be measured by the clarity of portfolio decisions, alignment of stakeholders and resources, and business outcomes delivered through the program.

FLSA Classification (US Only): Exempt

People Manager: No

What you’ll do

Turn strategy into an executable program and portfolio

Own the integrated program and project portfolio for IT Infrastructure Services, connecting related initiatives to service, capability, and business outcomes.

Translate strategic priorities into roadmaps, sequenced initiatives, decision points, and clear ownership across delivery teams.

Maintain a decision-ready view of portfolio health, business value, dependencies, risks, capacity constraints, resource needs, and expected outcomes.

Provide practical recommendations and facilitate tradeoff decisions so leaders can prioritize investment and focus limited capacity on the highest-value work.

Create disciplined demand, intake, and prioritization practices

Coordinate the intake and evaluation of proposed initiatives, ensuring requests have clear outcomes, sponsorship, scope, dependencies, risks, and resource implications.

Facilitate prioritization and sequencing discussions that balance strategic importance, operational needs, capacity, risk, and business value.

Partner with leaders and stakeholders to resolve ambiguity, clarify decisions, and communicate what will proceed, what will wait, and why.

Continuously improve portfolio and demand-management processes while using the minimum governance necessary to create clarity, accountability, and effective decisions.

Lead complex, high-value initiatives

Directly lead selected strategic, high-priority, or cross-departmental technology programs from definition through delivery and adoption.

Establish program structures, integrated plans, governance, communications, and escalation paths appropriate to the complexity and business impact of the work.

Anticipate and manage cross-project dependencies, resource conflicts, risks, and decisions that could affect outcomes or timelines.

Keep stakeholders aligned on commitments, tradeoffs, progress, and expected business outcomes while adapting plans as priorities and conditions change.

Build alignment across technology and business stakeholders

Coordinate work across Information Security, Online Services, Game Studio, Media Production, Automated Retail, Localization, and other partners whose priorities and decisions affect the portfolio.

Build trusted relationships with technical leaders, service owners, Project Managers, and business stakeholders, creating shared understanding of priorities and accountability.

Partner closely with Operational Excellence to provide accurate and consistent portfolio information for broader Technology planning and leadership decisions.

Translate complex technical, delivery, and capacity information into concise recommendations for technical and non-technical audiences.

Enable consistent portfolio execution

Establish common expectations for project governance, status reporting, risk and dependency management, decision tracking, and outcome measurement.

Enable delivery across multiple Project Managers, technical leaders, and service owners by improving visibility, coordination, and escalation across initiatives.

Use portfolio data and delivery insights to identify recurring issues, improve planning quality, and strengthen execution over time.

Promote practical use of tools, automation, and standardized practices that reduce administrative effort and improve portfolio transparency.

What you’ll bring

Program and portfolio leadership

8+ years of experience in technical program management, portfolio management, project management, or a related technology delivery role, including leadership of complex cross-functional programs.

Demonstrated ability to manage a portfolio or broad program domain involving multiple projects, technical teams, stakeholders, dependencies, and competing priorities.

Experience translating strategy into executable roadmaps, governance mechanisms, integrated plans, and measurable outcomes.

Sound judgment when balancing strategic value, delivery risk, operational priorities, resource constraints, and stakeholder needs.

Demand management and decision support

Experience designing or operating project intake, demand-management, prioritization, and portfolio-planning processes.

A record of turning complex or incomplete delivery information into clear analysis, recommendations, and decisions for senior leaders.

Ability to identify meaningful measures of portfolio health, delivery progress, capacity, risk, and business value without creating unnecessary process overhead.

Strong analytical skills and attention to detail, with the ability to distinguish facts, assumptions, risks, and recommendations.

Cross-functional influence and communication

Demonstrated success leading through influence where ownership, expertise, and decision authority are distributed across organizations.

Exceptional written and verbal communication, including executive-level reporting and the ability to translate technical and delivery information into business terms.

Strong facilitation, negotiation, conflict-resolution, and stakeholder-management skills.

Ability to build trust, create shared understanding, and maintain accountability across technical teams, business partners, vendors, and leaders.

Technical and delivery credibility

Sufficient technical breadth to understand infrastructure, workplace technology, cloud, networking, reliability, security, service management, and related dependencies.

Experience with program and project delivery methods, risk management, organizational change, and adoption planning.

Experience with portfolio, project, work-management, reporting, or collaboration platforms. Experience with Jira and Confluence is preferred.

Bachelor s degree in Computer Science, Information Systems, Business, or a related field, or equivalent practical experience. PgMP, PMP, or comparable program or portfolio management certification is preferred.
