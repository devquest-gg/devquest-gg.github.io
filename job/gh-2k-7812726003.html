What We Need

As part of our Global Loc 2.0 evolution, 2K is deepening its investment in language-specific leadership to improve the voice, cultural authenticity, and overall quality of our localized content. We are seeking a Game Localization Lead to own the linguistic direction and execution strategy for all Russian content across 2K’s game portfolio.

This is an individual contributor role within the Content Localization (CLOC) team. You’ll serve as the key Russian language authority, working with minimal supervision across complex projects. You’ll apply deep linguistic and cultural expertise while partnering with developers, producers, QA, and publishing stakeholders to ensure language quality meets player expectations and platform-holder standards.

As a Game Localization Lead, you will directly shape how our players in Russian-speaking regions experience our games. You’ll own language strategy, guide voice and tone across titles, and play a key role in 2K’s broader effort to deliver best-in-class localized content through a modern, embedded, and scalable localization model.

What You’ll Do

Linguistic Strategy Quality Ownership

Own the localization strategy, quality bar, and final sign-off for Russian content across all 2K titles.

Develop, maintain, and enforce glossaries, style guides, and cultural content policies for the Russian market.

Act as the primary advisor for cultural risk mitigation, proactively identifying regional sensitivities and communicating potential brand impacts to relevant stakeholders.

Issue Escalation Bug Triage: Serve as the main linguistic escalation point for tone disputes or QA challenges, and partner closely with the LQA team to investigate, triage, and resolve complex linguistic bugs.

Core Localization

Review and edit localized content (in-game text, UI, scripts, VO) for tone, clarity, fluency, and cultural relevance.

Regularly review in-development and near-final builds to audit for tone, consistency, and cultural resonance.

Lead VO casting, script review, and direct creative sessions during Russian voiceover production.

Cross-Functional Collaboration Compliance

Collaborate with cross-functional teams—localization producers, embedded LQA, marketing, publishing, and dev—to guide language decisions and ensure global alignment.

Work alongside the Age Rating team, game studios, and content owners to ensure localized content strictly adheres to Russian age rating requirements and visual/language compliance.

Advise on the selection of suitable, legible fonts for the target Russian, collaborating with technical and UI stakeholders to ensure any font-rendering or display issues are resolved in a timely manner.

Leadership, Mentorship, Continuous Improvement

Supervise and provide feedback to entry- and mid-level linguists or vendors working on Russian content.

Recommend and help implement process improvements that improve quality, reduce error rates, or optimize turnaround time for Russian localization.

Participate in Global Loc 2.0 initiatives, working with senior leadership to shape evolving localization standards, practices, and tooling.

What Will Make You A Great Fit

Professional localization experience in games, tech, or entertainment.

Native fluency in Russian, with demonstrated mastery of tone, grammar, and regional nuance.

Advanced English fluency (written and spoken); capable of representing language decisions across global teams.

Maintain a deep, current understanding of the regional gaming landscape, pop culture zeitgeist, and evolving localization methodologies.

Demonstrate a deep-rooted passion for the video game industry, paired with comprehensive knowledge of major gaming IPs, genres, and player culture.

Proven ability to work independently on complex language projects, exercising judgment and creativity in ambiguous or conflicting situations.

Strong working knowledge of platform-holder guidelines, naming conventions, and brand alignment principles.

Comfortable overseeing or mentoring junior linguists and freelance vendors.

Familiarity with localization tools (e.g., CAT tools, translation memory systems, XLOC, JIRA, CMS).

Deep understanding of Russian culture, media, and the Arabic-speaking gaming audience.

Experience with VO scripting, dubbing production, and linguistic performance direction is a plus.

Bachelor s degree in translation, linguistics, media, or related field preferred.

Core Competencies

Behavioral

A profound, innate understanding of the target market s cultural zeitgeist, allowing the individual to naturally flag sensitivities and adapt content for maximum regional resonance.

The tact and authoritative presence required to successfully act as the main escalation point for complex linguistic disputes, resolving QA challenges and tone disagreements while maintaining strong team relationships.

A highly detail-oriented mindset essential for successfully auditing in-development builds, spotting minute tone inconsistencies, and catching nuanced visual or linguistic compliance errors.

Technical

Exceptional proficiency in reviewing, editing, and polishing highly complex, multi-format content (UI, scripts, marketing) while strictly enforcing comprehensive glossaries and style guides.

Specialized technical and creative expertise required to successfully lead VO casting, conduct rigorous script reviews, and directly manage live creative sessions during voiceover production.

The technical capability to actively troubleshoot and advise on UI rendering, specifically regarding the selection and implementation of legible, aesthetically suitable fonts for the target language..

Leadership

The absolute accountability and leadership confidence to own the overarching localization strategy, quality bar, and final sign-off for all assigned titles.

A strong dedication to elevating the regional team by actively supervising, coaching, and providing actionable feedback to entry- and mid-level linguists and external vendors.

The ability to seamlessly lead collaborative efforts across diverse disciplines (LQA, Production, Marketing, Dev), ensuring all stakeholders are aligned on language decisions and bug triage.

Strategic Influence/Business Acumen

The strategic foresight to act as a trusted advisor on cultural risk mitigation, effectively communicating the business and brand impact of regional sensitivities to senior stakeholders.

Strong business understanding of regional legal and regulatory frameworks, ensuring all localized content strictly adheres to required age ratings and visual/language compliance standards.

A forward-looking, strategic mindset focused on actively contributing to global initiatives (like Global Loc 2.0), driving systemic improvements that reduce error rates and optimize turnaround times across the department.
