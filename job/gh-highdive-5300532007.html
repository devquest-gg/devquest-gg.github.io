Senior Animator - IGC/Gameplay

Highdive · Toronto

Senior Animation Posted 2026-11-18 Full time
Apply on Highdive’s site →

Join us on this journey to collaborate on and help bring Blood Message to life. It is a linear, story-driven game set in historical China, featuring a realistic visual aesthetic and a grounded animation style.

We are a co-development video game studio based in Toronto and Montreal and collaborating closely with our project headquarters in China.

This highly adaptive role focuses on helping us deliver high-quality Scripted Event content at the intersection of cinematic and gameplay systems. This includes interactive cutscenes, unique gameplay moments, and ambient animations that support Level and Narrative Design.

Responsibilities

Create high quality realistic, scripted animations to support minute to minute narrative and gameplay experience.

Combine Motion Capture data with keyframing in a seamless fashion without compromising original body mechanics and believability.

Help design and plan Scripted Events with efficient Prototypes/Previsualization iteration.

Implement, Test and Debug Animations in game engine in an organised manner following necessary conventions.

Collaborate with different departments and effectively understand needs and dependencies.

Ability to convey your ideas and feedback in a constructive and respectful manner.

Using Ai tools and workflows to help augment and increase efficiency if required.

Qualifications:

5 years professional animation experience working in video games.

Minimum of 1 shipped AAA game as a mid-level or Senior Animator.

Demonstrate in your demo reel strong animation principles with examples of realistic Body mechanics, Camera work and Facial animation.

Proficient in Motion builder/Maya pipelines and animation workflows.

Experience with game engine animation workflows and pipelines. (Unreal proficiency ideal), General technical knowledge of animation technologies, including state machines, blend trees, and motion warping.

Highly motivated, proactive, and solution-oriented, with a "can-do" attitude

Demonstrated ability to prioritize tasks, manage shifting priorities, and keep stakeholders informed.

Bonus Skills:

General Knowledge in cinematography and camera language.

Experience in Quadruped animation especially Horses.

Experience developing animation tools is a plus.

Autonomy in researching, studying and staying UpToDate with latest developments in game animation and Ai workflows is greatly valued.

Salary range: CAD 100,000 - 125,000

Why could we be the perfect place for you:

We offer the unique experience of being part of the foundation of a new international game team which benefits from the rich diversity of its members and the cultural exchange with our partners in Hangzhou.

We also offer:

- Insurance coverage and telemedicine from day one,

- Transportation and meal allowance,

- Referral bonus,

- RRSP with employer contribution.

Join us to work on an ambitious project, in a unique ‘Start-Up Atmosphere’ and with a team of passionate and energetic game developers!
