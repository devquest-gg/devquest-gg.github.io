Who We Are

Founded in 2005, 2K Games is a global video game company, publishing titles developed by some of the most influential game development studios in the world. Our studios responsible for developing 2K’s portfolio of world-class games across multiple platforms, include Visual Concepts, Firaxis, Hangar 13, CatDaddy, Cloud Chamber, and HB Studios. Our portfolio of titles is expanding due to our global strategic plan, building and acquiring exciting studios whose content continues to inspire all of us! 2K publishes titles in today’s most popular gaming genres, including sports, shooters, action, role-playing, strategy, casual, and family entertainment.

Our team of engineers, marketers, artists, writers, data scientists, producers, thinkers and doers, are the professional publishing stewards of our growing library of critically-acclaimed franchises such as NBA 2K, Battleborn, BioShock, Borderlands, The Darkness, Mafia, Sid Meier’s Civilization, WWE 2K, and XCOM.

At 2K, we pride ourselves on creating an inclusive work environment, which means encouraging our teams to Come as You Are and do your best work! We are dedicated to diversity and inclusion, and want our community of candidates to reflect this commitment. We encourage all qualified applicants to explore our global positions.

2K is headquartered in Novato, California and is a wholly owned label of Take-Two Interactive Software, Inc. (NASDAQ: TTWO). This role will be based in Novato or Los Angeles.

What We Need

We re looking for a Director of Marketing Operations to lead the operational structure and strategy that supports our marketing and player needs as we prepare for launch and live service.

This is a chance to build the operating foundation for marketing a new AAA live-service game at 2K. In partnership with the Head of Marketing and the Head of Operations, you ll define the publishing-level operating model for live-service marketing, including planning cadence, decision rights, readiness standards and how results shape future plans. Marketing owns what we do and why, and this role owns how it gets planned and delivered. The role reports to the Head of Operations, is dedicated to supporting Marketing, and leads the definition and execution of those processes, working closely with Brand, Creative, Media, Community, Partnerships and central 2K Marketing. It also directly manages the Marketing Operations team and helps standardize how Marketing plans and operates across the BU.

What You Will Do

Partner with the Head of Marketing and the Head of Operations to define the live-service marketing operating model: planning cadence, decision rights, readiness standards and the feedback loop from results to future plans

Build Marketing s readiness plan for launch and live service, covering the capabilities, processes, staffing and partners we ll need, and sequence the work to get there

Lead time-bound, high-ambiguity player first projects end to end across Studio, Publishing, and/or centralized 2K functions (Legal, Finance, Localization, Insights, Ratings, Core Tech, SDS, partnerships, etc.), building the plan, RACI, and workback schedule as needed

Act as a trusted advisor and operations partner to BU leadership, surfacing risks early and facilitating alignment across competing priorities.

Stand up and run marketing governance, including campaign greenlights, readiness reviews and marketing business reviews. Drive the agendas, materials and follow-through through the Marketing Ops team to ensure decisions turn into finished work

Build the marketing and live-service calendar and planning infrastructure, including workbacks, milestones and readiness criteria

With the Marketing Ops team, capture marketing decisions and action items, assign clear owners and track them to closure, escalating stalled items before they become risks

Represent Marketing s plans and needs in the BU s cross-functional workstreams, partnering with Business Operations on shared milestones and dependencies

Set up how Brand, Creative, Media, Community, Partnerships and central 2K Marketing plan and work together, including clear handoffs and ownership

Define Marketing s approval and requirements needs with central 2K functions (Legal, Finance, Localization, Insights, Ratings) and platform partners, and partner with Operation Services on the system that supports them at live-service scale

Build the marketing budget, forecasting, CAPS and resourcing model, and raise tradeoffs early

Shape the agency and vendor strategy, including roster, selection, expectations and performance management (Operation Services handles onboarding and procurement admin)

Build the plan and approach for delivering external events for players, creators, press, platforms and industry partners, etc.

Build the trackers and reporting that give Marketing leadership real-time visibility into plans, readiness and spend

Manage and coach the Marketing Operations team, and codify playbooks and best practices that can be reused as we move into live service

What Will Believe Will Make a Great Fit

7–10+ years in business operations, project/program management, management consulting, or related roles, including a minimum of 4 years managing people through recurring operating cadences and cross-functional programs.

Demonstrated experience owning executive-level governance and planning cadences (steering committees, business reviews) end to end, including agenda-setting and follow-through.

Track record leading ambiguous, undefined problems to structured outcomes, including scoping, staffing, and sequencing initiatives with no existing playbook.

Experience partnering with both centralized service organizations and product/BU-aligned teams, including hybrid structures.

Comfortable operating in global, matrixed, high-ambiguity environments; gaming, entertainment, or media experience a plus but not required.

Excellent communication and stakeholder management skills, with the judgment to know what to escalate and what to resolve independently.

Strong systems-thinking and organizational skills; able to bring clarity and structure to complex, cross-functional planning and operations.

Experience managing people and some exposure to budget and resource planning.

Bonus

Knowledge of Google suite applications (slides, sheets, docs, etc), Miro, Confluence.

Prior experience in a management consulting, corporate strategy, or PMO role.

Familiarity with live-service game production, publishing, or marketing operations.

Strong PC and Mac general computing skills

Excited about gaming, understand game terms, and be up-to-date with what is happening in gaming.
