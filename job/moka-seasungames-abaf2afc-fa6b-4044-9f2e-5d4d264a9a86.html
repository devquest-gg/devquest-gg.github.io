Game Narrative Designer - Mecha BREAK

Seasun Games · Guangdong

Mid Design Posted 2025-03-24 Full time
Apply on Seasun Games’s site →

Responsibilities: 1. Design and develop in-depth game narratives, contributing to all story-related content.

2. Create emotionally compelling stories and characters aligned with the game's world, crafting deep, engaging, and immersive scripts.

3. Oversee the implementation of narratives in the game, including coordination with artists on character design, cutscenes, and voice acting.

4. Package and adapt IP content for in-game use, including scene descriptions, item lore, and other textual elements.

Requirements: 1. Strong writing skills with experience in game storytelling or screenwriting, and a keen eye for quality content.

2. Passionate about games with broad knowledge, particularly in sci-fi, mecha, war, and anime-related works; aware of current pop culture trends.

3. Strong logical thinking and creativity, able to clearly articulate narrative concepts.

4. Team player with a curious and analytical mindset.

Bonus Points: 1. Experience in complete narrative design projects, showcasing skills in storytelling and character development.

2. Understanding of industry trends and the connection between narrative design and player experience, enhancing game immersion.

3. Strong cross-department communication skills to collaborate effectively with art, sound, and other teams.

Application Requirements: 1. Submit personal game narrative-related works, including but not limited to scripts, worldbuilding documents, or character designs.

2. For team projects, please describe your specific contributions.
