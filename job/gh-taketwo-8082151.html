The Challenge

We are seeking a mid-level Systems Engineer II with a focus on platform and monitoring systems to support and optimize our enterprise data pipeline and monitoring infrastructure. In this role, you will be a key administrator for a powerful suite of tools—Splunk, Cribl, Datadog, and Grafana—ensuring data flows efficiently and users have the access they need.

What You’ll Take On

Platform User Management: Act as a primary administrator for our monitoring ecosystem, handling user onboarding, access controls, permissions, and group management across Splunk, Datadog, Cribl, and Grafana.

Data Pipeline Configuration: Use Cribl and other tools to stream, route, and filter log data. When necessary, apply JavaScript to transform and reshape data payloads as they move through the pipeline.

Querying Dashboarding: Write SQL and platform-specific query languages to build reliable alerts, monitor data integrity, and create impactful dashboards.

System Administration: Use the Linux command line (CLI) to troubleshoot agents, manage configurations, and handle day-to-day platform maintenance.

Cross-Functional Support: Partner with internal teams to ensure their logging and metrics needs are met, assisting them with onboarding data sources and troubleshooting platform issues.

What You Bring

3+ years of experience administering enterprise software platforms, monitoring tools, or data systems.

Solid, hands-on experience with at least two of our primary platforms: Splunk, Cribl, Datadog, or Grafana.

Comfortable navigating the command line (CLI), with SQL proficiency and a working knowledge of using scripting languages for data manipulation.

Experience managing user roles, permissions (RBAC), and access policies within SaaS or enterprise environments.
