We’re on a mission to connect a billion people with optimism and civility, and looking for amazing talent to help us get there.

A career at Roblox means you’ll be working to shape the future of human interaction, solving unique technical challenges at scale, and helping to create safer, more civil shared experiences for everyone.

WHY SAFETY?

At Roblox, we strive to connect a billion people with optimism and civility, and the Safety organization’s mission is to become the leader in civil immersive online communities. We systematically detect, remove, and prevent problematic accounts, content and behavior, and we make Roblox accounts secure and free from compromise. We cover a broad area of the tech spectrum, including machine learning, classifiers for 3D models, experimentation, automation, detection workflows, and AI-powered text filters. Aligned and partnering with product teams, we use this toolbelt to discover new opportunities, influence and shape the product roadmap and prioritization, build safety products, and measure the impact on our community of users and developers. In doing so, we keep Roblox safe, civil, and inclusive, and we foster positive relationships between people around the world.

WHY ACCESS CONTROLS?

Roblox operates globally across a huge number of jurisdictions, each with their own laws and regulations. Every user’s platform experience is tailored based on their age, location, parental consents, and more to ensure that Roblox is safe and compliant for them. The Access Controls pod owns the systems that make this possible. Our platforms allow internal teams to set feature access rules based on compliance requirements and let users customize their platform experience within those boundaries.

Our services are some of the highest-traffic systems at the company, sitting in the hot path of nearly every user action across billions of accounts and millions of requests per second. As international regulations evolve and new features are built, our systems are how Roblox enforces, manages, and gives visibility into consistent behavior across dozens of features.

You Will:

Design, build, and operate high-throughput, low-latency services that shape what every Roblox user can see and do. The pod owns the full stack end-to-end, from gRPC services to caching, data pipelines, and client integrations.

Help set the technical direction for how feature access and user settings work across Roblox, evolving our systems as the platform and the regulatory landscape grow.

Partner directly with feature teams across Roblox to onboard them onto our platforms and turn evolving regulatory requirements into consistent product behavior. Past examples of this team’s contributions include:

Policy Deployment: Access rules used to ship as dynamic configuration, with no visibility into what was running in production and no way to revert without a code change. The team replaced that with a purpose-built deployment system with a custom UI, partial rollouts, shadow traffic, and one-click rollback. Policy deployment went from a source of anxiety to a routine, low-risk operation for the dozens of feature teams that depend on it daily.

Age-Checked Comms: In late 2025, Roblox introduced platform-wide Age Checks for all Communication features, including restrictions that only allow users to communicate with others in similar age ranges. Our access platform was the backbone of this rollout, giving every feature one centralized place to answer: Is this user Age Checked? Are they eligible to Age Check? Can these two users communicate?

Own the reliability, performance, and observability of systems that nearly every feature at Roblox depends on.

Mentor other engineers and add to the team’s culture of knowledge sharing, project retros, and bottoms-up technical decision-making.

You Have:

5+ years of industry experience building and operating backend or distributed systems at scale.

Strong fundamentals in service design, caching, data modeling, and performance. You know how to build systems that stay fast and correct under millions of requests per second.

A track record of leading projects from design through launch, including projects that span multiple teams or customers.

Bonus: Experience with authorization or access control systems, policy engines, configuration or feature-flag platforms, or regulatory compliance (e.g., age assurance, privacy, parental controls).

Educational Background: BS, MS, or PhD in Computer Science or a related technical field, or equivalent practical experience.

You Are:

Someone Who Thrives in Ambiguity: You take full ownership of problems that don’t come with a clear spec. When requirements are shifting or incomplete, you define the problem, make the call, and drive it to an outcome.

Innovative: You spot friction in existing processes, look for simple abstractions that remove a whole class of work, and turn your ideas into prototypes that shape where the team goes next.

Reliability-Focused: You understand what it means to own hot-path infrastructure. You make changes safe through rollouts, validation, and fast rollback.

Collaborative: You enjoy working across teams and product areas, and you’re comfortable translating ambiguous policy or regulatory requirements into concrete technical designs.

Mission Oriented: You care about keeping Roblox safe and compliant for users of all ages around the world.
