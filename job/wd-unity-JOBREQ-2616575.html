The opportunity Unity Spark is our browser-based game editor, with an ambitious vision for how real-time 3D games are created, shared, and played on the web. As a Senior Backend Engineer on the Spark team, you will design and run the cloud services behind the product: real-time multi-user collaboration, build and publishing pipelines, and the platform APIs the editor is built on.

Our team moves fast and uses AI coding tools every day. That shifts the work from typing code to deciding what to build, checking it is right, and owning it once it is live. We value generalists who pick up whatever the product needs next, whether that is a new service, a deploy pipeline, or a bug on the other side of the stack. You will work closely with engineers, designers, and product managers from day one, and own meaningful parts of the backend from first idea to production.

What you ll be doing Design, build, and operate the services and APIs behind real-time collaborative editing, game publishing, and content delivery, built to perform at scale Own features end to end, from a loosely defined problem to a design, shipped code, and a service running in production Weigh multiple approaches, make sensible trade-offs, and break complex problems into clear tasks while leading the design with your team Work across the stack when needed, including infrastructure as code, CI/CD, internal tooling, or the occasional frontend change Keep the platform reliable: anticipate failure modes, watch it, respond to production issues, and fix the cause rather than the symptom Use AI coding tools as a normal part of your work, and help the team learn where they help and where they don t Review code and designs, mentor engineers, and raise the team s engineering bar What we re looking for A track record building, operating, and owning backend services at scale in production, including monitoring, debugging, and fixing them when they break Strong proficiency in at least one modern backend language, and willingness to pick up others Deep knowledge of distributed systems, including API design, data persistence, data consistency, and state management Hands-on experience with cloud platforms and containerized, orchestrated deployments Experience with real-time or multiplayer systems, such as WebSockets, collaborative editing, or state synchronization Sound judgment that teammates trust, and effective written/verbal communication with technical and non-technical colleagues Comfort with ambiguity and with AI coding tools, reviewing what they produce as critically as a colleague s code You might also have Experience with the Unity Engine or other interactive 3D technology Experience building backend services for games or interactive media Additional information Relocation support is not available for this position.
