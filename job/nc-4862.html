Generative AI Creator

Nordcurrent · Vilnius

Mid Marketing Posted 2026-07-10 Full time
Apply on Nordcurrent’s site →

We re looking for a curious, creative, and AI-driven creator to help shape the future of advertising at Nordcurrent.

As a Generative AI Creator, you ll combine the latest AI technologies with creative storytelling to produce high-performing advertising content for millions of mobile game players worldwide. You ll experiment with cutting-edge AI tools, transform ideas into engaging videos, images, voices, and animations, and continuously explore new ways to make creative production faster, smarter, and more impactful.

If you re constantly testing new AI tools, following creative trends, and love turning ideas into content, we d love to hear from you.

You ll have the freedom to experiment with the newest AI technologies, influence how creative production evolves at Nordcurrent, and help build advertising content seen by millions of players around the world.

Your Key Responsibilities:

Create AI-powered performance marketing creatives for mobile games.

Generate videos, images, voiceovers, music, sound effects, and animations using generative AI tools.

Edit and polish AI-generated content into high-quality advertising assets.

Develop and optimize prompts to achieve the best creative results.

Produce multiple creative variations for rapid testing across Meta, TikTok, YouTube, Google, and other platforms.

Stay on the top of emerging AI technologies and recommend new tools and workflows.

Turn social media trends into engaging advertising concepts.

Work closely with User Acquisition, Marketing, Art and Creative teams to rapidly prototype and iterate on ideas.

Help build efficient AI-powered creative workflows across the team.

A Successful Candidate Must Have:

Passion for generative AI and digital creativity.

Experience with AI tools such as Veo, Runway, Kling, Midjourney, ChatGPT, Flux, ElevenLabs, HeyGen, Pika, Suno, Ideogram or similar tools.

Video editing skills using CapCut, Premiere Pro, DaVinci Resolve, Final Cut Pro or similar software.

Good understanding of visual storytelling, editing, pacing and sound.

Curiosity to experiment with new technologies and creative techniques.

Strong attention to detail and willingness to iterate quickly.

Interest in social media, advertising and mobile games.

Good English communication skills.

Extra Great to Have:

Experience creating performance marketing or social media creatives.

Basic motion graphics or animation skills.

Understanding of performance marketing metrics.

A portfolio featuring AI-assisted creative work.

Salary: From 2120 EUR/month (gross)
