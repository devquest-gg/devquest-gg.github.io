Technical Game Designer

Nordcurrent · Vilnius

Mid Design Posted 2022-12-07 Full time
Apply on Nordcurrent’s site →

As a member of the Development Team, you will create new game concepts and levels for world-class games such as Cooking Fever, Pocket Styler, Airplane Chefs, and new games which are currently in development. Your work will make an impact on the experience of millions of players around the world. Every day you will think creatively, learn new things, test original ideas, use your knowledge of various game types, collaborate with many professionals, and embrace your own growth and development. Your friends will be proud of you knowing, that you were the hero behind those games!

A Successful Candidate Must Have:

Knowledge of game structure planning;

Ability to use a user-centric approach to define game mechanics and loops;

Good writing skills: the ability to complete game-design documents with clarity and precision;

Ability to describe game structures and rules in a clear and concise way through documentation/presentation;

Fluency in English;

Ability to break down games in the systems they are composed of;

Good critical thinking and abstract reasoning.

Extra Great to Have:

Strong mathematical background;

Understanding of game-creation technologies;

Basic knowledge of programming/art;

Passion for the video game industry and knowledge of market trends and styles.

Salary:  From 2120 EUR/month (gross), based on experience and competence.
