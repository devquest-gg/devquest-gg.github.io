Senior Economy and Monetization Designer

Outfit7 · Ljubljana

Senior Design Posted 2026-05-11
Apply on Outfit7’s site →

Do you know how to make sure a game performs its best? Do you want to help create new games? We’re looking for a Monetization Designer to join the team and help us design, simulate, and balance new monetization-related features and events. With great knowledge and expertise in this area, as well as the ability to make data-driven decisions and work well as part of a team, you might just be our next hire! Read on to find out more.

In this role you will

Work in close cooperation with game designers and product managers to create the monetization, system, and economy design of new games.

Improve the monetization of existing Outfit7 games by optimizing their economy, IAP offers, ads, etc.

Analyze the work of competitors, as well as other top-performing games and games with interesting features.

Participate in knowledge-sharing activities within the department, including sharing the findings of any professional analysis and independent research

What we re looking for

Strong knowledge of IAP driven game monetization.

Good knowledge of system and economy design.

Good knowledge of economics and statistics.

Knowledge and understanding of game design fundamentals.

Knowledge of simulation approaches.

Clear understanding of what makes a good game.

Ability to make data-driven decisions.

Strong analytical thinking skills.

Positive, make-it-happen attitude.

Your experience

At least 5 years of experience as a Monetization Designer in the gaming industry.

Experience with various IAP game monetization approaches and best practices.

Familiarity with methods of data analytics and statistics is a plus.

What we offer

Employment relationship with Ekipa2 d.o.o. for an indefinite period of time, with a six-month probationary period.

Diverse work with exciting daily challenges, working as part of a team in a fun, relaxed environment.

Participation in attractive projects.

Development opportunities through training, seminars, and conferences.

Choice of sport, fitness, educational, and entertainment activities outside of work.

Healthy meals in the office.

Flexible working hours.

24 days of paid vacation.

Participation in a discretionary bonus plan.

Christmas bonus (subject to internal company policies).
