We’re looking for an experienced Project Manager to join our R&D team.

What are you working on?

- Genres: Puzzle, Casual

- Platforms: Mobile, iOS, Android

For which tasks (responsibilities)?

- Track delivery against release plans, both volume and deadlines

- Coordinate multiple development teams, producers, and adjacent departments

- Manage dependencies and surface blockers and risks before they affect timelines

- Allocate resources across teams and identify the need for new specialists and contractors

- Set up and evolve Jira: projects, workflows, boards, automations

- Build and run the process for setting up new apps

- Coordinate game publishing on the App Store and Google Play

- Improve processes and reduce manual work, including through AI tools

- Keep statuses, deadlines, and ownership transparent

- Take part in hiring developers and QA, and run 1:1s

What kind of professional are we looking for?

- 5+ years as a Project Manager or in a similar role in mobile game development

- Experience managing several development teams at the same time

- Experience in a fast-launch environment: R&D, prototyping, or parallel development of multiple games (10+ new releases per month)

- Understanding of the full mobile development cycle, from task setting and build assembly to publishing on the App Store and Google Play

- Strong Jira skills: setting up projects, workflows, boards, and automations

- Experience building processes between teams and adjacent functions such as producers, analytics, and monetization

- Experience managing resources and timelines: identifying blockers, reallocating resources, bringing in outsourcing

- Experience in hiring and team management: 1:1s, feedback

Nice to have

- Technical background, e.g. Unity development experience

- Your own network of outsourcing contacts

- Experience automating processes with AI tools

❤️‍🩹 100% Sick Leave compensation
