What We Need:

The Global Strategic Company Communications team at 2K oversees all company related communications across 2K’s Publishing and Studio organizations including Business Communications, Internal Communications, Executive Communications + Thought Leadership, People + HR related Communications, internal Change Management, Crisis Issues Management, Social Impact and Global Content creation. The Associate Manager of Global Strategic Company Communications will support all communications with a focus on People + HR related internal communications to our employee base across 2K Publishing and Studios, Change Management, Shared Development Studio and Technology team communications, Strategic Operations, etc.

Who You Are:

You have a passion for driving communications excellence, strategic operations, creativity, and relationships + team building to deliver more efficient and effective ways of working. You appreciate the vital role this work plays in building a deeper sense of culture and pride at 2K. You are interested in honing your communication skills and learning about new ways to optimize your performance. You are a self-starter, critical thinker, resourceful by nature and driven to optimize teams. You live and breathe the 2K culture and are a relationship builder. You are a team player who operates with heart and head

You have a service-oriented mindset and are capable of driving projects to completion. This role requires an ability to be agile, pivot quickly and prioritize daily, while remaining on-task for long-term projects. The ideal candidate has some experience working cross functionally within a matrixed global organization and a demonstrated ability to support projects through to fruition.

Key Skills and Experience:
