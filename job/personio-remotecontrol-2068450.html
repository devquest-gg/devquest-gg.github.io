We make the business of games work for the people who make them.

rcp is an independent production house from Munich, around since 2005. Part dealmaker, part production house, part backer. We sit on the business side of the table for a family of 15 partner studios across Europe, South America and South Asia, so they can spend their time making games. When our studios win, we win.

What that looks like day to day: We sell our studios’ games and teams to publishers and platforms We structure deals, funding and investment We broker co-development, porting and work-for-hire We build serious games for finance, health and defense More than 300 games and interactive projects so far, from Songs of Silence and Bus Simulator to Angry Birds Epic and Ostwind.

Where we’re going We’re building something the games industry doesn’t have yet: a 'United Artists' for game makers. Studios that own their work, share the upside and back each other.

rcp has a strong reputation and a weak brand. For you, that’s room: you get to put your own stamp on it.

How we work Roles, not positions.

Inspired by holacracy. One person often holds several roles, and roles change as the work changes Strikeforces.

Small cross-functional teams around a partner studio or a mission, with clear accountability for each role Radical candor.

We care personally and challenge directly. You get honest feedback, and we expect you to give it, including to the CEO AI-second, human-first.

Coordination and admin go to AI, so people spend their time on judgment, relationships and deals Remote (Europe or North America, on European hours) or Munich · Full-time · Exclusive freelance start possible, then permanent The short version We need a dealmaker who opens doors at the top table and brings the close home. You take over the relationships our CEO built over 20 years, and you build your own. No team to manage. A direct line to the CEO.

Why this role exists Our CEO built rcp’s top relationships over 20 years. We want someone to take them over, grow them, and become the face of rcp at the top table.

You open doors at Director and VP level. You bring opportunities that hold up. You bring the close home without giving terms away early. A door-opener with soft leads doesn’t help us. A door-opener who closes does.

This is a director-level role as an individual contributor. You don’t manage a team. You work directly with our CEO, our Biz team and the studio heads across the rcp family.

Open doors at the right level: Director and VP+ at large publishers and platforms, CEO level at smaller publishers, plus project financiers, games investors and fast-growing self-publishing developers Pitch and sell projects from the rcp family: original IP, co-development and work-for-hire Hold terms to the close.
