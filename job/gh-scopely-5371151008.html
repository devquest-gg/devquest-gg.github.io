What You’ll Do

Write, maintain, and execute test cases for studio games across mobile and web platforms, covering a broad range of devices, operating systems, and browsers.

Perform functional and non-functional testing of mobile applications, including network traffic, API responses, and log analysis.

Collaborate closely with Scrum teams, Producers, Developers, and QA Leads/Managers to ensure releases meet high quality standards.

Communicate proactively with Developers and business stakeholders during defect triage to reduce defect ageing and drive timely resolution.

Conduct root cause analysis for defects and provide corrective and preventive action plans.

Work effectively in an Agile environment to support timely and high-quality product delivery.

Identify quality trade-offs, project risks, and appropriate mitigation strategies.

Develop a detailed understanding of studio games, including gameplay flows, player journeys, edge cases, and game-specific scenarios.

Create and maintain QA documentation, including test cases, test plans, test reports, training material, and process documentation.

Mentor junior QA team members and support their technical and domain growth.

Partner with the QA Lead to continuously improve QA processes, test coverage, and release quality.

Support team coordination and delivery in the absence of the QA Lead.

What We re Looking For

4–7 years of experience in software testing, preferably across mobile, web, or gaming products.

Strong familiarity with mobile and web platforms from a QA perspective.

Good understanding of client-server architecture, APIs, application data flow, and network debugging.

Hands-on experience in test design, test execution, defect reporting, regression testing, and risk-based testing.

Strong exploratory testing skills, with the ability to select the right test approach based on feature complexity and risk.

Experience using QA tools such as Jira, TestRail, Charles Proxy, Fiddler, browser developer tools, or similar tools.

Ability to create clear and comprehensive QA documentation, including test cases, test plans, test reports, and training material.

Good understanding of software testing processes, SDLC/STLC, and Agile methodologies.

Basic knowledge of SQL and database concepts.

Strong communication, analytical thinking, and problem-solving skills.

Bonus Points

Experience testing mobile games, web games, or live-service products.

Passion for gaming, with a genuine interest in playing and understanding casual games.

Strong gaming mindset, including an understanding of player behaviour, engagement mechanics, progression, rewards, and LiveOps.
