Senior Asset & Portfolio Manager

Riot Games · Los Angeles

Senior Production $119K–$167K Posted 2026-11-19 Full time
Apply on Riot Games’s site →

The Senior Asset Portfolio Manager reporting to Director, Real Estate, stewards the performance, flexibility, and long-term value of Riot’s real estate portfolio. This role combines owned-asset management, subleased-property oversight, portfolio planning, lease-event coordination, capital and operating forecasting, and decision support.

The role turns property, occupancy, market, operational, and financial information into clear portfolio choices. It maintains a reliable view of asset health and obligations, identifies risks and opportunities early, and develops practical recommendations across reinvestment, right-sizing, renewal, sublease, disposition, redevelopment, and other portfolio actions.

Core responsibilities

Owned Asset Management

Establish and maintain a Plan of Record for Riot-owned properties, including asset condition, useful life, warranties, building systems, deferred maintenance, capital needs, operating costs, risks, and strategic options.

Build a recurring asset-health and performance view that connects physical condition, operating performance, occupancy, market position, total cost of ownership, and long-term value.

Develop lifecycle asset plans and capital reinvestment recommendations, including prioritization, sequencing, rough-order cost, timing, dependencies, and risk.

Coordinate with Facilities, Property Management, IFM partners, Engineering, Insurance, Legal, Treasury, Accounting, Finance, and external advisors to maintain a complete owned-asset view.

Track building rules, regulatory and insurance requirements, easements, warranties, infrastructure dependencies, and other ownership obligations.

Identify and evaluate hold, sell, sublease, redevelopment, repositioning, or other strategic options for owned properties and components of owned sites.

Prepare quarterly asset snapshots, executive summaries, risk registers, and decision materials for owned properties.

Translate technical, market, operational, and financial inputs into clear recommendations for leadership review.

Subleased Property Management

Maintain a complete view of Riot’s subleased properties, including lease terms, critical dates, tenant status, rent and operating-cost obligations, security deposits, landlord requirements, and remaining exposure.

Manage the sublease pipeline from market assessment and tenant outreach through diligence, business-case development, approvals, documentation, execution, and handoff.

Partner with brokers, landlords, Legal, Finance, Accounting, Treasury, Property Management, and internal business stakeholders to protect Riot’s economics and flexibility.

Evaluate sublease, assignment, contraction, early termination, renewal, right-sizing, or in-place alternatives as business needs and market conditions change.

Monitor tenant performance, landlord coordination, building obligations, maintenance responsibilities, access requirements, and operational risks associated with subleased space.

Maintain decision-ready views of sublease economics, market conditions, timing, risks, dependencies, and approval requirements.

Ensure sublease activities remain connected to the broader portfolio strategy rather than being managed as isolated transactions.

Portfolio Planning and Strategy

Maintain an integrated view of Riot’s owned, leased, and subleased portfolio, including footprint, occupancy, headcount, utilization, cost, lease events, capital needs, and strategic risks.

Support long-range portfolio planning and annual planning by translating workforce plans, business growth, ways of working, market conditions, and operating requirements into portfolio scenarios.

Develop stay, go, renew, relocate, right-size, consolidate, sublease, dispose, acquire, and redevelop scenarios with clear assumptions and trade-offs.

Evaluate lease-versus-own considerations, portfolio concentration, flexibility, total cost of ownership, operational resilience, employee experience, and long-term value.

Identify upcoming lease events and portfolio decision gates early; establish action plans that preserve negotiation leverage and optionality.

Partner with the Director and regional leaders to prioritize portfolio initiatives, investment needs, and decision timing.

Provide portfolio inputs to workplace strategy, capital planning, business cases, and operating-model decisions across the Americas and, where appropriate, global planning forums.

Forecasting, Budgeting, and Investment Planning

Own the real estate inputs to annual planning, quarterly forecasting, and multi-year portfolio outlooks.

Develop and maintain CapEx and OpEx forecasts for owned assets, subleased properties, material lease events, portfolio initiatives, and asset-management programs.

Track forecast-to-actual performance, commitments, burn rate, timing shifts, and emerging cost or funding risks.

Build scenario models that show the cost, timing, operational, and portfolio implications of major decisions.

Partner with Finance and Accounting to validate assumptions, affordability, funding path, cash flow, P L implications, accounting treatment, and financial risk.

Maintain a clear distinction between CRE-owned real estate assumptions and Finance-owned financial validation.

Recommend investment sequencing based on asset condition, risk, business need, regulatory or insurance requirements, value preservation, and available capacity.

Prepare budget narratives and investment recommendations that make portfolio trade-offs understandable to senior stakeholders.

Data, Governance, and Reporting

Establish reliable data sources and definitions for assets, leases, occupancy, utilization, costs, capital projects, operating performance, tenant activity, and portfolio risks.

Maintain portfolio dashboards, lease-event trackers, asset registers, risk registers, decision logs, assumptions, and action registers.

Create consistent reporting rhythms for asset reviews, portfolio reviews, forecast updates, and executive decision forums.

Improve data quality by reconciling information across lease administration, property management, Finance, Accounting, IFM, brokers, advisors, and internal systems.

Use data and stakeholder feedback to identify cost, service, risk, flexibility, and employee-experience opportunities.

Establish repeatable templates, playbooks, and governance practices that make portfolio and asset decisions easier to manage across regions.

Partner and Stakeholder Leadership

Serve as a trusted real estate partner to the Director, regional workplace leaders, Workplace Experience Partners, Finance, Legal, Procurement, IT, Security, EHS, HR, and business stakeholders.

Lead working relationships with brokers, property managers, IFM providers, consultants, engineers, appraisers, and other external advisors.

Define partner expectations, deliverables, decision rights, service levels, and escalation paths.

Protect Riot’s decision authority while using external partners for market intelligence, technical expertise, transaction support, benchmarking, and execution capacity.

Pre-wire recommendations with the right stakeholders and escalate material risks early, with a recommended path forward.

Translate global portfolio standards into practical regional execution while accounting for market, regulatory, cultural, and business differences.

Required Qualifications

7+ years of progressive experience in corporate real estate, asset management, portfolio planning, facilities, workplace strategy, finance operations, or a related field.

Experience managing owned assets, leased or subleased properties, or a multi-site corporate real estate portfolio.

Strong understanding of asset lifecycle planning, capital planning, lease events, sublease strategy, portfolio optimization, total cost of ownership, and real estate risk.

Demonstrated experience building budgets, forecasts, scenario models, business cases, or investment recommendations.

Ability to integrate property, market, occupancy, operational, and financial information into clear options and recommendations.

Strong working knowledge of lease administration, property-management relationships, transaction processes, and real estate documentation.

Ability to manage multiple workstreams, decision gates, dependencies, risks, and external partners with disciplined follow-through.

Strong written, verbal, presentation, and stakeholder-management skills, including the ability to communicate clearly with senior leaders.

Sound judgment with confidential, financially sensitive, and organizationally sensitive information.

Ability to influence across functions and regions without relying on formal authority.

Preferred Qualifications

Experience with owned corporate campuses, headquarters, mixed-use properties, or complex operating environments.

Experience with sublease, assignment, disposition, redevelopment, or landlord/tenant strategy.

Experience with portfolio, lease administration, capital project, property-management, financial, or asset-management systems.

Advanced capability with Excel and experience with Power BI, Tableau, or comparable analytics and visualization tools.

Experience partnering with Finance, Accounting, Treasury, Insurance, Legal, Property Management, IFM providers, and external advisors.

Professional certification in real estate, asset management, facilities management, finance, or project management.

Experience in technology, gaming, entertainment, sports, or another fast-paced multinational environment.
