Aspyr is a leading game publisher and developer, bringing the world s biggest franchises and bold new projects to players everywhere.

Since 1996, we’ve published over 200 games and our team is dedicated to bringing beloved franchises like Tomb Raider and Star Wars to new and familiar audiences across all platforms.

We focus on collaboration and creativity, crafting high-quality, engaging games that leave a lasting impact on players worldwide.

Responsibilities:

Reporting to the Head of Engineering, the Engineering Manager - Technical Partner Management is responsible for leading the team of Technical Leads, guiding the people and processes that ensure external partners deliver technical excellence. This role is the technical "bridge" between external studios and Aspyr s internal Production and QA teams, with a primary mission to verify artifacts, architecture, and planning, operating under the principle of "Trust but Verify".

Essential Functions:

Lead, mentor, and grow a team of Technical Leads, coaching them on how to effectively oversee external development without losing sight of technical rigor

Contribute, teach, and model best behavior on how to effectively audit third-party codebases, artifacts, and technical roadmaps

Conduct regular internal 1:1s to provide tactical advice on navigating complex partner dynamics and career growth

Partner with Production and QA to identify risks early and deliver firm truths to partners when quality isn t met, ensuring the relationship remains intact and collaborative

Review partner architectural plans and milestones to proactively identify critical risks and underlying technical debt that could derail a project months down the line.

Establish technical review cadences with external partners, including code reviews, architecture reviews, build reviews, risk reviews, and milestone readiness assessments

Own the technical partner management function for external engineering partners, ensuring partner work meets Aspyr’s standards for architecture, code quality, build stability, platform compliance, and milestone readiness

Work closely with the Head of Engineering to align partner technical strategies with Aspyr’s internal standards

Collaborate with Production to ensure technical milestones are realistic and with QA to ensure that "quality" is a measurable metric

Preferred Qualifications:

3+ years of experience managing, mentoring, and growing engineers, with a proven track record of developing their technical capabilities as well as their soft skills (communication, conflict resolution, and relationship management)

8+ years of hands-on professional experience in software engineering with a strong focus on coding, rigorous code reviews, and software architecture within the games industry

Bachelor s degree in computer science, related field, or equivalent hands-on professional experience

Strong, professional C++ programming experience to effectively audit codebases and guide technical conversations

Source Control Proficiency: Robust experience with modern source control systems (e.g., Perforce, Git) is required, including a deep understanding of branching, merging, and build pipelines

Exceptional ability to translate complex technical risks into actionable, direct feedback for partners and internal stakeholders

Experience working directly with video game publishers or managing first-party platform relationships

Production experience using Scrum, Agile, or other modern project management methodologies

Experience in a "Co-Dev" or "External Development" environment

Background in shipping high-profile ports, remasters, or multi-platform titles

Strong understanding of first-party platform dynamics, guidelines, submission processes (e.g., Sony, Microsoft, Nintendo)
