Product Manager

MobilityWare · Irvine

Mid Production $100K–$130K Posted 2026-07-02
Apply on MobilityWare’s site →

This is an exciting opportunity to work across MobilityWare s portfolio of beloved mobile games, supporting titles enjoyed by millions of players around the world. As a Product Manager, you ll help drive the development of our flagship card games while gaining hands-on experience across the full product development lifecycle. You ll contribute to product strategy and roadmap planning, leverage data to inform decisions, and develop deep expertise in the competitive mobile gaming landscape.

The ideal candidate is curious, data-driven, and passionate about mobile games. You thrive in a collaborative environment, take ownership of your work and professional growth, and are motivated to create player-first experiences informed by data, player feedback, and market insights.

Responsibilities:

Support Product Owners in sprint reviews, planning, and design backlog, and agile workflows.

Develop a deep understanding of player mindset and motivations and learn to connect design thinking to key performance indicators, business value, and feature success metrics.

Contribute to future roadmap ideation by tracking feature status, documenting Expected Outcomes tied to KPIs, and synthesizing player and stakeholder feedback.

Lead comprehensive market and competitive research across the casual mobile gaming landscape, identifying trends and player behavior patterns that inform product decisions and roadmap priorities.

Partner with the Data Analytics team to develop A/B test ideas, define success metrics and KPIs, and begin interpreting test results with guidance.

Support feature design and specification by applying player insight and business goal alignment to product requirements.

Prepare and deliver internal presentations with clear data and insights, building confidence in communicating ideas across teams.

Build strong cross functional relationships with Design, Engineering, and Analytics to support feature delivery and milestone planning.

Cultivate a positive and supportive work environment.

Qualifications:

2+ years of experience in product management, game development, or a related field.

Genuine passion for gaming and a solid understanding of current mobile marketplace offerings and competitors.

Curiosity and orientation toward data; comfortable reading dashboards, core KPIs, and learning how to derive actionable insights.

Strong written and verbal communication skills; able to collaborate across disciplines and levels of the organization.

Utilize AI to analyze data, market trends and feature analysis.

Positive, growth-oriented mindset with a desire to develop into a Product Owner.

(Nice to have) Experience in the Free to Play mobile gaming space.

A Little Bit About Us:

MobilityWare is one of the leading mobile game publishers of card and puzzle games. With our headquarters in the heart of Orange County and a growing studio in Utah, we’re dedicated to our mission of bringing joy to others one game at a time. Ever since our flagship title Solitaire hit the Apple App Store on the day it opened in 2008, MobilityWare has been entertaining gamers around the world. With a product portfolio that includes beloved hits such as FreeCell, Spades, Hearts, Mahjong, and Bubble Shooter Pop, our games have been downloaded over 600 MILLION times.

MobilityWare was recently named one of Built in LA’s Best Places to Work for the fifth year in a row and we’ve been recognized as one of the Best Places to Work in Orange County five times by the Orange County Business Journal and OC Register. While we’re proud to be the company behind some of mobile gaming’s most popular titles, our most important achievement is creating a work environment that encourages professional growth and prioritizes the wellness of all of our team members.
