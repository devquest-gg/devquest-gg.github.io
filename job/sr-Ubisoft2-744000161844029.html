Principal VFX Artist - March of Giants

Ubisoft Montreal · Montreal

Lead Art Posted 2026-11-16 Full time

As a Principal Visual Effects (VFX) Artist at Ubisoft Montréal, you will help define and develop the artistic and technical vision for the project’s effects. You will create effects that support gameplay, improve readability and strengthen player immersion.

You will serve as a reference for the discipline and guide production teams in creating quality effects. Working with the creative, art and technical directors, you will help maintain visual consistency and technical performance throughout development.

What you’ll do Help define and develop the artistic vision for VFX in line with the project’s creative goals Establish quality standards , production pipelines and best practices for the discipline Create and integrate VFX that serve as quality benchmarks for the team Collaborate with the art, creative and technical directors to maintain consistency across the project Balance visual quality , gameplay readability and real-time performance Oversee VFX integration , optimization and debugging in the game engine Support artists’ professional development , review their work and provide clear, constructive artistic guidance Identify VFX production risks and propose solutions to support project delivery goals Collaborate with external partners when needed and review their deliverables against established standards Explore new technologies , techniques and industry trends to improve the discipline’s quality and efficiency What you bring to the team Experience with Unreal Engine Demonstrated experience creating VFX for gameplay feedback Knowledge of Niagara systems , including Scratch Pad Modules , material, texture and mesh creation , and UV coordinate animation Skills in creating real-time VFX for video games or interactive media Proficiency in three-dimensional (3D) production workflows , including modeling, texturing, lighting or image processing Understanding of art fundamentals , including lighting, composition, proportion and color theory Ability to receive feedback and adapt your work accordingly Ability to collaborate across teams , clear communication skills and attention to detail What to send our way Your CV highlighting relevant skills and experiences Your portfolio or links to your VFX projects
