ABOUT VOODOO

Voodoo is a consumer tech company founded in Paris in 2013, with one mission: entertain the world. Today we are 1,000 people, generating $1b+ in annual revenue, profitably. Our games and apps have passed 7 billion downloads and reach 200 million+ active users every month, making Voodoo the #3 mobile publisher worldwide in downloads, after Google and Meta. We were backed by Goldman Sachs, Tencent and GBL along the way.

TEAM

Our Corporate teams support the company’s performance and growth whilst putting people at the core of everything they do. As a French tech leader and global successful company, our Corporate teams are constantly looking for ways to innovate and increase organization efficiency to keep Voodoo ahead of the market. Voodoo’s Corporate function is composed of three teams: HR, Legal and Finance, working together to drive Voodoo’s success.

ROLE

We're looking for an FP&A to join our Finance team. In this role, you'll act as the key financial partner to business leaders, driving performance analysis, forecasting, and data-driven decision-making across the organisation.

- Serve as the go-to financial partner for brand directors, production studios and commercial teams, tracking profitability per operation and supporting pricing and go/no-go decisions

- Own the monthly management close in coordination with accounting (cut-off, accruals, revenue recognition) and produce entity-, brand- and studio-level P&Ls with full budget vs. actuals variance analysis

- Build the annual budget and quarterly reforecasts with operational teams, and maintain the multi-year business plan and its scenarios

- Support the CFO in managing the 13-week and monthly cash flow forecast, monitoring working capital and customer collection KPIs

- Maintain and evolve the Group's dashboards and reporting for management, board and shareholders - from audience and monetisation metrics to margin, cash and unit economics

- Strengthen and automate the financial data chain (ERP, treasury tools, production tracking, platform data) and lead data and automation projects to reduce manual workloads

- Structure a financial data governance framework (definitions, data quality, single source of truth, access management) and enforce robust operational controls

- Contribute to the evolution of budgeting tools and processes (workflow, calendar, contributor governance) and lead the build-out of priority Finance BI dashboards

- Drive the deployment of AI agents to replace manual processes and improve the operational efficiency of the Finance team

- Contribute to M&A and integration projects (modelling, due diligence, synergies, 100-day plans) and ad hoc analyses requested by senior management

PROFILE

- 5 to 7+ years of experience in Finance, ideally combining a Big Four audit or Transaction Services background with hands-on FP&A, management control or Finance Data/Systems experience in a multi-entity, scale-up or high-growth environment

- Degree from a top business or engineering school, or a Master's in Finance / CCA / DSCG or equivalent

- Strong financial modelling skills with advanced proficiency in Excel and/or Google Sheets; comfortable with ERP systems (Odoo a plus), EPM tools and BI platforms (Looker, Tableau, Power BI or equivalent)

- Genuine interest in applying AI to Finance (no-code/low-code tools, agents, LLMs) and ability to structure a data governance approach at the function level

- Strong business acumen with the ability to challenge both operational teams and senior executives with clarity and conviction

- Proactive and autonomous - you seek out information, propose before being asked, and see your projects through to completion

- Fluent in French and English

- Knowledge of media, content, audiovisual production or digital advertising is a plus
