Privacy Project Coordinator

Gameloft · Bucharest

Senior Production Posted 2026-07-31
Apply on Gameloft’s site →

MAIN CHALLENGE Support the Privacy Product Owner in organizing and driving privacy initiatives by structuring information, coordinating operational follow-up across multiple projects, and helping ensure privacy requirements are well integrated into Gameloft's products and services.

WHAT YOU WILL BE WORKING ON Assist the Privacy Product Owner in reviewing privacy initiatives, gathering information, and structuring them into clear project plans.

Maintain structured documentation of requirements, action items, and decisions.

Coordinate operational follow-up with teams, ensuring tasks move forward and reporting back on progress.

Help prepare summaries, reports, and updates for the Privacy Product Owner and stakeholders.

Track timelines, dependencies, and risks across multiple initiatives to keep projects organized.

Provide ad hoc research, analysis, and documentation to support decision-making.

Assist in defining and reviewing testing approaches to verify privacy requirements have been implemented as specified.

Provide input on gaps, inefficiencies, or opportunities to improve how privacy is integrated into products and workflows.

WHAT YOU NEED TO SUCCEED Experience supporting cross-functional teams.

Strong analytical and problem-solving skills with the ability to grasp technical concepts and explain them in a simplified format.

Experience with project coordination, business analysis, or similar support functions.

Familiarity with privacy/data protection topics is a plus but not required; willingness to learn is essential.

WHO YOU ARE Proactive, detail-oriented, and organized with a collaborative mindset.

Demonstrates cross-functional thinking and the ability to connect the dots.

Who you would be working with Privacy Product Owner, stakeholders.
