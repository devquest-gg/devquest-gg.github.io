What it means on a daily basis Own end-to-end analysis: from querying raw data to delivering clear, actionable insights Build and optimize dashboards to track product and business performance in real time Identify growth opportunities across acquisition, retention, and monetization-&#xa0;and recommend data-driven actions Ensure data accuracy by investigating and resolving inconsistencies Partner closely with product and development teams to provide data-driven recommendations and drive impact Who you would be working with Cross-functional product and development teams on a daily basis An international team of data experts, sharing knowledge and best practices Your recruitment journey (1) Screening call (2) Test (3) Interview (4) Offer WHAT YOU NEED TO SUCCEED Proficient in SQL with experience working on large, complex datasets Practical experience with BI tools (Looker Studio, Tableau, Power BI, or similar) Solid experience in quantitative analysis (4–5 years) in tech or digital products Solid understanding of business metrics, especially monetization and performance tracking Ability to manage multiple projects in a rapid environment Fluent English communication (written and spoken) WHO YOU ARE Analytical and detail-oriented with effective problem-solving skills Consistently identifies opportunities for growth and executes improvement plans Effective communicator, able to present insights clearly to stakeholders Well-organized and able to prioritize effectively in a fast-paced environment Collaborative team player who can also take ownership of tasks Interested in video games WHAT'S IN IT FOR YOU Benefits A hybrid working model with 2 days working from home&#xa0;every week.
