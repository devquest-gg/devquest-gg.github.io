Join us and be part of the play! Position Overview:

Zynga is looking for a server-side developer in the Austin, Chicago, or Toronto areas, and more specifically, someone whose level of experience should be equivalent to a Senior Software Developer on the Wizard of Oz Slots.

We have a well-rounded and tight-knit, distributed team with a healthy mix of engineering skill levels, and are looking to strengthen our team.

We need a self-driven and motivated engineer who is a strong communicator, works well as a team player, and performs well under pressure of tight deadlines.

The candidate would have an opportunity to work on critical game components that contribute to user-facing features, gameplay functionality, existing functionality systems, and have a general focus on solving challenging problems for high profile titles.

What You ll Do:

Design, architect, and support back-end services running in AWS

Develop, integrate, and advocate for new technologies and tools

Load-test, benchmark and optimize infrastructure

Define, architect, and develop features and gameplay functionality in PHP

Maintain and develop CI/CD pipelines and infrastructure-as-code tooling

Provide escalation support and infrastructure monitoring

Follow engineering standard methodologies to ensure code quality, performance and reliability

Work to identify and help to improve team and process inefficiencies

Help to mentor other engineers

What You Bring:

Degree in a technical field (e.g., Computer Science, Computer Engineering) or equivalent experience

4+ Years of backend software development experience

Deep hands-on experience with AWS

Experience maintaining and developing CI/CD pipelines and infrastructure-as-code tooling

Experience with PHP or a similar programming language

Experience building and operating high-traffic, low-latency backend systems at scale

Strong familiarity and experience with database systems (SQL, NoSQL)

Solid foundation in data structures, algorithms and software methodologies

Strong communication skills and comfort working in a collaborative environment

Bonus Points:

Prior game development experience (on shipped titles or live games experience)

Experience with Chef, Terraform and/or Jenkins

Experience in Vue.js or similar frontend frameworks

Experience migrating monolithic architectures to distributed microservices
