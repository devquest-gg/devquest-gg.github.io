ABOUT SEGA SEGA is one of the leading interactive entertainment companies in the world, with our European HQ based in West London and development studios spanning the UK and Bulgaria. Known for quality and creativity above all else, our studios include Sports Interactive, Two Point Studios, Hardlight and Creative Assembly.

At the heart of SEGA is our publishing team based in London, where we help the studios bring their creative vision to life. Here we have a hardworking bunch of people spanning Tech, Sales, Marketing, Finance, QA and more.

Together, we drive the continued success and evolution of our Western business through living our company values of being product focused, embracing true partnerships and always having fun so that we can strive to come together as One SEGA.

The Role : As an IT Helpdesk Engineer, you will be at the forefront of technical support, ensuring employees across the studio receive prompt and effective assistance. You will contribute to a positive user experience, demonstrate initiative in resolving issues, and take ownership of problems through to completion. The role supports studio‑focused operations while working within central IT standards and processes.

Key Responsibilities: Monitoring and responding to incoming incident and service requests.

Providing technical support through multiple channels, including email, teams, service desk, and in-person.

Assisting with the onboarding and offboarding of user accounts and hardware.

Proactively monitor and maintain user devices and ensure they are compliant with company standards.

Create and maintain documentation for IT systems in our Atlassian Confluence instances for solutions and processes.

Support O365/Entra and other SaaS applications.

Installation and maintenance of a variety of hardware and software.

Help unblock recurring issues by gathering relevant details and escalating appropriately.

Provide insights into common problems to help anticipate future demand.

Ensure helpdesk activities remain aligned with central processes, standards, and service expectations.

Essential Skills/Experience: Solid Experience with Windows OS.

Experience with MacOS.

Basic knowledge of Active Directory & Entra ID.

Experience with Microsoft 365 services and products (Teams, SharePoint, OneDrive, Exchange).

Able to provide physical support (Cable management, setting up workstations) as well as technical support.

Desirable (Non-essential) Skills: Familiarity with Manage Engine Endpoint Central.

Experience with Microsoft Intune.

Experience with Jamf Experience with supporting Microsoft Teams Room Systems.

Personality (Soft Skills): Strong communication skills and the ability to articulate technical concepts to non-technical users.

Shows initiative, demonstrates a positive attitude towards problems, and takes ownership of challenges.

Keen problem-solving abilities and a proactive approach to anticipate and address issues before they arise.

Strong interpersonal skills with the ability to build strong relationships within the studio and across the wider business.

Ability to prioritize tasks and manage multiple requests within a fast-moving environment.

A curious nature and a desire to continuously learn and improve.
