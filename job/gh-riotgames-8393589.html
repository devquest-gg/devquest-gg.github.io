Esports Competitive Operations

Riot Games · Berlin

Mid Marketing Posted 2026-11-17 Full time
Apply on Riot Games’s site →

As Competitive Operations II you will report to the Competition Manager of EMEA and be part of the Competitive Operations Department in Berlin. You will join a lean but impactful team of esports professionals to govern and operate Riot Games Esports ecosystems in EMEA.

Your focus will be to support live match operations, as well as the design, administration, and operation esports properties in EMEA. In collaboration with your colleagues, your goal is to operate a competition ecosystem in EMEA that offers best-in-class sports media entertainment for fans, an aspirational career opportunity for pro players, a healthy business environment for teams and partners, and a competition that meets the highest standards of competitive integrity.

Responsibilities:

Maintain in-depth understanding of rules and regulations of the competition.

Direct and oversee match operations for in-house and third party Riot esports products in EMEA.

Drive development of match operations procedures with our ecosystem partners, sharing best practices and learnings from in-house productions.

Be the primary point of contact for our partner teams in relation to match operations and league administration.

Drive the design, development and maintenance of our competition rules and policies to match the ever-changing needs of the ecosystem.

Support our dispute resolution and investigation efforts to help resolve conflict and uphold the integrity of the competition.

Manage pro-team and player relations in collaboration with our ecosystem partners.

Required Qualifications:

You have 4+ years of experience or advanced degree in esports or sports league administration, sports media entertainment, or equivalent

You have very good knowledge of Riot esports games, especially League of Legends and VALORANT; you can tell if it’s a bug and not a feature

You know how esports tournaments work and you have a solid knowledge about competition rules and operations

You have experience in managing tournament partners, referees or match admins

You have strong knowledge of the esports industry in EMEA

Desired Qualifications:

Fluency in additional language(s) beyond English, such as Turkish, Russian, Arabic, French, German, or Spanish

You have a strong understanding of the esports and sports industry, league and team business

For this role, you ll find success through craft expertise and a collaborative spirit that prioritizes the delight of players. We will look at your past studies and experience, but for this role, we also look for dedicated people with a personal relationship with games. If you embody player empathy and care about players experiences, this is the role for you!
