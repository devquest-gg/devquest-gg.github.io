Communications & Creator Strategy Manager (M/F/NB)

Ubisoft (HQ) · Paris

Senior Marketing Posted 2026-11-27 Full time
Apply on Ubisoft (HQ)’s site →

As Communications & Creator Strategy Manager, you will support the planning and delivery of communications and creator activities for a portfolio of Ubisoft franchises: For Honor, The Crew, Riders Republic, Brawlhalla, Skull & Bones, and Just Dance.

You report to the Senior Manager, Communications & Creator Strategy, you will turn game features, updates, and studio insights into clear, engaging stories and deliver campaign content and creator activations across announcements, launches, and live-service beats.

You will work day to day with Creative House marketing teams and Engagement Hubs local teams, and external partners to ensure that activities are well coordinated, delivered on time, and consistent with agreed franchise narratives. You will own defined projects and workstreams, with growing autonomy within priorities, frameworks, and budgets set by your Senior Manager and the broader direction established by the Senior Director, Integrated Communications.

Key Responsibilities Content & communications delivery Support the development and maintenance of communications plans and calendars for assigned products, translating agreed campaign priorities into clear milestones, deliverables, audiences, and timelines.

Turn game features, updates, studio insights and both Creative Houses’ and IC strategies into accessible storylines, key messages, and content that resonates with journalists, players, and communities.

Draft and coordinate campaign materials, including announcements, messaging documents, faqs, briefing notes, press materials, and visual or video assets.

Work with regional and local Engagement Hubs teams with messaging, assets and creator guidelines so campaigns can be activated locally with market-relevant press and creators.

Press relations & communications moments Support media outreach, story pitching, interview coordination, and follow-up with journalists and relevant media contacts.

Organize press and creator previews and hands-on sessions, and contribute to showcases and trade shows, in partnership with IC Events.

Monitor press coverage, online conversations, and industry news; flag emerging opportunities, inaccuracies, or potential issues to the senior manager.

Creator collaborations & activations Support creator activity across the game lifecycle, including announcements, launches, updates, and ongoing live-service beats, combining organic advocacy and paid partnerships where relevant.

Coordinate creator briefs, key messages, access, assets, timelines, and deliverables in line with campaign plans and guidance from the senior manager.

Contribute to the Ubisoft creators program and creator-focused activations and events, coordinating with internal teams and external agencies.

Collect creator feedback, content links, performance data, and campaign learnings; synthesize findings for the Creative House marketing teams and the IC.

Campaign Operations, Budget & Measurement Track project plans, deliverables, timelines, dependencies, and approvals; proactively follow up to keep activities on schedule.

Collect and analyze available campaign indicators, including media coverage, creator content performance, engagement, and audience feedback for CH teams.

Prepare concise campaign reports and post-campaign reviews, highlighting results, risks, lessons learned, and practical recommendations.

Collaboration & Growing Autonomy Work collaboratively with cross-functional partners and Engagement Hubs to ensure clear ownership, timely information sharing, and consistent execution.

Share relevant audience, media, creator, and platform insights with the Senior Manager to inform recommendations to the CH.

First experience in communications, public relations, content production, or creator marketing, ideally within gaming, entertainment, technology, or a related industry.

Hands-on experience developing communication materials, supporting campaigns, and coordinating projects across multiple stakeholders.

Strong writing and storytelling skills, with the ability to translate game features and complex topics into clear, engaging messages for different audiences.

Good understanding of digital platforms, media landscapes, and content creator ecosystems.

Strong organizational skills, attention to detail, and the ability to manage multiple priorities and deadlines.

Ability to collaborate effectively with the Creative House marketing teams and the local Engagement Hubs in an international, cross-functional environment.

Analytical mindset, with the ability to monitor campaign performance, interpret audience and creator insights, and identify opportunities for improvement.

Ability to take ownership of assigned projects and deliver them with increasing autonomy, while aligning with the Senior Manager on priorities, key deliverables, and sensitive issues.

Excellent written and spoken English.
