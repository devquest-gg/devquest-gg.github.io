Product Manager

Bold Games · Istanbul

Mid Production Onsite Posted 2026-07-02 Full time

At Bold Games, we're a tight-knit team that genuinely loves what we do. We support each other through every step of our journey and keep pushing towards the best that has yet to come.

We're looking for a Product Manager who will contribute to each production step, and provide fun and engaging gameplay to our users. If you'd like to create the best-in-class game experience as we grow towards millions of users around the world, take the chance and join our team!

What you’ll be doing - at its core:

- Designing fun and engaging levels, game mechanics, features, and events to improve the user experience in collaboration with engineers and artists

- Analyzing and interpreting data to gain a deeper understanding of our product and users’ behaviour

- Being involved in every step of the production lifecycle from ideation to execution

- Defining strategic direction, planning, and driving the product roadmap

- Testing every task to ensure a seamless gaming experience

- Designing A/B tests to evaluate hypotheses and decide on changes and improvements

- Staying up-to-date by playing and analyzing mobile games as a team

What we’re looking for:

- University degree

- Analytical approach to think through production processes

- At least 2 year of experience in product management

- Gaming experience is a plus, but we'd be glad to meet you if you have a keen interest in mobile gaming.
