Role Overview

We re looking for a Playable Ad Developer to own playable ad production across all games at PeopleFun. You ll convert our existing titles into HTML5 playable experiences, build tools and processes that scale production and actively use AI to increase output and quality. This is a hands-on role with real scope - you re not just executing briefs, you re helping shape how we build playables as a studio.

Key Responsibilities

Own the end-to-end creation of playable ads across all PeopleFun games

Build scalable processes and tooling to increase production volume and consistency

Leverage AI tools to speed up and improve playable ad development

Optimise playables for user experience, performance and cross-platform compatibility

Collaborate with marketing, creative producers, artists and animators

Required Skills, Knowledge and Expertise

2+ years of experience developing Playable Ads or HTML games

Good knowledge of JavaScript , with experience in CSS, HTML5 and cross-platform composition

Hands-on use of AI tools in development workflow

Experience with adaptive composition and JS/CSS animation

Comfortable working with Git

Curious, self-driven and eager to explore new tools and approaches

Portfolio of playable ads (required)
