IT Infrastructure & Cloud Engineer

Moon Active · Tel Aviv

Mid Engineering Onsite Posted 2026-07-29 Full time
Apply on Moon Active’s site →

Moon Active is one of the world's fastest-growing mobile game companies, providing entertainment for millions of active users across the Universe. The company is headquartered in the heart of Tel Aviv.

We're looking for a talented IT Infrastructure & Cloud Engineer who will be responsible for our IT and Cloud corporate infrastructure.

You will be suggesting, implementing and deploying new solutions together with maintaining the existing infrastructure, assuring that all our systems are in optimal condition, providing the best service to our clients.

Responsibilities

- Be responsible for requests from the field, related to our global corporate on-premise and cloud infrastructure. This includes servers, storage appliances, active network equipment, firewalls, cloud services, cloud automations, CI/CD and other initiatives.

- Design and oversee the integration of core services and platforms such as AWS, Google Cloud and Workspace, Okta, Cloud Backups, Zero Trust Network Access (ZTNA), Secure Web Gateway, Cloud-based EDR and other cutting-edge technologies.

- Spearhead the strategy, implementation, and governance of GenAI tools and platforms across the company, focusing on process improvement, building internal AI agents, and driving organizational AI transformation.

- Lead the architectural design and deployment of scalable, reliable, and secure corporate infrastructure, emphasizing Infrastructure as Code (IaC) principles to manage on-premise and cloud environments.

- Define network policies and procedures, including design, planning network new communication systems and monitoring to allow high quality service for our global offices.

- Collaborating with our security team to implement technology and security policies, safeguarding our systems and data from potential threats.

Requirements

- At least 2 years of hands-on experience as a System Administrator, IT Infrastructure Engineer, Junior DevOps, Platforms Engineer or a similar role.

- Experience with cloud services (AWS / GCP), including compute, storage, databases, networking, security, and monitoring.

- Hands-on experience with GenAI implementation, including building and governing AI agents/bots for enterprise use cases.

- Experience designing and deploying scalable infrastructure solutions, utilizing Infrastructure as Code (IaC) tools such as Terraform, CloudFormation, or Ansible.

- Expertise in integrating and automating business workflows.

- Hands-on experience in networking, wireless, routing, switching, enterprise firewalls and VPNs

- Hands-on experience in managing Linux servers, including CentOS, Ubuntu or Red Hat.

- Experience scripting languages such as Bash, Python, PowerShell.

- High customer orientation and exceptional communication skills, enabling effective collaboration with employees in a fast-paced environment.

- Fluent Hebrew & English.

Advantages

- Familiarity with Kubernetes.
