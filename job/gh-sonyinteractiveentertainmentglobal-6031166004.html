Why Sony Interactive Entertainment?

Sony Interactive Entertainment isn’t just the Best Place to Play — it’s also the Best Place to Work. Sony Interactive Entertainment (SIE) is the company behind the PlayStation brand. As a subsidiary of Sony Group Corporation, we’re part of a proud legacy of innovation and excellence. SIE is a dynamic technology company, delivering cutting-edge hardware and network services to more than 100 million people and an entertainment leader, home to some of the most beloved and recognizable intellectual properties (IP) in the world. Our role at SIE is to create and nurture the experiences under the PlayStation brand, a name synonymous with entertainment excellence and creativity.

Manger, Corporate Development Finance San Mateo OR Los Angeles (hybrid)

SIE is seeking a highly motivated and versatile Manager in its Corporate Development Finance team. The team partners closely with senior leadership to support C-level decision-making across a broad range of strategic and financial priorities, including market intelligence, strategic planning, M A/investment evaluation and execution, post-investment management, and investor communications.

This role is designed for a highly independent and well-rounded strategic leader who can flex across multiple domains, including strategic initiatives, business insights, investor relations, and M A and investments . This will be a highly visible role, with frequent engagement with SIE’s leadership team, and close collaboration various stakeholders to help solve some of the business’ most important strategic priorities that will shape the future of SIE and PlayStation.

The ideal candidate is a structured thinker and strong executor who thrives in a fast-paced environment, can manage multiple high-priority workstreams, is comfortable engaging with senior stakeholders across global teams, and has a keen interest in driving innovation in gaming.

Key Responsibilities

Strategic Finance Corporate Strategy

Support development and articulation of SIE’s corporate strategy and mid- and long-range planning

Develop executive-level materials (e.g. strategy updates, board presentations, CEO briefings)

Partner with business units to evaluate strategic initiatives, business cases, and capital allocation decisions

Cross-Functional Project Leadership

Lead or support high-impact, cross-functional initiatives on behalf of senior leadership

Drive alignment across global stakeholders and functions

Manage multiple concurrent projects with strong attention to detail, timelines, and stakeholder communication

Investor Relations Executive Communications

Support investor relations activities, including earnings material preparation, investor presentations, and story development

Translate complex financial and strategic topics into clear, compelling narratives for internal and external stakeholders

Assist in responding to investor inquiries and preparing senior leadership for external engagements

M A, Investments Integration

Support due diligence and transaction execution for acquisitions, investments, or partnerships as needed

Contribute to integration planning and execution, including Day 1 readiness and tracking of value creation initiatives

Develop frameworks and KPIs to monitor performance of acquired or invested businesses

Investment Value Creation Management

Lead investment reviews to assess performance against investment thesis and identify lessons learned

Track and report on strategic and financial outcomes of investments

Help refine internal processes, playbooks, and tools across the investment lifecycle

Qualifications

5-8+ years of relevant experience in a highly analytic and strategic role, ideally in investment banking, management consulting, transaction advisory (Big 4 or equivalent), corporate development, strategic finance, investor relations, or similar roles

Strong generalist toolkit with experience spanning strategy, finance, and/or operations

Excellent problem-solving and analytical skills, including financial modeling and business case development

Strong communication skills with the ability to synthesize complex topics into clear, executive-ready materials

Demonstrated ability to manage multiple projects autonomously and adapt to evolving business priorities

Experience supporting and coordinating executive forums and leadership communications in a fast-paced setting

Experience and/or passion for technology, gaming, media and entertainment

Collaborative, low-ego team player with strong interpersonal skills and ability to influence across functions and cultures

High proficiency in Excel and PowerPoint

BA/BS required; MBA or equivalent advanced degree preferred
