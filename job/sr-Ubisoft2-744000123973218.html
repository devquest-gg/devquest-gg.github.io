HR Governance Lead – Job Catalogue

Ubisoft (HQ) · Saint-Mandé

Within the HR Strategy & Transformation team, we are looking for a Senior Project Manager to lead the implementation of our new Career Framework — including a job catalog, career streams, and grading structure — as well as to ensure its ongoing maintenance and integration within HR policies.

In this role, you’ll be exposed to a wide range of strategic topics, including: Governance Define and implement a governance model, in collaboration with HR, Business, and IT teams, to ensure the ongoing maintenance and updates of the Career Framework, with clear roles and responsibilities; Organize and lead governance forums to drive decisions on potential adjustments to the job catalog and associated grading structure; Act as the main point of contact and Group referent for governance matters, ensuring consistent application of the model and proper follow-up on process updates; Oversee the long-term consistency and coherence of the Career Framework through structured governance and monitoring.

Project Planning & Coordination Coordinate and align with HR, IT, and business stakeholders to ensure the Career Framework meets organizational needs; Drive coordination across multiple entities in an international context, ensuring both consistency and agility in execution; Identify next steps following the framework’s deployment, plan and oversee their implementation (e.g. HR process impacts, skills mapping, continuous improvement, etc.).

Change Management & Communication Support and guide teams through change management and communication efforts, ensuring engagement of key stakeholders throughout the process; Design and roll out communication and change management actions tailored to the different stages of the project and its future developments.

Risk Management & Continuous Improvement Track progress using key performance indicators (KPIs) and feedback loops to assess adoption and identify areas for improvement ; Conduct post-deployment reviews to capture learnings and drive continuous improvement.

Relevant Experience You have solid experience in Human Resources, either in-house or in consulting, ideally within an international environment; You have previously worked on job framework topics (such as Workforce Planning or Job Architecture) and grading methodologies; You are experienced in project management and in aligning practices across multiple entities.

Skills & Knowledge Excellent communication and stakeholder management skills, including at senior levels of the organization; Strong understanding of job framework design and grading methodologies; Proactive and solution-oriented mindset; Strong analytical and data-driven approach; Proficiency in Excel and PowerPoint; Fluency in English and French ; Ability to work autonomously.

Ubisoft Benefits 💰 Profit Sharing, yearly company saving plan. 25 paid time off + 12 additional paid days off. 50% of your transportation pass is paid by the company, lunch vouchers (9€/day), healthcare for you and your family, and lots of Ubisoft additional perks.

👶 Maternity leaves of 20 weeks, paternity/co-parental leaves of 7 weeks.

📍 Our office is located in Saint Mandé, (Metro line 1, Saint Mandé station).
