What will you do?

Support the Controller / Chief Accountant across their full range of responsibilities and contribute to the smooth running of the complete accounting cycle Prepare and record journal entries (accruals, prepaids, depreciation, payroll, etc.) Actively participate in month-end, quarter-end, and year-end closes Perform bank reconciliations and general ledger account analysis Contribute to the preparation of financial statements and monthly management reports Prepare government remittances (GST/QST, source deductions) and support tax filings Process intercompany transactions with the group's international entities Support the team during external audits (preparing working papers, responding to auditors) Review and support the accounting clerk's work (accounts payable and receivable) Validate payroll following the data entry performed by the Human Resources team Ensure compliance with internal controls, accounting policies, and sound practices Contribute to continuous process improvement and the optimization of tools (ERP / Workday) Collaborate with production and human resources teams to ensure the accuracy of project-related financial data

What do we expect from our Accountant?

Bachelor's degree in accounting (required) CPA designation completed or in progress (an asset) 3 to 5 years of experience in accounting (experience in a technology company or video game studio is an asset) Excellent proficiency in Excel and office tools Knowledge of accounting standards (ASPE / IFRS) Rigor, autonomy, analytical mindset, organizational skills, and attention to detail Ability to work in a dynamic and collaborative environment Strong asset: experience with Workday (finance or HR) Knowledge of an ERP or accounting software: a plus Bilingual French-English
