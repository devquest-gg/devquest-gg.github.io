Senior VFX Artist

CD Projekt Red · Warsaw · Remote

Senior Art Remote Posted 2026-04-17 Full time
Apply on CD Projekt Red’s site →

CD PROJEKT RED is looking for a Senior VFX Artist to join our studio and help bring to life Project Sirius—a multiplayer game set in The Witcher universe. This role will draw on your experience, attention to detail, and passion for creating high-quality assets to help drive the next generation of high-fidelity VFX in games.

Daily Responsibilities Create high-quality, real-time visual effects across all effects domains.

Collaborate across multiple disciplines to support gameplay, cinematics, and environments with high-quality VFX.

Partner with peers to innovate and research new workflows, tools, and techniques to push the boundaries of real-time VFX.

Deliver VFX work that is performant and within technical budgets.

Populate environments with visually engaging and immersive VFX.

Help foster a culture of creativity, trust, and technical excellence.

Work with department leads and production to estimate and plan work.

Professional experience in the gaming industry, with a focus on real-time VFX creation and experience contributing to shipped or completed game projects.

A strong portfolio that demonstrates VFX creation for games.

Expertise in both real-time and baked simulations.

Demonstrable knowledge of VFX pipelines and tools—including SideFX Houdini.

Expertise in Unreal Engine 5, Niagara, and interrelated systems.

Solid understanding of PBR concepts, real-time rendering techniques, shaders, and lighting.

Experience with performance and optimization procedures for real-time VFX.

Ability to think creatively and strategically to overcome complex technical challenges.

Ability to manage tasks, collaborate on shared goals, and contribute to team knowledge-sharing.

Excellent communication and interpersonal skills, with the ability to effectively communicate complex technical concepts to both technical and non-technical stakeholders.

Candidates should include a portfolio in their application. Applications without a portfolio will not be considered.
