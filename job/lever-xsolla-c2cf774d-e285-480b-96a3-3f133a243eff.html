ABOUT XSOLLA

Xsolla is a global video game commerce company headquartered in Los Angeles. It helps developers from indie to AAA fund, distribute, market and monetize their games, and acts as merchant of record for 1,500+ developers worldwide. For more information, visit xsolla.com.

HOW WE WORK

Decisions in writing. Significant technical decisions are written up as an RFC or ADR that lays out the options and trade-offs. We discuss the document, not a blank whiteboard.

AI is part of everyday engineering. We use AI coding tools for development and review, and we treat configuring them for a codebase as real engineering work.

HOW WE HIRE

Intro call. Your background, your motivation and the role.

Technical interview. Built directly on the What you will own and Required sections above. We won't ask about anything this posting doesn't promise.

Bar raiser. An engineer from outside the hiring team checks that you raise the overall engineering bar.

THE TEAM

You will work alongside product managers, designers, smart contract, backend and frontend engineers, QA and DevOps.
