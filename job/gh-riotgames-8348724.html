Illustration Artist - Riftbound

Riot Games · Shanghai

Mid Art Posted 2026-11-03 Full time

Riot Games was established in 2006 by entrepreneurial gamers who believe that player-focused game development can result in great games. In 2009, Riot released its debut title League of Legends to critical and player acclaim. As the most played video game in the world, over 100 million play every month. Players form the foundation of our community and it’s for them that we continue to evolve and improve the League of Legends experience.

We’re looking for humble but ambitious, razor-sharp professionals who can teach us a thing or two. We promise to return the favor. Like us, you take play seriously; you’re passionate about games. We embrace those who see things differently, aren’t afraid to experiment, and who have a healthy disregard for constraints.

That s where you come in.

As an Illustration Artist at Riot, you will play an important role in delivering high-quality card illustrations for Riftbound. Working closely with the Art Director, Senior Illustration Artist, and cross-disciplinary partners, you will help guide card art from concept through final, print-ready delivery.

You will review artwork, provide clear and actionable feedback, and create paintovers to help external artists strengthen composition, color, storytelling, and overall execution. Drawing on a strong understanding of illustration fundamentals and visual storytelling, you will help maintain Riftbound’s quality bar and contribute to creating memorable imagery within the League of Legends universe.

Working within the constraints of physical trading card production, you will apply your knowledge of print processes, color management, and readability to help ensure artwork reproduces beautifully and consistently. You will collaborate with designers and visual designers to ensure illustrations support gameplay needs and work well with frames, typography, and other graphic elements. Reporting to the game’s Art Director, you will work within established creative direction and art briefs while partnering with external artists and internal teams to deliver polished, compelling card art.

Responsibilities

Support outsourcing processes by helping review incoming artwork, communicate feedback, track revisions, and ensure external artists have the visual direction and resources needed to deliver high-quality work.

Create paintovers, drawovers, and visual references to clearly communicate art direction and help artists solve visual challenges

Apply strong illustration fundamentals, including composition, anatomy, lighting, color, perspective, and visual storytelling

Work closely with the Art Director and Senior Illustration Artist to execute Riftbound’s established illustration direction

Help maintain consistency and quality across card artwork while identifying opportunities to strengthen individual illustrations

Ensure card art supports gameplay clarity, mechanics, and readability in partnership with game designers

Work with visual designers to ensure illustrations complement frames, typography, and other graphic elements

Review artwork for polish, consistency, and fidelity to the League of Legends IP

Work within established art briefs and creative direction while helping external artists achieve the intended vision

Apply knowledge of print production, color management, and physical card constraints to help deliver print-ready artwork

Collaborate with producers, designers, external artists, and other disciplines to deliver artwork on schedule

Required Qualifications

4+ years of professional experience in illustration or a related creative discipline

Strong foundation in illustration fundamentals, including anatomy, composition, perspective, lighting, color theory, and visual storytelling

Strong drawing and painting skills, with the ability to create effective paintovers and visual feedback

Don’t forget to include a resume and cover letter. We receive a lot of applications, but we’ll notice a fun, well-written intro that shows us you take play seriously.

For this role, you ll find success through craft expertise, a collaborative spirit, and decision-making that prioritizes the delight of players. We will be looking at your past studies, experience, and your personal relationship with games. If you embody player empathy and care about players experiences, this could be your role!
