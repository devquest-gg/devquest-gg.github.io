The passion and energy that made Mortal Kombat a reality is still on display every day in our offices, our hallways and most definitely the studio’s arcade room. ㅤ The Job As a Quality Assurance Analyst, you’ll be working closely with the dev team to support the design, engineering and continuous testing of our games while they are being created.

The Quality Analyst will work closely with all departments to ensure they meet their goals, and ensure that the QA team is kept updated on developments. Testing both the game and supporting tools, the Quality Analyst will need a good eye for detail to spot issues, possess the skills to investigate them and clearly report this via verbal and written bug reports.

The Daily • Represent QA within the development team; be visible, vocal and integrated.

• Become a subject matter expert for areas of domain within the QA Team.

• Testing features, systems, feature sets, test cases and assets on assigned projects.

• Accurately and efficiently report bugs, defects.

• Assist the QA Lead or QA Manager in the execution of the QA Test Plan.

• Play a key role in the development process by attending project reviews and daily scrum meetings.

The Essentials • A passion for video games and the Quality Assurance discipline • Excellent interpersonal and written communication skills, with a logical and methodological approach to tasks.

• Detailed knowledge of MS Office applications and Issue/Test Management systems such as JIRA, TestRail etc.

• Experience with one or more game consoles (Nintendo, Microsoft, Sony) or PC.

The Nice to Haves • Experience shipping or working on a live game • Understanding of Fighting games • Experience with using mobile devices, and knowledge of differences between iOS and Android.
