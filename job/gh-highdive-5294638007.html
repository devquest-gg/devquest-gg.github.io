DevOps Specialist

The DevOps Specialist will join our Production Services team to maintain and improve the systems and services that support the studio’s development and production work. The role covers application deployments and updates, VM and CI/CD pipeline maintenance, and automation of recurring tasks. Working with IT, development and QA, the specialist will contribute to planned upgrades and day-to-day operations. Responsibilities also include troubleshooting infrastructure and applications, responding to user support tickets, and documenting changes and procedures so the team can maintain these services.

Responsibilities:

Execute application deployments and updates.

Maintain VMs and CI/CD pipelines.

Automate recurring operational tasks.

Troubleshoot infrastructure and applications.

Document changes and procedures to ensure sustainable service maintenance.

Supporting internal users and collaborating with IT and development teams.

Qualifications:

3+ years operating production infrastructure in DevOps, platform engineering, SRE, or systems administration with a strong automation focus.

Hands-on Linux administration with a methodical troubleshooting approach.

Docker and experience running containerized services in production.

Hands-on Kubernetes: deployments, services, configuration, secrets, persistent storage, and troubleshooting.

Hands-on Helm: deploying applications, managing upgrades, configuring values, and modifying existing charts.

CI/CD: GitLab pipelines and runners, or similar build and deployment systems.

Configuration management: Ansible or similar tools.

Scripting with Python or Bash, and experience automating recurring operational tasks.

Networking fundamentals: TCP/IP, DNS, VPNs, and load balancing.

Monitoring and logging: Prometheus, Grafana, or Elastic Stack.

Good operational judgment: testing changes, following review processes, planning rollback, documenting work, and knowing when to escalate.

Regular, hands-on use of AI tools for technical work, including understanding, testing, and validating output.

Virtualization: VMware or comparable platforms.

Nice to Have:

Windows administration and PowerShell scripting.

Practical experience with VM backups, restores, patching, and service recovery.

Identity and access: Active Directory, LDAP, and SSO integrations.

Operating databases, including maintenance and upgrades.

Experience in game development, including tools such as Perforce, TeamCity, or Unreal Engine.

Justification des exigences linguistiques

NetEase s engage à favoriser un environnement de travail francophone pour ses employés au Québec. Tous les efforts ont été faits pour éviter d exiger une langue autre que le français pour ce poste.

Cependant, la maîtrise de l anglais est essentielle pour ce poste, car l employé devra communiquer régulièrement avec des collègues et des parties prenantes situés à l extérieur du Québec, où l anglais est la principale langue de communication entre les bureaux.

Language Requirement Justification

NetEase is committed to supporting a French-speaking workenvironment for its employees in Quebec. Every effort has been made to avoidrequiring a language other than French for this position.

However, proficiency in English is essential for thisrole because the employee will be required to communicate regularly with colleagues and stakeholders located outside Quebec, where English is theprimary language of communication between offices.
