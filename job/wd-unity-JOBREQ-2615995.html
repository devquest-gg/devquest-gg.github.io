The opportunity This role requires a strong audit professional with deep expertise in technology risk, cybersecurity, privacy, and operational controls, who can work independently with minimal guidance and supervision.

What you ll be doing Lead End-to-End IT Audits: Own and execute comprehensive technology, cybersecurity, privacy, and operational control audits across the entire organization.

Provide Strategic Risk Advisory: Serve as a key subject matter expert and advisor on technology risks, privacy regulations, and compliance matters.

Drive AI \& Automation Assurance: Support and advise on the risk management, compliance, and governance frameworks for automated processes and AI initiatives.

Operate with High Autonomy: Lead complex audit projects independently with minimal guidance, taking full accountability for execution and delivery.

Technical Risk Expertise: Apply deep, specialized knowledge of cybersecurity frameworks, privacy controls, and modern technology risks to safeguard corporate infrastructure.

What we re looking for Expertise in leading and executing end-to-end security, privacy, and operational audits, including risk assessment, control testing, reporting, and remediation validation.

Strong knowledge of information security, cybersecurity governance, data privacy, IT general controls, operational controls, and risk management frameworks.

Proven ability to develop risk-based audit plans, audit programs, testing strategies, and impactful audit reports.

Skilled in identifying control deficiencies, evaluating risks, and recommending practical, effective remediation actions.

Proficiency in leveraging data analytics and AI-enabled audit techniques to enhance audit coverage, risk identification, and testing effectiveness.
