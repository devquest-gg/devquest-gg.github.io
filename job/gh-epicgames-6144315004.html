QUALITY ASSURANCE

What We Do

The Quality Assurance team owns the testing process, risk management, and quality evaluation of all products and functions across Epic. Our team works closely with developers and production to ensure the best possible user experience for our community and customers.

What You ll Do

Epic Games is looking for a full-time Senior Tester II for our testing group in Brazil. The person in this role will own the testing approach for a specialized domain within a major Epic product, and will be expected to make that domain measurably faster and more reliable to test over time through better methods, tooling, and use of data. You will work embedded with the development team, developing and executing tests with or without QA Analyst guidance, and proactively identifying problems beyond the day-to-day in order to solve them at the root rather than one case at a time. The Senior Tester II is also a technical reference point for the wider test team, mentoring testers and raising the quality bar through example. If you are interested in a fast-paced environment, capable of navigating interpersonal quandaries, and desire to work on a world-class QA team, then we would love to talk to you.

In this role, you will

Own the test approach for your area, developing and executing tests independently, with or without QA Analyst guidance

Design and introduce sustainable testing methods, including tooling, automation aids, or data analysis, that measurably increase the throughput or efficiency of testing efforts

Proactively identify systemic problems that go beyond day-to-day concerns and develop semi-permanent solutions instead of one-off fixes

Perform test gap and risk analysis for your domain, using defect data and coverage insights to redirect testing effort to where it matters most

Provide the development team with reports on blocking issues, test results, and overall quality status, framing risk in terms the team can act on

Escalate quality concerns that cannot be resolved within existing constraints, bringing a recommended path forward

Self-prioritize your work on a week-to-week basis and help shape the team s priorities aligned with product goals

Mentor testers on your team and act as a domain reference for adjacent teams, sharing context, methods, and standards

What we re looking for

A minimum of 4 years of experience in QA/Testing at games or software, including experience working embedded within a development team throughout the software development life cycle

Demonstrated depth in the product areas you have tested, with the judgment to know where risk concentrates and how to go after it

Fluent in English and Portuguese

A track record of improving how testing gets done, through tooling, process change, or data, with evidence of the resulting impact

Ability to analyze defect trends and coverage gaps, and to act on that analysis to prevent future issues

Experience with gaming consoles and/or mobile devices

Experience with game engines such as Unreal and Unity

Proficient with test management tools (e.g., TestRail, Xray, QMetry) and issue tracking systems like Jira

Strong written and verbal communication skills, with the ability to drive alignment on quality with developers, designers, and production

Experience mentoring less experienced testers
