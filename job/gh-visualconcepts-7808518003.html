Game Designer, Temporary

Visual Concepts · Remote · Remote

Mid Design Remote Posted 2026-07-22
Apply on Visual Concepts’s site →

At Visual Concepts, we believe great games are made by diverse and empowered teams with a shared passion for play. As one of the world’s top game development studios, we have shipped over 100 multi-sku titles to critical acclaim and commercial success. Our teams are independent and entrepreneurial. Our studios in Novato, Irvine, Budapest, Shanghai, Austin, Boise, Seoul, and Parksville, Canada are committed to artistry and technical innovation, offering top candidates the opportunity to learn and grow with some of the smartest and most creative minds in the industry.

What We Need:

Our WWE 2K is team looking for a talented Game Designer who is excited to work on the WWE franchise. WWE 2K is an exciting and challenging game with a diverse set of features and requirements. You will grow and develop in many different areas, learning from a highly skilled team while working on cutting-edge technology. This position reports into the Design Director.

What You Will Do:

Work collaboratively with the Lead Designer to deliver engaging, interactive stories

Implement and own cinematic sequences to help shape the narrative vision of the game using a proprietary scripting system

Collaborate with engineers, designers and artists to identify key issues and generate high-quality solutions

Who Will Be A Great Fit:

Minimum of two years working as a professional game designer on a shipped AAA title

A keen eye for cinematic framing, timing, and how scenes are presented to the player

Strong grasp of narrative craft and how storytelling deepens player engagement

Clear, confident communicator — both written and verbal — who works well across disciplines and teams

A technically-minded problem solver who can quickly pick up and get the most out of proprietary tools and pipelines

Preferred qualifications

Working knowledge of animation timelines, plus an intuitive grasp of human behavior — especially how it shapes character interactions and conversational flow.

A genuine passion for RPGs across a wide range of styles, with a strong handle on genre conventions, storytelling craft, and what makes the player experience land.

Scripting experience, especially with visual or node-based tools, is a plus
