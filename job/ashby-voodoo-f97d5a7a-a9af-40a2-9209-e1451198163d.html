ABOUT VOODOO

Founded in 2013, Voodoo is a tech company that creates mobile games and apps with a mission to entertain the world. Gathering 800 employees, 7 billion downloads, and over 200 million active users, Voodoo is the #3 mobile publisher worldwide in terms of downloads after Google and Meta. Our portfolio includes chart-topping games like Mob Control and Block Jam, alongside popular apps such as BeReal and Wizz.

TEAM

We are looking for a Technical Artist with strong Unity experience to help make the game feel more satisfying, polished, and modern.

Your mission will be to improve one of our legacy game’s visual feedback, animations, VFX, transitions, impact moments, rhythm, and overall game feel, while keeping everything lightweight and optimized for mobile.

ROLE

- Create high-quality 3D/2D assets (characters, assets, props and environment art) and ensure optimal performance across mobile devices.

- Create VFX's using particle system in Unity.

- Stay open to gameplay and feedback, and contribute ideas to improve game feel with an holistic approach

- Work directly in Unity to implement and iterate on animations and effects

- Improve readability, timing, tactile satisfaction, and overall game feel

- Iterate quickly based on product direction, player behavior, and A/B test learnings

- Contribute ideas to improve the game’s visual quality and player experience

PROFILE

- Strong Unity skills, with the ability to implement and optimise directly in engine.

- Good understanding of particle systems and core animation principles

- Ability to create VFXs using Unity's particle system

- A strong eye for game feel and art — rhythm, impact, and tactile satisfaction

- Comfortable balancing creative ambition with the practical constraints of mobile

- An art background, with at least 3 years of experience

- Excellent 3D /2D design skills, with a keen eye for visual hierarchy, composition, and readability.

- Capable of working with Maya for animation and rigging, and Blender for 3D modelling.

- Experience with A/B testing or working with LiveOps features.

- AI-first mindset: Comfortable using AI daily and building custom agents or reusable automations to speed up your work
