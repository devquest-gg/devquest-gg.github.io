For more information, visit www.zynga.com or follow Zynga on Twitter , Instagram , Facebook, or the Zynga blog We are looking for a Marketing Artist Intern to support the creation of engaging visual content for our games and marketing initiatives. The role will focus on generating images and videos, 2D touchups and clean up, game capture, video editing, basic animations and motion graphics.

The ideal candidate should have a good visual sense, strong fundamentals in graphic designing, an interest in AI driven creative workflows, and the ability to work across images, videos, and game assets.

What You ll Do

Create high quality images and visual assets based on creative briefs, references, and the visual style of the game.

Use Unity to capture high quality gameplay footage and screenshots for marketing and promotional requirements. Set up scenes, cameras, characters, environments, and compositions as required for the capture.

Create video content using AI video generation tools, experimenting with prompts.

Edit gameplay captures, videos, artwork, audio, text, and other assets into polished marketing videos using tools such as Adobe Premiere Pro or After Effects.

Create simple 2D animations, logo reveals, text animations, transitions, and other animated elements using After Effects.

Create engaging motion graphics for marketing videos, promotional content, social media, and other communication needs.

What We Require

Good understanding of composition, color, lighting, typography, and visual storytelling

Good knowledge of Photoshop, After effects and Premiere Pro

Interest in and hands on experience with AI image and video generation

Basic familiarity with Unity or willingness to learn game capture workflows

Ability to identify and fix visual inconsistencies in images and videos

Understanding of basic animation and motion graphics principles

Good attention to detail and ability to work with feedback Great, if you also have

Experience creating game related marketing content

Familiarity with generative AI workflows and prompt iteration

Basic understanding of 3D scenes, cameras, lighting, and animation in Unity

What we offer

Hands-on experience with a modern game marketing art pipeline, covering visual content creation, video editing, animation, motion graphics, and game production workflows.
