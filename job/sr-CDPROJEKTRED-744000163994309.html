Senior Engagement/Internal Events Coordinator

CD Projekt Red · Warsaw

Senior Other Posted 2026-11-27 Full time

At CD PROJEKT RED, games bring us together — but so do the people, shared experiences, and traditions we've built along the way.

We're looking for a Senior Engagement/Internal Events Coordinator to join our Engagement Team and help us keep that spirit alive.

You'll organize events and activities that bring REDs together, celebrate our games and achievements, and make our studios great places to be. From studio celebrations to smaller community events and everyday initiatives, you'll help create moments that people genuinely enjoy being part of.

We're looking for someone who has experience organizing events, enjoys working with people, and knows how to turn ideas into reality. Someone who's just as comfortable coming up with a creative concept as they are managing a budget, coordinating vendors, or solving last-minute problems.

Daily Responsibilities Plan and organize internal events, studio celebrations, and community-building activities.

Come up with ideas that bring REDs together, encourage people to connect, and keep our gaming culture alive.

Take care of the entire event process — planning, budgets, logistics, vendors, on-site coordination, and follow-up.

Work with teams across CD PROJEKT RED to organize events that meet their needs and bring value to our community.

Find new ways to engage REDs, including people working remotely or across different locations.

Collect feedback after events and use it to make the next ones even better.

Keep track of budgets, timelines, and all the details needed to make things run smoothly.

Hands-on experience organizing and coordinating events.

Ability to plan and deliver events independently, from the first idea to the final details.

Good organizational skills and the ability to manage several projects at once.

Experience working with vendors, managing budgets, and handling event logistics.

A practical approach to problem-solving — especially when things don't go according to plan.

Good communication skills and confidence working with different people and teams.

Creativity, initiative, and a willingness to try new things.

Fluency in both Polish and English (C1 level or higher).

Nice to have Experience organizing gaming events, tournaments, or community activities.

Interest in video games and gaming culture.

Experience working in an international company.

Experience with internal communication or employee engagement activities.
