Associate Director – Quality Assurance (QA)

16-20

India

Full-Time

Role Overview LightFury Games is looking for an experienced Associate Director – Quality Assurance to lead and drive the QA function across its AAA game development projects.

The ideal candidate should have extensive experience in AAA game development, with a strong understanding of Unreal Engine, PC and Console platforms, and end-to-end game testing processes. This role will be responsible for establishing quality standards, building scalable QA processes, leading QA teams, and ensuring the delivery of high-quality gaming experiences.

The candidate should bring a combination of strategic QA leadership, technical expertise, and hands-on understanding of complex game development environments.

Key Responsibilities Define and drive the overall QA strategy, vision, and quality standards across game development projects.

Lead and manage QA teams across functional, integration, regression, performance, compatibility, and certification testing.

Establish scalable QA processes, testing methodologies, and quality frameworks throughout the development lifecycle.

Work closely with Engineering, Game Design, Art, Production, and Technical teams to integrate quality practices into development workflows.

Oversee end-to-end testing of AAA games across mobile, PC and Console platforms.

Drive test automation initiatives, continuous testing, and improvements in QA infrastructure.

Establish effective defect management, bug triaging, prioritisation, and resolution processes.

Oversee performance, stability, memory, stress, and compatibility testing.

Ensure release readiness through structured build validation, regression testing, and platform certification processes.

Identify technical and production risks early and implement mitigation strategies.

Define and monitor QA metrics, quality benchmarks, defect trends, and release quality indicators.

Drive continuous improvement in testing efficiency, coverage, and overall product quality.

Build, mentor, and develop high-performing QA teams while establishing accountability and technical excellence.

Required Skills & Experience 16+ years of experience in Quality Assurance, preferably within AAA game development.

Proven experience leading QA functions for large-scale game development projects.

Strong experience in shipping AAA game titles across PC and Console platforms.

Excellent understanding of Unreal Engine and modern game development pipelines.

Deep knowledge of game testing methodologies, including functional, system, integration, regression, performance, and compatibility testing.

Experience with test automation frameworks, scripting, and continuous integration testing.

Strong understanding of game performance, memory optimisation, crash analysis, stability, and debugging.

Experience with multiplayer testing, networking, gameplay systems, and engine-level testing.

Familiarity with platform certification requirements and release management processes.

Strong experience managing cross-functional stakeholders and working with senior engineering and production leadership.

Excellent leadership, communication, analytical, and problem-solving skills.

Preferred Qualifications Experience working on multiple AAA game titles across different development stages.

Prior experience with Unreal Engine 5.

Exposure to PC, PlayStation, Xbox, and other gaming platforms.

Experience implementing automated testing and quality engineering practices at scale.

Understanding of game engines, rendering pipelines, physics systems, and multiplayer architecture.

Experience setting up or scaling QA teams within a game development studio.

What We Are Looking For A strong QA leader who understands both the technical and operational aspects of AAA game development.

Someone who can build and scale QA practices rather than simply manage testing execution.

A leader who can collaborate effectively with engineering and production teams.

Someone with a strong focus on quality, ownership, accountability, and timely delivery of complex gaming projects.
