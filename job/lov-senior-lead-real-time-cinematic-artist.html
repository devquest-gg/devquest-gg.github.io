Senior Real-Time Cinematic Artist

Black Banner Studios · České Budějovice · Remote

Senior Art Remote Posted 2026-11-20 Full time
Apply on Black Banner Studios’s site →

Legacy of Valor is an ambitious medieval RPG and strategy game combining massive real-time battles, third-person melee combat, and tactical army control in Unreal Engine 5. At Black Banner Studios, we're looking for an exceptional Senior Real-Time Cinematic Artist with outstanding artistic judgment, strong storytelling instincts, and the technical expertise to create beautiful, cinematic experiences within a real-time game environment. You'll take ownership of creating high-quality in-game cinematics, including movie-quality RPG conversations, narrative sequences, and scripted events such as tournament introductions. You'll also produce compelling gameplay trailers, promotional videos, and screenshots using actual in-game footage. We're looking for someone who understands that great cinematics and gameplay footage require much more than simply placing cameras or pressing the record button. Careful staging, composition, lighting, animation, timing, and editing are essential, along with the ability to recognize and work around visual imperfections, animation glitches, VFX issues, and performance limitations. You'll work closely with our Game Director, designers, animators, artists, and programmers, taking creative and technical ownership of cinematic production from initial concepts through polished final results. We're open to candidates at both Senior and Lead levels, with the final title and responsibilities determined by experience and the scope of technical or artistic leadership. If you believe you'd be a great fit for this role, even if you don't meet every requirement listed below, we'd still love to hear from you. Please don't hesitate to apply! Plan, direct, and create high-quality real-time in-game cinematics, including RPG conversations, narrative sequences, scripted events, and gameplay introductions. Develop cinematic sequences in Unreal Engine 5 using Sequencer, cameras, character animation, lighting, and other real-time cinematic tools. Stage characters, performances, camera movements, and environments to achieve convincing, movie-quality storytelling and presentation. Independently plan, capture, edit, and deliver beautiful gameplay trailers and promotional videos using actual in-game footage. Identify and work around visual issues such as animation glitches, clipping, VFX imperfections, and performance hiccups to produce polished, representative gameplay footage. Capture and refine high-quality in-game screenshots for Steam, social media, and other marketing purposes. Edit and assemble cinematic and gameplay footage with strong pacing, visual continuity, composition, and storytelling. Collaborate with animators, designers, artists, and programmers to integrate cinematic content into gameplay and maintain consistent visual quality. Establish efficient cinematic production workflows and maintain high artistic and technical standards. Extensive professional experience in real-time cinematics, in-game cutscenes, gameplay trailers, or related cinematic production for games. Exceptional artistic judgment, with a strong eye for cinematography, composition, lighting, color, timing, and visual storytelling. Advanced hands-on experience with Unreal Engine 4/5, particularly Sequencer, cinematic cameras, character animation integration, and real-time rendering. Demonstrated ability to independently create and deliver polished in-game cinematic sequences, including character-driven scenes and narrative storytelling. Strong experience capturing and editing professional-quality gameplay footage from real-time games. Advanced proficiency with professional video editing and post-production software such as DaVinci Resolve, Adobe Premiere Pro, or equivalent tools. Strong understanding of character staging, animation timing, camera direction, and continuity in cinematic sequences. Ability to recognize, troubleshoot, and work around visual imperfections and technical limitations during cinematic production and gameplay capture. Excellent attention to detail, creative problem-solving skills, and the ability to independently take projects from initial concepts to polished final results. Passion for video games, particularly medieval RPGs, open-world games, and strategy titles. Experience creating cinematics or gameplay trailers for AAA and / or open-world games. Experience directing cinematic RPG conversations, facial performances, and character-driven narrative scenes. Experience with Unreal Engine Movie Render Queue, Control Rig, facial animation, and advanced Sequencer workflows. Experience with motion capture, performance capture, facial animation pipelines, or dialogue and lip-sync systems. Experience creating cinematic gameplay events that transition seamlessly between scripted sequences and player control. Experience with color grading, compositing, visual effects, and motion graphics. Additional artistic skills in lighting, environment art, scene dressing, 3D modeling, or animation. Experience developing efficient cinematic production pipelines for games. Location: Remote / International, with the option to work in the Czech Republic. Start Date: As soon as mutually agreed. Language: Professional working proficiency in English. Compensation: Competitive, based on experience and expertise.
