QA Engineer (Console/PC, UE5) (8 months contract)

Yerevan (on-site / hybrid)

Jobs requirements

Nekki is a game development studio with over 20 years of experience. We create games for mobile, PC, and consoles, focusing on product quality and player experience.

The Shadow Fight series and Vector have been downloaded over 1 billion times , and we continue to grow and support these franchises. At the same time, we’re building new titles: SPINE and Fight Me .

We’re looking for a  QA Engineer for Testing the game on PlayStation, Xbox, and PC platforms for a 8 months contract to join our SPINE  team.

SPINE  is made with  Unreal Engine 5  and centered around cinematic  gun-fu shootouts . It’s a third-person beat’em’up with a full set of  PC gaming  features, including a deep storyline, a unique setting, cutscenes, intriguing enemies, memorable bosses, character progression, and, most importantly, breathtaking battles with a wide array of firearms.

This project represents a new ambitious challenge for the company as it marks our first game for both PC and consoles.

Responsibilities:

Testing the game on PlayStation, Xbox, and PC platforms

Testing all game functionality: gameplay systems, physics, materials, AI, and levels

Testing console-specific features and verifying compliance with platform requirements (TRC/XR);

Participation in continuous Smoke and Regression testing

Participation in preparing test reports and summaries

Creating and maintaining checklists and test cases

Collecting performance data using Unreal Insights

Writing and maintaining testing documentation

Ongoing communication with development teams, including game and level designers, etc.

Participation in Exploratory testing

Active involvement in improving testing processes

What we are looking for:

At least 2 years of experience in testing video games on Unreal Engine across console (PlayStation, Xbox) and PC platforms;

Understanding of game development and testing fundamentals in Unreal Engine

Experience working with Unreal Engine testing tools, including Unreal Editor, logging, and debugging tools

Experience testing core functionality such as gameplay systems and physics

High level of independence, decision-making ability, and self-organization

Ability to quickly switch between tasks and manage multiple priorities

Experience in writing and executing test cases and test plans for various game systems and features

Experience working with bug tracking systems such as Jira

Strong skills in analyzing and documenting bugs using detailed descriptions, screenshots, and video recordings

Understanding of basic performance testing and optimization principles for game systems

Knowledge of key testing methodologies, including functional testing, regression testing, and user testing

Experience working in a team environment with effective communication skills

Willingness to learn new technologies and testing approaches within the Unreal Engine ecosystem
