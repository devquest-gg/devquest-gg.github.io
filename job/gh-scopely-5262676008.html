Scopely is looking for an Systems Administrator, Enterprise Applications, IT to join our IT team in Dublin, Ireland or Barcelona Spain on a hybrid basis or Portugal or the UK on a remote basis.

At Scopely, we care deeply about what we do and want to inspire play, every day - whether in our work environments alongside our talented colleagues, or through our deep connections with our communities of players. We are a global team of game lovers who are developing, publishing and innovating the mobile games industry, connecting millions of people around the world daily.

The IT/Ops team keeps everything behind the scenes running smoothly and we make sure all teams are equipped and connected - whether across the office or halfway around the world. AV struggles? Don’t worry, we’ve got this.

What You Will Do We are seeking a highly experienced Enterprise Application Specialist who will serve as the owner and strategic lead for our core collaboration and productivity platforms. This individual will be responsible for the architecture, integrations, security, roadmap, operational excellence, and AI enablement of platforms including Google Workspace, Slack, Atlassian, and Zoom. The ideal candidate is both deeply technical and highly collaborative, with a passion for improving employee productivity through modern enterprise tooling, automation, and AI-driven workflows. This person should be a true expert in these platforms, proactively driving innovation, adoption, governance, and organizational enablement across the company.

Serve as the technical owner and subject matter expert for enterprise collaboration and productivity platforms including Google Workspace, Slack, Atlassian (Jira/Confluence), Zoom, Okta, and associated AI capabilities

Develop and communicate platform roadmaps, governance standards, and long-term strategies for supported applications

Own platform health, integrations, lifecycle management, licensing, and operational excellence

Stay current on vendor releases, AI enhancements, security updates, and industry best practices to continuously improve the employee experience

Lead the rollout, adoption, and organizational training of AI-powered workplace technologies including Gemini, Slack AI, Zoom AI Companion, Atlassian Rovo, and related tools

Create and maintain technical documentation, user training materials, and best practices for supported platforms

Partner cross-functionally with Security, HR, Engineering, Procurement, Legal, and business stakeholders to deliver scalable and secure solutions

Act as the escalation point for complex platform issues, integrations, automation opportunities, and optimization efforts

Drive workflow automation and operational improvements through integrations, scripting, and AI-driven solutions

Manage enterprise application projects and communicate platform updates, enhancements, and changes to technical and non-technical audiences

What We’re Looking For

3-5+ years of experience in Systems Administration, Enterprise Applications Administration or SaaS Platform Management

Deep hands-on experience administering and supporting enterprise collaboration platforms including Google Workspace, Slack, Atlassian (Jira/Confluence), Zoom and related SaaS applications

Experience owning enterprise application strategy, roadmaps, governance, lifecycle management, vendor relationships, licensing, and platform optimization initiatives

Experience implementing and managing AI-powered workplace technologies such as Gemini, Slack AI, Zoom AI Companion, Atlassian Rovo, or similar productivity and automation platforms

Proven ability to lead organizational AI enablement initiatives including user training, adoption programs, documentation, and change management efforts

Strong understanding of emerging technologies, vendor feature releases, and industry best practices with the ability to proactively drive innovation and continuous improvement

Strong organizational, communication, and stakeholder management skills with the ability to communicate technical concepts, roadmaps, and platform changes to both technical and non-technical audiences

Experience creating and maintaining technical documentation including SOPs, knowledge base articles, architecture diagrams, workflow diagrams, and end-user training materials

Strong project management and prioritization skills with the ability to independently own and execute multiple initiatives in fast-paced, high-growth environments supporting global teams

Experience partnering cross-functionally with Security, HR, Engineering, Legal, Procurement, and business stakeholders to deliver scalable technology solutions

Passion for improving employee productivity, collaboration, and user experience through technology, automation, and AI-driven solutions

About Scopely

Scopely is a leading video game and global interactive entertainment company, home to many of the world’s most beloved and enduring experiences, including two of the most successful mobile games of all-time “MONOPOLY GO!” and “Pokémon GO,” along with “Stumble Guys,” “Star Trek™ Fleet Command,” “MARVEL Strike Force,” “WWE Champions,” the Scrabble® franchise, “Yahtzee® With Buddies,” and many others. Across mobile, web, PC, and console, Scopely creates, develops, publishes, and live-operates one of the most diversified and award-winning portfolios in the games industry — bringing hundreds of millions of players together through a shared love of play.

Founded in 2011, Scopely is powered by its exceptional team — including thousands of world-class gamemakers around the globe, a distinctive tenet-driven culture, and its proprietary technology platform, Playgami. Together, these strengths have fueled Scopely’s position as the #1 mobile games company in the U.S. and #2 globally, generating more than $10 billion in lifetime revenue. Whether building global sensations like “MONOPOLY GO!” from the ground up, or expanding through strategic acquisitions, including the FoxNext, GSN, and Scopely Explore games businesses — Scopely consistently delivers experiences players love today and return to for years to come.

Recognized multiple times as one of the "100 Most Influential Companies in the World" by TIME magazine and one of Fast Company s "World s Most Innovative Companies" and “Best Workplaces for Innovators,” Scopely believes that video games can be a force for good — creating meaningful connections, vibrant communities, and making life better through play.

Scopely has global operations and partners across four continents in more than a dozen countries worldwide. For more information, visit: https://www.scopely.com/ .
