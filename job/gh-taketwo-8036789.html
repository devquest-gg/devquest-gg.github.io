THE CHALLENGE

We are looking for a Security Operations Engineer to support, maintain, and contribute to our Security Orchestration, Automation, and Response (SOAR) platform. In this role, you ll work closely with senior/lead engineers and play an active part in expanding our security automation capabilities. You will gain hands-on experience with a diverse set of technologies, including various security tools, APIs, and cloud services, helping to streamline our security operations and enhance our incident response efficiency. Your daily efforts will directly contribute to safeguarding Take-Two and its labels systems, networks, and data.

You will work with Take-Two s Information Security teams, reporting to the Director of Security Operations Engineering.

WHAT YOU’LL TAKE ON

SOAR Platform Support: Assist in the development, testing, and maintenance of SOAR playbooks, workflows, and integrations under the guidance of senior team members.

Automation Execution: Help identify repetitive, manual tasks within security operations and work to implement automated solutions that improve response speeds.

Collaboration and Learning: Partner with other security analysts to understand their workflows, acting as a collaborative peer while actively developing your own technical skills.

Documentation and Maintenance: Create and maintain clear, comprehensive documentation for automation workflows, scripts, and tool integrations.

System Health: Assist with basic SOAR platform maintenance, routine updates, tool migrations, and logging system changes

WHAT YOU BRING

Experience: 1–3 years of experience in a Security Engineering level role. Previous experience working as a Security Operations Center (SOC) analyst or IT support technician is a strong plus.

Technical Foundational Knowledge: A solid understanding of core security concepts and exposure to how tools like SOAR, SIEM, EDR, and threat intelligence platforms operate.

Scripting Skills: Foundational scripting skills in Python (or similar languages) with the ability to read, troubleshoot, and write basic scripts for API integrations.

Analytical Mindset: Strong problem-solving skills with a natural curiosity for breaking down manual workflows into logical, step-by-step processes.

Communication Skills: Clear written and verbal communication skills, with the ability to document technical workflows accurately and collaborate effectively with teammates.

Great to Have

Certifications: Entry-to-intermediate industry certifications such as CompTIA Security+, CySA+, GIAC Information Security Fundamentals (GISF), or GSEC.

Tool Exposure: Familiarity or academic/lab experience with a leading SOAR platform (such as Palo Alto Networks Cortex XSOAR, Tines, or Splunk SOAR).

Additional Scripting: Exposure to programming and scripting languages like Go, Bash, PowerShell, or JavaScript.

Security Frameworks: Basic familiarity with security frameworks like MITRE ATT CK or NIST.
