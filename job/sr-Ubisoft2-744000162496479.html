**Final designation will depend on candidate’s profile, experience, and selection outcome** Role Purpose: The Associate Director - Quality is responsible for leading the quality control portfolio while driving studio-wide process excellence, operational efficiency, and strategic transformation initiatives.

This role also combines project management, process transformation, and operational governance to ensure high-quality delivery across multiple projects in specialized teams (Technical Standards Division-TSD) & Mobile. The position partners closely with QC Leadership, Production teams, and WWQC stakeholders to standardize processes, optimize workforce utilization, improve delivery effectiveness, and embed a culture of continuous improvement and data-driven decision-making.

Conduct periodic project reviews with QC leadership to evaluate project performance and risk.

Monitor project KPIs and performance indicators to ensure quality and delivery effectiveness.

Validate key project deliverables including test strategies, test plans, and automation roadmaps.

Strategic Leadership & Transformation Drive large-scale transformation initiatives aligned with studio and global QC strategies.

Partner with QC leadership to define and execute studio-level strategic roadmaps.

Identify opportunities to improve testing frameworks, tools, processes, and delivery models.

Adapt QC strategies to evolving game development trends such as GAAS and live operations.

Interface with WWQC leadership, production teams, and studio management to ensure strategic alignment.

&#xa0;Process Excellence & Continuous Improvement Lead process re-engineering and simplification initiatives across QC and delivery functions.

Standardize QC and delivery workflows through defined process journeys and SOPs.
