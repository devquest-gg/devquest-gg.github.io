Role Overview

Reporting to the Associate Director of HR Operations, the HR Operations Manager, Northern Central Europe will be responsible for HR Operations in Poland and lead the German HR Operations Specialist based in Berlin. The role will have particular responsibility for Poland, being the country s HR Operations lead and key point of contact, with ownership of local compliance, employment practices and regulatory developments. As the region grows with expected new entities in Lithuania and Serbia in early 2027 this role will support an employee population of approximately 250.

As a people manager, the role will lead and develop a team of HR Operations Specialists, while remaining actively involved in day-to-day operational delivery where required. A key focus will be driving operational excellence through process improvement, automation and the effective use of AI, ensuring efficient, compliant and high-quality HR services that deliver an excellent employee experience across the region.

This role would suit someone detail-oriented, proactive, and collaborative, with solid local HR experience and a passion for people. The position is hybrid, with 4 days per week in our Warsaw office.

Key Responsibilities

Act as the hands-on HR Operations lead for Poland, taking an active role in day-to-day operational delivery, resolving complex queries and supporting the team with operational challenges as required.

Manage, coach and develop a team of HR Operations Specialists across the region ensuring clear accountability, effective workload management and consistently high standards of service delivery.

Oversee HR Operations across Poland, Germany, Lithuania and Serbia (approx. 250 employees), covering the employee lifecycle, benefits, payroll, compliance and employee relations, and act as the escalation point for complex cases.

Ensure the team delivers accurate, timely and compliant HR Operations support while maintaining a strong focus on employee experience and operational efficiency.

Partner closely with HR Business Partners, Finance, Legal and local stakeholders to ensure effective coordination, consistent application of policies and timely resolution of employee and operational matters.

Partner with external providers (payroll, benefits, legal counsel) and local stakeholders to ensure accurate, timely and compliant delivery in each country.

Lead on Poland-specific compliance and regulatory change while guiding and supporting the wider team in navigating local employment legislation and regulatory requirements across their respective jurisdictions. Bringing recommendations and practical solutions that balance compliance obligations with the operational needs of Tripledot Studios.

Proactively identify and address operational risks, compliance gaps and process inefficiencies, escalating material concerns where appropriate and recommending pragmatic solutions.

Drive continuous improvement, automation and the use of AI to streamline processes, improve data quality and reduce manual work across the region while ensuring our people have the best possible experience.

Contribute to group and region wide HR Operations initiatives, bringing local expertise and practical insight to support the development, implementation and continuous improvement of consistent, scalable HR processes.

Required Skills, Knowledge and Expertise

Proven experience managing a HR Operations team, across Central and Northern European countries.

Strong working knowledge of Polish employment law, including contracts, terminations, working time, and contractor (B2B) engagement.

Familiarity with employment practices in Germany, Lithuania and/or Serbia is a plus.

Experience working in a multinational environment, ideally in gaming or tech.

Solid understanding of payroll, benefits administration and compliance processes, and experience managing external vendors.

Experience with HRIS and workflow tools, and a track record of improving processes through automation or AI.

Ability to handle complex employee relations matters with sound judgement and discretion.

Fluent English and Polish (required).
