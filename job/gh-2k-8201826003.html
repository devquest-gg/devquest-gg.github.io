WHO WE ARE

Founded in 2023, Small Axe Studios is 2K’s newest stand-alone studio, built to push the boundaries of game development and bring 2K’s next groundbreaking sports franchise to life. Headquartered in Burnaby, we are assembling a world-class team of visionaries, builders, and passionate gamers who thrive on creative collaboration, technical excellence, and bold ideas. At Small Axe Studios, we believe big things start with sharp focus—and we’re crafting an exceptional, player-first experience from the ground up. If you’re ready to tackle unique challenges, leave your mark on a brand-new IP, and help shape the future of interactive sports entertainment, we want you on our team!

WHAT WE NEED

Our studio in Vancouver is seeking a Senior Audio / AI Software Engineer to join our development team in working on a new AAA Sports game. This role will partner with engineers from all game areas, audio artists, and designers. Within this role, you will be focused on writing new AI analysis logic, tools, and workflows to support the audio design team.

WHAT YOU’LL DO

Implement and debug core C++ game logic for audio features, such as dynamic crowd reactions, player-specific commentary, and contextual sound effects (SFX)

Work closely with Gameplay Engineers (AI, Animation) and Audio Designers to integrate new audio systems with game behavior and animation pipelines

Drive development of tools and workflows that leverage AI analysis to provide necessary data for the Audio team

Contribute to a large, performant C++ codebase, participating in code reviews and embracing a culture of technical excellence and continuous improvement

WHAT WILL MAKE YOU A GREAT FIT

Bachelor’s degree in Computer Science, or equivalent experience

5+ years of professional experience, or significant academic/personal project experience, writing C++ code for gameplay systems or large-scale applications

Proficiency in C++ and object-oriented programming is important, with a foundational understanding of game engine principles (ideally Unreal Engine)

Familiarity with AI, game/team-based behavior systems, and a passion for sports is a strong plus

Excellent 3D math and linear algebra skills

A commitment to writing clean, well-documented, and efficient code, paired with the ability to clearly communicate technical concepts to non-technical partners (Art, Design, Production)

BENEFICIAL QUALIFICATIONS

Experience developing and shipping AAA games on Unreal Engine 4 and 5

Familiarity with Python and/or C#
