Get to know the role

Job Title: Senior IT Support Specialist (Bilingual - Spanish/English)

Job Summary: This role provides support to end users for computer, application, system, device, access and hardware issues.

FLSA Classification (US Only): Non-exempt

People Manager: No

What you’ll do

Prepares help desk incident reports and assist in hardware and software evaluation.

Identifies, researches and resolves the most complex technical problems.

Responds to telephone, email and online requests for technical support.

Documents, tracks, and monitors the problem using applicable systems and tools.

Coordinates with other teams or specialists to resolve an issue.

What you’ll bring

Four (4) to six plus (6+) years of related experience.

Experience working in IT Operations including knowledge of common, tools, methods, and techniques.

Languages: Excellent verbal and written communication skills in both English and Latin American Spanish, at near-native level.

Basic knowledge of TCP/IP, ISP, routers and network protocols.

Experience in current Internet network hardware and software technologies.

Proficiency in Microsoft Office Suite.
