ABOUT YOU

We are looking for a talented Full Stack Developer to join our Communications Platform team at Xsolla! In this role, you will build and enhance our centralized communication solution that connects businesses with gamers and partners through messaging tools like chat widgets and popular messaging apps.

You will work with modern technologies including React.js, Node.js, and Nest.js to create interactive user interfaces and robust server-side solutions. You will take ownership of projects, modules, and components, ensuring seamless integration across Xsolla's product ecosystem while maintaining reliability and performance.

We are looking for an experienced, self-driven developer who thrives in a collaborative global environment and is passionate about building scalable, user-focused solutions using modern development practices.

ABOUT US

Xsolla is a global commerce company with robust tools and services to help developers solve the inherent challenges of the video game industry. From indie to AAA, companies partner with Xsolla to help them fund, distribute, market, and monetize their games. Grounded in the belief in the future of video games, Xsolla is resolute in the mission to bring opportunities together, and continually make new resources available to creators. Headquartered and incorporated in Los Angeles, California, Xsolla operates as the merchant of record and has helped over 1,500+ game developers to reach more players and grow their businesses around the world. With more paths to profits and ways to win, developers have all the things needed to enjoy the game.

For more information, visit xsolla.com.
