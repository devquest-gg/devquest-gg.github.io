Job Overview

This Game Rules Specialist role draws on your deep Magic rules expertise and ability to envision everything the game was, is, and can be. Part technical writer, part game designer, and part advisor, this role is unlike any other job. As a member of our vibrant and collaborative Rules Management team, you’ll serve as a bridge between game designers, editors, and engineers so new cards and mechanics work cleanly on every platform and in every format. Players love reading new Magic cards, and you play a crucial role in what awaits them!

Location and Work Arrangement

This role is based at our Renton, Washington office and follows a hybrid schedule designed to support in-person collaboration. Employees are expected to work onsite on Tuesdays, Wednesdays, and Thursdays, with the flexibility to work remotely or onsite on Mondays and Fridays.

What You ll Do

Build tomorrow s Magic rules today. Ensure new ideas work within the existing rules wherever possible, and amend existing rules or write new ones to support novel and ambitious designs.

Collaborate with design, editorial, and digital teams. Support constant innovation, answering questions about how cards work and how they could work differently to support both paper and digital play.

Manage and lead digital reviews. Partner with game designers and digital developers, advocating for inventive and fun solutions to complex problems.

Educate our players. Write and update the official documents and articles that keep our players in the know.

Steward Magic’s past, present, and future. Maintain the Oracle database and Comprehensive Rules, and build accurate and detailed documentation to support future game mechanics.

What You ll Bring

3+ years of experience in technical writing, game rules writing, technical game design, or similar in a professional setting.

Professional-level knowledge of the Magic Comprehensive Rules and card templates, demonstrated via Magic event experience, rules-related content creation, or other comparable experience or certifications.

A proven ability to present technical material in plain language that’s easy to understand.

Positive, engaging, and clear communication skills, with experience collaborating with cross-functional teams to improve a shared vision.

Flexibility, adaptability, and creativity, with a constant appetite for knowledge. Ability to manage multiple tasks and projects simultaneously.

Self-motivated curiosity, active listening, and a diverse toolbox of leadership skills.

Compassion, empathy, and a deep understanding of people.

What Sets You Apart

Experience judging Professional Rules Enforcement Level Magic events.

Familiarity with Smartsheet, Microsoft Office (especially Word and Excel), and/or Confluence.

Bilingual in Japanese a plus.

Notice Regarding Hasbro’s use of AI in the Recruitment Process

We use Greenhouse’s AI-powered Talent Matching tool to compare your application against our job requirements.

How it works: Talent Matching compares information from your resume and application responses with job-related criteria established and weighted by Hasbro’s recruiting and hiring teams. Depending on the role, these criteria may include relevant skills, work experience, prior job titles, and industry experience. The tool then assigns a match category to help organize applications for review.

Human oversight: Talent Matching supports — but does not replace — human review. It does not automatically advance or reject candidates or make employment decisions. Hasbro’s recruiters and hiring managers review applications and remain responsible for all hiring decisions.
