Why Sony Interactive Entertainment?

Sony Interactive Entertainment isn’t just the Best Place to Play — it’s also the Best Place to Work. Sony Interactive Entertainment (SIE) is the company behind the PlayStation brand. As a subsidiary of Sony Group Corporation, we’re part of a proud legacy of innovation and excellence. SIE is a dynamic technology company, delivering cutting-edge hardware and network services to more than 100 million people and an entertainment leader, home to some of the most beloved and recognizable intellectual properties (IP) in the world. Our role at SIE is to create and nurture the experiences under the PlayStation brand, a name synonymous with entertainment excellence and creativity.

About the Role

We are seeking a Senior Business Analyst to work on complex, high-impact exploration efforts and shape how early-stage analytics and hypothesis development are conducted. This role will focus on analytics projects that cross business lines and provide a holistic SIE view and, working closely with the PlayStation store team, there’ll be significant focus on trading performance, promotions and pricing and customer purchasing behaviour.

This role operates at the intersection of business, analytics and data science, translating complex, ambiguous business challenges into structured analytical approaches and actionable insights. Working as an embedded analytical partner within cross-functional project squads, the Senior Business Analyst will proactively identify opportunities, investigate performance drivers and provide insights into business outcomes. The role will collaborate with Data Science on experimentation and measurement, and work with BI Analysts and Data Engineering to support the development of scalable reporting and automated analytical solutions.

If you have significant experience turning complex business problems and processes into clear analytical requirements and working across teams to deliver effective solutions, we’d love to hear from you.

Key Responsibilities

Exploration Hypothesis Development

Lead exploration of complex, ambiguous problem spaces that span multiple teams or domains.

Experience with experimentation concepts (A/B testing, causal inference) and/or forecasting and scenario modeling.

Identify high-impact opportunities and work with team to shape them into structured, scalable analytical initiatives.

Drive clarity in areas with incomplete or evolving business requirements.

Rapid Prototyping Analytical Insight

Develop advanced SQL-based analyses, analytical datasets and prototypes to assess feasibility and potential impact.

Investigate performance trends, quantify contributing factors and explain underlying business outcomes.

Translate analytical findings into actionable insights and recommendations to inform business decisions, considering how best to evaluate their impact.

Stakeholder Collaboration

Align with Integrated Analytics partners and business stakeholders to understand priorities and define problem definitions.

Act as an embedded analytical partner within cross-functional project squads.

Communicate insights clearly and effectively to both technical and non-technical audiences.

Gather feedback and incorporate it into ongoing analysis.

Partnership with Data Science Product

Work closely with Data Scientists and Product Managers to ensure a strong pipeline of well-defined opportunities.

Support teams in navigating complex transitions from exploration to experimentation.

Collaborate with Data Science to shape experimentation opportunities, apply measurement frameworks and interpret results.

Partner with business and Product teams to evaluate initiatives and navigate complex transitions from exploration to phases of experimentation.

Translate validated analyses into requirements for Data Engineering pipelines and BI reporting solutions.

Standards Best Practices

Influence and promote best practices for early-stage analytics, including hypothesis development, exploratory analysis, and rapid prototyping.

Support the development of reusable frameworks, templates, and approaches to improve efficiency and consistency.

Leverage AI-enabled tools to accelerate analysis, automate repetitive tasks and improve the efficiency and accessibility of analytical insights.

As an experienced individual contributor, you’ll support team members through best practice sharing and guidance.

Qualifications and Education Requirements

Bachelor’s degree in Business, Data Science, Computer Science, or a related field.

7+ years of experience in analytics, data science, or a related field.

Proven experience navigating complex, ambiguous analytical problem spaces across teams or domains.

Advanced proficiency in analytical tools such as SQL, Python, R, or similar with deep understanding of structured data and data flows.

Demonstrated ability or strong willingness to leverage AI-enabled tools (e.g., generative or agentic AI) to enhance analytical workflows, accelerate insight generation, and improve decision support.

Demonstrated ability to influence stakeholders and drive alignment across cross-functional teams.

Preferred Skills

Proven ability to synthesize complex analyses into clear, strategic insights for senior stakeholders.

Experience working across multiple teams to align approaches and drive consistency.

Familiarity with data science workflows and transitioning work into experimentation and modelling phases.

Experience mentoring analysts and improving team-level analytical capabilities.
