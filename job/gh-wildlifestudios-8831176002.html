About The Role

We re looking for a talented and passionate person to join Wildlife s Legal Team as Counsel.

We re looking for someone motivated, detail-oriented, and with solid experience in Corporate Law and Contracts to act as a strategic business partner, in a role with a predominantly international scope, including entities based in the United States, Brazil, Canada and Finland. The position offers autonomy to own processes end-to-end and direct contact with senior leadership on strategic company matters.

We know that the work we do has a high impact on our company s success and culture. The right person for this position is curious by nature, comfortable in an autonomous, take-the-initiative environment, loves solving problems, and can juggle multiple demands at once.

What You ll Do

Provide legal opinion in relation to the Corporate Area

Provide legal support to all Wildlife entities, in Brazil and abroad

Structure and apply corporate governance rules for the group’s entities

Draft and coordinate board resolutions and board meeting materials

Handle the entities annual corporate compliance obligations, in Brazil and abroad

Coordinate with external law firms across different jurisdictions

Review and negotiate contracts (NDAs, vendor, licensing, service provider, real estate, insurance agreements, intercompany agreements, financial agreements, etc)

Manage the Company’s contract management system and related platforms

Manage and support the stock option plan: grants, exercises, repurchases, consulting, etc, and provide legal advice with respect to equity-related matters

Manage the cap table and the shareholder register

Support audits and KYC/AML reviews

Work closely with many different areas of the Company, such as Finance, Tax, Controllership, Facilities, Engineering, Live Games

Build playbooks and automation initiatives applied to the Company

Structure, prepare and review managerial reports and documents

What You ll Need

Solid experience in Corporate Law and contracts

Prior experience with stock option plans

Law degree and active Brazilian Bar (OAB) membership

Working proficiency in English, to independently handle international documents and meetings

Autonomous, organized profile, comfortable managing multiple projects simultaneously

Clear and transparent communication, keeping the team updated on work status

Team builder

Be a business partner and business oriented

Strong social skills

Experience with M A transactions (a plus)

Experience with foreign entities and international jurisdictions (a plus)

Background at startups, tech companies, or law firms with a venture capital practice (a plus)

LLM in Corporate Law (a plus)
