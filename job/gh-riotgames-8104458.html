Senior Revenue Accountant

Riot Games · Seoul

Senior Other Posted 2026-08-05 Full time
Apply on Riot Games’s site →

Riot Games was established in 2006 by entrepreneurial gamers who believe that player-focused game development can result in great games.In 2009, Riot released its debut title, League of Legends, to critical and player acclaim. Since then, Riot has expanded its portfolio and become a leader in global esports. Players form the foundation of our community, and it’s for them that we continue to evolve and improve the player experience. We are building a collaborative, tech-savvy accounting team that partners closely with local and global stakeholders to support Riot’s growing business in Korea. We are not just focused on recording transactions. We help teams operate with clarity, discipline, and strong financial judgment.

That’s where you come in.

As a Senior Revenue Accountant, you will support the revenue accounting operations of Riot Games Korea. You will own key operational workstreams related to billing, accounts receivable, collections, and month-end close, while also supporting revenue recognition, reporting, and analytical review activities across key revenue streams, with a primary focus on game revenue and esports revenue. This is a hands-on role that requires strong communication, high attention to detail, and the ability to work effectively with both local and global teams. As a Rioter in Seoul, you’ll join a tight-knit group of passionate professionals who care deeply about serving players in Korea and around the world.

You will report to Sr. Manager, Accounting.

Responsibilities

Own the day-to-day revenue operations for Riot Games Korea, including invoicing, accounts receivable monitoring, collection follow-up, and related month-end close activities

Prepare and enter journal entries related to accounts receivable, revenue, cash receipts, and related adjustments within the established month-end reporting schedule

Prepare account reconciliations and monthly reporting packages for revenue-related accounts

Support the monthly revenue recognition and close process in accordance with company policies and reporting requirements

Perform analytical reviews of revenue-related balances, trends, and fluctuations, investigating unusual items and escalating or proposing solutions as needed

Manage the operational billing process by preparing invoices accurately and timely, maintaining billing support, and coordinating with internal stakeholders to resolve billing issues

Review accounts receivable aging regularly, follow up on overdue balances, and support timely cash collection and issue resolution

Work with business teams to ensure revenue-related supporting documents are complete, accurate, and aligned with internal policies

Support the review of related-party and intercompany transactions that affect billing, settlements, or revenue-related balances

Collaborate with cross-functional stakeholders including Accounting, Tax, Treasury, Legal, Payments, and Central teams to support revenue operations and improve process execution

Maintain clear and reliable documentation for revenue processes, reconciliations, and operational decisions

Identify process improvement opportunities to strengthen controls, reduce manual work, and improve the scalability of revenue and AR operations

Support external audit requests and local statutory reporting matters related to revenue and accounts receivable

Required Qualifications

Bachelor’s degree in Accounting, Finance, or a related field

3+ years of experience in accounting, audit, or a related finance role, with exposure to month-end close and account reconciliation processes

Excellent verbal and written communication skills, with the ability to work effectively across functions and regions

Excellent English communication skills, both written and spoken

Strong attention to detail, documentation discipline, and organizational skills

A curious and proactive mindset, with a strong willingness to ask the right questions to uncover the bigger picture behind operational and financial matters.

Ability to work independently, manage multiple priorities, and follow through in a fast-paced environment

Positive, collaborative, and professional attitude, with the ability to build strong working relationships across teams

Coachable mindset with openness to direction, feedback, and continuous learning

Reliable execution and strong follow-through on assigned responsibilities

Desired Qualifications

CPA or equivalent professional certification preferred

Big 4 experience required

Knowledge of US GAAP or IFRS, particularly revenue recognition principles, is preferred

Experience supporting revenue accounting in a multi-entity or global business environment

Interest in the gaming, entertainment, digital platform, consumer products, or technology industries

Familiarity with ERP systems such as Oracle

Strong Excel skills and a genuine interest in tools, systems, and automation to improve accounting processes

Experience working with high-volume transactions and operational revenue processes

For this role, you ll find success through craft expertise, a collaborative spirit, and decision-making that prioritizes the delight of players. We will be looking at your past studies, experience, and your personal relationship with games. If you embody player empathy and care about players experiences, this could be your role!
