Location: Hong Kong Type: 12-month contract (with possibility of conversion to full-time permanent role)

The Role

As a Software Engineer in Test at Nex, you will own stability and reliability testing for the Motion Development Kit (MDK), firmware, and hardware modules that power Nex Playground s AOSP-based motion-sensing device. You will build automated test frameworks, stress/soak testing infrastructure, and validation tools that catch instability, regressions, and hardware-integration failures before they reach production. You will work closely with the Framework Engineering and firmware/hardware teams to embed testability across the full Android stack, from low-level sensor drivers and HAL layers up through the MDK APIs that game developers build on .

This role sits within a small, highly technical team that values both specialization and collaboration, with clear ownership of quality across core technology areas .

The Mindset

You are drawn to solving hard reliability problems in systems where hardware, firmware, and Android software all have to work together flawlessly, day after day. You care deeply about long-run stability, not just passing a test once, but proving a system holds up under sustained load, edge cases, and real-world conditions. You thrive in environments where deep systems debugging, Android/AOSP internals knowledge, and rigorous testing discipline directly shape what the platform can confidently ship .

What You ll Do

Design and build automated stability and reliability test frameworks for the Motion Development Kit (MDK), firmware, and hardware modules on an AOSP-based platform

Develop soak testing, stress testing, and endurance testing pipelines to surface memory leaks, crashes, thermal issues, ANRs, and long-running failures

Build test harnesses and hardware-in-the-loop (HIL) rigs to validate sensor, camera, and motion-tracking hardware under real and simulated conditions

Create regression suites for firmware, HAL, and MDK releases, integrated into CI/CD to catch stability issues pre-release

Investigate, reproduce, and drive root-cause analysis of field failures, crashes, kernel panics, and intermittent hardware/firmware defects using Android bug reports, logcat, and kernel logs

Define and track reliability metrics such as MTBF, crash rates, ANR rates, and failure recurrence across hardware revisions and firmware/AOSP builds

Partner with firmware, hardware, and platform engineers to define quality gates and exit criteria for new hardware/firmware/AOSP releases

Write clean, well-tested test code and tooling that raises the engineering bar across the team

Build dashboards and reporting for test coverage, defect trends, and release stability

Participate in code reviews and contribute to shared tools, infrastructure, and cross-role projects (20% Time)

Work with the dual-leadership model (Engineering Manager and Tech Lead) to align on quality priorities and technical direction

Document test strategies, systems, and findings to support team knowledge sharing

What You Bring

3+ years of professional experience in software test engineering, reliability engineering, or QA for embedded/firmware, Android, or hardware-integrated systems

Proficiency in Python, with experience in at least one systems language (C++, C#, Java, Rust, or Go)

Hands-on experience with stability, stress, soak, or endurance testing methodologies

Experience testing Android/AOSP platforms, HAL, embedded firmware, device drivers, or hardware modules (sensors, motion tracking, cameras, or similar)

Strong debugging skills across hardware, firmware, and software layers, including logcat, kernel logs, bug reports, logic analyzers, or serial/hardware diagnostics

Experience building or maintaining CI/CD pipelines and automated regression suites

Preferred

Experience with AOSP build systems (Soong/Make), Android Test Harness, or Vendor Test Suite (VTS)/Compatibility Test Suite (CTS)

Experience with hardware-in-the-loop (HIL) test rig design and automation

Familiarity with embedded Linux, Android kernel internals, and Treble/HAL architecture

Knowledge of communication protocols (I2C, SPI, UART, BLE) used in sensor and firmware integration

Experience with reliability metrics and frameworks (MTBF, FMEA, fault injection testing)

Familiarity with motion sensing, IMUs, computer vision, or signal processing validation

Experience working in small, high-performance technical teams

Background in consumer electronics, robotics, or Android-based hardware products

Passion for pushing reliability standards and deep root-cause problem-solving
