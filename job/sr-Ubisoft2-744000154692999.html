Human Resources Advisor

Ubisoft Quebec · Quebec City

Senior People & Ops Posted 2026-10-09 Full time

Job Description As a Human Resources Advisor at Ubisoft Quebec, you will be the main point of contact for employees and managers in a shared services model. In a dynamic environment with a high volume of requests, you will provide first-line support, practical guidance and tailored solutions across a wide range of human resources questions and processes. You will help deliver a consistent, efficient employee experience focused on service excellence, while supporting managers in their responsibilities for talent and team management.

What you’ll do: Answer employee and manager questions about human resources policies, programs and processes; Advise managers on employment laws, employment standards and team management; Guide managers on performance management, salary recommendations and employee development; Help managers address performance and workplace conduct concerns; Maintain employee records and check documents for accuracy and compliance; Support absence, disability and accommodation cases with relevant partners; Present human resources services and programs to new employees and managers in French and English; Work with other teams to improve human resources tools, processes and initiatives; Help meet legal obligations and maintain good human resources practices.

What you bring to the team: Apply your human resources experience to advising managers and employees; Work collaboratively with a service-focused approach; Communicate clearly and build trusting relationships, preferably in French and English to support talent across our Canadian studios; Bring attention to detail, sound judgment and a sense of responsibility; Organize your work flexibly and set priorities.
