Regular/Senior UI/UX Designer

We are looking for a passionate , experienced UI/UX Designer to join our team and elevate the player experience with intuitive , engaging interfaces. As a UX/UI Designer, you will drive design initiatives, from concept to implementation, ensuring seamless integration within Unreal Engine. This hands-on role involves close collaboration with cross-functional teams to deliver high-quality design that enhances gameplay and usability.

Key Responsibilities:

Feature Design : Design new features and game systems with the player’s needs in mind. Create user flows, information architecture and wireframes.

Prototyping in Figma : Create interactive prototypes for PC and consoles, and test them with the team.

High-Fidelity Asset Creation : Prepare polished UI mockups and assets in Photoshop/Illustrator that follow our art style and design system.

Assets in Engine : Prepare assets for Unreal Engine, including texture atlases.

UMG : Improve UI layouts and animations in UMG .

Research & Testing : Work with the UX Research team on playtests and use the results to improve your designs.

Design System : Help us keep the design system consistent and up to date.

Requirements:

3+ years of UI/UX experience (at least 1 year in game development, Unreal Engine preferred).

Game Design Basics : Good understanding of gameplay loops, feedback loops and accessibility.

Wireframing : Strong wireframing skills and the ability to explain your design decisions.

Figma : Good knowledge of Figma, including prototyping.

Photoshop/Illustrator : Very good skills in creating high-fidelity UI mockups and assets.

User-Centered Design : Strong in usability testing and feature ideation.

Collaborative & Passionate : Love working with teams and staying updated on gaming trends.

Strong Aesthetic Sense: Ability to create visually appealing and cohesive designs.

Passion for Games : Enthusiastic about both creating and playing games.

English : B2 level in speaking and writing.

Polish : B1/B2 level.

On-site work : Willingness to work from our office in Katowice three days a week. This is not a fully remote position.

Pluses:

Unreal Engine experience in UMG animations and simple UI visual effects.

Playtesting : Experience in preparing or supporting playtests.

Experience with version control software (Perforce preferred).

Multiplayer Game Experience: Track record in creating multiplayer games.

Multiplatform Development : Experience developing games for PC and consoles.

Salary:

(depending on skills and experience)

Contract of Employment: 7 550 – 13 800 PLN (gross) or equivalent on a B2B basis

We Offer:

Flexible Work Hours: Core hours 10:00 – 15:00.

Hybrid Work Model: 3 days in office, up to 2 days remote.

Private healthcare and life insurance.

Co-financed Multisport Cards.

English Lessons.

Relocation Assistance (optional).

No Crunch, No Non-Compete Policy.

Pet-Friendly Office.

Co-financed Participation in Conferences/Workshops (treated as work days).

Submission / Portfolio Instructions:

CV : Required.

Cover Letter: Optional.

Portfolio/Reel: Demonstrating relevant skills is required.

Test: Must be willing to complete a test if requested.
