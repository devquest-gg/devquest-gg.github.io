Position Title: Senior Game Producer

Studio/Department: Everquest Legends/Production

Employee Type: Full Time

Location: San Diego, CA or Remote from approved states: CA, TX, WA, MA, NY

Salary Range: $ 110,000 - $130,000 (USD)

THE OPPORTUNITY

The Senior Producer will be responsible for overseeing and managing the game development process for EverQuest Legends. This position will be reporting to the Executive Producer (EP) and will be responsible for:

Project Management Planning

Execute on game vision, design, and roadmap under the direction of EP

Build, maintain, and adjust development roadmaps, sprint plans, and milestone schedules

Work with the development team to ensure that all major engineering and design updates are documented in Confluence and tracked and updated in Jira

Ensure that engineering, art, and design efforts remain on schedule, that milestones are met, and that the game adheres to the approved design documents

Identify production bottlenecks or technical roadblocks early and actively remove team blockers to keep development moving from pitch to gold master

Cross-Functional Coordination

Bridge gaps between engineering, art, design, audio, QA, marketing, and external partners, ensuring alignment on what ships and when

Coordinate with publishing team on marketing plan, visual assets, gameplay capture, website updates, community management, and other publishing needs for the game

Stakeholder Communication

Serve as the primary communication link between the day-to-day development team and upper management or publishing executives

Communicate effectively with the development team to ensure that game development remains on track and adheres to design plan

Communicate effectively with EP and provide regular status updates on game development and obtain necessary approvals

Resource Budget Allocation

Track financial resources, manage contracts, control scope creep, and prevent project delays

Assist EP in setting the budget and managing the development team to ensure budget is met and updated as necessary

Assist EP in determining staffing needs and lead the hiring effort

Requirements/Preferences

Relevant/related Game Producer experience of 4 years.

SCRUM Master certification or equivalent work experience.

Strong project/program management skills.

Strong experience with JIRA/Agile/Waterfall/SCRUM development with hard deadlines.
