Senior Technical Producer - Central Technology, Data Foundations

Riot Games · Los Angeles

Senior Production $185K–$258K Posted 2026-08-04 Full time
Apply on Riot Games’s site →

As Technical Producers at Riot, we lead passionate technical teams and initiatives to create products and experiences that maximize developer and player value. We guide development, clear blockers, and help teams deliver in a sustainable way while creating a strong collaborative environment.

As a Senior Technical Producer on the Data Foundations (DF) Business Operations team, you will be a key steward of how the data platform organization operates — driving portfolio, vendor, and financial management. You will report to the Manager, DF Business Operations, and partner closely with DF engineering leads and other data / technology leaders across Riot.

Responsibilities:

Own the Data Foundations (DF) portfolio as a live, trusted picture of what s on track, at risk, or off course, and drive commitments to clarity before they become risks.

Own the strategy, posture, and renewal planning across DF s vendor portfolio — including our largest platform investments — and establish a durable operating model with Procurement, Purchasing, Finance, TSO, and Legal.

Partner with Finance to give leadership real-time, trusted visibility into DF s budget, and ensure DF meets its forecasting, capitalization, and financial reporting obligations.

Identify opportunities for DF and other related data disciplines across Riot to operate more effectively — whether streamlining internal operations, improving how we surface value to customers, or strengthening the business case for our investments and standards.

Represent DF Business Operations in cross-Riot data forums and stakeholder rituals, growing into ownership over time.

Required Qualifications:

Bachelor s degree in a related field, or equivalent experience.

Minimum of 6 years of experience as a Producer, Technical Program Manager, Chief of Staff, Business Operations lead, or comparable role in a technical organization.

Experience owning portfolio, vendor, or financial operations for a technical or platform team.

Excellent communication — able to translate complex operational detail into a clear "so what" for senior leaders.

Demonstrated ability to improve operating practices across teams and functions, increasing clarity, efficiency, and execution over time.

Experience applying AI and automation to streamline organizational workflows, accelerate decision-making, and increase operating leverage.

Desired Qualifications:

Experience supporting a data, developer, or infrastructure platform.

SaaS vendor management at scale (renewal strategy, contract prep, QBRs).

Finance partnership on budget forecasting, spend management, or capitalization.

Fluency with Jira, portfolio reporting tools, and lightweight BI (SQL / dashboards).

Prior experience in games or another creative-technical industry.

For this role, you ll find success through craft expertise, a collaborative spirit, and decision-making that prioritizes your fellow Rioters, who are the customers of your work. Being a dedicated fan of games is not necessary for this position!
