UI Designer

Wildlife Studios · São Paulo

Mid Design Hybrid Posted 2026-07-15 Full time
Apply on Wildlife Studios’s site →

We re looking for a talented and passionate Visual Designer to join Wildlife s Game Art Team in São Paulo.

The Game Art Team at Wildlife is growing. With an audience that numbers in the millions, our games have the power to impact countless lives. Our games connect people with interesting characters, vibrant worlds, and unforgettable experiences.

That s no small task, but we have a powerful secret weapon: our people. The Wildlife team is a collection of world-class talent, a team focused on synergy, collaboration, and growing strong together.

Our Visual Designers work on finding solutions to complex systems with a focus on visual clarity and design cohesiveness. The Visual Designers collaborate with artists, Product Managers, Engineering and creative leaders to establish the first and most important point of contact with the players, the User Interface (UI) and User Experience (UX). They also help build and maintain a solid visual identity for our IPs inside and outside our games.

We know that the work we do has a high impact on our company s success and culture. The right person for this position is curious by nature, and comfortable in a "take the initiative" environment, loves solving problems, and can thrive in a fast and growing business.

What you ll do

Craft: You will create the design of all elements related to user interfaces, including: menus, Illustrations, HUD, pop-ups, key visuals, UI style guides, Design System; you will also develop the UX of our games, including: prototypes, wireframe and mockups.

Collaboration: You will work closely with Art Directors to help establish a cohesive look for our games. You will also work with various other disciplines such as Product Managers, Engineers and Designers to deliver user-friendly experiences to our players.

Game Developer Mindset: You will create and implement optimized design assets with your understanding of how players experience content while working under tight deadlines.

Problem Solving: You will have the opportunity to work on different projects and use your skills to solve problems related to Visual Design and connected areas.

Communication: You will use your excellent communication skills in a collaborative and diverse work environment.

Branding: Support additional designs for game specific branding inside and outside the game.

What you ll need

Experience as a Visual Designer, UI Artist, UX/UI Designer or similar role.

Good oral and written communication skills in English.

A resume and an online portfolio showcasing your work.

Availability for hybrid work

Nice to Have

You have experience working in games; especially mobile titles

You have affinity with First-Person Shooter games

You know your way around at least one game engine (especially Unity) and understand the various parts of the game development pipeline.

You own a basic knowledge of motion graphics, illustration and 3d asset creation.

More about you

Craft Knowledge: You use your knowledge and taste in composition, colors, layout, and typography to create high quality design assets such features, menus, buttons, icons, etc. You also have experience building and maintaining Design System, Style Guides and Libraries.

Team Player: You are a self-motivated team player who proactively seeks feedback and shares knowledge.

Multidisciplinary: You are a visual designer with a multidisciplinary approach, you have illustration and UX design.

Visual Presentation: You care about presentation, you have an online portfolio with proven experience designing interfaces for products on mobile platforms.

Software Expertise: You know your way through Adobe Creative Suite and are curious to learn new software. You are also up to date with screen design tools, such as Figma.

About Wildlife

Wildlife is one of the leading mobile game developers and publishers in the world. We have released more than 60 titles, reaching billions of people around the globe. Today, we have offices in Brazil and the United States. Here, we create games that will excite, intrigue, and engage our players for years to come!
