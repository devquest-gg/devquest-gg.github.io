Gameloft · Barcelona

Are you interested by video games and have a passion for this industry? Join our award winning creation team and take part in creating user-interface assets and menu designs for our multiplatform games.

Your work will leverage the art direction, dress up the user-experience flow and enhance the visual narrative of the game.

Working under the supervision of the UI/UX Director you will be a key part of the art production, collaborating with the user-experience and design teams. You will be working alongside experts in various disciplines, ranging from engineering to design, art and UX.&#xa0; Your work will take you on a technological, design and artistic journey across all gaming hardware platforms: from tablet and mobile to PC and home-consoles.&#xa0; Solid experience in composition, colour and production.

Solid experience in typography.

Solid expertise in Photoshop.

Solid expertise in Illustrator.

Solid expertise in photomontage and 2D special effects.

Solid expertise in drawing and touching up using a graphics tablet.

Able to produce original graphical compositions (framing, camera angles, etc.).

Ability to take graphical initiative is desired and appreciated.

Proactive and able to work independently.

Able to work in a team.

Very good general knowledge of visual and graphical trends (TV, movies, web, games, etc.).

Assets: Portfolio demonstrating your sense of artistry and finesse.

Good personal experience desired.

High level of English appreciated.

Finesse and high quality of work are expected.
