Sr Data Analyst - Driving Empire

Voldex · Remote - Canada · Remote

Senior Data & Analytics Remote Posted 2026-11-20 Full time
Apply on Voldex’s site →

Voldex is a leading gaming company focused on Roblox, the most exciting ecosystem in gaming today. We are home to several of Roblox's top games, including Brookhaven, Driving Empire, and NFL Universe Football. With over 160 million monthly active players across our games, we are one of the biggest Roblox studios by audience size. Founded in 2015, we have since grown to 95+ full-time remote team members across the globe.

ROLE OVERVIEW

🏎️ Driving Empire is the #1 driving game on Roblox, with around 20 million monthly players and 500+ million rows of data generated every day. We’re looking for a Senior Data Analyst to be the go-to expert on that data and a close partner to the team shaping the game’s future.

You’ll own Driving Empire’s analytics end to end, from designing telemetry and building data models to running A/B tests, creating dashboards, and maintaining our AI analytics agent. You’ll work side by side with product managers, game designers, and our partnerships, licensing, and marketing teams to turn player data into clear insights and recommendations. Your work won’t sit in a report: your analyses and tests will shape decisions that go live within days or weeks, not quarters. 🚀

It’s also a chance to work on a game that partners with world-famous automotive and entertainment brands, on one of the biggest UGC platforms in gaming. If you love data, games, and cars, and want to see your impact on millions of players quickly, this role is for you.

WHAT YOU’LL DO

- Partner closely with product managers and game designers, using data to shape feature design and game priorities

- Own the full A/B testing process: design, spec, set-up, analysis and recommendations

- Deliver post-mortem analyses and key learnings after major updates and live events, and suggest what to test next

- Run deep dives into player behaviour, such as churn, conversion, LTV and player segments

- Investigate changes in key KPIs and deliver clear root cause analyses

- Build dashboards and regular reports

- Contribute to the weekly Numbers Review alongside the PM, shared with the whole company

- Work with game designers on balancing, review balancing changes before release, and playtest and support releases

- Spec telemetry for upcoming features, and help improve how telemetry is documented, specced and checked

- Maintain and develop Driving Empire's dbt models and data structure

- Maintain and develop the semantic view, skills and AI analytics agent for Driving Empire, and keep them accurate

- Provide insights, projections and reporting for partnerships, franchise, licensing and marketing

- Take part in cross-game and team-wide projects, and bring new methods and tools to the team

WHO YOU ARE

- 3–5+ years of experience in a Data Analyst role

- 2+ years of games industry experience strongly preferred (mobile, F2P, live service, or UGC platforms)

- A product mindset: you understand how features affect player behaviour, engagement and monetisation, and use data to shape product decisions

- Deep understanding of core gaming KPIs (such as DAU, retention, ARPDAU, conversion and LTV) and what drives them, as well as game economies and monetisation techniques

- Deep A/B testing expertise, from design and sample sizing to analysis, interpreting both statistical significance and practical impact

- Experience with advanced analyses, such as cohort analysis, player segmentation, KPI forecasts and churn models

- Advanced SQL (complex queries, window functions, performance tuning) on large datasets

- Experience designing clear, complete telemetry that answers product and design questions

- Experience with cloud data warehouses (we use Snowflake, but Redshift, BigQuery, or similar is welcome)

- Proficiency with a data visualisation tool (we use Tableau, but Looker, Power BI, or similar is welcome)

- Strong Excel / Google Sheets skills for analysis

- Clear, accurate, well-organised written reports, adapted for technical and non-technical readers

Nice to Have

- Understanding of how AI agents and semantic views work

- Experience building scalable data models with dbt, including data quality tests and documentation

- Python proficiency for deeper analysis and automation

Bonus Points for

- Experience working on Roblox games, or familiarity with the Roblox platform

- Passion for racing and driving games

- Passion for cars!

TECH STACK

- SQL (primary tool)

- Snowflake (data warehouse)

- dbt (analytics engineering)

- Tableau (data visualisation and dashboards)

- Excel / Google Sheets

- Python (optional but beneficial)

- Git (version control for analytics code)

- AWS (cloud infrastructure)

- Claude and Claude Code
