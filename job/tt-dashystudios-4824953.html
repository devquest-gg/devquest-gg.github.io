Why Dashy? We believe in creating meaningful and memorable digital experiences that leave lasting impressions. As a consultant at Dashy, you will get the opportunity to work with some of the most inspiring digital entertainment companies in the world, within fields such as games, music, and other forms of interactive media. We strive to work with clients that build products and services that they, and we, can be truly proud of.

At Dashy our people are always in the center - together we build and grow the company with happiness as our guiding principle. We enable growth and personal development, with collaboration and knowledge-sharing as an integral part in everything we do.

As a Full-Stack Engineer at Dashy: Design, build, and iterate on systems and features across the stack for digital entertainment products such as gaming, video and music streaming.

Work closely with a cross-functional team of developers, designers, producers, and product managers.

Bring your own ideas to the table - we want people who push projects forward, not just execute tickets.

Support colleagues as they grow, and grow alongside them.

We're looking for: 3+ years of experience as a Full-Stack Engineer.

Backend experience with Java, C#, Kotlin, Golang or similar languages.

Strong React skills, plus comfort picking up other frontend frameworks.

Solid grasp of RESTful and/or RPC API design.

Hands-on experience with cloud platforms (AWS, GCP) and containerization (Docker, Kubernetes).

Strong engineering fundamentals - clean code, testing, and thoughtful system design.

Clear, confident communicator who collaborates easily across disciplines.

Curious by nature - always exploring new tools, languages, and ways of working.

Genuine excitement for games, music, and/or interactive media.

You enjoy mentoring others, sharing knowledge, and helping teammates grow.

You use AI-assisted development tools thoughtfully and validate outputs with sound engineering judgment.

What we offer: Working with some of the most inspiring tech companies with a global footprint.

Office in central Stockholm with hybrid setup.

Structured mentorship program, tailored for each individual.

Competitive salary, occupational pension and wellness allowance.

Yearly company summits.

Office perks to make your workday smoother.

Strong group of passionate individuals, team activities and collaborative environment.

Nurture your passion for technology and entertainment, and turn it into a fulfilling career with us at Dashy Studios!
