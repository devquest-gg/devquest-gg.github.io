Every day, tens of millions of people come to Roblox to explore, create, play, learn, and connect with friends in 3D immersive digital experiences– all created by our global community of developers and creators.

At Roblox, we’re building the tools and platform that empower our community to bring any experience that they can imagine to life. Our vision is to reimagine the way people come together, from anywhere in the world, and on any device.

We’re on a mission to connect a billion people with optimism and civility, and looking for amazing talent to help us get there.

A career at Roblox means you’ll be working to shape the future of human interaction, solving unique technical challenges at scale, and helping to create safer, more civil shared experiences for everyone.

As Creative/Design Director, you will be responsible for guiding Incubator creators toward the successful development of novel games (Read about novel games, the Incubator program and its charter at https://create.roblox.com/build ). This design creative leader will be instrumental in helping Incubator participants find the right rich game dynamics for their games, whether these creators come from the Roblox creator community or the off-platform traditional game community. The ideal candidate has a history of creative leadership (including design) across a variety of games, along with a broad knowledge of design elements and their application in successful games across the industry. You will report to the Senior Lead, Incubator.

You will:

Own design feedback to all (external) Incubator creators

Oversee group feedback on specific builds and milestones

Ideate on alchemy of Roblox and traditional success to help creators find innovation and value in their games

Foster creator-to-creator community interactions, both individually and by creating sustainable processes and partnerships

Build and execute effective design related workflows between internal disciplines and Roblox creators

Collaborate cross-functionally (production, product, analytics, engineering, marketing, partnerships and more) to facilitate design related deliverables and deadlines

Work with marketing and creative teams to devise and execute on GTM strategies

Support analytics to set up goals, measure impact, and increase growth KPIs

Help the novel games initiative across non-Incubator projects as needed

You have:

15+ years of creative or game design experience with proven track record of conceptualizing, creating, guiding and refining game designs

Ability to adapt and learn new game and platform dynamics, in order to demonstrate expertise in both traditional and Roblox game design

Superb written and verbal communication skills that guide and convince

Experience leading cross-functional execution with various teams including production, product, analytics, engineering, marketing, partnerships and more

Domain experience with UGC, social and gaming; ability to understand and design for player behavior, expectations and trends

Understanding of game design, principles and mechanics

Experience working with 3rd party creators and developers

Ability to thrive in a fast-paced, agile and iterative development environment

Ability to adapt to changing circumstances and ambiguity

Excellent organization skills
