Job Title: Project Manager, People Retail Transformation (18-month FTC)

Job Summary: We are seeking a proactive, versatile and delivery-focused Project Manager to support the establishment of the People foundations for our new retail store. As we expand into retail, this is an exciting opportunity to help build something new. Working within the People team, you will play a central role in translating our retail ambition into a coordinated programme of work, bringing together the people processes, capabilities, systems and experiences needed to support a successful store opening. You will be responsible for managing the integrated People workstream, working closely with functional People specialists, retail leadership and cross-functional project teams to develop, coordinate and implement practical, fit-for-purpose solutions. This is a role for someone who enjoys bringing structure to ambiguity, connecting ideas and people, and turning plans into tangible outcomes. You do not need to be an HR subject matter expert. Instead, you will bring strong project management, transformation and stakeholder engagement skills, alongside the curiosity and confidence to work across a broad range of People topics.

People Manager: No

What you ll do Programme Management Delivery

Own and coordinate the integrated People project plan, ensuring clear deliverables, milestones, dependencies and timelines aligned to the store opening.

Translate the overall People direction and retail requirements into a structured, actionable programme of work.

Track progress, manage risks and issues, identify dependencies and proactively resolve delivery challenges across multiple workstreams.

Establish effective project governance, reporting and communication routines, providing clear visibility of progress, decisions and areas requiring escalation.

Work closely with retail leadership, the wider programme management team and People stakeholders to ensure People deliverables remain aligned with business priorities and operational readiness.

People Solution Development Integration

Partner with functional People subject matter experts to identify, define and coordinate the solutions and deliverables required to support the new retail operation.

Support the development and integration of solutions across the colleague lifecycle, including organisational design, workforce planning, recruitment, reward, onboarding, learning, people systems, policies and employee experience.

Bring together specialist inputs to create a coherent, practical and consistent People approach that meets the needs of the retail business.

Identify opportunities to leverage existing corporate People processes and frameworks, while highlighting where retail-specific approaches may need to be developed.

Ensure solutions are considered end-to-end, identifying gaps, overlaps and interdependencies across functional areas.

Coordinate the review, testing and refinement of proposed solutions with relevant experts and business stakeholders to ensure they are fit for purpose and ready for implementation.

Research, Problem-Solving Continuous Improvement

Research relevant internal and external practices, market insights and benchmarks to inform the development of effective People solutions.

Develop initial proposals, project documentation, process maps, presentations and recommendations to support discussion and decision-making.

Work collaboratively with functional experts to challenge assumptions, explore options and refine proposed approaches.

Identify opportunities to simplify processes, improve ways of working and create scalable, sustainable solutions.

Bring a pragmatic, solutions-oriented mindset to balancing corporate requirements with the practical realities of a retail environment.

Stakeholder Engagement Change Readiness

Build effective working relationships across People functions, retail leadership, corporate teams and external partners.

Facilitate cross-functional discussions, workshops and decision-making to maintain alignment and momentum.

Communicate complex information clearly, adapting messages for different audiences, including senior stakeholders.

Support change management and implementation activities to ensure new People processes, systems and ways of working are understood and ready for launch.

Coordinate People readiness activities, tracking outstanding actions and ensuring effective handover to business-as-usual teams ahead of store opening.

What you ll bring Essential Experience Skills

Five (5) to seven (7) years of experience in project management, programme coordination, business transformation, operational change or a related delivery-focused role.

Bachelor s degree in a related field or a demonstrated equivalent level of applicable experience.

Demonstrated experience managing complex, cross-functional projects with multiple stakeholders, dependencies and deliverables.

Experience translating broad business objectives into structured plans, practical solutions and measurable outcomes.

A track record of establishing or improving processes, ways of working or operational capabilities, ideally in a new or evolving business environment.

Strong project management skills, including planning, governance, risk management, reporting, stakeholder engagement and delivery against deadlines.

Excellent organisational, analytical and problem-solving skills, with the ability to prioritise competing demands, identify gaps, challenge assumptions and develop pragmatic solutions.

Excellent written, verbal, presentation and stakeholder management skills, including the ability to influence and build alignment across functions, cultures and seniority levels without direct authority.

Ability to work effectively across different functions, cultures and perspectives, building alignment without direct authority.

Comfortable navigating ambiguity, learning new subject areas quickly and taking a hands-on approach to delivery.

Excellent written and verbal communication skills in English required. Fluency in Japanese highly desirable.

Desirable Experience Skills

Experience delivering people-related transformation, organisational change, employee experience or workforce initiatives.

Experience supporting the launch of a new business operation, retail environment or other customer-facing organisation.

Exposure to People or HR workstreams such as organisational design, talent acquisition, reward, learning, HR systems or employee lifecycle processes.

Experience working within a multinational organisation or collaborating with geographically dispersed and culturally diverse teams.

Familiarity with project management methodologies, tools and continuous improvement approaches.
