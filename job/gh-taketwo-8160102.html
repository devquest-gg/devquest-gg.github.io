The Challenge:

Take-Two, a global leader in the fast-growing interactive entertainment industry, is seeking a Paralegal to provide day-to-day administrative and paralegal support to our immigration team, partnering with external immigration providers and internal HR to provide a curated experience for employees requiring work authorization support in the countries where we are located. The Paralegal will support seamless processing of North American employee immigration files by serving as a point of contact for employees, external counsel, and internal teams.

Responsibilities:

● Day-to-day case management of routine business immigration matters in the United States and Canada, including but not limited to H-1B, L-1, O-1, TN, PERM, and I-140 petitions (US), along with Canadian work permit applications and employment-based Permanent Residence applications ● Initiate and track matters with external counsel for new hires, intra-company transfers, labor certifications, and employment-based immigrant applications ● Provide regular case updates to employees and stakeholders, including email updates, HR-facing trackers, and status calls ● Give guidance on immigration processes to employees to ensure they have full understanding of the required documentation and timelines; ● Act as a resource for HRBP, Talent Acquisition, and external counsel for general workflow processes ● Prepare immigration-related letters and documents that align with country-specific immigration or entry requirements ● As necessary, perform research to support immigration cases, such as background on a specialization or industry, current wages, processing procedures, processing speed options, consular processes, etc.

Qualifications:

Bachelor’s Degree

At least 2-3 years of Canadian and US immigration law experience.

In-house legal experience highly desirable.

Excellent written and verbal communication skills.

Ability to multi-task in a fast-paced environment with competing demands.

Attention to detail and thorough documentation skills.

Ability to work flexibly and prioritize tasks in a fast-paced environment.

Broad knowledge of current events and pop culture.

Passion for video games highly desirable.
