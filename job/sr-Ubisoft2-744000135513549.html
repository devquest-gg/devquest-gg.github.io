As a Senior Manager, Creator Strategy at Ubisoft Paris, you will support the development of creator strategies across a portfolio of campaigns, brands, and franchises. You’ll work closely with Brand, Production, Community, Communications, Media, and regional teams to ensure creator perspectives are integrated into campaign planning and player engagement strategies from early development through launch and live operations.

You’ll help guide creator strategy priorities, support collaboration across teams, and contribute to scalable creator engagement approaches that align with audience needs, business goals, and Ubisoft’s player-first mindset.
