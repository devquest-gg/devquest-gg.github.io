Why Sony Interactive Entertainment?

Sony Interactive Entertainment isn’t just the Best Place to Play — it’s also the Best Place to Work. Sony Interactive Entertainment (SIE) is the company behind the PlayStation brand. As a subsidiary of Sony Group Corporation, we’re part of a proud legacy of innovation and excellence. SIE is a dynamic technology company, delivering cutting-edge hardware and network services to more than 100 million people and an entertainment leader, home to some of the most beloved and recognizable intellectual properties (IP) in the world. Our role at SIE is to create and nurture the experiences under the PlayStation brand, a name synonymous with entertainment excellence and creativity.

Why PlayStation

Our team is innovating and crafting the next generation of gaming experiences on PlayStation! You will work alongside partners in engineering, product, design, and research to build tools and technologies that keep our players safe.

PlayStation Network currently has more than 100 million monthly active users. This is a phenomenal opportunity to join our high leverage team working on building outstanding new experiences!

You will lead a passionate engineering team responsible for developing Microsoft Windows® PC based software for the PlayStation® and other applications/devices. You will be responsible for providing strong technical leadership in highly secure and performant PC-based software design and development.

Role Responsibilities

Lead, coach, mentor, grow, and empower your team to achieve their goals

Strong understanding of software engineering methodologies

Collaborate with colleagues to develop, implement, and uphold SDK and application security features for PC applications

Create technical specifications as a Product Owner

Guide the team technically to build high quality products on PC

Participate in code reviews and documentation

Lead day-to-day activities of the team using Agile/Scrum methodologies

Collaborate with geographical diverse teams to coordinate development activities

Contribute to engineering-wide initiatives as a member of PlayStation s engineering management team

Qualifications

2+ years of people management experience

A bachelor’s degree in computer science, or equivalent experience

Experience with requirements gathering and analysis, design and development of scalable code with unit tests across multiple technology stacks

Experience in building high-performance SDKs or Frameworks

Knowledge of client-side Operating System security fundamentals and features

Experience with securing consumer client-side applications

Extensive experience with C/C++

Experience or knowledge of cryptographic algorithms and ciphers

Experience recruiting and building great teams

Ability to work with a high level of autonomy and responsibility

Excellent written and verbal communication skills

Passion for supporting team members’ career growth

Nice-to-Have Skills

Similar experiences with security engineering on non-PC platforms

Experience with low-level engineering tools (e.g. disassemblers/decompiling)

Experience with game development or game engine development

Experience with securing PC games

Experience or knowledge of anti-cheat and/or anti-tamper concepts and products

Experience with codesigning and certificate verification workflows

Experience or knowledge with data integrity checking

Experience or knowledge of binary obfuscation

Experience securing IPC traffic

Experience using tools such as Ghidra, IDA or x64Dbg
