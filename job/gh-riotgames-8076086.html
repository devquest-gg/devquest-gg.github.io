Staff Software Engineer - Esports Platforms

Riot Games · Los Angeles

Lead Engineering $193K–$269K Posted 2026-07-23 Full time
Apply on Riot Games’s site →

Riot engineers bring deep knowledge of specific technical areas but also value the opportunity to work in a variety of broader domains. We work with both new and current technology, creating innovative solutions to overcome high-volume obstacles and to deliver player value.

Riot Games Esports is setting the standard for the industry. We re looking for passionate individuals with a desire to build products that serve as a gateway to sports fandom. As a Staff Software Engineer in the Esports Platforms team, you will work with a team of producers, designers, and engineers to prototype, build, and own products to foster the Esports ecosystem. The web application this role owns, develops, and maintains sits at the heart of Riot Esports. You’ll shape the experience of professional and amateur competitive players, from tournament creation to the most exciting moments, such as getting into a match.

Responsibilities:

Drive the development of Esports applications primarily using web technologies, while integrating with Esports and Game Services, providing technical guidance and hands-on code contributions

Provide technical leadership within the team and adjacent teams to deliver high-quality, scalable, and reliable engineering solutions

Contribute to technical planning and architecture decisions, balancing long-term sustainability with near-term delivery needs

Partner closely with cross-functional partners (product, production, QA, design, data) to deliver great Esports experiences

Mentor other engineers on technical and interpersonal skills

Write clear documentation and communicate technical details across disciplines

Participate in the on-call rotation, triage resolve live incidents and propose solutions through RCAs

Required Qualifications:

6+ years of industry experience in software engineering, with demonstrated technical leadership on complex projects

Bachelor s, Master’s degree in Computer Science or a related field, or comparable experience in software development.

Strong proficiency with web technologies, with working experience in Typescript, React NextJS

Have been responsible for driving the design and leading of development of large-sized efforts (6-month+ scope), including requirements gathering, planning, development, testing, and release

Solid communication and collaboration skills, with experience working across disciplines and teams

Demonstrated examples of setting engineering policy and standards across teams or initiative

Desired Qualifications:

Experience with PaaS Vendors like Netlify or Vercel

Experience with GraphQL

Experience with test automation frameworks like Playwright or Cypress

Familiarity with Riot’s Esports titles

Familiarity with tournaments and tournament formats

For this role, you ll find success through craft expertise, a collaborative spirit, and decision-making that prioritizes your fellow Rioters, who are the customers of your work. Being a dedicated fan of games is not necessary for this position!
