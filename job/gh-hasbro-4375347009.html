Creative Lead, D&D Universes Beyond

Wizards of the Coast · Renton

Lead Other Hybrid $109K–$181K Posted 2026-08-26 Full time

We take play seriously. We’re looking for curious adventurers ready to find their party, fueled by imagination and drive to build what’s never been built before. At Hasbro and Wizards of the Coast, you’ll collaborate with passionate teams to reimagine our iconic brands and create experiences that spark joy, connection, and community through the magic of play. This is your chance to shape legendary play that lasts a lifetime.

Do you love finding the heart of a story world and imagining how players might step inside it? Do you enjoy turning early possibilities into mood boards, creative north stars, kickoff guides, and clear direction for teams?

We’re looking for a Creative Lead, D D Universes Beyond who loves pop culture, immersive storytelling, tabletop games, and the earliest stages of bringing new ideas to life. The right person will bring strong story instincts, excellent taste, collaborative judgment, and a deep respect for Dungeons Dragons as a play experience — partnering closely with product strategy, Franchise creative partners, narrative freelancers, and subject matter experts to shape story, tone, and worldbuilding foundations.

This is a hybrid role requiring three days a week in our Renton, WA office.

What You ll Do:

Partner with product strategy on early concept exploration, creative opportunities, and topline ideas for new Dungeons Dragons collaboration experiences.

Develop mockups, pitches, tone references, and narrative north stars that align teams around a shared vision.

Turn high-level ideas into actionable direction through kickoff materials, guardrails, worldbuilding documentation, and brand guidelines.

Source and manage freelance writers, lore/IP experts, and other external creative talent.

What You ll Bring:

Experience in creative development, narrative design, worldbuilding, publishing, games, or entertainment/franchise storytelling.

Experience in creative development, narrative design, worldbuilding, publishing, games, or entertainment/franchise storytelling.

A track record of turning early concepts into pitches, franchise bibles, or editorial guidelines that align teams.

Sharp instincts for what makes a story, world, or creative opportunity compelling — and the ability to translate source material or brand guidance into clear, actionable direction.

Experience managing freelancers or external creative partners (assignments, feedback, schedules, deliverables).

Strong communication skills and comfort moving between blue-sky ideation and tactical execution, guiding decisions without stalling momentum.

Comfortable in fast-paced, iterative environments; strong organizational skills and a healthy relationship with feedback.

Familiarity with tabletop RPGs, fantasy storytelling, D D, or D D Beyond.

Nice to have

Comfort moving between different creative styles and IP expressions while preserving what makes each world distinctive.

Experience adapting existing stories or creator visions into new product concepts or team-facing guidance.

A love of fandoms and creating community through shared experiences.
