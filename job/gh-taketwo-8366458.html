The Challenge:

The selected candidate will serve as a Senior Paralegal for the Global Employment Immigration Legal team in the Take-Two offices in New York City supporting Take-Two and its publishing labels.

Candidate must possess critical thinking skills and strong business acumen, and be extremely organized, great at independently managing projects, proactive in driving projects forward, and able to engage in complex work, including drafting and reviewing legal documents and managing various employment-related matters in partnership with counsel. The selected candidate will also provide day-to-day administrative and paralegal services to the Global Employment Immigration Legal team at a dynamic entertainment company.

The role provides broad exposure to Take-Two’s activities and offers the opportunity to work in a collaborative, collegial, and engaging environment.

This role will serve as a critical member of the Global Employment Immigration Legal team, providing strategic paralegal support for substantive and complex legal matters, including threatened and actual litigation, employee relations investigations and follow-up, and restructurings, and will be primarily responsible for organizing and maintaining key records, filings, templates, and other materials for such matters as well as any pending immigration cases.

Candidate will at times work collaboratively with the larger Take-Two Legal team and other stakeholders, and will also work in a fast-paced, autonomous environment with the ability to implement efficient processes and procedures.

Candidate will strive to utilize technology and workflows in order to increase efficiency.

What You’ll Take On:

Organize, prepare, and track separation agreements based on contract templates and update such templates when needed.

Build and maintain comprehensive database for employment- and immigration-related templates, such as offer letters, non-disclosure agreements, and various other employee notices.

Analyze employee outside activities/employment disclosures and liaise with business partners for conflicts assessment.

Own the process for updating and distributing revised employee handbooks on an annual basis.

Analyze data for restructuring actions and prepare legal notices and related paperwork / communications.

Draft and finalize litigation hold memos in connection with threatened or actual litigation.

Summarize case documents and prepare chronologies and related work product for threatened or actual litigation.

Support the maintenance of accurate immigration data and tracking and planning systems.

Participate in the planning, selection, implementation and training of key technology resources for the Employment Legal team, including enhancements and upgrades to such systems.

Prepare materials for and assist with Global Employment Immigration Legal training initiatives and programs.

Identify, and collaborate with the wider team to implement processes and procedures for document management, litigation hold tracking, outside interest disclosures, and other tasks to bring about more efficiency and innovation within the Global Employment Immigration Legal team.

What You Bring:

Six or more years of experience as a paralegal or similar role in relevant field (Employment, Employee Relations, Human Resources, etc.).

Ability to work with document management software, such as iManage, Google Drive, or similar systems.

Must be tech-savvy, with a grasp of AI legal offerings or a proven ability to quickly learn new software applications, including AI software such as Gemini, Harvey, and similar tools.

Demonstrated ability over your career to handle progressively more complex tasks, with a commensurate demonstrated rise in business judgment.

High energy, positive attitude, and urgency to “get stuff done”.

College degree.

Strong academic background and excellent critical thinking skills.

Must possess common sense, patience, good humor, and a commitment to teamwork.

Strong administrative problem-solving skills and time management skills.

Excellent attention to detail, strong written and verbal communication skills and thorough follow-up.

Takes ownership of projects, shows initiative and commitment to drive matters to completion.

Team-player who can multi-task and work well under pressure, with constantly changing priorities.

Professionally interface with all levels of the organization, clients and vendors.

Knowledge of and enthusiasm for the videogame industry.

Proficient with MS Office (Word, Excel, PowerPoint).
