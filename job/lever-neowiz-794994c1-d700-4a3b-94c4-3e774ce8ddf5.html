About NEOWIZNEOWIZ is a game developer and publisher defined by gripping narratives and uncompromising quality.

Collaborating with creative talent worldwide, we create original gaming experiences that push boundaries and captivate players globally.

From Lies of P to DJMAX RESPECT V, Cats & Soup, SANABI, and Skul: The Hero Slayer, we service distinct IPs across a wide range of genres, delivering fresh gaming experiences to players worldwide.

NEOWIZ Taiwan 為 NEOWIZ 旗下的台灣子公司，致力於將我們的遊戲與 IP 深耕並拓展至台灣市場。

目前我們的營運重點聚焦於《棕色塵埃2》（BrownDust 2），主導在地化行銷、社群經營、線下活動、快閃店，以及官方週邊商品的企劃、製作與銷售，同時積極展開在地戰略合作。

我們深耕在地玩家需求與市場動態，與韓國總部及區域核心夥伴緊密協作，共同為 NEOWIZ 旗下作品在台灣打造永續成長的營運生態。

NEOWIZ Taiwan is a local subsidiary of NEOWIZ, dedicated to expanding the reach of our games and IPs across the Taiwanese market.

Currently, our primary focus is centered on BrownDust 2, where we drive localized marketing, community management, offline events, pop-up stores, and official merchandise operations (planning, production, and sales), alongside strategic local partnerships.

Deeply rooted in our understanding of local gamers and market dynamics, we work in close collaboration with our headquarters in Korea and key regional partners to build sustainable growth for NEOWIZ’s titles in Taiwan.
