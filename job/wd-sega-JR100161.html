Studio Overview We are Sports Interactive &#x1f3ae; The studio behind: ⚽ Football Manager.

Founded in 1994, Sports Interactive became a wholly owned subsidiary of SEGA Europe in 2006. Based at the Here East technology hub in Stratford, East London, the SI team continues to expand as we bring our titles to an ever-growing audience across an increasing variety of platforms.

To support our growth, we welcome the opportunity to connect with passionate people with the drive to match our ambitions and initiative to realise your individual potential.

Job Title:   Game Designer (Console)   Team:   Game Designer (FMC)   Reporting into:   Advanced Game Designer   W orking Model:   Hybrid   2-3 days   in studio   Location:   Here East, Queen Elizabeth Olympic Park, London, E15 2GW     Position Overview  Sports Interactive are looking for a talented Game Designer to join the studio and work on the   Football Manager franchise with a specific focus on the FM Console and FM Touch products.

As a Game Designer, you will   be responsible for   creating and guiding the latest feature designs for Football Manager projects. Typical activities will include features definition, systems, and interfaces; aiming to feed our football simulation with more quality,   depth   and authenticity.

You will join an ambitious and passionate team who care deeply about delivering an outstanding Football Manager experience for our players.

The ideal candidate will be a dynamic, self-motivated, and creative individual with   great communication   skills, solid design   methodology , and the ability to champion ideas from   inception   to delivery at pace within a cross-disciplinary team.

Key Responsibilities  Own   the design of gameplay features and systems to deliver the highest quality Football Manager experience.

Collaborate closely with UI/UX and Narrative designers on feature proposals and definitions.

Document features and systems through detailed and rigorous technical specifications to guide implementation from engineering teams.

Present design proposals to the key design stakeholders and consider feedback from colleagues.

Act as a conduit for cross team communication ensuring the navigation   and functionality of our   various   platforms is always given the greatest of   consideration .

Communicate progress, risks and opportunities to the Production and Design Teams.

Propose key ideas and game improvements to production and design leads to help build a high-quality feature roadmap, considering player feedback.

Champion the quality of the game through prototyping,   playtesting   and game reviews with cross-disciplinary teams.

Implement content, tune   data   and help balance systems directly in Unity when   appropriate .

Support the QA department to write detailed test plans for features.

Knowledge, Skills, and Experience  Essential Systems-driven game designer with an appreciation for depth,   replayability   and simulation-heavy topics.

Autonomous and proactive, taking ownership of gameplay features and systems while championing   high standards   of gameplay quality.

Experience designing for multiple platforms, with an understanding of the requirements, user experience guidelines and technical constraints of console and mobile platforms.

Strong written and verbal communication skills for effective collaboration with UX/UI designers, software engineers, QA, artists,   animators   and producers.

Familiar with UX design principles and best practices.

Passionate about both football and video game development.

Strong attention to detail and a methodical approach to design.

Good knowledge of Football Manager.

Experience working with modern game development tools and engines such as Unity or similar.

Proficient with Excel and/or other systems-driven tools.

Desirable  Proficient in using Figma and Miro to document, communicate and iterate on gameplay features and user experiences.
