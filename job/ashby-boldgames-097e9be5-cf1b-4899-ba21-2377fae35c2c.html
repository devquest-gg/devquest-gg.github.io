At Bold Games, we're a tight-knit team that genuinely loves what we do. We support each other through every step of our journey and keep pushing towards the best that has yet to come.

We are looking for a Marketing Artist who will be a creative force to attract users with engaging marketing videos. If you’d like to create videos from scratch and bring them to life, take the chance and join our team!

What you’ll be doing - at its core:

- Brainstorming creative concepts to improve user acquisition

- Playing our games and deeply understanding the gameplay to translate it into compelling, high-quality marketing videos and motion graphics

- Adapting marketing videos across different formats and aspect ratios for various ad platforms

- Understanding creative performance and iterating through the production process

- Staying up-to-date with marketing trends and exploring interesting and top-performing marketing creatives

What we’re looking for:

- A portfolio showcasing your skills

- Experience with After Effects

- At least 2 years of hands-on experience in marketing art

- University degree or background in Visual Communications, Animation, or related fields

- A mix of designer and marketer mindsets

- Knowledge of Blender, Maya or other 3D design tools is a plus.
