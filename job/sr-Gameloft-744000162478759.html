Sound Designer Intern

Gameloft · Hanoi

Entry Audio Posted 2026-11-19 Intern
Apply on Gameloft’s site →

WHAT YOU WILL BE WORKING ON What you will focus on Support the Audio Team in creating, editing, implementing, and managing audio assets for games, including sound effects and voice-over recordings. The internship provides hands-on experience in game audio production, audio asset management, and opportunities to explore AI-assisted tools and workflows.

What it means on a daily basis Assist in creating, editing, and implementing sound effects and audio assets for games.

Support audio integration using game engines and audio middleware.

Manage, edit, and maintain large libraries of voice-over (VO) recordings and other audio assets, ensuring proper organization and file management.

Organize and maintain audio libraries, project files, and production documentation.

Assist with audio testing, troubleshooting, and quality assurance.

Collaborate with designers, programmers, and the audio team on project requirements.

Explore new audio technologies, tools, and workflows, including AI-assisted production.

Who you would be working with Audio Team, Game Designers, Artists, Programmers, Composers Recruitment journey Online screening call with recruiter Distance Technical&#xa0;test Online interview with&#xa0;Creative Audio Director and&#xa0;Audio Project Manager WHAT YOU NEED TO SUCCEED Currently studying Audio Production, Sound Design, Music Technology, Game Development, or a related field.

Basic proficiency with DAWs such as Reaper, Pro Tools, Audition, or similar software.

Understanding of sound design fundamentals, audio editing, and asset management.

Passion for video games, interactive audio, and emerging technologies.

Familiarity with Unity, Unreal Engine, Wwise, or FMOD is a plus.

Portfolio or demo reel showcasing audio-related work is encouraged.

WHO YOU ARE Creative, curious, detail-oriented, and eager to learn.

Good communication, teamwork, and openness to feedback.

WHAT’S IN IT FOR YOU​ Benefit Internship allowance VND 7,000,000 gross/month.

Lunch allowance VND 700,000/month.

1 day of paid annual leave per month, Christmas leave, Birthday leave, CSR leave, paid sick leave.

Various training packages, including internal training and e-learning platforms (iLearn, Udemy, O'Reilly,...).

Opportunities to collaborate and train with Gameloft worldwide experts and develop yourselves.

A range of well-being events and policies to support employees physically, mentally, and emotionally.

The opportunity to become an official employee of Gameloft.

Life at Gameloft A multicultural environment with 6+ nationalities, where hundreds of world-renowned game titles were born.​ An open-space office, a cafeteria,&#xa0;a ping-pong table, and a foosball table.

A gaming area equipped with multiple devices (PS, Nintendo Switch, ...) A wide range of internal events and sports clubs to boost connection and work-life balance.
