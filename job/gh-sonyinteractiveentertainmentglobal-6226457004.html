Global Accounts Payable Expense Operations Analyst - Contingent Worker

Position Summary

The Accounts Payable Expense Operations Analyst is responsible for supporting end-to-end Accounts Payable (AP), Travel Expense (T E),operations while serving as the functional subject matter expert for enterprise financial systems. This role combines operational excellence with systems expertise to ensure accurate invoice processing, employee expense reimbursements, supplier management, corporate card administration, payment operations, and enterprise application support.

The position partners closely with Finance, Procurement, Treasury, Tax, Human Resources, Payroll, IT, external vendors, and Business Process Outsourcing (BPO) providers to ensure efficient, compliant, and reliable financial operations across a global organization. The ideal candidate is highly analytical, detail-oriented, customer-focused, and experienced in supporting enterprise applications including Coupa Invoicing, Coupa Supplier Information Management (SIM), SAP Concur, SAP S/4HANA, OpenText Vendor Invoice Management (VIM), and related financial systems.

Routine AP and Expense transactional operations are primarily performed by the BPO provider. This role oversees BPO service delivery, monitors operational performance and controls, supports the resolution of escalated issues, and drives process and system improvements. The Analyst may also directly perform selected operational activities to support exceptions, escalations, transitions, business continuity, or other operational requirements.

Work Arrangement: Hybrid (2–3 days per week in the office based on business and team requirements).

Key Responsibilities

Depending on business needs and the defined operating model, the responsibilities below may be fulfilled through oversight of the BPO provider, direct execution by the Analyst, or a combination of both.

Accounts Payable Supplier Operations

Process supplier invoices accurately using Coupa Invoicing, SAP S/4HANA, OpenText VIM, and related applications.

Process Relish invoices and ensure timely and accurate payment processing.

Manage supplier onboarding, registration, maintenance, and vendor master records through Coupa Supplier Information Management (SIM).

Review, validate, and resolve invoice discrepancies, exceptions, and workflow issues.

Perform vendor account reconciliations and resolve outstanding balances.

Process and monitor supplier payments while ensuring compliance with company policies and financial controls.

Support month-end and year-end closing activities related to Accounts Payable.

Ensure compliance with SOX, audit requirements, tax regulations, and internal controls.

Expense Corporate Card Operations

Serve as the primary escalation point for complex employee expense reimbursement and corporate card issues outside the scope of the BPO provider.

Administer Corporate Card programs across multiple regions, including account setup, maintenance, credit limit requests, delinquency monitoring, employee terminations, dormant account reviews, and dispute resolution.

Investigate reimbursement issues, duplicate payments, rejected payment files, payment exceptions, and policy violations.

Validate reimbursement payment batches and reconcile payment files prior to processing.

Partner with Treasury, Payroll, HR, Procurement, Finance, Tax, and external vendors to resolve operational issues.

Escalate significant financial, operational, or compliance risks to leadership.

Enterprise Systems Functional Support

Provide functional support for SAP Concur, Coupa, SAP S/4HANA, OpenText VIM, and related enterprise applications.

Investigate and resolve application issues including:

Employee provisioning failures

ERP interface failures

SAP IDoc processing issues

Corporate Card feed failures

Expense application and mobile issues

Enterprise integration failures

Analyze system dependencies, determine business impact, identify root causes, and coordinate resolution with IT and software vendors.

Support production issue validation and act as the functional liaison between Finance and IT.

Testing, Projects Continuous Improvement

Support User Acceptance Testing (UAT), regression testing, system upgrades, releases, implementations, and application enhancements.

Develop test plans, document defects, validate fixes, and coordinate production readiness.

Identify opportunities for automation, process improvements, and operational efficiencies across Accounts Payable and Expense functions.

Participate in Finance transformation initiatives, Procure-to-Pay projects, and cross-functional business initiatives.

Prepare operational reports and analyze performance metrics.

Documentation Knowledge Management

Develop and maintain Standard Operating Procedures (SOPs), business process documentation, workflows, and system documentation.

Create employee communications, FAQs, training materials, and job aids.

Support knowledge transfer, cross-training, and business continuity planning.

Maintain documentation repositories and ensure information remains current and accurate.

Cross-Functional Collaboration

Partner with Procurement, Finance, HR, Payroll, Treasury, Tax, Legal, IT, and external vendors to resolve operational issues.

Support supplier, employee, and stakeholder inquiries with a customer-focused approach.

Promote collaboration across global teams while maintaining strong internal controls and operational excellence.
