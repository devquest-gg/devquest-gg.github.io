Belka Games is a game development company. According to Sensor Tower, the company is among the top 30 highest-grossing game developers in Europe, and its audience has recently surpassed 110 million players.

Role Overview

Our team is looking for a proactive and product-oriented Game Designer to work on new R D projects in the hybrid-casual puzzle genre.

You will participate in the creation of new game concepts and prototypes from the earliest stages — from developing and iterating on core gameplay to building progression systems, balancing mechanics, and shaping the first layers of meta and LiveOps features.

We are looking for someone who enjoys experimenting, rapidly iterating on ideas, and turning raw gameplay concepts into strong product foundations.

Key Responsibilities

Design and develop gameplay systems and features for new game prototypes

Work on core gameplay, progression, balance, and early meta systems

Create and maintain game design documentation (concept docs, GDDs, feature specs)

Analyze gameplay problems and propose solutions to improve player experience and engagement

Participate in prototyping and iteration processes together with the R D team

Design and tune game balance, rewards, pacing, and progression

Work with product metrics and formulate gameplay hypotheses

Collaborate closely with game designers, producers, artists, and developers during early production stages

Required Skills, Knowledge and Expertise

3+ years of experience as a Game Designer

Experience in hyper-casual, hybrid-casual, or puzzle games

Participation in developing games or prototypes from scratch (R D / pre-production)

Experience working on at least 5 game projects or prototypes

Experience creating and maintaining game design documentation

Understanding of gameplay systems, progression, balancing, and player motivation

Strong knowledge of the mobile games market and current genre trends

Ability to clearly communicate ideas and work in a fast iteration environment

Strong analytical and problem-solving skills

Russian and English - must have
