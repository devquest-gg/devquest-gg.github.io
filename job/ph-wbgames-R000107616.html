The ideal candidate will possess the ability to work together with a team of developers in creating high quality user interfaces to deliver an intuitive and enjoyable experience.

Key Responsibilities: Work closely with the UI team to create, implement and maintain UI materials, shaders and Unreal Engine UMG that support the established art direction.

Collaborate on the development of UI visual concepts, wireframes and functional prototypes Support on design & user feedback to develop and polish features to a high standard of visual and functional quality.

Strong problem-solving skills with experience to action effective and practical solutions.

Skill Requirements: Essential Experience designing UI for games, or transferable interactive or product UI experience.

Experience implementing UI in Unreal Engine or a similar engine.

Confidence working with widgets, materials, shaders and visual scripting.

Graphic design fundamentals — layout, iconography, typography and colour theory.

A player-focused approach to design, with a solid grounding in core UI and usability principles.

Able to explain and justify design decisions clearly to artists, designers and engineers.

Confidence with industry-standard design tools such as Figma and the Adobe Suite, or equivalents.
