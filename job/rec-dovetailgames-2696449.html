Data & Business Insights Analyst

Dovetail Games · Remote job · Remote

Mid Data & Analytics Remote Posted 2026-07-31
Apply on Dovetail Games’s site →

At Dovetail Games, data plays an important role in helping us understand how our games and business are performing, where opportunities exist and where we may need to act differently.

We’re looking for an experienced Data & Business Insights Analyst who can bring together information from across the business and turn it into clear, compelling stories that support better decision-making.

The core focus of this role is business performance analysis. You’ll work with teams across Product, Marketing, Commercial, Finance, Development and Leadership to understand the questions that matter, explore the available evidence and explain what the data means in a way that is accessible, relevant and actionable.

This is not a data engineering role. You’ll work closely with Dovetail’s Analytics Engineer, key stakeholders across Marketing Commercial & finance and the wider PullUp Entertainment Data Team, who provide the technical foundations, pipelines, reporting infrastructure and analytics-ready datasets. Your role will be to bring those sources together, apply business context and help people understand not only what is happening, but why it matters and what we should do next.

The ideal candidate would be able to spend at least one day a week in the office, however we can be flexible.

Why Join Us? This role offers the opportunity to have a visible impact on how Dovetail Games makes decisions.

You’ll work across a broad range of topics, including game performance, player behaviour, market trends, marketing effectiveness and live revenue strategy. You’ll also collaborate with specialists across the PullUp Entertainment Group, drawing on expertise in areas such as market insights, game data, live revenue strategy and data science.

You’ll join a collaborative environment where curiosity, clear thinking and different perspectives are valued. We welcome applications from people of all backgrounds, experiences and career paths. If you’re excited by the role but do not meet every requirement, we would still encourage you to apply.
