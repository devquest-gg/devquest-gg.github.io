Who We Are

Take-Two Interactive Software, Inc. is a leading developer, publisher, and marketer of interactive entertainment for consumers around the globe. The Company develops and publishes products principally through Rockstar Games, 2K, Private Division, and Zynga. Our products are currently designed for console gaming systems, PC, and Mobile, including smartphones and tablets, and are delivered through physical retail, digital download, online platforms, and cloud streaming services. The Company’s common stock is publicly traded on NASDAQ under the symbol TTWO.

While our offices (physical and virtual) are casual and inviting, we are deeply committed to our core tenets of creativity, innovation and efficiency, and individual and team development opportunities. Our industry and business are continually evolving and fast-paced, providing numerous opportunities to learn and hone your skills. We work hard, but we also like to have fun and believe that we provide a great place to come to work each day to pursue your passions.

The Challenge

We are looking for an expert Senior Product Lead to revolutionize Take-Two s global Procure-to-Pay (P2P) financial technology architecture. This position owns the definition, modernization, and scaling of global enterprise tools that power procurement workflows, supplier management, sourcing, and accounts payable processing. You will optimize the operational efficiency of our backend systems, ensuring absolute compliance and global fluidity across all business units.

What You’ll Take On

Lead the comprehensive global product vision, roadmap, and delivery lifecycle for the end-to-end global Procure-to-Pay.

Exercise broad decision-making authority to harmonize complex global accounting, tax, and procurement requirements into streamlined digital workflows.

Collaborate closely with Global Procurement, Treasury, Accounts Payable, and external technical consultants to eliminate system friction and drive process automation.

Drive cross-functional solutions for enterprise system integrations (ERP systems, third-party vendor platforms, and banking portals).

Provide strategic guidance and mentor cross-functional agile squads to uphold exceptional execution standards.

What You Bring

8+ years of experience leading enterprise financial systems engineering or fintech product portfolios, with a specialized emphasis on global supply chains or procurement.

Advanced technical insight into major enterprise ERP platforms (e.g., JD Edwards, SAP, Oracle) and dedicated P2P software solutions (e.g., Coupa, Jaggaer), and modern Intake/Orchestration platforms (e.g. Zip, ORO, etc.)

Demonstrated mastery of complex multi-entity transactions, localized global compliance standards, and internal corporate controls (SOX).

Superb structural problem-solving abilities and a history of transforming dense administrative processes into intuitive digital products.

Bachelor’s degree in Management Information Systems (MIS), Finance, Business Administration, or a related technical field.
