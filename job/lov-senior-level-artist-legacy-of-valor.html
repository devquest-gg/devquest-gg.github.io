Senior Level Artist

Black Banner Studios · České Budějovice · Remote

Senior Art Remote Posted 2026-11-20 Full time
Apply on Black Banner Studios’s site →

We're looking for an experienced Senior Level Artist with an exceptional artistic eye and a passion for creating beautiful, believable, and performant game environments in Unreal Engine 5. Legacy of Valor features a large, interconnected medieval world inspired by 14th-century Europe, with diverse landscapes, villages, towns, castles, and battlefields. You'll help bring this world to life, transforming level design blockouts into visually stunning, immersive, and fully playable locations. This is primarily a level art role rather than an asset creation role. Your focus will be on world building, composition, visual storytelling, and creating cohesive environments using existing assets, modular kits, foliage, and procedural tools. You'll work closely with our artists, designers, and programmers, taking ownership of substantial areas of the game world while maintaining high artistic standards, gameplay functionality, and performance. If you believe you'd be a great fit for this role, even if you don't meet every requirement listed below, we'd still love to hear from you. Please don't hesitate to apply! Create beautiful, believable, immersive, and visually distinctive game environments using Unreal Engine 5. Transform level design blockouts into fully realized, production-quality locations while respecting gameplay requirements, navigation, and level design intent. Build and refine large-scale landscapes, forests, villages, towns, castles, fortifications, roads, and other medieval environments using existing assets, modular kits, materials, foliage, and procedural systems. Establish strong visual compositions, natural-looking layouts, convincing architectural arrangements, and cohesive environmental storytelling. Ensure that environments feel authentic, lived-in, and visually rich through thoughtful asset placement, set dressing, terrain integration, and attention to detail. Work closely with Level Designers to maintain gameplay readability, clear navigation, appropriate sightlines, and functional combat spaces. Collaborate with Environment Artists and Technical Artists to identify asset requirements, improve modular kits, and refine procedural workflows. Integrate and refine procedural environment content, ensuring that generated results meet the game's artistic and gameplay standards. Work with foliage, terrain materials, landscape systems, and environmental details to create natural, visually convincing transitions between different locations. Maintain high visual quality while respecting performance budgets, streaming requirements, and the technical limitations of large open-world environments. Use Unreal Engine profiling and visualization tools to identify and address level art performance issues in collaboration with technical specialists. Help establish and maintain visual quality standards, consistent workflows, and efficient level art production practices. Provide constructive artistic feedback and help maintain consistent level art quality across the project. Extensive professional experience creating high-quality level art for large-scale or technically complex game projects. Exceptional artistic eye for composition, scale, color, visual hierarchy, environmental storytelling, and the creation of beautiful, believable game worlds. Strong portfolio demonstrating complete, production-quality game environments rather than primarily individual assets or props. Advanced practical experience building and dressing environments in Unreal Engine 5. Strong practical experience with Unreal Engine 5 PCG and other procedural environment creation workflows. Strong ability to transform level design blockouts into visually impressive, coherent, and fully playable environments. Excellent understanding of asset placement, modular environment construction, terrain integration, set dressing, and the creation of natural, convincing spaces. Practical experience with Unreal Engine 5 World Partition, including large-world environment workflows, streaming, and content organization. Strong understanding of level art optimization, including LODs, Nanite, instancing, visibility, streaming, and performance considerations. Experience working with landscapes, foliage, materials, and environmental assets to create cohesive large-scale environments. Solid understanding of how level art affects gameplay, navigation, player orientation, sightlines, and combat readability. Ability to work independently, take artistic ownership of complex environments, and solve creative and technical challenges. Strong communication skills and the ability to collaborate effectively with Level Designers, Environment Artists, Technical Artists, and programmers. Experience with historically grounded environments, particularly medieval European settlements, architecture, castles, and landscapes. Experience with Houdini or integrating Houdini-generated content into Unreal Engine. Experience with environment lighting, atmosphere, and dynamic weather systems. Good 3D modeling and texturing skills, using tools such as Blender, Maya, 3ds Max, ZBrush, Substance Painter, or Substance Designer. Experience creating or improving modular environment kits and reusable world-building tools. Experience working on AAA and / or large open-world game projects. Previous experience mentoring artists, providing artistic feedback, or helping establish level art workflows and quality standards. Passion for video games, particularly medieval RPGs, open-world games, and strategy titles. Location: Remote / International, with the option to work in the Czech Republic. Engagement: Full-time, long-term collaboration. Contract: Contractor / B2B worldwide, or direct employment for candidates residing in or willing to relocate to the Czech Republic. Start Date: As soon as mutually agreed. Language: Professional working proficiency in English. Compensation: Competitive, based on experience and expertise.
