Location: Hong Kong

Type: Full-time

The role

Help shape the Android platform powering Nex Playground. You’ll develop a shared software foundation across hardware generations, modernize existing devices, and make them easier to update, secure, and test throughout their lifetime.

Working in a small firmware team, you’ll contribute to platform design and own implementation, integration, and device validation for defined projects. You’ll report to the Firmware Software Engineering Lead and collaborate with core technology teams, SDETs, and external hardware partners.

What you will do

Unify vendor codebases and hardware-specific implementations into a maintainable Android platform shared across device generations.

Upgrade supported devices to newer Android versions, resolving BSP, driver, and framework compatibility issues while preserving product behavior and upgrade continuity.

Develop modular update capabilities for independent application and firmware releases, enabling smaller, more frequent updates with compatibility checks and reliable recovery.

Strengthen platform security through regular Android and vendor security patches, vulnerability assessment and remediation, and ongoing hardening.

Build test interfaces, diagnostics, and controllable system behavior with SDETs to enable reliable automation and regression testing.

Diagnose performance, reliability, and compatibility issues across Android services, native code, HALs, and the Linux kernel; validate fixes on devices.

Coordinate integration across multiple ODMs and SoC/BSP providers: align requirements, review source and patches, and follow issues through to validated fixes.

Translate core technology requirements into firmware capabilities, implement platform changes, and coordinate work that depends on SoC or BSP providers.

What you bring

5+ years of relevant software engineering experience, including hands-on work on AOSP or Android-based embedded products.

Experience shipping and maintaining production devices, owning changes from implementation through integration and device validation.

Strong C/C++ skills, the ability to work in Android Java or Kotlin code, and Python or shell scripting for automation.

Practical knowledge of Android and Linux architecture, including how framework services, native components, HALs, drivers, and hardware interact.

Experience building AOSP, integrating vendor software, and debugging across software layers on devices.

Sound judgment about shared code, hardware-specific adaptations, compatibility, and long-term maintainability, with a methodical approach to debugging and validation.

Clear technical communication in English and Mandarin Chinese, turning cross-team requirements and vendor discussions into actionable engineering work.

Useful additional experience

Major Android version upgrades, BSP migrations, or consolidation of divergent vendor codebases.

Modular system components, independent application and firmware updates, OTA architecture, or staged rollout and recovery.

Android security patch assessment and backporting, vulnerability remediation, SELinux, verified boot, or platform hardening.

Hands-on experience with Android BSPs and SoC platforms from vendors such as Amlogic, Rockchip, MediaTek, or Qualcomm.

Technical delivery across multiple ODMs and SoC/BSP vendors, including patch review and acceptance testing.

Designing for testability, hardware test automation, diagnostic tooling, or close collaboration with SDETs.

Firmware support for camera/ISP, graphics, on-device compute, connectivity, or power management.
