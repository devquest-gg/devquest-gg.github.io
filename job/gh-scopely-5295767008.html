Senior Narrative Designer - Unannounced Title

Scopely is looking for a Senior Narrative Designer to join our Slate Development Group in the US on a hybrid or remote basis! (Hybrid in Culver City)

At Scopely, we care deeply about what we do and want to inspire play, every day - whether in our work environments alongside our talented colleagues, or through our deep connections with our communities of players. We are a global team of game lovers who are developing, publishing and innovating the mobile games industry, connecting millions of people around the world daily.

We are in the early stages of development on an ambitious, unannounced AAA mobile game, and we’re assembling a team of passionate game makers to craft unforgettable environments and immersive adventures that players will love.

What You Will Do

The Senior Narrative Designer will be responsible for creating compelling content within a beloved fantasy setting, developing stories, quests, characters, and dialogue authentic to the established IP. Working closely with the Narrative Lead, Narrative Director, and cross-disciplinary teams, they will help shape content from concept through production, ensuring story and gameplay work seamlessly together.

This role requires a creative, collaborative, and experienced narrative designer who can develop compelling RPG content within established creative constraints, contribute fresh ideas while respecting existing canon, and thrive in iterative development. The ideal candidate is passionate about storytelling, receptive to feedback, and excited by the challenge of creating experiences that resonate with players.

The Senior Narrative Designer will contribute to:

Story arcs, quests, branching narrative structures

Character creation, dialogue, voice

Worldbuilding

Cutscene scripts

Naming, flavor text, supporting narrative materials

Additional narrative content, within and outside the game

Narrative Content Development

Develop and pitch concepts, storylines, quests, characters, and narrative arcs that create compelling player experiences within an established fantasy IP.

Create and maintain documentation, including story outlines, character summaries, scripts, dialogue, and supporting materials.

Collaborate with the Narrative Lead and Narrative Director to ensure content aligns with project goals, creative direction, and player experience objectives.

Narrative Ownership

Own designated content from concept through production, driving development, iteration, and refinement throughout the content lifecycle.

Create content that is authentic to an established fantasy setting while introducing fresh ideas, memorable characters, and engaging player-facing stories.

Partner with production and other disciplines to help ensure timely delivery that supports broader project goals.

Collaboration Iteration

Collaborate closely with game designers, artists, producers, and other disciplines to integrate narrative seamlessly into gameplay systems and player experiences.

Work constructively with internal teams, external partners, and stakeholders to develop, refine, and deliver narrative content.

Participate in narrative reviews, workshops, and feedback discussions, incorporating feedback thoughtfully and effectively while maintaining the creative intent.

Narrative Craft

Design and develop branching dialogue, quest structures, and narrative experiences that support player agency and engagement.

Contribute to development of characters, relationships, and story moments that resonate with players.

Help maintain narrative consistency, quality, and authenticity across assigned content areas.

Support growth of junior team members and new hires, through constructive feedback and mentorship.

What We re Looking For

Experience developing narrative content for RPGs, including stories, quests, characters, dialogue, and branching narrative experiences.

Strong understanding of narrative design, storytelling, and game design principles.

Demonstrated ability to pitch, develop, and iterate narrative content from concept through production.

Strong collaboration and communication skills, with the ability to work effectively across disciplines and incorporate feedback from multiple stakeholders.

Experience creating original content within established settings, franchises, or IPs while maintaining authenticity and consistency.

Strong analytical, problem-solving, and organizational skills.

Detail-oriented, creative, and enthusiastic, with strong craft discipline and a passion for narrative design.

Desire to create an incredibly cool game that millions of people will love.

Nice to Have

Experience with live-service, multiplayer, or mobile games.

Experience collaborating across multiple teams, studios, or external partners.

Familiarity with major fantasy IPs.

Mandarin language proficiency.

About Scopely

Scopely is a leading video game and global interactive entertainment company, home to many of the world’s most beloved and enduring experiences, including two of the most successful mobile games of all-time “MONOPOLY GO!” and “Pokémon GO,” along with “Stumble Guys,” “Star Trek™ Fleet Command,” “MARVEL Strike Force,” “WWE Champions,” the Scrabble® franchise, “Yahtzee® With Buddies,” and many others. Across mobile, web, PC, and console, Scopely creates, develops, publishes, and live-operates one of the most diversified and award-winning portfolios in the games industry — bringing hundreds of millions of players together through a shared love of play.

Founded in 2011, Scopely is powered by its exceptional team — including thousands of world-class gamemakers around the globe, a distinctive tenet-driven culture, and its proprietary technology platform, Playgami. Together, these strengths have fueled Scopely’s position as the #1 mobile games company in the U.S. and #2 globally, generating more than $10 billion in lifetime revenue. Whether building global sensations like “MONOPOLY GO!” from the ground up, or expanding through strategic acquisitions, including the FoxNext, GSN, and Scopely Explore games businesses — Scopely consistently delivers experiences players love today and return to for years to come.

Recognized multiple times as one of the "100 Most Influential Companies in the World" by TIME magazine and one of Fast Company s "World s Most Innovative Companies" and “Best Workplaces for Innovators,” Scopely believes that video games can be a force for good — creating meaningful connections, vibrant communities, and making life better through play.

Scopely has global operations and partners across four continents in more than a dozen countries worldwide. For more information, visit: https://www.scopely.com/ .
