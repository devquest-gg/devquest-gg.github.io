SUMMARY: This role is part of the IT: Solutions department at Nintendo of America (NOA) and provides management and technical leadership for Development, Middleware, and QA, including delivery across multiple technology stacks and Galaxy (Oracle EBS) releases. It also owns quality assurance, technical roadmaps, team priorities, and continuous improvement across the development and QA lifecycle, and oversees key partners and technology vendors, including budgets, forecasts, and vendor personnel and services.

DESCRIPTION OF DUTIES

Provides overall direction and effectively manages information technology and computing systems/software associated with assigned area.

Plans, coordinates, directs, and designs technology-related activities of the organization, as well as providing administrative direction and support for daily operational activities.

Works closely with decision makers in other departments to identify, recommend, develop, implement, and support cost-effective technology solutions for all aspects of the organization.

Manages and develops team, providing guidance and training to effectively support professional growth and appropriate use of technology.

Monitors and maintains hardware/software technology to ensure maximum access and efficient, secure and stable environments.

Executes tactical plans through assigned team and ensures alignment with company priorities and long-term strategy.

Supports senior leadership with long-term tactical plan development for assigned area(s).

Oversees and manages contracts, budgets and vendors associated with assigned areas and projects.

Ensures work is compliant with established processes, J-SOX and other internal controls

SUMMARY OF REQUIREMENTS

Requires a minimum of six to eight (6 – 8) years of related experience, including previous people management experience.

Intermediate to advanced project and program management knowledge.

A proven track record of managing, organizing, overseeing and delivering on complex projects.

Undergraduate degree in Computer Science, a related field, or equivalent experience.

Experience in testing large scale services, or performance/scale/stress QA

Experience with vendor management and managed services teams (onshore and offshore)

End to end development experience with custom built as well as ERP business applications

Experience with middleware technologies such as Oracle Middleware (OSB/ODI/SOA) and MuleSoft, API Gateway, ActiveMQ, etc. preferred

Experience with Oracle EBS, Oracle Fusion Cloud preferred

Experience with Robotic Process Automation (RPA) software a plus

Applicants must be legally eligible to work in the United States to be considered. Visa sponsorship is not available for this role.

This position is hybrid in Redmond, WA and is not open to remote status at this time.

This position includes the base pay range listed below, potential for a semi-annual discretionary performance bonus, and a comprehensive benefits package that includes medical, dental, vision, 401(k), and paid time off.

Pay Range: $145,150 — $261,200 USD
