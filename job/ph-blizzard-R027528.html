Team Name: Overwatch Job Title: Senior UIUX Designer, Overwatch Requisition ID: R027528 Job Description: The Overwatch team is looking for an accomplished Senior UI/UX Designer with several years of experience creating engaging and immersive user interfaces. The work will be at the core of our player experience, from the intro screen to the in-game HUD. Our ideal candidate will understand the project vision and create user experiences that will be both intuitive and stay true to the visual identity of the game.

We will be asking you to work within our very collaborative team to create concepts and take them from a UX flow all the way to in-game UI. You will be working closely with other UI/UX designers, system & gameplay designers, engineers, VFX artists, and design/art Leads utilizing game engines to create awesome features for Overwatch.

This role offers a flexible hybrid work week, with a mix of remote and on ‑ site days, based out of our Seoul studio. While hybrid is the standard arrangement, candidates may also choose to work on ‑ site full-time if preferred.
