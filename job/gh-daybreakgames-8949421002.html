Position Title: Influencer Campaign Manager

Studio/Department: Marketing/Community

Employee Type: Full Time, Regular

Location : San Diego, CA/Remote (Daybreak Approved Remote Locations (CA, WA, TX, NY))

US Salary Range: $80,000 - $100,000 USD, pay may vary on location

Description

· Build and cultivate long-term relationships with content creators and influencers across streaming platforms, YouTube, TikTok, and emerging channels.

· Establish and maintain a strategic bench of creators at varying tiers—from micro-influencers to established streaming personalities—enabling agile collaboration across Daybreak s service game portfolio.

· Develop and manage both paid sponsorship programs and organic, non-paid partnership opportunities that align with creator authenticity and audience fit.

· Design influencer campaign strategies that span long-form content, short-form content, and live streaming to maximize reach and engagement.

· Drive measurable outcomes through strategic creator partnerships that amplify Daybreak s brand voice and grow player communities across long-tail service games.

This position will be responsible for:

· Identifying, recruiting, and onboarding content creators and influencers at all tier levels (nano, micro, mid-tier, and macro) across streaming platforms and social media.

· Building and maintaining a strategic creator bench with documented profiles, performance metrics, audience demographics, and content specializations.

· Negotiating, structuring, and managing paid sponsorship agreements including deliverables, timelines, compensation, and exclusivity terms.

· Developing organic partnership programs that incentivize creators to produce authentic, non-paid content around Daybreak titles.

· Cultivating long-term relationships with key creators through regular communication, community engagement, and strategic program planning.

· Coordinating multi-format campaigns leveraging long-form content (YouTube essays, in-depth reviews), short-form content (TikTok, Reels), and live streaming (Twitch, YouTube Live).

· Tracking and analyzing influencer campaign performance metrics including reach, engagement, views, conversions, and player acquisition attribution.

· Collaborating with Marketing, Community Management, and Brand teams to align influencer initiatives with broader campaign objectives and go-to-market strategies.

· Managing influencer program budgets, ROI analysis, and providing regular reporting on campaign performance and creator value.

· Staying informed on influencer landscape trends, emerging platforms, creator economy shifts, and gaming community dynamics.

Other Criteria

Requirements/Preferences

· 5+ years of influencer marketing, content creator management, or partnership development experience in the gaming industry.

· Proven track record building and managing creator networks and influencer campaigns that delivered measurable business results.

· Deep understanding of the gaming creator ecosystem, including streaming platforms (Twitch, YouTube Live), social media dynamics, and long-tail gaming communities.

· Experience managing paid sponsorships, affiliate programs, and organic creator partnership programs simultaneously.

· Strong negotiation and relationship management skills with ability to build trust with creators and maintain long-term partnerships.

· Experience with campaign analytics and influencer ROI measurement tools and methodologies.

· Strategic mindset combined with project management excellence and ability to juggle multiple creator relationships and campaigns simultaneously.

· Excellent communication and interpersonal skills with demonstrated ability to work cross-functionally with marketing, community, and product teams.

· Familiarity with long-tail service games and evergreen live-service titles is a strong plus.

· Avid gamer and active consumer of gaming content across streaming and social platforms.
