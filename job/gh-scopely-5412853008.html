Scopely is looking for a Senior Director of Publishing Production to join an unannounced game team remotely in the United States.

At Scopely, we care deeply about what we do and want to inspire play, every day - whether in our work environments alongside our talented colleagues, or through our deep connections with our communities of players. We are a global team of game lovers who are developing, publishing and innovating the mobile games industry, connecting millions of people around the world daily.

We are in the development stages of one of our new unannounced projects, and are looking for passionate game makers to collaborate, get in early and make their mark on this exciting venture!

What You Will Do

As Senior Director of Production, you will lead Publishing Production for one of Scopely s newest mobile titles. Reporting to the VP of Production, you ll sit at the intersection of Publishing, Development, and Marketing, ensuring ambitious ideas become exceptional player experiences through operational excellence, clear execution and trusted cross-functional partnerships.

This role is ideal for a production leader who enjoys turning strategy into action, thrives in complex cross-functional environments, and isn t afraid to roll up their sleeves to solve difficult execution challenges while continuously improving how teams work together.

Lead Publishing Production for the project, translating strategic priorities into coordinated execution across Development, Marketing, Licensing, Live Operations and Publishing teams

Serve as the primary operational bridge between Development and Publishing, ensuring product roadmaps, marketing campaigns, licensing initiatives and player-facing moments remain aligned from planning through execution

Drive end-to-end execution of major publishing initiatives, including soft launch, global launch, integrated marketing campaigns, licensed IP activations, community moments and other high-impact player experiences

Translate strategic direction into actionable execution by establishing clear ownership, priorities, planning frameworks and decision-making rhythms that enable teams to move quickly and confidently

Build trusted partnerships across Product, Development, Marketing, Community, User Acquisition, CRM, Customer Experience and external partners, aligning diverse teams around shared objectives while navigating competing priorities and dependencies

Balance immediate execution with long-term operational excellence by stepping directly into complex production challenges, removing blockers and continuously improving the systems, processes and ways of working behind them

Partner closely with Live Operations, Release Management and Development teams to ensure publishing priorities are fully integrated into release schedules and operational readiness, while maintaining clear ownership across organizations

Provide meaningful production visibility to senior leadership through clear communication, risk management, health reporting and actionable recommendations that support informed decision-making

Lead and develop a growing Publishing Production function, establishing high standards for execution, collaboration and accountability while creating an environment where producers can operate with increasing autonomy

What We re Looking For

Demonstrated experience leading Production, Publishing Production or Program Management within a mobile live-service game, partnering closely with both Development and Publishing organizations

Proven experience delivering complex cross-functional publishing initiatives, including product launches, integrated marketing campaigns, licensing or external partnerships, and large-scale player-facing experiences

Deep understanding of mobile game development and publishing, including the operational complexity, release cadence and planning required to support successful live-service games

Demonstrated ability to translate strategic priorities into clear execution plans while remaining hands-on enough to remove blockers, resolve ambiguity and keep teams moving forward

Experience influencing peers and senior leaders to drive alignment across multiple organizations without relying on direct authority

Proven ability to establish scalable production practices, governance and planning processes that improve execution quality, predictability and organizational effectiveness

Experience leading and developing producers or production leaders while fostering ownership, accountability and continuous improvement

Strong judgment balancing player experience, development realities, publishing priorities, operational risk and business objectives in a fast-moving environment

Excellent communication skills, with the ability to tailor messaging and build alignment across technical, creative and business audiences

Bonus Points

Experience shipping or operating successful mobile free-to-play games

Experience working with licensed IPs and complex external partnerships

Experience supporting games through soft launch, global launch and long-term live operations

Experience working with co-development studios or globally distributed development teams

Experience partnering closely with Marketing, User Acquisition, CRM, Community or Customer Experience organizations

Experience with release management, incident management or operational readiness for large-scale live games

About Scopely

Scopely is a leading video game and global interactive entertainment company, home to many of the world’s most beloved and enduring experiences, including two of the most successful mobile games of all-time “MONOPOLY GO!” and “Pokémon GO,” along with “Stumble Guys,” “Star Trek™ Fleet Command,” “MARVEL Strike Force,” “WWE Champions,” the Scrabble® franchise, “Yahtzee® With Buddies,” and many others. Across mobile, web, PC, and console, Scopely creates, develops, publishes, and live-operates one of the most diversified and award-winning portfolios in the games industry — bringing hundreds of millions of players together through a shared love of play.

Founded in 2011, Scopely is powered by its exceptional team — including thousands of world-class gamemakers around the globe, a distinctive tenet-driven culture, and its proprietary technology platform, Playgami. Together, these strengths have fueled Scopely’s position as the #1 mobile games company in the U.S. and #2 globally, generating more than $10 billion in lifetime revenue. Whether building global sensations like “MONOPOLY GO!” from the ground up, or expanding through strategic acquisitions, including the FoxNext, GSN, and Scopely Explore games businesses — Scopely consistently delivers experiences players love today and return to for years to come.

Recognized multiple times as one of the "100 Most Influential Companies in the World" by TIME magazine and one of Fast Company s "World s Most Innovative Companies" and “Best Workplaces for Innovators,” Scopely believes that video games can be a force for good — creating meaningful connections, vibrant communities, and making life better through play.

Scopely has global operations and partners across four continents in more than a dozen countries worldwide. For more information, visit: https://www.scopely.com/ .
