Junior 2D Artist

Ubisoft Da Nang · Da Nang

Entry Art Posted 2026-11-23 Full time

As a 2D Artist, you will be responsible for creating high-quality visuals for use in worldwide/internal materials, to support various marketing needs for Ubisoft’s gaming franchises.

Key responsibilities include: Participate in games’ visual assets creation and polish, including 2D renders of 3D assets, screenshots, graphic compositions, etc.

Ensure all assets created reach the required quality level and are in coherence with the artistic direction and visual identity of each game.

Produce all required assets for games and marketing campaigns, communication plan.

Propose solutions to given problems and advise/guide Art team members through excellent communication skills and assertiveness.

Anticipate the marketing team needs in the project and proactively streamline productivity.

Work with the Production Artists of the Studio in order to make sure branding assets are consistent with the games.

Educational background in art media, graphic design, or relevant professional experience.

Great command of the Adobe Creative Suite and Photoshop.

Great understanding and use of composition and color theory and knowledge of graphic/visual/stylistic trends in art, film, and games.

Strong illustration and artistic skills.

Interest and ability to move back and forth between different projects.

Excellent communication and organization skills, team spirit, and pro-activity.

As a plus: Experience in 3D art, 3D animation or video editing as well as photography and more traditional arts.

Good Command of Spoken English.

Experience with Digital painting, Key Arts or Concept Art.
