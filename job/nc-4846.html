Monetization & Web Shop Testing

Test live events, offers, and other server-side content before release;

Store & Release

Support game release preparation and deployment processes from the QA side across various mobile platforms;

Collaborate closely with development and other teams during releases and live operations.

Testing & Issue Management

Identify, reproduce, and report defects;

Execute functional and non-functional testing;

Maintain testing scenarios and QA checklists;

Collaborate with cross-functional teams to ensure stable and high-quality releases.

1+ years of experience in mobile software QA, including creating test scenarios/cases, executing different types of testing, and handling production issues;

Strong understanding of software testing and quality assurance processes;

Experience working with QA and project management tools such as TestRail, TestLink, Jira, or similar;

Good spoken and written English communication skills;

Passion for video games.

Extra Great to Have:

Previous experience in game QA, LiveOps, or in-app purchase testing is considered a strong advantage.
