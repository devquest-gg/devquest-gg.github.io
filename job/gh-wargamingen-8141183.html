Job Overview

As a UI/UX Designer, you will shape the web interfaces and interaction systems supporting the World of Warships product. You’ll work closely with developers, QA, analysts, PMs, and other designers to take concepts from initial sketches through implementation, while maintaining a consistent and high-quality visual style. You’ll use a data-driven, product-oriented approach to continuously improve features based on user feedback and contribute to clear UI guidelines and design documentation.

Reports to

Project manager

What will you do?

Design the web interfaces from a sketch to the final implementation and take control of the interface assembly

Maintain and improve the visual style of the web services

Control the visual quality of the UI at each stage of production: conception, design layouts, implementation

Iteratively improve the design of features in accordance with user feedback and possible changes in the technical requirements of the development

Prepare project documentation: interaction description, UI guidelines, fixing rules and improvements in the user interface system

Interact with other designers, analysts, developers, and QA

Create tasks for the 2D team and UI writers

What are we looking for?

3+ years of working experience

Deep understanding of UX principles and interaction design

Data-driven design approach and product-oriented mindset

Experience using analytics, user research, and feedback to drive design decisions

Ability to design scalable UI systems and establish reusable patterns and standards

Excellent knowledge of Figma

Experience working with Jira/Confluence

Advanced level of English and Russian language

What additional skills will help you stand out?

Experience working with AI tools

Basic understanding of layout frontend markup (HTML/CSS), and the use of DevTools to identify differences from the design

Work mode

Hybrid (3 days of work from the office)

About Wargaming

Wargaming is an award-winning online game developer and publisher headquartered in Nicosia, Cyprus. Operating since 1998, Wargaming has become one of the leaders in the gaming industry with 15 offices worldwide, including studios in Chicago, Prague, Shanghai, Tokyo, and Vilnius. Our diverse and multicultural team works together to deliver a top-class experience to millions of players who enjoy Wargaming s titles across all major gaming platforms. Our flagship products include free-to-play hits World of Tanks, World of Warships and World of Tanks Blitz.
