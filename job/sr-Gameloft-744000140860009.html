&#xa0; WHAT YOU WILL BE WORKING ON Cross-Studio Technical Enablement Work closely with studio Technical Directors, leads, and senior engineers to understand how development workflows operate in practice.

Gain hands-on familiarity with multiple engines, tech stacks, and build pipelines used across teams.

Identify recurring patterns, friction points, technical risks, and opportunities for simplification or convergence across teams (pipelines, tooling, CI/CD, testing, iteration speed).

Support teams in aligning on shared approaches where it brings clear benefits — without enforcing uniform solutions.

&#xa0; Initiative & Mission Building Turn observations from the field into clear, actionable technical initiatives.

Help scope these initiatives, define success criteria, and support the setup of small, focused missions with the right contributors.

Contribute to the evolution of shared pipelines and workflows when doing so improves consistency, reliability, or development velocity, and reduces delivery or scalability risks.

Help maintain momentum and clarity across initiatives through coordination and follow-up.

&#xa0; Shared Practices & References Contribute to the definition of lightweight shared practices, reference workflows, or examples when they help teams work more effectively.

Encourage reuse of tools, engine integrations, pipelines, and solutions that have proven value and acceptable risk profiles.

Monitor adoption and outcomes, adjusting approaches based on real usage, feedback, and observed risk trends.

&#xa0; AI-Assisted Development (Enablement-Focused) Explore where AI-assisted tooling can be practically useful for engineers (e.g. code review, refactoring, testing, migrations, documentation).

Support teams in experimenting with and adopting AI tools in a safe, responsible, and incremental way.

Help shape usage guidelines and guardrails together with engineers.

Maintain a clear view of where AI adds value and where it does not, including associated technical or operational risks.

&#xa0; Communication & Alignment Maintain close, ongoing exchange with technical teams across studios.

Share learnings, patterns, and examples across teams to encourage reuse and convergence.

Provide visibility on recurring technical themes, identified risks, and progress of ongoing initiatives.

&#xa0; WHAT YOU NEED TO SUCCEED 8+ years in game/software engineering; shipped titles or production tooling.

T-shaped: experience with multiple engines, tech stacks and build / CI-CD pipelines, with real depth in at least one (build/release, engine integration, or DX tooling).

Proven at driving cross-team change through influence, without direct authority - strong facilitation and stakeholder management.

Producer-like coordination: scoping initiatives, defining success criteria, running small focused missions, follow-through.

AI-assisted dev tools experience (code review, refactoring, testing, migrations) WHO YOU ARE Excellent communicator&#xa0;(English): turns messy technical reality into clear, shared narratives; credible with engineers and leadership alike.

Pragmatic and risk-aware: sound judgment on where convergence / AI tooling helps and where it does not.

Comfortable in a distributed, multi-studio, multi-timezone organization.

Nice to have: DevEx / build-release background.
