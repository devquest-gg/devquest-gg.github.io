Lead – Strategic Operations & Partnerships — SEA and OCE

Every day, tens of millions of people come to Roblox to explore, create, play, learn, and connect with friends in 3D immersive digital experiences– all created by our global community of developers and creators.

At Roblox, we’re building the tools and platform that empower our community to bring any experience that they can imagine to life. Our vision is to reimagine the way people come together, from anywhere in the world, and on any device.

We’re on a mission to connect a billion people with optimism and civility, and looking for amazing talent to help us get there.

A career at Roblox means you’ll be working to shape the future of human interaction, solving unique technical challenges at scale, and helping to create safer, more civil shared experiences for everyone.

As a key leader in Strategic Operations Partnerships, you will serve as a strategic and operational partner to regional leadership, driving business performance, planning, and execution across Singapore, Southeast Asia (SEA), and Oceania (OCE).

You will focus on driving operational excellence, enabling data-driven decision-making, managing business reviews, developing ROI submissions, and ensuring strong cross-functional alignment. Alongside this, you will support strategic partnerships and commercial initiatives that contribute to Roblox s regional business objectives.

You Will

Drive Strategic Business Operations: Lead regional business planning, Quarterly Business Reviews (QBRs), performance tracking, ROI submissions, investment proposals, and business cases.

Enable Data-Driven Decisions: Build and manage datasets, dashboards, and performance reports, translating business data into actionable insights and recommendations for leadership.

Lead Cross-Functional Execution: Drive alignment across Product, Finance, Marketing, Legal, Policy, and regional teams, ensuring clear ownership and execution of strategic priorities.

Support Strategic Partnerships: Evaluate and support commercial partnerships across payments, digital wallets, commerce, telecommunications, entertainment, and distribution, assessing opportunities through commercial and strategic lenses.

Partner with Regional Leadership : Act as a trusted thought partner and key execution counterpart, helping shape priorities, solve business challenges, and drive regional performance.

You Have

Proven Track Record: 7–10+ years of experience in strategic operations, business operations, strategy, management consulting, or commercial strategy, with demonstrated ownership of complex initiatives.

Strong Financial Analytical Acumen: Experience developing ROI submissions, investment proposals, business cases, financial assessments, and working with datasets to generate actionable insights.

Regional Industry Experience: Experience across multiple markets, ideally Southeast Asia, within consumer technology, digital platforms, gaming, fintech, payments, marketplaces, or telecommunications.

Executive Influence Cross-Functional Alignment: Strong stakeholder management skills, with experience driving senior-level discussions, QBRs, business reviews, and alignment across multiple functions.

Hands-On Operating Discipline: Strong ownership, structured problem-solving, and execution capabilities, with the ability to navigate ambiguity and independently drive initiatives end-to-end.

You Are

A strategic operator with strong analytical, commercial, and execution capabilities.

Comfortable moving between business planning, financial analysis, executive presentations, and cross-functional execution.

A trusted business partner who can connect strategy with execution and influence stakeholders without direct authority.

Excited by the opportunity to help shape Roblox s business across Southeast Asia and Oceania.

Roles that are based in an office are onsite Tuesday, Wednesday, and Thursday, with optional presence on Monday and Friday (unless otherwise noted).
