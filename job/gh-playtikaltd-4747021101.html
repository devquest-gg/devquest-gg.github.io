General Purpose of the Job The Senior VIP Account Manager owns a portfolio of our highest-value and most important players, responsible for driving their lifetime value, KPIs, satisfaction, and retention. This role requires a proactive, creative, and highly independent professional who can manage day-to-day player communication, deeply analyze account data, and work cross-functionally across the business to deliver a premium, personalized VIP experience.

Responsibilities

Own a portfolio of top-tier VIP players, managing their day-to-day communication, concerns, and overall relationship

Drive lifetime value, KPIs, satisfaction, and retention across the portfolio through proactive, personalized engagement

Analyze player data and behavior patterns to inform outreach strategy, identify risks, and uncover growth opportunities

Build and manage player segments to communicate updates, changes, and targeted messaging aligned with business goals

Collect and analyze player feedback and sentiment, flagging insights to management and relevant business units

Leverage AI tools to enhance efficiency, personalization, and decision-making within the account

Manage a VIP social community, fostering engagement and reflecting community sentiment back to the business

Drive revenue growth and engagement trends within the portfolio through data-driven, creative initiatives

Work independently and proactively across departments — Product, BA, Economy, and Monetization — to align player needs with business strategy

Build and maintain reports to track account performance and communicate progress to stakeholders

Requirements

3+ years of experience in customer success, account management, or VIP/relationship management — Must

Proven analytical competencies and strong data-oriented mindset — Must

Native or mother-tongue level English — Must

Demonstrated experience working independently and cross-functionally with multiple business units — Must

Strong understanding of or hands-on experience with AI tools in a professional context — Advantage

Technical background (SQL, relevant coursework, degree, or technological unit) — Nice to Have

Experience managing or building social media communities — Advantage

Experience performing on camera (hosting, narrating, acting) — Nice to Have

Target/revenue-driven mindset with a track record of measurable account growth
