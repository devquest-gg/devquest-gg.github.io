Experience Strategy Manager

San Mateo, CA (Hybrid)

Role overview:

Our team operates within the wider ‘Services Commercial’ group who are dedicated to growing PlayStation’s subscription services. This wider group strives to maximise new subscriber acquisition and retention and provide a compelling reason for people to return to the service year after year. Our product, PlayStation Plus, is critically important to PlayStation’s vision and strategy. PlayStation Plus is subscribed to by the majority of PlayStation users and has seen dynamic growth since re-launching with a multi-tiered proposition in 2022.

You will be joining the ‘Experience’ team – whose mission is to craft best-in-class customer experiences that foster an informed, engaged, and excited group of subscribers. What does that mean? Anywhere you find PlayStation Plus on a PlayStation console, or the PlayStation App, our team is determining what our customers see – the latest, the greatest, and the most relevant messages for each customer. We are responsible for both subscriber and non-subscriber experiences – maximising subscription purchases, tier upgrades, and engagement with service benefits.

We optimize user experiences through continuous test and learn activity and bring ‘moments-that-matter’ to life through innovative merchandising.

You will join a sub-team purely focused on developing the PlayStation Plus user experience and optimising customer journeys. As the Experience Strategy Manager, your primary responsibility will be to set the personalization strategy for PlayStation Plus digital experiences on console and app. You will work cross functionally with delivery teams to implement available personalization merchandising technology to help drive our business goals of Subscriber Growth, Retention, and Benefit Engagement – ensuring tools are properly tested for impact and player satisfaction.

In addition, you will also serve as the team lead for member lifecycle experiences. You will develop and optimize customer journeys that meet players where they are in their relationship with PlayStation Plus across the full player lifecycle (i.e., Acquisition, Early Life, In-Life Value Realization, Renewal, Win Back, etc.). You will coordinate with other teams across PlayStation to ensure the our member lifecycle experiences show up in proper contexts across the broader PlayStation Ecosystem of player journeys.

You will leverage your experience working with Product Engineering Stakeholders serve as the primary point of contact in the business for new personalization feature development – ensuring that the Business fully understands new personalization technology as is becomes available and that Product Engineering understand PlayStation Plus business needs as they plan and prioritize future development efforts.

The Experience team are on a growth journey – We’re expanding our team significantly, and we want you to help us with that journey.

What you’ll be doing:

Leading Personalization Strategy for PlayStation Plus on Console App

Leveraging insights from telemetry data, UX Research, test-and-learn activity, and industry best practices to set the Personalization Strategy for PlayStation Plus.

Working with Product Managers and experience operators to fully understand capabilities of existing Personalization technology and how it can benefit Plus.

Identifying key opportunities to implement and optimize use of personalized merchandising tools - Designing and executing experiments to gauge impact.

Exploring new ways to personalize the PlayStation Plus experience through cohorted experiences, celebrating of loyalty, membership value reinforcement, etc.

Crafting and Owning PlayStation Plus Lifecycle Experiences

Building maintaining intuitive lifecycle-based customer journeys for PlayStation Plus members on Console App such as new member onboarding, upsells, etc.

Establishing a strong culture for experimentation and optimization of evergreen lifecycle experiences such as the PlayStation Plus cancellation journey.

Working across PlayStation teams to represent the PlayStation Plus Experience team on the development of Next-Best-Action journeys and personalization tools.

Representing the Experience team with Personalization Product Engineering Teams

Work internally to understand business needs regarding personalization across teams and their estimated levels of priority and impact.

Work cross-functionally with Personalization Product Managers to ensure PlayStation Plus is included and properly planned for in development efforts.

Maintain regular two-way communication between Product Managers and internal business stakeholders regarding, roadmap updates, shifting business needs, etc.

Collaborate with Product Stakeholders to plan experiments across our owned channels that can help inform future development or optimizations to tooling.

What we are looking for:

5+ years’ experience roles focused on optimising a customer’s digital experience

Bachelor’s degree or equivalent

Experience working with digital products and channels (websites, apps, emails, etc.)

Experience working with personalization technology and/or customer journey mapping

Experience working cross functionally with Product Engineering stakeholders

Proven ability to lead projects with multiple stakeholder teams from ideation thru delivery

A data-led mindset, comfortable with measurement and interpreting analysis in Excel

Sound, logical approach to prioritising which audiences and opportunities to focus efforts on

A customer first mentality – If it leads to a negative customer experience – you don’t like it!

Strong presentation skills and fluency in PowerPoint

Bonus: Management Consulting or Subscriptions background
