Every day, tens of millions of people come to Roblox to explore, create, play, learn, and connect with friends in 3D immersive digital experiences– all created by our global community of developers and creators.

At Roblox, we’re building the tools and platform that empower our community to bring any experience that they can imagine to life. Our vision is to reimagine the way people come together, from anywhere in the world, and on any device.

We’re on a mission to connect a billion people with optimism and civility, and looking for amazing talent to help us get there.

A career at Roblox means you’ll be working to shape the future of human interaction, solving unique technical challenges at scale, and helping to create safer, more civil shared experiences for everyone.

As a Software Engineer on the Creator, Experience LiveOps team, you ll work full-stack across our backend services and the creator-hub web app (TypeScript/React) to ship features that let every creator run live-ops the way large studios do: safely, and at platform scale. You ll own meaningful pieces from day one and work closely with senior and principal engineers, our PM, and design. Join us and give the entire creator community live-ops superpowers.

The Experience LiveOps (ELO) Team builds the runtime tools creators use to operate and tune their live experiences without shipping a code release. Our surfaces include Remote Configs (targeted, scheduled, and AI-assisted configuration), A/B Experimentation with real metrics and early harm detection, audience Segmentation, and Open Cloud / public APIs that expose all of it programmatically.

You Will:

Build and ship features across ELO s config, experimentation, and segmentation surfaces, backend services and the creator-hub UI.

Own projects end-to-end (spec to launch) with support from senior/principal engineers on the team.

Partner with PM, design, DSA/Discovery, and Data Platform to deliver high-quality, well-instrumented features.

Participate in code reviews, share knowledge with teammates, and take part in the team s on-call rotation.

You Are:

An engineer with 1-3 years of experience building software systems (industry experience).

Full-stack-comfortable: you can move between backend services and a web frontend (TypeScript/React), and you re not afraid to learn the half you know less well.

A finisher: you take features from spec through launch with guidance from senior teammates, and you care that they keep working — testing, monitoring, and follow-through.

Pragmatic collaborative: you communicate clearly, ask good questions, and are low-ego about code and feedback.

Curious: you stay current and like understanding why a system behaves the way it does.
