ABOUT VOODOO

Voodoo is a consumer tech company founded in Paris in 2013, with one mission: entertain the world. Today we are 1,000 people, generating $1b+ in annual revenue, profitably. Our games and apps have passed 7 billion downloads and reach 200 million+ active users every month, making Voodoo the #3 mobile publisher worldwide in downloads, after Google and Meta. We were backed by Goldman Sachs, Tencent and GBL along the way.

TEAM

Our Corporate teams support the company’s performance and growth whilst putting people at the core of everything they do. As a French tech leader and global successful company, our Corporate teams are constantly looking for ways to innovate and increase organization efficiency to keep Voodoo ahead of the market. Voodoo’s Corporate function is composed of three teams: HR, Legal and Finance, working together to drive Voodoo’s success.

ROLE

We're looking for a Workplace Experience Specialist (Office Manager) based in Istanbul to join our Corporate team. In this role, you'll be the driving force behind the Istanbul office experience - keeping day-to-day operations running smoothly while continuously improving the workplace as the team scales from 15 to 40+ people.

You will report directly to the Workplace Manager in Paris, with a dotted-line relationship to the local General Manager on the ground.

Employee Equipment & IT Support

- Act as the first point of contact for all equipment and IT-related requests in Turkey, from onboarding to offboarding

- Ship devices and accessories to employees (newcomers and swaps) across all of Turkey

- Handle requests via the dedicated ticket inbox and set up employee workstations as part of onboarding (screen, keyboard/mouse, accessories)

- Manage equipment stock and allocate devices to employees based on tickets received from IT

- Manage offboarding: device reception and wiping

Vendor Management & Budget

- Run daily vendor relationship management: quality checks, invoice follow-up, and ensuring vendors respect the defined budget

- Flag underperforming vendors and lead the weekly Vendor & Budget review meeting with the Paris Workplace Manager and the local General Manager

- Maintain accurate, up-to-date budget views in Google Suite and deliver a structured monthly report to Paris, flagging risks early

- Keep snacking stocked and services running (cleaning, maintenance) at a high standard

Monthly Employee Satisfaction Survey

- Own the full satisfaction survey cycle: send monthly surveys, adjust questions as needed, synthesize results, and hold a monthly review with the Workplace Manager

- Build time-bound action plans from survey insights, deploy improvements, and report on progress and impact in the following month's review

Other Responsibilities

- Lead small office events and team-building activities that reinforce Voodoo's culture in Istanbul

- Scale operations proactively as headcount increases, anticipating needs and adapting processes to maintain a consistent workplace standard.

PROFILE

- 1 to 5 years of experience in Workplace, Office Management, Facilities, or a closely related role, ideally in a startup, scale-up, or high-growth tech environment

- Fluent in both English and Turkish (written and spoken); able to communicate clearly whether briefing Paris or negotiating with a local supplier - French is a plus

- Proficient in Google Suite (Docs, Sheets, Slides, Drive); Microsoft Suite experience is accepted; Slack & Notion are a plus

- Hands-on experience with employee equipment and IT support: ticket handling, device setup, stock management, shipping, and onboarding/offboarding workflows

- Solid local vendor network in Istanbul (cleaning, maintenance, snacking suppliers) and proven ability to manage supplier performance day to day

- Comfortable owning a budget line, tracking spend against plan, and reporting in a structured, data-driven way

- Able to run a survey process and translate feedback into actionable plans with clear owners and deadlines

- Entrepreneurial, autonomous, and proactive - you don't wait for a playbook, you write it

- Available to be on-site a minimum of 4 days per week in the Istanbul office

- Experience with ticketing or IT asset management tools is a plus
