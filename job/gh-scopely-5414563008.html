Director, Legal Operations

Scopely · Culver City

Director+ Other Hybrid $160K–$176K Posted 2026-09-03 Full time
Apply on Scopely’s site →

Scopely is seeking a Director, Legal Operations to join our Legal team. This role will lead the Legal Operations function and establish the strategy, operating model, technology roadmap, governance framework, and performance infrastructure for a global, high-growth Legal department.

This role will be a hybrid position in our Culver City, CA hub.

The Director will directly manage the Sr. Manager, Legal Operations, who owns day-to-day Ironclad CLM operations, knowledge management infrastructure, and AI-enabled legal technology implementation. The Director will provide strategic direction, prioritize the team’s roadmap, manage resources and vendors, and remain accountable for the quality, adoption, and business impact of these programs.

This is a senior people-leadership and functional-building role for someone who can translate Legal leadership’s priorities into scalable systems, processes, and measurable outcomes. The right candidate will combine strategic judgment, operational discipline, technical fluency, executive presence, and the ability to build trusted partnerships across Legal and the broader business . as a strategic business partner, not just a support or systems-administration function. They will be comfortable operating in ambiguity, creating structure where none exists, and leading through influence in a fast-paced global gaming company.

What You Will Do

Legal Operations Strategy Functional Leadership

Partner directly with Legal leadership to define and execute a multi-year Legal Operations strategy aligned with Scopely’s business priorities and growth

Translate Legal department priorities into an integrated roadmap covering technology, process improvement, data, knowledge management, automation, training, and organizational capability

Establish annual and quarterly priorities, success metrics, resource plans, and sequencing recommendations for the Legal Operations team

Advise Legal leadership on tradeoffs among business impact, risk, cost, speed, and available team capacity

Develop the Legal Operations budget, oversee spend, build business cases for new investments, and manage vendor and renewal strategy

Identify opportunities to standardize, automate, and simplify Legal processes across Commercial, Corporate, Employment, Product Compliance, and other Legal functions

Directly manage, coach, and develop the Sr. Manager, Legal Operations, who owns Ironclad CLM operations, knowledge management infrastructure, and AI implementation

Legal Technology Strategy Governance

Serve as the executive owner of Scopely’s Legal technology portfolio and establish a coherent architecture and investment strategy across CLM, knowledge management, matter management, e-billing, contract analytics, automation, and related tools

Own the Legal technology roadmap, including technology selection, implementation sequencing, integration priorities, system rationalization, and long-term scalability

Oversee Ironclad CLM as a critical Legal and business system, ensuring that the Sr. Manager has the direction, resources, governance framework, and stakeholder alignment necessary to operate the platform effectively

Establish governance standards for workflow design, metadata, templates, permissions, approval logic, integrations, repository integrity, data retention, change management, and system access

Review and approve significant platform changes, balancing user experience, business needs, control requirements, data integrity, and long-term maintainability

Partner with IT, Information Security, Privacy, Procurement, Finance, and external vendors on architecture, security, integrations, implementation, support, and risk management

Lead or oversee system migrations, data consolidation efforts, post-acquisition integrations, and other technology transformation initiatives

Knowledge Management, Automation AI

Establish the strategic vision and operating model for Legal knowledge management, including content architecture, taxonomy, ownership, curation, searchability, version control, and access governance

Ensure that Legal’s institutional knowledge is captured, structured, maintained, and made accessible in ways that support self-service, consistency, and future AI applications

Develop a portfolio of practical AI and automation use cases for Legal in partnership with Legal leadership, IT, and relevant business stakeholders

Oversee the implementation and responsible adoption of AI-enabled tools, including chatbots, knowledge-based assistants, workflow automation, contract analytics, and other emerging technologies

Direct the Sr. Manager’s hands-on execution of knowledge management and AI initiatives, including platform configuration, testing, content population, user feedback, optimization, and rollout

Define success metrics for AI and automation initiatives, including adoption, response quality, time saved, reduction in repetitive inquiries, and measurable improvements to the Legal user experience

Stay current on legal technology and AI developments and make informed recommendations regarding opportunities, risks, and investment priorities

Process Improvement Business Partnership

Serve as a strategic partner to Legal leadership, Procurement, Finance, Information Security, IT, People, Product, and other stakeholders to improve the end-to-end process of contracting and Legal support experience

Establish scalable intake, triage, and routing models that direct work to the appropriate Legal or business resource and minimize unnecessary attorney involvement

Use qualitative and quantitative data to identify recurring friction, diagnose root causes, and implement structural solutions rather than one-off fixes

Support Legal leadership to define and adopt practical playbooks, self-service resources, approval frameworks, and standardized workflows

Data, Analytics Performance Management

Act as a strategic partner with Commercial Legal to design and monitor metrics such as contracting cycle times, intake volumes, routing accuracy, self-service rates, workflow adoption, support trends, system reliability, automation impact, and stakeholder satisfaction

Establish a Legal Operations performance framework that measures the efficiency, quality, adoption, capacity, and business impact of Legal services and systems

Create executive-ready dashboards and reporting for Legal leadership, highlighting trends, risks, opportunities, and recommended actions

Use data to support capacity planning, prioritization, investment decisions, vendor management, and continuous improvement

What We’re Looking For

7-10+ years of experience in Legal Operations, legal technology, business operations, process transformation, consulting, or a related field, with clear progression in scope, responsibility, and organizational leadership

Demonstrated experience leading or building a Legal Operations function within a high-growth technology, gaming, entertainment, digital media, or similarly complex global company

At least 3–5 years of experience managing, coaching, and developing professionals, including experience setting priorities, delegating ownership, giving feedback, and building team capability

Experience establishing a Legal Operations strategy, operating model, roadmap, budget, governance framework, and performance-measurement program

Significant experience with enterprise CLM programs, including Ironclad or a comparable platform. Direct Ironclad administration experience is strongly preferred; the candidate must have sufficient technical depth to evaluate platform architecture, workflow design, data governance, integrations, controls, and adoption

Demonstrated ability to manage budgets, negotiate with vendors, assess technology investments, and measure realized value

Experience overseeing legal technology portfolios beyond CLM, such as knowledge management, e-billing, matter management, contract analytics, automation, compliance, or AI-enabled tools

Proven ability to build or mature a knowledge management function and translate institutional knowledge into structured, searchable, reusable content

Experience evaluating, implementing, governing, or scaling AI and automation tools in a professional-services, legal, technology, or corporate environment

Strong analytical and systems-thinking skills, with the ability to identify patterns, diagnose root causes, and design solutions that scale

Excellent executive presence and communication skills, with the ability to influence senior Legal and business leaders and translate technical or operational issues into clear business decisions

Strong project and change-management skills, including the ability to lead complex initiatives across multiple teams with competing priorities

A highly collaborative, practical, and service-oriented leadership style, with the judgment to balance user experience, business enablement, risk management, and operational discipline

Comfort operating with significant autonomy in a fast-paced, evolving environment and the ability to create clarity, structure, and momentum amid ambiguity

Nice to Have

Experience leading Legal Operations in a gaming, interactive entertainment, digital media, or consumer technology company

Direct experience with Ironclad, contract analytics, legal knowledge platforms, or similar tools

Experience with post-acquisition Legal technology integrations, data migrations, or operating-model integration

Experience managing outside counsel, legal vendors, e-billing, matter management, or Legal department spend

Experience developing responsible-use standards or governance frameworks for AI in a Legal or regulated environment

Experience partnering with geographically distributed teams and supporting global Legal operations

A sense of humor and a passion for mobile gaming

What Success Looks Like

Within the first year, the Director will have established a clear Legal Operations strategy and roadmap; created a well-functioning operating model for the Legal Operations team; enabled the Sr. Manager to own day-to-day CLM, knowledge management, and AI execution; implemented a consistent performance-reporting framework; strengthened governance across Legal technology; and delivered measurable improvements in self-service, workflow adoption, contracting efficiency, and Legal team capacity.

About Scopely

Scopely is a leading video game and global interactive entertainment company, home to many of the world’s most beloved and enduring experiences, including two of the most successful mobile games of all-time “MONOPOLY GO!” and “Pokémon GO,” along with “Stumble Guys,” “Star Trek™ Fleet Command,” “MARVEL Strike Force,” “WWE Champions,” the Scrabble® franchise, “Yahtzee® With Buddies,” and many others. Across mobile, web, PC, and console, Scopely creates, develops, publishes, and live-operates one of the most diversified and award-winning portfolios in the games industry — bringing hundreds of millions of players together through a shared love of play.

Founded in 2011, Scopely is powered by its exceptional team — including thousands of world-class gamemakers around the globe, a distinctive tenet-driven culture, and its proprietary technology platform, Playgami. Together, these strengths have fueled Scopely’s position as the #1 mobile games company in the U.S. and #2 globally, generating more than $10 billion in lifetime revenue. Whether building global sensations like “MONOPOLY GO!” from the ground up, or expanding through strategic acquisitions, including the FoxNext, GSN, and Scopely Explore games businesses — Scopely consistently delivers experiences players love today and return to for years to come.

Recognized multiple times as one of the "100 Most Influential Companies in the World" by TIME magazine and one of Fast Company s "World s Most Innovative Companies" and “Best Workplaces for Innovators,” Scopely believes that video games can be a force for good — creating meaningful connections, vibrant communities, and making life better through play.

Scopely has global operations and partners across four continents in more than a dozen countries worldwide. For more information, visit: https://www.scopely.com/ .
