Animate UIs and environmental elements in Unity

Animate characters and items in 2D

Create engaging animations for result screens, offers, widgets, pop-ups, transitions, effects, and micro-interactions

Required Skills, Knowledge and Expertise

Game development experience

Nice to have:

Drawing skills

Experience with 3D animation

Experience working with shaders in Unity 3D

Open-mindedness and hands-on experience with AI pipelines
