Job Overview

The Head of Workplace and Employee Experience is responsible for creating an engaging, effective and inspiring workplace experience for our employees in Vilnius. The role combines workplace strategy, employee experience, culture and cross-functional leadership, partnering closely with senior leaders and key corporate functions to ensure our people and workplace environment support the company s goals.

Reports to

General Manager, Wargaming Vilnius

What will you do?

Lead and drive workplace and employee experience initiatives across our locations, from concept to execution.

Ensure smooth, efficient, and high-quality day-to-day workplace operations.

Create an engaging, welcoming, and productive workplace environment that reflects our culture and supports our people.

Own and manage the workplace budget, ensuring effective allocation of resources and cost efficiency.

Lead complex workplace projects, managing multiple stakeholders, priorities, timelines, and challenges.

Oversee relationships with landlords, suppliers, service providers, and other external partners.

Ensure workplace operations comply with relevant health safety, building, waste management, and other regulatory requirements.

Identify opportunities to improve workplace services, processes, and employee experience, taking a proactive and forward-looking approach.

Partner closely with People, Finance, IT, Legal, and other teams to deliver effective workplace solutions.

What are we looking for?

Proven experience leading workplace, facilities, office operations, employee experience, or a similar function in an international environment.

Strong project and operational management skills, with the ability to get things done and navigate complex challenges.

Excellent communication and stakeholder management skills, with the ability to build strong relationships across different teams and levels.

Hands-on, proactive approach - you are comfortable getting into the details when needed while keeping the bigger picture in mind.

Strong organisational and problem-solving skills, with the ability to manage multiple priorities and make things happen.

Experience managing budgets, suppliers, and external partners.

Knowledge of relevant health safety, building codes, and waste management regulations.

What additional skills will help you stand out?

Hospitality mindset: you see workplace operations as service delivery, anticipate needs, resolve issues gracefully, and believe that how we care for our people matters as much as the work itself.

Open-minded and forward-looking: you are curious, embrace new ideas, and proactively think about what our workplace and employee experience should look like tomorrow.

Excellent communicator: you know how to bring people together, communicate clearly, and build trust across diverse teams and stakeholders.

Work mode

Onsite

About Wargaming

Wargaming is an award-winning online game developer and publisher headquartered in Nicosia, Cyprus. Operating since 1998, Wargaming has become one of the leaders in the gaming industry with 15 offices worldwide, including studios in Chicago, Prague, Shanghai, Tokyo, and Vilnius. Our diverse and multicultural team works together to deliver a top-class experience to millions of players who enjoy Wargaming s titles across all major gaming platforms. Our flagship products include free-to-play hits World of Tanks, World of Warships and World of Tanks Blitz.
