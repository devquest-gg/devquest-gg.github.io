Cloud Imperium Games is a leading game development company known for its innovative and immersive gaming experiences. We are dedicated to creating high-quality games that captivate and engage players worldwide.

Founded in 2012 by renowned game developer Chris Roberts, Cloud Imperium Games (CIG) is one of the largest global independent studios, 1,000+ employees, dedicated to delivering next-generation AAA games. CIG prides itself in thinking outside the box, encouraging creativity and developing ground-breaking technology. CIG believes in raising the bar for the gaming industry and the community as a whole.

What will you do?  Support the Controller / Chief Accountant across their full range of responsibilities and contribute to the smooth running of the complete accounting cycle  Prepare and record journal entries (accruals, prepaids, depreciation, payroll, etc.)  Actively participate in month-end, quarter-end, and year-end closes  Perform bank reconciliations and general ledger account analysis  Contribute to the preparation of financial statements and monthly management reports  Prepare government remittances (GST/QST, source deductions) and support tax filings  Process intercompany transactions with the group&#x27;s international entities  Support the team during external audits (preparing working papers, responding to auditors)  Review and support the accounting clerk&#x27;s work (accounts payable and receivable)  Validate payroll following the data entry performed by the Human Resources team  Ensure compliance with internal controls, accounting policies, and sound practices  Contribute to continuous process improvement and the optimization of tools (ERP / Workday)  Collaborate with production and human resources teams to ensure the accuracy of project-related financial data  What do we expect from our Accountant?  Bachelor&#x27;s degree in accounting (required)  CPA designation completed or in progress (an asset)  3 to 5 years of experience in accounting (experience in a technology company or video game studio is an asset)  Excellent proficiency in Excel and office tools  Knowledge of accounting standards (ASPE / IFRS)  Rigor, autonomy, analytical mindset, organizational skills, and attention to detail  Ability to work in a dynamic and collaborative environment  Strong asset: experience with Workday (finance or HR)  Knowledge of an ERP or accounting software: a plus  Bilingual French-English Turbulent is committed to employing a diverse workforce. Qualified applicants will receive consideration without regard to race, color, religion, sex, national origin, age, sexual orientation, gender identity, gender expression, or disability.
