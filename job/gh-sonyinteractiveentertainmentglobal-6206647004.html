Why Sony Interactive Entertainment?

Sony Interactive Entertainment isn’t just the Best Place to Play — it’s also the Best Place to Work. Sony Interactive Entertainment (SIE) is the company behind the PlayStation brand. As a subsidiary of Sony Group Corporation, we’re part of a proud legacy of innovation and excellence. SIE is a dynamic technology company, delivering cutting-edge hardware and network services to more than 100 million people and an entertainment leader, home to some of the most beloved and recognizable intellectual properties (IP) in the world. Our role at SIE is to create and nurture the experiences under the PlayStation brand, a name synonymous with entertainment excellence and creativity.

PlayStation Studios has created some of the most loved worlds, characters, and stories in entertainment. Our opportunity now is to build those IP with greater intention — growing audiences, deepening fandom, and creating more ways for people to engage with PlayStation franchises beyond the release of a game.

We are looking for a Lead Franchise Strategist to help shape that work.

This is not an integrated marketing role, a licensing or consumer products role, or a role focused on extending a launch campaign. We are looking for someone who understands how entertainment franchises are built over time — across audiences, stories, formats, products, experiences, and partners.

Gaming experience is valuable but not required. Experience in film, television, animation, publishing, consumer products, licensing, live experiences, or location-based entertainment may be just as relevant.

What matters most is that this person has helped build something. They can look at an IP, understand what makes it special, identify where the strongest audience and commercial opportunities exist, and turn that into a clear and credible plan for growth.

This role will work across PlayStation Studios, Portfolio Strategy, PlayStation Productions, brand, marketing, insights, finance, consumer products, licensing, legal, and Sony Group partners — connecting creative ambition, audience demand, and commercial opportunity while protecting what fans already love.

Key Responsibilities

Lead franchise strategy work for assigned priority PlayStation IP, from initial opportunity assessment through long-term planning and cross-functional alignment

Develop franchise strategies that define audience opportunity, positioning, expansion potential, commercial rationale, and priorities for growth

Proactively identify new ways priority IP could grow, including opportunities that may sit outside PlayStation’s existing franchise playbook

Identify where each franchise has the strongest potential across film, television, animation, publishing, consumer products, music, live and location-based experiences, partnerships, and digital/community engagement

Help define what each franchise should become over time — and just as importantly, where it should not go

Use audience research, fandom insights, market trends, competitive benchmarks, and commercial data to make clear recommendations to leadership and cross-functional partners

Translate franchise ambition into practical roadmaps, sequencing, decision frameworks, and execution priorities

Support the development of franchise bibles, IP briefs, creative guardrails, and other tools that enable partners to build from a shared understanding of the IP

Partner with finance and business teams to understand and articulate the value of franchise activity across games and adjacent businesses

Help evolve PlayStation’s approach to long-term franchise planning, bringing greater consistency and rigor to how opportunities are identified, evaluated, and prioritized

Champion the fan perspective — understanding what audiences love, where they want more, what will feel authentic, and where expansion could damage trust

What We’re Looking For

Strong experience in franchise strategy, IP development, entertainment strategy, brand expansion, audience growth, or a related field, with a track record of independently leading complex strategic work

A track record of helping build, shape, expand, or revitalize entertainment IP, ideally across multiple formats or business areas

Strong understanding of fandom — how audiences discover, engage with, spend around, advocate for, and stay connected to IP over time

Experience working across creative and commercial disciplines, including areas such as production, research, finance, marketing, licensing, consumer products, and external partnerships

Ability to synthesize complex creative, audience, and commercial information into clear recommendations, influence senior stakeholders, and build alignment around a direction

Strong creative taste and commercial judgment — someone who can see both the magic of an IP and the business around it

Experience with areas such as franchise architecture, audience segmentation, IP positioning, content roadmaps, commercial planning, or long-range portfolio strategy

Comfort operating in ambiguity and creating structure around IP that may not yet have a fully formed franchise ecosystem

Ability to challenge assumptions constructively and make thoughtful calls about what is worth doing, what should wait, and what should not happen at all

A practical, collaborative approach and the ability to influence across teams without always owning final execution

Experience with global entertainment brands or IP with multi-market potential is a plus

The Ideal Candidate

The ideal candidate is a builder, not a caretaker. They may have worked on established IP or helped build something from an earlier stage, but they are most energized by the question of what comes next.

They can look at a game world, character, story, or fanbase and see the broader franchise potential — not as a list of extensions, but as a connected system of creative expansion, audience growth, and long-term value.

They are equally comfortable talking about story, fandom, audience behavior, commercial models, and execution realities. They know that not every IP should become everything. They have the discipline to prioritize, the taste to protect what matters, and the strategic instincts to spot where PlayStation can create something fans will genuinely care about.

Success in This Role Looks Like

The priority PlayStation IP they support have clear, actionable long-term franchise strategies

Teams across PlayStation and Sony have a shared understanding of where those franchises can grow, where they should not, and why

New opportunities are identified proactively and evaluated earlier and more consistently, with strong audience, creative, and commercial rationale

Fans have meaningful ways to stay connected with PlayStation worlds between major game releases

The role contributes to a more coordinated and repeatable approach to building PlayStation franchises for the long term
