User Acquisition Manager

Easybrain · Limassol

Mid Marketing Hybrid Posted 2026-06-24 Full time
Apply on Easybrain’s site →

Easybrain is looking for a skilled User Acquisition Manager, to acquire users for iOS/Android games through different mobile advertising networks and sources (sources list is flexible/tbd). You’ll be running performance marketing campaigns, cooperate on creatives with design team, optimize targeting, volumes, action events etc. in order to maximize UA campaigns performance.

Responsibilities:

- Ads campaigns management;

- Ads campaigns optimization (any kind) in order to improve ROI (decrease CPI, increase LTV/ARPU);

- Users quality analysis (based on internal reporting tools) in order to optimize/improve UA performance;

- Communication with Team Lead, Designers, Marketing Managers, Ad networks’ account managers;

- Reporting.

Requirements:

- Mobile user acquisition experience of 2+ years;

- Analytical skills and mindset;

- Common sense;

- Russian language proficiency is a must;

- English language level - intermediate+;

- A significant advantage would be experience in working with and managing campaigns on Applovin, Unity, Mintegral, also with preinstalls and incentivised traffic sources.

Benefits:

Besides the engaging tasks, support from experienced colleagues, challenge, and drive, we offer:

- Full support in relocating to countries where our offices are located;

- High-end market salary with performance bonuses;

- All needed equipment;

- Regular company events and monthly Friday meetings;

- Social benefits (private medical cover, sports reimbursement, etc.);

- Paid vacations, sick days;

- English, Greek, and Polish online language classes;

- Reimbursement for education and professional development.
