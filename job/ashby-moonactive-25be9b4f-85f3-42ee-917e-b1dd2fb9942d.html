AI Designer

Moon Active · Remote · Remote

Mid Marketing Remote Posted 2026-07-29 Full time

Moon Active, a leading global mobile game developer entertaining millions worldwide, is seeking a highly skilled and creative AI Artist specialized in Gameplay and UI for this remote global position. You will join our innovative team to focus on gameplay, UI, game layout, wireframing, and brand theming, utilizing generative art tools and the Adobe Suite.

This role is focused on gameplay, UI, game layout, wireframing, and brand theming. You will leverage your artistic expertise alongside generative art tools—such as ChatGPT Image 2, Gemini 3.1 Flash Image, Flux, Seedream, Kling, Grok, ComfyUI, and other models for static and 3D assets—together with the Adobe Suite to shape the future of visual storytelling at Moon Active.

Apply today if you're passionate about art, gaming, and innovation!

A CET ±3-hour zone is required.

Responsibilities

- Utilize generative art tools and Adobe Suite to design intuitive UI elements, comprehensive game layouts, and detailed wireframes that enhance the player experience.

- Develop and maintain consistent brand theming across various game assets, ensuring all generative output aligns with Moon Active’s signature style and quality standards.

- You will work closely with our super-talented art teams to integrate AI-generated art into our game assets, marketing materials, and other creative projects, working closely with various teams of artists and developers.

- You will be a champion for exploring and implementing new Image and Video Generation Models, constantly pushing the boundaries of what's possible in art creation.

- You will be responsible for curating, creating and updating our datasets based on original artwork in alignment with Moon Active signature art style.

Requirements

- Proven ability in creating wireframes, game layouts, and user interfaces with a focus on usability and aesthetic excellence.

- Expert knowledge of Photoshop and Illustrator, coupled with hands-on experience using tools like ComfyUI or ChatGPT to accelerate creative workflows.

- You possess a keen eye for detail and a deep appreciation for artistic principles like composition, color theory, and form. You understand what makes AI standout as “wrong” and what naturally fits existing artwork.

- You are able to effectively communicate your ideas and collaborate with artists, designers, and engineers.

- You are excited about the possibilities of AI in the art world and are driven to explore new creative frontiers.

- Extensive experience in Adobe Suite including Photoshop, Illustrator, After Effects and Premiere.

- Experience with scripting languages (e.g., Python) for automation and pipeline integration. Knowledge of cloud computing platforms (e.g., AWS, GCP) for model training and deployment is a plus.

Advantages

- Experience in gaming related concept art and illustration.

- Experience with 3D software (Cinema 4D, Maya, Blender, ZBrush) and integrating 3D assets into AI pipelines.

- Experience with 3D generative AI tools such as Meshy, Hunyuan, and Tripo.

- Experience in game development tools such as Unity or Unreal.
