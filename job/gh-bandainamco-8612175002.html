POSITION SUMMARY

Bandai Namco Entertainment America Inc. embraces the idea of “Fun For All Into the Future,” using technology and innovation to create memorable and genuine experiences around our brands. Creativity is at the heart of all we do, and our talented teams across the world work collaboratively to deliver continued success and create a fulfilling work environment.

The Public Relations Specialist will support PR initiatives in regions across the Western Hemisphere for Bandai Namco’s games. This role will help develop and execute actionable PR plans that maximize awareness and drive purchase interest in our products, working closely with cross-discipline publishing teams across our organization as well as outside partners and agencies. They will be responsible for being a media relations representative for their assigned products, helping devise and execute PR strategies, communicating regularly with press, and attending trade shows and events.

Join us at our new campus in Irvine, California where a hybrid work schedule will be observed.

ESSENTIAL DUTIES AND RESPONSIBILITIES

Assist in executing PR plans and activations that support Bandai Namco’s products, working on video games from the company’s diverse portfolio of owned IP, globally beloved anime franchises, and blockbuster titles from partner developers.

Support the writing and editing of press materials, including product messaging, press releases, media alerts, and pitches.

Support planning and coordination of physical PR activations including press tours, bespoke press events, and PR presence at major trade shows.

Interact and develop relationships with press to foster positive engagement with Bandai Namco’s products.

Work closely with internal and agency PR teams as well as cross-functional teams in publishing to help coordinate PR needs (interviews, assets, event preparation).

Aid in monitoring and reporting on PR campaign KPIs and develop proficiency with PR tools to produce regular performance reports as required by the team and company stakeholders.

Be an effective ambassador for Bandai Namco Entertainment in and outside of the company, which includes effectively speaking and presenting to large groups.

Perform other duties as assigned.

MINIMUM QUALIFICATIONS

Bachelor s degree in Communications or related field.

1+ years of relevant PR or marketing experience.

Demonstrates initiative, attention to detail, and consistent follow-through.

Familiarity with game, tech, and entertainment press.

Excellent written and verbal communication skills, including comfort with public speaking and presenting.

Strong knowledge and passion for video games and anime culture and landscape.

Work in a physically demanding environment that includes domestic and global travel for 25% or more of the year to major shows and PR events, including occasional weekend trade shows.

About Bandai Namco Entertainment America Inc.

Bandai Namco Entertainment America Inc., part of the Bandai Namco Group, is a leading global publisher and developer of interactive entertainment for major video game consoles, PC, online, and mobile platforms. The company is known for creating many of the industry’s beloved classic franchises such as PAC-MAN®, GALAGA®, TEKKEN®, SOULCALIBUR®, and ACE COMBAT®, and publishing the critically acclaimed DARK SOULS™ series and the blockbuster title ELDEN RING™. Bandai Namco Entertainment America Inc. is also the premier publisher in the Western hemisphere for anime-based video games including GUNDAM™, NARUTO SHIPPUDEN™, DRAGON BALL™, and ONE PIECE®. Bandai Namco Entertainment America Inc. is headquartered in Irvine, California. More information about the company and its products can be found at http://www.bandainamcoent.com .
