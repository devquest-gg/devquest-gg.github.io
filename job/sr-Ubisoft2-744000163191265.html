As an HR Business Partner at Ubisoft, you will be a trusted advisor to business leaders, helping them build the capabilities, behaviours, structures and processes their teams need. You will link your business line with HR, lead projects from the annual HR operational plan, and contribute to cross-functional initiatives.&#xa0; You will learn the processes and policies of all our departments (Production, Testing, ITD & UDO, Support), so you can cover HR Business Partner responsibilities in any of them when needed.&#xa0; What you will do&#xa0; Performance management: run the annual performance cycle, facilitate calibration sessions, coach and train managers on assessment skills, and build improvement plans where needed.&#xa0; Talent and succession: identify key people with leaders, plan successors for key positions, advise on transfers and promotions, and monitor engagement.&#xa0; Development: work with Learning & Development to meet learning needs, and mentor and coach managers on leadership, difficult conversations and feedback.&#xa0; Compensation and benefits: partner with the C&B team to deliver annual processes.&#xa0; Engagement surveys: help leaders turn survey results into clear action plans.&#xa0; Data and analytics: use HR data and insights to guide decisions.&#xa0; Talent acquisition: work with recruiters on internal and external hiring for key and management roles, and support onboarding.&#xa0; HR operations and employee relations: keep HR data and contracts accurate and implement employee relations processes with HR colleagues.&#xa0; What we are looking for&#xa0; Education: university degree in a relevant field.&#xa0; Experience: relevant experience&#xa0;in HR as a Generalist, Business Partner or Specialist; Learning & Development or Talent Management is a plus, as is work in fast-paced industries such as media, entertainment, technology or software.&#xa0; Language: advanced written and spoken English.&#xa0; Personal skills: strong leadership, mentoring and communication skills; rigorous, flexible, creative and analytical; calm under pressure; confident in coaching and public speaking.&#xa0; Competencies: proactive about development needs, familiar with HR tools, skilled at managing time, budget and resources, and keen to improve ways of working and learn new things.&#xa0; What do we offer benefits wise? &#xa0;
