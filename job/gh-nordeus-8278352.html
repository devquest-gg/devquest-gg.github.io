Senior User Acquisition Manager

Nordeus · Belgrade

Senior Marketing Posted 2026-10-20 Full time
Apply on Nordeus’s site →

Do you have a data-driven mindset and find the thought of applying analytics to creative campaigns for mobile games exhilarating?

Working on category-leading sports mobile games and growing them further excites you?

This just might be the role for you!

We are looking for a Senior User Acquisition Manager to join our UA team and become the driving force behind scaling the player base and maximizing revenue for one of our category-leading titles, Golf Rival. While your primary focus will be championing growth for Golf Rival, you will be part of a world class UA hub that oversees four major sports powerhouse titles within the Zynga and Take-Two Interactive portfolio: Top Eleven Football Manager , Golf Rival , CSR Racing 2 , and Top Goal: Football Champion .

THE DIFFERENCE YOU’LL MAKE:

In this role, you won’t be just executing campaigns - you will act as a true business owner driving the commercial success of a world-class mobile game. By mastering the intersection of data, media buying, and creative strategy, your decisions will directly fuel our global growth and efficiently scale multi-million-dollar budgets

Because we operate in small, agile teams, your impact will be immediate and visible across the studio. Whether you are unlocking a highly profitable new channel, pioneering a breakthrough creative concept, or proactively driving a new cross-functional initiative, your work will directly put our games into the hands of millions of new players and shape the future of Nordeus within the Zynga and Take-Two Interactive family

WHAT YOU’LL DO:

Work within a small, agile, and multifunctional marketing team dedicated to a specific game. We don t believe in red tape- you will enjoy a high level of autonomy, empowering you to make fast, strategic decisions that have a direct and measurable impact on the product’s overarching success

Plan, execute, and optimize multi-million-dollar UA campaigns across global markets and major ad networks (Meta, Google, AppLovin, Unity, TikTok, PPE networks, etc.)

Deep-dive into campaign data and analytics to uncover actionable insights. You will track, report, and forecast performance, running rigorous A/B tests to improve campaign efficiencies. Collaborate closely with our dedicated Analytics team to help them build advanced predictive models, further optimise reporting tools and constantly refine our LTV formulas

Champion the UA creative process. Partner closely with the Creative and Art teams to brainstorm, test, and iterate on high-performing ad creatives, understanding exactly what visual elements drive conversion and lower CPIs.

We value proactive thinkers and entrepreneurs at heart. Beyond your core UA duties, you will have the freedom to shape your own career trajectory and role. You are encouraged to proactively identify untapped opportunities, suggest innovative growth strategies, and drive additional cross-functional initiatives that help us smash our studio goals

WHO YOU’LL WORK WITH

Marketing Creative and Art team: Work hand-in-hand to brainstorm, test, and iterate on ad concepts, turning your performance insights into high-converting visual masterpieces

Marketing Analytics: Partner up to refine LTV formulas, elevate predictive modeling, and ensure every strategic media buying decision is backed by bulletproof data

Product Marketing team: Align your user acquisition tactics with overall brand positioning, app store optimization (ASO), and broader go-to-market campaigns to ensure a seamless player journey from ad view to install

Product team: Bridge the gap between marketing and development by sharing market trends and acquisition insights, helping to inform live ops and in-game features that drive long-term retention and monetization

WE ARE A MATCH, IF YOU:

Have 3 to 5+ years of hands-on experience in User Acquisition / Performance Marketing (Experience in Mobile Gaming is highly preferred, but strong backgrounds in Mobile Apps or Web Performance Marketing are also welcome)

Have a proven track record of managing and scaling large advertising budgets (comfortably handling $500k - $1M+ per month)

Have exceptional analytical skills with the ability to manipulate and interpret complex data sets, work with MMPs (e.g., AppsFlyer, Adjust), and translate numbers into clear marketing strategies

Have a deep understanding of what makes a winning ad creative and how to continuously iterate on concepts

Have a strong strategic and commercial mindset. You don’t just look at the CPIs; you look at the ROI, LTV, and overall business growth.

Have an ability to operate effectively in a team-oriented and collaborative environment

Are curious and committed to fast and lifelong learning

Are a self-starter who thrives in a fast-paced, high-impact environment

BONUS POINTS:

A Player at Heart: A genuine passion for mobile gaming, the broader tech landscape, or sports (specifically football, golf, or racing). You understand the player mindset because you are a player

Ad-Tech Savvy: A deep, structural understanding of the mobile marketing ecosystem. You are well-versed in marketing measurement tools, mobile attribution (MMPs like AppsFlyer or Adjust), programmatic advertising, and predictive modeling

Technical Chops: Hands-on experience with SQL and data visualization tools (like Tableau) to pull your own data and build custom dashboards without waiting on others

AI-Empowered: You proactively leverage AI tools to streamline your workflows, speed up creative ideation, and multiply your daily efficiency

WHY YOU’LL LOVE WORKING HERE:

The team behind the game: transparency and trust from day one, paired with a strong sense of teamwork- that s the essence of who we are. It s not just what you do - it’s how you do it and who you do it with. With 280+ teammates from around the world, we’re on a mission to bring the joy of winning to millions.

Perks? Make your mark on games played by millions: with over 300M registered users, Top Eleven is the world’s most successful football management game. Golf Rival, with more than 60M registered users, is the #2 mobile golf game, aiming to claim the top spot. With one more game in the works, we are continuing our efforts to create powerful mobile sports gaming experiences built to last.

Meaningful career experience: work with experienced game makers and mentors who will support you from day one, helping you map a career path that s true to you.
