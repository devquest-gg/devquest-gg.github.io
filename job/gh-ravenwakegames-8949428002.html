Position Title: Senior Publishing Producer

Studio/Department: Marketing/Community

Employee Type: Full Time, Regular

Location : Remote ( within BC, Canada)

CAD Salary Range: $110,000 - $150,000 CAD, pay may vary on location

Ravenwake Games is the newest studio within Enad Global 7, a global leader in video game development and publishing. Backed by iconic franchises such as EverQuest®, PlanetSide®, DC Universe™ Online, Dungeons Dragons Online®, The Lord of the Rings Online™, Magic: The Gathering Online, and MechWarrior®, we draw on a legacy of genre‑defining experiences. We’re committed to delivering entertainment that inspires, engages, and unites players around the world. At Ravenwake Games, our mission is to craft immersive worlds that bring players together, build vibrant communities, and deliver unforgettable gameplay at every turn.

Description

· Serve as the central project manager for Ravenwake s Publishing organization, orchestrating complex cross-functional initiatives.

· Manage publishing-facing projects spanning marketing, creative services, community management, PR, influencer partnerships, and events.

· Drive exceptional organizational and stakeholder management in a fast-paced, multi-game franchise environment.

· Facilitate seamless communication between Publishing teams and internal development studios.

· Own project lifecycles from conception through execution, ensuring alignment with game launch schedules and live service roadmaps.

This position will be responsible for:

· Owning the complete project lifecycle for Publishing initiatives, from campaign conception through launch and evaluation.

· Managing project schedules, task prioritization, risk identification, and resource allocation across all Publishing stakeholders.

· Facilitating strategic communication between Publishing teams and development studios to ensure alignment on release schedules, live operations, and publishing-facing initiatives.

· Coordinating cross-functional projects spanning Marketing, Creative Services, Community Management, PR, and Events.

· Establishing and maintaining efficient workflows through documentation, status reporting, and facilitation of project meetings.

· Identifying, tracking, and mitigating project risks through proactive problem-solving and contingency planning.

· Collaborating with external partners including influencers, agencies, and event organizers to execute campaigns.

· Recommending and implementing process improvements to enhance team efficiency and on-time project delivery.

Other Criteria

Requirements/Preferences

· 6+ years of project management experience in game publishing, marketing operations, or publishing-related fields.

· Proven experience managing cross-functional teams and multiple stakeholders in fast-paced, deadline-driven environments.

· Expert-level organizational and communication skills with demonstrated ability to facilitate stakeholder alignment.

· Experience with project management tools and methodologies (JIRA, Confluence, Asana, Agile, etc.).

· Knowledge of game publishing processes, live operations, and marketing execution a plus.

· Passion for gaming and familiarity with MMO and online multiplayer titles.
