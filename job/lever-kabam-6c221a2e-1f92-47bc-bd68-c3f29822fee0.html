At Kabam, we’re constantly raising the bar of excellence in free-to-play gaming. We hire for passion and diversity so that we can triumph in our collective skill. Our teams are made up of a fusion of personalities and interests bound together by a collaborative nature, fun-loving culture, and a drive to do what it takes to make great games. And this is where you come in…

A Senior Project Manager works within the Marketing department and manages in-house creative teams, external agencies, and freelancers across advertising projects for current and upcoming game releases. Working in close partnership with senior leads across the team's three creative functions - Video, Graphic, and Illustration - this role maintains a clear view of team-wide project allocation, surfaces and resolves scheduling conflicts, and ensures that projects involving multiple functions are executed in a cohesive, integrated way.

This role acts as the connective tissue between Creative Services, Marketing, and Studio. It involves close collaboration with internal Marketing teams,  including Product Marketing, Community Team, and Growth Marketing, to align on briefs, timelines, and deliverables, as well as proactive coordination with Studios to secure the game assets and resources needed for high-quality creative production.

The ideal candidate brings a holistic view of the creative production ecosystem, a passion for working with high-performing creative professionals, communication skills, and a process-improvement mindset to keep a multi-functional team operating at its best. Travel may be required.

This role is a full-time, on-site position based 5 days a week at our Downtown Vancouver studio.

About Kabam

Kabam is a world leader in developing entertaining, immersive, and highly social multiplayer games, bringing high-quality graphics, next-generation technology, and revolutionary gameplay to players around the world.

Kabam’s games, Marvel Contest of Champions, Shop Titans, and more have generated hundreds of millions of downloads and have received multiple awards including Apple’s Editor’s Choice and Google Play’s Best Game of the Year.

Founded in 2006, Kabam has studios and offices in Vancouver & Montreal in Canada, and Los Angeles & San Francisco in the United States. Kabam is a wholly-owned subsidiary of Netmarble Games.
