Join us and be part of the play! Position Overview

We are seeking a Senior Product Manager with experience in Ad monetization or Ad Technology systems to shape, execute distribute our Ad Technology SDK across 50+ Games at Zynga! This role specializes in key aspects of our product lifecycle, building customized ad formats for our games and delivering experiments, readouts and optimizations.

This role requires a blend of product management and ad monetization experience. Ideal candidates should have experience working on monetization/payments systems and should be deeply familiar with how games balance engagement with IAA/IAP monetization.

What You ll Do

Lead experiments for our Advertising platform from experiment design through analysis and rollout -> fine-tuning supply and demand side features that impact how our players see ads, and how we price them

Support the distribution and updates of our SDK releases across 50+ games through different studio build paths

Execute against our product strategy customizing game-side logic on ad loading, surfacing and trigger points

Identify problems or areas of optimization with our core ad serving logic, and design new solutions that challenge our product s status quo

Maintain a pulse on the rapidly evolving Ad Tech landscape, including new ad formats, platform requirements, and privacy changes

Help us develop 0-1 systems, including automating our SDK upgrades through build pipelines

Support our live operations business lines on an ongoing basis, providing technical insight and expertise into how to fix problems and triage issues

Conduct market research, competitive analysis, and user interviews to identify new product opportunities and improvements.

What You Bring

Experience: 5+ years of product management experience, with a minimum of 3 years focused on Ad Technology, monetization, or similar domains in the mobile or gaming space.

Ad Tech Knowledge: Strong understanding of the mobile advertising ecosystem, including ad mediation, ad networks, SDKs, DSPs, SSPs, ad exchanges, and programmatic advertising.

Analytical Skills: Excellent analytical and quantitative skills; ability to use data and metrics to back up assumptions and drive product decisions. Required experience writing SQL queries to extract data and articulate your analysis

Communication: Exceptional written and verbal communication skills, with the ability to describe complex technical concepts to a non-technical audience.

Technical Aptitude: Ability to work closely with engineering teams and discuss technical trade-offs.

Experience working on ad technology systems either on the buy or sell side of the industry (DSP or SSP)

Experience working on games or in the free-to-play mobile market.

Prior experience with ad platform integrations and third-party vendor management

B.S. or M.S. in Computer Science, Engineering, Finance/Economics or a related field.
