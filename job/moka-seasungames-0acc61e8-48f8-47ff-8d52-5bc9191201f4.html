Responsibilities: 1. Participate in professional training courses and complete game programming exercises.

2. Work in teams to develop a game demo.

Requirements: 1. Strong logical thinking and clear communication skills.

2. Proficiency in C++ or C# (required for the written test; self-study before the test is allowed).

3. Experience with Unity is preferred (Unity will be required during the camp; self-study before the program is recommended for those unfamiliar).

Participants will receive professional training in game design, gain insights into game development, and be mentored by industry experts while experiencing the full creative process—from concept to prototype development.

Eligibility: 1. Graduation period: September 2025 – August 2026.

2. University students passionate about gaming and game development, regardless of school or major.
