Principal Cinematic Producer - Production

Firesprite · Remote · Remote

Director+ Production Remote Posted 2026-07-17 Full time

Firesprite is a visionary game development studio where immersive experiences meet unforgettable storytelling. We build showcase titles for PlayStation platforms as part of the PlayStation Studios family.

Our innovative games take players on thrilling journeys, with memorable characters and captivating narratives. Our commitment to the core values of “We are Adaptable, We are Creators and We act as One” is a key driving force behind our culture.

Join the team who have created awarded winning experiences, now creating Until Dawn 2! Role Overview:

We are looking for an experienced, driven planner with a background in Cinematic Production for games. You will be overseeing the cinematic pipeline from data ingest through delivery of cinematics.

You are an expert in bringing together multi-disciplinary teams to make memorable, emotional, and cinematic experiences, with a strong understanding of Narrative, Cinematic Production and Post-Production pipelines within video games.

As Principal Cinematic Producer you will work in partnership with the Production Director and other Lead Producers to develop and maintain the Cinematic workflow from concept to final delivery. Working collaboratively cross-discipline with designers, programmers, and audio teams to blur the lines between cinematics and gameplay to develop high-quality narrative gameplay experiences.

This role is suitable for remote workers with occasional travel to the UK office and to UK based shoots when possible.

What you’ll be doing:

Responsible for the project management of the Cinematic pillar, overseeing multi-discipline Feature Teams.

Responsible for overall development strategy and roadmap of the Cinematic Pillar, ensuring cohesion with wider project goals.

Accountable for the discipline, consistency, efficiency and quality of output of the Cinematic Teams, including all the work produced for key milestones, alongside close collaboration with the Cinematic and Cinematography directors.

Establishes and maintains best practices for cinematic production, leading the charge on refining and maintaining cinematic backlogs and roadmaps.

Works with the Principal Producer responsible for shoots to ensure crystal clear communication of shoot deliverables, priorities, last minute changes and updates both to, and from, the shoot team.

Responsible for maintaining scope/cap plans, driving realistic targets for the teams and working with the Production Director to maintain appropriate outsourcing levels based on accurate, well-maintained data.

Accountable for the Risk Register for all Features Teams they oversee, effectively reports on risk, and works collaboratively with the Production Director and other Lead Producers to mitigate risk where possible.

What we’re looking for:

Minimum of two years of games industry experience in an internal development environment in a Cinematic Production leadership role.

At least one shipped PC and/or console title in a Senior Producer or Lead Producer role.

Has an excellent understanding of Cinematic pipelines and is able to create and maintain data driven and data informed timelines for cinematics production.

Ability to troubleshoot production challenges and devise creative solutions on the fly.

Flexibility to adjust plans and processes in response to new challenges, feedback or changes in scope.

Competence in using industry-standard software (e.g., JIRA).

Confident uniting Leads and Directors across departments in the pursuit of pragmatic but quality minded solutions.

Experience planning and working on motion capture shoots.

Enthusiasm for the Medium: A genuine interest in video games and a commitment to creating engaging, immersive experiences for players.

Desirable Skills:

Certified Scrum Master (CSM).

Experience on multiple large scale, 150+ person AAA games through all phases of production from concept to release.
