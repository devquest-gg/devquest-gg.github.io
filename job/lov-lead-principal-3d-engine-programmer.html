Lead / Principal 3D Engine Programmer

Black Banner Studios · České Budějovice · Remote

Lead Engineering Remote Posted 2026-11-20 Full time
Apply on Black Banner Studios’s site →

Legacy of Valor combines large-scale, real-time battles with third-person melee combat and tactical army control in Unreal Engine 5. We are looking for an experienced 3D Engine Programmer to develop and optimize our rendering and animation technology. Our proprietary Large Army system already supports thousands of simultaneously animated soldiers, and we are now working to further improve its visual quality, scalability, and performance. You will work closely with our Lead Programmer, technical animators, artists, and gameplay engineers to deliver high-quality visuals and smooth performance, from close-up character interactions to massive battlefield scenes. If you believe you'd be a great fit for this role, even if you don't meet every requirement listed below, we'd still love to hear from you. Please don't hesitate to apply! Take technical ownership of rendering, animation, and engine systems from design through release. Research, prototype, and implement new graphics and animation techniques. Develop and optimize split-body animation and locomotion systems, including upper / lower-body blending, pelvis handling, and procedural animation corrections. Improve rendering and animation systems for large numbers of characters, including skeletal animation, GPU-driven animation, and LOD systems. Write robust, high-performance, maintainable C++ code and shaders. Analyze and optimize CPU and GPU performance, memory usage, and rendering stutter. Investigate complex technical problems, conduct thorough code reviews, and maintain the technical quality of the codebase. Work closely with gameplay programmers, technical animators, technical artists, and other content creators. At least two shipped games in which you took technical ownership of major rendering or engine systems from design through release. Excellent C++ skills, including the ability to understand and review complex engine code. Deep experience working at engine source level with Unreal Engine 4/5 or a comparable C++-based game engine. Practical knowledge of modern rendering pipelines, GPU architecture, and shader programming. Strong profiling, debugging, and optimization skills. Experience working with or extending real-time character animation systems. Openness to AI-assisted development workflows, with a strong emphasis on code quality, performance, and maintainability. Passion for video games, particularly medieval RPGs, open-world games, and strategy titles. Experience rendering large crowds, instancing, and GPU-driven animation, including Vertex Animation Textures (VAT). Experience developing split-body animation and locomotion systems, including upper/lower-body blending, pelvis control, foot placement, IK, and procedural corrections. Experience with DirectX 12, HLSL, and compute shaders. Experience with visibility systems, levels of detail, and world and resource streaming. Experience using GPU profiling and graphics debugging tools. Experience developing graphics and animation technology for large-scale strategy, action, or open-world games. Experience working on AAA projects. Location: Remote / International, with the option to work in the Czech Republic. Engagement: Full-time, long-term collaboration. Contract: Contractor / B2B worldwide, or direct employment for candidates residing in or willing to relocate to the Czech Republic. Start Date: As soon as mutually agreed. Language: Professional working proficiency in English. Compensation: Competitive, based on experience and expertise.
