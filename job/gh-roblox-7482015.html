Who We Are:

We are seeking an Engineering Manager to lead our Engine UI team. Engine UI builds the UI capability of the Roblox engine: the layout, text, styling, and 2D rendering systems behind the UI in millions of Roblox experiences, and behind the Roblox App and Roblox Studio themselves. Whether creators target mobile, desktop, console, or VR, your team will make it possible to author UI once and have it work well on every device, with the quality and performance that meaningful interaction needs.

You Will:

Lead and grow a team of C++ engineers building the engine’s core UI systems: layout, text and typography, styling, and 2D rendering and effects

Own the Engine UI roadmap and its execution, balancing new creator-facing capabilities with performance, stability, and timely fixes for creator-reported bugs

Guide the design and review of the public APIs that expose UI capability to Luau, our fast, Lua-derived scripting language that powers user-generated creation

Set a performance-first bar so UI stays fast on everything from low-end phones to 8K monitors, using profiling data to drive decisions

Partner with the Roblox App, Studio, Rendering, and Localization teams, as well as product and design, all of whom build on the same UI engine

Work directly with our creator community to understand their needs and help them achieve their creative vision

Recruit, mentor, and develop engineers from early career to senior, and build a culture of ownership and high engineering standards

Have fun learning what makes Roblox an amazing platform and place to work, while contributing to industry-leading technologies

You have:

8+ years of professional software development experience, primarily in C++, including 5+ years managing engineering teams

Strong systems-level C++ judgment: 3D math, templates, and memory management are tools for writing high-performance code, and you use profilers and data to find bottlenecks and measure impact

A track record of hiring and growing engineers, and of leading a team through multi-quarter technical projects

Experience building or leading work on UI frameworks, browser/layout engines, or game engines, for example WebKit, Chromium/Blink, Gecko, Unreal, Unity, UIKit, the Android UI framework, Qt, or Yoga

Shipped APIs and tools that developers of varying disciplines, such as engineers, game designers, and UI designers, find intuitive

Clear communication of technical trade-offs and project status to engineers, partner teams, and leadership

Experience shipping on constrained platforms such as mobile devices or game consoles (preferred)High standards for design rigor, edge-case handling, and automated testingExperience with Lua/Luau, text shaping and localization (such as HarfBuzz, FreeType, or right-to-left text), or graphics APIs such as Vulkan, Metal, or DirectX is a plus
