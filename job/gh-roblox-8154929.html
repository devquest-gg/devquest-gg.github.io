Every day, tens of millions of people come to Roblox to explore, create, play, learn, and connect with friends in 3D immersive digital experiences– all created by our global community of developers and creators.

At Roblox, we’re building the tools and platform that empower our community to bring any experience that they can imagine to life. Our vision is to reimagine the way people come together, from anywhere in the world, and on any device.

We’re on a mission to connect a billion people with optimism and civility, and looking for amazing talent to help us get there.

A career at Roblox means you’ll be working to shape the future of human interaction, solving unique technical challenges at scale, and helping to create safer, more civil shared experiences for everyone.

We are seeking an experienced Senior Software Engineer with a deep background in 3D computer graphics and engine development to join the ML Data Pod within the Foundation AI team. In this role, you will build the foundational infrastructure that powers our next-generation AI capabilities.

As the AI landscape rapidly shifts toward spatial and 3D generation, high-quality data has become the ultimate bottleneck. You will be instrumental in solving this challenge by unlocking the Roblox engine as a massive, scalable platform for synthetic data generation.

This is a unique opportunity to bridge the gap between cutting-edge AI research and low-level engine architecture. You will be navigating unmapped territory—shaping how we represent and structure complex 3D data (such as meshes, materials, lighting, and scene graphs) to effectively train state-of-the-art 3DGen models. Ultimately, the synthetic pipelines you architect will directly fuel the frontier AI models that will define the future of immersive 3D content creation for millions of creators worldwide.

Responsibilities

Synthetic Data Generation: Spearhead initiatives to leverage the Roblox engine to procedurally generate the massive, high-fidelity 3D datasets required for training frontier 3DGen models.

Cross-Functional Collaboration: Partner closely with AI research scientists to translate complex model training requirements into actionable engine features, while collaborating with core engine teams to implement them safely and efficiently.

Pipeline Architecture: Design, build, and optimize scalable graphics and engine-level systems capable of generating diverse spatial, geometric, and visual data at scale.

Technical Integration: Drive the technical direction for embedding synthetic data generation workflows into the existing engine architecture without compromising core performance.

Minimum Qualifications

5+ years of experience building and shipping complex, production-grade software systems

Deep expertise in 3D computer graphics, rendering pipelines, and game engine architecture.

Strong proficiency in systems programming (e.g., C++) with a proven track record of writing highly optimized, scalable code.

Experience extracting, modifying, or manipulating programmatic data from within a complex engine environment.

Strong communication skills with a demonstrated ability to bridge the gap between machine learning researchers and low-level systems engineers.

Bonus: Familiarity with machine learning data pipelines, automated synthetic data generation, or 3D generative AI architectures.

Preferred Qualifications

Background in geometry, linear algebra, or related mathematical domains

Experience with graphics APIs or frameworks a plus but not required
