UX Designer

Nordeus · Belgrade

Mid Design Posted 2026-05-06
Apply on Nordeus’s site →

WHY WE NEED YOU:

We are working on a genre-defining and charts-ruling hit mobile game and are looking for a seasoned and experienced games UX designer to help us elevate the experience to the next level for our players! Is getting in the weeds, fixing what’s broken and coming up with innovative solutions your thing? Let’s talk!

WHAT YOU’LL DO:

Concept, design, wireframe, prototype edit and optimize user flows for our game

Work closely with all disciplines within your team, including Art, Engineers, Design, User Research and Analytics to create designs that solve problems

Incorporate iterative user and team feedback into the development process

Create wireframes and prototypes for testing and communicating your interactive design solutions to key stakeholders and teammates

Provide an amazing user experience to our players by determining the information architecture and visual guidelines

Define and implement standards that will help create a consistent, quality experience for players

Assisting the Marketing team with user testing and implementing those results in the iterative process

Work with the User Research team to learn about player needs and validate design decisions

Follow latest design trends, AI based UX/UR tools and experiments aimed at discovering innovative solutions

WHAT WE’RE LOOKING FOR:

3+ years of relevant experience

Previous experience in designing user experience for games, ideally for mobile

Educational background in Web Design, Interaction Design, UI/UX, Human Computer Interaction, Psychology, or other related fields

Up­-to-­date knowledge of the latest trends in UX design and major platform interaction guidelines

Capability to use a combination of research and personal knowledge of gaming culture to empathize with players, and develop strategies and best practices that are tailored to their needs

Capability to animate prototypes that demonstrate UI interactions

Understanding cross ­platform implementation and technical limitations

Desire to work in cross­ functional teams

A self starter with a “get things done” attitude that is characterized by efficient and speedy work practices

Love for video games and a passion for game development

WE CAN’T WAIT TO MEET YOU SO DON’T FORGET TO:

Share your latest portfolio, including at least one project that shows your full UX process- from initial brief to final outcome. Tip: Prioritize showcasing your UX thinking over visual design.

What we re assessing:

Your wireframing process

Iterations and how your ideas evolved

Research, analysis, and any user testing insights that informed your decisions

The final UX outcome (prototypes are a plus)

WHY YOU’LL LOVE WORKING HERE:

The competitive compensation package, perks and benefits: unlimited vacation days, flexible working hours, hybrid ways of working, premium private medical insurance for you and your family (additional coverage for your parents is available with a participation fee), offsites for the whole company, fully-paid maternity leave, employee stock purchase plan, access to L D platforms and opportunities, fitness reimbursement and many more.

State-of-the-art workspace: Nordeus provided breakfast, lunch, snacks, and beverages, a 24/7 available fully-equipped gym, organised sports activities (yoga, Brazilian jiu-jitsu, basketball, football), an ergonomic workstation, top-notch tech equipment (including laptop, mobile phone and bill coverage, and other tech), a kids’ playroom, a music corner, golf simulator, board and video games + latest consoles. We make sure your life at Nordeus is hassle-free.

High level of creative freedom: trust, respect, and teamwork are the essence of who we are. We inspire and support each other to bring the joy of winning to the lives of millions.

Playing your part in the future of our games: with over 260M registered users, Top Eleven is the world’s most successful football management game. Golf Rival, with more than 60M registered users, is the #2 mobile golf game aiming to claim the top spot. With one more game in the works, we are continuing our efforts to create powerful gaming experiences built to last.

Working with experienced game makers: you will be a part of a multicultural team of over 200 employees from different countries.

Making an impact: we are putting Serbia on the global gaming map and working closely with the local community to develop the gaming ecosystem.

GOOD TO KNOW:

This is a hybrid, on-site position based in Belgrade.

If you are not a Serbian citizen, we offer a relocation package.
