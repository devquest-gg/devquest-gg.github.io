Senior Concept Artist

Black Banner Studios · České Budějovice · Remote

Senior Art Remote Posted 2026-11-20 Full time
Apply on Black Banner Studios’s site →

We're looking for an experienced Senior Concept Artist with an exceptional artistic eye, strong design skills, and a passion for creating beautiful, believable characters, equipment, and environments inspired by medieval Europe. Legacy of Valor is set in a grounded fantasy world inspired by 14th-century Europe. We're looking for someone who can help define its visual identity through outstanding character, armour, clothing, weapon, prop, and environment concepts, combining historical authenticity with artistic creativity. A major focus of this role will be creating beautiful, detailed, and production-ready character concepts that our Character Artists can confidently translate into high-quality 3D models. Beyond character design, you'll also contribute to props, environments, and atmospheric key shots that help establish the look and feel of the game world. You'll work closely with our Game Director, Art Director, Character Artists, Environment Artists, and other team members, taking ownership of concept designs from initial exploration through to final production-ready artwork. If you believe you'd be a great fit for this role, even if you don't meet every requirement listed below, we'd still love to hear from you. Please don't hesitate to apply! Create exceptional, visually distinctive, and believable concept art for characters, armour, clothing, weapons, equipment, props, and environments. Design historically grounded medieval characters, with particular attention to 14th-century European armour, clothing, materials, craftsmanship, and social distinctions. Produce detailed, production-ready character concepts that provide our 3D artists with clear visual guidance, including multiple views, material references, construction details, and other necessary information. Develop original and visually compelling designs that balance historical authenticity, practical functionality, artistic quality, and the distinctive visual identity of Legacy of Valor. Create concept art for medieval weapons, shields, equipment, architectural elements, furniture, and other environmental props. Develop environment concepts and atmospheric key shots depicting landscapes, settlements, castles, interiors, battlefields, and other important locations. Explore different visual directions through sketches, thumbnails, colour studies, and design iterations before producing final concepts. Maintain consistency in design language, materials, proportions, and visual quality across characters, equipment, and environments. Collaborate closely with Character Artists and Environment Artists throughout production, providing clarifications, additional views, and design adjustments when needed. Incorporate feedback from the Game Director, Art Director, and other team members while maintaining a high artistic standard. Contribute to the development and refinement of the game's visual identity, reference libraries, and concept art guidelines. Extensive professional experience creating high-quality concept art for video games or similarly demanding entertainment productions. Exceptional drawing, painting, design, and illustration skills, demonstrated through an outstanding portfolio. Strong experience designing believable, visually appealing characters, including realistic human anatomy, proportions, silhouettes, costumes, and equipment. Proven ability to create clear, detailed, production-ready character concepts that can be translated efficiently into high-quality 3D game assets. Strong knowledge of medieval European armour, clothing, weapons, materials, and craftsmanship, including the 14th century. Excellent understanding of how clothing, armour, and equipment are constructed, layered, worn, and used, with designs that remain believable and practical. Strong skills in prop and equipment design, including weapons, shields, armour components, accessories, and environmental objects. Experience creating visually compelling environment concepts and key shots, with strong composition, perspective, lighting, colour, and atmosphere. Excellent understanding of shape language, visual storytelling, material definition, and the relationship between artistic design and practical production requirements. Advanced proficiency with professional digital painting and concept art tools, such as Adobe Photoshop or equivalent software. Ability to explore different design directions efficiently and develop selected concepts into polished, production-ready artwork. Ability to work independently, take artistic ownership of complex designs, and collaborate effectively with multidisciplinary teams. Passion for video games, particularly medieval RPGs, open-world games, and strategy titles. Previous experience working on historically grounded medieval or realistic fantasy games. Advanced knowledge of European medieval history, architecture, heraldry, military equipment, and material culture. Experience designing characters and equipment for games requiring multiple armour variants, interchangeable clothing, or modular character customization. Experience creating high-quality key art or illustrations for marketing, promotional materials, or narrative presentation. Familiarity with 3D modeling and sculpting tools such as Blender, Maya, or ZBrush, including their use in concept art workflows. Experience with Unreal Engine 5 and an understanding of how concept designs translate into real-time game environments and characters. Experience working on AAA and / or large open-world game projects. Previous experience mentoring artists, providing artistic feedback, or helping establish concept art workflows and visual quality standards. Location: Remote / International, with the option to work in the Czech Republic. Engagement: Full-time, long-term collaboration. Contract: Contractor / B2B worldwide, or direct employment for candidates residing in or willing to relocate to the Czech Republic. Start Date: As soon as mutually agreed. Language: Professional working proficiency in English. Compensation: Competitive, based on experience and expertise.
