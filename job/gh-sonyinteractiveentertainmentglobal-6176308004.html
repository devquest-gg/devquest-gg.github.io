Why Sony Interactive Entertainment?

Sony Interactive Entertainment isn’t just the Best Place to Play — it’s also the Best Place to Work. Sony Interactive Entertainment (SIE) is the company behind the PlayStation brand. As a subsidiary of Sony Group Corporation, we’re part of a proud legacy of innovation and excellence. SIE is a dynamic technology company, delivering cutting-edge hardware and network services to more than 100 million people and an entertainment leader, home to some of the most beloved and recognizable intellectual properties (IP) in the world. Our role at SIE is to create and nurture the experiences under the PlayStation brand, a name synonymous with entertainment excellence and creativity.

We re looking for a solutions-oriented Executive Administrator to provide extensive executive administrative support to a Product Development Group leadership team and general support to the broader international team. This role requires independent judgment, discretion, and confidentiality while managing a wide range of administrative responsibilities.

Responsibilities

Manage and maintain complex executive calendars, including scheduling travel, conferences, appointments, and meetings

Prepare and submit expense reports for leadership and/or the department as assigned

Conduct research, handle information requests, prepare correspondence, welcome visitors, and arrange conference calls

Act as a liaison between different teams and international departments

Coordinate group functions, including reserving conference rooms and arranging catering for the leadership team

Plan and coordinate team/department events with internal and external stakeholders

Maintain and organize team operations and procedures

Take on special projects such as budget tracking, vendor/contract management, and org chart updates

Qualifications

Experience coordinating with executive-level stakeholders and cross-functional teams

Ability to work in a fast-paced, dynamic environment and adapt to changing priorities

Excellent communication skills, sound judgment, and strong attention to detail

Administrative or relevant work experience with a technical workforce; experience in the video games industry a plus

Proficiency with calendar, international travel, and expense management tools

Familiar world-wide business cultures and customers; fluency in Japanese a plus
