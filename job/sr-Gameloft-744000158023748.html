3D Animator (Middle/Senior)

Gameloft · Ho Chi Minh City

Senior Animation Posted 2026-10-27 Full time
Apply on Gameloft’s site →

WHAT YOU WILL BE WORKING ON What you will focus on Do you bring characters and game objects to life through animation? Join Gameloft Saigon's Animation team. You'll create believable, high-quality animations for ongoing projects. You'll work with Lead Animators, Lead Artists, designers and programmers to get the best result on every platform. Middle-level animators will grow their craft in a strong art team. Senior animators will also help shape our guidelines and coach junior talent.

What it means on a daily basis Create believable, high-quality key-framed animations for real-time and pre-rendered sequences.

Animate characters and game objects, in either a cartoony, stylized or realistic style.

Rig and skin your assets, including complex rigs.

Work with game designers and programmers to deliver the best animation within platform limits.

Work with the production team, Lead Animator and Lead Artists, and meet project milestones and deadlines.

Help establish art production guidelines, and spot and prevent future mistakes.

Coach and train junior animators.

What success will look like Pipeline: Read the production pipeline documents and follow them.

Quality: Deliver animation assets that meet the high quality standards set by the 3D Leads.

Feedback: Take feedback and adapt to technical requirements with a positive attitude. Express ideas and concerns calmly.

Communication: &#xa0;Give your Animator Lead&#xa0;and peers regular verbal and written updates on progress.

Collaboration: Work closely with artists and developers and share your knowledge to improve production.

Who you would be working with You will be reporting to the Animator Supervisor, Art Producer, and Art Lead/Director.

Closely collaborating with other disciplines in the Art team (e.g., 2D Art, 3D Art, Technical Art, VFX) and other teams in the project (e.g., Game Design, Programming).

Where can this role take you? Advance vertically to higher level in the&#xa0;Animation team such as Senior or Principal position.

Transition to other disciplines within the Art team: 3D Artist, Technical Artist, VFX Artist, and so on.

Recruitment journey Online screening call with recruiter Distance art test Offline interview with Animator Lead, Art Director, and Producer WHAT YOU NEED TO SUCCEED Games industry experience, with high proficiency in&#xa0;3ds Max or Maya.&#xa0; Advanced knowledge of the principles of animation, character motion and physics-based motion.

Excellent skinning and rigging skills with low to medium polygon counts, and a solid understanding of topology and production constraints.

Strong grasp of human anatomy, proportion, perspective, composition, color, shading and lighting.

WHO YOU ARE Good spoken and written English, plus clear communication, with the ability to take direction, follow a brief and solve artistic or technical issues.

Proactive, organized and creative, with a good eye for detail.

Ability to work well under pressure and in a team.

Passionate about games across platforms.

WHAT'S IN IT FOR YOU Benefits A hybrid working model with 2 days working from home&#xa0;every week.

A fun place to work in the heart of the city.
