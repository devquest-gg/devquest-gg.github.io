UA Specialist

Playwing · București

As part of the Live operation of a cross-platform strategy game, we are looking for a passionate and highly organized  UA Specialist  to join our team.

🎖  Bonus points Agency background or experience working with digital media agencies; Experience in SEO or ASO; Experience working on influencer campaigns or with influencers; Passionate about mobile games.

✍🏻  Your mission will be: Managing the full user acquisition process for a mobile game: Planning and execution all UA campaigns across multiple mobile channels and ad platforms; Produce comprehensive UA reporting, analyze ROI and identify new opportunities to acquire new users; Experiment and analyze campaigns for existing and upcoming games; Stay on top of industry trends and best practices across a variety of marketing channels such as Facebook, Snapchat or Tiktok; Researching new UA channels, creative resources, and tools to streamline marketing efforts.

✍🏻  What you will bring to the table: Strong experience in paid digital marketing and media buying (3+ years); Extensive knowledge of various mobile marketing channels such as Facebook, Google Ads; Hands-on experience creating and optimizing campaigns on multiple channels; A strong customer focus and a keen eye for creativity that drives results; Excellent analytical skills and outstanding attention to detail; The ability to communicate and express yourself in English; Be proactive: a desire to suggest new ideas and take the lead on projects, or explore opportunities.

Benefits: WFH hybrid program; 2 extra days off, plus a day off for the IT industry; Private medical insurance package (in relationship with Sanador or Regina Maria); Meal tickets.

Scope: Full-time Location: Bucharest
