Description

The Associate Designer is responsible for supporting the design and implementation of game systems, mechanics, and itemization, and working with the Lord of the Rings Online team in the Design team at Standing Stone Games reporting to the Producer, this position will be responsible for:

Work on compelling systems for a massively multiplayer game

Support the design team in improving current systems and delivering new ones

Collaborate with and learn from an internal team of world builders, artists, and designers

Learn proprietary tools quickly and work within a complex pipeline
