Join us and be part of the play! Position Overview

Zynga is looking for an ambitious analyst to join our world-class advertising team. The mission of this role will be to maximize the value of our advertising inventory through operational excellence and creative analytics. This role’s responsibilities will be to model the short and long term economic value of our ad products, identify new supply opportunities for our users, and scale our findings into actionable reports. This is a collaborative, highly visible position that will directly impact the daily revenues of the business. The ideal candidate will enjoy working with numbers and identifying patterns and trends, and will be eager to develop skills in econometrics, user segmentation, process management, and product business intelligence .

What You ll Do

Maximize the value of advertising inventory through manual and automated processes.

Design, execute, and analyze innovative pre/post and A/B tests to identify new opportunities.

Synthesize data into dashboards and reports using Excel, SQL, and Tableau.

Refine data processes, set up tools, and create automation to improve operational efficiency.

Execute ad hoc analysis to identify revenue optimization opportunities and improve business performance.

Assist with daily operations and analytics as needed.

Role may also require researching recent trends in the mobile advertising, mobile gaming, and advertising technology industries.

What You Bring

Bachelor’s degree in a quantitative field

At least 2 years of experience in a data-intensive role

Prior experience in mobile gaming or advertising is a big plus

Advanced skills in Microsoft Excel including pivot tables, lookups, conditional functions, charting, and keyboard shortcuts

Advanced skills in SQL including joins, creating tables, case statements, and subqueries

Prior experience using dashboarding software (especially Tableau) or Python/R is a big plus

Strong problem-solving skills, teamwork, and creativity

Strong work ethic to consistently meet deadlines in a fast-paced business

Intellectual curiosity to dig through large volumes of data in order to uncover the insights within it

Exceptional accuracy and attention to detail, time management skills, and openness toward growth

Excellent written, verbal, and visual communication skills

Enthusiasm about mobile gaming is a plus
