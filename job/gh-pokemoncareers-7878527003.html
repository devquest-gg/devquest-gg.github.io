Get to know The Pokémon Company International

The Pokémon Company International manages the Pokémon property outside of Asia and is responsible for brand management, licensing and marketing, the Pokémon Trading Card Game, the animated TV series, home entertainment, and the official Pokémon website. Pokémon was launched in Japan in 1996 and today is one of the most popular children’s entertainment properties in the world.

Get to know the role

Job Title: Digital Merchandiser

Job Summary: The Digital Merchandiser is responsible for translating category and business strategies into a compelling, customer-focused digital experience that drives discovery, engagement, and sales. They own the on-site merchandising and performance of their categories, using customer and business insights to optimize navigation, content, product placement, and storytelling. They also collaborate cross-functionally to ensure their categories are represented effectively across key site experiences and aligned to broader business priorities.

FLSA Classification (US Only): Exempt

People Manager: No

What you’ll do

Site Merchandising

Owns on‑site category presentation, ensuring products are accurately categorized, prioritized, and visually compelling.

Manages product setup and enrichment, including attributes, categorization, and launch readiness.

Maintains and updates site content across all placements to ensure relevance, accuracy, and alignment with brand standards.

Builds and executes seasonal, promotional, and brand storytelling moments across the site.

Contributes to digital merchandising strategy execution for Automated Retail

Product Presentation

Ensures product detail pages (PDPs) are complete and optimized, including imagery, copy, attributes, and SEO elements.

Partners with content production teams to communicate photography needs and content goals.

Requests, builds, and implements content that supports category strategy and drives conversion.

Analytics Performance

Analyzes category, product, and content performance using web analytics tools.

Uses data‑backed insights to drive decision‑making,

Reports weekly on KPIs, trends, and opportunities to cross‑functional partners.

Develops and evaluates testing ideas in partnership with digital optimization teams.

Cross‑Functional Collaboration

Works closely with Category Management, Planning, Marketing, and Content Production to align on category strategy and execution.

Communicates performance insights clearly to educate partners and influence strategy.

Collaborates with Digital Optimization and Tech teams to improve site experience and discoverability.

Leads seasonal and unique product campaigns on behalf of the Digital Merchandising team, working closely with partnering teams.

Foster team growth through active collaboration, mentorship, and knowledge‑sharing, maintaining a confident presence and perspective that elevates the team’s standards and motivates excellence.

Optimization Continuous Improvement

Stays current on SEO best practices and applies them to product and category pages.

Continuously deepen expertise in UX/UI, digital merchandising, and digital optimization by actively monitoring industry trends, tools, and innovations that enhance customer experience and business performance

Identifies opportunities to improve navigation, filters, and search relevance.

Continuously tests and iterates on content, layouts, and merchandising strategies to improve conversion and engagement.

Monitors ecommerce landscape to identify trends and opportunities.

Operational Excellence

Ensures accuracy and consistency of product data across systems and site placements.

Manages timelines and workflows for product launches, content updates, and seasonal refreshes.

Maintains strong attention to detail to ensure a seamless customer experience.

Serve as a role model for deadline discipline and customer‑first execution, maintaining exceptional attention to every detail of the site experience and consistently upholding rigorous quality assurance standards.

What you’ll bring

Five (5) to seven (7) years of relevant ecommerce experience or a demonstrated equivalent level of expertise.

Bachelor s degree in a related field or a demonstrated equivalent level of applicable experience.

Experience with content management.

Knowledge of creative software programs for digital content building.

Knowledge of HTML and web publishing.

Knowledge of SEO and web traffic metrics.

Excellent verbal and writing skills in English.

Good organizational and proactive time-management skills; comfortable working independently.

Passionate about educating the customer as well as the consumer.

Proficiency in Microsoft Office Suite.
