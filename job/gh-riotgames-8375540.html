Live Broadcast Producer II

Riot Games · Los Angeles

Mid Production $109K–$153K Posted 2026-11-18 Full time
Apply on Riot Games’s site →

As a Live Broadcast Producer II for the Americas Esports team at Riot Games, you will drive the weekly creative and editorial execution of LCS broadcasts, translating product and broadcast strategies into compelling live experiences for fans. You will lead weekly story development, creative ideation, interviews, features, graphics, and show preparation, ensuring each broadcast delivers engaging narratives that highlight the competition, teams, and players.

You will collaborate with Broadcast, Product, Publishing, Business Development, Talent Management, and other cross-functional partners to deliver cohesive, high-quality productions. From developing authentic sponsor integrations to identifying opportunities for on-air talent development, you will balance creative ambition with production realities. Using audience insights, competitive knowledge, and strong editorial judgment, you will identify the stories and Moments that matter most to fans and make informed decisions in a fast-paced live environment.

In addition to shaping LCS broadcasts, you will support and lead production efforts across Riot s global esports calendar, including but not limited to First Stand, MSI, Worlds, VCT Masters, and VCT Champions. You will work closely with global broadcast and talent partners across EMEA, Korea, China, Brazil, LATAM and others to align on creative direction, editorial priorities, and production execution, helping deliver cohesive, compelling broadcasts for fans around the world. This role requires flexibility to travel domestically and internationally, work nontraditional hours to accommodate global time zones, and support productions both on-site and remotely based on the needs of each event.

Beyond the live shows, you will help evolve how Riot connects with esports audiences through innovative production techniques, emerging broadcast technologies, and content experiences that extend across platforms. While your primary focus will be LCS, you may also support or lead productions across other Riot esports properties, including but not limited to VCT, TFT, and Riftbound.

Responsibilities:

Shape the overall narrative of the broadcast based on product and broadcast strategies by identifying key storylines, building compelling content, and creating moments that highlight the league and its pro players.

Execute live productions in coordination with technical and creative teams, making fast, informed decisions as the competition unfolds and ensuring the production enhances and adapts to the live action.

Oversee week-to-week narrative and storytelling priorities and creative development for live broadcasts, overseeing story meetings, read-throughs, rehearsals, and live shows.

Collaborate with broadcast leads to design runs-of-show and guide rehearsals for both in-studio, road show, and global events.

Design systems to improve Live Broadcast Production processes, ensuring sustainability and continuous performance improvement.

Exercise quality control across all aspects of production, including video, sound, graphics, observing, and music.

Innovate with tech and creative teams to ensure Riot Esports continues to push the boundaries of broadcast technology.

Integrate into global production teams to execute international broadcasts, adapting to different production structures, workflows, and show formats while aligning creative and editorial priorities across broadcast, talent, and technical partners.

Mentor junior producers and help ensure the craft of live broadcast production remains healthy, innovative, and high-performing.

Partner with Publishing, Features, Product, Business Development, League Operations, and other cross-functional teams to expand the reach and impact of our broadcasts, ensuring content is adapted and amplified across platforms.

Collaborate with Talent Management and broadcast leadership to ensure talent receives consistent feedback and continues to develop over time.

Track performance metrics and viewer behavior to inform strategic adjustments and improve audience engagement.

Required Qualifications:

4+ years of experience in broadcast production, ideally in esports, live entertainment, or sports

Deep understanding of the esports ecosystem, including League of Legends and VALORANT

Ability to travel domestically and internationally for extended periods, including weekends, and work nontraditional hours to support live broadcasts across global time zones.

Adaptability and composure in fast paced and diverse environments

Creative and technical expertise to push the limits of live storytelling and broadcast technology

Experience working on large-scale live events

Strong collaboration skills with cross-functional teams

Familiarity with Riot’s esports ecosystem and values

For this role, you ll find success through craft expertise, a collaborative spirit, and decision-making that prioritizes the delight of players. We will be looking at your past studies, experience, and your personal relationship with games. If you embody player empathy and care about players experiences, this could be your role!
