Description

Forge alternate worlds that deepen players lives! We re a game development studio based in Los Angeles, dedicated to the idea that online games can deliver deeper, more meaningful experiences. As a Mid-Level Character Artist you will provide a source of expression and representation for our players by creating cosmetic items including outfits, pets and tool skins. You’ll be expected to have a keen sense of aesthetic and design. You should be self driven and open to providing feedback just as much as receiving it. Cosmetics will need to fit within specific metrics whilst still being visually interesting and varied.

Responsibilities

Interpret and push concept designs to the next level while accounting for technical and design constraints such as poly count, texture memory limits, topology and metrics.

Participate in group asset reviews to give and receive peer feedback on all aspects of the character art process. Be willing to learn and teach.

Collaborate with other departments such as Concept, Animation Tech

Art to ensure art meets specifications.

Be able to make design decisions when interpreting concepts should things need to change for our outfit metrics.

Expect to be flexible. Outside of asset creation you should be willing to create renders for the store page as well as implement your assets into the game

Work with Production to provide estimates, review schedules, anddeliver assets on time.

Clearly communicate regular updates and proactively voice concerns or struggles to appropriate team members and manager(s).

Attend all planning and team meetings and produce time estimates for project planning and communicate with the team when you are unable.

Utilize artistic techniques and processes to define and achieve the product’s visual style and benchmarks

Be willing to suggest and execute on ways to improve quality whilst maintaining the aesthetic of Palia.

Requirements

3+ Years Experience on video game asset production teams.

Shipped AAA game(s).

Strong foundational skills with an emphasis on proportion, anatomy, composition, color theory, and design.

Advanced understanding of industry standard software such as Maya,

Photoshop, ZBrush, Substance Painter and Unreal Engine 5

Familiarity with all stages of the character creation pipeline including sculpting, lowpoly modeling, UV mapping, texture painting, PBR material setup, and asset implementation.

Desire to strive for greatness and improvement.

Passionate in pursuing personal creative endeavors.

Possess strong time management and prioritization skills.

Good communication, organizational, and interpersonal skills.
