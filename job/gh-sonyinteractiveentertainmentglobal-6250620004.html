Why Sony Interactive Entertainment?

Sony Interactive Entertainment isn’t just the Best Place to Play — it’s also the Best Place to Work. Sony Interactive Entertainment (SIE) is the company behind the PlayStation brand. As a subsidiary of Sony Group Corporation, we’re part of a proud legacy of innovation and excellence. SIE is a dynamic technology company, delivering cutting-edge hardware and network services to more than 100 million people and an entertainment leader, home to some of the most beloved and recognizable intellectual properties (IP) in the world. Our role at SIE is to create and nurture the experiences under the PlayStation brand, a name synonymous with entertainment excellence and creativity.

The Senior Executive, Supply Chain Management will support end-to-end supply chain and operational activities across Southeast Asia markets, ensuring effective order fulfilment, inventory management, logistics coordination, warehouse operations, and operational compliance. The role requires close collaboration with internal stakeholders, distributors, logistics service providers, and regional teams to ensure smooth execution of supply chain processes and continuous operational improvement.

Key Responsibilities:

Supply Chain Operations

Manage end-to-end order fulfilment activities including purchase orders, sales orders, delivery coordination, invoicing, and shipment execution.

Coordinate with distributors, commercial teams, and third-party logistics (3PL) partners to ensure timely and accurate order processing.

Monitor operational performance and resolve supply chain issues related to logistics, inventory discrepancies, damaged stock, and system transactions.

Support product launches, replenishment planning, and order allocation activities across assigned markets.

Take ownership of assigned markets and drive resolution of operational issues with minimal supervision.

Provide guidance and knowledge sharing to junior team members where required.

Inventory Warehouse Management

Perform inventory monitoring and reconciliation between SAP and warehouse records.

Support periodic stock counts and inventory audits.

Coordinate warehouse activities including rework planning, inspection requirements, and inventory movement tracking.

Ensure inventory accuracy and provide recommendations to improve stock management processes.

Logistics Distribution

Work closely with freight forwarders, warehouses, distributors, and regional stakeholders on inbound and outbound logistics activities.

Coordinate import/export shipments and ensure compliance with documentation requirements.

Manage shipping arrangements and support logistics-related operational projects.

Monitor logistics service performance and address operational issues promptly.

Invoice Vendor Management

Review and verify supplier invoices for accuracy and policy compliance.

Support invoice processing and payment submissions through corporate systems.

Coordinate with vendors and internal finance teams to resolve billing discrepancies.

Maintain proper documentation and records related to operational expenditures and vendor transactions.

Process Improvement Projects

Support implementation of new operational initiatives, systems, and process improvements.

Participate in regional projects involving procurement, logistics, warehouse operations, and system enhancements.

Identify opportunities to improve efficiency, accuracy, and service quality.

Prepare reports and analysis to support business decision-making.

Support and participate in regional supply chain improvement initiatives and system enhancement projects.

Compliance Reporting

Ensure compliance with company policies and regulatory requirements related to logistics and supply chain activities.

Support insurance declarations, shipping documentation, and audit requirements.

Prepare operational reports, inventory reports, and KPI tracking as required.

Requirements:

Education

Diploma or Degree in Supply Chain Management, Logistics, Business Administration, or related discipline.

Experience

Minimum 5 to 7 years of experience in supply chain, logistics, operations, inventory management, or distribution.

Experience working with regional operations and third-party logistics providers.

Strong understanding of import/export, warehousing, inventory management, and order fulfilment processes.

Hands-on experience in managing operational issues and stakeholder coordination independently.

Experience in SAP S/4HANA and supply chain transaction processing.

Prior experience in consumer electronics, technology, FMCG, or related industries would be advantageous.

Technical Skills

Advanced SAP S/4HANA knowledge.

Advanced Microsoft Excel skills (Pivot Tables, Power Query, dashboards, data analysis).

Experience with inventory planning and supply chain reporting tools.

Knowledge of international trade compliance, import/export regulations, and free trade agreements (FTA/RCEP) is preferred.

Competencies

Strong stakeholder management and influencing skills.

Ability to work with regional and global teams across different cultures and time zones.

Strong commercial awareness and business acumen.

Excellent project management and organizational skills.

Ability to work independently with minimal supervision.
