Player Services Specialist – Player Services

3-6

India

Full-Time

About the Role: We are looking for an experienced Player Services Specialist to join our Player Services team. The role is focused on managing day-to-day player Services concerns, handling incoming email and Services tickets, maintaining an organised ticket queue, and ensuring players receive timely, accurate, and helpful responses.

We are looking for someone with prior hands-on experience in ticket-based Player Services who can independently manage assigned tickets, understand player concerns, perform basic troubleshooting, and follow established Services processes.

This is an individual contributor position with no people-management or team leadership responsibilities. The candidate will work under the guidance of the Player Services Manager, with structured processes, training, and ongoing Services.

Key Responsibilities: 1. Ticket & Queue Responsibilities: Handle daily incoming player Services tickets across email, web forms, and in-game Services channels.

Monitor and manage the assigned ticket queue to ensure timely responses and follow-ups.

Categorise, tag, prioritise, assign, and update tickets accurately.

Maintain proper ticket statuses and ensure pending tickets are followed up within defined timelines.

Identify and manage duplicate tickets, aged tickets, and unresolved queries.

Maintain queue hygiene and ensure no player queries are overlooked.

2. Player Query Handling: Understand player concerns and provide clear, accurate, and professional responses.

Handle common player issues relating to account access, login, gameplay, in-game purchases, missing items, and technical difficulties.

Use approved macros, templates, FAQs, and knowledge base articles to maintain response consistency.

Demonstrate ownership of assigned tickets until resolution or the appropriate next action.

Maintain a helpful, empathetic, and player-focused approach in all interactions.

3. Basic Troubleshooting & Resolution: Analyse player-reported issues using the information provided, including screenshots, videos, device details, and error messages.

Perform basic troubleshooting and provide appropriate guidance to players.

Follow established procedures to resolve common issues.

Identify cases that require further attention and flag them to the reporting manager with relevant details.

Ensure responses and resolutions are aligned with approved Services guidelines.

4. Services Quality & Process Adherence: Meet defined response and resolution timelines.

Maintain accuracy, consistency, and professionalism in all player communications.

Follow established SLAs, standard operating procedures, and ticket handling guidelines.

Ensure proper documentation and internal notes wherever required.

Maintain quality and productivity while handling daily ticket volumes.

Required Skills & Experience: 3+ years of experience in Player Services, Email Services, Technical Services, or Player Services.

Hands-on experience with ticket management platforms such as Zendesk, Freshdesk, Helpshift, or similar tools.

Strong written English communication skills.

Good understanding of ticket lifecycle management and queue operations.

Ability to independently handle Player queries and routine troubleshooting.

Strong attention to detail and organisational skills.

Ability to manage multiple tickets while maintaining response quality and SLA compliance.

Comfortable working with defined processes, guidelines, and reporting structures.

Good to Have: Previous experience in mobile gaming or the gaming industry.

Familiarity with Google Play Store and Apple App Store.

Experience handling player account issues, payment queries, missing rewards, and gameplay-related concerns.

Understanding of Services metrics such as First Response Time, Resolution Time, CSAT, and SLA compliance.

Performance Expectations: Performance will be evaluated based on individual operational performance, including: Ticket handling productivity.

First Response Time (FRT).

Resolution Time.

SLA compliance.

Customer Satisfaction (CSAT).

Ticket handling accuracy and response quality.

Queue management and follow-up discipline.

Adherence to Services processes and guidelines.

Training & Development: The selected candidate will receive structured onboarding and training covering the game, product knowledge, Services tools, ticket management workflows, troubleshooting procedures, and standard operating practices.

The role offers opportunities to strengthen product knowledge, develop Player Services expertise, and expand operational capabilities over time. Additional responsibilities and future growth opportunities will be based on individual performance, demonstrated capabilities, and evolving business requirements.

Working Requirements: Willingness to work in rotational shifts, including weekends and public holidays, based on business requirements.

Flexibility to adapt to changing ticket volumes and operational priorities.

Ability to work independently while operating within established processes and reporting structures.
