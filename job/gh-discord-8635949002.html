Program Manager, Detection & Enforcement, Youth Safety

Discord · San Francisco Bay Area

Mid Production $160K–$180K Posted 2026-07-21
Apply on Discord’s site →

Discord has a highly engaged community of millions of daily active users who use the platform for many different reasons, but there’s one thing that nearly everyone does: play video games.

Discord plays a uniquely important role in the future of gaming, and we are focused on making it easier and more fun for people to hang out before, during, and after playing games.

As the Program Manager, Detection Enforcement, Youth Safety on the Youth Safety team, you will program manage product and process initiatives that drive more effective and efficient mitigations across the minor safety remit. You will serve as the internal subject matter expert across product, detection, tooling, and bugs, including working with XFN teams to drive safety improvements. You will report into the Senior Manager of Youth Safety.

Please note: This role involves exposure to potentially disturbing topics such as child sexual abuse material, exploitation imagery, and grooming-related content. Wellness support is provided.

What You’ll be Doing:

Drive product and process improvements

Recommend and drive youth safety initiatives that reduce average handle time, escalation rates, and help identify additional harms

Own product and process improvements for all NCMEC reports, CSAM enforcement, and other child sexual exploitation harms

Manage the tooling roadmap for the team, develop and prioritize tool requests

Audit operations and report handling, in collaboration with our QA team, to identify processing gaps and root cause issues to drive continuous improvement in detection, moderation, and tooling

Partner with our Product Operations teams to ensure internal workflows and tools are supportive of new product launches

Advise and guide efforts by supporting Discord stakeholder teams, including Vendor Operations, Data Engineering, Threat Research, and Policy to incorporate user safety initiatives, projects, and planning

Ensure delivery of high quality, trauma-informed, person-centered services to prevent crimes against children and other vulnerable populations

Manage and drive the proactive detection roadmap:

Work cross functionally with our Safety product team to develop ML tooling for detection and automated enforcement of high-harm groups and behavior

Work with engineering to build out heuristic models for detection across content types, regions, and languages and harms

Work with engineering to assess the effectiveness of image hashing, novel CSAM classifiers, and other detection for CSAM content; drive roadmap goals for detection improvements

Act as the liaison between our team and Product and Engineering

Conduct comprehensive product audits to understand where we can deliver more impact for young people, driving policy, product, tooling, and process recommendations

Leverage data insights and strategic communication to make recommendations for large-scale initiatives and validate the safety-related impact and tradeoffs

What You Should Have:

In depth knowledge of child sexual exploitation investigation, detection, and reporting obligations for industry, equivalent of 6-10 years of project or program management

Intermediate SQL Proficiency and technical skills

A Bachelor’s degree

Program management experience establishing and driving operational projects at scale

Ability to create effective relationships, influence, and collaborate at all organizational levels

Demonstrated experience with organizational capacity, namely building and strengthening teams at scale, as well as building and strengthening stakeholder partnerships

Experience analyzing and mitigating high-impact threats and online threat actors, with a track record of managing tactical and strategic initiatives resulting in measurable change

Proven leadership in developing, articulating, and advocating policies and strategies relating to teen and child safety

Preferred Qualifications (bonus points):

Direct experience in creating and up-leveling harm detection strategies such as ML models, image hashing, or user reports

Previous experience with incident management in other global trust and safety teams

Data analysis skills
