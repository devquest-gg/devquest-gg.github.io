Senior Franchise Producer

Franchise Development // PlayStation Studios™

PlayStation Studios has created some of the most loved worlds, characters, and stories in entertainment. Our opportunity now is to build those IP with greater intention — growing audiences, deepening fandom, and creating more ways for people to engage with PlayStation franchises beyond the release of a game.

We are looking for a Senior Franchise Producer to help turn that ambition into coordinated high-quality work across a varied portfolio of franchise initiatives.

This is a project management and production role with an unusually broad remit. The producer may support or lead work across franchise resources and documentation, location-based experience, linear media, publishing, internal partner and contractor-led projects, and other initiatives that bring franchise strategy to life.

The role sits within a broader production team and contributes to shared sprint planning, prioritization, and delivery. While the team works collectively, this senior producer will independently own a defined set of workstreams from planning through completion.

A major part of the role is partnering with teams across Sony Interactive Entertainment to translate franchise strategies and roadmaps into executable plans, track activity and dependencies, and directly produce selected initiatives where appropriate.

This producer will also work closely with stakeholders within the PlayStation Studios’ development studios, coordinating approvals and reviews throughout production, maintaining trusted relationships, and ensuring that franchise work reflects both studio intent and wider PlayStation priorities.

Key Responsibilities

Own a portfolio of franchise production workstreams from initial brief and scoping through delivery, review, launch, and closeout.

Translate franchise strategies and roadmaps into practical project plans with clear objectives, milestones, owners, dependencies, budgets, risks, and decision points.

Track franchise activity across teams, maintain integrated plans and status reporting, and provide clear visibility of progress, risks, trade-offs, and required decisions.

Produce selected franchise initiatives directly, coordinating internal contributors, studios, external partners, and vendors to deliver against agreed creative, quality, schedule, and budget expectations.

Support a varied range of productions, which may include location-based experiences, publishing projects, franchise resources, documentation, presentations, toolkits, and partner-facing materials.

Lead day-to-day project communication, meetings, action tracking, production reviews, approvals, and follow-up across multiple concurrent workstreams.

Partner across PlayStation Studios, Portfolio Strategy, PlayStation Productions, brand, marketing, insights, finance, consumer products, licensing, legal, and Sony Group teams to align plans and unblock delivery.

Scope, onboard, brief, and oversee contractors, agencies, and specialist production partners; manage deliverables, capacity, performance, and associated resource needs.

Contribute actively to team sprint planning, backlog management, prioritization, estimation, retrospectives, and continuous improvement as part of the wider franchise production team.

Create and maintain production standards, templates, trackers, documentation, and repeatable ways of working that improve clarity and consistency across the team.

Identify issues early, recommend practical solutions, escalate appropriately, and keep work moving through ambiguity and changing priorities.

What We’re Looking For

Substantial production or project management experience in games, entertainment, media, experiential, publishing, consumer products, licensing, or a related creative industry.

A track record of independently leading complex, cross-functional projects with multiple stakeholders, dependencies, and concurrent deadlines.

Strong command of production fundamentals, including scoping, scheduling, budgeting, resource planning, risk management, approval workflows, status reporting, and delivery governance.

Experience translating strategic or creative ambition into structured plans, practical next steps, and high-quality deliverables.

Excellent stakeholder management skills, with the judgment and confidence to work with senior leaders, creative teams, specialist functions, and external partners.

Clear and concise communication, with the ability to adapt detail for working teams, studio leadership, and executive audiences.

A highly collaborative approach, combined with the accountability to own workstreams and drive them through to completion.

Experience working in agile or sprint-based teams and contributing to shared planning, backlog, prioritization, and review routines.

Experience briefing and managing agencies, contractors, vendors, or other external production resources.

Experience in one or more of the following is a plus: location-based entertainment; publishing, including books or comics; franchise or IP documentation; licensing or consumer products; live experiences; or multi-format entertainment production.

Knowledge of games and the PlayStation ecosystem is valuable, but relevant experience from adjacent entertainment sectors is equally welcome.

The Ideal Candidate

The ideal candidate is a calm, proactive, and highly organized senior producer who enjoys bringing structure to ambitious creative work. They can move comfortably between long-range roadmaps and the operational detail needed to deliver a specific project.

They are equally effective facilitating a team sprint, building an integrated plan, briefing a contractor, preparing a studio review, or helping cross-functional partners understand how their activity contributes to a wider franchise roadmap.

They build trust through clarity, follow-through, and good judgment. They know when to drive, when to facilitate, and when to escalate. They are motivated by collective success, while being fully accountable for the workstreams placed in their care.

Success in This Role Looks Like

Assigned productions have clear plans, accountable owners, realistic schedules, visible dependencies, and well-managed risks.

Franchise strategies and roadmaps are translated into coordinated activity that progresses across PlayStation and Sony teams.

Studio stakeholders and cross-functional partners experience a clear, responsive, and trusted production process with timely reviews and approvals.

Projects and resources are managed responsibly, with issues surfaced early and delivery kept aligned to creative intent, quality, timing, and budget.

The wider production team benefits from strong sprint participation, transparent workload planning, dependable ownership, and improved tools and ways of working.

The producer successfully owns multiple workstreams while contributing flexibly to the collective priorities of the Franchise Development team.
