Motion Designer

Nordcurrent · Vilnius

Mid Marketing Posted 2026-09-02 Full time
Apply on Nordcurrent’s site →

At Nordcurrent, we develop mobile games that bring joy to millions of players worldwide. As a Motion Designer in our Digital Creatives Team, you will create video and motion content for Cooking Fever, Pocket Styler, Airplane Chefs and games in development. Your work will help players discover our games, and you will see how it performs in global campaigns.

Your Key Responsibilities:

Create video ads and motion content for our games across digital advertising and social media.

Bring ideas to life with animation and visual storytelling, and test new creative concepts.

Work with gameplay footage, game assets, UI and typography.

Make variations of the best-performing ads and improve them based on campaign results.

Use AI-powered tools to speed up production and explore new ideas.

Work closely with designers, artists, marketing and user acquisition specialists.

Follow gaming, advertising and creative trends and bring them into your work.

A Successful Candidate Must Have:

Strong motion design, animation and visual storytelling skills.

Experience creating video for digital advertising, social media or other marketing channels.

Good knowledge of Adobe After Effects, Premiere Pro, Photoshop and Illustrator.

You pay attention to timing, rhythm and the small details that make a video work.

Experience with short-form video for different platforms and audiences.

Hands-on experience with AI-powered creative tools.

Good written and spoken English.

Extra Great to Have:

Experience working with gameplay footage, game assets, UI or VFX.

Performance marketing or user acquisition creatives for mobile games.

3D skills (Blender, Cinema 4D).

Knowledge of creative testing, A/B testing and marketing metrics (CTR, IPM, ROAS).

Interest in games, digital advertising and creative trends.

Please note: applications without samples of work will not be considered.

Salary: From 2630 EUR/month (gross), based on experience and competence.
