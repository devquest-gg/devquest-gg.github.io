Division Overview

The PlayStation Studios: Creative team is an award winning, global internal creative agency within PlayStation Studios. We endeavour to build a working environment where we can flourish and create industry leading work. Structured around 2 teams; Brand Strategy and the Creative Studio, comprising of four core disciplines; Design illustration, UI/UX, Motion 3D, Film and Video. We deliver projects in collaboration with our game development and marketing/PR teams.

Role Overview

As a Senior Compositor, you will play a key role in creating photorealistic, high-end visual content for PlayStation s marketing and brand campaigns. Working closely with Creative Directors, CG Supervisors, 3D Artists, Motion Designers, Producers, and external partners, you will seamlessly integrate CGI renders , matte paintings, photographic elements, and motion graphics into premium-quality still imagery and motion content.

With strong artistic judgment and technical expertise , you will ensure exceptional visual quality, consistency, and realism while supporting multiple creative projects and major PlayStation product and game launches within demanding production timelines.

Responsibilities

CG Integration Image Assembly

i ntegrate CGI renders , matte paintings, photographic assets, and motion graphic elements into cohesive final imagery.

Combine multiple render passes and source elements while maintaining realistic depth, perspective, lighting, reflections, and shadows.

Ensure all composited assets meet PlayStation’s premium visual standards across still and motion deliverables.

Visual Quality Consistency

Maintain aesthetic continuity, colour balance, and photorealism across campaign assets and image sequences.

Collaborate with Creative Directors and 3D teams to preserve creative intent throughout the finishing process.

Ensure consistency across multiple formats, platforms, and product campaigns.

Technical Execution Finishing

Perform complex matte extractions, masking, cleanup, and image reconstruction work.

Troubleshoot compositing challenges and deliver technically accurate final assets.

Interpret and implement stakeholder feedback efficiently while maintaining creative quality.

Prepare and deliver final files according to agency, platform, and production specifications.

Collaboration Workflow

Work closely with internal creative teams, producers, and external vendors throughout the production process.

Contribute to efficient pipeline management and best-practice compositing workflows.

Support multiple projects and deliver high-quality assets within demanding production schedules .

Requirements

Minimum 5 years of professional experience working within a creative agency, production studio, post-production environment, or equivalent.

Expert-level proficiency in Nuke Adobe Photoshop and relevant compositing tools and plugins.

Strong understanding of CGI workflows, with assets typically rendered from VRay , Redshift, Renderman or Arnold.

Working knowledge of deep compositing methodologies and advanced image assembly techniques.

Demonstrable experience producing high-end commercial, entertainment, or branded photorealistic CGI content.

Experience compositing both static imagery and motion-based CGI deliverables is highly desirable.

Strong artistic understanding of lighting, composition, perspective, colour theory, and photographic principles.

Familiarity with agency production pipelines, ACES colour management workflows, and render output specifications.

Excellent communication skills with the ability to interpret creative and technical feedback.

A portfolio or showreel demonstrating premium-quality compositing , CGI integration, and finishing work is required .
