Project Manager

Production / Tokyo, Japan

Apply Now

WORK DESCRIPTION Game production (including support) and project management Strategy formulation related to planning, income and expenditure forecasting, system building, and staff retention Support for promotional activities Development, selection, progress planning, contract establishment, and progress management of external production companies in Japan and overseas REQUIREMENTS Native level Japanese language proficiency (JLPT N1 required) Those who are in tune with the attitude that Kojima Productions is striving for in producing their products At least one year of work experience in professions such as producer/project manager/director in various industries (we will also assess your experience from the perspective of businesses other than game production) Experience in project management such as budgeting, quality control, and schedule management Understanding of the process of game production PREFERRED QUALIFICATIONS Those who have experience producing hit content Those who have the ability to communicate in English Expertise and practical experience in product design, promotion, marketing, etc.

Expertise and practical experience in management tools such as Hansoft and JIRA Those who are highly knowledgeable in online games/social games Those who are highly knowledgeable in games and entertainment content other than games Ability to analyse data based on a database, construct hypotheses, and propose measures Ability to respond flexibly and think logically Good at coordination, smooth communication with internal and external stakeholders, and good at summarizing matters REQUIRED DOCUMENTS Japanese CV (Rirekisho with photo) ※if available CV with the following information: Please specify all game titles you have been involved with in the past and the details of your duties while working on them.

If you are from a business other than game production, please explain so that we can understand what work you do.

Please briefly specify the titles of two of the most recent games you played and why you played them.

NOTES Please exclude information about confidential matters before submitting.

Since our system is unable to recognize an overseas post code, applicants from outside Japan do not have to enter a post code that matches their actual address. However, please ensure that all other address information are valid.

[EMPLOYMENT STATUS] Contract employee (promotion to Permanent Employee is possible) [LOCATION] Minato-ku Tokyo Japan (Nearest train station: Shinagawa station) [WORK HOURS] 10:00-18:45 [SALARY] Annual salary, performance incentive system in place We will take into consideration your past experiences and abilities and match them to our company regulations.

[BENEFITS] Various insurance, transportation expenses, salary revision (once a year), medical checkups, congratulatory and condolence allowances, corporate defined contribution pension plan (for Permanent Employees only), incentives (for Permanent Employees only) [TIME-OFF] Five-day work week (2 days off, Saturday and Sunday), official holidays, summer vacation, end-of-year/New Year, paid vacation, congratulatory/bereavement leave
