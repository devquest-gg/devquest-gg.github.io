Every day, tens of millions of people come to Roblox to explore, create, play, learn, and connect with friends in 3D immersive digital experiences– all created by our global community of developers and creators.

At Roblox, we’re building the tools and platform that empower our community to bring any experience that they can imagine to life. Our vision is to reimagine the way people come together, from anywhere in the world, and on any device.

We’re on a mission to connect a billion people with optimism and civility, and looking for amazing talent to help us get there.

A career at Roblox means you’ll be working to shape the future of human interaction, solving unique technical challenges at scale, and helping to create safer, more civil shared experiences for everyone.

As a Software Engineer on the Account Authentication Team, you will help authenticate and authorize Roblox s user base, setting the foundation for every other feature on our platform.

You will build the authentication and security controls that meet each user s needs, along with just-in-time friction that adapts to each user s unique risk profile, all while scaling to 1 billion active users.

This is a rare opportunity to sit at the convergence of security, user experience, and scale. You ll join Roblox s Safety organization, whose mission is to become the leader in civil immersive online communities by detecting, removing, and preventing problematic accounts, content, and behavior, and by keeping accounts secure and free from compromise. The team draws on a broad tech spectrum, including machine learning, classifiers, experimentation, automation, and AI-powered filters, partnering with product teams to shape the roadmap, build safety products, and measure impact on our community of users and developers.

You Will:

Own the design, implementation and landing of features that make Roblox more secure and more accessible for users of all ages on all platforms.

Collaborate cross-organizationally with partner teams in Growth, Discovery and the broader Roblox Safety org.

Help define how goals get accomplished and have real influence over product direction.

Influence how users first experience Roblox, often forming their baseline expectations for what security should look like on the internet.

Instrument and analyze user interaction data (A/B tests, usage metrics) to inform design decisions and maximize product impact.

Own implementation end to end across the stack, contributing in Rust, C++, and Lua as projects require, using AI tools wherever possible.

You Have :

1-3 years of experience in Software Engineering/Computer Science

Experience working on user facing applications or services

Proficiency in one or more programming languages such as Rust, C#, and Javascript.

Experience collaborating cross-functionally with Product stakeholders to define requirements and deliver technical solutions.

A strong commitment to designing, implementing, and maintaining systems that prioritize user safety, data integrity, and compliance with safety policies.
