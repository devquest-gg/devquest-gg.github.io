Who are Loop Games?

Loop Games is a leader in the mobile gaming industry, known for pushing the boundaries of creativity and innovation. As the pioneers behind the Match 3D genre, we’ve captivated millions of players worldwide. Our success is reflected in our accomplishments:

Top-Grossing Hits: Our games consistently dominate global charts.

100+ Million Downloads: Connecting with a vast, international audience.

$100+ Million Revenue: Exceptional success driven by in-app purchases.

At Loop Games, we blend creativity with cutting-edge technology to foster a dynamic, collaborative culture. We are looking for passionate, innovative individuals to join us in shaping the future of gaming. If you're excited about crafting the next big hit, we want to hear from you!

A Bit About The Role?

As a Product Manager, you will define innovative product strategies, oversee the complete lifecycle of mobile games, and utilize player insights to create compelling gaming experiences.

This role requires a deep understanding of the mobile gaming industry, strong analytical skills to interpret player behavior, and the ability to lead products to success. You will drive development, navigate challenges with creativity, and ensure projects are delivered seamlessly.

We’re looking for someone who flourishes entertainment space, understands the dynamics of the gaming industry, and can merge strategic thinking with a passion for creating memorable experiences.

If you're excited about playing games and shaping the future of the gaming world, we’d love to hear from you.

You’re passionate about games, not just as a creator but as a fan of what you build. Innovation excites you, and you’re always eager to explore the latest trends and bring fresh ideas to life. If you’re driven by creativity, synergy, and a sense of purpose, we’d love to have you on our team!
