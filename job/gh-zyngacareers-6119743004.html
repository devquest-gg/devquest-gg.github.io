Position Overview:

NaturalMotion is looking for a talented and passionate Meta Designer to join our London studio, helping shape the combat experience for our first-person shooter mobile title. This role focuses on designing and managing metagame, live, and progression features to create a satisfying and sustainable mobile experience.

You will own key metagame features from conception through live, coordinating with design, art, UX, engineering, and product management throughout the whole process. As a live game you will need to design features with iteration and extensibility in mind and analyze live data to expand and improve your features over time. We are looking for someone who brings both craft knowledge, an excitement to try new things, and a genuine love of action games.

We embrace a hybrid work environment. Within this role you will work 2 days per week in our London office and the remaining days at home.

What You ll Do:

Ownership of designing, prototyping, and implementing progression, economy, and engagement features that align with the creative vision of the game

Help define, analyze, and take action on analytics, feature data, and KPIs

Work closely with live and product management teams to define goals and implement solutions to boost engagement

Collaborate closely with art, animation, audio, design and engineering teams to build content and solve problems in a multi-disciplinary environment

Develop and balance in-game economies across many different metagame features

Work closely with UX and engineering to ensure features are implemented in ways that meet the engagement goals

Analyse player data, feedback, and behaviours to identify and fix metagame issues and retention barriers

Work concurrently on multiple features at varying stages of development, managing priorities effectively as content pipelines run in parallel

Document and communicate metagame feature concepts clearly to ensure alignment across all development teams

What You Bring:

Previous experience in Metagame / Progression / Engagement Design or an equivalent role in the video game industryAt least one shipped title in a design role for a live service titleExperience designing and implementing progression, economy, or engagement features for live gamesKnowledge and experience analyzing KPIs and player behaviour dataFamiliarity with the Unreal game engineAbility to analyse gameplay data and player feedback to inform and balance design decisionsStrong communication skills with the ability to work effectively across cross-disciplinary teamsThe ability to manage multiple designs in a fast-paced development environmentPassionate about action games with a deep understanding of what makes enemy designs engaging and memorable

Bonus Points:

Background in mobile games with an understanding of mobile player behaviour and market leaders

Experience with visual scripting or rapid prototyping tools

An avid gamer with a genuine enthusiasm for crafting engaging progression and metagame features and motivating players over a long period of time
