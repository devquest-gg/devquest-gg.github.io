Why Sony Interactive Entertainment?

Sony Interactive Entertainment isn’t just the Best Place to Play — it’s also the Best Place to Work. Sony Interactive Entertainment (SIE) is the company behind the PlayStation brand. As a subsidiary of Sony Group Corporation, we’re part of a proud legacy of innovation and excellence. SIE is a dynamic technology company, delivering cutting-edge hardware and network services to more than 100 million people and an entertainment leader, home to some of the most beloved and recognizable intellectual properties (IP) in the world. Our role at SIE is to create and nurture the experiences under the PlayStation brand, a name synonymous with entertainment excellence and creativity.

Role overview:

The Commercial Planning Specialist plays a key role in supporting the Commercial Planning team through financial analysis, operational coordination, reporting and process excellence. Working closely with the Commercial Planning Manager, Commercial Planner, Channel Experience Manager and Commercial Managers, you ll help ensure accurate forecasting, effective budget management and high-quality commercial insights that enable informed decision-making across the business.

This role combines commercial operations, financial administration, reporting and continuous process improvement, making it ideal for someone who enjoys working with data, improving ways of working and partnering with stakeholders across multiple functions.

What you ll be doing:

Systems Process Management

Act as the local SAP key user for Commercial Planning and Finance processes.

Maintain and improve operating procedures to drive consistency and efficiency.

Support system troubleshooting and identify opportunities to optimise commercial processes.

Ensure compliance with financial controls and sales management processes.

Partner with regional and global stakeholders on initiatives to enhance systems and ways of working.

Business Tools Reporting

Maintain commercial reporting dashboards, databases and planning tools.

Ensure the accuracy, integrity and consistency of commercial data.

Produce regular and ad hoc reporting to support commercial decision-making.

Analyse trends and performance, highlighting risks, opportunities and actionable insights.

Provide reporting support and cover across the Commercial Planning team when required.

Commercial Planning Support

Support the Commercial Planner in forecast management, allocations and operational follow-up

Assist in the preparation and maintenance of planning files and reporting tools.

Participate in operational reviews and help identify risks and mitigation actions.

Work closely with Commercial Planner to ensure data consistency.

Provide backup support for the Commercial Planner on reporting tools and trackers.

Budget Management Finance Support

Support the Channel Experience Manager in tracking Trade Marketing and Commercial budgets.

Maintain budget trackers and investment reporting.

Monitor actual spend versus forecast on Commercial Terms.

Support ROI and investment performance analysis.

What we re looking for:

Proven experience working in a commercial planning, commercial operations, or business planning role.

Strong SAP knowledge, ideally within Finance and Controlling modules.

Advanced Excel skills (Macro, Visual Basic, Automation, IA)

Strong analytical and reporting skills.

Strong attention to detail and process discipline.

Excellent communication and stakeholder management skills.

Commercial awareness and understanding of retail environments.

Proactive and solution-oriented mindset.

Desirable:

Experience in FMCG, Consumer Electronics or Retail environments.

Experience working with ERP systems and financial processes.

Experience supporting commercial, planning or operations teams.
