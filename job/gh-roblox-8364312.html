Principal Product Manager, Creation Platform

Roblox · San Mateo

Director+ Production $331K–$383K Posted 2026-11-09 Full time

Every day, tens of millions of people come to Roblox to explore, create, play, learn, and connect with friends in 3D immersive digital experiences– all created by our global community of developers and creators.

At Roblox, we’re building the tools and platform that empower our community to bring any experience that they can imagine to life. Our vision is to reimagine the way people come together, from anywhere in the world, and on any device.

We’re on a mission to connect a billion people with optimism and civility, and looking for amazing talent to help us get there.

A career at Roblox means you’ll be working to shape the future of human interaction, solving unique technical challenges at scale, and helping to create safer, more civil shared experiences for everyone.

The Creation Platform is the foundation every Roblox creator builds on - it stores and serves all content on Roblox, and provides the cloud services that power live games. Every mesh, image, sound, animation, script, and place lives here, and is ingested, processed, permissioned, and delivered to millions of players across billions of requests a day. Alongside content, the platform gives creators the backend they would otherwise have to build themselves: data storage, serverless compute, queues, and the Open Cloud APIs that let creators, third-party apps, and AI agents build on Roblox. As one of the most foundational systems at Roblox, it is the backbone that every experience, every creator workflow, and every player session depends on—reliably, securely, and at scale.

The way games get made is changing fast. AI and agents are about to multiply the volume of content and code created on Roblox by orders of magnitude, and creators expect the same cloud primitives and developer workflows they get from modern software and game backends. As the Product Leader for the team, you will define and lead the roadmap for how content and live game services are created, stored, governed, and served on Roblox. Your work will shape the core infrastructure, cloud services, APIs, and creator workflows that power every experience, and make the platform ready for the wave of AI creation. This is a rare opportunity to build the cloud platform for one of the largest creator ecosystems in the world.

You will:

Own the vision and roadmap for the Creation Platform, spanning content storage and delivery, cloud services for live games, and APIs creators and AI agents build on.

Scale mission-critical services that store and serve all content on Roblox and power game backends reliably and performantly, across billions of daily requests and a globally distributed infrastructure.

Build the cloud services creators need to run live games - data stores and player data, serverless compute functions, queues, and leaderboards - so they can build matchmaking, trading, and async game mechanics without standing up their own backend.

Make the platform ready for AI creation: scale for orders of magnitude more content and code created by agents as well as people, and build the structured metadata and APIs that agentic workflows, search, and safety depend on.

Support core developer workflows for creators, including drafts, versioning, branching and rollback, packages and dependencies, import and export from external tools, and Open Cloud APIs that work as well for an automated pipeline as for a creator in Studio.

Define a fair and sustainable model for cloud usage, with generous free limits, observability tools that help creators understand their consumption, and paid scaling through Extended Services as creators and the platform grow.

Evolve our permissions, licensing, and privacy models so creators, IP holders, and players can trust how their content and data are shared and protected.

Collaborate cross-functionally with engine, Studio, Creator Store, Trust Safety, infrastructure, and AI teams to build systems that are reliable, extensible, and impactful at global scale.

You are:

Experienced, with 8+ years of product management experience, ideally in cloud platforms, developer platforms, game backends, or large-scale storage and content delivery systems.

Formal degree in a technical field such as: Computer Science, Software Engineering, or similar experience.

Deeply technical, with a strong understanding of distributed systems, storage, serverless compute, APIs, and data modeling; capable of working closely with engineers on architecture and trade-offs.

A builder of developer products, with great taste in APIs and creator workflows and a deep understanding of how developers build, iterate, ship, and operate live services.

AI-fluent, with a point of view on how agents and generative models will change the way games and content are created, managed, and operated.

Metrics- and impact-driven, you can translate platform changes into measurable outcomes like reliability, latency, cost to serve, adoption, and creator productivity.

Highly collaborative, with a track record of leading through influence across product, engineering, design, and data teams.

Self-directed, comfortable operating in ambiguity, and driven by curiosity, creativity, and a desire to scale systems that touch millions.
