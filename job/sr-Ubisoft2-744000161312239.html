Lighting Team Lead - Assassin’s Creed Franchise

As a Lighting Team Lead at Ubisoft Quebec City, you will support a small team of artists working on lighting for the Assassin’s Creed franchise while contributing directly to production.

You’ll combine your lighting expertise with people management, planning, and coordination to support your team’s growth and artistic and technical goals. You’ll help maintain visual quality, artistic consistency, and game performance while working within engine constraints and production schedules.

What you’ll do Support and coach team members through regular one-on-one meetings , taking their strengths, needs, and goals into account and encouraging mentoring, learning, and career development Foster a collaborative work environment that supports self-direction, knowledge sharing, and well-being, while providing constructive feedback and recognizing contributions Plan and coordinate the team’s work , clarify goals and expectations, and assign tasks based on skills, capacity, workload, and schedules Monitor deliverables , anticipate production risks, adjust plans, and communicate priorities, issues, and team needs to relevant stakeholders Facilitate collaboration across disciplines , studio teams, and outsourcing partners to manage dependencies and resolve lighting issues, drawing on an understanding of artists’ day-to-day work Contribute directly to lighting production , including lighting setups, moods, and atmospheric elements for environments, gameplay sequences, and cinematics Ensure lighting meets artistic direction , gameplay needs, narrative requirements, and engine constraints, while optimizing the balance between visual quality and performance Collaborate with the Graphics Technical Director and programming teams to develop and improve lighting tools and features Participate in artistic and technical reviews , incorporate feedback, maintain deliverable quality, and improve lighting guidelines, best practices, and collaboration methods Carry out other related tasks What you bring to the team Relevant lighting experience in the video game plan and coordinate a team’s work while contributing directly to production and adapting priorities as needs change An interest in people management and artist development , with a collaborative approach that supports trust, self-direction, accountability, clear communication, constructive feedback, sound judgment, and empathy Resourcefulness , proactivity, and effective problem-solving
