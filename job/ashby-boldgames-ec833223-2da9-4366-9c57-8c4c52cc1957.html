Visual Development Artist

Bold Games · Istanbul

Mid Art Onsite Posted 2026-11-09 Full time
Apply on Bold Games’s site →

At Bold Games, we’re a tight-knit team that genuinely loves what we do. We support each other through every step of our journey and keep pushing towards the best that has yet to come.

We’re looking for a Visual Development Artist who will contribute to shaping the look of our games and get hands-on wherever the art pipeline needs it, from concepts to in-game UI.

What you’ll be doing - at its core:

- Shaping the visual direction of our games as a team, from references and early concepts to a consistent art style

- Designing and producing UI: screens, popups, buttons, icons and other interface elements

- Designing characters, environments and in-game items that fit the game’s style

- Producing quick visual prototypes and iterating on them based on team feedback

- Finding creative ways to solve production challenges

- Working closely with product and engineering teams throughout production

- Staying up-to-date with new tools and techniques to improve our art production pipeline and workflows

What we’re looking for:

- At least 1 year of experience as a 2D artist

- A portfolio that shows range across styles

- Proficiency in digital art tools such as Photoshop or Illustrator

- A good eye for color, shape and readability on small mobile screens

- Enjoying variety in your work, from familiar tasks to new ones

- Games industry experience is a plus, but we’d be glad to meet you if you’re passionate about games.
