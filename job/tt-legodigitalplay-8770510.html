At LEGO Digital Play, we’re bringing the LEGO® System-in-Play to the digital builders of tomorrow. We’re building the LEGO brand’s digital home: a place where builders of every age can create, share and discover, held to the standard of craft the LEGO brand is known for.

Our mission is to ensure the LEGO brand remains as powerful a part of people’s lives in the coming decades as it has ever been. We aim to reach players all around the world, and give them meaningful, magical and playful new experiences.

We are at an early phase of this new company, offering a unique opportunity to build a new entity for the world's most beloved and trusted brand. Our culture is open, collaborative, intellectually rigorous, and creatively vibrant.

Role Summary We are looking for a Games Community Lead to build a games community from the ground up. You will design its foundations, find and activate the first players, and grow a community that becomes the trusted home for news, early access and content.

Our community starts with gamers, many of whom are also keen builders and fans of the LEGO brand. You will get to know how the LEGO Group’s community teams work, including its long-standing fan communities, while building something new with its own identity. As the community grows, you will hire and lead a small team to scale it.

Key Responsibilities Design and deliver the foundational community strategy: choose and set up the right platforms across established and emerging channels, programmes such as early access and creator schemes, and the moderation and health metrics that keep the community thriving.

Find, recruit and activate the first core group of players, with a focus on gamers who are also keen builders and fans of the LEGO brand.

Build and run a playtester and advocate programme that gives game teams structured feedback before launch.

Create welcome journeys and activation hooks that turn casual testers into highly engaged community members.

Build and scale a creator programme that develops, rewards and spotlights creators and super-fans, while keeping LEGO Digital Play the trusted source for news, access and content.

Set up workflows to find, curate and feature outstanding community created content, and run contests, community challenges, live streams and workshops that bring the community together.

Track community sentiment and turn player and creator feedback into clear, prioritised insight for game teams.

Hire, lead and develop a small community team as the programme grows.

Required Qualifications A proven track record of building a gaming community from zero, ideally around a new or pre-launch title, including how you found and activated the first core group.

Experience setting up community programmes end to end: platforms, early access or playtest schemes, moderation and health metrics.

Hands-on experience across community platforms such as Discord, Reddit and social channels.

Experience developing creators and super-fans who build their own communities, while keeping your studio or publisher at the centre.

Experience leading, mentoring or developing others in a community or marketing team.

Confident using data and sentiment insight to shape decisions and influence game teams.

A real affection for the LEGO brand and building, and the curiosity to learn how established LEGO fan communities work.

Preferred Qualifications Background in UGC or creator platforms.

Experience hosting live streams or running community workshops.

Experience running contests, design challenges or creator events.

Hybrid Working: This role is based in our London office. Our hybrid working policy is three days a week in the office.
