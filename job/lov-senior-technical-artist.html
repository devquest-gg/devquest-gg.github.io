Senior Technical Artist

Black Banner Studios · České Budějovice · Remote

Senior Art Remote Posted 2026-11-20 Full time
Apply on Black Banner Studios’s site →

Legacy of Valor is an ambitious medieval RPG and strategy game combining massive real-time battles, third-person melee combat, and tactical army control in Unreal Engine 5. At Black Banner Studios, we're looking for an exceptional Senior Technical Artist who combines strong artistic judgment with deep technical expertise in Unreal Engine 5 PCG, Houdini, and procedural environment creation. One of your main challenges will be working with a small, highly experienced team to develop a procedural settlement and castle creation system, based on authentic 14th-century European architecture and settlement layouts. The goal is to create flexible, intuitive tools in Houdini and / or Unreal Engine that allow a small art team to produce dozens of beautiful, varied, historically grounded, and performant villages, towns, and castles. We're looking for someone who can create not only sophisticated procedural systems, but also beautiful environments, with tools that empower artists and give them meaningful creative control. We're open to candidates at both Senior and Lead levels, with the final title and responsibilities determined by experience and the scope of technical or artistic leadership. If you believe you'd be a great fit for this role, even if you don't meet every requirement listed below, we'd still love to hear from you. Please don't hesitate to apply! Design and develop sophisticated, well-organized, reusable, and performant PCG systems in Unreal Engine 5. Help create a flexible procedural settlement and castle system using Houdini, UE5 PCG, and custom tools. Develop procedural solutions for settlement layouts, roads, building placement, terrain adaptation, architectural variation, and environmental dressing. Build intuitive, artist-friendly tools for efficiently creating, customizing, and polishing medieval environments. Ensure high artistic quality, believable composition, historical authenticity, and visual variety across procedurally generated environments. Optimize procedural assets, instancing, materials, memory usage, and rendering performance for large-scale environments. Work closely with environment artists, designers, and programmers, contributing directly to production and improving workflows. Extensive professional experience in technical art or procedural environment development for games, including contributions to at least one shipped commercial title. Advanced hands-on experience with Unreal Engine 5 PCG, creating complex, organized, reusable, and performant procedural systems. Strong practical experience with Houdini, including procedural modeling, Houdini Digital Assets (HDAs), and Unreal Engine integration. Demonstrated ability to create intuitive procedural tools that enable artists to produce and customize high-quality environments efficiently. Strong artistic judgment, with an excellent understanding of composition, environment design, and believable world-building. Good understanding of modular asset creation, 3D pipelines, materials, instancing, LODs, and real-time rendering constraints. Strong profiling, optimization, problem-solving, and communication skills, with the ability to work independently within a small multidisciplinary team. Passion for video games, particularly medieval RPGs, open-world games, and strategy titles. Experience developing procedural settlements, cities, castles, architecture, or road systems. Familiarity with medieval European architecture and historically grounded environment design. Strong hands-on environment art skills, including 3D modeling, sculpting, and game-ready asset creation. Experience creating beautiful PBR materials and textures using Substance Designer and Unreal Engine's material system. Experience with Houdini VEX, Python, Blueprints, or procedural pipeline automation. Experience with terrain, foliage, landscape systems, and open-world optimization, including Nanite, instancing, and world streaming. Experience working on AAA and / or open-world game projects. Location: Remote / International, with the option to work in the Czech Republic. Engagement: Full-time, long-term collaboration. Contract: Contractor / B2B worldwide, or direct employment for candidates residing in or willing to relocate to the Czech Republic. Start Date: As soon as mutually agreed. Language: Professional working proficiency in English. Compensation: Competitive, based on experience and expertise.
