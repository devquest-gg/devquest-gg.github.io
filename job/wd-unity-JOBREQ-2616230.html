Unity · Beijing

The opportunity We are looking for a Developer Support Engineer to join our growing team. In this role, you will become a deep product expert across our full suite of supported products, taking end-to-end ownership of technical issues raised by customers and internal teams alike. You ll be the bridge between our partners and our engineering organization — delivering fast, accurate, and high-quality technical support while driving complex escalations to resolution.

What you ll be doing Serve as the primary technical point of contact for strategic partners, delivering timely and accurate support across all supported products and platforms Investigate complex technical issues end-to-end — identifying root causes, forming hypotheses, and driving solutions using diagnostic and analysis tooling Own and drive high-profile technical escalations, coordinating across R&D and Product Management to reach resolution Build and maintain strong working relationships with internal teams, providing structured product feedback and behavioral clarifications Prioritize and manage a queue of concurrent customer issues with professionalism and accountability Create and maintain knowledge articles and internal documentation to scale team expertise.

What we re looking for 2–4 years of experience in a technical role (technical support, solutions engineering, QA, software development, or similar) Bachelor s degree in a technical field or related experience Strong technical problem-solving skills — ability to trace system and application flows, dive deep into ambiguous issues, and identify root cause Comfortable working independently with high personal accountability and a positive, professional attitude Ability to multitask and perform under pressure in a fast-paced environment Excellent written and verbal English communication skills Hands-on experience with SQL, Excel, and data analysis Familiarity with mobile IDEs — Xcode, Android Studio, or Unity3D Nice to Have Experience with diagnostic tools such as Kibana, Charles Proxy, Wireshark, or Postman Basic knowledge of Java, Objective-C, or C# Hands-on experience with LLM tools such as Claude, Cursor, or Codex Base Salary Range: ¥298,100 – ¥529,900 CNY gross This range reflects the anticipated base salary for this position. Beyond base salary, this role may be eligible for equity awards and participation in our company incentive plans (such as annual discretionary bonuses or sales commissions). The final offer amount will depend on several factors, including geographic location and the candidate’s relevant experience, professional background, and skill set.
