Owlcat Games - We create cRPGs Games Warhammer 40,000: Dark Heresy The Expanse: Osiris Reborn WARHAMMER 40,000: ROGUE TRADER Pathfinder: Kingmaker Pathfinder: Wrath of the Righteous News Press Careers Publishing Learning CO-DEV Contacts ENG en de fr es ru zh back to vacancies Game Designer July 07, 2026 Design OWLCAT GAMES was founded in 2016. The studio employs experienced developers who worked on projects such as Pathfinder: Kingmaker , Pathfinder: Wrath of the Righteous , Warhammer 40,000: Rogue Trader , and is currently working on titles Warhammer 40,000: Dark Heresy and The Expanse: Osiris Reborn .

TASKS :

Writing and updating game documentation

Working on game mechanics and combat mechanics, developing new systems and game elements

Overseeing the complete implementation of new game elements by providing design specifications and interacting with other development teams

Scripting game elements in Unity.

REQUIREMENTS:

Extensive experience in playing video games, in particular RPGs and turn-based tactics, understanding of the ins and outs of these game genres and their mechanics

Experience in working on game projects (preferably RPGs, tactics games, and strategy games), at least one of which was launched on a major gaming platform (Steam, Google Play, etc.)

Experience in writing design documents and their implementation

Experience in developing elements of game mechanics and combat mechanics

Experience in creating and scripting game entities

Ability to make a convincing argument

Russian language skills at B2 level are necessary due to the team s Russian-language production pipeline and internal communication.

BENEFITS:

Comfortable offices (if you want to work from office) fitted with areas for recreation and games, coffee points, and snacks;

Convenient work schedule: adjust work time to your preferences and/or work remotely;

Strong internal community: we are passionate about what we do. Outside of work, we engage in activities together like playing tabletop games in the office or on Roll20;

Environment for personal growth: work together with the best professionals and enjoy the benefits of our developed mentoring system and training courses;

Competitive salary based on the interview, opportunities for career growth.
