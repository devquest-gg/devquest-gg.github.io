Investment Designer (Contract)

Bungie · Remote · Remote

Mid Design Remote Posted 2026-09-18 Contractor
Apply on Bungie’s site →

Do you dream about creating worlds imbued with real value and consequence? Can you imagine reward chases that excite and delight players without feeling like chores? Can you devise ways for players to meaningfully express themselves? If you answered yes to these questions, Bungie wants you on our team as our newest Investment Designer.

RESPONSIBILITIES

Implement a wide variety of cosmetic rewards that complement Marathon activities and store.

Perform scripting and tool work

Design and implement sustainable content that excite and engage players

Tune progression values to create compelling moments and a satisfying progression

REQUIRED SKILLS

Professional game development experience

Some experience working with game development software and tools

Knowledgeable in reward and cosmetic systems

Strong written and verbal communication skills

Desire to work as part of a large team and ability to take and provide feedback and direction appropriately

NICE-TO-HAVE SKILLS

Technical experience writing text-based scripts to manage game systems (scripting in a Lua, Python, or similar language)

Experience understanding player motivation; knowing what drives people to engage with content

Experience working on games as a service/live content
