VFX Artist - Games

We’re after experienced VFX artists to create evocative visual effects in the dark and gritty game Path of Exile. You will develop real-time effects from concept to implementation and work to maintain a consistent aesthetic across all of the effects in the game. The ideal candidate has a firm grasp of colour, texture, timing, and impact as relates to VFX and demonstrates an ability to sketch and storyboard to convey design ideas.

We are seeking someone with:

Three years of experience creating VFX for games

Experience creating real-time game effects in a wide range of software

A superior eye for lighting, value, colour, details, and the ability to use them in creating efficient and impactful visual effects

Self-motivation, excellent communications skills, and able to work within a team

A positive attitude and able to receive and provide objective feedback

Passion for creating dark and gritty spells and microtransactions

Portfolio, and/or reel demonstrating relevant artistic skills required.

Must be willing to complete an art test if requested to do so.

Web Programmer
