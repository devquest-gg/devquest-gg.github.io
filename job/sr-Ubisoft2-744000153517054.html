We are looking for a Public Cloud Ops Administrator&#xa0;to join our Public Cloud Operations team&#xa0;and play a key role in building, automating, and operating our multi-cloud platforms.This role combines strong hands-on technical expertise with a collaborative mindset, ensuring our cloud environments are reliable, secure, scalable, and aligned with modern operational best practices.

As a member of the team, you will drive automation initiatives, design and maintain CI/CD pipelines, and strengthen our Linux-based and cloud-native platforms. You will also contribute to team growth by sharing knowledge, promoting best practices, and supporting an automation-first and reliability-driven culture.

KEY RESPONSIBILITIES Public Cloud Operations Operate and support public cloud environments (AWS, Azure, GCP or equivalent), including monitoring, troubleshooting, cost awareness, and day-to-day operational excellence.

Ensure platform reliability, availability, and security in production environments.

Automation & CI/CD Design, implement, and maintain GitLab CI/CD pipelines&#xa0;to streamline deployments, testing, and infrastructure provisioning.

Promote automation to reduce manual operations and improve delivery speed and reliability.

Infrastructure as Code Build and manage cloud infrastructure using Terraform, Ansible, and other automation tools to ensure consistency, traceability, and repeatability.

Cloud-Native Operations Deploy, operate, and optimize containerized workloads&#xa0;using Docker and Kubernetes&#xa0;in public cloud environments.

Support cloud-native patterns and platform integrations.

Linux Administration Administer and optimize Linux systems&#xa0;(Ubuntu, CentOS/RHEL) with strong expertise in networking, storage, and virtualization.

Perform performance analysis, troubleshooting, and system hardening.

On-Call Support Participate in an on-call rotation&#xa0;to ensure timely response to critical production incidents and service availability.

Collaboration & Knowledge Sharing Actively contribute to team discussions, documentation, and knowledge sharing.

Support peers and help promote DevOps and SRE best practices across the organization.
