The Challenge

As a Lead, Systems Administrator at Take-Two Interactive Software, you ll play a pivotal role in maintaining and improving our world-class IT infrastructure. You will be the on-site contact for technical operations and support, working closely with development teams to ensure flawless service delivery. This role offers an outstanding opportunity to craft the IT landscape of a leading entertainment company!

What You’ll Take On

On-site contact for technical operations and support of all production and development environments acting as the highest escalation point within the team.

Must work jointly with development groups to assess their support needs to identify and deliver the resources required to meet ambitious goals.

Works independently to manage Global Support projects at the local and regional level including professionally actioning leadership provided strategy with limited oversight.

Ensure all client requests and operational problems are updated/resolved within a timely manner.

Providing an expert level of communication to the business, partner teams, and leadership.

Mentors junior staff and provides technical oversight on their requests and projects.

Provide cross functional team contact for technical assistance.

Assist with offsite events including setup, event support, and teardown.

Passionate about delivering outstanding client experience and support.

Inquisitiveness and ambition towards looking for opportunities to automate and improve service delivery.

Enjoys working in a fast-paced environment with the ability to change priorities.

Assist with employee on-boarding ensuring new staff is ready to work on day one.

Maintain inventory/asset management for company provided equipment.

Assist with audio/visual hardware setup and break down, along with conference rooms and video conferences.

Ability to work in an autonomous fashion, communicating in an efficient manner is key. May include communicating after hours and on weekends. Further must be reachable by mobile in the event of an emergency or escalation.

Some local travel may be required.

What You Bring

5-8 Years experience with the Windows 10, MacOS, Microsoft Office 365, Active Directory and Exchange, TCP/IP based networks (including DHCP, DNS, etc.), installation and maintenance of workstations and infrastructure hardware

Experience with providing coordination with onsite and remote IT teams to support requests projects

Ability to multitasks, organize shifting priorities requests, managing task resources with local support team

Solid understanding of ITIL

Experience with white glove service with VIP/Stakeholders

Expert familiarity with Operating System configuration, management, and support for desktop/laptop and server

Proven expertise in managing and resolving network devices and infrastructure.

Experience using Okta or similar SAML and Two-Factor Authentication solutions

Experience with using SCCM for Desktop OS deployment and software management

Proficient with AirWatch/Intelligent Hub/Jamf or equivalent Mobile Device Management experience

Expert knowledge on Computer Hardware: Desktops, Laptops, Mobile devices, tablets, and a wide variety of devices and peripherals

Productivity Tools: MS Office, Video Conferencing (Zoom), Slack

Remote Work Solutions: VPN, VDI (Horizon View)

Security: Solid grasp of security standards and methodologies

Intermediate PowerShell and scripting experience

Experience with Conference Room and AV technology experience

Experience with ServiceNow or equivalent helpdesk ticket system experience

Outstanding communication and customer service skills.

Ability to communicate effectively with end users to successfully resolve queries and problems and translate technical information and jargon for a non-technical audience
