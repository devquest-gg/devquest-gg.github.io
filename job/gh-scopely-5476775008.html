Scopely is hiring a Senior Legal Operations Manager based in our Culver City, CA hub on a hybrid basis.

As a Senior Legal Operations Manager, you will be the driving force of innovation, scale, technology, and automation for the Scopely legal team. You will be responsible for building and streamlining the strategic technologies of the Legal department as our company expands around the world. You will be part of a team that implements key systems, optimizes processes, manages projects, creates objectives, and reports performance metrics to deliver legal services in the most collaborative, friendly, and efficient manner, including supporting both internal and external resource management. You ll get to work with some super inspiring people, including our Finance, IT, Workplace, and People teams and a growing team of external vendors and law firms.

Reporting to our Director, Legal Operations, you ll be the second member of our growing Legal Operations team and have a significant impact on how the broader Legal department and Scopely continue to grow.

What You Will Do:

Oversee operational processes to maintain and support legal systems and technology, including contract management, ebilling/matter management, e-signature, workflow automation, and document storage

Identify opportunities where AI can transform how legal work gets done, partnering with Legal, IT, Security, and business stakeholders to select, pilot, and operationalize AI-enabled solutions that reduce manual effort, improve speed and quality of service, and free up attorneys to focus on higher-value strategic work

Assist in developing relevant project and process documentation and self-help tools as needed: legal intranet, playbooks, legal training, Q A, process flows, etc

Support the legal operations function on assigned cross-functional and departmental initiatives

Contract Management:

Serve as the key administrator of Ironclad, owning templates, workflows, repository design, system configuration, and technical integrations with a particular focus on continuous optimization of existing workflows and development of new ones

Oversee and help streamline contract workflow processes, including creation, assignment, organization, tracking, document storage, and template/playbook management

Manage process for verifying existence of an active services agreement and associating the correct parent contract for Statement of Work workflows in Ironclad

Manage process for archiving executed agreements and capturing appropriate metadata fields for our repository

Be a primary point of contact to the business to answer questions regarding legal contract review support and processes, and triage incoming contract-related requests

Develop and maintain detailed process documentation and training materials that you will use to drive continuous adoption across the Legal team and relevant cross-functional partners

E-Billing/Matter Management:

Oversee our outside counsel and e-billing/matter management processes, including

Manage the strategic use of Brightflag for e-billing and spend management, including invoice review and spend-analysis tools to enforce outside counsel billing guidelines, flag exceptions, and surface spend trends

Manage law firm engagement letters and onboarding with AP, opening and closing matters

Oversee monthly accrual process and prepare monthly accrual reports for AP

Manage integration with Oracle for approved invoices sent to AP

Manage new timekeeper review and approval

Interact independently with in-house legal team and outside counsel regarding missing accruals or invoices, disputes, or to answer other questions that may arise concerning invoicing/status, onboarding, guidelines, and any system or process issues

Assist in building and maintaining the Legal team s annual budget and forecast, tracking spend against plan and flagging variances early

Assist in the development of data-driven reporting on legal spend, matter volume, and operational KPIs for Legal leadership

What We re Looking For:

At least 6-8 years of relevant, progressively complex experience with a proven track record of leading successful implementation and rollout of key technologies and processes

Experience with Ironclad or similar contract lifecycle management platforms

Experience with API integration

Experience leveraging AI-powered tools, automation platforms, or emerging legal technologies to optimize legal workflows and improve operational efficiency

Experience supporting legal operations across multiple business units, subsidiaries, or shared-services environments

Ability to collect and analyze available information and execute effectively and autonomously in complex or ambiguous situations

Preference for candidates with direct experience implementing and/or managing Ironclad or Brightflag

Nice to have: Clear understanding of contracts and how they are used, including their abbreviated names, and can speak to them without reference

About Scopely

Scopely is a leading video game and global interactive entertainment company, home to many of the world’s most beloved and enduring experiences, including two of the most successful mobile games of all-time “MONOPOLY GO!” and “Pokémon GO,” along with “Stumble Guys,” “Star Trek™ Fleet Command,” “MARVEL Strike Force,” “WWE Champions,” the Scrabble® franchise, “Yahtzee® With Buddies,” and many others. Across mobile, web, PC, and console, Scopely creates, develops, publishes, and live-operates one of the most diversified and award-winning portfolios in the games industry — bringing hundreds of millions of players together through a shared love of play.

Founded in 2011, Scopely is powered by its exceptional team — including thousands of world-class gamemakers around the globe, a distinctive tenet-driven culture, and its proprietary technology platform, Playgami. Together, these strengths have fueled Scopely’s position as the #1 mobile games company in the U.S. and #2 globally, generating more than $10 billion in lifetime revenue. Whether building global sensations like “MONOPOLY GO!” from the ground up, or expanding through strategic acquisitions, including the FoxNext, GSN, and Scopely Explore games businesses — Scopely consistently delivers experiences players love today and return to for years to come.

Recognized multiple times as one of the "100 Most Influential Companies in the World" by TIME magazine and one of Fast Company s "World s Most Innovative Companies" and “Best Workplaces for Innovators,” Scopely believes that video games can be a force for good — creating meaningful connections, vibrant communities, and making life better through play.

Scopely has global operations and partners across four continents in more than a dozen countries worldwide. For more information, visit: https://www.scopely.com/ .
