QA Lead — Subway Surfers SYBO is part of the Miniclip family and known for the widely popular game, Subway Surfers - the most downloaded mobile game in the world, with over four and a half billion downloads. Located in the heart of Copenhagen , we aim to create playful moments that inspire our fans to care.

Running one of the biggest LiveOps games in the world, managing millions and millions of daily players and keeping pole position as most downloaded game on the planet, requires quality - and now we’re looking for a QA Lead to help us deliver exactly that; quality .

As a QA Lead on Subway Surfers, you’ll lead and coach our internal QA team while staying close to the craft - even jumping in with manual testing if really needed. You will work closely with the entire production team on Subway Surfers, and report into our QA Coordinator who oversees all over games' QA.

You’ll shape the next chapter of QA at Subway Surfers: strengthening our in-house capability, exploring, implementing and maintaining great automation, and just as importantly: Drive how AI-assisted tools can improve how we work.

If you love ownership, developing people and your craft and finding bugs - this is it! Responsibilities Planning, prioritizing and improving QA processes and tasks Follow up closely on QA progress, ensuring that QA & team sprint goals are met Train, lead and coach the internal QA team Helping the product team by prioritizing bugs and grooming bug backlog Support roadmap alignment and planning, taking in consideration QA risks and capacity Review and support with maintenance of test cases and test design Reporting QA status to leads, identify risks and flag any issues, while suggesting possible solutions Collaborate with external partners to ensure quality standards are met, while supporting the transition to an enhanced in-house capability through exploring automation and AI Life at SYBO In your first month , you’ll get to know SYBO, Subway Surfers, and how the team works. You’ll work closely with the QA director, QA team and other leads, to familiarize yourself with the processes as a whole and QA processes in particular.

In your first three months , you’ll be contributing independently to the planning, prioritizing and improving of QA processes, as well as getting involved in your team's development focus and goals.

By your first twelve months , you'll play an integral role in helping the team release more quickly, safely and with confidence. This is an exciting opportunity to drive automation, shift-left testing, quality standards and engineering excellence while building an in-house capability that supports our AI-first strategy and modern product delivery approach.

Requirements Great interest in mobile games.

Experience leading and coaching a QA team, while also still enjoying being hands-on with testing Experience collaborating with multiple disciplines to deliver high quality game features Experience working in Agile methodologies Exposure to AI-assisted testing tools and an understanding of their practical benefits and limitations.

Bonus points Experience working in Unity Experience introducing test automation into a predominantly manual testing environment.

Experience integrating automated tests into CI/CD pipelines.

About SYBO We're SYBO, the mobile games studio behind the legendary Subway Surfers, with over 4.5 billion downloads worldwide! Join our team in the heart of Copenhagen, where creativity flourishes in our new, state-of-the-art studio.

If you join us, you'll have the opportunity to dive into exciting and creative projects alongside a diverse group of like-minded colleagues from over 40 countries across the globe.

We truly believe you perform your best, when you have the equipment you need - and our IT team is always on hand to ensure you have access to the latest tools and gear, enabling you to be at your best - even remotely with a home-office budget! We also pride ourselves in taking care of your well-being, offering a delicious lunch buffet, a variety of healthy snacks, and regular social events to keep the energy flowing.

Not in Copenhagen? Not a problem! We have in-house relocation specialists who will help you relocate, and feel right at home in Copenhagen and at SYBO!
