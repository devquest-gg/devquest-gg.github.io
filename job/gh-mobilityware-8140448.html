Data Analyst II

MobilityWare · Irvine

Mid Data & Analytics $105K–$130K Posted 2026-08-26 Full time
Apply on MobilityWare’s site →

We’re looking for a strategic, curious, and highly analytical Data Analyst II to join our Growth team and support MobilityWare’s portfolio of mobile games. In this role, your primary focus will be partnering closely with teams across User Acquisition (UA), Creative, Product, and Finance to help drive smart, data-informed growth decisions.

This role is specifically focused on User Acquisition and Growth Analytics within mobile gaming . We’re primarily seeking candidates with mobile gaming experience and a strong interest in UA and growth marketing.

This is much more than a reporting role. We’re looking for someone who enjoys digging into complex data, identifying meaningful trends and opportunities, and translating insights into clear recommendations that influence strategy. The ideal candidate is both analytical and collaborative, someone who can think critically about performance, communicate insights clearly, and build strong partnerships across teams.

You’ll play an important role in shaping acquisition strategy across channels such as Google Ads, Apple Search Ads, Meta, and other ad networks. From campaign performance and creative testing to monetization trends and growth forecasting, your insights will help guide decisions across multiple game titles.

Responsibilities

Partner closely with the User Acquisition team across 2–4 mobile game titles to drive growth through thoughtful, data-informed decision-making

Analyze UA performance data to identify trends, opportunities, risks, and key drivers impacting growth and efficiency

Deliver clear, actionable insights and recommendations around campaign optimization, audience targeting, bidding strategies, creative performance, and budget allocation

Evaluate key performance metrics including ROAS, CPI, retention, LTV, payback periods, and install growth

Design and analyze A/B tests across creative concepts, acquisition channels, targeting strategies, and app store optimization efforts

Collaborate with Creative teams to understand ad performance, funnel conversion, and opportunities for iteration and experimentation

Work cross-functionally with Product, Finance, and Data teams to connect acquisition performance with broader business goals

Build and improve dashboards, reporting frameworks, and analytical tools that help teams make faster, more informed decisions

Conduct competitive and market analysis to identify emerging trends and growth opportunities

Present findings and recommendations clearly and effectively to both technical and non-technical stakeholders

Bring structure, curiosity, and strong analytical thinking to open-ended and ambiguous business questions

Qualifications

3–5 years of data analytics experience, with direct experience in the mobile gaming industry

Strong understanding of and interest in mobile user acquisition and performance marketing

Experience supporting products with hybrid IAP + IAA monetization models is strongly preferred

Advanced proficiency in Excel or Google Sheets

Strong SQL experience preferred

Experience with analytics and visualization tools such as Tableau and Looker

Experience using Python for data analysis is a plus

Strong problem-solving skills, with the ability to explain not only the answer but also the thinking and analysis behind it

Excellent communication and presentation skills, with the ability to tailor insights to different audiences

Highly organized and detail-oriented, with the ability to balance multiple priorities in a fast-paced environment

Passion for mobile games, experimentation, player behavior, and continuous learning

Collaborative mindset with a proactive and positive approach to problem-solving
