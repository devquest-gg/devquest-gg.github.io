Senior Cinematic Animator

Ubisoft Toronto · Toronto

Senior Animation Posted 2026-10-07 Full time
Apply on Ubisoft Toronto’s site →

As a Senior Cinematic Animator, you are a key element in delivering an amazing story. You will be responsible for delivering high quality animations, either from enhancing motion capture or key-framing, for our cinematic and scripted event scenes.

What you'll do: Work closely with the Cinematic and Animation Directors, animators, scripters, tech team and other departments to create our Cinematics and scripted events; Work in coordination with animation lead to help mentor and build a collaboration within the team Enhance and polish character performances (body and facial) from video and motion capture; Must have an excellent understanding of the animation principles (posing, timing, balance and weight) to create realistic and believable key-frame animations; Construct previs from story scripts and/or storyboards, and have the ability to create scene layouts, staging, camera and editing; Communicate with the Cinematic and Animation Directors for reviews and feedback; Work closely with scripters and the animation TDs to ensure the quality of the integration of the animations; Detect inconsistencies or bugs and validate that the cinematic scene plays correctly.

Strong experience creating&#xa0;cinematic animations&#xa0;for video games; Excellent grasp for storytelling, acting and cinematography; Understanding of game cinematic&#xa0;production pipelines and workflows; Relevant 2D and 3D software experience - MotionBuilder, Maya, 3DSMax, Photoshop, Premiere, Final Cut; Good collaboration with members of the animation team and other team members on the project; Must be open to direction, criticism and able to accept change; Extensive animation experience with hand-keyed disciplines, experience with motion capture a plus; Attention to detail and experience solving technical&#xa0;problems.

What to send our way A&#xa0; portfolio &#xa0;showcasing your best game cinematic animation work.

Your&#xa0; CV &#xa0;highlighting your experience and skills.
