Mission Design Lead (Campaign)

Absurd Ventures · Santa Monica

Lead Design $160K–$190K Posted 2026-03-18 Full time

Summary

As Mission Design Lead (Campaign), you will be the primary vision holder and a hands-on contributor for story missions and single-player campaigns. Lead a multi-disciplinary team to deliver critical path and side missions that combine best-in-class storytelling and action gameplay in an open world setting.

Core Responsibilities

Drive the design and implementation of engaging, diverse, innovative missions within a story-driven, open-world game.

Work closely with the narrative team to develop the flow of main story missions.

Develop and execute the mission design vision and strategy, ensuring that missions align with the game s overall narrative, thematic, and player experience goals.

Continuously seek opportunities to innovate and push the boundaries of mission design.

Leverage the game s open-world systems and mechanics to create dynamic and unpredictable mission experiences that adapt to player choices and actions.

Lead and mentor a team of mission designers, providing direction, feedback and support throughout the development process.

Go beyond documentation and management to lead from the trenches, with hands-on contributions to the playable experience.

Foster a culture of collaboration, innovation and iteration.

Core Qualifications

5 or more years of experience in mission design, ideally within an open-world game.

Excellent communication, interpersonal, and leadership skills.

Innovative thinker with creative problem-solving skills who thrives in a collaborative team environment.

Strong understanding of storytelling and narrative design.

Plus If...

Experience leading multidisciplinary teams

Experience in UE4 or UE5

Experience working with co-development partners

Description

Full time job with benefits
