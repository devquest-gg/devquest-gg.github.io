Nex is on a mission to help families rediscover the joy of movement. Created by parents for parents, Nex combines technology and play to deliver fun, social, and interactive experiences powered by natural body motion, encouraging kids and adults to move more, play more, and have fun together. Nex Playground, the company’s award-winning active play system, is purpose-built to get families moving year-round, with safety and privacy as core considerations in its intentional design. It is certified kidSAFE+ COPPA compliant and built to support healthy, active play for all ages and abilities.

Nex Playground features a growing library of 50+ experiences, including motion and dance games, fitness and educational experiences, and Nex Originals. Content includes collaborations with partners like Hasbro, Sesame Workshop, and NBCUniversal. Nex has been recognized by Fast Company’s Most Innovative Companies, TIME’s Best Inventions, and Parents’ Best Entertainment System for Families, and has earned Red Dot, IDEA, and Core77 international design awards. We encourage you to explore Have Fun and Is Motion Gaming Back? , as they offer a deeper look into our culture, values, and explain how our approach to motion gaming differs from previous generations.

About this Role

We are seeking a community-driven and highly organized Senior Community Manager to join our growing Brand Values team.

At Nex, our community isn t just a support channel, but it s one of our most valuable sources of insight, advocacy, and storytelling. This role builds relationships with families, helps them connect with one another, and ensures their voices directly influence our products, content, and marketing.

You will work across our Facebook community, Reddit, and Circle to help ensure that family voices remain at the center of everything we do.

This is an excellent opportunity for someone who loves building relationships, uncovering customer insights, and turning community conversations into action.

Success in this role is measured not only by community engagement and health, but by the quality of insights, advocacy, and customer stories surfaced from the community.

Core Responsibilities

Community Health, Engagement Operations

Own day-to-day community engagement and member experience across Nex community channels.

Publish community content, prompts, announcements, and product education.

Support and provide direction to moderators.

Maintain moderation workflows, escalation paths, and community guidelines.

Identify emerging questions, concerns, trends, and opportunities.

Customer Advocacy Storytelling

Build relationships with engaged families, advocates, beta testers, and super users.

Identify compelling customer stories, testimonials, and use cases.

Conduct interviews and surface stories for Brand, Content, PR, and Marketing.

Help create pathways for customers to become advocates, testers, ambassadors, or contributors.

Community Insights Reporting

Identify recurring themes, needs, sentiment, and customer feedback across community channels.

Gather insights through discussions, surveys, interviews, and research initiatives.

Produce recurring Voice of Community reports with actionable recommendations.

Partner with Product, Marketing, Support, and Research to ensure customer feedback reaches the right teams.

Preferred Qualifications

Familiarity with community analytics, survey tools, and customer feedback platforms.

Experience working with family, gaming, education, consumer technology, or lifestyle brands.

Experience with ambassador, advocate, or champion programs.

Experience supporting customer research initiatives.

Experience creating customer stories, case studies, interviews, or editorial content.

Requirements

5+ years managing online communities or customer advocacy programs.

Excellent written communication and relationship-building skills.

Experience managing Facebook Groups, Reddit, Circle, or similar community platforms.

Strong organizational and project management skills.

Ability to analyze qualitative feedback and identify actionable insights.

Comfortable working cross-functionally across Product, Marketing, Customer Support, and Research.

Experience managing multiple projects simultaneously in a fast-paced environment.
