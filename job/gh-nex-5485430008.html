The Role

Nex is looking for a Senior Manager, FP A, Game Development Publishing to serve as the finance partner to our content and subscription businesses. This role sits at the intersection of two of the company’s most important growth drivers: the games that bring families to Nex Playground and keep them playing, and Play Pass, the subscription service that turns that engagement into recurring revenue. The ideal candidate combines strong modeling and analytical skills with the curiosity and business judgment to influence decisions made by game development, publishing, product, and marketing leaders.

This is a hands-on, high-visibility role with regular exposure to finance leadership and executive stakeholders. The role will own the financial plan for game development and Play Pass, build the models and reporting that guide content investment, and translate data into decisions that grow subscribers, engagement, and recurring revenue.

What You’ll Do

Game development investment and portfolio planning

You will be the finance partner to our game development and publishing teams, building the budgets, forecasts, and financial models behind our content roadmap. This includes tracking development spend by title and team (internal studios and external partners), modeling project-level economics and ROI, and helping leadership prioritize the portfolio based on expected engagement, retention, and return. You’ll partner with producers to monitor budget-to-actual performance across milestones and flag risks early.

Play Pass subscription performance

You will own the financial analysis and forecasting for Play Pass, our subscription service, across the full subscriber lifecycle: acquisition, trial-to-paid conversion, renewal, churn, and reactivation. You’ll build cohort-based forecasts, maintain the subscription revenue model, track ARPU and LTV, and translate subscriber behavior into recommendations on pricing, bundling, promotions, and retention initiatives.

Content engagement and monetization analytics

You will connect game-level engagement data to subscription outcomes, helping the business understand which titles and content drive conversion, retention, and reactivation. You’ll partner with product, data, and marketing teams to define KPIs, build dashboards, and quantify the impact of content releases, pricing tests, and lifecycle campaigns on Play Pass revenue.

Licensing, partnerships, and publishing economics

You will evaluate the economics of licensed and third-party content, including IP partnerships, co-development deals, minimum guarantees, royalty structures, and revenue shares. You’ll build the deal models used to evaluate and approve new partnerships, and track royalty obligations and partner performance once titles launch.

Planning, reporting, and business partnership

You will support the annual operating plan and monthly/quarterly forecasts for the game development and Play Pass businesses, and prepare variance analyses, business reviews, and executive-ready materials. You will be expected to bring an independent, data-driven point of view to the table — not just report the numbers, but help shape the decisions behind them.

What You Bring

7–10 years of experience in FP A, strategic finance, investment banking, consulting, or a related field, with meaningful exposure to video games, digital media/entertainment, or a subscription business

Demonstrated experience partnering with product, content, or creative teams on financial decision-making — not just reporting results after the fact

Strong financial modeling skills, including cohort analysis, project-level P Ls, and budget-to-actual tracking

Hands-on experience with subscription metrics (conversion, churn, retention, ARPU, LTV) and subscription revenue forecasting

Familiarity with game development budgeting, publishing economics, or licensing/royalty structures is a strong plus

Comfort working with large engagement or product datasets; SQL experience is a plus

Ability to manage multiple priorities and operate independently in a fast-paced, evolving environment

Excellent communication skills, with experience presenting to finance leadership and executive audiences

Advanced Excel/financial modeling required; experience with BI tools (e.g., Tableau, Looker) and planning systems (e.g., Abacum, Anaplan, Adaptive) preferred

Bachelor s degree in Finance, Economics, Accounting, or related field; MBA or equivalent experience a plus
