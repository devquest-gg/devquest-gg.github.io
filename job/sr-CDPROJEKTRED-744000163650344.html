Brand Creative Expert

CD Projekt Red · Warsaw

Senior Marketing Posted 2026-11-25 Full time
Apply on CD Projekt Red’s site →

We're looking for a Brand Creative Expert to help shape and evolve the creative direction and brand expression of one of our IPs. This is a multidisciplinary role at the intersection of brand strategy, marketing, storytelling, art, design, and game development.

In this role, you'll connect the creative vision and identity of the game with how it is presented and experienced by audiences. You'll translate the game's unique qualities, creative ambitions, and audience insights into distinctive brand strategies, original concepts, and compelling marketing ideas. Combining strategic thinking with hands-on creative experience, you'll work closely with teams across Brand, Marketing, Franchise, and Game Development, as well as external creative partners, to ensure that our creative work authentically represents the game and resonates with players worldwide.

Daily Responsibilities Develop and contribute to the brand strategy and creative direction of the assigned IP, translating its creative vision, unique qualities, and business objectives into a distinctive and consistent brand identity.

Conceptualize and develop original creative ideas for marketing campaigns, brand experiences, content, and communications, providing creative direction for key initiatives and major promotional assets.

Develop compelling narratives, campaign concepts, messaging frameworks, scripts, and presentations, translating complex game worlds and ideas into engaging communications that reflect the identity of the IP.

Work closely with Game Development teams to understand the game's creative vision, world-building, gameplay, and defining features, ensuring that marketing accurately represents the player experience while highlighting what makes the game unique.

Guide the development of visual and creative concepts, collaborating with artists, designers, writers, motion teams, and external partners to ensure a high standard of craft and consistency across creative executions.

Bring together Creative, Marketing, Franchise, Production, and Game Development stakeholders, building alignment around creative direction and helping balance artistic ambition, audience expectations, and business objectives.

Act as a creative subject matter expert for the assigned IP, providing constructive feedback, sharing knowledge, and supporting the development of other creatives through collaboration and mentoring.

At least 7 years of experience in brand building and creative development within the gaming, film, TV, animation, entertainment, or related creative industries.

Experience working within AAA game development.

Hands-on experience in at least one creative discipline, such as graphic design, illustration, motion design, video production, writing, or creative technology.

Experience developing or launching major entertainment properties, franchises, or original IPs, including contributing to integrated marketing campaigns.

Significant expertise in brand strategy, creative direction, advertising, marketing, entertainment, or related creative disciplines.

Proven ability to develop original creative concepts, connecting strategic thinking with creative execution.

Exceptional storytelling, creative writing, and verbal communication skills, with the ability to present and gain support for creative ideas among senior stakeholders.

Strong visual literacy and understanding of art direction, graphic design, visual communication, and creative production.

Deep familiarity with video game development processes, including game design, gameplay mechanics, narrative, world-building, and player experience.

Ability to work effectively with multidisciplinary teams, influence stakeholders across different disciplines, and provide constructive, actionable feedback.

Strong creative judgment, strategic thinking, and intellectual curiosity, with the ability to navigate ambiguity and balance artistic integrity with audience and business needs.

Genuine passion for video games as an artistic medium, form of entertainment, and cultural force.

Nice to Have Familiarity with game engines, development tools, or interactive prototyping.
