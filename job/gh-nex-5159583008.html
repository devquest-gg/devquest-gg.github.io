Nex is on a mission to help families rediscover the joy of movement. Created by parents for parents, Nex combines technology and play to deliver fun, social, and interactive experiences powered by natural body motion, encouraging kids and adults to move more, play more, and have fun together. Nex Playground, the company’s award-winning active play system, is purpose-built to get families moving year-round, with safety and privacy as core considerations in its intentional design. It is certified kidSAFE+ COPPA compliant and built to support healthy, active play for all ages and abilities.

Nex Playground features a growing library of 50+ experiences, including motion and dance games, fitness and educational experiences, and Nex Originals. Content includes collaborations with partners like Hasbro, Sesame Workshop, and NBCUniversal. Nex has been recognized by Fast Company’s Most Innovative Companies, TIME’s Best Inventions, and Parents’ Best Entertainment System for Families, and has earned Red Dot, IDEA, and Core77 international design awards. We encourage you to explore Have Fun and Is Motion Gaming Back? , as they offer a deeper look into our culture, values, and explain how our approach to motion gaming differs from previous generations.

Nex employees may be expected to collaborate with colleagues across different time zones. Depending on the role and location, occasional travel may also be required for company events, external meetings, and other business-related activities.

About the Role

Nex is looking for an IT Director to own the strategy, delivery, and governance of our business systems and technology operations as we scale Nex Playground globally. This is a senior leadership role with broad scope — spanning enterprise systems, business systems integration and vendor relationships — and direct influence on how Nex operates and grows.

Key Responsibilities

Corporate Technology Stack Oversight

Lead the roadmap and execution of Nex’s corporate IT and business systems.

Oversee the administration, maintenance, and reliability of the full corporate tech stack, ensuring systems are well-integrated, performant, and scalable.

Own the company’s non-coding artificial intelligence use and access policy.

Corporate IT Procurement Vendor Strategy

Own all corporate IT and business system vendor relationships, including contract negotiation, performance accountability, and portfolio optimisation.

Lead corporate IT system procurement — evaluating, selecting, and onboarding tools and platforms that meet Nex’s operational and security requirements.

Partner on solution architecture and integration decisions with a focus on scalability and cost discipline.

Systems Implementation Interoperability

Serve as the internal consultancy lead for IT system rollouts, implementations, and integrations across business functions.

Partner with respective internal system owners on integration and architectural decisions for payments, taxation, order orchestration, and global commerce workflows.

Ensure business systems can communicate effectively with one another, maintaining data integrity and operational continuity across platforms.

IT Operations Access Provisioning

Own end-to-end corporate IT system maintenance and access provisioning, including identity and access management (IAM), user onboarding/offboarding, role-based access controls, and periodic access reviews.

Own the full employee technology lifecycle, including device procurement, provisioning, asset management, refresh cycles, and secure device retirement.

Oversee enterprise productivity and collaboration platforms (eg Google Workspace, Slack, Notion, Zoom, Microsoft 365) and ensure reliable employee access and support.

Partner with People Operations to deliver a seamless and secure new-hire experience across global locations.

Manage IT Operations Specialist to support and execute the above responsibilities.

Corporate Data Security Employee Training

Own the implementation of Nex’s corporate data security posture — including identity management, endpoint management, device security, MDM, anti-virus, firewall, VPN, backup infrastructure, and corporate SaaS and cloud systems security.

Support audit and due diligence readiness for corporate IT systems and implement compliance initiatives across corporate IT systems.

Deliver employee security training programs, including anti-phishing, access hygiene, and applicable statutory requirements.

Set the direction for Nex’s corporate IT infrastructure for internal use in partnership with internal teams and vendors, ensuring data integrity and appropriate access controls.

Team Organisational Leadership

Build and lead a high-performing team of IT professionals.

Serve as a thought partner to the executive team on technology investments, build vs. buy decisions, and organisational capability building.

Qualifications

What you must have

10+ years of progressive experience in IT, Business Systems, or Technical Program Management, with at least 3 years in a senior IT leadership role, ideally as the most senior IT leader at a company of roughly 100–500 people

Demonstrated success owning enterprise-wide technology strategy and delivery in a fast-scaling company

Track record of building and managing high-performing teams and external vendor relationships

Strong executive presence and communication skills across technical and non-technical audiences

Experience leading corporate IT operations, including endpoint management, IAM, employee onboarding/offboarding, and IT asset lifecycle management

Deep understanding of enterprise security controls, device management, access governance, and compliance best practices

Experience administering and securing productivity and collaboration platforms such as Google Workspace, Slack, Notion, Zoom and Microsoft 365

Preferred Qualifications

Hands-on experience with NetSuite, Shopify, Amazon ecosystems, and data/reporting platforms

Deep expertise across e-commerce and D2C technology ecosystems

Background in high-growth, hardware-enabled, or consumer tech environments

Experience managing global, multi-region systems and distributed teams

What Success Looks Like

A reliable, scalable technology foundation that supports Nex’s global growth without friction

A vendor portfolio that is well-governed, cost-efficient, and accountable

A team that is trusted, capable, and operating with increasing independence
