Remote - US & Canada

thatgamecompany is looking for an experienced Senior Software Engineer to support our flagship title Sky: Children of the Light, by developing applications to facilitate game operation functions. Our internally-developed applications are a fundamental capability enabling our company to live-operate Sky: Children of Light with millions of active users generating terabytes of data per day.

As a Senior Software Engineer, you will report to the Lead Software Engineer, developing software for applications that support internal and external customers.

KEY RESPONSIBILITIES

Software Development

- Identify issues and create solutions independently, understanding how to complete a task from start to finish

- Participate in brainstorming sessions to design and document features with scalability in mind

- Build backend API and frontend components and pages with strong knowledge of Node JS, Next JS, and SQL

- Meet with individual team members and the full ITS team weekly to discuss progress and ideas

- Interact with different departments of the company to discover and address problems and needs

- Contribute to thatgamecompany’s unique culture and vision

KEY QUALIFICATIONS

PROFESSIONAL EXPERIENCE

- 4+ years of professional programming experience

- Experience building modern REST APIs in Node.js

- Experience with writing Typescript packages

- Experience using modern front-end Javascript frameworks (e.g., React, Next, Hooks)

- Experience designing data structures and querying relational and non-relational database technologies such as Postgresql, Redis, MongoDB, etc.

- Experience with Git version control system

- Experience with cloud service providers such as AWS and Google Cloud

PROBLEM-SOLVING

- Strong analytical and problem-solving skills, with a proactive and adaptable approach to dynamic challenges

- Ability to thrive in a highly collaborative environment involving cross-functional and multi-disciplinary teams

- Strong initiative to find ways to improve solutions, systems, and processes

BONUS POINTS

- Experience developing with Python and Go

- Experience with Google Cloud Project, Datadog, Prometheus, Serverless, CI/CD, Gitlab, Kubernetes, Terraform

- Experience with Sky: Children of the Light and its community

- Have a personal portfolio to share with us.
