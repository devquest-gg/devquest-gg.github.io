As the Studio Director for our Montreal Co-Development Team, you will lead and manage an internal creative studio supporting multiple video game studios within our organization. You will be responsible for fostering a collaborative culture, building great partnerships with other studios and achieving operational excellence across various game development projects. This role requires a dynamic, strategic, and innovative leader who can build and guide teams, manage resources effectively, and maintain alignment with organizational objectives.

What you ll do

Develop and execute a clear strategic vision for the Co-Development Studio aligned with overall organizational goals.

Drive effective cross-studio collaboration, ensuring alignment on project timelines, resource allocation, and build an outstanding studio from the ground up.

Oversee daily operations, budgeting, resource allocation, and project scheduling.

Ensure projects are delivered on time, within budget, and meet high-quality standards.

Continually optimize internal processes for efficiency and effectiveness.

Build, mentor, and develop a high performing studio leadership group.

Foster a culture of innovation, accountability, collaboration, and continuous improvement.

Conduct regular performance reviews, provide feedback, and encourage professional growth.

Set the culture for the studio and build a great working environment for all.

Maintain strong partnerships with internal and external stakeholders, effectively communicating studio capabilities, progress, and challenges.

Act as a liaison between the Co-Development team and supported video game studios, facilitating productive dialogue and cooperation.

Manage the studio’s profit and loss (P L), ensuring fiscal responsibility, profitability, and growth.

Collaborate with finance and operations teams to accurately forecast, budget, and report financial performance.

What you ll bring

Minimum 10 years of experience in the video game industry with at least 10years in a senior leadership role.

Proven track record in studio/functional team management, team leadership, and budgeting.

Exceptional strategic, operational, and financial acumen.

Deep understanding of video game development processes.

Excellent interpersonal and communication skills; ability to build and maintain effective relationships across diverse teams.

Demonstrated experience in fostering innovative and collaborative cultures.

Inspirational leader with strong strategic vision

Collaborative, approachable, and a trusted mentor

Strong analytical, organizational, and problem-solving skills.

Resilient and able to navigate change and uncertainty effectively.
