Principal VFX Artist

Our team in Montreal is growing and we are looking for a Principal VFX Artist to help us lay the groundwork for our stylized original project in UE5 and lead a team of great VFX Artists creating worlds together!

You will work closely towards the established Art Direction, manage and task the VFX Team. If you have a great collaborative spirit, like to get involved and look for an exceptional opportunity to leave your mark on a completely new IP, come join us!

What will you be doing:

Serve as liaison for all things VFX (Environment, Gameplay, etc.)

Plan, organize and task work for the team; including using software such as JIRA to track the progress and dependencies

Supervise and scope the VFX assets, prepare them for the team and outsourcing to work on.

Work closely with the Directors to assist in maintaining Art Direction throughout the project

Collaborate with all other departments in our cross-discipline approach to ensure we maintain the highest visual quality, optimization and the best pipeline throughout production

Assist in setting up the outsourcing pipeline for the VFX Assets

Be a role model with the drive to help others succeed with a positive attitude inspire, mentor, and manage the VFX Team

Document and share your knowledge often and freely with other members of the team

Proactively assist with the recruitment for the department

Support the creation of assets as a lower priority task

The skills we’re looking for:

1+ shipped AAA title as a VFX Artist

6+ years of experience as VFX Artist or equivalent with a AAA game engine

Knowledge of industry standard tools and workflows, such as 3ds Max/Maya, Photoshop, Embergen, Houdini.

Extensive Portfolio that highlights your skills and passion for the Stylized VFX Assets

Openness to try new things and explore different styles.

Experience in full game development cycle (Pre-Production to Shipped)

Entrepreneurial spirit that motivates you to move the needle forward in a fresh studio and the drive to do things better.

Unreal Engine experience in Game Development is a plus

JIRA or other task tracking software experience is a plus

Why could we be the perfect place for you:

We are Highdive, a NetEase Games ThunderFire development studio from Montreal. Bond by no borders, we create games that let us dive into pure emotions, together.

We also offer:

- Insurance coverage and telemedicine from day one,

- Transportation and meal allowance,

- Referral bonus,

- RRSP with employer contribution.

Join us to work on an ambitious project, in a unique ‘Start-Up Atmosphere’ and with a team of passionate and energetic game developers!

NetEase is committed to supporting a French-speaking workenvironment for its employees in Quebec. Every effort has been made to avoidrequiring a language other than French for this position.
