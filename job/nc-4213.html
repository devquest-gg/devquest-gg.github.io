Game Designer

Nordcurrent · Vilnius

Mid Design Posted 2025-10-20 Full time
Apply on Nordcurrent’s site →

As a member of the Development Team, you will create new game concepts and levels for world-class titles such as Cooking Fever, Pocket Styler, Airplane Chefs, and new games currently in development. Your work will impact the experience of millions of players worldwide.

Every day, you will think creatively, learn new things, test original ideas, apply your knowledge of different game genres, collaborate with a talented team, and grow professionally. Your friends will be proud of you, knowing that you were the hero behind those games!

A Successful Candidate Must Have:

Knowledge of game structure and planning;

Understanding of game processes, balance, and mechanics;

Ability to create levels that are exciting and engaging;

Solid technical writing skills, with the ability to produce clear and precise game design documents;

Ability to describe game structures and rules clearly through documentation and presentations;

Understanding of game development tools and technologies;

Good written and spoken English skills;

A keen interest in casual games.

Extra Great to Have:

Basic knowledge of programming and/or art;

Experience with iOS and Android platforms;

Good knowledge of the Unity game engine;

Passion for the video game industry, including awareness of market trends.

Salary:  From 2120 EUR/month (gross), based on experience and competence.
