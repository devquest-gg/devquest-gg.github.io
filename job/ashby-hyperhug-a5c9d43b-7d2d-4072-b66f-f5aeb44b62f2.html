Senior UI/UX Designer

HyperHug · Remote · Remote

Senior Design Remote Posted 2026-07-31 Full time
Apply on HyperHug’s site →

HyperHug is an international game development studio specializing in the shooter genre. Our main office is based in Cyprus, but our talented team works remotely from around the world. We’re developing Oxide: Survival Island, an immersive multiplayer survival shooter for mobile platforms, and we’re always looking for motivated individuals who want to grow with us, contribute their creativity, and help shape the future of our games.

As a Senior Game UI/UX Designer, you will design and implement complex interfaces for the core and meta systems of Oxide: Survival Island, our multiplayer mobile survival game.

This is a hands-on role for someone who can take a feature from an initial game design document to a polished, functional interface in Unity. You will work on feature UI, monetization systems, and scalable design solutions, combining strong visual design with technical implementation.

WHAT YOU’LL BE DOING

- Design complete UI/UX solutions for new game features—from user flows and wireframes to polished, production-ready layouts.

- Work on interfaces for core and meta systems, ensuring a consistent player experience across the game.

- Design monetization features such as battle passes, gacha mechanics, limited-time offers, reward flows, and other live-ops systems.

- Build and maintain a scalable design system, reusable components, templates, and UI guidelines.

- Implement and assemble interfaces directly in Unity, including layouts, states, transitions, animations, effects, and responsive behaviour.

- Connect UI elements to existing events, calls, and game data with the support and review of developers.

- Use AI-assisted development and vibe-coding tools to prototype and implement UI functionality independently.

- Translate game design documentation into clear, intuitive, and visually polished interfaces.

- Collaborate closely with game designers, artists, Unity developers, and other team members throughout implementation.

- Review interfaces in the game, identify inconsistencies, and continuously improve usability and visual quality.

WHAT WE EXPECT FROM YOU

- 5+ years of professional UI/UX design experience in game development.

- A strong portfolio featuring complex game interfaces—not only individual screens or visual concepts.

- Experience with mid-core or hardcore mobile games, or relevant PC/console projects will be a plus.

- Strong hands-on experience with Unity and its UI systems.

- Experience independently assembling and configuring interfaces in Unity.

- Understanding of how UI interacts with game events, data, states, animations, and visual effects.

- Experience using AI coding tools to prototype or implement UI functionality.

- Advanced proficiency in Figma.

- Strong understanding of game UX, information hierarchy, player behaviour, and interface flows.

- Experience designing core, meta, and monetization systems.

- Ability to create visually expressive interfaces while respecting technical constraints.

- Ability to communicate design decisions clearly and work independently with limited developer support.

WHAT WE OFFER:

- Competitive salary.

- Relocation support to Cyprus, including visa and housing assistance.

- Monthly lunch allowances via Wolt.

- Comprehensive medical insurance.

- Car rental.

- A passionate and supportive team that values creativity and ownership.

- Opportunities for professional growth.

- A culture built on initiative, transparency, and collaboration.
