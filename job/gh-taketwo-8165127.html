The Challenge

Take-Two Interactive, Inc., a leader in the interactive entertainment industry, is looking for a talented Business Intelligence Developer to join a team responsible for transforming complex data into clear, actionable insights that drive decision-making across the organization.

The ideal candidate has a solid foundation in SQL-based data analysis, has experience with Tableau Server/Online Administration. If you are self-starter who thrives in a collaborative, fast-paced environment, can communicate data-driven insights clearly to both technical and non-technical stakeholders, have a strong eye for design of dashboards and data sources and are excited by the opportunity to work at the intersection of data, design, and emerging AI/NLP technologies, this is the opportunity for you.

What You’ll Take On

• Maintain and administer Tableau Server as a platform.

• Build and optimize Tableau data sources, including live connections and scheduled extracts, ensuring high availability and rapid load performance. • Write, tune, and maintain SQL queries to extract, transform, and validate data from relational databases and cloud data warehouses. • Partner with business teams, product managers, and analysts to translate complex requirements into scalable BI solutions that support a mix of strategic, tactical and operational reporting to ensure intuitive and visually compelling user experiences. • Conduct exploratory data analysis to identify trends, anomalies, and opportunities that inform product and business decisions. • Define and enforce data visualization standards, naming conventions, and best practices to ensure consistency and scalability across all BI deliverables. • Evaluate and implement emerging AI and NLP tools, including Amazon Q, to enhance self-service analytics and natural language querying capabilities for business users. • Maintain clear documentation for dashboards, data sources, and analytical processes to support knowledge sharing, onboarding, and audit readiness.

What You Bring

• 4+ years of hands-on experience with building BI solutions preferably in Tableau.

• 2+ years of Tableau Server/Online administration experience, including user and permissions management, site configuration, workbook publishing, extract scheduling, and performance optimization of workbooks and data sources. • Solid SQL proficiency for data extraction, transformation, and analysis across relational databases and cloud data warehouses (e.g., Amazon Redshift, Snowflake). • Experience conducting data analysis tasks including data cleaning, blending, aggregation, and root-cause investigation of data quality issues. • Strong communication skills with the ability to present data-driven insights clearly to non-technical stakeholders and senior leadership. • Comfortable working cross-functionally with business partners, data developers, and product teams in an agile environment to gather requirements and deliver BI solutions iteratively. • Bachelor’s degree or equivalent in Computer Science, Statistics, Data Analytics, Business Analytics, Information Systems, or a related field.

• Tableau Desktop and Tableau Server/Online Administration — 2+ years (required) • SQL — intermediate to advanced (required) • Figma or equivalent design/prototyping tool (required)

Great to Have

• Amazon Q or other NLP/AI analytics tools • AWS data services — Redshift, S3, QuickSight • Snowflake or other cloud data warehouse platforms • Python for data analysis and automation • Power BI or Looker • Experience in the gaming or interactive entertainment industry
