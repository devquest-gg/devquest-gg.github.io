Senior Game Product Manager - Riftbound

Riot Games · Shanghai

Senior Production Posted 2026-11-17 Full time
Apply on Riot Games’s site →

Riot Games was established in 2006 by entrepreneurial gamers who believe that player-focused game development can result in great games. In 2009, Riot released its debut title League of Legends to critical and player acclaim. As the most played video game in the world, over 100 million play every month. Players form the foundation of our community and it’s for them that we continue to evolve and improve the League of Legends experience.

We’re looking for humble but ambitious, razor-sharp professionals who can teach us a thing or two. We promise to return the favor. Like us, you take play seriously; you’re passionate about games. We embrace those who see things differently, aren’t afraid to experiment, and who have a healthy disregard for constraints.

Riftbound is our new physical trading card game (TCG) where you battle to control locations with famous League champions! On our team, we’re looking for Product Designers at multiple levels to design experiences!

As a Senior Game Product Manager on the Riftbound team, you will help shape product strategy and drive initiatives that improve the player experience and support the long-term health of the game. You will partner with design, insights, and other interdisciplinary teams to understand player needs, identify opportunities, and prioritize investments that deliver meaningful player value. By combining player insights, data, and product judgment, you will help guide decisions, align partners around shared goals, and evaluate the impact of product initiatives. You will report into the Product Design Director of the team.

Responsibilities

Develop a deep understanding of player audiences, their needs, motivations, and behaviors to inform product strategy and priorities.

Use quantitative and qualitative insights to identify opportunities that improve player experiences and support the long-term health of the product.

Define product goals, success metrics, and strategic priorities in collaboration with interdisciplinary partners.

Evaluate product opportunities, assess tradeoffs, and recommend approaches that balance player value, product goals, and development considerations.

Partner with design, development, insights, and other teams to translate product opportunities into actionable initiatives.

Monitor product performance, evaluate outcomes, and use insights to inform continuous improvement.

Communicate product strategy, priorities, and recommendations clearly to stakeholders and cross-functional partners.

Contribute to the evolution of product management practices, decision-making frameworks, and collaborative processes across the team.

Required Qualifications

At least 5 years of professional experience in product management, game product management, or a related product-focused discipline.

Experience developing product strategies and translating player or customer insights into product priorities and decisions.

Demonstrated experience using qualitative and quantitative data to evaluate product performance and identify opportunities for improvement.

Experience partnering with interdisciplinary teams to develop, prioritize, and deliver product initiatives.

Experience defining product goals, evaluating tradeoffs, and measuring outcomes against established success metrics.

Desired Qualifications

Experience working on live games or products with ongoing player engagement.

Knowledge of collectible card games (CCGs) and their player experiences.

Experience working with competitive or multiplayer games.

Experience developing product strategies for products serving diverse player audiences.

Experience mentoring other product managers or contributing to product management practices.

For this role, you ll find success through craft expertise, a collaborative spirit, and decision-making that prioritizes the delight of players. We will be looking at your past studies, experience, and your personal relationship with games. If you embody player empathy and care about players experiences, this could be your role!
