Job Description

Senior Software Quality Engineer

Summary

Do you want to shape how quality is built into PlayStation® peripherals and firmware, not just verify it at the end? Join the team crafting thoughtful test strategies for PlayStation® Link™ devices. Your work will directly support product performance, stability, and the overall player experience!

We re growing the Quality Engineering team and looking for a senior engineer who enjoys owning test strategy for complex features, bringing clarity to unclear requirements, and helping the engineers around them grow.

This role combines hands-on testing with technical leadership. You ll lead planning and testing for major features, partner with developers and product teams to build in quality early, and mentor other QEs as they develop.

We value curiosity, continuous learning, and a thoughtful approach to quality at every stage.

Responsibilities

We test early, often, and across teams to help catch issues before they reach customers.

• Own test strategy and test plans for major features, including end-to-end flows, and turn unclear requirements into concrete coverage

• Lead test plan walkthroughs with developers, product managers, and other partners ahead of key release milestones

• Run and guide functional, regression, exploratory, and integration testing, and close coverage gaps across the product area

• Investigate complex failures using logs, traces, and system metrics, and lead triage and defect prioritization

• Mentor junior and mid-level QEs on test design, tooling, problem-solving, and quality practices

• Improve QE processes, tooling, and documentation, and share what you learn with nearby teams

Qualifications

We ll succeed by working together to learn from bugs and build test coverage with intention.

• Several years of experience in software quality engineering or a related testing role

• Comfortable owning test strategy and planning for complex features, including scope, release criteria, and test boundaries

• Experienced across functional, exploratory, regression, and integration testing, with good judgment about which approach fits a situation, plus some experience testing APIs

• Familiar with tools like JIRA, TestRail, or qTest, and open to picking up new tooling as needs change

• Able to explain problems, risks, and trade-offs clearly to engineers, product partners, and leadership

• Organized and detail-minded, with experience mentoring others in Agile development environments

Nice to Have

If you bring any of these, we d love to hear about them!

• Experience testing consumer electronics, especially peripherals or embedded firmware

• Familiarity with PlayStation® products

• Experience testing on Microsoft Windows platforms

• Passion for gaming

• Experience with telemetry tools, validating event tracking, or performance testing

• Familiarity with CI/CD pipelines and test automation frameworks
