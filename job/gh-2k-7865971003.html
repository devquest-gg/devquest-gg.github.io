What We Need

We are seeking a DevOps Engineer who can build, support, and improve infrastructure, CI/CD pipelines, and operational tooling that help teams deliver reliable services and better developer experience. This role focuses on hands-on engineering, automation, and day-to-day operational excellence in partnership with other engineers and teams.

What You’ll Do

Build and maintain CI/CD pipelines, cloud infrastructure, and deployment automation.

Support the reliability, scalability, and performance of development and production environments.

Improve developer workflows through automation, tooling, and documentation.

Contribute to observability efforts, including monitoring, alerting, dashboards, and incident response.

Partner with software engineers and platform teams to troubleshoot issues and improve delivery processes.

Participate in on-call rotations and help resolve operational incidents.

Help maintain infrastructure standards, security practices, and operational runbooks.

Support cost-conscious engineering through efficient infrastructure usage and tagging practices.

What Will Make You a Great Fit

4+ years of experience in DevOps, SRE, or Platform Engineering roles within an enterprise or multi-team environment.

Hands-on experience with production cloud infrastructure (AWS or GCP) and containerized/orchestration platforms (e.g., Kubernetes/EKS).

Experience with Infrastructure-as-Code (IaC) workflows (e.g., Terraform) and managing environment-specific configurations.

Experience building or maintaining CI/CD pipelines using platforms such as GitHub Actions or Jenkins.

Demonstrated operational experience, including participating in on-call rotations, managing production incidents, and using observability tools (e.g., Datadog, Splunk, Grafana).

Familiarity with managing work via formal ticketing systems (e.g., Jira, ServiceNow) and cross-functional coordination.

A focus on service reliability, documentation (runbooks), and operational excellence.

Strong troubleshooting and analytical skills, with a proven ability to identify root causes and work collaboratively across diverse engineering teams.

Clear and effective communication skills, particularly in conveying technical information, documenting processes, and adapting to fast-paced environments. Bonus Points

Experience supporting GitOps-based deployment workflows and tools such as ArgoCD.

Familiarity with cost optimization practices, including tagging strategies, rightsizing, and resource usage analysis.

Exposure to security-focused automation, such as secrets management, policy enforcement, or vulnerability/scanning workflows.

Experience writing scripts or lightweight tooling in languages such as Python, Bash, or Go.
