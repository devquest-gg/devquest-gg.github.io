The Challenge

We are looking for a Lead SOC Engineer to spearhead our integrated security engineering capabilities across Security Orchestration, Automation, and Response (SOAR) , Detection Engineering , and Threat Intelligence Engineering . In this pivotal technical leadership role, you will be responsible for designing, building, and optimizing end-to-end defensive engineering solutions—translating emerging threat intelligence into high-fidelity detection logic and automating incident response workflows at scale.

You will work closely with a diverse set of technologies, including SIEMs, SOAR platforms, Threat Intelligence Platforms (TIPs), EDR/XDR solutions, APIs, and cloud services to proactively defend Take-Two and its labels systems, networks, and data. Reporting to the Director of Security Operations Engineering, you will mentor fellow engineers, drive architectural strategy, and collaborate seamlessly with the Global Security Operations Center (SOC), Incident Response (IR), Vulnerability Management, and Offensive Security teams.

What You’ll Take On

Technical Leadership Strategic Direction

Domain Leadership Mentorship: Act as the senior technical authority across SOAR, Detection Engineering, and Threat Intelligence Engineering, providing architectural guidance, code/rule reviews, and mentorship to security engineers and analysts.

Cross-Functional Alignment: Partner with SOC, Incident Response, Red/Purple Teams, and GRC to align engineering roadmaps with Take-Two’s overarching security strategy and regulatory requirements.

Documentation Program Metrics: Establish and maintain comprehensive documentation for alerting lifecycles, playbook architectures, and engineering workflows, while gathering metrics to measure detection fidelity and automation ROI.

SOAR Security Automation Engineering

SOAR Platform Architecture Management: Lead the design, development, deployment, and maintenance of complex SOAR playbooks, workflows, and custom integrations (e.g., Palo Alto Networks Cortex XSOAR/XSIAM, Tines, Splunk SOAR).

End-to-End Workflow Automation: Identify repetitive manual tasks across SOC triage and incident response to build automated enrichment, containment, and remediation pipelines that reduce Mean Time to Detect (MTTD) and Mean Time to Respond (MTTR).

System Integration Change Management: Architect resilient API integrations across SIEM, EDR, cloud security, and IT ticketing systems, and lead platform upgrades, migrations, and lifecycle management.

Detection Engineering Log Management

Detection Rule Development Tuning: Design, build, and maintain threat detection rules, signatures, and behavioral/anomaly-based models across SIEM, EDR/XDR, and cloud environments (AWS, Azure, GCP).

TTP-Driven Coverage Validation: Analyze adversary Tactics, Techniques, and Procedures (TTPs) mapped to the MITRE ATT CK framework to close detection gaps, validating rule efficacy through purple teaming, attack simulations (e.g., Atomic Red Team), and post-incident reviews.

Log Ingestion Pipeline Optimization: Audit log data quality and oversee the onboarding, parsing, and optimization of network, endpoint, authentication, and cloud telemetry to maximize visibility while preventing alert fatigue.

Threat Intelligence Engineering

TIP Operations Feed Automation: Engineer and maintain automated pipelines to ingest, normalize, enrich, and operationalize Indicators of Compromise (IOCs) and Indicators of Attack (IOAs) from OSINT, commercial feeds, and internal telemetry using STIX/TAXII and custom APIs.

Intelligence-to-Detection Translation: Partner with Threat Intelligence analysts to transform strategic and tactical threat reports into proactive detection rules, automated threat-hunting queries, and preventive controls across SIEM, EDR, and firewalls.

What You Bring

Education Experience: Bachelor’s degree in Computer Science, Information Security, or a related field (or equivalent practical experience), with 5+ years of hands-on experience in Security Operations Engineering, Detection Engineering, SOAR Engineering, or Threat Intelligence Engineering, including prior technical leadership experience.

SOAR Automation Mastery: Deep expertise designing, building, and maintaining automated workflows and playbooks in a leading SOAR platform (e.g., Palo Alto Networks Cortex XSOAR/XSIAM, Tines, or Splunk SOAR) coupled with advanced scripting skills in Python (and familiarity with PowerShell, Bash, or Go) to build custom integrations.

Detection SIEM/EDR Expertise: Proven track record writing, testing, and tuning high-fidelity detection rules and queries across SIEM platforms (e.g., Splunk, Elastic), EDR/XDR tools (e.g., CrowdStrike), and multi-cloud environments (AWS, Azure, GCP), alongside deep knowledge of Windows, Linux, and macOS internals and log management.

Threat Intelligence Engineering Acumen: Hands-on experience administering Threat Intelligence Platforms (TIPs) and operationalizing threat feeds using APIs and structured data formats (JSON, XML, STIX/TAXII) to automate IOC correlation and enrichment.

Adversary Framework Knowledge: In-depth understanding of attacker TTPs, malware analysis concepts, network traffic analysis, and defensive frameworks including MITRE ATT CK , the Cyber Kill Chain, and NIST.

Analytical Communication Excellence: Strong problem-solving abilities to analyze large, disparate datasets and translate complex technical challenges into clear, actionable documentation and presentations for both technical teams and leadership.

Great to Have

Industry Certifications: Relevant advanced or intermediate certifications such as CISSP, GIAC (GCTI, GCIA, GCIH, GREM, GSEC), OSCP, or AWS/Azure/GCP Security specialties.

Detection-as-Code DevOps Practices: Experience managing detection rules and SOAR playbooks using CI/CD pipelines, Git version control, and automated testing frameworks.

AI/ML Behavioral Analytics: Experience leveraging machine learning or behavioral analytics models for anomaly detection and large-scale security data analysis.

Offensive Threat Hunting Background: Experience conducting proactive threat hunting, purple team exercises, vulnerability research, or reverse engineering.
