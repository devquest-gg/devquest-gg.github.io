About Mob Entertainment

Mob Entertainment is the indie studio behind the breakout horror survival franchise Poppy Playtime , pushing the boundaries of interactive storytelling and multimedia entertainment. As we expand our universe across games, animation, merchandise, and digital content, we re looking for a talented Marketing Video Production Designer to craft the visuals that define how the world sees our brand.

The Role

As Marketing Video Production Designer, you ll be the creative force behind Mob Entertainment s marketing video output. Working closely with the Marketing Manager and broader creative team, you ll design and execute on creative marketing materials such as cinematic and gameplay trailers, narrative lore videos, static marketing images, video thumbnail art, and other visual elements for various marketing campaigns. If you live in After Effects, know how to quickly bring an idea to life in Blender/Maya, and have a strong sense for pacing and visual storytelling, this role is for you.

What You ll Do

Evaluate marketing creative briefs and develop video and static marketing content in support of Mob Entertainment content across owned and partner channels

Collaborate with fellow designers and Marketing leadership to ensure projects are successful and cleared through leadership approval channels

Create cinematic animations using a combination of Unreal Engine, Blender, and/or Maya, and combine them with gameplay capture for game trailers and YouTube content

Adapt deliverables for multiple formats and aspect ratios (16:9, 9:16, 1:1, etc.) across platforms along with creating versions for various partner channels

Contribute to concepting sessions, bringing visual ideas and references to the table during pre-production

Iterate quickly on feedback from internal stakeholders, keeping projects moving without sacrificing quality

What We re Looking For

3+ years of experience in video production, animation, or a closely related field

Moderate proficiency in video editing and vfx (Davinci Resolve or Adobe Suite); Autodesk Maya or Blender experience is a strong plus

A strong portfolio demonstrating motion graphics/animation work across trailers, social content, or entertainment marketing

Video editing alone is not sufficient. You must be able to stage scenes with light animation in Unreal, Blender, and/or Maya

Solid understanding of design fundamentals in typography, color, composition, and visual hierarchy and how they translate to motion

Ability to work efficiently across multiple projects with tight turnaround times, with a demonstrated organizational method that accounts for multiple channels of feedback

A sharp eye for detail and a high personal bar for quality

Enthusiasm for the evolving zeitgeist around social media assets, what succeeds, and what doesn’t

Bonus Points

Familiarity with Mob Entertainment s IP ( Poppy Playtime ) and its aesthetic — childhood nostalgia through a horror lens

Familiarity versioning assets to first-party requirements (PlayStation, Xbox, Nintendo, etc.)

Experience working with rating board guidelines (ESRB, PEGI, etc.)

Experience with game capture, gameplay editing, or working alongside game development teams

Scrappiness is key - the ideal candidate can work creatively with limited resources to make impactful and exciting marketing content

Experience with Unreal Engine, particularly in cinematics.

Background in illustration, animation or character-driven design

Comfort designing for global audiences and adapting assets for localization

What We Offer

A collaborative, tight-knit team that genuinely cares about the work
