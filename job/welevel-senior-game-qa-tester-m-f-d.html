Your Role

As a Senior Game QA Tester, you will actively contribute to the quality of our upcoming survival-to-city-builder RPG Solid River. The position involves hands-on testing of game builds throughout development, identifying bugs and gameplay issues with real impact on game priorities and improvements. The role requires ownership of quality across complex gameplay systems, leveraging proven AAA game development QA experience.

Responsibilities

Own the QA strategy for assigned systems and drive testing standards across the team

Mentor junior testers and help shape QA processes and best practices

Test game builds and features across different development stages

Identify, reproduce, and clearly describe bugs and unexpected behavior

Verify bug fixes and support regression testing

Create and maintain structured test cases for assigned areas

Participate in regular playtests and provide actionable gameplay feedback

Work closely with designers and programmers to clarify expected behavior

Support internal and external playtests, including preparation and follow-up

Take ownership of specific systems or test areas over time

Contribute to improving QA workflows, documentation, and tooling

Requirements

2+ years of professional AAA game QA experience

Experience testing PC or console games

Passion for survival, city-building, or RPG games

Fluent English (C1 or above)

Ability to work onsite in Munich, Germany

Valid EU work authorization

What we offer

The opportunity to work on an ambitious AAA title.

A dynamic, fast-moving work environment.

Competitive salary and benefits, including relocation assistance.

A central Munich location.
