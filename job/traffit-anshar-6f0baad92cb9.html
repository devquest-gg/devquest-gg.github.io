Freelance VFX Artist

Our teams are fully staffed at the moment, but we’re always expanding and planning next steps. This is a Talent Pool opening for both future internal roles and project-based outsourcing. We’re looking to build relationships with talented creators today, so we can jump straight into a conversation as soon as our next hiring wave kicks off or a new outsourcing need arises.

What you will do: • Create particle systems in Niagara (UE5) • Create necessary building blocks for VFX systems: - textures (flipbooks, noises, tileable textures, masks) - materials (base materials, requested custom materials) - meshes (particle meshes, VATs) - simulations for all of the above • Optimize VFXs according to guidelines • Operate closely with programmers, designers and artists • Work on the software that we use in our pipeline: - EmberGen - Blender - Substance Designer What you need: • Minimum 2 years of relevant industry experience • Good knowledge of Unreal Engine and Niagara • Rudimentary knowledge of Blueprints • Understanding of the basic artistic concepts (color theory, composition, pacing etc) • Strong communication skills • Intermediate Polish (B1/B2 level) • Intermediate English (B1/B2 level) Bonus points for: • Experience in any of our preferred software • Passion for video games • Strong technical skills concerning VFX production • Fluent English (C1/C2 level) We offer:

Salary: 10000 - 18000 PLN month on B2B contract (depending on experience and skills)

Flexible working hours.

No crunch.

No non-compete policy.

Submission / Portfolio Instructions:

CV is required.

Portfolio or demo reel is required.

Cover letter is optional.

Must be willing to complete an art test if requested.
