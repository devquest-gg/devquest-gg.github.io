Marketing Artist

Turtle Rock Studios · Irvine

Mid Art Hybrid Posted 2026-11-11 Full time
Apply on Turtle Rock Studios’s site →

At Turtle Rock, we make the games we want to play. We re dedicated to providing a collaborative environment where our team members feel empowered with the creative freedom to contribute and grow. While we are based in Orange County, CA, we support flexible work arrangements ranging from in-studio, hybrid, to fully remote throughout North America. Join our award-winning game development team and help create our next hit title.

We re looking for a talented Marketing Artist to join our Marketing team and help bring our games, campaigns, and studio identity to life through compelling visual design. As a Marketing Artist, you ll collaborate with the Marketing team and cross-functional partners to develop visually engaging creative assets that support our games, marketing campaigns, community initiatives, and studio communications. You ll help shape how our games and studio are presented to players, partners, and the broader gaming community. You ll bring creativity, consistency, and thoughtful execution to every project, and can take creative direction, contribute ideas, manage multiple projects, and deliver polished work that captures the personality and visual identity of our games. This position will report to the Head of Marketing.

Responsibilities

Design and produce compelling creative assets for marketing campaigns, social media, websites, digital advertising, and other promotional channels

Develop graphics and layouts that support game launches, updates, announcements, community engagement, and ongoing marketing initiatives

Create and adapt artwork for a variety of formats and platforms, ensuring visual consistency and quality across deliverables

Collaborate with Marketing, Brand, Community, Production, and other internal teams to understand project goals and translate creative briefs into effective visual solutions

Support the development and execution of marketing campaign visuals, including creating and adapting key art compositions, promotional graphics, banners, thumbnails, and other campaign materials

Design presentation decks, internal communications, studio materials, and event-related graphics as needed

Work with existing brand guidelines and creative direction while contributing fresh ideas and thoughtful design solutions

Organize and maintain design files, assets, templates, and working documents

Manage multiple projects and deadlines while maintaining strong attention to detail and quality

Incorporate feedback and iterate on designs to meet creative objectives and project requirements

Stay current with design trends, digital marketing practices, and visual communication techniques relevant to the gaming industry

Playtest with the studio and respective teams to share playtest feedback to help enhance player experience

Requirements

3+ years of professional graphic design experience, preferably within gaming

Strong portfolio demonstrating graphic design fundamentals, illustration and paint-over skills. typography, layout, composition, color theory, and visual storytelling (not limited to, but including gameplay and marketing screenshots)

Proficiency in Adobe Creative Suite, particularly Photoshop, Illustrator, and InDesign

Experience creating assets for digital marketing, social media, websites, and promotional campaigns

Ability to adapt existing artwork and creative assets into multiple formats, dimensions, and platform requirements

Strong understanding of visual branding and maintaining consistency across different marketing channels

Excellent organizational and time-management skills, with the ability to manage multiple projects and competing priorities

Strong communication and collaboration skills, with the ability to receive feedback and work effectively with cross-functional teams

Ability to work independently, take ownership of assigned projects, and follow creative direction

A genuine interest in video games and the gaming industry

Preferences

Experience working with video game studios, publishers, or entertainment brands

Familiarity with gaming marketing campaigns, game launches, and digital storefront requirements

Proficiency working in Unreal Engine and Sequencer

Experience creating motion graphics or animated marketing assets using After Effects

Basic video editing skills and familiarity with Premiere Pro

Experience designing assets for Steam, console storefronts, Twitch, YouTube, and other gaming platforms

Familiarity with Figma and collaborative design workflows

Experience working with game key art, promotional renders, screenshots, and other game-related visual assets

Understanding of print production and preparing artwork for physical promotional materials and events
