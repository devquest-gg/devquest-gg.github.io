Job Summary : We are looking for an experienced Technical Architect to join our technology team at Ubisoft Pune.

In this role, you will shape the technical architecture of solutions spanning Automation, Engineering Tools, and R&D initiatives. You will work closely with Programming Leads, engineers, project teams, and global technical stakeholders to design and deliver scalable, reliable, and maintainable technology solutions.

As a senior individual contributor, you will take ownership of key architectural decisions, evaluate emerging technologies, guide engineering teams, and help transform innovative concepts into production-ready solutions.

This is an opportunity to work across multiple technical domains, solve complex engineering problems, and influence technology decisions that can benefit multiple Ubisoft projects and studios worldwide.
