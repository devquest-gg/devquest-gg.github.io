Ubisoft Romania is seeking an IT Contract Project Manager to support the governance and optimization of IT contracts across Infrastructure & Platforms (I&P).

This role focuses on driving operational excellence in contract lifecycle management, ensuring that contract renewals are anticipated, structured, and optimized. Acting as a key partner to IT Vendor Management, Procurement, and Finance teams, the role will help improve contract visibility, support decision-making through data insights, and enable better cost and usage optimization.

What you’ll do Contract Lifecycle Coordination & Project Management Own and drive the renewal tracking and planning for a portfolio of IT contracts (timeline management, stakeholder alignment, risk anticipation).

Coordinate cross-functional stakeholders (IT, Procurement, Legal, Finance) to ensure smooth and timely contract renewals.

Support business teams in preparing contract renewals, ensuring they challenge scope, usage, and commercial conditions.

Lead end-to-end management of smaller contracts, from preparation to renewal, in collaboration with Procurement.

Contribute to defining and implementing contract renewal processes, guidelines, and best practices for internal stakeholders.

Ensure proper documentation and visibility of contract activities (e.g., Confluence one-pagers, renewal summaries).

&#xa0; Contract Analysis & Optimization Analyze contract usage (e.g., licenses, infrastructure usage) to identify optimization opportunities and cost-saving initiatives.

Support tracking of financial commitments vs. actual consumption, highlighting risks and optimization levers.

Build and maintain simple dashboards or reports (Excel / Power BI as a plus) to provide visibility on contract performance and usage trends.

Research licensing models, market benchmarks, and alternative options to support optimization and informed decision-making.

Identify aging or under-optimized contracts and proactively highlight improvement opportunities.

&#xa0; Vendor Interaction & Support Manage day-to-day interactions with smaller vendors and support vendor follow-ups.

Participate in vendor discussions and renewals in collaboration with Procurement.

Contribute to preparation activities for larger strategic contracts (data gathering, usage insights, internal alignment).

What you’ll bring: Proven experience in IT project management, contract management, procurement, or a related field.

Experience working in cross-functional environments (IT, Finance, Procurement, Legal).

Exposure to contract lifecycle management and/or vendor-related topics.

&#xa0; Skills & Competencies Project Management & Coordination Strong organizational skills with the ability to manage multiple timelines, stakeholders, and priorities.

Ability to structure processes and bring clarity to complex or unstructured topics.

Comfortable driving topics autonomously after ramp-up.

&#xa0; Analytical & Data Skills Ability to analyze data and translate it into actionable insights.

Experience with Excel required; exposure to Power BI or data visualization tools is a plus.

Interest in understanding license models, consumption-based pricing, and cost optimization levers.

&#xa0; Contract & Business Understanding Basic understanding of contractual terms, vendor processes, and procurement workflows.

Ability to support contract renewals by challenging usage, scope, and business needs.

Awareness of negotiation processes is a plus (no direct negotiation ownership required).

&#xa0; Soft Skills Highly structured, detail-oriented, and proactive.

Strong communication skills with the ability to interact with multiple stakeholders.

Curious mindset with a strong interest in understanding and improving existing practices.

Pragmatic and solution-oriented approach.
