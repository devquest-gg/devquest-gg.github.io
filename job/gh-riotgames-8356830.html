Riot Games · Los Angeles

As a Technical Artist, you will focus on supporting artists with art asset prototypes so they can realize their work in-game. You will work with artists across all departments from modeling to lighting to ensure all assets work within the scope of the technical pipeline requirements. Working closely with each artistic department, you will standardize and improve development processes for the production of in game assets, materials, shaders, and scalable development pipelines.

As a Rendering Technical Artist Intern on League, you will focus on supporting artists with shaders, materials, and art asset prototypes so they can realize their work in-game. You will work with artists across all departments from modeling to VFX to ensure all assets work within the scope of the technical pipeline requirements. Working closely with each artistic department, you will help standardize and improve development processes for the production of assets, materials, shaders, and interactions in the pipeline.

Responsibilities:

Collaborate directly with Art and Design disciplines to understand desired visual behaviors and translate them into specific visual outputs

Produce shaders and materials that help the team realize visual targets for environments, characters, VFX, and/or UI

Optimize 2D and 3D content as necessary to meet the technical requirements for League

Interface with producers and Art leads when assessing and completing tasks

Use your skills as a 3D generalist to improve, maintain and provide support for Art workflows on League

Required Qualifications:

Currently enrolled in a college, university or non degree program

Graduating in the 2028 calendar year

Available to work full-time hours for the 12 week internship duration

Real-time shading experience with GLSL, HLSL, or node-based material workflows

Experience with content creation tools, such as Maya, Photoshop, and/or Substance

Desired Qualifications:

Experience in game development using modern engines, such as Unity or Unreal

Understanding of 3D math and linear algebra

Experience creating and/or lighting 3D environments and characters

Experience creating bespoke materials for environments, characters, VFX, or UI

Exposure to shader performance fundamentals and engine profiling tools

Exposure to digital asset repositories, such as Perforce

For this role, you ll find success through craft expertise, a collaborative spirit, and decision-making that prioritizes the delight of players. We will be looking at your past studies, experience, and your personal relationship with games. If you embody player empathy and care about players experiences, this could be your role!
