The role As Designer at CSN, you will work hands on in engine, build and implement features/content, be part of content ideation, fix issues, as well as support collaboration between disciplines.

You will: Carry ideas from conceptualization through to detailed design.

Create prototypes and make final implementations for gameplay mechanics and objectives.

Ensure that features developed by the design team are coherent with other game systems and the overall game vision.

Help with organizing cross-disciplinary strike-teams to shape and deliver content for the game.

Your background To succeed in the role, we believe that you have about four to six years of experience in a game designer role and have been part of at least one full project cycle. For us it isn’t the current title you have that matters, but the experience you’ve gained by doing the work - collaborating, building, and shipping games.

We work in Unreal Engine and Blueprint visual scripting, and prior work experience with these tools is a requirement.

To enjoy this role, we see that you are a person who can be part of fostering a collaborative environment with a focus on ideas and solutions. Your previous experience enables you to identify and implement improvements that enhance efficiency and productivity, all with the end goal of creating designs that prioritize the player s experience.

At CSN we have employees from different parts of the world, and it’s therefore mandatory to be fluent in English, both verbally and in writing.

Lastly, we believe we can do even better as a team when your creativity shapes not only your own work but also our studio culture and our games. We actively work to empower our employees by helping everyone strive toward more responsibility, ownership, and autonomy. You will be an important part of this, and it’s therefor crucial to us that you have the same mindset.

Perks of working at Coffee Stain We offer competitive salaries and excellent benefits that go beyond the usual payments to your pension fund and insurances.

We believe that a healthy body leads to a healthy mind, so on top of the wellness grant you get some paid time off to work out. Did we mention there are several gyms right beside our office.

Every fourth week it’s creative Friday which lets your inner passion blossom.

To have fun together and get inspired, we play games on the clock every fourth Friday.

We also have great parental benefits, including extra pay on top of what you get from the social insurance agency during your parental leave.

And, of course, you will be part of a wonderful team of colleagues working on an exciting high-profile project.

Practical info The position is permanent, full-time, 40 hours per week. We have a hybrid setup and offer the possibility of working from home two days per week. Our office is newly renovated, and located in Kungsholmen, Fridhemsplan . We have, among other things, a ping-pong table, a big lounge area where we hang out, watch movies, or play games together, and last but not least – a giant goat statue (what more do you need?).

Apart from us, the Coffee Stain Group consists of a myriad of successful studios, among them COFFEE STAIN STUDIOS (Sanctum and Satisfactory), Ghost Ship Games (Deep Rock Galactic), Tuxedo Labs (Teardown) and COFFEE STAIN PUBLISHING who work with close partners like Iron Gate ( Valheim ), to name a few.

Your application If you are interested, please apply! Due to GDPR, we can’t accept applications through email. If you have any questions regarding the role, please reach out to Maria Sjöman, HR, maria.sjoman@coffeestain.com .
