Scopely is looking for a Director, India Corporate Operations to join our team in Bangalore!

At Scopely, we care deeply about what we do and want to inspire play, every day - whether in our work environments alongside our talented colleagues, or through our deep connections with our communities of players. We are a global team of game lovers who are developing, publishing and innovating the mobile games industry, connecting millions of people around the world daily.

About the Role

We are seeking a strategic, operationally excellent leader to build and lead the Corporate Operations function for Scopely India.

This leadership role is central to maintaining and evolving the cultural foundation of the company, shaping our presence and perception in India, and ensuring operational excellence in collaboration with its local and global counterparts. You will own culture and employee experience for Scopely India, and serve as the organizational integrator across the game teams and functions represented in the Bangalore studio. You will partner across Scopely’s global People, Legal, Finance, IT, Workplace, and Communications teams while bringing India-specific needs and values to the table to localize global initiatives and ensure smooth execution across the business. You will work cross-functionally to ensure that Scopely operates compliantly in India and is represented at the highest standard in the market, while building Scopely’s reputation as a premier destination for the industry’s top game-making talent.

This is a critical executive role that not only ensures operational excellence, but also strengthens employee engagement, cultural evolution, and organizational efficiency. The ideal candidate will balance global alignment with local needs, ensuring that the Bangalore team continues to thrive with its unique culture while meeting international standards and goals.

What you’ll do

Culture Employee Experience

Own and evolve Scopely India’s culture, in co-stewardship with local product and functional leaders

Design and run programs that build connection, belonging, and engagement across teams and disciplines (e.g., Product, Engineering, Design, Art)

Design, convene, and execute internal community-building forums — all-hands meetings, celebration moments, knowledge-sharing and innovation events, hackathons, mentorship, etc. — as instruments of culture

Partner with People and Talent Acquisition to improve the employee experience from onboarding through career growth

Measure organizational health through annual employee engagement data and drive action on what it shows

Represent India workplace needs — space, build-out, lease, and service matters — in global planning

Organizational Effectiveness Leadership Alignment

Serve as the organizational integrator for India: surface cross-studio issues, drive them to resolution, and improve how information and context flow across teams and functions

Convene and drive alignment and action on cross-cutting topics such as return-to-office adherence

Ensure that all functional teams supporting India — People, IT, Finance, Legal, Workplace, Talent Acquisition, etc. — operate as one cohesive support system rather than disconnected verticals

Partner with GMs and functional leaders as a neutral, objective advisor on organizational structure and ways of working

Build systems, processes, and ownership models so the organization runs well at scale

People Leadership

Serve as a senior local partner to global and local People teams with respect to India-specific employee policies and programs, ensuring India’s context is represented in global decisions

Partner with game teams, functional teams, and People to promote talent development, internal mobility, and retention of key talent

Act as a local escalation path for cultural and organizational concerns, handing employee relations matters for decision and resolution in partnership with our People and Employee Relations teams

Partner with People and Finance teams employment compliance matters — labor practice, payroll process, and statutory employer obligations — ensuring India requirements are visible, current, and actioned; stewart, escalate and own labor compliance matters as appropriate

Scopely India Brand Enablement

Partner with Recruiting and global People to strengthen Scopely India’s employer value proposition and candidate experience

Build relationships with and represent Scopely India with universities, industry bodies, and professional communities to support talent pipelines, internships, and employee engagement

Identify and secure opportunities for Scopely to engage in India’s gaming, tech, and entertainment industry, raising the company’s external profile and employer brand proposition

Global, Cross-Functional Leadership Bridge

Act as the first-stop local counterpart for Scopely’s global teams, enabling clear and responsive collaboration across time zones and functions

Adapt global initiatives thoughtfully for local relevance and effectiveness, ensuring alignment with Scopely’s broader vision while tailoring execution to India’s market and cultural context

Represent Scopely India’s presence and voice within the global organization, and bring global context back to India

What We’re Looking For

Demonstrated track record of leadership in corporate operations, organizational development, workplace or studio operations, or a People-adjacent leadership function in a gaming, tech, or product-led organization

Experience operating within a global organization and/or representing a local market to international leadership

Project management skills with the ability to set goals and prioritize programs

Experience in gaming, technology, or entertainment

Established relationships across India’s technology or gaming ecosystem

Track record in change management and organizational design

About Scopely

Scopely is a leading video game and global interactive entertainment company, home to many of the world’s most beloved and enduring experiences, including two of the most successful mobile games of all-time “MONOPOLY GO!” and “Pokémon GO,” along with “Stumble Guys,” “Star Trek™ Fleet Command,” “MARVEL Strike Force,” “WWE Champions,” the Scrabble® franchise, “Yahtzee® With Buddies,” and many others. Across mobile, web, PC, and console, Scopely creates, develops, publishes, and live-operates one of the most diversified and award-winning portfolios in the games industry — bringing hundreds of millions of players together through a shared love of play.

Founded in 2011, Scopely is powered by its exceptional team — including thousands of world-class gamemakers around the globe, a distinctive tenet-driven culture, and its proprietary technology platform, Playgami. Together, these strengths have fueled Scopely’s position as the #1 mobile games company in the U.S. and #2 globally, generating more than $10 billion in lifetime revenue. Whether building global sensations like “MONOPOLY GO!” from the ground up, or expanding through strategic acquisitions, including the FoxNext, GSN, and Scopely Explore games businesses — Scopely consistently delivers experiences players love today and return to for years to come.

Recognized multiple times as one of the "100 Most Influential Companies in the World" by TIME magazine and one of Fast Company s "World s Most Innovative Companies" and “Best Workplaces for Innovators,” Scopely believes that video games can be a force for good — creating meaningful connections, vibrant communities, and making life better through play.

Scopely has global operations and partners across four continents in more than a dozen countries worldwide. For more information, visit: https://www.scopely.com/ .
