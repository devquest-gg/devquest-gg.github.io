Why Sony Interactive Entertainment?

Sony Interactive Entertainment isn’t just the Best Place to Play — it’s also the Best Place to Work. Sony Interactive Entertainment (SIE) is the company behind the PlayStation brand. JOB PURPOSE:

The Technical Program Governance team enables complex technical initiatives across PlayStation engineering organizations by creating clarity, alignment, and disciplined execution across organizational boundaries.

As a Staff Technical Program Manager, you will lead large, strategic technical programs that span multiple teams, technologies, and stakeholders. You will translate business and technical objectives into executable plans, bring structure to ambiguity, and ensure risks, dependencies, decisions, and tradeoffs remain visible and actively managed.

This role requires a strong technical foundation and the judgment to operate as a trusted partner to engineering and architecture leaders. You will influence without relying on positional authority, select and adapt program approaches to fit the problem, and improve the operating systems that help teams deliver effectively at scale.

EXPERIENCE QUALIFICATIONS:

Essential • 8+ years of relevant technical program or project management experience, including ownership of complex software or technology initiatives spanning multiple teams or organizations. • Demonstrated technical fluency across software systems, platforms, architecture, integrations, and engineering practices; able to understand system interactions, ask informed technical questions, and translate technical complexity into program decisions. • Proven ability to lead large strategic programs end to end, from problem definition and planning through execution, delivery, and measurable outcomes. • Strong independent judgment and problem-solving skills, with experience operating effectively in ambiguous environments where the path forward is not predefined. • Experience managing cross-team dependencies, technical risks, roadmaps, milestones, requirements, decision points, and delivery tradeoffs across the software development lifecycle. • Ability to influence senior technical and business leaders, build alignment across competing priorities, and drive decisions without direct authority. • Experience designing or improving program operating models, governance, planning cadences, metrics, or ways of working that scale beyond a single initiative. • Strong written and verbal communication skills, including the ability to create concise executive narratives while maintaining sufficient technical depth for engineering audiences. • Demonstrated ability to mentor, coach, or raise the effectiveness of other TPMs and cross-functional partners through reusable practices and knowledge sharing.

Desirable • Prior hands-on experience in software engineering, systems engineering, QA, architecture, or another technical discipline. • Experience supporting platform, infrastructure, developer tooling, cloud, distributed systems, or other complex engineering environments. • Experience leading standardization, transformation, or organizational change initiatives across multiple technical teams. • Background in the games or interactive entertainment industry.

KEY RESPONSIBILITIES SCOPE FOR DECISION MAKING:

Technical Program Management • Lead large, strategic technical programs spanning multiple engineering teams, organizations, technologies, or geographies, with accountability for end-to-end outcomes. • Build integrated program plans and roadmaps that connect technical requirements, architecture, dependencies, milestones, resources, risks, and decision points. • Develop sufficient understanding of system architecture and technical constraints to identify cross-system impacts, challenge assumptions constructively, and improve planning and decision quality. • Partner closely with engineering leaders, architects, product partners, and other technical stakeholders to translate objectives into clear execution strategies and drive resolution of complex issues. • Anticipate and actively manage technical and organizational dependencies, surfacing tradeoffs early and driving decisions before they become delivery blockers. • Establish clear program health measures and provide decision-quality visibility into progress, risk, scope, and outcomes for senior stakeholders.

Strategy and Decision Support • Operate as a trusted partner to senior leaders, using technical context, program data, and organizational knowledge to shape recommendations and execution strategy. • Use independent judgment to determine the right program methodology, governance, and operating model for the work rather than applying process mechanically. • Analyze complex problems across organizations and technologies, identify patterns and root causes, and drive practical solutions that balance technical, business, schedule, and resource considerations. • Strengthen requirements and major program decisions by connecting business objectives with technical realities, long-term implications, and customer or organizational value. • Create clear narratives and recommendations that enable senior leaders to understand options, tradeoffs, risks, and required decisions.

Organizational Impact and TPM Practice • Design and improve scalable program practices, governance, planning cadences, OKRs/KPIs, and decision mechanisms that increase clarity and execution effectiveness across multiple teams. • Identify where existing processes or methodologies create unnecessary friction and adapt or simplify them while preserving appropriate rigor and accountability. • Lead change and standardization efforts where needed, building adoption through stakeholder engagement, practical implementation, and measurable outcomes. • Mentor TPMs and cross-functional partners, share reusable practices, and contribute to stronger technical program management capability across the organization.

KEY PERFORMANCE INDICATORS

The individual will be measured on: • Successful delivery of large, complex technical programs with clear business and engineering outcomes.

• Quality and timeliness of decisions, risk management, dependency resolution, and executive visibility.

• Effectiveness in creating alignment across technical teams and senior stakeholders without direct authority.

• Measurable improvements to program operating models, governance, and organizational execution capability.

• Demonstrated technical credibility, independent judgment, and ability to raise the effectiveness of others.
