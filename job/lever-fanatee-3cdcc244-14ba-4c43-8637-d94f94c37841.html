About the position

We're looking for a Game Developer to join our Development team and help bring Fanatee's mobile games to life. You'll be responsible for planning, developing, and maintaining the code behind our games, working closely with different teams to build high-quality experiences for millions of players around the world. If you're passionate about technology, innovation, and games, this could be the opportunity for you!

What you'll do

Participate in the planning and development of games.

Optimize game performance to ensure smooth and efficient experiences.

Collaborate with front-end and back-end teams to deliver the best product possible.

Contribute to feature specifications, balancing technical costs and benefits when evaluating solutions.

Track project schedules and report progress clearly and objectively.

Independently develop robust and scalable code.

Explore Artificial Intelligence solutions that can improve feature development and code quality.

What you'll need

Bachelor's degree in Computer Science, Engineering, Game Design, or a related field.

Advanced knowledge of Unity and C#.

Experience integrating native Android and iOS components, such as push notifications and in-app purchases (IAP).

Knowledge of local databases and offline data management.

Interest in applying AI coding tools, such as Claude Code, Codex, Gemini CLI, and similar solutions, to game development.

Excellent communication and teamwork skills.

Proactive mindset, fast learner, and commitment to high standards of quality.

Nice to have

Advanced English.

Experience with data architecture for games.

Hands-on experience with projects released to the market.

Career development opportunities and professional growth

While the role is primarily remote, our São Paulo office is available for those seeking a dedicated workspace. There, you’ll find a calm environment, a stocked fridge with snacks and drinks, and even a ping-pong table for a bit of fun. Come help shape the future whith our amazing team!

About Fanatee

Fanatee is a mobile gaming startup founded in 2013. Since then, our goal has been to enable people to play our games anytime, anywhere. We believe that quality is the key to success.Our creative processes are well planned and carefully detailed, and our talent lies in turning great ideas into great businesses: our games hit over 250M downloads worldwide. We believe that a diverse work environment contributes to an increasingly responsible society that values differences. Our dream allows us to do what we love the most: work with talented, passionate people and give them the opportunity to achieve goals as big as they can dream of.

If you want to share this ride with us and join a lightweight, fun, and focused environment that breathes innovation and game culture, apply now!
