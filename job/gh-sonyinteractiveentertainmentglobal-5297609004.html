Why Sony Interactive Entertainment?

Sony Interactive Entertainment isn’t just the Best Place to Play — it’s also the Best Place to Work. Sony Interactive Entertainment (SIE) is the company behind the PlayStation brand. As a subsidiary of Sony Group Corporation, we’re part of a proud legacy of innovation and excellence. SIE is a dynamic technology company, delivering cutting-edge hardware and network services to more than 100 million people and an entertainment leader, home to some of the most beloved and recognizable intellectual properties (IP) in the world. Our role at SIE is to create and nurture the experiences under the PlayStation brand, a name synonymous with entertainment excellence and creativity.

Role Overview (Role must start on October 26th)

This role follows a 4 days on, 4 days off shift pattern, working from 8am to 7pm , and is based on site at our PlayStation office on Old Hall Street, Liverpool.

Join our fast-paced environment where your drive, curiosity, and attention to detail will make a real impact. We re seeking passionate individuals to help ensure all bugs are swiftly identified, thoroughly investigated, and clearly reported within the Certification Operations team.

This role is critical to the delivery of projects and releases throughout the year.

What you’ll be doing:

Thoroughly test all submissions to ensure compliance with the Technical Requirements Checklist across all supported platforms, including PSVR.

Develop a comprehensive knowledge of SIEE CertOps testing practices and procedures

Quickly learn any updated CertOps procedures and/or TRC requirements and alter testing accordingly

Raise any issues with test procedure and processes to Team Leaders, offering solutions where applicable.

Support and assist in the training of any member of staff when necessary

Check the content of applications for Legal issues, copyrights and age rating issues

Bug Testing

Ensure all bugs are identified and thoroughly investigated and recorded (existing new version codes)

Communication - Provide accurate progress updates to direct line manager, when requested

Ensure all relevant CertOps procedures are followed and compliance adhered too

Keep up to date with all PlayStation platforms, their respective TRCs and requirements.

Learn and maintain knowledge on the use of key Certops systems, such as SHIP and Unity.
