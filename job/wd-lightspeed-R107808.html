What the Role Entails Develop agents based on large language models and optimizing their performance.

Collaborate with game designers and engineers to integrate LLM-powered agents into live game environments, ensuring latency, cost, and player experience requirements are met.

Research cutting-edge techniques in LLM reasoning, planning, tool-use, and memory, and adapt them to game agent scenarios.

Who We Look For Familiar with machine learning foundations, with a strong understanding of statistical models and experience working on NLP related projects.

Proficient in at least one programming language, familiar with basic data structures and algorithms.

Publication of academic results in top-tier conferences in AI and NLP-related fields (such as: ICML, NeurIPS, ICLR, EMNLP, ACL) is preferred.
