Localization QA Lead

CD Projekt Red · Warsaw

Lead QA Posted 2026-11-23 Full time
Apply on CD Projekt Red’s site →

CD PROJEKT RED is looking for a Localization QA Lead to lead and develop our Localization QA capability within Shared Development. This is a central role supporting multiple projects at different stages of development, from early production through release and live updates.

You will help projects deliver high-quality localized experiences by setting clear LQA plans and standards, developing the team, and coordinating internal and external resources. You will work closely with project QA teams and partners across Localization Production, Narrative, UI/UX, Audio, Engineering, and Publishing.

The Localization QA Lead contributes to project QA direction and owns LQA planning and execution within the discipline. You will balance the needs of individual projects with the opportunity to improve shared practices, visibility, and consistency across the studio.

Responsibilities: Lead, mentor, and develop Localization QA Analysts, setting clear expectations, priorities, goals, and quality standards.

Plan and coordinate LQA work across multiple projects, aligning coverage and resources with project milestones, schedules, risks, and capacity.

Translate project QA direction into practical LQA strategies, test plans, checklists, and measurable quality expectations.

Oversee localization testing across target languages and regional variants, including in-game text, UI, voice-over, subtitles and captions, store metadata, and legal content.

Ensure localization issues are clearly reported, appropriately prioritized, followed up, and verified through resolution.

Maintain visibility of LQA status, coverage, risks, blockers, and readiness; provide reliable input to project milestone and release assessments.

Identify quality risks, dependencies, and delivery concerns early, and work with project stakeholders to agree and follow through on mitigation plans.

Lead and coordinate external LQA vendors, setting scope, schedules, acceptance criteria, and reporting expectations; review their work and drive corrective actions when needed.

Collaborate with Localization Production, Narrative, UI/UX, Audio, Engineering, Publishing, project QA, and Production to align localization quality with creative intent and technical requirements.

Use LQA results, defect trends, and player feedback to inform priorities and recommendations.

Maintain and improve LQA documentation, glossaries, style guides, workflows, tools, and reporting practices across projects.

Support recruitment, onboarding, performance feedback, career development, and sustainable workload planning for the team.

Share effective practices across projects and contribute to the development of consistent LQA standards within Shared Development and the wider QA organization.

Experience in Localization QA for games or other complex software products, including testing localized content across multiple languages or regions.

Proven experience leading a QA team, including direct people management, setting expectations, providing performance feedback, and supporting career development.

Strong understanding of localization testing workflows, common linguistic and functional quality issues, and the relationship between localized content and the player experience.

Experience planning and coordinating test coverage against schedules, milestones, risks, and available resources.

Experience working with external localization or QA vendors, including defining expectations, reviewing results, and addressing quality or delivery issues.

Ability to assess quality data, identify risks and trends, and communicate clear conclusions and recommendations.

Strong organizational skills and the ability to coordinate work across multiple projects and stakeholders.

Clear communication and collaboration skills, including the confidence to raise concerns, challenge plans constructively, and follow through on decisions.

Professional fluency in English.

A proactive, accountable approach to problem-solving and continuous improvement.

Nice to Have Experience with large-scale or AAA game development.

Experience supporting multiple projects or products at different stages of development.

Familiarity with localization tools, bug-tracking systems, and QA reporting practices.

Experience with live games, post-release updates, or localization certification.
