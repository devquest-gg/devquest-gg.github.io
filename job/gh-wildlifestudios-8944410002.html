Tech Art Intern

Wildlife is looking for a Tech Art Intern to join our Tech Art team! Tech Art is the bridge between creativity and technology. As a Tech Art Intern, you’ll work alongside artists and engineers to build the pipelines and tools that bring our games to life. You’ll optimize assets and workflows so millions of players get stunning visuals at smooth performance. From automating production processes to solving technical challenges, you’ll turn constraints into creative possibilities.

What you ll do

Content: Support the production of 3D and 2D game content, such as characters, props, environments and UI elements, helping artists bring their work from concept to a game-ready asset.

Engine integration: Set up and integrate assets in Unity, making sure they follow project standards, and help solve the technical issues that come up along the way.

Look-dev: Assist with material, VFX and lighting setup in the engine, helping assets and scenes reach the visual quality the game is aiming for.

Game developer mindset: Help to optimize assets so they look great and run smoothly, keeping in mind that our players use a wide range of mobile devices. Pipelines and tools: Help build art pipelines and tools, including AI-based ones, that automate repetitive tasks and streamline production for the whole art team. Collaboration: Work closely with artists, engineers and other teams, sharing ideas, asking questions and taking feedback to grow your craft.

What you ll need

Currently enrolled in an undergraduate program in Computer Science, Game Development, Design or a related field.

Knowledge of Unity or another game engine.

Basic programming or scripting skills (C#, Python or similar).

Knowledge of technical aspects of 3D models.

Intermediate English.

Nice to have

Familiarity with Blender, Maya or another 3D tool.

Interest in shaders and render pipelines.

Interest in AI tools for art production.

Personal or academic projects showing technical art work.

More about you

Curious: You like understanding how things work under the hood and aren’t afraid to dig into a problem until it makes sense.

Problem solver: You see technical limits as a puzzle, not a blocker, and enjoy finding creative ways around them.

Team player: You communicate clearly with both artists and engineers, seek feedback and share what you learn.

Multidisciplinary: You enjoy the mix of art and technology and want to grow in both.

Open to new tools: You’re comfortable learning new software and exploring new ways of working, including AI.
