Every day, tens of millions of people come to Roblox to explore, create, play, learn, and connect with friends in 3D immersive digital experiences– all created by our global community of developers and creators.

At Roblox, we’re building the tools and platform that empower our community to bring any experience that they can imagine to life. Our vision is to reimagine the way people come together, from anywhere in the world, and on any device.

We’re on a mission to connect a billion people with optimism and civility, and looking for amazing talent to help us get there.

A career at Roblox means you’ll be working to shape the future of human interaction, solving unique technical challenges at scale, and helping to create safer, more civil shared experiences for everyone.

Roblox’s Safety Operations team is dedicated to protecting our users, developers, and creators from individuals who want to cause harm on our platform. The Moderation Operations team are subject matter experts for content safety policies, and apply their knowledge to empower moderation at scale across the Roblox platform. This is a critical team for our users, developers, and creators!

The Product Support Specialist, Game Safety runs day-to-day product support for games and experience moderation: experience escalations, SOP upkeep, bug intake, launch checklists, and the BPO cadence for existing games workflows. This role works closely with the Senior Product Support Manager for Game Safety, Policy, Legal, Safety Product Engineering, Creator Support, and safety leadership, and improves the tooling, processes, and workflows used to moderate experiences at scale.

Please note: This role may review graphic, controversial, and sometimes offensive content in line with Roblox’s policies. This role will also require some on-call or shift support.

You will report to the Manager, Moderation Product Support.

You will:

Act as policy enforcement subject matter expert for games and experience moderation, owning escalations end-to-end — assessing experiences, thumbnails, titles, descriptions, and in-experience content against Community Standards, Experience Guidelines, Content Maturity Labels, and internal enforcement guidelines — and routing cross-harm cases to Critical Harms or Child Exploitation

Implement and maintain standard operating procedures, playbooks, and enforcement guidance for games and experience queues, keeping enforcement guidance in sync with policy as it evolves

Triage and resolve escalations from agents, BPO leads, Creator Support, and developers (experience takedowns and restores, maturity label disputes, developer appeals), meeting SLA and quality targets

Communicate effectively with internal customers (leadership up to Roblox c-suite) and cross-functional stakeholders, performing comprehensive analysis of ambiguous incidents and making business-critical decisions under time constraints

Maintain professionalism, confidentiality, and good judgement when handling high-visibility experiences and press- or regulatory-sensitive matters

Manage, maintain, and develop highly effective processes that meet or exceed relevant service level agreements

Coordinate with cross-functional partners and a large team of Roblox moderation agents and their respective staff

Analyze operational and QA data (volume, handle time, backlog, quality) and conduct root cause analysis to identify trends, bottlenecks, and tooling gaps; log bugs and repeated agent pain points and route them to the PSM, Games

Participate in an on-call or shift rotating basis to ensure coverage, which may include weekends

You Have:

5+ years of experience handling escalations or content review at a gaming, UGC, social media, or other communications platform company.

Experience with managing escalation requests and responses, or online safety issues in a compliance enforcement

Strong program management skills, experience developing processes to improve operational efficiency

Experience using data to lead decision-making

Strong collaboration skills, with the ability to work with cross-functional teams, specifically product, engineering, and BPO workforces

Experience on a trust and safety, and/or have worked closely with policy or content moderation

Familiarity with game design, Roblox Studio or other game engines, or age ratings (ESRB, PEGI, IARC) is a plus

An interest in online safety, policy development, regulations and emerging trends .

Roles that are based in an office are onsite Tuesday, Wednesday, and Thursday, with optional presence on Monday and Friday (unless otherwise noted).
