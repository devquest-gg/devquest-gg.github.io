Senior Player Engagement Manager

Aspyr Media · Austin

Senior Other Onsite Posted 2026-05-28

**Please note that all positions posted for Aspyr Media, Inc. are in-person/in-office only; remote positions are not available.**

Aspyr is a leading game publisher and developer, bringing the world s biggest franchises and bold new projects to players everywhere.

Since 1996, we’ve published over 200 games and our team is dedicated to bringing beloved franchises like Tomb Raider and Star Wars to new and familiar audiences across all platforms.

We focus on collaboration and creativity, crafting high-quality, engaging games that leave a lasting impact on players worldwide.

Responsibilities:

Reporting to the Head of Marketing , the Senior Player Engagement Manager is the bridge between Aspyr and the millions of fans who cherish legendary IPs. This role will lead the global community vision for multiple properties from the early announcement phase through launch and long term live support. This individual serves as the definitive champion for our players within the studio and acts as the influential ambassador for Aspyr to the global gaming community.

Essential Functions:

Develop and execute a comprehensive community roadmap for AAA legacy titles and new releases across Discord, Reddit, X (Twitter), and emerging platforms

Build structured pipelines to gather qualitative player feedback and sentiment. Synthesize global community sentiment into high-impact, actionable intelligence that drives product innovation and elevates overall game quality

Partner with the marketing, product and creative teams to create engaging community first content, including Dev Diaries, AMAs, and Behind the Scenes looks at the remastering process

Identify and nurture relationships with super fans, creators, and lore experts to build a network of advocates for our franchises

Develop and execute transparent communication strategies for all game lifecycle releases, including patches, updates, and hotfixes, to foster and sustain player trust during critical launch windows

Serve as the lead liaison for community sentiment in sensitive scenarios, offering steady and tactical counsel to the leadership team

Collaborate with our Studio partners and internal Core Games teams to advocate for player interests and drive enhancements to the overall game experience

Preferred Qualifications:

6+ years experience in Community Management or Social Media within the gaming industry (licensed IP experience a must)

Expert level knowledge of Discord, Reddit, and Steam Community Hubs

Proven experience managing community growth for a major launch or live service title

Familiarity with project management tools and the game development lifecycle

Extensive knowledge of the video game landscape, with specific knowledge of remasters and remakes

Ability to manage numerous AA and AAA projects simultaneously, while prioritizing high return work

Skilled in articulating community strategies and integrating stakeholder input

Possesses excellent interpersonal and communication abilities to bolster global player engagement and cultivate a positive brand sentiment for various franchises

Proficiency in producing high-quality copy and written content across a variety of platforms and formats
