Job Summary

The Senior Data AI Analytics Engineer’s key objective is to own NC America’s data from raw table to finished insight. They will turn raw game, player, and business data into clean, well-modeled summary tables, write the analysis themselves, and build the Tableau dashboards that teams across the company use to make decisions.

This is a senior, hands-on individual contributor role that sits across data engineering, analytics, and AI. NC America is looking for someone who uses AI tools as a core part of how they work, building workflows that let one engineer support several projects at once without sacrificing quality. They ll set the standard for how data is modeled, documented, and used at NC America.

Essential Job Functions The following section contains key work that will be performed. NC is a dynamic organization, and the environment can be fluid. Roles and responsibilities can often be expanded to accommodate changing business conditions and objectives. Data Modeling Transformation

· Design and build the data layer between raw source tables and our analysts, from staging to cleaned to summarized tables.

· Build and maintain dbt models with tests, documentation, and clear naming, so anyone can find and trust the right table.

· Define core business and player metrics once, so every report and dashboard counts the same way.

· Monitor data quality and pipeline health, and fix issues before they reach a decision-maker.

Analytics Insights

· Write SQL to answer business questions end to end, from scoping the question to presenting the findings.

· Partner with product, marketing, live operations, and leadership to turn open-ended requests into clear metrics and deliverables.

· Review other analysts queries and guide them to the right tables and methods.

Dashboards Reporting

· Build and maintain Tableau dashboards that teams use to run the business day to day.

· Design dashboards for non-technical audiences: clear, fast, and focused on the decisions they support.

· Audit existing reporting, consolidate duplicates, and retire dashboards that are no longer used.

AI-Enabled Workflows Scale

· Use AI tools daily to write, review, and debug SQL, dbt models, and Python faster and with fewer errors.

· Build repeatable AI-assisted workflows, such as auto-generated documentation, data quality checks, and first-draft analyses.

· Identify routine reporting work that AI can take on, and put those solutions into production.

· Set practical guardrails for AI use, including output review and protection of sensitive data, and share what works with the team.

Required

· Experience in the games industry, especially with player telemetry, live-service, or monetization data.

· Expert SQL, including window functions, CTEs, performance tuning, and modeling large, messy source data.

· Production experience with dbt: models, tests, sources, macros, and documentation.

· Strong Python for data work, such as pandas, automation, and pulling data from APIs.

· Deep Tableau experience, including data sources, calculated fields, LOD expressions, and dashboards built for non-technical users.

· Solid grounding in dimensional modeling (facts, dimensions, grain).

· A proven track record of using AI tools to speed up real data work, with examples you can walk us through.

· Hands-on experience building with LLM APIs or AI agents, beyond chat-based use.

Nice to Have

· Familiarity with MMOs and shooters, as a player or from working on them.

· Experience with a cloud data warehouse such as Snowflake, BigQuery, Redshift, or Databricks.
