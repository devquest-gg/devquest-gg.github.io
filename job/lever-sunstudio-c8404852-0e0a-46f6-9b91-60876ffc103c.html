Established in 2018, Sun Studio is a game developer on web platforms and mobile devices with more than 1 billion plays, a strategic partner of the world's leading web-games platforms such as: Facebook Instant Games, Game Distribution, Glance Gaming.

The team consists of over 70 members who are passionate about creating games that are accessible and enjoyable for everyone.

Our team is growing and we are looking for Lead 2D Artist to strengthen our wing of game development and bring more beautiful and user-focused games to our players.

What you will work on

• Genres: Casual / Puzzle

• Platforms: HTML5 Games, iOS, Android
