Video Artist

Gameloft · Sofia

Senior Art Posted 2026-09-22 Full time
Apply on Gameloft’s site →

Main challenge Help our games stand out by creating marketing content that attracts new players across a variety of channels and audiences.

You’ll work on live titles including Disney Magic Kingdoms, March of Empires, Disney Speedstorm, and War Planet Online, as well as other games from Gameloft’s portfolio, creating trailers, video ads, and marketing content that support user acquisition and promotional campaigns What it means on a daily basis Create trailers, video ads, app store videos, and social content for multiple games.

Capture and stage gameplay footage in the game’s engines.

Adapt and improve existing 3D assets in an Unreal Engine sequencer for marketing and UA use Combine editing, motion graphics, sound, and visual effects into polished final videos.

Explore new creative approaches and content trends to keep campaigns fresh.

What success will look like During your first 3 months Learn the team’s workflows, tools, and production standards.

Build strong working relationships with artists, marketers, and project managers.

Deliver reliable, campaign-ready content with the expected level of quality.

Become comfortable working across several games at the same time.

After 6-12 months Independently work on projects from brief to final delivery.

Handle increasingly complex campaigns with minimal guidance.

Contribute ideas that improve creative quality and production efficiency.

Become a trusted partner for marketing and user acquisition teams.

Who you would be working with You’ll be part of a multidisciplinary creative team that includes Motion Designers, 3D Artists, a Creative Project Manager, and the Creative Agency Lead. You'll work closely with User Acquisition, Marketing, production teams, and colleagues from other Gameloft studios, including the Disney Speedstorm team in Barcelona.

The team values feedback, knowledge sharing, and collaboration, with regular creative reviews and brainstorming sessions.

Where this role can take you Senior Video Artist.

More responsibility on larger projects.

More involvement in cinematic trailers and cross-game campaigns.

What you need to succeed Experience creating video content, motion graphics, or marketing assets.

Strong knowledge of Adobe Premiere Pro, After Effects, Unreal Engine and Photoshop.

Experience working with 3D software such as Blender, 3ds Max, or similar.

Understanding of rendering, animation, and 3D production workflows.

Ability to take a project from concept to final delivery.

Good English communication skills.

Who you are You communicate openly and collaborate well with different teams.

You stay organized when working on multiple projects and deadlines.

You enjoy solving creative challenges and bringing new ideas forward.

You care about the details that make content look polished and professional.

You take ownership of your projects and see them through from brief to final delivery.

What's in it for you Additional health insurance including dental care and glasses reimbursement Hybrid work model with flexible working hours and shorter Fridays Food vouchers – 102 EUR per month Access to preferential-rate Multisport or Coolfit sports cards 20 days paid annual leave plus one additional day off for your birthday Referral bonus and baby bonus Modern office at Sofia Tower 2 (Mall of Sofia) Gaming area with table tennis, foosball, PlayStation 5, and board games Weekly fruit and healthy snacks, daily hot beverages Access to internal trainings, studio library, and career development support Studio events, social gatherings, and charity initiatives Occasional gifts for special occasions Discount program with selected retailers and services Salary Ranges &#xa0;1800 EUR -2000 EUR monthly gross salary.
