At IO Interactive , we believe great games are built by great teams, and great teams thrive in great environments.

We are looking for an Office Assistant who will help shape the everyday experience of our Copenhagen studio. Someone who notices the details others miss, takes ownership naturally, and enjoys creating a workplace where people feel welcome, supported, and excited to be.

From celebrating team milestones and organising studio events to solving last-minute challenges and keeping our spaces looking their best, you'll be at the centre of what makes IOI a great place to work. Above all, you're the type of person who sees something that needs doing and says: "Don't worry. I've got this." What you will do: Studio Experience & Facilities Help create a welcoming, vibrant, and inspiring studio environment where people enjoy spending their day.

Take ownership of the spaces around you, ensuring kitchens, lounges, meeting rooms, gaming areas, and communal spaces are clean, organised, and inviting.

Proactively spot issues before they become problems and take action to resolve them.

Ensure fridges are stocked, coffee is flowing, supplies are available, and the studio feels cared for.

Support meeting preparations and help keep shared spaces looking their best throughout the day.

Be hands-on when needed. If something has spilled, needs tidying, fixing, moving, or organising, you jump in and make it happen.

Operations & Administration Manage deliveries, supplies, merchandise, groceries, and other studio essentials.

Welcome guests and visitors and ensure everyone receives a warm IOI experience.

Coordinate practical studio activities and support colleagues with day-to-day requests.

Handle a wide variety of ad hoc tasks, large and small, that help keep the studio running smoothly.

Events & Culture Support the planning and execution of social events, celebrations, team activities, and studio gatherings.

Help bring energy and personality to studio life through decorations, initiatives, and memorable experiences.

Build and maintain relationships with local vendors, suppliers, venues, and partners, so you always know who to call when something is needed quickly.

Be part of creating the moments that make IOI feel special, from milestone celebrations to spontaneous team activities.

(Occasional evening support may be needed after events.) Who You Are You're the kind of person who sees something that needs doing and simply gets it done.

You don't believe in "that's not my job". If something needs doing and it helps the studio, the people, or the culture, you step in and make it happen.

One minute you're welcoming a guest; the next you're arranging catering for a team that just hit a major milestone, finding a DJ for tomorrow's studio event, cleaning up a coffee spill before anyone notices, or helping solve a problem nobody knew was theirs to own.

You are resourceful, connected, and proactive. When someone says, "We need this by tomorrow," your instinct is not to explain why it's difficult. It's to say: "I've got this. I know exactly who to call." You naturally take ownership of your surroundings. You notice the details others walk past. Whether it's empty coffee cups, a messy meeting room, supplies running low, decorations for a celebration, or creating a warm welcome for a guest, you understand that every interaction contributes to the experience people have at IO Interactive.

You are the type of person who treats everything within three meters of you as your responsibility.

Most importantly, you bring positive energy. You're approachable, enthusiastic, service-minded, and genuinely enjoy helping people. You help create the atmosphere that makes people excited to come to the studio every day.

Who we are: IO Interactive is an independent video game development and publishing company with studios in Copenhagen, Malmö, Barcelona, Istanbul and Brighton. As the creative force behind some of the most talked-about multiplatform video games in the last decade, we are committed to creating unforgettable characters and experiences – all powered by our award-winning, proprietary Glacier technology.

IOI is a studio that values in-person collaboration. Being together helps us focus our collective energy on our immediate goals. For us, being both in-office as well as connected across our studios helps us integrate our teams faster, strengthen relationships, and improve knowledge-sharing. We believe that the more time we spend together, the more quality and progress we achieve for our games and players.

We know that to achieve those goals, we need courage, talented people and a great working environment – and we do our utmost to have all of that. Across our multiple studios, we’re working on several projects. Crucially, though, we’re all one team. We value the work and impact that each person brings to the table, and we actively encourage new ideas, whilst listening to your insights along the way.

We have a dedicated team of People Managers who look after you as an individual and as an employee. With more than 40 nationalities, we know that everyone is different and we are proud to have a reputation for being a friendly workplace with highly-talent people.
