What your weeks are going to look like Determine the most appropriate technical means to achieve the project’s objectives, taking into account constraints (technical, budgetary, and time-related); Ensure smooth communication with other departments (design, art, production) to ensure optimal integration of the project’s various components; Monitor schedules and deliverables in accordance with the initial technical plan and anticipate any necessary adjustments; Identify and propose solutions to technical issues that could impact production; Ensure code optimization and system performance to guarantee optimal fluidity and stability; Organize and lead regular meetings with the team to track progress, anticipate risks, and verify that deadlines are being met; Assist programmers in resolving technical issues and provide support when they encounter roadblocks; Motivate, guide, and support the direct team by providing coaching through continuous feedback; Contribute to the development of skills and internal talent in your area of expertise by rigorously monitoring performance and developing relevant training plans tailored to corporate needs and strategic directions; Build a relationship of trust with the partner and serve as the point of contact in your area of expertise; Participate in project reviews and post-mortems; Ensure partner satisfaction by maintaining transparency and rigorously tracking requests and necessary adjustments; Participate in discussions with other leads and Director to share best practices and technical innovations; Develop and optimize, in collaboration with your team, reusable tools and features to improve the efficiency of your project; Contribute to the improvement of development processes and methods to optimize productivity and the quality of deliverables.

What we're looking for Bachelor’s degree in computer science or a related field, combined with experience deemed equivalent; At least five (5) years of experience in video game programming; Experience as a lead programmer or in a similar role; Strong proficiency in C++ and optimization skills; Proficiency with the Unreal game engine is a significant asset; Good understanding of the various hardware and software layers involved in the execution of a video game; Experience in mobile game development (a significant asset); Excellent command of French and English (written and spoken). English skills are required to perform the job, as we collaborate with English-speaking partners; Ability to travel internationally as needed.
