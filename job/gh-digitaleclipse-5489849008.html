The Company

Classic video games deserve better. At Digital Eclipse, we treat them with the same care and respect given to other artistic mediums. In its original incarnation in 1994, Digital Eclipse pioneered accurate video game re-releases, emulating vintage arcade games well before “emulation” was a household word. 30 years later, we’ve been reborn as a new kind of studio - one dedicated to not only restoring gaming’s heritage, but to preserving it and keeping it alive and available for future generations.

We ve launched critically acclaimed titles like Mortal Kombat: Legacy Kollection, Mighty Morphin Power Rangers: Rita’s Rewind , Teenage Mutant Ninja Turtles: The Cowabunga Collection , and Atari 50: The Anniversary Celebration , while also developing successful original IPs like #IDARB and collaborating with industry leaders such as Capcom and Disney. And we re just getting started.

The Role

As a Producer, you ll work with the Digital Eclipse team across a variety of upcoming projects. You ll collaborate with a cross-discipline team to craft our documentary-style, classic game compilations as well as totally original titles and help take these projects to the finish line, solving problems and improving our internal development processes along the way. You ll report to the Head of Production and interact directly with all disciplines of the development team. The ideal Producer is organized, proactive, and an effective communicator who takes ownership of their projects, anticipates challenges, exercises sound judgment, and continuously looks for ways to improve how we work.

What You’ll Do

Own the day-to-day production and delivery of one or more projects, maintaining schedules, tracking milestones and dependencies, proactively managing risks, and ensuring issues are addressed or escalated in a timely manner.

Work with development leads to establish priorities, manage scope, and balance quality, schedule, budget, and available resources throughout development.

Coordinate communication across development teams and external partners, including Publishing, QA, Localization, and Licensing, providing clear visibility into project progress, risks, dependencies, and decisions requiring support.

Learn new production, design, and communication techniques as needed per project requirements

Contribute to improving production practices, including milestone planning, risk management, reporting, and development workflows, while supporting the studio s creative and collaborative culture.

Must Haves

4+ years of experience in game production, with demonstrated responsibility for managing development schedules, milestones, and cross-functional teams.

Strong project management skills, with demonstrated experience managing schedules, budgets, resources, dependencies, and delivery risks across the development lifecycle.

Experience contributing to at least one shipped game, with an understanding of the development, certification, and launch process.

Excellent written and verbal communication skills

Demonstrated strong critical thinking and problem solving skills

Demonstrated strong project management skills, balancing quality, time and budget constraints

Demonstrated effective team-building skills between stakeholders, including licensing partners, internal and external teams

Solid knowledge of Jira, Google Docs, and MS Office

Demonstrated ability to communicate project status clearly and concisely, proactively identifying risks, recommending mitigation plans, and escalating issues when appropriate.

Bonus Points

University-level degree

Video Game Production or similar Project Management related training

Detail orientation without forgetting the bigger picture

Additional skills outside of production, including art, writing, coding, scripting, etc., that could assist in a game development environment
