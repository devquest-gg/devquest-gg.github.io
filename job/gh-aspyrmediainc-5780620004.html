**Please note that all positions posted for Aspyr Media, Inc. are in-person/in-office only; remote positions are not available.**

Aspyr is a leading game publisher and developer, bringing the world s biggest franchises and bold new projects to players everywhere.

Since 1996, we’ve published over 200 games and our team is dedicated to bringing beloved franchises like Tomb Raider and Star Wars to new and familiar audiences across all platforms.

We focus on collaboration and creativity, crafting high-quality, engaging games that leave a lasting impact on players worldwide.

Responsibilities:

The Contractor - Associate Game Producer supports project momentum and quality by coordinating cross-functional efforts and resolving production roadblocks. This person executes task tracking and feature implementation while prioritizing rigorous data discipline to ensure accurate reporting and informed decision making. Through schedule management and workflow optimization, they will deliver high quality internal game development projects.

Support the schedule execution and track milestones to ensure the game ships on time

Work with leads to clear developmental roadblocks and keep everyone moving in the same direction

Keep the team and stakeholders in the loop with updates

Coordinate cross-functional meetings, document takeaways, and follow up on action items

Ensure project documentation accuracy

Maintain efficiency and organization among the development team through communication and task tracking

Preferred Qualifications:

Bachelor’s degree or equivalent work experience

2+ years of experience in game development production

Familiarity with production workflows, milestone planning, and internal development pipelines

Demonstrated ability to manage tasks across teams and support the execution of schedules and requirements

Strong organizational skills and attention to detail, with a track record of supporting multiple projects simultaneously

Effective communicator with experience in cross-disciplinary coordination

Understanding of production methodologies, with exposure to Agile or similar development frameworks
