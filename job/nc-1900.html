Data Analyst

Nordcurrent · Vilnius

Mid Data & Analytics Posted 2023-05-31 Full time
Apply on Nordcurrent’s site →

As a Data Analyst, you will collaborate with Game Designers, Data Engineers, and Data Scientists to create reports and visualizations, automate data processing pipelines, and build data management systems. Your primary responsibility will be to translate business needs to technical requirements and to ensure we are meeting our players needs and providing a great gaming experience.

If you are a data-driven individual with a passion for games and a solid technical background, we encourage you to apply and join our dynamic team of professionals.

Your Key Responsibilities:

Translate business needs to technical requirements and AB-tests;

Maintain analytical dashboards;

Create ad-hoc reports and visualizations;

Automate data processing pipelines;

Define analytical events schema and work with development teams to ensure correct implementation.

A Successful Candidate Must Have:

Passion for games;

Experience with cross-functional interaction and the ability to communicate comfortably in business terms;

Excellent knowledge of and experience with SQL and Python;

Knowledge of mathematics at a college-degree level;

Familiarity with massive data sets and tools to deal with them (BigQuery/AWS/Hadoop etc.).

Extra Great to Have:

Knowledge of Clickhouse;

Familiarity with Apache Superset and/or Apache Airflow;

Knowledge of the principles of work via Git, GitLab;

Experience with Pandas, NumPy, Matplotlib;

Knowledge of CatBoost;

Experience with Flask, FastAPI;

Knowledge of Docker.

Salary: From 2630 EUR/month (gross)
