Experienced QA Mobile Game Tester

Nordcurrent · Vilnius

Mid QA Posted 2022-12-07 Full time
Apply on Nordcurrent’s site →

Join Lithuania s largest gaming studio, where 250 dedicated professionals are committed to producing world-class games. With your experience and expertise, you will enhance our game quality control. You will be at the forefront of identifying and reporting issues, actively engaging in their resolution through collaboration with our development teams. You will also design test scenarios and conduct quality control on new features, continuously refining our testing methods to boost the overall effectiveness of our quality assurance processes.

Your Key Responsibilities:

Maintaining quality control throughout the entire testing cycle :

Independently implement test scenarios to validate the functionality of new/existing game features.

Perform non-functional tests.

Manage and update test documentation.

Defect identification and reporting :

Identify defects arising from unexpected game behavior.

Create detailed bug reports.

Production issues monitoring :

Monitor technical anomalies and disruptions within the game production environment.

Identify defects based on player feedback and complaints.

Communication :

Maintain clear communication with the QA team, game developers and other cross functional teams regarding tasks, test plans and the status of identified defects.

Provide feedback and collaborate to prioritize and address defects.

A Successful Candidate Must Have:

Over 2 years of experience in the QA (mobile software) field, including creating test scenarios/cases, executing various types of tests, and managing production defects.

Have a strong knowledge of software quality control methodologies.

Extensive experience using QA tools such as TestRail, TestLink, Jira (or similar).

Demonstrate good spoken and written English language skills.

Love for video games.

Extra Great to Have:

Experience with mobile game testing.

Basic knowledge of software development tools, such as Android Studio, Xcode (or similar).

Knowledge about software client-server communication.

Salary:  From 2210 EUR/month (gross)
