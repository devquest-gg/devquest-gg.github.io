Job Overview

Wargaming is looking for a Manual QA Engineer to join the Global IT Products department and help ensure the quality of internal corporate systems and services. You will work with a variety of products, from web applications to complex microservices and integrations, applying testing approaches and techniques based on the specific needs of each product.

The role offers an opportunity to work with complex business logic, large datasets, and different types of internal systems while taking ownership of the quality of products.

We also value initiative: while the core tools and approaches are defined by each product s needs, we encourage QA Engineers to identify opportunities for improvement and suggest additional tools or practices when they can bring value to the product and team.

Reports to

Head of Quality Assurance

What will you do

Independent implementation of the full cycle of project testing (functional testing of web applications, microservices, API)

Product testing without ready requirements

Creation and maintenance of test documentation

Acceptance of builds before releases, quality control of defect fixes

Monitoring of reports on errors and failures

Analysis and testing of project/product requirements

Evaluation of testing work

Participation in team meetings

What are we looking for?

2+ years of experience as a QA Engineer

Strong analytical skills and experience working with big datasets, calculations, corporate applications with complex business logic

Experience in testing web applications and microservices

Strong knowledge of testing methodologies and software development life cycles

Understanding of client-server architecture principles

Experience in writing test cases and checklists

Experience with bug and task tracking, documentation, and test case management tools (Jira, Confluence, TestRail)

Experience working with databases (PostgreSQL, MS SQL Server) and SQL

Good Excel skills and experience working with data, calculations, and spreadsheets.

Fast learner

Upper-Intermediate or higher level of English

Comfortable with switching between tasks and projects when needed - flexibility is essential in our workflow

Open to working with legacy or outdated documentation without frustration

Communicates respectfully and constructively, even in challenging situations

Proactive mindset - we value teammates who ask questions, seek clarity, and strive to improve processes

Able to work independently and take responsibility for assigned tasks

Previous experience in ERP, HRIS, payroll, or similar domains is a plus

Strong attention to detail and ability to stay focused while working with complex calculations and large datasets

What additional skills will help you stand out?

Working with API (Postman), SoapUI, and web services

Work mode

Hybrid (2-3 days of work from the office)

This role isn t eligible for relocation immigration support

About Wargaming

Wargaming is an award-winning online game developer and publisher headquartered in Nicosia, Cyprus. Operating since 1998, Wargaming has become one of the leaders in the gaming industry with 15 offices worldwide, including studios in Chicago, Prague, Shanghai, Tokyo, and Vilnius. Our diverse and multicultural team works together to deliver a top-class experience to millions of players who enjoy Wargaming s titles across all major gaming platforms. Our flagship products include free-to-play hits World of Tanks, World of Warships and World of Tanks Blitz.
