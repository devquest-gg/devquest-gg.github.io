Job Overview

Wargaming is looking for a LiveOps Monetization Manager to join the World of Warships team in our Belgrade office.

You will become part of the Live Operations Monetization Team. The team responsible for driving revenue performance, shaping in-game monetization activities, and keeping millions of players engaged through live service operations.

This role is perfect for someone who combines a strong business mindset with analytical thinking and a genuine passion for games. You’ll work at the intersection of monetization, player experience, analytics, and live operations, directly influencing the performance of one of the most successful live-service PC games in the industry.

Reports to

Associate Monetization Team Lead

What will you do?

Drive revenue KPIs through in-game events, offers, promotions, and monetization initiatives

Plan and operate live activities that improve player engagement and retention

Collaborate closely with Game Design, Product, Analytics, Publishing, and Development teams

Analyze player behavior, monetization funnels, and feature performance to identify growth opportunities

Develop and test monetization hypotheses and evaluate experiment results

Participate in planning and execution of major in-game events and releases

Support publishing and regional initiatives from the monetization perspective

Contribute to the long-term monetization strategy of World of Warships

What are we looking for?

Strong understanding of F2P game monetization principles

Analytical mindset with the ability to work confidently with data

Solid Excel / Google Sheets skills

Understanding of core product and business metrics

Basic knowledge of unit economics and KPI-driven product management

Strong communication and collaboration skills

Business-oriented and proactive approach to problem-solving

Intermediate English or higher

Readiness for full-time office work in Belgrade

What additional skills will help you stand out?

Experience in LiveOps, Product Management, Monetization, or Game Economy

Experience in the gaming industry

Experience working with A/B tests and data analysis

Russian language skills

Passion for games, especially online or live-service titles

Work mode

Hybrid (2 days of work from the office)

This role isn t eligible for relocation immigration support.

About Wargaming

Wargaming is an award-winning online game developer and publisher headquartered in Nicosia, Cyprus. Operating since 1998, Wargaming has become one of the leaders in the gaming industry with 15 offices worldwide, including studios in Chicago, Prague, Shanghai, Tokyo, and Vilnius. Our diverse and multicultural team works together to deliver a top-class experience to millions of players who enjoy Wargaming s titles across all major gaming platforms. Our flagship products include free-to-play hits World of Tanks, World of Warships and World of Tanks Blitz.
