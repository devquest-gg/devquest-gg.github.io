The opportunity Come join Unity and play a pivotal role in our global tax team. As the Director of Tax Operations you will manage Unity’s global tax provision, US and Americas tax compliance, and play a pivotal strategic role on our team as we streamline and transform our processes for the future. We value curiosity and encourage new ideas that will challenge the status-quo! We move with a bias for action, partner closely with Accounting, FP&A, and Finance Systems, and use AI to make the team more productive.

Inclusion, at Unity, is driven by one overarching framework: Empathy, Respect, and Opportunity. In a collaborative, fast-growing environment, Unity is democratizing development, solving hard problems, and enabling the success of our community. At Unity, you’ll make a difference.

Come join our incredible team! What you’ll be doing Managing the consolidated tax provision, quarterly filings, and other tax-related financial statements in compliance with US Generally Accepted Accounting Principles (GAAP), and leading the tax operations team in completing the end to end provision process, from planning and forecasting through calculations, recording, analysis, audit, and reporting Managing the team (internal and external) to deliver all US and AMER income tax reporting including federal and state tax returns, and US and Canada R&D tax incentive efforts Driving continuous process improvements to standardize and streamline the global tax provision process, while applying a risk-based approach to controls and ensuring processes, documentation and related systems are in compliance with Sarbanes Oxley requirements Ensuring accuracy of the reported effective tax rate components as well as the forecasted effective tax rate and cash taxes globally, applying a risk- and materiality-based approach to prioritize tax technical analysis, documentation, and team resources where the risk is greatest Championing operational excellence through process improvement, leveraging best practices, driving the evolution of tax software tools, and leading adoption of AI and automation to enhance team productivity, efficiency and scalability Partnering with Accounting, FP&A, Treasury, Legal, and Finance Systems to deliver accurate tax outputs, embed tax considerations into business decisions, and translate tax requirements into clear asks for non-tax stakeholders Coaching and developing the tax operations team and managing external advisors as extensions of the team What we’re looking for Demonstrated expertise in accounting for income tax issues and US GAAP, with the ability to prioritize tax technical positions and related documentation, using sound judgment on risk and materiality to focus effort where it matters 10 years of experience in global corporate tax, including public accounting experience, and 3 years managing a team in a public company setting or corporate tax department Highly organized individual with a proven ability to effectively manage tasks, prioritize responsibilities, and coordinate multiple projects simultaneously Excellent communication skills, with an ability to explain complex tax topics in digestible terms to management and cross-functional teams, build trusted working relationships with non-tax partners, and influence and drive results across teams without direct authority Strong bias for action: makes timely, risk-informed decisions and moves work forward with incomplete information Curiosity and hands-on experience applying AI tools to improve team productivity, with a track record of simplifying or automating tax processes and measurable results such as time saved or errors reduced You might also have Self-starter with drive and a team-player, with experience leading in a global environment Ability to accurately identify problems and apply efficient problem-solving skills to drive resolution Bachelor’s degree in accounting or finance, CPA preferred, Masters degree is a plus Experience with complex ASC 740 areas such as valuation allowances, stock-based compensation, acquisition accounting, and Pillar Two Experience in the technology industry is a strong plus Additional information Reports to: Senior Director, Global Tax Location: New York, in office 3 days per week.
