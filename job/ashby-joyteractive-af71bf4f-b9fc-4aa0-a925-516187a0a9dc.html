We’re on the hunt for a skilled User Acquisition Manager with experience in incentivized traffic to build and scale this new acquisition channel across our mobile games.

What are you working on?

- Genres: Puzzle, Word Games, Casual

- Platforms: Mobile, iOS, Android

For which tasks (responsibilities)?

- Launch and scale incentivized traffic as part of the overall UA strategy

- Work with offerwall and rewarded networks: Mistplay, Adjoe, Exmox, Tapjoy, and similar platforms

- Select and optimize payout models (CPI / CPA / CPE), event-based goals, and partnership terms

- Analyze traffic quality: event completion speed, post-install behavior, retention, and fraud

- Track cohort profitability and analyze the full funnel from install to revenue

- Collaborate with Product and Monetization teams to improve incentivized user conversion through milestone structure, early-game progression, and reward pacing

What kind of professional are we looking for?

- 2+ years in mobile UA, including at least 1 year in incentivized traffic / offerwall

- Strong understanding of the rewarded ecosystem, including event goals, payouts, and network auction mechanics

- Experience with IAA / hybrid monetization

- Data-driven approach to decision-making based on ROI, payback, and LTV

- Hands-on experience with MMPs (AppsFlyer, Adjust): event tracking, attribution, and fraud prevention

- Ability to independently manage the channel and build processes from scratch

Nice to Have

- Experience with puzzle / word games

- Experience with long LTV projects (180+ day payback)

- Experience in fraud prevention and traffic quality control

- Experience designing progression systems for incentivized users
