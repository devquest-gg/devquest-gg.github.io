HUNDREDS OF MILLIONS OF PEOPLE PLAY OUR GAMES WITH FRIENDS, NEW AND OLD. WE WANT OUR GAMES TO BE THE BEST PLACES IN THE WORLD TO DO THAT, WHICH MEANS KEEPING THEM SAFE, FAIR, AND WELCOMING, ESPECIALLY FOR OUR YOUNGEST AND MOST VULNERABLE PLAYERS.

As Player Safety Manager, you'll own the strategy for some of our biggest player safety and wellbeing challenges. You'll decide which problems matter most, define what success looks like, and bring the right people together to deliver it. There's no fixed playbook: you'll join a team that's rethinking how Trust & Safety works.

WHAT YOU'LL OWN

The challenges are big: spotting harmful behaviour before players report it, reducing the disruptive behaviour that drives players away, building safety into new social features like voice chat or other forms of UGC, protecting vulnerable players from serious harm, and using novel tech and civility-by-design to change what's possible.

You'll own these problems end to end, from understanding them to delivering measurable results. You'll work closely with our independent game teams, Trust & Safety Operations, Product & Engineering, Player Platform, and Legal. You'll be accountable for outcomes that depend on people outside your reporting line.

[https://app.ashbyhq.com/api/images/user-content/1246dead-d30e-4c70-a1d3-ef7e5023d542/7a053edc-bf2e-4db4-85ee-5630e2b5d305/banner_narrow_customs.jpg]

WHAT YOU'LL BE DOING

- Set strategy. Use research, data, operational insight, and outside expertise to spot emerging risks, set priorities, and define outcomes for your areas of player safety.

- Prevent harm and disruption. Turn strategy into policies, programmes, and interventions that shift us from reactive enforcement to proactive prevention. Work with Trust & Safety Operations so programmes scale, and with our High Risk & Safety Investigations Manager to tackle serious harm upstream.

- Shape our games. Work with game teams early on new social features. Turn safety needs into product and technical requirements, including proactive detection, tech-assisted moderation, and player safety tools.

- Stay ahead of regulation. Track online safety regulation in key markets and work with Legal so our policies, products, and processes are ready before new requirements take effect.

- Represent our thinking. Build relationships with researchers, NGOs, industry partners, and policymakers, and help shape Supercell's voice on player safety.

- Measure and improve. Define how we measure success, learn what works, and keep improving.

WHAT YOU HAVE

- Significant experience building strategy, policy, or programmes in Trust & Safety, platform governance, child protection, or a similarly complex field.

- A strong grasp of online behaviour and what makes social spaces healthy, and the systems thinking to look past individual incidents to their causes.

- A track record of owning complex problems and delivering results across teams through influence, not authority.

- Working knowledge of safety technology (content moderation, automated detection, AI) and global online safety regulation.

- Clear communication and sound judgement on sensitive issues, including with senior stakeholders.

- Empathy for players and a genuine interest in games.

WOULD BE NICE IF YOU ALSO HAVE

- Experience in child safety, youth wellbeing, or age-appropriate design.

- Experience with high-risk harms such as exploitation, credible threats, self-harm, or real-world violence.

- Experience working with regulators, NGOs, or industry bodies across international markets.

WHAT SUCCESS LOOKS LIKE

- We have a clear, evidence-based view of the biggest risks to our players and how we're addressing them.

- We prevent more harm upstream and rely less on reactive enforcement.

- Players encounter less disruptive behaviour, and we can show its impact on engagement and retention.

- Game teams come to us early, and new social features are designed with safety in mind.

- We're ready for new regulation before it arrives.

WHERE YOU'LL BE

We are recruiting this role to be based out of our Helsinki or London offices with travel required between each.

ABOUT SUPERCELL

Supercell is a games company from Helsinki, Finland, with offices https://supr.cl/jd-offices also in San Francisco, Seoul, Shanghai, and London. You might know us as the makers of Hay Day, Clash of Clans, Boom Beach, Clash Royale, and Brawl Stars. Our mission is to create great games https://supr.cl/jd-games that as many people as possible play for years and that are remembered forever.

So, how do we make great games? By putting together the best teams and giving them the freedom and independence to succeed. And by taking risks, failing, sharing learnings https://supercell.com/en/news/learning-from-failures/, and killing lots of projects.
