As a Data Developer Trainee that will join the Corporate Domain, one of the first missions will be to become familiar with the existing ETL solutions covering the Corporate domains like HR and IT sub-domains.

The objectives are to ensure the solution’s correct functioning, identify ways to support improvements under the guidance of experienced team members according to needs while always focusing on data value and quality.

Also, you will participate in other team projects while expanding your knowledge and skills.

This solution is part of a platform, currently relying on the following technologies: Snowflake and Databricks for Data Warehousing GitLab versioning and a custom, internal ETL framework Power BI as official analytics platform What you'll do: Deepen knowledge on Data warehouse techniques and practices by receiving onboarding and guidance from experienced colleagues Learn the team’s development standards, deployment process, and documentation practices Develop and maintain SQL ETL jobs and related data warehouse models, objects, and supporting systems using internal frameworks Test implemented solutions and analyze potential data quality issues to determine the root cause Create and maintain clear technical documentation based on existing models for assigned developments Actively participate in team ceremonies with contributing ideas and constructive input to support team collaboration and continuous improvement Collaborate with IT and business partners and learn to communicate data solutions and best practices clearly. &#xa0;
