ABOUT VOODOO

Founded in 2013, Voodoo is a tech company that creates mobile games and apps with a mission to entertain the world. Gathering 800 employees, 7 billion downloads, and over 200 million active users, Voodoo is the #3 mobile publisher worldwide in terms of downloads after Google and Meta. Our portfolio includes chart-topping games like Mob Control and Block Jam, alongside popular apps such as BeReal and Wizz.

TEAM

At WeMoms, we understand the complexities of motherhood. More than an app, we're a guiding compass for mothers globally, making motherhood feel intuitive and supported.

Our vision is to create an ever-present circle of trust, wisdom, and support for every mom. We deliver personalized, empowering guidance and resources to mothers at every stage of their journey.

Our partnership with Voodoo, a global leader in mobile gaming and apps, propels us on a hyper-growth path. We are a passionate team of 30 professionals.

As we embark on this exciting growth phase, we're looking for a Community Manager to help us scale our social presence and create high-performing content.

ROLE

WeMoms is looking for a Community Manager to join our Studio team and play a key role in shaping and growing our social media presence.

You will be at the intersection of content strategy and execution, ensuring that our platforms (Instagram, TikTok, etc.) deliver high-performing, relevant, and engaging content.

KEY RESPONSIBILITIES

CONTENT STRATEGY & IDEATION

- Contribute to building and managing the editorial calendar

- Propose engaging and potentially viral content concepts

- Identify and leverage trends (formats, sounds, storytelling angles)

- Source relevant high-performing content (UGC, videos, trends)

SOCIAL MEDIA EXECUTION

- Publish content across platforms (Instagram, TikTok, etc.)

- Optimize posts based on platform best practices (formats, timing, hooks)

- Adapt content to evolving algorithms and user behaviors

PERFORMANCE & INSIGHTS

- Track content performance (reach, engagement, watch time, etc.)

- Analyze what works and what doesn’t

- Provide actionable recommendations (formats, timing, angles)

PROFILE

- You have a first experience in community management (internship or apprenticeship included)

- You are highly fluent in social media codes, especially TikTok and Instagram

- You understand algorithms and engagement mechanics

- You have a strong instinct for viral content and trends

- You are curious, proactive, and constantly monitoring trends

- You are organized and able to manage an editorial calendar

- You enjoy analyzing performance and extracting insights

- Native or Bilingual in French

SKILLS & TOOLS

- Comfortable with creative tools (Photoshop, Premiere Pro, After Effects is a plus)

- Strong video editing skills (basic to advanced)

- Familiar with AI tools for content ideation and optimization

- Strong creative sensitivity (format, rhythm, storytelling)
