Reporting to the Senior Accountant , the Accountant Trainee will support the Finance team in ensuring the accuracy and integrity of the company's accounting records while gaining hands-on experience across key accounting processes.

What We're Looking For * A university agreement (convenio universitario) is required for this position ** We will not consider applications that are not eligible to a university agreement The ideal candidate has a solid understanding or interest in learning fundamental accounting principles, including double-entry bookkeeping, accruals, prudence, and other core accounting concepts.

They should be comfortable recording routine accounting transactions and preparing journal entries accurately and confidently.

Key Responsibilities As an Accountant Trainee, you will: Support the day-to-day general accounting activities of the company, with a primary focus on processing and recording supplier invoices.

Ensure the correct VAT treatment and coding of invoices to support accurate tax reporting and compliance with the Spanish Tax Agency (AEAT).

Record and maintain fixed assets within the asset management module, ensuring monthly depreciation calculations are accurate and properly reflected in the accounts.

Assist with bank reconciliations and investigate any discrepancies identified.

Participate in the monthly reporting process, including the preparation and analysis of operating expenses and sales invoices.

Collaborate with the Finance team to ensure accurate and timely financial information.

This position offers an excellent opportunity for someone looking to develop their accounting career in a dynamic multinational environment while working closely with an experienced Senior Accountant.
