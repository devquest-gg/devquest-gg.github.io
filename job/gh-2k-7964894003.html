Vous aimez les jeux vidéo? Vous aimez les langues? Ce que vous ferez

Réaliser des passes de test sur les versions localisées afin de vérifier l exactitude linguistique, fonctionnelle et visuelle.

Identifier et consigner les bogues de localisation, des problèmes de traduction aux défauts d interface, en fournissant des étapes détaillées et reproductibles.

Veiller à la cohérence de l orthographe, de la grammaire, de la terminologie, du ton, de l utilisation des variables et de la pertinence culturelle.

Corriger ou proposer des modifications et des traductions pour les problèmes linguistiques objectifs, directement dans les systèmes de gestion de texte.

Analyser les problèmes signalés afin d en déterminer les causes potentielles et de repérer les tendances révélatrices de risques systémiques de localisation ou d internationalisation (i18n).

Signaler les risques de localisation tôt dans le cycle de développement, y compris les problèmes potentiels liés aux variables, aux contraintes d interface ou à la clarté du texte source.

Contribuer à la validation des glossaires et des guides de style dans le cadre des cycles de révision qualité plus larges.

Suivre les plans de test documentés et contribuer à la création et à l amélioration des cas de test.

Évaluer la qualité de la localisation au regard des normes de certification, de la terminologie des fabricants et des exigences de marque.

Examiner les tickets de localisation et les signalements de joueurs, en aidant à reproduire et à faire remonter les problèmes confirmés.

Participer aux réunions quotidiennes de l équipe LQA, au tri des bogues et aux revues qualité de jalon.

Se tenir à jour sur la terminologie clé des projets et servir de référence pour votre langue maternelle.

TITLE : Localization Quality Assurance Analyst - LQA Arabic

LOCATION : Montreal, Canada

DEPT : Global Localization

COMPANY : 2K (Take-Two Interactive)

TYPE : Full-Time, onsite

WHAT WE NEED

Love games? Love languages? As part of 2K’s Global Loc 2.0 initiative, we’re building a more embedded and quality-focused approach to localization—and LQA Analysts are at the heart of it. As an LQA Analyst I, you ll be among the first to experience our games in your native language and help shape how they resonate with players across the globe.

This is an entry-level role perfect for linguistically sharp, detail-oriented professionals looking to break into games or localization. You’ll test pre-release content for linguistic accuracy, cultural relevance, and UI functionality—while learning the tools, systems, and collaboration models that bring 2K’s global experiences to life.

WHAT YOU WILL DO

Execute test passes on localized builds to verify linguistic, functional, and visual accuracy

Identify and log localization bugs—ranging from translation issues to UI problems—using detailed, reproducible steps

Ensure consistency in spelling, grammar, terminology, tone, variable usage, and cultural appropriateness

Amend or propose edits and translations to objective linguistic issues directly in text management systems

Analyze reported issues to identify potential root causes and flag patterns that may indicate systemic localization or i18n risks.

Flag localization risks early in the development cycle, including potential issues with variables, UI constraints, or source text clarity

Support glossary and style guide validation as part of broader quality review cycles

Follow documented test plans and contribute to test case creation and improvement

Evaluate localization quality against first-party standards, platform-holder terminology, and branding requirements

Review localization tickets or player reports, helping reproduce and escalate verified issues

Participate in LQA team standups, bug triage, and milestone QA reviews

Stay current on key project terminology and serve as a resource for your native language Arabic

What Will Make You A Great Fit

Native fluency in your supported language - Arabic

Strong written and verbal English communication skills

1–3 years of experience in QA (functional QA, localization QA, or related testing roles), with demonstrated linguistic proficiency in the supported language.

Experience with game consoles, mobile platforms, or game development terminology

Familiarity with first-party certification standards (Sony, Nintendo, Microsoft)

Familiarity with QA and localization workflows, including bug logging and test execution

Knowledge of JIRA, bug tracking platforms, or text management systems

Comfortable with Microsoft Office, Google Workspace, and version control platforms

Proactive, detail-oriented, and organized, with a collaborative mindset

Ability to work under deadlines, follow instructions, and ask questions when needed

Bachelor s degree or equivalent experience required

Passion for video games and cross-cultural storytelling

Exposure to localization or CAT tools (e.g., Trados, MemoQ, XLOC)

Experience working with or overseeing external QA partners or freelance reviewers

ISTQB certification or equivalent QA accreditation

Knowledge of regional age rating standards , particularly in supported territories

WHY JOIN US?

As an LQA Analyst I , you’ll begin your journey at the heart of 2K’s localization efforts—helping ensure our games are clear, culturally relevant, and enjoyable for players around the world. You’ll build foundational skills in linguistic QA within a collaborative, multilingual environment, while gaining hands-on experience with the tools, processes, and workflows that support global game development.
