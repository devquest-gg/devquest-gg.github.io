Job Summary

This position is a 5-month contract role.

The Social Media Specialist’s key objective is to strategize, develop, and grow NC America’s social media accounts for multiple game titles on behalf of NC America and NC’s portfolio for the Western market. They will be responsible for creating original text and video conte4nt, managing posts, and responding to followers. This contract role will manage our company image in a cohesive way to achieve our marketing goals, assist and support the Brand Marketing team to publicize and market NC America titles by directly engaging in social channels and amplifying excitement across a global audience. The Social Media Specialist will aid the Marketing team and Community leads by managing social media, social engagement, and analyzing campaign performance.

Key Responsibilities:

· Develop and implement social media strategies aligned with brand goals.

· Set specific channel growth objectives and report on ROI.

· Create and curate engaging content for various platforms including X.com, Instagram, YouTube, TikTok, etc. (e.g., text, images, video).

· Collaborate with NCA and external Community teams.

· Manage social media accounts and campaigns, including posting and monitoring.

· Analyze social media data to identify trends, measure performance, and optimize strategies.

· Engage with followers, respond to comments and inquiries, and resolve issues.

· Stay updated on social media trends and new features.

· Collaborate with other departments to ensure consistent messaging and improvement of social media experiences.
