🚀 We are Joyteractive - a leading mobile game developer and publisher specializing in the captivating world of puzzle games. Today, our team boasts 80+ rock star professionals, and we're on the lookout for talented individuals to help us dominate the world of puzzles!

🔝 Our leader is Cryptogram: Word Brain Puzzle - #3 spot among all iOS/Android word games in the US.

We're looking for an experienced Game Designer to strengthen our RnD team.

For which tasks (responsibilities)?

- Drive the evolution of core gameplay by proposing, validating and refining new mechanics, features and gameplay improvements.

- Work closely with the Producer to shape game design solutions, evaluate ideas and define development priorities.

- Analyze player behavior and game metrics to make informed design decisions and continuously improve the player experience.

- Research competitors and market trends to identify opportunities and translate insights into product improvements.

- Create and maintain clear game design documentation (GDDs, feature specifications, balance documentation).

- Balance gameplay, progression, rewards and difficulty to deliver an engaging long-term player experience.

- Maintain and prioritize the design backlog, ensuring ideas are well documented and ready for implementation.

- Collaborate with developers, artists and QA throughout the feature development process.

What kind of professional are we looking for?

- 2+ years of commercial experience as a Game Designer on mobile games.

- Strong understanding of core gameplay design and player motivation.

- Proven ability to proactively identify product opportunities and independently propose design solutions.

- Experience working with game metrics and applying data to design decisions.

- Strong analytical mindset and systematic approach to competitor research.

- Understanding of mobile F2P design principles, progression and monetization.

- Excellent documentation and communication skills.

- Passion for mobile games and genuine interest in following market trends and new releases.

- Experience with puzzle or casual mobile games will be a strong advantage.
