UI/UX Designer

Moon Active · Tel Aviv · Remote

Mid Design Remote Posted 2026-09-08 Full time
Apply on Moon Active’s site →

Moon Active is one of the world's fastest-growing mobile game companies, providing entertainment to millions of active users across the universe. The company is headquartered in the heart of Tel Aviv.

As a UI/UX Designer at Moon Active, you will play a crucial role in creating exceptional user experiences for our mobile games. You will work closely with the Product Manager to understand the exact feature requirements and gather feedback to ensure the product meets user expectations. You will also collaborate with other teams to conduct user testing and improve/create user flow scenarios.

Join our team and be part of creating engaging and delightful user experiences for millions of players worldwide!

Responsibilities

- Collaborate closely with the PM to understand feature requirements, gather feedback, and contribute to product strategy.

- Create wireframes, black and white mockups, and interactive prototypes with simple animations to visualize the user journey.

- Ideate and sketch necessary assets to support the design process.

- Execute the final designs of screens and graphic elements, ensuring a consistent and visually appealing user interface.

- Complete the UX/UI part of the Product Requirements Document (PRD), ensuring that it reflects the desired user experience.סס

- Support the feature's full life cycle, from concept to release, by collaborating with developers, artists, and other relevant stakeholders.

- Work closely with user feedback to continuously improve the user experience.

Requirements

- 2+ years of proven experience as a UX/UI designer in the mobile gaming industry.

- Proficient in design tools such as Figma and Adobe Creative Suite

- Proven experience with generative AI tools (e.g. Layer AI, ComfyUI) to enhance the design process.

- Strong understanding of user-centered design principles and methodologies.

- Ability to create visually appealing designs while considering usability and accessibility.

- Excellent communication and collaboration skills to effectively work with cross-functional teams and gather feedback from stakeholders.

- Analytical mindset and attention to detail to identify and address usability issues.

- Strong design, drawing, color, composition, and conceptual ideation abilities.

- Knowledge of mobile design best practices and the latest trends in UX/UI design.
