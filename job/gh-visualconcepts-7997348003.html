Lead Engineer, Dev Ops - WWE 2K

Visual Concepts · California

Lead Engineering Posted 2026-09-16 Full time
Apply on Visual Concepts’s site →

At Visual Concepts, we believe great games are made by diverse and empowered teams with a shared passion for play. As one of the world’s top game development studios, we have shipped over 100 multi-sku titles to critical acclaim and commercial success. Our teams are independent and entrepreneurial. Our studios in Novato, Irvine, Budapest, Shanghai, Austin, Boise, Seoul, and Parksville, Canada are committed to artistry and technical innovation, offering top candidates the opportunity to learn and grow with some of the smartest and most creative minds in the industry.

What We Need:

The WWE 2K team is seeking a Lead Engineer to join our DevOps team, crafting the technical roadmap that supports efficient, collaborative development across the studio. In this role you will take ownership of the technical vision and strategy for the DevOps team, collaborating across teams and disciplines to create tools and systems that enable us to make the next installment of WWE 2K the best yet!

What You Will Do:

Design, maintain, and improve CI/CD pipelines, infrastructure, and deployment automation

Ensure the reliability, scalability, and performance of development and production environments

Improve developer workflows through automation and tooling, while ensuring high quality documentation and support

Establish standards and best practices for deployments, environments, observability, and operations

Collaborate with studio partners to refine cross-team workflows and processes

Act as a technical leader, developing and driving technical strategy and vision for your team. Push the limits of what is possible.

Who Will Be A Great Fit:

5+ years of experience in DevOps, SRE, or Platform Engineering.

Bachelor’s or Master’s Degree in Computer Science or related field, or equivalent experience

Experience with Perforce, Jenkins, Git, and/or other version control and automated build systems

Hands-on expertise with cloud infrastructure (AWS/GCP) and containerized environments

Experience with Infrastructure-as-Code (IaC) workflows and configuration management tools such as Puppet

Strong understanding of object oriented programming, modern architectural design patterns, and C/C++/C#

Systems engineering expertise across Windows and Linux environments, including scripting languages (Python, PowerShell, Bash) and familiarity with the .NET ecosystem

Ability to balance technical depth with cross-team collaboration and clear communication

Comfortable operating in ambiguous environments and driving alignment

Might Be Nice:

Experience working on multi-platform software projects

Experience with web technologies like React or Node.js

Familiarity with leveraging AI to enhance developer workflows

Shipped game titles, preferably on console

Experience with distributed, multi-team, or multi-studio environments

Love for video games (not just ours!)
