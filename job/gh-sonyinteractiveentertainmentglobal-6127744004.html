Position – Community Social Media Intern - Fall-Winter 2026

About the Role:

We re looking for a curious, creative, and community-minded intern who s passionate about multiplayer games and the communities that form around them. This internship is designed for someone who lives online—whether that s creating content on TikTok, spending time in Discord communities, watching Twitch, or following the latest multiplayer games.

You ll work alongside our Community team to help engage players, support social and creator initiatives, and learn how community management operates inside an AAA game studio. No two days are the same—you ll contribute to player conversations, content creation, feedback analysis, creator programs, and live playtests while gaining firsthand experience helping launch a brand-new live service game.

This role offers broad exposure across Community, Marketing, Production, Player Experience, and Live Operations, making it an excellent opportunity for someone eager to build a career in the games industry.

What You’ll Do

Community Engagement

Engage daily with players across Discord and other community platforms.

Help answer questions, encourage conversations, and foster a welcoming, inclusive environment.

Identify recurring questions, trends, and emerging discussions.

Social Content:

Support the creation of short-form social content for platforms like TikTok, YouTube Shorts, Instagram, and X.

Collaborate with the Community Content Manager to plan, prepare, and publish community communications.

Assist with basic video editing, image creation, and social asset production.

Player Insights:

Monitor player conversations and sentiment across social platforms and Discord.

Capture community feedback and summarize actionable insights for development teams.

Help identify emerging community trends and opportunities.

Team Partner Coordination:

Partner with Community, Marketing, and Player Experience teams.

Learn the tools and workflows that support a modern live game.

How You’ll Be Supported

Dedicated mentorship: You ll work closely with the Community Lead, Community Content Manager, and broader Player Experience team, receiving regular coaching, feedback, and guidance throughout your internship.

Cross-functional exposure: Collaborate with teams across Production, Marketing, Creative, Design, Player Experience, and Live Operations to see how an AAA game comes together before launch.

Meaningful ownership: You ll contribute to real community initiatives, social content, playtests, and player engagement—not just observe from the sidelines.

Room to grow: You ll be encouraged to bring new ideas, experiment with creative content, and take ownership of projects while receiving support along the way.

Professional development: Gain hands-on experience with industry-standard community, social media, and collaboration tools while learning best practices for community management in the games industry.

What We’re Looking For

Currently pursuing (or recently completed) a degree in Marketing, Communications, Digital Media, Creative Media, or a related field.

Passionate about multiplayer games and the communities that surround them.

Active in online communities such as Discord, Reddit, Twitch, or similar platforms—you understand how players communicate online.

Familiar with social platforms including TikTok, YouTube Shorts, Instagram, Twitch, and X.

A creative storyteller who enjoys creating engaging social content.

Strong written communication skills and an eye for detail.

Curious, proactive, and comfortable wearing multiple hats in a fast-paced environment.

Excited to learn community management and how player feedback helps shape game development.

Based in Montreal and able to work in our studio environment.

Nice to Have:

Streaming or content creation experience.

Fluent in both English and French.

Familiarity with live service or multiplayer games.

Tools You’ll Use with Guidance

Discord

TikTok, YouTube Shorts, Instagram, X, Reddit, Steam

Photoshop, Premiere Pro, CapCut, Canva (or similar creative tools)

Jira

Google Workspace

Community analytics and social listening tools

What You’ll Gain

Hands-on experience helping launch a AAA multiplayer live service game.

Mentorship from experienced Community, Creator, Marketing, and Player Experience professionals.

Exposure to cross-functional collaboration with Production, Design, Marketing, Creative, and Live Operations teams.

Experience creating social campaigns, community content, and player engagement initiatives.

Practical experience analyzing player sentiment and turning community feedback into actionable insights.

A portfolio of published social content, creator campaigns, playtest initiatives, and community programs.

A deep understanding of how successful gaming communities are built before and after launch.
