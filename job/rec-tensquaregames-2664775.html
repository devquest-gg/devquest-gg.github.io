Location: Wrocław, Poland Work model: Hybrid, 3 days per week in the Wrocław office First 3 months: 5 days per week in the office to support onboarding and team connection About us Hi, we are Ten Square Games , and we live games! No, not a typo -but a level up from loving them. Our flagship title, Fishing Clash , has been live since 2018. It is a successful F2P game, and we are looking for a designer who can help make it clearer, deeper and more fun for players.

We create mobile games for players around the world, with a focus on outdoor experiences, competition, progression and fair play. Fishing Clash has grown into a well-established title, but we believe there is still a huge opportunity to make it more understandable, engaging and rewarding.

What is this job all about? We are looking for a hands-on Senior Game Designer to help improve Fishing Clash. This role is about understanding existing systems, finding opportunities, simplifying complexity and turning ideas into features that players can actually feel.

You will work closely with Product, Development, Analytics, UI/UX, Art and Live Ops to improve core gameplay systems, FTUE, progression, rewards and live game experiences. You will not be expected to know everything from day one — we will support you in learning the game, the players and the systems.

We need someone who can create a clear design direction, write great specs, help the team build confidence in the solution, and work closely with implementation so great gameplay moments make it into the final experience.
