Senior Concept Artist, Character (On-site Only)

Goodname · Vilnius

Senior Art Onsite Posted 2026-01-08 Full time
Apply on Goodname’s site →

In this position, you will collaborate closely with Art Director to develop early-stage ideas for game projects. As a Senior Concept Artist, Characters , you will be responsible for transforming briefing materials into final artwork that defines mood, style, and intent. You have to be able to set visual standards, offer constructive feedback, and elevate the overall quality of the team’s work.

You are expected to refine and iterate on concepts based on feedback while aligned with the projects visual vision and working confidently across both realistic and stylized art styles. Artist in this position should focus on creating immersive and realistic characters designs.

Your role:

Work closely with our art director and the rest of the team to create the best concept art and illustration possible according to clients’ briefing material.

Work on high importance projects with various schedules , so you need to adapt to any style quickly. Either realistic or stylized, you must be able to create characters and creatures.

Communicate with teammates , help one another in your everyday work to give the best of yourself. You should have a critical mind, making you receive or give feedback well.

Can be required to give some advice and guide Junior Concept Artists and illustrators with their work.

Focus on creating concept art and designs for various characters.

What we look for:

Experience in digital painting using Photoshop

Portfolio proving your level as senior concept artist showing a diversity of projects

Solid traditional art skills , anatomical knowledge, excellent aesthetic ability,

Eye for detail and excellent composition, lighting and color skills

Ability to stick to a given workflow and adjust to clients’ feedback.

Strong communication skills in English (both written and spoken).

Skills Considered as Bonus:

Ability to use 3D software

Previous experience in the game industry

Interest in games, pop culture

Please note: Applications without portfolios showcasing your work will not be considered. Also, we will contact only selected applicants after an initial portfolio and submission review.

Location: Vilnius, Lithuania Job Type: On-Site Contract: Full time

All expenses for relocation costs will be covered in full.
