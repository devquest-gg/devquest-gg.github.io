Job Summary: This role is part of the Digital Marketing department at Nintendo of America (NOA) and influences and contributes to the design of various Nintendo.com applications using best practices and programming methodology to build and write scalable code. This is an 8-month contract position.

Description of Duties:

Writes secure code and architecting/designing smaller scale applications.

Participates in all phases of the development life cycle, (planning, coding, launching) focusing on coding, testing, and debugging.

Works on the maintenance or enhancement of existing data structures.

Research, evaluates and analyzes technical and design requirements.

Prepares draft proposals regarding content, security, features, standards, and creative new approaches for the use of technology.

Plans and implements model data structures for scalability.

Participates in cross-functional discussions and effectively communicate information, both written and verbally to members of the development teams.

Maintains up-to-date knowledge of related tools, trends and developments.

Demonstrates good judgment in selecting methods and techniques for obtaining solutions.

Document all technology changes through appropriate change control.

Provides support and troubleshooting of prior developed technology solutions.

Ensures work is completed in such a way to comply with established J-SOX and other internal controls

Summary of Requirements:

Minimum five years’ experience using Java, SQL Server, and related technologies. Javascript (Node.js) experience is a plus.

Minimum five years’ experience on scalable, high-volume, internet-facing websites—preferably at the enterprise level required.

Familiarity with integrated development environments such as Visual Studio 2012, Eclipse, and version control systems such as SVN, GitHub, and TFS required

Solid verbal and written communication skills required.

Proficiency in GraphQL or REST-based web services, LINQ, XML, and JSON.

Proficient and comfortable working on multi-tier applications.

Mobile experience a plus.

Strong API development skills.

Experience with SQL databases (including T-SQL and creation/use of stored procedures and triggers).

Experience using NHibernate or Hibernate.

Experience in both web forms and MVC 3.

Excellent technical troubleshooting skills.

Conceptual understanding and proficiency in CMS tools and frameworks

Excellent time management and teamwork skills.

Bachelor degree in related field or equivalent relevant experience.

Applicants must be legally eligible to work in the United States to be considered. Visa sponsorship is not available for this role.

This position is hybrid in Redmond, WA. Hybrid positions require regular onsite work following the schedule and guidelines for their division. This position is not open to fully remote status at this time.
