Who We Are

Take-Two Interactive Software, Inc. is a leading developer, publisher, and marketer of interactive entertainment for consumers around the globe. For more than 25 years, our development teams have created some of the most critically acclaimed and commercially successful entertainment experiences, captivating and engaging audiences around the world. We are incredibly proud of our ability to consistently deliver the highest-quality titles, as well as our colleagues who help to create our unique culture and work environment that is inclusive, diverse, and dynamic.

While our offices are casual and inviting, we are deeply committed to our core tenets of creativity, innovation and efficiency, and individual and team development opportunities. Our industry and business are continually evolving and fast-paced, providing numerous opportunities to learn and hone your skills. We work hard, but we also like to have fun, and believe that we provide a great place to come to work each day to pursue your passions.

Our presence in Austin, TX, is expanding, building on the foundation established by our 2K and Zynga labels in the area. Located in the Domain/North Austin. Joining us in Austin means becoming part of a growing hub within Take-Two, surrounded by a culture of creativity, innovation, and cross-label collaboration.

The Challenge

We are seeking a Senior Talent Partner to join us as a foundational hire, spearheading the growth and success of our presence in Austin. This role will be a dual-vertical recruiter to drive full life cycle recruiting processes for both Technology and Media Services (TMS) and General Administrative (G A) corporate functions as needed.

In this position, you will champion the execution of global talent strategies for the business across North America. We are seeking a collaborative recruiter with a passion for attracting and hiring world-class talent to support our mission to become the #1 pure play interactive entertainment company.

What You’ll Take On

Lead the full talent acquisition lifecycle—from initial requisition intake and strategic sourcing through to offer negotiation and closing—for positions within the Technology/TMS (Infrastructure, Cyber, PMO, Software Data Engineering, AI) and G+A functions (Finance, Legal, HR, Sales, Workplace, etc.), ensuring a positive candidate experience.

Partner with Global Lead Talent Partners, hiring managers, and executive leadership as a strategic talent advisor, providing expertise on market trends, compensation, the competitive talent landscape, and best practices for attracting a diverse, global talent pool.

Navigate a fast-paced, scaling environment, utilizing innovative sourcing techniques to build dynamic, high-performing teams.

Develop and execute creative, data-driven sourcing strategies to build robust pipelines of qualified candidates for complex and specialized roles.

Conduct thorough intake meetings, screen candidates for qualifications and motivation, and guide hiring teams on effective, bias-mitigated interview techniques and equitable selection best practices.

Act as a champion for the candidate and stakeholder experience, with the opportunity to optimize and lead the local recruiting experience for the Austin Hub.

Maintain meticulous record-keeping and data integrity within the Applicant Tracking System (ATS), providing regular reports on recruiting metrics and progress.

Act as a brand ambassador, promoting our unique culture to attract passive and active talent.

What You Bring

Extensive experience (5+ years) performing full life cycle recruiting supporting diverse corporate functions, with a preference for prior experience in gaming, entertainment, media/creative or technology industries.

2+ years of experience hiring technical talent in the Austin Market.

Proven experience recruiting for high-growth teams across various technical, general administration, and technology functions globally.

Proven ability to thrive in a self-directed environment, balancing multiple strategic talent priorities while maintaining a high standard of candidate care.

Ability to successfully manage through rapid organizational evolution and contribute to the transition toward a centralized Global Shared Service model.

Demonstrate commitment as a strong partner to the broader HR organization, collaborating effectively across teams to support shared goals.

A clear communicator with strong interpersonal skills, capable of influencing hiring decisions at the senior management level.

Experience using Greenhouse or comparable applicant tracking systems.
