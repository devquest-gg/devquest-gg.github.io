Office Assistant

Gameloft · Brisbane

Senior Other Posted 2026-08-14 Full time

We're looking for an enthusiastic and proactive Office Assistant to join our team on a part-time basis, with the opportunity to possibly grow into a full-time role in 2027.

This role is all about being the behind-the-scenes hero who helps keep the studio running smoothly. From keeping shared spaces organised and stocked to making sure the little details don't get missed, you'll help create an environment where our team can focus on making great games.

You’ll work closely with the Office Manager to support studio events, team celebrations, visitors, deliveries, and other projects that help make our studio a great place to work. If you get satisfaction from ticking things off a checklist and making life easier for others, and gets a kick out of creating memorable experiences, we'd love to hear from you.

What You'll Be Doing Keeping the studio running smoothly by maintaining shared spaces, kitchen stock levels, and general studio presentation Supporting the Office Manager with day-to-day tasks, requests, and special projects Assisting with the planning, organisation, and delivery of team events and celebrations Managing deliveries and liaising with suppliers, contractors, and other external vendors Contributing to studio safety, security, and workplace wellbeing initiatives Helping onboard new starters and ensuring they feel welcome from day one Creating signage, internal communications, and event materials using Canva Being an extra set of eyes and hands around the studio, proactively identifying opportunities to improve the workplace experience

What We're Looking For Experience in Office Administration, Facilities Management, Event Coordination, or a similar role Availability to work on-site 3-5 days per week (we're flexible for the right person) Excellent organisational and time management skills with strong attention to detail Confidence managing multiple priorities and adapting to changing needs Ability to maintain confidentiality and handle sensitive information appropriately Proficiency with Microsoft Office and Canva Experience with Microsoft Planner is a bonus A genuine interest in games, creative industries, or building great workplace experiences is highly regarded &#xa0; The Kind of Person Who'll Thrive Here You're not afraid to roll up your sleeves and get stuck in. No task is beneath you. One moment you could be cleaning out the fridge, the next you could be welcoming VIP visitors or helping organise a studio-wide event.

You love a well-organised to-do list, but you're also creative and enjoy bringing ideas to life. Maybe you're into crafting, DIY projects, event styling, decorating, or finding ways to make everyday experiences a little more memorable.

Most importantly, you genuinely enjoy helping others and take pride in creating a workplace that people love being part of. 🎮✨
