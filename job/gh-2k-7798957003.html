What We Need

We’re looking for a highly analytical and strategic thinker to join our team as a Manager, Commercial Strategy. In this role, you’ll help define the strategy behind how our games are launched, monetized, and supported over time. This is a 0-to-1 role. You won t be inheriting a mature P L and tuning it at the margins, instead you ll be defining the commercial model from the ground up: designing the monetization framework, building the forecasting models, standing up the KPIs and dashboards, and partnering closely with product and design as we move between development milestones toward a global launch and ongoing live operations. If you’re the kind of person who enjoys diving into data, building business cases, and translating insights into clear, actionable plans, then this is a great opportunity!

You ll sit at the intersection of product, design, marketing, data analytics, and finance, and you ll be the person who connects the dots between "what s good for the player" to "what s good for the business."

What You Will Do

Modeling Business Case Development: Build and maintain Excel-based financial and strategic models to support business cases, investment decisions, and scenario planning

KPI Frameworks Forecasting: Create and deliver forecast and reporting packages, showcasing key business metrics and financial results, to the broader Business Unit leadership teams

Continuously refine the free-to-play monetization framework, including virtual currency, battle/season pass, cosmetics, bundles, offers, etc.

Define and maintain the monetization roadmap in partnership with product and design, always balancing revenue against player experience, fairness, and long-term retention

Stand up commercial reporting and dashboards in partnership with the data / analytics team so the business has a single source of truth

Live Services P L Support: Develop a clear understanding of the drivers behind a live services P L. Interpret weekly business performance, identifying trends and levers that can be pulled to optimize player engagement and profitability

Market Competitive Analysis: Maintain a clear view of the F2P / live-service competitive landscape, including benchmarks, market sizing, and emerging trends. Translate findings into actionable recommendations to inform leadership and support strategic planning

Partner with the Product, Marketing Analytics teams to design and prioritize strategic projects that provide deep commercial insight and inform product and marketing decisions

What Will Make You a Great Fit

4-6+ years of experience in a strategy, finance, consulting, or analytics role, ideally within gaming, entertainment, or a digital consumer business

Advanced Excel / financial modeling skills; comfortable building models from scratch

Strong understanding of live service game economics and the gaming market landscape

Ability to distill complex data into clear narratives and recommendations for senior stakeholders

Experience collaborating with data teams and familiarity with data tools (e.g., SQL, Tableau, Looker) is a plus

You’re intellectually curious: you enjoy asking why, digging into the details, and connecting dots others might miss.

You’re comfortable working in gray areas: ambiguity doesn’t throw you off; in fact, you enjoy bringing structure to messy or open-ended problems.

You know how to manage up: you’re proactive in keeping stakeholders informed, setting expectations, and getting buy-in from senior leaders.
