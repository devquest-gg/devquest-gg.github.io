Mid-Level 3D Environment Artist (Contract)

Remote - Beverly Hills, CA

About Snail Games USA Snail Games USA develops and publishes digital entertainment, bringing together creative and technical teams to build engaging and immersive game experiences.

About the Role We are seeking a Mid-Level 3D Environment Artist contractor to create high-quality modular building assets, props, and game-ready environments using Blender and Unreal Engine.

You will be responsible for taking assigned buildings and environments from initial briefs and visual references through modeling, texturing, assembly, and final implementation in Unreal Engine. This includes creating architectural assets, assembling interiors and exteriors, and dressing environments that support the project’s visual direction and gameplay needs.

The ideal candidate is a self-directed artist who can independently own assigned environments, maintain consistent artistic quality, and deliver finished work without requiring step-by-step supervision.

Scope of work Create high-quality modular building pieces, architectural elements, and environmental props in Blender.

Assemble buildings, interiors, and exteriors in Unreal Engine.

Perform modeling, UV mapping, texturing, and material setup.

Create visually cohesive environments with attention to composition, proportion, material consistency, and environmental storytelling.

Prepare game-ready assets with appropriate scale, pivots, collision, naming, and organization.

Optimize environmental assets to meet established performance and technical requirements.

Take assigned environments from initial brief through final in-engine implementation.

Participate in regular art reviews and incorporate feedback throughout production.

Collaborate with artists, designers, and technical artists to ensure environments meet visual and gameplay requirements.

Independently manage assigned tasks, communicate progress, identify blockers, and meet production deadlines.

Required experience A strong portfolio demonstrating 3D environment art, including architectural assets, buildings, interiors, or complete environments.

Proficiency in Blender for creating game-ready 3D assets.

Practical production experience with Unreal Engine.

Strong understanding of modular environment construction, UV mapping, texturing, and material workflows.

Understanding of collision setup, asset optimization, scale, and pivot placement.

Strong artistic fundamentals, including composition, proportion, and attention to detail.

Ability to interpret concept art and visual references while maintaining an established artistic direction.

Ability to independently complete assigned environments from brief to final implementation.

Strong communication, organization, and problem-solving skills.

Ability to receive and implement constructive feedback.

Preferred experience Experience developing environments for open-world or large-scale games.

Familiarity with Unreal Engine Level Instances and Packed Level Actors.

Familiarity with Blueprints for reusable environment setups.

Experience developing reusable modular building kits.

Understanding of Unreal Engine 5 features, including Nanite and Lumen.

Experience with Substance Painter or Substance Designer.

Experience working within a collaborative game production pipeline.

Portfolio Submission Please include a portfolio link with your application. For collaborative projects, clearly identify your individual contributions.

Examples demonstrating both original asset creation and completed environments within Unreal Engine are strongly encouraged.

How to apply Applications will be reviewed on a rolling basis until the contract is filled. Applicants must be legally authorized to work in the United States at the time of application. Visa sponsorship, including H-1B sponsorship, is unavailable for this engagement.

Additional Information This work may involve mature content, including depictions of combat, violence, or other sensitive themes. Contractors are expected to engage with this material professionally as part of the project.
