Every day, tens of millions of people come to Roblox to explore, create, play, learn, and connect with friends in 3D immersive digital experiences– all created by our global community of developers and creators.

At Roblox, we’re building the tools and platform that empower our community to bring any experience that they can imagine to life. Our vision is to reimagine the way people come together, from anywhere in the world, and on any device.

We’re on a mission to connect a billion people with optimism and civility, and looking for amazing talent to help us get there.

A career at Roblox means you’ll be working to shape the future of human interaction, solving unique technical challenges at scale, and helping to create safer, more civil shared experiences for everyone.

You Will:

Partner with Executive Recruiters, cross functional recruiting team members, business and HR leaders to share intel and find great executive level people across Roblox

Manage executive search engagements from requisition kick-off meetings to engagement to hire

Understand and identify competitor landscape and apply intel to research strategies

Develop unique research strategies in multiple disciplines/domains simultaneously while managing high volume of data

Come up with fresh ideas and create new ways to attack problems

Clear articulation in all forms of communication with an emphasis on executive style and approach to each deliverable and presentation

You Have:

6 years of sourcing/executive search ideally with experience at an in-house recruiting team

Experience using talent market insights and data to influence decisions within the leadership groups and cross-functional teams

Sourced high-level passive executive talent; Experience within: Product, Product Design, G A, Marketing or Communications

Experience developing candidate generation and research techniques

Well versed understanding and skills in identifying and engaging executive-level candidates

Experience building working relationships with leaders and cross-functional partners

Experience advising senior management on talent trends and influence direction based on your research

Experience screening resumes to match qualifications

Experience as a Sourcer partnering with Recruiters and hiring partners across different offices
