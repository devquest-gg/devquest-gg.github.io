ABOUT THE ROLE

We are seeking a Senior Data Ops Project Manager that will serve as the primary link between product requests and engineering execution. They will ensure all projects are properly prioritized, executed, and delivered on time to the team s high quality standards, while balancing and sustaining the developers workload.

RESPONSIBILITIES

Drive or facilitate team projects based on technical complexity to ensure quality standards and deadlines are met. Act as the primary driver for familiar domains, or as a facilitator to unblock decisions and align stakeholders when navigating technical projects.

Coordinate across domains with the various teams we support and help manage the areas our team owns (data infrastructure, tracking, payments, internal tools, and GDPR maintenance etc.,) to effectively manage risks, quality standards, and scope adjustments.

Unblocking Delivery: Actively identify and clear technical blockers, dynamically adjusting timelines and keeping stakeholders informed to keep engineers focused.

Manage incoming, existing, and ad-hoc tasks to properly prioritize and meet the needs of internal and external stakeholders, while balancing capacity constraints and minimizing project delays

Oversee day-to-day project support to keep tasks on track, including leading standups and team meetings as needed.

Conduct check-ins with team members to identify workflow bottlenecks or challenges. Independently resolve recurring operational issues where possible, and escalate complex blockers to the appropriate parties.

Continuously improve the team s workflow by leading post-mortems, identifying inefficiencies, and proposing solutions to try.

Identify opportunities to support other teams or improve internal processes without overextending our team s bandwidth, always looking for ways to optimize systems across the company.

Other responsibilities as assigned.

REQUIREMENTS

Experience: 8+ years of experience in project management, production, or technical operations, ideally within gaming, data infrastructure, or live-service tech.

Technical Literacy: Strong conceptual understanding of modern software architecture, such as API integrations, database logic, and the relationship between frontend and backend systems.

Tooling: Experience using organizational tools like Jira, Basecamp, and Confluence to build workflows and dashboards that provide clear visibility into team progress and capacity.

Strategic Inquiry: An ability to talk to developers and ask the questions that uncover risks or hidden work before it’s too late.

Problem Solving: Ability to identify workflow bottlenecks and suggest structural improvements rather than simply moving tasks through a queue.

Independent Judgement: A track record of managing project workflows independently, with the professional maturity to know when to ask for guidance on high-stakes decisions.

Communication: Ability to update stakeholders on progress and coordinate with QA and Support to ensure a high-quality end product.

ABOUT DIGITAL EXTREMES

Founded in 1993 by James Schmalz, Digital Extremes ranks as one of the world s top independent video game development studios. Originating with the co-creation of Epic Games multi-million unit selling Unreal® franchise including Unreal and Unreal Tournament, Digital Extremes went on to develop Dark Sector®, BioShock® for the PlayStation®3, the BioShock 2 multiplayer campaign, and The Darkness® II. The studio has reached its greatest critical and commercial success with the free-to-play action game, Warframe® , boasting a global community of 75 million registered players on PC, PS4™, PS5™, Xbox One™, Xbox Series X/S™, Nintendo Switch™, and iOS™. The studio s most recently announced project is the much anticipated Soulframe® , an open-world action-fantasy MMORPG title that is currently under development.

Digital Extremes is headquartered in London, Ontario, Canada, and is proud to be selected as one of Canada s Top 100 Employers.
