Customer Experience Specialist

Brazil

The Customer Experience Specialist serves as a critical escalation point for regional Executive Escalations and consumer advocacy-related cases. This role requires advanced problem-solving, cross-functional collaboration, and a strong focus on delivering fair, timely, and high-quality resolutions. The specialist also partners with frontline and support teams to provide guidance, share insights, and drive improvements in processes that enhance both the player/customer and agent experience, supporting overall Quality Management System (QMS) objectives.

The Job

Executive Escalations Case Resolution

Manage and resolve high-profile regional Executive Escalations with professionalism, empathy, and a customer-first mindset.

Act as a subject-matter expert and escalation point for cases originating from consumer advocacy teams, ensuring consistent, accurate, and policy-aligned resolutions.

Partner with Legal, Compliance, and regional leadership teams to address sensitive or complex cases.

Coordinate the efforts to respond the local customer platforms such as Reclame Aqui, Procon, etc to support better legal cases management

Coordinate and support the team hired to respond directly the RA platform (Foundever), with extension to managing the contract, reviewing the KPIs, and managing necessary conflicts

Support Guidance

Provide real-time support, coaching, and guidance to frontline and support teams handling escalated cases.

Act as a trusted advisor, helping agents navigate policy interpretation, tone, and resolution strategies.

Create and share best practices and case-handling frameworks to elevate resolution quality across teams.

Undertake ad-hoc administrative responsibilities and actively participate in business-driven special projects.

Occasional travel to contact centres may be required to support training on new processes, products, services, or agent forums.

Process Improvement QMS Alignment

Proactively review case handling processes, workflows, and escalation triggers to identify opportunities for improvement.

Collaborate with cross-functional partners (Operations, Policy, Training, Quality) to design solutions that reduce friction for customers and agents.

Champion initiatives that directly improve player and agent experiences, aligning with QMS standards and driving measurable quality outcomes.

Track and analyse escalation trends to recommend actionable solutions and process enhancements.

Collaboration Reporting

Maintain strong relationships with internal stakeholders, ensuring alignment on escalation handling and process updates.

Provide timely feedback and insights to leadership regarding emerging trends, risks, or opportunities.

Contribute to reporting efforts that demonstrate impact, quality improvements, and customer experience outcomes.

The Requirements

Bachelor s degree in business, Communications, or related field (or equivalent work experience).

Proven experience handling executive or senior-level escalations, ideally in a corporate, consumer advocacy, or customer support environment.

Strong interpersonal and communication skills, with the ability to manage sensitive cases under pressure.

Demonstrated ability to analyse complex problems, identify root causes, and recommend scalable solutions.

Experience contributing to process improvement initiatives and working within a Quality Management System (QMS) framework.

High degree of empathy, resilience, and professionalism.

Strong collaboration skills with the ability to influence without authority.

Success in this Role Looks Like

Timely, fair, and policy-aligned resolutions for high-profile escalations.

Increased confidence and effectiveness among frontline teams through expert guidance and support.

Measurable improvements in QMS results, customer satisfaction, and agent experience.

Reduced escalation volume through proactive process improvements and insights.
