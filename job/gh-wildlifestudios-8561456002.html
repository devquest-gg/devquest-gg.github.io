About Wildlife

Wildlife is one of the leading mobile game developers and publishers in the world. We have released more than 60 titles, reaching billions of people around the globe. Here, we create and maintain games that will excite, intrigue, and engage our players for years to come!

About the Programs

At Wildlife Studios, our Internship Programs are designed to develop students that are eager to start their careers in the gaming industry. As an intern, you’ll work alongside experienced professionals, contribute to real projects, and be part of a company that creates games played by millions worldwide. Throughout the program, you’ll have access to technical and soft-skill training, mentorship, and structured development opportunities to prepare you for future career growth.

What You’ll Do

Support analysis, projects, and initiatives that directly impact our games and business;

Participate in technical and business cases, problem-solving challenges, and brainstorming sessions with peers and leaders;

Engage in structured learning sessions and mentorship to accelerate your development;

Bring fresh perspectives and innovative ideas to help shape the future of our products.

Where You Can Work

Regular Internship

Strategic Finance: Strategic Finance is where business, data, and storytelling meet. This is the team that turns numbers into decisions, partnering with leadership to shape how Wildlife invests, grows, and competes in the global gaming market.

Engineering (Game Tech Platform Eng): Work on the maintenance of released games, with millions of daily players. Engage in activities required to keep the apps alive as well as implementing new features to enhance player experience.

Product Management: Support product managers to plan and deliver new features, run A/B tests, and analyze data to guide product decisions. Help shape the game vision, align with engineering, art, data, and distribution, and take ownership of your deliveries with real autonomy to impact the player experience.

Data Science: Expand game potential by providing non-trivial solutions to business questions, such as how to analyze game performance, engage players, customize content, run successful experiments, and optimize game economy.

Data Engineering: Design, build, and maintain the scalable data pipelines that process billions of events from our global player base. You ll work with massive datasets, ensuring the reliability and quality of the data infrastructure that empowers data-driven decisions across the company, and collaborating closely with data scientists and analysts.

Business Marketing: User Acquisition (UA) brings players into our worlds and gets smarter every day. We mix data, code, and creativity to reach the right players across Meta, Google, and TikTok. Think of us as growth hackers who love testing, building, and optimizing. We run global campaigns, partner with engineers and data scientists, and make every decision driven by data and curiosity. In UA, your work shapes how millions of players discover and enjoy our games.

Tech Art: Tech Art is the bridge between creativity and technology. Work alongside artists and engineers to build the tools, pipelines, and shaders that bring our games to life, optimizing assets and workflows so millions of players get stunning visuals at smooth performance. From automating production processes to solving rendering challenges, you ll turn technical constraints into creative possibilities.

Visual Design (UI/UX) : Design the interfaces that millions of players interact with every day. Create intuitive flows, polished screens, and delightful interactions that make our games easy to learn and hard to put down. You ll partner with product, art, and engineering to shape how players navigate, progress, and connect with our worlds, balancing usability, aesthetics, and game feel in every decision.
