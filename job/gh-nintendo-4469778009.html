We’re looking for a highly motivated and self-starting Software Engineer Intern to join Nintendo Technology Development’s (NTD) Platform Security team for the Summer of 2027.

The Opportunity

This is an excellent opportunity for a candidate with passion for excellence and a desire to grow in a fast-paced, engineering-focused environment.

In this role, you will develop more robust static analysis tooling for security purposes, likely leveraging AI generated code.

What we’re looking for...

Strong knowledge of compilers such as Clang or GCC

Experience with C or C++

Solid understanding of operating systems

Strong knowledge of software engineering fundamentals

A rising senior pursuing BS or MS in Computer Science or Engineering related field

Ready to learn quickly, ask questions, and jump into a small, dynamic team setting

An effective communicator with strong writing skills

Available for full-time work during the summer of 2027

This position is onsite in Redmond, WA, and not open to remote status at this time.
