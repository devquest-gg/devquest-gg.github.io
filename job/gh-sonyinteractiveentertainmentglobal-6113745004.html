Director, Client Partnerships (Sales) San Mateo, CA (hybrid) - fully remote may be considered

Role Overview

The Global Director of Client Partnerships is a senior functional leader responsible for defining and driving the global strategy, structure, and performance of SIE Media Solutions Client Partnership (Sales) organization. This role leads a global team of managers and client partners, scaling the function to deliver sustained revenue growth, strategic partner value, and long-term business impact.

Operating at the intersection of sales and platform strategy, this leader moves beyond individual account ownership to shape portfolio-level direction, establish global frameworks, and influence enterprise-wide partnership and decision-making. The role requires the application of expertise in the PlayStation ecosystem, global advertising markets, and complex commercial partnerships, combined with strong organizational leadership and operational rigor.

What You ll be Doing:

Global Strategy Portfolio Leadership

Define and drive the global Client Partnerships strategy, translating SIE Media Solution’s priorities into scalable partner frameworks and multi-year growth plans

Develop and execute growth strategies that expand SIE Media Solutions’ advertising business with non-endemic brands, with a focus on scaling demand across Programmatic, FAST/CTV, and emerging ad solutions

Oversee a portfolio of strategic partnerships, ensuring alignment to revenue growth, retention, and broader ecosystem objectives

Build strategic engagement frameworks with media holding companies and multinational advertisers to accelerate adoption of new commercial offerings and unlock incremental revenue streams

Identify market trends, client needs, and emerging opportunities to inform long-term commercial strategy and investment decisions

Organizational Leadership Capability Building

Lead and develop a global organization of client partners

Establish team structures, operating models, and performance expectations to enable scale and consistency aligned with existing and new sales models and offerings

Build organizational capability through talent development, succession planning, and leadership coaching

Revenue Ownership Business Performance

Own and deliver against global revenue targets and core business KPIs across the Client Partnerships function

Drive forecasting accuracy, pipeline health, and performance accountability through managers and team members

Oversee complex, high-impact partnership negotiations and ensure alignment to commercial and platform priorities

Drive revenue diversification through expansion of non-endemic advertising partnerships across categories such as CPG, retail, automotive, financial services, telecom, and entertainment

Lead commercialization efforts for new advertising products and go-to-market initiatives

Cross-Functional Leadership Operational Excellence

Partner with senior leaders across Operations, Product, Marketing and other SIE Business lines to align strategies and unlock growth opportunities

Influence internal decision-making by representing partner needs, market insights, and commercial priorities

Establish and evolve global processes, frameworks, and metrics to drive execution, consistency, and continuous improvement

What We re Looking for:

18+ years of experience in advertising sales or partnerships within gaming, media, or entertainment verticals

Proven experience and established relationships with senior level, decision makers at global media holding companies in the US that covers global brands

Proven track record of scaling advertising sales organizations during periods of platform and capability expansion, particularly across Programmatic, CTV/FAST, video, or data-driven advertising solutions

Demonstrated success owning large-scale revenue portfolios and complex strategic partnerships

Deep understanding of platform ecosystems, advertising/monetization models, and global markets

Experience leading high-performing sales organizations through transformational growth, including product expansion and evolving monetization strategies

Develop and execute growth strategies that expand SIE Media Solutions’ advertising business with non-endemic brands, with a focus on scaling demand across Programmatic, FAST, and emerging ad solutions in the US and Canada markets
