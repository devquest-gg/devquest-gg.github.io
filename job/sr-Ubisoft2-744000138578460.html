As a Director, Campaign Program Management at Ubisoft Paris, you’ll build, structure, and lead our Campaign Program Management team. This team acts as the operational hub between our Creative Houses, who define brand and campaign strategy, and our cross-functional experts, such as media, shopper marketing, and social media. You’ll ensure rigorous global planning and the seamless delivery of campaigns and related content on time, on budget, and at scale.

Reporting to the Head of Campaign Content Operations, you’ll drive visibility, structure, budget efficiency, and operational excellence across end-to-end campaign production. You’ll lead a team of Campaign Program Managers aligned to specific franchises and partner with senior franchise stakeholders to ensure smooth campaign execution, strong coordination, and timely asset delivery.

What you’ll do Define the vision, priorities, and operating model for the Campaign Program Management team Build, mentor, and develop a team of Campaign Program Managers supporting specific franchises Build and maintain complex campaign timelines and milestone plans with interdependent deliverables Identify delivery risks early, define mitigation plans, and remove roadblocks to keep campaigns on track Own and evolve governance for campaign planning, budgeting, orchestration, and cross-functional collaboration Drive adoption of shared tools, standardized processes, and unified planning frameworks across teams Establish clear documentation standards, including accessible briefs, trackers, and delivery records across phases Facilitate cross-team rituals such as kickoffs, reviews, and postmortems to improve alignment and decision-making Align stakeholders on scope, resourcing, and prioritization tradeoffs across franchises and cross-functional needs Monitor delivery and financial health through dashboards and planning reviews, and share learnings and best practices with leadership What you bring to the team Demonstrated background in program management, marketing operations, or creative operations with people leadership responsibilities Proven ability to manage complex, matrixed environments and cross-functional dependencies Experience building and running timeline and milestone management, including multi-stream delivery with attention to detail Ability to lead risk management and structured problem solving, including proactive escalation and mitigation planning Strong capability in stakeholder management, facilitation, and clear communication to align teams with different priorities Practical understanding of the campaign lifecycle, from briefing through delivery and postmortem Working knowledge of content and asset management, including version control, asset readiness, naming conventions, and specs Proficiency with project management and
