Job Overview

Wargaming is looking for a UI Designer to join the World of Warships project.

You’ll be part of the team responsible for creating and evolving the game s user interface, making sure it is both engaging and comfortable for our players. If you’re passionate about game UI and want to help shape the way players interact with the game, we’d be happy to have you on board and create great experiences together.

Reports to

Team Lead

What will you do?

Designs and maintains complex UI systems that support multiple gameplay mechanics, features, or interconnected product areas

Defines interaction patterns, scalable information architecture, and reusable design solutions within assigned scope

Creates and maintains comprehensive implementation documentation, UI guidelines, component usage rules, and design standards

Leads UX analysis of complex features using player feedback, research findings, analytics, and usability data

Ensures UI solutions support gameplay clarity, accessibility, engagement, retention, and overall player experience goals

Identifies usability risks, implementation constraints, and design inconsistencies and drives improvements across systems

Mentors less experienced UI designers and supports design quality through reviews, feedback, and knowledge sharing

Collaborates with cross-functional stakeholders to align UI solutions with product, technical, and artistic objectives

What are we looking for?

2+ years of experience designing complex, intuitive user interfaces for games or interactive products

Deep understanding of UX principles, interaction design, information architecture, and player behavior

Ability to design scalable UI systems and establish reusable patterns and standards

Experience using analytics, user research, and feedback to drive design decisions

Ability to lead discussions, resolve design challenges, and influence cross-functional decisions

Experience mentoring designers and supporting professional development of team members

Excellent communication, stakeholder management, and problem-solving skills

Upper-Intermediate (B2) or higher English knowledge

What additional skills will help you stand out?

Experience working with AI tools

What would we like to see in your portfolio?

We would like to see examples of UI design work you have created for games, showcasing your design process and final results

Work mode

During the probation period: onsite work

After the probation period: onsite or hybrid (3 days per week in the office)

About Wargaming

Wargaming is an award-winning online game developer and publisher headquartered in Nicosia, Cyprus. Operating since 1998, Wargaming has become one of the leaders in the gaming industry with 15 offices worldwide, including studios in Chicago, Prague, Shanghai, Tokyo, and Vilnius. Our diverse and multicultural team works together to deliver a top-class experience to millions of players who enjoy Wargaming s titles across all major gaming platforms. Our flagship products include free-to-play hits World of Tanks, World of Warships and World of Tanks Blitz.
