Overview: We are looking for a highly ambitious and visionary creative all-rounder who thrives in the "white space" of early development and possesses a true pioneer spirit to join our team as a Realisation Designer.

Working as a key player in our IP team and working day-to-day/directly with the Creative Portfolio Director, the role demands versatility and creative problem solving.

The right candidate is a creative generalist who thrives in the early stages of ideation and pre-production, focusing on how visual design supports gameplay clarity, player readability, and the "moment-to-moment" experience across our console and PC titles.

This role requires a natural curiosity for cutting-edge workflows and the latest technologies to solve visual problems quickly. Ultimately, you are a versatile visual communicator who enjoys the challenge of wearing many hats and feels at home where the goal is to bridge the gap between a raw idea and a compelling, playable reality. This is a high-impact role where you will support the development of new project proposals, internal prototypes, and co-development partnerships by bringing ideas to life through a wide range of visual disciplines.

What you'll do: Create the visual assets needed for new project proposals and internal concept development.

Create 2D game art, 3D mockups (low-poly/block-outs), and UI/UX art and design.

Graphic design for high-stakes proposals and creating unique project identities and logos.

Creating motion graphic concepts and video editing to communicate gameplay intent dynamically.

Work closely with the creative leadership of Lucid to understand and interpret the creative vision behind each project.

Work closely with Game Designers to visually define mechanics, states, progression, and player actions.

Partner with Art Directors to ensure prototype visuals align with the overall visual style and tone of the game.

Help create high-quality deliverables and presentations that communicate gameplay intent to external partners.

Work alongside Developers and Technical Designers to implement, test, and iterate on gameplay visuals in-engine.
