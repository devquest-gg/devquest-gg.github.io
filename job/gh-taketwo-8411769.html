THE CHALLENGE

Take-Two Interactive is seeking a highly motivated, collaborative and experienced FP A professional to join the Finance team. Take-Two’s FP A team provides financial and analytical expertise and support to executive management and various labels and department s across the organization. The FP A Lead will focus on optimizing financial systems and automating processes to enable improved accuracy and faster delivery of business plans and decision support across the organization. This role will take lead on the FP A digital acceleration roadmap and partner closely with our label finance and technology teams. The ideal candidate will be highly skilled and familiar with financial systems and underlying business processes, strong financial forecasting and modeling skills, solid understanding of accounting rules, a great attitude, interest in the video game industry and excellent interpersonal and communication skills. They must have proven ability to be resourceful and business-minded, be eager to dive into the data and systems and be very process orie nted.

WHAT YOU’LL TAKE ON

Lead and manage multiple FP A system projects and partner closely with technology teams and business stakeholders

Drive automation of financial processes to drive efficiency and accuracy that will support decision-making across the organization. Works closely with the T2 technology teams to build out system capabilities.

Streamline the data collection and submission process for increased efficiency in the forecasting process

Collaborate with and assist all FP A teams across the organization in providing timely and accurate forecasts. Serves as point of contact and viable resource for these teams to strengthen their processes.

Partner with the accounting team to obtain the latest understanding of relevant financial matters and accounting rules

Performs other duties as assigned

WHAT YOU BRING

Bachelor’s degree in Finance, Business, Accounting, or relevant field of study is required

8+ years corporate finance, financial planning or other relevant experience

Must have excellent financial modelling and analytical skills

Knowledge of financial statements (P L, Balance Sheet, Cash Flow) required

Advanced Excel modeling and strong PowerPoint presentation skills

Proficiency with an enterprise planning system (Oracle Planning, Anaplan)

Highly organized and detail oriented with the ability to multitask in a fast-paced setting

Proven ability to manage processes

Excellent verbal/written communication skills

Must be a strong team player who is able to work collaboratively with all departments and levels within the company

Strong sense of urgency able to perform well under pr essure

Flexible to meet tight deadlines

Self-starter, quick learner, proactive and highly motivated

Eager to “get in the weeds” and pitch in with data mining and validat ion
