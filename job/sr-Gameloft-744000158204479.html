3D Environment Artist (Middle/Senior)

Gameloft · Ho Chi Minh City

Senior Art Posted 2026-10-28 Full time
Apply on Gameloft’s site →

WHAT YOU WILL BE WORKING ON What you will focus on Join Gameloft’s Artist team to bring game worlds to life by creating high-quality 3D environments, from terrain and architecture to props and key landmarks. You’ll transform concepts and references into visually compelling, technically optimized assets that meet the project’s art direction and production requirements.

What it means on a daily basis Create immersive 3D environments: Model and texture terrain, architecture, props, and landmarks based on concepts, references, 3D mock-ups, and art direction.

Build and optimize assets: Create high-poly and low-poly models, develop PBR assets and textures, and optimize environments for real-time rendering on mobile and console platforms.

Support visual quality: Help define material and lighting properties for environment shaders, ensuring consistency with the project's artistic direction.

Own your work and solve problems: Identify and resolve modeling, texturing, and workflow issues while meeting deadlines and technical specifications.

Collaborate and improve workflows: Work closely with the Art Lead, Design, and other team members to coordinate production, improve workflows, and share technical knowledge.

Who you would be working with You will be reporting to the Artist Supervisor, Art Producer, and Art Lead/Director.

Closely collaborating with other disciplines in the Art team (e.g., 2D Art, Animation, Technical Art, VFX) and other teams in the project (e.g., Game Design, Programming).

Where can this role take you? Advance vertically to higher level in the 3D team such as Senior or Principal position.

Transition to other disciplines within the Art team: 2D Artist, 3D Animator, VFX Artist, and so on.

Recruitment journey Online screening call with recruiter Distance art test Offline interview with Artist Supervisor, Art Director, and Producer WHAT YOU NEED TO SUCCEED Professional experience as a 3D Environment Artist in the video game industry.

Strong modeling and texturing skills using 3ds Max or Maya, and Photoshop.

Experience creating game environments and textures for mobile and/or console games.

Ability to model both organic and hard-surface assets in high-poly and low-poly.

Strong understanding of PBR asset creation and PBR lighting workflows.

Good sense of composition, color, lighting, and visual detail.

Knowledge of real-time game engines such as Unreal Engine, Unity, or CryEngine.

For senior-level candidates: 3–4+ years of experience in video game production, with the ability to take ownership of a level, optimize workflows, and provide technical guidance to teammates.

WHO YOU ARE Good spoken and written English, plus clear communication, with the ability to take direction, follow a brief and solve artistic or technical issues.

Proactive, organized and creative, with a good eye for detail.

Ability to work well under pressure and in a team.

Passionate about games across platforms.
