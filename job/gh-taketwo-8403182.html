The Challenge

The global Employee Relations function serves as the strategic backbone for maintaining a positive, compliant, and productive workplace across the operations for the entire enterprise by serving as an objective, impartial partner focused on fostering a fair and respectful environment. The role of Employee Relations Specialist is central to the success of function and is responsible for independently managing Employee Relations cases of varied complexity and risk and providing guidance on workplace conflict, performance management, performance improvement plans, disciplinary actions, grievances, policy interpretation and the consistent application of employment policies and procedures fairly, consistently, and effectively. In addition, the role will independently manage workplace accommodations for all employees across North America. Working closely with our HR Business Partners, Legal, Compliance and Business Leaders, this role will help promote equity, uphold company values and reinforce accountability throughout the enterprise. The role also collaborates with Learning Development on initiatives to strengthen manager capability, employee engagement and retention, contributing practical insights from ER cases and recurring employee concerns.

What You’ll Take On

Independently manage routine to moderately complex ER cases and investigations, applying sound professional judgement and established policies and procedures, in a timely and efficient manner.

Promote the consistent and equitable application of company policies, procedures and practices.

Respond to sensitive employee concerns and allegations, through internal processes and external agencies.

Independently manage North American workplace accommodations.

Build strong, credible, collaborative partnerships with management, global HR Business Partners, Legal and other stakeholders.

Coach and advise HRBP’s and Managers on resolving complex employee relations issues, including conflict resolution and mediation conversations.

Educate employees and managers on company policies, procedures, and employment compliance requirements.

Partner with Compliance to ensure timely completion of mandatory federal, state, and local training requirements.

Develop and enhance employee relations policies, compliance resources, training materials and learning sessions.

Analyze employee relations trends and collaborate with cross-functional partners to identify opportunities for improvement based on data- driven solutions.

Maintain accurate and timely employee relations records and documentation in Workday and Navex.

What You Bring

Typically 3–5 years of relevant HR experience, including approximately 1–2 years of hands-on ER case management, investigations or employee advisory work.

Sound practical knowledge of relevant employment law, ER policy and established case-management principles. Understands investigation methodology, evidence gathering, conflict management and procedural requirements. Understands when Legal or specialist ER input is required.

Strong working knowledge of relevant country-specific employment legislation, with the ability to identify legal, procedural and organizational risks and recognize when specialist guidance is required.

Familiarity with US and Canadian employment, particular as it pertains to leave entitlements (i.e. FMLA, FLSA, ADA) and policies is essential.

Effective investigation skills, including conducting interviews, assessing evidence and producing clear, impartial reports including well-supported recommendations.

Understanding of core ER policies and employment-law principles, with the ability to apply established procedures consistently and appropriately.

Excellent communication, interpersonal and relationship-building skills, with the ability to work effectively across diverse backgrounds and organizational levels.

Proven ability to independently handle multiple priorities and projects, meet deadlines, and adapt in a collaborative and evolving team environment.

Strong organizational and time management skills, with exceptional attention to detail and the ability to handle sensitive and confidential information with discretion.

Proficient in Google Suite, and HRIS systems; Workday and Navex (or similar) experience a plus.

Intermediate HR/ER qualification or certification desirable (e.g. CIPD Level 5, SHRM-CP, PHR or equivalent). As is practical training in workplace investigations, relevant employment law, conflict resolution and ER case management.

For Canadian applicants: The ability to speak French is preferred but not essential.
