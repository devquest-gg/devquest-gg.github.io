About Role

NEOWIZ is evolving its brand communications from execution-driven PR activities into a more strategic, globally connected narrative function.

We are looking for a brand planning professional who can develop clear, well-reasoned proposals for how NEOWIZ should be understood as a company, and translate agreed direction into tangible brand initiatives.

This role is expected to form an initial point of view, explain the reasoning behind it, and refine proposals through discussion with leadership and colleagues. It combines strategic proposal development with hands-on project leadership.
