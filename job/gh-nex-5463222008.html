Nex is on a mission to help families rediscover the joy of movement. Created by parents for parents, Nex combines technology and play to deliver fun, social, and interactive experiences powered by natural body motion, encouraging kids and adults to move more, play more, and have fun together. Nex Playground, the company’s award-winning active play system, is purpose-built to get families moving year-round, with safety and privacy as core considerations in its intentional design. It is certified kidSAFE+ COPPA compliant and built to support healthy, active play for all ages and abilities.

Nex Playground features a growing library of 50+ experiences, including motion and dance games, fitness and educational experiences, and Nex Originals. Content includes collaborations with partners like Hasbro, Sesame Workshop, and NBCUniversal. Nex has been recognized by Fast Company’s Most Innovative Companies, TIME’s Best Inventions, and Parents’ Best Entertainment System for Families, and has earned Red Dot, IDEA, and Core77 international design awards. We encourage you to explore Have Fun and Is Motion Gaming Back? , as they offer a deeper look into our culture, values, and explain how our approach to motion gaming differs from previous generations.

Nex employees may be expected to collaborate with colleagues across different time zones. Depending on the role and location, occasional travel may also be required for company events, external meetings, and other business-related activities.

Location: Remote / Hybrid (Los Angeles-based preferred) Annual salary: USD 85,000 - 115,000 per year (Final base salary within this range will be determined based on job-related factors, including the candidate’s experience, skills, qualifications, and geographic location.) Type: Full-time Reports to: Senior Game Producer

About the Role

As an Associate Producer, you will support the end-to-end development of motion-based games — from concept through launch — ensuring projects stay on track, teams stay aligned, and our player experience remains magical, intuitive, and family-friendly.

You’ll work closely with design, engineering, art, QA, and external partners to coordinate deliverables, manage schedules, and maintain clear communication across stakeholders.

This is an ideal role for someone who loves games, thrives in fast-moving environments, and cares deeply about creating meaningful experiences for kids and families.

What You’ll Do

Production Execution

Maintain production schedules, and milestone tracking

Participate in and help run stand-ups and cross-functional sync meetings

Track dependencies, risks, and blockers across teams

Ensure builds and deliverables are on time and aligned with scope

Prepare milestone reports and stakeholder updates

Motion-Based Gameplay Support

Coordinate implementation of camera-based motion gameplay systems

Support playtesting with kids and families; synthesize feedback into actionable insights

Track motion calibration, usability improvements, and accessibility features

Ensure experiences are intuitive for a wide age range and skill levels

Evaluate milestones, write build notes and help compile stakeholder feedback.

Kids Family Focus

Advocate for age-appropriate design and safety considerations

Support external brand or IP collaboration workflows when applicable

Keep fun, accessibility, and inclusivity at the center of development

Cross-Functional Collaboration

Act as the communication bridge between creative, technical, and business teams

Partner with QA to prioritize and track bug resolution

Support publishing needs (build delivery, feature documentation, gameplay capture coordination)

Help coordinate external development partners when needed

What We’re Looking For

Required

3+ years experience in game production or project management

Experience working on shipped game titles (mobile, console, or interactive platform)

Strong organizational and communication skills

Familiarity with Agile/Scrum workflows

Experience with tools such as GitHub, Notion, Slack, Asana, or equivalent

Passion for kids, family entertainment, and active play

Flexibility to collaborate across global time zones, including Asia (particularly Hong Kong), with the understanding that recurring meetings and coordination will be required outside standard U.S. business hours.

Bonus Points

Experience with motion-based, camera-based, or fitness-style games

Experience working with licensed IP or external partners

Background in consumer hardware or platform-based products

Experience conducting or coordinating playtests with children

Who You Are

Highly organized and detail-oriented

Proactive problem solver
