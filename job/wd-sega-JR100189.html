ABOUT SEGA SEGA is one of the leading interactive entertainment companies in the world, with our European HQ based in West London and development studios spanning the UK and Bulgaria. Known for quality and creativity above all else, our studios include Sports Interactive, Two Point Studios, Hardlight and Creative Assembly.

At the heart of SEGA is our publishing team based in London, where we help the studios bring their creative vision to life. Here we have a hardworking bunch of people spanning Tech, Sales, Marketing, Finance, QA and more.

Together, we drive the continued success and evolution of our Western business through living our company values of being product focused, embracing true partnerships and always having fun so that we can strive to come together as One SEGA.

Role: Financial Accountant  Company: SEGA Europe Ltd  Working model: Hybrid (3 days in office per week)  Location: Building 12, Chiswick Business Park, 566 Chiswick High Road, London, W4 5AN    POSITION OVERVIEW The Financial Accountant is a broad position within the Finance Department at SEGA. The role will provide key direct support to the UK Financial Manager and the wider Finance team, and cover areas of both Financial and Management Accounting.

The Financial Accountant will play a vital role in the smooth running of the month end processes, including suggesting and implementing process and efficiency improvements together with the UK Financial Manager. The position requires excellent accounting skills and high attention to detail and will allow the successful candidate to gain further experience within the SEGA finance function as they grow into the position.

KEY RESPONSIBILITIES • Responsibility for all Intercompany balances, the group intercompany reconciliation and reporting processes including Cash Management deposits.

• Oversight of the monthly UK Marketing spend and forecast (Getsuji), ensuring recognition of the costs are in following with the applicable accounting standards • Manage VGEC B2B invoicing, update the VGEC matrix, and reconcile to source data.

• Manage bank transaction reconciliations within the Cash Management ledger and perform monthly bank reconciliations • Manage the intercompany recharge process and accounts • Responsible for month end journals for these areas including VAT reallocations between studios monthly • Close involvement in both the yearend group audit and the statutory accounts audit process • Preparation and submission of Group Reporting (SSHD) Packs for the UK Entities • Manage the preparation and submission of the National Statistics information • Support the preparation and submission of VAT returns • ​Coordinate and submit ONS statistical surveys and returns in a timely and accurate manner • Process Control and improvement ​​​ KNOWLEDGE, SKILLS & EXPERIENCE • Part Qualified accountant (ACA, ACCA, CIMA) • Minimum three-years’ experience in accounting role.

• Intermediate level in Microsoft Excel and ability to process large data sets.

• Prior experience of Oracle or similar ERP system • Strong communication skills being able to work with internal stakeholder at all levels of the business and external suppliers.
