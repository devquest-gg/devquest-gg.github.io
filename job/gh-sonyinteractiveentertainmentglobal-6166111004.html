We are seeking a Lead Character Tools Engineer to join the PlayStation Central Technology team focusing on Character technology from creation, rigging, and animation.

Developing and distributing tools and optimizing for making the best characters in games more efficiently and integrating emergent technology into the future of game making.

Candidate expected to prototype, create, and collaborate to deploy and maintain technical tools and practices from experience on traditional game development and run-time workflows with internal teams and seek new opportunities for bringing increased usage of AI and ML to make those processes more efficient.

This internal team of artists, engineers, and technical artists partner with engineering teams, studio stakeholders, research engineers, and other Central Technology groups to provide cohesion and standardization across PSS activities.

This position requires a demonstratable understanding of game development pipelines, extensive experience with Autodesk Maya software, and strong programming skills.

Familiarity with leveraging agentic workflows to accelerate code creation and testing.

Responsibilities:

Collaborate with internal team members and studio stakeholders to prototype and harden create tools considering contemporary development workflows, data management, art creation, animation, and game engine integration

Setting up and maintaining project specific environments and automation opportunities

Learning and supporting varying client working environments, assets, and tools

Writing, maintaining, and customizing internal tools and workflows

Building and maintaining technical documentation

Willingness to attend and participate in PlayStation internal demonstrations and conferences, travel required

Ability to work in a collaborative team environment as well as self-driven experimentation and research

Core Experience:

Experience as technical artist or engineer to develop and deploy tools to support character creation, rigging, simulation, or animation pipelines in a run-time environment

10+ years in a game development studio or previous experience creating artist-facing tools on technical art or engineering team in a production environment

BS degree in Computer Science, Engineering, Graphic Art, or a related field, or equivalent experience

Extensive experience in working with game editors and excellent working knowledge of technical specifications of workflow from DCC to complex real-time environments

Capable of writing and debugging C++

Excellent knowledge of Maya with general knowledge of Maya rigging

Developing or maintaining project pipeline tools and system environments

Excellent troubleshooting skills and the ability to creatively solve problems

Experience leveraging Agentic AI models

Strong understanding of deformation and mesh processing

Familiarity with industry trends in character rigging, gameplay animation, cinematic animation, deformation and simulation usage in character animation pipelines

Decent sense of humor

Extended Experience:

Strong Python programming skills and experience with PyQt/Pyside

Motionbuilder, Maya, Houdini, experience

Experience with HLSL, MaterialX

Experience with other programming languages, especially C++.

Experience with Perforce, Git, Confluence

Exposure to photogrammetry, motion capture/performance capture
