Business Development Assistant – Internship (6-month) January 2027 (W/M/NB)

Ubisoft (HQ) · Paris

Entry Other Posted 2026-10-07 Intern

Within the Strategic Partnerships & Business Development teams, your responsibilities will include: Internal Coordination & Opportunity Tracking Act as the internal point of contact to centralize studios’ needs related to Third-Party Payments and alternative distribution initiatives.

Support the qualification and follow-up of opportunities (pre- and post-sales), assisting the partner discussions led by the manager.

Coordinate with HQ Marketing and Studios teams on partner activations and the production of summary materials.

Performance Analysis & Reporting Analyze performance by partner, channel, and region (Third-Party Providers and alternative distribution).

Track key performance indicators (payment funnels, performance of marketing activities).

Build dashboards and prepare internal reporting and business reviews.

Digital Distribution Monitoring via Third-Party Partners Ensure the smooth execution of distribution campaigns (items, vouchers, games) and compliance with commercial terms.

Monitor the terms and conditions governing indirect distribution and resales, and contribute to the analysis of revenue by channel.

Support the monitoring of fraud, refunds, and chargebacks, as well as the preparation of internal materials.

Commercial Activation of Distribution Channels Contribute to the coordination of commercial activities (promotions, activations, merchandising).

Participate in the preparation of co-branded activations and the monitoring of visibility assets.

Coordinate with marketing teams to ensure the delivery of partner assets.

You are currently pursuing a degree at a business school, university, in a gaming school or equivalent program, with a specialization in business, digital marketing, or e-commerce.

You are recognized for your analytical skills, attention to detail, and strong interpersonal skills, enabling you to work effectively with cross-functional teams.

You are proficient in Microsoft Office (Excel, Outlook, PowerPoint) and have a strong ability to structure and synthesize information. Knowledge of Power BI is a plus.

You are comfortable analyzing performance and interpreting key performance indicators.

You are proficient in English, both written and spoken, in an international collaboration environment.

You are passionate about the mobile gaming industry! Recruitment process If your application is chosen, you will be contacted by a member of the Campus Team to schedule a phone exchange in order to determine if the job description matches your profile, but also what you are looking for.

If that exchange is successful, an interview will be organized (on site or through Teams) with your future manager.

&#xa0; What can I expect during an internship or apprenticeship at Ubisoft? From your very first days, you will attend a Welcome Session introducing you to Ubisoft, its organisation and your working environment for the coming months. Your manager and your team will support you on a daily basis, both during your first few weeks and throughout your entire experience! Once you are autonomous, your tasks will be varied and give you a sense of responsibility, regardless of the type of contract. Working at Ubisoft means working on real projects on a day-to-day basis.

A dedicated Teams group will also allow you to interact with other interns and apprentices.
