ABOUT US

Scorewarrior is a game developer and publisher based in Limassol, Cyprus.

We are working on Total Battle — a cross-platform MMO 4X strategy game with a deep social system and extensive player interaction. We have 2 million monthly active players and continue to grow with the goal of becoming the #1 game in the genre.

Our team consists of 200+ passionate, talented, self-driven, and continually developing professionals who love making high-quality games.

ABOUT THE POSITION

We are looking for a Senior UI Artist to craft high-quality, production-ready interfaces for the gameplay and event features our most engaged players interact with every day.

You will join a cross-functional team of 25+ professionals focused on developing gameplay and monetization for mid- and high-level players. You will own the visual side of features end to end — from visual direction to release-quality UI — while UX flows and product logic are handled by a dedicated UX specialist, so you can focus on craft, systems, assets, and implementation quality.

WHAT YOU WILL BE DOING

- Develop UI for gameplay and event features based on approved UX flows and wireframes.

- Develop the visual direction of a feature: research references, explore concepts, create a key screen, and define visual rules for related screens and states.

- Create UI elements, icons, decorative assets, and small illustrations in a realistic or semi-realistic style.

- Build complete sets of visual states for elements and screens, and prepare motion references or rough animations for the animator.

- Create clear and scalable Figma files using components, variants, auto layout, styles, and libraries.

- Maintain and develop the UI kit, component library, and asset library while preserving consistency across the product.

- Prepare production-ready UI and assets for implementation.

- Collaborate closely with UX, Game Design, Product, Development, QA, Art, and Animation.

- Contribute to UI production workflows, documentation, guidelines, and shared quality standards.

REQUIRED QUALIFICATIONS

- Understanding of game UI usability principles.

- Experience creating game interfaces, ideally 5+ years, with a primary focus on mobile.

- A portfolio with strong game UI work and a clear explanation of your personal contribution.

- Strong Photoshop skills, including raster graphics, UI elements, icons, decorative assets, and small illustrations.

- Strong Figma skills, including components, variants, auto layout, styles, libraries, and well-structured production files.

- Ability to build complex multi-screen clickable prototypes in Figma.

- A solid understanding of composition, color, typography, visual hierarchy, readability, form, volume, materials, light, and detail.

- The ability to work within an established realistic or semi-realistic game style.

- Experience preparing UI and assets for development.

- Genuine interest in games, including mobile titles.

- Experience working with AI tools such as GPT, Gemini, and Midjourney.

- English - B1 or higher, Russian - B2 or higher.

PREFERRED QUALIFICATIONS

- A portfolio of mid-core-style game interfaces.

- Some experience designing UI for PC.

- Experience working with variables and complex component animations in Figma, or creating motion effects.

- Experience with 4X strategy games is a significant plus.

- Interest in current industry trends.

A test task is part of the hiring process.
