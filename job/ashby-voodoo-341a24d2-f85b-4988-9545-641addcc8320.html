Community Manager - Midcore Games - Draft Showdown

Voodoo · Paris

Mid Marketing Onsite Posted 2026-07-17

ABOUT VOODOO

Founded in 2013, Voodoo is a tech company that creates mobile games and apps with a mission to entertain the world. Gathering 800 employees, 7 billion downloads, and over 200 million active users, Voodoo is the #3 mobile publisher worldwide in terms of downloads after Google and Meta. Our portfolio includes chart-topping games like Mob Control and Block Jam, alongside popular apps such as BeReal and Wizz.

TEAM

You will be an integral part of a newly established internal studio, a dynamic and focused team dedicated to one of our most promising games in scale - Draft Showdown . This studio is specifically chartered to improve this high-potential game up to the successful and market-ready title it is meant to become.

In this role, you'll be responsible for building and nurturing an engaged player community while providing exceptional support through our Discord channel, creating a dynamic and welcoming environment that strengthens player relationships and drives game success. This is a unique chance to join a project with established momentum while still having a significant impact on its future direction within a dedicated and agile studio environment

ROLE

- Lead and evolve our Discord community strategy to create an engaging, informative, and supportive player hub

- Social Listening, analyse players’ feedback and data and suggest game improvements

- Moderate and actively participate in community discussions to maintain a positive atmosphere

- Plan and execute community events, contests, Q&As, and other engagement initiatives

- Provide first-line player support and coordinate with relevant teams for issue resolution

- Monitor community health metrics and prepare weekly engagement reports

- Train and oversee community moderators to ensure consistent community management

- Create and maintain community guidelines and best practices

- Collaborate with internal teams to align community initiatives with game objectives

PROFILE

- A bachelor’s degree in marketing or communications would be a plus

- A first experience in the gaming industry, using Discord and/or reddit and an experience in F2P (gaming) would be a plus

- Experience with marketing strategies and social media management would be a plus

- Strong communication skills

- Enthusiasm and assertiveness are your two main qualities

- Native level of English

BENEFITS

- Competitive salary

- Premium healthcare (SideCare) for you and your family 100% borne by Voodoo

- Swile Lunch voucher

- Gymlib (100% borne by Voodoo)

- Wellness activities in our Paris office

- Child daycare facilities (Les Petits Chaperons rouges)
