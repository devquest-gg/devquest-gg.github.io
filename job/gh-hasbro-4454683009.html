Wizards of the Coast · Renton

We take play seriously. We’re looking for curious adventurers ready to find their party, fueled by imagination and drive to build what’s never been built before. At Hasbro and Wizards of the Coast, you’ll collaborate with passionate teams to reimagine our iconic brands and create experiences that spark joy, connection, and community through the magic of play. This is your chance to shape legendary play that lasts a lifetime.

This role does require three days a week in our Renton, WA office.

What You ll Do:

Collaborate with both the D D studio team and D D Beyond digital team to ensure visual consistency and innovation across platforms

Build captivating graphic design elements, style guides, and layout formats that uphold D D s iconic aesthetic while optimizing for digital experiences across both owned IP and 3P partnership projects

Partner with D D Beyond s Content Specialists and UI/UX team to inform design decisions and provide creative direction aligned with the D D brand

Develop design style guides and systems that can flexibly adapt between print and digital formats

Build and maintain technical documentation for asset specifications, file organization, and workflow processes in collaboration with D D production

Collaborate with engineering teams to optimize asset delivery and implementation

Serve as a design resource for asset optimization and delivery questions

What You ll Bring:

5+ years of professional graphic design experience, with shown expertise in both print and digital design

Familiarity with Dungeons Dragons and fantasy art direction

Strong portfolio showcasing versatile design work across digital and print mediums

Proficiency in Adobe Creative Suite and relevant digital design tools

Solid knowledge of typography, color theory, layout design, and user experience (UX) principles

Understanding of technical requirements for digital asset optimization and delivery

Experience crafting and maintaining technical documentation

Excellent interpersonal skills and ability to collaborate across multiple teams

Strong understanding of responsive design and digital asset optimization

Experience working on 3p licensed products and navigating brand adherence for partner IP

Nice to Have:

Experience in gaming, entertainment, or publishing industries

Previous work involving print-to-digital adaptation

Experience with version control systems for design assets

Knowledge of build pipelines and asset management systems

Understanding of accessibility standards in digital design

Employment Details: Please note that this is a temporary assignment through an external staffing agency. If you are selected for this assignment, you will be employed by Workwell as a W2 employee and will not be an employee of Wizards or Hasbro.

Employment Type: Temporary

Duration: 6 months with opportunity to extend up to one year

Location: This is a hybrid role open to candidates whose work location is within commuting distance of Renton, WA.
