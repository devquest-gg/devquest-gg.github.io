Playable Ads Strategist - Internship

Voodoo · Paris

Mid Other Onsite Posted 2026-08-12 Intern

Founded in 2013, Voodoo is a tech company that creates mobile games and apps with a mission to entertain the world. Gathering 900 employees, 7 billion downloads, and over 200 million active users, Voodoo is the #3 mobile publisher worldwide in terms of downloads after Google and Meta. Our portfolio includes chart-topping games like Mob Control and Block Jam, alongside popular apps such as BeReal and Wizz.

TEAM

Voodoo’s Growth team is responsible for turning our games into successful products, combining user acquisition, creative, monetization, and data.

Within Growth, the Playable Ads team turns our games into short, interactive experiences designed to make players stop, play, and want more. Working closely with playable developers and creative teams, we design, produce, test, and iterate on playable ads across our 20+ game portfolio.

ROLE

You’ll join our high-performing Playable Ads team and get hands-on experience creating interactive ads for some of the biggest mobile games in the market. You’ll:

- Create and edit video ads designed to grab attention and drive performance.

- Design 2D assets and UI elements that make playable and video ads feel polished and engaging.

- Stay ahead of the market by benchmarking competitor ads, spotting creative trends, and keeping up with what’s happening on social media.

- Track ad performance and turn data into insights to understand what makes players engage.

- Bring your own ideas to the table: pitch new concepts, experiment with creative formats, and help us find new ways to make players want to play.

PROFILE

- Final-year student from a top engineering or business school (Programme Grande Ecole), looking for your end-of-study internship for January 2027

- Clear communicator, fluent both in English and French

- Strong analytical mindset; comfortable digging into data to make decisions

- Genuine interest in mobile apps, games, or performance marketing

- Autonomous and proactive; you'd rather figure things out than wait to be told

- Good understanding of the casual mobile game market and mobile advertising

- Ability to create clean 2D assets in Photoshop

- A sharp eye for social media trends and mobile marketing

For all our opportunities, it starts as a final-year internship, and converts to a permanent contract based on performance.

- Start: ASAP

- Duration: 6 months minimum

- Location: Paris (on-site)

BENEFITS

- Swile Lunch voucher

- Gymlib (100% covered by Voodoo)

- Wellness activities in our Paris office

- Remote Fridays
