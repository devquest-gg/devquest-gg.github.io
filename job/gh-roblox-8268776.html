Why User Behavior?

As a Senior Software Engineer on the User Behavior ML Platform, you will build the data and serving foundation behind Roblox s user-level safety understanding, so that every safety system can see who a user is, not just what a single piece of content looks like.

You will own the core ML platform for a new capability at Roblox: a safety user feature store, user embeddings, and risk scores that power personalized consequences, proactive detection, and early education. This is a rare opportunity to build foundational, high-scale ML systems that shape how Roblox understands and responds to risk across hundreds of millions of users.

You will feel a deep sense of responsibility building systems that directly affect millions of users, especially minors, on one of the world s largest platforms. Your work will reduce harm, improve fairness, and help ensure Roblox remains a place where creativity and community can thrive safely.

You will:

Build the Safety User Feature Store: Design and operate the pipelines that turn violations, reports, appeals, filter activity, and engagement signals into reliable, well-documented user features for training and serving.

Serve User Embeddings and Risk Scores: Take user embeddings and risk models from offline training to production, including low-latency online lookup and similar-user search for detection teams.

Build the Real-Time Path: Extend batch pipelines to streaming, so fresh behavioral signals reach models and downstream safety systems within minutes, not days.

Enable ML Teams to Move Fast: Partner with MLEs and Data Scientists to provide self-serve tooling for feature discovery, training data generation, offline evaluation, and A/B experiment hooks.

Own Data Quality and Governance: Build monitoring for feature freshness, drift, lineage, and ownership, so teams across Safety can trust and reuse signals.

Design for Trust and Auditability: Build systems that support explainability, fairness review, and appeals correction, since these signals directly shape consequences for millions of users.

Ship Code, Not Just Designs: Own end-to-end delivery, from system design through migration, testing, production operation, and oncall.

You have:

5+ years of experience building and operating production ML platforms, data infrastructure, or large-scale backend systems.

Strong experience with batch and streaming data pipelines (e.g., Spark, Flink, Kafka) feeding feature stores or similar ingestion layers.

Experience designing low-latency serving systems: service APIs (RPC/gRPC), key-value storage, caching, and high-throughput read paths.

Proficiency in Python and a statically-typed backend language (Go, Java, or similar).

Solid production operations fundamentals: monitoring, alerting, oncall, and incident response for systems with real user-facing impact.

Comfort partnering with ML engineers, with a working understanding of the model lifecycle: training data generation, offline evaluation, deployment, and drift monitoring.

Nice to have:

Experience in Trust Safety, content moderation, user detection, or other adversarial domains

Experience with user-level modeling or user understanding

Experience with embeddings and vector similarity search in production

Familiarity with feature store platforms and signal governance

Experience building agentic workflows or tool interfaces for LLM agents (e.g., MCP)

You are:

A Systems Thinker: capable of designing platforms that ML and detection teams build on, not just point solutions.

A Pragmatic Builder: scrappy and impact-oriented, comfortable turning scattered, undocumented signals into clean, well-owned data products.

Comfortable with Ambiguity: this space is still being defined, and you will help set the standard rather than follow an existing one.

A Reliability-Minded Engineer: you treat data quality, testing, migrations, and oncall as first-class parts of the job.

A Strong Collaborator: you work effectively across ML, Policy, Product, and Detection teams, and make the people around you faster.

Committed to Ethical, Responsible Engineering: dedicated to building systems that are fair, auditable, and operate with the utmost responsibility toward user safety.
