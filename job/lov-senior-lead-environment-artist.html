Senior / Lead Environment Artist

Black Banner Studios · České Budějovice · Remote

Lead Art Remote Posted 2026-11-20 Full time
Apply on Black Banner Studios’s site →

Legacy of Valor is an ambitious medieval RPG and strategy game combining massive real-time battles, third-person melee combat, and tactical army control in Unreal Engine 5. At Black Banner Studios, we're looking for an exceptional Senior / Lead Environment Artist with outstanding modeling, sculpting, and texturing skills, strong artistic judgment, and extensive experience creating beautiful, detailed, game-ready environment assets. Our world draws inspiration from 14th-century Europe, combining historical authenticity with a grounded fantasy aesthetic. We want to create visually rich, believable environments filled with distinctive architecture, props, and materials that reflect the character, history, and everyday life of our world. You'll focus primarily on creating high-quality 3D assets, from individual props and architectural elements to modular building kits, with particular attention to natural materials, surface details, weathering, and artistic consistency. You'll work closely with our Art Director, level artists, technical artists, and other environment artists to ensure your creations look beautiful and perform efficiently in Unreal Engine 5. We're open to candidates at both Senior and Lead levels, with the final title and responsibilities determined by experience and expertise. If you believe you'd be a great fit for this role, even if you don't meet every requirement listed below, we'd still love to hear from you. Please don't hesitate to apply! Create beautiful, highly detailed, game-ready 3D props, architecture, and environmental assets, including modeling, sculpting, UV mapping, texturing, and material creation. Capture the natural characteristics of wood, stone, metal, fabric, and other materials, including believable wear, aging, decay, and environmental effects in both geometry and textures. Develop high-quality modular asset kits and reusable architectural elements to support efficient environment production. Take ownership of asset look development, refining textures, materials, and material instances in Unreal Engine to achieve the desired artistic quality and visual consistency. Ensure assets meet technical and performance requirements through efficient geometry, topology, UVs, materials, LODs, and optimization. Collaborate closely with level artists, lighting artists, technical artists, and other developers to ensure assets integrate naturally into the game world. Work with internal procedural generation tools, providing feedback and helping improve asset creation and integration workflows. Mentor junior artists, provide constructive feedback, and help maintain consistent artistic and technical quality across the project. Extensive professional experience creating high-quality environment assets for large-scale or technically complex game projects. Exceptional 3D modeling, sculpting, and texturing skills, demonstrated through a strong portfolio of beautiful, believable, and technically well-executed game assets. Strong ability to capture the characteristics of natural and manufactured materials, including realistic surface details, wear, aging, weathering, and decay. Advanced proficiency with professional 3D modeling and sculpting tools such as Blender, Maya, 3ds Max, or ZBrush. Strong skills in PBR texturing and material creation using Substance Painter, Substance Designer, or equivalent tools. Practical experience creating optimized, game-ready assets, including retopology, UV mapping, LODs, materials, and real-time performance considerations. Strong understanding of modular environment asset creation, architectural construction, scale, and visual consistency. Experience integrating and refining assets and materials in Unreal Engine 5. Ability to work independently, solve complex artistic and technical challenges, and collaborate effectively within a multidisciplinary team. Strong practical experience with complete level art production, transforming level design blockouts into beautiful, believable, and optimized playable environments. Experience with Unreal Engine 5 World Partition, including large-world environment workflows, level streaming, and content organization. Excellent understanding of composition, environmental storytelling, player navigation, sightlines, and gameplay readability. Experience creating and integrating foliage and natural environments. Experience with environment lighting, atmosphere, and weather systems. Experience with historically grounded architecture, particularly medieval European settlements and castles. Experience with Unreal Engine 5 PCG, Nanite, or other large-scale environment workflows. Experience with Houdini or procedural environment creation. Experience working on AAA and/or large open-world game projects. Passion for video games, particularly medieval RPGs, open-world games, and strategy titles. Location: Remote / International, with the option to work in the Czech Republic. Engagement: Full-time, long-term collaboration. Contract: Contractor / B2B worldwide, or direct employment for candidates residing in or willing to relocate to the Czech Republic. Start Date: As soon as mutually agreed. Language: Professional working proficiency in English. Compensation: Competitive, based on experience and expertise.
