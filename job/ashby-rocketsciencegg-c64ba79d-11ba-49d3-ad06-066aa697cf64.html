Senior Product Designer / Manager - 1099 Contract

Rocket Science · Albany · Remote

Senior Design Remote Posted 2026-11-16 Contractor

This is a project-based 1099 contract starting December 2026. We expect the engagement to last up to 9 months (until August 2027), depending on project needs.

This role is remote however our office is in Albany, NY, so welcome applicants that want to work in a hybrid set up.

Part of the Rocket Science Group, Atomic Theory is a multinational team of seasoned AAA veterans with roots extending from Blizzard, PUBG, Epic, and Unity. We have a track record of delivering player-centric, AAA-quality experiences, and we're unwavering in our commitment to performance, accessibility, and the engineering frameworks that underpin exceptional gameplay.

Atomic Theory is more than just a studio — it's a collaborative space where diverse experiences intersect to solve the toughest challenges in modern game development. With offices in Albany NY, Cardiff UK, and Brighton UK, we thrive at the opportunity to Get Sh!t Done on a global scale.

ABOUT THE ROLE

You own the design of each feature from first conversation through client acceptance. The role is part systems designer and part product manager, with enough UX skill to shape the screens alongside our UI/UX Designer. You define how new simulation systems work, gather requirements directly from subject matter experts and end users, and keep scope, documentation and the build aligned on a fixed timeline. This role travels to client and customer sites roughly once a month.

The engagement builds features for a large-scale real-time simulation used for professional training. The client sets high-level design direction. Beyond that, most of the detailed design is ours to define: how each feature works, how it looks, and how it holds up in front of end users. Our team's job is to turn that direction into designs the client signs off on and engineers can build. Experience on military simulations, wargames or complex strategy games is required.

THE PROGRAM

The program runs from December 2026 to a hard code lock in June 2027. The date does not move. Most milestones land between late March and mid May, and each one needs a demo and client sign-off.

The scope splits into two tracks:

- Core delivery: a backlog of roughly 250 tickets plus larger pieces including user roles and permissions, operator control tools, post-session reporting, scenario import, integrations with systems from other vendors, and large-scale swarm behaviors.

- New features: new simulation systems such as underwater units, in-flight refueling, weather, satellites, and electronic effects, plus a logistics interface and an overhaul of AI unit behaviors. The client provides high-level direction for each, starting mid January. The detailed design is ours to define.

The engineering team works heavily with AI coding agents and moves fast. The bottleneck is rarely build speed. It is unclear requirements, design decisions nobody has made yet, slow reviews, and dependencies on people outside our control.

WHAT WE'RE LOOKING FOR

- Simulation and systems design: You turn operational concepts into concrete, buildable mechanics: rules, states, interactions and tunable values such as speeds, ranges, capacities and timings. You have experience designing for military simulations, wargames or complex strategy games, where the right number matters as much as the right screen.

- Requirements gathering with domain experts: You're comfortable working directly with subject matter experts, end users and other stakeholders who know the problem better than you do. You run interviews and working sessions, translate specialized language and real-world workflows into clear requirements, and ask the follow-up questions that surface edge cases. You build enough trust that people bring you the details that matter.

- Clear, testable design documentation: You write design documents engineers can build from and stakeholders can sign off on. They include numbered requirements, pass/fail acceptance tests, edge cases, risks, assumptions and explicit out-of-scope items. You keep documents current as decisions change, so the design, the backlog and the build stay aligned.

- Product ownership: You own the backlog for your features: prioritizing with the client, writing tickets, tracking dependencies and preparing demos that get to sign-off quickly. You spot scope creep and route it into the change process instead of absorbing it.

- UX fluency: You sketch flows and wireframes yourself and understand usability, information architecture and interaction design well enough to judge whether a screen supports the mechanic. You give the UI/UX designer clear direction to work from and can hold your own in a design critique.

- Scope judgment on fixed timelines: You separate the essential first version from later enhancements and design within fixed timelines and budgets. You work with engineers to understand feasibility trade-offs early, and you can carry several designs at once through interim reviews and approvals.

- Feature leadership and collaboration: You lead each feature from first conversation through acceptance. You bring the UI/UX designer in early so mechanics and screens are designed together, and you stay available to engineers during the build.

BACKGROUND

- 6+ years across systems design, game design and product management or production, with shipped games or simulations.

- A portfolio or case studies showing systems you designed and features you carried through to release.

- Hands-on experience with a game engine such as Unity, Unreal or Godot.

- Fluency with AI tools in your own workflow, and an understanding of what changes when the engineering team builds with agents.

- Willingness to travel within the US roughly once a month.

PLEASE NOTE

- Training: you will complete the client's data-handling training before getting access to their systems and channels.

- Start: as soon as possible, with the first milestone due in mid December 2026.

- Duration: 9-month contract, December 2026 through August 2027. That covers delivery through the June code lock plus the support period after it. We are open to extending beyond nine months, with a possible path to a full-time role.

- Location: US-based with overlap on US Eastern business hours. A move to full time would mean relocating to our office in upstate New York.

- Travel: roughly once a month to client and customer sites within the Mid-West US.
