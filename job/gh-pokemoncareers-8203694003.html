Get to know The Pokémon Company International

The Pokémon Company International manages the Pokémon property outside of Asia and is responsible for brand management, licensing and marketing, the Pokémon Trading Card Game, the animated TV series, home entertainment, and the official Pokémon website. Pokémon was launched in Japan in 1996 and today is one of the most popular children’s entertainment properties in the world.

Get to know the role

Job Title: Bilingual Project Manager (Bilingual Japanese/English)

Job Summary: This role leads product development operations and Trading Card Game (TCG) specific confidential information management efforts, coordinating prototype and confirming product specification with internal teams and external partners while ensuring the secure handling of TCG-specific, sensitive product information throughout the development lifecycle.

FLSA Classification (US Only): Exempt

People Manager: No

What you’ll do

Lead the planning and coordination of product prototypes, samples, and specification reviews across a wide range of products and initiatives.

Serve as a key liaison between internal stakeholders and external business partners to communicate prototype requirements, product specifications, project updates, and technical information.

Review, validate, and manage product specifications to ensure accuracy, consistency, and alignment with business requirements and manufacturing capabilities.

Collaborate closely with cross-functional teams, vendors, manufacturers, and other partners to resolve specification-related questions, risks, and issues throughout the product development process.

Work closely with Japanese counterparts to develop and deliver TCG specific information security and confidentiality training programs for internal relevant stakeholders.

Evaluate, strengthen, and continuously improve internal processes, policies, and governance related to the protection of TCG specific confidential information.

Promote a culture of TCG specific information security awareness by partnering with teams across relevant organization to ensure compliance with confidentiality requirements and best practices related to TCG.

Act as a primary point of contact for TCG specific information security and related confidential information management matters, facilitating communication and alignment between internal teams and external partners.

Support the implementation, monitoring, and enforcement of TCG specific information security controls and procedures within the relevant organizations.

Work closely with Global Security Operations Center (GSOC) team and report confidential information leakage and/or product error related incidents to external business partners in a timely manner.

Prepare and deliver timely reports, updates, and incident summaries to internal leadership, affected stakeholders, and external partners as required.

Support Sr. Manager, Bi-lingual Project Manager to maintain and promote excellent relationships with internal and external stakeholders.

Seek to optimize and simplify processes as they are developed with a focus on continual improvement.

Translate various product/project related documents from English to Japanese and/or Japanese to English.

Interpret between Japanese and English in various meetings and/or events to support attendees/participants/competitors communications.

Document decisions and action items with meetings notes, following up on action items as required.

Contribute to driving various team initiatives that are tied to the team goals.

Responsible for other administrative tasks based on business needs.

What you’ll bring

Required

Five (5) to eight (8) years of related professional experience, including at least four (4) years in a project management or similar role.

Bachelors degree.

Excellent verbal and written communication skills in both Japanese and English at a near-native level, with the ability to effectively bridge communication between global teams and external partners.

Experience working in a bilingual and multicultural business environment.

Experience working in toy, consumer products, trading card games, printing, packaging, or related manufacturing industries.

Working knowledge of manufacturing and production processes for printed materials, toys, collectibles, or other physical products, including prototype development, pre-production, and mass production workflows.

Proven experience coordinating with cross-functional teams, manufacturers, suppliers, and external partners to manage product specifications, production requirements, and project deliverables.

Familiarity with supplier, factory, or vendor management in a global manufacturing environment.

Demonstrated ability to identify risks, strengthen processes, and drive continuous improvement in confidential information management and operational controls.

Preferred

Experience managing projects involving highly confidential information, intellectual property, unreleased products, or sensitive business initiatives.

Experience managing confidential information controls on both the product development and production/manufacturing sides of an organization.

Experience developing, implementing, or maintaining confidential information management practices and processes within production, product development, or business operations environments.

Experience conducting information security, confidentiality, or compliance-related training for internal stakeholders.

Knowledge of information security frameworks, policies, and best practices related to confidential information handling and intellectual property protection.

Strong understanding of information security and confidentiality requirements throughout the product development and manufacturing lifecycle.

Familiarity with Pokémon brand including Pokémon names in both English and Japanese.

Strong organizational and project management skills, with the ability to manage multiple priorities in a fast-paced environment.

Detail-oriented, dedicated to accuracy, and able to maintain focus under pressure.

Ability to manage time efficiently and ability to organize work effectively.

Ability to interpret between Japanese and English in meetings and/or events.

Proficient in Word, Outlook, Excel, and PowerPoint.

Passion for learning and growth.
