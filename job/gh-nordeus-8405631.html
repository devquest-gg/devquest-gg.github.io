Senior Ad Monetization Manager

Nordeus · Belgrade

Senior Other Posted 2026-11-20 Full time
Apply on Nordeus’s site →

Ads are a core part of how our games grow, and we’re looking for someone who has already owned ad revenue and wants to take it to the next level. Your primary responsibility will be to lead the roadmap and day-to-day optimization of ad monetization on one of the world-class mobile games in our portfolio. You’ll also work closely with the rest of the AdMon team to safeguard and grow ad revenue across the rest of our portfolio, including Top Eleven, Golf Rival, CSR2 and Top Goal. Together with game teams, analysts and data scientists, you’ll find new ways to grow revenue without hurting the player experience. If you’re a data-driven monetization expert who likes owning outcomes, building partner relationships and shaping product decisions, this role is for you.

THE DIFFERENCE YOU’LL MAKE:

In this role, you won’t be just optimizing placements - you will act as a true business owner driving the commercial success of a world-class mobile game. By leveraging our data and your deep understanding of different monetization formats, your decisions will directly fuel our revenue growth and help scale a multi-million-dollar ad business. Because we operate in small, agile teams, your impact will be immediate and visible across the studio, as well as Zynga and Take-Two Interactive. Whether you are unlocking a highly profitable new ad partner, introducing a breakthrough monetization format, or proactively driving a new cross-functional initiative, your work will directly strengthen the commercial foundation of our games and shape the future of Nordeus within the Zynga and Take-Two Interactive family.

WHAT YOU’LL DO:

Own ad revenue across our portfolio (rewarded video, offerwall, and other formats), and set the roadmap and targets for each stream

Manage and optimize the mediation setup to maximize overall ad revenue

Design, run and analyze A/B tests with analysts and data scientists, and turn the results into clear decisions

Work with game teams on ad placement and experience design that balances ad revenue, retention, engagement and IAP

Track and report on key metrics such as ARPDAU, eCPM, impressions per DAU, ad LTV and revenue forecasts, and flag risks and opportunities early

Research and evaluate new ad networks, partners, formats, tools and mechanisms, and lead their rollout from test to scale

Build and maintain strong relationships with ad networks, mediation platforms and industry peers, and negotiate terms where relevant

Keep up with competitors and market trends, including privacy and platform changes (ATT, GDPR, Privacy Sandbox), and adapt our strategy accordingly

Present insights and recommendations to cross-functional teams and leadership

Share knowledge, set best practices and, where relevant, mentor junior colleagues

WHO YOU’LL WORK WITH:

Game Team: You will work hand-in-hand with virtually all disciplines within the product team: Product Managers, Producers, Developers, Data Scientists, UX/UI, Community and others. In the ideation phase, your direct communication will mostly flow through a Product Manager and a Producer. Together, you will shape the AdMon roadmap for the game and include other disciplines as the features take shape.

Analytics: our Data Scientists and Analytics experts will be there to support you throughout the feature life-cycle: ideation, opportunity estimates, testing, monitoring and analysis. There will be ample opportunity for you to run your own analysis and make initial assessments. However, you will have expert support, especially for more complex analyses.

User Acquisition: Our ability to monetize depends highly on UA activity. On the flipside, the user acquisition team depends highly on the Product Team’s ability to monetize the users they bring in. Given how significant the AdMon pillar is in our monetization stack, it will be vital for you to run two-way communication with the UA lead, in order to maximize the revenue potential of the game.

Internal AdMon Team: You will work closely with peers managing the rest of the games in our portfolio - sharing insights, shaping roadmaps and looking for synergies where applicable.

Zynga AdMon Teams: You will also get a chance to work with a wide range of admon experts across the Zynga family: people specializing in supply, yield, ad tech and business development.

WE ARE A MATCH, IF YOU:

4+ years of hands-on experience in ad monetization, ideally in mobile games or apps

Experience with ad mediation platforms and ad networks (such as AppLovin MAX, Unity LevelPlay, AdMob or similar)

A solid grasp of ad-economy metrics and how they connect to user behavior, retention and LTV

Advanced analytical skills and proficiency with Excel, Sheets or other software

Experience designing and interpreting A/B tests and turning data into clear business recommendations

Strong communication and stakeholder management skills, including influencing game and product teams

Comfort with ambiguity and ownership: you spot opportunities, prioritize, and drive them to completion

A degree in Marketing, Statistics, Finance, Economics, Business or a similar field (or equivalent experience)

BONUS POINTS:

Experience with hybrid monetization (ads and IAP) in free-to-play games

Knowledge of UA and its relationship to ad revenue (e.g. ROAS-driven decisions)

Experience with ad quality, fraud prevention and consent management

Passion for mobile games and tech

Love for sports, especially football or golf
