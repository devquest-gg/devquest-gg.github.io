Senior Brand Manager

Riot Games · Los Angeles

Senior Marketing $150K–$208K Posted 2026-04-06
Apply on Riot Games’s site →

Brand Management at Riot is focused on players and fans. Our mission is to make it better to be a player by building trust, inspiring fandom, and creating meaningful, resonant experiences across every touchpoint. For Teamfight Tactics, this means celebrating the strategy, creativity, and playfulness at the heart of the game and bringing these values to life.

As a Brand Manager III for Teamfight Tactics (TFT), you will lead high-impact initiatives and deliver world-class campaigns that strengthen TFT’s brand identity. You will shape global perception by defining how the game shows up to players, crafting integrated publishing strategies and guiding creative expression across sets, features, and player moments. This role builds a cohesive, enduring brand, ensuring consistency in tone, voice, and visual identity while partnering closely with cross-functional teams to bring the brand to life.

Reporting to the Senior Manager, Brand Marketing, you will own go-to-market strategy and execution for global player experiences, balancing long-term brand building with moment-based impact. Operating at the intersection of product, publishing, and creative, you will lead campaign development, align partners, manage budgets, and uphold a high bar for craft to deliver culturally resonant, distinctly TFT work.

Responsibilities:

Brand Campaign Leadership

Lead the creation of high-quality global campaigns in partnership with creative, production, and community teams

Partner with game development leadership to shape campaign strategy across sets, features, and key moments

Develop integrated, cross-platform campaign plans that express TFT’s gameplay, creativity, and evolving set fantasies

Ensure campaigns reinforce a cohesive brand identity, tone, and player promise

Cross-Functional Collaboration Global Publishing

Partner with product, communications, social, and community teams to align on campaign narratives, publishing priorities, and evergreen programs

Collaborate with insights to inform strategy and global publishing decisions

Lead and support Regional Brand Leads through campaign cycles, ensuring global alignment and effective local execution

Creative Operational Excellence

Provide strategic direction to guide creative development with a consistent tone, voice, and narrative

Translate insights into clear creative briefs and campaign strategies

Manage brand marketing budgets and execution, ensuring delivery on time and on budget

Stay connected to players, culture, and the competitive landscape to inform brand direction

Required Qualifications:

6+ years of experience in brand strategy, product marketing, publishing, or equivalent roles

Experience leading integrated go-to-market campaigns from strategy through execution and measurement

Strong understanding of growth and performance marketing, with the ability to connect brand strategy to business outcomes

Proven ability to lead cross-functional teams and influence stakeholders across disciplines and regions

Strong strategic thinking, with experience translating insights into plans, briefs, and campaign direction

Experience working with gaming audiences, ideally within live-service or strategy-driven genres

Excellent communication skills, with experience aligning diverse stakeholders

Strong organizational and project management skills, managing complex timelines and dependencies

Desired Qualifications:

Familiarity with TFT, Riot IP, and the strategy or live-service game landscape

Experience working with game development teams and understanding development cycles

Experience with media planning, user acquisition, or channel strategy within full-funnel campaigns

Experience managing campaigns across PC, mobile, and console

Strong awareness of digital culture and player communities

Passion for games across PC, mobile, and/or console

For this role, you ll find success through craft expertise, a collaborative spirit, and decision-making that prioritizes the delight of players. We will be looking at your past studies, experience, and your personal relationship with games. If you embody player empathy and care about players experiences, this could be your role!
