Brand Manager - VALORANT Mobile

Riot Games · Shanghai

Mid Marketing Posted 2026-11-18 Full time
Apply on Riot Games’s site →

Riot Games was established in 2006 by entrepreneurial gamers who believe that player-focused game development can result in genre-defining games. Back in 2009, that vision gave birth to our debut title League of Legends, which achieved critical acclaim among players and became the most played PC game in the world. We are honored to serve over 100 million players worldwide every month.

We’re looking for humble but ambitious, razor-sharp professionals who can teach us a thing or two. We promise to return the favor. We embrace those who see things differently, aren’t afraid to experiment, and who have a healthy disregard for constraints. As a Rioter in Team China, you ll join a team of culturally diverse professionals that share our prioritization of player experience, rejection of convention and maniacal passion for gaming.

Brand Management at Riot, like everything else at Riot, is focused on our players and fans and our philosophy is that brand at Riot exists to ensure trust, to help inspire interest and to deliver meaning for all things that we do - this includes the Riot Brand, League of Legends MOBA, VALORANT, League of Legends esports and our other games.

As a Brand Manager at Riot, you will build connections between players and our games. You will translate player insights into brand strategies and creative marketing that express each game’s identity and build lasting player relationships. You will set brand direction and lead marketing for VALORANT Mobile, helping grow the game into a category leader in China. You will turn VALORANT’s distinctive style, cultural relevance, and competitive DNA into a competitive advantage in China’s mobile market. You will report to the Brand Lead for VALORANT in China.

Responsibilities:

Brand Strategy Planning: Develop annual brand and publishing strategies for China across key moments, events, and game updates.

Mobile Publishing Marketing: Plan and deliver integrated campaigns with publishing partners, connecting content, community, and channels to support player acquisition, engagement, and retention.

Creative Development: Develop briefs, shape campaign concepts, and guide execution to express VALORANT’s distinctive style and tone.

Cross-functional Collaboration: Drive effective communication across teams and functions to align priorities, clarify responsibilities, and enable timely decisions.

Problem Solving: Assess strategic options and their business implications to resolve complex brand and publishing challenges and recommend actionable solutions.

Insights Measurement: Use player research, market trends, and campaign results to assess effectiveness and refine plans.

Required Qualifications:

Mobile Publishing Marketing: 8+ years of brand management or marketing experience, including mobile game publishing and integrated campaign planning, execution, and evaluation in China.

Brand Strategy: Ability to translate player and market insights into brand positioning and actionable marketing plans.

Creative Judgment: Experience developing and evaluating creative concepts against brand strategy and player needs.

Cross-functional Collaboration: Experience aligning stakeholders across teams, regions, and functions and coordinating delivery across different priorities.

Problem Solving Judgment: Ability to structure ambiguous problems, evaluate strategic options, and make recommendations grounded in business impact.

English Communication: English proficiency sufficient to discuss complex topics in cross-regional meetings and present proposals to global stakeholders.

Desired Qualifications:

Competitive Games: Experience marketing competitive multiplayer mobile games.

Brand Knowledge: Familiarity with VALORANT, esports, and cultural trends among gaming communities in China.

Agency Partnerships: Experience working with creative or brand agencies.

For this role, you ll find success through craft expertise, a collaborative spirit, and decision-making that prioritizes the delight of players. We will be looking at your past studies, experience, and your personal relationship with games. If you embody player empathy and care about the experiences of players, this could be the role for you!
