Senior / Lead Project Manager - Art Production Focus

Black Banner Studios · České Budějovice · Remote

Lead Production Remote Posted 2026-11-20 Full time
Apply on Black Banner Studios’s site →

Legacy of Valor is an ambitious medieval RPG and strategy game combining massive real-time battles, third-person melee combat, and tactical army control in Unreal Engine 5. At Black Banner Studios, we're looking for an experienced Senior Project Manager with a strong background in hands-on game art production and a deep understanding of the creative and technical processes involved in developing high-quality game assets. You'll help manage the development of Legacy of Valor, with a particular focus on our growing art team, which includes character artists, animators, environment artists, technical artists, concept artists, and external collaborators. We're looking for someone who understands art production from practical experience, not just project management theory. Someone who can independently create realistic schedules, identify dependencies, anticipate bottlenecks, and help artists deliver their best work without unnecessary bureaucracy. You'll work closely with our Game Director, Lead Programmer, Art Director, and other team members, taking ownership of production planning, coordination, and progress tracking in alignment with the studio's production leadership and overall development priorities. We're open to candidates at both Senior and Lead levels, with the final title and responsibilities determined by experience, production management expertise, and the scope of leadership responsibilities. If you believe you'd be a great fit for this role, even if you don't meet every requirement listed below, we'd still love to hear from you. Please don't hesitate to apply! Plan, organize, and coordinate art production across multiple disciplines, from concept art and asset creation to animation, technical integration, and final in-game implementation. Independently develop and maintain realistic production schedules, milestones, and task dependencies, while coordinating resource planning and allocation with department leads and studio management. Understand and evaluate the actual work involved in creating game assets, helping establish achievable estimates and identify production risks. Track progress, anticipate bottlenecks, and proactively resolve scheduling, dependency, and resource conflicts. Coordinate internal artists, programmers, designers, and external contractors, ensuring smooth communication and timely delivery. Oversee programming and other development tasks as part of the overall production plan, working closely with the relevant technical leads. Help establish efficient production workflows, task management practices, and clear deliverable requirements without unnecessary overhead. Monitor production capacity, priorities, and deadlines, keeping the team focused on the most important objectives. Provide clear, accurate progress reports and communicate risks, delays, and proposed solutions to management. Support artists and other developers by removing obstacles, improving coordination, and protecting productive development time. Extensive professional experience in game development project management or production, including significant responsibility for art production on at least one shipped commercial title. Practical, hands-on experience creating game art assets, with a strong understanding of at least one major art production discipline. Broad knowledge of game art pipelines, including concept art, 3D modeling, texturing, rigging, animation, environment creation, and integration into a real-time engine. Demonstrated ability to independently create realistic production plans, estimates, schedules, milestones, and task dependencies. Strong understanding of the technical and artistic challenges involved in producing high-quality, game-ready assets. Experience managing multidisciplinary teams, external contractors, and complex production dependencies. Strong organizational, analytical, and problem-solving skills, with the ability to identify risks and propose practical solutions. Strong practical experience with production and project management tools such as ClickUp, Jira, ShotGrid, HacknPlan, Monday.com, or equivalent systems, including task tracking, scheduling, dependency management, and production reporting. Excellent communication skills and the ability to work independently, make informed decisions, and collaborate effectively with experienced developers. Professional working proficiency in English. Passion for video games, particularly medieval RPGs, open-world games, and strategy titles. Experience working on AAA and / or open-world game projects. Previous professional experience as an environment artist, character artist, animator, technical artist, or another game art production specialist. Experience managing art production involving Unreal Engine 5, including asset optimization and technical integration. Experience coordinating large-scale environment production, character pipelines, animation, or procedural content generation. Experience managing external art outsourcing and reviewing deliverables against technical and artistic requirements. Experience with development budgeting, resource forecasting, and production capacity planning. Experience working in small, highly experienced teams where autonomy, flexibility, and practical problem-solving are essential. Experience establishing, customizing, or improving production management workflows and tools to support growing multidisciplinary development teams. Location: Remote / International, with the option to work in the Czech Republic. Engagement: Full-time, long-term collaboration. Contract: Contractor / B2B worldwide, or direct employment for candidates residing in or willing to relocate to the Czech Republic. Start Date: As soon as mutually agreed. Language: Professional working proficiency in English. Compensation: Competitive, based on experience and expertise.
