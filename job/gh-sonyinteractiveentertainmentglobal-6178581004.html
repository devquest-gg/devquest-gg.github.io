Sony Interactive Entertainment isn’t just the Best Place to Play — it’s also the Best Place to Work. Sony Interactive Entertainment (SIE) is the company behind the PlayStation brand. As a subsidiary of Sony Group Corporation, we’re part of a proud legacy of innovation and excellence. SIE is a dynamic technology company, delivering cutting-edge hardware and network services to more than 100 million people and an entertainment leader, home to some of the most beloved and recognizable intellectual properties (IP) in the world. Our role at SIE is to create and nurture the experiences under the PlayStation brand, a name synonymous with entertainment excellence and creativity.

About the Role

PlayStation Studios is seeking a Senior Production Manager – Studio Operations to join our Head of Production organization. This is a high-impact role focused on improving how production operates across a portfolio of world-class game studios.

In this role, you will partner closely with development teams and cross-functional leaders to drive alignment, improve operational consistency, and help translate strategic priorities into effective execution. You’ll play a key part in enabling scalable, high-performing production practices across a spectrum of game development environments.

This position is ideal for someone who thrives in complex, fast-paced environments and enjoys working at the intersection of production, strategy, and operations.

What You’ll Do

Partner with Studios

Build strong relationships with development teams to understand production challenges, risks, and opportunities

Provide targeted support on high-priority initiatives and areas of need

Identify common themes across studios to inform broader improvements

Improve Production Operations

Establish and maintain clear, consistent reporting frameworks that support leadership visibility

Ensure project information is accurate, actionable, and accessible for decision-making

Support and manage key operating rhythms, including studio reviews and leadership forums

Drive Alignment Execution

Translate strategic initiatives into clear, actionable plans for studios and partner teams

Collaborate with production, product, finance, and other stakeholders to ensure alignment

Deliver structured, high-quality communication to support executive decision-making

Scale Best Practices

Document and standardize effective production processes and workflows

Identify opportunities to improve efficiency and reduce operational friction

Partner with central teams to align tools, processes, and ways of working

Provide Advisory Support

Help teams navigate ambiguity, change, and complex production challenges

Develop practical frameworks, recommendations, and plans that improve outcomes

What We’re Looking For

Minimum Qualifications

Senior level experience in game development or game publishing

Strong communication skills with the ability to clearly convey complex ideas

Experience working in cross-functional, collaborative environments

Proven ability to manage ambiguity and drive progress in evolving situations

Preferred Qualifications

Background working across production, product, program management, or operations

Experience supporting leadership decision-making or executive operating rhythms

Familiarity with scaling processes and best practices across teams

Key Skills

Strong organizational and prioritization abilities

Problem-solving mindset with a proactive approach

Leading impactful change through relationships and influence, rather than authority

Ability to quickly understand new projects and contribute effectively

Comfortable influencing without direct authority and building strong working relationships
