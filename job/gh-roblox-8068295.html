Join our People and Systems Group as part of the Prosper Team as a temporary contractor, International Benefits Program Manager. The Prosper team’s mission is to inspire employees to achieve their fullest potential by offering innovative and competitive total rewards that support financial, mental, and physical wellbeing. This backfill role will primarily lead international benefits administration and report to the Director of Global Benefits.

This position is hybrid and located in San Mateo, California.

You will:

Lead international group benefit implementations in Australia, Belgium, Brazil, Germany, Japan, Singapore, and the Netherlands

Ensure international benefits and leaves compliance

Manage international group benefit renewals in India and the UK

Oversee international leave administration

Manage broker and insurer/vendor relationships

Manage annual international benefit overview sessions

Escalate employee inquiries/issues for international benefits

Partner with People, Payroll, Finance, Procurement, Communications, and Legal teams

Provide ad hoc support for U.S. benefits and Proactive Care initiatives

You have:

4+ years of experience in supporting benefit programs

Experience with international group benefits implementations

Experience with leaves of absence administration

The capability to manage multiple implementations and programs simultaneously

Knowledge of Google Suite and Workday (HCM and Benefits modules)
