Senior Gameplay Engineer – Unreal Engine / Blueprints (Contract)

Remote - Loveland, CO

About Studio Sirens Founded in 2024 under Snail Games, Studio Sirens is a development team focused on prototyping innovative gameplay systems, crafting bold new IP, and co-development projects. Blending technical precision with narrative ambition, the studio brings immersive worlds to life through a gameplay-first approach, supporting major releases while forging its own creative path within the Snail Games portfolio.

Contract term This engagement is expected to begin with a three-month term. Any extension would depend on project needs and mutual agreement. The contract may end earlier under the terms of the written agreement.

Scope of work Implement and improve gameplay systems using Unreal Engine Blueprints.

Diagnose and resolve gameplay bugs, integration issues, and performance bottlenecks in an existing production project.

Optimize Blueprint systems for runtime performance, maintainability, and multiplayer functionality.

Build and extend player-facing interfaces, including UMG widgets, menus, HUD elements, and gameplay interactions.

Implement network-aware gameplay features and troubleshoot replication issues.

Prototype solutions from high-level design goals and refine them with designers through feedback and playtesting.

Improve existing systems and workflows while maintaining compatibility with the project’s architecture.

Participate in technical reviews and use established AI-assisted development tools where appropriate to support implementation, debugging, and iteration.

Required experience Five or more years of professional game development experience, or equivalent experience delivering production-quality gameplay systems.

Strong proficiency with Unreal Engine 4 or 5, especially Blueprint-based gameplay development.

Hands-on experience implementing, debugging, and optimizing gameplay systems within an established project.

Working knowledge of Unreal gameplay architecture, replication, and network-aware Blueprint practices.

Ability to own loosely defined gameplay tasks, build practical solutions independently, and refine them through feedback.

Experience collaborating with designers, artists, and engineers in a production environment.

Ability to deliver maintainable implementations while balancing quality and development speed.

Preferred experience UMG, Blueprint widgets, HUDs, menus, and other player-facing interfaces.

ARK: Survival Ascended , the ARK DevKit or ModKit, or a comparable large-scale modding environment.

Competitive multiplayer, survival, sandbox, or systemic multiplayer games.

Unreal Engine C++ for extending Blueprint functionality or addressing performance bottlenecks.

Console development, platform performance constraints, or Unreal Engine internals.

AI-assisted development tools such as Codex, MCP integrations, or comparable workflows.

How to apply Applications will be reviewed on a rolling basis until the contract is filled. Applicants must be legally authorized to work in the United States at the time of application. Visa sponsorship, including H-1B sponsorship, is unavailable for this engagement.

Additional Information This work may involve mature content, including depictions of combat, violence, or other sensitive themes. Contractors are expected to engage with this material professionally as part of the project.
