Public Affairs Advisor

Discord · San Francisco Bay Area

Mid Other $192K–$216K Posted 2026-10-21 Full time
Apply on Discord’s site →

Discord has a highly engaged community of millions of daily active users who use the platform for many different reasons, but there’s one thing that nearly everyone does: play video games.

Discord plays a uniquely important role in the future of gaming, and we are focused on making it easier and more fun for people to hang out before, during, and after playing games.

Discord s Public Policy team builds and maintains relationships with the policymakers, regulators, civil society organizations, academics, and industry partners who shape the future of online platforms. We help these stakeholders understand how Discord works and how we approach safety and privacy, and we bring their perspectives back to inform how Discord builds products and policies.

In this role, you ll help lead Discord s external engagement strategy, building lasting partnerships with civil society, industry groups, and other stakeholders around the world. You ll manage our external partnerships portfolio, run our program for policymakers and other stakeholders visiting Discord s San Francisco headquarters, and lead special projects that advance the team s global priorities. You ll also keep the team running smoothly by owning budgets and operational projects. This person will report to the Head of Public Policy, Americas.

What You ll Be Doing

Help shape and execute a global external affairs strategy that strengthens Discord s relationships and reputation with policymakers, regulators, civil society, academics, and industry partners

Build and manage a portfolio of strategic partnerships, making sure each one advances clear policy and business objectives

Measure the impact of partnerships and engagements, and report outcomes to team and company leadership

Own Discord s headquarters visit program for policymakers, government officials, and other key stakeholders

Lead special projects for the Public Policy team, such as external events, policy research initiatives, and cross-regional campaigns

Develop external-facing materials such as briefing books, talking points, presentations, partnership proposals, and event programming

Own the Public Policy team s budgets and related operational projects

What you should have

5+ years of experience in public policy, public affairs, and/or civil society partnerships

Experience owning and executing a multi-year public affairs or partnership program

Experience building and managing partnerships with civil society, academic experts, and industry leaders across multiple regions

A track record of planning and executing high-level visits, delegations, or events for senior government officials and executives

Familiarity with technology policy issues

Outstanding written and verbal communication skills, especially executive briefings, and the ability to translate complex technical information into clear, compelling narratives for non-technical audiences

Strong project management skills and the judgment to juggle multiple workstreams, stakeholders, and deadlines

Candidates must reside in or be willing to relocate to the San Francisco Bay Area (Alameda, Contra Costa, Marin, Napa, San Francisco, San Mateo, Santa Clara, Solano, and Sonoma counties). Relocation assistance may be available.

For this role, the Hiring Manager would like folks to be in the office 1-2 days a week.
