Senior Community Manager

Nordeus · Belgrade

Senior Marketing Posted 2026-10-20 Full time
Apply on Nordeus’s site →

Ready to turn your passion for gaming into legendary community experiences? If connecting players is your main quest, you are at the right place.

We are looking for a Senior Community Manager to shape the community strategy for Top Goal, lead player-centric initiatives, and champion our players worldwide.

If you love turning player insights into action, driving meaningful engagement, and collaborating with a multidisciplinary team to shape the future of gaming, let’s connect!

THE DIFFERENCE YOU’LL MAKE:

You’ll shape how players connect with our games and each other. You’ll own the community strategy, turn player sentiment and feedback into actionable insights that drive user-centric development, and shape the player community journey end to end.

You’ll champion the player perspective across teams, ensuring we build games for the community, with the community.

WHAT YOU’LL DO:

Own core community platforms (Discord, Facebook Group, Reddit), set KPIs to track and optimize community channel performance

Leverage platform analytics and market trends to optimize channel strategy and guide strategic decision-making

Form and lead moderation teams to maintain quality and enforce Code of Conduct standards, while driving player communications for day-to-day inquiries and time-sensitive live-service incidents.

Translate community feedback into actionable insights to inform feature development and product roadmaps

Build relationships with early advocates and foster community-generated content

Lead official messaging, patch notes, and release communications in alignment with the Marketing and Game teams

Own community content creation, translating technical news and complex features into player-friendly explanations and educational content

Drive community challenges, playtests, giveaways, and events that drive active, long-term participation

Build and manage creator and community ambassador programs from end-to-end

WHO YOU’LL WORK WITH:

You’ll be part of a multidisciplinary Player Support and Social Media team, working with the wider Marketing Team at Nordeus to align and deliver exciting player-facing campaigns.

You’ll closely collaborate with the Game Designers and Developers on the Game Team to translate game knowledge to our global audience and bring player feedback and insights back to the team.

WE ARE A MATCH, IF YOU:

Are an active gamer with deep knowledge of mobile gaming spaces and social media platforms (Discord, FB, Reddit)

Have a background in Marketing, Communications, Journalism, PR, Psychology, or another related field

Have 5+ years of experience in community management, social media management, customer support field

Have strong copywriting and communication skills with an ability to tailor messages to international audiences

Keep up to date with the latest gaming trends and mobile games

Are passionate about building communities and connecting with end-users

BONUS POINTS:

Have previous experience in the gaming industry or with moderation for game titles

WHY YOU’LL LOVE WORKING HERE:

The team behind the game: transparency and trust from day one, paired with a strong sense of teamwork- that s the essence of who we are. It s not just what you do - it’s how you do it and who you do it with. With 280+ teammates from around the world, we’re on a mission to bring the joy of winning to millions.

We make your life at Nordeus hassle-free: enjoy Nordeus provided breakfast, lunch, snacks, and beverages, a fully-equipped gym, organized sports activities (yoga, Brazilian jiu-jitsu, basketball, football), an ergonomic workstation, top-notch tech equipment (including laptop, mobile phone and bill coverage, and other tech), a kids’ playroom, a music corner, board and video games + latest consoles.

Perks? We’ve got plenty: premium private medical insurance for you and your family, flexible working hours, take-what-you-need vacation policy, offsites abroad for the whole company, fully-paid maternity and paternity leave, employee stock purchase plan, access to L D platforms and opportunities and many more.

Make your mark on games played by millions: with over 300M registered users, Top Eleven is the world’s most successful football management game. Golf Rival, with more than 60M registered users, is the #2 mobile golf game, aiming to claim the top spot. With one more game in the works, we are continuing our efforts to create powerful mobile sports gaming experiences built to last.

Meaningful career experience: work with experienced game makers and mentors who will support you from day one, helping you map a career path that s true to you.

Impact beyond the screen: It is not just about us. We are part of something bigger than a job. We create a better future for generations to come through the efforts of the Nordeus Foundation.

GOOD TO KNOW:

This is a hybrid, on-site position based in Belgrade.

Our 3+2 hybrid model remains the standard - Monday to Wednesday in the office, Thursday and Friday are optional remote days. While we prefer working together from the office, we also understand that having some alone time or working from home (or some other location) can be beneficial. It’s about what works, not what’s prescribed.
