At Visual Concepts, we believe great games are made by diverse and empowered teams with a shared passion for play. Our studios in Novato, Irvine, Budapest, Shanghai, Austin, Boise, Seoul, and Parksville, Canada are committed to artistry and technical

What We Need:

The WWE 2K team is seeking a Senior Engineer to join our DevOps team to build, support, and improve infrastructure and pipelines to enable efficient, collaborative development across the studio. In this role, you will create and implement strategies for continuous integration, build operations, and automation that will deliver better, more reliable, developer experiences. You will collaborate across disciplines to create tools and systems that will help us make the next installment of WWE 2K the best yet!

What You Will Do:

Develop, maintain and improve CI/CD pipelines, build infrastructure, and deployment automation

Ensure the reliability, scalability, and performance of development and production environments

Improve developer workflows through automation and tooling, while ensuring high quality documentation and support

Oversee automated systems for build validation, test, and game performance

Who Will Be A Great Fit:

3+ years of experience in game development

Bachelor’s or Master’s Degree in Computer Science or related field, or equivalent experience

Experience with Perforce, Jenkins, Git, and/or other version control and automated build systems

Hands-on expertise with cloud infrastructure (AWS/GCP) and containerized environments

Experience with Infrastructure-as-Code (IaC) workflows and configuration management tools such as Puppet

Strong understanding of object oriented programming, modern architectural design patterns, and C#/C++

Systems engineering expertise across Windows and Linux environments, including scripting languages (Python, PowerShell, Bash) and familiarity with the .NET ecosystem

Self-motivated with excellent problem-solving skills

Ability to work and collaborate effectively as part of a team

Might Be Nice:

Experience working on multi-platform software projects

Experience with web technologies like React or Node.js

Familiarity with leveraging AI to enhance developer workflows

Shipped game titles, preferably on console

Experience with distributed, multi-team, or multi-studio environments

Love for video games (not just ours!)
