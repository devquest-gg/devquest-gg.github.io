The Role

We are seeking a detail-oriented Brand Ad Operations Specialist with experience in digital advertising to join our team. In this role, you ll be responsible for the end-to-end execution of campaigns from media plan development to trafficking, tracking, reporting, and optimization. You ll play a vital role in ensuring campaigns are launched accurately, perform efficiently, and deliver against client goals.

You Will

Campaign Setup Execution:

Build media plans in response to RFPs, incorporating platform capabilities, targeting strategies, and inventory availability.

Work closely with internal teams to traffic and launch campaigns across various platforms (e.g., Google Ads Roblox Ads).

Ensure accurate campaign setup and tracking, coordinating with internal teams as needed.

Campaign Management Optimization:

Monitor pacing, performance, and delivery across live campaigns, proactively flagging and resolving issues.

Analyze campaign data to recommend optimizations that drive improved performance (e.g., CTR, ROAS, CPM).

Reporting Insights:

Develop and maintain standardized and custom performance reports using internal dashboards and data tools.

Translate performance data into actionable insights for client-facing recaps and optimization recommendations.

Cross-Functional Process Support:

Partner with Product, and Engineering teams to enhance campaign setup processes and troubleshoot issues.

Contribute to building scalable workflows and documentation that support repeatable campaign operations.

You Have

6-8 years of experience in ad operations, programmatic, campaign management, or digital media planning.

Familiarity with digital ad platforms (e.g., Google Ads, Meta Ads Manager, The Trade Desk, DV360) and tracking/tagging tools (e.g., Google Tag Manager, Google Analytics).

Strong understanding of digital advertising KPIs, attribution models, and media math.

Experience building or managing media plans and flighting schedules.

Excellent organizational skills and attention to detail, with the ability to manage multiple campaigns at once.

Basic understanding of SQL and/or experience working with data platforms is a plus.

Strong communication skills; able to clearly explain campaign performance and strategy to cross-functional stakeholders.
