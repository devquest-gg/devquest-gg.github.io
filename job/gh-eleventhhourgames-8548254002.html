Social Media and Influencer Manager

Eleventh Hour Games is a fast growing studio, focused on a passion for top-down action RPGs.

We value open communication, close-knit teamwork, and opportunity for ownership and growth! Our mission is to gather and empower the next generation of creators to forge extraordinary games. Extraordinary games build passionate communities, which create passionate people.

Passionate people turn into next year s creators. With our breakout title Last Epoch, we are moving fast into the next round of adventure!

THE OPPORTUNITY

EHG is looking for a passionate and highly organized Social Media and Influencer Manager to help grow, engage, and cultivate our Last Epoch social media presence and creator partnerships. This role will focus on developing social strategies, managing creator relationships, supporting our marketing and community campaigns, and bring the world of Last Epoch to life through our social media channels.

RESPONSIBILITIES:

Develop and execute social media strategies across platforms including X, Instagram, Facebook, Youtube, Bluesky, Tik Tok, YouTube, and other emerging channels

Plan, schedule, and publish social media content, supporting seasonal releases, campaigns, live updates, and community initiatives

Collaborate with marketing, publishing, community, and creative teams to support go to market campaigns and events

Build and maintain relationships with influencers, streamers, content creators, brand ambassadors, and agencies

Track, analyze, and report on social media and influencer campaign performance

Create engaging social media content and associated written social media copy

Stay up to date with social media trends, updates, and creator programs

Identify new opportunities that align with Last Epoch’s audience and core values

Proactive approach to engage with players, cultivate positive relationships, and build brand trust with our community

QUALIFICATIONS:

4+ years of experience managing large scale social media channels

Strong understanding of gaming culture, ARPG players and communities, creator platforms, and social media trends

Experience managing influencer campaigns across multiple platforms

Prior experience running community based social and influencer campaigns

Familiarity with social publishing tools (e.g. Hootsuite, Sprout, etc.)

Strong written communication skills with the ability to adapt to each channel specific audience

Highly organized with the ability to manage multiple campaigns and priorities with tight deadline

PREFERENCES:

Experience with Twitch Drop campaigns and creator reward programs

Experience with asset management, video editing, and template management
