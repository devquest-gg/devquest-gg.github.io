Performance Tracking: Actively track progress against targets across the fiscal year (FY) and confidently implement course corrections as required to safeguard our commercial trajectory.

Team Tooling Advocacy: Define the team and tooling needs of the game, collaborating closely with the HoF and DoL to secure resources.

2. Strategic Roadmap Design Track Orchestration Full Roadmap Ownership: Own the long-term product roadmap and prioritize the backlog to balance commercial beats with player engagement.

Functional Track Leadership: Set clear strategic goals, guardrails, and commercial frameworks for the LiveOps and Strategic Roadmap tracks, ensuring both pipelines are deeply synchronized.

High-Level Feasibility Partnering: Work directly with the track PMs to ensure the high-level roadmap and priorities are well-planned, feasible, and realistic given the team’s resources and constraints.

Strategic vs. Tactical Boundary: Empower the track PMs and PJMs to fully own their day-to-day sprint execution, scheduling, and agile ceremonies, while you maintain the strategic "what" and "why".

3. Feature Design Process Optimization Strategic KPI Input: While you do not have direct ownership of detailed feature design (which belongs to our design discipline), you will provide critical input on design goals and target KPIs to ensure all features align with strategic growth.

Process Support: Support resource allocation to continuously implement, review, and improve internal team processes alongside the project managers.

Key Recruitment Stakeholder: Act as a critical stakeholder and decision-maker in the recruitment process for key project roles.

Strategic Functional Leadership: Proven experience leading and aligning other PMs and PJMs, coordinating complex multi-track roadmap strategies, and keeping diverse product tracks in sync.

Commercial DNA: Strong background in managing product P Ls, designing forecasts, and executing high-yield monetization/LiveOps strategies.

Empowered Collaborator: Exceptional ability to lead and align a large, cross-functional team (~25 people) without needing direct HR reports, relying instead on strong partnership, clear logic, and collaborative influence.

Master of Delegation: A leader who understands how to empower PMs and PJMs to run daily sprint mechanics, focusing instead on high-level production milestones, commercial strategy, and team alignment.

Analytical Rigor: Deep comfort with data validation, cohort analysis, and transforming player metrics into strategic product decisions.

Time Zone Alignment: Located in a time zone within +/- 3 hours of CEST to ensure smooth, real-time collaboration within our remote-first setup.

WHAT WE OFFER The opportunity to pilot the fastest-growing, highest-potential game in our portfolio.

A highly autonomous environment where your strategic decisions directly impact the game s P L and growth.

A mature, collaborative studio culture with a dedicated team of 25 world-class craft experts.

A competitive compensation package and flexible remote/hybrid working conditions.

About New Moon Production

Since 2016 we have released multiple games on many different platforms including Empire: World War 3, Empire: Age of Knights, Big Company: Skytopia and the much beloved Big Farm: Mobile Harvest that is enjoyed by a quarter million players daily. At New Moon Production we embrace diversity and we are an equal opportunities employer. Our studio, or ‘New Moon Family’ is made up of highly talented individuals from all over the world from more than 17 nationalities.

New Moon Production is part of Stillfront. Stillfront is a global gaming company with 1200+ employees. We develop a wide range of digital games that attract over 40 million players each month. From well-established franchises like Supremacy, Big Farm, and BitLife to niche games, we span many different genres, including strategy, simulation, RPG and action, and casual and mash-up games.
