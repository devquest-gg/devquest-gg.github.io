We re looking for a strategic Finance and Accounting leader to join our Operations group to define Bonfire’s long-term approach to revenue, expense recognition, and reporting as our business model evolves. This isn t a role about executing the close and moving on. It s about setting the direction other teams build on, so that every close, every forecast, and every decision leadership makes is grounded in numbers people can trust. Your work will shape how the whole studio understands its own performance, and how confidently we act on what we learn. If you want real ownership over how that story gets told, and want to build the systems and judgment other people lean on to tell it well, this role was built for you.

YOU’LL CONTRIBUTE BY:

Setting the company s revenue recognition policy and methodology under ASC 606 (including box sales, microtransactions, and virtual currency), and partnering with the studio to keep the data behind it accurate and timely

Leading the close process end-to-end, directing an external accounting team and AP/AR resources, while building the judgment and quality bar they operate against

Owning platform and publisher reporting, including reconciling statements from Sony, Microsoft, Valve, and PC cafés, and using that visibility for crucial internal reporting, resolving systemic discrepancies, and improving royalty/licensing reporting to our partners

Leading cross-functional alignment with business, publishing, analytics, and live ops so that we have a cohesive structure for financial data and analytics across the studio

Owning treasury strategy and execution, including banking controls, liquidity management, excess cash investment, and corporate insurance strategy, with authority to set policy, not just execute it

Leading ERP transformation, owning the evaluation, business case, and roadmap for our next accounting systems, and building the automation strategy that will scale with the business for years, not just the next close

Leading cross-functional alignment with business, publishing, analytics, live ops, and FP A, connecting forecasting, KPI reporting, and revenue insight directly to studio-wide product and live-ops strategy, and shaping how those teams think about revenue before decisions get made

Serving as a crucial partner in Finance Leadership, representing the finance function in high-stakes, high-ambiguity conversations such as external audits, tax structuring, and financial due diligence

Establishing the financial rigor Bonfire operates by today, with room to mentor and develop the team as it scales

WE’RE EXCITED ABOUT YOU BECAUSE YOU:

Have 10+ years of accounting experience, including 7+ years in video games, with a track record of building or overhauling revenue recognition frameworks, not just applying existing ones

Know ASC 606 well enough to originate policy positions on novel monetization models before precedent exists

Have led relationships with major publishers and platform holders, not just processed their reporting

Are deeply fluent in deferred revenue modeling, virtual currency accounting, and international royalty structures, and have shaped how a studio approaches them

Have led an ERP evaluation or migration and can make the business case for systems investment

CPA required.

Are comfortable operating with significant autonomy in ambiguous, high-stakes, fast-changing environments

Bonus points if you ve supported multi-platform live-service titles at scale and have experience building or leading a team

THIS MIGHT NOT BE THE ROLE FOR YOU IF:

You haven t worked with premium games, DLC, virtual currency, subscriptions, or live-service monetization; this role assumes that context

You want a clearly scoped, well-defined function with an established roadmap to execute against; this role is about building the definition and the roadmap, not inheriting either

You want to lead from a distance and don t want to stay close to the details; we re looking for someone who still finds satisfaction in getting reconciliations and journal entries right, even while leading the function

You re looking for a purely functional accounting role; this spans treasury, FP A strategy, ERP transformation, and org-wide financial rigor
