Intermediate Animator

Got a strong eye for anime style? Join us as an Intermediate Animator, creating game-ready animation in Unreal Engine and bringing characters to life on console.

blocks--cover--scroll#handleScrollDown" title="Scroll to content">

Join our team at Shapefarm, where art, technology, and storytelling collide. We’re building games that connect players around the world, and we’re looking for talented creators who want to shape unforgettable experiences.

We’re looking for an experienced and self-motivated intermediate animator to join our team and help bring our characters and worlds to life on console.

We’re particularly interested in a passionate animator with a strong eye for anime style and experience creating game animation in Unreal Engine.

You’ll get to work on exciting IP by creating high-quality animation that reinforces the anime aesthetic of our titles, owning key features, improving how we work, and helping raise the quality of our animation.

You’ll work closely with designers, artists, tech artists and engineers to get animation working in-game and bring amazing visions to life. Strong communication and collaboration skills are key to success at Shapefarm.

📍 Location: Wellington, New Zealand OR Tokyo, Japan 🏢 Working arrangement: Office-based - no permanent remote work or relocation support available.

🚀What you’ll do: Create high-quality keyframe animation for characters, creatures and environments, with a focus on responsiveness, weight and player feedback Animate core gameplay systems such as locomotion, combat and interactions so they look great and feel tight in the player’s hands Work with gameplay designers to prototype and polish animation-driven mechanics, from early block out through to final quality Create in-game narrative and cinematic animation that conveys character, emotion and intent Make sure all animation matches the game’s art style and tone across characters, creatures and environments Implement animation in Unreal Engine using Animation Blueprints, state machines and blend spaces, making sure transitions are clean and timing holds up with player input, physics and procedural systems Work with tech artists, riggers and engineers to test and improve rigs, skinning and export pipelines Optimise animation to meet performance and memory budgets on console 🛠️What we’re looking for: Essential 3+ years of professional animation experience in games, ideally with at least one shipped title A demo reel showing game-ready animation, especially gameplay such as locomotion, combat or interactions A strong grasp of body mechanics, weight, timing, spacing and pose readability Strong animation skills in Maya.

Experience implementing animation in Unreal Engine, including Animation Blueprints, state machines and blend spaces An understanding of how animation affects game feel, responsiveness and player feedback Experience working closely with designers, tech artists and engineers to get animation working in-game The ability to take on feedback and use it to improve your work, and to work well on your own and as part of a team Desirable Experience developing for console, including optimising. animation for performance and memory budgets.

Experience with stylised animation, particularly anime aesthetic and animation principles.

Experience with combat, facial animation or cinematics.

Experience giving feedback and receiving feedback in the form of animation reviews.

Experience in an iterative or Agile development team.

Comfort working in an English and Japanese bilingual environment.

Animation skills with Control Rig and Level Sequence in Unreal Engine.

🤝At Shapefarm, we value people who Take initiative and see problems through to solutions.

Are comfortable owning their work and operating with a good degree of independence.

Know when to ask questions, share concerns or bring others into a decision.

Welcome feedback, share knowledge and are always looking for ways to improve.

Care about doing things well without losing sight of the bigger picture.

Treat people with respect, value different perspectives and contribute to an inclusive team.

Take responsibility for your decisions and learn from challenges along the way.

🌏 Two locations. One opportunity.

We’re welcoming applications from candidates who can work from either of our office locations: Wellington, New Zealand Tokyo, Japan You can apply for the location that works for you, provided you’re able to work from the relevant office.

Please note : This is an in-office role. Permanent remote work is not available, and we’re unable to provide relocation assistance. Applicants preferrable already live in Wellington or Tokyo and be able to work from that office. You’ll also need to have the existing legal right to work in your chosen location.
