English:

Location: This role is based in Madrid and is open to internal applicants who are based in Madrid, or within a reasonable commuting distance and can attend the Madrid office twice per week.

Role Overview:

We are looking for an experienced Finance Business Partner to join the Iberia Finance team.

The role acts as the financial partner for the Commercial and Marketing organisations, providing financial insight, challenging business decisions and supporting profitable growth through planning, analysis and performance management.

The successful candidate will work closely with local leadership, Sales, Marketing and Head Office to drive business performance while ensuring robust financial governance.

What you ll be doing

Business Partnering

Act as the trusted finance partner for Commercial and Marketing teams.

Support strategic and operational decision-making through financial analysis.

Challenge assumptions and identify risks and opportunities.

Influence business decisions to maximise profitability.

Financial Planning Performance Management

Lead Budget, Forecast and Flash planning activities with budget owners.

Perform detailed variance analysis and communicate business drivers.

Monitor financial performance against targets.

Develop recommendations to improve commercial results.

Commercial Finance

Evaluate ROI of commercial initiatives and marketing investments.

Provide financial support for commercial negotiations and business cases.

Marketing Trade Investment

Partner with Marketing to monitor Trade Marketing and MDF investments.

Analyse promotional effectiveness and spending.

Ensure investment decisions are aligned with financial objectives.

Financial Reporting Insights

Prepare monthly management reporting.

Produce dashboards and KPIs for senior management.

Deliver meaningful insights rather than only financial reporting.

Ensure high quality and integrity of financial information.

Systems Data

Advanced SAP User.

Support Anaplan (ERP) planning processes.

Ensure master data accuracy.

Drive continuous process improvements and automation opportunities.

Stakeholder Management

Partner with Sales, Marketing and Supply Chain.

Collaborate with European and Global Finance teams.

Interface with Head Office on pricing and commercial finance topics.

Support internal and external audit activities.
