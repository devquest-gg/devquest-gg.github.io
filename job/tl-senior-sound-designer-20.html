Techland Games Explore Explore Explore Explore Explore All Games News About Us Careers Dying Light Outpost Support Menu Unia Europejska See all open positions Senior Sound Designer Work Model: onsite / hybrid Warszawa, Poland; Wrocław, Poland Share offer on Company Description Techland is one of the biggest video game companies in Poland, with over 30 years of experience in the gaming industry. From our studios in Wrocław and Warsaw, we’ve built an international team of more than 500 talented professionals, all dedicated to pushing the boundaries of game development.

We’re known for creating iconic franchises like Call of Juarez and the zombie genre-defining Dying Light, which has been played by over 45 million players worldwide. With a focus on open-world action, storytelling, and community engagement, we’re committed to delivering unforgettable experiences to our players.

We’re constantly striving to improve, innovate, and take on new challenges. With ambitious plans for the future, we’re looking for passionate people to be part of this exciting journey.

Job Description Create unforgettable audio experiences for players Record and design high-quality audio assets for the game's core systems Implement and pre-mix audio elements Develop audio systems that effectively combine artistic creativity with technical accuracy Ensure audio consistency with the game's overall direction and vision Craft audio designs for linear media, including marketing videos Collaborate effectively with cross-functional development teams Gather and provide constructive feedback across the team Qualifications A passion for creating unique sound design in video games 5+ years of experience in developing core audio elements for AAA games, with a proven track record Participation in the full development lifecycle for at least one published title Proficiency with industry-standard audio software such as Reaper, WaveLab, Izotope RX, Sound Forge and various audio plugins Expertise in using Audiokinetic Wwise for audio implementation Technical skills that allow for innovative sound design and problem-solving Excellent communications skills A degree in Audio or Sound Design is advantageous but not mandatory Why Join Us? Work with a talented and passionate team dedicated to pushing the boundaries of audio in gaming Opportunity to lead and innovate in sound design, influencing the gaming experiences of players worldwide Supportive and dynamic environment that values creativity and collaboration Additional Information What we can offer: A wide array of benefits: private medical care, life insurance,&#xa0;pro-health campaigns,&#xa0;gifts for different occasions.

An outstanding work atmosphere in a highly-skilled team of professionals, with flexible working hours, no dress code, and full support of the dedicated HR Business Partner.

A constant stream of company newsletters, PR & project updates so you will always be in the know.

Many opportunities for personal development: a dedicated development budget for each employee, extra two paid days for training and CSR, stable career paths, extensive internal and external training, and financing of English and Polish language classes.

State-of-the-art offices filled with chillout zones, a fully equipped kitchen, a gym (Wrocław office), and a free car park (Warsaw limited amount of space).
