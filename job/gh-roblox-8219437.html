Senior Product Manager, Developer Monetization and Wallets

Roblox · San Mateo

Senior Engineering $281K–$331K Posted 2026-09-22 Full time

Every day, tens of millions of people come to Roblox to explore, create, play, learn, and connect with friends in 3D immersive digital experiences– all created by our global community of developers and creators.

At Roblox, we’re building the tools and platform that empower our community to bring any experience that they can imagine to life. Our vision is to reimagine the way people come together, from anywhere in the world, and on any device.

We’re on a mission to connect a billion people with optimism and civility, and looking for amazing talent to help us get there.

A career at Roblox means you’ll be working to shape the future of human interaction, solving unique technical challenges at scale, and helping to create safer, more civil shared experiences for everyone.

As the Senior Product Manager for this group, you are the product owner for developer earnings supporting all creators in managing earnings from their game and re-investing those earnings in their growth.

You drive innovation in ways in which creators can maximize their earnings by making it easy and safe to manage their earnings and spend on the platform. You lead the strategy and execution for helping our run their businesses better while making sure our users have the best experience on the platform.

You drive automation for creators that results in outcomes that are accretive for the developer, the user and the platform.

You help businesses on Roblox manage their payouts to their collaborators and members.

If you are an entrepreneurial product leader, who doesn’t hesitate to propose bold ideas and put them into action, you’ll be a great fit.

This role based in San Mateo, CA reports to the Director, Virtual Economy Monetization and Platforms.

You Will:

You will drive the product strategy and vision for an innovative developer monetization platform on Roblox, leading the industry in defining ways in which users and developers can find value on this platform.

Have full responsibility for developing features and capabilities to facilitate in-app monetization through developer products and optimize pricing, while minimizing Roblox’s exposure to fraud and abuse

Partner with other product, design, engineering and business stakeholders at Roblox, and drive strategic alignment. Enroll other teams into your product vision and align roadmaps to ensure cohesive product functionality and value proposition.

Work closely with cross-functional partners to define scope, review technical capabilities, prioritize projects for release, and define new opportunities

Lead and facilitate brainstorming, then take these early ideas and iterate, experiment, and build

Defend your POV against skeptics, while being open to feedback and adapting your ideas to deliver the best output

Use relentless customer understanding and empathy to discover and define the right problems and products we should be pursuing

Balance and prioritize new features with existing backlog initiatives and align them with the product vision.

You Have:

5+ years of product management experience, with experience in the in-app monetization, game analytics and group management.

Entrepreneurial drive: Worked in a fast paced environment, with competing resources and priorities, through the product life cycle from planning, development, through to launch. Strong opinions and a willingness to make calls with limited information. Comfort advocating for resources and operating in a dynamic, fluid organization. A high degree of self-direction and a get stuff done attitude.

Technical mindset: Comfort delving into the product, platform and implementation details. Ability to roll up your sleeves and engage with engineering to design scalable and extensible solutions

Data-driven approach: Proficiency identifying, defining, tracking and acting upon metrics. Familiarity with experimentation and A/B testing methods. Experience identifying KPIs and working with engineering and analytics on instrumentation and monitoring.

Strong written and verbal communication skills: Ability to convey strategies, priorities, and tradeoffs at the 30K foot level, as well as produce clear and detailed product and technical specifications
