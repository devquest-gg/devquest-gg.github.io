ABOUT THIS POSITION

Digital Extremes is seeking an energetic, highly organized Recruitment and Talent Acquisition Specialist. In this dual-impact role, you will own the end-to-end lifecycle of our Co-Op Internship Program—serving as the primary connector and community builder for student cohorts to ensure they are fully integrated into studio culture. Simultaneously, you will assist the Recruitment Manager with core recruitment operations.

This role is ideal for a self-starter who thrives in an evolving environment. You will have the autonomy to drive programs forward while collaborating closely with a supportive HR team. You must be comfortable operating in a fast-paced game studio or high-tech environment where priorities evolve rapidly—demanding the ability to pivot on a dime while maintaining peak execution, taking constructive feedback in stride, and delivering immediate and measurable impact!.

The Recruitment and Talent Acquisition Specialist is a role that will be onsite in our London, Ontario Studio. RESPONSIBILITIES

Assist with active studio requisitions by screening applications, proactively sourcing top talent, and conducting thorough initial screen calls across discipline pipelines.

Maintain high data standards within our Applicant Tracking System (ATS), manage active candidate stages, and deliver clear metrics on pipeline health.

Drive interview scheduling, candidate communications, and hiring team debriefs to deliver an efficient, high-touch candidate experience.

Support studio recruitment branding initiatives, campus outreach, and LinkedIn campaigns highlighting life at Digital Extremes

Oversee onboarding, orientation, mid/end-of-term check-ins, and exit interviews to deliver a best-in-class student experience.

Facilitate co-op meetups, peer support networks, and social connections so students build strong relationships and feel integrated from day one.

Act as the central contact for students, team leads, and hiring managers—facilitating continuous feedback loops and resolving operational friction quickly.

Manage relationships with university and college co-op offices, represent the studio at career fairs, and host studio presentations.

Track student growth to build a reliable talent pipeline for future opportunities

Support the Recruitment Manager and the Director, HR with any HR projects or administrative tasks as needed

REQUIREMENTS

3+ years of total HR/Recruitment experience, with exposure to campus recruitment, tech/gaming, and generalist HR operations.

Deep expertise with ATS platforms (e.g., Greenhouse/Dayforce), LinkedIn Recruiter, and workspace tools (Google Workspace, Slack, MS Office).

Proven ability to operate with high autonomy, adapt to shifting studio priorities on a dime, and execute with precision under minimal supervision.

Demonstrated expertise in generating, interpreting, and presenting recruitment analytics and reporting using Dayforce and Greenhouse or similar platforms.

Energetic interpersonal skills paired with strong group facilitation and presentation skills.

ABOUT DIGITAL EXTREMES

Founded in 1993 by James Schmalz, Digital Extremes ranks as one of the world s top independent video game development studios. Originating with the co-creation of Epic Games multi-million unit selling Unreal® franchise including Unreal and Unreal Tournament, Digital Extremes went on to develop Dark Sector®, BioShock® for the PlayStation®3, the BioShock 2 multiplayer campaign, and The Darkness® II. The studio has reached its greatest critical and commercial success with the free-to-play action game, Warframe® , boasting a global community of 75 million registered players on PC, PS4™, PS5™, Xbox One™, Xbox Series X/S™, Nintendo Switch™, and iOS™. The studio s most recently announced project is the much anticipated Soulframe® , an open-world action-fantasy MMORPG title that is currently under development.

Digital Extremes is headquartered in London, Ontario, Canada, and is proud to be selected as one of Canada s Top 100 Employers.

For more information and exciting news related to Digital Extremes, please visit www.digitalextremes.com .
