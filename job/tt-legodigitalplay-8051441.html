LEGO Digital Play will bring the LEGO brand into digital entertainment in new, innovative, and wholly-owned ways. Our mission is to ensure the LEGO Brand remains as powerfully a part of children’s lives in the coming decades as it has ever been. We aim to reach every kid on the planet, their parents, and adult fans of LEGO—and provide them with meaningful, magical, and playful new experiences.

Central Technology is the engineering heart of LEGO Digital Play, a venture within the LEGO ecosystem dedicated to pioneering creative and joyful digital experiences. Our teams build the foundational platforms, tools, and capabilities that power LEGO's digital future. We work at the intersection of play and developer tooling to bring the LEGO brand to a new generation of digital builders. It's a rare opportunity to shape something from the ground up within one of the world's most loved brands.

We are at the earliest phases of this new company, offering a unique opportunity to build a new entity for the world's most beloved and trusted brand. Our culture is open, collaborative, intellectually rigorous, and creatively vibrant.

Role Summary We are looking for a Senior Systems Software Engineer who enjoys working across the whole performance stack, from how data sits in memory to how services behave across a cluster. You will design and build high-performance services and tools in C++ or Rust that process large volumes of data at low latency and high throughput in a distributed, cloud-hosted system.

This is a broad role for a T-shaped engineer: deep in performance programming and concurrency, comfortable with distributed systems and cloud deployment, and happy to build the tooling other engineers rely on.

Key Responsibilities Design and implement high-performance services and tools in C++ or Rust, owning correctness, throughput, latency and memory behaviour from design through production.

Build concurrent and parallel components that make full use of modern hardware: multithreading, async I/O, SIMD and cache-aware data layouts.

Design components that run reliably as part of a distributed system, with sound approaches to partitioning, back-pressure, fault tolerance and observability.

Profile, benchmark and optimise hot paths; set performance budgets and write the specifications other engineers build to.

Build the internal tools, test harnesses and benchmarking infrastructure that help teams develop, measure and debug performance-critical code.

Work with infrastructure engineers on how services deploy and scale in the cloud: containerisation, resource limits, scaling behaviour and network I/O.

Apply secure engineering practice to systems-level code, and partner with security and privacy colleagues so data handling meets regulatory requirements.

Write clear interface specifications and documentation for engineers who use or extend your work.

Required Qualifications Substantial hands-on experience writing production C++ or Rust where performance matters. You reason fluently about memory layout, throughput, latency and concurrency.

A strong grasp of concurrency and parallelism, such as threading models, synchronisation, lock-free techniques, async I/O or SIMD. We value breadth over having done all of them.

Experience building and optimising data-intensive systems where profiling, benchmarking and performance debugging are routine.

A working understanding of distributed systems: networking, failure modes, consistency, messaging or streaming.

Practical experience deploying and running services in a public cloud, including containers.

A track record of building tools or infrastructure that other engineers use every day.

Security-minded systems coding: input validation, bounds checking and memory safety.

Clear technical writing, and sound judgement about when to optimise, when to simplify and when a design is done.

Preferred Qualifications A games industry background as a tools, engine or technology engineer.

Experience integrating ML inference into native services, using ONNX Runtime, TensorRT, LibTorch or similar.

Familiarity with applied cryptography libraries for hashing, signing and verification, and with using them correctly.

Experience with content-processing systems such as media pipelines, content moderation or content authentication.

Go or Python for orchestration, tooling or test harnesses.

Experience with Kubernetes, ECS or equivalent, including resource tuning at scale.
