Position Title: Senior Narrative Writer (Temp)

Studio/Department: Singularity 6

Employee Type: 6 week assignment

Location: Remote ( within BC, Canada)

Salary Range: $95,000 - $115,000 CAD

Ravenwake Games is the newest studio within Enad Global 7, a global leader in video game development and publishing. Backed by iconic franchises such as EverQuest®, PlanetSide®, DC Universe™ Online, Dungeons Dragons Online®, The Lord of the Rings Online™, Magic: The Gathering Online, and MechWarrior®, we draw on a legacy of genre‑defining experiences. We’re committed to delivering entertainment that inspires, engages, and unites players around the world. At Ravenwake Games, our mission is to craft immersive worlds that bring players together, build vibrant communities, and deliver unforgettable gameplay at every turn.

Description

We’re seeking a contract Narrative Writer for a 12-week engagement to help define the story of a new, unannounced cozy life sim built on an iconic, globally loved brand. You’ll write for a young adult audience (core 19–35) in a grounded, cozy town, where the tone is light, humorous, and ironic with a few intentional, heartfelt beats. This is a writing role, not an implementation role: you’ll own the story outline, cast, opening experience, and early dialogue and quest scripts, and our game designers will bring them into the engine.

What You’ll Do

Shape the Story Outline: Create a story outline deck plus an extended doc version. Land a strong opening hook, a clear antagonist, and 1–2 core villains with complex, reasonable motivations.

Build the Cast: Create a first-pass list of town NPCs with metadata and a town registrar.

Write Character Briefs: Develop 5 detailed character briefs for key townsfolk.

Draft the Opening Experience: Write a detailed first draft of the first-time user experience story.

Write Dialogue and Quest Scripts: Write a handful of mid-game dialogue scripts and 1–2 simple quest scripts for our vertical slice area, ready for a game designer to implement.

Hold the Tone: Keep writing light, witty, and hopeful

Collaborate and Iterate: Partner with production and design leads, take feedback quickly, and revise to meet brand partner guidelines.

Who You Are

A Game Writer: You have professional writing credits on at least one game.

You’re a writer first; engine implementation is not required.

Life Sim Literate: You understand cozy and life sim games such as Stardew

Valley, Palia, Animal Crossing, and Disney Dreamlight Valley, and how story drives their progression.

Brand Enthusiast: You’re genuinely excited to write for an iconic, aspirational brand.

Funny with Heart: You write sitcom-style banter, irony, and situational humor, and can land a serious beat without tipping into darkness.

Concise: You write tight, readable dialogue within strict length limits.

Brand-Safe: You use sound judgment on tone and themes when writing within licensed IP guardrails.

Self-Starter: You thrive in early, ambiguous development and deliver organized, high-quality docs on a fast 12-week timeline.

Strong Communicator: You articulate story decisions clearly and give and receive feedback well.

Nice to Have

A deep understanding of games, including how narrative ties into quests, careers, and relationship systems.

Experience writing for licensed IP and working through licensor reviews.

Experience writing for short in-engine cinematics and voice-over.

Experience writing for a young adult, primarily female audience.
