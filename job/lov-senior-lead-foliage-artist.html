Senior Foliage Artist

Black Banner Studios · České Budějovice · Remote

Senior Art Remote Posted 2026-11-20 Full time
Apply on Black Banner Studios’s site →

Legacy of Valor is an ambitious medieval RPG and strategy game combining massive real-time battles, third-person melee combat, and tactical army control in Unreal Engine 5. At Black Banner Studios, we're looking for an exceptional Senior Foliage Artist who combines outstanding artistic judgment with deep technical expertise in vegetation creation, Unreal Engine 5 PCG, and real-time performance optimization. Our goal is to create beautiful, natural, and believable landscapes inspired by 14th-century Europe, from dense forests and open countryside to fields, villages, and vast battlefields. We're looking for someone who can create and place stunning vegetation using both traditional alpha-card and modern Nanite-compatible approaches, understanding their advantages, limitations, and performance implications. You'll work closely with our environment artists, technical artists, and programmers to develop efficient foliage pipelines and create visually rich, varied environments that remain performant at a large scale. We're open to candidates at both Senior and Lead levels, with the final title and responsibilities determined by experience and the scope of technical or artistic leadership. If you believe you'd be a great fit for this role, even if you don't meet every requirement listed below, we'd still love to hear from you. Please don't hesitate to apply! Create beautiful, realistic, game-ready vegetation assets, including trees, bushes, grasses, plants, and other natural elements. Develop and maintain foliage assets using both traditional alpha-card techniques and fully Nanite-compatible geometry. Build sophisticated, well-organized, reusable, and performant foliage distribution systems using Unreal Engine 5 PCG. Create natural, visually compelling vegetation compositions with believable species distribution, density, variation, and environmental transitions. Profile, identify, and resolve foliage performance bottlenecks involving geometry, overdraw, materials, shadows, wind animation, memory usage, and rendering costs. Establish efficient workflows for foliage creation, placement, LODs, materials, and optimization across large environments. Collaborate closely with environment and technical artists to maintain consistent artistic quality and performance. Mentor junior artists, provide constructive feedback, and help establish foliage production standards. Extensive professional experience creating foliage and vegetation for large-scale or technically complex game projects, with substantial hands-on responsibility for creating and delivering complete, production-ready environments. Advanced expertise in traditional alpha-card foliage creation, including geometry, textures, materials, LODs, and optimization. Advanced expertise in Nanite-compatible foliage creation, including appropriate geometry, materials, wind animation, and performance considerations. Deep hands-on experience with Unreal Engine 5 PCG, creating complex, organized, artist-friendly, and performant vegetation distribution systems. Excellent artistic judgment, with a strong understanding of natural composition, vegetation variety, scale, color, and believable environmental design. Strong understanding of real-time foliage rendering, including overdraw, masked materials, shader complexity, instancing, shadows, and GPU/CPU performance. Demonstrated experience profiling and optimizing dense vegetation using Unreal Engine performance analysis and debugging tools. Strong proficiency with professional 3D modeling, texturing, and foliage creation tools. Ability to work independently, solve complex artistic and technical challenges, and collaborate effectively within a multidisciplinary team. Passion for video games, particularly medieval RPGs, open-world games, and strategy titles. Experience working on AAA and / or large open-world game projects. Strong additional environment art skills, including terrain creation, landscape sculpting, 3D modeling, and environmental composition. Experience creating high-quality PBR textures and materials using Substance Designer, Substance Painter, or equivalent tools. Experience with Houdini, procedural modeling, or advanced environment generation workflows. Experience with Unreal Engine material systems, procedural wind animation, and advanced vegetation shaders. Experience creating atmospheric effects, environmental VFX, or other natural environment details. Experience establishing foliage pipelines and production standards across an entire game project. Location: Remote / International, with the option to work in the Czech Republic. Engagement: Full-time, long-term collaboration. Contract: Contractor / B2B worldwide, or direct employment for candidates residing in or willing to relocate to the Czech Republic. Start Date: As soon as mutually agreed. Language: Professional working proficiency in English. Compensation: Competitive, based on experience and expertise.
