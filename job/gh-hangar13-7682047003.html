Who We Are

Hangar 13 is an internal 2K development studio that creates influential AAA video game experiences across all major platforms. The studio has three locations around the world: Brighton (UK), Brno Prague (Czech Republic), working collaboratively as one team. Each of our locations provides the opportunity to work with some of the most talented developers in the industry, on creatively challenging and exciting projects, in a diverse and welcoming environment.

Our focus is on making the highest quality games with sustainable, healthy development plans; to tell stories that engage players through worlds and experiences that keep them hooked from start to finish.

We strive to be a studio where our team members know they can make a difference and grow professionally alongside creating games they are proud of.

What We Need

Hangar 13 is in search of an experienced and highly skilled Gameplay Designer who has a passion for creating compelling gameplay features and mechanics. You will take ownership of large portions of the player character, driving core combat and moment-to-moment gameplay from initial ideation to final implementation. Working closely in cross-discipline pods, you will ensure these features are fun, balanced, and seamlessly integrated into the overall player experience.

What You’ll Be Doing:

Owning player character systems, mechanics and features. You are expected to continuously shepherd, develop, progress and iterate the gameplay topics that you are responsible for.

Pitching, prototyping, implementing and polishing moment-to-moment and minute-to-minute gameplay mechanics

Continuously collaborating with stakeholders to ensure feature direction and development is aligned and on-track, and dependencies consistently accounted for.

Proactively identifying and mitigating potential issues or risks with your features before they impact the team.

Continually ensuring the effective utilization of your features in the game through proactive evangelism and skillful communication with studio team members

Thoughtfully analysing, validating and incorporating feedback from the team and external user testing

Authoring and maintaining well-written, quality design documentation

What We’re Looking For:

Passion for owning and iterating on mechanics that meet the project vision and deliver the highest quality gameplay experience.

Possesses a first-principles approach to design, with the ability to break down complex problems and prioritize requirements to achieve the desired gameplay feel within the project’s vision and scope.

Self-sufficient and a proactive problem-solver who persistently and consistently resolves blockers and finds solutions independently - but not at the expense of collaboration

Highly capable of communicating with and being mindful of the needs of and effects to other stakeholders (e.g. programming, animation, tech anim, AI) across multiple locations

Strong understanding of balancing and tuning systems to achieve the target feel of a feature

High level of technical expertise with strong provable scripting ability

Shipped one or more AAA game titles in a Gameplay / 3Cs Design role or similar

You Might Also Have: (desirable skills - 5 bullet points max.)

Experience working with Unreal Engine

Previous programming experience or familiarity with text-based scripting

Strong documentation skills, proficiency with Wiki systems or Confluence is a plus
