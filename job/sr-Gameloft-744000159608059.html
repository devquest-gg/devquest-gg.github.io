Unity Game Developer

Gameloft · Lviv

Senior Engineering Posted 2026-11-04 Full time
Apply on Gameloft’s site →

We are looking for a talented Unity Game Developer to join our team working on Disney Dreamlight Valley, a magical life-simulation adventure game featuring beloved Disney and Pixar characters.

As part of the development team, you will create and implement gameplay features, improve existing systems, and help bring new ideas to life using Unity and C#.

You will be involved in the full game development cycle, working closely with designers, artists, and other developers to deliver engaging experiences for players around the world.

What it means on a daily basis Develop and implement gameplay features using Unity and C#.

Turn game design specifications into functional game mechanics.

Integrate game elements, including UI, graphics, animations, and audio.

Write clean, efficient, and reusable code.

Identify and fix technical issues, improve performance, and ensure game stability.

Contribute to technical discussions and game development improvements.

What success will look like You will deliver high-quality gameplay features that work smoothly and meet the project's technical and design requirements.

You will become a reliable member of the team, contributing ideas, solving technical challenges, and helping improve the overall player experience.

Who you would be working with You will work alongside a multidisciplinary team of Unity Developers, Game Designers, Level Designers, 2D/3D Artists, Animators, and Backend Developers.

Together, you will help bring the magical world of Disney Dreamlight Valley to life.

Your recruitment journey First interview: A conversation with our Recruiter to discuss your experience, expectations, and the role.

Test: A technical assignment to demonstrate your programming skills.

Second interview: A technical discussion with the development team.

Final interview: If needed, a final conversation with the team.

Where this role can take you You will have the opportunity to strengthen your Unity development skills, work on a globally recognized game, and take on new technical challenges.

As you grow, you may explore opportunities to become a Senior Developer, specialize in specific technical areas, or move toward technical leadership roles.

WHAT YOU NEED TO SUCCEED At least 3 years of experience with C# and Unity.

Good understanding of software architecture and object-oriented programming (OOP).

Experience with multithreading.

Ability to write clean, efficient, and maintainable code.

Good English reading and writing skills.

Nice to have: Experience developing games for mobile devices and/or consoles.

Previous experience in game development or other 3D applications, including personal projects.

Knowledge of networking and online communication technologies.

Experience with C/C++, Objective-C, Swift, Lua, or Python.

Knowledge of graphics engines, AI, Qt, or POSIX systems.

WHO YOU ARE A team player who works well with others.

A problem solver with a practical approach.

Responsible and committed to deadlines.

Eager to learn and develop new skills.

Passionate about games and creating great player experiences.

WHAT'S IN IT FOR YOU Loots & Perks Possibility to work remotely in a global product company; Competitive salary based on your experience and qualifications; 21 days of paid vacation; 5 days of paid sick leave; National holidays as non-working days; Medical insurance; Access to psychologist support; Parental leave; Access to our educational platform with courses, training programs, and certifications; Employee Referral Program; Technical support and equipment.

Qualified candidates will be invited to complete an online technical assessment as part of the selection process.

Ready to bring your Unity expertise to the Valley? Apply and join our team!
