Senior Level Artist (Splinter Cell Remake)

Ubisoft Toronto · Toronto

Senior Art Posted 2026-11-24 Full time
Apply on Ubisoft Toronto’s site →

As a Senior Level Artist at Ubisoft Toronto, you will shape environments for Splinter Cell from early visual direction through production and polish. You will bring together art, gameplay, storytelling and technology to create cohesive, believable levels in collaboration with teams across the project.

What you’ll do Shape the artistic direction and visual quality of assigned levels and environments Establish each location’s theme, mood, composition and environmental storytelling using research and visual references Partner with Level Design to support gameplay flow, navigation, player guidance and visual clarity Create and refine placeholder geometry, structural models and blockouts to develop visual ideas Build, refine and troubleshoot environments using Snowdrop and project tools Apply lighting principles to support mood, color, composition and gameplay clarity Collaborate with Art Direction, Level Design, Lighting, Modeling, Technical Art, Tech Design and Production Work within technical, performance and production constraints Identify issues early, communicate risks and propose practical creative solutions Adapt levels to changing gameplay and artistic needs, contribute to reviews and share solutions with the team What you bring to the team Experience in level art, environment art or a related triple-A game development discipline Knowledge of Snowdrop or comparable real-time game engines and technical workflows Understanding of composition, environmental storytelling, architecture, scale, lighting and visual hierarchy Ability to turn references and art direction into playable environments 3D skills for creating and modifying placeholder and structural geometry Understanding of optimization, performance budgets and technical constraints Creative problem-solving skills and clear communication across disciplines, including the ability to present and explain artistic choices A self-directed, organized approach to prioritizing work, incorporating feedback and maintaining the quality of an assigned level What to send our way Your CV highlighting relevant skills and experiences A portfolio or project links showing relevant level or environment art This posting is for an existing vacancy and is not intended to build a candidate pool for future opportunities.
