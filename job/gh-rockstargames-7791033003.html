Workplace Manager

Rockstar Games · Carlsbad

Mid Other Onsite $98K–$137K Posted 2026-07-02

At Rockstar Games, we create world-class entertainment experiences.

Become part of a team working on some of the most rewarding, large-scale creative projects to be found in any entertainment medium - all within an inclusive, highly-motivated environment where you can learn and collaborate with some of the most talented people in the industry.

Rockstar is on the lookout for a passionate Workplace Manager. The ideal candidate will help to provide key management of the studios day-to-day operation and facilities services.

This is a full-time, in-office position based out of Rockstar’s new state-of-the-art game development studio in Carlsbad, CA.

WHAT WE DO

The Rockstar Workplace Services Team is responsible for ensuring the workplace, building, facilities, and services work harmoniously together to support the business.

We manage and develop both hard and soft facilities services, including building maintenance, security, cleaning, catering, and space utilization and strategy.

We ensure the site complies with all relevant compliance and legislation.

We partner with senior leadership to drive proactive real estate growth strategies, leasehold improvement, and capital expenditure projects.

RESPONSIBILITIES

Work with Health Safety vendors to ensure all fire/life safety inspections and local compliance requirements are current. Work with outsourced provider on development of local response procedures.

Oversee and manage the day-to-day Workplace Service operations of the office, including janitorial services and maintaining FF E.

Coordinating services and trades such as repairs and maintenance, plant vendor, furniture delivery, etc.

Sourcing equipment, furniture, and supplies.

Supplier management – quality of services, contract management, POs and payments.

Maintain relationship with Landlord/Property Management teams.

Maintain internal ticketing system and ensure SLA’s are met.

Space management – keeping floorplans up to date.

Oversee the office snack and beverage program.

Support Workplace Services Project Management team on all design/build projects for the office.

Special event coordination.

REQUIREMENTS

Knowledge of Microsoft Word, Excel and Outlook.

Experience with work order ticketing, project management and space management software tools. (ServiceNow, Monday.com, iOffice, etc.)

Budgeting and financial acumen for operational and capital expenses.

Be able to lift 40 pounds, if needed.

Light skills around maintenance: ability to patch a hole, spot paint, assemble a shelf, glue a carpet tile.

Professional experience in real estate/workplace services/facilities/property management.

Familiarity with KPIs, OKRs, SLAs and measuring metrics helpful.

Possess a basic understanding of office systems (HVAC, Lighting, Electrical, plumbing) and ability to talk to trades about needs and problem solving.

PLUSES

Please note that these are desirable skills and are not required to apply for the position.

In possession of a car and valid Driver’s License (suggested, not required).

IFMA qualification.

OSHA Safety training or equivalent.

Corporate office construction project experience. Basic knowledge of reading drawings and trade coordination.

Previous experience within the Creative Industries – video games, film, television, music – would be beneficial.

Experience with work order ticketing systems, space management tools and construction project management platforms.

Passion for Rockstar Games.
