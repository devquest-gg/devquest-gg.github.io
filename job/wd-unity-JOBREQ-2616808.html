Senior Manager, Content and Customer Marketing

Unity · New York

Senior Marketing $170K–$233K Posted 2026-11-24 Full time
Apply on Unity’s site →

The opportunity We re hiring a manager to lead a shared-service team that makes content and runs customer programs for every business unit: Games, Ads, Industry, Asset Store, and AI. You ll manage three people: two Senior Content Marketing Managers and an Integrated Customer Marketing Manager. You ll set their priorities, edit what they produce, and report on what the team s work does for the business, not just how much of it ships.

The team supports many businesses at once, each with its own products, customers, and sales process, so the job comes down to choosing well.

The team also includes Voices, a new customer advocacy program that turns our biggest customers into public proof. It hasn t launched yet, and your direct report will build it.

You ll work closely with Product Marketing, Integrated Marketing, Sales, Marketing Operations, Web, Social, Events, and Brand.

What you ll be doing Manage, coach, and develop three direct reports. Set goals, give regular feedback, and help each person grow.

Prioritize the team s work across all business units, say no to low-impact requests, and put in place the intake, briefs, and capacity planning that keep the team efficient.

Be the final editor on everything the team publishes, keeping out errors, off-brand language, and AI-generated slop, and edit in a way that coaches writers.

Oversee a steady pipeline of content that supports go-to-market strategy across the customer journey, including thought leadership, reports, case studies, ebooks, webinars, and newsletters.

Guide the launch and growth of Voices with your Integrated Customer Marketing Manager.

Own the team s SEO and GEO (AI search) approach, including how Unity shows up in AI-powered answers.

Report on what the team s work achieved, such as pipeline influence, engagement quality, AI search visibility, and Voices activity, and change priorities when the data says to.

What we re looking for Experience managing a team, including coaching, setting priorities, and giving feedback, with an instinct for keeping it efficient.

Content marketing experience, with a track record of producing content that supports demand generation and go-to-market goals.

An excellent editor and a good writer, who catches the grammar mistake, the weak claim, and the paragraph that sounds machine-written.

Excellent understanding of SEO and GEO, with experience optimizing content for both and reporting on performance in terms of business results, not just output.

Critical thinking and the discipline to say no, picking the requests with the biggest impact from a long list.

Strong collaboration skills, comfort coordinating with many teams at once, and curiosity about how different businesses, products, and sales processes work.

Comfort with AI and content tools such as Claude and Profound, and a view on where AI helps and where it hurts the writing.

You might also have Experience building a customer advocacy or community program. A willingness to learn and help build Voices is enough.

Experience with Sanity or another CMS.

Familiarity with mobile gaming, ad tech, real-time 3D, or developer tools.
