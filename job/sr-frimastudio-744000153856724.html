Technical UI Designer

Frima Studio · Québec

Entry Design Posted 2026-10-06 Full time
Apply on Frima Studio’s site →

What your weeks will look like Design and integrate complex UI systems directly in Unreal Engine (UMG/Slate); Create and optimize custom shaders and materials for advanced visual effects; Collaborate with designers, artists, and programmers to solve cross-platform integration challenges; Debug, profile, and optimize UI systems to ensure smooth performance across PC, consoles, and other platforms; Develop the UI development pipeline in Unreal Engine in collaboration with the technology leadership team; Participate in project retrospectives to improve future UI processes; Contribute to the evolution of UI tools, templates, and workflows used across the studio.

What we’re looking for Bachelor’s degree in Graphic Design, UX/UI Design, Digital Arts, or another related field, combined with equivalent relevant experience; Approximately five (5) years of experience in UI design for video games; Excellent command of Unreal UMG and Blueprints (Slate is considered an asset); Experience with Unreal animation systems is considered an asset; Advanced skills in shader and material creation; Good understanding of UX and interaction design principles; Strong interest in complex technical challenges and optimization; Strong written and verbal communication skills in both French and English; Portfolio and/or demo required; Availability to travel internationally when required.

&#xa0; What we value on our team Passion for functional design, layout, and delivering a high-quality user experience; Autonomy, resourcefulness, and attention to detail when handling a variety of technical tasks; Ability to work within established standards, technical constraints, and production requirements; Ability to analyze and solve UI navigation, readability, and behavior issues; Ability to adapt designs to different target audiences and platforms; Ability to collaborate effectively within a multidisciplinary team; Strong time management, prioritization skills, and ability to meet deadlines; Constructive critical thinking and the ability to reassess and improve your own work.
