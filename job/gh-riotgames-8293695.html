Senior Software Engineer, Social & Store, Unpublished R&D Product

Riot Games · Sydney

Senior Engineering Posted 2026-10-20 Full time
Apply on Riot Games’s site →

As a Senior Software Engineer on an unpublished R D project, you will be responsible for designing and implementing core social and store systems for one of Riot’s most exciting new games. You will work closely with Product, UX Design, and central teams to design, integrate, and build engaging social and purchasing systems in our game. In this role you will own the full vertical integration of social systems in our game, meeting and exceeding player expectations for our genre while adhering to our social safety standards. Additionally you will own the full integration of our in-game store and purchasing systems, offering players a world-class and secure experience in discovering, purchasing, and enjoying in-game purchases. Both of these objectives require extensive collaboration with central teams and other partners as well as legal and regulatory oversight to ensure all our systems are meeting and exceeding both player and industry expectations.

Riot engineers bring deep knowledge of specific technical areas but also value the opportunity to work in a variety of broader domains. As a Senior Software Engineer, you’ll be expected to fully own the delivery of a complex, full stack feature including UI, implementation code, and integration with central systems. We work with both new and current technology, creating innovative solutions to overcome high-volume obstacles and to deliver player value. You will report to a Senior Engineering Manager supporting the Tech Foundations team within an Unpublished R D Product. Internal Job Title: Software Engineer II

Responsibilities:

Gather a deep understanding of the requirements for Social Store integration

Coordinate close with central platform teams on existing solutions

Collaborate with Product and UX Design on the product specific goals

Provide technical implementation proposals consistent with company and product goals

Implement the full solution including utilizing Unreal C++, Blueprint, and UI

Debug the full stack of the deliverable from UI to Platform in coordination with Services and Central teams

Ensure compliance with Riot safety and regulatory requirements for global social store systems

Coordinate with Product and Insights on Playtest feedback for social and purchasing experiences

Required Qualifications:

5+ years (or equivalent depth) of professional experience in software engineering, including experience delivering live or shipped products at scale

2+ years (or equivalent depth) working on game development tools

Proficiency in C++

B.S. in computer science or equivalent experience

Proven ability to navigate ambiguity and influence cross-functional stakeholders

Proven ability to work with Central Teams and Vendors to achieve product goals

Strong communication skills across technical and non-technical audiences

Desired Qualifications:

Experience in Unreal Engine, Subsystems, View Models, Service Integration

Experience with Social systems and player engagement requirements

Experience with in-game Purchasing flows, entitlement flows, and vendor platform purchasing and entitlement systems such as Sony Playstation, XBox, etc.

Experience success at working on a distributed team

Experience with AI assisted engineering workflows

For this role, you ll find success through craft expertise, a collaborative spirit, and decision-making that prioritizes the delight of players. We will be looking at your past studies, experience, and your personal relationship with games. If you embody player empathy and care about players experiences, this could be your role!
