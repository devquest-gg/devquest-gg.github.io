Ton nouveau rôle… Dans ce rôle, tu supervises et révises le travail de nos partenaires d’art externes , qui créent des accessoires et des environnements réalistes de haute qualité, et tu es une référence reconnue au sein du studio . Tu collabores étroitement avec la direction et le leadership artistique pour que la qualité esthétique reste élevée et cohérente dans l’ensemble du contenu. Tu maîtrises les intégrations aux moteurs de jeu et les outils artistiques standards de l’industrie, et tu coordonnes les livrables des équipes d’artistes externes pour les environnements narratifs et cinématiques dont tu es responsable. Même si le rôle est surtout axé sur la revue du développement externe, tu es aussi un.e excellent.e artiste, prêt.e à passer à la création en direct au besoin .

Tu as une solide expérience de la collaboration avec des partenaires externes , de fortes habiletés de communication et la capacité de donner une rétroaction artistique et technique claire . Tu mentores aussi les artistes juniors, et tu recherches, développes et recommandes de nouvelles technologies et approches d’art et de design qui font progresser la qualité et les pipelines du studio.

Tes responsabilités… Fournir clairement de la rétroaction aux partenaires externes sur les objectifs esthétiques, les exigences techniques et les métriques du « build » du projet.

Collaborer avec les équipes des Cinématiques, des Accessoires (Props), de l’Éclairage et de l’Animation afin de s’assurer que le contenu des environnements répond aux besoins narratifs.

Créer la géométrie de blockout, établir les layouts, réaliser la décoration de décors et ajouter les détails.

Créer du contenu artistique d’environnement de haute qualité, très réaliste et à la fine pointe de l’industrie, incluant la modélisation, la sculpture, le texturage et le développement de matériaux pour les accessoires, l’architecture, la géologie et les éléments organiques.

Rechercher, développer et présenter de nouvelles méthodes, de nouvelles techniques et de nouveaux outils qui font passer la qualité artistique au niveau supérieur.

Participer activement aux revues par les pairs et aux réunions, et mentorer les autres artistes de l’équipe.

Excellente compréhension de la géologie, des formations rocheuses, du terrain et du feuillage.

Expérience de création d’environnements à l’aide de systèmes modulaires.

--------------------   WB Games Montreal, a division of Warner Bros. Interactive Entertainment (WBIE), seeks an Advanced Level Artist .

Your New Role… In this role, you review and oversee the work of external art partners who create high-quality, realistic props and environments, and you are a recognized leading contributor at the studio . You work closely with the Lead Environment Artist and the Art Directors to keep the aesthetic quality high and consistent across all content. You master game engine integrations and industry-standard art tools, and you coordinate the deliverables of external artist teams for the narrative and cinematic environments you are responsible for. While the role focuses mainly on reviewing external development, you are also an excellent artist, ready to move into hands-on content creation as needed .

You have strong experience working with external partners , strong communication skills and the ability to give clear artistic and technical feedback . You also mentor junior artists and research, develop and recommend new art tech and design that push the studio s quality bar and pipelines forward.

Your Role Accountabilities...

Clearly provide feedback to external partners on the project’s aesthetic goals, technical requirements, and build metrics.

Collaborate with Cinematics, Props, Lighting, and Animation teams to ensure environment content supports Narrative needs.

Create blockout geometry, establish layouts, apply set decoration and detailing.

Create high quality, highly realistic, industry leading environment art content that includes modeling, sculpting, texturing, and material development for props, architecture, geology and organic features.

Research, develop, and present new methods, techniques, and tools that push our art quality to the next level.

Actively participate in peer reviews, meetings, mentor other artists on team.

Qualifications & Experience… 8 years of progressively complex related experience in the industry.

At least 3 games, at least one of them published, preferably including at least one Xbox S/X or PS5 title.

Experience with AAA open world game development (a plus).

Experience with industry standard 3D packages (3DS Max and Maya). Maya preferred.

Experience with ZBrush and/or other digital sculpting tools, as well as Photoshop and/or Painter.

High level of comfort with Unreal 5, Sequencer, Blueprint, and Materials.

Strong ability and experience in working with outsourcing partners towards the creation of high-quality world art assets that meet metric and technical requirements.

Excellent high- and low-resolution modeling and sculpting skills.

Excellent understanding of geology, rock formations, terrain, and foliage.

Excellent understanding of a variety of architectural styles with an ability to apply knowledge from historical references.

Experience creating environments using modular systems.

Strong understanding of foundational art fundamentals including color theory, composition, internal/external silhouette, and value structures.

Strong understanding of new gen shader technology with an ability to create a wide range of highly realistic materials.

Must be able to embrace the established art vision of the game and ensure it is delivered in its entirety at the end of the project.

Strong experience and understanding of art pipelines with experience in development for Xbox X/S and/or PS5.

Ability to adapt rapidly to new or changing technology with an interest in finding ways to evolve and improve the environment art creation pipeline.

Must have an active, passionate interest in current video games to help drive the game to the next level.

Solid organization skills with adherence to file structures, naming conventions and other established protocols.

Excellent ability to self-manage and accurately predict and adhere to task length estimates and deadlines.

Must be an excellent collaborator with a willingness to provide/accept direction and the ability to embrace change.

Ability to clearly articulate ideas both written and verbally.

Other Requirements Portfolio/website demonstrating fundamental foundation art skills, design sensibilities, and attention to detail.
