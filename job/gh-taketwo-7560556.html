THE CHALLENGE

As a seasoned ServiceNow IT Operations Implementation Specialist, you will execute our evolving Configuration Management Database (CMDB) strategy, governance, and infrastructure roadmap. You will build a foundational system from the ground up, engaging with CMDB product and data owners to navigate diverse viewpoints, establish clear standards, and foster a culture of data integrity. Your work will empower the organization with accurate, actionable insights into technology assets within a fast-paced, creative environment.

WHAT YOU’LL TAKE ON

Architect Execute: Lead the implementation of CMDB and CSDM strategies, including the definition of CI classes, attributes, dependencies, and service mapping approaches.

Configure Customize: Manage the end-to-end lifecycle of ServiceNow Discovery and Service Mapping, including MID servers, network/security settings, probes, patterns, and schedules.

Integrate Automate: Build and maintain integrations via IntegrationHub, Service Graph Connectors, and scripted REST/SOAP APIs.

AI Innovation: Identify and implement high-impact opportunities for embedding GenAI and Agentic Workflows into IT Operations for alert analysis, remediation, and data anomaly detection.

Governance Health: Oversee CMDB health maintenance, data normalization, and reconciliation processes to ensure a "self-healing" IT environment.

Collaborate: Act as a bridge between technical infrastructure and business stakeholders to translate complex operational challenges into scalable, AI-driven solutions.

WHAT YOU BRING

ServiceNow Expertise: Deep technical proficiency in the ServiceNow platform, including base system data structures and ITOM Visibility solutions.

Technical Skillset: Advanced JavaScript, GlideRecord, GlideAjax, PowerShell, Business Rules, and Flow Designer.

Discovery: Expert-level knowledge of Discovery patterns, credentials, and error handling.

Frameworks: Strong understanding of ITSM, ITAM, VR, and ITIL (specifically CI and asset lifecycle management).

Infrastructure Knowledge: Familiarity with cloud platforms (AWS, Azure, GCP), virtualization (hypervisors, containerization), and Infrastructure as Code (IaC).

Data Mastery: Proven experience in data analysis, reporting, and complex data quality governance.

Soft Skills: Exceptional interpersonal and leadership skills with a track record of driving consensus across diverse teams.

Emerging Tech: Demonstrated experience integrating AI/ML-assisted features and Agentic Workflows to enhance team productivity.
