You will be part of a team of around 12 people and report to NBFWM’s Senior Director, Marketing. Our team stands out for its multidisciplinary expertise, the diversity of its projects, its close collaboration with Wealth Advisor teams, and, above all, the fun atmosphere and strong camaraderie among Studio team members. Together, we’re building a strong in-house marketing team with a mission to evolve National Bank Financial – Wealth Management into an exemplary, world-class brand.

What will you do?

As a Graphic Designer for the National Bank at Turbulent, you will be responsible for graphic production, creating and adapting templates and marketing tools while ensuring brand compliance.

Graphic Production & Visual Communications

Create, adapt, resize and update visual assets for digital and print marketing tools, including presentations, social media graphics and promotional materials.

Manage a high volume of production requests across multiple formats with speed, accuracy, consistency and attention to quality.

Interpret and properly apply brand guidelines.

Support the evolution and enhancement of visual norms and templates.

Conduct photo research, ensuring the quality and relevance of selected images.

Print Production & Technical Expertise

Prepare print-ready files and manage technical specifications for commercial production.

Ensure quality control throughout the production process.

Studio Collaboration

Work closely with Marketing advisors by participating in brainstorming and creative reviews.

Contribute to process improvements and workflow optimization.

Demonstrate excellent team spirit by being solution-oriented and supporting teammates.

Perform quality control on various assets to ensure compliance with the brief.

What do we expect from our Graphic Designer ?

The ideal candidate has strong technical expertise in creating print and digital materials, demonstrates creativity and efficiency, and has great attention to detail.

A test will be sent to selected candidates between the pre-selection and selection interviews to assess proficiency in graphic design software.

Required Skills

Fluent in French and English, as English is essential for effective collaboration with team members located across different provinces.

Highly efficient, with excellent attention to detail and a strong commitment to quality.

Ability to work quickly and accurately across a high volume of production requests.

Strong organisational, prioritisation and file-management skills.

Excellent communication and collaboration skills.

Self-motivated and comfortable working both independently and as part of a team.

Innovative, solution-oriented, and committed to continuous improvement.

Experience

Education in graphic design or a related field.

3+ years of experience in graphic design.

Strong portfolio demonstrating both creative design and technical production work.

Experience with digital production and commercial printing processes.

Technical Skills

Highly proficient in technical specifications for print production.

Strong proficiency in Adobe Creative Suite (Photoshop, Illustrator, InDesign, Acrobat).

Strong understanding of typography, layout design and universal accessibility principles.

Good knowledge of Microsoft Office template design (PowerPoint, Word and Excel).

Basic knowledge of photo editing.

Bonus skills: Motion design and video editing.

Note: You must be in Canada and have a valid work permit to apply.

At Turbulent, work-life balance is a top priority.

Turbulent is committed to employing a diverse workforce. Qualified candidates will be assessed without regard to ethnicity, color, gender, religion, national origin, age, sexual orientation, gender identity, gender expression, or disability.
