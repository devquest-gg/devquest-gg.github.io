Senior Director, Product Management

Scopely · Tokyo

Director+ Production Posted 2026-11-27 Full time
Apply on Scopely’s site →

Scopely Explore is looking for a Senior Director, Product Management to lead Pokémon GO product development in our Tokyo office. Pokémon GO has brought hundreds of millions of people into the real world to discover new places, connect with others, and experience Pokémon together. Japan is one of the game’s most important markets. We believe having a world-class product development organization on the ground in Tokyo is essential to building the future of Pokémon GO. This is a product leadership role with a broad organizational mandate. You will lead a multidisciplinary Tokyo-based Pokémon GO development organization spanning Product Management, Production, Engineering, Game Design, UX Design, and other development disciplines. You will be responsible for building a cohesive local team with the context, authority, and capabilities to make decisions quickly, develop major player-facing features, and support the health of the live game during Japan and APAC hours. At the same time, this is not a Japan-only product role. You will work as part of Pokémon GO’s global leadership team, partnering closely with colleagues around the world to build experiences for Trainers everywhere. A central part of your mandate will be developing ambitious new features grounded in our mission: inspire people to explore the world together.

At Scopely, we care deeply about what we do and want to inspire play every day—whether in our work environments alongside our talented colleagues or through our deep connections with our communities of players. We are a global team of game lovers developing, publishing, and innovating in interactive entertainment, connecting millions of people around the world daily.

What You Will Do

Lead the Tokyo Pokémon GO Development Organization: Build and lead a high-performing, multidisciplinary organization encompassing Product Management, Production, Engineering, Game Design, UX Design, and other product development disciplines. Create clear local accountability, strong people leadership, and an environment where teams can move quickly together.

Own Product Strategy and Outcomes: Define product opportunities, priorities, and roadmaps that advance Pokémon GO’s long-term player and business goals. Balance quantitative analysis, qualitative player insight, creative intuition, technical realities, and the unique possibilities of Pokémon GO.

Build Mission-Defining Features: Lead the creation of significant new experiences rooted in Pokémon GO’s mission to inspire people to explore the world together. Look beyond existing game conventions and identify opportunities that make the real world, social connection, exploration, and shared experiences fundamental parts of play.

Champion Our Players in Japan and APAC: Develop a deep understanding of Japanese Trainers and ensure their needs and perspectives consistently influence product strategy and development. Build strong feedback loops across Product, User Research, Community, Player Experience, Marketing, and Data Science and translate insight into action.

Transform How We Build with AI: Lead adoption of agentic AI across product development, using it to accelerate prototyping, create internal tools, automate repetitive workflows, and improve testing and quality.

Help Lead the Tokyo Office: Serve as a senior leader for the broader Tokyo office, connecting Pokémon GO to its shared culture, priorities, and leadership community. Help build a cohesive “One Tokyo” organization across products and functions.

Create True Local Ownership: Establish the people, processes, decision rights, and technical capabilities that allow the Tokyo organization to independently own meaningful areas of the product. Ensure the team can respond effectively to live-game issues during Japan and APAC hours without unnecessary dependency on teams in other time zones.

Partner Deeply: Build a trusted, highly collaborative relationship with our brand partners in Tokyo using proximity and shared working hours to increase development velocity while maintaining an exceptionally high quality bar.

Connect Tokyo and the Global Pokémon GO Team: Operate as a senior member of Pokémon GO’s global product leadership team. Ensure Tokyo-developed features serve the global player base, share context and learnings across regions, and create effective working relationships between teams in Japan, the United States, and elsewhere.

Drive the Health of the Live Game: Partner with Live Game, Engineering, Production, Data Science, and Player Experience leaders to monitor product and operational health. Help the organization anticipate risks, respond rapidly to incidents, learn from failures, and continually improve the reliability and quality of the player experience.

Own Business and Product Performance: Establish clear goals and success metrics for product performance. Use data to understand player behavior, forecast impact, evaluate launches, and make decisions across engagement, retention, monetization, and player satisfaction.

Develop Exceptional Leaders and Teams: Recruit, coach, and retain outstanding managers and individual contributors. Empower Tokyo-based and US leaders, create clear accountability, and build an organization that combines strong local leadership with continued connection to global functional communities and craft standards.

Foster Innovation: Create the conditions for teams to experiment, challenge assumptions, and pursue ambitious ideas. Use Japan as both a critical player market and a source of product insight while identifying innovations that can make Pokémon GO more compelling around the world.

What You Will Need

12+ years of experience in product management, game development, or related product leadership roles, including 5+ years leading senior teams or organizations.

Significant experience building and operating successful free-to-play, live-service games or similarly complex consumer products at global scale.

Demonstrated success leading multidisciplinary product development teams across functions such as Product Management, Engineering, Production, Game Design, UX/Design, and Data.

Experience managing managers and senior discipline leaders, with a track record of building healthy organizations, developing talent, and creating clear accountability.

Exceptional product judgment and the ability to move fluidly between long-term product vision, player experience, business performance, organizational design, and execution details.

Deep understanding of player engagement, retention, live operations, experimentation, and free-to-play product economics.

Strong analytical capabilities and experience using quantitative and qualitative insight to make product decisions and evaluate outcomes.

Experience operating a live product where reliability, incident response, and rapid cross-functional decision-making are critical to the customer experience.

Proven ability to work effectively with major external partners and navigate complex stakeholder and approval environments.

Excellent communication and influence skills, with the ability to create alignment across cultures, disciplines, organizational boundaries, and levels of seniority.

Business-level fluency in both Japanese and English, with the ability to lead teams and substantive partner discussions in Japan while operating effectively within a global English-speaking leadership organization.

Deep empathy for players and a passion for creating experiences that bring people together.

Ability to work from our Tokyo office at least three days per week, currently Tuesday, Wednesday, and Thursday.

Plus If...

You have experience building or scaling a multidisciplinary game development studio or regional product organization.

You have worked closely with major Japanese entertainment, gaming, or IP partners.

You have experience with location-based games, real-world social products, live events, or augmented reality.

You have deep familiarity with Japanese mobile gaming audiences and the broader Japanese games industry.

You regularly play Pokémon GO and have strong intuition for its players, systems, communities, and possibilities.

About Scopely

Scopely is a leading video game and global interactive entertainment company, home to many of the world’s most beloved and enduring experiences, including two of the most successful mobile games of all-time “MONOPOLY GO!” and “Pokémon GO,” along with “Stumble Guys,” “Star Trek™ Fleet Command,” “MARVEL Strike Force,” “WWE Champions,” the Scrabble® franchise, “Yahtzee® With Buddies,” and many others. Across mobile, web, PC, and console, Scopely creates, develops, publishes, and live-operates one of the most diversified and award-winning portfolios in the games industry — bringing hundreds of millions of players together through a shared love of play.

Founded in 2011, Scopely is powered by its exceptional team — including thousands of world-class gamemakers around the globe, a distinctive tenet-driven culture, and its proprietary technology platform, Playgami. Together, these strengths have fueled Scopely’s position as the #1 mobile games company in the U.S. and #2 globally, generating more than $10 billion in lifetime revenue. Whether building global sensations like “MONOPOLY GO!” from the ground up, or expanding through strategic acquisitions, including the FoxNext, GSN, and Scopely Explore games businesses — Scopely consistently delivers experiences players love today and return to for years to come.

Recognized multiple times as one of the "100 Most Influential Companies in the World" by TIME magazine and one of Fast Company s "World s Most Innovative Companies" and “Best Workplaces for Innovators,” Scopely believes that video games can be a force for good — creating meaningful connections, vibrant communities, and making life better through play.

Scopely has global operations and partners across four continents in more than a dozen countries worldwide. For more information, visit: https://www.scopely.com/ .
