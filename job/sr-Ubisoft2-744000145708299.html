Community Developer - March of Giants

Ubisoft (HQ) · Paris

Senior Marketing Posted 2026-08-26 Full time
Apply on Ubisoft (HQ)’s site →

As a Community Developer at Ubisoft Paris, you will help establish, grow, and support player communities around our games. You will drive the global community strategy for a specific brand, March of Giants, creating and executing plans with an international, distributed team. You will help teams stay aligned through clear planning, strong feedback cycles, and consistent best practices, while building trust with players around the world.

What you’ll do Develop and execute global community strategies that support Production objectives and the wider marketing strategy Grow existing player communities in line with business goals Coordinate community communications and initiatives with global teams to ensure consistent messaging Partner with Community Managers, transversal teams, and external partners to create player-facing content such as blogs, podcasts, interviews, and infographics Represent the brand as a community spokesperson and support community events such as studio visits, developer dialogues, and tournaments Collaborate with the portfolio Social Media Manager to define, track, and report content KPIs and community program impact Support key milestones such as betas and launches by helping teams meet KPIs and advocating for player experience needs Represent player needs within Production and Publishing teams to inform development and communications decisions Shape player feedback cycles by gathering, tracking, and reporting insights on community feedback, activity, and expectations to influence game improvements, player retention, and communication planning Coordinate with live teams on post-launch communications, including expectations for patch content, status, and delivery What you bring to the team Experience managing online communities and building community plans or programs with measurable results Demonstrated ability to communicate clearly with players and internal stakeholders Knowledge of, or strong interest in, March of Giants Hands-on knowledge of social platforms such as Instagram, X, Facebook, TikTok, and Discord Experience working in a Production environment with multiple deadlines, success metrics, and stakeholders Understanding of community growth and player engagement strategy for games Experience gathering, filtering, and applying player feedback with cross-functional partners Experience supporting a AAA live title as a plus What to send our way Your CV in English highlighting relevant skills and experiences Examples of community programs or content you have led, such as links to blogs, posts, campaigns, podcasts, videos, dashboards, or KPI reports Note: Our Talent Acquisition team for this role is based in the UK, so please send CVs in English International travel may be required.

This position will be part of multiple cross-functional projects, working with Community Managers, Marketing, Production, and Video Editor teams, among others.

Ubisoft's perks 💰 Profit Sharing, yearly company saving plan. 25 paid time off + 12 additional paid days off. 50% of your transportation pass is paid by the company, lunch vouchers (9€/day), healthcare for you and your family, and lots of Ubisoft additional perks.

👶 Maternity leaves of 20 weeks, paternity/co-parental leaves of 7 weeks.

📍 Our office is located in Saint Mandé, (Metro line 1, Saint Mandé station). Gym available in the building.
