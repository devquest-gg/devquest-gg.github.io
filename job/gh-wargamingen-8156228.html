Job Overview

We are looking for an Server-Side Events Engineer to join the Server-Side Events team. As part of the Operations Management department (LiveOps division), the team’s main goal is to create and support in-game activities for World of Tanks in close cooperation with Development, Localization, Platform, and other teams. The candidate must be ready to work in a rapidly changing environment where concentration and attention to detail are prerequisites for timely and adequate reaction to daily challenges.

What will you do?

Check monthly plans for battle missions, in-game events, and specials before their technical implementation

Implement battle missions, in-game events, and specials

Introduce on-the-fly changes to battle missions, in-game events, and specials (on live servers)

Communicate with colleagues in local and remote offices in English/Russian

Provide technical assistance on battle missions, in-game events, and specials functionality

What are we looking for?

Intermediate or higher level of English

Written and verbal communication skills in Russian

Technical education

Good communication skills

Attention to details

Quality-oriented attitude

Gaming experience in World of Tanks project

Readiness for duty on schedule (availability 24/7)

What additional skills will help you stand out?

Basic knowledge of XML and JSON

Basic level of Python

Knowledge of server-side architecture

Experience with Git, SVN, or other version control systems

Knowledge of JIRA and Confluence

Experience in game development

Work mode

Hybrid (3 days of work from the office)

This role isn t eligible for relocation immigration support.

The salary range for this role in Vilnius is EUR 1560-2200 monthly gross.

About Wargaming

Wargaming is an award-winning online game developer and publisher headquartered in Nicosia, Cyprus. Operating since 1998, Wargaming has become one of the leaders in the gaming industry with 15 offices worldwide, including studios in Chicago, Prague, Shanghai, Tokyo, and Vilnius. Our diverse and multicultural team works together to deliver a top-class experience to millions of players who enjoy Wargaming s titles across all major gaming platforms. Our flagship products include free-to-play hits World of Tanks, World of Warships and World of Tanks Blitz.
