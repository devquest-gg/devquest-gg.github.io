ABOUT THIS POSITION

The Digital Extremes Web team is looking to hire a motivated Junior Designer interested in helping us shape Warframe.com and Soulframe.com. You should be creative and curious, passionate about gaming, and have completed an Associate’s or Bachelor’s degree in web/visual design or a related discipline. You will work with us primarily on website designs and occasionally related touchpoints like the Warframe companion app or the PC game launcher. At Digital Extremes, you’ll learn how to balance content and accessibility with interactive visual designs. If these last few sentences have gotten you excited, we’d love to chat with you!

RESPONSIBILITIES

Participate in the creative process for Warframe and Soulframe websites, from research and ideation to final execution. You will take ownership of projects from start to finish.

Partner with our Senior, Principal, and Lead Designers to collaborate on big ideas and to create web-ready assets.

Prepare Figma files and final image assets for dev handoff and provide design QA on websites in development.

Collaborate with marketing, multimedia, community, development, and other teams to align goals and maintain consistency across the Warframe and Soulframe universe.

Learn and uphold UX principles for web and mobile.

Create flexible, screen-agnostic designs and prototypes in Figma.

Use Adobe Photoshop to edit game art and create textures and images for the web.

Document and contribute to our design system.

REQUIREMENTS

A two or four year degree in Visual Design or related degree: UX/UI, Interactive Design, Product Design, Web Design, Graphic Design, etc.

Exceptional visual and technical understanding of the creation of interactive websites.

A strong attention to detail; someone who strives for pixel-perfection and polish on their work.

Comfort with ambiguity; the ability to create concepts and make decisions with incomplete information as our games are constantly in development.

Knowledge or experience related to information architecture, motion design and branding.

Excellent communication and interpersonal skills for clear, inclusive, and collaborative feedback.

An online portfolio is required for consideration.

Please include three or more projects that demonstrate your knowledge and experience in UI/UX, product design, interactive design or web design.

ABOUT DIGITAL EXTREMES

Founded in 1993 by James Schmalz, Digital Extremes ranks as one of the world s top independent video game development studios. Originating with the co-creation of Epic Games multi-million unit selling Unreal® franchise including Unreal and Unreal Tournament, Digital Extremes went on to develop Dark Sector®, BioShock® for the PlayStation®3, the BioShock 2 multiplayer campaign, and The Darkness® II. The studio has reached its greatest critical and commercial success with the free-to-play action game, Warframe® , boasting a global community of 75 million registered players on PC, PS4™, PS5™, Xbox One™, Xbox Series X/S™, Nintendo Switch™, and iOS™. The studio s most recently announced project is the much anticipated Soulframe® , an open-world action-fantasy MMORPG title that is currently under development.

Digital Extremes is headquartered in London, Ontario, Canada, and is proud to be selected as one of Canada s Top 100 Employers.

For more information and exciting news related to Digital Extremes, please visit www.digitalextremes.com .
