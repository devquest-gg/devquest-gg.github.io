FP A Analyst Atari East Coast (Remote) Reports to: Director of FP A

About Us

Atari, one of the world s most iconic entertainment brands, is a pioneer in interactive entertainment. With operations spanning gaming, e-commerce, licensing, and hardware, Atari thrives at the intersection of innovation and legacy. Join our dynamic team and play a key role in shaping the financial health of our diverse business operations.

Position Summary

The FP A Analyst will support financial planning and analysis across Atari s business units, including games, e-commerce, licensing, and hardware. Reporting to the Director of FP A, this individual will contribute to budgeting, forecasting, and financial reporting in a fast-moving environment where not all systems are fully built out yet. This is a growth role and ideal for someone who blends genuine passion for games with solid financial chops and wants to grow alongside the business.

Key Responsibilities

Support the annual budgeting and forecasting process, with hands-on ownership of spreadsheet models and financial data

Prepare regular financial reports and variance analyses across business units

Partner with and help coordinate the India-based FP A team on day-to-day deliverables and reporting workflows

Build and maintain financial models to support business decisions, M A activity, and scenario planning

Contribute to the development of KPI dashboards and data reporting tools

Work cross-functionally with department heads to gather inputs and provide financial insights

Help identify and implement process improvements as systems and infrastructure continue to evolve

Qualifications

Experience

3–5 years of experience in finance; FP A, corporate reporting, or a mix of both welcome

Experience in the video game industry (studio, publisher, or platform) strongly preferred. A plus if you understand how games are made, distributed, and monetized

Exposure to multi-entity or global organizations a plus

Advanced Excel skills. Spreadsheets are central to this role

Comfort working in environments where not everything is systematized yet; a builder mindset

Proficiency with AI tools and enthusiasm for applying them to financial workflows

Familiarity with Power Query or similar dataset tools is a plus.

Strong analytical skills with an ability to communicate financial concepts clearly

Education

Bachelor s degree in Finance, Accounting, or a related field

Target Salary: $80,000 - $95,000
