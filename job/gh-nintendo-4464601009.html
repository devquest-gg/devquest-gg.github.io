SUMMARY:

This role is part of the IT: Solutions department at Nintendo of America (NOA). It provides strategic and operational leadership for technology platforms supporting Nintendo’s retail, eCommerce, point-of-sale, and digital retailer operations. This position is responsible for establishing product and technology roadmaps, ensuring reliable and secure platform operations, overseeing vendors and managed services, and partnering with business and IT teams to deliver commerce capabilities aligned with business priorities. The manager will oversee a diverse portfolio that includes Salesforce Commerce Cloud, Salesforce Order Management, Magento-based storefronts, Salesforce Commerce POS, retailer integrations, payment and tax integrations, and supporting eCommerce applications. The role serves as a key connection point among IT, business teams, NCL, software providers, implementation partners, and managed-service vendors.

DESCRIPTION OF DUTIES

Lead the strategy, roadmap, and lifecycle management of commerce and retail technology platforms.

Partner with business stakeholders to prioritize and deliver solutions that support growth, customer experience, and operational efficiency.

Oversee application delivery, production support, platform upgrades, and release management.

Drive continuous improvement across eCommerce, retail, order management, and point-of-sale capabilities.

Manage vendor and managed-services relationships, ensuring high-quality service delivery and accountability.

Provide leadership for security, compliance, risk management, and operational resilience initiatives.

Collaborate with cross-functional teams including Digital Marketing, Customer Service, Finance, Supply Chain, Store Operations, and Global Nintendo partners.

Lead and develop a high-performing team while fostering a culture of innovation, accountability, and collaboration.

SUMMARY OF REQUIREMENTS

Experience leading enterprise commerce, retail, digital, or customer-facing technology platforms.

Proven success managing complex application portfolios through strategy, delivery, support, and continuous improvement.

Experience leading cross-functional teams, vendors, and technology partners.

Strong understanding of software delivery, production support, release management, and operational governance.

Excellent stakeholder management, communication, and leadership skills.

Knowledge of retail, eCommerce, point-of-sale, payment, or order management ecosystems.

Experience with vendor management, managed services, and technology road mapping.

Experience with Salesforce Commerce Cloud, Salesforce Order Management, Salesforce Service Cloud, or related Salesforce technologies

Experience with Adobe Commerce/Magento or similar eCommerce platforms

Familiarity with security, PCI compliance, and enterprise application governance preferred .

Applicants must be legally eligible to work in the United States to be considered. Visa sponsorship is not available for this role.

This position is on-site in Redmond, WA and is not open to remote status at this time.
