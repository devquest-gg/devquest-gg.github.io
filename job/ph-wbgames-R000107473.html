Your New Role We re looking for an Advanced Product Manager who thrives at the intersection of player empathy and analytical rigor. You ve shipped games and navigated the intensity of launch and/or found your rhythm in the ongoing discipline of live operations. You know your way around a telemetry pipeline as well as a product roadmap, and you’re able to rally the cross-functional team around a product vision.
