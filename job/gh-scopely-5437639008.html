What You’ll Do

As a Senior Technical Artist, you will focus on converting, integrating, and optimizing existing slot games and legacy assets within our Unity production pipeline. You will work closely with Art, Design, Engineering, QA, and Production to deliver high-quality game content while maintaining visual consistency, mobile performance, and compatibility across supported devices.

You will also contribute to improving technical art workflows, resolving integration challenges, and supporting efficient content delivery for our live-service game.

Key responsibilities include:

Convert and integrate existing slot games and legacy assets into Unity, including UI elements, symbols, backgrounds, animations, VFX, and supporting game screens

Adapt and optimize Unity UI layouts, Canvas configurations, prefabs, masking, sorting layers, and asset scaling to ensure visual consistency across supported mobile devices and resolutions

Create, edit, integrate, and optimize Spine 2D and Unity animations, ensuring smooth playback, correct layering, and efficient runtime performance

Integrate and optimize shaders, materials, visual effects, and other 2D/3D assets while maintaining visual quality and mobile performance requirements

Profile and troubleshoot rendering, memory usage, asset loading, animation performance, and runtime efficiency using Unity profiling and debugging tools

Support slot game conversions, re-skins, LiveOps events, and regular content releases, including playtesting, bug fixing, visual polish, and production readiness

Develop and improve Unity tools, editor extensions, automation scripts, and technical art workflows to reduce manual effort and improve content integration efficiency

Collaborate with cross-functional teams to resolve technical challenges, document production standards, and share technical knowledge with other Technical Artists

What We’re Looking For

5+ years of experience as a Technical Artist in the game industry, preferably with at least one shipped mobile or console title.

Practical experience converting, integrating, or adapting existing game assets into Unity pipelines, including slot games, game re-skins, or comparable content conversion workflows

Hands-on expertise in Spine 2D animation, including rigging, skeletons, skins, slots, attachments, atlases, animation states, events, draw order, and Unity runtime integration

Strong understanding of Unity UI systems, including Canvas, prefabs, layout management, masking, sorting layers, animation setup, and mobile resolution compatibility

Experience integrating and optimizing 2D assets, animations, VFX, shaders, and materials while balancing visual quality with mobile performance constraints

Experience profiling and resolving rendering, memory, asset loading, and animation performance issues using Unity Profiler, Frame Debugger, or equivalent tools

Ability to independently troubleshoot technical art challenges, maintain production workflows, and contribute practical improvements to asset integration and delivery processes

Experience collaborating with Art, Design, Engineering, QA, and Production teams, communicating technical requirements clearly, and sharing knowledge with colleagues

Bonus Points

Experience supporting slot games, social casino games, or other content-intensive live-service mobile games

Experience developing Unity editor tools, custom automation scripts, or reusable technical art solutions

Familiarity with Jenkins or similar CI/CD systems, version control, and automated content integration workflows

Understanding of Unity Addressables, Asset Bundles, asset packaging, and build processes

Experience with advanced 3D content pipelines or additional animation and VFX production workflows

Experience using generative AI or AI-assisted tools to improve asset creation, content processing, validation, or production efficiency
