For more information, visit www.zynga.com or follow Zynga on Twitter , Instagram , Facebook, or the Zynga blog We are seeking a creative and motivated Animator Intern to join our dynamic game art team. This is an opportunity to contribute to high-quality game animation while learning and working closely with experienced artists, animators, designers and technical artists.

What you will do

Create and support high-quality 3D animations for characters, creatures, props and gameplay elements under the guidance of senior animators.

· Work across 3D animation workflows using 3ds Max, Maya, Spine and Unity.

· Create, refine and integrate animations into Unity and support animation setup, rigs and prefabs.

· Work with character and prop rigs, keyframe animation, timing, posing and animation principles.

· Support 2D/2.5D animation requirements in Spine where needed.

· Collaborate with artists, designers, technical artists and engineers to deliver animation assets for games.

· Explore AI-assisted workflows for animation ideation, reference generation, asset preparation and creative experimentation.

· Learn and follow game art pipelines, naming conventions, optimization practices and production workflows.

· Take feedback positively and iterate on animation work to meet quality and gameplay requirements.

What we require

Strong foundation in 3D animation principles, including timing, spacing, posing, weight and anticipation.

· Hands-on expertise/familiarity with 3ds Max and Maya for 3D animation.

· Strong working knowledge of Unity for animation integration and game-engine workflows.

· Working knowledge of Spine for 2D/2.5D animation workflows.

· Good understanding of character animation, rigging and keyframe-based workflows.

· Photoshop skills for basic asset preparation and supporting animation workflows.

· Basic understanding of VFX, particles, cameras and animation-related effects in Unity.

· Familiarity with game art pipelines and mobile game development.

· Interest in animation, concept art or visual storytelling, and passion for games and interactive storytelling.

· Experience with Autodesk 3ds Max / Maya / Spine After Effects.

· Please include your animation portfolio/reel in the application. 3D animation, Unity, Spine or AI-assisted creative work samples are encouraged.

It will be great if you also have: · Experience experimenting with generative AI tools for image, video, animation or creative ideation.

· Exposure to AI tools or AI-assisted creative workflows, with an interest in exploring how AI can improve animation workflows.

· Ability to collaborate effectively, learn quickly and work within a game production environment.

· Strong visual communication, observation and problem-solving skills.
