Shape how Ubisoft finds and grows talent in the region! The right person, in the right role, at the right time — whether they join us from outside or grow from within. That's the job in one line.&#xa0; As Talent Acquisition & Internal Mobility Manager for Northern & Eastern Europe, you'll run talent acquisition and internal mobility for a whole region. You'll lead a team that works side by side with hiring managers and candidates, and you'll make sure every hire, and every internal move delivers on quality, speed and experience.&#xa0; You'll be part of a federated function where our Global Centre of Excellence sets the vision, standards, tools and innovation and you and your team are the operational arm closest to the business — the people who turn global strategy into great hires on the ground, and who tell the COE what's really happening in the market.&#xa0; What you'll do&#xa0; Lead your regional TA and mobility team&#xa0; Run sourcing, selection and recruitment with hiring managers, using the tools and standards set by the COE&#xa0; Drive internal mobility: support internal candidates, connect releasing and receiving managers, and keep moves aligned with our global program and business needs&#xa0; Set hiring priorities across studios and cross-functional teams, in line with Ubisoft's strategy and business plan&#xa0; Own the budget for your scope&#xa0; Bring global frameworks to life locally&#xa0; Adapt employer branding, legal compliance and sourcing channels to each market in your region&#xa0; Feed market intelligence back to the COE: market shifts, competitor practices, candidate feedback and hard-to-attract profiles — your insight shapes our global strategy&#xa0; Raise the bar on performance and efficiency&#xa0; Manage your team's performance against COE KPIs and report on efficiency and stakeholder satisfaction for each entity you support&#xa0; Simplify how we work: cut admin, clarify who decides what, streamline workflows&#xa0; Lead adoption of the AI and automation tools we use for screening, skills matching and pre-selection&#xa0; Build the selection skills of your team and your hiring managers&#xa0; Build talent density for the long term&#xa0; Create proactive talent pools for our critical and strategic roles, to shorten sourcing times&#xa0; Strengthen the internal succession pipeline for critical roles in your region&#xa0; What you bring&#xa0; Proven experience leading recruitment teams across mixed volume and complexity — early careers, G&A/business and R&D/tech — ideally in a federated model&#xa0; Strong knowledge of regional labour markets, and the judgment to adapt global standards to local regulatory, cultural and sector realities&#xa0; Command of TA metrics such as time to fill, time to hire, offer acceptance rate and cost per hire — and you manage with data&#xa0; Genuine curiosity about AI and automation in recruitment, from screening and matching to job creation&#xa0; How you'll lead&#xa0; You unite and mobilise people. You build a culture of excellence and accountability, even when things are changing fast.&#xa0; You think like a business leader. You understand Ubisoft's strategy and priorities, and you see every hire in the context of our production needs and ambitions.&#xa0; You're a trusted advisor, not an order-taker.
