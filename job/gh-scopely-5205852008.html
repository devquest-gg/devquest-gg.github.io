Scopely is looking for a VIP Lead to join the GSN Casino team . The position can be based in Bangalore, India, Tel Aviv, Israel or Barcelona, Spain.

At Scopely, we care deeply about what we do and want to inspire play, every day - whether in our work environments alongside our talented colleagues, or through our deep connections with our communities of players. We are a global team of game lovers who are developing, publishing and innovating the mobile games industry, connecting millions of people around the world daily.

With 30M+ downloads, GSN Casino is home to slots, bingo, cards, and more! Team members are distributed across the globe with hubs in Bengaluru, Tel Aviv, Barcelona, among others.

What You’ll Do

As the VIP Lead, you will manage a team of VIP Managers and look after our most valuable players. You will manage the work, internal and external resources associated within the VIP department, including crafting requirements, delegating responsibilities, developing and executing strategies that offer a new, vibrant layer of service for the VIP players. The right candidate will have creative and effective solutions to maximize VIP engagement, strengthen player loyalty, and showcase our appreciation for their dedication.

In this role, you need to be collaborative, organized and resourceful, with the ability to communicate effectively across different levels within the company as you guide the VIP team and stakeholders whom you will work with closely to successfully execute on multiple projects and initiatives simultaneously. You will also need to have full grasp into VIP operations to continue to propose ways to refine and improve existing programs/projects to meet the changing needs of the department by regularly reviewing processes, reporting and industry standards.

The VIP Lead will report directly to the Head of Product and will work in collaboration with multiple cross-functional teams, such as LiveOps, Roadmap, Game Design etc. Responsibilities may include:

Manage the VIP Account management program and overarching strategy and performance KPIs in collaboration with the PX manager

Guiding training and supporting staff performance and growth

Motivate and support internal and external teams to ensure project goals, objectives, milestones, and deliverables are achieved

Onboarding: in charge of coordinating new hires orientation and training

Centralize VIP documentation on Confluence/Drive

Draw insights from PX data and VIP Feedback, and assist stakeholders in designing solutions to improve the VIP experience

Oversee and direct a group of VIP support agents to keep VIP attrition low and VIP satisfaction high

Collaborate on the creation of loyalty programs

Work with the Community and PX teams to understand upcoming events, product updates, and ensure that your VIPs are kept up to date

Analyze performance, identify the best opportunities for optimization, and regularly iterate to maximize results

What We’re Looking For

4+ years experience in VIP account management for a mobile game or any mobile product with a dynamic roadmap

Experience in revenue generation/sales campaigns

Solid experience supporting teams and working with multiple business units (customer support, developers, business, operations, etc.) to drive large projects focused on service and products with measured results

You are highly organized, detail-focused, timely, and results-driven. You have a strong sense of ownership and accountability for success of operations

Experience in mobile products and agile environments

Experience with marketing automation and CRM tools

Data-driven with an ability to understand trends and find ROI-positive solutions

Tremendous communications skills, including excellent written and spoken English

Must have a passion for conflict resolution and problem-solving

Strong evidence of contributing to customer satisfaction, retention engagement

Ability to analyze and draw conclusions from raw data

Need to have creative impulses and the confidence to share ideas with teammates

Outgoing, likable, enthusiastic, and driven to achieve results

Bonus Points

Ability to produce target-specific program and report accordingly

Proven experience in VIP portfolio / ROI driven growth

Proven experience in analytical, reporting, and budget management

About Scopely

Scopely is a leading video game and global interactive entertainment company, home to many of the world’s most beloved and enduring experiences, including two of the most successful mobile games of all-time “MONOPOLY GO!” and “Pokémon GO,” along with “Stumble Guys,” “Star Trek™ Fleet Command,” “MARVEL Strike Force,” “WWE Champions,” the Scrabble® franchise, “Yahtzee® With Buddies,” and many others. Across mobile, web, PC, and console, Scopely creates, develops, publishes, and live-operates one of the most diversified and award-winning portfolios in the games industry — bringing hundreds of millions of players together through a shared love of play.

Founded in 2011, Scopely is powered by its exceptional team — including thousands of world-class gamemakers around the globe, a distinctive tenet-driven culture, and its proprietary technology platform, Playgami. Together, these strengths have fueled Scopely’s position as the #1 mobile games company in the U.S. and #2 globally, generating more than $10 billion in lifetime revenue. Whether building global sensations like “MONOPOLY GO!” from the ground up, or expanding through strategic acquisitions, including the FoxNext, GSN, and Scopely Explore games businesses — Scopely consistently delivers experiences players love today and return to for years to come.

Recognized multiple times as one of the "100 Most Influential Companies in the World" by TIME magazine and one of Fast Company s "World s Most Innovative Companies" and “Best Workplaces for Innovators,” Scopely believes that video games can be a force for good — creating meaningful connections, vibrant communities, and making life better through play.

Scopely has global operations and partners across four continents in more than a dozen countries worldwide. For more information, visit: https://www.scopely.com/ .
