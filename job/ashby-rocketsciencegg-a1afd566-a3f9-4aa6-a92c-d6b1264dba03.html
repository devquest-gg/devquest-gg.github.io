UI/UX Designer

Rocket Science · Cardiff

Mid Design Hybrid Posted 2026-08-12 Full time

Please note that this is a hybrid position based in Cardiff, requiring 3 days onsite each week, with the option for remote work on the remaining days.

Atomic Theory is a multinational team of seasoned AAA veterans with roots at studios like Blizzard, PUBG, Epic Games, and Unity. We’re passionate about building player-first experiences with strong engineering foundations, performance excellence, and accessibility baked in from day one.

With offices in Albany, NY; Austin, TX; Cardiff, UK; and Brighton, UK, we operate globally with one mindset: Get Sh!t Done.

ABOUT THE ROLE:

As an Mid-Level UI/UX Designer, you are a fully capable, independent contributor who handles complex UI tasks and systems within your domain.

You take ownership of mid-scope UI features and solve complex design or technical challenges. You are expected to work autonomously, unblock peers when necessary, and ensure your work is high-quality, stable, and integrated into the project pipeline.

At this level, you are not leading the team, but you are a key contributor whose technical and design expertise keeps features moving forward efficiently.

WHAT YOU'LL DO:

- Design, implement, and optimise complex UI features and systems (menus, HUD modules, dynamic flows, procedural elements).

- Develop and maintain UI libraries, reusable components, and critical systems to support your feature domain.

- Implement and optimise or have knowledge of custom UI shaders, materials, or advanced animations to achieve specific visual or functional goals.

- Debug and solve technical UI issues (engine, blueprint, middleware) to unblock your feature work.

- Validate UI functionality and appearance across resolutions, aspect ratios, and gameplay contexts.

- Improve personal and small-team workflows to increase efficiency and reliability.

- Contribute to UX flows, usability, and interface consistency within your domain.

- Maintain documentation for tools, systems, and components to ensure clarity and reproducibility.

- Collaborate with engineers, designers, and producers to integrate features smoothly.

WHAT WE VALUE:

- 3+ years of experience in UI/UX design (games or interactive products).

- Proven ability to deliver mid-scope UI features independently, including complex interactions or procedural systems.

- Proficiency with design tools (Figma, Photoshop, Illustrator) and engine implementation (Unreal, Unity).

- Strong understanding of UI technical fundamentals, including layout, anchoring, slicing, optimisation, and Blueprint/scripting basics.

- Ability to debug and resolve complex technical and design challenges within your feature domain.

- Experience developing or optimising UI systems or libraries that improve efficiency and consistency.

- Clear communication skills, including the ability to flag blockers and propose practical solutions.

- Strong grasp of UI/UX fundamentals, usability, readability, and accessibility.

- Passion for polished, performant, and intuitive player experiences.

WHAT YOU MAY ALSO HAVE:

- Experience with dynamic HUDs, procedural UI, or live-service UI elements.

- Familiarity with UI middleware or advanced engine pipelines.

- Experience building shader-based UI, advanced materials, or complex animations.

- Exposure to UX metrics, usability testing, or iterative optimisation.
