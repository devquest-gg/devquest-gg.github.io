SENIOR UI PROGRAMMER Rocksteady is looking to add to its family of staff: The Senior UI Programmer will write, extend and refine UI features in world-class triple-A videogaming experiences for our players across multiple platforms. Reporting to the Lead UI Programmer, you’ll take on advanced architectural and implementation challenges to push the boundaries of UI coding for video games, and in collaboration with artists and designers, deliver the highest quality game experience for our players.

This role is offered on a hybrid basis. You can choose to work from home or in the office on Mondays, Thursdays and Fridays. On Tuesdays and Wednesdays, the team collaborates onsite at Rocksteady Studios in Kentish Town, London.

Responsibilities Driving and implementing UI features and systems through all stages of production: design, scheduling, implementation and polish Giving constructive and proactive input into the development of new features and the iteration of existing ones Working closely with artists and designers to produce outstanding user experiences Exemplifying development best practices with strong communication, proactive problem solving and a strong commitment to quality Conduct code reviews and contribute to maintaining high engineering standards Ensure UI features meet performance, memory, and scalability requirements Requirements Advanced understanding of C Solid understanding of relevant computer graphics and game technology principles Experience architecting and maintaining UI systems for complex video games, including the use of Model-View-ViewModel architecture experience with Unreal Engine 5 and UMG Excellent communication skills Experience working on and shipping at least one AAA video game title Desired Experience with Unreal Engine’s Common UI, Enhanced Input, MVVM plugin and Slate Experience with shaders and Unreal Engine materials Experience with accessibility and localization A passion for creating intuitive and polished user experiences Hybrid Working - This role is advertised as a Hybrid work model, that combines remote and in-office work, following our current company policy and to be agreed with your Line Manager. Subject to any applicable laws, WBD / your Line Manager reserves the right to change this working agreement where this is essential to business needs and upon reasonable notice to you.
