This role owns Stillfront's data privacy work end to end, with real scope to shape how it's run and where it goes next. It's a genuinely global mandate too, not a Swedish role with a global label attached: your remit covers every studio and function we have, wherever they sit. You'll be based in Stockholm, working alongside colleagues in Sweden, Germany, India and the UAE, with regular contact across studios, functions and advisors worldwide. You'll report to the Group General Counsel.

You'll work closely with our external Data Protection Officer on the formal DPO function. The substance underneath it, strategy, policies, assessments, and the judgment calls that come up most weeks, is yours. On top of that you'll advise on commercial and studio agreements, and instruct and manage outside counsel when we need them for disputes.

The work is genuinely international, in the same week you might be advising a studio in Hamburg, a function in Stockholm, and a partner in APAC. It's demanding, but it's fun too.

Real autonomy: you decide where privacy at Stillfront goes next, not the other way around Direct exposure to Group leadership and studios worldwide, work that reaches products played by tens of millions of people An international, genuinely mixed team, with colleagues worth knowing Stillfront is a global gaming company. We develop a wide range of digital games that attract millions of players each month. From well-established franchises like Supremacy, BIG, and Jawaker to niche games, we span multiple genres, including strategy, simulation, RPG, action, and casual and mash-up games.

Our HQ is in Stockholm, Sweden, but our game development is done by teams and studios all over the world. Our main markets are the US, Japan, MENA, Germany, and the UK. We’re proud that our company shares are listed on the Nasdaq Stockholm Large Cap.
