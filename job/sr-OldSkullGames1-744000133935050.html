At Old Skull Games, we create games built around strong gameplay, sharp game feel, and meaningful player experiences.

We believe great games are meant to be played, shared, and talked about — whether that’s through tight multiplayer moments with friends, or deeper single-player experiences that resonate with more engaged players.

Today, the studio is evolving:

Continuing to build high-energy multiplayer experiences, while also developing more ambitious, gameplay-driven titles

Working on recognized IPs alongside original creations

Raising the bar on both creative ambition and production quality

We are a 60+ people studio based in Lyon, collaborating with leading industry partners and building the next generation of our games.

Why is this role different ?

This is not a “plug-and-play” business role.

We are intentionally open on seniority, from strong mid-level to experienced senior, because what matters most to us is the person, the mindset, and the ambition.

Depending on your profile:

- You might own deals end-to-end and drive the commercial strategy of the company&#xa0;

- Or start with a strong focus on deal execution and progressively take ownership of strategy, partner portfolio, and long-term positioning of the studio

- Or work closely with the CEO (whose original background is in business development) to progressively expand your scope We will shape the role around the right person.

What you’ll work on

- Identify and develop new business opportunities (publishers, platforms, IP holders)

- Build and nurture long-term partnerships, this is key to OSG vision.

- Drive our pitching efforts and present our projects with clarity and conviction

- Collaborate closely with production, creative, and leadership teams

- Represent the studio on trade shows, worldwide.&#xa0;

- Help position Old Skull Games as a trusted, proactive, high-quality partner Depending on your experience, you may also:

- Lead negotiations and deal structuring

- Drive strategic partnerships and co-development opportunities

- Contribute to long-term studio growth and positioning Who we’re looking for

More than a CV, we’re looking for a fit.

- You have experience in game industry business development (5 years minimum)

- You understand how games are made, or you are deeply curious about it

- You’re comfortable navigating both creative conversations and business constraints

- You have strong communication and relationship-building skills

- You show initiative, you don’t wait to be told what to do

- You are organized, determined and able to handle multiple projects at once

- You are fluent in French and English.

Most importantly you care about building something impactful with a team, not just closing deals.

&#xa0; What you’ll find at Old Skull Games :&#xa0;

- A studio where initiative is encouraged and trust is given

- A culture that values clarity, collaboration, and shared ambition

- Projects that balance creative ownership and production excellence

- A team passionate about making games that people genuinely enjoy together

- Direct access to leadership and real impact on the studio’s trajectory If you’re looking for a role where you can shape, build, and grow not just execute, we’d love to talk
