Short-Term Level Designer / World Designer – Unreal Engine

Remote - Beverly Hills, CA

Short-Term Level Designer / World Designer – Unreal Engine 5 Remote | Approximately 4-Week Engagement Snail Games USA is looking for experienced Level Designers / World Designers for a short-term engagement supporting For the Stars , an Unreal Engine 5 game currently in development.

This is a hands-on Unreal Engine 5 role. Designers are expected to build and iterate directly in-engine, working from established product pillars and technical constraints to create playable spaces and experiences.

What We’re Looking For Professional level design or world design experience.

Strong hands-on Unreal Engine 5 experience.

Ability to independently turn broad direction into playable content.

Strong spatial design, player flow, pacing, and gameplay judgment.

Comfortable working directly with existing systems and assets.

Blueprint or light scripting experience is helpful.

Portfolio or shipped work showing implemented game content.

Engagement Initial engagement expected to last approximately four weeks.

Remote work is supported.

Work will be reviewed at defined milestones.

Engagement structure will depend on location and individual business/employment circumstances and will be established in compliance with applicable requirements.

About Snail Games USA Snail Games USA develops and supports original and established game properties across internal and external development teams.
