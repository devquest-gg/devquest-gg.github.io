IT Support Specialist, US Studio Technology

Location: On-Site - Emeryville, CA Employment Type: Full-Time Experience: 3+ years Reports to: Manager, IT Operations Role Level: Experienced Individual Contributor

About Atari

Atari is an interactive entertainment company and an iconic gaming industry brand recognized worldwide for its multi-platform games and licensed products. Atari owns and/or manages a portfolio of more than 400 games and franchises.

The Atari family of brands includes Digital Eclipse, Nightdive Studios, Infogrames, AtariAge, MobyGames, as well as Coatsink, Early Morning Studios, and Stormteller Games spanning game development, publishing, and community experiences worldwide.

Atari operates internationally with offices in the US, UK, France, Germany, Sweden, and India.

About the Role

The IT Support Specialist, US Studio Technology provides first-line IT support for Atari’s US employees, with hands-on studio support for Digital Eclipse in Emeryville.

You will support employees across engineering, art, design, production, QA, and studio operations, as well as colleagues working remotely across the United States. You will troubleshoot workplace technology, coordinate equipment deployment and recovery, support onboarding and offboarding, maintain accurate asset records, and partner with Global IT on issues requiring advanced technical, infrastructure, security, or systems support.

Experience supporting game development environments, including development kits, test devices, specialized peripherals, and high-performance workstations, is valuable but not required.

This is an individual contributor role. It does not include direct people management or ownership of global infrastructure, security, identity, systems administration, or IT strategy.

Responsibilities

Respond to support requests from employees across the United States, troubleshoot common hardware, software, access, and connectivity issues, and provide clear updates through the ticketing system.

Serve as the primary hands-on IT contact for employees at Digital Eclipse in Emeryville, including support for workstations, peripherals, conference rooms, printers, mobile devices, and other workplace technology.

Assist with the setup, movement, maintenance, and recovery of development kits, test devices, specialized peripherals, high-performance workstations, and other equipment used by engineering, art, production, and QA teams.

Maintain accurate asset records and coordinate equipment assignments, checkout, shipping, returns, replacements, storage, and secure retirement for US employees.

Prepare equipment and coordinate standard access requests for new hires, role changes, transfers, and offboarding in partnership with HR, hiring managers, Global IT, and other stakeholders.

Resolve common first-line issues independently and escalate infrastructure, security, identity, systems, vendor, and complex technical issues to Global IT using established procedures.

Troubleshoot common Wi-Fi, VPN, printer, conference room, and device connectivity problems. Global IT will retain responsibility for network architecture, major changes, security controls, and complex connectivity issues.

Create and update setup guides, troubleshooting instructions, equipment procedures, and knowledge-base content used by US employees and the broader IT team.

Track repeated issues, provide feedback to Global IT, and suggest practical improvements to support processes, documentation, and employee experience.

Use approved AI and automation tools where appropriate to improve troubleshooting, documentation, reporting, and repeatable support tasks. Review outputs for accuracy and confidentiality before use.

Qualifications

You have experience providing end-user IT support in an office, studio, production, or similar environment.

You can independently troubleshoot Windows and macOS devices, mobile devices, peripherals, printers, conference room technology, Google Workspace, VPN tools, Wi-Fi, and common workplace applications.

You have experience using a ticketing system and maintaining accurate equipment or asset records.

You communicate clearly with employees in person, by email, chat, and remotely, and can manage support requests from employees working across different US time zones.

You are organized, service-oriented, comfortable working on-site, and able to determine when to resolve an issue independently versus escalate it.

You can handle confidential information and high-value equipment responsibly.

Working Hours

The role works standard US business hours aligned primarily with Pacific Time, with occasional planned flexibility to support employees in Central, Mountain, and Eastern time zones.
