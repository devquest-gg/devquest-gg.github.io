Embedded QA

Gameloft · Paris

Senior QA Posted 2026-07-09 Full time
Apply on Gameloft’s site →

Main challenge Join us at a pivotal moment: the launch of pre-production for an ambitious new AA PC/Console project. You will have for mission to build our Studio quality assurance foundations from the ground up.

You will become the studio's ground truth for quality: owning the relationship with our internal QA team, defining what and how we test, and standardizing the tools and processes that let developers turn features into stable, well-tested builds — quickly and with confidence.

What it means on a daily basis You'll be the link between the studio and our global internal test team, from build handoff to the final bug report.

Deliver builds to the QA team, document their scope and content, and define what needs to be tested based on content maturity and test type (progression, compliance, etc.) Build and continuously refine the studio's overall test strategy Set up and maintain smoke tests across PC and PS5 — with Switch2 and Xbox on the roadmap — grounded in console TRC requirements and their implementation in Unreal Engine Develop scripting and tooling (Python, AI-assisted tools) to speed up and standardize testing Standardize the team's ticketing process and act as their day-to-day point of contact, keeping bug reports and build status clearly visible to Producing What success will look like A few months in, you'll know the role is working when: Builds are delivered to QA reliably, on time, and clearly documented A test strategy is in place and consistently applied across the distributed team Smoke test coverage is live and expanding across platforms The ticketing process is standardized and saves developers real time Quality issues are caught early, with clear, always-available reporting on bug and build status Who you would be working with You'll report to the Producing lead, with daily syncs and a weekly team meeting, and work closely with two other members of the Producing team on a daily basis.

Your internal stakeholders will be&#xa0;: The internal QA team Programming, Art and Game Design teams, to define unit and functional test strategies Internal studio teams building Switch2 and Xbox builds What you need to succeed Comfortable with JIRA, including JQL and building dashboard filters Python scripting skills, with an interest in building or adapting AI-assisted testing tools Knowledge of console TRC (certification/compliance requirements) and how to implement them Familiarity with Unreal Engine, including its automated testing systems and automation pipelines Experience setting up smoke tests across PC and console platforms Who you are You're also highly organized, with a knack for clear, intelligible reporting — able to communicate the state of things at a glance. You're autonomous, comfortable standardizing or improving existing processes, and at ease working with distributed, cross-timezone teams. French and English both come naturally to you, with English increasingly used day to day.

Where this role can take you This is a foundational role in a discipline the studio is building from scratch, with real, lasting influence on how testing gets done.

You can get full ownership of the quality assurance strategy and process for a title, from pre-production onward
