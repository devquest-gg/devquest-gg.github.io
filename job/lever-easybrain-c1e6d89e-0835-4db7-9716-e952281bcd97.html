Junior User Acquisition Manager

Easybrain · Warsaw

Entry Marketing Hybrid Posted 2026-06-25
Apply on Easybrain’s site →

Easybrain is looking for a Junior User Acquisition Manager who has experience working at Deloitte, KPMG, PwC or EY and is eager to launch user acquisition campaigns worldwide, execute marketing initiatives, and contribute to the growth of our projects. We believe that a background in audit provides a strong foundation for success in User Acquisition, thanks to its emphasis on analytical thinking, data-driven decision-making, attention to detail, and working with large datasets.

Responsibilities:

- Own and execute Easybrain's mobile user acquisition strategy across multiple advertising networks;

- Work with data sets to create and optimize campaigns, and target users based on performance metrics;

- Closely collaborate with designers in the creative process for ads.

Requirements:

- Excellent knowledge of English (at least Upper Intermediate);

- Knowledge of Russian language is a must;

- Basic knowledge of Excel or Google Spreadsheets;

- Interest in the mobile application and game market;

- Combo of analytical mindset and creativity;

- Being a fast learner.

Benefits:

Besides the engaging tasks, support from experienced colleagues, challenge, and drive, we offer:

- Full support in relocating to countries where our offices are located;

- High-end market salary with performance bonuses;

- All needed equipment;

- Regular company events and monthly Friday meetings;

- Social benefits (private medical cover, sports reimbursement, etc.);

- Paid vacations, sick days;

- English, Greek, and Polish online language classes;

- Reimbursement for education and professional development.
