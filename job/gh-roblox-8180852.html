Roblox is looking for an experienced Senior Counsel, Litigation to join our Legal team. In this role, you will provide management and support for a variety of litigation, disputes, and investigations, including regulatory, consumer protection, product liability (including mass tort and coordinated proceedings), employment, and international disputes.

You will report to our Director, Litigation.

Location: The position is seated at Roblox Headquarters in San Mateo, CA. Roblox’s mandatory in-office days are Tuesday through Thursday with optional in-office days on Monday and Friday.

You Will:

Manage a high-volume docket of coordinated and parallel proceedings across multiple jurisdictions, including state JCCP proceedings and other coordinated matters

Support international investigations and regulatory inquiries

Collaborate with and manage outside litigation counsel

Advise cross-functional teams on litigation strategy and risks

Coordinate and actively participate in internal discovery activities and subpoena responses

Address pre-litigation disputes

Assist with internal investigations

Support Legal department and team related projects, as assigned

You Have:

7+ years of litigation experience (law firm experience is required)

Technology company experience preferred

Experience in a fast-paced and dynamic organization preferred

Membership in good standing with the California Bar (or other bar association with the ability to register as in-house counsel in California)

Experience managing high-volume dockets and coordinated or parallel litigation across multiple jurisdictions

Broad complex litigation experience, with emphasis in regulatory, and experience in consumer, products liability, trade secrets, and employment

Exposure to international regulatory inquiries or cross-border litigation preferred

Excellent judgment, discretion, and risk assessment

Strong and effective drafting skills

Exceptional communication skills and the ability to establish and maintain effective relationships

Practical business acumen, driving pragmatic business-oriented solutions

A highly approachable, collegial, and collaborative approach

Accountable and proactive self-starter, comfortable working independently

Superb organizational skills and the ability to manage and prioritize multiple projects at once
