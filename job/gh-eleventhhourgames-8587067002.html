Lead Level Designer

Eleventh Hour Games is a fast growing studio, focused on a passion for top-down action RPGs.

We value open communication, close-knit teamwork, and opportunity for ownership and growth! Our mission is to gather and empower the next generation of creators to forge extraordinary games. Extraordinary games build passionate communities, which create passionate people. Passionate people turn into next year s creators.

With our breakout title Last Epoch, we are moving fast into the next round of adventure!

THE OPPORTUNITY:

As a Lead Level Designer at Eleventh Hour Games, you will be responsible for shaping the environments, encounters, and player experiences that bring Last Epoch to life.

You will guide a team of designers in creating compelling levels that balance gameplay flow, narrative integration, and visual storytelling. This role requires both creative vision and production discipline. You’ll partner closely with design, art, and engineering teams to ensure levels are fun, functional, and performant. As a leader, you’ll mentor designers, refine workflows, and keep the level design team aligned with project goals and deadlines.

RESPONSIBILITIES:

Lead, mentor, and grow a team of level designers, fostering collaboration and professional development

Drive the creative vision and execution of levels, ensuring they support gameplay, narrative, and artistic goals

Work closely with environment artists, narrative designers, and systems designers to deliver cohesive experiences

Review and provide feedback on level layouts, encounter balance, and worldbuilding consistency

Ensure all levels meet performance, quality, and accessibility standards

Contribute hands-on to level blockouts, encounter scripting, and gameplay prototyping

Establish and refine level design workflows, pipelines, and best practices for scalability and efficiency

Collaborate with production to scope, schedule, and track level design deliverables

Advocate for player experience, ensuring levels feel engaging, rewarding, and true to the vision of the game

QUALIFICATIONS:

5+ years of professional level design experience in the game industry, with at least 2+ years in a lead or senior design role

Track record of shipping at least one title in a senior or leadership capacity

Understanding of encounter design, pacing, and player progression

Experience working with level design tools in engines such as Unity or Unreal

Collaborative mindset with experience guiding cross-disciplinary teams (art, design, and engineering)

Communication and documentation skills in a remote-first environment

Problem-solving and organizational skills

PREFERENCES:

Familiarity with ARPGs, live-service pipelines, and multi-team collaboration
