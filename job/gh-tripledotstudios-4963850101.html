The role is working within our studio: Belka Games

Belka Games is a game development company. According to Sensor Tower, the company is among the top 30 highest-grossing game developers in Europe, and its audience has recently surpassed 110 million players.

Role Overview

As a Marketing Creatives Producer, you ll have plenty of opportunity for research and complete responsibility for your project. Developing your own framework for analyzing creative content will allow you to create better content, and having a good relationship with partners will let you conduct extensive market research.

If this challenge excites you and you want to join our amazing marketing team, make extremely effective, high-budget marketing content with us, get in touch.

Key Responsibilities

Define and shape the creative strategy for one of our projects

Set creative production priorities and contribute to planning

Lead the creative development process end-to-end: generate hypotheses, select ideas, and oversee production

Analyze creative performance and hypotheses, turning findings into actionable insights for the production team

Improve creative analytics tools in collaboration with the Marketing Analytics team

Monitor and analyze the mobile advertising market, trends, and best practices

Maintain the Creative Knowledge Base and share relevant insights with the team

Collaborate with cross-functional teams and external partners

Required Skills, Knowledge and Expertise

Extensive experience in a similar role in GameDev, with a portfolio demonstrating successful creative production

Deep understanding of the mobile gaming and mobile advertising market

Strong knowledge of advertising channels, placements, and performance marketing

Solid understanding of end-to-end art, video, and playable ad production

Experience working with creative performance data and analytics

Hands-on experience using AI tools and generative technologies in creative production workflows

Strong written and verbal communication skills, with the ability to generate, prioritize, and evaluate ideas

Experience launching creative production for new games from scratch will be a plus
