Japanese Bilingual Localisation Specialist

Initial six-month fixed-term contract | Potential extension | UK-based hybrid working (primarily remote, typically one office visit per month)

The Role

Join a small team of technical authors and translators responsible for creating and maintaining technical documentation for SIE Development Tools used by studios developing PlayStation games. Your core responsibility will be to deliver high-quality English-to-Japanese technical translations and help maintain Japanese documentation. You will also have opportunities to improve localisation workflows and support broader documentation initiatives, drawing on your experience and skills.

What You’ll Be Doing

Translate, edit and proofread technical content from English into Japanese, including user guides, release notes, UI text, technical notes, blog posts and presentation materials.

Own assigned translation tasks from initial review through delivery, ensuring technical accuracy, consistent terminology, clear Japanese and timely completion.

Help maintain and improve translation and documentation workflows, including the effective use and quality evaluation of AI-assisted tools, CAT tools and other automation where appropriate.

Work with technical authors to support migration to a new documentation portal and the establishment of an efficient publishing process.

Provide Japanese linguistic and terminology support to the developer support team to help improve the developer experience.

Collaborate with technical authors, developers, subject matter experts and stakeholders to resolve source-content questions, maintain consistency and improve documentation quality.

What we re looking for:

Professional proficiency in English and Japanese, with excellent written Japanese.

At least two years of professional translation experience, including technical documentation.

Ability to deliver accurate, clear and consistent English-to-Japanese translations independently.

Working knowledge of CAT tools, translation memories and terminology resources.

Ability to research unfamiliar technical concepts, raise focused questions with subject matter experts and manage deadlines with minimal supervision.

Strong attention to detail and a quality-focused approach to proofreading and review.

Ability to collaborate effectively in a multicultural, cross-functional team.

Basic familiarity with Git or other source control software.

Nice to Have

Experience working at a language service provider (LSP) or in an in-house localisation or documentation team.

Experience reviewing or improving translation, localisation or documentation workflows.

Experience with AI-assisted translation, machine translation post-editing (MTPE) and translation quality evaluation.

Experience in terminology management, localisation QA and related tools.

Experience writing or editing technical documentation in Japanese.

Familiarity with software development, developer tools, APIs, game development or documentation publishing systems.
