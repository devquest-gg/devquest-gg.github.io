About the role:

We are seeking a customer-centric and highly experienced Manager to lead our Campus Security and Emergency Response program. Reporting to the Global Security Manager, Workplace Events, you will be responsible for ensuring a safe and secure environment for our employees, visitors, and assets across our buildings at our headquarters. This role requires a strong leader with exceptional communication, interpersonal, and organizational skills, and a proven ability to build relationships with internal stakeholders, law enforcement agencies, and external vendors.

This role is based in San Mateo, Ca (Mon - Fri)

You will:

Develop and implement comprehensive security strategies and procedures to mitigate risks and ensure the safety and security of our campuses.

Lead the emergency response program, including developing and conducting regular trainings and drills to ensure preparedness for various emergency situations.

Cultivate and maintain strong relationships with local law enforcement agencies and emergency response organizations to facilitate collaboration and information sharing.

Oversee the management of security vendors, including contract negotiations, performance monitoring, and ensuring service level agreements are met.

Manage access control systems and procedures to ensure the appropriate levels of security are maintained while providing a seamless experience for employees and visitors.

Conduct regular security assessments and audits to identify vulnerabilities and implement corrective actions.

Promote a culture of safety and security awareness through communication, training, and engagement with employees.

Stay updated on standard methodologies and emerging security threats to proactively improve campus security measures.

Responsible for managing, forecasting, and maintaining Campus Security Budgets

May support the expansion and launch of new Roblox offices globally.

Develop and maintain Standard Operating Procedures (SOPs), policies, and workflows for Campus Security Operations.

Provide support to event security operations for high-profile internal and external events

You Have:

Bachelor s degree in Security Management, Criminal Justice, or a related field; advanced degree preferred.

7+ years of experience in security management, with a focus on campus security and emergency response.

Proven experience leading and managing a team of security professionals.

Strong understanding of security technologies, risk assessment methodologies, and emergency management procedures.

Excellent communication, interpersonal, and presentation skills, with the ability to effectively interact with diverse audiences.

Experience in vendor management and contract negotiation.

Proficient in access control systems and security management software.

You are:

Customer-centric with a strong commitment to providing a safe and secure environment for all.

A highly motivated leader with a proactive approach to problem-solving.

An exceptional communicator with the ability to build relationships and foster collaboration.

Calm and decisive under pressure, with exceptional crisis management skills.

Adaptable and flexible, thriving in dynamic and evolving environments.

A great teammate, encouraging trust and cooperation among colleagues.

Committed to maintaining the highest standards of professionalism and integrity.
