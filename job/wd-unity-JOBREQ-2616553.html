Accounting Manager (CAPEX/ Leasing Team/ Cap Software)

Unity · IND-Bengaluru

Mid Engineering Posted 2026-09-15 Full time
Apply on Unity’s site →

The opportunity The Accounting Manager will be responsible for accounting documentation over a variety of areas and support external reporting requirements. This role will entail overseeing the monthly/quarterly close process and ensuring accurate and timely deliverables are prepared. In addition, the role will have the opportunity to research technical accounting guidance and prepare technical memos. In addition, the candidate will develop strong working relationships with cross-functional partners and various levels of management.

This position requires working during late evening hours from 2 PM to 11 PM (IST), with a 1-hour lunch break included. Are you available to work during these Non-Shift hours? What you ll be doing Manage Ledger Close Process: Oversee the accurate and timely monthly, quarterly, and annual general ledger close process, including the review of journal entries, reconciliations, and close deliverables.

Financial Reporting and Analysis: Prepare monthly reporting flux analysis and review deliverables supporting consolidated financial statements and footnotes. Research and analyze complex accounting issues, providing recommendations to management for proper accounting treatment and potential financial statement impact.

Process Improvements: Develop and implement process improvements, lead automation initiatives, and maintain standard operating procedures.

Audit Support: Support both external and internal audit requirements related to areas of responsibility.

Leadership Support and Customer Service: Lead special projects and provide ad-hoc reporting and analysis for leadership. Deliver best-in-class customer service to internal stakeholders.

What we re looking for Chartered Accountant, with strong background in US GAAP, SEC regulations, internal controls Outstanding oral and written communication skills Project management skills, ability to prioritize, and experience driving process improvements. Ability to manage multiple tasks simultaneously and work in time-sensitive situations.

High level of attention to detail People Management Experience You might also have Adept at handling shifting priorities and meeting deadlines Ability to work with team members spread across geographies Experience with Workday Financials and/or Netsuite Additional information Relocation support is not available for this position.

International relocation support is not available for this position.
