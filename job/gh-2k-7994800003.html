Who We Are

Founded in 2023, Small Axe Studios is 2K’s newest stand-alone studio, built to push the boundaries of game development and bring 2K’s next groundbreaking sports franchise to life. Headquartered in Burnaby, we are assembling a world-class team of visionaries, builders, and passionate gamers who thrive on creative collaboration, technical excellence, and bold ideas. At Small Axe Studios, we believe big things start with sharp focus—and we’re crafting an exceptional, player-first experience from the ground up. If you’re ready to tackle unique challenges, leave your mark on a brand-new IP, and help shape the future of interactive sports entertainment, we want you on our team!

What We Need

We’re looking for an experienced and versatile Technical Designer to help drive the implementation and iteration of our gameplay systems and features. You will play a key role in bridging the gap between design and engineering—prototyping features, building robust content workflows, and ensuring technical feasibility for our sports-focused core fantasy experience.

As a Technical Designer, you’ll work across disciplines to bring features to life—from paper design to implementation—and guide the team in crafting flexible, scalable systems. You’ll be instrumental in building our toolsets, scripting gameplay behaviors, and helping define the future of how we build and iterate on gameplay content.

This role reports to the Lead Technical Designer and collaborates with engineering, design, and production leadership to deliver compelling, player-driven experiences.

What You’ll Do

Prototype and Implement Gameplay Systems

Design and build functional prototypes in collaboration with engineers and gameplay designers

Implement and iterate on gameplay mechanics, core loops, and systemic features using visual scripting or other proprietary tools

Ensure technical feasibility of design solutions while maintaining creative goals

Own Technical Implementation of Features

Collaborate with designers to translate high-level concepts into robust in-game behaviors and systems

Script game logic, define system flows, and integrate features into live builds

Optimize Workflows and Tool Usage

Partner with tools engineers to improve designer workflows and content pipelines

Develop best practices and reusable templates for design teams to improve iteration speed and reduce bugs

Support Other Designers

Provide technical guidance to other designers and help raise the team’s scripting and tool proficiency

Review and troubleshoot design implementations and help resolve blockers across features

Champion technical documentation and help maintain a healthy tech design culture

Collaborate Across Disciplines

Work hand-in-hand with engineering to ensure designs are performant, scalable, and shippable

Partner with animators, audio, and UI/UX teams to deliver features with high-quality execution

Champion the Core Fantasy Vision

Ensure technical design solutions reinforce the overall player experience goals

Help prototype and validate systemic interactions that create emergent or player-driven outcomes

Contribute to creative problem-solving that enables new gameplay opportunities

What Will Make You A Great Fit

3+ years experience in technical design or related gameplay implementation roles

Deep expertise with scripting systems (e.g., Unreal Blueprint)

Proven ability to implement gameplay systems from prototype through ship

Good understanding of game systems, mechanics, and their interactions

Experience working in collaboration with engineers, especially on feature integrations

Excellent problem-solving, debugging, and optimization skills

Strong documentation habits and ability to explain technical solutions to non-technical teammates

A proactive, collaborative attitude and a passion for empowering creative teams

Beneficial Qualifications

Familiarity with content pipelines, modular design approaches, or data-driven systems

Experience with tool development or technical design documentation

Experience in performance profiling and optimization on console/PC

Background in programming or computer science is a plus
