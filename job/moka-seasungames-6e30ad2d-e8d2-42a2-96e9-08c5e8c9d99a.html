[SEED Camp] Game Artist

Seasun Games · Guangdong

Entry Art Posted 2025-03-24 Full time
Apply on Seasun Games’s site →

1. Provide professional art support for team game projects, including but not limited to character and environment design.

2. Optionally participate in planning workshops, collaborating closely with designers to discuss game concepts and establish art direction.

Requirements: 1. Experience in game art production and familiarity with the game art workflow.

2. Ability to create various art assets (e.g., characters, environments) is preferred.

3. Prior experience working on a complete game project is a plus.

4. Strong communication skills, able to collaborate efficiently with designers and developers to align art design with overall project goals.

Additional Notes: The Game Art track in SEED Camp is primarily hands-on, focusing on team-based game development rather than structured courses.

About SEED Camp: SEED Camp is a summer program hosted by the SEED Lab of Amazing Seasun Games, designed for university students aspiring to explore the gaming industry. Participants will receive professional training in game design, gain insights into game development, and be mentored by industry experts while experiencing the full creative process—from concept to prototype development.

Eligibility: 1. Graduation period: September 2025 – August 2026.

2. University students passionate about gaming and game development, regardless of school or major.
