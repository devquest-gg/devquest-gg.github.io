WE ARE LOOKING FOR A SENIOR PRODUCER TO JOIN GAMEPLAY CONTENT, THIS TEAM MAKES MOST OF WHAT CLASH OF CLANS PLAYERS PLAY THROUGH THE YEAR. IN 2027 THE TEAM SHIPS NEW EQUIPMENT, TEMPORARY TROOPS, CRAFTED DEFENSES AND THE NEXT TOWN HALL, FOR A GAME PLAYED BY TENS OF MILLIONS OF PLAYERS.

Within this role you'll be the Producer for a multidisciplinary team across design, engineering, art and analytics, plus external partners for art, VFX and audio. Features are built by feature teams that form around each piece of work and own it from idea to release. You own how that work gets planned and delivered, from a rolling 12–18 month roadmap down to each release, and you make sure the other Clash teams we work with know what's coming.

You work most closely with the team's leads. You build the roadmap and bring recommendations on priorities, and you decide them together. Feature teams own design, art direction sits with the Art Director, and release management and QA sit with Tech & Quality. The role doesn't include people management.

WHAT MATTERS MOST

High Emotional Intelligence. You build trust quickly with people at every level, read the room, and stay calm and warm when things get busy.

You learn how the team works first. You spend your first months understanding how we work and why. Then you help us find what to improve, shaping your experience to this team.

Outcomes come first. Every system serves an outcome we need. You're flexible about tools and ways of working: you add structure where it helps, and leave room where the team works better without it. Success means better outcomes, not better processes.

You're steady when things change. Live games move fast. Priorities shift, partners slip and new opportunities land mid-cycle. You adapt the plan and keep the team focused.

[https://app.ashbyhq.com/api/images/user-content/1246dead-d30e-4c70-a1d3-ef7e5023d542/7a053edc-bf2e-4db4-85ee-5630e2b5d305/banner_narrow_customs.jpg]

WHAT YOU'LL DO

- The roadmap - A rolling 12-18 month roadmap tied to Clash strategy, plus the annual planning, strategy workshops and follow-up rhythm that keep it current.

- Clear priorities - The team and the teams around us know what matters most, so the most important work gets done first.

- Release delivery - Every release planned a release ahead, with ready-made plans for the types of content we make every year, including the new Town Hall, our biggest release of the year.

- Systems that fit the team - Tracking, reporting and documentation come out of the work itself, with routine updates automated, so anyone at Clash can see where gameplay work stands.

- Learning from what ships - Time in the plan for the team's analyst, so insights from each release shape the next one and are captured where other teams can build on them.

- A team that levels up - Coaching on planning, scoping and estimating, and retros that lead to visible change.

WHAT YOU'LL BRING

- 6+ years as a Producer or Development Director, owning delivery for a live-service team of around 20 people plus external partners.

- Experience creating long-range roadmaps tied to a game's strategy, and the planning workshops that build them.

- Strong delivery experience through cross-functional feature teams.

- A track record of automating routine production work using AI, so your time goes to the team, and a view on what you'd keep human.

- Recommendations and your reasoning behind them.

WOULD BE NICE IF YOU ALSO HAVE

- Knowledge of Clash of Clans as a player.

- Experience with a monthly or faster release cadence.

WHERE YOU'LL BE

We are recruiting this role to be based out of our Helsinki Studio

ABOUT SUPERCELL

Supercell is a games company from Helsinki, Finland, with offices https://supr.cl/jd-offices also in San Francisco, Seoul, Shanghai, and London. You might know us as the makers of Hay Day, Clash of Clans, Boom Beach, Clash Royale, and Brawl Stars. Our mission is to create great games https://supr.cl/jd-games that as many people as possible play for years and that are remembered forever.

So, how do we make great games? By putting together the best teams and giving them the freedom and independence to succeed. And by taking risks, failing, sharing learnings https://supercell.com/en/news/learning-from-failures/, and killing lots of projects.
