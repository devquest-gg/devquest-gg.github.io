Senior Machine Learning Engineer

Unity · Montreal

Senior Engineering Posted 2026-08-19 Full time
Apply on Unity’s site →

What you ll be doing * Design, implement, and scale AI agent systems in Unity environments that enable goal-directed behavior, task planning and execution, and environment-aware decision-making.

* Develop reusable agentic frameworks - such as planning modules, memory systems or policy adaptation layers - that creators can extend in their own projects.

* Develop AI models for the Unity AI agentic system. The AI models perform various tasks for building Unity games.

* Integrate Machine Learning models seamlessly within real-time game engine constraints, optimizing for latency, compute, and determinism.

* Lead high-impact initiatives, including hierarchical reinforcement learning for scalable behaviors, AI planning and goal inference frameworks for NPCs and simulations, and agent memory and world modeling systems for persistent, believable behavior.

* Collaborate across internal and external teams to unlock innovative use cases in gaming, simulation, and interactive storytelling.

* Contribute to the long-term vision and roadmap for Unity’s agentic AI offerings, ensuring modularity, usability, and performance across platforms.

* Establish and champion best practices for model quality, simulation-based evaluation, and ML/AI safety within real-time systems.

* Mentor engineers and researchers while cultivating a technical culture of innovation, excellence, and collaboration.

What we re looking for * Advanced degree (MS or Ph.D.) in Computer Science, Machine Learning, Robotics, or a related field—or equivalent practical experience.

* 5 years of hands-on experience developing ML systems in production, ideally within real-time or interactive environments.

* Strong background in one or more of the following: reinforcement learning, fine-tuning, decision-making under uncertainty, AI planning, or multi-agent systems.
