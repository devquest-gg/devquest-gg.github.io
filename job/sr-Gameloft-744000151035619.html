Lead Animator

Gameloft · Barcelona

Lead Animation Posted 2026-09-22 Full time

WHAT YOU WILL BE WORKING ON Your main challenge Gameloft Barcelona is looking for a Lead Animator to lead the animation team working on a new action-based, multi-platform IP featuring world-famous characters from a major licensor.

Your challenge will be to establish and maintain a high animation quality bar while leading and developing the team behind it. You will combine people leadership with strong animation expertise, working closely with Art, Game Design, Programming and external partners to ensure every animation supports the personality of our characters, gameplay readability and the overall player experience.

What it means on a daily basis Lead and develop the animation team , providing regular feedback, mentoring and guidance while supporting the individual growth of animators across different seniority levels.

Own the animation quality bar , ensuring character animation delivers the expected level of movement, weight, acting, storytelling and personality while remaining consistent with the project's artistic direction and licensor expectations.

Plan and oversee animation production , managing priorities, workload and delivery while keeping stakeholders informed and stepping into hands-on animation work when the team needs additional support.

Collaborate closely with Game Design, Programming, Art and Technical Animation to ensure animations work effectively within gameplay, including topics related to rigging, skinning and game-engine animation states.

Coordinate animation work with external partners , providing clear briefs, reviews and actionable feedback to ensure outsourced animation integrates consistently with the work produced internally.

What success will look like Within your first 3 months , you will have developed a strong understanding of the project's animation direction, characters, production pipeline and team, establishing effective ways of working with the animators and key partner disciplines.

Within 6 months , you will be confidently leading animation production and team development, maintaining a consistent quality bar, identifying production or quality risks early and ensuring the team receives the direction and feedback needed to deliver.

Within 9 months , you will be a key reference for animation across the project, helping the team operate with increasing autonomy while continuously raising animation quality and strengthening collaboration between Animation, Art, Design, Programming and external partners.

Who you would be working with You will report to the Studio Art Development Director and lead a team of 3D Animators and Technical 3D Animators .

You will work closely with the Studio Art Director, 3D Art Lead, Game Design, Programming and other production disciplines, while also collaborating with external animation partners and the project's licensor.

Your recruitment journey Our recruitment process is designed to give you the opportunity to meet the people you would work with and explore both your animation expertise and leadership experience.

Recruiter interview Hiring team interview Animation assessment/test, where applicable Final interview Where this role can take you This role gives you the opportunity to take ownership of animation at project level, developing both your craft and leadership expertise while contributing to a major multi-platform IP.

You will have the opportunity to grow as an animation leader, influence animation standards and workflows, develop other artists and collaborate closely with senior creative and technical stakeholders across the project.

WHAT YOU NEED TO SUCCEED Strong expertise in 3D character animation for games , with the ability to create and evaluate both realistic and stylized/cartoon animation.

Excellent understanding of movement, weight, acting, timing and storytelling , with the ability to preserve the identity and personality of a character through animation.

Ability to establish, communicate and maintain animation quality standards , translating animation principles into clear visual, written and verbal feedback.

Strong understanding of game animation production and real-time workflows , including effective collaboration with Technical Animators on rigging, skinning and game-engine animation states.

Ability to plan and prioritize animation work, manage production constraints and communicate clearly with stakeholders regarding scope, quality and delivery.

Professional experience with industry-standard 3D animation tools and workflows , particularly character animation pipelines such as Maya and/or 3ds Max.

Fluent English and the ability to communicate effectively within an international and multidisciplinary development environment.

A portfolio or showreel demonstrating strong character animation work and relevant game animation experience.

WHO YOU ARE A supportive people leader who can mentor and develop animators across different levels of seniority.

Comfortable giving clear, constructive and actionable feedback while maintaining a high creative quality bar.

Collaborative and able to act as a central point of communication between Animation, Art, Game Design, Programming and external partners.

Organized and able to manage changing priorities in a dynamic production environment.

Hands-on when needed, with a lead-by-example approach and the ability to balance team leadership with direct creative contribution.
