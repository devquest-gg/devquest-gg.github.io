Publishing Operations Manager - Teamfight Tactics (Contract)

Riot Games

Mid Marketing Posted 2026-07-09
Apply on Riot Games’s site →

Riot Games was established in 2006 by avid gamers who believe that player-focused game development can result in great games. In 2009, Riot released its debut title League of Legends to critical acclaim. Players form the foundation of our community and it’s for them that we continue to evolve and improve the League of Legends experience, and beyond! We’re looking for passionate professionals who can teach us a thing or two. We promise to return the favor. We embrace those who see things differently and aren’t afraid to experiment. That s where you come in.

As Publishing Operations Manager, Teamfight Tactics (Contract) , you will be responsible for ensuring that our marketing and publishing efforts run smoothly, efficiently, and at a high standard across the region. You will sit at the centre of campaign execution, working across regional and global teams to bring major initiatives, including Set launches and integrated campaigns, from planning through to delivery. You will design and run the processes that enable teams to scale, while also stepping in to directly manage critical projects and operational needs.

This role requires a strong operator who can bring structure to complexity, connect teams, and ensure that great ideas translate into high-quality player experiences.

Responsibilities:

Campaign Project Management

Manage the end-to-end delivery of key marketing initiatives, including Set launches and regional campaigns, from kick-off through to go-live

Coordinate cross-functional teams (marketing, community, content, product, and markets) to ensure alignment on timelines, deliverables, and priorities

Partner with Brand, Community and Esports leads to operationalise marketing activities, including influencer and content integration

Operations, Process Systems

Maintain and evolve project tracking tools, including campaign trackers, influencer management tools, and content management systems.

Identify and introduce tools (including AI workflows) to improve team productivity

Vendor, Partner Financial Management

Manage relationships with regional agencies and external partners, ensuring smooth collaboration and delivery

Oversee contract processes in partnership with legal teams, including negotiation, onboarding, and compliance

Own operational finance processes, including invoice management, budget tracking, and internal systems.

Influencer Content Operations Support

Support the execution of influencer and co-streamer activations, including logistics such as travel, access, payments, and event coordination

Coordinate content operations across regional and central teams, ensuring timelines, assets, and deliverables are aligned

Facilitate vendor and agency communication for content production and platform operations

Manage access and onboarding processes for partners and contributors across Riot systems

Cross-Team Coordination Market Support

Support market-level initiatives and transitions, helping teams adopt new processes and responsibilities

Contribute to broader product and publishing discussions by bringing an operational perspective on feasibility and execution

Required Qualifications:

5+ years of experience in marketing operations, project/programme management, or related roles in gaming, entertainment, or creative industries

Proven experience managing complex, cross-functional projects

Strong organisational skills, with the ability to manage multiple workstreams and stakeholders simultaneously

Experience working with external partners, agencies, or vendors

Familiarity with budgeting, invoicing, and operational finance processes

Strong communication and stakeholder management skills across diverse teams

Ability to bring structure to ambiguity and improve ways of working

Passion for games and player experiences

Desired Qualifications:

Experience working across multiple markets in APAC

Familiarity with influencer marketing, content production, or community initiatives

Experience designing or scaling operational processes in growing teams

Comfort working with tools and systems (e.g. project management, CMS.)

Interest in leveraging AI or automation to improve workflows

Active player of Teamfight Tactics

For this role, you ll find success through craft expertise, a collaborative spirit, and decision-making that prioritizes the delight of players. We will be looking at your past studies, experience, and your personal relationship with games. If you embody player empathy and care about players experiences, this could be your role!
