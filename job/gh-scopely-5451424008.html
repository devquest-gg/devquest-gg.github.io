Social Media Manager (TikTok Specialist)

Scopely · Mexico City

Mid Marketing Hybrid Posted 2026-10-21 Full time
Apply on Scopely’s site →

Scopely is looking for a Social Media Manager (TikTok Specialist) to join our Pokémon GO! Team in Mexico City on a hybrid basis, working 3 days a week from the hub.

At Scopely, we care deeply about what we do and want to inspire play, every day - whether in our work environments alongside our talented colleagues, or through our deep connections with our communities of players. We are a global team of game lovers who are developing, publishing and innovating the mobile games industry, connecting millions of people around the world daily.

What You Will Do

We’re looking for someone who is highly creative, quick-witted, and excited to experiment. You should be overflowing with social and short-form video ideas, comfortable moving quickly from concept to execution, and confident identifying cultural moments that Pokémon GO can authentically join.This is also a hands-on, on-camera role. You should feel comfortable and confident appearing in videos across Pokémon GO’s official social channels and be excited to help give the brand a relatable, engaging presence.

The ideal candidate is energized by interacting with players, not simply responsible for it. You’ll engage directly with the Pokémon GO community, develop content that speaks to regional and global audiences, and translate trends into ideas that feel unmistakably Pokémon GO.

You’ll need to be a strong self-starter who can manage a full workload, shift quickly between projects, and build and leverage AI tools and workflows to assist you in your day-to-day projects. If you’re a short-form video expert who can generate a steady stream of executable ideas and bring those ideas to life quickly—we want to hear from you.

Support the day-to-day planning and execution of content across Pokémon GO’s official social media channels, including copywriting, publishing, community engagement, performance tracking, and contributing to an energetic, authentic brand voice

Generate a consistent pipeline of creative, timely, and executable social content ideas, with a strong emphasis on short-form video. Identify promising video formats, trends, hooks, and cultural moments, then translate them into content that feels authentic to Pokémon GO and its community

Move quickly from idea to execution by independently developing, producing, and appearing in social video content for TikTok, Instagram Reels, YouTube Shorts, and other emerging platforms while remaining aligned with brand standards

Develop campaign, content, and video briefs that clearly communicate creative direction, messaging, format, and cultural relevance

Monitor trends and emerging digital behaviors, particularly those influencing Gen Z and Gen Alpha audiences

Lead player engagement across official Pokémon GO social channels, building meaningful connections with players and contributing an energetic, authentic brand voice.

Analyze social performance and player sentiment, using insights to improve content, engagement strategies, and future campaign planning.

Leverage agentic AI tools to improve daily workflows, identify trends, and elevate reporting

Independently manage multiple concurrent projects, reprioritizing and switching between assignments as business and cultural opportunities evolve. Operate with a high degree of ownership and minimal management oversight while maintaining strong communication and stakeholder alignment across global teams and asynchronous workflows

What We’re Looking For

3+ years of experience managing social media for a consumer brand

Expert-level understanding of short-form video, including TikTok, Instagram Reels, and YouTube Shorts

Experience independently creating social video content from concept through execution, and confidence appearing on camera as a regular part of the role

Excellent copywriting skills, with the ability to develop sharp, playful, timely, and culturally relevant social content. Strong understanding of Gen Z and Gen Alpha audiences, emerging behaviors, fandom dynamics, internet culture, and platform ecosystems

Capable of building and leveraging the latest AI tools to optimize your workflows

Genuine enthusiasm for engaging directly with online communities and building relationships with fans and players

A self-starter mentality, with demonstrated ability to manage a full workload and move rapidly between projects, priorities, and deadlines

Ability to work independently, make sound creative decisions, and deliver high-quality work with minimal oversight

Experience working with licensors or multiple brand stakeholders through creative review and approval processes

Strong English language skills are required for this role as we have a highly diverse and global business

Proficiency with social media management and listening tools such as Sprout Social, Sprinklr, or similar platforms

Ability to remain adaptable, solutions-oriented, and effective in a fast-paced environment with changing priorities and limited advance notice

Experience collaborating effectively through meetings, written communication, and asynchronous workflows

Bonus Points

Social media management experience within gaming, entertainment, technology, or youth culture

Familiarity with Pokémon GO, the Pokémon franchise, mobile gaming, or gaming communities

About Scopely

Scopely is a leading video game and global interactive entertainment company, home to many of the world’s most beloved and enduring experiences, including two of the most successful mobile games of all-time “MONOPOLY GO!” and “Pokémon GO,” along with “Stumble Guys,” “Star Trek™ Fleet Command,” “MARVEL Strike Force,” “WWE Champions,” the Scrabble® franchise, “Yahtzee® With Buddies,” and many others. Across mobile, web, PC, and console, Scopely creates, develops, publishes, and live-operates one of the most diversified and award-winning portfolios in the games industry — bringing hundreds of millions of players together through a shared love of play.

Founded in 2011, Scopely is powered by its exceptional team — including thousands of world-class gamemakers around the globe, a distinctive tenet-driven culture, and its proprietary technology platform, Playgami. Together, these strengths have fueled Scopely’s position as the #1 mobile games company in the U.S. and #2 globally, generating more than $10 billion in lifetime revenue. Whether building global sensations like “MONOPOLY GO!” from the ground up, or expanding through strategic acquisitions, including the FoxNext, GSN, and Scopely Explore games businesses — Scopely consistently delivers experiences players love today and return to for years to come.

Recognized multiple times as one of the "100 Most Influential Companies in the World" by TIME magazine and one of Fast Company s "World s Most Innovative Companies" and “Best Workplaces for Innovators,” Scopely believes that video games can be a force for good — creating meaningful connections, vibrant communities, and making life better through play.

Scopely has global operations and partners across four continents in more than a dozen countries worldwide. For more information, visit: https://www.scopely.com/ .
