Motion Capture Specialist

CD Projekt Red · Warsaw

Senior Animation Posted 2026-11-02 Full time

CD PROJEKT RED is looking for a Motion Capture Specialist to join our team in Warsaw. In this hands-on role, you'll be involved in every stage of the motion capture process, from preparing and running recording sessions to processing captured data. You'll work closely with our teams to help bring characters and performances to life in our games.

You'll also help maintain and improve our motion capture technology, tools, and workflows, contributing directly to the quality of our animations. We're looking for someone who enjoys combining technical problem-solving with creative collaboration and is eager to keep learning and improving the way we work.

Daily Responsibilities Prepare, set up, and operate motion capture equipment for recording sessions.

Support and coordinate motion capture shoots, working closely with internal teams to meet production needs.

Process motion capture data, including tracking, solving, retargeting, and preparing data for further animation work.

Maintain and troubleshoot motion capture hardware, software, and studio equipment.

Identify and resolve technical issues during recording sessions and post-production.

Contribute to improving our motion capture pipeline, tools, and scripts to support efficient workflows.

Collaborate with animators, technical teams, and other disciplines to ensure high-quality motion capture data.

At least 3 years of professional experience working with motion capture systems, such as Vicon, OptiTrack, or Qualisys.

Experience working with motion capture in a production environment.

Familiarity with Autodesk MotionBuilder or Maya.

Attention to detail and the ability to troubleshoot technical challenges during recording sessions.

Good organisational skills and the ability to adapt to changing production needs.

Strong communication and collaboration skills, including working with performers and production teams.

Openness to feedback, sharing knowledge, and continuously improving workflows.

Working proficiency in English, both spoken and written.

Nice to Have Basic understanding of timecode synchronisation.

Experience working with game engines such as Unreal Engine or Unity.

Basic scripting skills (e.g., Python, HSL, or JavaScript) or a willingness to develop them.

Additional scripting or technical skills that could help improve motion capture tools and workflows.

How to Apply Please attach your CV and cover letter in English to your application. We'd love to hear about your experience with motion capture, the tools you've worked with, and what interests you about joining our team.

A portfolio or examples of relevant work are welcome but not required.
