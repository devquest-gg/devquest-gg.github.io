Engineer Manager (Unity)

Scopely is looking for a Engineering Manager (Unity) to join the WWE Champions team in Bangalore!

At Scopely, we care deeply about what we do and want to inspire play, every day - whether in our work environments alongside our talented colleagues, or through our deep connections with our communities of players. We are a global team of game lovers who are developing, publishing and innovating the mobile games industry, connecting millions of people around the world daily.

WWE Champions is our ultimate and unique RPG puzzle match game featuring WWE superstars and legends across mobile and PC platforms. WWE Champions is the most downloaded WWE game to date, with a highly engaged and passionate community.

What You’ll Do

As an Engineering Manager, you will lead a team responsible for building and maintaining key client systems for the Unity game. You will support engineers working across gameplay, live events, UI, monetization, platform integrations, and client infrastructure, while ensuring strong execution, healthy team dynamics, and sustainable engineering practices in a live-service mobile game environment. This role is ideal for a leader who can combine delivery ownership, people management, and technical partnership in a complex production codebase.

Lead and support a team of engineers building and maintaining player-facing client systems in a large Unity/C# mobile game codebase.

Drive execution across feature development, live-ops support, bug fixing, technical improvements, and release readiness.

Partner with product, design, QA, analytics, art, and technical leadership to plan work, manage priorities, and deliver high-quality outcomes.

Work closely with senior engineers and technical leads to shape implementation plans, reduce risk, and improve system maintainability.

Help the team navigate the challenges of a live-service environment, including operational deadlines, cross-team dependencies, and production issues.

Coach and develop engineers through feedback, career growth, performance management, and day-to-day support.

Improve team health and effectiveness through better planning, estimation, communication, development processes, and engineering standards.

Support incident response, production issue triage, and continuous improvement across quality, stability, and delivery practices.

What We’re Looking For

Experience Technical Skills

6+ years of software engineering experience, with 2+ years of people management or technical team leadership experience

Experience working on Unity-based products, game clients, or other complex interactive applications is strongly preferred

Strong understanding of software engineering fundamentals, client architecture, and large-scale production development practices

Ability to partner effectively with senior engineers on system design, technical tradeoffs, and execution planning

Experience leading teams that ship features in live-service, mobile, or operationally complex product environments

Familiarity with performance, quality, testing, build/release processes, and production stability concerns

Comfortable working with multi-system feature delivery involving gameplay, UI, platform services, monetization, and live content

Technical credibility to guide discussions, unblock engineers, and make sound prioritization decisions without needing to be the primary implementer

Cross-Functional and Domain Knowledge

Experience supporting live-service development, roadmap planning, and event-driven delivery cycles

Understanding of game feature development such as progression, economy, monetization, social systems, live events, or PvP is a plus

Strong cross-functional leadership skills and ability to align teams around scope, priorities, and outcomes

Able to balance short-term delivery pressure with long-term technical health and team sustainability

Soft Skills Education

Bachelor’s degree in Computer Science or a related field, or equivalent practical experience

Strong communication, collaboration, and organizational leadership skills

Demonstrated ability to coach, mentor, and grow engineers across multiple levels

Ownership mindset, sound judgment, and calm decision-making in a fast-moving environment

Passion for games, team development, and building strong engineering organizations

About Scopely

Scopely is a leading video game and global interactive entertainment company, home to many of the world’s most beloved and enduring experiences, including two of the most successful mobile games of all-time “MONOPOLY GO!” and “Pokémon GO,” along with “Stumble Guys,” “Star Trek™ Fleet Command,” “MARVEL Strike Force,” “WWE Champions,” the Scrabble® franchise, “Yahtzee® With Buddies,” and many others. Whether building global sensations like “MONOPOLY GO!” from the ground up, or expanding through strategic acquisitions, including the FoxNext, GSN, and Scopely Explore games businesses — Scopely consistently delivers experiences players love today and return to for years to come.

Recognized multiple times as one of the "100 Most Influential Companies in the World" by TIME magazine and one of Fast Company s "World s Most Innovative Companies" and “Best Workplaces for Innovators,” Scopely believes that video games can be a force for good — creating meaningful connections, vibrant communities, and making life better through play.
