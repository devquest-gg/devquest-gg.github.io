Job Overview

Wargaming Kyiv is looking for an experienced VFX Artist to work on the legendary project World of Tanks. Our team is focused on becoming a reference point for art and technology in game real-time visual effects creation. This position provides a wide range of opportunities for skill development, artistic and career growth.

What will you do?

Design and implement high-quality visual effects (e.g., snow, rain, fire, smoke, explosions, and other environmental effects) to support story, art direction, and gameplay.

Handle technical setup, integration, optimization, and troubleshooting of effects in our internal real-time engine.

Manage VFX performance (particle and overdraw budgets, LODs, profiling on min-spec hardware).

Work closely with the VFX Lead, Art Director, Design, Engineering, and Tech Art.

Improve the VFX pipeline and tools, including AI-assisted workflows.

Be an active participant in team art discussions, critiques, and reviews.

What are we looking for?

6+ years of experience as a VFX Artist in game development.

Experience leading a team or a group of artists, including mentoring and giving feedback.

Strong experience with real-time effects editors (UE5 Niagara, Unity VFX Graph, or similar).

High proficiency with Houdini for simulations and VFX asset creation.

Good skills in modeling, UV mapping, and animation in any 3D package (3ds Max, Maya, Blender, etc.).

Excellent skills in creating and adjusting the full spectrum of VFX textures in Photoshop or another 2D package.

Clear understanding of rendering and real-time performance.

Proactive attitude, excellent communicator, with ability to self-manage and meet deadlines.

Analytical approach to solving tasks and bugs.

English at B1 level or higher.

A portfolio or showreel with breakdowns.

Passion for video games.

What additional skills will help you stand out?

Composition and lighting, photography.

Experience writing HLSL/GLSL shaders or node-based materials.

Knowledge of EmberGen, Substance Designer, scripting.

Work mode

Remote (from anywhere within Ukraine).

This role isn t eligible for relocation immigration support.

About Wargaming

Wargaming is an award-winning online game developer and publisher headquartered in Nicosia, Cyprus. Operating since 1998, Wargaming has become one of the leaders in the gaming industry with 15 offices worldwide, including studios in Chicago, Prague, Shanghai, Tokyo, and Vilnius. Our diverse and multicultural team works together to deliver a top-class experience to millions of players who enjoy Wargaming s titles across all major gaming platforms. Our flagship products include free-to-play hits World of Tanks, World of Warships and World of Tanks Blitz.
