Lead Game Designer

Frima Studio · Québec

Lead Design Posted 2026-06-30 Full time
Apply on Frima Studio’s site →

What your weeks are going to look like Determine and implement the most appropriate technical and conceptual approaches to achieve the project’s vision while adhering to production constraints (technical, budgetary, and time-related); Organize and lead regular meetings with the team to monitor progress, anticipate risks, and verify adherence to deadlines; Monitor the development of deliverables and ensure that standards for quality, consistency, and playability are met; Motivate, guide, and support the direct team by providing coaching through ongoing feedback; Build a relationship of trust with the partner and serve as the primary point of contact within your area of expertise; Participate in project reviews and post-mortems to continuously improve processes and deliverables; Collaborate with the studio-wide Design team to develop and share best design practices.

What we're looking for Bachelor’s degree in video game design or another degree related to the position, combined with experience deemed equivalent; A minimum of five (5) years of experience in game design; Experience as a Lead Game Designer or in a similar role; Proficiency with the Unreal Engine; Excellent understanding of the synergy between game mechanics, narrative, and visuals; Excellent knowledge of the various stages involved in creating a video game and the technical constraints specific to the video game industry; Bilingual (English/French); Willing to travel internationally as needed.

&#xa0; What we value in our team Ability to anticipate and manage risks throughout the development process; Strong organizational and prioritization skills to ensure projects are delivered on time; Excellent communication and interdisciplinary collaboration skills; Demonstrated curiosity about new technologies; Proven strong team and project leadership skills; Ability to motivate and mentor; Strong analytical and synthesis skills, and the ability to communicate ideas clearly; Excellent critical eye for a finished product; Ability to question assumptions; Diplomacy, patience, resourcefulness, organization, and attention to detail.
