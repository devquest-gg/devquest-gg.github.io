Sound Designer Intern

Gameloft · Ho Chi Minh City

Entry Audio Posted 2026-11-18 Intern

WHAT YOU WILL BE WORKING ON What you will focus on Support the Audio Team in creating, editing, implementing, and managing audio assets for games, including sound effects and voice-over recordings. The internship provides hands-on experience in game audio production, audio asset management, and opportunities to explore AI-assisted tools and workflows.

What it means on a daily basis Assist in creating, editing, and implementing sound effects and audio assets for games.

Support audio integration using game engines and audio middleware.

Manage, edit, and maintain large libraries of voice-over (VO) recordings and other audio assets, ensuring proper organization and file management.

Organize and maintain audio libraries, project files, and production documentation.

Assist with audio testing, troubleshooting, and quality assurance.

Collaborate with designers, programmers, and the audio team on project requirements.

Explore new audio technologies, tools, and workflows, including AI-assisted production.

Who you would be working with Audio Team, Game Designers, Artists, Programmers, Composers Where can this role take you? Understanding of sound design fundamentals, audio editing, and asset management.

Passion for video games, interactive audio, and emerging technologies.

Familiarity with Unity, Unreal Engine, Wwise, or FMOD is a plus.

Portfolio or demo reel showcasing audio-related work is encouraged.
