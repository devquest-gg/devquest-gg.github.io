ABOUT VOODOO

Founded in 2013, Voodoo is a tech company that creates mobile games and apps with a mission to entertain the world. With 800 employees, 7 billion downloads, and over 200 million active users, Voodoo is the #3 mobile publisher worldwide in downloads, behind Google and Meta. Our portfolio includes games like Mob Control and Block Jam, as well as apps like BeReal and Wizz.

TEAM

Our Legal team is part of Voodoo's Corporate functions, alongside M&A and Finance. It covers regulatory, compliance, commercial, IP, and litigation matters, across the gaming and social media verticals.

ROLE

6-month internship (mandatory internship/apprenticeship agreement as part of your Master's degree curriculum), within the Legal team, reporting to the Head of Legal - Regulatory. You will work alongside the lawyers in charge of regulatory compliance, on concrete matters for products used by over 200 million people.

RESPONSIBILITIES

- Records of processing and DPIAs: help maintain compliance documentation and the records of processing activities, and support data protection impact assessments.

- Substantive work: work on all various data related topics across the verticals of gaming and social media (new features, AB tests, etc.).

- Regulatory monitoring: track developments in GDPR and US data protection laws (CCPA, COPPA...).

- Documentation: draft and update privacy policies, notices, and internal training materials.

- Data subject rights: handle requests to exercise rights (access, erasure, objection).

- Regulator relations: support communications with the CNIL and the EDPB, and follow up on ongoing matters.

- Digital regulation: games and platforms.

PROFILE

- Master's student (M2) in Law (Digital Law, Data Protection Law, Business Law or equivalent), looking for a final-year, gap-year internship of 6 months

- Prior experience in tech law or data protection (internship, apprenticeship) is a plus

- Strong command of GDPR

- Strong knowledge of US data protection laws (CCPA, VCDPA, COPPA)

- Strong interest in digital regulation

- Strong interest in tech and gaming

- Rigorous, autonomous, able to summarize complex topics

- Good level of English, essential in an international environment

BENEFITS

- Swile, meal vouchers

- Gymlib fully covered by Voodoo

- Wellness activities in our Paris office

- Remote days
