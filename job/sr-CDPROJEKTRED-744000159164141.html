Social Media Video Editor

CD Projekt Red · Warsaw

Senior Marketing Posted 2026-11-02 Full time
Apply on CD Projekt Red’s site →

CD PROJEKT RED is looking for a creative and versatile Social Media Video Editor to join our Video Post-Production team in Warsaw. In this role, you'll create dynamic, platform-ready video content tailored to our global social media channels, with a strong focus on short-form and mobile-first formats (TikTok, Instagram Reels, YouTube Shorts, and Stories) across our games and franchises.

Working closely with Social Media Managers, Community teams, Brand Directors, Motion Designers, and Game Capture Artists, you'll combine strong editing skills with hands-on experience across different areas of video production — from gameplay capture and motion design to sound editing and fast-turnaround versioning. You'll help create engaging social content that captures attention and connects with our community worldwide.

Daily Responsibilities Edit engaging, high-impact short-form videos tailored for mobile and social media platforms, adapting content to different formats (9:16, 1:1, 16:9) with a focus on strong opening hooks, dynamic pacing, readable on-screen text, and platform-specific trends.

Translate creative briefs, campaign objectives, and community trends into video concepts, simple scripts, shot lists, and engaging content delivered within short turnaround times.

Create and adapt motion graphics, including animated typography, captions, and UI overlays, while handling basic color correction, sound effects, music editing, and voice-over balancing.

Capture gameplay footage directly from game builds using internal tools, including specific gameplay moments, b-roll, character sequences, and custom shots for social media content.

Prepare and adapt videos for different social media platforms, including subtitles, localized on-screen text, safe zones, aspect ratios, and export settings, ensuring consistency with platform requirements and CD PROJEKT RED's branding standards.

Collaborate closely with Social Media, Community, Brand, and Marketing teams, incorporating feedback and staying up to date with social media trends, editing styles, meme culture, and emerging video formats.

At least 3 years of professional experience in video editing, preferably focused on short-form social media content, entertainment marketing, agency work, or branded digital content.

Strong proficiency in industry-standard editing software, such as Adobe Premiere Pro and/or DaVinci Resolve.

Good knowledge of CD PROJEKT RED's games, characters, lore, and universes.

Strong understanding of mobile-first storytelling, engaging opening hooks, pacing, audience retention, and platform conventions across TikTok, Instagram Reels, and YouTube Shorts.

Working knowledge of After Effects, including animated typography, UI animations, templates, and motion graphics.

Practical experience with dialogue cleanup, sound design, music editing, basic color correction, and audio optimization for mobile platforms.

Ability to independently manage video projects from brief to delivery, work within tight deadlines, and maintain high quality and brand consistency.

Very good English skills, both written and spoken, for daily collaboration with international teams.

Genuine passion for video games, social media trends, and internet culture.

Nice to have Previous experience capturing gameplay footage using game engines or internal development builds.

Familiarity with smartphone videography, lighting, or basic on-set shooting.

Experience managing or growing brand or gaming-focused social media channels.

Please include a link to your portfolio or showreel showcasing examples of video content you've created.
