Business Analytics Intern

Wildlife Studios · São Paulo

Entry Data & Analytics Hybrid Posted 2026-04-23 Intern

Ready to take your first steps in the mobile gaming industry? We re looking for a Business Analytics Intern to join our User Acquisition / Marketing team at Wildlife Studios.

User Acquisition (UA) is about bringing players into our worlds — and doing it smarter every day. We are a highly analytical, data-driven team that uses technology and creativity to find and engage the right players across global platforms like Meta, Google, and TikTok. If you re curious, enjoy working with numbers, and want to learn in a fast-paced environment, this is where gaming and growth collide.

What you ll do

Performance Analysis: Monitor and analyze user acquisition campaigns, helping identify optimization opportunities.

Creative Testing: Support the design and evaluation of A/B tests, exploring different approaches to engage audiences.

Project Prioritization: Develop your ability to use good judgment to solve problems and drive impact in a dynamic environment.

Cross-functional Collaboration: Work alongside different teams, supporting the communication of results and alignment with key stakeholders.

What you ll need

Currently pursuing a Bachelor s degree in Engineering, Computer Science, Business, or a related field, with expected graduation between July 2027 and December 2027 .

Strong analytical mindset, organizational skills, and clear communication.

Interest or foundational knowledge in programming — SQL, R, or Python is a plus.

Nice to have

Passion for the mobile gaming industry.

Familiarity with performance marketing or data analysis concepts.

Exposure to testing methodologies such as A/B testing, even in an academic context.

More about you

Proactive and curious — you don t wait for all the answers to be handed to you.

A good communicator who is eager to learn how to influence through data and reasoning.

Comfortable working in fast-paced environments and able to manage your own priorities.

Detail-oriented, even when juggling multiple tasks at once.

About Wildlife

Wildlife is one of the leading mobile game developers and publishers in the world. We have released more than 60 titles, reaching billions of people around the globe. Today, we have offices in Brazil, Argentina, Ireland, and the United States. Here, we create games that will excite, intrigue, and engage our players for years to come!
