ABOUT OUR GAME

Taonga: the Island Farm http://taongafarm.com/ is an immersive exploration and adventure game set on a colorful tropical archipelago.

What begins as a casual farming experience gradually evolves into a rich, multi-layered adventure. Players discover increasing depth through travel, story-driven quests, strategic battles, engaging mini-games, competitive events, and much more—transforming the game into a captivating midcore adventure with sophisticated mechanics.

We are looking for a talented 3d Artist to join our marketing team!

Responsibilities

- Full-cycle creation of stylized low-poly environment assets—including landscapes, vegetation, buildings, props, and decorative objects—based on concept art:

- high-poly and low-poly modeling, retopology;

- creation of optimized UV layouts;

- creation of model textures and environment surface textures.

- Maintaining a consistent visual style across the project.

- Creating and maintaining internal asset libraries.

Requirements

- Strong modeling skills in Autodesk Maya or Blender.

- At least 2 years of experience as a 3D Artist or in a similar role.

- A portfolio showcasing environment and vegetation models.

- Experience creating stylized low-poly environment assets.

- Solid understanding of composition, form, color, and lighting.

- Proficiency in Substance 3D Painter.

- Basic hand-painted texturing skills.
