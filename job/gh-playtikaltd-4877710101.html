The Indirect Tax Senior will report to the Indirect Tax Manager/ VP of Tax and will play a critical role in managing the Company’s growing global Indirect Tax footprint. This is a great opportunity for someone who enjoys working in an extremely dynamic, diverse and fast-paced environment and who is looking to grow themselves as an Indirect Tax professional.

Main responsibilities:

Review reporting and compliance obligations for Company’s growing indirect tax (i.e., VAT, GST and other similar taxes) footprint

Coordinate with third-party advisors and compliance teams globally to oversee compliance requirements in the jurisdictions where we operate

Perform indirect tax research on existing and new business offerings

Manage notice resolution process with third-party advisors

Support Indirect Tax Manager with various remediation workstreams to address historical exposures

Review Company’s various tax engines (Vertex, Stripe) to ensure tax is correctly applied to customer sales

Prepare VAT account reconciliations on a quarterly basis for Indirect Tax Manager review

Interface with internal e-payments team, finance department, and external payment processors to address data needs

Serve as a point of contact in conjunction with the Indirect Tax Manager for indirect tax guidance on strategic business initiatives

Support indirect tax manager/ local controllers to address local indirect tax requirements

Coordinate/ communicate with income tax team and local finance departments for global planning and reporting

Main requirements:

Bachelor’s degree in Accounting or related field

2-3 years of relevant Indirect tax experience in Big 4 and/or multi-national public company (emphasis on experience with managing compliance workflows)

Advanced Excel skills

Excellent research and writing skills

Tax research skills in Checkpoint and/or BNA

CPA and/or Master’s degree in Business Taxation is preferred
