Brand Manager IBIT – VALORANT

Riot Games · Barcelona

Mid Marketing Posted 2026-07-16
Apply on Riot Games’s site →

As a Brand Manager III for VALORANT IBIT, you will lead the strategic and tactical delivery of publishing across multiple markets, translating VALORANT’s global vision into a cohesive, multi‑market strategy that drives deep player engagement and meaningful business results. You will act as the senior publishing leader for your region, connecting product direction, marketing strategy, and esports delivery to create authentic and impactful experiences for players.

You will be accountable for defining and executing publishing strategies that balance global consistency with local relevance, ensuring that each market’s cultural and competitive context is reflected in how we show up. Partnering closely with global and regional product leadership, you will influence product priorities, shape multi‑market go‑to‑market plans, and guide execution across campaigns, creator partnerships, esports integrations, and community initiatives.

Operating at both a strategic and tactical level, you will lead cross‑functional teams, manage significant budgets, and act as the primary advocate for your markets to global leadership. Your ability to connect insight‑driven strategy with high‑impact execution will be critical in growing VALORANT’s presence, deepening community connection, and delivering on both player and business outcomes.

Reporting to the Director of Publishing and Product Management, EMEA, the Brand Manager III IBIT will be based in Barcelona (Spain).

Responsibilities:

Develop and own a cohesive multi‑market publishing strategy for your region that delivers against global product goals and local player needs

Translate global product priorities into tailored market plans that maximize player engagement and business impact

Act as the primary publishing voice for your region in global product discussions, shaping product and publishing decisions with regional insight

Lead integrated go‑to‑market plans that connect product beats, esports moments, creator activations, and cultural opportunities

Oversee large‑scale budgets, making data‑driven investment decisions across multiple markets

Build, guide, and align cross‑disciplinary teams across publishing, esports, community, creative, and commercial functions

Track and assess performance across campaigns and markets, applying insights to continuously optimize strategy and execution

Cultivate strong relationships with regional partners, media, agencies, and key influencers to amplify VALORANT’s impact across your cluster

Required Qualifications:

7+ years in publishing, marketing, or product management, preferably in games, entertainment, or tech

Proven experience leading high‑impact, multi‑market campaigns from strategic planning through to execution

Demonstrated ability to influence product direction through market insight and player advocacy

Strong leadership skills with experience managing cross‑functional and distributed teams

Deep understanding of FPS gaming culture and the VALORANT player community

Exceptional communication skills, fluent in Spanish English

Strong budget ownership experience with a track record of maximizing ROI across multiple markets

Desired Qualifications:

Experience driving publishing strategy for major game launches or sustained live service products across multiple countries

Proven success integrating esports, live events, and cultural moments into holistic brand strategies

Strong analytical and strategic planning skills, with the ability to translate complex insights into actionable publishing tactics

Experience mentoring and developing other publishing professionals

For this role, you ll find success through craft expertise and a collaborative spirit that prioritizes the delight of players. We will look at your past studies and experience, but for this role, we also look for dedicated people with a personal relationship with games. If you embody player empathy and care about players experiences, this is the role for you!
