Senior Investment Designer

Do you dream about creating worlds imbued with real value and consequence? Can you imagine reward chases that excite and delight players without feeling like chores? Can you devise ways for players to meaningfully express themselves? If you answered yes to these questions, Bungie wants you on our team as our newest Senior Investment Designer.

RESPONSIBILITIES

Collaborate with an interdisciplinary team to create a wide variety of pursuit and reward-based systems content that complement Destiny’s activities

Perform advanced scripting and tool work

Design and create sustainable systems content that excite and engage players

Design creative additions and improvements for existing Rewards and Commerce systems

Tune gameplay values to create compelling moments and a satisfying progression curve

REQUIRED SKILLS

Professional experience as a designer

Experience working with game development software and tools

Experience building rewards, player customization, and MTX systems

Excellent written and verbal communication skills

Experience owning features or leading small teams

Desire to work as part of a large team and ability to take feedback and direction appropriately

NICE-TO-HAVE SKILLS

Technical experience writing text-based scripts to manage game systems (scripting in a Lua, Python, or similar language)

Multiple shipped game systems within the console or pc space

Experience working on games as a service/live content
