Join us and be part of the play! Position Overview

We’re looking for a Mobile Engineer Intern to join our Game Development team. You ll work with senior developers on our internal game development tools and libraries, and contribute to the efficiency of our game production pipeline. This is hands-on engineering work with real ownership, and we expect a high standard from day one.

What You ll Do

Develop and improve internal libraries together with other teams.

Implement scoped tasks end to end, from understanding the problem to delivering a reviewed, tested solution.

Write short technical design documents before implementing, to guide your own work.

Profile, debug and optimize on real iOS and Android devices.

Work on third-party integrations and platform-specific functionality.

Improve technical documentation and training resources.

Work with people from different teams and areas, and keep them informed of progress and blockers.

Take part in code reviews, giving and receiving direct feedback.

What You Bring

Strong C# and programming fundamentals: data structures, algorithms and object-oriented design.

Proficiency in Unity3D, demonstrated by at least one complete project we can see (published, or a repository we can read).

Working knowledge of computer math (vectors, matrices, quaternions) and 2D/3D graphics.

Effective debugging: you find root causes rather than patching symptoms.

Disciplined coding habits and awareness of industry best practices.

Very good interpersonal and communication skills, and the ability to collaborate inside a group of senior developers.

Bonus

Having built and run your own app or game on iOS and/or Android devices.

Experience with performance analysis tools (Unity Profiler, Xcode Instruments, Android Studio Profiler).

Experience integrating a third-party SDK.

Knowledge of Swift/Objective-C or Kotlin/Java.
