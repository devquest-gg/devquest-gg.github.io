Who We Are:

Take-Two Interactive Software, Inc. is a leading developer, publisher, and marketer of interactive entertainment for consumers around the globe. The Company develops and publishes products principally through Rockstar Games, 2K, Private Division, and Zynga. Our products are currently designed for console gaming systems, PC, and Mobile, including smartphones and tablets, and are delivered through physical retail, digital download, online platforms, and cloud streaming services. The Company’s common stock is publicly traded on NASDAQ under the symbol TTWO.

While our offices (physical and virtual) are casual and inviting, we are deeply committed to our core tenets of creativity, innovation and efficiency, and individual and team development opportunities. Our industry and business are continually evolving and fast-paced, providing numerous opportunities to learn and hone your skills. We work hard, but we also like to have fun, and believe that we provide a great place to come to work each day to pursue your passions.

The Challenge

We seek a self-motivated individual with demonstrated experience and awareness of Corporate Events, Hospitality operations. They must possess strong interpersonal and communication skills that will be used cross functionally to provide a high level of support in employee facing experiences by way of culinary service and event design. They must have a keen sense of “back of house” operations and a passion for hospitality, with a very high level of attention to detail. They will be responsible for elevating established employee engagement, food, set up, decor, and event pantry operations supporting the multiple studio locations, across the portfolio. This individual will also drive in-house employee event activities, while creating new and exciting opportunities to engage and drive office culture, within said Region. The ideal candidate is consistently reliable, proactive, a detail-oriented problem-solver, and overall positive team player.

What You’ll Take On

Food Beverage Operations

Coordinate end-to-end food and beverage programs including oversight of menu, pantry stocking, budgeting, sourcing, staff execution, reconciliation and usage reporting.

Arrange staff scheduled internal group meals, designing set up and overseeing process of breakdown and clean up

Maintain organization, inventory cleanliness of pantry and equipment inventory

Acts as main point of contact for multiple locations, Regionally, for in-house food service vendor

Act as Regional expert on all things food beverage related

Ensure budget and food service safety is being met and adhered to

Continuously review vendor agreements for Scope deliverables, spend accuracy and optimization.

Event Operations Support

Conceptualize and execute innovative and creative in-house event programming including monthly employee social hours, webinars, Town Halls and ERG programming and support the roll out of said programming, including internal communications via slack, EPOCH, outlook

Maintain master event database to include event venues, vendors, history of events, event costs, and images

Source catering venues and team on and offsite

Assist with white glove requests as they arise, Regionally

Assist with expenses and accounting, including but not limited to monthly budget credit card expense reconciliation, vendor invoicing, payroll/tax compliance tracking reports and Certificates of Insurance, as required by location

Oversight of physical event inventory storage management

Assist with administrative duties related to vendor relationships including meeting scheduling, contract management with Legal, and tracking payment scheduling with AP/Finance

Work alongside team members on developing local guides, while liaising with Regional Local offices to leverage for a more efficient, sustainable cost-saving event planning and execution

Update team calendars support other initiatives as requested

Employee Gifting Merchandise

Support the design and implementation of the internal online merchandise store for employees and partners

Assist with the logistics of the sourcing and distribution of employee swag holiday gifts

Order other merchandise as requested by internal teams

What You Bring

A strong candidate for a Senior Coordinator, Corporate Events Hospitality role should bring a combination of hands-on event experience, strong organisation and polished guest service.

Relevant event experience: A track record of coordinating corporate events, executive dinners, conferences and VIP hospitality, from planning through on-site delivery.

Excellent attention to detail: Confidence managing guest lists, invitations, RSVPs, itineraries, rooming lists and transport arrangements.

A hospitality mindset: The ability to anticipate guest needs, manage dietary and accessibility requirements, and deliver a thoughtful, professional experience.

Supplier and venue management: Experience sourcing and coordinating UK venues, hotels, caterers, production partners and transport providers, with commercial awareness when reviewing proposals.

Budget discipline: Comfort tracking costs, raising purchase orders, reconciling invoices and keeping accurate records.

Strong stakeholder communication: Clear, timely communication with colleagues, executive assistants, senior leaders and external partners.

Ownership and sound judgement: The ability to manage several workstreams, resolve issues calmly and recognise when to escalate decisions.

Discretion: Confidence handling confidential event plans, executive travel details and personal guest information.

Practical systems skills: Proficiency in Microsoft and Google Suites, and event registration or guest management platforms.

Flexibility: Willingness to travel within London, and work evenings or weekends when events require it.
