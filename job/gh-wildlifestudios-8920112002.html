About The Role

We re looking for a talented and passionate Senior FP A Analyst to join Wildlife s FP A Team in São Paulo. We’re looking for an FP A analyst to act as a business partner to key leaders: run financial routines, analyses and reports, guiding how we allocate resources, and finding savings and efficiency opportunities, with ownership and a mindset that goes beyond the obvious.

What you’ll do

Own the FP A cycle (budgeting, forecasting, 1-Year and 3-Year Plans, results tracking) as Finance Business Partner for Operations and Engineering.

Lead cross-functional processes with partner teams: Cloud Economics (IT Infrastructure), Corporate Technology (Licenses) and Payroll (People Costs).

Analyze financial and accounting results in depth and turn them into actionable insights for the executive team and investors.

Track the execution of company strategy in the financials, the impact of new decisions, risks and opportunities.

Own the Working Capital and Income Taxes models, and drive efficiency initiatives;

Contribute with AI automation projects (Closing analyses and rolling forecasts).

What you’ll need

Bachelor’s degree in Engineering, Economics or a related field;

Experience in finance and/or cost management;

Advanced Excel / Google Sheets;

Advanced English;

Excellent analytical, problem-solving, and critical thinking skills;

Curiosity about AI and interest in using it to automate and improve financial processes
