ABOUT US

Scorewarrior is a game developer and publisher based in Limassol, Cyprus.

We are working on Total Battle — a cross-platform MMO 4X strategy game with a deep social system and extensive player interaction. We have 2 million monthly active players and continue to grow with the goal of becoming the #1 game in the genre.

Our team consists of 200+ passionate, talented, self-driven, and continually developing professionals who love making high-quality games.

ABOUT THE POSITION

We are looking for a Product Analyst to join our Gameplay team.

You will partner closely with game designers to support data-driven decisions and shape features for our most experienced and demanding player segment. The focus of the role is on late-game content and live operations, including recurring seasonal mechanics and large-scale in-game events.

You will be responsible for designing analytical approaches, conducting product research, and evaluating player behavior and feature performance in complex, long-lived systems with strong social and retention dynamics.

WHAT YOU WILL BE DOING

- Analyze engagement, retention, progression, and monetization of late-game features (seasonal kingdoms, holiday events, battle passes, etc.)

- Design and validate product metrics and analytical events for new and iterated features

- Plan, run, and interpret product experiments (quasi-experiments, before/after analyses)

- Conduct product research to understand player behavior, motivations, and pain points in mature segments

- Develop and maintain dashboards, reports, and data marts to support decision-making within the Old Players team

- Perform ad-hoc analyses to answer product and business questions

- Communicate insights and recommendations clearly to designers and stakeholders

REQUIRED QUALIFICATIONS

- Strong SQL skills

- Experience with BI tools (e.g. Tableau or equivalents)

- Proven experience in product analytics and product research

- Ability to design analytical events and metrics aligned with product goals

- Strong analytical thinking and ability to translate data into actionable insights

- Good communication skills and experience working closely with product and design teams

- Confident knowledge of mathematical statistics and probability theory

PREFERRED QUALIFICATIONS

- Hands-on experience with ClickHouse

- Experience analyzing long-lived or live-ops features in games

- Experience working with network effects, social mechanics, or competitive systems

- Understanding of F2P game economics and progression systems

- Experience working with large player bases and complex event-driven systems

- Hands-on experience with causal inference, A/B testing, and quasi-experimental methods
