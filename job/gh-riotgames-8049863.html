Technical Producer II - Publishing Platform, Commerce

Riot Games · Los Angeles

Mid Production $150K–$210K Posted 2026-07-21 Full time
Apply on Riot Games’s site →

Technical Producers at Riot lead passionate technical teams to create products and experiences that achieve maximum player and fan value. They guide development, clear blockers, create processes, and push projects across the finish line while making sure that the teams are sustainable and have a great collaborative environment.

As a Technical Producer II for the Commerce Initiative teams on Publishing Platform, you will be the custodian of strong delivery processes for a team that uses a mix of internal development and external partners. You will report to the Senior Manager, Production and will partner with Product and Engineering Managers to drive efficiency, accuracy and accountability for planned and emergent initiatives. You will ensure stakeholder visibility as you track progress, deliver features, mitigate risks and help organize priorities. You will be responsible for ensuring roadmaps are accurate, delivery meets quality expectations and team health is maintained.

Responsibilities:

Lead the production practice for the team.

Execute work systems, monitor team health, and drive efficiency to ensure team delivery success.

Gauge feasibility of product roadmap and partner with product and engineering leaders to mitigate risks, right-size product scope and establish realistic timelines.

Partner with the Product Lead, Engineering Manager, and Designers to help develop the technical product vision and execute the development strategy for the team.

Create and contribute ideas to areas beyond your immediate ownership across different teams and disciplines.

Identify dependencies with external teams and coordinate to ensure they are delivered on time.

Communicate team health and progress to the organization through regular reporting rituals.

Provide essential product feedback and insight to the team and leadership through playtesting and other feedback mechanisms

Manage stakeholder expectations through interpreting and communicating stages to invested audiences

Required Qualifications:

4+ years of experience leading development and delivery efforts for medium-to-large teams

Experience working with engineers to deliver technical features, specifically on services and tools

Experience working with teams, especially remote, across multiple disciplines, time zones, and cultures

Interest in developing deep understanding of Riot’s Games as a player

Bachelor s degree in Computer Science or a related field, or equivalent experience with strong technical knowledge and problem-solving skills.

Experience working on technical initiatives and digital product development with a capability to engage with customers and development teams in technical discussions

Experience leading teams through feature development using agile methodologies (planning, execution, delivery, post-release support)

Extremely organized with proficiency in Jira, Notion and other project management tools

Desired Qualifications:

Experience working in med-large size organizations where collaboration across organizational boundaries is critical to success.

Experience with Product and Design tools such as Miro and Figma

For this role, you ll find success through craft expertise, a collaborative spirit, and decision-making that prioritizes your fellow Rioters, who are the customers of your work. Being a dedicated fan of games is not necessary for this position!
