About the role

We’re looking for a proactive, organised Office Coordinator to help keep our Bristol office running smoothly. You’ll be a welcoming first point of contact for colleagues and visitors, and a dependable source of support for the wider team.

This is a varied, hands-on role for someone who enjoys helping others, spotting what needs doing and making the workplace a great place to be.

What you’ll do

Welcome staff and visitors and respond to everyday queries

Provide administrative support to the Senior Leadership Team and colleagues

Raise purchase orders, submit goods receipts and keep purchasing records accurate

Support business travel arrangements and general HR administration

Manage correspondence, records, reports and ad hoc administrative projects

Liaise with suppliers and building management

Order and monitor office supplies, and coordinate visitors and couriers

Organise catering and small internal events

Support office health, safety and security procedures

What we are looking for:

Experience in office coordination, reception, workplace operations or a similar customer-facing administrative role

Strong organisational skills and the ability to manage competing requests

A welcoming, service-minded approach to supporting colleagues and visitors

Confidence using everyday office systems and maintaining accurate records

Good judgement and discretion when handling confidential information

A practical, adaptable approach, with the confidence to take initiative
