CD PROJEKT RED is looking for an experienced Senior Research Manager with strong problem-solving skills to join our AI R&D team and help lead the development and delivery of solutions that will shape the next generation of immersive gaming experiences and tools. In this role, you will manage cross-functional projects that enhance user experience, game development pipelines, and our teams' creative capabilities.

Responsibilities Work closely with the AI Director in driving new major initiatives within the AI R&D Team.

Serve as a bridge between East Coast and Europe time zone across both the AI R&D teams and projects.

Align and motivate your team towards clearly defined goals. Form and nurture cohesive, high-performing teams that are empowered to self-organize and achieve outstanding results.

Unblock others. Identify and address bottlenecks and queues, foster incremental delivery.

Project manage AI initiatives, ensuring the resource, scope and time constraints are balanced. Identify risks and establish mitigation plans. Continuously look at improving production processes (project planning, scoping, scheduling, roadmaps, requirements gathering, etc…).

Foster and maintain relations with key external partners.

Advocate for actionable and beneficial improvements, challenging the status quo with bravery and innovation.

Gather, acknowledge, and respond to internal feedback, adjusting processes and approaches as necessary.

5+ years of software project management experience, with 3+ years in a leadership role.

Full cycle of an AI product, AI research or Game development. Ideally all.&#xa0; Demonstrated experience in leading cross-functional teams, preferably in an Agile environment, with a focus on team empowerment and self-organization.
