Legal Counsel

Easybrain · Limassol

Mid People & Ops Hybrid Posted 2026-07-16 Full time
Apply on Easybrain’s site →

Easybrain is a fast-growing international IT company building products used by millions of users worldwide. Now we're looking for an experienced In-House Legal Counsel to join the legal team in our Cyprus office. You'll be the go-to legal partner for a fast-moving product and business team - advising on everything from app store compliance to ad-tech contracts to IP protection, in an industry that moves faster than most legal frameworks anticipate.

Responsibilities:

- Provide practical, business-oriented legal advice across product, monetization, marketing, and HR teams - this is a generalist role with real ownership, not a narrow specialism

- Own GDPR and data protection compliance, including liaising with DPOs/regulators and keeping policies current as EU and international privacy law evolves

- Draft and maintain EULAs, Terms of Service, Privacy Policies, and internal policies

- Negotiate and draft commercial and tech-transactional agreements: publishing, business development, content licensing, distribution, and ad monetization network agreements

- Handle IP matters: trademarks, copyright infringement/enforcement letters, and protecting game IP across jurisdictions.

- Advise on app store policies (Apple App Store, Google Play) and platform-specific compliance risks

- Flag and mitigate legal risk proactively, before it becomes a business problem

Requirements:

- Law degree

- 5+ years of relevant experience, ideally with exposure to tech, gaming, or digital media

- Strong background in commercial/tech transactions, data protection, and IP

- Familiarity with legal issues specific to mobile apps and platforms (app store terms, ad networks) - a strong plus

- Excellent drafting, analytical, and negotiation skills

- Comfortable managing a high volume of concurrent matters independently, with sound judgment and minimal need for oversight

- Fluent English

- Strong digital literacy

Benefits:

Besides the engaging tasks, support from experienced colleagues, challenge, and drive, we offer:

- High-end market salary with performance bonuses

- All needed equipment

- Regular company events and monthly Friday meetings

- Social benefits (private medical cover, sports reimbursement, etc.)

- Paid vacations, sick days

- English, Greek, and Polish online language classes

- Reimbursement for education and professional development
