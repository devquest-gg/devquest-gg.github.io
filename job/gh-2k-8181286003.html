What we need:

31st Union is looking for a Senior Platform Performance Engineer to help support our AAA multiplatform Unreal Engine 5 title on consoles and PC with 60+ FPS Quality and 120+ FPS Performance modes. In this role, you will balance rendering (optimizing existing systems and developing novel approximations) and other platform considerations, such as memory, CPU, and streaming performance.

Responsibilities:

Profile and optimize GPU, CPU, memory utilization, and streaming on PS5, PS5 Pro, Xbox Series X|S, and minimum-specification PC.

Implement Build, Cook and Runtime systems to support multiplatform/multiresolution game content within memory and performance targets.

Partner with Rendering Engineering and Technical Art to define construction guidelines and scalability pipelines for 60+ FPS Quality and 120+ FPS Performance modes.

Implement platform and scalability-specific rendering features.

Resolve platform-specific technical requirements.

Required Qualifications:

Minimum 5+ years experience in AAA game development, including at least one shipped console title

Deep understanding of modern real-time rendering techniques and GPU hardware

Deep understanding of Unreal Engine 5 and underlying technologies

Ability to write, debug, and analyze shaders and pipelines using platform GPU performance and debugging tools

A shipped rendering optimization or approximation of your own design that you can walk us through in detail

Strong communication skills and ability to collaborate effectively with cross-functional teams

Relevant Experience:

Experience with PS5, PS5 Pro, and Xbox Series X|S development tools and platform requirements

Unreal Engine 5 optimization

Experience defining content budgets and standards with technical artists, such as LOD, asset validation, mesh and texture streaming, etc.
