Get to know The Pokémon Company International

The Pokémon Company International manages the Pokémon property outside of Asia and is responsible for brand management, licensing and marketing, the Pokémon Trading Card Game, the animated TV series, home entertainment, and the official Pokémon website. Pokémon was launched in Japan in 1996 and today is one of the most popular children’s entertainment properties in the world.

Get to Know the Role

Job Title: People Partner

Job Summary: A People Partner who coaches managers through performance, talent, and organizational challenges today while building the leadership capability, frameworks, tools, and operating practices that improve organizational effectiveness and create consistent talent outcomes tomorrow.

The People Partner partners with managers to build leadership capability, improve organizational effectiveness, and create consistent talent outcomes across the organization.

This role serves as a trusted advisor and coach to leaders, helping them navigate performance challenges, talent decisions, workforce planning, organizational change, and team effectiveness. Through coaching, consultation, and manager enablement, the People Partner helps leaders become increasingly capable and self-sufficient in leading their teams.

Working closely with Business Partners, Talent Partners, Talent Acquisition, Legal, and Centers of Excellence, the People Partner contributes to scalable frameworks, tools, and operating practices that improve manager effectiveness and strengthen organizational capability over time.

Success is measured by stronger manager effectiveness, improved talent outcomes, greater consistency in people decisions, increased manager self-sufficiency, and the development of scalable solutions that enable the organization to grow effectively.
