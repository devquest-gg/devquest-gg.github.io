Do you enjoy solving complex technical challenges and shaping the architecture of games played by millions? As a Principal Server Engineer at Snowprint, you are part of a game team, designing, developing and operating the game. You'll combine hands-on development with long-term technical vision. Everything you do impacts the player experience and how we build games.

At Snowprint Studios, we believe that kindness, collaboration, curiosity, and taking ownership are what make great games possible. We work in small, cross-disciplinary teams where people are trusted to take responsibility, share ideas openly, and continuously improve how we build games together.

What you’ll be doing Together with the team, you'll shape the architecture and technical direction of our backend systems, designing and implementing solutions that support both current game features and future needs. You'll take the lead on complex technical features.

You'll work on both player-facing features and the systems, tooling and infrastructure behind them. This includes evolving our existing architecture, identifying opportunities for improvement, and ensuring our backend solutions remain scalable, maintainable, efficient and secure as our games grow.

You'll also play an important role in the technical development of the team. Working closely with other developers, you'll share knowledge, challenge ideas, provide technical guidance and help establish good practices. You'll collaborate with designers and other disciplines to ensure that technical decisions support both development needs and the player experience.

Is this you? You're a highly experienced server/backend engineer with a strong background in designing, building, and operating complex backend systems. You're fluent in Kotlin and/or Java .

You have experience shaping backend architecture and making technical decisions that balance immediate requirements with long-term scalability and maintainability.

You're comfortable taking ownership of cloud infrastructure and have hands-on experience operating cloud environments.

You have extensive experience with live services and understand the demands of keeping a game running. You're comfortable troubleshooting production issues, adapting to changing priorities, and finding solutions when things don't go as planned.

You're a strong technical voice who enjoys sharing knowledge, challenging ideas, and helping others grow . You communicate clearly, collaborate well and can explain complex technical decisions to people from different disciplines.

Where you’ll work Our Stockholm studio is located in Södermalm, and we work in a hybrid setup. We work from the office on Tuesdays and Fridays to support collaboration and social interaction, while other days offer flexibility depending on where you work best. We provide the setup and tools you need to work comfortably both at home and in the studio.

About Snowprint Studios Snowprint Studios believes great games are created by teams of talented, kind and creative individuals in a friendly, supportive and inspiring environment - while maintaining a sustainable life-work balance. For more information on Snowprint Studios go to snowprintstudios.com and follow us on Facebook.

At Snowprint Studios, we embrace diversity both in the worlds we create and in our workplace. Join us in shaping the future of gaming!
