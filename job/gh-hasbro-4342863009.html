Content Specialist - D&D Beyond (Contract/Temporary)

Wizards of the Coast · Canada Remote · Remote

Mid Other Remote Posted 2026-08-01 Contractor
Apply on Wizards of the Coast’s site →

We take play seriously. We’re looking for curious adventurers ready to find their party, fueled by imagination and drive to build what’s never been built before. At Hasbro and Wizards of the Coast, you’ll collaborate with passionate teams to reimagine our iconic brands and create experiences that spark joy, connection, and community through the magic of play. This is your chance to shape legendary play that lasts a lifetime.

As a Content Specialist supporting D D Beyond, you ll help transform tabletop sourcebooks into interactive digital experiences used by millions of players. You ll collaborate with designers, engineers, producers, and QA to ensure Dungeons Dragons content is accurate, functional, and delivered on schedule. Success in this role means delivering high-quality digital implementations that faithfully represent Dungeons Dragons sourcebooks, support product launch schedules, and provide players with a seamless experience across D D Beyond.

What You ll Do:

Enter game content to directly support D D Beyond tools

Validate digital implementations against sourcebooks and recommend solutions for any shortfalls.

Test content functionality across the character builder, compendium, encounters, maps, and other D D Beyond tools.

Author and maintain structured digital content using HTML, CSS, JSON, and proprietary content authoring tools.

Identify and document bugs or inconsistencies for engineering and production teams.

Perform site content updates using our Content Management System (CMS).

Collaborate with Product, Engineering, Design, and QA throughout content releases.

Maintain internal documentation and content standards.

Prioritize multiple concurrent content releases while meeting deadlines.

Review work from peers to maintain content quality and consistency.

Other graphic editing and editorial work as assigned.

What You ll Bring:

2+ years in web content management, graphic design, web design or related field.

2+ years experience working with HTML, CSS, and JSON to build web content.

Deep familiarity with Dungeons Dragons Fifth Edition (5e and/or 5.5e rules), including the ability to accurately interpret game mechanics.

Photoshop and graphic design experience.

Experience with Content Management Systems (CMS).

Outstanding written and verbal communication skills, with strong attention to detail and quality assurance.

Demonstrated initiative, self-motivation, and proactivity in spotting and making recommendations for process performance improvement.

Experience working in a highly collaborative environment.

Exceptional organizational and time management skills to manage multiple projects and deadlines simultaneously.

Strong analytical and problem-solving skills.

Comfort learning new proprietary tools and workflows.

Ability to interpret technical documentation and game rules.

Nice to Have:

Experience with tabletop RPGs beyond Dungeons Dragons.

Familiarity with accessibility best practices for digital content.

Experience with Jira, Confluence, or similar project management tools.

Experience performing software or content QA.

Familiarity with Git or version control workflows.

Knowledge of responsive web design principles.

Experience working in digital publishing or game development.

Experience editing or preparing technical documentation
