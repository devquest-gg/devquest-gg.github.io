IT Engineer

We're seeking an experienced, generalist IT Engineer - someone who thrives in a fast-paced, creative environment and enjoys tackling a wide range of IT challenges.

You'll be the go-to person for IT systems, infrastructure, event support, and user requests. This is a hands-on, high-impact role with significant independence and real influence over the direction of our IT department.

Responsibilities:

IT support - daily technical support for developers and all staff; diagnosing and resolving issues efficiently.

Workstations & hardware - preparing, configuring, and optimizing machines for different development roles, plus ongoing maintenance, asset tracking, and software license records.

Identity & access - managing user accounts, permissions, and security policies across company systems, including onboarding and offboarding.

Security & continuity - maintaining backups, disaster recovery procedures, patching and update cycles, and running basic vulnerability checks.

Network - administering, monitoring, and troubleshooting wired and wireless infrastructure.

Event support - setting up and running equipment for company events (streaming, recording, cameras, microphones, sound).

You'll also have input into how the IT department develops - our tooling, policies, and technical direction - working closely with the rest of the team.

Requirements:

Generalist mindset - comfortable across IT support, infrastructure, and operations rather than specialized in one narrow area.

Strong communication - explains technical topics clearly to both technical and non-technical colleagues; responsive and proactive.

Self-organization and ownership - manages own priorities and takes tasks through to completion.

Quick learner - picks up new tools and adapts to shifting requirements.

Technical expertise:

Solid hands-on experience in IT support, hardware setup, and Windows troubleshooting.

Familiarity with device management and patch management for workstations.

Experience administering and monitoring wired and wireless network infrastructure.

Working knowledge of IT security practices and how policies are applied in day-to-day operations.

Practical experience administering Microsoft 365 and Entra ID (Azure AD) - users, groups, licenses, MFA, conditional access.

Solid working knowledge in administration, synchronization, and integration of identities in hybrid Active Directory and Entra ID environments.

Experience in configuring and securing hybrid and internet-facing services with modern authentication and security best practices.

Experience with a ticketing system and some form of IT asset inventory (ideally Jira Service Management).

Scripting for automation (PowerShell, Python, or similar) - enough to remove repetitive work.

Experience with backup tools and processes.

Nice to have

Familiarity with game development environments, workflows, and tools.

Experience with console devkits and platform holder requirements, or willingness to learn (NDA-bound environment).

Linux administration.

Security and compliance auditing of internal or third-party infrastructure.

Languages:

Polish - advanced (C1 minimum).

English - intermediate (B2 minimum).

Location:

Onsite in Katowice with a hybrid option after onboarding (minimum 3 days onsite, up to 2 remote).

Salary:

Salary starting from 7500 PLN gross (Contract of Employment).

What we offer:

Flexible work hours - core hours 10:00–15:00.

Office-first hybrid work model: three days in the office, with up to two days of remote work available per week.

Private healthcare and life insurance.

Co-financed Multisport card.

English lessons.

Relocation assistance (optional).

No crunch, no non-compete policy.

Pet-friendly office.

Co-financed participation in conferences and workshops.
