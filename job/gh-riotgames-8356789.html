Anti-Cheat Engineering Intern (Masters/PhD) - Summer 2027

Riot Games · Los Angeles

Entry Engineering Posted 2026-11-11 Intern
Apply on Riot Games’s site →

Riot’s Anti-Cheat team is passionate about protecting the integrity of multiplayer games and creating a fair experience for players. We work across low-level systems, software protection, and security research to help protect the legitimacy of players’ games.

As an Anti-Cheat Engineering Intern , you’ll work with experienced Vanguard engineers on a summer research project exploring how agentic AI is changing the effectiveness of software obfuscation and virtualization. With mentorship from the team, you’ll use and improve research harnesses that iteratively challenge code-protection techniques, measure the boundaries of AI-assisted reverse engineering, and investigate methods for making protected code more resilient to AI analysis. You’ll turn your findings into repeatable experiments, clear technical documentation, and make recommendations that inform the future of Anti-Cheat and client software protection.

Potential Responsibilities:

Work with Anti-Cheat engineers to scope and complete a bounded research project focused on AI-assisted analysis of obfuscated and virtualized code.

Build and improve AI harnesses that iteratively improve and challenge code-protection techniques to study the boundaries of their design

Design and run experiments that measure where AI systems succeed, struggle, or fail when reasoning about protected binaries.

Research and evaluate techniques that make software obfuscation and virtualization more resilient to AI-assisted reverse engineering.

Reproduce and validate results, documenting experimental methods, evidence, limitations, and potential defensive implications.

Contribute improvements to shared research tools and workflows, and present progress and findings to engineers and technical partners.

Required Qualifications:

Currently enrolled in a Masters or Doctoral program in the following fields: Computer Science, Computer Engineering, Cybersecurity, or related graduate degree program.

Experience programming in C or C++, demonstrated through coursework, research, internships, or personal projects.

Experience with agentic AI tools or harnesses used to automate iterative workflows such as code generation, testing, analysis, or evaluation.

Foundational understanding and relevant coursework in operating systems, computer architecture, compilers, and software security.

Available to work full-time hours for the 12-week program.

Minimum GPA of 2.5.

Desired Qualifications:

Graduating in the 2028 calendar year.

Exposure to reverse engineering, binary analysis, code obfuscation, software virtualization, or related security techniques through coursework or hands-on projects.

Experience with Python, scripting, Git, testing, or building repeatable research and evaluation tools.

Familiarity with program-analysis techniques; interest in Windows internals, game security, or software protection is a plus.

Work Authorization

Candidates must be legally authorized to work in the United States for the duration of the program.

This position is not eligible for visa sponsorship. We do not sponsor visas for this position or internship conversions.

For this role, you’ll find success through craft expertise, a collaborative spirit, and decision-making that prioritizes the delight of players. We’ll consider your studies and experience, as well as your personal relationship with games. If you embody player empathy and care about players’ experiences, this could be the role for you!
