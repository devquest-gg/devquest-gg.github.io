Why Sony Interactive Entertainment?

Sony Interactive Entertainment isn’t just the Best Place to Play — it’s also the Best Place to Work. Sony Interactive Entertainment (SIE) is the company behind the PlayStation brand. As a subsidiary of Sony Group Corporation, we’re part of a proud legacy of innovation and excellence. SIE is a dynamic technology company, delivering cutting-edge hardware and network services to more than 100 million people and an entertainment leader, home to some of the most beloved and recognizable intellectual properties (IP) in the world. Our role at SIE is to create and nurture the experiences under the PlayStation brand, a name synonymous with entertainment excellence and creativity.

Bungie is seeking a Senior Systems Engineer with a focus on End User Experience (EUX) and Endpoint Engineering to join our Studio IT team. This role will help design, build, and improve the technology experience used by Bungie employees every day, with a focus on secure, reliable, and scalable endpoint platforms and services.

Are you passionate about making technology easier and more dependable for the people using it? Do you enjoy solving complex endpoint problems, automating repetitive work, and improving how devices and applications are deployed and managed? Can you balance security, usability, and operational needs without creating unnecessary friction for our users? Then come help Bungie IT Engineering support the teams creating our world-class games.

As a Senior Systems Engineer at Bungie, you will architect, implement, and support endpoint platforms, device management, application delivery, and supporting infrastructure across Bungie’s environment. You’ll work with a team of experienced IT engineers and partner closely with Studio IT Support, Information Security, infrastructure teams, application owners, and development teams to continuously improve the technology experience for our users.

This role is expected to take technical ownership of EUX platforms and services, identify recurring pain points, and build solutions that reduce friction for both users and support teams. You will help establish standards, automate common workflows, and ensure endpoint services can scale with the changing needs of game development.

Successful candidates will have strong experience engineering and supporting enterprise endpoint environments, be comfortable owning complex technical problems, and be able to turn user and business needs into practical technical solutions. They will also be an effective mentor, collaborator, project owner, and communicator.

Responsibilities

Architect, implement, and maintain endpoint management platforms and services supporting Bungie employees and development teams

Own and improve the lifecycle of Bungie’s endpoints from provisioning and configuration through ongoing management and retirement

Engineer solutions for Windows and macOS endpoint management, configuration, compliance, and application delivery

Design and automate device provisioning and deployment workflows that reduce manual effort and improve the onboarding experience

Develop automation and tooling to improve endpoint reliability, consistency, and supportability at scale

Identify recurring user and support pain points and engineer solutions that address their underlying causes

Improve software packaging, deployment, patching, configuration, and lifecycle management across managed endpoints

Partner with Information Security to implement and meet endpoint security requirements while balancing security, usability, and development needs

Partner closely with Studio IT Support to understand recurring issues, improve escalation paths, and reduce support demand through engineering

Serve as a senior escalation point for complex endpoint, operating system, application, and device-management issues

Evaluate endpoint health, performance, and operational data to identify opportunities for improvement

Lead technical projects from requirements and design through implementation, validation, documentation, and operational handoff

Evaluate new technologies and determine where they can improve the employee and developer technology experience

Mentor engineers and provide technical guidance and training to Studio IT Support and other technical teams

Maintain clear and useful documentation of systems, standards, architecture, runbooks, and processes

Required Skills

Strong experience engineering and operating endpoint management solutions in a large enterprise environment

Experience with Microsoft Intune, Ivanti and other modern endpoint management technologies

Strong knowledge of Windows endpoint architecture, configuration, deployment, troubleshooting, and lifecycle management

Experience managing macOS endpoints using enterprise device-management technologies

Experience with automated device provisioning technologies such as Windows Autopilot, Apple Automated Device Enrollment, or comparable solutions

Experience with enterprise software packaging, deployment, patching, and application lifecycle management

Experience designing and enforcing endpoint configuration and compliance policies

Experience automating administrative and endpoint workflows using PowerShell, Python, APIs, or comparable technologies

Experience troubleshooting complex endpoint issues across operating systems, applications, identity, networking, and security layers

Understanding of Active Directory and Microsoft Entra ID as they relate to endpoint authentication, device identity, policy, and access

Understanding of endpoint security technologies and concepts including EDR, disk encryption, local privilege management, certificates, and device compliance

Experience independently leading complex technical projects involving multiple teams and stakeholders

Demonstrated ability to partner with support organizations and turn recurring operational issues into engineering improvements

Ability to evaluate technical decisions from both an engineering and end-user perspective

Ability to mentor other engineers and help establish technical standards and best practices

Strong written and verbal communication skills

Bachelor s degree or equivalent

Nice-to-Have Skills

Experience with Jamf Pro or other enterprise Apple management platforms

Experience with Microsoft Configuration Manager (SCCM/MECM) and migrations from traditional to modern endpoint management

Experience with Windows Autopilot and modern Windows provisioning

Experience managing developer workstations or other high-performance/specialized endpoint environments

Experience supporting endpoints within game development, software development, media production, or another highly technical creative environment

Experience with endpoint analytics, digital employee experience (DEX), or observability platforms

Experience with application virtualization, VDI, or remote workstation technologies

Experience with privileged access and endpoint privilege management

Experience with PKI, certificate deployment, and certificate lifecycle management

Experience integrating endpoint platforms with identity, security, ITSM, and asset-management systems

Experience with infrastructure-as-code, configuration management, or CI/CD practices

Experience designing technology solutions for distributed, hybrid, or globally located teams
