CD PROJEKT RED is looking for a Senior Gameplay Programmer , with a focus on Camera features, to join our team to work on Cyberpunk 2, the follow-up to Cyberpunk 2077. In this role you will collaborate with our team working in Unreal Engine 5 to build new camera mechanics and features to generate compelling gameplay and cinematic moments. You’ll be in charge of defining, building, and maintaining core features concerning camera systems for cinematic and gameplay.

Responsibilities: Work closely with gameplay and cinematic designers and animators to fulfill the vision of the game.

Build and maintain solid and flexible gameplay systems.

Write high-quality code that is optimized, bug-free, and aligned with project goals.

At least 5+ years of professional experience as a programmer within the games industry.

Strong proficiency in C++ programming and knowledge of Unreal Engine.

Experience collaborating&#xa0;cross-functionally with gameplay and cinematics to prototype and deploy core in-game camera systems.

Knowledge of game engine architecture and algorithms.

Strong multi-threaded programming skills.

Ability to think creatively and strategically to overcome technical challenges and drive innovation in technology.

Excellent communication skills: We have a multilingual and multicultural working environment and the ability to communicate across language and time zone barriers is essential.

Nice to have (Please note that these skills are not required to apply for the position) Shipped at least one Unreal-based game.

Experience developing for recent generation consoles.
