------------------------------------------     WB Games Montréal, a division of Warner Bros. Interactive Entertainment (WBIE), seeks an Advanced Technical Character Artist .

Your New Role… In this role, you will create high-quality character assets and serve as a key contributor to the studio’s artistic and technical quality. You will work closely with Character Art leadership and Art Direction to ensure a high and consistent quality bar across character content.

You will produce and iterate on a variety of assets through all phases of production, from blockout and basic rigging through final, engine-ready content. This includes organic assets, hard-surface models, complex props, materials, and custom effects. You will collaborate with Tech Art, Cinematics, Animation, and Design teams to ensure character content supports the project’s creative, technical, and production needs.

You will also work closely with external development partners, providing clear direction and constructive artistic and technical feedback . You will help ensure their work aligns with the project’s aesthetic goals, technical requirements, and build metrics.

As an experienced member of the team, you will share your expertise through mentorship and peer reviews. You will also research, develop, and present new methods, techniques, and tools that advance our art quality, pipelines, and workflows.

Your Role Accountabilities… Produce and iterate on a variety of assets through all phases of production, from blockout and basic rigging through final, engine-ready content, including organic assets, hard-surface models, and more.

Provide clear feedback to external partners on the project’s aesthetic goals, technical requirements, and build metrics.

Create high-quality, highly realistic, industry-leading character art content, including modeling, sculpting, texturing, and material development.

Create custom material effects for character and prop assets.

Collaborate with Tech Art, Cinematics, Animation, and Design teams to ensure character content supports project needs.

Research, develop, and present new methods, techniques, and tools that push our art quality to the next level.

Actively participate in peer reviews and meetings, and mentor other artists on the team.

Qualifications & Experience… 8 years of progressively complex experience as a Technical Artist or Technical Character Artist, including experience on at least one published AAA title.

Strong experience creating high-quality character assets through multiple phases of production, from blockout through final, engine-ready content.

Strong experience with modeling, sculpting, texturing, material development, and basic rigging.

Strong experience with industry-standard DCC software such as Maya, 3ds Max, ZBrush, Houdini, or similar tools.

Strong understanding of modern real-time 3D engines and rendering technology, including physically based rendering and bidirectional reflectance distribution function workflows. Experience with Unreal Engine is strongly preferred.

Strong understanding of scripting for DCC applications, Unreal Engine, and shader development using HLSL or similar technologies.

Experience developing shaders and materials and/or scripting using Python or similar languages.

Strong understanding of methods for optimizing character art assets for real-time game engines.

Strong troubleshooting and problem-solving skills, with the ability to identify and resolve complex artistic and technical issues.

Strong understanding of current character art techniques, pipelines, and workflows, with an interest in continuously improving tools and production processes.

Excellent communication and organizational skills, with the ability to collaborate effectively across artistic and technical disciplines.

Ability to provide clear, constructive artistic and technical feedback to internal teams and external development partners.

A collaborative mindset, curiosity, and a desire to bring new ideas, technologies, and solutions to the team.
