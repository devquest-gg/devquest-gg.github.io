Role Overview

We re looking for a Lead Game Designer to shape the creative direction of our existing portfolio while driving the creation of new titles — from initial concept and design through production and release.

This role is ideal for a lead designer with deep craft expertise, strong creative instincts, and a genuine passion for building high-performing teams. You ll be the design voice in the room — partnering with Product, Development, and Analytics leadership to turn player insight into compelling gameplay, features, and progression systems that players come back to. You ll set the bar for design quality across the studio and mentor designers to do their best work.

You bring a rare combination of hands-on design craft and the leadership presence to drive alignment at a senior level. You re also someone who actively uses AI tools in your day-to-day work and can help the team do the same.

Key Responsibilities

Own and uphold the product vision and design standards for our live titles and new products from concept through release and post-launch analysis

Design features, systems, and live ops events grounded in player research and behavioural data, with measurable impact on engagement, retention, and monetisation

Develop and refine the game s economy and balance fine tuning currency sinks and sources, progression pacing, and reward structures to keep the player experience compelling over the long term

Own the A/B testing roadmap for design-led features: scoping hypotheses with Analytics, reviewing results, and deciding what ships, iterates, or gets cancelled

Prepare clear, detailed design documentation covering gameplay mechanics, UX flows, progression systems, and balance using AI-supported workflows with a sharp eye for vetting generated outputs

Collaborate closely with development, art, product, production, and marketing to keep designs aligned and execution smooth

Work within defined guidelines for our IP partners and ensure designs, content and updates represent our brands effectively

Continually evolve the design team s workflows, integrating AI tooling where it raises quality or velocity without compromising craft

Lead, mentor, and grow a team of designers: running design syncs and reviews, setting performance expectations, supporting career development, and fostering a culture of ownership, adaptability, and curiosity

Skills, Knowledge and Expertise

7+ years in game design with proven experience leading or mentoring a design team

Strong portfolio of shipped features and titles demonstrating design craft and commercial impact; direct experience in social casino or bingo titles preferred

Hands-on across the full development lifecycle: market research, prototyping, development, live ops, post-launch analysis and LiveOps

Deep expertise in economy design, balancing, and progression systems, with a strong grasp of mobile UX principles and player journey design

Analytical fluency with key mobile metrics and player behaviour, including practical experience designing and interpreting A/B tests

Practical experience using AI tools (e.g. ChatGPT, Claude, Copilot) to accelerate ideation, documentation, balance modelling, and team communication

Excellent written communicator and cross-functional collaborator, comfortable navigating production trade-offs and influencing senior stakeholders with clear, well-evidenced recommendations

Passion for games and genuine player empathy
