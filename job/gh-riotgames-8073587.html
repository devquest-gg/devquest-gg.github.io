Manager, VFX Art - VALORANT

Riot Games

Mid Art Posted 2026-07-20 Full time
Apply on Riot Games’s site →

Riot Games was established in 2006 by entrepreneurial gamers who believe that player-focused game development can result in great games. In 2009, Riot released its debut title League of Legends to critical and player acclaim. As the most played PC game in the world, over 100 million play every month. Players form the foundation of our community and it’s for them that we continue to evolve and improve the League of Legends experience.

We’re looking for humble but ambitious, razor-sharp professionals who can teach us a thing or two. We promise to return the favor. Like us, you take play seriously; you’re passionate about games. We embrace those who see things differently, aren’t afraid to experiment, and who have a healthy disregard for constraints.

That s where you come in.

As part of the Real-Time VFX team, you will work closely with a team of artists to deliver high quality premium content skin experiences. With your experience, you’ll create aesthetically astounding visual effects that wow and delight our fans adding richness and excitement to the games experience.

As a VFX Lead , you will provide creative, technical, and people leadership to a team of VFX artists responsible for delivering high-quality, player-resonant visual effects for VALORANT. You will help shape the VFX vision and craft direction, ensuring our work meets Riot s quality bar while supporting the creative ambitions, gameplay clarity, and overall player experience of our products. Beyond craft excellence, you will develop a high-performing team, empower artists and emerging leaders, and establish sustainable practices that enable consistent delivery at scale. Working closely with Art Directors, Product Leads, Production, and cross-disciplinary partners, you will translate creative aspirations into compelling player experiences while balancing quality, scope, and execution.

Responsibilities:

People Leadership Talent Development

Lead, mentor, and develop VFX artists, fostering a culture of trust, accountability, collaboration, and continuous growth.

Establish clear expectations, manage performance, and support career development while nurturing emerging talent and future craft leaders.

Build a resilient team through effective delegation, succession planning, and greater distribution of ownership and expertise.

Cultivate a healthy, inclusive environment where artists are empowered to contribute ideas, challenge assumptions, and deliver their best work.

Lead hiring, onboarding, talent assessment, and long-term team capability planning.

Craft Leadership Creative Excellence

Own and elevate VFX quality, consistency, and artistic direction across assigned products and initiatives.

Establish a clear VFX vision, craft standards, and creative principles aligned with VALORANT s artistic identity, gameplay requirements, and product goals.

Provide creative judgment, actionable feedback, and strategic problem-solving to guide teams through complex artistic and technical challenges.

Drive innovation and continuously evolve VFX capabilities, techniques, and visual storytelling to elevate player experiences.

Balance creative ambition with gameplay readability, technical performance, production constraints, and player value.

Sustainable Operations Product Excellence

Partner with Art Directors, Product Leads, Production, and cross-functional stakeholders to align creative ambitions, product priorities, and execution strategies.

Drive operational excellence through clear ownership, effective collaboration, continuous improvement, and proactive resolution of systemic challenges.

Establish scalable, resilient workflows that reduce dependencies, mitigate delivery risks, and maintain quality through changing priorities.

Balance creative ambition, resources, scope, and timelines through strategic prioritization, informed decision-making, and sustainable workload management.

Continuously improve tools, pipelines, and production practices to strengthen team capabilities, operational effectiveness, and meaningful product impact.

Take accountability for the team s contribution to product outcomes while protecting long-term team health and delivery sustainability.

Required Qualifications:

Significant professional experience in real-time VFX development for games, including experience managing and developing a team of VFX artists.

Strong artistic and technical understanding of real-time VFX, including visual storytelling, timing, composition, readability, shaders, particle systems, and performance optimization.

Demonstrated ability to establish creative direction and guide teams in delivering high-quality VFX across complex products or initiatives.

Proven experience in people management, mentorship, performance development, and talent growth.

Strong understanding of game development pipelines, production workflows, and cross-disciplinary collaboration.

Ability to balance artistic quality, technical constraints, player experience, and business priorities.

Strong communication, stakeholder management, and decision-making skills.

Experience improving team processes, craft practices, and delivery effectiveness.

Working proficiency in spoken and written English sufficient for cross-functional collaboration.

Desired Qualifications:

Experience developing VFX for competitive multiplayer games, particularly tactical shooters.

Familiarity with Unreal Engine, Niagara, Houdini, or equivalent real-time VFX tools and workflows.

Experience working with external development partners or distributed teams.

Experience leading VFX for premium cosmetic content, including weapons, character abilities, or other high-fidelity gameplay experiences.

Demonstrated success developing senior artists into independent craft owners or emerging leaders.

Experience contributing to broader craft strategies, technical standards, or organizational improvements.

Mandarin proficiency to facilitate effective collaboration with Mandarin-speaking partner studios.

As part of the application process, candidates will be expected to provide a portfolio or demo reel demonstrating their VFX expertise and creative leadership. Candidates may also be asked to complete an art assessment or participate in a craft leadership evaluation.
