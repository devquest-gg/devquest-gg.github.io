Senior Cinematic Animator [Rainbow Six Siege]

Ubisoft Montreal · Montreal

Senior Animation Posted 2026-07-24 Full time
Apply on Ubisoft Montreal’s site →

As a Senior Cinematic Animator at Ubisoft Montreal, you will create high-quality animations for customization content and in-game cinematic sequences for Rainbow Six Siege. Your work will directly enhance the player experience through third-person content such as Victory Dances, loadout poses, and event introductions.

What you’ll do: Create animations that align with the visual language and quality standards of Rainbow Six Siege; Produce expressive sequences using keyframe animation , motion capture data, and camera work; Explore visual approaches and build prototypes to support creative content development; Refine animations to ensure strong in-game feel and presentation; Collaborate with technical animators, directors, and partner teams to deliver content on schedule; Support the evolution of the game’s visual quality by sharing expertise across teams; Solve artistic, technical, and production challenges through creative problem-solving; Adapt workflows to the realities of a live game production environment; Maintain a high level of quality and consistency across delivered content; Contribute to cross-discipline collaboration throughout production.

Experience creating cinematic animation for third-person character content; Knowledge of keyframe animation techniques and motion capture workflows; Expert with&#xa0;Motion Builder and Maya; Understanding of camera work for cinematic sequences; Strong attention to visual quality and detail; Ability to collaborate effectively with multidisciplinary teams; Creative and solution-oriented mindset; Adaptability within a live production environment; Passion for creating memorable player experiences.
