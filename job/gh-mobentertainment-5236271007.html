Principal Technical Sound Designer

Mob Entertainment

Lead Audio $164K–$215K Posted 2026-11-10 Full time
Apply on Mob Entertainment’s site →

Mob Entertainment is a startup multimedia studio that is best known for our hit indie horror game " Poppy Playtime" .

This franchise is one of the hottest new properties in gaming, and our team is naturally expanding as our ambition expands.

To give a taste of our brand s popularity, more than 200 billion YouTube views of Poppy Playtime related content have occurred since the game s launch 6 years ago.

We’re looking for a Principal Technical Sound Designer with deep production experience building and shipping high-quality technical audio systems for AA/AAA games. This is a senior individual-contributor role for someone equally comfortable with audio systems architecture, Unreal Engine, Wwise, C++, Blueprints, optimization, mixing, tooling, and hands-on audio production.

What You ll Do

Architect and build modular, scalable, and portable audio systems from the ground up using C++, Blueprints, Wwise, and Unreal Engine.

Work across multiple AA/AAA game projects simultaneously, designing solutions that can scale across projects and teams.

Develop deep integrations using the Wwise Unreal Engine API.

Design and maintain sophisticated dynamic mixing and spatial audio systems in Wwise, supporting gameplay, dialogue, environments, cinematics, and changing player context.

Develop advanced audio functionality using Unreal Engine MetaSounds.

Profile and optimize audio for CPU, memory, runtime performance, and platform constraints.

Architect and refine scalable, modular, and portable dialogue systems, balancing in-world immersion with gameplay and dialogue clarity.

Build Unreal Editor modules, utilities, and tools that improve audio implementation workflows and reduce repetitive work.

Create clear technical documentation and how-to guides that enable sound designers and junior technical sound designers to independently use and maintain the systems you build.

Contribute hands-on sound design when needed.

Deliver high-quality, production-ready results under short deadlines and high-pressure development conditions.

What We re Looking For

Multiple shipped AA or AAA titles, with professional experience mixing shipped game projects.

Experience supporting multiple AA/AAA projects concurrently.

Deep expertise with Wwise, its Unreal Engine integration/API, dynamic mixing, and Wwise Spatial Audio.

Extensive professional experience with Unreal Engine, C++, Blueprints, and MetaSounds.

Proven experience in architecting and building core game-audio systems from scratch.

Proven expertise optimizing game audio for CPU and memory performance.

Proven experience developing audio systems for networked multiplayer games, including challenges such as replicated audio state/events, latency and synchronization, and multiplayer voice chat.

Multiple proven examples of building and refining dialogue systems from the ground up.

Deep experience developing Unreal Editor modules, utilities, and workflow tools.

Strong technical documentation and knowledge-transfer skills.

Professional sound design experience and a strong understanding of how technical systems support creative audio goals.

Experience working in horror games or horror-focused interactive audio is strongly valued. At minimum, candidates should demonstrate familiarity with and enthusiasm for the genre and an understanding of how audio systems can support tension, atmosphere, player uncertainty, pacing, and dynamic scares.

A demonstrated ability to make strong technical and creative decisions when time, performance, or production constraints are significant.

Principal-Level Expectations

We’re looking for more than familiarity with these technologies. Strong candidates should be able to demonstrate specific shipped examples of systems they personally architected, built, optimized, documented, and supported in production.
