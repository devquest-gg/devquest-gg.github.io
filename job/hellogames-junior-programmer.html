Graduate / Junior Programmer

Joining Hello Games as a graduate means you get stuck in with the team making code for our games from day one. You will be surrounded by an experienced multi-discipline team and will get to contribute to and learn from the whole team.

Responsibilities

Create and implement features into the game.

Create high quality code.

To learn about all aspects of game development and process at Hello Games.

To work collaboratively with a multi-disciplinary team to resolve problems and contribute to the game.

To take ownership for quality and hitting deadlines.

Qualifications

Great grades from a computer science degree, or a similar degree.

A strong math background, comfortable with 3D math, vector and matrix math. You will probably have at least A-Level math.

You have a good understanding of C++ and for fun you probably write games in C++ in your spare time.

Have some amazing demos of things you’ve been working on to show us.

You have good communication skills to work across a multidiscipline team.

Apply HERE with your CV and links to your work.
