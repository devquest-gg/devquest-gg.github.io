Senior eCommerce Manager

Exploding Kittens · Los Angeles

Senior Other Hybrid $120K–$150K Posted 2026-07-08 Full time

We're looking for an experienced ecommerce leader to own and grow Exploding Kittens' global digital business. This role is responsible for the day-to-day performance and execution across our ecommerce channels, including Amazon (US, Canada, UK, and EU), Target.com, Walmart.com (1P & 3P), DTC, TikTok Shop, and emerging marketplaces.

Reporting to the VP of Sales & Business Development, you'll serve as the connective tissue across every digital point of sale, ensuring our pricing, promotions, product launches, content, and operations are aligned across channels and markets. You'll be both a strategic thinker and a hands-on operator, personally managing our highest-volume channels while partnering closely with Sales, Marketing, Production, Finance, and our international teams to drive sustainable growth.

This is an ideal opportunity for someone who enjoys rolling up their sleeves, leading through influence, and building scalable ecommerce operations in a fast-paced consumer products environment.
