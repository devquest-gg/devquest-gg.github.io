Director, Program Management

Portfolio Program Management Office (PPMO)

About the Role

Sony Interactive Entertainment is seeking a Director, Program Management to lead the delivery, governance, and strategic execution of critical e-commerce, payments, and monetization initiatives across the PlayStation platform.

This role is responsible for leading a portfolio of large-scale, cross-functional programs that span Product, Engineering, Finance, Tax, Legal, Operations, Customer Experience, and Commercial organizations. The Director will drive alignment across senior leaders, influence strategic investment decisions, and ensure complex initiatives deliver measurable customer and business outcomes.

The ideal candidate combines deep program leadership expertise with strong domain knowledge in digital commerce, payments, subscription businesses, marketplaces, checkout experiences, fraud prevention, taxation, and financial operations. They must be capable of operating at both strategic and execution levels, translating business objectives into scalable delivery plans while ensuring disciplined program execution.

What You ll Do

Strategic Portfolio Leadership

Lead a portfolio of complex, high-visibility initiatives supporting PlayStation s commerce and monetization ecosystem.

Partner with executive leaders to define strategic objectives, roadmaps, priorities, success metrics, and investment plans.

Drive alignment across global organizations and mission groups with competing priorities and dependencies.

Establish operating models and governance frameworks that enable informed decision-making, accountability, and effective risk management.

Serve as a strategic partner to senior leadership on portfolio health, execution strategy, organizational readiness, capacity planning, and investment decisions.

Commerce Payments Transformation

Lead programs related to digital commerce, checkout modernization, payment processing, payment orchestration, subscriptions, entitlements, taxation, fraud prevention, and financial platform capabilities.

Partner with Product Management and Engineering leaders to evaluate business opportunities and develop scalable execution strategies.

Ensure business, operational, regulatory, compliance, and customer experience requirements are incorporated into planning and delivery.

Drive initiatives that improve conversion, payment success rates, operational efficiency, platform scalability, compliance readiness, and customer satisfaction.

Guide modernization efforts across the end-to-end commerce ecosystem, including customer acquisition, checkout, payment authorization, fraud controls, subscription lifecycle management, settlement processes, taxation, and post-purchase customer experiences.

Portfolio Execution Benefits Realization

Establish and maintain portfolio-level planning, dependency management, reporting, forecasting, and executive communications.

Identify delivery risks, resource constraints, and execution challenges early and drive mitigation plans.

Oversee complex cross-organizational dependencies across multiple teams, business units, and external partners.

Facilitate executive reviews, planning cadences, governance forums, and portfolio decision-making processes.

Define and track outcome-based KPIs, benefits realization plans, and success metrics that demonstrate measurable business value.

Organizational Leadership

Build and lead a program management organization focused on execution excellence, accountability, and continuous improvement.

Support the professional development and growth of program managers and senior program leaders.

Build strong partnerships with senior leaders across Product, Engineering, Finance, Legal, Tax, Operations, and Commercial organizations.

Drive a culture of accountability, clarity, transparency, operational excellence, and customer focus.

Influence execution across organizations without direct authority while maintaining strong stakeholder alignment.
