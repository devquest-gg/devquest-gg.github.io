WHAT YOU WILL BE WORKING ON Main challenge Your main challenge will be to deliver high-quality, creative 2D visual assets for diverse marketing campaigns and in-game content while strictly adhering to brand guidelines and project requirements. Balancing technical precision with artistic adaptability, you will ensure all designs align with the legal, marketing, and licensing standards of major global franchises.
