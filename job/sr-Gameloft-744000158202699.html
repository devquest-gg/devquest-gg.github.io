3D Character Artist (Middle/Senior)

Gameloft · Ho Chi Minh City

Senior Art Posted 2026-10-28 Full time
Apply on Gameloft’s site →

WHAT YOU WILL BE WORKING ON What you will focus on Join Gameloft’s GFX team to create high-quality 3D characters and objects for our games, bringing artistic concepts to life while meeting technical requirements and production deadlines. You’ll work with different art styles, from realistic to stylized, ensuring every asset meets the project’s visual and technical standards.

What it means on a daily basis Create high-quality 3D assets: Model and texture characters and objects in realistic or stylized art styles, following the project's art direction and creative guidelines.

Optimize assets for games: Produce high- and low-poly models, handle UV mapping and texture creation, and optimize assets for real-time rendering and next-generation games.

Ensure quality and implementation: Check that assets meet artistic and technical standards, troubleshoot issues, and work within production constraints and deadlines.

Collaborate with the team: Work closely with the 3D Artist Supervisor, Producer, and Art Team to maintain visual consistency, communicate progress, and resolve production challenges.

Contribute to team development: Share ideas, suggest quality and workflow improvements, and provide guidance or training to other artists, depending on your experience level.

Who you would be working with You will be reporting to the Artist Supervisor, Art Producer, and Art Lead/Director.

Closely collaborating with other disciplines in the Art team (e.g., 2D Art, Animation, Technical Art, VFX) and other teams in the project (e.g., Game Design, Programming).

Where can this role take you? Advance vertically to higher level in the 3D team such as Senior or Principal position.

Transition to other disciplines within the Art team: 2D Artist, 3D Animator, VFX Artist, and so on.

Recruitment journey Online screening call with recruiter Distance art test Offline interview with Artist Supervisor, Art Director, and Producer WHAT YOU NEED TO SUCCEED Games industry experienc e&#xa0;as a 3D Character&#xa0;Artist.

Strong understanding of human anatomy, proportions, perspective, composition, color, shading, and lighting.

Excellent 3D modeling and texturing skills across realistic and stylized art styles.

Ability to create and optimize high-poly and low-poly models for real-time rendering and next-generation games.

Strong UV mapping skills and the ability to create detailed, high-quality texture maps.

Proficiency in relevant 3D tools, including 3ds Max, Maya or Blender, ZBrush, Photoshop, and Substance Painter . Knowledge of Unity or other game engines is also valuable.

WHO YOU ARE Good spoken and written English, plus clear communication, with the ability to take direction, follow a brief and solve artistic or technical issues.

Proactive, organized and creative, with a good eye for detail.

Ability to work well under pressure and in a team.

Passionate about games across platforms.
