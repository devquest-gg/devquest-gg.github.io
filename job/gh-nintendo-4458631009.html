Position Summary We are seeking a Digital Archivist, Historical Systems to support the preservation, organization, and accessibility of historical hardware and software information. This role will design and implement a structured framework for organizing technical records, engineering documentation, source materials, and institutional knowledge related to legacy systems and platforms.

The ideal candidate combines expertise in archives, metadata management, information architecture, and research. Working closely with subject matter experts across engineering, product development, legal, and business teams, this individual will establish standards and processes that ensure historical information remains discoverable, usable, and preserved for future generations. This is an approximately 12-month contract position based in the Redmond, WA area.

DESCRIPTION OF DUTIES

Schema Information Architecture

Design and maintain metadata schemas for historical hardware and software assets.

Develop taxonomies, controlled vocabularies, naming conventions, and classification frameworks.

Create standards for organizing engineering, development, and technical documentation.

Establish relationships between platforms, software, peripherals, technical specifications, source materials, and supporting documentation.

Develop data dictionaries and supporting documentation to ensure consistent metadata application.

Knowledge Repository Development

Build and maintain a searchable repository of historical systems information.

Define repository structure and organizational standards based on business and user needs.

Ensure historical records can be efficiently discovered and reused by engineering, legal, product development, research, and business teams.

Recommend improvements to information organization, accessibility, and long-term usability.

Research Knowledge Capture

Interview subject matter experts and long-tenured employees to capture institutional knowledge.

Translate informal knowledge into structured, searchable documentation.

Validate historical information through multiple sources and document findings.

Create reference materials, summaries, and supporting documentation for future use.

Governance Documentation Standards

Develop documentation standards and archival procedures.

Maintain data quality and metadata consistency.

Recommend long-term retention and preservation strategies.

SUMMARY OF REQUIREMENTS

Required

Bachelor’s degree in library information science, Archives, Museum Studies, Information Management, History, Technical Communication, or a related field; equivalent professional experience may be considered.

2+ years of experience in one or more of the following:

Digital archives

Records management

Metadata management

Knowledge management

Information architecture

Digital asset management

Library science

Experience developing metadata structures, taxonomies, classification systems, or naming conventions.

Experience organizing and managing large collections of digital content and documentation.

Strong research, analytical, and documentation skills.

Ability to work independently and manage ambiguous or incomplete information.

Excellent written and verbal communication skills.

Preferred

Archives Records Management certification or comparable archival training.

Museum Studies or Public History background.

Experience working with digital asset management systems, content management systems, archives platforms, knowledge repositories, or relational databases.

Familiarity with metadata and archival standards such as Dublin Core, DACS, EAD, PREMIS, MODS, or similar frameworks.

Experience conducting oral history interviews or structured subject matter expert interviews.

Experience organizing technical documentation, engineering records, product development materials, or software/hardware collections.

Interest in or experience with technology history, consumer electronics, gaming, software development, or technical product archives.
