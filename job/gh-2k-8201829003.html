What we need

2K’s Partnerships and Licensing team works across several disciplines to lead our partnerships with industry-leading First Party partners like Sony, Microsoft, Nintendo, Steam, and Epic to drive successful title publishing, brand integrations and go-to-market operations while working cross-functionally to ensure go-to-market excellence across all workflows. Our team culture is fast-paced, global and goal-oriented with a focus on driving revenue and exposure for our games worldwide. We hire self-starters who work hard and get the job done.

We are seeking an extremely organized and detail-oriented individual who can work closely with our First Party partners and internal stakeholders to successfully lead publishing and partner operations as the Console Account Manager (Microsoft, Sony, Nintendo). The ideal candidate should be highly cross-functional, excellent at partner operations and relations, and be experienced with both long-term planning and day-to-day execution. You will be the cross-functional lead for many contexts and with many teams: Product, Production, Marketing, Commercial, Sales, Go-to-Market, Operations, Legal and others. This work can also include First Party deal execution and compliance across 2K internal teams. This role will require you to own and lead projects at multiple levels of seniority, embracing a constantly changing environment and multitasking on numerous high-level projects at once while remaining cool, calm and collected.

What you ll do

Lead First Party Relationships Growth: Manage day-to-day relationships and operational alignment with Console First Party platform account teams, evaluating, evangelizing, and executing new platform technologies and growth opportunities.

Support Launch Release Operations: Serve as the primary business point of contact for Console First Party platforms for game launches, pre-orders, and title releases to guarantee accurate, on-time delivery while keeping global stakeholders aligned.

Serve as Platform Subject Matter Expert: Maintain deep internal documentation on platform features, guidelines, and policies to educate internal teams, leveraging title and company performance data to advocate for platform-level process and policy improvements.

Execute Joint Strategic Data Initiatives: Collaborate cross-functionally to aggregate, analyze, and present regular product and business performance data to platform partners to advance shared business goals.

Drive Cross-Departmental Problem Solving: Partner across Product, Production, Marketing, Commercial, Sales and Legal to resolve roadblocks and ensure operational consistency and efficiency across all titles.

Who we think will be a great fit

4-6 years of project/product management, production experience and/or operations experience with tangible go-to-market results

Experience leading time-sensitive projects in a highly matrixed and cross-functional organization working with globally distributed teams in multiple time zones globally. Must be equally fluent in IRL, Zoom, Slack and email

Passion for video games and game development

Ability to communicate effectively, with strong oral and written skills

Disciplined and organized, with meticulous attention to detail. Experience with core project management principles a plus

Self-starter with experience working in a fast-paced and pressure-filled environment

Team player with the ability to multitask, prioritize assignments and work with multiple clients simultaneously and effectively

Education: Bachelor’s degree or higher

Technology: Google Docs/Sheets/Slides + Powerpoint/Word/Excel

Experience with game publishing tools (e.g. TPRnet/Partner Center/NCMS/Steamworks) a big plus
