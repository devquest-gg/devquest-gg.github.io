Senior DevOps Engineer

Founded in 2012, Anshar Studios focuses on 1st and 3rd party game development for all major gaming platforms.

Currently, we're employing over 200 specialists in our studio in Katowice.

Working with Anshar gives you the opportunity to develop your skills, participate in challenging projects, and team up with a bunch of skilled people with a passion for video games.

What You will do:

Architect, implement and continuously improve Jenkins-based CI/CD pipelines for Unreal Engine projects, including build-farm provisioning, automated testing & reporting.

Administer and optimize Perforce: server setup/upgrades, backups, performance tuning, access and stream configuration, etc.

Configure project build pipelines for automatic validations (e.g., code review validations, code integration checks).

Scale and manage the build farm: capacity planning, provisioning, lifecycle automation of agents/VMs (Proxmox) and containerized runners.

Mentor junior and regular DevOps engineers, provide documentation, training sessions and best-practice guidelines.

Evaluate, test and introduce new tools or plugins that enhance reliability, performance and developer experience.

Provide first-line support for developers, swiftly diagnosing VCS or build issues and ensuring smooth information flow via Slack, email, etc.

What is needed:

Strong understanding of the software development lifecycle.

Proficiency in scripting languages (Python, Groovy, Bash, PowerShell).

Proven track record of designing and running highly available CI/CD infrastructures (VMs, containers, on-prem or cloud).

Solid grasp of server & network fundamentals, performance tuning, monitoring and alerting (Grafana, Prometheus, Zabbix, etc.).

Capacity-planning and incident-response experience for build farms or similar critical services.

Ability to communicate effectively with various stakeholders.

Mentoring capability and excellent communication skills (English B2/C1 both spoken and written).

Pluses:

In-depth, hands-on knowledge of Jenkins and Perforce.

Deep familiarity with Unreal Engine build workflows and Unreal BuildGraph.

Infrastructure-as-Code know-how (e.g., Terraform, Ansible).

Hands-on experience with Docker.

Familiarity with cloud platforms (AWS, Azure, GCP).

Understanding of security best practices: SSO, secrets management, least-privilege policies.

What we offer:

Wage 15 000 - 23 000 zł gross (depending on skills and experience).

Flexible work hours - core hours 10:00–15:00.

Office-first hybrid work model: three days in the office, with up to two days of remote work available per week.

Private healthcare and life insurance.

Co-financed Multisport card.

English lessons.

Relocation assistance (optional).

No crunch, no non-compete policy.

Pet-friendly office.

Co-financed participation in conferences and workshops.
