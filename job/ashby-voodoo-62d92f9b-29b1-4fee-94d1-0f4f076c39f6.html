ABOUT VOODOO

Founded in 2013, Voodoo is a tech company that creates mobile games and apps with a mission to entertain the world. Gathering 800 employees, 7 billion downloads, and over 200 million active users, Voodoo is the #3 mobile publisher worldwide in terms of downloads after Google and Meta. Our portfolio includes chart-topping games like Mob Control and Block Jam, alongside popular apps such as BeReal and Wizz.

TEAM

Our Corporate teams support Voodoo’s performance and growth whilst putting people at the core of everything they do. As a French tech leader and global successful company, our Corporate teams are constantly looking for ways to innovate and increase organization efficiency to keep Voodoo ahead of the competitive market. Voodoo’s Corporate function is composed of five teams: HR, Talent acquisition, Legal, M&A and Finance, working together to drive Voodoo’s success.

If you join us… You will be a key member of the Legal – Corporate/M&A team ensuring efficiency in Corporate and M&A processes. You will be part of a collaborative team ready to partner in initiatives influencing all aspects of the business, and willing to go the extra mile.

ROLE

As a member of the Legal – Corporate/M&A team, you will notably focus on the following missions:

- Setting-up and support of gaming studios in the entire world including assistance in the drafting and negotiation of the documentation, coordination with French and local legal advisors, and organization of signing and closing processes

- Corporate support to the French and foreign entities of the Voodoo group, including corporate governance issues, implementation and follow-up of incentive mechanisms for employees (free shares, BSPCE, etc.), corporate support to the Voodoo group entities (capital increases, organization of shareholders’ and board meetings, drafting of PoAs, etc.)

- Assistance on M&A transactions made by the Voodoo group

- Legal guidance and solutions provided to the operational teams

PROFILE

- Previous experience in the Corporate/M&A department of a law firm as an intern

- Fluency in English and French absolutely required (most of the work is in English)

- Pragmatic, problem solver, fast executor and rigorous

- Personality is key: Good sense of humor, positive energy, with a strong team spirit

- “CRFPA” is mandatory for this position

BENEFITS

- Swile, meal vouchers

- Gymlib fully covered by Voodoo

- Wellness activities in our Paris office

- Remote days
