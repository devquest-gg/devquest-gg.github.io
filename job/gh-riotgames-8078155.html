Senior User Experience Designer, Accessibility - Central QA Ops

Riot Games · Los Angeles

Senior QA $185K–$258K Posted 2026-08-04 Full time
Apply on Riot Games’s site →

As a User Experience Designer, you will design, tweak, and optimize the player experience. Your analytical skills, player insight, and creativity aid in crafting in- and out-of-game experiences that engage and surprise players. You will collaborate with interdisciplinary teams and work with every tool at hand to innovate and iterate player-focused designs that’ll delight millions around the world.

As a Senior Accessibility User Experience Designer , you will function as a part of the Central Product Accessibility team and work with product teams across the organization to drive and facilitate accessible and inclusive design. This role will collaborate with design and engineering teams to inform the creation of accessible games and experiences. The Accessibility UX Designer should have a deep knowledge of accessible game design, and will be responsible for cross-training other designers and defining requirements and best practices. This position will report to the Director of Product Accessibility.

Responsibilities:

Creating guidelines and tools to help designers, developers, and architects comply with accessibility standards

Create design requirements for both game and web products

Work with UX designers to define strategy, roadmap, and priorities

Perform accessibility reviews and provide feedback on designs and features

Incorporate accessibility requirements into design documents and specifications

Manage the photosensitive epilepsy flash rate testing process and training

Propose improvements to existing processes and features in order to increase usability in our products

Work with UX researchers to identify and gather data around features

Use data insights and user feedback to make recommendations to product teams

Create Accessibility personas and user stories

Triage and prioritize accessibility issues and provide guidance for product teams on how these issues can be remediated

Train and educate product teams on accessibility requirements

Audit games and web applications for compliance with best practices and accessibility standards

Keep up to date with current changes and trends in game, web, application, and software accessibility

Required Qualifications:

5+ years of UX design experience

3+ years of accessibility experience

Expert knowledge of accessible and inclusive design principles and methodologies

Have a strong understanding of core game design technologies (Unity, Unreal etc.)

Have experience with user experience software development using HTML, JavaScript, XML, CSS and other related UI technologies

Experience with UI prototyping tools such as Figma, Sketch or Adobe XD

Experience using common screen readers: JAWS, NVDA, VoiceOver

Experience using keyboard navigation and other assistive technology

Experience testing with flash-rate testing tools (eg. Harding, PEAT)

Knowledge and understanding of accessibility standards, principles and techniques, including (WCAG, WAI-ARIA, Section 508 C), with the ability to interpret and advise on these standards

Possess empathy for our diverse user and player base, a deep understanding of user-centered design, and strong analytical abilities

Ability to partner and collaborate with cross-functional teams to achieve product goals

Ability to translate end-user feedback into actionable items

Ability to deliver training to product teams

Experience using task and ticket management tools (e.g. JIRA, Confluence etc)

Excellent organizational and communication skills

Desired Qualifications:

Experience designing interfaces across various platforms (i.e. mobile, PC, console, etc.) with strong understanding of interaction modalities across phone, controller, keyboard, and mouse

Experience with accessible product, game and web design

For this role, you ll find success through craft expertise, a collaborative spirit, and decision-making that prioritizes the delight of players. We will be looking at your past studies, experience, and your personal relationship with games. If you embody player empathy and care about players experiences, this could be your role!
