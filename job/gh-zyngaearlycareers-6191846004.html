For more information, visit www.zynga.com or follow Zynga on Twitter , Instagram , Facebook, or the Zynga blog Position Overview:

Developing a game requires passion, innovation, and creativity. Zynga wants to take exceptional new graduates and turn them into world-class product managers who will be responsible for creating the games of tomorrow. Our team will immerse you in a 12-month program composed of two rotations. You’ll work across disciplines alongside our best and brightest to solve some of our highest priority and most challenging business problems. This is a hybrid position. If chosen for the role you will be working out of our San Mateo, Austin or Toronto office, and will be expected to go into office a minimum of twice a week.

What You ll Do:

During your program, you may work on crafting and testing new merchandising strategies or sales in our live products, designing onboarding flows for a new feature, or operating and designing live in-game events to delight our players. You will need to work seamlessly with developers, artists, designers, testers, and other product managers to get your ideas into software and into the hands of our millions of players.

As a Rotational Product Manager you will:

Write feature specs, run data analyses and investigations, write readouts, design and release experiments on new or existing features, and coordinate collaboration and alignment cross subject area

Be paired with senior product mentors both on your game and from other teams in the portfolio to get a wide range of perspective and support your development.

Learn about similarities and differences between game genres

Learn how different teams work together and develop your communication skills

Access training/workshops run by our skilled product community

What You Bring:

A deep enthusiasm for gaming and a drive to develop successful/enduring products

Data driven critical/analytical thinking and natural curiosity

Demonstrated leadership capabilities and strong communications skills

Completed undergraduate or masters degree between December 2026 - June 2027

Bonus Points:

Expertise with Excel, Powerpoint

Experience with tools like SQL, Miro, Figma

Previous Product Management intern experience
