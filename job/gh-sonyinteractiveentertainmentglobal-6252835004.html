Role Overview:

A 6 month contract - reporting into the Stock and Supply Chain Finance Manager, this is a fantastic role ideally suited for an ACCA/CIMA student who is close to completing their studies or newly qualified. The role is primarily responsible for the correct maintenance and set up of all the Prices at SIEE.

What you’ll be doing:

Maintenance of material pricing and pricing matrix in SAP

Promotion pricing and any additional pricing queries

Posting journals in SAP

Internal orders set up in SAP

Raising credit notes related to material adjustments

Support team during month end and year end activities and any other specific requests (compliance, SOX, audit)

Ad-hoc requests as required (from both internal SONY team and external stakeholders)

What we are looking for:

At least 2 years’ experience working within a finance team of a multinational organisation

User level proficiency in Microsoft Office and SAP / BI

Studying towards finance qualification (ACCA/ACA/CIMA)

Ability to work in a time pressured environment and to strict deadlines

Strong time-management and organisational skills

Good communication skills

Experience of working closely with non-finance business stakeholders and ability to build effective working relationships across the organisation

A proactive and conscientious working attitude, as well as ability to use initiative

Accuracy and attention to detail to ensure accurate reporting
