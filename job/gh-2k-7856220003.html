What We Need

A Lead Developer focused on pipeline products and data systems. You are a multi-functional engineer who loves building software across all tech stacks. You might develop a data warehouse with an AI enabled interface or automate repeatable dev processes that are slowing us down. You have a strong sense of systems architecture and a passion for creating tools that solve high level bottlenecks. You can lead engineers and collaborate with leaders and stakeholders.

What You’ll Do

Lead a team of developers to develop products and tools across a breadth of domains, including game engine, pipeline, DCC, and web tools.

Partner with the Directors and Product Managers to estimate and solve complex pipeline inefficiencies with innovative, often AI enabled solutions.

Collaborate with studio partners to assess technical requirements for build systems and deployment tools.

Participate in project planning, estimation, and the technical breakdown of complex initiatives.

Maintain and improve existing pipeline software while ensuring high quality documentation and support.

Establish and enforce code review standards and professional development initiatives for your team.

What Will Make You A Great Fit

Extensive experience across domains: data systems, dev tools, AI integrations, pipeline automation, custom apps, game engines, DevOps/DevEx, or web tools.

Proven ability to lead and mentor a team of engineers.

Proficiency in multiple languages such as C#, Python, JavaScript, and C++.

Experience building and maintaining distributed systems, web frameworks, or CI CD pipelines.

Strong understanding of object oriented programming and modern architectural design patterns.

Ability to manage large and diverse codebases with multiple branches and stakeholders.

Excellent interpersonal skills to navigate a fast paced environment and align with studio partners.

Bachelor of Computer Science or equivalent work experience.

Bonus Points

Experience with web technologies like React, Django, or Node.js.

Familiarity with Docker, Kubernetes, or cloud service providers like AWS or Azure.

Hands on experience with game engines such as Unreal or Unity.

Background in building automated testing frameworks or data visualization tools.
