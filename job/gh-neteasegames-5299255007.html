User Researcher

NetEase Games · Bothell - Onsite

Mid Design Onsite Posted 2026-11-18 Full time
Apply on NetEase Games’s site →

Job Responsibilities

Conduct user research on game players to uncover target audiences behavioral patterns, motivations, emotional drivers, and gameplay experience needs, providing actionable insights to inform game development and design decisions.

Support user research initiatives across various game projects through a wide range of research methods, including but not limited to expanding player outreach channels, conducting surveys, recruiting participants, facilitating interviews, and organizing and executing playtests.

Work closely with game development teams throughout the development lifecycle, providing professional insights and recommendations to improve game design, and tracking identified user experience issues to ensure they are effectively addressed.

Stay informed about emerging trends and developments across the gaming, entertainment, and digital media industries. Actively explore and experience games and entertainment products to develop industry expertise and deliver valuable market insights and research support.

Job Requirements

Passion for Gaming Market Knowledge: A strong passion for gaming, with a solid understanding of the North American gaming market and player behaviors. Preference will be given to candidates with extensive hands-on experience (200+ hours) in PC shooter games such as Marvel Rivals, or MMORPGs such as Where Winds Meet.

Research Analytical Skills: Hands-on experience with surveys and/or user interviews, strong interpersonal and communication skills, and a genuine enthusiasm for engaging with players. Ability to observe, interpret, and analyze user behaviors and verbal feedback with strong attention to detail. An academic background in psychology, sociology, anthropology, communications, statistics, computer science, or other user research-related disciplines is preferred.

Professional Experience: At least one year of professional experience in user research, game design, market research, or a related field.

AI Literacy Tool Proficiency: Strong interest in emerging AI technologies and their practical applications. Familiarity with at least two AI-powered tools (e.g., Claude, Codex) for learning, research, or professional workflows. Demonstrated awareness of AI capabilities and the ability to integrate AI tools into daily work.

Responsibility Cross-Time-Zone Collaboration: Strong sense of ownership, reliability, and the ability to collaborate effectively with international teams across different time zones.

Bilingual Communication: Proficiency in both Mandarin Chinese and English, with the ability to independently conduct player interviews, facilitate in-depth discussions, and communicate effectively in professional settings.
