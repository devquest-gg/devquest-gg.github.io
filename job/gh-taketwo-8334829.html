The Challenge

The Manager, International Benefits Operations role is an exciting opportunity to join a highly successful, forward-thinking team where you will be responsible for tactical management of country-specific benefits operations in all countries outside North America as well as our global programs.

The role manages a team that has a focus on efficiency requiring strong internal and external stakeholder management with areas such as benefit plan vendors, brokers, Payroll, HR Technology, Finance and Project Management as well as local HR teams.

This role also supports preparation of non-North American country-specific and global benefits program communications, with our in-house communications team, and is responsible for delivering a positive user experience.

This role reports into the Director, International Benefits and is based in our London office where we work on a hybrid model of four days in-office and one day working from home.

NOTE: This is a fixed-term contract position expected to last between 12-15 months

What You’ll Take On

Oversee the management of all aspects of benefits operations for International harmonised countries (UK, Spain, India, Ireland, and Turkey) including maintenance of benefits plans, reporting and integrations for plans in Workday

As needed, manage the international benefits operations team to carry out operational work to support the benefits team

Support the management of all other benefit plans outside of North America:

Understand end-to-end benefits processes managed by local HR teams, and identify opportunities to improve efficiency

Manage vendor and broker relationships, serving as a point of escalation to resolve operational issue escalations as well as managing ongoing activities

Support the renewal process by providing data such as census, cost, utilization, as well as feedback on vendor service

Support internal teams by managing RFP processes from an operational perspective during searches for new vendors

When new programs/vendors, routine renewals and/or changes to processes or systems occur, review configurations, file integration set-ups and other related updates, and undertake necessary testing

Ensure compliance with all local regulations, payroll and government data submissions, as well as internal data security protocols

Manage global program operations e.g., Fitness reimbursement, EAP, family building, business and assignee medical.

Responsible for operational aspects of all non-North American country-specific and global benefit program projects including:

Own the relationship with Project Management, use of RACI charts and creation of project plans

Ensure stakeholders are aware of what is going on and what their roles are

Manage testing processes including writing scenarios, resolving issues and obtaining sign-off

Collaborate with key stakeholders to resolve operational issues

Support with the preparation, and provide operational input for, non-North American country-specific and global benefit program communications

Manage operational support in the event of any acquisitions

Manage Benefits Operations team of 3

What You Bring

Bachelor’s degree and minimum of 4 years’ international benefits operations experience.

Knowledge of plan structures and familiarity with typical benefits offerings across all territories.

Understanding of all applicable statutory regulations, filings and compliance obligations related to employee benefit programs.

Exceptional high-touch customer service and follow-up skills.

Understanding of, and sensitivity to, cultural and regional differences.

Deep understanding of benefits plan configurations, integrations and reporting functionality in Workday.

Strong analytical, problem-solving, interpersonal and vendor management skills.

Excellent written, verbal, influencing and presentation skills.

Must be detail-oriented and take ownership of keeping track of and closing out open items.

Proficiency with Word, Excel, PowerPoint and Google Suite.

Proven ability to work collaboratively and effectively in a team environment, under pressure, with remote stakeholders in different time zones.

Capable of effective planning and priority setting.

Ability to manage several complex projects simultaneously while working under pressure to meet deadlines. Project management experience is required.

Strong track record of identifying and implementing process improvement.
