Senior Technical Animator

Love the space where art, animation and tech meet? Build the character pipeline and cinematic systems for our games in Unreal Engine 5, and shape how we make them.

careersite--jobs--form-overlay#showFormOverlay" data-careersite--jobs--form-overlay-target="coverButton">

Apply for this job

blocks--cover--scroll#handleScrollDown" title="Scroll to content">

Join our team at Shapefarm, where art, technology, and storytelling collide. We’re building games that connect players around the world, and we’re looking for talented creators who want to shape unforgettable experiences.

We're looking for a Senior Technical Animator who will play a key role in building and evolving the character and animation technology that bring our games to life. Working closely with Animation, Character Art, Technical Art, Design and Engineering, you will solve complex technical and creative challenges across the character and animation pipeline. You will be integral to the development of cinematics, including the animation systems and tools needed to deliver high-quality, performant cinematic experiences in Unreal Engine 5.

This role is suited to an experienced Technical Animator who enjoys working at the intersection of art, animation and technology and wants to have a meaningful influence on both the game and the way we make it.

📍 Location: Wellington, New Zealand 🏢 Working arrangement: Office-based - no permanent remote work or relocation support available.

🚀What you’ll do: Build and maintain Control Rigs and other runtime animation systems that support both animation authoring and in-game requirements.

Develop performant solutions for character dynamics, including cloth, hair, secondary motion and other procedural or simulated elements.

Work with Animators and Engineers to implement systems that provide the right balance between artistic control, visual quality and runtime performance.

Build, iterate and maintain Level Sequence and BP-driven cinematic assets.

Work with the Creative Director to realise their narrative vision via in-game 3D cinematics.

Drive the review schedule with Creative and Game Direction.

Act as a technical point of reference for the Animation and Character teams, providing guidance on complex technical challenges.

Mentor and support less experienced Technical Animators and share knowledge across the wider team.

Help establish technical standards and best practices and encourage consistent adoption across the team.

🛠️What we’re looking for: Qualifications & Portfolio 5+ years' professional experience in Technical Animation, Rigging or a related game development discipline.

Demonstrable experience working on AAA or high-quality console game production is highly desirable.

Strong Unreal Engine 5 and Maya experience.

Strong character rigging, skinning and deformation knowledge.

Experience with rigging and skinning tools in maya to create game ready rigs and skeletal meshes Experience with Unreal Control Rig and real-time animation systems.

Experience with character dynamics, procedural animation or secondary motion.

Some Python and working MEL knowledge.

Understanding of real-time performance considerations and optimisation.

Strong understanding of character anatomy and deformation.

A cohesive portfolio/reel demonstrating technical animation, rigging, tools and/or runtime animation systems. Where possible, clearly explain your role, the problem you were solving, your technical approach and the outcome.

Strong written and verbal communication skills.

A collaborative mindset and willingness to mentor and support other team members.

Desirable Experience The following would be advantageous: Experience shipping a console game.

Experience working on PlayStation and/or Xbox platforms.

Experience with complex creature or non-humanoid rigs desirable Experience with Unreal Animation Blueprints, Anim Dynamics, Control Rig, IK Rig/Retargeting and procedural animation systems.

Experience with mGear or an equivalent rigging framework 🤝At Shapefarm, we value people who Take initiative and see problems through to solutions.

Are comfortable owning their work and operating with a good degree of independence.

Know when to ask questions, share concerns or bring others into a decision.

Welcome feedback, share knowledge and are always looking for ways to improve.

Care about doing things well without losing sight of the bigger picture.

Treat people with respect, value different perspectives and contribute to an inclusive team.

Take responsibility for your decisions and learn from challenges along the way.

🌏 Location.

We’re welcoming applications from candidates who can work from our Wellington, New Zealand office.

Please note : This is an in-office role. Permanent remote work is not available, and we’re unable to provide relocation assistance. Applicants preferably already live in Wellington and can work from that office. You’ll also need to have the existing legal right to work in New Zealand.
