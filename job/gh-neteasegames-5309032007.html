Job Title: Specialist / Manager, Game Investments and Partnerships

Location: Singapore, Hong Kong

About the Role

As a core member of our corporate investment team, this role works closely with senior investment leaders on global initiatives spanning the full investment lifecycle. You will act as a trusted partner to our portfolio companies, help drive complex transactions to closing, and contribute to identifying the next wave of games and investment opportunities through industry research and network building.

We are looking for candidates who are passionate about games, and with strong business acumen, a high-ownership mindset, and strategic thinking, people who can see the full picture, identify what truly matters, and turn complexity into clear insights and actions.

Key Responsibilities

Portfolio Management: Serve as the primary day-to-day contact for portfolio companies. Monitor operational, financial, and business progress; build and maintain trusted relationships; prepare regular post-investment reports; identify risks and opportunities; and support the development and execution of post-investment strategies.

New Investment Opportunities Sourcing: Continuously monitor global gaming industry trends, gameplay innovations, and technologies to help identify new investment opportunities. Expand industry networks, explore commercial collaborations, and deepen the team’s industry knowledge and business insights.

Transactions and Exits: Identify and map potential buyers globally and explore transaction and exit opportunities. Support deal structuring, the development of exit proposals, commercial negotiations, and transaction closing.

Cross-Functional Collaboration: Work closely with internal business partners to advance complex transactions and address post-investment matters. Ensure that strategic priorities, management decisions, and key messages are communicated consistently across global teams.

AI Tools Application: Stay current on the latest AI developments, and actively use AI tools and develop AI applications tailored to the team.

Requirements

3–5 years of experience in investment-related fields (VC/PE, investment banking, consulting), or in corporate investment / strategy at gaming or technology companies.

Candidates with experience in game development related functions (Game Design, User Research, Business Development, Marketing, Publishing), who are interested in game investment are also welcome to apply.

Strong strategic thinking, business acumen and judgment, analytical and financial capability, and problem-solving skills.

Excellent cross-functional project management skills; ability to manage multiple independent workstreams simultaneously. Strong communication and negotiation skills, with integrity and empathy.

Proficiency in AI tools; experience developing or implementing AI tools or applications is a plus.

Fluency in both Chinese and English, required for effective communication with local and global teams.
