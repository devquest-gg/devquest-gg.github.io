CD PROJEKT RED is looking for a Senior Environment Concept Artist to join our studio in Boston and help us develop Cyberpunk 2 - the follow-up to Cyberpunk 2077 and the next major video game in the Cyberpunk franchise. As a Senior Environment Concept Artist, you’ll be a part of a team of talented artists, working in close collaboration with Art Directors to help establish the vision and visual direction for the game. If you are an experienced artist with a passion for world building and creating industry-leading RPGs, we want to hear from you! Responsibilities: Create high quality, creative and original environment concept art.&#xa0; Work closely with directors to establish a clear vision and direction for the game.

Collaborate and work as part of a team of artists, sharing information, ideas and knowledge to benefit the project as a whole.

Participate in peer group feedback sessions regularly, attending reviews, receiving feedback and making iterations.

Collaborate closely with other departments and teams to get the information and support needed to achieve goals and solve problems.

Maintain the production pipeline and meet deadlines.

A minimum of 5 years experience as a Concept Artist&#xa0;in game development.

In-depth knowledge of the Cyberpunk genre and RPGs with a passion for environment design.

Excellent communication skills with the ability to share creative ideas and concepts clearly and effectively.

Exceptional creative and artistic skills, with a portfolio demonstrating expertise in realistic and sci-fi environment concept art, mood paintings, 3D environment designs, blockouts and paintovers, key-frames, and the ability to create fast ideation sketches such as storyboards.

A solid background in traditional art such as industrial design, landscape painting or illustration with core foundational skills.

Excellent knowledge of both 2d and 3d design software.

Openness for constant feedback and sharing knowledge with other concept artists.

Ability to work collaboratively with other teams online in different time zones.
