Job Description

As a Senior Game Designer at Ubisoft Bucharest, you will design and refine game systems and gameplay for Tom Clancy's Division 2. You’ll work with the Game Director, design team and other disciplines to bring designs from concept to final polish.

What you’ll do Design, implement and tune game systems and gameplay , including balancing, character controls, cameras and enemy behavior Develop systems for the living world, economy, progression and onboarding Refine designs through an iterative approach and feedback from the team Collaborate with designers, programmers and artists to bring designs from concept to final polish Align your work with the game’s gameplay vision alongside the Game Director and design team Present design concepts and workflows through clear documentation and presentations Guide junior designers through feedback and collaboration What you bring to the team Experience Designing game systems and an understanding of game development processes and best practices Experience in developing video games , especially online games and shooters Ability to work with Blueprints in Unreal Engine, Snowdrop or similar game engines Work in a self-directed way and take ownership of designs from idea to implementation Listen attentively, welcome feedback and adapt your approach as designs evolve Solve design problems through creative thinking and iteration Communicate clearly and concisely in spoken and written English , including documentation and presentations Ability to comfortably use Confluence, Excel and Power Point to write your designs and present them.
