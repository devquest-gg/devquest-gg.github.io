Scopely is looking for a Mid/Senior 3D Animator to join the WWE Champions team in Bangalore!

At Scopely, we care deeply about what we do and want to inspire play, every day - whether in our work environments alongside our talented colleagues, or through our deep connections with our communities of players. We are a global team of game lovers who are developing, publishing and innovating the mobile games industry, connecting millions of people around the world daily.

WWE Champions is our ultimate and unique RPG puzzle match game featuring WWE superstars and legends across mobile and PC platforms. WWE Champions is the most downloaded WWE game to date, with a highly engaged and passionate community.

What You’ll Do

We’re looking for a Mid/Senior 3D Animator to help bring the biggest WWE Superstars to life in WWE Champions. In this role, you’ll create high-quality, expressive character animations that capture the unique personality, style, and signature moves of WWE Superstars while delivering responsive and engaging gameplay experiences. You’ll collaborate closely with character artists, tech art, design, engineering, and production to develop polished, game-ready animations that seamlessly integrate into the game. This is a hands-on role for an animator with a deep understanding of character performance, gameplay animation, and technical implementation, who can take animations from initial blocking through final polish and engine-ready delivery.

Create gameplay animations (combat, locomotion, reactions, traversal) using keyframe animation and motion capture, with strong posing, body mechanics, timing, and character performance

Clean up, retarget, and polish motion capture animations into responsive, production-ready gameplay sets, balancing visual quality with in-engine feel

Create or modify character rigs, transfer skin weights, and prepare animation-ready assets using HumanIK or similar rigging systems

Work with Gameplay Designers and Engineers on move sets, hit reactions, and animation-driven gameplay systems (blend trees, state machines, root motion)

Work closely with cross-functional teams, adapt quickly to feedback, and deliver high-quality animations within the constraints of a live-service, licensed IP production

Prepare, export, and validate animation assets (FBX) for Unity, and help fix rigging, skinning, and pipeline issues as they come up

What We’re Looking For

5+ years of professional experience as an animator in games, with a focus on gameplay/combat animation

Shipped at least one title with significant gameplay animation work (fighting, action, or sports genres a strong plus)

Strong understanding of rigging and skinning, ideally in Autodesk Maya

Strong understanding of mocap data, including cleanup and retargeting for gameplay.

Experience with facial and cinematic animation

Proficient in Autodesk Maya, plus at least one additional 3D animation package such as MotionBuilder, 3ds Max, or similar

Experience with HumanIK or similar rigging/retargeting systems

Good understanding of body mechanics, weight, timing, and readability under gameplay constraints (input lag, blending, canceling)

Experience preparing, exporting, and validating FBX assets for real-time engines, ideally Unity, including familiarity with animation state machines and blend trees

Good collaboration and communication skills working with Design, Engineering, and Art

A demo reel or portfolio showing shipped or production-quality gameplay animation work

Bonus

Have experience with AI-assisted animation tools in a production pipeline
