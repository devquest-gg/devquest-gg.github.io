Senior Manager, Game Production - Unpublished R&D Product

Riot Games

Senior Engineering Posted 2026-01-27
Apply on Riot Games’s site →

Riot Games was established in 2006 by entrepreneurial gamers who believe that player-focused development leads to exceptional games. In 2009, Riot released its debut title League of Legends to critical and player acclaim. Since then, Riot has expanded its portfolio and studios globally, continuing to create games that inspire and connect players around the world.

That’s where you come in.

As a Senior Manager, Game Production for one of Riot’s Unpublished R D products, you’ll lead the production effort in our Singapore studio, orchestrating the local development team and ensuring strong alignment with the senior game leadership based in Los Angeles. You’ll be the team captain responsible for translating the game’s creative vision into clear, actionable plans, maintaining the health of both the collaboration and the local production organization.

Your role is to foster a cohesive, high-performing team environment where creative excellence and operational discipline meet. You’ll drive delivery predictability, strengthen cross-studio communication, and ensure that our Singapore team contributes effectively to the overall success of the project.

Responsibilities:

Lead the Singapore production organization , aligning team goals and priorities with the game’s vision, strategy, and milestones set by the Los Angeles leadership team.

Serve as the primary accountable owner for collaboration health , ensuring clear communication, mutual trust, and smooth integration between studios.

Drive operational excellence by designing and maintaining scalable production systems, workflows, and accountability rituals that enable sustainable and predictable delivery.

Cascade strategic direction from senior game leadership into clear objectives and execution plans for Singapore-based teams.

Oversee cross-disciplinary coordination (art, design, engineering, QA, etc.) to ensure efficient collaboration and timely delivery of features, prototypes, and content.

Assess and manage risks , proactively addressing production challenges, dependencies, and resourcing needs.

Monitor production and collaboration health metrics , fostering transparency, alignment, and continuous improvement across all teams.

Support people management efforts in partnership with discipline leads, ensuring clear goals, career growth, and strong team morale.

Represent the Singapore team in strategic discussions, status updates, and cross-studio alignment meetings, ensuring both clarity and advocacy for local needs.

Model Riot’s values and leadership expectations , fostering psychological safety, inclusion, and a feedback-driven culture across teams.

Champion a player-focused mindset and be the voice of the player

Required Qualifications:

8+ years of experience in game production, operations, or project management roles within the gaming or entertainment industry.

Proven experience leading large, cross-disciplinary teams across multiple locations or studios.

Strong background in managing co-development or distributed development environments.

Experience translating creative vision into execution frameworks, milestone plans, and deliverables.

Track record of building and maintaining strong cross-functional relationships with leadership, partners, and vendors.

Skilled in risk assessment, capacity planning, and driving accountability through structured processes.

Deep understanding of production methodologies (Agile, Scrum, Kanban) and adaptability to R D environments.

Demonstrated success in improving delivery predictability, communication, and collaboration health.

Excellent communication, facilitation, and conflict-resolution skills.

Desired Qualifications:

2+ years managing large-scale collaborations between internal and external studios.

Experience shipping or supporting AAA, MMO, or live-service titles.

Proven ability to lead through ambiguity and rapid iteration in R D or early-stage development.

Experience managing production organizations of 30+ people or multi-team operations.

Experience in working with outsourcing vendors and teams

Our Perks:

Full relocation support

Full health insurance for you, your spouse and children

Open paid time off

Retirement benefits with company matching

Life insurance, parental leave, plus short-term and long-term disability

Play Fund so you can broaden and deepen your knowledge of our players and community through games

We will double down on your donations of time and money to non-profits
