The role

We are looking for a strong Software Development Manager to lead high-impact teams within the PlayStation SDK Developer Host Tools division.

This role requires a balanced leader, someone who can manage a high-performing team, while also maintaining strong technical depth to guide architecture, review designs, and support engineers at a code level when needed.

You will operate across both backend distributed systems and web-based and/or native frontends, helping to shape solutions that are both technically sound and highly usable for developers.

What you’ll be doing

Support, encourage, and help develop a team of engineers, fostering a positive and collaborative work environment

Assist with technical discussions and contribute to code reviews and system design

Work together with other groups and teams across different time zones

Help carry out the team s goals and follow the established plans

Manage daily tasks while keeping in mind the team’s long-term objectives

Promote a strong developer experience by focusing on ease of use, performance, and reliability

Recognize challenges, help remove obstacles, and clarify tasks in situations that are not well defined

What we are looking for

Must have

7–10 years of overall software engineering experience with 3–5+ years of experience managing engineering teams.

Strong technical background with hands-on understanding of backend/platform fundamentals

Ability to operate at code-level depth when needed (design reviews, debugging, guiding engineers)

Proven experience designing and delivering complex, distributed systems

Track record of building and scaling high-performing engineering teams

Experience working on developer tools, internal platforms, or productivity systems

Strong ability to influence and align stakeholders across teams

Excellent communication skills across both technical and non-technical audiences

Ability to operate effectively in fast-moving, ambiguous environments

Nice to have

Solid grasp of frontend systems, including JavaScript, React, and React Native

Familiarity with AI/ML-driven workflows or developer tooling

Background in performance profiling and developer ecosystems

Skilled in C# and .NET technologies

Experience with low-level languages such as Rust and C++
