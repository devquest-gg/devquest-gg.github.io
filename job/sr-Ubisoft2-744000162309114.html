3D Animator

Ubisoft Barcelona · Barcelona

Senior Animation Posted 2026-11-18 Full time
Apply on Ubisoft Barcelona’s site →

RESPONSIBILITIES: Create believable, high-quality animations and directly participating in implementing them in games.

Define the animation style and workflows for new products under the supervision of the Producer and Lead Artist.

Be able to rigging/skinning with low to medium polygon counts.

Take active part in the game creation process by interacting with the design team.

Contribute effectively to the art pipeline while understanding target platform constraints.

Have a strong time management skills to reach project deadlines/milestones.

Collaborate with the rest of the Art Team to obtain a consistent visual style for the game.

Work with the Programming Team to integrate your animations in the game while respecting technical constraints.

PORTFOLIO REQUIREMENTS In order for your application to be considered, you must submit a portfolio or other samples of your work.&#xa0;All portfolios must contain a detailed shot breakdown of the work. It must be very clear what you, the applicant, are responsible for in your reel, screenshots, scanned pictures, web page portfolio, etc.

QUALIFICATIONS Examples of basic cycles of a character. Cycle of walk, run, idle, attack, jump, etc.

Examples of physics of movement. It can be a character doing parkour, moving weight, a skateboarder, etc.

Examples of combination of cycles. Idle + attack, idle + run + attack, etc.

It would also be interesting if among all the examples there is some quadrupeds, or characters of more than two feet.

At least 4 or more years of experience in 3D Studio Max, Maya or Blender.

Have a clear understanding of character motion, camera movement, physics-based motion and of the fundamental principles of animation.

Be able to define and execute creative and stylish animations (vehicles, objects, characters).

Positive attitude with the ability to receive and provide objective feedback Be a team player: we use agile development methods, where the team members are in permanent contact. Open mind and good communication skills are a must.

Attention to detail, meticulous and organized working habits. Ability to multi-task and to adapt easily to change.

Good command of English.

Work permit for Spain obtained prior to applying for the position is a must.

Bonus Solid background in academic drawing, sketching and/or illustration.

Working Unity experience.

Have modelling skills (2D/3D), knowledge and experience of Blender, Unity, particle effects creation programs (e.g. combustion, particle illusion, Fork) and motion graphics programs (eg After Effects).

Experience working on mobile games.

Passion for games and some knowledge of the games industry.

Spanish or Catalan is a plus.
