Position Overview：

Support acquisition and operations decisions with data; responsible for growth attribution, retention / LTV modeling and ad monetization analysis. The analytical core for single-product data review.

Responsibilities：

Build and maintain core metric dashboards (DAU / retention / ARPU / CPI / ROAS / LTV)

Conduct acquisition attribution analysis and channel ROI evaluation to support budget allocation

Analyze retention funnels and user segmentation; identify churn points and optimization opportunities

Support A/B experiment design and effectiveness evaluation for version updates and events

Produce aggregated data briefs to support decision-makers (including aggregated figures compliant with regulatory requirements)

Requirements:

Bachelor s degree or above in Data Science, Statistics, Computer Science, Mathematics, Economics or related fields

Proficient in SQL; master Python or R for data processing and analysis

Familiar with data visualization tools (Tableau / Power BI / Looker, etc.)

Statistical thinking and foundational knowledge of A/B testing concepts

Fluent English

Preferred Qualifications:

Internship experience in data analysis / growth / the gaming industry

Understanding of mobile ad attribution (MMP: AppsFlyer / Adjust, etc.)

Understanding of game economy systems and monetization models
