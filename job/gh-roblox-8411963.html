The GSOC is Roblox’s centralized 24/7 security operations function, monitoring, analyzing, and responding to incidents across our global footprint. As Manager, GSOC, you will own the day-to-day operation and maturation of the GSOC: the shift supervisors, operators, and analysts who turn phone calls, alarms, access issues, travel alerts, and sensitive escalations into standardized action, stakeholder notification, and documentation. You will report to the Sr. Manager, Global Security, Intelligence Operations, and partner closely with Campus Security, Executive Protection, Protective Intelligence, Facilities, Legal, People, Communications, and InfoSec. This is a hands-on leadership role in a maturing program: you will run the operation as it is today while building the processes, metrics, and regional consistency it needs to scale globally.

You Will:

Lead 24/7 operations across day, swing, and overnight shifts, including staffing, scheduling, passdowns, and the contract workforce and vendor partners that support the operation

Own incident management: ensure incidents are classified, escalated, communicated, and documented against the GSOC severity model and response SLAs, and serve as an escalation point and incident commander for declared physical security incidents

Mature the GSOC SOP library, run regular SOP audits, and build the training, certification, and quality-assurance programs that keep operators consistent and prepared

Define and report GSOC KPIs, lead after-action reviews, and turn lessons learned into process, tooling, and training improvements

Steward the GSOC technology stack for access control, video, alarm monitoring, threat monitoring, emergency notification, and travel risk, working with vendors and internal partners to improve configuration, integration, and reliability

Extend GSOC capabilities to new regions and offices, and support travel security, events, data centers, and executive protection programs

Build trusted relationships with Campus Security, Facilities, Legal, Communications, People, and InfoSec, and brief senior leaders clearly during and after incidents

You Have:

A Bachelor’s degree or equivalent experience in security, intelligence, criminal justice, operations management, or a related field [confirm HR-approved phrasing]

[8+] years of experience in security operations, emergency or crisis management, or intelligence, in corporate, government, or military settings, including [3+] years leading a 24/7 operations center or similar team

Demonstrated people leadership, including hiring, coaching, performance management, shift coordination, and managing contract or vendor teams

Proven experience managing incidents in high-pressure environments, with strong judgment, escalation discipline, and clear written and verbal communication

Experience building or improving SOPs, training programs, and performance metrics for an operations team

Familiarity with physical security and access control systems, video management, critical event management or travel risk platforms, and collaboration tools such as Slack, Jira, and Google Workspace

A calm, decisive, and collaborative approach, with high standards for yourself and your team and openness to feedback

The ability to work flexible hours, including nights, weekends, and on-call, to support a global 24/7 environment

Preferred: a Master’s degree; ICS/NIMS training; experience with global or multi-site security operations; experience with workflow automation or AI-assisted monitoring tools; relevant certifications (CPP, PSP, CEM, or similar)
