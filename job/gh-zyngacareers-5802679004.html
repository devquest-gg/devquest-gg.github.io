Position Overview:

As a Senior Game Designer, you will be responsible for new features, up to and including bold beat scope and impact. You will take on more complex design challenges and lead the development of key game mechanics, levels, and features. As an adept in systems, mechanics, and player experience; you will be accountable for maintaining coherence and balance across entire game. Support the leaders by providing experiential insights from the player perspective.

What You ll Do:

Ideate and design new levels, gameplay, systems or features with minimal supervision

Collaborate with developers, artists, product managers other stakeholders to integrate design elements into the game

Ensure technical feasibility, clear visual direction and high ROI from design solutions

Conduct research and apply competition insights to support design concepts and ideas

Create design solutions to drive business impact while leveraging player experience and game KPI’s

Analyze and deconstruct competitor games to stay up to date on the market share actionable insights learnings

Present compelling design pitches project visions to stakeholders

Create maintain comprehensive game design documentation for the team

Tune and balance complex gameplay mechanics to ensure an engaging player experience

Playtest designs, conduct player research and iterate on designs based on feedback

Prototype, propose new designs, and develop solutions to improve existing systems and address complex design problems, ensuring all efforts are explicitly aligned with the high-level strategic goals of the game

Ensure a high level of quality to achieve the best player experience

What You Bring:

Previous experience as a Game Designer

Established verbal, written, and interpersonal communication skills

Ability to understand and translate players’ requirements into product

Clear cross‐functional communication at the team level, capable of explaining and evangelizing a design in conversation with peers
