Who are we?

Tripledot Studios is one of the largest independent mobile games companies in the world.

We are a multi-award-winning organisation, with a global 2,500+ strong team across 12 studios.

Our expanded portfolio includes some of the biggest titles in mobile gaming, collectively reaching top chart positions around the world and engaging over 25 million daily active users.

Tripledot’s guiding principle is that when people love what they do, what they do will be loved by others.

This role is based within our studio, Belka Games

Belka Games is a game development company ranked by Sensor Tower among the top 30 highest-grossing game developers in Europe. Our games have reached more than 110 million players worldwide

Role Overview

We are looking for a Product Director to lead and strengthen our Hybrid Casual direction , combining ownership and development of existing live products with the creation, validation, and launch of new product initiatives

As part of the Belka Games leadership team, you will contribute to the company’s broader product strategy and long-term development

The role includes responsibility for product strategy, business performance of the Hybrid Casual portfolio, including its revenue, profitability, long-term value, and investment efficiency. You will also build strong product teams and establish a systematic and repeatable approach to creating and scaling successful Hybrid Casual products.

Key Responsibilities

Part 1 - Live product ownership

Define and implement the product strategy for the Hybrid Casual portfolio, ensuring sustainable growth and long-term product health

Drive the business performance of the direction, including revenue growth, profitability, long-term portfolio value, and investment efficiency

Drive improvements in retention, engagement, monetization, product quality, and other key product and business metrics

Balance short-term performance priorities with long-term product investments and allocate resources across the portfolio accordingly

Define product and LiveOps priorities based on data, business impact, investment efficiency, and growth potential

Make decisions on the launch, scaling, transformation, or termination of product initiatives

Ensure reliable execution of product updates and scale successful features, mechanics, and practices across live products

Build transparent cross-functional decision-making processes and align Product, Development, Analytics, Marketing, and other business functions around shared goals and priorities

Partner with the Belka Games leadership team on the company’s product strategy and long-term development

Hire and develop senior product leaders, build autonomous and scalable teams

Part 2 - RnD

Define and implement the R D strategy for the Hybrid Casual direction

Identify new product opportunities, segments, and mechanics, and manage a pipeline of hypotheses, experiments, prototypes, and product initiatives

Apply a data-informed and hypothesis-driven approach to identifying, prioritizing, and validating new product opportunities

Own the development of new product concepts from ideation and prototyping through validation, soft launch, global launch, and scaling

Define success criteria and evaluate initiatives against relevant product, market, and business benchmarks

Develop business cases for new product investments, including the opportunity, expected business impact, required resources, and key risks

Make investment decisions on the further development, scaling, transformation, or termination of product initiatives

Establish clear validation stages, decision criteria, and investment gates to improve the predictability and efficiency of R D outcomes

Systematize learnings from experiments and launches, scale effective practices across initiatives, and eliminate consistently ineffective approaches

Required Skills, Knowledge and Expertise

Senior product leadership experience in the games industry as a Product Director, Head of Product, Executive Producer, or in a comparable role

Strong experience with live mobile F2P games, ideally within Hybrid Casual or adjacent Casual genres

Proven experience defining and implementing product strategy

Experience managing a portfolio of live products and new product initiatives across the full product lifecycle

Experience owning business performance and allocating investment and resources across a product portfolio

Deep understanding of F2P monetization, LiveOps, product economies, progression systems, and mobile games market dynamics, with proven experience improving retention, engagement, monetization, revenue, profitability, and long-term product health

Strong analytical and business skills, including experience building business cases and making data-informed product and investment decisions

Experience building autonomous cross-functional teams and hiring and developing senior Product Managers, Game Producers, or other product leaders

Excellent leadership, communication, cross-functional management, and stakeholder alignment skills

Ability to make clear decisions in uncertain conditions and take ownership of their business consequences

Fluent in English and Russian

What Success Looks Like

A strong and scalable Hybrid Casual portfolio with sustainable product and business performance

Growth in the key product and business metrics of live products

Strong, autonomous product teams led by capable product leaders

A predictable and repeatable approach to creating, validating, and scaling new products

A continuous pipeline of high-potential R D initiatives

Increased maturity and effectiveness of the product function across the company
