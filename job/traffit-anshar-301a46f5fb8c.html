UE Gameplay Programmer

Founded in 2012, Anshar Studios focuses on 1st and 3rd party game development for all major gaming platforms.

Currently, we're employing over 180 specialists in our studio in Katowice and we are very open to new talented developers willing to join us.

Working with Anshar gives you the opportunity to develop your skills, participate in challenging projects, and team up with a bunch of skilled people with a passion for video games. with Anshar gives you the opportunity to develop your skills, participate in challenging projects, and team up with a bunch of skilled people with a passion for video games.

Anshar Studios is looking for a talented UE Gameplay Programmer to join our team on-site in Katowice, Poland or remotely.

Responsibilities:

- Develop features and contribute to sub-systems under the guidance of senior programmers. - Participate in technical discussions and propose solutions for assigned tasks. - Troubleshoot technical issues within your area of work and escalate when needed. - Collaborate with designers, artists, and other programmers to ensure seamless integration of gameplay elements. - Implement gameplay features in Unreal Engine according to provided designs and specifications. - Write clean, efficient, and maintainable code following team standards and best practices. - Debug and optimize your own code to ensure performance and stability. - Participate in code reviews, both giving and receiving constructive feedback. - Contribute ideas to gameplay design and implementation discussions. - Create clear technical documentation for the features you develop. - Demonstrate care and commitment to quality software development.

What You need:

Requirements:

At least of 3 years of experience as Gameplay Programmer

Credited as a programmer in at least 1 commercial video game

Working knowledge of Gameplay Ability System and Gameplay Tags.

Basic familiarity with multiplayer game development and networking in UE.

Good C++ knowledge

Problem-solving and debugging skills.

Reliable, organized, eager to learn.

Good team communication and teamwork skills.

Understanding of game creation processes and pipelines.

Intermediate Polish (B1/B2 level)

Intermediate English (B1/B2 level)

Pluses:

Unreal Engine proficiency

Familiarity with Gameplay Ability System and Gameplay Tags.

C++ proficiency

Knowledge of SOLID principles and common design patterns.

Fluent English (C1/C2 level)

Passion for video games

Passion for cRPG and P&P RPG

What we offer:

Wage 9 000 up to 17 000 zł on a contract (B2B + VAT) or recalculated on contract of employment depending on skills and experience.

Flexible work hours

Private life and health care insurance

Co-financing of Multisport cards

English lessons

Help with relocation

No crunch

No non-compete policy

Pet-friendly office

Co-financed, and treated as workdays, participation in a selection of conferences and workshops
