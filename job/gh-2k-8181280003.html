WHO WE ARE

We are a diverse team of developers driven by a passion for our art, united by our core values and inspired by a culture of inclusivity to build amazing games that thrill players everywhere. We pursue growth and innovation in an environment of safety and trust. Our culture is built on the belief that the more varied voic es in our collective will strengthen our team and our games. We are looking for our next teammate who will raise our bar and make u s better.

WHAT WE NEED

We’re looking for a Gameplay UX Designer with a deep interest in problem solving, excellent communication skills, and a player-first mindset to join our Experience Design team. You thrive in collaborative, highly iterative environments where you balance player needs with design constraints and production realities. Working side-by-side with UI artists, technical designers, engineers, and game designers, you act as the bridge between creative vision and technical execution.

WHAT YOU LL DO

Help Drive the UX Vision: Guide conversations with cross-functional partners and take ownership of feature design against product specifications.

Cross-Functional Collaboration: Shepherd UX design efforts through leadership approvals, iteration cycles, and final implementation with support from Production and Leadership.

Build Comprehensive Documentation: Communicate the UX vision clearly by developing competitive analyses, persona designs, user journeys, wireframes, flow diagrams, and interactive prototypes.

Advocate for the Player: Ensure all designs are rigorously tested and validated, prioritizing usability, accessibility, and an exceptional player experience.

Bridge Design and Execution: Work seamlessly alongside engineering and UI art to ensure technical feasibility and flawless execution of the user experience.

WHAT WILL MAKE YOU A GREAT FIT

Effective Communicator: Exceptional ability to present complex design ideas and rationale clearly through both written and verbal channels to varied stakeholders.

Design Expertise: Deep understanding of interaction and layout design.

Design Systems: Proven background in creating, maintaining, and extending robust design systems.

Tool Proficiency: High proficiency in industry standard UX tools (e.g., Figma, ProtoPie, etc.).

BENEFICIAL QUALIFICATIONS / NICE TO HAVES

Proficiency in motion graphics, animation, or visual effects as they relate to UI/UX feedback loops.

Technical Familiarity: Experience working directly in game engines (e.g., Unreal Engine, Unity, or proprietary engines), utilizing design toolsets, visual scripting, and production pipelines.
