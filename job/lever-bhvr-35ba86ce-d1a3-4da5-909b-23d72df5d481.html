Make your mark on a live game enjoyed by players worldwide, join Behaviour Interactive as a Senior DevOps Programmer.

Are you ready to take on new technical challenges within a passionate and collaborative team? Behaviour Interactive is looking for an experienced DevOps to contribute to the development of a live online game, co-developed with an external partner and released on PC and consoles.

In this senior role,  you’ll play a key role in shaping cloud computing strategies and infrastructure for a game that’s already live and evolving. You’ll be responsible for designing and maintaining backend systems, implementing cloud-based services, and ensuring smooth deployment pipelines. This position requires deep expertise in cloud technologies, DevOps principles, Infrastructure as Code, and cloud environments.
