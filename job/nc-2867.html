UI/UX Designer

Nordcurrent · Vilnius

Mid Design Posted 2024-05-07 Full time
Apply on Nordcurrent’s site →

As a UI/UX Designer, you ll be responsible for producing intuitive interfaces that form a positive player experience. You ll collaborate with other members of our game team to create beautiful and engaging mobile games.

Your Key Responsibilities:

Designing intuitive, accessible, and appealing interfaces for mobile games;

Working together with the project manager, designers, and artists to create a seamless game experience;

Creating game-ready visual elements according to specifications (windows, pop-ups, buttons, etc.);

Taking ownership of establishing and maintaining guidelines for cohesive visual language throughout the game’s UI;

Providing mock-ups and prototypes in Figma;

Staying up to date with the latest UI/UX design trends and techniques.

A Successful Candidate Must Have :

1+ year of hands-on experience as a UI Designer in mobile game development;

Understanding of principles of developing user interfaces for mobile games and have a strong grasp on specifics of mobile device usability;

A high-quality portfolio of UI designs for (mobile) games;

A good command of English (B1+) and solid communication/collaboration skills;

A deep proficiency in graphic design software, such as Adobe Creative Suite and Figma.

Extra Great to Have :

Experience working in related fields (graphic designer, 2D artist, or similar). Extra points for artwork examples in game dev industry!

Familiarity with Unity game engine;

An interest and understanding of typography.

Please note: applications without samples of work will not be considered.

Salary : From 2630 EUR/month (gross)
