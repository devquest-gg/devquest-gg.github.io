ABOUT VOODOO

Founded in 2013, Voodoo is a tech company that creates mobile games and apps with a mission to entertain the world. Gathering 800 employees, 7 billion downloads, and over 200 million active users, Voodoo is the #3 mobile publisher worldwide in terms of downloads after Google and Meta. Our portfolio includes chart-topping games like Mob Control and Block Jam, alongside popular apps such as BeReal and Wizz.

TEAM

Our Corporate teams support the company's performance and growth whilst putting people at the core of everything they do. As a French tech leader and global successful company, our Corporate teams are constantly looking for ways to innovate and increase organization efficiency to keep Voodoo ahead of the market. Voodoo's Corporate function is composed of three teams: HR, Legal and Finance, working together to drive Voodoo's success.

ROLE

We're looking for a Senior Finance Data & Systems Analyst – Pigment Lead, AI & BI to join our Corporate team. In this role, you'll be the internal expert and owner of our Pigment platform, while driving data, automation, and AI initiatives to elevate the Finance team's performance and service quality across FP&A and Accounting.

- Progressively take full ownership of Pigment's rollout and upkeep, reducing dependency on the external service provider and becoming the go-to internal expert on the tool

- Optimize platform configuration, drive integration development, and build a strategic roadmap to support consolidation and reporting needs across FP&A and Accounting

- Design and implement, together with the Head of FP&A, a structured budgeting process in Pigment, including workflow, calendar, and contributor governance

- Adapt and evolve Pigment use cases tied to the Accounting close process, in collaboration with the Head of Accounting

- Establish robust operational controls, including disciplined change management and a comprehensive financial data governance framework (definitions, quality, single source of truth, access governance)

- Lead data and automation projects beyond Pigment to improve Finance team performance, including self-service analytics, reconciliation automation, and variance analysis

- Drive the deployment of AI agents to replace manual processes and enhance the Finance team's operational efficiency

- Scope and contribute to building priority Finance dashboards (Looker, Tableau, Power BI, or similar), ensuring metric consistency across Pigment, BI, and Finance reporting

- Develop and maintain documentation, training materials, and user support processes to champion best practices across the Finance team

- Mentor and grow a junior analyst or intern, progressively delegating Pigment, data, or reporting responsibilities

PROFILE

- 5–8 years of experience in Finance Data, FP&A, or Finance Systems roles, ideally in consulting or a tech scale-up environment

- Hands-on experience with a Pigment rollout or run, or a similar EPM tool (Anaplan, Adaptive Insights, Hyperion, etc.) - strongly preferred

- Demonstrated ability to internalize a tool previously managed by an external service provider, with a mandate to build internal autonomy quickly

- Comfortable working with FP&A, Accounting, and Data/IT stakeholders alike

- Some experience with SQL or Python and BI tools (Looker, Tableau, Power BI, or similar)

- Genuine enthusiasm for applying AI to Finance, including no-code/low-code tools, agents, and LLMs

- Ability to structure a data governance approach at the scale of a Finance function

- Familiarity with accounting close processes is a plus

- Prior mentoring experience (junior analyst, intern, or apprentice) is a plus

- Fluent in both French and English
