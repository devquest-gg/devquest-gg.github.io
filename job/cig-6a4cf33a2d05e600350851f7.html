About Games Latest Join us 54 Senior Motion Capture Specialist Studio: Manchester See all offers Category: Animation See all offers This is a rare opportunity to join Cloud Imperium Games in the exciting, challenging and rewarding world of Performance Capture. In this role, you will be working in a state-of-the-art performance capture studio, on one of the largest sound stages in the UK. This opening is a unique chance to work in a dynamic, engaging position experiencing wildly varied shoots with incredible talent, and a depth which encompasses every aspect of the motion capture pipeline. Over the years, our team has set the standard for exceptional shoot execution, world-class facilities and continuous pursuit of high fidelity, accurate data solving.

We’re looking for a determined, ambitious and motivated individual who can influence, drive and elevate our team.

For a glimpse into our world, take a look at the entertaining and insightful Inside Star Citizen episode, Dev Diary - Performance Artist, revealing our shoots from the perspective of a performer.

Founded in 2012, Cloud Imperium Games creates cutting-edge videogames that defy expectations. We’re currently developing Star Citizen, a record-breaking multiplayer online space sim, and Squadron 42, a cinematic single-player adventure set in the same universe. Join us as we break boundaries and make videogame history.

What do our Senior Motion Capture Specialists do? Influence all aspects of performance capture shoots, including:

Preparing talent for capture with precision and attention to wellbeing and actor comfort

Calibration and body capture with our Optitrack system, responsible for data quality, take management, team communication and backups

Fitting, aiming and recording facial performances using Faceware HMCs and collaborating with the Facial Animation team to optimise footage

Set up and operate our in-house realtime solution, streaming live data into our game engine. Work alongside Directors, Designers and Animators managing characters, props, levels, animation and cameras throughout live shoots

Prop building and set construction using timber and scaffolding systems

Manage and maintain the motion capture system and stage. Test and troubleshoot issues, actively participate and contribute to innovative solutions and improvements, embracing new technology and development Trim, track, solve and retarget motion capture data utilising strong attention to detail and commitment to achieving the highest standards of quality Actively pursue technological advances and practice improvements through prescribed and self-initiated R&D What do we expect from our Senior Motion Capture Specialists? Proven experience working in motion capture, television, theatre or film productions On-set and post experience using Motive, Shogun or equivalent optical capture software In-depth experience working with MotionBuilder or other 3D software packages Confident and composed in active, fast-paced environments Team-orientated with excellent interpersonal and communication skills working with a variety of personalities Solution-focused individual with the ability to react to challenges with both openness and urgency Highly time-proficient, capable of providing accurate time estimates and exceptionally efficient with workload balancing and prioritisation Ability to gather, analyse, and act on feedback. Openness to critique and willingness to improve Strong work ethic and motivation to facilitate and meet high expectations during challenging shoot days Pluses Experience supervising or floor-managing shoots Clear leadership qualities and proficiency managing a team Hands-on familiarity with building and maintaining motion capture systems Diversity Statement

Fraud Statement

We are aware of people receiving job offers that fraudulently allege to be from CIG. These types of fraud can be carried out through false websites, fake email addresses claiming to be from our company, or social media. We do not ask for your personal info like bank account numbers, identification numbers, etc through social media or chat-based apps, nor do we request or send money for the purchase of business equipment.

If you suspect fraud, please report it to your local authorities, as well as reaching out to us at info @ cloudimperiumgames.com with any information you may have.
