About the Role

As the Head of Market Development for Roblox in Japan, you will serve as a cross-functional growth leader driving our content ecosystem, cultural relevance, and local market expansion. You will also assist with global market expansion by bringing Japan IP, studios, companies and related entities to the global audience. You will wear multiple hats across IP acquisition, influencer and creator initiatives, go-to-market partnerships, and commercial expansion— bringing leading Japanese IP and partners to Roblox while developing new opportunities to grow our presence across the market.

You will report directly to the Head of Japan for Roblox and work closely with teams across Roblox’s global International Division.

You Will

Lead IP Content Acquisition: Identify, negotiate, and secure partnerships with leading Japanese IP holders, entertainment companies, and game publishers to bring compelling content and experiences to Roblox.

Drive Influencer Creator Initiatives: Work with Marketing and Developer Relations to build collaborations with Japanese influencers, streamers, talent agencies, and social/video platforms that drive user growth and cultural relevance.

Expand Platform Commercial Partnerships: Manage local app store relationships and featuring opportunities, while growing Roblox’s licensing and retail gift card distribution across Japan.

Lead Strategic Market Initiatives: Build new business opportunities, test localized growth strategies, and take ownership of high-priority projects to address emerging market needs.

Drive Cross-Functional Execution: Lead partner initiatives from deal development through launch and ongoing optimization, using financial analysis and market insights to inform Japan leadership and global stakeholders.

You Have

Extensive experience in business development, strategic partnerships, or content acquisition within gaming, digital media, entertainment, or the creator economy.

Deep knowledge of Japan’s entertainment and IP landscape, with a strong network across IP holders, talent agencies, digital platforms, and distribution partners.

A proven track record of negotiating commercial agreements and delivering consumer-facing partnerships and growth initiatives.

Experience across platform and distribution ecosystems, including app stores, retail, licensing, or multi-channel user acquisition.

Professional fluency in Japanese and English, with the ability to navigate local business practices and align global stakeholders.

You Are

An agile, builder-mindset leader who thrives in ambiguity, enjoys wearing multiple hats, and can seamlessly pivot between high-level deal negotiations and hands-on market execution.

Culturally plugged-in and creative, with a sharp instinct for what resonates with Japanese gamers, creators, and mainstream audiences.

Strategic yet resourceful , able to balance immediate growth opportunities with a long-term roadmap for Japan market expansion.
