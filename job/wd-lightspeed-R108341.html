Business Unit LIGHTSPEED STUDIOS is made up of passionate players who advance the art & science of game development through great stories, great gameplay, and advanced technology. We are focused on bringing next generation experiences to gamers who want to enjoy them anywhere, anytime, across multiple genres and devices.

What the Role Entails As Technical Director, you will define and drive technical strategy, ensuring our engineering decisions directly support our long-term product goals. You'll work closely with the Creative Director and core leadership to set and sustain the technical vision, keeping engineering decisions aligned with the creative direction and built to serve a global audience. The role leads a distributed engineering organization, partnering day to day with development teams across multiple regions and time zones.

Partner with the Creative Director and core leadership to define and maintain the project s technical vision, and build the technical roadmap that supports it.

Work alongside design, art, and other disciplines to keep development efficient and unblocked.

Set the studio s technical standards and practices, holding the bar on stability, performance, and quality across all technical deliverables.

Ensure the technical foundation complements and enhances the game s narrative and gameplay.

Build and lead a high-performing core tech team; mentor and develop the broader engineering group.

Own implementation of core technical systems, from architecture through ship.

Foster an environment that values creativity, professional growth, and diversity.

Keep the team and studio leadership aligned on technical direction, trade-offs, and priorities.

Who We Look For Bachelor s degree in Computer Science, or equivalent practical experience.

10 years in the game industry, including 5 years leading AAA engineering teams.

Strong hands-on programming skills, proficient in C and a track record of building scalable core game systems.

Deep understanding of the game production pipeline.

Experience managing technical organizations at scale — process, structure, and technical governance.

Multi-platform AAA development and shipping experience.

Preferred Experience leading distributed engineering teams across multiple regions and time zones.

Unreal Engine experience.
