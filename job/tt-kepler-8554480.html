Assist in multiplatform development to ensure projects can ship to a range of different platforms.

Profile and optimise projects to meet required frame budgets across different platforms Work closely with other developers to craft fun and engaging gameplay.

🛠️ What we’re looking for: All-rounder when it comes to C++ programming, with an in depth understanding of the language and how to apply it effectively to game development.

Can translate game design and creative intentions into technical solutions.

In depth knowledge of shipping games using Unreal Engine.

Excellent analytical and problem-solving skills.

Proficiency in maths and 3D geometry.

Ability to work with accountability in a high-autonomy environment.

Working experience on multiple platforms.

Intimate knowledge of feature development from prototype to completion.
