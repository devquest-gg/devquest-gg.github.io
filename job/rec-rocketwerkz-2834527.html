SENIOR BUILD ENGINEER Auckland | Full-time, 40 hrs a week | Salary NZ$120K - 160K negotiable depending on experience | Enterprise team A bit about us… RocketWerkz is an ambitious PC video games studio based on Auckland’s waterfront in New Zealand. Founded by Dean Hall, creator of DayZ, we’re independently run and backed by one of the world’s largest games companies. We make deep, systems-driven games, including Icarus, Stationeers and Kitten Space Agency.

Our Enterprise team is a broad group of senior specialists who help develop BRUTAL , the framework our next generation of games is built on. Today, BRUTAL ships to our own studios and publicly on nuget.org , across Windows, Linux, macOS and Android.

We need you! BRUTAL is still growing, reaching more platforms, more teams and larger projects, so every release gets more complex. We’re looking for a Senior Build Engineer to own how it’s built, tested and released: deciding how it should work, not just keeping it running. You’ll join our existing DevOps engineer, so the two of you can cover each other and share the load.

A day in the life… As part of the Enterprise team, you’ll: Design BRUTAL’s build pipelines for both native C/C++ and managed .NET binaries, across every platform we support Assist developers with the release of the dozens of BRUTAL packages we maintain, across multiple NuGet feeds Advocate for processes that verify packages are well-tested, complete, with correct licenses attached and binaries signed Scale up our testing and benchmarking suites, striking the balance between regular triggered checks, and staggered scheduled testing to catch regressions over time Ensure our runners, containers, and images are kept healthy, up-to-date, and reproducible as we scale Respond quickly to events as they happen, and provide support when machines/services stop working Support the team as we expand the scope of BRUTAL to additional platforms going forward Anticipate hardware and infrastructure requirements, and turn those into clear (and persuasive) IT requests Crush recurring pain-points in our local and automated processes, to deliver better outcomes for the team Measure time to delivery, and continue to find ways to eliminate bottlenecks and improve reliability Continue to learn and be curious about what we do, and keep up to date with modern practices, and document what you build Promote a positive culture around CI/CD and source control within the team, while ensuring developers can stay on-task doing what they do best
