Game Designer - Playable Ads

Moon Active · Kyiv · Remote

Mid Design Remote Posted 2026-07-29 Full time
Apply on Moon Active’s site →

Playable Ads are interactive mini-games that are used to engage and attract new users to download and play our games.

As a Game Designer you will be in charge of the Game design and polishing the game mechanics and game-flows of our playable ads as well as testing and gaining insights based on how the playable performed.

Responsibilities

- Design levels for top performing playable ads for leading games, to excite, engage, and bring new players to download our games.

- Research and develop innovative game mechanics for playable ads.

- Work closely with game developers and provide constructive feedback on playable ad mechanics and experience.

- Analyze performance data to optimize game mechanics and conversions.

- Collaborate with the game design team with improving our content pipeline and processes.

- Stay current on mobile game advertising trends and best practices.

- Support and collaborate with the team from a level design perspective (Production, QA, Art, Game Design, etc).

Requirements

- Product management role experience in the casual gaming space, or 2+ years of experience designing levels and game mechanics for casual mobile games.

- Strong passion for gaming, particularly with regards to casual mobile games.

- Positive and creative mind with a passion for the level design craft (showcased with a relevant work portfolio).

- Solid grasp of game design methodologies and up-to-date knowledge of the casual audience, casual game markets and player motivations.

- Excellent interpersonal skills to clearly and effectively communicate feedback and design ideas.

- Strong self-driven work ethic and ability to work effectively in an agile team environment.

- Ability to incorporate feedback from your team into your work and use it to learn and grow in your role.

- Good spoken and written English is a MUST.
