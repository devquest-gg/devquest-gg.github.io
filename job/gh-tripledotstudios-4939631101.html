Who are we?

Tripledot Studios is one of the largest independent mobile games companies in the world.

We are a multi-award-winning organisation, with a global 2,500+ strong team across 12 studios.

Our expanded portfolio includes some of the biggest titles in mobile gaming, collectively reaching top chart positions around the world and engaging over 25 million daily active users.

Tripledot’s guiding principle is that when people love what they do, what they do will be loved by others.

We’re building a company we’re proud of. One filled with driven, incredibly smart and detail-orientated people, who LOVE making games.

Our ambition is to be the most successful games company in the world, and we’re just getting started.

Role Overview

We re looking for a smart, driven User Acquisition Manager to help grow our rewarded user acquisition business across multiple Tripledot Games.

You will manage a portfolio of rewarded channels, identify the strongest channel-and-game opportunities, and scale successful campaigns through continuous experimentation and optimisation. Working alongside senior growth leaders and cross-functional teams, you will combine data, creative thinking and commercial judgement to drive sustainable player growth.

Key Responsibilities

Manage and grow a portfolio of rewarded user acquisition channels across multiple Tripledot Games.

Launch, monitor and optimise paid user acquisition campaigns across rewarded partners and other key acquisition channels.

Own campaign performance, budget allocation, ROI and spend optimisation across your portfolio.

Analyse campaign performance and player acquisition data, turning insights into clear optimisation decisions.

Scale successful channel-and-game combinations while continuously refining the portfolio based on performance.

Partner with Product Managers, Analytics, BI and UA Leads to define experiments, evaluate results and support growth priorities.

Collaborate with designers to develop, test and iterate on creative concepts that improve campaign performance.

Use BI, measurement and AI tools to improve analysis, execution and decision-making.

Work with cross-functional teams to uncover new growth opportunities through experimentation.

Communicate performance, insights and strategic recommendations clearly to stakeholders.

Take initiative in expanding the team s capabilities and identifying new rewarded acquisition opportunities.

Required Skills, Knowledge and Expertise

2–5 years of experience in User Acquisition, Performance Marketing or a similar growth-focused role.

Experience in mobile gaming is strongly preferred; experience with rewarded apps or rewarded acquisition partners is highly desirable.

Strong understanding of mobile user acquisition, performance marketing principles and rewarded acquisition channels.

Experience managing paid campaigns across platforms

Experience with mobile measurement platforms such as AppsFlyer, Adjust or similar MMPs.

Strong analytical and technical skills, with the ability to make data-driven decisions using large datasets.

Experience working with BI and visualisation tools such as Looker or similar platforms.

Comfortable managing campaign budgets, analysing ROI and optimising marketing spend.

Strong problem-solving skills with a proactive, experimentation-driven mindset.

Excellent communication and presentation skills, with the ability to explain performance insights clearly to both technical and non-technical stakeholders.

Ability to work independently while collaborating effectively across Product, Analytics, Creative and Growth teams.

Curious, highly motivated and eager to continuously test, learn and improve.
