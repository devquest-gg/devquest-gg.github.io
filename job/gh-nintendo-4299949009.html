RETRO STUDIOS:

Founded in 1998, Retro Studios is a wholly owned subsidiary of Nintendo Co., Ltd., and a powerhouse in game development. Working hand-in-hand with Nintendo, we craft award-winning titles for the company s cutting-edge platforms, blending innovation, artistry, and gameplay excellence.

Our home base is Austin, Texas, a vibrant city known for its creative energy, live music, stunning natural scenery, and forward-thinking culture. With year-round sunshine and endless options for outdoor adventures, Austin offers the perfect backdrop for world-class developers to stay inspired, balanced, and at the top of their game.

DESCRIPTION OF DUTIES:

Architect and evolve large-scale systems, defining and driving a clear technical vision and roadmap

Adapt system designs to changing requirements while maintaining scalability, performance, and stability

Provide technical leadership, mentoring engineers and setting standards for engineering excellence

Collaborate with cross-functional teams to define requirements, communicate decisions, and deliver effective solutions

Design, build, and maintain tools, applications, libraries, and plugins for game content creation

Own the full lifecycle of tools: requirements, implementation, documentation, maintenance, and continuous improvement

Identify bottlenecks and drive technical solutions to improve workflows and productivity

Conduct code and technical reviews to ensure quality and identify opportunities for improvement

Perform technical reviews of current tools and evaluate new technologies for possible use by the team

Coordinate and estimate work to support planning, execution, and delivery of team commitments

Contribute hands-on as an individual contributor while supporting overall project and studio goals

*This job description outlines primary duties and requirements and is not intended to identify all tasks that may be performed; individuals occupying the position may be required to perform other duties.

The company may modify job duties from time to time, either in practice or in writing.

SUMMARY OF REQUIREMENTS:

History of developing pipelines from initial concept to shipping content.

Demonstrated ability to focus on minimizing redundancy by leveraging clean, API-first design principles

Must possess strong communication skills

10 - 15 years’ experience in game development or related industry preferred.

PREFERRED:

A history working with build systems.

Strong understanding of WPF

Previous GameDev experience

Familiarity with build pipelines

Familiarity with workflow needs of different departments.
