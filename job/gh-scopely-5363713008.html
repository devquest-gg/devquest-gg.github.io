Scopely is looking for a Director of Live Roadmap Release Operations to join our MONOPOLY GO! game team in Barcelona on a hybrid basis.

About the Role

As Director of Live Roadmap Release Operations , you will own the operational health, integrity, and readiness of MONOPOLY GO!’s live roadmap and release ecosystem.

You will lead the systems, processes, and operating rhythms that turn product, content, events, promotions, tooling, and technical initiatives into coordinated and reliable live releases. This includes roadmap governance, dependency management, release readiness, risk assessment, deployment coordination, operational tooling, incident response, and post-release validation.

This role sits at the intersection of Production, Live Operations, Product, Engineering, and QA . We are looking for someone who combines strong operational leadership with enough technical depth to work credibly with Engineering teams and improve the systems behind live delivery.

The background could come from Live Operations, Production, Release Management, Technical Production, or Engineering Management . What matters most is demonstrated experience operating close to live releases and production environments, coordinating complex cross-functional delivery, and improving the processes and technical systems that support execution at scale.

You should be equally comfortable shaping long-term operating strategy, working through a complex dependency or release plan with delivery teams, partnering with engineers on tooling and automation, or stepping into a critical launch or incident to help drive resolution.

What You ll Do

Own the operating health of the live roadmap, ensuring priorities, sequencing, dependencies, capacity, and release plans are realistic and executable

Establish and evolve the end-to-end operating model for taking work from roadmap definition through development, validation, approval, deployment, monitoring, and post-release review

Lead release governance and readiness, including risk-based gates, clear ownership, go/no-go processes, escalation paths, rollback planning, and post-release validation

Coordinate complex, high-frequency live releases across Product, Engineering, Live Operations, Production, QA, Content, and other partner teams

Identify roadmap, technical, operational, and dependency risks early and drive clear mitigation and decision-making

Partner closely with Engineering to improve deployment pipelines, configuration workflows, validation systems, observability, dashboards, automation, and operational tooling

Translate operational needs into clear technical requirements and help prioritize tooling and platform improvements that reduce manual work and release risk

Establish metrics and reporting that provide visibility into roadmap health, release readiness, throughput, incidents, rework, operational load, and time to resolution

Improve production and release workflows by removing unnecessary handoffs, clarifying ownership, increasing automation, and creating sustainable operating standards

Step into critical launches, incidents, or escalations when cross-functional coordination and rapid decision-making are required

Lead operational change across teams, helping new processes, tooling, and ways of working become embedded rather than simply introduced

Build trusted partnerships across senior Product, Engineering, Production, QA, Data, and Live Operations leadership

Mentor Producers, Release Managers, Engineering Managers, or other operational leaders involved in the live delivery ecosystem

What We re Looking For

Significant experience leading complex live operations, release management, technical production, engineering delivery, or a closely related function within a live-service environment

Demonstrated experience owning or influencing high-frequency releases across multiple teams, systems, dependencies, and concurrent workstreams

Strong operational judgment, with experience balancing delivery speed, technical risk, player experience, business priorities, and sustainable team execution

Technical fluency in software delivery and production environments, including deployment workflows, configuration management, validation, monitoring, rollback, and incident response

Experience partnering closely with Engineering teams on release tooling, automation, production systems, or workflow improvements

Strong understanding of roadmap planning, dependency management, release readiness, risk management, and operational governance

Experience improving operating models, processes, tooling, or organizational ways of working across multiple teams

Ability to move comfortably between strategic planning and detailed execution, remaining close enough to delivery to identify issues and unblock teams

Strong stakeholder management and cross-functional leadership, including the ability to align teams with different priorities without relying on direct authority

Experience leading teams or developing leaders within Production, Live Operations, Release Management, Engineering, or adjacent functions

Clear communication of technical, operational, and delivery risks to both technical and non-technical audiences

AI Capability Readiness

We believe AI can meaningfully transform how Live Operations and release organizations execute at scale. In this role, you’ll be expected to actively leverage AI-enabled tools to improve roadmap analysis, dependency mapping, release readiness, documentation, risk identification, incident response, production analytics, decision support, and workflow automation.

We value leaders who experiment thoughtfully with emerging AI technologies to reduce operational overhead, improve visibility and decision-making, increase release quality, and help teams move faster while maintaining strong standards of safety, accountability, and execution.

About Scopely

Scopely is a leading video game and global interactive entertainment company, home to many of the world’s most beloved and enduring experiences, including two of the most successful mobile games of all-time “MONOPOLY GO!” and “Pokémon GO,” along with “Stumble Guys,” “Star Trek™ Fleet Command,” “MARVEL Strike Force,” “WWE Champions,” the Scrabble® franchise, “Yahtzee® With Buddies,” and many others. Across mobile, web, PC, and console, Scopely creates, develops, publishes, and live-operates one of the most diversified and award-winning portfolios in the games industry — bringing hundreds of millions of players together through a shared love of play.

Founded in 2011, Scopely is powered by its exceptional team — including thousands of world-class gamemakers around the globe, a distinctive tenet-driven culture, and its proprietary technology platform, Playgami. Together, these strengths have fueled Scopely’s position as the #1 mobile games company in the U.S. and #2 globally, generating more than $10 billion in lifetime revenue. Whether building global sensations like “MONOPOLY GO!” from the ground up, or expanding through strategic acquisitions, including the FoxNext, GSN, and Scopely Explore games businesses — Scopely consistently delivers experiences players love today and return to for years to come.

Recognized multiple times as one of the "100 Most Influential Companies in the World" by TIME magazine and one of Fast Company s "World s Most Innovative Companies" and “Best Workplaces for Innovators,” Scopely believes that video games can be a force for good — creating meaningful connections, vibrant communities, and making life better through play.

Scopely has global operations and partners across four continents in more than a dozen countries worldwide. For more information, visit: https://www.scopely.com/ .
