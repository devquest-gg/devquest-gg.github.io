Role Overview:

We are looking for a Senior Client Lead who will be responsible for leading the engineering direction of a core product that drives player engagement across our games portfolio.

You will own the technical roadmap for PuzzlePlay, working closely with Product, Design, QA and Commercial teams to prioritise and deliver new features and enhancements. As the engineering lead, you will directly manage a team of Developers, fostering a collaborative, high-performing culture while maintaining engineering excellence.

This is a hands-on leadership role where you ll balance people management, technical decision-making, architecture, and delivery.

Puzzleplay is Tripledot’s new player rewards platform, designed to make playing our games even more fun and engaging. By playing our casual, free to play mobile games, users can earn points, complete challenges, and win real prizes. It’s our way of giving back to players and rewarding them for doing what they already love. Puzzleplay is a key part of our growth strategy, combining smart engagement mechanics with high-quality game content.

Remove delivery blockers and ensure projects are delivered on time and to a high standard.

Solution Design Architecture

Lead the design and implementation of scalable front-end solution using React Native

Ensure applications are performant, secure, accessible, and maintainable.

Drive technical innovation and recommend improvements to architecture and tooling.

Maintain technical documentation and architectural standards.

Stakeholder management

Translate technical concepts into clear business outcomes for non-technical stakeholders.

Manage delivery expectations, risks, and dependencies.

Engineering Excellence

Promote Agile delivery methodologies and continuous improvement.

Drive improvements in development velocity, code quality, and platform reliability.

Encourage knowledge sharing and continuous learning across the team.
