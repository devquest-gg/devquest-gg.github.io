HR Advisor

Lucid Games · Liverpool

Mid People & Ops Hybrid £35K–£40K Posted 2026-08-05 Full time
Apply on Lucid Games’s site →

Salary: £35-40k Overview: We’re looking for a driven HR professional to join our People team at Lucid.

You will work directly within a small team and you’ll be involved in a wide range of HR activities, supporting a workforce of approximately 250 employees.

This role will be hybrid, 3 days in the office and 2 days can be remote if you wish.

Our Values: Collaboration - We collaborate as a team, communicate openly to learn and share.

Respect - We value diverse perspectives and treat others with kindness.

Excellence - We continuously strive for improvement in creative and technical excellence.

Wellbeing - We prioritise wellbeing to create a positive and supportive community.

What you'll do: First point of contact for all HR related queries across the business.

Supporting managers, giving advice and guidance and leaning on company policy and procedure where appropriate.

Support with the HR system Hibob, keeping data accurate and managing reporting and analytics.

Supporting the performance management cycle including objective setting, performance reviews and managing 1:1s.

Providing advice and guidance on managing absence, ensuring completion of return-to-work conversations, providing support and escalating where necessary.

Supporting on drafting new policies and procedures, identifying process efficiencies and automations.

Supporting with payroll administration by collating employee data including new starters, leavers and changes.

Support on employee relations cases including disciplinaries, grievances, absences and performance management etc Supporting managers on induction of new starters, probation reviews and ensuring onboarding and induction tasks are completed.

Manage and coordinate DSARs Support on HSE Comfortable and confident to educate managers, support or lead workshops for employees/ managers on a range of HR topics.

Identify gaps, inconsistencies, or risks in HR processes and proactively raise and resolve them.

Supporting family-friendly policies including maternity, paternity, flexible working, and parental leave requests Assisting with employee initiatives and wider People Team projects Proactively stay up to date with current trends in the market and share these within the team Collaborate and engage with the People team, sharing knowledge, experience and ideas that can be implemented to drive success.
