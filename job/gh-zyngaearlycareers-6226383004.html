For more information, visit www.zynga.com or follow Zynga on Twitter , Instagram , Facebook, or the Zynga blog We are looking for a Game Mathematician Intern to assist our game development team in designing, analysing, and optimizing mathematical models for our slot games. This is an exciting opportunity for someone with a passion for mathematics, statistics, and game design to gain hands-on experience in the casino gaming industry.

What You Will Do:

Design Mathematical Models: Assist in designing and testing the mathematical logic for new slot games, including pay tables, game mechanics, and bonus features.

RTP (Return to Player) Calculation: Support the calculation and verification of the theoretical RTP, hit frequency, and volatility levels for slot games.

Simulation and Testing: Run simulations to ensure the mathematical accuracy of game models, including payout distributions and randomness.

Documentation: Assist in creating detailed game math specifications, flowcharts, and reports for game designs and regulatory submissions. Offer detailed analysis and explanation of mathematical models

Collaboration: Work closely with game designers, developers, and QA teams to ensure the mathematical integrity of slot games.

What We Require:

Currently pursuing or recently completed a Bachelor’s or Master’s degree in Mathematics, Statistics, Computer Science or a related field.

Strong analytical and problem-solving skills.

Proficiency in mathematical tools and software such as Excel, C# or Python.

Basic understanding of probability theory, combinatorics, and statistics.

Interest in casino games and game theory is a plus.

Strong attention to detail and ability to work in a collaborative environment.

Good communication skills for presenting mathematical concepts to non-technical stakeholders.
