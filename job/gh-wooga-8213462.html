As a Marketing Data Analyst, you will analyze large-scale data across user acquisition touchpoints in order to generate insightful metrics, custom dashboards, and deep-dive analyses to measure marketing performance.

Our marketing teams aim for best-in-class user acquisition strategies, bringing high-quality players to our games while driving innovation around performance marketing and targeted campaigns. We re looking for a passionate, results-oriented talent to join our team, develop new insights to shape data-driven decisions, continuously improve marketing efficiency, and help drive business growth for years to come.

About the job

Design, build, and maintain automated, interactive dashboards in Tableau to give the Marketing team up-to-date visibility into performance and KPIs.

Dive deep into large-scale marketing data to identify key insights that will inform user acquisition strategies and business growth.

Help define how best to measure and monitor our marketing channels, creatives, budget allocation, and overall campaign performance.

Help to develop an analysis strategy and perform analysis of complex campaigns and A/B tests, both systematically and on a one-off basis.

Provide data-driven recommendations to help Marketing Managers optimize their campaigns across various channels (e.g., Facebook Audience Network, Snapchat, TikTok).

Partner with Data Engineering on data gathering and signal sharing, integrating 3rd-party data providers (e.g., AppsFlyer, Kochava), democratizing data with reporting tools, AI Agents, and innovating workflows.

Communicate actionable results and data-driven findings across the organization clearly and concisely.

About you

You have recent experience in a data analyst role, preferably in digital advertising, performance marketing, or the app industry.

You have experience with (app) Attribution Services (eg: AppsFlyer or Kochava).

Literacy on Agentic Workflows, and other productivity and modern self-service data innovations.

You can use SQL effectively within an enterprise data environment and you have a good understanding of commonly applied statistical techniques. Basic understanding of programming is not required, but is appreciated.

You have strong experience using Tableau (or similar) to build interactive dashboards and visualizations.

You have good problem-solving skills and can look for logical connections between marketing spend, consumer behavior, and data.

You have a deep understanding of the digital advertising market,including communication patterns as they relate to consumer marketing. You are in tune with user acquisition patterns, and you understand mobile app growth.

You know your way around designing, communicating, visualizing, or reporting your results/analysis so they are clear and unambiguous to both technical and non-technical audiences.
