The Strategic Recruiting Programs team plays an integral role in connecting the business to talent via early career and strategic sourcing programs. We are looking for Sourcing Recruiters to focus on our priority roles and strategic sourcing programs to proactively identify and recruit top talent for business-critical roles at Nintendo.

DESCRIPTION OF DUTIES

Develop deep understanding of Nintendo s talent segments to build a pipeline of qualified talent to fill current and future hiring needs via proactive market research and ongoing relationship management

Support and execute on strategic recruiting programs

Partner with recruiters and hiring managers in creating sourcing strategies for priority roles

Source, assess and deliver pipeline of top talent to hiring teams

May require limited travel to talent networking and recruiting events

SUMMARY OF REQUIREMENTS

5+ years of experience sourcing and/or full cycle recruiting experience in a high-volume environment (via search firm or in-house recruiting environment).

3+ years of experience recruiting passive candidates for technical roles

Knowledgeable in sourcing tools, social media, emerging technologies, and techniques utilized for identifying highly qualified talent to drive results

Have curiosity and strong market research skills with the ability to create customized value proposition to attract and connect with talent

Strong organization and communication skills

Experience managing and prioritizing multiple searches, projects and client relationships

Strong analytical skills with ability to create, measure, and scale the right workflow

Undergraduate degree in Business, Human Resources, a related field, or equivalent

This position is hybrid in Redmond, WA. Hybrid positions require regular onsite work following the schedule and guidelines for their division. This position is not open to fully remote status at this time.
