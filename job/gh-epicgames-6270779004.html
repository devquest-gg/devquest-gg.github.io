INFOSEC

What We Do

Keep Epic’s networks, hardware, and people safe from security risks. Install security measures and operate software to protect systems and information infrastructure, including firewalls and encryption programs. Document issues as they arise and assess report any impact caused.

What You ll Do

Epic s Cyber Defense Operations team protects one of the world s largest player communities, as well as the people, infrastructure, and technology that support it. As Cyber Defense Operations Director, you will own Epic s detection and response function end-to-end, leading the teams who detect, investigate, and respond to threats across the company. You will set the long-term vision for security operations, build an organization that scales with Epic s growth, and lead the shift to automated, Al-assisted operations so the team can focus on the threats that matter most. This is a player-coach role: you will lead from the front, stay hands-on alongside the team, and hold yourself to the same high standard you set for others.

In this role, you will

Build and lead the Cyber Defense Operations organization as a hands-on player-coach: hire and develop strong analysts, set the org structure, and stay in the work on investigations, detection, and major incidents. Coach the team through real cases, put their growth first, and clear the obstacles that slow them down

Set the multi-year strategy and roadmap for detection, response, automation, and tooling, aligned with Epic s risk profile, business priorities, and Information Security leadership goals

Act as the senior incident lead for Epic s most significant security events, directing response across programming, legal, privacy, communications, and executive leadership, and briefing senior leaders on impact and risk

Own detection and response coverage across cloud, corporate, endpoint, identity, and production environments, directing investment toward the threats that pose the most risk to Epic

Champion automation and AI-agent workflows across security operations, partnering with security programming leadership to set guardrails, validation standards, and where human review is required

Run Cyber Defense Operations as a business and a partner to the rest of Epic: shape the budget and vendor portfolio (managed-service partners, SLAs, contracts, and build/buy/retire calls on tooling), define and report the metrics that show how well we protect Epic (time to detect, time to respond, coverage, alert quality), keep playbooks, on-call, and escalation paths tested and ready to scale, and build partnerships across Epic so emerging threats turn into coordinated action

What we re looking for

8+ years of experience in security operations, incident response, or threat detection, including 6+ years leading security teams

A track record of building and scaling a SOC or detection and response organization, including org design, hiring, and developing leaders

Strong technical grounding in SIEM and EDR platforms (e.g., Splunk, Google SecOps, CrowdStrike, etc.), enough to guide design and tooling decisions and challenge technical approaches

Deep understanding of attacker tradecraft and frameworks such as MITRE ATT CK, and how to use them to prioritize coverage and investment

Experience leading response to major incidents in cloud environments (AWS and/or GCP), SaaS, and identity platforms, including executive and regulatory communication

Experience leading automation and SOAR programs at scale, and a clear point of view on applying Al to security operations responsibly

Experience shaping budgets and managing vendors and managed security service providers, including contracts, SLAs, and performance

A calm, accountable, people-first leader: decisive under pressure, clear when explaining risk and trade-offs to executives in briefings and incident reports, hands-on alongside the team on investigations, detections, and incidents, and willing to challenge how things have always been done. You own outcomes, follow through, take responsibility when things go wrong, and measure your success by the success of your team and the people they protect
