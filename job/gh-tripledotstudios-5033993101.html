Role Overview

We’re looking for a Senior Content Game Designer with strong AI expertise to create new game locations, from storyline and questline to approval of final art and cutscenes. You’ll define player flow, write stories and questlines within the established setting, prepare inputs for all teams, and build AI-powered workflows that make content production significantly faster.

Key Responsibilities

Defining location feel. Develop the concept, environment, mood, and key story beats for new locations within the established setting and global storyline

Writing stories and questlines. Create storylines, questlines, and dialogues for each location

Controlling the player flow. Define how the player will engage with the game mechanics throughout the location

Preparing inputs for all teams. Turn location vision into clear briefs for Art, Animation, and Development

Ordering and accepting art and cutscenes. Brief artists, set the vision for cutscenes, give feedback, and accept final results

Building AI-powered content workflows. Identify where AI can speed up location production and redesign the process around it

Required Skills, Knowledge and Expertise

4+ years of experience as a Content or Narrative Designer in live-service mobile games

Proven experience creating location-based content

Strong storytelling skills within an existing setting and tone of voice

Experience briefing and accepting art and cutscenes

A portfolio of real AI cases, beyond generating texts with LLMs, where you can clearly explain:

which process you accelerated

which tools or agents you used

how you controlled quality

what results were achieved

English and Russian spoken and written at B2 level and above
