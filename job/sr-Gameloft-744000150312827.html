UI UX Designer Intern

Gameloft · Hanoi

Entry Design Posted 2026-09-18 Intern
Apply on Gameloft’s site →

This role offers practical experience in UI/UX design for games, with opportunities to learn from experienced designers and contribute to real design tasks on live projects. Build practical skills and develop a solid foundation for a career in game development.

What It Means on a Daily Basis Explore player needs, platform requirements, and control schemes to inform interface design.

Develop user flows, information architecture, and wireframes with guidance from experienced UI/UX Designers.

Collaborate with Game Designers, Developers, and Artists throughout the game development process.

Contribute to accessible, intuitive, and comfortable player experiences.

Participate in playtesting and use feedback from playtests to improve design solutions.

Who You Would Be Working With Studio Game Designer Lead, Senior Game Designers, and Game Designer Project Leads Producers, Artists, and Programmers Cross-functional game development teams Your Recruitment Journey Screening Call Test Interview Offer WHAT YOU NEED TO SUCCEED Technical Skills Basic understanding of UI/UX fundamentals, including user flows, information architecture, and wireframing.

Familiarity with design and wireframing tools such as Figma and Miro .

Basic understanding of ergonomics and control schemes across mobile, PC, and console games.

Awareness of accessibility principles in interface design.

Who You Are Currently pursuing a degree in Design, Multimedia, Human-Computer Interaction, Information Technology, Fine Arts , or a related field.

Positive attitude, self-motivated, and willing to learn.

Open to feedback and comfortable improving through trial and practice.

Comfortable working with people from different disciplines.

Interested in developing a career in game UI/UX.

Portfolio Required Please include a working portfolio link in your application. Applications without a portfolio link may not be reviewed.
