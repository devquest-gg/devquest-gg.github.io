Executive Producer, Pikmin Bloom

Scopely · Tokyo

Director+ Production Posted 2026-09-09 Full time

Scopely Explore is looking for an Executive Producer for Pikmin Bloom to join its Leadership Team and help shape the future of one of the world’s leading location-based live service games.

The Executive Producer owns execution across the game organization, enabling teams to consistently deliver exceptional player experiences through operational excellence, cross-functional leadership, and predictable execution. Working closely with the General Manager and functional leaders, this role establishes production practices, operating rhythms, and organizational processes that allow globally distributed teams to operate as One Team.

The Executive Producer owns execution across the game organization, enabling teams to consistently deliver exceptional player experiences through operational excellence, cross-functional leadership, and predictable execution. Working closely with the General Manager and functional leaders, this role establishes production practices, operating rhythms, and organizational processes that allow globally distributed teams—primarily across Japan and the United States—to operate as One Team.

From long-term roadmap planning to LiveOps execution, the Executive Producer ensures that strategy is translated into clear plans, teams remain aligned, risks are proactively managed, and the organization consistently delivers high-quality outcomes.

What You Will Do

Lead planning and execution across the game organization, ensuring timely, high-quality delivery of the product roadmap and LiveOps.

Partner closely with the General Manager and Leadership Team to translate the game’s vision and strategy into actionable roadmaps, milestones, and operating plans.

Establish world-class production practices, operating rhythms, and planning processes that improve transparency, predictability, and execution.

Create and foster a One Team mindset across Design, Product, Engineering, Marketing, Analytics, and external partners.

Build strong alignment across globally distributed teams, primarily across Japan and the United States, while maintaining efficient communication and clear ownership.

Build and maintain strong, trusted relationships with key external partners, including major IP partners such as Nintendo, ensuring effective collaboration and alignment throughout development and live operations.

Develop and grow a high-performing Production organization that serves as a force multiplier for the entire game team.

Maintain a comprehensive understanding of the overall project, proactively identifying risks, removing organizational blockers, and driving timely decisions.

Ensure production supports player-centric development through iterative planning, playtesting, and continuous improvement.

Drive scalable production processes that support the long-term operation of a global live service game.

Provide clear visibility into project health, execution risks, and mitigation plans to senior leadership.

AI-Enabled Operational Excellence: Champion the adoption of AI-powered workflows that improve planning, communication, documentation, risk management, and production visibility. Continuously identify opportunities to automate routine work, accelerate execution, and increase the effectiveness of globally distributed teams.

Required in-office 3 days per week on Tuesday, Wednesday, and Thursday

What We re Looking For

Proven experience leading production for a successful live service mobile game.

Demonstrated track record establishing production practices that improve execution across cross-functional teams.

Experience leading globally distributed development teams, including collaboration with external partners, including major IP partners.

Strong understanding of software development methodologies and modern game production practices.

Excellent organizational, analytical, and communication skills.

Exceptional ability to influence, align, and lead without direct authority.

Strong judgment with the ability to identify risks early and drive effective mitigation.

Demonstrated ability to design and implement AI-native workflows, custom tools, and automation that measurably improve planning, execution, and overall team efficiency.

Passion for building high-performing teams and healthy organizational culture.

Excellent verbal and written communication skills in both English and Japanese

Plus if…

Experience leading production on a globally operated free-to-play mobile game.

Experience partnering with major entertainment or gaming IP holders.

Experience operating games with seasonal LiveOps, experimentation, and continuous content delivery.

Experience building or scaling Production organizations through periods of significant growth.

Familiarity with geolocation-based or outdoor gaming experiences.

Deep appreciation for player-first product development and long-term live service operations.

About Scopely

Scopely is a leading video game and global interactive entertainment company, home to many of the world’s most beloved and enduring experiences, including two of the most successful mobile games of all-time “MONOPOLY GO!” and “Pokémon GO,” along with “Stumble Guys,” “Star Trek™ Fleet Command,” “MARVEL Strike Force,” “WWE Champions,” the Scrabble® franchise, “Yahtzee® With Buddies,” and many others. Across mobile, web, PC, and console, Scopely creates, develops, publishes, and live-operates one of the most diversified and award-winning portfolios in the games industry — bringing hundreds of millions of players together through a shared love of play.

Founded in 2011, Scopely is powered by its exceptional team — including thousands of world-class gamemakers around the globe, a distinctive tenet-driven culture, and its proprietary technology platform, Playgami. Together, these strengths have fueled Scopely’s position as the #1 mobile games company in the U.S. and #2 globally, generating more than $10 billion in lifetime revenue. Whether building global sensations like “MONOPOLY GO!” from the ground up, or expanding through strategic acquisitions, including the FoxNext, GSN, and Scopely Explore games businesses — Scopely consistently delivers experiences players love today and return to for years to come.

Recognized multiple times as one of the "100 Most Influential Companies in the World" by TIME magazine and one of Fast Company s "World s Most Innovative Companies" and “Best Workplaces for Innovators,” Scopely believes that video games can be a force for good — creating meaningful connections, vibrant communities, and making life better through play.

Scopely has global operations and partners across four continents in more than a dozen countries worldwide. For more information, visit: https://www.scopely.com/ .
