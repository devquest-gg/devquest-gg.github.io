Associate 2 Brand Manager

Aspyr Media · Austin

Entry Marketing Onsite Posted 2026-09-26 Full time

**Please note that all positions posted for Aspyr Media, Inc. are in-person/in-office only; remote positions are not available.**

Aspyr is a leading game publisher and developer, bringing the world s biggest franchises and bold new projects to players everywhere.

Since 1996, we’ve published over 200 games and our team is dedicated to bringing beloved franchises like Tomb Raider and Star Wars to new and familiar audiences across all platforms.

We focus on collaboration and creativity, crafting high-quality, engaging games that leave a lasting impact on players worldwide.

Responsibilities:

Reporting to the Head of Marketing, the Associate 2 Brand Manager is responsible for supporting the development and execution of go-to-market campaigns for game launches. Responsibilities include assisting with overall campaign strategy, product and brand positioning, target audience analysis, competitive research, creative reviews, asset tracking, and brand marketing budget administration. The Associate 2 Brand Manager will collaborate closely with a wide range of internal cross-functional teams and external partners to support high-quality creative execution and execute unified launch campaigns.

Essential Functions:

Collaborate within the marketing team to work with cross-functional teams (e.g., product, QA, publishing) and external creative and media agencies to support brand initiatives and execute go-to-market strategies for game launches that meet or exceed sales targets.

Assist in developing product and brand positioning aligned with current audience insights, behaviors, and attitudes

Support the creation and production of campaign materials (e.g., key art, trailers, advertising, digital, social, and promotional assets), including routing briefs, agency research, and tracking deliverables across internal and external teams

Assist with managing and tracking brand marketing campaign budgets, processing purchase orders, and maintaining spend records for assigned titles

Help manage day-to-day interactions and prepare materials for external stakeholders and licensing partners (e.g., presenting go-to-market plans, game updates, asset submissions, creative briefs, and post-launch performance metrics)

Assist with tracking game development progress, and updating action plans based on project evolution

Preferred Qualifications:

Bachelor’s degree in a related field

2–5 years of experience successfully bringing products to market or supporting marketing campaigns within the games and/or entertainment industry

Ability to work with marketing team, internal cross-functional teams, and external partners to deliver on campaign milestones

Experience with successful, integrated campaign management

Experience managing relationships with external partners

Demonstrated understanding of video game publishing and marketing content pipeline from concept to shipping

Actively plays games and is aware of cultural trends, current events, and other activities in the gaming industry

Understands industry marketing, digital media and social marketing, and trends

Possesses a balance of both creative and quantitative/analytical skills

Strong written and oral communication and presentation skills
