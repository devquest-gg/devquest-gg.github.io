We’re looking for a 3D Art Intern to join our team and contribute to worldwide 3D production projects. If you’re excited to learn, grow your skills, and experience how game assets are created in a real production environment, we’d like to hear from you.

Responsibility: Demonstrate understanding of clean topology, proper UVs, and organized files, and deliver assets that meet basic integration standards.

Follow the studio's 3D production workflow, including file naming and folder structure, low-poly modeling standards, and feedback and revision process.

Show measurable improvement in modeling efficiency, UV unwrapping, and texture cleanup.

Apply at least one new tool or technique learned during the internship.

&#xa0; Requirements: Currently studying or recently graduated in 3D Art, Animation, Game Design, or a related field.

Basic understanding of 3D modeling fundamentals (form, proportion, and topology).

Curious, willing to learn, and open to feedback.
