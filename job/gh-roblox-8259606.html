Senior Software Engineer, Consumer Apps

Roblox · San Mateo

Senior Engineering $197K–$243K Posted 2026-10-16 Full time
Apply on Roblox’s site →

Every day, tens of millions of people come to Roblox to explore, create, play, learn, and connect with friends in 3D immersive digital experiences– all created by our global community of developers and creators.

At Roblox, we’re building the tools and platform that empower our community to bring any experience that they can imagine to life. Our vision is to reimagine the way people come together, from anywhere in the world, and on any device.

We’re on a mission to connect a billion people with optimism and civility, and looking for amazing talent to help us get there.

A career at Roblox means you’ll be working to shape the future of human interaction, solving unique technical challenges at scale, and helping to create safer, more civil shared experiences for everyone.

Most engineering roles own one area. On the Consumer Apps team, you’ll make an impact across every surface of the Roblox app. We build the shared platform that Roblox s user interface runs on, including the component library, server-driven UI, the core app frame and navigation, and the frameworks underneath them all. When we get it right, people feel it every time they open the app, and every product team ships faster. Recently, we shipped app-wide themes, including an anniversary Classic theme and a Halloween theme, that changed how Roblox looks and feels across every surface at once.

We re looking for a Senior Software Engineer who thinks beyond the usual playbook: someone who questions how things are done, works with AI agents as a natural part of how they build, and moves fast with good judgment about what matters to the people using our product.

You Will

Make an impact across every surface of the app, building the frameworks, APIs, and systems that product teams across Roblox build on, and shipping cross-app capabilities, like app-wide theming, that change the whole app at once

Build and evolve the core systems beneath the UI, such as navigation, routing, state management, and data flow, so features load quickly and behave consistently

Drive app performance, transition smoothness, and layout stability across phones, tablets, and desktop, using traces and telemetry to set measurable quality bars

Roll out platform changes safely at scale, using feature flags, staged rollouts, and side-by-side validation of old and new implementations, with fast rollback when needed

Drive adoption of the platform across Roblox by partnering with product, design, and feature engineering teams to guide them onto our frameworks and components, and turning their feedback into a better platform

Use AI agents and automation to move faster, and help shape how our team and our partner teams build with them

Raise the quality bar through thoughtful code review, strong test coverage, and clear documentation, and mentor other engineers

You Have

4+ years of professional experience building and shipping client applications, ideally on mobile (iOS, Android) or desktop, with a strong foundation in application architecture

Hands-on experience with React or React Native, with enough depth to reason about how components, state, and rendering behave, and the curiosity to pick up frameworks and tools that are new to you

Experience designing shared frameworks, UI platforms, or core libraries that other engineers build on

Strong debugging and profiling skills, with experience diagnosing client performance issues such as slow startup, visual stutter, and memory pressure across screen sizes and form factors

Comfort working in a large, evolving codebase, including migrating legacy code incrementally using feature flags and staged rollouts

An agentic mindset and fluency with AI coding tools: you look for ways to automate, delegate, and structure work so that both people and agents can do it well

Strong written and verbal communication skills, and the ability to align multiple teams around a technical plan

Who You Are

Someone who challenges the status quo, asks why we do things the way we do, and proposes better options

A creative, outside-the-box thinker who looks for the approach nobody has tried yet

Someone who moves fast and is comfortable shipping boldly, and when something breaks, diagnoses it, fixes it, and learns from it quickly

Someone with an eye for product who cares how what you build feels to the people using it

A candid, constructive voice who gives clear feedback to engineers, designers, and product partners, and takes it well in return

Bonus Points

Experience with Luau or Lua

Experience with React Navigation or comparable routing libraries

Experience with native mobile development on iOS or Android

Experience with server-driven UI or dynamic UI delivery systems

Experience building cross-platform applications that run on mobile, desktop, and console

Experience supporting other engineers in adopting a shared platform, through documentation, migration guides, or direct developer support
