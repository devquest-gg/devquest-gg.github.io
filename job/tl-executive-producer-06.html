Techland Games Explore Explore Explore Explore Explore All Games News About Us Careers Dying Light Outpost Support Menu Unia Europejska See all open positions Executive Producer Work Model: onsite Warszawa, Poland; Wrocław, Poland Share offer on Company Description Techland is one of the biggest video game companies in Poland, with over 30 years of experience in the gaming industry. From our studios in Wroclaw and Warsaw, we’ve built an international team of more than 500 talented professionals, all dedicated to pushing the boundaries of game development.

We’re known for creating iconic franchises like Call of Juarez and the zombie genre-defining Dying Light, which has been played by over 45 million players worldwide. With a focus on open-world action, storytelling, and community engagement, we’re committed to delivering unforgettable experiences to our players.

We’re constantly striving to improve, innovate, and take on new challenges. With ambitious plans for the future, we’re looking for passionate people to be part of this exciting journey.

Job Description The Executive Producer is the senior leader and business owner of the game/franchise, responsible for its creative direction, production execution, and commercial success.

As EP, You will ensure alignment, quality, and long-term growth across all stages of development and live operations.

Your daily tasks: Setting strategic vision: defining&#xa0;and leading the creative, technical, and long-term strategic direction of the game/franchise.

Owning the P&L: maintaining full responsibility for budget, revenue forecasts, and financial performance, prioritizing growth and value creation.

Overseeing game development: guiding&#xa0;the product through all phases, pre-production to live, ensuring execution aligns with strategy and publishing goals.

Ensuring quality: driving&#xa0;delivery of high-quality, engaging content that meets technical and creative standards.

Making final decisions: serving&#xa0;as the primary escalation point for scope, schedule, and financial decisions, including veto power.

Risks management: identifying and mitigating&#xa0;major production and operational risks.

Driving commercial initiatives: seeking and executing&#xa0;new opportunities for revenue growth, brand expansion, and market impact.

Aligning stakeholders: delivering clear communication and reporting to Techland’s C-Level and&#xa0;Strategic Partners.

Improving production practices: optimizing&#xa0;processes to enhance efficiency, predictability, and quality.

Qualifications 10+ years of senior leadership experience in AAA video game production.

Proven shipping experience across PC/console platforms.

Strong leadership and team development capabilities.

Advanced business and financial understanding, including live-service models.

Ability to define long-term vision and deliver against it.

Flexibility and&#xa0;problem-solving mindset.

Deep passion for video games.

Very good command of English.

Additional Information What we can offer: A wide array of benefits: private medical care, life insurance,&#xa0;pro-health campaigns,&#xa0;gifts for different occasions.

An outstanding work atmosphere in a highly-skilled team of professionals, with flexible working hours, no dress code, and full support of the dedicated HR Business Partner.

Many opportunities for personal development: a dedicated development budget for each employee, extra two paid days for training and CSR, stable career paths, extensive internal and external training, and financing of English and Polish language classes.

State-of-the-art offices filled with chillout zones, a fully equipped kitchen, a gym (Wrocław office), and a free car park (Warsaw limited amount of space).
