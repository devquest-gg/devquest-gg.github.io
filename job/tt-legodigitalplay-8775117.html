At LEGO Digital Play, we’re bringing the LEGO® System-in-Play to the digital builders of tomorrow. We’re building the LEGO brand’s digital home: a place where builders of every age can create, share and discover, held to the standard of craft the LEGO brand is known for.

We are at an early phase of this new company, offering a unique opportunity to build a new entity for the world's most beloved and trusted brand. Our culture is open, collaborative, intellectually rigorous, and creatively vibrant.

We are looking for an Operations Manager to join Enterprise Operations at LEGO Digital Play. Reporting directly to the VP of Operations, you will take on cross-functional projects where the brief is broad, setting your own direction and priorities based on the evolving needs of the business. The work is varied, no two months look the same, and the people who do well in it are self-guided, comfortable owning something end to end, and good at building structure and a playbook.

Key Responsibilities Take on cross-functional projects for the VP of Operations, turning broad asks into clearly defined problems, actionable recommendations, structured plans and measurable outcomes.

Own analytical problem-solving from end to end, breaking down complex or ambiguous business questions, identifying the data and inputs required, building robust models and frameworks, and translating findings into actionable recommendations. Be equally comfortable working with financial data, operational metrics, and qualitative information.

Partner across the business including with the People & Culture, IT, Finance, and Legal departments to connect the dots on operational readiness and integration work.

Set up new operational processes in a fast-scaling environment, bringing structure to areas that do not yet have it.

Turn executive priorities into working documents different audiences can act on, adapting the format to who is reading it and ensuring complex information is distilled into clear insights, recommendations, and decisions.

Hold the rhythm of each project: timelines, dependencies, blockers, decisions made, follow-through.

Keep the longer view in frame while the day-to-day moves fast, flagging risks and trade-offs before they become problems.

Qualifications Must Have: A strong operations craft background covering both the analytical and the delivery sides: comfortable building a model, running a plan, and seeing it through.

Demonstrated ability to independently structure and solve complex problems, translate analysis into decisions, and personally execute solutions.

Strong quantitative and analytical capabilities, including advanced proficiency in Excel or Google Sheets, financial and operational modeling, data analysis, and scenario planning. Able to work with imperfect information, identify patterns and inconsistencies, and draw sound conclusions.

Experience in environments where structure had to be built from ambiguity, whether in a strategy or operations function, a corporate development team, or embedded in a fast-moving part of a larger organisation.

Fluent working across functions, with a track record of getting the right information out of the right people and turning it into action.

Strong written and visual communication: the materials you produce land with senior leaders without constant rework.

Highly resourceful, intellectually curious, and self-directed, with the ability to navigate unfamiliar subjects, ask insightful questions, exercise independent judgment, and identify and implement practical solutions Nice to Have: Background in business operations, strategy and operations, management consulting, corporate development, or a similarly analytical, execution-oriented role in a scaling business.

Experience in a creative or product-driven industry (gaming, entertainment, consumer technology), especially during a growth or integration phase.

Production operations experience at a games company, for example as an Executive Producer or Senior Producer looking to apply those skills in a broader operations role.
