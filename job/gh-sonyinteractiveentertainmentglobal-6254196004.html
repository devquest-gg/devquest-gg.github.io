Senior Platform Engineer – CI/CD

Company Overview

At Sony Interactive Entertainment (SIE), we are passionate about creating extraordinary gaming experiences and pushing the boundaries of technology. Our engineering teams build and operate the platforms and services that power the PlayStation ecosystem, enabling millions of players around the world to connect, play, and enjoy incredible experiences.

We believe great developer platforms are essential to delivering great products. By investing in automation, cloud technologies, and modern engineering practices, we empower our development teams to innovate faster, improve software quality, and deliver reliable services at scale.

What You Bring to the Team

As a Senior Platform Engineer , you have a strong technical background and enjoy working with high-performing engineering teams. You are passionate about building scalable, reliable, and secure developer platforms that simplify software delivery and improve engineering productivity.

You understand the value CI/CD brings to modern software development and can empathize with the challenges engineers face when building, testing, and deploying applications. You enjoy developing tools, frameworks, and automation that allow developers to focus on writing great software rather than managing infrastructure.

You bring strong architectural judgment and hands-on engineering expertise . You are comfortable leading system and API design discussions, evaluating technical tradeoffs, and collaborating across team boundaries to develop scalable, reliable, and maintainable solutions. You lead by example, combining practical engineering skills with the ability to guide technical decisions and help other engineers succeed.

You thrive in a fast-paced, collaborative environment, are eager to learn new technologies, and are comfortable adapting to evolving engineering needs.

Most importantly, you will contribute to our vision of automation-first engineering, Infrastructure as Code, and modern software delivery practices . Your expertise will help improve developer experience, platform reliability, observability, and operational efficiency across our engineering organization.
