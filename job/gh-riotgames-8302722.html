Manager, Quality Assurance - VALORANT

Riot Games · Sydney

Mid QA Posted 2026-10-20 Full time
Apply on Riot Games’s site →

At Riot, we believe Quality is a set of values, practices, and behaviors that inform every part of the development process. By honing our craft of quality and demonstrating cross-discipline partnerships we help Riot sustainably craft excellent experiences for players, globally.

As a Manager of Quality Assurance on VALORANT , you will lead quality strategy and people across some of VALORANT’s most technically complex and cross-cutting domains.

You will manage quality owners responsible for a variety of areas within the game and your primary focus will be on the Game Engine, Technical Art, and Release Engineering that power VALORANT. You will be accountable for defining and executing a comprehensive test strategy that supports multiple cross-functional development teams, while shaping and promoting the vision of quality assurance across Riot—so that, “Gamers worldwide know that every experience they have with Riot will be a quality experience.” You will also ensure the execution of all test coverage within your product space, including playtest activities, manage testing, telemetry, defect data and report regularly on product quality. You will mentor your reports on the testing activities needed to support production within the teams you support. You will report into Riot Sydney Studio Leadership for VALORANT.

Responsibilities:

Lead and manage QA professionals working across multiple teams and areas of VALORANT, with a specific focus on Foundations.

Hold teams accountable for upholding quality and player impact as an essential factor in decision-making

Partner with other craft leaders to influence craft and product decisions through quality insights and risk analysis

Assesses quality, risk, and complexity, and communicates clear mitigations and priorities to development teams and leadership

Coaches and supports direct reports through complex quality challenges, representing their plans and needs with initiative leadership

Partners with other disciplines to improve development workflows and define quality bars or targets that align with initiative goals

Monitors quality health and defect trends, maintaining data-driven reporting and continuous improvement systems that inform decision-making

Drives quality feature planning and executes testing coverage plans with teams

Required Qualifications:

5+ years Quality Assurance Experience, with experience in a leadership role

Performance test tooling and automation

Experience creating test strategies for products

Experience with test design, planning and control

Experience with product development pipelines and workflows

Experience managing performance of embedded QA professionals

Experience working with remote testing teams

Experience establishing quality metrics that influence strategy and drive change

Desired Qualifications:

Experience in agile work systems

Experience delivering GaaS on any platform

ISTQB Foundation Level

ISTQB Agile Tester or ICAgile Agile Testing (ICP-TST)

ISTQB Expert Level

ISTQB Advanced Test Manager

Experience with Game Engine quality

Experience with software release and deployment pipeline quality

Experience testing on the Playstation 5 or Xbox Series S/X

For this role, you ll find success through craft expertise, a collaborative spirit, and decision-making that prioritizes the delight of players. We will be looking at your past studies, experience, and your personal relationship with games. If you embody player empathy and care about players experiences, this could be your role!
