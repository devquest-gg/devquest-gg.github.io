ABOUT VOODOO

Founded in 2013, Voodoo is a tech company that creates mobile games and apps with a mission to entertain the world. Gathering 800 employees, 7 billion downloads, and over 200 million active users, Voodoo is the #3 mobile publisher worldwide in terms of downloads after Google and Meta. Our portfolio includes chart-topping games like Mob Control and Block Jam, alongside popular apps such as BeReal and Wizz.

TEAM

Blitz is a leading real-money mobile gaming platform where players compete in skill-based games for cash prizes. Our ecosystem includes flagship titles like Blitz Win Cash, alongside Rush Arena and Bingo Win Cash.

Every day, hundreds of thousands of players compete on Blitz, with over $1M in prizes distributed daily across the platform. Built for both casual and competitive mobile gamers, Blitz combines accessible gameplay, high-performing live operations, and a creator-native approach to entertainment.

Blitz is part of the Voodoo group, a global leader of mobile entertainment.

ROLE

We’re looking for a Growth Lead to own and scale Blitz’s organic growth engine in the US.

This is a hands-on role at the intersection of content, creators, community, and culture. You’ll build scalable acquisition loops through influencers, streamers, social content, partnerships, referral mechanics, and IRL activations — turning Blitz into a brand players actively talk about and share.

You’ll work closely with product, marketing, and leadership teams to drive awareness, engagement, and user growth across our portfolio.

- Own Blitz’s organic growth strategy across social, content, community, referrals, creator partnerships, and emerging channels

- Build and scale a best-in-class Creator Program from the ground up, including UGC, Streaming and affiliates

- Source, recruit, and manage creators, influencers, streamers, and gaming communities aligned with the Blitz brand

- Manage IRL/community initiatives

- Develop repeatable growth loops that drive awareness, engagement, and user acquisition

- Track, analyze, and optimize organic growth performance across channels using data and experimentation

- Continuously test new ideas, channels, formats, and partnerships to unlock scalable growth
