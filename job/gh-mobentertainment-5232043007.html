About The Game And Company:

MOB Entertainment is looking for a talented Game Animator to join our team on a contract basis to help bring characters, creatures, and gameplay mechanics to life. You ll work closely with our art, design, and engineering teams to create polished, expressive 3D animations that fit our game s style and feel great in the hands of players.

What You ll Do:

Collaborate with a team of animators to develop high-quality in-game animations and cinematics

Work from storyboards when provided

Craft strong poses and compelling visual frames that represent the characters and serve the game s design goals

Present work to the team, seek input, and incorporate feedback from leads and teammates

Own your deliverables, managing your goals within milestones and deadlines while maintaining consistent style across the project

Continually refine animation processes throughout production to improve team productivity

Animate a diverse range of compelling, alluring, and fear-inducing creatures and characters that support unique and dynamic gameplay

What You Need:

5+ years of experience animating in Autodesk Maya

Ability to match the style and quality bar for a project through consistently high-quality animation

Strong understanding of body mechanics

A keen eye for performance and weight

Experience with creature animation (bipedal and quadrupedal)

Self-motivated with a strong work ethic; able to work collaboratively and inspire a team environment

Creative problem-solving skills and the ability to think beyond established solutions

Pluses:

Experience implementing animation directly into a game engine

Experience with animation blend trees and node-based animation editors in a commercial or proprietary game engine

You’ll Need To Provide Us With:

Your Resume

Your Portfolio or Reel – Only digital applications will be accepted such as a website, blog, or online album. Physical media will not be accepted.

An animation test might be required
