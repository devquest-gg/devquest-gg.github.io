Corporate Development Manager

Job type: Full-time Location: United States (Remote) Reporting to: Chief Financial Officer

About Atari

Atari is an interactive entertainment company and an iconic gaming industry brand recognized worldwide for its multi-platform games and licensed products. Atari owns and/or manages a portfolio of more than 400 games and franchises.

The Atari family of brands includes Digital Eclipse, Nightdive Studios, Infogrames, AtariAge, MobyGames, as well as Coatsink, Early Morning Studios, and Stormteller Games spanning game development, publishing, and community experiences worldwide.

Atari operates internationally with offices in the US, UK, France, Germany, Sweden, and India.

Role Overview

Atari is growing through studio and IP acquisitions, strategic investments, and new sources of capital to fund its development slate. Reporting to the Chief Financial Officer, with a dotted line to the Chief Operating Officer, the Corporate Development Manager will be the analytical and executional engine behind that work. You will run financings and transactions from initial screening through close, build the models and investment cases that support decision-making, and help ensure that acquired businesses deliver against the original investment case.

This is a hands-on role across structured and debt financing for game development, IP and back-catalog acquisitions, studio acquisitions, joint ventures, and minority investments. You will own the pipeline, keep live workstreams moving, challenge assumptions, and bring a consistent process to how Atari evaluates, approves, executes, and tracks transactions. You will work closely with the CEO, COO, General Counsel, accounting and business-unit leaders, as well as bankers, lenders, legal counsel, and diligence advisers.

What You ll Do

Own Atari’s corporate development pipeline, ensuring every opportunity has an accountable owner, current stage, next step, decision date, key risks, and clear weekly follow-through.

Screen, research, and prioritize opportunities across game studios, IP and back catalogs, hardware, digital platforms, financings, joint ventures, and minority investments.

Build and maintain valuation, transaction, financing, and capital-planning models, including DCF, trading comparables, precedent transactions, IP and catalog valuation, title-level investment cases, slate cash flows, waterfalls, sweeps, and scenario analysis.

Run transactions and financings from initial review through signing and closing, coordinating NDAs, data rooms, diligence requests, advisers, timetables, issues lists, and open actions.

Coordinate financial, commercial, tax, legal, and IP diligence, including ownership, chain of title, and third-party consents, and translate findings into price, structure, conditions, and risk assessments.

Support the CFO and COO in raising and managing capital, including development-slate funding, structured and asset-backed facilities, corporate debt, equity, and co-investment.

Prepare investment memos, Board papers, financing materials, term sheet comparisons, investor and lender materials, briefing notes, and Q A that clearly set out recommendations, trade-offs, risks, costs, cash impact, and implications for control and IP ownership.

Lead strategic analysis on capital allocation, build, buy, or partner decisions, portfolio choices, and turnaround cases, keeping recommendations aligned with Atari’s long-term value goals and group reporting.

Own post-close financing requirements, including draw requests, repayment calculations, covenant compliance, lender reporting, and the wider financing calendar.

Define investment-case KPIs, support post-merger integration plans, and track performance against the original case at Day 180 and 12 months, recommending corrective action where needed.

Support Board, investor, listed-company, results, and annual report materials, including disclosure obligations related to Euronext Growth Paris.

What We re Looking For

Requirements

5 to 7 years of experience in investment banking, leveraged or structured finance, private equity, transaction services, Big Four corporate finance, or in-house corporate development.

Experience running, rather than only supporting, workstreams on several closed transactions. Experience with debt or structured financing is strongly preferred.

The ability to build three-statement, DCF, waterfall, and scenario models from scratch in Excel.

The ability to write concise, decision-ready investment memos and Board- or lender-grade materials where accuracy and deadlines matter.

Confidence working directly with lenders, bankers, counsel, and diligence advisers.

The judgment to challenge a business case or set of numbers that does not hold up, and to document why.

Strong follow-through and the ability to coordinate data, advisers, and decisions across teams and time zones.

Practical use of AI tools to accelerate research, diligence review, modeling, and drafting, with the judgment to verify their output.

Fluent written and spoken English.

Preferred

Experience in video games, media, or entertainment, especially IP, royalty, or catalog valuation.

Experience with cross-border transactions involving multiple entities, jurisdictions, or currencies.

Experience with post-merger integration or tracking acquired businesses within a group.

Listed-company experience, including disclosure, results, or investor meetings.
