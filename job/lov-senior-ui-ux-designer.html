Senior UI / UX Designer

Black Banner Studios · České Budějovice · Remote

Senior Design Remote Posted 2026-11-20 Full time
Apply on Black Banner Studios’s site →

We're looking for an exceptional Senior UI / UX Designer with a strong artistic background and extensive experience creating beautiful, intuitive, and functional interfaces for complex PC and console games. Legacy of Valor combines third-person RPG gameplay, massive real-time battles, tactical army control, settlement management, and a strategic world map. We're looking for someone who can bring these different gameplay systems together through a cohesive, visually distinctive, and highly intuitive user interface. This is a hands-on design role combining outstanding visual design skills with a deep understanding of user experience, information architecture, player interaction, and in-game usability. You'll work closely with our Game Director, Design Director, Art Director, Gameplay Designers, and programmers to design, prototype, and refine the game's interfaces, from combat HUDs and tactical controls to inventory screens, settlement management, and strategic gameplay systems. We're looking for someone who understands that a great game interface must not only look beautiful, but also communicate complex information clearly, respond naturally to player input, and enhance the overall gameplay experience. If you believe you'd be a great fit for this role, even if you don't meet every requirement listed below, we'd still love to hear from you. Please don't hesitate to apply! Design beautiful, visually distinctive, intuitive, and consistent user interfaces across all major gameplay systems. Create and refine HUDs, menus, inventory screens, character interfaces, tactical controls, world maps, settlement management screens, and other interactive UI elements. Develop clear and efficient UX flows for complex RPG, strategy, and management systems, ensuring that important information remains accessible and easy to understand. Establish a cohesive visual language for the game's UI, inspired by its grounded medieval setting while maintaining excellent readability and usability. Design interfaces that support smooth transitions between third-person character control, tactical army command, strategic gameplay, and settlement management. Produce wireframes, mockups, interactive prototypes, visual assets, and detailed UI / UX specifications. Prototype and implement UI functionality using Unreal Engine 5 UMG and Blueprint tools, working closely with programmers on production integration. Design and refine interaction patterns for mouse and keyboard, including customizable controls, tooltips, contextual actions, and efficient navigation. Collaborate with Gameplay Designers to translate complex mechanics, statistics, resources, and player decisions into clear, understandable interfaces. Create and maintain reusable UI components, visual guidelines, and consistent interaction standards. Continuously test and refine interfaces through playtesting, usability feedback, and iteration. Work with the Art Director and technical specialists to ensure that UI design maintains high artistic standards while respecting performance and production requirements. A genuine passion for playing video games, particularly on PC and consoles, with broad familiarity across different genres. Extensive professional experience designing and delivering high-quality UI / UX for PC and / or console video games. Previous significant UI / UX design responsibility on at least one commercially released game, preferably an RPG, strategy, simulation, or other systems-heavy title. Exceptional visual design skills, demonstrated through an outstanding portfolio of beautiful, polished, and production-quality game interfaces. Strong understanding of UX design, including information architecture, interaction design, usability, player navigation, and the presentation of complex gameplay systems. Proven experience designing interfaces for RPGs, strategy games, management games, or similarly complex gameplay experiences. Strong practical experience with Unreal Engine 5 UMG and Blueprints, including the creation and refinement of functional UI prototypes. Advanced proficiency with professional UI / UX design tools such as Figma, Adobe Photoshop, Illustrator, or equivalent software. Excellent understanding of typography, composition, colour, iconography, visual hierarchy, readability, and interface consistency. Strong ability to create clear wireframes, interactive prototypes, detailed specifications, and implementation-ready UI designs. Experience designing efficient interfaces for mouse and keyboard, with a strong understanding of PC gaming conventions. Ability to balance visual aesthetics with usability, gameplay readability, accessibility, and performance. Proven ability to independently design, prototype, test, and refine interfaces based on gameplay requirements and player feedback. Excellent communication and collaboration skills, particularly when working with Gameplay Designers, artists, and programmers. Experience designing interfaces for games combining RPG, real-time strategy, and management systems. Experience with medieval, historical, or grounded fantasy games, including creating UI styles that complement their visual identity. Experience designing complex tactical interfaces, strategic world maps, resource management screens, and settlement-building systems. Experience designing UI for gamepad input and adapting interfaces between mouse / keyboard and controller navigation. Experience with accessibility features, including scalable interfaces, colour-blind accessibility, and customizable UI elements. Experience with Unreal Engine Common UI or other advanced UI frameworks. Background in graphic design, illustration, concept art, or motion graphics. Experience creating UI animations, transitions, and interactive visual feedback. Experience with Unreal Engine C++ programming or other UI-related programming languages. Experience working on AAA and / or large open-world game projects. Location: Remote / International, with the option to work in the Czech Republic. Engagement: Full-time, long-term collaboration. Contract: Contractor / B2B worldwide, or direct employment for candidates residing in or willing to relocate to the Czech Republic. Start Date: As soon as mutually agreed. Language: Professional working proficiency in English. Compensation: Competitive, based on experience and expertise.
