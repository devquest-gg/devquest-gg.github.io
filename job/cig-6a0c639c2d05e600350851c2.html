About Games Latest Join us 58 Senior Lighting Artist Studio: Manchester See all offers Category: Art See all offers Founded in 2012 by renowned game developer Chris Roberts, Cloud Imperium Games (CIG) is one of the largest global independent studios, dedicated to delivering next-generation AAA games. CIG prides itself in thinking outside the box, encouraging creativity and developing ground-breaking technology. CIG believes in raising the bar for the gaming industry and the gaming community as a whole.

About the Role As a Senior Lighting Artist at CIG, you will be joining a team responsible for lighting all the amazing locations in Star Citizen.

Star Citizen is a universe filled with solar systems, each of which are filled with diverse locations all of which the player can seamlessly fly down to from space and explore on foot.

In this senior position, you will take ownership of implementing and overseeing high-quality, complex, and cinematic realtime lighting scenarios for Star Citizen. This could be anything from a moody old Space Station in low orbit around a planet to a neon-filled cityscape located on a planet. The art pillars of the game are Cinematic Sci-Fi Realism. You will play a pivotal role in establishing the mood, tone, and visual quality for environments, ensuring the final frame showcases the artwork to the highest possible industry standard.

This is an onsite role based at our studio in Manchester, UK.

Check out what our Lighting team get up to:

https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=Cqt1fl56BlE&t=1s What does a Senior Lighting Artist at CIG do? Create and champion believable, cinematic and narrative-driven lighting scenarios for major locations in the game.

Optimise and ensure complex lighting setups work seamlessly within strict defined realtime rendering budgets, proactively identifying performance bottlenecks.

Work closely with the Lighting Lead and Art Director to interpret, execute, and help maintain the overall artistic vision across the project.

Collaborate deeply with other departments (Environment Art, Tech Art, Design) to pioneer workflows and help create the best player experience possible.

Act as a technical and artistic resource for junior and mid-level artists, helping elevate the team’s collective skillset.

What do we expect from our Senior Lighting Artists? Extensive experience in creating high-quality, AAA realtime lighting scenarios, with a strong portfolio showcasing a masterful handle on mood and composition.

Someone who is deeply passionate about creating inspirational lighting moods and storytelling through light.

Deep understanding of material shading, physically based rendering (PBR), and how the interplay of different materials impacts the final frame under varying light conditions.

A profound knowledge of cinematic lighting principles (composition, colour theory, exposure) combined with the technical know-how to implement them in a cutting-edge engine.

Someone who draws from a vast variety of reference material to constantly challenge themselves and push the boundaries of what is possible in realtime games.

Excellent communication skills, with a collaborative mindset and the ability to thrive within a highly passionate, fast-paced team environment.

If you have a proven track record of creating beautiful, exotic sci-fi environments that players get lost in, and you are ready to help lead the charge on a ground-breaking universe, please apply immediately! Diversity Statement

Fraud Statement

We are aware of people receiving job offers that fraudulently allege to be from CIG. These types of fraud can be carried out through false websites, fake email addresses claiming to be from our company, or social media. We do not ask for your personal info like bank account numbers, identification numbers, etc through social media or chat-based apps, nor do we request or send money for the purchase of business equipment.

If you suspect fraud, please report it to your local authorities, as well as reaching out to us at info @ cloudimperiumgames.com with any information you may have.
