The Opportunity We&#x27;re looking for a Senior Game Artist to join our New Game team in London. If you love building worlds that tell a story, and you care about how art improves the player experience, you&#x27;ll fit right in.

We&#x27;re making an original casual mobile game, and there&#x27;s real room to shape it. You&#x27;ll own the art for its expanding isometric map and world: the 2D environments and assets that carry the narrative, mark the player&#x27;s progress and tell the story visually. You&#x27;ll take that work from first concept to final implementation in Unity, reporting to our Art Director and working closely with artists, designers and developers.

We&#x27;re a small team of artists with big ambition, in a Supercell-backed studio. Seniority here is about more than your own artwork. Success looks like a world players want to keep exploring, a way of making content that&#x27;s quicker and clearer because of you, and a visual bar that&#x27;s higher across the whole team.

The Day to Day Own the map and world: design and build the expanding isometric map, using visual storytelling to support narrative, world-building and player progression.

Create beautiful 2D art: render environments, map content and game assets in Photoshop to a polished, production-ready finish.

Concept quickly: explore new content, features and visual ideas fast, and help define the game&#x27;s visual direction.

Take your art into the game: prepare, implement and manage your work in Unity, from early concept to final asset.

Animate with economy: create animations and effects that land with impact while staying light on a phone.

Make gameplay clear: work with design and engineering so the art communicates progression, mechanics and features, and cut the detail that distracts.

Support UI and features: help with UI and player-facing feature art where the game needs it.

Scope and plan: plan your own work, be clear about what&#x27;s achievable in the time, and help build a steady rhythm of new content for players.

Work with outsourcing partners: brief and review external art, giving clear feedback that keeps quality and style consistent.

Improve how we make art with AI: help shape our asset pipelines and production processes, and use AI tools in your workflow where they speed up production without costing quality or creative intent.

Learn from players: evolve the art as we test with our targeted demographics, primarily women aged 30-50, and learn what our players respond to.

Raise the bar together: start creative conversations when they&#x27;ll make the game better, give and take feedback openly, share what you know and support the artists around you.

About You You&#x27;re a versatile artist with excellent rendering, strong creative instincts and the technical confidence to get your work into the game. You know great game art has to do more than look good: it guides the player.

Excellent 2D art, rendering and concepting skills, with a portfolio that shows both creative thinking and polished final work.

Expert Photoshop skills.

Experience creating art for casual mobile games.

Experience designing and producing isometric maps or environments.

Strong working knowledge of Unity, including preparing and implementing 2D assets in-engine.

Animating economically for mobile in Unity.

A strong eye for visual hierarchy: you know which details strengthen the player&#x27;s experience and which get in the way.

Experience on a game with a regular flow of new player content, and the planning skills to scope and deliver it on time.

Hands-on experience using AI tools in your art workflow, with good judgement about where they help and where they don&#x27;t.

A track record of making the work around you better, such as improving a workflow, raising a visual standard or helping other artists grow.

Great to Have Experience briefing and reviewing work from outsourcing partners.

Experience animating and rigging in Spine or a similar tool.

Experience designing player-facing UI feature art.

If you don&#x27;t tick every box but this sounds like you, we&#x27;d still love to hear from you. Please apply with your portfolio.
