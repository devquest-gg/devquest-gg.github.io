Senior Producer, Multimedia (Contract)

Bungie · Remote · Remote

Senior Production Remote Posted 2026-08-07 Contractor
Apply on Bungie’s site →

Bungie is seeking a Senior Producer, Multimedia to lead creative media production from intake to delivery. The Senior Producer will oversee projects across multiple mediums, including gameplay capture, art design, video and livestream production. This role will drive alignment between Creative Leads, Brand Marketing teams, external vendors, and finance stakeholders, ensuring projects are strategically scoped and delivered to AAA studio standards. The ideal candidate combines game industry expertise with a strong track record of facilitating cross-functional collaboration and operational excellence.

This role reports to the Principal Operations Manager.

Responsibilities :

Oversee the production of multimedia content throughout the project pipeline, ensuring timely delivery across platforms, including short-form gameplay videos, GIFs, screen shots, composites, live action video, livestreams, etc.

Serve as production partner for studio stakeholders, creative leads, game development leads, and external vendors, building relationships and driving alignment across project goals, scope, timelines, approvals, and delivery

Maintain streamlined, proactive cross-team communications with partner teams, including legal, PR, finance, localization and game development teams

Evaluate project scope, hours, and costs with a critical eye toward efficiency, value, and stakeholder goals, including diligence on bids and external estimates

Manage project meetings, timelines and documentation, including production schedules, budgets, key milestones, and stakeholder reviews

Establish and refine production workflows, tools, and documentation to improve clarity and efficiency, driving toward a maximized sustainable output

Maintain assessment of creative team capacity and capabilities, scaling talent as needed to foster sustainability while meeting business needs

Required Skills :

6+ years of experience producing digital media, ideally in the gaming or entertainment industries

Proven track record delivering multiple concurrent projects on agile timelines

Demonstrated rigor in project tracking throughout asset lifespan, adhering to communication norms, transparent processes, and industry best practices

Strong business acumen, including experience with budgeting, scoping, tracking, and resource planning

Deep familiarity with video production pipelines, both gameplay and live action

Exceptional interpersonal and communication skills with ability to speak in creative and operational contexts across all levels of the organization

Ability to build trusted partnerships and navigate complex stakeholder environments with diplomacy and confidence

Must be a creative problem solver who is highly adaptable and solutions-driven

Mastery of Microsoft Outlook, Excel, Word, PowerPoint, Miro, and project management software such as Asana/Jira

Preferred skills:

Hands-on expertise producing live-action and/or livestream events

Familiarity with Adobe Creative Suite
