3D Gameplay Animator

Gameloft · Lviv

Senior Animation Posted 2026-11-17 Full time
Apply on Gameloft’s site →

WHAT YOU WILL BE WORKING ON Main challenge Bring beloved Disney and Pixar characters to life through high-quality, expressive, and engaging gameplay animations for Disney Magic Kingdoms , a mobile game enjoyed by players around the world.

What it means on a daily basis Create high-quality, stylized character and creature animations that reflect the personality and charm of Disney characters.

Develop and refine gameplay animations while ensuring smooth integration into the game engine.

Collaborate with artists, game designers, and developers to ensure animations support gameplay and creative objectives.

Identify and resolve technical and artistic animation issues while respecting mobile performance constraints.

Contribute to improving animation workflows, tools, and production pipelines.

What success will look like You consistently deliver polished, expressive, and technically optimized animations that enhance the player experience, meet production deadlines, and align with the game's established visual style.

Who you would be working with You'll join the Disney Magic Kingdoms production team, collaborating closely with animators, artists, game designers, and developers in an international environment.

Your recruitment journey First interview: Introductory conversation with the recruiter.

Test: Practical animation assignment.

Second interview: Technical discussion with the hiring team.

Final interview: Additional team discussion, if needed.

Where this role can take you Develop your expertise in gameplay animation, contribute to a globally recognized Disney title, and explore professional growth opportunities within Gameloft's international teams.

WHAT YOU NEED TO SUCCEED Professional experience in 3D animation within the video game industry.

Strong portfolio demonstrating stylized character and gameplay animations.

Advanced proficiency in 3D animation software, with Autodesk 3ds Max being a strong advantage.

Solid understanding of keyframe animation principles, character movement, timing, and posing.

Understanding of game engine animation systems, technical constraints, and animation pipelines.

WHO YOU ARE Passionate about video games and bringing characters to life.

Creative, detail-oriented, and attentive to animation quality.

Collaborative, proactive, and comfortable receiving feedback.

Organized, adaptable, and able to meet production deadlines.

WHAT'S IN IT FOR YOU Benefits Remote work opportunities within Ukraine.

Competitive salary based on your experience and skills.

21 days of paid vacation, 5 paid sick days, plus national holidays.

Medical insurance and access to psychological support.

Parental leave support.

Internal learning platform with courses, training, and certifications.

Employee Referral Program with bonuses.

Full technical support and equipment provided.

Life at Gameloft Join a global video game company where creativity, collaboration, and passion for gaming come together. Work with an experienced international team on Disney Magic Kingdoms, bringing iconic characters and memorable experiences to players worldwide.

How to proceed Interested in bringing Disney characters to life? Apply with your CV and animation portfolio or demo reel . We'd love to see your work!
