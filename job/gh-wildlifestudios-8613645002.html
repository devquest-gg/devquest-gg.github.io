About the Team The Art Team at Wildlife is growing. With an audience that numbers in the millions, our games have the power to impact countless lives. Our games connect people with interesting characters, vibrant worlds, and unforgettable experiences. That’s no small task, but we have a powerful secret weapon: our people. The Wildlife team is a collection of world-class talent, a team focused on synergy, collaboration, and growing strong together.

About the Role Our Real-Time VFX Artists help breath life, depth and immersion to our game experiences. For that to happen, the Real-Time VFX Artists work closely on all aspects of the game development process. Our Real-Time VFX Artists have a strong sense of elevating gameplay by adding dynamic, clear and breathtaking effects that will astonish our players.

Who you are: • Craft Knowledge: You craft and implement compelling moments using your knowledge of shape language, values, colors, textures and animation principles. • Team Player: You are a self-motivated team player who proactively seeks feedback and shares knowledge. • Pipeline: You are always on top of the latest tools and techniques to help shape our pipelines. • Software Expertise: You have experience working across multiple software packages to create effective VFX assets such as Photoshop, After Effects, Maya, Substance Designer, Unity, etc.

What you’ll do: • Collaboration: You will work closely with Art Directors to help establish a cohesive look for our games. You will also work with various other artists and disciplines such as engineering, design and audio to bring viscerality to our gameplay. • Problem Solving: You will prototype VFX and shaders which effectively develop tone and pacing of gameplay events. • Communication : You will use your excellent communication skills in a collaborative and diverse work environment. • Game Developer Mindset: You will balance both the quality and efficiency of your VFX assets while working under tight deadlines. • Prototyper : To achieve the best product value, you will iterate on what feels right. You’ll be tasked with concepting and blocking out solutions to test in game during the development process. It’s not all about the polishing.

What you’ll need: • Experience working as a VFX Artist in games or proven ability to execute real-time assets with a style varying from realistic to stylized • A resume and an online portfolio showcasing your work.

You know your way around at least one game engine (Unity) and understand the various parts of the game development pipeline.

Pluses:

You have experience working in mobile titles.

You own a basic knowledge of motion graphics and 3d asset creation.

We welcome people from all backgrounds who seek the opportunity to help build the best gaming company, where everyone thrives.

Please note that an art test may be required during the evaluation process.
