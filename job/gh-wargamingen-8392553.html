Job Overview

Wargaming is looking for a Learning Development Business Partner who knows how to turn business needs into engaging and practical learning experiences.

In this role, you will work closely with leaders and teams to identify development needs, design training programs and facilitate workshops that help people grow and perform better.

We are looking for someone who enjoys both creating and delivering training , is confident working with different audiences and is curious about how AI and technology can make learning more effective.

Reports to

Head of Global L D.

What will you do?

Partner with business leaders and teams to understand their challenges, identify development needs and create relevant learning solutions.

Design and develop training programs and workshops from scratch, including content, exercises and learning materials.

Facilitate engaging training sessions and workshops, both in person and virtually.

Adapt learning content and delivery to different audiences and business needs.

Explore how AI and digital tools can improve training design, content creation, learning experiences and L D processes.

Work with internal experts and external providers to develop and deliver learning initiatives.

Manage L D projects from initial needs analysis through delivery and evaluation.

Measure learning effectiveness, gather feedback and continuously improve our programs.

Bring fresh ideas and new approaches to how we develop people at Wargaming.

What are we looking for?

5+ years of experience in Learning Development, including hands on experience designing and delivering training.

Strong facilitation skills and confidence running interactive workshops for different audiences.

Experience developing training content from scratch, including presentations, exercises and learning activities.

Good understanding of adult learning principles and instructional design.

Ability to translate business needs into practical learning solutions.

Experience using AI tools and an interest in how technology can improve learning.

Strong communication and stakeholder management skills.

Experience managing learning projects and working with multiple stakeholders.

A curious, creative and practical mindset, with a willingness to try new approaches.

What additional skills will help you stand out?

Experience designing and facilitating leadership, management or soft skills training.

Experience with digital learning tools, learning platforms and AI powered content creation.

Experience working with external training providers.

Experience in gaming, technology or startup environments.

Russian language skills.

Work mode

On-site or Hybrid (2-3 days of work from the office).

This role can be based in one of our offices in Belgrade (Serbia), Nicosia (Cyprus), Vilnius (Lithuania) or Prague (Czech Republic). Immigration support is available where required.

About Wargaming

Wargaming is an award-winning online game developer and publisher headquartered in Nicosia, Cyprus. Operating since 1998, Wargaming has become one of the leaders in the gaming industry with 15 offices worldwide, including studios in Chicago, Prague, Shanghai, Tokyo, and Vilnius. Our diverse and multicultural team works together to deliver a top-class experience to millions of players who enjoy Wargaming s titles across all major gaming platforms. Our flagship products include free-to-play hits World of Tanks, World of Warships and World of Tanks Blitz.
