Director, Technical Program Management Engineering Business Operations

San Mateo, CA (Hybrid)

Sony Interactive Entertainment is seeking a Director of Technical Program Management Engineering Business Operations to lead the Technical Program Management function within Foundational Systems Experiences Engineering (FSEE) while serving as a strategic partner to executive leadership.

This role combines leadership of the FSEE TPM organization in the Americas with responsibility for Engineering Business Operations in support of the VP s organization. The Director will lead and grow a team of Technical Program Managers driving execution across FSEE while partnering closely with executive leadership on organizational planning, strategic initiatives, communications, and operational excellence across a globally distributed organization.

You ll report to the VP of Engineering and serve as a member of their Engineering Leadership Team, acting as a trusted advisor on organizational strategy, planning, and execution. You will help shape the direction of the organization, drive alignment across their leadership teams, and ensure strategic priorities are translated into measurable outcomes.

This is a hybrid role based in San Mateo, California. Occasional international travel is expected to support our globally distributed teams.

Responsibilities

Technical Program Management Leadership

Lead, mentor, and grow the Americas-based FSEE TPM team, building a culture of ownership, collaboration, and continuous improvement.

Establish the operating models, delivery standards, governance, and reporting mechanisms that make complex cross-functional programs predictable and transparent.

Partner with engineering, product, architecture, and operations leaders to drive successful execution of strategic initiatives across FSEE.

Collaborate closely with TPM leadership in EMEA to establish consistent practices, operating mechanisms, and execution standards across regions.

Develop talent, succession plans, and career paths across the TPM organization.

Serve as the functional leader for Technical Program Management, ensuring the organization has the capabilities, operating mechanisms, and talent required to support current and future business priorities.

Engineering Business Operations

Partner directly with the VP and Engineering Leadership Team to drive organizational priorities and long-term strategic objectives.

Own annual planning, quarterly business reviews, goal setting, and leadership operating cadences across the regional engineering organization.

Own executive communications and the narrative for leadership reviews, strategic updates, and organizational reporting for the regional VP.

Drive operational excellence through the establishment of metrics, dashboards, and KPIs that provide leadership with visibility into organizational health and performance.

Partner with the VP on organizational design, workforce planning, budget management, and investment prioritization.

Surface organizational risks, opportunities, execution gaps, and develop plans to address them.

Improve organizational effectiveness through scalable operating mechanisms, decision-making frameworks, and continuous improvement.

Strategic Execution Executive Partnership

Serve as a member of the VPs Engineering Leadership Team and act as a trusted advisor on organizational strategy, planning, and execution.

Facilitate executive decision-making through clear analysis, structured problem solving, and concise communication.

Drive accountability on leadership commitments, turning strategic priorities into measurable outcomes.

Required Qualifications

12+ years of experience leading complex technical programs within software, platform, infrastructure, cloud, or related engineering organizations.

5+ years of experience managing Technical Program Managers, Program Managers, or similar functions.

A track record of leading large-scale, cross-functional initiatives with senior stakeholders and influencing outcomes without direct authority.

Strong people leadership skills with experience building, developing, and growing high-performing teams.

Exceptional written, verbal, and executive communication skills.

Experience with annual planning, portfolio management, organizational operations, and executive reviews.

A solid understanding of software development lifecycles, engineering processes, and technical architectures.

Ability to operate effectively at both strategic and tactical levels, balancing long-term objectives with near-term execution.

Preferred Qualifications

Experience in Engineering Business Operations, strategic planning, or Chief of Staff-type roles supporting senior executives.

Experience leading Technical Program Management or Program Management organizations within large technology companies.

Experience working across globally distributed teams and organizations.

Experience within gaming, consumer technology, platforms, cloud services, or developer ecosystems.

MBA, advanced technical degree, or equivalent experience.

Key Outcomes

In Your First 12 Months

Build and scale a high-performing Americas-based TPM organization within FSEE with clear operating models, development paths, and execution standards.

Improve visibility, predictability, and delivery of strategic engineering initiatives across the organization.

Establish effective planning, review, reporting, and decision-making mechanisms for the Engineering Leadership Team.

Successfully lead high-impact initiatives on behalf of the VP and their Engineering Leadership Team.

Build a strong partnership with other TPM Directors to improve coordination, consistency, and execution across the global organization.

Become a trusted advisor and operational partner to the engineering VP.

What Success Looks Like

You create clarity in complex environments and bring structure to ambiguity. You help leaders make informed decisions, align organizations around shared priorities, and drive execution of critical initiatives. You build trust across teams, improve organizational effectiveness, and ensure strategic objectives translate into measurable results.

Most importantly, you serve as a force multiplier for the VP’s Engineering Leadership Team—enabling teams to focus on what matters most while helping leadership operate more effectively at scale. You successfully balance leadership of the Americas-based FSEE TPM organization with strategic partnership responsibilities that help shape and operate a global engineering organization.
