Manual QA

Gameloft · Lviv · Remote

Senior QA Remote Posted 2026-09-04 Full time
Apply on Gameloft’s site →

As a Junior Manual QA , your main challenge will be to help ensure the quality of our mobile games by identifying, documenting, and investigating issues throughout the development process.

You will work closely with Developers, Producers, and QA teams, gradually building your expertise in game testing and development processes.

What it means on a daily basis Execute test cases and perform manual testing of mobile games on iOS and Android devices; Identify, reproduce, document, prioritize, and track bugs; Verify developer fixes and make sure reported issues have been properly resolved; Work directly with Developers and Producers to investigate and reproduce issues; Collaborate with internal development and remote testing teams; Use debugging and profiling tools to investigate more complex problems; Play and analyze competitive games to help maintain a high quality standard.

What success will look like You consistently identify and clearly document issues, understand their impact on the player experience, and effectively collaborate with the development team to help deliver stable and high-quality game updates.

Who you would be working with You will work closely with QA specialists, Developers, and Producers , as well as collaborate with remote testing teams across different locations.

Your recruitment journey First interview: Recruiter interview; Second interview: Technical/team interview.

Where this role can take you This role will give you an opportunity to deepen your knowledge of game development and QA processes, work on global mobile titles, and grow toward more advanced QA responsibilities, including the possibility of developing your skills in test automation .

What you need to succeed At least 1+ year&#xa0;of experience in Manual QA ; Understanding of QA fundamentals and the game development process; Experience testing iOS and Android applications ; Good knowledge of mobile and tablet navigation; Experience with bug tracking and test management systems such as Jira, TestRail, Mantis, DevTrack , or similar; Working knowledge of Microsoft Office; English level Intermediate or higher .

Who you are You are passionate about mobile and free-to-play games; You are attentive to detail and enjoy investigating problems; You communicate clearly and can describe issues in a structured way; You are eager to learn and develop your QA skills; Interest in automation testing and basic knowledge of a scripting language would be a plus.

Benefits Possibility to work remotely in a global product company; Competitive compensation based on your experience and qualifications; 21 paid vacation days and 5 paid sick days ; National holidays as non-working days; Medical insurance and psychologist support; Parental leave; Access to an educational platform with courses, training programs, and certifications; Employee Referral Program; Technical support and necessary equipment.

Life at Gameloft You will join an international team working on global gaming products in a friendly and creative environment. You’ll have the opportunity to learn from experienced colleagues, contribute to games played worldwide, and develop your career within the video game industry.

How to proceed If this sounds like the right opportunity for you, apply and share your CV with us . We’ll review your profile and contact you to discuss the role and the next steps.
