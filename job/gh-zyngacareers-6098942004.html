Join us and be part of the play! Position Overview:

Join us in our mission to connect the world through games! Zynga’s Words with Friends is seeking a dedicated and passionate Senior Product Manager to help shape the future of one of the world s most beloved social games.

We are looking for a product leader who can own critical projects, drive results, and directly impact the business performance of a large-scale live service. In this role, you will work closely with a cross-functional team of engineers, designers, analysts, and marketers to define, build, and launch features that delight millions of players every day.

What You ll Do:

Own the Product Lifecycle: Manage the end-to-end lifecycle for new features and optimizations, from initial concept and specification writing through to launch and post-launch analysis.

Design Features: Author clear and comprehensive product specifications, user stories, and wireframes to guide the development team.

Execute on Strategy: Partner with leadership to inform and execute the product strategy and roadmap for your area of focus, ensuring alignment with our core business goals.

Be Data-Driven: Define, monitor, and analyze core metrics and KPIs. Use a hypothesis-driven approach to design and execute A/B tests and other experiments to drive continuous improvement.

Lead a Cross-Functional Team: Collaborate daily with engineering, design, art, and data science to balance business objectives, player experience, and technical investment.

Be the Voice of the Player: Translate player feedback, community insights, and market analysis into actionable product requirements and innovative feature ideas.

Drive Results: Take full ownership of your features performance, consistently tracking results against defined expectations and communicating outcomes to the broader team.

What You Bring:

Product Management Experience: 3+ years of product management experience, preferably in consumer-facing mobile apps, web, or game development.

Strong Product Sense: A proven ability to concept new feature ideas, understand the competitive market landscape, and deconstruct user-facing problems.

Analytical Acumen: Excellent analytical and quantitative skills; a natural curiosity for data and the ability to use it to make informed, high-impact decisions.

Exceptional Communication: Outstanding written, oral, and presentation skills. You can effectively articulate complex ideas to diverse audiences, from small teams to senior leadership.

Execution and Organization: Proven ability to manage multiple projects in a fast-paced environment, with strong organizational skills and meticulous attention to detail.

Collaborative Spirit: A team-first mentality, with experience interpreting community feedback and a passion for sharing insights that help other PMs grow.

Passion for Games: A genuine passion for games and crafting fun, compelling, and exciting user experiences.

Education: BA/BS degree or equivalent practical experience.
