Director of User Experience (UX) Research

Discord · Remote (U.S.) · Remote

Director+ Design Remote $320K–$400K Posted 2026-11-25 Full time
Apply on Discord’s site →

Discord has a highly engaged community of millions of daily active users who use the platform for many different reasons, but there’s one thing that nearly everyone does: play video games.

Discord plays a uniquely important role in the future of gaming, and we are focused on making it easier and more fun for people to hang out before, during, and after playing games.

Discord is hiring a Director of User Experience Research to lead our research team, drive a user-centered understanding of Players (the people who use Discord), and turn that understanding into a better product. You ll own the research strategy for Discord, build and lead a team of researchers, and make sure a deep, evidence-based understanding of user needs shapes what we build before decisions are made.

You ll uncover and forecast the problems our users face and the opportunities that will add the most value to Discord. You ll deepen our understanding of every user cohort, from longtime gamers to new communities, creators, moderators, and teens. And you ll make sure what we ship results in user value for them. As Discord brings more AI-powered experiences to users, you ll ensure they re grounded in real needs and held to a clear, user-centered quality bar. You ll also modernize how research gets done, using AI to increase our speed and reach without losing rigor.

You ll partner closely with Product, Design, Engineering, Data Science, and Safety. This person will report to the VP of Product Design and Insights.

What You ll Be Doing

Set the research vision and make research a strategic partner.

Own the research roadmap across Discord, balancing foundational, generative, and evaluative work and prioritizing by user impact, business impact, and risk. Deliver clear, compelling readouts that shape prioritization and go/no-go calls, and measure research s impact on product outcomes.

Uncover opportunities and build a shared understanding of our users.

Lead foundational and generative research that surfaces unmet needs, pain points, and emerging behaviors, and use segmentation, longitudinal, and international research to understand how different user cohorts use Discord and where their needs differ, so teams design for real people instead of assumptions.

Own usability and concept validation.

Establish a consistent program for testing concepts, prototypes, and live experiences, so teams learn early whether ideas are desirable, usable, and valuable before investing heavily.

Bring a mixed-methods lens to every major decision.

Partner with Data Science to combine qualitative insight with quantitative evidence (surveys, behavioral data, experimentation) that explains both what users do and why. Define and track experience-health measures such as satisfaction, ease, and trust over time.

Define user-centered quality for AI-powered experiences.

Work with Product, Engineering, and ML partners to set quality dimensions, rubrics, and human evaluation programs that complement automated and model-based evaluation, so AI features solve real Player problems and earn user trust.

Build and scale the practice.

Hire and develop researchers and research leads, and create the career paths, rituals, and research operations they need. Equip PMs and designers to run lightweight research with the right guardrails.

Modernize research with care and integrity.

Adopt and govern AI-assisted tools for recruiting, moderation, synthesis, and insight management that help the team move faster and reach more users. Set the standard for ethical research, including rigor, bias, privacy, consent, and research with teens and vulnerable communities, in partnership with Legal, Privacy, and Safety.

What you should have

12+ years of experience in UX research or a closely related field (such as HCI, Psychology, or Cognitive Science), including 6+ years leading researchers and research leads

Deep expertise across generative and evaluative methods, both qualitative and quantitative, with the judgment to turn ambiguous questions into a clear set of risks and design right-sized research for fast-moving product contexts

A track record of research that uncovered new opportunities and changed product direction, not only validated existing plans, including usability and concept-testing programs that teams relied on

Experience understanding and segmenting diverse user populations, ideally in consumer social, community, communication, or multi-sided platforms

Experience researching AI or LLM-powered products, including human evaluation, rubric design, and inter-rater reliability, with a clear view of where automated evaluation helps and where it falls short

Fluency with AI-enabled research and data tools, and sound judgment about when they strengthen research and when they weaken it

A history of earning buy-in from executives and cross-functional partners by telling the user story and making the "so what" impossible to ignore

Bonus Points

Experience in gaming, creator, or real-time communication products

Experience conducting research with teens or in trust and safety contexts

Global or multi-market research experience

Background in survey science, experimentation, or behavioral data analysis
