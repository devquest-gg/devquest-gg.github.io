Lead Systems Designer (Economy and Engagement Focus)

Reports to: Design Lead

We are a small studio dedicated to crafting unique and high-quality interactive experiences, and we are looking for a talented Lead Systems Designer to guide and shape the holistic design of our game economy, core features, and player engagement. You’ll combine leadership with direct hands-on contributions, helping to refine the structure and our gameplay experiences while managing a small team of designers.

This role is ideal for someone who thrives at the intersection of systems thinking, live game design, and practical implementation with an intense player focus — someone who can lead a feature from concept to live operations as well as refine existing systems.

What you'll do here:

- Lead the design and evolution of core systems such as in-game economies (large and small), progression, rewards, and feature loops that drive long-term engagement

- Design and implement interactive features and gameplay patterns with a focus on simplicity and relatability

- Collaborate across disciplines — with engineering, art, and production — to ensure designs are holistic, feasible, and aligned with creative goals

- Analyze player data and behavior to tune economies and experiences that inspire players to return for years to come

- Mentor and manage a small number of designers, helping them grow and thrive in a collaborative environment

- Drive and evangelize best practices in systems design, economy balancing, and live feature iteration

- Help improve and extend internal design tools and pipelines to empower the full design team

You Might Be a Fit If You:

- Help improve and extend internal design tools and pipelines to empower the full design team

- Have deep experience designing and tuning systems for live games, including progression mechanics, currencies, economies, and player engagement loops

- Are skilled in both conceptual design and hands-on implementation (e.g., using scripting or prototyping tools to bring systems to life)

- Think holistically about feature ecosystems and their impact on player motivation and long-term retention

- Thrive in small, collaborative teams and are comfortable wearing multiple hats

- Have experience mentoring or managing other designers, even in a flat or hybrid leadership model

- Care deeply about the player experience and designing systems that are meaningful, ethical, and fun

- Don't mind taking an assessment/proprietary test during the interview process.

Must-Haves:

- Extensive experience with live games and economy design

- Strong background in systems design, meta systems, and player progression

- Experience balancing game systems based on player feedback and analytics

- Ability to understand complex variable-driven relationships and how to use them to create simple, relatable gameplay

- Experience working in 3D engines (Unity, Unreal, or similar)

- Code literacy to read and understand scripts and do minor tweaks

- Familiarity with multiplayer and online game systems

Nice-to-Haves:

- Proficiency in scripting languages or light programming (e.g., C#, C++, or visual scripting)

- Seek adventure and uncertainty in exploring experimental and risky game concept

- Familiarity with rapid iteration and data-informed design decisions

- Experience integrating monetization thoughtfully within player-centric systems

- Have a desire to create meaningful, enriching experiences that touch and inspire players

- A strong desire to work directly with data scientists to create a deep understanding of players that helps everyone create better player experiences
