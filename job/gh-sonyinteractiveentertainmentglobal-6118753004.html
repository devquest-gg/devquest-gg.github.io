Technical Operations Support (TOS) Analyst Lead

As part of Sony Interactive Entertainment, GDFT is helping lead the cloud gaming revolution, delivering console-quality gaming experiences across televisions, consoles, mobile devices, and beyond.

The Technical Operations Support (TOS) team is a critical component of the operational foundation that enables GDFT to support a global cloud gaming platform at scale. The team provides frontline operational support for infrastructure, services, and internal processes that help ensure reliable delivery of world-class streaming experiences to players around the world.

As a Technical Operations Support Analyst Lead, you will serve as a senior operational leader and subject matter expert responsible for supporting operational processes, driving continuous improvement initiatives, mentoring analysts, and helping ensure the successful execution of regional and global operational objectives.

In addition to providing advanced operational support, you will lead process improvement efforts, coordinate team priorities, report operational metrics, and partner closely with Technical Operations, Engineering, and leadership teams to improve operational efficiency and platform reliability.

This role is based in Aliso Viejo, California.

Key Responsibilities

Operational Support Leadership

Serve as a Technical Operations Support subject matter expert (SME) and primary point of contact for operational processes, tooling, and procedures.

Acknowledge, triage, and resolve support requests by following established Standard Operating Procedures (SOPs).

Perform advanced operational tasks and maintenance activities while meeting service level agreement (SLA) commitments.

Escalate issues appropriately when incidents or requests fall outside established support procedures.

Support day-to-day operational activities required to maintain platform stability and service availability.

Monitor operational queues and ensure timely resolution of assigned work.

Generate workload forecasts and assist in organizing ticket priorities across the team to ensure effective resource utilization and SLA compliance.

Maintain awareness of team workload distribution and operational performance to proactively address support challenges.

Documentation Process Management

Create, update, and maintain SOPs, knowledge base articles, and operational documentation.

Ensure documentation follows team standards and remains accurate and up to date.

Drive process standardization efforts that improve operational effectiveness across teams and regions.

Identify gaps in existing procedures and implement improvements that enhance service quality and operational maturity.

Continuous Improvement Technical Development

Identify recurring issues, repetitive tasks, and operational inefficiencies.

Lead initiatives focused on automation opportunities and operational workflow improvements.

Partner with technical teams to improve workflows, tooling, and operational processes.

Develop advanced troubleshooting knowledge and technical expertise across infrastructure, cloud services, and operational systems.

Continuously enhance technical capabilities through structured self-development, training, and hands-on learning.

Contribute ideas that help reduce manual effort and improve team productivity.

Team Development Mentorship

Provide guidance, coaching, and operational support to Tier 1 and Tier 2 analysts.

Share knowledge and best practices to improve overall team capability and operational effectiveness.

Identify learning and development opportunities within the team and help drive knowledge-building initiatives.

Foster a culture of accountability, continuous improvement, and operational excellence.

Support onboarding and mentoring efforts for new team members.

Collaboration

Work closely with team leads, engineers, and global operational teams to support business objectives.

Participate in operational reviews, process improvement initiatives, and cross-functional projects.

Communicate clearly regarding ticket status, operational risks, and support activities.
