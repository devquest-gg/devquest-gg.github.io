Senior Combat Designer

Black Banner Studios · České Budějovice · Remote

Senior Design Remote Posted 2026-11-20 Full time
Apply on Black Banner Studios’s site →

We're looking for an exceptional Senior Combat Designer with extensive hands-on experience creating dynamic, responsive, and deeply satisfying third-person or first-person melee combat systems, particularly for medieval or grounded fantasy games. Legacy of Valor combines direct third-person character control with massive medieval battles and tactical army command. We're developing a new proprietary Player Character Control & Combat System, with a strong focus on directional melee combat, responsive movement, sophisticated animation blending, and rewarding player skill. We're looking for someone who understands what makes combat genuinely enjoyable, from movement, timing, attacks, blocking, and counters to animation responsiveness, hit feedback, and the subtle details that make every action feel right. This is a hands-on design and prototyping role. You'll work closely with our Game Director, Gameplay Programmers, Technical Animators, and other team members to design, prototype, implement, test, and refine the combat system in Unreal Engine 5. We're looking for someone who can take ownership of combat design, independently experiment with different approaches, and help us develop a system that is both accessible and rewarding to master. If you believe you'd be a great fit for this role, even if you don't meet every requirement listed below, we'd still love to hear from you. Please don't hesitate to apply! Design, prototype, playtest, and refine a responsive, skill-based third-person melee combat system, using Unreal Engine 5 Blueprints to develop and validate gameplay mechanics. Design and fine-tune directional attacks, blocking, parrying, countering, and other combat interactions, working closely with Gameplay Programmers and Technical Animators to bring them to life. Design and tune character movement, camera controls, attack timing, stamina, combat transitions, and input responsiveness. Work closely with Technical Animators to develop high-quality split-body animation systems supporting independent upper- and lower-body movement, directional attacks, and fluid combat transitions. Prototype, test, and refine combat mechanics directly in Unreal Engine 5, primarily using Blueprints and animation-related tools. Design and refine weapon behaviour, hit detection, damage windows, hit reactions, and combat feedback. Develop combat mechanics that remain satisfying and readable in both duels and large-scale battles involving multiple opponents. Collaborate with Gameplay Programmers to translate gameplay prototypes into robust, performant, and network-compatible production systems. Create detailed combat design documentation, including mechanics, player controls, state transitions, balancing parameters, and implementation requirements. Continuously playtest, evaluate, and iterate on combat mechanics, using both internal feedback and player testing to improve the experience. Help establish consistent combat design principles that can support additional weapons, fighting styles, and character abilities. A genuine passion for playing video games, particularly on PC and consoles, with broad familiarity across different genres. Previous experience as Senior Combat Designer on at least one major commercially released game, with significant responsibility for its combat mechanics and overall combat experience. Extensive hands-on experience designing and implementing dynamic, responsive, and enjoyable third-person and / or first-person combat systems. Proven experience designing and implementing directional melee combat and split-body character combat systems. Deep practical knowledge of Unreal Engine 5's animation systems, including Animation Blueprints, montages, blend spaces, animation layering, IK, and animation state management. Advanced practical experience with Unreal Engine 5 Blueprints, including independently prototyping, testing, and tuning combat mechanics. Excellent understanding of player controls, movement, camera behaviour, input responsiveness, combat timing, and animation-driven gameplay. Strong understanding of melee combat mechanics, including directional attacks, blocking, counters, hit detection, stamina management, and combat feedback. Ability to identify and solve animation and gameplay issues such as input latency, unresponsive transitions, foot sliding, unnatural body rotation, and inconsistent attack timing. Strong ability to create clear, comprehensive design documentation and translate gameplay concepts into practical implementation requirements. Proven ability to independently prototype, playtest, evaluate, and refine combat systems until they achieve exceptional gameplay quality. Excellent communication and collaboration skills, particularly when working with programmers, animators, and other designers. Experience developing combat systems inspired by games such as Chivalry 2, Mordhau, Mount & Blade II: Bannerlord, or similar titles. Experience designing combat for multiplayer or networked action games, including latency, prediction, and replication considerations. Experience with mounted combat, fighting multiple opponents, or combat within large-scale battles. Background in character animation, with practical experience creating, editing, or integrating gameplay animations. Familiarity with Unreal Engine's Gameplay Ability System (GAS), Motion Matching, and other advanced gameplay and animation systems. Experience working closely with C++ programmers on custom gameplay and animation systems. Experience with Unreal Engine C++ programming or direct involvement in the development of custom gameplay and animation systems. Experience working on AAA action games, RPGs, or large open-world projects. Strong interest in medieval combat, historical weapons, armour, and fighting techniques. Location: Remote / International, with the option to work in the Czech Republic. Engagement: Full-time, long-term collaboration. Contract: Contractor / B2B worldwide, or direct employment for candidates residing in or willing to relocate to the Czech Republic. Start Date: As soon as mutually agreed. Language: Professional working proficiency in English. Compensation: Competitive, based on experience and expertise.
