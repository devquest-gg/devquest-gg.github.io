About the role Reel Saga is Nanobit’s AI-generated short drama app, growing through paid user acquisition and monetising through web subscriptions and in-app purchases.

As Head of Operations, you will run the day-to-day operating system of Reel Saga. You will turn the GM’s priorities into clear plans, owners and delivery, while leading the teams responsible for product execution, marketing, UA and monetization operations.

You will be accountable for operating performance and execution within the agreed strategy, budget and targets. The GM owns product direction, roadmap decisions, content strategy, monetization model and major investment choices. You bring the data, recommendations and execution needed to move the business forward.

What you will do Run the weekly business rhythm across performance, delivery, UA, monetization and content production.

Turn the approved roadmap and quarterly priorities into plans, milestones, owners and targets.

Track delivery across product and technology, identify risks early and drive recovery when work slips.

Lead the Performance Marketing, Product Marketing, Product, UI, Web-to-App and Social Media teams.

Own UA execution within the approved budget, including channel allocation, pacing, creative testing and attribution.

Run monetization operations across offers, promotions, payments, renewal recovery and the web-to-app funnel.

Coordinate product, content and marketing readiness around releases.

Manage forecasts, KPI reporting, budget-versus-actuals and the analysis behind Stillfront reporting.

Hold team leads accountable for outcomes and manage hiring within the approved headcount plan.

Act as the GM’s deputy and make day-to-day operating decisions when needed.

What you will bring 6+ years in operations, growth, product or commercial leadership in mobile apps, games, streaming or subscription products.

Experience running the operating side of a live product.

Hands-on knowledge of paid UA and subscription or IAP monetization.

Strong understanding of ROAS, payback, retention, conversion and cohort metrics.

Experience coordinating product and technology delivery.

Experience leading managers or senior specialists across product, design or marketing.

Strong analytical and written communication skills.

Fluent Croatian and English.

Experience with short drama, streaming, web-to-app funnels or AI-generated content is a plus.

About Nanobit

Nanobit is a leading game development studio with a mission to put extra into ordinary. Our games reached 15M players worldwide amassing over 200M downloads! Nanobit is part of Stillfront. Stillfront is a global gaming company with 1400+ employees. We develop a wide range of digital games that attract over 50 million players each month. From well-established franchises like Supremacy, Big Farm, and BitLife to niche games, we span many different genres, including strategy, simulation, RPG and action, and casual and mash-up games.
