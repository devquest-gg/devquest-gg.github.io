Nex is on a mission to help families rediscover the joy of movement. Created by parents for parents, Nex combines technology and play to deliver fun, social, and interactive experiences powered by natural body motion, encouraging kids and adults to move more, play more, and have fun together. Nex Playground, the company’s award-winning active play system, is purpose-built to get families moving year-round, with safety and privacy as core considerations in its intentional design. It is certified kidSAFE+ COPPA compliant and built to support healthy, active play for all ages and abilities.

Nex Playground features a growing library of 50+ experiences, including motion and dance games, fitness and educational experiences, and Nex Originals. Content includes collaborations with partners like Hasbro, Sesame Workshop, and NBCUniversal. Nex has been recognized by Fast Company’s Most Innovative Companies, TIME’s Best Inventions, and Parents’ Best Entertainment System for Families, and has earned Red Dot, IDEA, and Core77 international design awards. We encourage you to explore Have Fun and Is Motion Gaming Back? , as they offer a deeper look into our culture, values, and explain how our approach to motion gaming differs from previous generations.

To protect yourself from recruitment scams, please note that Nex communicates through official company emails ending in @nex.inc. If you receive a suspicious email or message, please do not respond and report it immediately to us.

Location: Hybrid - Bay Area (Preferred), Remote - PST/MST only Annual salary: $130,000 - $150,000 Type: Full Time Reports to: Sr. Director, Finance

The Role

We are seeking a Sr. Financial Analyst to be a key member and strong contributor toward scaling our Corporate FP A capabilities, and lead business partnership across our G A functions.

This is a high-visibility role working closely with senior management that requires the ability to dive deep while operating strategically to support and enable Nex’s mission and long term goals.

What You’ll Do

Build comprehensive financial models that span investment cases, strategic planning, and cost of capital considerations.

Design new financial and management reporting that effectively measures our KPIs, business and financial performance.

Manage the G A function’s annual budgeting, recurring forecasts, and long-range planning processes including variance analysis, operational support and ad hoc modeling.

Identify and reach alignment with G A stakeholders on key performance indicators that drive financial ownership, visibility, and operational efficiency.

Provide analytic support and develop actionable insights that will influence resource and capital allocation decisions more effectively.

Partner and support business case development on investment proposals, and facilitate through the approval decision process.

What You Bring

Bachelor’s degree in Finance, Accounting, Economics, or related field; MBA or CFA a plus.

4+ years of experience with 2-3 years in investment banking, private equity or corporate finance

Strong proficiency with financial modeling and investment evaluation

Excel / Google Sheets expertise; experience with ERP systems and financial planning tools (e.g., Netsuite, Adaptive, Anaplan, etc.) preferred.

Excellent analytical, communication, and presentation skills—you can turn complex data into actionable insights

Entrepreneurial mindset and comfortable operating in a fast-moving, ambiguous environment.

Passion for gaming and emerging technologies

We Offer

Competitive compensation package.

Flexible working hours and vacation policy.

Product-driven culture that treasures talents and individual growth.

Front-row seat and hands-on experience with cutting edge technologies in the evolving gaming field
