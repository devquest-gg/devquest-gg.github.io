GET SERIOUS ABOUT FUN

As a Nintendo of America intern, you will actively contribute to meaningful projects and gain valuable hands-on work experience with a leading company in the entertainment industry. During the program from June through August, your work experience will be enhanced with mentoring, networking opportunities, and exposure to industry leaders from across the company! Whether you are in a technical or non-technical role, you will be part of a team committed to putting smiles on the faces of millions around the world.

Application deadline is December 1, 2026 .

DESCRIPTION OF DUTIES

Contribute to Nintendo’s Sustainability team to develop practical compliance tools, presentations, and recommendations to support business decision-making and sustainability program improvements.

Support responsible sourcing, labor standards compliance, and supply chain transparency initiatives.

Collaborate with teams to advance internal sustainability initiatives.

Assist with Scope 3 emissions reporting and metrics tracking and analysis.

Conduct sustainability related regulatory research and compile key documentation summarizing potential impacts to Nintendo and considerations for compliance planning.

SUMMARY OF REQUIREMENTS

3.0+ GPA.

Currently enrolled in an accredited college or university degree program with a graduation conferral date between May 2027 and June 2028.

Pursuing a degree in Sustainability, Environmental Science/Sociology, Supply Chain Management, Business Administration or related field.

Organized and detail-oriented with the ability to assist with project management.

Shows initiative, action-oriented and applies analytical and problem-solving skills.

Ability to research independently and evaluate sources.

Keen interest and desire to learn sustainability’s impact on business.

Strong organization and ability to manage multiple deadlines.

Strong communication and collaboration skills to partner with stakeholders.

Attention to detail and data accuracy.

Adaptable and eager to learn and contribute to a dynamic environment.

Curiosity to learn and share unique knowledge and insights with the team.

Proficient with Microsoft Office Suite: Outlook, Word, Excel, PowerPoint.

Strong interest in sustainability, responsible sourcing, and environmental, social, and governance topics.

Legally authorized to work in the United States.

Able to work 40 hours a week onsite throughout the course of the 10-week summer internship between June 14 – August 20.

Applicants must be legally eligible to work in the United States to be considered. Visa sponsorship is not available for this role.

This position is 100% onsite in Redmond, WA.

This internship includes the base pay rate listed below, paid sick and holiday time, and perks such as a local transit pass and access to the onsite fitness center. Given the limited duration of the role, interns are not eligible for full-time employee benefits such as healthcare or 401(k).

Pay Rate: $30 — $30 USD
