------ What will you do?

As a member of Turbulent’s RICARDO team , you will contribute to the evolution of the RICARDO digital platform, a leading culinary product used by millions of users across Quebec and internationally. You will work closely with a multidisciplinary team (development, product, and design) on RICARDO’s digital ecosystem.

You will serve as a key contributor on this mandate, helping modernize the platform and develop new features.

The RICARDO project leverages several technologies, including: PHP, Symfony, Laravel, Node.js, TypeScript, React, MySQL, PostgreSQL, Redis, Docker, AWS, HTML5, CSS3, Sass, and Storybook.

Your strengths

Communicate and collaborate closely with colleagues from various disciplines, while translating technical jargon into clear concepts. Simplifying complex ideas comes naturally to you.

Transform complex problems into elegant, robust technical solutions that meet high quality standards.

Adapt to different contexts and shifting priorities.

Provide constructive feedback and maintain a solution-oriented mindset.

Write clear documentation and value knowledge sharing across your team.

Demonstrate strong autonomy, rigor, and curiosity for emerging technologies.

Leverage artificial intelligence to improve efficiency and productivity.

Drive initiatives forward, promote development best practices, and contribute to implementing effective processes.

Collaborate closely with the Product Owner on new client projects.

Identify, document, and mitigate risks associated with proposed solutions.

Your experience and background

8+ years of experience as a Full Stack Developer.

Degree in Computer Science, Software Development, or a related field.

Advanced expertise in PHP and strong proficiency with frameworks such as Symfony or Laravel.

Extensive experience with Node.js, TypeScript, and React.

Strong experience designing and implementing REST APIs.

Deep understanding of relational and non-relational databases (MySQL, Redis).

Familiarity with indexing and search technologies such as Solr and Elasticsearch.

Experience with cloud services (AWS or similar) and Docker.

Proven track record designing, developing, and maintaining highly scalable, high-volume systems.

Experience with testing tools and a constant focus on improving test coverage.

Strong knowledge of version control systems (Git) and deployment processes.

Comfortable working in an Agile environment.

Experience using AI-powered development tools is considered an asset.

Excellent French and good English communication skills, both written and spoken.

What you’re eager to accomplish

Join a strong, highly skilled team where you can continue learning and growing.

Participate constructively in code reviews and enjoy sharing knowledge to help teammates improve their technical skills.

Contribute to the continuous improvement of the codebase.

Participate in Scrum ceremonies (daily stand-ups, sprint planning, sprint reviews, and retrospectives).

Foster a positive and supportive team culture.

Contribute to process improvements and technology decisions related to the RICARDO product.

Design efficient architectures and build robust solutions through an iterative development process.

Integrate AI into your development practices.

Support team members in their day-to-day work.

Own and maintain the build, compilation, and deployment systems for the projects assigned to you.
