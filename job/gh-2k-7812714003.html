What We Need We are seeking a Senior Data Developer to design, build, and optimize the data infrastructure powering our internal product platforms and BI tools. You will centralize fragmented studio data, including project roadmaps, telemetry, feature backlogs, and release timelines, to create clean, structured data layers that provide critical visibility into our games, products, and business.

What You Will Do ● Architect and automate scalable ETL pipelines to ingest and centralize diverse studio tracking data.

● Standardize and model disparate data sources into optimized database schemas that serve as a single source of truth for executive BI dashboards.

● Partner with product managers and studio leaders to solve data bottlenecks and design backends for new portfolio-tracking tools.

● Implement automated data-validation frameworks to ensure absolute reliability for critical leadership reports.

Who We Think Will Be A Great Fit ● 5+ years of experience in Data Warehousing and Data Engineering. ● Deep understanding of RDBMS, database syntax, and SQL technologies. ● Strong familiarity with REST protocols and JSON data structures. ● Hands-on experience designing and maintaining production-level ETL processes. ● Proficiency with Python, Google App Scripts, or other scripting tools. ● Familiarity with data privacy and compliance practices (e.g., GDPR, CCPA). ● Ability to work iteratively within agile methodologies and communicate technical concepts to non-technical audiences. ● BS in Engineering, Data Analytics, Computer Science, or equivalent experience.

Bonus Points ● Direct experience with Tableau or other enterprise-level BI visualization tools. ● Understanding of business operations, corporate finance, or AAA game development pipelines. ● Experience building or supporting front-end software services and custom web tools. ● Familiarity with machine learning frameworks and concepts.
