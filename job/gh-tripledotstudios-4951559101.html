Role Overview: We are looking for a talented and experienced professional to join our CentralTech team! You will work on some of the largest titles in Tripledot s portfolio, ensuring the quality of existing features while supporting the development and release of new ones. The role involves close collaboration with the Monetization team, with a strong focus on validating advertising integrations and monetization features. At Tripledot, we play as a team – collaborating closely to deliver high-impact work through clear communication and continuous improvement.

Key Responsibilities:

Analyze requirements and design high-quality test cases/checklists

Execute tests following best QA practices across Android and iOS platforms in Platform, LiveOps Monetizastion areas

Manage bug lifecycle: identification, documentation, prioritization and communication

Report the results of the current testing efforts to the project stakeholders

Work closely with developers aligning on debug tools and test strategy

Investigate production incidents with an analytical mindset

Act as a voice for quality suggesting improvements to testing practices and workflows.
