About the Role

As a Material Supply Planning Manager at Nex, you will make sure our products are available when and where customers want them. You will turn demand into supply and material plans that manufacturers and suppliers can deliver, spot risks early, and drive decisions across the company.

You will own Nex Playground supply planning for a designated region and key-material planning across the Playground portfolio. You will be accountable for supply reliability while balancing inventory, material commitments and excess and obsolete (E O) exposure. Working with Sourcing, contract manufacturers, component suppliers, Business, Finance, Engineering and Product, you will connect demand, materials, capacity and inventory into one plan, drive it through execution, and build planning processes that scale with Nex s growth.

What You ll Do

1. Own Material Planning Clear to Build (CTB)

Own component-level material planning across the Playground portfolio, translating FG production plans into component requirements.

Manage BOM-based MRP, material coverage, MOQ, lead times, safety stock and supplier constraints.

Own Clear to Build (CTB) analysis and proactively identify material shortages and production constraints.

Secure long-lead and constrained components, managing supplier commitments and material liabilities.

Coordinate material allocation across suppliers and manufacturing sites.

2. Own Supply Reliability

Own the Nex Playground supply plan for a designated region, translating demand forecast to supply requirements, purchase orders, and inventory plans.

Work with Sourcing, contract manufacturers and component suppliers to secure capacity, materials and delivery commitments; lead regular reviews and drive recovery when commitments are at risk.

Evaluate production scenarios and supplier flexibility to balance availability, cost and inventory.

3. Build Scalable Planning

Support scaling integrated planning process that connects demand, finished goods, materials, capacity and supplier commitments.

Optimize planning metrics and operating cadence to track supply reliability, inventory health and supplier performance.

Design planning templates, data structures, reporting and automation to improve planning efficiency.

Drive improvements in capacity planning, material planning and supply planning processes.

What you bring

5-8+ years of experience in supply planning, material planning, or related operations roles, with demonstrated ownership of planning outcomes.

Deep, hands-on experience owning key-component planning and Clear to Build (CTB), long-lead and constrained parts, supplier commitments and material liabilities.

A track record of working directly with contract manufacturers, EMS / ODM partners, or component suppliers in a hardware, electronics, or consumer products environment.

Experience developing or improving planning processes, MRP methodologies or supply planning systems.

Strong analytical capabilities and the ability to translate complex planning data into actionable decisions.

Comfortable operating in a fast-paced, evolving environment where processes and systems are still being built.

Fluent in English and Mandarin.

Preferred

Experience in consumer electronics, hardware, or other component-intensive manufacturing environments.

Experience supporting multiple manufacturing sites, product transitions, or international launches.

Proven ability to partner with cross-functional teams across time zones, building alignment and driving decisions remotely, with flexibility for early or late calls when needed.

Experience with seasonal, peak-driven consumer demand.

Experience scaling planning processes and moving from spreadsheets to planning systems.
