ABOUT YOU

We are looking for a Graphic Designer who is creative, detail-oriented, and collaborative to join our Brand & Creative team. The best candidate will be someone who thrives in a fast-paced, highly collaborative, and exceptionally dynamic environment and is excited to bring brand visuals to life across print, digital, and event touchpoints.

Strong visual design skills, mastery of design tools, and a solid understanding of print production processes are essential, along with experience in developing brand materials, working on merchandise design, and creating engaging marketing assets. The ability to manage multiple design requests, adapt to evolving brand needs, and ensure consistent visual quality will be key to your success in this role.

If you’re passionate about crafting visually compelling content and love supporting game industry events, brand storytelling, and internal design initiatives, we’d love to hear from you!
