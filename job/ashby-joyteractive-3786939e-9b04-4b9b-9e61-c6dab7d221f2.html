🚀 We are Joyteractive - a leading mobile game developer and publisher specializing in the captivating world of puzzle games. Today, our team boasts 70+ rock star professionals, and we're on the lookout for talented individuals to help us dominate the world of puzzles!

🔝 Our leader is Cryptogram: Word Brain Puzzle - #3 spot among all iOS/Android word games in the US.

We're looking for an experienced Game Designer to strengthen our RnD team.

For what tasks?

- Core Gameplay Development & Ideation: Focus on the creation, iteration, and refinement of core gameplay mechanics. Work closely on short iteration cycles to quickly test and improve gameplay concepts

- Documentation: Write clear and concise game design documents (GDDs) that communicate ideas and provide detailed specifications for the development team

- Competitor Analysis: Conduct deconstruction and analysis of competing products to identify trends, opportunities, and areas for improvement

- Data-driven Design: Use analytical metrics to guide design decisions and improve gameplay through data insights

What kind of professional are you looking for?

- 3+ years of game designer experience

- Deep understanding of core gameplay mechanics

- Experience working as a project’s feature-owner

- Knowledge of monetization trends and general mobile market trends

- Systematic experience in market research with a strong interest in and ability to apply findings to design decisions.

- Understanding of product and business goals, ability to prioritize

- Writing clear documentation, interacting with team members

- Adaptability and eagerness to work in a fast-paced, iterative environment.

- Understanding of basic analytical metrics

- Experience with Unity

Will be a plus:

- Active use of AI tools in day-to-day work and a genuine interest in exploring new ways to improve workflows with AI

- English proficiency at B2 level or higher

What are the conditions and bonuses?

👉 Flexible Public Holidays policy

❤️‍🩹 100% Sick Leave compensation
