Job Overview

Wizards of the Coast and Hasbro are looking for a Labor Relations expert to build the capability within our HR organization. Working at the enterprise level, this role will partner with HR, Legal, Finance, Business Leadership, and internal and external counsel to participate in bargaining, build manager and HR capability, and establish grievance and arbitration processes. A successful candidate has labor relations experience, ideally in tech, gaming, or entertainment, and can build labor relations capability across the company so that managers and HR teams are prepared to work effectively with union representatives.

Location and Work Arrangement

This role is based at our Renton, Washington office and follows a hybrid schedule designed to support in-person collaboration. Employees are expected to work onsite on Tuesdays, Wednesdays, and Thursdays, with the flexibility to work remotely or onsite on Mondays and Fridays.

What You ll Do

Co-lead collective bargaining for a first contract in collaboration with the VP of HR and internal and external counsel

Lead cross-functional work across HR, Legal, Finance, and Business Leadership on bargaining proposals and their costing

Develop effective working relationships with union representatives

Partner with Legal on risk; bring labor data to leadership decisions

Develop and lead internal HR processes for grievance and arbitration and contract interpretation

Train Managers of bargaining unit members on specific processes, documentation, consistency, contract administration

Lead enterprise-wide manager training and readiness for working with union-represented employees

Centers of Excellence (COEs)

What You ll Bring

Bachelor’s degree or equivalent experience, plus 8+ years of labor relations experience in a unionized and salary environment

Experience as a lead negotiator or as a second-chair negotiator supporting the lead, ideally including first contracts

Unfair labor practice knowledge (ULP)

What Sets You Apart

Degree in labor or industrial relations

Games, tech, or entertainment industry experience, or experience in other industries that transfers to this work

Notice Regarding Hasbro’s use of AI in the Recruitment Process

We use Greenhouse’s AI-powered Talent Matching tool to compare your application against our job requirements.

How it works: Talent Matching compares information from your resume and application responses with job-related criteria established and weighted by Hasbro’s recruiting and hiring teams. Depending on the role, these criteria may include relevant skills, work experience, prior job titles, and industry experience. The tool then assigns a match category to help organize applications for review.

Human oversight: Talent Matching supports — but does not replace — human review. It does not automatically advance or reject candidates or make employment decisions. Hasbro’s recruiters and hiring managers review applications and remain responsible for all hiring decisions.
