Director Creative Intelligence

Scopely · Barcelona

Director+ Other Hybrid Posted 2026-10-01 Full time

Scopely is looking for a Director, Creative Intelligence to join our creative Intelligence team working in our Barcelona studio on a hybrid basis.

At Scopely, we care deeply about what we do and want to inspire play, every day - whether in our work environments alongside our talented colleagues, or through our deep connections with our communities of players. We are a global team of game lovers who are developing, publishing and innovating the mobile games industry, connecting millions of people around the world daily.

Creative Intelligence, a central team within Performance Marketing, is responsible for creating the tools and analyzing the results of our creative data to improve our marketing and UA strategy across our entire game portfolio.

What You Will Do

The Director of Creative Intelligence will lead the strategy, measurement, and tooling, and team that help us understand what drives creative success. Based in Barcelona, this advanced marketing analytics leader will use tagging, performance data, research, experimentation, and AI to explain what creative works and why across our portfolio.

The role partners closely with Marketing, User Acquisition, Analytics, Community, and Flamed, our internal creative agency. It also supports unlaunched games by identifying creative whitespace and answering early questions about audiences, positioning, concepts, messaging, and naming.

Set the Creative Intelligence strategy and roadmap.

Define priorities, products, measurement needs, and team goals. Lead and develop a multidisciplinary team across creative analytics and AI enablement.

Measure creative success and support testing across live and slate games.

Bring together tagging, performance data, audience research, competitive intelligence, and other relevant signals to identify what drives performance and where opportunities exist. Recommend and support tests that answer key creative questions and inform strategy and decisions.

Guide games before launch.

Use competitor intelligence, audience research, category trends, and portfolio data to identify creative whitespace and inform audiences, positioning, concepts, messages, and naming.

Own the creative analytics product roadmap.

Prioritize dashboards, stakeholder views, tagging, data quality, reporting, and self-service tools. Drive adoption and continued improvement across the portfolio.

Partner with Analytics.

Articulate business questions, measurement needs, and desired outcomes. Secure support and drive projects through delivery, validation, and adoption while those teams own the technical infrastructure.

Use AI to expand the function.

Use tools such as Claude to interrogate data, explore hypotheses, prototype analyses, automate reporting, and simplify competitor intelligence and recurring insights.

Build trust and turn insights into action.

Develop strong partnerships across Marketing, Creative, Analytics, and game teams. Deliver clear recommendations, track how they influence decisions, and measure their impact.

Partner with Marketing and our internal creative agency.

Help marketers and creative teams use performance data and competitive intelligence to define creative strategy, identify opportunities, and make evidence-based changes to their plans.

Translate data into clear creative guidance.

Turn complex performance data, tagging, and analysis into simple explanations of what is working, why it is effective, and what teams should do next. Own game-level reporting as needed to inform creative and marketing strategy.

What We Are Looking For

A curious and resourceful problem solver who thrives in ambiguity, enjoys tackling new questions, and takes ownership of finding a path to an answer, even when the problem falls outside established processes or familiar areas of expertise.

Experience leading advanced marketing analytics, data science, marketing science, measurement, consumer insights, or a related function.

A strong record of developing measurement frameworks and metrics to answer questions that connect marketing or creative inputs to business outcomes.

Experience using quantitative and qualitative evidence to answer ambiguous questions, with strong knowledge of experimentation, attribution, incrementality, benchmarking, and predictive analysis.

Hands-on comfort with modern AI tools and enough technical fluency to partner effectively with Analytics, Data Science, and Engineering. SQL, Python, data pipeline, or business intelligence experience is valuable but not the role s primary responsibility.

Strong people leadership and analytics product ownership, paired with excellent communication, adaptability, urgency, follow-through, and a track record of earning trust across functions.

About Scopely

Scopely is a leading video game and global interactive entertainment company, home to many of the world’s most beloved and enduring experiences, including two of the most successful mobile games of all-time “MONOPOLY GO!” and “Pokémon GO,” along with “Stumble Guys,” “Star Trek™ Fleet Command,” “MARVEL Strike Force,” “WWE Champions,” the Scrabble® franchise, “Yahtzee® With Buddies,” and many others. Across mobile, web, PC, and console, Scopely creates, develops, publishes, and live-operates one of the most diversified and award-winning portfolios in the games industry — bringing hundreds of millions of players together through a shared love of play.

Founded in 2011, Scopely is powered by its exceptional team — including thousands of world-class gamemakers around the globe, a distinctive tenet-driven culture, and its proprietary technology platform, Playgami. Together, these strengths have fueled Scopely’s position as the #1 mobile games company in the U.S. and #2 globally, generating more than $10 billion in lifetime revenue. Whether building global sensations like “MONOPOLY GO!” from the ground up, or expanding through strategic acquisitions, including the FoxNext, GSN, and Scopely Explore games businesses — Scopely consistently delivers experiences players love today and return to for years to come.

Recognized multiple times as one of the "100 Most Influential Companies in the World" by TIME magazine and one of Fast Company s "World s Most Innovative Companies" and “Best Workplaces for Innovators,” Scopely believes that video games can be a force for good — creating meaningful connections, vibrant communities, and making life better through play.

Scopely has global operations and partners across four continents in more than a dozen countries worldwide. For more information, visit: https://www.scopely.com/ .
