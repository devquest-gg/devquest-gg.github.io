Content & Creator Program Manager - Japan / ★ソーシャル・コンテンツストラテジスト

thatgamecompany · Remote

Mid Production Remote Posted 2026-05-19
Apply on thatgamecompany’s site →

【Responsibilities】

Official Account Management: Lead the operation of Sky’s official Japanese social media accounts. This includes managing content scheduling and publishing, planning and executing online events for the Japan region, handling community interactions/replies, and monitoring public sentiment and data performance.

Growth & Strategy: Align seasonal game content with platform-specific features to drive user growth. You will plan the marketing and social media strategy for each season and event, execute end-to-end campaigns, and perform integrated data analysis to provide insights for content optimization.

UGC Operations: Manage User-Generated Content (UGC) strategies. Based on Sky’s unique brand identity and the characteristics of Japanese players, you will plan and implement creator incentive programs focused on new user acquisition.

Creator Ecosystem (KOL/KOC): Oversee the management of KOLs, KOCs, and core creators. This includes handling incentives, content support, and Q&A, as well as establishing performance evaluation standards and content review SOPs.

Event Support: Assist in planning exhibitions and offline events, including integrated social media PR strategies and player-focused content operations.

【Job Requirements】

Education & Experience: Bachelor’s degree or above; 3+ years of experience in Japan social media operations for live-ops games. Experience independently managing official accounts and creator ecosystems is a plus.

Language & Aesthetics: Native or near-native Japanese proficiency with a strong aesthetic sense. You must be able to independently draft official Japanese announcements and copy.

Project Management: Strong project management and team communication skills. You should be capable of driving decision-making in complex collaborative environments, proactively optimizing workflows, and standardizing content publishing processes.

Platform Expertise: Deep understanding of the content tone and mechanics of various social platforms (X/Twitter, YouTube, Discord, LINE, TikTok, etc.). You should understand the preferences of Japanese players and have high sensitivity toward UGC trends.

Game Knowledge: Deep experience with Sky: Children of the Light and a profound understanding of its world and mechanics is a must.

コンテンツ＆クリエイター・プログラムマネージャー
