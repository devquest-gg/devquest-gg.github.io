ABOUT VOODOO

Founded in 2013, Voodoo is a tech company that creates mobile games and apps with a mission to entertain the world. Gathering 800 employees, 7 billion downloads, and over 200 million active users, Voodoo is the #3 mobile publisher worldwide in terms of downloads after Google and Meta. Our portfolio includes chart-topping games like Mob Control and Block Jam, alongside popular apps such as BeReal and Wizz.

TEAM

Blitz is a gaming platform launched in 2021, allowing players to compete in mobile games for real cash prizes (head-up, tournaments, leagues, etc.). It is leveraging a unique game portfolio as well as exclusive games crafted for Blitz to engage users in a fun and rewarding way.

ROLE

As a Head of Growth, you will own the scaling of a top priority game app, acting as the CMO and strategic counterpart to the CEO. Your mission is simple: grow the game aggressively. You will leverage our full Performance Marketing engine and activate non-paid growth levers to push performance even further.

You will be onboarded by our Growth Central team to build a deep expertise and own the following scope :

- Build and own the growth strategy to scale our leading mobile games.

- Execute all our acquisition channels: paid UA but also non-paid such as partnerships, featuring, influencer marketing, growth hacking, ASO, and community building (not exhaustive).

- Monitor market trends and competitors to identify emerging high-performing growth channels.

- Collaborate with cross-functional teams to ensure flawless execution and maximize the impact of available resources.

- Conduct rigorous data analysis to evaluate channel performance, guide decisions, prioritize initiatives, and uncover new growth opportunities.

PROFILE

- Master’s degree from a top engineering or business school (Grande École program).

- 2+ years of experience in a strategy consulting or investment banking firm

- Strong strategic thinking and analytical capabilities.

- Genuine interest in mobile gaming

- Genuine interest in Growth, content creation, performance marketing.

- Autonomous, proactive, high energy and comfortable rolling up your sleeves.

- Excellent communication and interpersonal skills.

- Fluent in English.

BENEFITS

- Competitive salary based on experience

- Swile Lunch voucher

- Gymlib (100% covered by Voodoo)

- Premium healthcare coverage with SideCare, 100% covered for you and your family

- Wellness activities in our Paris office

- Remote days on Fridays
