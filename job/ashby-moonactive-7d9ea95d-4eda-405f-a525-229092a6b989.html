Moon Active is one of the world's fastest-growing mobile game companies, providing entertainment to millions of active users across the universe. The company is headquartered in the heart of Tel Aviv.

We're looking for a Junior User Acquisition Manager to join our growing Marketing team. You’ll be responsible for promoting our amazing products via various marketing channels.

Responsibilities

- Hands-on campaign management with various mobile ad networks.

- Build and maintain strategic relationships with key acquisition partners.

- Conduct data analysis (identifying trends, opportunities & discrepancies).

- Manage fast-paced and dynamic projects in a highly organized manner, under tight schedules, and within the budget.

- Collaborate with different team members within the Marketing department to deliver and execute successful marketing campaigns.

Requirements

- Bsc in Industrial Engineering, Economics, Statistics or similar field.

- Willingness to learn and use third-party platforms.

- Ability to take on multiple projects at the same time.

- Strong organizational, analytical, presentation and problem solving skills.

- Profound knowledge of MS-Office products (Excel in particular).

- Fluent English - verbal and written.

- Passion for marketing and mobile games.

Advantages

- Previous experience in a data driven environment

- Ability to manipulate and analyze large quantities of data and present it in reports.
