Senior Advisor, HR Practice Development and Compensation

Frima Studio · Québec

Senior People & Ops Posted 2026-09-14 Full time
Apply on Frima Studio’s site →

What your weeks will look like Evolve HR practices, processes and tools based on the organization’s needs and best practices; Ensure the evolution of total compensation practices, including salary structures, positioning, internal equity and the annual salary review process; Conduct market analyses and transform HR data into findings and recommendations to support decision-making; Develop and evolve HR indicators and dashboards to measure the effectiveness of practices and identify opportunities for improvement; Contribute to the simplification and automation of processes through technology and artificial intelligence; Ensure the evolution of HR policies and frameworks, as well as the compliance of practices with applicable requirements; Act as a subject matter expert and advisor to the Vice President, Talent and Culture and the HR team by conducting assessments and proposing solutions adapted to organizational challenges; Support the Talent and Culture team and managers in understanding and applying HR practices.

What we’re looking for Bachelor’s degree in Human Resources, Industrial Relations, Business Administration, or any combination of education and experience deemed equivalent; Minimum of ten (10) years of experience in an HR generalist role and/or in HR practice development; In-depth experience in total compensation, particularly in market analysis, salary structures and internal equity; Strong knowledge of the various areas of HR expertise, sound management practices and labour law; Good knowledge of HRIS and a strong interest in technology; CHRP or CIRC designation, a strong asset; Ability to travel outside the country as needed; Excellent command of French and English, both written and spoken. English skills are required to perform the work, as we collaborate with English-speaking partners.
