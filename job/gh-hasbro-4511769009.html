Job Overview

Are you a motivated, organized, and collaborative person who enjoys improving processes and managing projects? We’re looking for a hardworking Business Coordinator to join our team for Magic: The Gathering’s experimental sub-brand, Secret Lair. Bring your passion for spreadsheets and documentation and join us!

As a Business Coordinator, you’ll work with the Secret Lair team to plan product launches, own sale event schedules, handle our website assets, maintain meticulously organized recordkeeping, and communicate clearly with cross-functional team members and external partners.

Location and Work Arrangement

This role is based at our Renton, Washington office and follows a hybrid schedule designed to support in-person collaboration. Employees are expected to work onsite on Tuesdays, Wednesdays, and Thursdays, with the flexibility to work remotely or onsite on Mondays and Fridays.

What You ll Do

DRIVE the rhythm of business for the Secret Lair marketing, financial, and digital teams.

ENSURE partners receive assets, product information, SKU level documentation and KSPs accurately and on time.

PARTNER with producers and creative teams ensuring successful creation and launch of all Secret Lair products.

BUILD user friendly dashboards, reports and communications that are audience specific to ensure clarity and alignment.

ANTICIPATE and adapt to new situations and/or changing demands and respond proactively to business needs.

What You ll Bring

Proven track record as a Producer, Program Manager, Marketing/Business Analyst, or similar role.

Ability to jump into fast-paced and evolving situations and make things better for your team.

An organized, meticulous approach to bookkeeping and building efficiencies to further streamline processes.

Excellent communication skills including: clear and concise written and verbal communication, editing, as well as accuracy and attention to detail.

You possess a growth mindset.

Proficiency with Microsoft Office Suite (especially Excel) and familiarity with Smartsheet.

Experience using AI tools to organize project information, draft reports and communications, and streamline schedules, with sound judgment about accuracy, data privacy and responsible use.

What Sets You Apart

First-hand experience in online/Direct-to-Consumer (DTC) sales or product management.

Notice Regarding Hasbro’s use of AI in the Recruitment Process

We use Greenhouse’s AI-powered Talent Matching tool to compare your application against our job requirements.

How it works: Talent Matching compares information from your resume and application responses with job-related criteria established and weighted by Hasbro’s recruiting and hiring teams. Depending on the role, these criteria may include relevant skills, work experience, prior job titles, and industry experience. The tool then assigns a match category to help organize applications for review.

Human oversight: Talent Matching supports — but does not replace — human review. It does not automatically advance or reject candidates or make employment decisions. Hasbro’s recruiters and hiring managers review applications and remain responsible for all hiring decisions.
