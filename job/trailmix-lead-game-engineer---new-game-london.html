The Opportunity As Lead Game Engineer on a New Game Project in London, you are accountable for the technical direction, delivery confidence, and people leadership of the engineering team building our next game. This role carries full hands-on engineering craft and extends into people management and delivery accountability. You will still write code - often on the systems that carry live traffic - but your primary impact comes from enabling others, owning the technical approach, and turning a game that works into one that keeps working at scale. You hold direct line-management responsibility for the engineering team (initially small), and are accountable for their performance, growth, and clarity of ownership. We are hiring this role to build toward a sustainable Live Games team - so the architecture and standards you set now have to hold as the game grows in players, content and complexity, year after year.

The Day to Day Our New Games are being built to run for years, not to be proven and handed on. Your job is to get it live and keep it moving - a dependable release cadence, fast diagnosis when something breaks, and a codebase that can absorb continuous new content without seizing up. You optimise for sustainable velocity: shipping change into a running game without putting the player experience, the data, or the team at risk.

Technical Leadership & Architecture Own the game’s architecture and technical direction; make the major technical calls and bring the team in where it matters. You define which decisions you own, delegate, or take to the team - and you are accountable for the outcome, not for making every call alone.

Match engineering investment to risk - keep routine content, tuning and config changes cheap and fast to ship, and know when something touching live data, economy or player accounts warrants full production rigour.

Uphold engineering standards, code quality, and performance; stay hands-on in complex or high-impact work, often on the systems where a mistake reaches players fastest.

Set the architecture and standards the game will be operated on for years - so growth in players, content, and team size doesn&#x27;t mean rebuilding.

People Leadership & Team Development Line manage the engineering team, including Senior Engineers - accountable for their performance, growth, and clarity of ownership.

Run regular 1:1s and give kind, direct, actionable feedback; support career-progression conversations and development planning.

Set clear expectations, address performance concerns early and constructively, and grow engineers who can make strong calls without you - your impact is measured by the team’s effectiveness, not just your own output.

Delivery & Outcome Accountability Break large initiatives into clear, parallelisable deliverables; identify critical-path risks early and communicate trade-offs.

Partner with Product/Design and Production to align on scope and feasibility.

Improve processes, tooling, and automation to reduce last-minute heroics; protect both delivery predictability and team sustainability.

Instrument features so their effect on player behaviour and product metrics is visible quickly, and so regressions surface in hours rather than weeks.

Technical Craft & Live Operations Build and evolve the gameplay and live-service systems behind the game - features shipped behind flags, released incrementally, and rollback-able when they misbehave.

Own the health of the running game: telemetry, alerting and on-call practice that surface problems before players report them, and blameless diagnosis when they don&#x27;t.

Apply AI tooling to accelerate day-to-day engineering, live-ops workflows, and content pipelines (e.g. code generation, internal tools, automated checks).

Collaboration & Stakeholder Impact Represent Engineering in cross-functional discussions; communicate technical constraints and trade-offs clearly to non-technical stakeholders.

Raise risks early; encourage healthy debate while ensuring decisions are made; foster trust through clarity, transparency, and follow-through.

Work closely with the Head of Technology to shape engineering standards and technical strategy across the studio, connecting the team&#x27;s work to the bigger technical picture.

Own the New Game Project’s technical roadmap - where the stack needs to evolve, what debt gets paid down, and what platform and tooling investment the game needs to keep scaling - then lead the team that delivers it.

About You Extensive experience within F2P mobile games, including at least one title you helped ship and operate live.

Track record of shipping into a live game on a regular cadence without destabilising what is already running - release process, feature flags, migrations, rollback.

Experience line-managing engineers (including Senior Engineers) - running 1:1s, owning performance, and supporting growth.

Hands-on experience applying AI tools to real engineering work - code, tests, tooling, and content pipelines - not just experiments.

Familiarity with, and curiosity about, web and backend technologies - the internal tools, dashboards, and services that make a live game operable day to day.

Strong architectural understanding and systems thinking; strong grasp of CI/CD, testing strategy, performance, and scalability.

Demonstrable depth in the core stack: engine/runtime Unity / C#, gameplay/client architecture, and profiling/optimisation.

Working knowledge of the server side of a live game - backend services, live-ops configuration, economy and telemetry data - enough to own the end-to-end behaviour of a feature, hold a sensible architecture conversation with backend specialists, and judge where the risk really sits.

Comfort operating in a live environment where priorities move with player data and incidents don&#x27;t wait for a sprint boundary.
