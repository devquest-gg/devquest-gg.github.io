Get to know The Pokémon Company International

The Pokémon Company International manages the Pokémon property outside of Asia and is responsible for brand management, licensing and marketing, the Pokémon Trading Card Game, the animated TV series, home entertainment, and the official Pokémon website. Job Title: Graphic Designer III, TCG Design Production and Finalization (12-month Fixed Term Contract)

Job Summary: The Design Production Finalization Designer plays a key role in building, evolving, and supporting the systems, standards, workflows, and production practices that enable high-quality design execution across the Trading Card Game Design organization.

FLSA Classification (US Only): Exempt

People Manager: No

What you’ll do

Production Systems Ownership

Independently manage complex production and finalization activities across multiple products, initiatives, and design programs.

Prepare, validate, and deliver production-ready assets that meet quality, manufacturing, and organizational requirements.

Partner with stakeholders to solve production challenges while balancing efficiency, quality, and project needs.

Ensure design deliverables are accurate, complete, and ready for downstream production processes.

Standards Development Quality Leadership

Partner in the creation, refinement, implementation, and adoption of production standards, workflows, and operational best practices.

Help define quality benchmarks and production readiness requirements for design deliverables.

Conduct quality reviews and identify opportunities to improve consistency, accuracy, and execution.

Serve as a trusted resource for designers and stakeholders regarding production requirements and operational expectations.

Champion adoption of newly established standards and processes across supported teams.

Process Improvement Operational Support

Identify opportunities to improve workflows, reduce inefficiencies, and enhance the overall quality of production operations.

Contribute to the development of documentation, process maps, training materials, and operational resources.

Support implementation of new tools, systems, and workflow improvements.

Provide practical recommendations based on production expertise and day-to-day operational experience.

Participate in continuous improvement initiatives that help scale organizational capabilities.

Cross-Functional Collaboration

Collaborate with designers, project managers, producers, and business partners to ensure successful project delivery.

Communicate production requirements, risks, dependencies, and recommendations effectively.

Support cross-functional initiatives designed to improve workflow efficiency and organizational effectiveness.

Help bridge creative intent with production feasibility and execution requirements.

Knowledge Sharing Team Development

Share expertise, best practices, and lessons learned with peers and developing designers.

Assist with onboarding, training, and team enablement efforts.

Contribute to operational documentation and knowledge-management resources.

Foster a culture of collaboration, continuous learning, and operational excellence.

Innovation Future Capability Development

Stay informed on evolving production practices, workflows, technologies, and industry trends.

Explore opportunities to improve efficiency through automation, emerging technologies, and scalable operational solutions.

Support pilots, testing efforts, and adoption of new tools and capabilities.

Contribute ideas that help modernize and future-proof Design Production Operations.

What You ll Bring

4+ years of experience in production design, creative operations, graphic production, or related disciplines.

Strong understanding of production workflows, asset preparation, quality control, and design standards.

Proficiency in Adobe Illustrator, InDesign, and Photoshop.

Demonstrated ability to independently manage multiple projects and priorities.

Experience contributing to workflow, process, or operational improvements.

Strong organizational, communication, and problem-solving skills.

Excellent attention to detail and commitment to quality.

Ability to collaborate effectively across teams and functions.
