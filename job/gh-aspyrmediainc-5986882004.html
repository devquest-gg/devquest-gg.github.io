**Please note that all positions posted for Aspyr Media, Inc. are in-person/in-office only; remote positions are not available.**

Aspyr is a leading game publisher and developer, bringing the world s biggest franchises and bold new projects to players everywhere.

Since 1996, we’ve published over 200 games and our team is dedicated to bringing beloved franchises like Tomb Raider and Star Wars to new and familiar audiences across all platforms.

We focus on collaboration and creativity, crafting high-quality, engaging games that leave a lasting impact on players worldwide.

Responsibilities:

Reporting to the Co-CEO, the Head of Marketing is responsible for maximizing the commercial success of Aspyr’s game portfolio by driving demand, shaping go to market (GTM) strategy, and ensuring each title reaches its full market potential. This role leads the development and execution of integrated marketing strategies across all titles, aligning closely with Product Management, Business Development, and Sales to deliver predictable, high impact results.

This leader balances creativity with operational discipline leveraging data, player insights, and experimentation to grow awareness, engagement, and revenue while maintaining alignment with company financial and lifecycle objectives.

Essential Functions:

Marketing Strategy Go-To-Market

Define and lead global go to market (GTM) strategies across all titles

Partner with Product Management to influence product definition, positioning, and audience targeting early in the lifecycle

Establish clear success metrics and KPIs for each title, aligned to revenue and ROI targets

Ensure marketing strategies align with Aspyr’s midmarket AAA positioning and IP driven approach

Own the marketing budget across all titles, allocating resources against portfolio priorities and performance expectations.

Demand Generation Campaign Execution

Lead execution of integrated marketing campaigns across PR, digital, paid media, influencer, and social channels

Drive awareness, engagement, and conversion through innovative and scalable campaign strategies

Optimize marketing mix and channel strategy based on performance data and audience insights

Continuously test and scale new marketing approaches to improve reach and efficiency

Data, Insights Performance Optimization

Partner with Business Intelligence to build robust reporting and attribution models

Track campaign performance across defined data points (organic, paid, conversion, sentiment, etc.)

Translate insights into actionable improvements across campaigns and future titles

Drive forecasting accuracy and marketing ROI discipline

Partnerships External Relationships

Serve as lead marketing point of contact with licensors, IP holders, and brand partners

Collaborate with licensors, platform partners (PlayStation, Xbox, Nintendo, Steam) , and agencies to amplify reach and comarketing opportunities

Manage external marketing vendors and ensure quality and performance standards

Align with Business Development and Sales to maximize distribution impact and promotional effectiveness

Leadership Team Development

Build, lead, and develop a high performing marketing team across key functions (brand, digital, PR, lifecycle, etc.)

Establish clear roles, accountability, and performance expectations

Foster a culture of experimentation, accountability, and continuous improvement

Ensure alignment with cross-functional teams (Product, Production, Creative, Sales)

Preferred Qualifications:

Bachelor’s degree in Business, Game Design, Marketing, or a related field; MBA a plus

12 + years of experience in marketing or public relations within the video game industry

5 + years of experience managing and mentoring high performing teams

Proven success leading GTM strategies for premium products

Strong understanding of player behavior, gaming audiences, and platform ecosystems

Experience managing multichannel campaigns (digital, influencer, PR, paid media)

Demonstrated ability to use data and insights to drive decision making

Experience working with external partners, licensors, and agencies

Proven success delivering and growing commercially successful video game franchises

Strong strategic thinking with the ability to synthesize complex market, product, and partner inputs into actionable roadmaps

Exceptional collaboration and communication skills across creative, technical, and executive teams

Experience working with licensed IPs and external stakeholders

Familiarity with console and PC game markets, platform holder expectations, and evolving monetization models

Passion for gaming and a deep respect for both the art and business of making games
