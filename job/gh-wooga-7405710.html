Our Story

Founded in 2009, we’re one of the world’s leading game studios, and proud of our extremely international team, consisting of more than 50 different nations. We are part of Playtika and the global leader in the category of Hidden Object Games. With June’s Journey, we’re providing joy for millions of players around the world every day.

How we work

Our aim is to keep doing so for many more years, if not decades. In order to be able to do so, we’ve defined a distinct way of how we’re working at Wooga, so that all relevant aspects intertwine and have a positive impact on the game. No matter if you’re part of a game team or support the success of Wooga as part of a central team, all 250+ employees from around the world can make an impact. We’re proud of the environment we’ve built, and believe you’ll enjoy working with some of the best people in the industry.

Our drivers: Speed, Focus, Ownership, and Impact, guide our working style at Wooga and help us make a deliberate choice to focus on what is important to us, while working towards a shared goal. These drivers shape how we work together and what we expect from ourselves and our leaders.

As a Senior FP A Controller at Wooga, you will partner closely with business leaders and cross-functional teams to ensure strong financial planning, accuracy, and strategic insight across the company. Reporting to and working hand-in-hand with our FP A Lead, as well as teams in our Israel headquarters you’ll play a key role in forecasting, budgeting, and analyzing financial performance. In this role, you will directly contribute to shaping data-driven decisions, optimizing financial processes, and delivering high-quality reporting that supports Wooga’s continued growth and success.

About the role

This role is an 18 Months Fixed Term Contract

Partnering with business leaders to understand their financial needs and challenges

Managing the Oracle PR process, including monitoring and approving new purchase orders and budget requests

Collaborating closely with multiple teams in the headquarters in Israel, including FP A, Accounting and Bookkeeping departments

Preparing and analyzing forecasts monthly

Executing month close processes, including preparing accrual run, reconciliations and analysis of Budget vs Actual performance

Creating and delivering periodic financial summaries to senior management

Contributing to the timely, complete, accurate and consistent preparation of monthly ad-hoc reports for internal and external stakeholders

Contributing to optimization of the existing FP A processes

Participating in the annual budgeting process

About you

Educational and professional background as a Financial Analyst

Professional experience working in the finance team of a global company

Solid understanding of accounting principles and concepts

Proficiency in Advanced Excel and PowerPoint skills, including Google spreadsheets and slides

Excellent communication skills, with the capacity to effectively present ideas to different departments

Strong analytical skills and solution-oriented

Ability to work effectively both independently and in a team environment

Understanding of the high level of accuracy needed for the role, and executing with exceptional attention to detail

Highly motivated and proactive individual, showing initiative to improve processes

Strong verbal and written English skills

Familiarity with Oracle ERP and Knowledge of EPM Oracle is an advantage
