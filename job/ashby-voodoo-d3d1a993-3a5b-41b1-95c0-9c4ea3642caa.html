ABOUT VOODOO

Voodoo is a consumer tech company founded in Paris in 2013, with one mission: entertain the world. Today we are 1,000 people, generating $1b+ in annual revenue, profitably. Our games and apps have passed 8 billion downloads and reach 200 million+ active users every month, making Voodoo the #3 mobile publisher worldwide in downloads, after Google and Meta. We were backed by Goldman Sachs, Tencent and GBL along the way.

TEAM

Our Growth team is a transversal entity aiming to maximize and deliver the potential of any mobile product. It combines user acquisition, creative content production, monetization, and the tech capabilities required to turn applications into global hits. With over 150 experts, we operate at the forefront of mobile marketing and ad tech.

Today, our Creative team makes 10K+ ads a month, mostly by hand. We are rebuilding that process with AI to multiply both our output and our hit rate. The challenge is to produce at massive scale, with quality that holds up.

ROLE

As an AI Content Producer, you own the production of our ads (Playables, video ads, ASO visuals, statics) and build the AI workflows that let us ship them at scale. You think in systems: you don’t just make creatives, you build the pipelines that scale them.

You will:

- Produce creatives with AI, end to end: turn briefs into finished ads for the games you cover, from first draft to launch.

- Design and implement AI workflows: build pipelines that automate variations and repetitive production steps, and roll them out so the whole team can ship more, faster.

- Own our AI toolbox: test and combine the best tools (Veo, Sora, Kling, Seedance, Runway, Scenario, etc.) and integrate the ones that matter into our production workflows.

- Learn from performance: analyze results (hook rate, CTR, CVR, IPM) and feed the learnings into the next batches and into the workflows themselves.

- Bring your own ideas: with your game taste, benchmark competitors, spot trends, and suggest concepts and angles for the games you work on.

PROFILE

- 1-3 years of experience in a creative, growth, or technical role. We care more about what you’ve built than your title.

- Very AI-native, with real projects shipped using these tools.

- Hacker mindset: you automate what’s repetitive and find shortcuts others don’t see.

- Strong game taste and visual instincts: you know what works on mobile and why.

- Comfortable with workflows, APIs, or no-code tools. Coding is a plus.

- Fluent in English and French.

BENEFITS

- Premium healthcare coverage with SideCare, 100% covered for you and your family

- Wellness activities in our Paris office
