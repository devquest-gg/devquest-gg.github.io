Scopely is looking for a Sr. Director Business Development to join our Global Publishing team!

The Business Development team is responsible for business negotiations, partner management, and new expansion opportunities with our largest IP partners, new distribution platforms (e.g. storefronts, hardware manufacturers, subscription services, etc.), and digital payment partners (e.g. Stripe, Visa, Checkout.com , etc.) around the world.

Through these relationships, the team is responsible for creating and executing a strategy for each partner that drives audience and revenue growth for Scopely’s portfolio of games.

What You Will Do

Reporting to the SVP Publishing, the Senior Director, Business Development will be responsible for working closely with internal game development teams and key external partners to drive Scopely’s distribution and growth strategy globally.

Develop key relationships with top IP partners, new distribution platforms, and digital payment vendors in the gaming industry to support and grow the Scopely global business.

Articulate the Scopely vision and how we can most effectively partner together

Create and execute on a global business development strategy that connects to our overall distribution strategy and principles.

Source and negotiate large complex partnerships that grow Scopely’s overall consumer and commercial presence globally.

Partner closely with internal teams to ensure the needs of the business are being met in a timely manner

Willingness to travel domestically and internationally and an appreciation for the way business is conducted in different parts of the world

Keep tabs on industry trends by obsessively analyzing the market, playing all the new releases, and attending industry events and conferences

What We’re Looking For

8+ years of Business Development / Partnership experience within the digital entertainment industry

Experience creating and executing a global business development strategy

Ability to identify and negotiate large scale commercial agreements

Gaming experience preferred, and particularly free-to-play games

Passion love for video games

Ability to work effectively with cross-functional teams and all levels of management

Ability to manage multiple projects with strong attention to detail

Ability to work in a fast-paced environment and adapt to changes

Ability to analyze data to make data-driven decisions - skills in Excel and Google Sheets necessary

Strong presentation and communication skills - Keynote, Powerpoint, or Google Slides skills required

Bachelor s degree or equivalent required

About Scopely

Scopely is a leading video game and global interactive entertainment company, home to many of the world’s most beloved and enduring experiences, including two of the most successful mobile games of all-time “MONOPOLY GO!” and “Pokémon GO,” along with “Stumble Guys,” “Star Trek™ Fleet Command,” “MARVEL Strike Force,” “WWE Champions,” the Scrabble® franchise, “Yahtzee® With Buddies,” and many others. Across mobile, web, PC, and console, Scopely creates, develops, publishes, and live-operates one of the most diversified and award-winning portfolios in the games industry — bringing hundreds of millions of players together through a shared love of play.

Founded in 2011, Scopely is powered by its exceptional team — including thousands of world-class gamemakers around the globe, a distinctive tenet-driven culture, and its proprietary technology platform, Playgami. Together, these strengths have fueled Scopely’s position as the #1 mobile games company in the U.S. and #2 globally, generating more than $10 billion in lifetime revenue. Whether building global sensations like “MONOPOLY GO!” from the ground up, or expanding through strategic acquisitions, including the FoxNext, GSN, and Scopely Explore games businesses — Scopely consistently delivers experiences players love today and return to for years to come.

Recognized multiple times as one of the "100 Most Influential Companies in the World" by TIME magazine and one of Fast Company s "World s Most Innovative Companies" and “Best Workplaces for Innovators,” Scopely believes that video games can be a force for good — creating meaningful connections, vibrant communities, and making life better through play.

Scopely has global operations and partners across four continents in more than a dozen countries worldwide. For more information, visit: https://www.scopely.com/ .
