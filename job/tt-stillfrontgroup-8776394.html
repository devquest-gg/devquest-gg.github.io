Empire: Four Kingdoms launched in January 2013 and is the world’s all-time highest-grossing app produced by a German company. It is part of our Empire brand, which has passed €1 billion in revenue and 220 million registered players. Now we are moving the game from Adobe AIR to Unity. Quality is the heart of that move: the Unity client has to behave exactly like today’s client, 1:1, including its known quirks, while the live team keeps shipping new content every month.

We are looking for a QA Automation Engineer, Mobile Games - Freelance who builds that proof as an automated, AI-assisted test setup: automation first, AI agents for the legwork, people for the judgement calls, and no endless manual passes.

This is a senior freelance role where you work autonomously, full-time from February 2027 until the end of 2027 including handover. It is fully remote within ±3 hours of CET, with the option to meet as a team in one of our Hamburg offices.

YOUR MISSION Design the test strategy and test plans for the port based on the live game, game design documents and the list of features to migrate. Prioritise by risk and agree the standards with the game’s QA lead.

Build automated tests that run the AIR client and the Unity client side by side: same input, same result. Think replayed server traffic, state comparison and pixel diffs, where pass or fail is deterministic, not a judgement call.

Use AI tools like Claude as a real part of your work: to generate and maintain test scripts, explore the game, cluster crashes and draft bug reports, with a human checking the result. Build tooling where it pays off, and measure whether it does.

Perform smoke tests, sanity checks and full regression testing for every port build, and turn each new live feature and content release into regression cases for the Unity client.

Test what players feel: payments and in-app purchases, store builds, login, push notifications, localisation, and the analytics events the business relies on, checked event by event with the data team.

Run a device matrix on real devices, including low-end Android phones and older iPhones, and track load time, memory, frame rate, heat and battery against the live client.

Identify, document and report defects in Jira, manage tickets and coordinate with the port team so that release deadlines are met.

Support the release in a first market and then on all stores: release candidates, store certification, a tested rollback path and clear go or no-go input.

Hand over a test suite and documentation that the live QA team can run and extend after the port.

WHO YOU’LL WORK WITH You join the port team of Empire: Four Kingdoms and report to the project lead.

Project lead: the owner of the port, who sets priorities and is your day-to-day contact.

The game’s QA lead: reviews your test approach and signs off quality for the Empire team.

The port team: the Lead Unity Engineer, the Unity developers and a second QA Engineer joining later. You share test plans, review each other’s work and keep one testing standard.

The Empire team: producers, game designers, client and server developers who know the game inside out. You involve them early and learn from them.

Data team: to check that the tracking events from the Unity client mean the same as today.

External QA partners: where we use them, you help coordinate their testing.

YOUR BACKSTORY 6+ years of hands-on QA experience in games, with a focus on test automation and a solid understanding of the bug life cycle and testing methodologies.

You have tested at least one live mobile game with a significant user base, through store release and live updates. This is a hard requirement.

Hands-on mobile and game test automation, for example Appium, AltTester, GameDriver, Unity Test Framework or comparable tools, and scripting in Python, C# or a similar language, including running tests in CI.

You work autonomously: you own your test approach, set priorities with the project lead and raise risks early, without needing day-to-day direction.

Practical experience using AI tools (Claude, Cursor, Copilot or similar) in QA: generating and maintaining tests, triaging failures, writing tooling. You know where they help and where they don’t.

Understanding of client-server games: you can read network traffic and tell a client bug from a server bug.

Experience testing payments and in-app purchases on iOS and Android.

Fluent English; you can explain complex technical issues to cross-functional teams.

NICE TO HAVE Experience testing a port, migration or major rewrite against the original Automated visual regression testing or replay-based testing Building agentic tooling for QA teams. This is a real differentiator for us.

Adobe AIR, Flash or Unity client testing Live mobile F2P and strategy games (live ops cadence, events, A/B tests) Analytics and tracking validation German WHY JOIN US? A classic at a turning point. You work on one of Europe’s most successful mobile strategy games at the moment it moves to a modern engine, and millions of players will feel the result.

Work your way. Fully remote within ±3 hours of CET, with the option to meet the team in our Hamburg offices for kick-offs and key milestones.

AI in daily work. We use AI tools like Claude every day and give you room to build the QA tooling you always wanted, judged by results.

A team that knows the game. You build on more than a decade of knowledge in the Empire team and leave behind a test setup that lasts.

HOW WE WILL INTERVIEW Heads up: we will ask you to show your AI testing workflow live in the interview. Bring your real setup, your real prompts, your real failures. We care more about how you actually work than about a polished demo.

ABOUT GOODGAME STUDIOS Goodgame Studios is known for data-driven game development and successful live-service strategy titles. We self-publish our games in-house with all relevant disciplines under one roof. Our studio is based in Hamburg, Germany, and we welcome applications from international candidates. English is our working language. As part of Stillfront Group , one of Europe’s leading game publishers, we work on long-term IP growth with a strong focus on execution.

About Goodgame Studios

Goodgame Studios is a leading free-to-play, online games development company active on web and mobile platforms. We self-publish our games in-house and have all relevant professions under one roof: Game Development, Quality Assurance, Community Management and Customer Support to Business Development, Marketing, Analytics and Central IT services. These departments work hand in hand to provide quality gaming experiences to over 200 million registered users worldwide.

Goodgame Studios is part of Stillfront. Stillfront is a global gaming company with 1200+ employees. We develop a wide range of digital games that attract over 40 million players each month. From well-established franchises like Supremacy, Big Farm, and BitLife to niche games, we span many different genres, including strategy, simulation, RPG and action, and casual and mash-up games.
