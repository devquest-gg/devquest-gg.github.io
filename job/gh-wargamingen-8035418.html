Job Overview

We are looking for a Game Designer focused on meta-game and systemic design to help shape engaging long-term player experiences in a live-service, AAA console environment. You will translate high-level game vision into structured, playable systems that drive retention, competition, and meaningful progression across the player journey.

This role sits at the intersection of design, data, and live operations. You will be responsible for designing and maintaining core meta systems such as progression, matchmaking, ranking, and player economy—ensuring they work cohesively with moment-to-moment gameplay and overall product vision.

What will you do?

Design, implement, and maintain meta-game and economic systems, such as multiplayer matchmaking, ranking systems, progression, etc.;

Assess player needs and find opportunities for improving the player experience in whichever ways are most impactful outside of the game;

Conceptualize and create detailed game design documentation;

Work collaboratively with the Game Director and Design Team to deliver a thrilling, high-quality product.

What are we looking for?

Experience as a Game Designer of products with successful releases ;

Experience designing progression, matchmaking, or social systems;

Game design experience on live service/games-as-a-service titles in the midcore/hardcore action game sphere;

Strong communication skills;

A progressive critical thinker who can develop solutions to overcome the obstacles and challenges that come with new game development.

Work mode

Hybrid (3 days of work from the office).

About Wargaming

Wargaming is an award-winning online game developer and publisher headquartered in Nicosia, Cyprus. Operating since 1998, Wargaming has become one of the leaders in the gaming industry with 15 offices worldwide, including studios in Chicago, Prague, Shanghai, Tokyo, and Vilnius. Our diverse and multicultural team works together to deliver a top-class experience to millions of players who enjoy Wargaming s titles across all major gaming platforms. Our flagship products include free-to-play hits World of Tanks, World of Warships and World of Tanks Blitz.
