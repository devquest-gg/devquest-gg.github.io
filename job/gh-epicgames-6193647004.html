GAME DESIGN INTERN

What You ll Do

We are looking for a Game Design Intern to join the Fortnite Events team and help create memorable large scale multiplayer experiences. You will work with internal and co-dev designers, artists, engineers and producers to prototype, implement and polish gameplay features for upcoming live events.

In this role, you will

Help design and implement gameplay features for live events

Learn how to prototype dynamic player experiences in a large scale client-server replicated environment and expand on those ideas

Work within level sequences to help align keyframed event triggers, work with cinematic artists to manage structure and timing, and implement gameplay features into the event

Participate in team playtests, providing and addressing feedback

Collaborate with others and independently iterate on work based on feedback and evolving needs

Clearly communicate updates, progress, and potential risks to relevant stakeholders

Make and update documents for designs, technical adjustments, or reference materials

What we re looking for

Practical experience with Unreal Engine

Proficiency with Blueprints and the Gameplay Ability System

Portfolio of playable projects demonstrating experience across the video game production cycle, including student, indie, or freelance work

Interest in Fortnite, particularly live events and ambitious, narrative-driven content

Experience with level design systems such as World Partition and Data Layers, Level Sequencer, and basic multiplayer replication

Ability to independently troubleshoot problems and use available resources to identify solutions to blocking issues

Ability to clearly communicate needs, risks, ideas, scope, and progress to the team

Ability to collaborate effectively across disciplines to safely implement content into a project

Ability to debug systems and interactive gameplay issues and develop test plans that support both debugging and QA validation

This internship has a flexible start date in 2027. Recruitment will be ongoing until teams find an ideal match.
