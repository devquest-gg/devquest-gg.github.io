Position: Commercial Operations Coordinator Experience: 4-8 years Location: Netaji Subhash Place, Pitampura, Delhi Employment Type: Full-Time Work Timings: Split-shift timings (2:00 PM to 6:00 PM and 9:00 PM to 1:00 AM.)

Overview

We’re looking for a high functioning Project Coordinator who can work in a fast paced environment efficiently and without errors.

The Project Coordinator will support all commercialization activities through coordination and execution. The candidate must be able to context-shift without making mistakes; working with both internal and external partners across many systems such as Asana, Google Sheets, email, and digital game store backends. The role requires someone with strong communication skills, the ability to prioritize, consistent adherence to process, and a positive attitude.

What You’ll Do

The foundation of this job will be the coordination of multiple concurrent projects that require communication with internal and external partners, and the initiative to push projects to completion.

Coordinate and drive to completion the projects assigned by the Business Operations Manager

Manage and maintain project tracking in Asana, keeping tasks, owners, and due dates accurate and up-to-date

Track details, commitments, and follow-ups across email threads with internal teams and external partners, and ensure they are captured in Asana and our trackers

Coordinate game discounts across all platforms (Steam, Nintendo, etc.) in partnership with the Commercial Data Coordinator

Keep internal spreadsheets and trackers updated and error-free, including maintaining their formatting

Follow key processes and ensure data is clear, reliable, and current

Communicate status, timelines, and blockers to stakeholders across internal teams and external partners

Partner with the Business Operations Manager and the Commercial Data Coordinator to maintain ongoing areas of commercialization

Make recommendations to improve the efficiency of our processes, including opportunities to automate recurring tasks

As needed, create SOPs to document your processes

Qualifications Requirements

Bachelor’s degree

4+ years of project coordination or commercial operations experience

Proficiency in the Google Suite, particularly Google Sheets.

Familiarity with project management tools (Asana, Jira, etc.)

Ability to quickly learn new systems by reviewing documentation

Excellent written and verbal communication skills

Biases for efficiency and detail-orientation

Able to work independently, but also highly collaborative and cross-functional

Reports to the Business Operations Manager

Preferred

A passion for video games is strongly preferred.

Microsoft Excel a plus.

A working knowledge of AI tools (such as ChatGPT, Gemini, and Claude), a willingness to keep learning as they evolve, and the ability to identify opportunities to automate your own tasks are nice to have.
