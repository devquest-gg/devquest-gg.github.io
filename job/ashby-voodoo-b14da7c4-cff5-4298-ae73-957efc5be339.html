ABOUT VOODOO

Founded in 2013, Voodoo is a tech company that creates mobile games and apps with a mission to entertain the world. Gathering 800 employees, 7 billion downloads, and over 200 million active users, Voodoo is the #3 mobile publisher worldwide in terms of downloads after Google and Meta. Our portfolio includes chart-topping games like Mob Control and Block Jam, alongside popular Studios like BeachBum and apps such as BeReal and Wizz.

TEAM

Join a small, agile team working on The Almanac https://apps.apple.com/fr/app/the-almanac/id6742649363, a smart puzzle app built to sharpen your mind and cut through brain rot. With elegant word and logic challenges, it's a daily habit that actually makes you smarter. You’ll grow fast and make a real impact on a product loved by thousands.

ROLE

As a key member of the founding team, you’ll own the product’s user experience and visual identity. You’ll design and iterate on clean, intuitive, and polished user flows—from core gameplay to monetization and onboarding. Your work will directly shape how players feel and interact with The Almanac every day.

- Lead the end-to-end design of intuitive, beautiful, and efficient user experiences

- Design wireframes, mockups, and interactive prototypes for both core gameplay and meta systems

- Develop and evolve the visual language of the app across UI components, typography, layout, and transitions

- Collaborate closely with developers to ensure seamless implementation and polish

- Design and test UI for monetization features (ads, IAP), progression systems, and events

- Help define and improve user flows through research, data, and testing

- Ensure the product feels premium, smooth, and delightful

PROFILE

- Figma expert

- Gaming background

- Founder mentality: proactive, hands-on, and ownership-driven

- Obsessed with quality and attention to detail

- Able to prototype, iterate quickly, and work in short feedback loops

- Fluent in English

BENEFITS

Competitive salary + performance bonus and other benefits according to the country you reside.
