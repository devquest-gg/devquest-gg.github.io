Job Overview

Do you want to work in the e-commerce payments and fraud operations at one of the world’s largest gaming companies?

This role is part of the Payments Team which is responsible for setting and implementing the payment strategy for Wargaming and preventing payments fraud. Payments is considered a key stakeholder, ensuring global coverage of payment methods for all existing and new products along with making sure the level of payment fraud is kept low.

In this role you will have the opportunity to learn about payments and fraud on a global scale from a team of experts with many years of experience in the payments industry.

Reports to

The Junior Payments Specialist will report to the Senior Payments and Fraud Manager.

What will you do?

Manage new and existing relationships with Payment Service Providers (PSPs) and Payment processors

Onboard new payment methods and/or new PSPs: assist in negotiating contracts, fees, and technical set ups

Perform analytics on payment performance and identify areas of improvement to lower cost and improve approval rates

Interact with other stakeholders on the payments related questions – Development, Legal, DPO, Products

Monitor KPI’s ensuring optimum performance

Monitor new regulations and implement them

What are we looking for?

Basic understanding of payment processing financial transactions

Strong written and verbal communication and presentation skills

Strong analytical Excel skills

Data-driven decision making with good problem-solving skills

Time management skills and a high attention to detail

University degree or any related qualification in any of the following: Mathematics, Statistics, Finance, Economics

What additional skills will help you stand out?

Understanding of e-commerce principles

Experience in payments

Understanding of business models and economics within Gaming companies

About Wargaming

Wargaming is an award-winning online game developer and publisher headquartered in Nicosia, Cyprus. Operating since 1998, Wargaming has become one of the leaders in the gaming industry with 15 offices worldwide, including studios in Chicago, Prague, Shanghai, Tokyo, and Vilnius. Our diverse and multicultural team works together to deliver a top-class experience to millions of players who enjoy Wargaming s titles across all major gaming platforms. Our flagship products include free-to-play hits World of Tanks, World of Warships and World of Tanks Blitz.
