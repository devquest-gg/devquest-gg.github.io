The role is working within our studio: Ludios

About Ludios

Ludios takes its name from the Latin, it means "The Game People". Led by Eyal, one of the co-founders of Tripledot Studios, and some of the most experienced people in mobile games, we are a studio that makes and publishes our own casual, free-to-play mobile games. Our team is based across APAC, with the majority of our development happening in Jakarta and Melbourne. We are data-driven, creative, and genuinely passionate about the industry we work in. Right now, we are working on a number of exciting projects, including one built around a globally recognised IP, alongside several original titles we are equally excited about. If crafting games and reaching millions of players is what gets you out of bed, we would love to hear from you.

Role Overview

Overseeing a highly profitable portfolio of established mobile games, this role will manage the long-term sustainability and commercial lifespan of our scaled legacy titles. Instead of building new games from scratch, you will focus on operational excellence, data-driven optimizations, and continuous value creation to elongate the life of these profitable products.

Because this role focuses on managing, optimizing, and scaling established product ecosystems rather than pre-launch R D, it represents the perfect entry point and learning opportunity for high-potential Product Managers looking to transition into the mobile gaming industry.

Utilizing your analytical depth, structural thinking, and collaboration skills, you will own the live ecosystem, manage the product roadmap, and ensure these titles continue to deliver high-margin returns.

Responsibilities - What you’ll be doing…

Product Lifespans: Lead cross-functional teams in maintaining and optimizing scaled games, executing smart, high-impact iterations that sustain user engagement and profitability over the long term.

Drive Efficient Roadmaps: Utilize real-time player data, live economy health, and churn metrics to craft product roadmaps focused on retention and stabilization. You will own the 3-6 month roadmap, focusing on backlog prioritization that maximizes ROI with lean development resources.

Data-Informed Lifecycle Management: Leverage robust analytics and historical player data to make critical balancing decisions, ensuring our mature player base experiences engaging, intuitive, and accessible live operations.

Optimize Live Ops Economy: Transition games smoothly from their historical growth phases into highly efficient, steady-state scaled operations. You will oversee live event cadences, store configurations, and economy sinks to extract sustainable value.

Smart Feature Implementation: Lead targeted feature enhancements and live-ops functionality specifically designed to optimize already scaled games—moving from requirement wireframes to deployment and post-launch analysis.

Adapt and Get Involved: Thrive in a stable yet dynamic live-services environment, applying your structured PM expertise to various operational areas of game sustainability as required.

Required Skills, Knowledge and Expertise

Required Skills, Knowledge, and Expertise

Strong Core PM Foundations: Proven track record as a Product Manager in tech, SaaS, e-commerce, or digital products; prior mobile gaming experience is NOT required —we will teach you the gaming frameworks if you bring strong product fundamentals.

Analytical Data-Driven Mindset: Excellent critical thinking and problem-solving skills, with a strong comfort level using data dashboards to track product health and user behavior.

Operational Excellence: Proven ability to establish, manage, and improve agile processes within a project to keep workflows highly efficient.

Stakeholder Management: Excellent communication skills, both internally and externally, with a talent for transparently synthesizing complex viewpoints for diverse audiences.

Delivery Management: Capable of confidently managing the day-to-day work of cross-functional teams, resources, or projects within defined operational parameters.

Economy Process Orientation: A strong interest in or aptitude for learning game economy management, monetization mechanics, and live service optimization.
