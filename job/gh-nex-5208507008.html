Nex is on a mission to help families rediscover the joy of movement. Created by parents for parents, Nex combines technology and play to deliver fun, social, and interactive experiences powered by natural body motion, encouraging kids and adults to move more, play more, and have fun together. Nex Playground, the company’s award-winning active play system, is purpose-built to get families moving year-round, with safety and privacy as core considerations in its intentional design. It is certified kidSAFE+ COPPA compliant and built to support healthy, active play for all ages and abilities.

Nex Playground features a growing library of 50+ experiences, including motion and dance games, fitness and educational experiences, and Nex Originals. Content includes collaborations with partners like Hasbro, Sesame Workshop, and NBCUniversal. Nex has been recognized by Fast Company’s Most Innovative Companies, TIME’s Best Inventions, and Parents’ Best Entertainment System for Families, and has earned Red Dot, IDEA, and Core77 international design awards. We encourage you to explore Have Fun and Is Motion Gaming Back? , as they offer a deeper look into our culture, values, and explain how our approach to motion gaming differs from previous generations.

Location: San Jose, CA (On-site) Annual salary: USD 90,000– USD 115,000 Employment Type: Full-time Reports to: People Operations Manager

About the Role

We are looking for a dedicated and detail-oriented People Operations Specialist to help build and run the people experience at Nex. This is a hybrid role—roughly split between People Operations and executive/administrative support for our San Jose office—giving you broad exposure across HR, office operations, and business support as we scale.

You will play a critical part in delivering seamless support to employees and stakeholders across North America and Europe, ensuring an exceptional employee experience. You will handle sensitive employee information with the utmost confidentiality and integrity, focusing on data accuracy and process efficiency. The ideal candidate is a proactive team player with a client-service mindset, a passion for continuous improvement, and the ability to thrive in a fast-paced, dynamic startup environment.

This is an on-site role requiring a minimum of 3 days per week in our San Jose office, with occasional travel. You handle sensitive employee data and confidential company information—discretion is essential.

What You ll Do

People Operations

Own the new hire onboarding process end-to-end—HRIS administration, IT coordination, orientation and Day 1 readiness—so every new hire feels the Nex culture from the start

Manage employee lifecycle events including role changes, promotions, compensation adjustments, international transfers, and offboarding with precision and care

Create, maintain, and audit employee records and documentation, ensuring data accuracy and compliance across all systems

Serve as the first point of contact for employee questions on People topics, benefits, and policies—responding with speed, clarity, and a genuine desire to help

Maintain and improve People dashboards and reporting—headcount, attrition, onboarding milestones, and other key operational metrics

Support various People projects and initiatives—HRIS implementation, People policies design, implementation and communication, etc.

Office Administrative Support

Oversee day-to-day operations of the San Jose office—facilities, vendors, supplies, space management, mail and filing management

Provide administrative support to senior leadership, helping keep priorities organized and operations running smoothly

Hardware inventory budgeting, planning and lifecycle management

Act as the primary contact person for all matters related to corporate and workplace insurance, including renewals and claim administration

Plan and execute team events, company offsites, and employee experience moments—including venue/vendor sourcing, logistics, and coordination

Ensure a high-quality in-office experience that reflects how Nex works: free, flexible, and intentional

Who You Are

Required Qualifications

4+ years of experience in People Operations, HR administration, or a similar people-facing role—ideally in a fast-paced technology or startup environment

Exceptional organizational skills and attention to detail, with a strong commitment to data integrity and confidentiality

Strong written and verbal communication skills across all channels and audiences

Experience with office management, workplace operations, or event planning

Ability to work independently while collaborating seamlessly with a global team

Ability to manage multiple priorities and pivot quickly in a dynamic environment

Preferred Qualifications

Experience with tools in our stack: Notion, Slack, TriNet, Lattice, Deel, and AI tools like Claude

Exposure to People analytics, reporting, or dashboard maintenance

Exposure to office management or workplace experience in a hybrid or distributed setting

Experience in consumer tech, gaming, or hardware companies

Mindset Attributes

You are a proactive, people-caring and ownership-driven operator—you see gaps and fill them before anyone asks

You are comfortable with ambiguity and genuinely excited about building processes from scratch

You bring Marie Kondo energy to employee records, people systems, and office spaces alike—chaos doesn t stand a chance around you

You are a fast learner who operates with high agency—when you don t know something, you figure it out; when you see an opportunity, you run with it

You are a self-starter who follows through relentlessly, whether it s closing out an onboarding checklist or sourcing a venue for an offsite

You understand that supporting a growing, global team means flexibility—no two days will look the same, and that s what excites you
