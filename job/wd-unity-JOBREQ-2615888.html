Senior DevOps Engineer

Unity · Copenhagen

Senior Engineering Posted 2026-08-04 Full time
Apply on Unity’s site →

The opportunity Are you a visionary DevOps Engineer with a keen focus on driving modern infrastructure through Automation, Infrastructure-as-Code (IaC), and advanced CI/CD practices? At Unity Technologies IT Engineering, you will be pivotal in evolving our global, multi-cloud environment.

This includes architecting and managing highly available, containerized solutions, with a strong emphasis on Kubernetes (specifically GKE), and integrating AI tooling to enhance efficiency. Your expertise will directly shape the dynamic IT infrastructure that empowers Unity’s engineering teams to create groundbreaking software utilized by millions of developers worldwide. You will be part of a high-performing, multi-region team dedicated to continuous innovation and automation of our systems, setting a high standard for excellence.

If you’re not afraid to roll up your sleeves and enjoy new challenges supporting a globally leading company, we are looking for you! What you ll be doing Designing, implementing, and maintaining scalable cloud infrastructure using Infrastructure-as-Code (IaC) principles.

Building containerized applications and managing Kubernetes clusters, with a focus on Google Kubernetes Engine (GKE).

Developing and maintaining robust CI/CD pipelines using modern tools like ArgoCD, GitHub Actions, and Jenkins for rapid deployment.

Driving automation across all aspects of the IT infrastructure and operations to minimize manual effort, improve reliability and improve the user experience.

Exploring and implementing AI tooling to enhance operational efficiency, security, and predictive maintenance capabilities.

What we re looking for Expertise with Kubernetes (GKE) and containerization technologies.

Proven experience with Infrastructure-as-Code (IaC) tools (e.g., Terraform and Ansible.) Deep understanding of CI/CD methodologies and tools (ArgoCD, GitHub Actions, Jenkins).

Strong background working in a cloud environment (GCP, AWS, or Azure).

Ability to write high-quality, production-grade code in languages like Python or Golang for automation and service development.

You might also have Experience with developing or integrating AI/ML tooling.

Familiarity with modern Observability practices, including robust logging, metrics, and alerting, e.g. Elastic Stack (Elasticsearch, Kibana) and Prometheus/Grafana.

Expertise in advanced troubleshooting and performance optimization of complex services running in high-traffic Linux-based production environments.

Familiarity with cloud-native services and experience with SQL/NoSQL database infrastructure automation.

Strong ownership mindset to drive technical roadmaps and initiatives, coupled with excellent collaboration skills to work effectively within a multi-region team.
