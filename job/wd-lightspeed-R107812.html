What the Role Entails Research the model s ability to understand generated content, parsing semantics, objects, relationships, and spatial structures.

Implement state tracking and consistency modeling for generated videos.

Explore unified generation-understanding model architectures.

Research causal consistency control and physical constraints for the action input → video output pipeline.

Research hybrid architectures to achieve low-latency feedback for real-time interactive generation.

Collaborate with Agent/RL teams to drive an end-to-end closed loop of generation → understanding → control → feedback.

Validate controllable interactive capabilities in game scenarios and lead the establishment of evaluation systems.

Continuously track industry-related work and formulate proprietary technical roadmaps.

Who We Look For Ph.D. in AI-related fields, covering areas such as video understanding, video prediction, reinforcement learning, or multimodal generation and understanding.

Deep understanding of the internal mechanisms of both video generation and VLM models; familiar with diffusion model principles.

Research experience in interactive video generation or controllable video generation.

Ability to independently design complex model training pipelines with strong engineering skills.

Proficient in Python/PyTorch, with publications in video understanding, controllable generation, or reinforcement learning.

Experience with Game AI, simulation environments, or closed-loop systems is preferred.

Relevant publications in top-tier conferences or journals are preferred.
