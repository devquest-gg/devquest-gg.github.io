About the Role:

As a Game Designer focused on Feature Design, you will own the creation and optimization of new gameplay features that directly impact Engagement and Retention.

You will design systems from concept to specification, ensure smooth adoption funnels, align features with the in-game economy, and continuously optimize performance through live data and structured experimentation.

This is a high-impact role requiring strong ownership, executive-level communication, and the ability to balance player experience with business performance.

You will have the opportunity to:

Design and specify new features that drive Engagement and Retention

Analyze feature adoption funnels (introduction → activation → retention) and optimize user flows, friction points, and reward structures

Define unlock conditions, rewards, and Expected Value (EV) models by player segment

Ensure economic alignment with existing systems, including Source/Sink balance

Collaborate cross-functionally to establish A/B testing plans and measurement requirements

Design and balance mechanics and progression systems that strengthen player flow and long-term retention

Continuously tune features post-launch using live data

Analyze win rates, retention deltas, gameplay curves, and economic metrics to optimize balance

Lead live tuning cycles (hypothesis → parameter adjustment → measurement → iteration)

Maintain structured difficulty tiers (Normal / Hard / Boss / Event where applicable)

Partner with UX, Art, Engineering, Analytics, and LiveOps to optimize clarity, feedback loops, and system cohesion

Document tuning frameworks and economic design principles for alignment across teams

Qualifications:

Proven development and live-operations experience in F2P Puzzle and/or Merge genres, with deep understanding of gameplay mechanics

Ability to communicate with ownership and confidently engage with executive leadership (including CEO-level discussions)

Capable of building Unity mock-ups independently and communicating ideas grounded in implementation feasibility

Strong interest in game design, level design, player psychology, and data science
