Zynga is looking for a highly organized and proactive Program Business Operations Coordinator to join our CTO Strike Team - Contract Vendor Management Team. This role sits at the intersection of contract management, business operations, and cross-functional coordination, making it a uniquely visible and impactful position within our global organization. You ll own critical operational workflows end-to-end, support contract administration and procurement processes, and serve as a trusted communication hub within the CTO Strike team.

What You ll Do:

End-to-End Contract Vendor Management Program Coordination Provide operational and administrative support to the program director of the Contract Vendor Management team. This will include:

Contract Administration Procurement Support

Coordinating renewal workflows end-to-end

Contract administration, purchase Order (PO) requests, other legal and compliance related due diligence

Own the day-to-day administration of vendor contracts, including tracking contract lifecycle milestones

Maintaining an organized repository of executed agreements.

Ensuring accuracy, timely approvals, and proper documentation throughout

Creating renewal approval requests for pre-approvals and tracking approvals accordingly

Other Ad-hoc related contract requests

Communication Reporting

Coordinate program updates via written summaries, status reports, and stakeholder communications.

Prepare and maintain polished presentations for leadership reviews, team briefings, and cross-functional meetings, translating data and program progress into clear narratives as requested

Risk Mitigation Data Integrity

Software clearances, VSA, DPA, etc., for compliance and risk

Coordination with IT / Key partner teams on accurate usage data

Maintain contract management database with critical and key information

Flag and coordinate with the program director on any non-compliance issues for quick resolution

Assist with usage optimization process to align on accurate data usage information to ensure proper pricing and scale.

Program/Project Management Support Provide project management support for ad-hoc operations initiatives

General Business Operations Coordination Support

Ad-hoc administrative and operational requests on special projects

What You Bring:

Operational Business Experience

1-2+ years of experience in program coordination, business operations, or a similar role, preferably within software development, gaming, or compliance-heavy environments.

Some experience with contract administration and/or PO creation is strongly preferred.

Relationship Builder

Demonstrated ability to earn trust, empower teams, and build strong, collaborative relationships across various disciplines, and work styles.

Exceptional Communicator

Strong written and verbal communication skills, with a proven ability to write clear program summaries, updates, and business correspondence.

Comfortable building and presenting polished slide decks to program leads and senior stakeholders.

Process-Oriented

Experience managing high-volume intake workflows, meticulously tracking approvals, and maintaining flawless documentation across contracts, POs, and program deliverables.

Autonomous Operator

Proven ability to work independently, manage competing priorities, and drive results with minimal supervision.

Technical Proficiency

Hands-on experience with Google Workspace (Docs, Sheets, Slides).

Familiarity with ERP or procurement systems (e.g., JDE, Oracle, etc.) is a plus.

Bonus Points:

Familiarity with generative AI tools (like Gemini, Claude, etc.) to optimize daily operations, accelerate reporting, and streamline workflows.
