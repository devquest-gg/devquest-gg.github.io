Role Overview

Our team is looking for a creative and independent AI Game Developer to work on new R D projects in hybrid-casual mobile games market. You will independently develop game concepts and prototypes from scratch, using AI tools to accelerate development, test hypotheses, and identify promising product opportunities. We are looking for someone who combines game design skills, hands-on development experience, and strong product thinking to turn ideas into games.

Key Responsibilities

Generate and develop new game concepts based on market trends and player preferences

Independently create game prototypes and MVPs using AI-powered development tools

Design and implement core gameplay mechanics, progression, and basic game systems

Select and implement monetization suited to each game.

Use AI tools for coding, asset creation, debugging, and content generation

Improve internal AI tools by reporting reproducible issues, proposing enhancements, and validating updates with their developer.

Prepare prototypes for marketability and early product tests

Analyze test results and iterate on prototypes based on player feedback and product metrics

Identify promising concepts for further development

Required Skills, Knowledge and Expertise

Hands-on experience in mobile game development

Experience prototyping hybrid-casual games from scratch

Strong practical understanding of core loops, level design, progression, balancing, game economies, F2P mechanics, and mobile UI/UX.

Ability to interpret marketing and product metrics in the context of each development stage.

Portfolio of independently developed or AI-assisted game prototypes

Hands-on experience with AI development tools such as Claude Code, Cursor, Codex, or similar

Practical knowledge of Unity and C#, including building and debugging game prototypes

Understanding of core gameplay, progression, balancing, and F2P mechanics

Knowledge of mobile gaming market trends

Ability to work independently, iterate quickly, and make data-driven decisions

English and Russian spoken and written at B2 level and above
