Procurement Manager

Job type: Full-time

Location: United States (remote)

Reporting to: Chief Operating Officer

About Atari

Atari is an interactive entertainment company and an iconic gaming industry brand recognized worldwide for its multi-platform games and licensed products. Atari owns and/or manages a portfolio of more than 400 games and franchises.

The Atari group of brands includes Digital Eclipse, Nightdive Studios, Infogrames, AtariAge, MobyGames, as well as Coatsink, Early Morning Studio, Stormteller Games, Hipster Whale, and Implicit Conversions spanning game development, publishing, and community experiences worldwide.

Atari operates internationally with offices in the US, UK, France, Germany, Sweden, India, and Australia.

Role Overview

Atari is establishing a dedicated procurement function, and you will lead its development and day-to-day operation. In this hands-on role, you will own how Atari buys across its entities, from sourcing and negotiation through contract management, vendor performance, risk assessment, and reporting.

In your first months, you will lead live negotiations and renewals while building the policies, controls, playbook, and reporting that make effective purchasing the standard across the business.

You will work closely with the executive leadership team, Finance, Legal, IT, HR, and studio and business-unit leaders to improve commercial outcomes, manage risk, and support disciplined growth.

What You’ll Do

Own Atari’s procurement policy and vendor playbook, including intake processes, spend thresholds, competitive bidding requirements, minimum contract standards, and approval responsibilities.

Build and maintain a single vendor and contract register across Atari’s entities, covering owners, terms, spend, renewal dates, and notice periods.

Partner with Finance to enforce purchase-to-pay controls, including requiring an executed contract, statement of work, or purchase order before payment.

Bring contractor and consultant engagements under consistent procurement standards with People and Finance, including a named owner, budget approval, executed agreement before work begins, and periodic review of extensions.

Lead sourcing, RFPs, and negotiations for priority categories, including game engines and development tools, outsourced quality assurance and development services, software and cloud, IT hardware, travel, and professional services.

Consolidate vendors where Atari buys similar products or services across entities, moving toward group agreements, rate cards, and master services agreements.

Run a renewal calendar so contracts do not renew by default and each renewal begins early enough to renegotiate, re-tender, or exit.

Negotiate commercial terms and prepare contracts with Legal, covering scope, service levels, pricing, renewal and termination, liability, data protection, and intellectual property.

Set performance expectations for key vendors and run regular reviews with their internal owners.

Assess new vendors for financial, operational, security, and data privacy risk with IT and Legal before onboarding.

Work with Finance and Tax to ensure vendors bill the correct Atari entity and apply the correct tax treatment.

Track savings against agreed baselines, validate savings with Finance, and report progress to the executive leadership team.

Analyze spend by category, entity, and vendor to identify new opportunities, including categories growing faster than the business.

Bring procurement data into the annual budgeting process, including reviews of significant vendor spend.

Use automation and AI tools to keep the clerical side of procurement efficient, allowing more time for sourcing, negotiation, and vendor management.

What We’re Looking For

Requirements

7+ years of experience in procurement, strategic sourcing, or vendor management, with end-to-end ownership of sourcing and negotiation for indirect spend.

A track record of negotiating software, SaaS, and services contracts, with results that can be quantified.

Experience building or substantially rebuilding procurement processes, policies, or controls, rather than only operating existing ones.

Working knowledge of commercial contract terms, including MSAs, SOWs, service levels, renewal and termination provisions, and liability, together with experience partnering with legal counsel.

Experience buying across multiple legal entities or countries, including managing multi-currency spend.

Strong analytical skills, with the ability to build a spend analysis and savings model in a spreadsheet from imperfect data.

The ability to influence senior leaders and budget owners without direct authority, and to communicate clearly when a request does not meet procurement standards.

Strong critical-thinking and problem-solving skills, and alignment with Atari’s core values.

Preferred

Experience in video games, entertainment, media, or technology.

Category experience in game engines and development tools, outsourced quality assurance or development, cloud infrastructure, or IT hardware.

Experience establishing contractor governance with People and Finance teams.

Familiarity with spend, payables, and travel platforms such as Bill.com, Brex, or Navan.

Experience implementing contract management or procurement software, or automating procurement work with AI tools.

A procurement credential such as CIPS or CPSM.

If you meet most of the required qualifications and can show that you have built something similar before, we encourage you to apply.
