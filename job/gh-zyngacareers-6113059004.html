Join us and be part of the play! Position Overview:

Zynga is looking for a Principal Software Engineer level in the Austin, Eugene, Toronto, San Mateo, Carlsbad, or Chicago areas.

This position will be to develop and maintain the core client technology, engine-level systems, and infrastructure for a game with a cozy vibe targeting a casual audience.

You should have a passion for engineering excellence and be comfortable diagnosing and solving deep, systemic technical problems, such as improving load times, frame rate, memory footprint, and other cross-cutting performance concerns.

You’ll need to operate with rigorous engineering discipline while working largely independently to plan and execute complex, multi-week technical initiatives.

We have a well-rounded, highly motivated, distributed team with a mission-driven culture working on a new game.

We need a self-driven engineer who confronts challenges with optimism, communicates clearly and easily, and takes a great deal of pride in their work.

You’ll have an opportunity to own core engine and infrastructure work, including upgrading and maintaining third-party libraries and dependencies, evolving build and tooling pipelines, and making foundational changes to the client architecture that unlock future feature roadmaps for the rest of the engineering team.

What You ll Do:

Define, architect, and develop core systems and client infrastructure in Unity and C#

Diagnose and resolve complex, game-wide technical issues such as slow load times, low frame rates, memory pressure, and build/runtime stability

Own the evaluation, upgrade, and maintenance of third-party libraries, SDKs, and engine dependencies

Design and implement foundational architecture changes that enable future feature roadmaps for feature-focused engineers to build upon

Produce technical design documentation and implementation plans for infrastructure and systemic initiatives

Scope and cost work accurately from loosely defined requirements, presenting estimates with clear assumptions and identified risks

Adapt plans and technical approaches when requirements or platform constraints change

Review code, technical design documentation, and implementation plans for other team members

Proactively profile, optimize, and refactor code to meet team performance and stability KPI goals

Explore, develop, and integrate new technologies and tools that benefit the broader engineering team

Work closely with other engineers to lead multi-week or month-long infrastructure and tooling efforts

Give team and divisional tech talks on architectures that you’ve built

Work to improve and evolve team process and engineering standards

Help to mentor other engineers

What You Bring:

B.Sc. in Computer Science, Engineering, or equivalent education / work experience

6+ Years game industry / mobile app experience as a software developer

Shipped titles and/or live games experience, especially mobile (iOS / Android)

Expert knowledge in C#

3+ Years Unity experience, including engine-level and low-level systems work

A solid understanding of client/server architecture in modern mobile games

Understanding, interest and knowledge of server-side architecture in mobile games

Solid foundation in data structures, algorithms and software methodologies

Demonstrated experience diagnosing and resolving performance issues such as load times, frame rate, and memory usage

Experience upgrading, integrating, or maintaining third-party libraries, SDKs, or engine versions

Experience with the AWS ecosystem, Kubernetes, and back end monitoring technologies such as Datadog

Excellent communication skills and comfort working in a collaborative environment

Excellent ability to foresee and proactively resolve blockers, technical risk, and points of failure

Excellent ability to function in an asynchronous distributed work environment while still tuning in to key team meetings

Experience with git

Experience with performance profiling and optimization tools

Bonus Points:

Experience with Unity engine source modification or custom engine forks

Experience with build pipeline, CI/CD, or dev tooling for game teams

Experience playing and working on games that target a casual audience

Experience using Claude Code or a similar AI coding tool
