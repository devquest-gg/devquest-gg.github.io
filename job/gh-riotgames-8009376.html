Sr. Manager, Game Production (Production Lead) - Unpublished R&D Product

Riot Games · Los Angeles

Lead Engineering $207K–$289K Posted 2026-07-06 Full time
Apply on Riot Games’s site →

Producers at Riot lead passionate cross-functional teams to craft creative content and incredible experiences for our players. We encourage teams to collaborate and drive development, smashing through obstacles to deliver products into players’ hands. We support concepting, building and delivering content at all stages of production

As a Sr. Manager, Game Production on an unannounced R D product, you will own the production approach that turns creative ambition, product strategy, and team capabilities into clear plans, healthy operating rhythms, and shipped player value. You will help move games from early ideas through production, launch, and live operations. You will build production systems that create clarity without over-prescribing the work, helping the team adapt as the product, audience, and opportunity become clearer. You’ll work through ambiguity, energized by hard tradeoffs, and work across design, art, engineering, product, QA, analytics, publishing, and external development. You will partner closely with game leadership and discipline leads to build and own the production operating model for the game. This includes how teams plan, make commitments, manage dependencies, use vendors and co-development partners, and prepare for future stages of development. You will help the team scale from a small, adaptive group into a larger production organization without losing momentum, accountability, or care for players and team health. You will report to the Director, Game Production.

Responsibilities:

Own the game’s production approach through each phase of development, helping the team make strong decisions today while preparing for future launch and live-service needs.

Translate product strategy and creative direction into actionable plans, milestones, rituals, and decision-making systems.

Partner with game leadership and discipline leads to align scope, quality, staffing, timelines, and delivery expectations.

Lead cross-functional production across design, art, engineering, product, QA, analytics, publishing, and external development.

Build scalable planning, dependency, risk, and accountability systems as the team grows.

Identify delivery risks, organizational gaps, and execution constraints early; drive resolution with the appropriate leaders and teams.

Manage production relationships with vendors, outsourcing teams, and co-development partners.

Maintain clear visibility for stakeholders on progress, tradeoffs, risks, and decisions.

Foster healthy team dynamics, strong feedback loops, psychological safety, and productive conflict.

Required Qualifications:

10+ years of professional experience working in game production.

6+ years of in production leadership roles on cross-disciplinary game teams.

Experience shipping at least one game or major game initiative with production responsibility from pre-production through launch.

Background in cross-platform and live-service game development or live operations.

Strong command of milestone planning, roadmap execution, dependency management, risk management, and launch readiness.

Proven ability to lead through ambiguity, scale, changing priorities, and complex production tradeoffs.

Strong cross-discipline fluency across game design, art, engineering, product, QA, analytics, publishing, and external development.

Vendor, outsourcing, or co-development management background.

Deep understanding of games, player audiences, team health, modern development tools, production technology, and emerging AI-enabled tools and workflows.

Desired Qualifications:

Experience as a Lead Producer, Principal Producer, Production Director, or equivalent senior production owner on a shipped game.

Shipped and operated multiple cross-platform, multiplayer, or live-service games.

Experience building production practices for a new game, new team, or ambiguous development environment as it scaled.

Familiarity with studio operations, business planning, workforce planning, budgeting, player insights, economy, content operations, release management, or liveops planning.

For this role, you ll find success through craft expertise and a collaborative spirit that prioritizes the delight of players. We will look at your past studies and experience, but for this role, we also look for dedicated people with a personal relationship with games. If you embody player empathy and care about players experiences, this is the role for you!
