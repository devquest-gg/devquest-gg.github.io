HR Specialist [1 year fixed term contract]

Ubisoft Singapore

Senior People & Ops Posted 2026-06-12 Contractor
Apply on Ubisoft Singapore’s site →

YOUR PURPOSE Join our People Services team and help power the experience behind the scenes. You’ll be at the heart of everything that shapes our team members’ journey—from their first day to their biggest milestones.

In this role, you’ll bring together operational excellence and human experience , making sure everything runs smoothly while creating moments that truly matter.

YOUR DAILY ADVENTURE Onboarding Be the go-to onboarding partner, orchestrating a seamless and welcoming start for new joiners Deliver engaging orientation sessions that bring our culture to life from day one Ensure new joiner information is updated in all systems accurately so everything (including first-month payroll!) runs without a hitch Work Pass Matters Own the end-to-end work pass journey—from application to renewal—with precision and care Partner with authorities (like MOM) to keep things moving smoothly Stay ahead of regulation updates and make sure we’re always compliant Guide team members through their work pass journey with clarity and confidence Keep records clean, organised, and always up to date HR Operations Excellence You’ll keep the engine running smoothly across: Payroll: Partner with vendor to ensure timely and accurate processing Benefits: Keep our benefits running like clockwork and support queries Vendor Management: Work with external partners to deliver top-notch services Milestones: Organise and celebrate key moments—confirmations, promotions, long service awards, and more Data Management: Be the guardian of accurate, secure, and reliable people data ServiceNow: Handle HR tickets efficiently (you’re basically the problem-solver-in-chief) Compliance & Reporting: Support surveys and ensure we meet regulatory requirements Engagement: Help create meaningful experiences, events, and moments that bring teams together Mobility: Support international moves and make transitions as smooth as possible HR Projects Active participation in projects to automate, to implement AI solutions and to enhance team members journey in Ubisoft.

WHAT YOU BRING Preferably at least 3-5 years of related professional experience Previous experience in HR operations, ideally within a multinational environment Good knowledge of local Singapore Employment act and regulations Project management experience is a plus Strong attention to detail and organizational skills to ensure accurate documentation and compliance Excellent communication and interpersonal skills Strong work ethics; able to handle confidential information with discretion and maintain a high level of professionalism Dependable, fast learner, proactive and adaptable to thrive within a fast-paced environment and able to consistently meet deadlines.

Able to work both as a team and independently to deliver results with minimal supervision We are working to enrich players’ lives through unique and memorable gaming experiences and by improving the positive impacts of our games. To get there, we are creating a safer, more inclusive work environment, we are giving back to the communities where Ubisoft operates by working with local non-profit partners and by working to reduce the environmental impact of our business. We are an equal-opportunity employer and value diversity at our company. We do not discriminate on the basis of race, ethnicity, religion, gender, sexual orientation, age or disability status.

Come on board and experience #LifeAtUbi for yourself, we are always on the lookout for talents who thrive in bringing their A-game to work. If this sounds like you, shoot us your resume, portfolio, and anything else to show that you’re serious about fun!
