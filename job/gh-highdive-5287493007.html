Project Manager – Concept Art Outsourcing

As Project Manager (Concept Art Outsourcing), you will lead our project in their concept art and outsourcing mandates, with internal and external partners. You will lead the project s concept art team, steering the artistic vision and ensuring production alignment. You will oversee long-term relations with our internal and external partners and make sure to propose the right partners to productions according to their needs. You will collaborate with the various departments involved in multi-site collaboration and ensure that the processes and guidelines are followed by our productions.

What will you be doing:

Propose partners that meet the specific needs and state of mind of each production team.

Set up and iterate on outsourcing pipeline

Support and challenge the production teams in setting up their partner management structure.

Monitor projects, use KPI and tools to evaluate the health of our collaborations and gather feedback from external and internal collaborators.

Evaluate the evolution of needs for the next few years, in collaboration with our Brands and Recruitment teams.

Support production teams in training the co-development and outsourcing managers.

Continuous adaptation of our processes with the production teams and act as a facilitator in the event of difficulties.

Maintain and continue to develop a strong network of internal partners.

Source new external partnership opportunities, answer to solicitations.

As the Lead of the Concept Art team:

Drive the concept team’s output through planning and proactive advocacy; translating high-level directorial feedback into actionable guidance for the art team.

Act as a dedicated people manager for the concept team; streamlining operational tasks like time-tracking and leave approvals while driving professional growth through regular performance reviews.

Coordinate with the Associate Producer to balance production demands against team capacity, driving the prioritization of sprint tasks to ensure timely delivery of all milestone assets.

Provide visibility on the concept team production timeline and identify deliverable risks.

The skills we’re looking for:

At Least 5 years of experience in production or project management in the video game industry (or other relevant experience)

Shipped AAA Titles

Plenty of flexibility to adapt to a range of situations navigate interdependence.

Familiarity with a variety of project management methodologies.

Excellent communication, interpersonal, and presentation skills.

An attentive, collaborative, and innovative spirit.

A people-oriented approach: sure, you care about results, but you never lose sight of the dedicated people behind them.

Excellent communication and leadership abilities to effectively manage teams and stakeholders.

Passion for gaming and a deep appreciation for the art of game development.

Why could we be the perfect place for you:
