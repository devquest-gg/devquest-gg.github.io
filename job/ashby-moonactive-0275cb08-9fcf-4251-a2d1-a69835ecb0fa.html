Moon Active is one of the world's fastest-growing mobile game companies, providing entertainment for millions of active users across the universe. The company is headquartered in the heart of Tel Aviv.

We're looking for a Talent Acquisition Associate to join our recruitment team.

In this role you will be on the front line of our hiring processes, engaging and evaluating exceptional talent at scale.

Responsibilities

- Conduct high-volume, in-depth phone and video interviews and evaluate candidates' skills and potential.

- Create a welcoming and transparent experience for every candidate.

- Collaborate closely with Hiring Managers and the broader TA team to align on role requirements, provide candidate insights, and keep recruitment pipelines moving efficiently.

- Proactively reach out to passive top talent using diverse sourcing strategies.

Requirements

- Passion for behavioral interviewing and evaluating talent effectively in a high-volume environment.

- High empathy and a commitment to delivering a top-tier candidate experience.

- Strong communication skills, both in Hebrew and English.

- A proactive, self-motivated approach.

- Bachelor’s degree in Behavioral Sciences, Psychology, Human Resources, or a related field.

Advantages

- 1-2 years of hands-on recruitment experience (agency or in-house).

- Experience using modern Applicant Tracking Systems and LinkedIn Recruiter.
