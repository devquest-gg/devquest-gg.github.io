Job Description

As a Front-end Programmer at Ubisoft Montréal, you’ll develop web platforms that support Rainbow Six Siege esports tournaments. You’ll work on r6esports.com, web applications and internal tools used by our teams and partners.

Reporting to your team manager, you’ll work with other developers, the product team and Rainbow Six Siege developers. Your ideas and technical choices will help shape platforms used by our teams, esports fans, tournament organizers and professional players.

What you’ll do Design, develop and maintain our web platforms with a focus on application performance and reliability Define technical choices and front-end architecture for our applications Develop features from design through release and maintenance Contribute to product design and maintain the quality of technical solutions Diagnose, debug and resolve technical issues Document technical choices and features to support maintenance Collaborate with the product team to shape and improve our esports platforms Explore tools and practices that could benefit the team What you bring to the team Bring experience in modern web application development Apply strong skills in TypeScript, React and Next.js Work with modern web architectures, including micro-frontends, WebSocket and Web Components Use Node.js, npm and Vite Resolve complex technical issues Communicate in English and collaborate with cross-functional teams Nice to have: Bring an interest in esports or sports that helps you understand our environment Nice to have: Bring knowledge of C# and full-stack development What to send our way Your CV highlighting relevant skills and experiences Links to your web development projects or code repositories
