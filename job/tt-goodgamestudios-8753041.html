Lead Unity Engineer - Empire: Four Kingdoms Unity Port - Freelance

We are looking for a freelancer, full-time, for the duration of the project, from January through the end of 2027, including the handover.

At Goodgame Studios we are moving Empire: Four Kingdoms from Adobe AIR to Unity.

Empire: Four Kingdoms launched in January 2013 and is the world's all-time highest-grossing app produced by a German company. It is part of our Empire brand, which has passed €1 billion in revenue and 220 million registered players. The game gets new content every month, and its client codebase has grown over more than a decade. The goal is clear: the Unity client behaves exactly like today's client, 1:1, including its known quirks. There is no redesign and no feature freeze. The live team keeps shipping, and the port follows.

We are looking for the Unity expert for this port. You build the Unity client, AI-assisted, and you set its technical direction together with the game's client lead and the Empire team: you work alongside them, not under them. You keep the codebase clean and maintainable, make sure we can prove that it behaves like the original and runs well on real devices, train the team in Unity and hand the port over at the end. You work closely with the project lead, the game's client and backend leads, QA and data.

The engagement is a freelance contract for the duration of the project: from the start of the build, through the release on all stores, to the handover of the client to the live team at the end of 2027. Fully remote work is possible (within ±3 hours of CET), with the option to meet as a team at one of our Hamburg offices.

YOUR MISSION Be the Unity expert of the port . Own the architecture of the Unity client and agree the technical direction with the game's client lead and the Empire team. Set coding standards, review code and guide a small port team of Unity developers and QA specialists.

Port a large live client 1:1 . Bring a large ActionScript 3 codebase to C# and Unity, using automated code migration and AI-assisted development where they help, and careful engineering where they don't. Same behaviour, same timing, same server protocol, same quirks.

Prove parity, don't assume it . Build automated, deterministic tests that compare the Unity client against the live client: same input, same result. Think recorded server traffic, state comparison and pixel diffs, not judgement calls.

Platform and integrations . Payments and in-app purchases, store builds and certification, login, push notifications, crash reporting and analytics events, all behaving exactly as they do today.

Performance on real devices . Load time, memory, frame rate, heat and battery on low-end Android and older iPhones. Establish profiling practice and performance gates.

Keep up with a live game . The live team keeps releasing new features and content while we build. Work with them to track the delta and keep the Unity client current.

Hand over cleanly . Leave a codebase the live team is happy to own: documented, tested and maintainable. Train the in-house client developers in Unity during the build, so that they can own the client after handover.

Agentic engineering practice . Use agentic tools (Claude Code, Cursor, Copilot agents or similar) as a real part of how the team works, and build tooling where it pays off, for example for migration, test generation or regression detection.

Heads up : we will ask you to demonstrate your agentic workflow live in the interview. Bring your real setup, your real prompts, your real failures. We're more interested in how you actually work than in a polished demo.

YOUR BACKSTORY Senior-level Unity and C# experience, Unity 6 or recent versions, iOS and Android.

Shipped at least one live mobile game with a significant user base . This is a hard requirement. You have lived through low-end Android, store certification, live ops and the gap between "works on my device" and "works for a million players".

Experience as a tech lead or lead developer: you have set the architecture of a Unity client, guided and trained other developers, and worked as a peer with other leads.

Experience with large legacy codebases: a port, a migration or a major rewrite that had to keep behaving like the original.

Solid client-server experience: networking, protocols, state synchronisation in an online game.

Platform integrations: in-app purchases and receipt validation, store submission, SDKs for login, push, crash reporting and analytics.

Hands-on profiling experience with Unity tools (Profiler, Memory Profiler, Frame Debugger) and platform tools (Xcode Instruments, Android Studio Profiler or similar).

A habit of automated testing and measurable results.

Comfortable taking ownership: you set the standard and don't wait to be told.

Highly proficient in English.

Nice to have skills ActionScript 3, Adobe AIR or Flash experience.

Experience with source-to-source translation, transpilers, Roslyn or code generation.

Experience building agentic tooling for engineering teams. This is a real differentiator for us.

Automated visual regression testing or replay-based testing.

Live mobile F2P and MMO strategy games specifically (live ops cadence, A/B testing, events).

Addressables at scale, URP, IL2CPP build pipelines, CI for Unity.

German language skills are a nice to have skills, but not mandatory.

Goodgame Studios is known for data-driven game development and successful live-service strategy titles. We self-publish our games in-house with all relevant disciplines under one roof. Our studio is based in Hamburg, Germany, and we welcome applications from international candidates. English is our working language. As part of Stillfront Group , one of Europe's leading game publishers with 200M+ registered players, an €1B+ in lifetime revenue, we work on long-term IP growth with a strong focus on execution.

About Goodgame Studios

Goodgame Studios is a leading free-to-play, online games development company active on web and mobile platforms. We self-publish our games in-house and have all relevant professions under one roof: Game Development, Quality Assurance, Community Management and Customer Support to Business Development, Marketing, Analytics and Central IT services. These departments work hand in hand to provide quality gaming experiences to over 200 million registered users worldwide.

Goodgame Studios is part of Stillfront. Stillfront is a global gaming company with 1200+ employees. We develop a wide range of digital games that attract over 40 million players each month. From well-established franchises like Supremacy, Big Farm, and BitLife to niche games, we span many different genres, including strategy, simulation, RPG and action, and casual and mash-up games.
