Technical Planning Analytics Analyst

Remote, United States

*This is a 6 month contract assignment

At Sony Interactive Entertainment (SIE), we are committed to redefining the future of Finance through innovation, transformation, and operational excellence.

This role sits within SIE’s Finance Transformation Office (FTO) and supports PARIS, a business-critical IBM Planning Analytics (TM1) platform used across the SBG Finance planning process. The Business Analyst will help ensure the platform is reliable, available, and appropriately configured throughout budget and forecast cycles.

Working across Finance and technical teams, this role will support users, troubleshoot system and data issues, contribute to requirements and testing, and help drive improvements in planning processes, automation, and data flows. The ideal candidate brings a strong understanding of FP A and financial planning systems, with TM1 experience preferred and other enterprise planning platforms also considered.

Role Responsibilities

Provide support to users of the IBM Planning Analytics finance planning tool throughout the planning cycle, including annual budgeting and quarterly forecasting. Responsibilities include:

Application administration

New user setup

Support for reports created using Planning Analytics for Excel

Resolution of ad-hoc user queries

Maintenance of system documentation

Delivery of end-user training

Partner with FP A and technical teams to identify and implement system improvements within IBM Planning Analytics in alignment with the departmental roadmap.

Work alongside the development team on key workstreams, including:

Requirements gathering

Test case preparation

Unit testing

Documentation

Training and user handover

Delivery of system upgrades and enhancements

Project manage and deliver system upgrades and improvements based on change requests, including testing code updates and roadmap changes as required.

Understand data flows and identify opportunities to make them more dependable and repeatable while strengthening integrations with other enterprise tools.

Continually improve reporting and analysis processes by automating or simplifying self-service support for customers.

Challenge the status quo and identify process improvement opportunities that drive innovation.

Perform other system-oriented duties as required by business needs.

Skills Experience

Strong understanding of finance principles, with previous experience supporting financial planning, budgeting, and forecasting processes and working with finance analysts.

Ability to interact effectively with users at all levels of seniority and manage stakeholder expectations across the organization.

4+ years of IBM Planning Analytics development experience (v2.0 or higher), including knowledge of:

Server installation and system configuration

Cube and application design

Planning Analytics TI and Chores

Rules and Feeders

Planning Analytics Security, including Element Security

MDX and SQL

Planning Analytics REST API, preferably through Cubewise Canvas

Hands-on experience with:

Python

TM1Py

HTML

JavaScript

Excellent verbal, written, and presentation skills.

Proficiency with Microsoft Excel.

Preferred Skills

Exposure to master data management solutions.

Experience with Cognos BI integration.

Knowledge of Lean/Six Sigma process improvement methodologies.

Educational or professional background in Computer Science, Finance, Economics, Statistics, or a related field.

Desire to continue developing technical knowledge and skill sets.
