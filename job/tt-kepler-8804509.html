Narrative Designer

Love anime and great stories? Shape the world of Orbitals as our Narrative Designer and Writer, from first idea to final line, including 2D anime sequences, in English and Japanese.

We’re looking for a narrative designer and writer with experience in Unreal Engine and a deep love of anime to shape the stories, characters and worlds in our games. In a studio our size, you’ll work on the narrative from the first idea to the final line: building the story structure and how players experience it, writing the words they read and hear, and bringing it to life in the game.

You’ll work very closely with our Creative Director, designers, artists, animators and engineers, and play a central role in one of the most distinctive parts of our project: 2D animated sequences, produced with a partner animation studio. You’ll help plan and deliver this work from script through to final animation, making sure it serves the story and sits naturally alongside the rest of the game. You’ll also lead the narrative structure of the game across 3D cutscenes and gameplay, so the story holds together however the player experiences it.

We see this as a Japanese-first project, even though most of our team is international and English-speaking. Japanese and English are the project’s two primary languages, so you’ll be writing for both audiences from the start. Being able to write and speak Japanese would be a huge advantage, but it isn’t a requirement.

This role suits an experienced narrative designer who enjoys both the big-picture design and the hands-on writing and implementation, and who wants a real say in the creative direction of our games.

Above all, we’re looking for an excellent writer who is also a dependable implementer. Most of your time will go into writing and getting narrative content into the game in Unreal Engine, not building or maintaining game systems and features. Because you’ll be working with a lot of autonomy, you’ll also need to be comfortable solving technical problems on your own.

If you love anime and the world of Orbitals, we want you to join our team! 🚀 What you’ll be doing Story, world and character Working with our Creative Director and Game Director, develop the story, worldbuilding, lore and characters for our games, in line with the creative vision Write and maintain the narrative bible, character profiles and story outlines so the whole team understands the world Shape character arcs, themes and tone, and keep the story consistent across the game, marketing and any wider IP Pitch and develop narrative ideas for new projects and features, from early concept through to production Narrative design and gameplay integration Design how players experience the story through our unique blend of gameplay and animated content, so that players feel like they are playing through an Orbitals anime experience Work with game designers to make sure story and gameplay support each other, and the narrative strengthens what the player is doing Script narrative content and implement it in-engine, and test it in the game Work with the QA and engineering teams to log and fix bugs as needed Work with artists, animators, audio and cinematics to plan cutscenes, in-game moments and storytelling through both our traditional 2D and Unreal Engine 3D pipelines Use playtests and player feedback to check that the story is clear and engaging, and revise it where needed Writing and narrative production Write a high-quality, polished narrative experience across all aspects of the game, including dialogue, cinematic scripts, UI text and tutorials Using your deep knowledge of anime, give characters distinct voices, and keep tone and style consistent across all writing and in line with the established Orbitals IP Prepare scripts and direction for voice recording, and take part in casting and recording sessions where needed, with a particular focus on Japanese and English, the project’s two primary languages Write with localisation in mind, and work with translation and localisation partners to protect meaning and tone in every language Keep narrative content, dialogue databases and scripts organised and ready for production Support and review
