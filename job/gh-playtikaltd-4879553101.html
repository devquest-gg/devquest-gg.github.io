Ambition Needs a Place to Grow.

At Playtika, we create mobile games enjoyed by millions worldwide.

Combining the scale of a public company (NASDAQ: PLTK) with the speed and ownership of a startup, we are a global leader where innovation, data, and creativity shape the future of entertainment.

Here, your ideas matter, your decisions impact products, and your growth is supported across global studios.

Bring your hunger where it belongs: at the front of the pack.

We’re looking for a motivated and ambitious Technical Artist to join us and help bridge animation, art, and development. This role focuses on integrating animations, assets, and VFX into Unity, ensuring they work smoothly and maintain high visual quality in-game.

You’ll work closely with animators, developers, and UX to bring content to life while balancing quality and performance.

Responsibilities:

Integrate animations and assets (art, VFX) into Unity, including states, timing, and transitions

Set up and maintain Unity components and particle systems

Collaborate with animators, developers, and UX to ensure accurate implementation

Troubleshoot visual and technical issues

Explore and apply new tools, techniques, and workflows

Improve pipelines through tools, scripts, and optimizations

Requirements:

4+ years of experience as a Tech Artist (or similar role)

Strong experience with Unity

Experience working on mobile games

Good understanding of animation pipelines and asset integration

Experience with Unity components and particle systems

Strong communication and problem-solving skills

High attention to details

Nice to have:

Background in animation (2D/3D)

Experience with Spine, VFX, or C#
