Why Sony Interactive Entertainment?

Sony Interactive Entertainment isn’t just the Best Place to Play — it’s also the Best Place to Work. Sony Interactive Entertainment (SIE) is the company behind the PlayStation brand. As a subsidiary of Sony Group Corporation, we’re part of a proud legacy of innovation and excellence. SIE is a dynamic technology company, delivering cutting-edge hardware and network services to more than 100 million people and an entertainment leader, home to some of the most beloved and recognizable intellectual properties (IP) in the world. Our role at SIE is to create and nurture the experiences under the PlayStation brand, a name synonymous with entertainment excellence and creativity.

Build the Cloud Foundation Behind Sony Interactive Entertainment

Every day, engineering teams across Sony Interactive Entertainment rely on the platforms we build to deliver secure, resilient, and scalable services. IT Cloud Foundations engineers the cloud capabilities that power enterprise business systems, developer platforms, and data services across the company.

Our customers are both engineers and the businesses they support. Every improvement we make to automation, reliability, security, and cloud infrastructure directly accelerates engineering teams while improving the reliability of critical business services.

The solutions we build become reusable capabilities that scale across the enterprise—not just for a single application or project.

Our mission isn t simply to manage cloud infrastructure—it s to engineer the secure, scalable cloud platform that enables Sony Interactive Entertainment to operate, innovate, and grow.

More Than Infrastructure

Some days you ll provision new AWS infrastructure, expand cloud networking, or enable new application environments. Other days you ll automate repetitive work, improve observability, or build reusable cloud capabilities. We believe every infrastructure request is an opportunity to improve the platform through automation, reusable engineering patterns, and thoughtful design.

The Challenges You ll Help Us Solve

Deliver secure, resilient AWS infrastructure for business-critical services.

What We re Looking For

We re looking for engineers who enjoy solving problems, are curious about how things work, and continually look for better ways to deliver secure, reliable cloud infrastructure. If you see automation opportunities where others see repetitive work and enjoy partnering across engineering teams, you ll fit right in.

Qualifications

Bachelor s degree or equivalent in Computer Science, Information Technology, Engineering, or related discipline.

Hands-on experience building and operating AWS infrastructure using IaC.

Experience with Terraform, Kubernetes, Python, CI/CD, and cloud networking.

Strong troubleshooting and automation skills.

AWS certification is a plus.

3+ years of relevant experience.

Required Experience

Experience building, deploying, and supporting AWS cloud infrastructure.

Hands-on IaC experience and Terraform.
