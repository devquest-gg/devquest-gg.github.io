Tax Expert

CD Projekt Red · Boston

Senior Other $145K–$200K Posted 2026-11-16 Full time

CD PROJEKT RED is looking for an experienced Tax Expert to play a key role in managing tax matters across our North American operations.

This is a crucial position that combines responsibility for complex tax matters and long-term tax planning with hands-on ownership of day-to-day tax compliance, oversight of tax reporting accuracy, documentation, and coordination of tax-related data and information. We are looking for a professional who can leverage their extensive experience and deep technical expertise to independently manage key tax matters, proactively identify and assess tax risks, recognize opportunities for tax optimization, and drive process improvements. A key part of this role involves providing well-founded recommendations and developing practical solutions that support informed business decisions. Success requires sound judgment, strong analytical skills, initiative, and a results-oriented mindset.

Working closely with colleagues in the United States, Canada, and Poland, the Tax Expert will serve as a key subject matter expert and trusted advisor on North American tax matters, ensuring that tax implications are thoroughly assessed and appropriately considered in important business decisions across the CD PROJEKT Group. This position offers a high degree of autonomy, the opportunity to drive improvements and build new processes, and the ability to combine strategic thinking and advanced technical expertise with hands-on execution in a dynamic, collaborative environment, without direct people management responsibilities.

Responsibilities Interpret and apply U.S. and Canadian tax laws and regulations, with a strong focus on income tax and R&D incentives, including analyzing legislative provisions, regulatory guidance, and tax authority interpretations to ensure their accurate and consistent application in practice.

Analyze contracts, agreements, and corporate documents to assess tax obligations and mitigate risks.

Prepare tax documentation, including supporting the preparation of transfer pricing documentation, responses to tax authority inquiries, materials for tax audits, and tax opinions and analyses.

Coordinate payroll tax compliance with support from the Employment & Payroll team, including researching and resolving inquiries related to withholding, employer taxes, and other local tax matters.

Independently lead tax projects, including setting timelines, coordinating with international teams and external tax partners, and ensuring timely and effective delivery of project objectives.

Monitor and advise on changes to tax laws and their implications for the business.

Prepare or review quarterly and annual income tax provision calculations, including current and deferred tax schedules, reconciliations, and supporting analyses, and provide tax reporting packages for consolidation with the parent company.

Work with external tax partners to prepare annual corporate tax returns across the U.S. and Canada, with the long-term goal of bringing these processes in-house.

Help optimize business structures and strategies from a tax perspective.

Identify and manage U.S. and Canadian R&D tax credits, game development incentives, grants, and other funding programs relevant to the company's activities.

Collaborate with and guide internal stakeholders across Finance, Legal, HR, Accounting, and Game Production to address tax-related matters.

Continuously seek ways to improve tax-related processes, documentation practices, and cross-team workflows.

Work in conjunction with internal tax teams to ensure compliance across cross-border transactions within the CD PROJEKT Group.

Own the implementation and management of tax processes within the D365 ERP system, including setup, reporting, and controls that improve the accuracy and availability of information for tax filings and reporting.

At least 7 years of professional experience in tax or finance, with experience in grant advisory services and incentive programs considered an asset.

Deep expertise in income tax regulations, particularly in relation to tax credits and innovation grants.

Strong analytical and research skills, with the ability to independently assess complex tax scenarios.

Solid understanding of accounting principles and the ability to navigate financial records.

Proven ability to communicate clearly, persuasively, and respectfully across teams and cultures.

Experience managing projects from planning through execution.

Comfortable balancing day-to-day execution with strategic thinking.

Self-starter who thrives in a cross-functional, international environment.
