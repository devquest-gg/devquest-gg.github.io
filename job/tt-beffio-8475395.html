This is a senior individual contributor role with significant R&D and technical ownership , taking ideas from early prototypes through production-ready systems that can be used and maintained by the wider team.

Key Responsibilities Develop and optimize animation runtime systems in Unity.

Work with state machines, blend trees, layers, masks, root motion, animation events, and compression.

Develop or integrate data-oriented animation solutions using ECS/DOTS, Jobs, Burst, and baking workflows.

Explore GPU skinning, deformation, compute shaders, vertex shaders, and vertex animation techniques.

Build Unity editor tools, validators, asset processors, and production workflows for animation teams.

Support rigging, skeleton, skinning, and FBX workflows between Maya or Blender and Unity.

Profile and optimize animation systems across mobile hardware.

Collaborate closely with Animators, Technical Artists, Gameplay Engineers, and Rendering Engineers.

Take technical R&D from prototype to production-ready feature.

Establish clean documentation, workflows, and technical standards for the wider team.

Requirements Strong C# and Unity engineering experience.

Hands-on experience with ECS/DOTS , including Entities, Burst, Jobs, and baking, or deep equivalent data-oriented engine experience.

Strong understanding of animation runtimes including state machines, blend trees, layers, masks, root motion, events, and animation compression.

Experience with GPU skinning and deformation using structured or constant buffers, compute shaders, vertex shaders, or similar techniques.

Shader development experience in HLSL and a node-based tool such as Shader Graph or Amplify.

Practical knowledge of character rigs, skeletons, skinning, and FBX pipelines in Maya, Blender, or similar tools.

Experience creating Unity editor tools such as asset postprocessors, validators, custom inspectors, or editor windows.

Experience profiling and optimizing mobile graphics workloads, particularly on Adreno and Mali hardware.

Familiarity with GLES and Vulkan.

Strong communication skills and the ability to collaborate with both artists and engineers.

Fluent English.

Nice to Have Experience with DOTS animation frameworks such as Rukhanka, Latios Kinemation , or similar technology.

Experience building or extending a custom animation runtime.

Networked animation involving prediction and rollback.

Netcode for Entities or similar multiplayer technology.

About Beffio

Beffio Studios is a co-development art studio with a deep passion for the entertainment and game development industries. Established in 2013 our studio has grown to become a trusted partner for some of the world’s most renowned game developers and entertainment publishers.

At Beffio, we excel in delivering top-tier game art production services, offering a full spectrum of support from initial concept art to final implementation. Our expertise covers both realistic and stylized 3D art, art direction, technical art, and real-time VFX, ensuring that every project we undertake meets the highest industry standards. Our team is proficient in a wide range of tools and software, including Unreal Engine, Unity3D, Nvidia Omniverse, Houdini, and the Adobe Suite, allowing us to seamlessly integrate artistic vision with cutting-edge technology.

Over the years, Beffio has been recognized for its innovative approach and exceptional quality, highlighted by our Unity Award for Best Artistic Content in 2018. We have collaborated with industry giants like Nike, Square Enix, and MIT, contributing to high-profile projects that showcase our ability to deliver AAA-quality content.

Beffio Studios is committed to pushing the boundaries of what is possible in game development, creating immersive, visually stunning experiences that captivate audiences and set new benchmarks for excellence in the industry.
