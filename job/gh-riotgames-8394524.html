Director, Insights - Central Product Insights

Riot Games · Los Angeles

Director+ Data & Analytics $225K–$338K Posted 2026-11-18 Full time
Apply on Riot Games’s site →

The Insights discipline helps empower Rioters decision-making to be more data-informed. We re an interdisciplinary group of scientists, consultants, and strategists from all sorts of backgrounds who join together to represent and advocate for gamers around the world. We believe that illuminating the globally diverse experiences, behaviors, and needs of gamers is critical to Riot living up to our player-focused mission. We re a group united by our love of games, science, and helping others succeed.

As a Director, Insights on the Central Product Insights Team (CPI) , you’ll be responsible for overseeing a team of researchers, analysts, and data scientists that drive scaled impact across Riot’s games portfolio. You’ll lead studies on foundational product challenges and opportunity areas for live games, guide the team’s development and socialization of central insights tools services, and act as a strategic advisor to Product Insights Leadership as a whole. You will manage workstreams that help optimize decision-making at the portfolio level, facilitating strategic collaboration between games. You’ll also deeply study the overall games market to inform our competitive strategy and portfolio approach. In doing so, you will need to partner tightly with Game insights leadership, central product leadership, and product decision-makers on Games. Your depth of understanding across insights modalities, team leadership experience, and expert knowledge of the games industry will ensure that you’re an effective leader able to achieve CPI’s mission of advancing player and product understanding at scale. This role reports to the Sr. Director of Product Insights.

Responsibilities:

Oversee the work priorities, vision, and culture of the Central Product Insights team, ensuring the team is meeting the needs of Games, Product Executives, and Product Insights Leadership with a high degree of sophistication, craft execution, and stakeholder understanding.

Manage the design, development, and socialization of CPI’s suite of scaled analytics, research, data science insights products, covering player behaviors, sentiment, and game-related predictions across Riot’s portfolio (e.g., central insights dashboards, trackers, models, alert systems, panels, etc.).

Lead foundational and exploratory studies on common cross-product challenges and opportunities, driving impact through strategic recommendations and follow-through with stakeholders that turns insights into action.

Partner closely with Product Insights Leaders and Product Executives to understand their needs, advise on the highest-opportunity targets for central insights work, align on the scope and goals of CPI’s involvement, and ensure follow-through.

Drive work results efficiently and effectively (both through individual contribution and through your team), collaborating with other Insighters across Riot where appropriate.

Guide partnerships with central Data Engineering, Insights Operations, BizHealth, and other central Insights teams, chartering and managing shared workstreams where necessary appropriate to address Riot-wide Insights or Product needs that reach beyond the scope of CPI.

Handle formal management, workforce planning, staffing, and hiring for Insighters on Central Product Insights.

Lead, mentor, and support career growth for Insighters on Central Product Insights, developing Riot’s talent base.

Required Qualifications:

12+ years of professional work experience in data analytics, research, business strategy, management consulting, data science, product management, or related experience (an advanced degree can account for up to 2 years of experience)

Working knowledge across all Insights crafts – analytics, research, and data science – with 5+ years specializing in at least one

4+ years of experience managing individuals and teams

3+ years of experience working in game development, with knowledge of development practices and practical considerations involved in making and publishing games

Desired Qualifications:

Experience supporting live service multiplayer games

Experience managing complex Insights workstreams involving coordination across many distinct stakeholder groups

Experience managing managers

Experience building, developing, and socializing scaled Insights products, tools, or services for cross-functional stakeholders

Experience building strong, collaborative relationships with both peers and senior stakeholders

Experience influencing senior leadership with strong executive communication skills

Experience with (or demonstrated understanding of) games industry trends, the competitive games landscape, and global gamer audiences

Experience with cross-game portfolio insights, portfolio strategy, or portfolio management

For this role, you ll find success through craft expertise and a collaborative spirit that prioritizes the delight of players. We will look at your past projects and experience, but for this role, we also look for dedicated people with a personal relationship with games. If you embody player empathy and care about players experiences, this is the role for you!
