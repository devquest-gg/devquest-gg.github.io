Overseas Influencer/KOL Operations Intern (Indonesia)

NetEase Games

Entry Marketing Hybrid Posted 2026-04-30
Apply on NetEase Games’s site →

Responsibilities:

Be familiar with major overseas social media platforms (e.g., YouTube, Instagram, TikTok), identify and source suitable influencers based on campaign requirements, evaluate accounts based on data and content quality, and proactively reach out to establish collaborations;

Assist in end-to-end KOL marketing campaigns, including script ideation, market research, video translation/transcription, and post-campaign analysis;

Maintain relationships with influencer channels, support pricing negotiations, and assist in exploring deeper strategic partnerships;

Deliver assigned tasks on time with high quality.

Requirements:

Familiar with various social media platforms, especially international ones;

Strong communication and collaboration skills, with a proactive attitude toward tasks and responsiveness;

Proficient language skills at a working level (to be assessed during interviews);

Available to work at least 4 days per week, with a minimum internship duration of 4 months;

Currently pursuing or holding a Bachelor’s degree or above; candidates with overseas study experience and strong understanding of local culture are preferred.
