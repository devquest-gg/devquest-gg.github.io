About Atari

Founded in 1972, Atari is one of the world’s most iconic consumer brands and a pioneer in the video game industry, recognized worldwide for its multi-platform games and licensed products. Atari owns and/or manages a portfolio of more than 400 games and franchises, including globally recognized brands such as Asteroids®, Centipede®, Missile Command®, Pong®, and RollerCoaster Tycoon®.

The Atari family of brands includes Digital Eclipse, Nightdive Studios, Infogrames, AtariAge, MobyGames, as well as Coatsink, Early Morning Studios, and Stormteller Games—spanning game development, publishing, and community experiences worldwide. Today, Atari Inc. continues to build on its legacy by developing games, hardware, and experiences that honor the past while driving innovation for the future.

Over the past two years, we ve been building Atari India, a growing team that plays a critical role in supporting our global operations. We re proud of the team we ve assembled so far, and we’re just getting started. As part of a lean, high-impact organization, the team in India works closely with colleagues in North America and Europe on projects that move the company forward. Whether you re helping launch a new game, keeping our infrastructure secure, or supporting day-to-day operations, your work here matters. Join us as we continue to grow Atari India and build the future of a legendary brand.

Position: Game Producer Experience: 3–6 years Location: Remote Employment Type: Contractual(3 months) Work Timings: 9AM - 6PM IST

Overview

We re reimagining Atari s classic arcade catalog as short-session games for Reddit, MSN and emerging channels. We need a Game Producer to own the pipeline end to end, shaping game strategy, turning concepts into shipped live titles, and keeping developers, designers, QA and external studios moving in sync.

What You ll Do

Own each title across its full lifecycle: strategy, concept, production, testing, launch and live-ops

Shape game strategy and pitch new concepts and IP for the roster

Coordinate internal developers/designers and external studios, unblocking the team to hit timelines

Run agile/scrum planning: sprint schedules, standups and roadmap tracking across multiple titles at once

Own and keep the game tracker/dashboard current: status, ownership, timelines

Manage platform partner relationships (Reddit, MSN): format, submission and certification

Manage external studio and vendor relationships, and drive IP intake and rights clearance with legal/licensing

Own KPIs (games live, revenue/daily player, engagement) and drive initiatives to improve them; report weekly

Manage risk across game timelines: flag slippage early and drive mitigation and release management

Scout new IP and channels (Discord, YouTube Playables, Telegram) for expansion

Qualifications

Required

3+ years as a game producer/associate producer (casual, mobile or browser games)

Shipped games end to end, managing both internal and external/outsourced teams

Working knowledge of Unity and HTML5 pipelines, and staying current with platform game technology

Hands-on experience with agile/scrum planning and sprint scheduling in a live production pipeline

Strong PM and communication skills; comfortable juggling multiple titles at once

Experience with platform partner launch/certification requirements

Experience managing external studio/vendor relationships and timeline risk

Preferred

Passion for retro/arcade gaming and the Atari IP library

Comfort using data (dashboards, revenue-per-player) to make ship/stop calls
