Job Overview

Magic: The Gathering, the world’s first trading card game, is hiring a Commissioning Art Director for our Universes Beyond product line. In this role, you will commission and art direct illustrations from freelance artists, working with external partner brands to deliver card art that fits both the partner’s visual identity and Magic’s quality standards.

You are an experienced visual creative with a background in fantasy art and an interest in pop culture. You want to help grow an established brand’s audience and visual expression.

Location and Work Arrangement

This role is based at our Renton, Washington office and follows a hybrid schedule designed to support in-person collaboration. Employees are expected to work onsite on Tuesdays, Wednesdays, and Thursdays, with the flexibility to work remotely or onsite on Mondays and Fridays.

What You ll Do

Commission a high volume of artwork by identifying freelance illustrators for each piece and providing continuous art direction on individual pieces. Maintain strong relationships with freelance illustrators and provide regular updates to the card concept producer on art status

Research and understand partner brand IP, translate reference material into customized style guides, and collaborate with external partners to art direct to their brand specifications

Review print-ready files to ensure final card quality

What You ll Bring

2+ yrs commercial art experience.

Strong communication and organizational skills, attention to detail, and comfortable giving and receiving feedback

A portfolio demonstrating strong creative vision with a cinematic lens

A passion for pop culture and a pulse on current entertainment industry trends

Understanding of Magic as both a game and a brand

Deep knowledge of current fantasy illustration market/talent and modern print/press techniques and processes. Interest in discovering diverse, emerging artists

Skilled in Adobe Photoshop and proficient in Microsoft Office (Word, Excel, PowerPoint, Outlook)

Notice Regarding Hasbro’s use of AI in the Recruitment Process

We use Greenhouse’s AI-powered Talent Matching tool to compare your application against our job requirements.

How it works: Talent Matching compares information from your resume and application responses with job-related criteria established and weighted by Hasbro’s recruiting and hiring teams. Depending on the role, these criteria may include relevant skills, work experience, prior job titles, and industry experience. The tool then assigns a match category to help organize applications for review.

Human oversight: Talent Matching supports — but does not replace — human review. It does not automatically advance or reject candidates or make employment decisions. Hasbro’s recruiters and hiring managers review applications and remain responsible for all hiring decisions.
