Senior Technical UI Designer

2K · San Mateo

Senior Design Posted 2026-11-14 Full time

Who we are: We are a diverse team of developers driven by a passion for our art, united by our core values and inspired by a culture of inclusivity to build amazing games that thrill players everywhere . We pursue growth and innovation in an environment of safety and trust . Our culture is built on the belief that the more varied voices in our collective will strengthen our team and our games . We are looking for our next teammate who wi ll raise our bar and make us better .

What We Need: We’re seeking a Senior UI Technical Designer with exceptional technical fluency, creative vision, and system-level thinking to own and evol ve our in-game UI systems at scale. You’ll own and drive the implementation of key UI features, working closely with designers, artists, producers, engineers and more. We’re a studio that values autonomy and self-drive in developers who are empowered to do the best work of their careers. If this is you, let’s talk!

Responsibilities:

System Implementation Maintenance: Champion the design, iteration, and maintenance of various UI systems. This includes dynamic widgets, responsive menus, and high-fidelity animations using Unreal Engine, Blueprint visual scripting, and AngelScript at an expert level.

Architecture Best Practices: Establish, document, and promote UI Blueprint architecture best practices and workflows, driving optimization strategies to ensure consistency, scalability, and performance across all UI systems for multiple platforms.

Tool Technology Integration: Evaluate and integrate emerging tools and technologies to enhance Experience Design (XD) across gameplay and other interactive elements.

Design to Engine Translation: Translate and improve UX designs and UI art into performant in-engine implementations while ensuring seamless integration of UI visuals and behavior with underlying game systems.

UI Material Workflows: Establish and reinforce UI Material workflows for widgets, creating scalable, clear, and performant UI Materials.

Cross-Functional Collaboration: Partner closely with UX designers, UI artists, gameplay engineers, backend developers, and audio teams to align UI implementation with gameplay vision and technical feasibility.

Foster Development Culture: Champion a collaborative development culture across the Experience Design department (UX, UI, UITD, and UI Engineering) by leading by example and sharing technical insights.

Peer Mentorship: Act as a technical mentor to junior and intermediate technical designers and cross-functional peers, elevating the team s technical capabilities and standard of execution.

Required Qualifications:

Engine Expertise: Extensive experience building, debugging, and optimizing UI systems in UE4 or UE5 using Blueprints and UMG. Familiarity with UI materials, animation systems, and widget architecture is essential.

Problem Solving: Ability to analyze and solve sophisticated technical and design problems while collaborating closely with engineers to contribute to the technical roadmap for XD features.

System Architecture: Deep understanding of data flow, modularity, and performance in UI systems.

Autonomy: Demonstrated ability to lead initiatives independently, drive decision-making, and take full ownership of complex UI systems.

Adaptability: Comfortable working in fast-paced environments and evolving pipelines. Eager to prototype, iterate, and respond constructively to feedback.

Relevant Experience:

Prior experience shipping high-quality UI in a AAA game environment is highly desirable.

Solid grasp of scripting and programming fundamentals such as C++ or C#.

A proven track record as a senior individual contributor within a UI tech design team, taking point on complex, systemic UI challenges.
