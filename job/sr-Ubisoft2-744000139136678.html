Special Effects Artist

As a Special Effects Artist at Ubisoft Montreal, you will create and integrate high-quality special effects, in keeping with the creative direction, technological constraints and production deadlines.&#xa0;&#xa0; What you'll do (Tasks): Understand the game design documents and artistic direction to comprehend the creative direction and have an overall idea of the sign off features;&#xa0; Find reference images and videos of the intended effects and validate these references with the artistic director;&#xa0; See with the graphic design TD if the intended special effects can be created in the engine and suggest alternatives or development options (ex: tools) whenever necessary;&#xa0;&#xa0; Collaborate with the DT to adapt the pipeline to ensure that it fosters the production of the special effects and determine memory and performance budgets;&#xa0; Work in collaboration with programmers to develop adapted tools to meet special effects requirements;&#xa0; Use available tools (particle systems, modelling, texturing, etc.) to create the special effects requested by the artistic team and integrate these effects into the engine;&#xa0; Validate the quality of the effects once they are imported into the game and make any necessary changes in an effort to maximize the user experience;&#xa0; Optimize the special effects on an ongoing basis and account for modifications and technological constraints throughout the project to ensure that the special effects remain of high quality;&#xa0; Foster game accessibility by adapting the various special effects based on comments from the game design team before, during and after the creation of the special effects;&#xa0; Fix the bugs entered by testers and other working units;&#xa0; Communicate with other working units to help members integrate special effects by providing reference documents and other types of support;&#xa0; Carry out all other related tasks.&#xa0; What you bring to the team (Skills and Knowledge): Attention to detail;&#xa0; Sense of initiative and proactivity;&#xa0; Ability to accept feedback and adapt to change;&#xa0; Ability to work under pressure;&#xa0; Originality and resourcefulness in problem-solving;&#xa0; Knowledge and interest in programming languages an asset (e.g. Max Script);&#xa0; Knowledge of game editors (e.g. Unreal);&#xa0; Knowledge of compositionning software (e.g. After Effects, Illustrator, Houdini, Toxic, etc.).&#xa0; Training: College diploma in a video game-related field, bachelor’s degree in special effects/film or any other relevant training.&#xa0; Relevant Experience: Minimum 5&#xa0;years’ experience in creating special effects for video games, including the design, integration, and optimization of real-time effects within a game engine.
