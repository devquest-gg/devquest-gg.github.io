Staff Partner Relations Manager The opportunity Unity is looking for a Staff Partner Relations Manager to join our Industry Customer Success team, working with our highest-value enterprise customers across automotive, manufacturing, and other industrial sectors. These organizations use Unity to design, simulate, visualize, and collaborate in real-time 3D — from product configurators and immersive training to large-scale digital twins — and rely on us to turn that technology into measurable business outcomes.

This is our most senior individual-contributor role in the Partner Relations team: a strategic business partner to customers and a leader-without-title inside Unity. You ll own engagement, adoption, and expansion across a portfolio of top enterprise accounts, act as a senior advisor to Sales, Product, and Engineering leadership, and translate what you hear from customers into recommendations that shape Unity s Industry strategy. Beyond your accounts, you ll lead cross-functional initiatives, mentor other PRMs, and help define how the role itself evolves. It s a great opportunity for someone who wants genuine ownership of strategic outcomes — commercial, product, and organizational — at a pivotal moment for Unity in Industry.
