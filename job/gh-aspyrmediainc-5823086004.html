**Please note that all positions posted for Aspyr Media, Inc. are in-person/in-office only; remote positions are not available.**

Aspyr is a leading game publisher and developer, bringing the world s biggest franchises and bold new projects to players everywhere.

Since 1996, we’ve published over 200 games and our team is dedicated to bringing beloved franchises like Tomb Raider and Star Wars to new and familiar audiences across all platforms.

We focus on collaboration and creativity, crafting high-quality, engaging games that leave a lasting impact on players worldwide.

Responsibilities:

The Contractor - Senior Designer will work on specific projects to function as a design authority by reviewing design decisions, shaping design direction, and ensuring that the projects stay true to the original games’ identity. This person will work closely with Product Management, Engineering, Art, external partners, and IP holders to help shape requirements, validate design choices, and influence the evolution of gameplay systems across genres and platforms.

Review and influence design decisions for assigned projects, ensuring coherence of vision, mechanics, and player experience

Deconstruct legacy mechanics and partner with Product Management to determine what must be preserved for authenticity and what should evolve for modern audiences

Provide analysis and insight into core loops, progression, pacing, and player motivation to ensure each game feels intentional and robust

Serve as a creative advisor during development reviews, build critiques, and milestone evaluations with internal teams and external partners

Evaluate gameplay with a focus on:

Responsiveness and camera feel

Character control and feedback fidelity

Consistency of pacing, difficulty curve, and engagement

Deliver clear, objective, and actionable feedback that improves overall player experience while honoring original design intent

Partner with the Product Management team to help write, review, and refine Product Requirement Documents (PRDs) to clearly capture:

Gameplay goals and measurable success criteria

System requirements and dependencies

How original mechanics are adapted or modernized

Conformance with IP holder direction and quality standards

Validate that requirements are actionable, feasible, and aligned with design vision before implementation begins

Help interpret, communicate, and defend design intent to partners while being responsive to technical needs and feedback

Work with IP holders to ensure creative and mechanical decisions honor their direction and quality expectations

Assess how narrative beats and progression are supported by gameplay systems

Help teams integrate environmental storytelling and pacing that reinforces engagement and clarity

Standardize practices for design documentation, tuning, and feedback loops

Preferred Qualifications:

7+ years of professional game design experience

At least 2 shipped titles in Senior, Lead, or Principal design roles

Deep understanding of game systems and player experience fundamentals across genres

Strong analytical skills with the ability to translate experiential qualities into actionable improvement plans

Comfortable using spreadsheets (Excel / Sheets) for balance, progression, and system modeling

Familiarity with engine tools (Unreal and/or proprietary) sufficient to navigate builds, understand technical constraints, and communicate effectively with engineering

Experience on remasters, remakes, or major ports of legacy IP and collaborating directly with IP holders of licensed properties

Background in narrative design, UX/UI, or player psychology

Prior experience shaping or reviewing PRDs, feature specs, partner deliverables, or partner QA processes

Experience in cross-project design leadership within a creative organization
