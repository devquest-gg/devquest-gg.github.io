Jam City is on the hunt for a Lead Product Manager to support Cookie Jam, one of Jam City’s flagship titles. You will manage a F2P game and use that data to inform strategic roadmap decisions in the pursuit of delivering amazing player experiences and driving business outcomes.

As a Lead Product Manager you'll have a direct impact on the success of Cookie Jam.

You will make decisions and understand our “Game as a Business Strategy”, looking to achieve great player experiences while maximizing ROI.

You will help us to define, set up and maintain in-game offers, sales, promotional events, along with new content and features to drive KPI goals.

The ideal candidate is passionate about casual and puzzle games games, can pull, analyze, and report data from the game and use this to inform effective live ops decisions and product prioritization. They have the agility to manage projects on their own and understand how they work in a fast evolving, data driven company. They have the people skills to work smoothly and communicate effectively with Producers, Designers, and Engineers.
