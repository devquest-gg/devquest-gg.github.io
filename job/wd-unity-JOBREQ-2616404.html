The opportunity Unity’s Accounting team is looking for a highly skilled Senior Accountant to be an integral part of our dynamic, global finance organization. In this role, you will serve as the crucial gatekeeper for our Indian entity s financial compliance, bridging our global US GAAP ledger with local regulatory requirements. You will step into a tech-forward, high-impact environment where you will own end-to-end Ind AS statutory reporting, drive process engineering, and leverage modern technology to streamline GAAP-to-GAAP adjustments. If you are a problem-solver who thrives at the intersection of local accounting compliance, global collaboration, and AI-driven automation, you will find an exciting home with our team.

You will have an immediate impact by tightening our local compliance footprint and ensuring our Indian statutory reporting is bulletproof. Working closely under the guidance of your Singapore-based manager, you will serve as the localized expert who reviews global transactions, flags compliance issues early, and collaborates regionally on how to resolve them.

At Unity, you’ll make a difference. In a collaborative, fast paced environment, we’re redefining development, solving hard problems, and enabling the success of our community. Inclusion at Unity is driven by one overarching framework: Empathy, Respect, and Opportunity.

What you ll be doing India Statutory Reporting: Drive end-to-end statutory financial reporting for the India entity in strict compliance with Ind AS and Schedule III of the Companies Act.

GAAP Review & Alignment: On a regular basis, thoroughly review global US GAAP transactions posted by other teams to understand their nature, and calculate, prepare, and post the necessary adjustments to align the financial numbers with Ind AS for local statutory purposes.

Direct Manager Collaboration: Proactively identify and discuss any compliance issues, structural variances, or transaction anomalies with your direct manager, collaboratively developing practical solutions to resolve them.

Tech-Driven Problem Solving: Actively identify variances between US GAAP and Ind AS, trace them to their root cause, and resolve them by reviewing operational processes, system enhancement, and utilizing AI tools or automation to track and reconcile cross-border data.

What we re looking for Ind AS & Schedule III Financial Preparation: Proven ability to independently prepare a complete set of financial statements (Balance Sheet, P&L, Cash Flow, and Notes) in strict compliance with Ind AS and Schedule III of the Indian Companies Act, 2013.

US GAAP to Ind AS Reconciliation: Technical capability to analyze transactions recorded under US GAAP, map the ledger differences, and post accurate Ind AS adjustment entries.

CARO & Statutory Audit Management: Experience drafting audit schedules and directly addressing queries for Companies Auditor’s Report Order (CARO) clauses, including fixed assets and related-party transaction testing.
