Lead 2D/3D Artist

Nordcurrent · Vilnius

Lead Art Posted 2024-06-25 Full time
Apply on Nordcurrent’s site →

Our Vilnius office is looking for a talented and experienced Lead 2D/3D Artist with a strong interest in casual mobile games. If you are a professional eager to grow and develop within a team of experts, we want to hear from you!

Your Key Responsibilities:

Establish, maintain, and develop the clear artistic vision and integrity for the game’s visual elements, both 2D and 3D;

Envision and create various aspects of the game, including art style, characters, and environments;

Lead the art team for assigned projects, including task assignment, strategic feedback, motivation, and mentoring of team members;

Manage art content, including planning, production, and delivery;

Ensure quality control by reviewing art content, updating pipelines, and creating guides and tutorials;

Collaborate closely with the project team, including game design and development departments.

A Successful Candidate Must Have:

2+ years of leadership experience in the game industry in positions such as Lead 2D/3D Artist, Lead Artist, Art Manager, or other relevant roles;

Understanding of key 2D and 3D art production specifics;

Basic or better understanding of animation;

Basic understanding of Unity engine;

Strong team management experience and communication skills;

Proficiency in Blender and Substance Designer/Painter;

Understanding of principles and pipelines for developing graphics for mobile games;

Ability to work in a casual style with a good understanding of the mobile games market;

High-quality portfolio demonstrating excellent artistic skills;

Upper-Intermediate English proficiency or higher.

Extra Great to Have:

Familiarity with Marvelous Designer, or similar software;

Formal art education.

Please note: applications without samples of work will not be considered.

Salary:  From 4400 EUR/month (gross)
