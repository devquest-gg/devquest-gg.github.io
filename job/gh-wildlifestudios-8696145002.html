Product Marketing Manager (PMM)

Wildlife Studios · São Paulo

Mid Marketing Hybrid Posted 2026-08-11 Full time
Apply on Wildlife Studios’s site →

Are you ready to shape the future of mobile gaming? We re looking for a Product Marketing Manager (PMM) to join our User Acquisition / Marketing team at WildLife Studios.

User Acquisition (UA) is about bringing players into our worlds — and doing it smarter every day. We are highly analytical growth hackers who use data, code, and creativity to find and engage the right players across global platforms like Meta, Google, and TikTok. If you’re analytical, tech-driven, and excited by fast feedback loops, this is where gaming and growth collide.

The right person for this position is curious by nature, very proactive, has high judgement to prioritize and solve problems, and can thrive in a fast and growing business.

What you ll do

Strategic Analyses : Monitor, analyze, and optimize new user acquisition campaigns to maximize profit.

Creative Testing : Designing A/B tests and finding innovative ways to engage audiences.

Project Prioritization : Using high judgment to solve problems and drive impact in a fast-paced environment.

Cross-functional teamwork : Drive alignment with key stakeholders on user acquisition performance.

What you ll need

Bachelor’s degree in Engineering, Computer Science, Business, or equivalent practical experience.

Strong analytical, organizational, and communication skills.

Proficiency in programming, preferably in SQL, R or Python is a plus.

Nice to have

Familiarity with the mobile gaming industry landscape.

Previous experience in user acquisition, performance marketing, banking, or consulting roles is a plus.

Familiarity with A/B testing, multivariate testing, and other methodologies for evaluating creative performance.

More about you

Proactive and autonomous; you drive initiatives without waiting for instructions but ensuring necessary visibility and buy in from key stakeholders.

Exceptional communicator with a knack for influencing others.

Thrives in high-demand environments and excels at prioritization.

Experienced in managing complex projects with precision and clarity.

About Wildlife

Wildlife is one of the leading mobile game developers and publishers in the world. We have released more than 60 titles, reaching billions of people around the globe. Today, we have offices in Brazil, Argentina, Ireland, and the United States. Here, we create games that will excite, intrigue, and engage our players for years to come!
