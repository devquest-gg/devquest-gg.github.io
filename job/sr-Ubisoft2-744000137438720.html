Group Manager, Creative Project Management

Ubisoft (HQ) · Paris

Senior Production Posted 2026-07-13 Full time

As a Senior Manager, Creative Project Management at Ubisoft, you will lead and support a team of Creative Project Managers within the Campaign Content Hub. You will oversee the day-to-day execution of creative asset production, ensuring briefs are translated into high-quality deliverables through effective coordination with internal creative teams and external agencies.

In this role, you will focus on production execution rather than campaign strategy or governance. You will help ensure projects move efficiently from briefing through delivery by aligning resources, maintaining production standards, and supporting smooth collaboration across teams.

You will work closely with Campaign Managers, who oversee campaign planning and stakeholder alignment, while your team manages the operational delivery of creative assets across multiple channels and formats.

What you’ll do Lead, support, and develop a team of Creative Project Managers through regular feedback, coaching, and performance guidance Coordinate workload distribution across the CPM team while maintaining flexibility across campaign priorities Establish consistent production workflows, briefing practices, tracking standards, and agency collaboration processes Support the resolution of production challenges related to timelines, resources, or unclear briefs Oversee the execution of creative asset production across static, video, CRM, paid media, D2P, and localized content formats Monitor production progress using shared workflow and delivery management tools to maintain accurate project visibility Collaborate with internal creative teams and external agencies to ensure timely and high-quality asset delivery Maintain quality standards by reviewing deliverables for brand consistency, technical compliance, and alignment with approved creative direction Integrate quality control processes, including localization and compliance reviews, into production workflows Contribute to continuous improvement initiatives by identifying recurring production issues and helping optimize processes and templates What you bring to the team Experience in creative project management, integrated production, or creative operations within a collaborative environment Experience leading or mentoring project management teams Knowledge of creative production workflows across digital, video, print, and marketing asset production Familiarity with project management and workflow tools such as JIRA, Workfront, Airtable, Monday.com, Asana, or similar platforms Ability to manage multiple productions simultaneously while maintaining attention to detail and delivery quality Strong communication skills with the ability to translate creative feedback into actionable production guidance Experience collaborating with internal stakeholders, creative teams, and external agencies
