THE MISSION OF THIS ROLE

This is a hands-on engineering role: you design and implement solutions yourself, working side by side with Infrastructure, DevOps, and development teams. Leadership experience is not required here and not expected.

KEY OBJECTIVES

- Strengthen cloud and infrastructure security in practice: secrets management, audit logging and controlled production access.

- Close high-impact risks: start from an honest assessment of the current state and build a consistent picture of gaps and risks as you go.

- Work secure-by-default: wherever possible, remove the security burden from teams through automation and platform-level solutions rather than rules and manual procedures.

- Roll up your sleeves: dive into what Infrastructure, DevOps, and development teams are building right now, understand it from the inside, and make security part of it.

JOB REQUIREMENTS

- Real experience building platform and infrastructure security — you have designed and implemented security solutions yourself: not audited or monitored them, and you can walk us through the architecture, the decisions, and what you'd do differently.

- Cloud security as your core strength. Recent hands-on experience securing cloud environments:

- A strong infrastructure background. You may have grown entirely within security engineering or moved into security from DevOps, SRE, system administration, or network engineering. What matters is that your thinking is an engineer's thinking: architecture first, building over prescribing.

- The ability to explain risks with facts. You have real cases where you make technical teams see why a risk matters and why a change is needed through evidence, architecture, and concrete scenarios.

AppSec, SIEM, and SOC experience are useful but secondary.

NICE TO HAVE

- Infrastructure-as-Code in practice.

- Container and Kubernetes security experience.

- Scripting and automation as a habit: Python, Go, or Bash — you'd rather automate a control than write a procedure for humans to follow.

- GCP specifically. Our stack is GCP; deep experience with it will shorten your path to impact, though strong AWS/Azure experience translates well.

- Experience with securing AI tool usage.

WHO THIS ROLE IS NOT FOR

We believe in being upfront about fit. This role is probably not for you if:

- Your current focus is on monitoring, alert triage and incident analysis.

- Your specialization is threat research, malware analysis, cryptography, or lab-style cybersecurity.

- Your recent work is mostly paper security: policies, compliance documents, and audits.

- You are looking for a management track or ownership of a security function right now — strategy and team building belong to the security leadership role we're hiring separately.
