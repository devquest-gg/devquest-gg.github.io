Role overview

The role will initially require someone who is willing to be heavily involved with embedding new systems and processes, who is practical, pragmatic and commercially minded and who can work closely with finance colleagues and manage external advisors. The role will cover all taxes except payroll taxes globally and would suit someone who enjoys variety. The group has operations in circa 10 countries with 3 significant territories. Revenue is in excess of $1bn and as a result of significant international expansion the group will fall into both CbCR and Pillar 2 in the coming years.

Key Responsibilities

Supporting 3rd party advisors with the preparation and filing of the Group’s direct and indirect tax returns

Maintaining the annual tax compliance calendar

Ensuring all payments are made in a timely manner

Running all tax audits and responding to tax authority queries with the support of 3rd party advisors

Maintaining the integrity of the tax accounts and provisions in the financial statements and group accounts and managing the year end statutory audit for tax

Identifying and understanding changing global tax legislation and implementing this for the Group

Working with the Interim Head of Tax to implement the Transfer Pricing policy, Pillar 2 requirements and Country by Country reporting

Working with the Interim Head of tax on global advisory projects and tax saving initiatives

Embedding and maintaining a suitable tax governance framework

Improving existing processes and embedding new processes where required to ensure accurate tax accounting and reporting

Supporting new system implementations to ensure tax requirements are appropriately identified and embedded

Required skills, knowledge and expertise

Extensive experience in tax advisory and compliance gained either inhouse or in practice

Strong knowledge of UK tax legislation including VAT and corporation tax and familiarity with international taxation

Recognised accountancy or tax qualification alongside degree-level education (or equivalent); minimum five years post-qualification experience preferred

Experience engaging and influencing constructively with senior stakeholders

Strong communication skills across technical and non-technical audiences

Process improvement and systems implementation experience is desirable
