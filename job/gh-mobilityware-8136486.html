A rare opportunity: lead a product team at a profitable and self-funded studio.

You ll own the vision and roadmap for our Hybrid puzzle titles—an unannounced title, and whatever we build next—working shoulder-to-shoulder with design, engineering, art, marketing, and analytics. The role is big. The bar is high.

You ve spent years embedded with dev teams, shipping and operating live free-to-play games. You know how games actually get made, and you re not afraid of the weeds.

You re also a gamer. You play for fun, you know what makes a game great, and you understand not just how IAP games monetize but why players spend. Business sense and love of craft, in the same person.

You lead by example—passion, hard work, attention to detail, and empathy. You lift morale on the hard days and raise the bar on the good ones.

We re a small, ambitious, data-informed team, and we re looking for the person who leads us to success.

Expectations are high. So is the ceiling.

Come bring joy to millions of players, one game at a time.

Responsibilities:

Product vision strategy: Define and drive the product vision, roadmap, and strategy for the Blue Team’s titles (Unannounced Puzzle game and future games), ensuring alignment with broader company objectives.

Cross-functional leadership: Lead across engineering, design, art, marketing, and analytics to deliver high-quality, impactful live-service experiences.

Product development oversight: Own the full product lifecycle from ideation through live operation, prioritizing features and balancing player needs with market trends and business goals.

Team leadership mentorship: Guide and mentor other product managers and team members, fostering a high-performing, collaborative team culture.

Stakeholder management: Communicate product goals, progress, and performance to executives, senior leadership, and cross-functional partners.

Qualifications for Position:

4+ years of Product Management experience in Mobile F2P games with experience in leading features that drive engagement and monetization.

Proven track record shipping and operating live free-to-play (IAP) games; experience in puzzle or casual titles is a strong plus. Firm understanding of current marketplace offerings and competitors.

Strong orientation towards data insights, AB testing, and deriving actionable insights that can be meaningful for the business.

Must communicate effectively with all disciplines and all levels of the organization and be a team player.

(Plus) Experience with building and operating tiles with hybrid monetization (IAP + ads: rewarded video, interstitials, ad mediation and eCPM optimization).

A Little Bit About Us:

MobilityWare is one of the leading mobile game publishers of card and puzzle games. With our headquarters in the heart of Orange County and a growing studio in Utah, we’re dedicated to our mission of bringing joy to others one game at a time. Ever since our flagship title Solitaire hit the Apple App Store on the day it opened in 2008, MobilityWare has been entertaining gamers around the world. With a product portfolio that includes beloved hits such as FreeCell, MONOPOLY Solitaire, Spades, Hearts, Mahjong, and Bubble Shooter Pop, our games have been downloaded over 600 MILLION times.

MobilityWare was recently named one of Built in LA’s Best Places to Work for the fifth year in a row and we’ve been recognized as one of the Best Places to Work in Orange County five times by the Orange County Business Journal and OC Register. While we’re proud to be the company behind some of mobile gaming’s most popular titles, our most important achievement is creating a work environment that encourages professional growth and prioritizes the wellness of all of our team members.
