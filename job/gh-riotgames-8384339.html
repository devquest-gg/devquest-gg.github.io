Brand Manager III - Valorant

Riot Games · Los Angeles

Mid Marketing $150K–$208K Posted 2026-11-18 Full time
Apply on Riot Games’s site →

As the VALORANT Brand Manager III for the Americas, you will report to the Director of Publishing Product Management for the Americas and lead publishing campaigns for VALORANT across the region. You will bridge global campaign integration with regional execution, designing cohesive, full-year brand roadmaps that address our most mission-critical market challenges—driving cultural relevance, player retention, and audience growth across both PC and Console.

As a P4 Brand Manager, you will operate with high autonomy: moving beyond executing individual campaigns to establishing normative brand positioning, steering cross-functional rooms, stewarding regional brand budgets, and challenging creative partners to elevate the work across our diverse subregions.

Responsibilities:

Regional Campaign Execution: Lead end-to-end publishing campaign strategy across North America, Brazil, and LATAM for major seasonal beats. Your work will lean heavily towards N. America, while partnering with Brand Managers in Brazil and LATAM to elevate and support their key regional campaigns.

VCT Americas Brand Stewardship: Drive the regional publishing efforts for VCT Americas, partnering with Esports, Dev, and Regional teams to convert competitive milestones into cultural, must-watch moments.

Briefing Creative Stewardship: Deliver decision-enabling briefs grounded in regional player empathy. Partner with Creative, Production, and agency partners to safeguard brand tone, pushing for bold creative while keeping campaigns on-strategy and on-schedule.

Cross-Craft Campaign Collaboration: Partner closely with regional Comms, Community, Social, and Influencer leads to coordinate full 360-degree campaign beats without operational bottlenecks.

Agency Vendor Management: Direct agency partners in creating localized marketing assets and campaign toolkits, managing project-level deliverables and vendor budgets responsibly.

Player Insights Measurement: Track campaign reception and community sentiment across subregions in partnership with Insights, establishing post-campaign retrospectives that inform future seasonal planning.

Required Qualifications:

Experience: 5+ years of experience in brand strategy, game publishing, or creative marketing within gaming or digital entertainment.

Campaign Ownership: Proven track record of independently leading large-scale, multi-channel marketing campaigns from initial brief through post-launch analysis.

FPS Gaming Knowledge: Deep understanding of competitive gaming, live-service operational cycles, and the VALORANT community across PC and Console.

Regional Resonance: Strong familiarity with gaming audiences and youth culture in North America, with working exposure to LATAM and/or Brazilian gaming ecosystems.

Briefing Stakeholder Craft: Exceptional written and verbal communication skills, with demonstrated ability to manage upwards and gain alignment from matrixed partners.

Desired Qualifications:

Multilingual Capability: Working proficiency or fluency in Spanish and/or Brazilian Portuguese is a strong plus.

Matrixed Publishing Acumen: Experience navigating a global-to-regional matrixed organization, effectively balancing central brand standards with local execution needs.

Cross-Platform Shooter Familiarity: Deep familiarity with tactical shooters (VALORANT specifically) and experience shipping campaigns across PC and Console.

Agency Vendor Ecosystem: Prior experience directing external creative, media, or influencer agencies across North America and Latin America

For this role, you ll find success through craft expertise, a collaborative spirit, and decision-making that prioritizes the delight of players. We will be looking at your past studies, experience, and your personal relationship with games. If you embody player empathy and care about players experiences, this could be your role!
