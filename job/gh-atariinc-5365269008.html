Position: Security Compliance Lead Experience: 8-12 years Location: Netaji Subhash Place, Pitampura, Delhi Employment Type: Full-Time Work Timings: 9AM - 6PM IST Reports to: Sr. Director of Engineering

Overview

As Security Compliance Lead, you ll define and shape Atari s global security strategy — covering operational security, AI security, and applicable compliance requirements — while acting as a strategic partner to leadership. You ll own the compliance framework end-to-end, from policy design through audit readiness, and champion AI-driven automation to make security processes more efficient and scalable. You ll work closely with IT, Development, Operations, HR, Legal, and Finance, and will regularly engage with executive leadership and the board.

What You’ll Do

Security Strategy Leadership

Define and shape global security strategy — operational security, AI security, and compliance — and act as a strategic partner to leadership, positioning security as a business enabler

Define security metrics and KPIs that demonstrate program maturity and business value

Compliance Risk Management

Develop and oversee security and compliance policy frameworks across all departments, continuously monitoring posture and closing gaps

Design risk assessment frameworks and oversee organizational risk management initiatives

Drive adoption of compliance tools like Sprinto and define workflows for evidence collection and audit readiness

Audits Security Assessments

Establish and oversee internal audit programs, coordinating with internal teams and external auditors

Review security assessment findings and define strategies to reduce the attack surface area

Stakeholder Vendor Management

Build strategic relationships across IT, Development, Operations, HR, Legal, and Finance to drive alignment on security initiatives

Establish a vendor security management framework covering risk criteria, evaluation, and ongoing monitoring

Present security and compliance posture to executive leadership and the board with strategic recommendations

Incident Response

Oversee the incident response program, provide strategic direction on major incidents, and drive them to closure with root cause analysis and preventive measures

AI Automation

Champion AI and automation initiatives across compliance processes to improve efficiency and scalability

Decision-Making Authority

Security strategy, risk appetite, and organizational security standards

Security budget, vendor selections, and major tool implementations

Security policy frameworks and compliance program structure

Executive/board escalations and presentation content

Audit schedules, scope, and strategic remediation timelines

Automation and AI initiative investments

Stakeholder engagement priorities

Qualifications

Required

8+ years of progressive experience in information security and compliance roles with demonstrated strategic leadership

Deep knowledge of security frameworks and regulatory requirements, including operational security, AI security considerations, and applicable compliance standards

Proven track record in developing and executing global security strategies across diverse organizational contexts

Experience implementing compliance management platforms (Sprinto, Vanta, Drata, or similar) at scale

Vision for leveraging AI-driven automation and process optimization in security and compliance workflows

Exceptional stakeholder management and influence skills across all organizational levels

Strategic incident response leadership with experience overseeing complex security incidents and driving organizational learning

Preferred

Industry certifications such as CISSP, CISM, CISA, or equivalent

Experience in the gaming or entertainment industry

Thought leadership in AI security and emerging technology risk management

Experience designing security automation and orchestration strategies

Track record of driving organizational transformation and security culture change

Experience serving as a trusted advisor to board-level committees on security and risk matters
