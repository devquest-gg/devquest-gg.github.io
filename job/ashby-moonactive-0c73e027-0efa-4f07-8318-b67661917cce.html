IP and Brand Protection Specialist - Student Position

Moon Active · Tel Aviv

Mid People & Ops Onsite Posted 2026-10-07 Full time
Apply on Moon Active’s site →

Moon Active is one of the world’s fastest-growing mobile gaming companies, entertaining millions of players worldwide. Headquartered in Tel Aviv, we’re looking for an enthusiastic, detail-oriented law student to join our team as an IP & Brand Protection Specialist.

In this role, you’ll help protect Moon Active’s intellectual property and brands by investigating potential infringements, supporting enforcement efforts, and collaborating with teams across the company. This is an opportunity to gain hands-on experience in IP and brand protection in a dynamic, innovative environment.

Responsibilities

- Support IP and brand protection efforts by submitting takedown requests manually and through automated online brand protection tools.

- Conduct in-depth research into potential intellectual property infringements and document findings.

- Prepare clear, accurate reports on infringement cases and communicate findings to relevant stakeholders.

- Collaborate with teams across Moon Active to gather information and support the investigation and resolution of IP rights violations.

Requirements

- Currently pursuing a law degree, with at least two years of study remaining.

- Native-level English proficiency.

- Strong interpersonal and communication skills, with the ability to build effective relationships across the organization.

- Strong organizational, project management, and time management skills, with the ability to prioritize tasks and manage multiple deadlines.

- Ability to manage complex, long-term projects, adapt to changing priorities, and develop practical solutions.

- Strong attention to detail, a proactive approach, and an enthusiastic work ethic.

- Ability to handle confidential and sensitive information with discretion.
