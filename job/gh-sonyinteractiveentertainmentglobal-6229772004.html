You re a creative and passionate artist who lives for world building and crafting textures and materials that tell a story within game environments. You re inspired to achieve only the highest quality in your work and have a passion for contributing to the artistic vision and development of the industry s most anticipated AAA games.

We are seeking a highly talented and motivated Senior 3D Material Artist with a passion for crafting high quality, game-ready materials. The ideal candidate will have a strong understanding of delivering high quality content during the later stages of development, working efficiently while maintaining an established visual quality bar. This role involves creating both in game and cinematic quality materials.

Responsibilities:

Collaborate with Environment Artists directly in levels, iterate on materials in context, and bring environments to final visual polish

Partner with Art Directors, Leads, Technical Artists, implementing feedback quickly and effectively

Create and refine realistic PBR materials and surface treatments that support and elevate the project s visual direction, for both in game and cinematic levels

Manage and author materials within propriety tools

Contribute to the development and documentation of material workflows, standards, and libraries

Track and address material-related bugs during production and post-production

Help define and improve the game s visual direction with each deliverable

Balance visual quality with performance constraints

Solve production challenges creatively

Estimate scope and manage workload effectively

Deliver work on schedule / on time in a production environment

Requirements:

A strong portfolio with recent work demonstrating an artistic approach to material and surface creation for real-world surface types

6+ years of experience in AAA video game production with at least one shipped title

Strong grasp of composition, color theory, and the physical properties of real-world surfaces

Experience showing a strong understanding of game development with a focus on materials usage within a world building pipeline

Proficient at applying surface details and controlling shader features that represent a wide variety of material types

Proficiency in Substance Designer and Substance Painter

Solid understanding of PBR workflows and material creation pipelines

Technical proficiency in game art workflows including UV layout, texel density, and texture optimization

Experience optimizing games for performance and memory

Strong proactive collaboration and communication skills

Work core hours aligned with PST and team located in California

English proficiency

Pluses:

Knowledge of Substance Designer workflows

Shader authoring

Houdini experience

Experience working with external outsource vendors

2D graphics experience

Conceptual level layout experience
