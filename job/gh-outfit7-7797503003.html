Legal Counsel

Outfit7 · shanghai

Mid Other Posted 2026-07-08

Do you want to be legal counsel for a global company that works on the global scale in the fast-paced and exciting gaming industry?

We’re looking for someone to join our team and provide legal support for the company across various fields of our operations. A team player would be ideal – someone who can work both collaboratively and independently – as would someone with great communication skills, a detail-oriented working style, and a passion for their work. Read on to find out more about the role!

你是否希望加入一家全球化企业，在快速发展且充满活力的移动游戏行业担任法务顾问？

我们正在寻找一位专业、积极且富有责任心的法务顾问，为公司在多个业务领域提供法律支持。我们期待你既能够与团队紧密协作，也能够独立推动工作；具备优秀的沟通能力、严谨细致的工作态度，以及对法律工作的热情。如果你希望在国际化环境中发挥专业价值，我们期待你的加入！

Responsibilities - Review, draft, and support the negotiation of various contracts (advertising, licensing, employment, etc.) and execute legal reviews of digital platforms to be used by the company (including legal reviews and negotiating terms and conditions, and the privacy documentation of digital platforms). - Monitor, analyze, and implement PRC compliance, advertising, and other relevant regulations. - Provide legal support to departments company-wide, as well as legal advice to support easier decision-making and operational activities on the Chinese market.

- Manage various activities in the field of personal data protection (including PIPL and other privacy regulations in place in PRC), advertising, employee data protection, and other fields. - Review and prepare internal acts needed for the operation and local compliance of the company, including GPID documentation and other relevant documentation in accordance with the needs of the company. - Collect and prepare data and documentation for the authorities. - Coordinate and cooperate with external contractors and legal experts. - Administer the process of approving and signing contracts, as well as other legal documents. - Monitor compliance with regards to internal acts and external regulations. - Participate in projects, provide legal assistance in various fields of the company s operations, and collaborate with other departments.

- Monitor legislation and standards, and manage any activities necessary to ensure internal compliance. - Manage local corporate governance and company administrative matters as per the operational needs of the company and its related companies.

- 审核、起草并协助谈判各类合同，包括但不限于广告、授权许可、劳动用工等合同。

- 对公司拟使用的各类数字平台开展法律审查，包括平台条款（Terms Conditions）、隐私政策及相关法律文件的审核与谈判。

- 持续关注、分析并推动中华人民共和国（PRC）关于合规、广告监管及其他相关法律法规的落地实施。

- 为公司各业务部门提供法律支持及专业法律意见，协助业务团队在中国市场高效开展运营并做出合理决策。

- 负责个人信息保护相关法律事务，包括《中华人民共和国个人信息保护法》（PIPL）及其他中国隐私法律法规，同时支持广告合规、员工个人信息保护等相关工作。

- 起草、审核及更新公司内部制度、政策及运营所需法律文件，包括 GPID 相关文件及其他合规文件，确保满足公司运营及本地合规要求。

- 根据监管部门要求，收集、整理并准备相关资料及法律文件。

- 协调并管理外部律师事务所、法律顾问及其他专业服务机构的合作。

- 负责合同审批、签署流程及其他法律文件的管理工作。

- 持续监督公司内部制度及外部法律法规的执行情况，确保公司保持合规运营。

- 参与公司各类项目，为不同业务场景提供法律支持，并与各部门保持密切合作。

- 持续跟踪法律法规及行业标准变化，推动公司内部合规体系持续完善。

- 根据公司及关联公司的运营需求，负责本地公司治理（Corporate Governance）及相关公司行政法律事务。

What we are looking for - Passion for legal work, information technology, and the mobile game industry. - Good knowledge of and experience with contract drafting and negotiations, compliance work, and privacy regulations in PRC. - Good communication skills, with excellent written and spoken English and Chinese. - Precise and resourceful working style. - Ability to consistently deliver high quality work in a timely and efficient manner. - Understanding of and consideration for the business needs of the company. - A positive attitude towards work and self-improvement. - Ability to work both cooperatively in a team, and proactively as an individual.

- 热爱法律工作，对信息技术及移动游戏行业具有浓厚兴趣。

- 熟悉合同起草、审核及商务谈判，具备中国法律体系下合规及个人信息保护相关工作经验。

- 具备优秀的中英文书面及口头沟通能力。

- 工作严谨细致，责任心强，具备较强的问题解决能力和资源整合能力。

- 能够高质量、高效率地完成各项工作任务，并按时交付成果。

- 具备良好的商业意识，能够平衡法律风险与业务需求。

- 积极主动，具备持续学习和自我提升意识。

既能够作为团队成员高效协作，也能够独立承担和推进工作。

Required Education, Experiences and Skills - University law degree. - Five years of experience (minimum) working as a legal counsel or other comparable position in the legal profession (preferably in the gaming industry). - Good knowledge of compliance (including privacy laws and regulations) and the advertising field. - Good knowledge of English and Chinese legal terminology.

- 法学本科及以上学历。

- 至少 5 年 法务顾问或相关法律岗位工作经验；具有游戏行业或互联网科技行业工作经验者优先。

- 熟悉企业合规体系建设，包括个人信息保护、数据合规及广告相关法律法规。

- 熟悉中英文法律术语，能够独立处理英文合同及法律文件。

- 具备较强的合同审阅、法律分析、风险识别及跨部门沟通协调能力。

- 具有国际化企业、跨国公司或涉外法律事务经验者优先。

What we offer

- Employment relationship with Outfit7 Network Technology Co., Ltd Shanghai Branch for a fixed term period of three years, with a six-month probationary period and the option to prolong the contract. - Diverse work with exciting daily challenges, working as part of a team in a fun, relaxed environment. - Participation in attractive projects. - Development opportunities through training, seminars, and conferences. - Choice of sport and fitness activities outside of work. - Work-life balance.

- Participation in a discretionary bonus plan. - Christmas bonus (subject to internal company policies)

- 签订为期三年 的固定期限劳动合同，试用期 6 个月 ，合同期满可根据双方意愿续签。

- 国际化的工作环境，与优秀团队共同协作，每天都能接触富有挑战性且多元化的工作内容。

- 参与具有影响力和创新性的项目，与全球团队共同合作。

- 丰富的职业发展机会，包括培训课程、专业研讨会及行业会议等学习资源。

- 公司提供运动及健身福利，鼓励健康积极的生活方式。

- 注重工作与生活的平衡（Work-Life Balance），营造开放、轻松的工作氛围。

- 参与公司年度绩效奖金计划（根据公司奖金政策执行）。
