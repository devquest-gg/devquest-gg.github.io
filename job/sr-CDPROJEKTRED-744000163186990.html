Shared Tech QA Lead

CD Projekt Red · Warsaw

Lead QA Posted 2026-11-23 Full time
Apply on CD Projekt Red’s site →

CD PROJEKT RED is looking for a Shared Tech QA Lead to lead and develop our Shared Tech QA team within Shared Development. This is a central role supporting technologies used across multiple projects, including shared engine systems, internal tools, development pipelines, and cross-project features.

You will establish effective testing approaches, develop the team’s capabilities, and work closely with technology owners, project QA teams, Development, and Production. Your focus will be on ensuring shared solutions are reliable and fit for use, with clear visibility of their quality, limitations, and impact on the teams that depend on them.

The Shared Tech QA Lead owns the team’s QA planning and execution, aligning priorities with shared technology roadmaps and project needs. You will balance immediate delivery requirements with reusable test coverage and long-term improvements, while agreeing clear testing responsibilities with project QA teams.

Responsibilities Lead, mentor, and develop Shared Tech QA Analysts, setting clear responsibilities, priorities, goals, and quality expectations.

Plan and coordinate Shared Tech QA work across multiple projects, aligning coverage and resources with technology roadmaps, project milestones, risks, dependencies, and team capacity.

Work with technology owners and project stakeholders to define testable acceptance criteria and translate them into practical testing strategies, plans, and measurable quality expectations.

Oversee validation of shared engine systems, internal tools, development pipelines, and cross-project features, including functional, integration, regression, performance, stability, and cross-platform testing.

Establish reusable and maintainable test coverage, accounting for differences in how individual projects configure, integrate, and use shared technologies.

Coordinate testing of shared technology changes with embedded project QA teams, agreeing ownership, dependencies, and handovers to avoid coverage gaps and unnecessary duplication.

Support complex technical investigations, helping the team distinguish issues in shared systems from project-specific integration, content, or configuration problems.

Ensure issues are clearly documented, assessed for their impact on dependent teams and projects, prioritized with the relevant owners, and followed through to verification.

Maintain visibility of testing status, coverage, known limitations, risks, and blockers; provide reliable input to shared technology delivery and project readiness assessments.

Identify competing priorities and capacity constraints early, work with stakeholders to agree trade-offs and mitigation plans, and escalate unresolved concerns.

Collaborate with QA Engineering and development teams to identify automation, diagnostic tooling, and test-environment needs that improve testing effectiveness and repeatability.

Use test results, defect trends, and feedback from teams using shared technologies to identify recurring problems and recommend improvements.

Improve Shared Tech QA workflows, documentation, reporting, and knowledge-sharing practices, making effective approaches reusable across projects.

Support recruitment, onboarding, performance management, career development, and sustainable workload planning for the team.

Significant experience in technical QA within game development, including responsibility for the quality of complex engine systems, tools, pipelines, or technical features.

Proven experience leading a QA team, including direct people management, setting expectations, providing performance feedback, and supporting career development.

Strong understanding of testing methodologies for complex systems, including integration, regression, performance, stress, and stability testing.

Experience creating and maintaining risk-based test strategies, test plans, and structured coverage for technologies that evolve throughout production.

Practical experience with Unreal Engine or another game engine, including its editor tools and development workflows.

Understanding of content and asset pipelines, technical dependencies, and how changes to shared systems can affect multiple teams and projects.

Familiarity with version control, build systems, and continuous integration workflows, and their role in technical validation.

Experience using diagnostic or profiling tools to support technical investigations and interpret evidence such as logs, crash reports, and performance captures.

Ability to assess quality risks, distinguish local issues from broader systemic problems, and communicate clear, actionable recommendations.

Strong organizational and stakeholder-management skills, with the ability to negotiate priorities, explain trade-offs, and coordinate work across teams.

Clear spoken and written English, including the ability to discuss issues with technical specialists and explain their impact to non-specialist stakeholders.

An accountable, collaborative leadership approach, with the confidence to challenge plans constructively and make sound decisions under uncertainty.

Nice to Have Experience supporting shared technologies, internal platforms, or tools used by multiple game projects.

Experience contributing to at least one shipped AA or AAA game.

Experience with test automation, build verification, or integrating validation into development pipelines.

Programming or scripting experience, particularly with Python, C++, or similar languages.

Familiarity with tools such as Perforce, RoboMerge, and Unreal Insights, or equivalent version control, integration, and profiling solutions.

Experience testing across PC and console environments, or coordinating work with distributed teams and external QA partners.

Knowledge of narrative or localization tools and pipelines.
