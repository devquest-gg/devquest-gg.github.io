POSITION SUMMARY

Bandai Namco Entertainment America Inc.’s PAC-MAN Social M edia Coordinator will support the execution of a variety of social media and influencer campaigns to support PAC-MAN brand initiatives . They will collaborate with internal teams and global social marketing teams to execute a high volume of tasks and coordinate responsibilities with related teams. They will be expected to provide ongoing support to overall brand campaigns for gam es , community management, content creation, social publishing , copywriting, and influencer outreach.

This will be a 12-month long contract . Join us at our new campus in Irvine, California where a hybrid work schedule will be observed.

ESSENTIAL RESPONSIBILITIES

Support social editorial calendars, with an emphasis on production management/approvals with global teams.

Engage and manage community spaces such as the PAC-MAN Discord.

Collaborate with internal stakeholders to create campaign content and assets.

Support content plans and manage daily social publishing across all PAC-MAN social channels.

Provide coordination and support for influencer marketing by maintaining updated target lists of brand influencers and communicating with active influencers and external agencies.

Manage the development of captions and copy across PAC-MAN social media channels (including text overlay for videos) to maintain the brand’s voice.

Continuously keep a pulse on social, game, and cultural trends and analytics to support content ideation and optimize PAC-MAN’s social presence.

Support content capture and editing.

Manage talent bookings and scheduling with external agencies for activations.

Perform other duties as assigned.

MINIMUM QUALIFICATIONS

Bachelor s degree in a related field or equivalent experience.

2-4+ years of progressively responsible experience with hands-on social media coordination

Bachelor’s degree or higher in a related field or equivalent experience

Highly organized and meticulous attention to detail with an ongoing dedication to achieving high-quality results.

PREFERRED QUALIFICATIONS

Passion for pop culture and gaming

Familiarity with popular video game influencers

About Bandai Namco Entertainment America Inc.

Bandai Namco Entertainment America Inc., part of the Bandai Namco Group, is a leading global publisher and developer of interactive entertainment for major video game consoles, PC, online, and mobile platforms. The company is known for creating many of the industry’s beloved classic franchises such as PAC-MAN®, GALAGA®, TEKKEN®, SOULCALIBUR®, and ACE COMBAT®, and publishing the critically acclaimed DARK SOULS™ series and the blockbuster title ELDEN RING™. Bandai Namco Entertainment America Inc. is also the premier publisher in the Western hemisphere for anime-based video games including GUNDAM™, NARUTO SHIPPUDEN™, DRAGON BALL™, and ONE PIECE®. Bandai Namco Entertainment America Inc. is headquartered in Irvine, California. More information about the company and its products can be found at http://www.bandainamcoent.com .
