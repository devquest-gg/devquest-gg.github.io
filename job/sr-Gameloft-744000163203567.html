Main challenge: Great games start with great teams. Help us make them happen.

What do game developers, artists, designers and producers have in common? They all need the right environment, strong leadership and the support to do their best work. That's where you come in.

At Gameloft Bucharest, we're looking for an experienced HR Business Partner who understands that people are at the heart of every successful creative business.

You'll work closely with the people behind our games, partnering with Studio leadership and managers to build strong teams, develop talent and create an environment where people can grow, collaborate and thrive.

Your challenge is to connect people, business and culture: helping managers navigate people challenges, translating business priorities into meaningful HR actions and making a real difference in the everyday experience of our employees.

What it means on a daily basis Business partnership & leadership: Partner with Studio leaders and managers to understand business priorities, anticipate people-related challenges and translate them into effective HR solutions. Coach and support managers in building effective teams, strengthening leadership capabilities and managing performance.

Talent & growth: Contribute to talent reviews, career development, succession planning and internal mobility, helping identify and develop talent across the Studio.

Engagement, culture & change: Turn employee feedback and people insights into meaningful initiatives that strengthen collaboration, motivation and a sense of belonging. Support leaders and teams through organizational changes and new ways of working, ensuring effective communication and engagement.

HR operations & global collaboration: Ensure effective execution of employee lifecycle processes while continuously identifying opportunities to improve HR practices and the employee experience. Collaborate with the wider HR community to implement global people initiatives, ensuring alignment with local business priorities.

&#xa0; What success looks like: Trusted partnership & leadership: Managers see you as a trusted advisor in addressing people and business challenges, while leaders are better equipped to develop, engage and manage their teams.

Talent & performance: Talent and performance management processes contribute to stronger team effectiveness, better performance and employee development.

Engagement & change: Employee feedback translates into concrete actions that improve engagement and the overall employee experience. Organizational changes and HR initiatives are implemented effectively, with a clear understanding of their impact on people and the business.

Continuous improvement: HR practices continuously evolve to meet the changing needs of the Studio.

Who you'll collaborate with You will be part of a wider HR community, working closely with Studio leadership and business managers, as well as Game Production, Development and Creative teams, including Artists, Designers and other specialists.

You will also collaborate with HR Leadership and Management, Recruitment & Talent Acquisition, HR Operations and global HR teams across Gameloft studios.

What you need to succeed Experience & HR expertise: Solid experience as an HR Business Partner or in a similar HR role, with proven experience partnering with managers and business leaders in a dynamic organization. Good understanding of organizational development, performance management, employee relations and talent management.

People & business skills: Strong coaching, consulting, influencing and communication skills, with the ability to build trusted relationships and support business leaders.

Education & background: University degree in Human Resources, Business, Psychology or a related field. Experience in gaming, technology, digital or other creative industries is a plus.

Languages: Fluency in English, both written and spoken.

Who you are People-oriented & pragmatic: You understand that successful teams are at the heart of successful businesses. You are proactive, solution-oriented and able to balance strategic priorities with day-to-day challenges.

Strong communicator & relationship builder: You know how to influence, challenge constructively and build trust with different stakeholders.

Adaptable & organized: You are curious and comfortable working in a dynamic environment where priorities can evolve, while being able to manage multiple priorities and keep an eye on the bigger picture.

Passionate about people & creativity: You have a genuine interest in people, creativity and the world of video games.
