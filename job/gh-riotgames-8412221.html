Marketing Specialist (Contract)

Riot Games

Mid Marketing Posted 2026-11-24 Contractor
Apply on Riot Games’s site →

Riot Games was established in 2006 by entrepreneurial gamers who believe that player-focused game development can result in great games. In 2009, Riot released its debut title League of Legends to critical and player acclaim. As the most played PC game in the world, over 100 million play every month. Players form the foundation of our community and it’s for them that we continue to evolve and improve the League of Legends experience.

We’re looking for humble but ambitious, razor-sharp professionals who can teach us a thing or two. We promise to return the favor. Like us, you take play seriously; you’re passionate about games. We embrace those who see things differently, aren’t afraid to experiment, and who have a healthy disregard for constraints.

That s where you come in.

As a Marketing Specialist (Contract) in APAC LoL publishing team, you will help turn approved brand and campaign strategies into clear, well-coordinated player experiences across social and other publishing channels. The role combines campaign coordination with channel and content operations: keeping plans, people, assets, and timelines connected from brief through launch and wrap. You will partner with Brand, Esports, Creative, Community, Influencer, regional teams, and external agencies or partners. You will own the operational delivery of assigned campaigns and channel work, playing a critical role in making sure that the player experience is cohesive with the campaign strategy.

You will report to Brand Manager, League of Legends (APAC).

Responsibilities:

Campaign Management and Execution.

Partner with Brand and Publishing leads to translate approved campaign strategy into actionable briefs, channel plans, and deliverable trackers that support the intended player journey. Coordinate assigned campaigns and tactics from planning and production through launch and wrap, and work with internal partners and external agencies to support onboarding, contracts, creative development, and review. Consolidate feedback, track approvals, and help ensure creative assets are accurate, on-brief, and ready for use.

Project Coordination.

Manage day-to-day delivery across assigned campaigns and channel work by tracking milestones, dependencies, owners, approvals, budgets, and deliverables. Maintain clear project documentation and status updates; keep stakeholders aligned on progress, changes, risks, and decisions needed; and proactively resolve or escalate issues that could affect timelines or player-facing delivery.

Channels and Content Operations.

Maintain social and publishing calendars, release plans, and content trackers across assigned markets and channels. Coordinate content intake, copy and asset readiness, approvals, scheduling, publishing, and post-publish quality checks. Work with global and in-market teams, Community and Esports stakeholders, agencies, and publishing partners to deliver content accurately and on time; support live or real-time channel coverage for priority moments when needed; and maintain clear records of channel requirements, publishing status, links, and final assets.

Campaign Performance Reporting.

Gather campaign and channel performance data and player-sentiment signals; consolidate results into recaps and retrospectives; and share relevant learnings with stakeholders. Apply those learnings to improve future campaign and channel execution, content presentation, publishing workflows, and stakeholder handoffs.

Localization and Regional Publishing.

Coordinate regional adaptation and localization requirements, assets, timelines, and reviews with local teams and publishing partners. Help ensure localized content is accurate, culturally appropriate, aligned with approved campaign direction, and ready for publication in each assigned market; surface regional dependencies or risks early and coordinate timely resolution.

Required Qualifications

3+ years of experience in marketing, campaign coordination, social media, content operations, project coordination, or a related field.

Experience coordinating multi-stakeholder projects with deadlines, dependencies, reviews, and external partners or vendors.

Familiarity with social publishing workflows, content calendars, asset management, and quality assurance.

Strong written and verbal communication skills, with the ability to make complex status and next steps clear.

Highly organized and detail-oriented; able to manage multiple workstreams and adapt as priorities change.

Sound judgment when handling sensitive information, last-minute changes, and time-sensitive player-facing content.

Comfortable using collaborative productivity and project-tracking tools; able to maintain accurate documentation and status reporting.

Desired Qualifications

Experience in gaming, esports, entertainment, or other fan-focused brands.

Familiarity with Riot Games, its games, and player communities; regular play or active engagement with games is a plus, especially League of Legends.

Experience supporting campaigns across multiple APAC markets or working across time zones and cultures.

Proficiency in Mandarin and Traditional Chinese comprehension capabilities.

Familiarity with localization, content management systems, social publishing tools, or campaign operations platforms. Basic image or video editing skills and an eye for creative quality.

For this role, you ll find success through craft expertise, a collaborative spirit, and decision-making that prioritizes the delight of players. We will be looking at your past studies, experience, and your personal relationship with games. If you embody player empathy and care about players experiences, this could be your role!
