2D Concept Artist

Gameloft · Sofia

Senior Art Posted 2026-09-21 Full time

WHAT YOU WILL BE WORKING ON Main challenge Create polished 2D concept art assets and illustrations/splash screens that fit the visual direction of the game and support a consistent player experience. You will translate artistic requirements and feedback into production-ready assets while working within established visual styles, guidelines and project requirements.

&#xa0; What it means on a daily basis •Create concept art for buildings , characters, vehicles and items, following the game's art direction and guidelines •Create illustrations, splash screens and loading screens following the game's art direction and guidelines •Adapt your work to different game styles while maintaining visual consistency and quality •Collaborate with Artists, Animators, Designers and Developers to ensure assets are ready for integration into the game •Review and implement feedback and revisions, while delivering work according to agreed requirements and timelines •Research and present ideas for new assets and features, taking ownership of your work from concept to delivery &#xa0; What success will look like •High-quality, polished assets that elevate game ergonomics and overall player experience •Smooth integration of the assets into the development pipeline without technical friction •Proactive ownership of assigned tasks and steady creative adaptation to different game genres and graphic styles.

&#xa0; Who you would be working with You will report to the Lead Artist, who will provide direction on your tasks, revisions, workflows, training and creative development. You will collaborate with another Artists, Animators, Designers, Developers, Producers and other members of the game team.

&#xa0; Your recruitment journey •Step 1 – CV & Portfolio screening •Step 2 - A practical art task to showcase your creative logic and technical skills •Step 3 - Final interview in person at our office with the game Producer, Lead Artist and HR Representative &#xa0; Where this role can take you This position offers direct room to deepen your expertise in game art, master new tools, take on broader creative ownership within international projects, and progress towards Senior or even Lead Artist roles WHAT YOU NEED TO SUCCEED •Previous professional experience as an Artist in the gaming industry •A strong portfolio demonstrating art skills in general - concept art, hard surface design, illustrations and polished rendering techniques •Advanced proficiency in Photoshop, Blender or any other 3D software will be considered a plus •Excellent communication, task ownership, and organizational skills •Professional working proficiency in English &#xa0; WHO YOU ARE •You are a 2D Concept Artist who can work within an established visual direction while maintaining a high level of attention to detail and visual quality •You are comfortable receiving feedback, making revisions and collaborating with people from different disciplines •You can organize your work, communicate clearly about your tasks and adapt your approach when project requirements or visual directions change •You are interested in developing your skills and learning new tools and techniques as part of your work WHAT’S IN IT FOR YOU Benefits •Additional health insurance including dental care and glasses reimbursement •Hybrid work model with flexible working hours and shorter Fridays •Food vouchers – 102 EUR per month •Access to Multisport or Coolfit sports cards •20 days paid annual leave plus one additional day off for your birthday •Referral bonus and baby bonus •Modern office at Sofia Tower 2 (Mall of Sofia) •Gaming area with table tennis, air hockey, PlayStation 5, and board games •Weekly fruit and snacks, daily hot beverages •Access to internal trainings, studio library, and career development support •Studio events, social gatherings, and charity initiatives •Occasional gifts for special occasions •Discount program with selected retailers and services Salary Ranges:
