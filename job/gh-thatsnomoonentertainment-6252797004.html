Infrastructure Engineer

That's No Moon · Los Angeles or Remote · Remote

That s No Moon is an independent studio of storytellers and game-makers who believe in the power of narrative-driven experiences. With a diverse team of talented developers, our shared vision is to create the future of AAA experiences with a culture of creativity, collaboration, and responsible production practices. Our passionate team of developers helps shape our studio, our projects, and our future.

We are looking for a Infrastructure Engineer with a strong customer focus for a AAA title. The Infrastructure Engineer will assist in developing and maintaining studio-wide software infrastructure with a focus on repeatable infrastructure-as-code(IaC) deployments. Workflow, iteration speed and automation are of high priority to our studio, and a successful candidate will have a meaningful impact on the production of the game. In this role, you will work with everyone in the studio providing outstanding infrastructure and support to deliver the best game possible and improve developer quality of life.

Responsibilities:

Maintain, improve, and optimize our internal infrastructure leveraging DevOps/ IaC methodologies ( Puppet/Salt/Terraform etc ).

Design, implement, and maintain monitoring, alerting, and centralized logging for studio infrastructure, build farms, and cloud resources (e.g., Prometheus, Grafana, Azure Monitor).

Be the first point of contact for infrastructure deployments or change requests, testing and documenting changes through release.

Managing and supporting cloud infrastructure in Azure/AWS/GCP for internal/outsource end-users.

Manage enterprise networks in a hybrid environment, with deployments in our LA office, Azure, and remote users’ home offices.

Assist in triaging and troubleshooting downstream issues related to build delivery, enterprise storage, and managed VPN access to private resources.

Develop, enhance, and debug automation scripts.

Analyze different approaches to solve problems and discuss the tradeoffs between them.

Work collaboratively with all members of the studio .

Write and maintain documentation related to areas of ownership.

Work with the production team and engineering leadership to provide visibility and planning.

Requirements Skills:

Extensive knowledge with DevOps/SecOps/ IaC methodologies involving a distributed environment, remote employees, and/or outsourcing.

Hands-on experience with observability tooling (metrics, logging, alerting) and building actionable dashboards.

Worked on at least one AAA game or equivalent complexity.

Solid knowledge of scripting or programming languages.

(Bonus) Familiarity with build systems and build farms.

(Bonus) Experience with agentic software development workflows/pipelines in the scope of IT/Infrastructure management.

(Bonus) Unreal Engine experience.

Qualities:

Excellent communication skills visually, verbally and written

Work independently with minimal supervision

Ability to resolve and de-escalate conflicts

Great troubleshooting skills
