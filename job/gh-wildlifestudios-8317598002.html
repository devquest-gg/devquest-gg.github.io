Business Operations Associate

Wildlife Studios · São Paulo

Entry Other Hybrid Posted 2025-12-02
Apply on Wildlife Studios’s site →

We re looking for a talented Business Operations (BizOps) Associate to join Wildlife s BizOps team.

Within the BizOps team, you will work on high-priority initiatives directly with senior leadership, gain visibility across the whole business, and own end-to-end impact from diagnosis to execution. It’s a rare blend of strategy, operations, and selective M A exposure in one of the largest mobile gaming companies in the world.

The role requires resourcefulness and a high sense of ownership, in addition to the analytical and problem-solving skills typical of strategic roles. You will have autonomy to lead workstreams and will be accountable for delivering complete end products. You will need to navigate most areas of the company, build relationships, and have a strong sense of how our business and industry operate.

The high exposure nature of the role also requires good communication skills, mainly written, which is our standard way of running meetings (no PPTs!). For that, you will need to be structured, clearly articulating issues, alternatives, and recommendations. Since you are going to be involved in a number of different projects, as well as ad-hoc analyses, there will be no routine for this job.

What you ll do

Lead strategic initiatives end-to-end, from problem structuring to implementation, partnering with leaders across product, marketing, finance, and operations

Support our M A and investment efforts through sourcing, market mapping, financial modeling, and selective diligence / integration workstreams

Run analyses and write clear and concise docs that shape our strategic priorities and help leadership move fast

Improve how the company operates, helping design and implement scalable processes and routines that raise the bar across teams

What you ll need

4+ years of experience in management consulting, private equity, venture capital, or strategy operations

Strong analytical skills and a track record of delivering high-quality work under ambiguity

Ability to break down complex problems and write clear, structured documents

Comfort navigating a fast-paced, high-growth environment and working directly with senior stakeholders

Fluent in English and Portuguese

More about you

You have strong analytical and problem-solving skills

You take ownership, operate with autonomy, and push things to completion

You communicate with clarity and can influence without authority

You are hands-on, curious, and energized by working across different topics and teams

About Wildlife

Wildlife is one of the leading mobile game developers and publishers in the world. We have released more than 60 titles, reaching billions of people around the globe. Today, we have offices in Brazil, Finland, and the United States. Here, we create games that will excite, intrigue, and engage our players for years to come!
