About Wildlife

Wildlife is one of the leading mobile game developers and publishers in the world. We have released more than 60 titles, reaching billions of people around the globe. Here, we create and maintain games that will excite, intrigue, and engage our players for years to come!

About the role

We re looking for a passionate Tax Intern to join our team and help fuel our global impact. This is your chance to gain hands-on experience in fiscal and tax operations at one of the world s leading mobile game developers. You’ll support critical tax compliance activities, sharpen your technical expertise, and collaborate with our team to keep our operations running smoothly. If you re eager to build a solid foundation for your career in tax within a fast-paced, high-growth environment, we’d love to have you with us.

What you ll own

Invoice Receiving.

Own end-to-end invoice receiving workflow, which includes partnering with the business (requesters), Procurement, Accounting, and Tax to validate documents against purchase orders, validate accounting rules, and determine taxes applicable to each transaction

Support Tax Compliance Routines.

Support your direct leader in accounting closing routines and less complex tax compliance activities, such as reconciliation, tax returns and other reports delivered to tax authorities and audit, and tax assessments

Support ERP Governance.

Oversee and maintain ERP master data by accurately registering, updating, and inactivating vendors, clients, items, taxes, and purchase categories. Also, collaborate with Finance IT to build automations and improve the invoice receiving workflow.

What success looks like

Performance is measured on your direct contribution, which will depend on:

Agile Learning.

Demonstrates openness to learning and resistance to being defensive in the face of adversity

Effective Communication.

Can be clear and succinct in a variety of settings and styles. Can get messages across to achieve the desired effect

Self-Development.

Actively looks for new ways to build skills to help in the future. Seeks out ways to grow and be challenged by formal and informal development channels. Understands that growing and changing are beneficial to a career.

Growth Mindset.

Strong desire to discover and self-motivated to pursue understanding.

Who you are

Pursuing a bachelor s degree from the 2nd or 3rd semester onwards in Accounting, Business Administration, Economics, or related fields;

Proven Excel expertise and preferably domain of basic formulas, pivot tables, and conditional analysis to facilitate daily activities;

Intermediate level of reading and written English as they’ll need to read invoices and documents.

Nice to Have:

Ability to work in SAP S/4HANA

Familiarity with tax and accounting concepts and routines.
