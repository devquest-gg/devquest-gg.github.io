Senior / Lead Character Artist

Black Banner Studios · České Budějovice · Remote

Lead Art Remote Posted 2026-11-20 Full time
Apply on Black Banner Studios’s site →

Legacy of Valor is an ambitious medieval RPG and strategy game combining massive real-time battles, third-person melee combat, and tactical army control in Unreal Engine 5. At Black Banner Studios, we're looking for an exceptional Senior / Lead Character Artist with outstanding sculpting skills, strong artistic vision, and the ability to create beautiful, realistic, yet distinctive characters. Our world draws inspiration from 14th-century Europe, combining historical authenticity with a grounded medieval aesthetic. We want characters that feel believable and full of personality, with natural, effortless artistic quality rather than stiff or overly mechanical detail. You'll work closely with our Art Director, concept artists, technical artists, and animators to bring memorable characters to life, from detailed human faces to complete characters, clothing, and armour. We're open to candidates at both Senior and Lead levels, with the final title and responsibilities determined by experience and the scope of technical or artistic leadership. If you believe you'd be a great fit for this role, even if you don't meet every requirement listed below, we'd still love to hear from you. Please don't hesitate to apply! Sculpt beautiful, highly detailed, realistic human characters with distinctive artistic quality and strong anatomical foundations. Create convincing, expressive human faces, capturing natural proportions, subtle details, personality, and believable skin characteristics. Model and sculpt character bodies, clothing, armour, and accessories based on concept art and historical references. Create high-quality PBR textures and materials, including realistic skin, fabric, leather, and metal surfaces. Produce optimized, game-ready character assets, collaborating with technical artists on topology, UVs, LODs, and Unreal Engine integration. Maintain artistic consistency and high visual quality across characters, from close-up gameplay to large-scale battlefield scenes. Collaborate closely with other artists, mentor junior team members, provide constructive artistic and technical feedback, and help establish efficient workflows and consistent quality standards. Extensive professional experience in character art for games, with significant contributions to at least one shipped commercial title. Exceptional sculpting skills, demonstrated through a strong portfolio of realistic, beautifully crafted human characters and faces. Excellent understanding of human anatomy, facial structure, proportions, clothing, and believable surface details. Strong artistic judgment, with the ability to create distinctive, natural-looking characters rather than merely technically accurate models. Advanced proficiency with ZBrush or equivalent sculpting software and professional 3D modeling tools. Strong practical experience creating high-quality PBR textures using Substance Painter or equivalent tools. Experience developing game-ready character assets, including retopology, UV mapping, and optimization. Ability to work independently from concept to finished asset while collaborating closely with a multidisciplinary art team. Passion for video games, particularly medieval RPGs, open-world games, and strategy titles. Experience working on AAA and / or large open-world game projects. Experience creating realistic human likenesses based on photographic references or scans. Experience with historically grounded medieval characters, clothing, armour, and equipment. Experience with Unreal Engine 5 character pipelines and material systems. Experience creating advanced skin materials, including subsurface scattering and realistic facial shading. Experience with Substance Designer and procedural material creation. Experience preparing characters for facial animation, blendshapes, and high-quality real-time cinematics. Experience with grooming workflows or character hair creation. Experience working on AAA and / or open-world game projects. Location: Remote / International, with the option to work in the Czech Republic. Engagement: Full-time, long-term collaboration. Contract: Contractor / B2B worldwide, or direct employment for candidates residing in or willing to relocate to the Czech Republic. Start Date: As soon as mutually agreed. Language: Professional working proficiency in English. Compensation: Competitive, based on experience and expertise.
