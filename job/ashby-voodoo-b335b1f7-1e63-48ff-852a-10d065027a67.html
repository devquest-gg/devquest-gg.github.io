ABOUT VOODOO

Founded in 2013, Voodoo is a tech company that creates mobile games and apps with a mission to entertain the world. Gathering 800 employees, 7 billion downloads, and over 200 million active users, Voodoo is the #3 mobile publisher worldwide in terms of downloads after Google and Meta. Our portfolio includes chart-topping games like Mob Control and Block Jam, alongside popular apps such as BeReal and Wizz.

TEAM

The Engineering & Data team builds innovative tech products and platforms to support the impressive growth of their gaming and consumer apps which allow Voodoo to stay at the forefront of the mobile gaming industry.

As a Senior Data Product Manager, you will be joining the BI/Prediction Team which is an autonomous squad of around 10 people, delivering best-in-class predictive solutions using Machine Learning algorithms for our growth operations. The team is composed of top-tier data scientists and analysts.

ROLE

We are looking for a Senior Data Product Manager who is deeply passionate about machine learning and data-intensive products, leveraging among the largest datasets available in the industry. You will play a crucial role in building ML prediction products for our growth operations while working in direct cooperation with our Prediction Tech Lead.

- Drive and own product vision, strategy and roadmap, guide feature prioritization and scope.

- Conduct discovery with growth and other stakeholders (e.g., Gaming) to anticipate business needs and detail the specifications for the tech team.

- Coordinate new product releases (e.g., prediction for a new app), assess impact and collect users feedback.

- Address stakeholders questions/feedback, onboard them on the product, perform analysis, and flag key prediction/analytics issues to tech.

- Collaborate with external partners (e.g., networks, MMM) on prediction projects, and identify new opportunities via benchmarking.

- Stay updated on privacy topics (e.g., Privacy Sandbox, ATT) through monitoring and testing.

- Support the tech team by identifying new organizational tools and processes to improve efficiency.

- Act as the main point of contact for top management on deliveries and decisions

- Apply analytical thinking to continuously evaluate and improve product performance

PROFILE

- Extensive proven experience as a Product Manager in the tech industry

- Data analyst or Data Scientist background is a strong plus

- Value-driven, data-driven, and open-minded problem solver

- Great communicator and a team player, who likes to drive results together.

- You are fluent in English and have a French conversational level.

- Interest in gaming and mobile apps
