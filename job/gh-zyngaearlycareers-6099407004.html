Are you a bright, passionate student ready to dive into the world of game design? At Zynga India, we foster an environment of collaboration, experimentation, and most importantly—fun!

We’re seeking a Game Designer Intern to contribute to Social Slots, a high-profile title enjoyed by millions worldwide. If you re excited to kickstart your career as a game maker, we’d love to hear from you!

What You’ll Do • Collaborate closely with designers, UX specialists, engineers, and product managers to conceptualize and implement innovative game features. • Design and oversee the creation of engaging in-game events and content to delight players. • Craft comprehensive game design documentation for features and content, ensuring clarity and precision. • Analyze qualitative and quantitative feedback to refine gameplay, balancing mechanics and enhancing the player experience.

What We Require • Strong knowledge of mechanics and systems design. • Ability to effectively communicate ideas through documentation, sketches, storyboards, and mock-ups. • A solid understanding of popular mobile, casual, and social games—and what makes them fun. • Self-motivated and organized, with a proactive approach to challenges. • A passionate gamer with experience across multiple platforms.

What We Offer • Hands-on experience in designing casino slot games • Mentorship from industry experts with years of experience in game design • Opportunities to contribute to live projects that impact millions of players worldwide. • Catered breakfast, lunch and evening snacks • Well stocked pantry • Frequent employee events • Casual dress every single day • Work with cool people and impact millions of daily players!
