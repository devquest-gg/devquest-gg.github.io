What We Need

The Manager, FinOps Observability leads two teams that answer the same questions about 2K s live games: what our infrastructure costs, and how well it performs. Both run on the same telemetry, and both matter most when a game launches or a live event brings a surge of players. You ll help 2K understand the cost and health of the services behind those moments, down to cost per player-hour.

You ll be a frontline engineering manager in 2K Tech s Platform Engineering organization, reporting to the Senior Manager, DevOps Operations. Tech leads own technical direction. You own priorities, staffing and delivery, and you make sure the team has what it needs to do its best work.

The ideal candidate brings strong engineering leadership experience and a strong background in cloud infrastructure, with experience in cost management or observability. We welcome both experienced managers and tech leads ready for their first management role.

Success looks like a healthy team, growing cost allocation coverage, better operational efficiency, and stronger observability outcomes.

What You’ll Do

Manage engineers across the FinOps and Observability teams, including performance reviews, coaching and career development.

Hire and grow the team, and plan capacity so operational support, technical debt and platform work stay in balance.

Run 1–2 quarter planning and prioritization, set clear ownership, and flag delivery risks early.

Lead cloud cost work: connect architecture and usage decisions to cost, build cost allocation and unit economics (such as cost per concurrent user or per player-hour), and partner with Finance on forecasting.

Oversee the observability platform, including telemetry, monitoring, alerting and SLO adoption.

Make sure observability supports incident response, reliability, and readiness for game launches and live events.

Improve operational efficiency across alert quality, cloud resource utilization, and the cost of observability itself.

Keep stakeholders informed on progress and risks, and work with Finance, Procurement, engineering teams and vendors.

Build a collaborative team culture with a healthy on-call rotation.

What Will Make You A Great Fit

5+ years of experience in SRE, platform engineering, cloud infrastructure, FinOps, or related technical disciplines, including 2+ years leading engineers as a people manager or tech lead.

Experience mentoring engineers and contributing to hiring and performance decisions.

Deep hands-on experience with AWS and/or GCP, including how architecture and usage decisions drive cost.

Hands-on experience in cloud cost management or observability, with working knowledge of the other.

Experience planning and prioritizing across multiple workstreams and managing delivery risk.

Sound technical judgment: you can follow technical discussions, weigh trade-offs, and know when to challenge a proposal.

Strong communication and stakeholder management skills, including with non-technical partners such as Finance.

Bachelor s degree in Computer Science, Information Technology, a related engineering field, or equivalent practical work experience.

Nice-to-Haves

Direct people management experience.

Gaming industry background, especially supporting large-scale services through game launches and live events.

Experience with our stack: Datadog, Vantage, OpenTelemetry and Kubernetes.

Experience with cloud commitment management (AWS Savings Plans, GCP Committed Use Discounts) and FOCUS billing data.
