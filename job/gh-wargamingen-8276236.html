Job Overview

Wargaming is looking for a proactive and hands-on Facility Officer to join our team in Belgrade. In this role, you will help ensure that our office facilities are safe, functional, and well-maintained, supporting smooth day-to-day operations and providing a comfortable working environment for our employees.

You will be responsible for carrying out basic maintenance tasks, monitoring the condition of office facilities and equipment, coordinating external contractors and service providers, and assisting with workspace arrangements and office logistics. This role is ideal for someone who enjoys practical work, takes initiative, and can efficiently handle a variety of facility-related tasks.

Reports to

Head of Workplace Employee Experience

What will you do?

Assists with maintaining daily operations of the physical facility.

Supports maintenance mechanics and facilities staff by performing “handyman” maintenance duties such as furniture moves, simple electrical projects

Works with outside contractors on building and equipment repairs

Operates site equipment as needed, using hand and power tools as necessary

Coordinates vendors/employees for performance of specialized maintenance, repair, or certification of buildings, infrastructure, tech appliances. Inspects completed work for conformance with contract requirements and safety codes

Performs regular checks of buildings, infrastructure, and premises to ensure safety and uninterrupted use

Organize storage areas, inventory management

Providing support to office team in everyday operations: assist with setting up workspaces for new employees

Arrange furniture and office equipment as needed

Provide logistical support for meetings, events, and office functions

What are we looking for?

Previous experience in facility maintenance, handyman services, technical support, or a similar hands-on role.

Practical skills in basic repairs, furniture assembly, and the safe use of tools.

Basic knowledge of building systems and the ability to identify maintenance issues.

Experience coordinating external contractors and following up on maintenance tasks.

A proactive, solution-oriented approach, with good organizational skills and attention to detail.

Ability to work independently, prioritize tasks, and collaborate effectively with colleagues.

Awareness of workplace health and safety practices.

Good communication skills and a service-oriented attitude.

Basic computer skills and good knowledge of Serbian, with basic working knowledge of English.

What additional skills will help you stand out?

Experience maintaining facilities in a corporate office environment.

Basic knowledge of electrical, plumbing, HVAC, or other building systems.

Experience coordinating maintenance contractors and service providers.

Familiarity with preventive maintenance, facility inspections, and maintenance tracking.

Experience with inventory management and organizing storage areas.

Relevant technical education, vocational training, or maintenance-related certifications.

Experience with workspace setups, office relocations, or event logistics .

About Wargaming

Wargaming is an award-winning online game developer and publisher headquartered in Nicosia, Cyprus. Our diverse and multicultural team works together to deliver a top-class experience to millions of players who enjoy Wargaming s titles across all major gaming platforms.
