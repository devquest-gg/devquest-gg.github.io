ABOUT VOODOO

Founded in 2013, Voodoo is a tech company that creates mobile games and apps with a mission to entertain the world. Gathering 800 employees, 7 billion downloads, and over 200 million active users, Voodoo is the #3 mobile publisher worldwide in terms of downloads after Google and Meta. Our portfolio includes chart-topping games like Mob Control and Block Jam, alongside popular apps such as BeReal and Wizz.

TEAM

Our Growth team is a transversal entity aiming to maximize and deliver the potential of any mobile product. It combines user acquisition, creative content production, monetization, and the tech capabilities required to turn applications into global hits. With over 150 experts, we operate at the forefront of mobile marketing and ad tech across various types of games and apps.

ROLE

Beach Bum is a dynamic and innovative gaming company that brings a fresh twist to classic games by introducing them to the world of casual online games. Beach Bum was created in 2015 by industry leaders out of a genuine passion for gaming and technology. In 2021, Beach Bum was acquired by Voodoo, opening the door to countless new opportunities and millions of players worldwide!

The Growth team is looking for a highly motivated and creative Marketing Artist/Motion Designer to join our marketing team. As a Marketing Artist/Motion Designer, you will be responsible for creating compelling and engaging visual content that will help us promote and grow our brand. You will work closely with our User Acquisition team to create content for social media, online advertising, OOH campaigns, and other marketing materials.

- Develop and maintain brand guidelines for video and motion graphics

- Create original and innovative video content that stands out and captivates audiences for use in social media, and online advertising

- Continuously improve upon existing video content by testing and analyzing performance metrics

- Participate in brainstorming sessions to develop new and creative ideas for video content

- Conduct research and identify emerging trends in video marketing and storytelling

- Become a creative expert in mobile gaming: benchmark what our competitors are doing, experiment with new ideas

- Stay up-to-date with the latest design trends, techniques, and software tools

- Manage multiple projects simultaneously, ensuring deadlines are met and deliverables are of the highest quality

PROFILE

- Be creative and have a hacker mindset

- Strong proficiency in video editing and post-production software such as After Effect but also the Adobe Creative Suite

- Ability to create and implement creative concepts that align with the brand’s identity and messaging

- Excellent communication skills and ability to work collaboratively with cross-functional teams

- Strong attention to detail and ability to deliver high-quality work in a fast-paced environment

- Bachelor’s degree in graphic design, motion graphics, or related field

- 3+ years of experience in graphic design, motion graphics, or related field

- Knowledge of the gaming industry is a plus

- 3D/animation/Unity knowledge are a +
