----------------------------------     WB Games Montreal, a division of Warner Bros. Interactive Entertainment (WBIE), seeks an Advanced Artist, Content .

Your New Role… In this role, you will work closely with the Lead Artist, Production, and other members of the team to create high-quality environmental and/or character props. You will be equally responsible for creating art assets and supporting the assignment, review, and approval of work produced by internal artists and external development partners .

You will create and refine assets for assigned aspects of the game, help define processes, pipelines, and aesthetic goals, and ensure that internal and external submissions meet the established artistic and technical quality bar. As an Advanced Artist on the Content team, you will improve workflows, provide clear and constructive feedback, and share your expertise through mentorship and hands-on collaboration.

Your Role Accountabilities...

Produce and iterate on a variety of assets through all phases of production (from blockout and basic rigging through final, engine-ready), including organic assets, hard-surface models, and more.

Aid in the assignment and review of tasks for internal and external prop artists.

Share your expertise and knowledge with team members through coaching, setting the quality bar, active and constructive feedback.

Actively participate in team meetings, brainstorms, and pipeline improvement projects; work with the team to define best practices and workflows for production pipelines.

Identify and address art and concept challenges that may impact production, providing constructive solutions.

Optimize and troubleshoot assets to ensure they adhere to memory budgets and technical specifications.

Qualifications & Experience… 8 years of experience as a games artist and having shipped at least 2 games and participated in the creation of a AAA title.

Excellent skills in industry standard software like Maya, ZBrush, Substance Painter/Designer and Photoshop.

Demonstrate an understanding of form, shape, hierarchy of visual detail, proportion, silhouette, and topology, and how it relates to rendering and deformation.

Basic knowledge of technical animation fundamentals (rigging and skinning) to ease collaboration.

Strong knowledge of the latest industry-standard tools, workflows, and techniques including physically based rendering, hair grooming, Unreal Material System, and more.

Good communication, organizational, time management and interpersonal skills Experience with a professional game engine is essential. Knowledge of Unreal Engine is a desired asset.

Knowledge of Nanite pipeline is a desired asset.

Other Requirements Portfolio/website demonstrating fundamental foundation art skills, design sensibilities, and attention to detail.
