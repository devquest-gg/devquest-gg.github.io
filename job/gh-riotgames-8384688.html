Staff Security Engineer

Riot Games · Shanghai

Lead Engineering Posted 2026-11-24 Full time

Riot Games was established in 2006 by entrepreneurial gamers who believe that player-focused game development can result in great games. In 2009, Riot released its debut title League of Legends to critical and player acclaim. As the most played video game in the world, over 100 million play every month. Players form the foundation of our community and it’s for them that we continue to evolve and improve the League of Legends experience.

We’re looking for humble but ambitious, razor-sharp professionals who can teach us a thing or two. We promise to return the favor. Like us, you take play seriously; you’re passionate about games. We embrace those who see things differently, aren’t afraid to experiment, and who have a healthy disregard for constraints.

That s where you come in.

As a Staff Security Engineer focused on Application and Game Platform Security, you will partner with product and engineering teams in China and globally to build secure applications and game experiences. You will own security outcomes across the software development lifecycle (SDLC)—from architecture and design through development, testing, release, and vulnerability remediation. You will combine hands-on application security expertise, including penetration testing and code review, with the ability to influence engineering practices and lead security improvements across complex products and platforms.

You will act as a subject matter expert in application security, help teams make risk-informed decisions, and contribute to the broader InfoSec program in China. You will report to the China Security Head.

Responsibilities

Set and drive application security direction for assigned products and platforms, translating security risks into practical, prioritized improvements.

Build trusted partnerships with engineering and product teams throughout the SDLC, integrating security early and continuously while supporting effective delivery.

Assess application and platform architectures, including iOS and Android clients, to identify trust boundaries, attack surfaces, and security risks, and recommend actionable mitigations.

Define and execute security testing strategies for complex systems, including hands-on penetration testing and focused security assessments.

Own vulnerability management from discovery through remediation verification, including triaging findings, prioritizing risks, agreeing on remediation plans, tracking resolution, and validating fixes.

Help engineering teams adopt application security tooling and practices, including static and dynamic analysis, software composition analysis, and security testing automation.

Contribute to product security standards, risk assessments, and security control effectiveness measurement in China, coordinating with relevant teams on local governance and regulatory requirements.

Collaborate with global InfoSec partners and contribute to security support and operational assessments as needed.

Required Qualifications

7+ years of experience in application security, platform security, or a closely related security engineering role, with demonstrated ownership of security work for complex applications or platforms.

Strong understanding of the SDLC, with practical experience integrating security activities into design, development, testing, release, and operations, along with programming or scripting experience in one or more languages used to build or assess applications.

Hands-on experience conducting application or platform penetration tests, analyzing findings, explaining risks, and working with engineering teams to remediate and verify vulnerabilities.

Experience performing application security reviews, including architecture or design reviews, code reviews, threat modeling, or security testing.

Demonstrated experience assessing mobile application security on iOS and/or Android, or equivalent hands-on capability to assess mobile application risks.

Familiarity with application security testing approaches and tools, such as web application testing tools (e.g., Burp Suite) and static analysis, with the ability to interpret results and guide effective remediation.

Experience prioritizing security issues based on exploitability, impact, and product context, and managing remediation across multiple teams and competing priorities.

Desired Qualifications

Experience securing game clients, game services, online platforms, or other products with large-scale internet-facing systems.

Familiarity with mobile security assessment areas, including local data protection, authentication and session handling, network communications, permissions, application signing and updates, and resistance to tampering or reverse engineering.

Experience evaluating AI-generated security analyses or recommendations, verifying findings against supporting evidence, and applying AI-assisted security tools to security engineering workflows.

Experience with security automation, custom tooling, CI/CD security integrations, software composition analysis, dependency risk, container security, cloud security, or API security.

Knowledge of information security frameworks and China regulatory requirements, including MLPS, CSL, DSL, and PIPL.

For this role, you ll find success through craft expertise, a collaborative spirit, and decision-making that prioritizes the delight of players. We will be looking at your past studies, experience, and your personal relationship with games. If you embody player empathy and care about players experiences, this could be your role!
