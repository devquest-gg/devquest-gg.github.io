ABOUT US

Scorewarrior is a game developer and publisher based in Limassol, Cyprus.

We are working on Total Battle — a cross-platform MMO 4X strategy game with a deep social system and extensive player interaction. We have 2 million monthly active players and continue to grow with the goal of becoming the #1 game in the genre.

Our team consists of 200+ passionate, talented, self-driven, and continually developing professionals who love making high-quality games.

ABOUT THE POSITION

We are looking for a UI/UX designer who focuses not only on how the interface looks, but how it’s actually used. Someone who can take on large features end-to-end — from early UX concepts to final UI — staying true to the project’s overall style and working closely with the team throughout.

You will join a cross-functional team of 25+ professionals focused on developing gameplay and monetization for mid- and high-level players.

WHAT YOU WILL BE DOING

- Improving and creating UX/UI solutions for the game.

- Designing interfaces based on documentation.

- Developing user flows, navigation logic, and interaction patterns.

- Creating wireframes, low-fidelity prototypes, and UX concepts for new features.

- Conducting basic UX research: analyzing player behavior, identifying pain points, and proposing improvements.

- Validating ideas through prototypes and iterating based on feedback.

- Drawing technical elements of the interface (icons, dice, buttons, windows).

- Drawing art for interface windows, icons, collages, and small illustrations.

- Thinking over and describing animations, and interface effects for further work of developers and animators.

- Preparation of assets and transfer to developers.

- Author's control of layout.

REQUIRED QUALIFICATIONS

- 3+ years of working experience as a UI/UX Designer

- Understanding the principles of usability of game interfaces.

- Experience working on interfaces for mobile and browser/PC games.

- Proficiency in Adobe Photoshop and Figma. Skill in drawing 2D graphics.

- Having an appropriate portfolio especially with strong UX cases.

- Desire to develop in UX/UI.

- Love games.

- Willing to complete a test task.

PREFERRED QUALIFICATIONS

- A portfolio of mid-core style game interfaces.

- Experience with 4X strategy games is a significant plus.

- Interest in mobile games and current industry trends.

WHAT WE OFFER

- Lunch compensation.

- Company-funded personal development opportunities (professional and language courses, books, etc.).

- Bonuses for outstanding results, years of service, marriage, and childbirth.

- Friendly monthly office parties and epic annual offsite company birthday and Christmas celebrations.
