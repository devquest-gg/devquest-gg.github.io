ABOUT BALOR GAMES

Balor Games is a "Triple I" independent game publisher dedicated to long-term stewardship of high-quality creative works. Formed by former leaders of Humble Games, Balor Games oversees an expansive catalog of acclaimed indie games, following the acquisition of the Humble Games and Firestoke catalogs.

Balor Games aims to serve as a cultural curator for independent games, while providing expertise, resources, and belief-driven support to visionary developers.

The Head of Corporate & Business Development owns the deal pipeline for Balor Games. You will source, evaluate, structure, negotiate and close the transactions that grow the company: studio and catalog acquisitions, minority investments and co-financing arrangements, IP licensing in and out, and platform, subscription and regional distribution agreements. You will lead negotiations directly, run diligence with support from finance, legal and product, and stay accountable for a deal after it closes, from integrating an acquired catalog to making sure a licensing partner delivers.

This is a hands-on role at a lean company. You will build the function, the pipeline and the process yourself, working closely with the co-founders, the CFO and the heads of production, publishing and marketing. It suits someone who has closed deals in games and wants to own the whole arc of a transaction rather than one slice of it.

RESPONSIBILITIES

CORPORATE DEVELOPMENT AND M&A

- Build and maintain a pipeline of acquisition targets across studios, catalogs and individual game IP, and keep it prioritized against the company's strategic and financial goals.

- Lead the evaluation of targets: commercial modeling of title and catalog revenue, review of platform and storefront performance, assessment of technical and live-ops obligations, and identification of rights, chain-of-title and contractual risks.

- Structure and negotiate deal terms, including purchase price, earnouts, revenue shares, retained economic rights and transition arrangements, and lead the negotiation of LOIs, APAs, SPAs and related agreements with external counsel.

- Run the due diligence process end to end, coordinating finance, legal, product, technology and external advisors, and present findings and recommendations to the co-founders and the board.

- Working with any sellers and our own internal teams to ensure smooth post-close integration of acquired catalogs and teams: storefront and publisher account transitions, platform and distribution contract assignments, royalty and reporting migration, and partner communications.

- Manage relationships with capital partners, advisors, bankers and brokers active in games M&A.

LICENSING AND DISTRIBUTION

- Source, negotiate and close third-party licensing deals in both directions: licensing Balor and partner IP out (board games, merchandise, adaptations, regional publishing) and bringing licensed IP into the portfolio for new game development.

- Negotiate platform and distribution agreements, including console and PC storefront deals, subscription services, cloud and mobile platforms, physical distribution and regional publishing partnerships in markets such as China, Japan and Korea.

- Negotiate co-financing, co-publishing and investment arrangements with developers, platforms and strategic partners for new titles.

- Manage the commercial relationship with licensors and licensees after signature: approvals, reporting, minimum guarantees, renewals and term extensions.

- Maintain a working knowledge of platform programs, first-party incentive deals and the terms other publishers are securing, so that Balor's agreements stay competitive.

STRATEGY AND LEADERSHIP

- Work with the co-founders and CFO on the company's growth plan and the capital allocation decisions behind it, including the trade-offs between acquiring, reinvesting in existing IP and funding new development.

- Set targets and reporting for the corporate and business development function and give the leadership team a regular, honest view of pipeline status, deal risks and expected outcomes.

- Coordinate with publishing, marketing, finance and legal operations so that signed deals are executed as negotiated.

- Represent Balor Games with partners, at industry events, and in the developer community.

MINIMUM QUALIFICATIONS

- 10+ years of experience in corporate development, business development, investment banking, private equity or a comparable deal-making role in the video game industry.

- A track record of leading negotiations and closing transactions in games: studio or catalog acquisitions, IP licensing agreements, platform or distribution deals, or publishing and co-financing agreements.

- Direct experience managing due diligence on a games business, including rights and chain-of-title review, commercial and financial analysis and coordination of external advisors.

- Strong financial modeling and analytical skills, including valuation of game titles and catalogs and construction of earnout, revenue-share and minimum-guarantee structures.

- Working fluency in the commercial terms of games agreements: platform distribution terms, revenue shares, recoupment, royalty structures, licensing minimums and approval rights.

- Established relationships across developers, platforms, licensors, and the games investment community.

- Ability to operate independently in a small team, work directly with senior leadership and communicate clearly to technical, creative, and financial audiences.

- Bachelor's degree in business, finance, economics or a related field, or equivalent experience.

PREFERRED QUALIFICATIONS

- Experience on both sides of a game acquisition: acquiring and integrating a catalog, and operating or selling one.

- Experience negotiating with major entertainment or toy licensors on licensed-IP game projects, including brand approval processes.

- Experience with first-party platform relationships (Sony, Microsoft, Nintendo, Valve, Apple) and subscription service deals.

- Experience managing earnout, retained-rights or seller-financing structures after closing.

- Familiarity with regional publishing and distribution in Asian markets.

- MBA, JD or another relevant advanced degree.

- Experience building and leading a small deal or partnerships team.

HOW WE WORK

Our values guide how we hire, make decisions and evaluate work:

- Own the Outcome. You are accountable for the deals you bring in, from first conversation to the results after close.

- Outcomes Over Activity. A long pipeline is not the goal; closed deals that perform are.

- Every Dollar Should Earn its Place. We buy and license with discipline and walk away from deals that do not make sense.

- Win or Learn. Not every deal closes. We take the lessons and use them on the next one.

- We Win as a Team. Deals succeed because finance, legal, production and marketing deliver on them together.
