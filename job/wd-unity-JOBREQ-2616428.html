The opportunity We are looking for a Software Engineering Manager to lead a new team delivering Unity platform support for a key platform partner bringing games to TV and mobile form factors. To reach every screen, we will be leveraging a combination of platforms, including streaming from Linux in the cloud, Web builds to be instantly loadable, and native iOS and Android.

The team’s goals include delivering stable platform support, realizing developer workflows for Unity users to unlock the unique features of this platform, and leading optimization initiatives to enable Unity to be efficient on the widest possible array of target devices.

This role is a combination of technical leadership and people management. The right person will attract, develop and retain high quality engineering talent at all levels. If you thrive on shaping software that is loved and used by millions, and leading the engineers who build and maintain it, then we’ve got a great job opportunity for you!   What you’ll be doing Own and drive the near-term execution and technical strategy for the team Collaborate internally with other core Unity engineering and graphics teams  Communicate your team’s vision and progress internally and externally Foster a high-performance, supportive culture by mentoring and coaching your team, actively guiding their professional development and career growth.

What we’re looking for Experience developing and managing a high-performance software engineering team Proven technical knowledge in desktop/mobile/web platform technology stacks using platform APIs to build native applications Demonstrated success in collaborating with internal Product owners and external partners to achieve alignment on technical specifications and interdependencies.

Expertise as a software engineer, and a working familiarity with C , C# and JavaScript Ability to assimilate and understand a large and complex software product You might also have (optional) Familiarity with real time 3D rendering techniques and graphics APIs including Vulkan and/or WebGPU A methodical approach to prioritizing and developing innovative optimization solutions A passion for the future of computer games or real-time 3D experiences in a variety of settings Expertise leading remote teams using Agile development methodologies Familiarity with when and how to best use AI in the software development process.
