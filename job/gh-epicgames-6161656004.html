What You ll Do

Epic Games is looking for a Technical Designer in Animation (TDiA) to join our team working across various animation systems. As a TDiA, you will use your animation sense and technical understanding of character animation to design and create gameplay animation systems that facilitate appealing, high-quality character motion. You will be the primary point of contact for animation implementation issues and any animation-related technical challenges, and you will work closely with Animation, Design, and Programming colleagues to ensure the animation pipeline operates smoothly and efficiently. You will also help identify potential issues and deploy correct solutions, using your technical knowledge to help bridge and facilitate communication between the animation team and other departments.

In this role, you will

Creation of animation behavior tools/techniques/philosophies that allow the building of complex movement systems

Design and implement animation state machines/behaviors and runtime solutions

Work closely with the Animation, Design, and Programming departments to analyze, troubleshoot, and solve in-game animation implementation issues and animation-related technical challenges

Closely collaborate with the animation leadership to maintain the style and quality bar for animations in the game

Look for opportunities to improve the animation pipeline/workflow and help provide solutions for technical animation problems, including performing training and creating documentation for the animation team

What we re looking for

A strong animation sense with a clear understanding of realistic and stylized character motion and how to achieve them with modern animation methods

Expertise with animation pipelines, including source content creation tools such as Maya, MotionBuilder, and the Unreal Engine, using our Control Rig tools

Exceptional problem-solving ability with strong written communication and teamwork skills

Familiarity with gameplay animation tools and concepts, such as blend trees and state machines; experience with Unreal Engine Animation Blueprints required

Experience with AAA game development, preferably on a large team using Unreal Engine

Self-motivation, a strong work ethic, and the ability to work independently

Coding knowledge or experience with C++ is not required
