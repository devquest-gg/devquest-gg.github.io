Get to know the role

Job Title: Associate Retail Marketing Manager (12-month FTC)

Job Summary: Associate Retail Marketing Manager supports the successful delivery of retail marketing campaigns, promotions, and events across Europe. Working with internal and external stakeholders, the role coordinates assets, agencies, retailers, and project timelines to ensure high-quality, compliant, and effective retail activations that support commercial objectives. This role is a 12-month Fixed-Term Contract.

People Manager: No

What you’ll do

Execute retail programs from planning through to launch as directed by the Retail Marketing Manager.

Own and coordinate the master asset briefing process for campaigns across the Retail Marketing team, ensuring all requirements are captured, aligned, and delivered on time.

Manage retail promotions across Europe as required, including video game distributions, promo card offers, and other gift-with-purchase (GWP) initiatives.

Maintain strong relationships with internal and external stakeholders.

Arrange and lead kick-off meetings for upcoming projects, establishing critical paths to keep stakeholders on track and escalating issues to the Retail Marketing Manager as required.

Create and manage creative briefs for agencies and US-based design teams, ensuring clear direction to reduce creative amendments, improve efficiency, and control costs.

Coordinate the onboarding of new partners and agencies, presenting brand and creative guidelines to ensure compliance and brand consistency.

Supply all necessary assets to retailers and retail marketing agencies.

Ensure all marketing materials are approved, within brand guidelines and compliant with Legal team requirements.

Raise Statements of Work (SOWs) and secure approval for scope, budget, and external partner engagement.

Assist with the planning and execution of retail events, including Trade Play events, costume appearances, and event POS, ensuring timely delivery and high-quality execution.

Assist with retail compliance and store checks.

Assist the TCG (Trading Card Game) Sales team and Retail Marketing Managers in preparing retailer summary presentations.

Maintain and keep the retail marketing activations calendar up to date.

Gather promotional performance data and produce post-campaign reports with insights and recommendations.

Compile and present competitor release reports for internal stakeholders.

Prepare merchandising briefs and collate store lists within agreed deadlines.

Maintain the PO tracker and ensure payments with accounts are processed within the financial year.

Support the Licensing team with retail executions as necessary with guidance from the Retail Marketing Manager.

Collaborate with internal stakeholders to ensure campaigns are supported through online, B2B, and B2C communications where required.

Obtain competitive cost comparisons for all projects to achieve best value without compromising quality.

What you’ll bring

Three (3) to four (4) years of relevant professional experience or a demonstrated equivalent level of expertise.

Bachelor’s degree in a related field or a demonstrated equivalent level of applicable experience.

Excellent verbal and written communication skills.

Experience in retail marketing preferred.

Excellent attention to detail.

Comfortable working in an administration-heavy role, managing project trackers, budgets, timelines and reporting.

Confident managing and executing retail marketing programmes from concept through to delivery and evaluation.

Strong organisational skills with the ability to prioritise multiple projects across Europe.

Experience working in retail stores or retailer Head Office is beneficial.

Able to work independently while also being a strong team player.

Proficient in Microsoft Office, particularly PowerPoint and Excel, and comfortable using other relevant software packages.

German language skills are a plus, with additional European languages considered an advantage.
