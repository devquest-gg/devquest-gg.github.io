Who We Are

Founded in 2005, 2K Games is a global video game company, publishing titles developed by some of the most influential game development studios in the world. Our studios responsible for developing 2K s portfolio of world-class games across multiple platforms, include Visual Concepts, Firaxis, Hangar 13, CatDaddy, Cloud Chamber, and HB Studios. Our portfolio of titles is expanding due to our global strategic plan, building and acquiring exciting studios whose content continues to inspire all of us! 2K publishes titles in today s most popular gaming genres, including sports, shooters, action, role-playing, strategy, casual, and family entertainment.

Our team of engineers, marketers, artists, writers, data scientists, producers, thinkers and doers, are the professional publishing stewards of our growing library of critically-acclaimed franchises such as NBA 2K, Battleborn, BioShock, Borderlands, The Darkness, Mafia, Sid Meier s Civilization, WWE 2K, and XCOM.

At 2K, we pride ourselves on creating an inclusive work environment, which means encouraging our teams to Come as You Are and do your best work! We are dedicated to diversity and inclusion, and want our community of candidates to reflect this commitment. We encourage all qualified applicants to explore our global positions.

2K is headquartered in Novato, California and is a wholly owned label of Take-Two Interactive Software, Inc. (NASDAQ: TTWO).

What We Need:

2K Publishing Tech delivers innovative solutions to our studios and supports all of their technology needs to ensure they can focus on building incredible games. The Senior Technical Program Manager will need to apply their experience and skillset across a variety of roles including project and program management, change management, process documentation and improvement, business requirements definition, tracking schedules, action items, forging strong relationships across the business and most importantly, is experienced with influencing and driving consensus with stakeholders and other team members. They need the interpersonal and organizational skills to navigate our complex and fast-paced environment and have an absolute passion for getting things done.

What You Will Do:

Lead Technology engagement with selected 2K studios to Facilitate adoption of Technology shared services and platforms

Advocate on behalf of studios for new features and capabilities

Communicate updates related to requested features integration

Liaise between multiple 2K departments including Studios, Analytics, Global Services, Commercial, and Marketing to ensure requirements are met holistically

Establish, maintain, and drive delivery against multiple program and project schedules

Tracking all project/program in your purview

Partner with team to establish a development timeline

Assist with forecasting and scoping tasks

Delegate work to Scrum Masters, other Technical Program Managers, and Technical Project Coordinators as needed

Partner with IT leads to identify, document, and communicate project / program scope, schedule, risks, and issues

Establish and drive successful studio deployments by reviewing test plans and scheduling demos as necessary

Proactively identify, track and drive resolution of blocking issues and non-engineering action items

Establish, maintain and drive consumption of a high-level project status dashboards and reporting for each Studio assigned

Establish and lead regular partner syncs

Transition projects into run state and handoff to appropriate team

Who We Believe Will Be A Phenomenal Fit:

You are passionate about technology and you are excited about bringing cutting-edge capabilities to empower studios to make incredible game experiences for consumers. You understand what it means to bring organization in a fast-paced environment that is minimally disruptive. You are interested in exploring opportunities to apply technology to address opportunities in the area of video game backend services, digital sales, marketing, and distribution. You consider excellence in communication to be one of, if not your strongest asset across all mediums.

5+ years in technical program management, technical production, or engineering leadership, ideally including game development and live-service operations

Hands-on infrastructure experience across on-premise and cloud environments

Video game development knowledge; shipped one or two AAA titles a plus

Demonstrated end-to-end program ownership, inception through launch to run-state

Proven ability to influence department leaders and drive consensus up, down, and across organizational lines

Agile experience a plus

Experience with Jira, Confluence, Google Suite
