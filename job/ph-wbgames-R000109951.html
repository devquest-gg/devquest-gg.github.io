LET’S GO! ㅤ NetherRealm Studios, a division of WB Games Inc., seeks a Associate Marketing Artist responsible for creating high-quality AAA renders / screenshots and other marketing materials for high profile game title(s), under the direction of the Marketing Artist.

This is an individual contributor position and requires a good understanding of lighting, composition, character weighting and posing, and all aesthetic sensibilities required in creating industry competitive marketing visuals.

In addition, candidates should have a strong understanding of current and next gen game pipelines and associated technology and software, including the Adobe suite of software. Knowledge of 3D software such as Unreal Engine preferred. A portfolio/demo reel demonstrating an ability to create high-quality marketing materials and concept/illustrations is a plus.

This is a temporary, full-time contract position through the end of April 2027 , requiring on-site work five days per week . Priority will be given to local applicants.

Major Areas of Responsibility The Daily Conceive, develop, and execute AAA marketing materials to include in-game screenshots, character renders, promotional materials and social media creative.

In conjunction with Marketing team and Art Director, help establish the creative direction for public facing visual ID for NetherRealm games.

Develop and maintain deadlines and production schedules for Marketing Art projects.

The Essentials Strong background in traditional/fundamental art principles including excellent color theory, typography, composition, lighting, and realistic character posing skills.

Solid understanding of anatomy, lighting/rendering technologies Familiar with Unreal Engine and Photoshop. Knowledge of unreal sequencer and character touch ups in Photoshop is a plus.

Capacity to learn new tools, and artistic method Shows an able understanding of fundamental technical topics, capable of implementing them into their workflow to execute assignments.

Excellent communication skills A portfolio/website demonstrating your work.
