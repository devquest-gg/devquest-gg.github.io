Job Title: Staff/Lead SDET Engineer (Test Automation Platform)

Product: Total Battle

Total Battle is a massive MMO 4X strategy game available on mobile and web, uniting millions of active players. As our product and engineering teams scale, we are shifting our testing paradigm: automated testing must become a daily, self-service engineering tool rather than a bottleneck owned by a dedicated team.

We are looking for a Staff/Lead SDET to take technical ownership of our internal test automation tooling and make this vision a reality.

Why this role exists

Your core mission is to empower product QA teams. You won't write tests; you will build the tools, APIs, and abstractions that allow QA engineers (with basic Python & OOP skills) to independently create checks, manage test states, run tests in CI/CD, and diagnose failures.

- The Product Teams own the tests.

- You own the tooling layer that makes their work possible.

[https://app.ashbyhq.com/api/images/user-content/55591132-5874-4888-add0-f14f70025dca/2aa3a273-986b-4c05-b6a2-27fee33e607f/image.png]

💡 Core Focus Areas:

1. Client Testing Tools: UI scenarios, method/component checks across Android, WebGL, and (soon) iOS.

2. Server Integration: Component testing tools built over existing Python clients, SDKs, and product testability capabilities.(Note: We aren't starting from scratch. We need a visionary who can assess our existing solutions, define a target architecture, and evolve them incrementally).

What You’ll Work On

- Drive Technical Strategy: Define the roadmap for QA tooling, evolve its architecture, and personally implement key solutions.

- Build the Platform: Develop domain APIs, fixtures, and state-preparation tools for integration/component testing over existing SDKs.

- Enhance Client Automation: Extend client UI automation and enable sub-UI checks for specific client methods.

- Unify Execution: Ensure local and CI execution follow a predictable, unified path across Android and WebGL.

- Create Diagnostics: Build clear diagnostic tools to help QA engineers easily distinguish between product, test, tooling, and infrastructure failures.

- Cross-Team Collaboration: Translate recurring QA needs into product testability requirements and establish technical contracts with client/backend teams.

- Evangelize & Mentor: Drive adoption of your tools through pilots, solid documentation, and feedback loops. (Where needed, help define engineering roles and participate in hiring/onboarding).

Who You Are We are looking for a hands-on technical leader who thrives in long-term ownership of internal platforms.

- Python Expert: You regularly write, debug, and maintain Python code for libraries, frameworks, SDKs, or internal tools.

- Platform Builder: You have designed or substantially evolved a framework/SDK used by multiple teams, bringing it to a point where teams can use it independently.

- API Designer: You know how to build stable public APIs over existing SDKs, hiding low-level details from the end-user.

- Cross-Functional Driver: You’ve successfully worked with backend, client, or framework teams to deliver testability capabilities, driving changes even when you don't directly manage the teams.

- CI/CD & Diagnostics Guru: You have integrated automated checks into CI quality gates with robust reproducibility and diagnostics.

- AI Adopter: You leverage LLMs and coding agents to investigate codebases, develop tooling, or create tests efficiently.

- Deep Domain Expertise: You have profound hands-on experience in at least one of the following (and can take technical responsibility for the other):

- Client/UI automation: Drivers, custom/non-native UI, synchronization, and sub-UI checks.

- Backend/integration tooling: Python clients/SDKs, protocols, sessions, fixtures, and test state.

Nice to Have (Bonus Points)

- Experience in both client/UI and backend/integration tooling.

- Background in modernizing legacy automation and conducting incremental user migrations.

- Experience with complex test lifecycles (data isolation, flaky checks, state management).

- Experience in GameDev or complex live-service products (Unity, Cocos/Axmol, WebGL).

- Familiarity with Selenium, Appium, Playwright, XCUITest, Android ADB, or iOS infrastructure.

- Experience with asynchronous Python, typed models (Pydantic, mypy), or schema-based code generation.
