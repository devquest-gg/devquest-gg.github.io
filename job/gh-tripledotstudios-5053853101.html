Job Summary

PeopleFun is seeking a Senior Product Manager to own retention for Wordscapes, our flagship word game with more than 100M Google Play downloads. This role will own the player journey from the first session through a long-term daily habit, and set the strategy for the features, systems, and experiments that keep more players coming back.

You will partner with the Lead Product Manager and lead a cross-functional pod to set and deliver the retention roadmap, with a focus on the new-player experience, daily engagement loops, and bringing lapsed players back. The ideal candidate thrives in a fast-paced environment, moves quickly from insight to shipped experiment, and pairs strong product judgment with a deep understanding of why players stay and why they leave.

Responsibilities

Set the retention strategy and own the roadmap for Wordscapes, partnering with the Lead Product Manager to set priorities that improve D1, D7, and D30 retention across new, existing, and returning players.

Own the new-player experience end to end, from first session and onboarding through the early levels, turning installs into engaged players.

Build and improve the daily habit loops, progression milestones, goals, and rewards that give players a reason to return every day.

Identify where and why players churn using cohort, funnel, segment, and level-progression data, and turn those findings into prioritized product bets.

Develop strategies to re-engage lapsed and returning players, including reactivation experiences, notifications, and welcome-back flows.

Lead a high-velocity A/B testing program: define hypotheses, success metrics, and guardrails; read results; and decide what ships, iterates, or stops.

Lead a crossfunctional pod of Design, Production, Engineering, Analytics, and User Acquisition partners to deliver features and experiments, balancing retention gains against monetization and long-term game health.

Move quickly in a fast-paced live-ops environment, reprioritizing as test results, player data, and business needs change while keeping multiple workstreams on track.

Share learnings across the team so each test and launch informs the next, and mentor other product managers on experimentation and retention best practices.

Requirements

5+ years of product management experience, including 3+ years on a scaled free-to-play mobile game or long-running live product.

Proven track record of driving measurable retention gains (D1, D7, D30) through onboarding (FTUE), progression, engagement features, or re-engagement work.

Hands-on analytical skills: comfortable with SQL and using BigQuery, Looker, or similar tools to analyze cohorts, funnels, and segments independently.

Experience designing and interpreting A/B tests including guardrail metrics, segment-level reads, and designing follow-up tests.

Strong understanding of free-to-play player motivation, progression systems, and the trade-offs between retention and monetization.

Ability to own a broad problem area end to end, from strategy and opportunity sizing through launch and follow-up, and to lead cross-functional teams without direct authority.

Thrives in a fast-paced environment: comfortable managing multiple priorities at once, making quick, data-informed decisions with incomplete information, and adjusting course as results come in.

Clear written and verbal communication, with the ability to turn data into concise recommendations and influence senior leadership.

Passion for casual mobile games; puzzle or word game experience is a plus.
