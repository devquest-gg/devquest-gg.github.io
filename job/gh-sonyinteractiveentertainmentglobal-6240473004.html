Why Sony Interactive Entertainment?

Sony Interactive Entertainment isn’t just the Best Place to Play — it’s also the Best Place to Work. Sony Interactive Entertainment (SIE) is the company behind the PlayStation brand. As a subsidiary of Sony Group Corporation, we’re part of a proud legacy of innovation and excellence. SIE is a dynamic technology company, delivering cutting-edge hardware and network services to more than 100 million people and an entertainment leader, home to some of the most beloved and recognizable intellectual properties (IP) in the world. Our role at SIE is to create and nurture the experiences under the PlayStation brand, a name synonymous with entertainment excellence and creativity.

Role Overview:

The PlayStation Store and Commerce platform brings together content generated by thousands of creators to millions of players around the world to discover, purchase, enjoy, and engage with the PlayStation community. Our Technical Operations organization is responsible for ensuring these experiences remain highly available, resilient, performant, and ready to support some of the largest entertainment launches in the world.

We are looking for a Senior Engineering Manager to lead a globally distributed team and take ownership for Site Reliability Engineering keeping player and creator experiences available, resilient, performant, and secure, while also managing full-stack development to improve both the day-to-day reliability, security, and long term sustainability of the PlayStation platform.

As Senior Engineering Manager, you will lead Site Reliability Engineering (SRE) and full-stack Software Development teams responsible for the reliability, resilience, quality, and long-term sustainability of critical services and customer experiences across the PlayStation ecosystem.

Your teams will partner closely with globally distributed product development, architecture, security, program management, and other technical operations teams to ensure our platforms are prepared for everyday customer demand and major global business events.

This role goes beyond traditional production operations. You will help evolve our SRE practices from reactive support toward proactive, data-driven, and increasingly adaptive operations, leveraging emerging technologies such as AI and machine learning. On a day-to-day basis you will apply strong technical judgement along with guiding and inspiring your team to achieve outstanding results in a fast-paced environment.

What You’ll Be Doing:

Own the reliability, availability, performance, and operational health of critical PlayStation services and be responsible for maintaining availability SLOs/SLIs for Partner experiences.

Establish and evolve SRE practices across observability, SLOs, capacity, resilience, automation, incident management, and operational readiness.

Partner with engineering teams to embed reliability and operability into the software development lifecycle , improving production readiness and reducing operational risk.

Lead operational readiness for major game or hardware launches, seasonal events, and high-traffic business moments , ensuring platforms are tested, scaled, monitored, and prepared.

Drive continuous improvement through incident learning, automation, and systemic remediation , reducing operational toil and improving platform resilience.

Drive adoption of industry and platform standard methodologies and practices for Infrastructure as Code, deployment, observability, and reliability.

Leverage operational data and emerging AI/ML capabilities to advance proactive, adaptive operations.

Develop engineering roadmaps balancing operational priorities, software delivery, and long-term reliability and modernization investments.

Recruit, mentor, and develop engineers and engineering leaders, fostering a culture of ownership, technical excellence, and continuous improvement .

Build strong cross-functional partnerships and clearly communicate technical health, delivery progress, risks, and priorities to engineering and senior leadership.

Lead full-stack software development teams responsible for sustaining, enhancing, and modernizing customer-facing applications and backend services.

Provide technical leadership across front-end/client development (JavaScript, React) and backend engineering (Java, Spring Boot) , emphasizing scalable architecture, maintainability, and software quality.

Champion quality engineering and test automation , including unit, integration, and end-to-end testing, CI/CD practices, and reliable software delivery.

What We’re Looking For:

Significant experience leading SRE, production engineering, or software engineering teams supporting large-scale, distributed systems.

Demonstrated experience leading one or more engineering teams , developing talent, empowering engineers, and building high-performing organizations.

Strong understanding of SRE principles and practices , including availability, SLOs/SLIs, observability, incident management, capacity planning, resilience, and automation.

Experience operating high-volume, customer-facing platforms where reliability and performance directly impact customers and the business.

Strong technical background in AWS, cloud-native architectures, Kubernetes, infrastructure automation, and modern observability tooling .

Experience leading or providing technical oversight for full-stack software development , including front-end applications, backend services, APIs, and distributed architectures.

Understanding of modern software engineering practices , including CI/CD, QA automation, testing strategies, and software development lifecycle management.

Proven ability to lead through complex production incidents , drive systemic improvements, and balance operational reliability with software delivery.

Experience developing and executing multi-quarter technical strategies and engineering roadmaps .

Excellent written, verbal, and presentation skills , with the ability to communicate complex technical topics, influence decisions, and align technical teams and executive stakeholders.

Nice to Have:

Experience deploying, operating, and supporting commerce, e-commerce, payments, digital storefronts, or large-scale consumer platforms .

Experience designing or operating multi-region, highly available distributed architectures .

Familiarity with security, compliance, vulnerability, and operational risk management at scale.

Experience applying AI/ML, intelligent automation, or advanced analytics to operational engineering challenges.

Experience preparing platforms for major global events and unpredictable traffic patterns .

Hands-on engineering experience with JavaScript, React, Java, Spring Boot , or comparable modern development technologies.

Experience with automated testing frameworks, quality engineering, and modern software delivery practices .
