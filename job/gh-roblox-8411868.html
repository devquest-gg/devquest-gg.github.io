Every day, tens of millions of people come to Roblox to explore, create, play, learn, and connect with friends in 3D immersive digital experiences– all created by our global community of developers and creators.

At Roblox, we’re building the tools and platform that empower our community to bring any experience that they can imagine to life. Our vision is to reimagine the way people come together, from anywhere in the world, and on any device.

We’re on a mission to connect a billion people with optimism and civility, and looking for amazing talent to help us get there.

A career at Roblox means you’ll be working to shape the future of human interaction, solving unique technical challenges at scale, and helping to create safer, more civil shared experiences for everyone.

Roblox’s Safety Operations - Moderation Product Support team is dedicated to protecting our users and developers from individuals who want to cause harm on our platform, within the online gaming environment. The Moderation Operations team are subject matter experts for content safety policies, and apply their knowledge to empower moderation scale across the Roblox platform. This is a critical team for our users, developers, and creators.

As the Manager of the Product Support Specialist team, you will directly manage a team of Product Support Specialists (PSS) embedded across our Safety Moderation Operations verticals. Your team owns the tactical, day-to-day execution of each vertical: queue health and service level agreements (SLAs), case escalations, vendor quality and calibration, SOP and workflow maintenance, and root cause analysis from ground-level work. Each PSS reports directly to you and has a dotted line to the Senior Product Support Manager (PSM) who owns operations and enforcement strategy for their vertical. You will partner closely with the PSMs, Vendor Ops, Policy, Product, and Engineering so your team executes with excellence and their frontline findings turn into systemic fixes.

Please note: This role may review graphic, controversial, and sometimes offensive content in line with Roblox’s policies.

You will report to the Senior Manager, Moderation Escalations.

You will:

Coach and lead a team of Product Support Specialists across Moderation Ops verticals, fostering a culture of ownership, accountability, and continuous learning.

Set shared standards and operating rhythms for queue health, SLA management, escalation handling, vendor calibration, and SOP maintenance, so every vertical runs to the same high bar while accounting for their unique needs.

Define and track success metrics for the team, including SLA attainment, backlog, escalation turnaround, vendor quality and average handle time, and root cause analysis (RCA) throughput and impact. Report performance and trends to Moderation Ops leadership.

Partner with Senior PSMs to align priorities, workload, and project work for each PSS.

Coach the team to run rigorous RCAs from ground-level work, especially after policy launches, enforcement changes, outages, and tooling or feature releases. Route findings to PSMs, Policy, Product, and Engineering so they lead to systemic fixes.

Help the team build strong working relationships with moderator team leads, quality assurance (QA) specialists, and Vendor Ops, and improve calibration, quality, and efficiency across vendor workforces.

Define and enforce excellent frameworks for procedures, playbooks, and enforcement guidance, keeping enforcement guidance in sync with policy as it evolves.

Make sure the team handles requests with professionalism and sound judgment, and that processes and tooling keep pace with workflow requirements.

Serve as the escalation point and backstop for your team’s sensitive cases, and help specialists prepare clear, accurate briefings for senior leadership.

This role requires collaboration with team members based in California, USA, and other global locations.

Plan and support 24/7 coverage across time zones, including an on-call or shift rotation that may include weekends.

This role will require on-call work at weekends on a rotational basis;

This role will involve exposure to graphic and/or objectionable content;

You have:

Bachelor’s degree in business, operations, or a related field

8 years of experience in an operations or policy role in Trust and Safety or content moderation, with a focus on moderation workflows, queue management, and escalations

2-4 years of experience managing people, including coaching and development

Experience partnering with BPO or vendor workforces on quality, calibration, and efficiency

Strong program management skills, with experience leading projects at scale

Experience developing processes to improve operational efficiency

Experience using data to lead decision-making

Strong collaboration skills, with the ability to work with cross-functional teams, specifically Product, Engineering, Policy, Legal, Public Policy, and BPO workforces

Experience with regulatory requests, compliance, or risk operations is a plus

You are:

A people leader: You hire well, coach directly, and build a team that takes ownership of its work.

Operationally rigorous: You hold a high bar for SLAs, quality, and process across the focus areas your team supports.

Analytical: You investigate ambiguous problems and measure both the problems and the solutions.

Proactive problem solver: You anticipate potential problems and take decisive action.

Collaborative: You build trust with PSMs and cross-functional partners and align priorities in a matrixed organization.

Team-oriented: You lift up your peers and find ways to make the team stronger.

Roles that are based in an office are onsite Tuesday, Wednesday, and Thursday, with optional presence on Monday and Friday (unless otherwise noted).
