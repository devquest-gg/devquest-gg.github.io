Job Overview

We are looking for a Level Artist to join our team and help create immersive, visually compelling, and optimized game environments. You will work closely with the Art Owner and other departments to bring our creative vision to life.

What will you do?

Build game levels using pre-made 3D assets and modular kits.

Create textures and decals (Adobe Photoshop required; Substance tools, AI tools are a plus).

Collaborate with the Art Director to develop the mood and storytelling of locations.

Ensure all levels meet performance and optimization requirements.

Work closely with designers, VFX artists, and engineers for seamless gameplay.

Maintain a high visual standard while balancing technical constraints.

What are we looking for?

Proven experience as a Level Artist or Environment Artist.

Strong understanding of art fundamentals: color, composition, contrast, depth, and scale.

Hands-on experience with game engines (Unreal Engine, Unity, or proprietary).

Excellent communication and teamwork skills.

Knowledge of optimization techniques for in-game environments.

Familiarity with the World of Tanks universe and gameplay will be a strong advantage.

Work mode

Hybrid (2-3 days of work from the office) or Remote (from anywhere within Ukraine).

This role isn t eligible for relocation immigration support.

About Wargaming

Wargaming is an award-winning online game developer and publisher headquartered in Nicosia, Cyprus. Operating since 1998, Wargaming has become one of the leaders in the gaming industry with 15 offices worldwide, including studios in Chicago, Prague, Shanghai, Tokyo, and Vilnius. Our diverse and multicultural team works together to deliver a top-class experience to millions of players who enjoy Wargaming s titles across all major gaming platforms. Our flagship products include free-to-play hits World of Tanks, World of Warships and World of Tanks Blitz.
