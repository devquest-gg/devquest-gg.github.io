This is an opportunity to work for a remote-first company. Must be currently living in and legally eligible to work in Canada.

Location: Remote (BC preferred) Reports To: Senior UA Growth Manager / Marketing Lead

About Kano

Kano is a successful, founder-led game studio with a 15+ year track record in building free-to-play games with loyal communities. With two product lines — mobile idle games and long-running text-based social RPGs — and a fully remote team of 40, we re focused on sustainable growth, strong player engagement, and profitable user acquisition.

We re looking for a Growth Marketer to help drive new player acquisition across our portfolio. This is a hands-on role for someone early in their growth marketing career who wants to own real campaigns, learn fast, and grow their impact. You ll manage a meaningful ad budget from day one, run and optimize campaigns across major platforms, and work closely with our senior UA and LiveOps teams to scale our games profitably.

What You ll Do

Run and Optimize Paid Campaigns

Manage and optimize paid user acquisition campaigns across Meta Ads and Google UAC, with room to expand into Apple Search Ads, TikTok, Unity, AppLovin, and other networks.

Own day-to-day management of a $100K+ monthly ad budget, including daily optimizations, budget pacing, and bid adjustments.

Support retargeting and re-engagement campaigns to improve player LTV.

Help set up and manage creative tests across static, video, and playable ad formats.

Analyze and Report on Performance

Track and report on core UA metrics (ROAS, CAC, retention, LTV) to the team on a regular cadence.

Work with analysts and senior UA to interpret campaign performance and cohort data across platforms and geos.

Help build and maintain dashboards in AppsFlyer and internal BI tools to communicate results clearly.

Set up and analyze A/B tests across creatives, audiences, and geos.

Collaborate Across Teams

Partner with the LiveOps marketing team to develop and iterate on ad creatives.

Align acquisition activity with game updates, events, and product launches.

Play our games and run light competitive analysis to spot emerging UA, monetization, and engagement tactics.

Support Organic Growth

Support ASO efforts including keyword testing, metadata updates, and screenshot/video asset alignment.

Help ensure paid and organic efforts complement each other to drive installs and conversion.

What You Bring

1–3 years of digital marketing experience, ideally including paid social or app/mobile campaigns.

Hands-on experience managing campaigns on Meta Ads and/or Google UAC.

Comfort reading performance data and a genuine curiosity about what s driving results.

Strong attention to detail and an experimentation mindset — you like to test, learn, and iterate.

Clear communicator who thrives in a remote-first team.

Eagerness to learn the ins and outs of mobile free-to-play user acquisition.

Bonus Points

Experience with free-to-play, idle, sim, or LiveOps-heavy games.

Familiarity with MMPs (AppsFlyer, Singular, Adjust) or ASO tools.

Experience with creative testing and ad creative production.

Exposure to AI tools for creative or analysis.

Basic SQL or BI tool exposure (Looker, Tableau, Quicksight).

Why This Role Matters

At Kano, you won t be a small cog buying ads to a fixed playbook. You ll get real ownership of live campaigns and budget, direct mentorship from experienced UA leaders, and a clear path to grow into more senior growth marketing roles. If you re hungry to learn how to scale games profitably, this is a place to do it.

About Kano

Since inception, Kano has released twelve gaming titles (seven of which are live serviced today), with over 35 million downloads and close to $100M in sales.

Bootstrapped in 2008, and achieving profitability in 2009, Kano continues to grow its team, game portfolio and player base as an independent gaming studio, with hit games like Mob Wars , Idle Distiller and Monster Country .

Canadian owned and full-remote, we believe in giving small teams of talented people challenging problems and getting out of their way. That’s why we look for the best and brightest to join our team; people who have talent, vision, and commitment.
