Job Title: QA Engineer - II Location: Bangalore, India Company: Lightfury Games About LightFury: LightFury is poised to become India’s premier AAA game development company. We’re driven by a passion for gaming and innovation, aiming to set new benchmarks in the industry. We value diversity and are committed to creating an inclusive environment where everyone can thrive. We are expanding our team and looking for exceptional talent eager to develop and lead in groundbreaking ways.

Job Overview: We are seeking a detail-oriented Senior QA Engineer - III with experience in testing mobile and console games developed using Unreal Engine. The successful candidate will be responsible for ensuring the game meets high-quality standards by thoroughly testing gameplay, features, and performance across multiple platforms. You will work closely with developers, designers, and producers to identify, report, and track bugs, as well as suggest improvements to optimize the overall player experience.

Key Responsibilities: Perform hands-on testing of mobile and console game builds developed in Unreal Engine.

Execute functional, regression, and performance testing on different platforms, including iOS, Android, PlayStation, Xbox, and Nintendo Switch.

Collaborate with developers and designers to identify, reproduce, and document bugs and gameplay issues.

Write detailed and accurate bug reports, including reproduction steps and severity ratings.

Verify fixes and track open issues through to resolution.

Contribute to the creation and execution of test plans and test cases.

Perform testing on multiplayer features and ensure compatibility across different network conditions.

Provide feedback on game mechanics, user experience, and performance.

Participate in design and feature reviews to provide input from a QA perspective.

Assist in improving QA processes, tools, and techniques to enhance testing efficiency.

Collaborate with external QA teams as needed for additional testing resources.

Required Skills & Experience: 3-4 years of QA experience in the gaming industry.

Proven experience testing Unreal Engine games, specifically on mobile and console platforms.

Strong understanding of Unreal Engine, including build processes, gameplay mechanics, and engine features.

Experience with mobile device testing tools, as well as console development kits (PlayStation, Xbox, or Nintendo Switch).

Familiarity with bug tracking tools such as Jira, Bugzilla, or similar software.

Strong analytical skills and attention to detail.

Excellent communication skills to articulate issues and work collaboratively across teams.

Ability to work in a fast-paced environment and handle multiple projects simultaneously.

Nice-to-Have Skills: Experience with automated testing frameworks and scripting.

Knowledge of performance profiling and optimization techniques for mobile and console platforms.

Familiarity with multiplayer testing methodologies.

Experience with submission processes for mobile stores (iOS App Store, Google Play) or console certification (Sony, Microsoft, Nintendo).

Passion for games and a solid understanding of game development pipelines.

You’ll have the opportunity to grow personally and professionally, pushing the boundaries of technology and creativity. We believe in nurturing talent from all backgrounds, offering a space where everyone can contribute to our collective success.
