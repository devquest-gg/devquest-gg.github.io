Role Summary

As a Software Engineer, you will build and maintain backend systems that power core gameplay and live-service operations. Working within an established Python and Django codebase, you will deliver features, investigate issues, and improve the reliability and performance of a live game. You will collaborate with engineers and cross-functional partners, taking ownership of scoped work from implementation through testing and release.

What You’ll Do

Develop and maintain backend features supporting gameplay, live events, progression, rewards, social features, and platform capabilities.

Build and update APIs, game logic, background jobs, caching, persistence, and internal operational tools.

Extend existing systems with readable, maintainable code that follows established patterns.

Write automated tests and work with QA to validate functionality, edge cases, and regression risks.

Investigate bugs and production issues using code, logs, and available monitoring tools, collaborating with teammates on fixes.

Contribute improvements to performance, reliability, observability, and code quality.

Work with product, design, QA, analytics, and other engineers to clarify requirements and deliver features.

Participate in code reviews and technical discussions, incorporating feedback and sharing what you learn.

Identify technical debt and operational risks, communicate concerns, and contribute to prioritized improvements.

Support releases and live operations by validating changes, monitoring outcomes, and escalating issues when needed.

What We’re Looking For

Experience Technical Skills

2+ years of professional software development experience, preferably working on backend services or applications.

Proficiency in Python and object-oriented programming.

Experience building or maintaining backend services and RESTful APIs using Django or a comparable framework.

Understanding of data modeling, database access patterns, and the role of caching in backend applications.

Familiarity with relational or NoSQL databases; experience with DynamoDB or Redis is a plus.

Understanding of background jobs, scheduled tasks, and common failure scenarios such as retries and duplicate processing.

Ability to read unfamiliar code, trace application behavior, and debug issues methodically.

Experience writing automated tests, using version control, and participating in code reviews.

Solid understanding of data structures, algorithms, and practical software design principles.

Familiarity with Docker and cloud services, particularly AWS, is a plus.

Cross-Functional and Game-Specific Knowledge

Ability to collaborate with product, design, QA, and engineering teammates to clarify requirements and deliver reliable features.

Awareness of how backend correctness and performance affect users, particularly when handling progression, rewards, or other persistent state.

Willingness to learn an existing game’s systems, business rules, and live operations workflows.

Experience with games, live-service products, game-client integrations such as Unity, or analytics and operational tooling is a plus.

Comfortable working iteratively, communicating progress and blockers, and adapting as requirements become clearer.

Soft Skills Education

Bachelor’s degree in Computer Science or a related field, or equivalent practical experience.

Clear communication skills and the ability to explain your approach, ask questions, and document relevant decisions.

Ownership of assigned work, including testing, follow-through, and timely escalation of risks or blockers.

A collaborative approach, openness to feedback, and willingness to learn from others.

Interest in games, player experience, and building reliable live services.

About Scopely

Scopely is a leading video game and global interactive entertainment company, home to many of the world’s most beloved and enduring experiences, including two of the most successful mobile games of all-time “MONOPOLY GO!” and “Pokémon GO,” along with “Stumble Guys,” “Star Trek™ Fleet Command,” “MARVEL Strike Force,” “WWE Champions,” the Scrabble® franchise, “Yahtzee® With Buddies,” and many others. Across mobile, web, PC, and console, Scopely creates, develops, publishes, and live-operates one of the most diversified and award-winning portfolios in the games industry — bringing hundreds of millions of players together through a shared love of play.

Founded in 2011, Scopely is powered by its exceptional team — including thousands of world-class gamemakers around the globe, a distinctive tenet-driven culture, and its proprietary technology platform, Playgami. Together, these strengths have fueled Scopely’s position as the #1 mobile games company in the U.S. and #2 globally, generating more than $10 billion in lifetime revenue. Whether building global sensations like “MONOPOLY GO!” from the ground up, or expanding through strategic acquisitions, including the FoxNext, GSN, and Scopely Explore games businesses — Scopely consistently delivers experiences players love today and return to for years to come.

Recognized multiple times as one of the "100 Most Influential Companies in the World" by TIME magazine and one of Fast Company s "World s Most Innovative Companies" and “Best Workplaces for Innovators,” Scopely believes that video games can be a force for good — creating meaningful connections, vibrant communities, and making life better through play.
