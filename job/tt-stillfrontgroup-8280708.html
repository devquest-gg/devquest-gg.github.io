The Multimedia Designer collaborates closely with directors, stakeholders, and team members to ensure the final product aligns with the project s vision and objectives.

Responsibilities: Video Editing and Post-production: Utilize professional video editing software, such as Adobe Premiere Pro, Final Cut Pro, or other relevant tools, to assemble and edit video footage.

Arrange clips, trim or extend sequences, and make creative decisions regarding pacing, timing, and transitions.

Enhance the visual quality of the video through color grading, adjustments, and applying visual effects or motion graphics as necessary.

Operate video and photo cameras, as well as lighting equipment.

Direct actors and technicians on set to ensure the desired visual and audio outcomes.

Audio Integration and Sound Design: Integrate audio elements, including dialogue, voiceovers, music, sound effects, or ambient noise, to enhance the video s storytelling and impact.

Edit and synchronize audio tracks, ensuring a balanced mix and proper timing with the visuals.

Visual Effects, Animation, and Motion Graphics: Incorporate visual effects, animation, motion graphics, titles, and lower thirds into the video to enhance the storytelling and visual appeal.

Collaborate with motion graphics artists or visual effects specialists to integrate high-quality effects seamlessly.

Ensure that visual effects, animation, and motion graphics align with the project s style and maintain consistency throughout the video.

Utilize 3D modeling, texturing, and animation skills tailored towards marketing and game development applications.

Seamlessly integrate visual effects into both live-action and animated sequences.

AI Tools Utilization: Utilize AI-powered tools for video editing and enhancement such as higgsfield.

Apply AI-driven techniques for audio enhancement, noise reduction, and sound design, such as Auphonic, Adobe Audition s AI features, or similar tools.

Stay updated with the latest AI tools and technologies in video and audio editing to incorporate innovative solutions into the editing process.

Artistic and Design Skills: Embrace creative freedom to propose and implement innovative ideas that enhance multimedia projects.

Translate abstract ideas, moods, and themes into strong visual concepts.

Combine different artistic influences and techniques to create distinctive results.

Market Research: Stay updated on industry trends, best practices, and emerging technologies in multimedia production and audiovisual design.

Conduct market research to identify opportunities for innovation and improvement in content creation and promotion.

Requirements: Proficiency in professional video editing software such as Adobe Premiere Pro, After Effects, Final Cut Pro, or similar tools.

Strong understanding of video editing principles, including pacing, timing, and storytelling techniques.

Knowledge of audio editing and sound design principles, with the ability to integrate audio elements effectively.

Familiarity with visual effects, motion graphics, animation, and color correction software/tools, with the ability to incorporate effects seamlessly.

Experience with AI-powered tools for video and audio editing.

Excellent attention to detail and organizational skills to manage and review large volumes of footage.

Effective communication and collaboration skills to work closely with directors, stakeholders, and team members.

Creativity and problem-solving abilities to make creative decisions and overcome challenges during the editing process.

Qualifications: Bachelor s degree in Film Production, Media Arts, or a related field (preferred but not required).

1+ years of experience in video editing, animation, or a related role.

A strong portfolio showcasing previous video editing, animation, and motion graphics work.

About Babil Games

Babil Games was created to elevate the gaming landscape for the Arabic-speaking world. We saw an opportunity to engage with the world’s fastest-growing gaming market, a community eager for content that truly reflects their culture and preferences. As such, we dedicated ourselves to filling this gap with games crafted specifically for our MENA players.

Headquartered in Dubai and Amman, we are deeply embedded in the region we serve. Since our acquisition by Stillfront in 2016, we have been part of an international network of top-tier game developers and publishers. This partnership has enabled us to enhance our offerings with global best practices while staying true to the cultural nuances that make our games resonate with our players.

Today, we are proud to deliver exceptional gaming experiences that bring excitement, engagement, and a strong sense of community to our players. By leveraging our deep MENA market insights and global expertise, we drive growth in this dynamic region.
