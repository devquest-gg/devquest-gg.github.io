Scopely is looking for a Technical Product Manager - AI t o join our Gen AI team in Bangalore!

At Scopely, we care deeply about what we do and want to inspire play, every day - whether in our work environments alongside our talented colleagues, or through our deep connections with our communities of players. We are a global team of game lovers who are developing, publishing and innovating the mobile games industry, connecting millions of people around the world daily.

Role Summary:

Scopely is seeking a strategic and execution-focused Technical Product Manager (AI Solutions) to lead the development, adoption, and scaling of AI-powered products and solutions that serve a wide range of internal stakeholders across multiple business units.

The role is focused on identifying, building, and scaling AI-driven tools and platforms that improve efficiency, unlock new capabilities, and drive measurable business impact across the organization.

Reporting to the Director of AI, this role requires strong collaboration with engineering, design, production, and business stakeholders across Scopely s global studios, as well as close alignment with Scopely s broader data and platform teams.

What You ll Do:

1.Product Strategy Ownership

Own the Product Lifecycle: Own the end-to-end product lifecycle for AI solutions, from ideation to launch, adoption, and iteration.

Define Vision Roadmap: Define product vision, strategy, and roadmap aligned with company objectives and senior stakeholders.

Identify High-Impact Opportunities: Work with stakeholders to identify high-impact opportunities for AI across business units and translate them into product initiatives.

Co-Own Prioritization: Co-own roadmap and prioritization decisions in collaboration with the Director of AI and Scopely s CTO, balancing short-term delivery with long-term platform thinking.

2.Adoption Impact Delivery

Drive Adoption: Lead adoption of AI solutions across internal stakeholders and business units.

Define Success Metrics: Align closely with stakeholders to define success metrics, expected outcomes, and measurable impact per initiative.

Track and Report: Track and report on product performance, usage, and business outcomes, ensuring solutions deliver tangible efficiency gains, capability uplift, or revenue impact.

3.Cross-Functional Collaboration

Bridge Technical Business Teams: Partner with engineering, design, and business stakeholders to define requirements and deliver solutions, acting as the bridge between technical teams and non-technical stakeholders.

Translate Needs into Architecture: Work closely with the GenAI Agentic Engineering Team to translate business and stakeholder needs into technical architecture and product plans.

Align Across Scopely: Collaborate with other Scopely teams across regions and studios to align on best practices and leverage existing platforms and solutions.

4.Delivery Execution

Manage Delivery: Collaborate with Project Management and Lead Engineers to ensure proper backlog prioritization, product specifications, and release planning.

Drive Agile Process: Drive agile development processes and ensure timely, high-quality delivery.

Iterate on Feedback: Monitor adoption, performance, and user feedback to continuously improve products.

5.AI Solutions Development

Build Across the Business: Lead development of AI-enabled tools that support internal workflows across multiple business units, including production, operations, marketing, HR, business development, customer support, and finance.

Ensure Scalability: Ensure solutions are scalable, usable, and aligned with real business needs.

Practice Responsible AI: Support responsible AI practices, including governance, transparency, security, and appropriate use.

6.Stakeholder Change Management

Drive Alignment: Engage stakeholders across multiple functions to drive alignment and adoption.

Communicate Clearly: Communicate product vision, updates, and outcomes clearly to leadership and teams.

Challenge Recommend: Challenge assumptions and provide structured, well-reasoned recommendations.

What We re Looking For:

5+ years of experience in product management or related roles

Proven experience owning the product lifecycle from concept to delivery

Experience working with cross-functional teams in a fast-paced environment

Strong understanding of AI, data-driven products, and platform-based solutions

Excellent communication and stakeholder management skills

Strong problem-solving and structured thinking ability

Experience in gaming, tech, and/or digital product environments

Exposure to AI/ML concepts and tools

Experience working across multiple business functions

About Scopely

Scopely is a leading video game and global interactive entertainment company, home to many of the world’s most beloved and enduring experiences, including two of the most successful mobile games of all-time “MONOPOLY GO!” and “Pokémon GO,” along with “Stumble Guys,” “Star Trek™ Fleet Command,” “MARVEL Strike Force,” “WWE Champions,” the Scrabble® franchise, “Yahtzee® With Buddies,” and many others. Across mobile, web, PC, and console, Scopely creates, develops, publishes, and live-operates one of the most diversified and award-winning portfolios in the games industry — bringing hundreds of millions of players together through a shared love of play.

Founded in 2011, Scopely is powered by its exceptional team — including thousands of world-class gamemakers around the globe, a distinctive tenet-driven culture, and its proprietary technology platform, Playgami. Together, these strengths have fueled Scopely’s position as the #1 mobile games company in the U.S. and #2 globally, generating more than $10 billion in lifetime revenue. Whether building global sensations like “MONOPOLY GO!” from the ground up, or expanding through strategic acquisitions, including the FoxNext, GSN, and Scopely Explore games businesses — Scopely consistently delivers experiences players love today and return to for years to come.

Recognized multiple times as one of the "100 Most Influential Companies in the World" by TIME magazine and one of Fast Company s "World s Most Innovative Companies" and “Best Workplaces for Innovators,” Scopely believes that video games can be a force for good — creating meaningful connections, vibrant communities, and making life better through play.

Scopely has global operations and partners across four continents in more than a dozen countries worldwide. For more information, visit: https://www.scopely.com/ .
