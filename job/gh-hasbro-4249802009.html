The Role

We re looking for a Manager, Data Science to help us make better, faster, and more confident decisions for Magic: The Gathering: Arena. You ll take on complex, undefined problems, develop models to predict outcomes, and guide how we use analytics to understand player lifecycle and identify growth opportunities.

This is a hands-on role for someone who can see both the big picture and the details - translating complex business questions, leading analysis projects, and helping shape our long-term player strategy. You ll partner with Arena Business leaders, Economic Analysts, Data Engineers, and Go-To-Market teams to drive data-informed decision making.

What You ll Do

We rely on our analysts and scientists to bring clarity to complexity. In this role, you will:

Analytics - Develop empirical models to investigate the MTG: Arena Player lifecycle, digital play format preferences, and interactions with analog play formats.

Modeling - Drawing on your aforementioned analyses, develop projections of revenue and engagement impact. Apply those models to inform digital game design, go-to-market executions, and play format strategies.

Ecosystem - Partner with other analysts to better understand Magic fandom from longtime fans to the new fans.

Flex - While your focus is Arena players, occasionally dive into other aspects of Magic to help us learn and grow the entire ecosystem.

Reporting - Build dashboards and performance reporting in Tableau to provide real-time visibility into player lifecycle interactions and key KPIs.

We value curiosity and initiative. We expect you to look for better ways to measure success, simplify processes, and help others make sense of complex information.

What You ll Bring

We re looking for someone who combines technical depth with business sense:

8+ years of experience conducting predictive and causal analysis

Degree in finance, economics, computer science, mathematics, or another quantitative discipline; graduate degree strongly preferred

Self-directed, ability to drive projects to completion and proactively communicate timelines and document processes

Capable of translating smart questions into rigorous research plans, and translating quantitative estimates into actionable findings for non-technical audiences

Experience conducting quantitative analyses in Python, R, or another programming language

Working knowledge of SQL

Knowledge of and familiarity with trading card games, and especially Magic: The Gathering, is a strong bonus

We know that great scientists don t just deliver reports - they help teams see what matters, ask better questions, and make decisions with confidence.
