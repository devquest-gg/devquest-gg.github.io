Senior Game Producer - VALORANT, Narrative

Riot Games · Los Angeles

Senior Production $136K–$189K Posted 2026-04-15 Full time
Apply on Riot Games’s site →

Game Producers lead teams, initiatives and products from conception through launch and operation inside of the gaming space. They create and align teams to product vision, have a deep understanding of player needs, competitors and market conditions, and are able to balance scope, quality and time to predictably deliver at a high quality bar for players.

As a Senior Game Producer on VALORANT , you’ll help with the production efforts of the VALORANT Narrative and Character teams to successfully shepherd production of Narrative in support of all game content, as well as launch new characters. You’ll help manage delivery schedules on the day to day, and facilitate efficient team organization that leads to high team morale and output. You’ll work with Narrative Leadership and Character producers, as well as liaise with other teams to ensure both run smoothly and produces world class content.

Responsibilities:

Drive and improve team work systems

Teams have clear work systems that accurately track and deliver on production efforts

Help ensures all teams have clean, up-to-date work backlogs

Facilitates meetings, discussions, and decisions

Teams have predictable and measurable capacity for work through

Cross-functional work planning with accurate estimations

Velocity tracking on sprint and release basis

Teams track and communicate progress to stakeholders and rest of team

Tracks all work (planned, unplanned, emergent) consistently

Ensures stakeholders consulted and informed on all team progress and brought in to discussions as needed for project success

End to end feature delivery tracking

Ensures teams set and meet definition of done for all work

Updates and tracks against common delivery checklist for shipping all work

Ensures strong team health, culture, and efficiency

Team work systems are efficient and adopted by everyone on the team

Monitors team morale, ensures the team has a safe and supportive environment where they can succeed.

Celebrates successes, makes sure team-building happens regularly

Resolves team problems quickly

Cascade work systems and best practices across teams

Required Experience:

5+ years industry experience

3+ years experience delivering player-facing characters to both PC and Console platforms

Experience leading cross functional teams (including engineers/designers/artists/QA) through all phases of content development

Experience with live service games or games that have had multiple post launch content updates

Experience working on a shooter / affinity for shooter genre

Desired Qualifications:

2+ years experience delivering Narrative content

Full Feature pipeline experience on character driven projects like Hero based games.

Understanding of common design, engineering, and art pipelines relevant to character creation

Experience working within a large game organization (team of 50 or more developers)

For this role, you ll find success through craft expertise, a collaborative spirit, and decision-making that prioritizes the delight of players. We will be looking at your past studies, experience, and your personal relationship with games. If you embody player empathy and care about players experiences, this could be your role!
