Get to know the role

Job Title: Sr. Network Security Engineer

Job Summary: The Sr. Network Security Engineer designs and evolves secure, resilient network services across TPCi’s global environment. This role combines deep hands-on network engineering expertise with strong security knowledge to balance risk reduction with availability, application compatibility, operational requirements, and business needs.

Working within IT Infrastructure and closely with Information Security, you will translate security objectives into practical infrastructure solutions, evaluate technologies and approaches, and develop technical recommendations. You will bring strong expertise in network security architecture and Palo Alto Networks technologies while understanding how network controls work with broader security capabilities.

FLSA Classification (US Only): Exempt

People Manager: No

What you’ll do

Own the day-to-day engineering and governance of enterprise firewall and network security services.

Partner with Information Security to translate security objectives and risk requirements into practical, supportable infrastructure solutions.

Develop network security architecture, engineering standards, and lifecycle recommendations that align security requirements with infrastructure and business needs.

Evaluate security technologies, controls, and design options and make recommendations based on risk reduction, resilience, operational impact, cost, and supportability.

Design secure connectivity across enterprise, cloud, and external environments, applying network controls where appropriate and partnering with other teams where complementary security controls are needed.

Lead complex network and cross-technology incident investigations, identify root causes, and develop durable solutions that improve service resilience.

Support threat prevention, vulnerability remediation, security monitoring, compliance, and audit activities within the network domain, and provide network expertise and analysis during security incidents.

Improve the scalability and supportability of network security through automation, operational visibility, documentation, and repeatable engineering practices.

Oversee the day-to-day technical work of contractors and professional services and provide technical guidance across complex cross-functional initiatives.

Mentor IT professionals and share expertise in network and security technologies and practices.

Participate in the team s on-call rotation, including after-hours response to critical network and network security incidents.

What you’ll bring

7+ years of network security, firewall, or network engineering experience in complex enterprise environments.

Deep hands-on experience with Palo Alto Networks firewalls and Panorama, including security policy, threat prevention, network address translation, and secure connectivity.

Advanced enterprise networking knowledge, including routing, BGP, and network segmentation.

Strong understanding of network security architecture and related controls, including segmentation, threat prevention, identity, encryption, and cloud security.

Deep experience troubleshooting complex network, security, and application issues using packet capture, logs, telemetry, and related diagnostic tools.

Ability to translate security and business requirements into scalable, supportable solutions and make recommendations that balance risk reduction with business and operational impact.

Ability to independently navigate ambiguous technical problems, communicate complex tradeoffs, exercise sound engineering judgment, and recognize when decisions require broader review or escalation.

Experience overseeing third-party technical work and collaborating effectively across technical, security, and business teams.

Experience automating network or security controls through infrastructure-as-code, APIs, scripting, or similar approaches.

Relevant networking or security certifications such as PCNSE, CCNP, CISSP, or GIAC are preferred.
