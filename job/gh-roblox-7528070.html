You Have:

Experience:

You have 5+ years of experience delivering projects that include complex user workflows.

Domain Knowledge: You have Android and C++ expertise

You are highly skilled at performance tuning, and debugging and fixing production problems.

Domain Knowledge: You have some iOS, and Windows experience. You love to pick up new technologies as the landscape evolves.

Versatility: You can adapt to any situation when the need arises and come up with the right solution.

Creativity: You take a creative and strategic approach to solving hard problems. Bringing real-time interactivity to build an immersive 3D world should get you thinking about many problems to solve… and make you excited as a result.

Professionalism: You take pride in delivering a high quality product and user experience.

Skills:

C++ programming

Experience designing refining and tuning algorithms

Troubleshooting bugs and performance issues

Background: You have a BS in a STEM related field or equivalent experience.

Bonus: Console programming experience (PlayStation, Xbox, etc.)

Bonus: Systems-level Bluetooth experience

Bonus: Experience working on a product that’s used at a massive scale (millions of DAUs)

Bonus: Server experience or experience designing/interacting with server APIs

Bonus: Experience with neighboring domains like Computer Graphics or Physics

You Will:

Develop new functionality, pipelines, and workflows, and enhance existing ones.

Help implement our next generation real time immersive audio simulation platform.

Adapt performance for the best quality on all platforms from low end phones to high end desktops.

Take a practical approach to apply research efforts into the Roblox products.

Collaborate with different teams to deliver innovative new features.

Be guided by the company vision to put the Roblox community first.
