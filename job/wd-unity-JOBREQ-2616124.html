Senior Marketing Manager, Industry – APAC

Unity · Seoul

Senior Marketing Posted 2026-07-24 Full time
Apply on Unity’s site →

The opportunity This role sits at the intersection of global Industry product strategy, APAC business needs, and local market execution. As Unity s dedicated Industry marketer in APAC, you will own the go-to-market for Unity s Industry verticals – Automotive, Manufacturing, AEC, and Digital Twins – across Japan, Korea, and APAC.Short-term (first 60-90 days): establish key partnerships with APAC Industry Sales/BD, integrate into HQ PMM/CMM planning, and land the first Japan-Korea Industry campaign. Long-term: build APAC s Industry marketing capability as a strategic function – driving pipeline growth and amplifying local customer success stories globally.The successful hire will work directly with Unity s global product leadership, APAC business teams, and country marketers across Japan, Korea, and APAC.

What you’ll be doing • Own APAC Industry campaign strategy and execution end-to-end – from messaging and channel planning through performance reporting – in active partnership with global PMM and CMM teams.

• Build structured, ongoing partnerships with APAC Industry Sales and Business Development in Japan, Korea, and across APAC, translating pipeline needs into marketing campaigns, events, and content programs.

• Lead and co-lead field events (U-Day Industry, vertical seminars, executive roundtables) in Japan, Korea, and across APAC; contribute Industry depth to anchor events (Unite Tokyo, Unite Seoul).

• Activate global Industry content meaningfully across APAC markets and develop local customer success stories (Japan, Korea, and across APAC) for amplification on the global stage.

• Serve as APAC s Industry marketing authority – bringing regional intelligence into HQ planning cycles and enabling country field teams with campaign assets and expertise.

What we’re looking for • 10 years in B2B Industry, field, or GTM marketing within tech/SaaS, with a track record of campaigns that drive measurable pipeline impact.

• Trilingual fluency in Japanese, Korean, and English – required.

• Proven ownership of integrated B2B marketing campaigns across multiple markets and channels.

• Demonstrated ability to build trusted, structured partnerships with Sales and Business Development teams.

• Solid understanding of Industrial B2B verticals: Automotive, Manufacturing, AEC, or Digital Twins.

You might also have (optional) • Experience marketing 3D visualization, simulation, CAD, PLM, or real-time 3D technology solutions.

• Prior APAC-wide marketing role balancing global HQ alignment and local country execution.

• Multilingual content production experience with external agencies (Japanese, Korean, English).

• Strong presentation and storytelling skills for events, webinars, and executive briefings.

• Passion for industrial digital transformation and real-time 3D technology.
