About Us We are a global technology company at the intersection of Artificial Intelligence and Gaming and we are redefining the next generation of interactive entertainment.

As we continue expanding across multiple markets, we are building a strong finance and compliance foundation in Boston to support our fast-growing digital operations.

About The Role We are seeking a detail-oriented Senior Indirect Tax Accountant to join our Boston finance team. In this role, you will serve as the primary owner of our US sales and use tax compliance function, ensuring multistate compliance as our digital products scale across jurisdictions.

You will collaborate closely with finance, operations, and HQ tax teams to ensure accurate reporting and scalable processes in a dynamic tech environment.

Key Responsibilities • Manage multistate sales and use tax compliance, ensuring accurate and timely filings.

• Coordinate with external tax advisors to oversee preparation and filing of Sales and Use Tax returns.

• Monitor evolving state and local tax regulations impacting digital goods and SaaS operations.

• Maintain sales tax registrations and support nexus analysis across jurisdictions.

• Support tax audits and respond to state and local tax inquiries.

• Partner with internal teams to ensure proper tax treatment for new products and business initiatives.

Requirements • 5+ years of US indirect tax experience. Prefer candidates with a background in Big 4 or in-house corporate tax experience within the Technology, SaaS, Gaming, or E-commerce industry.

• Bachelor’s degree or higher in Accounting, Finance, or Economics.

• CPA or EA (Enrolled Agent) is preferred.

• Familiarity with Sales Tax for Digital Goods (SaaS/Gaming experience is a strong plus).

• Excel proficiency to handle transaction-level data.

• Highly prefer candidates who speak both Mandarin and English.

Employee Benefits • Health, dental, and vision insurance • 401(k) plan with employer match • Life and disability insurance • Generous paid time off, including vacation, sick leave, and company holidays • Free daily lunch This position does not provide visa sponsorship.
