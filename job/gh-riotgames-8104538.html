Riot engineers bring deep knowledge of specific technical areas but also value the opportunity to work in a variety of broader domains. We work with both new and current technology, creating innovative solutions to solve complex, large-scale challenges and deliver player value.

As a Principal Software Engineer on Wild Rift , you’ll lead the development of key features and model high standards of engineering excellence. You will leverage your deep technical expertise, technical vision to enhance the player experience across the game. You ll work closely with the leadership team to set technical vision, align stakeholders across Riot to deliver high-impact results and memorable moments that last a lifetime. You’ll support and grow a cross-disciplinary engineering team. Joining a passionate and driven team, you ll help shape the premier mobile League of Legends experience enjoyed by millions of players worldwide.

Responsibilities:

Define the engineering strategy and technical roadmap to develop our global service and infrastructure with an understanding of our audience and product goals

Manage and scale multiple engineering disciplines to support product needs across the product lifecycle and ongoing live operations

Lead the long-term evolution of game services and infrastructure ownership, including transitioning capabilities into the team and building the organization and operating model to run them sustainably over time.

Collaborate in technical/architectural discussions, provide direction and drive decision-making

Partner with product and delivery as they define product features and product roadmap, and collaborate to develop strategies for improving team health and effectiveness

Collaborate and coordinate with Riot Central technology teams to efficiently deliver world-class solutions

Drive the planning and execution of engineering work including requirements gathering, technical discovery, technical design, and work breakdown and estimation.

Directly accountable for the technical success of the team including ownership of our systems, services, AI adoption, infrastructure, and devops operating well and in a cost-effective manner in live environments

Identify, interview, and hire software engineers to support Wild Rift team and goals

Support engineers with performance management, and career management and development including stretch opportunities for them to grow

Create a culture of leadership across engineering by seeking different perspectives at all levels to solve problems and contribute ideas

Required Qualifications:

BS in Computer Science, computer engineering or related field (or equivalent experience).

8+ years professional experience as a software engineer working in a game development team, Infra/SRE, or Software engineering teams

4+ years as a tech lead guiding the direction and implementation of game services, infrastructure, devops, and live operations

Experience translating a long-term technical vision into a roadmap that your teams successfully executed against, with evidence of course-correcting when priorities shifted.

Experience leading technical discussions such as technical design reviews and code reviews across organizations

Mandarin fluency required

Desired Qualifications:

Experience working in co-dev environments

Experience working on Game Client, Around game systems, Infrastructure, privacy/security, Trust and Safety and operating live services games

For this role, you ll find success through craft expertise, a collaborative spirit, and decision-making that prioritizes the delight of players and fans. We will be looking at your past studies, experience, and your personal relationship with games. If you embody player empathy and care about the experiences of players, this could be the role for you!
