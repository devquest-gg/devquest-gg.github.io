The opportunity AI is changing how games are made and played, and Unity is building the intelligence behind that shift. Our Gaming AI organization turns applied research into products used by millions of creators around the world. We want you to join us as Director, Machine Learning, leading an applied AI research team in Singapore. You will turn groundbreaking research into models and systems that ship, build and grow a team of researchers and engineers, and help shape how Unity uses AI across its platform. If you are a forward thinker who loves to build, learn, and see research reach real users, this is your opportunity.

What you ll be doing Lead and grow an applied AI research team of ML researchers and engineers in Singapore, including hiring, mentoring, and developing the team Set the technical direction and roadmap for your team s research, in partnership with Gaming AI leadership Move research from prototype to production, shipping models and systems that create real value for Unity products and creators Partner with product, engineering, and research teams across Unity s global AI organization to align research with business priorities Establish strong practices for experimentation, evaluation, and model quality Stay ahead of advances in machine learning and AI, and bring the most promising ideas into Unity s work Represent Unity s applied research internally and in the broader AI community What we re looking for Track record of leading applied ML or AI research teams, including managing researchers and engineers Experience taking ML models from research into production at scale Deep expertise in modern machine learning, including deep learning and large language or multimodal models Hands-on experience with ML frameworks such as PyTorch or JAX Experience setting technical strategy and roadmaps for a research or engineering team Effective written and verbal communication, including explaining complex technical work to non-technical leaders Experience working with distributed, multi-functional teams across time zones You might also have A PhD in Computer Science, Machine Learning, or a related field Publications at top venues such as NeurIPS, ICML, ICLR, or CVPR Experience applying AI to games, 3D content, or creative tools Base Salary Range: SGD 291,500 - SGD 437,300 Gross This range reflects the anticipated base salary for this position. Beyond base salary, this role may be eligible for equity awards and participation in our company incentive plans (such as annual discretionary bonuses or sales commissions).
