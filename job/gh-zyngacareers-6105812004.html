Join us and be part of the play! Position Overview:

We are seeking an AI Business Partner to join our team as we build, support, and implement our AI strategy at Zynga . In this role, you play a key part in ensuring individuals throughout the business are well-positioned to benefit from this exciting new technology, including collaborating directly with partner teams, deploying limited evaluations, working with vendors, developing important resources and reference materials, and assessing the overall impact of these tools.

What You ll Do:

Foster strong relationships and open communication across Zynga to educate, share best practices, and overall set our customers up for success with our tools.

Stay current on the trends and advancements in the AI ecosystem.

Work with stakeholders across disciplines (e.g. the Art community) to develop, track and report on business KPIs relevant to AI implementation

Assess the adoption and efficiency of these services to inform if we should modify the terms of our engagement, migrate to a new solution, or eventually build our own.

Be responsible for onboarding and sunsetting these vendors.

Raise issues and feature requests to our vendors.

Evaluate AI vendors for fitness, ethics, security and legality.

Assist in AI governance and compliance of third-party providers by working with Legal and other partners including maintaining the status of each vendor, tracking issues, managing tickets, coordinating meetings, and reporting to management.

Expedite across dependencies such as Legal, Security, or Procurement to reduce delays in getting tools into the hands of our internal customers.

Coordinate closely with other fields (especially Production, Product Management, and Engineering) to ensure alignment against the AI team and T2 s goals.​

What You Bring:

B.S. or equivalent experience in Computer Science, Business Administration or other relevant fields

Understanding and knowledge of AI applications and methods

Experience using or building AI

Experience in project management, production, and/or product management

Experience managing relationships with third-party vendors

Strong communication and analytical skills

Ability to manage workload and consistently achieve delivery timelines

Ability to work independently with minimal direction

Ability to challenge and influence in a collaborative way

Bonus Points:

Prior work in game development and/or customer success

Experience gathering user feedback through playtesting or focus testing to draw insights and guide decisions
