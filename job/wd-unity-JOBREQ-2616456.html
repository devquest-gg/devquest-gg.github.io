Senior Data Scientist

Unity · Tel Aviv

Senior Data & Analytics Posted 2026-09-23 Full time

The opportunity Aura from Unity is a large-scale mobile platform embedded on hundreds of millions of devices, delivering personalized content — news, video, and apps — to users at the moments that matter most.

If you love doing real applied research — training deep learning models and seeing the impact on millions of users — this is that role.

Our AI team builds content recommendation systems for news and video at massive scale, with challenges that are fun and far from trivial.

We work with advanced architectures across multimodal signals - text, video, and behavioral data - and, naturally, with GenAI and a growing fleet of agents.

We re looking for a Senior Data Scientist who loves what they do: a strong foundation in machine learning, statistics, and software engineering, a passion for modern AI-accelerated development, and the drive to own research, design, and end-to-end delivery of ML systems that ship to millions.

What you ll be doing   Research, train, and deploy state-of-the-art recommender systems using deep learning and GenAI Own projects end-to-end — from business idea through modeling, experimentation, and production deployment — including the parts that involve aligning other people around them Build online, scalable models and serving solutions that leverage massive datasets and reach hundreds of millions of users Apply multimodal and sequence-modeling techniques to real product problems where model improvements translate directly to business impact Lead cross-team initiatives, improve the tooling the team works with every day, and mentor others as you go Collaborate with ML experts, big data engineers, product managers, and developers across the organization to solve complex problems What we re looking for 5 years of hands-on experience as a data scientist or applied ML researcher/engineer A builder s mindset: you ve taken ML projects from idea to production, across domains and problem types, and you care about what ships Strong Python and PyTorch skills, with solid grounding in deep learning theory Strong analytical skills and product sense — you can frame a business problem and choose the right ML solution for it, not just the fanciest one A track record of being the person others come to: you ve guided less experienced teammates and driven projects that spanned more than one person You might also have Experience with applied recommender systems, ranking, or ads/marketplace ML Experience with large-scale training and serving (distributed training, low-latency inference) Experience with GenAI/LLM applications, embeddings, or multimodal models Familiarity with JAX or other ML frameworks beyond PyTorch Life at Unity Unity [NYSE: U] is the world s leading platform of tools for creators to build and grow real-time games, apps, and experiences across multiple platforms. Creators, ranging from game developers to artists, architects, automotive designers, infrastructure experts, filmmakers, and more, use Unity to bring their imaginations to life across multiple platforms, from mobile, PC, and console, to spatial computing.
