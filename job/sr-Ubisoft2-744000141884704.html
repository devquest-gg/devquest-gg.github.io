Data accuracy in Oracle ERP tool for Forecast, Plan, Macro-plan, Strat-plan submission.

Your main mission: Administrate the Group's financial applications (perform tests on instrument developments and corrections, run specific procedures, input/import data into the reporting systems, check and identify anomalies, track corrections and contribute to the improvement of the Group's instruments in the long term Manage the assigned processes for the monthly closing and forecast activities.
