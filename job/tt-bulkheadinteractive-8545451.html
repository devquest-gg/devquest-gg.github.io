BULKHEAD are looking for a Junior QA Tester to help ensure Wardogs delivers a great player experience. You’ll play a key role in identifying bugs, testing fixes and monitoring build quality, working closely with developers across multiple disciplines.

At BULKHEAD, our QA team is embedded within development, meaning you’ll work directly with the people building the game. You’ll stay up to date with changes, take ownership of testing features and communicate findings clearly to help the team deliver the best possible experience.

What you'll be doing: -Carry out daily test cases and report results accurately and on time.

-Identify, document and track bugs using Jira or other bug-tracking tools.

-Perform regression testing and verify fixes have been implemented correctly.

-Review and validate bug reports, improving clarity and detail where needed.

-Communicate regularly with developers about issues, fixes and testing progress.

-Help monitor build health and maintain testing output in line with team priorities.

What we need from you: -A passion for FPS games and an interest in game development.

-Strong organisation skills and attention to detail.

-Clear communication skills and the ability to work collaboratively with different teams.

-A proactive approach to identifying issues, potential risks and opportunities to improve quality.
