Graphics Programmer

Graphics Programmer

Code

India

Permanent / Remote

As a Graphics programmer at Sumo India, you will be responsible for the delivery and integration of robust, performant graphical features and associated engine and pipeline functionality, in collaboration with our multi-disciplined development teams.

Your knowledge and expertise will also be invaluable in supporting projects across a multitude of platforms, genres and game areas.

Responsibilities

Be instrumental in the development and maintenance of high performance and feature rich graphics pipelines by hands on programming.

Be responsible for the delivery and integration of robust graphical features and associated engine and pipeline functionality to fulfil varied and technically challenging project requirements.

What you ll bring with you:

Shipped multiple game titles as a graphics programmer.

Delivered at least one title in DX12 or Vulkan.

5+ years strong C++ programming experience Strong 3D math and problem-solving skills.

Strong working knowledge of shaders.

In depth knowledge of GPU architecture and contemporary graphics techniques.

Command over profiling tools (e.g.PIX, VTune).

A passion for graphics, games and game technology.

Nice to have skills and experience: Prior console development experience.

Adoption of efficient development methodologies (continuous integration, automated testing, tools pipelines etc.).

What s in it for you?

Sumo India also offers a highly personable benefits package that has something for everyone, no matter where you are in your career.

Financial: Annual bonus, an employee referral scheme, 1st time home buyer bonus, Wedding bonus.

Work-life balance: Hybrid work model, Flexible working, 22 contractual days holiday, 8 festive holidays, 4 statutory holidays, birthday day-off, 4 weeks of paternity leave, Maternity leave as per law, and options to buy or sell up to a week of annual leave*.

Health and Wellbeing: Health insurance, life insurance, Mental health service, critical illness cover*, and funded flu vaccines/eye tests.

Personal Development: Structured pathways, regular appraisals, annual salary reviews, 5 learning days per annum, access to on demand Sumo Developers Conference learning SDCXtra.

Extra Perks: A variety of in-studio perks, regular care packages, studio days out (family welcome), sabbaticals (at 5 years of service), and an annual gaming subscription.
