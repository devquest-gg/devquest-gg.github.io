Lead / Principal Gameplay Programmer

Black Banner Studios · České Budějovice · Remote

Lead Engineering Remote Posted 2026-11-20 Full time

Legacy of Valor is a medieval RPG and strategy game combining large-scale, real-time battles, third-person melee combat, and tactical army control in Unreal Engine 5. At Black Banner Studios, we are looking for an experienced gameplay programmer to develop new gameplay systems and improve existing ones. You will work closely with our technical lead, designers, animators, and other developers, taking technical ownership of gameplay features from design through implementation and polish. Our proprietary Large Army technology already supports thousands of soldiers fighting simultaneously. We are currently developing our vertical slice, focusing on third-person combat, battlefield AI, and the interaction between direct character control and tactical army commands. If you believe you'd be a great fit for this role, even if you don't meet every requirement listed below, we'd still love to hear from you. Please don't hesitate to apply! Take technical ownership of gameplay systems from design through release. Prototype, implement, and refine new gameplay mechanics. Write robust, high-performance, maintainable C++ code. Improve existing systems, debug issues, and optimize performance. Conduct thorough code reviews and maintain the technical quality of the codebase. Work closely with designers, animators, and other developers. At least two shipped games in which you took technical ownership of major gameplay systems from design through release. Excellent C++ skills, including the ability to understand and review complex code. Strong debugging, profiling, and optimization skills. Practical experience with Unreal Engine 4/5 or a comparable C++-based engine. Experience working in large, existing codebases and building new systems. Broad experience across gameplay areas. Openness to AI-assisted development workflows, with a strong emphasis on code quality, performance, and maintainability. Extensive experience in at least one of the following areas: Character gameplay: First- or third-person character controls, cameras, movement, combat, weapons, abilities, and interactions. Large-scale unit gameplay: Large-scale battles, unit behaviors, tactical commands, formations, pathfinding, and crowd movement. Passion for video games, particularly medieval RPGs, open-world games, and strategy titles. Experience integrating gameplay systems with complex animation, locomotion, or mounted combat mechanics. Experience integrating direct character control with tactical army command systems. Experience with Unreal Engine gameplay frameworks, including Gameplay Ability System (GAS), AI frameworks, or third-party combat systems. Experience developing strategy, action RPG, melee combat, or open-world games. Experience working on AAA projects. Multiplayer gameplay and network synchronization experience. Location: Remote / International, with the option to work in the Czech Republic. Engagement: Full-time, long-term collaboration. Contract: Contractor / B2B worldwide, or direct employment for candidates residing in or willing to relocate to the Czech Republic. Start Date: As soon as mutually agreed. Language: Professional working proficiency in English. Compensation: Competitive, based on experience and expertise.
