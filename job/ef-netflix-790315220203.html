Infrastructure Familiarity: Experience with model fine-tuning, and managing the end-to-end ML lifecycle (data prep to deployment). Problem Solver: You thrive in the "grey area" of emerging tech and can pivot quickly when a specific model or approach doesn't meet the needs of the game design.
