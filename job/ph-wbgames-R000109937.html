We’re looking for an exceptionally talented Senior Technical Artist to join the Rocksteady team.

You’ll collaborate with developers across the studio to create high-quality, intuitive user interfaces that contribute to an engaging and enjoyable player experience.

You will support our large art team to bring our game’s world to life. You will be working closely with both art and tech teams to find the best balance between fidelity and performance and will play a key role in monitoring for and diagnosing performance bottlenecks related to content.

Who you are You have a passion for really digging deep into technical problems related to game visuals – whether that’s optimization or just understanding WHY that one material graph node costs you 200 shader instructions, and you don’t let it go until you’ve seen the shader disassembly directly in PIX or RenderDoc to find out.

You build strong, collaborative relationships with the art teams you support, helping them simplify requirements to reduce technical risk or identify workarounds without compromising visual quality. When useful, you can also create editor or DCC tools to help them work more efficiently.

What you will do - Strong understanding of shaders for Characters and Environments in Unreal 5, especially within the context of Lumen, Nanite and MegaLights - Proficient understanding of the performance characteristics of different content types, and how to scale their performance footprint for different platforms - Ability to investigate and diagnose performance bottlenecks or bugs, and work with the tech teams to propose or implement fixes and improvements - Ability to nurture a strong relationship with the art teams we support, and to collaborate with them to maintain that delicate balance between fidelity and performance - Understanding of asset pipelines and the logistical challenges of AAA game development, and how to work within those guidelines - Understanding of the core workings of Unreal Engine 5 and its editor toolkit  - Drive to mentor others and share knowledge in your area of expertise - Commitment to championing best practices, continuously monitoring how your shaders and tools are used, and encouraging others to follow them       Onsite Working - This role is advertised as Onsite, meaning all working hours and days (regardless of shift pattern) will be carried out at the place of work.
