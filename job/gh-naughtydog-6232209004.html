Associate Producer

Naughty Dog · Santa Monica

Entry Production Hybrid Posted 2026-10-23 Full time
Apply on Naughty Dog’s site →

This position is for temporary engagement through our staffing partner, Yoh Enterprise Solutions.

Candidates must be authorized to work in the United States for this position.

This is a hybrid / onsite position.

Naughty Dog is seeking an Associate Producer to play an integral role in organizing and coordinating our AAA projects. This candidate will help develop the studio s production processes and assist in the management of the day-to-day coordination across multiple disciplines.

What you will do :

Partner with the production team and discipline leads to develop and maintain specific project efforts and discipline plans

Schedule and maintain tasking of departmental and interdisciplinary teams Break down large complex tasks into meaningful sub-tasks and track team progress

Manage communication between disciplines and team members for specific project efforts

Identify all groups that may be affected by an aspect of the project, and involve them - proactively solicit their input and follow up through execution

Help onboard new hires, and work with Production Leadership to mentor junior members of the Production team

Improve our games by participating in internal playtests and communicating feedback to the relevant stakeholders

Work with the tools team to advance the development of our production-related software

Additional responsibilities may be assigned as needed

What skills you will use:

Minimum of 2 years of relevant work experience in game production with one or more published titles

Experience with Microsoft Office/GSuite, and task tracking software

Intermediate experience with spreadsheets

Excellent organizational and prioritization skills, attention to detail, process knowledge and ability to multitask

Communication skills with both large groups and individuals, with excellent written and oral communication abilities

Eagerness to learn new software tools and technologies

Bonus Skills:

AAA experience

Working knowledge of project schedules and milestone documentation, monitoring the progress of department milestones and individual tasks against those schedules

Knowledge of project management methodologies such as Waterfall, Agile, etc Experience with a variety of project management tools (Jira, Shotgun, Microsoft Project, or any other scheduling/task-tracking software)
