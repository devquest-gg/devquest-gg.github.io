Job Overview

Are you a curious, highly engaged, and experienced visual creative who is immersed in fantasy art and passionate about pop culture? Are you interested in working within an established brand/IP while constantly looking to grow the brand’s audience and visual expression?

We’re looking for a Contract Art Director to join our award-winning team. You will guide the collaboration between the identity of Dungeons Dragons (D D) and a partner s IP through commissioning and strategic, hands-on conceptual development.

Location and Work Arrangement

This is a remote role open to candidates who reside and will work in an eligible U.S. state. Currently, we are unable to employ candidates working from Alaska, Colorado, Idaho, Montana, North Dakota, Vermont, or Washington, D.C.

What You ll Do

Research, brainstorm, and understand partner brand IP(s), creating authentic and resonant visual direction for Tabletop Role-Playing Game (TTRPG) products.

Review print-ready files to ensure final print quality (books, cards, game pieces, marketing materials, etc).

Strategize, review, and lead Art Orders and Concept Art Pushes for D D products, collaborating with key stakeholders.

Commission, critique, and track high volumes of artwork to populate D D products, ensuring assets are crafted efficiently and with industry-leading quality standards in mind.

Complete daily feedback cycles and approvals for freelance artists and internal teams by maintaining server structure and art submissions from artists and clients, and updating tracking systems with notes and status updates.

Think strategically about the artwork you commission and how it represents D D’s and our partner s IP identities, and enhances the player experience.

Provide creative direction to both internal and external partners by curating references and pitching the vision to partners.

What You ll Bring

4+ years of relevant, creative-focused experience, especially in directing and commissioning artists across multiple subject areas and working with existing IPs.

Strong communication and organizational skills, attention to detail, and comfortable giving and receiving feedback.

General knowledge of Pre-Press processes for book and other printed products.

A proactive “yes, and” approach to concepting, feedback, and mentorship. You are a strong visual storyteller and collaborator at heart, using loose sketching, paint-overs, and other artist-focused methodologies to communicate direction.

A genuine and contagious love for Dungeons Dragons, tabletop, and pop culture.

Experience with licensed products, entertainment franchises, external IP, brand extensions, or partner approvals.

The ability to prioritize and multi-task in fast-paced environment that revolves around multiple product development calendars.

Adept in Adobe InDesign and Photoshop, proficient in Illustrator.
