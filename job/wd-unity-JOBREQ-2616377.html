Unity · IND-Bengaluru

We seek a skilled Manager, IT Operations to join our remote helpdesk team and support all Unity s employees. As the first point of contact, you ll play a vital role in ensuring the smooth operation of Unity s technology systems.

In this fast-paced, 24/7 environment, you ll diagnose and resolve software and hardware problems, provide technical assistance, work with the IT team on new technology solutions and system maintenance, and identify areas for improvement.

Your excellent communication skills will be critical, as you ll need to explain technical information to non-technical individuals effectively. The goal of this role is to provide outstanding customer service in a timely manner.

What you’ll be doing Lead and mentor Unity s first-line internal support team, helping each person grow while keeping the team s day-to-day running smoothly.

Receive and respond to customer requests via our ticketing system with timely, accurate, and courteous replies.

Prioritize, triage, troubleshoot, and solve requests, or when unable to assist, escalate to the relevant team.

Keep a finger on the pulse of the company, spotting patterns in requests, surfacing recurring pain points, and turning that insight into better support Find and build opportunities for automation, and grow our IT Knowledge Base with clear, useful documentation.

What we’re looking for Proven ability to lead, coach, and manage a small support or helpdesk team An IT-related degree or relevant professional experience Experience with both macOS and Windows computers Excellent customer service skills, and outstanding communication skills in English, both oral and written.

Good at adapting to change, delays, and unexpected events You might also have Good knowledge of networking and experience with network troubleshooting SaaS administration experience Google Workspace & Slack preferred Linux experience Relocation support is not available for this position.

Work visa/immigration sponsorship is not available for this position.
