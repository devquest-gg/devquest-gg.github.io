HR Manager

Easybrain · Limassol

Mid People & Ops Hybrid Posted 2018-10-26 Full time
Apply on Easybrain’s site →

Easybrain is looking for a skilled HR Manager for our Cyprus office, who will be a part of our current HR team and responsible for our HR processes and new recruitments. This is a great opportunity for you to make a meaningful impact on our growing team that develops top-notch products.

Responsibilities:

- Recruiting, pre-screening, on-site/online interviews;

- Coordinating adaptation and performance;

- Working with current HR procedures and policies and implementing new ones;

- Advising management on personnel management issues;

- Handling relocation and immigration processes for new employees;

- Providing support to employees and resolving any HR-related issues;

- Organising corporate and social events;

- Preparing various reports and ad-hoc analysis;

- Supervising health and safety policies.

Requirements:

- 3+ years of experience in HR field, preferably in an international IT/gaming company;

- Managerial skills;

- Great interpersonal and communication skills;

- Russian language is a must;

- Very good command of the English language;

- Good command of the Greek language will be a strong advantage;

- Knowledge of Cyprus Labour and Immigration Law and other requirements of Governmental Authorities;

- Computer Skills.

Benefits:

Besides the engaging tasks, support from experienced colleagues, challenge, and drive, we offer:

- High-end market salary with performance bonuses;

- All needed equipment;

- Regular company events and monthly Friday meetings;

- Social benefits (private medical cover, sports reimbursement, etc.);

- Paid vacations, sick days;

- English or Greek online language classes;

- Reimbursement for education and professional development.
