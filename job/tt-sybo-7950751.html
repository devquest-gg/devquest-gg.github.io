Skilled at designing, rendering, and animating high-quality UI elements that bring personality and clarity to the player experience.

Advanced proficiency with modern UI/UX and prototyping tools, particularly Figma.

Experience working with Unity and a solid understanding of UI implementation Bonus Points Knowledge of conducting user research and translating player insights into actionable design improvements.

Experience working on large-scale live service mobile games.
