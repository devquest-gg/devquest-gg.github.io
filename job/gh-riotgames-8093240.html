Riot Games Shanghai is both our China publishing headquarters and a world-class development studio shaping the future of play. Established in 2015 with just 20 Rioters, we have grown into a team of over 200 passionate professionals across game development, publishing, entertainment, enterprise, and esports, all working together to craft incredible experiences for players in China and around the world.

Located in downtown Shanghai with a stunning view of the Bund, our office is a hub for creativity, innovation, and collaboration. With the launch of Shanghai Game Studio in 2021 and the expansion of the China Game Studio Group in 2022, we continue to grow our capabilities and build games that will inspire players for decades.

We’re looking for humble but ambitious, game-loving professionals who challenge convention, take play seriously, and are ready to help us make bold ideas a reality.

That s where you come in.

Recruiters at Riot are trusted partners who blend craft expertise with strategic insight to shape how we find and hire world-class talent. We build strong partnerships with our teams, deeply understand their needs, and guide them through every stage of the hiring journey. Our recruiters act as brand ambassadors, problem solvers, and advocates for an outstanding candidate experience, while continually improving how we recruit across Riot.

As a Recruiter - Games (Contract) on the China Game Studio Recruiting Team , you will lead recruiting efforts for both contract and full-time positions supporting our game development teams. You ll partner closely with hiring managers and cross-functional stakeholders to attract and hire exceptional talent across a wide range of disciplines. Drawing on your recruiting expertise, you ll develop sourcing strategies, advise hiring teams on market insights and hiring approaches, and deliver an outstanding candidate experience throughout the recruitment lifecycle. Your work will play a critical role in building high-performing teams that create exceptional experiences for players around the world.

You will report to the APAC Recruiting Lead.

Responsibilities:

Lead full-cycle recruitment for both contract and full-time hiring across multiple game development disciplines.

Partner with hiring managers and business stakeholders to develop hiring strategies and deliver against hiring goals.

Build and maintain diverse, high-quality talent pipelines through proactive sourcing of passive candidates globally.

Manage the end-to-end recruitment process, including intake meetings, sourcing, interviewing, offer management, and onboarding coordination.

Deliver an exceptional candidate experience through clear communication, thoughtful engagement, and efficient hiring processes.

Partner with People team stakeholders to continuously improve recruiting operations, onboarding, and workforce planning initiatives.

Leverage recruiting metrics, market insights, and talent intelligence to inform hiring strategies and improve recruiting outcomes.

Employment Type: employment contract with FESCO Adecco, a professional personnel company in China
