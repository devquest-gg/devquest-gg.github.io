UX DESIGN

What We Do

We design customer and user-focused experiences that are fresh and innovative, while problem solving by anticipating user behavior and hurdles. We approach the most complex of problems and address them with simple, intuitive, and useful solutions.

What You ll Do

As an experienced Senior Technical UI Designer, you will work closely with UX Designers, UI Artists, and UI Engineers to implement UI systems and features. Much of our work starts undefined, and you will help to shape the direction. You will raise the bar for the discipline through the standards you set, the workflows you improve, and the people you mentor. Above all, you advocate for the player experience.

In this role, you will

Break a feature or wireframe down into clear, scoped tasks, surfacing unknowns and dependencies early

Prototype and prove out new UI techniques, pushing the limits of UMG and shaping the patterns the team adopts

Architect robust, reusable component systems that stay flexible as designs change

Implement data-driven UI through ViewModels and view bindings, working closely with UI Engineering

Build for every player, accounting for screen sizes and safe areas, localization, and accessibility

Design within performance and memory budgets, profiling the cost of UMG, Slate, C++, and texture memory

Collaborate closely with UX, UI Art, and Engineering, improving workflows, pipelines, and documentation, and giving feedback as readily as you accept it

What we re looking for

You are a project expert in technical UI implementation, having previously built UI in game engines that has shipped to a large audience across PC, console, and mobile on a regular basis. Comparable experience with transferable skills is welcome

You have hands-on Unreal and UMG experience, or strong experience in another game engine, custom or commercial, and a real appetite to go deep in Unreal

You are at your best in ambiguity, quickly picking up new and sparsely documented toolsets and technologies like AI, finding a path where there is no precedent, and asking the questions no one else has thought to ask

You have proven experience with data-driven UI and the separation of data from presentation, through MVVM and ViewModels or an equivalent, and have strong component-architecture instincts, designing for flexibility by nature

You have a strong visual eye and working knowledge of UX and UI, layout, composition, hierarchy, and interaction patterns, reflexively anticipating states, edge cases, overflow, localization, dynamic content, and responsive scaling

You have a strong feel for controller-first navigation and for building UI that works across input methods and platforms

You design with performance and runtime cost in mind, comfortable profiling and working to a budget

You have real craft in UI materials and motion, or the adjacent skill and appetite to grow it quickly.

You translate fluidly across UX, UI Art, Engineering, production, and leadership, meeting non-technical teammates where they are, and documenting work so a distributed team can build on it

You have solid programming fundamentals, object-oriented design, data structures, and game systems, and you are comfortable with the math UI and motion work depend on, such as transforms, geometry, and interpolation
