THE CHALLENGE

The Director, Capital Projects is responsible for the successful delivery of the company’s global capital project portfolio, leading projects from initial concept and programming through design, procurement, construction, commissioning, and closeout.

This role provides strategic project leadership while maintaining direct accountability for scope, schedule, budget, quality, risk, procurement, and execution across a diverse portfolio of workplace, office, infrastructure, renovation, expansion, and other real estate capital projects.

The Director partners closely with Real Estate leadership, business stakeholders, Workplace, Facilities, Finance, IT, Security, Procurement, Legal, and external consultants and contractors to translate business requirements into well-planned and successfully executed capital projects.

The role leads and develops project management resources and establishes consistent project delivery standards, processes, and controls across the portfolio. The Director serves as the senior project delivery escalation point for complex projects and provides executive leadership with clear visibility into project status, risks, financial performance, and key decisions.

Lead the delivery of complex capital projects across the company’s global real estate portfolio.

Oversee and/or run projects from initial programming and feasibility through design, permitting, procurement, construction, commissioning, and closeout.

Establish project objectives, scope, schedule, budget, quality expectations, and success criteria.

WHAT YOU BRING

Bachelor’s degree in Architecture, Engineering, Construction Management, Real Estate, or related discipline.

10+ years of progressive experience managing complex capital projects, preferably within a corporate real estate, technology, workplace, or similarly complex environment.

5+ years of experience leading project managers or multidisciplinary project teams.

Demonstrated experience delivering complex projects from programming through construction and closeout.

Strong knowledge of design, construction, procurement, project controls, budgeting, scheduling, and contract administration.

Demonstrated experience managing large project budgets and multiple concurrent projects.

Strong understanding of building codes, permitting, construction practices, and regulatory requirements.

Exceptional communication, negotiation, leadership, and stakeholder-management skills.

Demonstrated ability to manage competing priorities in a fast-paced environment.

Strong analytical and problem-solving skills.

PMP, architecture, engineering, construction, or other relevant professional certification preferred.
