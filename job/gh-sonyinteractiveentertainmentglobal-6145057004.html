Audio and UX Software Engineer - SW

We are building a small team to create new products for the PlayStation community. Our team recently delivered the new PlayStation Link, PULSE Explore and PULSE Elite products, recently announced the FlexStrike and PULSE Elevate products, and are hard at work on what comes next. We are looking for someone with a data driven mindset, physics background interested or experience with any of the following: audio, embedded firmware, UI/UX and gaming intersection. If you are keen on being methodical, data driven, software and low level embedded firmware to bring together critical thinking, clear communication,

Come and play with us!

Responsibilities

In this role, you will lead firmware development for high profile product development efforts. You will be responsible for technical leadership, system architecture, and execution across the firmware domain, working closely with internal teams and external partners.

· Contribute to UI/UX development to support audio devices

· Contribute to developing audio device testing to improve the utilization of audio DSP and develop algorithmic improvements

· Own build and smoke testing process with mentorship from senior engineers

· Author clear specifications for code you contribute to

· Contribute to developing unit test framework

· Communicate clearly, commit and deliver on deadlines

· Support manufacturing builds and coordinate firmware activities during product bring-up

· Support products post-launch

Skills

· C/C++ embedded software development

· Experience with UI development – React or other frameworks;

· Knowledge of frequency domain analysis of audio, video streaming

· Knowledge and/or experience with RF/wireless systems (e.g., Bluetooth, low-latency audio, or similar technologies)

· Strong system-level thinking across hardware and software boundaries

· Strong problem-solving and debugging skills to drive iterative design process

Qualifications

· Bachelors or Masters in electrical engineering, Computer Engineering, Computer Science, Physics or related field (or equivalent experience)

· 1-2 years of experience in embedded firmware development

· Excellent written and verbal communication skills

· Critical thinking and data driven mindset

· Passion for gaming, audio, digital entertainment and consumer electronics is a plus

Nice to Have

· Experience with wireless audio or ultra-low latency systems

· Experience with kernel or driver development (Linux, Windows, or similar)

Working Conditions

· Small team environment; feels like a start-up within a large organization
