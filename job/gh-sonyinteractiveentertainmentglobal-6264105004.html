Revenue Operations Business Analyst San Mateo, CA (hybrid)

Role Overview

The Revenue Operations Business Analyst is a highly analytical and collaborative individual contributor responsible for transforming data into actionable insights, improving operational efficiency, and supporting the administration and ongoing management of revenue systems across PlayStation Media Solutions.

This role partners closely with Revenue Operations, Sales, Campaign Management, Finance, Product, and other cross-functional teams to support strategic decision-making, resolve operational challenges, and deliver scalable business solutions. The ideal candidate combines strong analytical and technical capabilities with intellectual curiosity, business acumen, and a passion for continuous improvement.

Key Responsibilities

Business Analysis, Reporting Insights

Analyze business, operational, and revenue data to identify trends, opportunities, and actionable recommendations.

Develop dashboards, reports, and executive-facing insights that support decision-making across Media Solutions.

Partner with stakeholders to define reporting requirements, success metrics, and business needs.

Translate complex data into clear narratives and recommendations for technical and non-technical audiences.

Support planning, forecasting, and business performance reviews through data gathering, validation, and analysis.

Collaborate with cross-functional partners to ensure accurate, reliable data supports business performance reporting.

Understand data structures, relationships, and dependencies across CRM, order management, finance, and reporting platforms.

Develop an understanding of data flows and dependencies between CRM, Order Management, finance and reporting platforms.

Process Improvement Operational Excellence

Independently manage small to medium-sized operational projects and system enhancements from requirements gathering through implementation, documentation, and completion.

Evaluate workflows and business processes to identify opportunities for efficiency, scalability, and improved user experience.

Serve as a Salesforce administrator, supporting system configuration, user management, permissions, troubleshooting, and ongoing enhancements.

Own the day-to-day administration, maintenance, and operational support of assigned revenue systems, ensuring data integrity and system reliability.

Manage and resolve Revenue Operations support tickets, troubleshoot system, workflow, and data-related issues, and coordinate escalations with technical teams as needed.

Partner with technical teams and system stakeholders to support integrations, data workflows, and system improvements.

Perform user acceptance testing, document processes, and support training and adoption of new solutions.

Required Experience Qualifications

Bachelor s degree in Business, Analytics, Economics, Marketing, Information Systems, or a related field preferred

4+ years of experience in business analysis, revenue operations, sales operations, advertising operations, or a similar analytical role.

Salesforce Administrator certification or equivalent hands-on administration experience.

Proficiency in SQL, with experience querying enterprise data environments and working with relational data tables, schemas, and joins.

Experience working with large datasets, business intelligence platforms, and reporting tools.

Experience building dashboards and reports using Domo, Tableau, Looker, or similar BI platforms.

Demonstrated ability to gather requirements, solve business problems, and deliver scalable solutions.

Familiarity with ticketing systems, change management, and system support processes.

Strong analytical, communication, and stakeholder management skills, with the ability to work independently.

Demonstrated ability to balance competing priorities, adapt to shifting business needs, and manage workloads in a fast-paced, high-growth environment.

Preferred Qualifications

Experience within advertising, media, gaming, entertainment, or technology industries.

Familiarity with Snowflake or similar data warehouse platforms and experience working with CRM, OMS, and other enterprise revenue systems.

Experience with ETL processes, workflow automation, or data transformation tools.

Exposure to Python or similar scripting languages used for analytics and automation.

Knowledge of advertising sales processes, performance metrics, and revenue operations best practices.
