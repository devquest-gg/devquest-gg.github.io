We’re on a mission to connect a billion people with optimism and civility, and looking for amazing talent to help us get there.

A career at Roblox means you’ll be working to shape the future of human interaction, solving unique technical challenges at scale, and helping to create safer, more civil shared experiences for everyone.

As a Safety Threat Investigator on the Safety Investigations team, you will primarily support Roblox’s Core Investigations capability: the operational backbone for repeatable, high-sensitivity matters that require speed, consistency, evidence discipline, and strong documentation. You will independently manage investigations from intake through resolution and produce clear, defensible work product that supports high-confidence safety decisions.

You will also identify when a matter requires deeper attribution, network-centric analysis, cross-platform research, proactive threat hunting, or externally meaningful work product, and partner with or escalate to Specialized Investigations as appropriate. At the CL9 level, you will own a defined safety-risk domain or pod-level workstream, improve investigative processes and standards, serve as a subject-matter expert, and mentor other investigators with limited day-to-day oversight.

Please note: This role may require reviewing graphic, controversial, and offensive content in accordance with Roblox’s handling policies. It may also require participation in an on-call rotation during weekends and holidays.

You Will

Own repeatable, high-sensitivity investigations involving serious safety threats, including child exploitation, violent extremism, organized fraud, compromised accounts, coordinated abuse, and other high-risk matters.

Apply structured investigative methods, evidence checklists, common templates, and approved data pulls to produce timely and consistent case outcomes.

Gather, preserve, analyze, and corroborate evidence while maintaining accurate provenance, confidentiality, and auditability.

Define investigative questions, scope, hypotheses, evidence plans, milestones, risk controls, and exit criteria for sensitive or ambiguous matters.

Conduct focused account, entity, and timeline linkage as needed, and identify when a case requires deeper attribution, network mapping, or cross-platform investigation.

Escalate cases to Specialized Investigations when they develop meaningful attribution complexity, broader network risk, external actionability, or a need for deeper synthesis.

Prepare clear case summaries and standardized downstream work product for partners such as Legal, Safety Intelligence, Trust Safety Operations, Law Enforcement Operations, or NCMEC, as appropriate.

Track recurring patterns, operational pain points, and escalation trends, and translate findings into recommendations for Policy, Product, Engineering, Detection, Legal, and Safety Operations.

Lead or contribute to durable improvements such as investigation playbooks, evidence standards, intake criteria, triage processes, tooling requirements, and quality-review mechanisms.

Represent Safety Investigations with cross-functional stakeholders, explain and defend investigative decisions, navigate disagreement, and maintain alignment on sensitive matters.

Mentor investigators, provide constructive feedback on casework, and raise the team’s standards for investigative rigor, documentation, and inclusive collaboration.

Maintain strong case hygiene, document decisions and approvals, and ensure investigations are closed with clear outcomes, residual-risk ownership, and follow-up actions.

Deliver rigorous, timely, and consistent outcomes on high-sensitivity investigations.

Apply sound judgment in triage, lane assignment, escalation, and case closure.

Improve the team’s investigative methods, operating processes, templates, and quality standards.

Convert case findings into concrete safety improvements and upstream action.

Build trust with cross-functional partners and mentor others to strengthen the team’s investigative capability.

You Have

4+ years conducting sensitive investigations involving severe safety, abuse, fraud, security, threat, or other high-risk matters.

Demonstrated ability to independently manage investigations end-to-end, including intake, scoping, evidence development, analysis, findings, recommendations, and follow-through.

Experience using SQL and/or Python, large datasets, and open-source intelligence in an investigative context.

Strong investigative judgment and the ability to distinguish facts, inferences, allegations, unknowns, assumptions, and analytical conclusions.

Experience with attribution, account or entity linkage, network analysis, open-source intelligence, external intelligence, or cross-platform research.

Strong written and verbal communication skills, with the ability to produce clear, concise, and defensible work product for investigators, leadership, Legal, Product, Engineering, and external-facing stakeholders.

Ability to turn ambiguous questions into precise investigation plans and drive complex, multi-week or multi-quarter initiatives with minimal oversight.

Systems-oriented thinking and the ability to connect individual cases to policy, product, process, detection, legal, reputational, and user-trust considerations.

Experience influencing partners and creating alignment in high-stakes or time-sensitive situations.

Demonstrated mentorship, informal leadership, or subject-matter expertise that helps other investigators grow.

Sound judgment, discretion, curiosity, resilience, and a commitment to evidence-based, fair, and proportionate investigations.
