Community Development & Advocacy Manager Assassin's Creed | Ubisoft China – Shanghai Your Mission We are looking for a passionate, creative and hands-on Community Development & Advocacy Manager to build and grow the Assassin's Creed community in China.

Your mission is to turn players into engaged fans, and passionate fans into true brand advocates.

You will own the local community strategy and bring it to life every day: connecting with players, creating exciting activities, growing loyal fan communities and making Assassin's Creed a brand that players love, share and champion.

What You Will Do 1. Build & Grow the Community ● Define and execute a community growth and engagement strategy for Assassin's Creed in China.

● Identify different player audiences, their interests and the best platforms to reach and engage them.

● Grow an active, passionate community that strengthens brand visibility, loyalty and advocacy.

2. Turn Fans into Brand Advocates ● Identify, recruit and develop core fans, community leaders and passionate ambassadors.

● Build different levels of community engagement, from wider fan groups to dedicated VIP communities, with activities and benefits tailored to each audience.

● Inspire fans to create, share, recommend and champion Assassin's Creed, including during challenging moments.

3. Be at the Heart of the Community ● Engage directly with players every day: start conversations, answer questions, listen to feedback and build meaningful relationships.

● Keep communities active through discussions, interactions and regular engagement initiatives.

● Understand community sentiment, manage sensitive conversations and maintain a welcoming, positive and professional environment.

4. Create Experiences That Bring Fans Together ● Imagine, plan and deliver engaging community activities: online and offline events, livestreams, contests, fan challenges, VIP experiences and more.

● Encourage fan creativity through cosplay, fan art, videos, guides and other community-generated content.

● Work with creative teams and partners to source, develop and adapt content that keeps the community excited and involved.

5. Connect People & Make Things Happen ● Work hand in hand with Ubisoft's Global Publishing teams, our local partners and local teams to build a consistent community strategy for China.

● Bring ideas to the table, challenge proposals constructively and mobilize the right people and resources to execute activities.

● Act as the voice of Chinese players, sharing their feedback, expectations and concerns with internal teams.

6. Own the Results ● Define clear objectives and measure community growth, participation, engagement, loyalty and advocacy.

● Understand what works, what doesn't and continuously improve your activities.

● Take ownership of your community's development and long-term health.

&#xa0; What Success Looks Like A growing, active and loyal Assassin's Creed community in China. Fans who don't just follow the brand, but participate, create, share, recommend it and help it thrive.

You won't just manage a community. You'll build one.
