Systems Engineer (Esports)

Riot Games · Los Angeles

Mid Engineering $149K–$208K Posted 2026-06-23
Apply on Riot Games’s site →

At Riot Games, we’re committed to delivering unforgettable esports experiences to fans around the globe. Riot engineers bring deep knowledge of specific technical areas but also value the opportunity to work across multiple domains. As a Systems Engineer in the Esports Event Technology (EET) Team, you will support a team focused on building, supporting and maintaining the infrastructure, tooling and applications that power Riot s esports events around the world. You will work in a self-guiding, cross-functional team with highly skilled individuals to ensure that Riot s infrastructure is capable of supporting all Riot Esports initiatives.

This role may require international travel to support global and regional events. Expected travel ranges from 10% to 40% depending on event schedules and business needs.

Responsibilities:

Act as the SME for core infrastructure systems, platforms, and services

Design, support and evolve our global esports infrastructure

Collaborate within a diverse, cross functional team across engineering and operations

Develop technical standards and enable operations teams through documentation, automation, and coaching

Participate in global event support as needed, including occasional non standard working hours

Required Qualifications:

5+ years as a Systems, Support or Infrastructure Engineering roles

Experience with Ansible and Terraform for automation and configuration management

Experience building and maintaining CI/CD pipelines (e.g., Jenkins)

Experience with virtualization technologies (e.g., VMware, Hyper-V, Proxmox, AHV).

Experience designing and operating containerized workloads (e.g., Docker)

Experience with imaging and deployment tools (e.g., FOG, SCCM, Autopilot)

Expertise in Linux and Windows systems

Track record of reducing operational toil through automation

Desired Qualifications:

Experience building or maintaining global infrastructure, systems at scale

Experience defining and implementing monitoring, alerting, and observability standards

Proficiency in scripting and automation (e.g., PowerShell, Bash)

Experience using Git for version control

Experience with operating infrastructure in cloud platforms (e.g., AWS, Azure)

Relevant certifications such as RHCE or RHCSA, or equivalent hands-on experience, are a plus

For this role, you ll find success through craft expertise, a collaborative spirit, and decision-making that prioritizes the delight of players. We will be looking at your past studies, experience, and your personal relationship with games. If you embody player empathy and care about the experiences of players, this could be the role for you!
