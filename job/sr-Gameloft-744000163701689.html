Senior Gameplay/Network Programmer

Gameloft · Paris

Senior Engineering Posted 2026-11-25 Full time
Apply on Gameloft’s site →

WHAT YOU WILL BE WORKING ON Main challenge We're building a game designed multiplayer from day one, not bolted on afterwards. You'll join the project right from preproduction to build the gameplay that players will actually feel, and to make sure it holds up online, in real conditions, on every platform.

What it means on a daily basis You'll be a gameplay programmer first, with network expertise that makes the whole team's features work in multiplayer. Concretely, you will: Design and implement core gameplay features in Unreal Engine (abilities, combat and player actions, interactions, game rules), built for multiplayer from the first line of code Make gameplay feel responsive and fair online through replication, RPCs, client-side prediction and reconciliation, and clear server authority choices for each feature, working hand in hand with Game Designers and GPPs to prototype and tune Lay the network foundations of the project, define the Gameplay team's networking strategy through concrete features, and hit network performance targets (latency, bandwidth, stability) with Unreal Insights Own network TRC compliance on every console, and flag certification blockers before they happen Set up automated network testing in CI, and share good practices with the GPPs by documenting your technical choices for them and for QA What success will look like You’re fully embedded in the team and you understand the game’s gameplay and networking end to end. You’ve already rolled out your first network recommendations and training sessions for the GPPs.

Who you would be working with You’ll report to François Karr, our Technical Director and work daily with people from across the studio: The whole GPP team Our Online Lead Our Studio Technical Coordinator The Game Design team and QA Team rituals: 3 stand-ups a week, plus dedicated syncs per theme or feature.

Your recruitment journey Our process takes place in four steps: First interview: with our HR Manager Test: a CodinGames exercise Second interview: with our Technical Director and our Studio Technical Coordinator Third interview: with our Game Manager and our Studio Manager Where this role can take you By becoming the studio’s go-to person for gamelplay/networking on Unreal, you’ll build a rare, highly sought-after expertise in the industry, with real potential to grow into a technical lead role.

WHAT YOU NEED TO SUCCEED To hit the ground running, you'll need a solid technical base in Unreal gameplay programming, with hands-on experience of networked gameplay. Here is what we're looking for: Strong command of Unreal Engine and its Gameplay building blocks (Gameplay Framework, Actors and Components, input, animation hooks), in C++ Hands-on experience shipping or prototyping networked gameplay: replication, RPCs, prediction Experience with console certification (TRC) and its networking requirements Knowledge of network profiling and simulation tools (Unreal Insights, Clumsy or equivalents) Experience with the Gameplay Ability System is a real plus Professional French and English WHO YOU ARE Beyond the technical skills, this role is about how you work with others and how you approach problems. We’re looking for someone who is: A gameplay programmer at heart, who cares about game feel and about the player's experience, even under latency A natural teacher: you can make complex technical topics easy to grasp Autonomous and solution-driven: you dig into a problem until it’s solved Open to feedback and comfortable questioning your own choices Curious about low-level systems; an interest in physics (Chaos) is a plus WHAT’S IN IT FOR YOU This is a rare chance to shape the network of a game from day one, with a lot of ownership and a front-row seat at the crossroads of several teams. Here is what you’ll get: A founding role on the network of a game built from preproduction onward Real technical autonomy, from prototype to certification A role at the crossroads of several teams (GPP, Game Design, QA) Health insurance, RTT days, meal vouchers, and Gameloft’s studio perks
