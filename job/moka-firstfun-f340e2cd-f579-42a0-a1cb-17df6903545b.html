Senior Game Data & Live Operations Lead

FirstFun

Lead Data & Analytics Posted 2026-07-24 Full time
Apply on FirstFun’s site →

Role Overview We are looking for a Senior Game Data & Live Operations Lead to drive data-informed improvements across our global mobile game portfolio. The role will analyse player behaviour, game ecosystems, live operations, and monetisation performance, and translate findings into practical product and operational improvements.

Key Responsibilities Analyse player engagement, retention, monetisation, churn, server health, matchmaking, and live-operations performance.

Identify gameplay and operational issues and recommend data-backed solutions.

Develop dashboards, monitoring frameworks, analytical models, and automated tools using SQL and Python.

Support the optimisation of game events, server ecosystems, player segmentation, cross-server activities, and monetisation initiatives.

Design and evaluate A/B tests and measure the impact of product or operational changes.

Work closely with product, development, publishing, and operations teams to implement recommendations.

Lead or mentor analysts and improve analytical standards and processes.

Requirements Bachelor’s degree in Data Analytics, Statistics, Information Systems, Computer Science, or a related field.

At least 8 years of relevant experience in game analytics, product analytics, or live operations.

Strong proficiency in SQL and Python.

Good understanding of retention, monetisation, player lifecycle, and live-service game metrics.

Proven ability to translate data insights into measurable business or product improvements.

Strong stakeholder management, communication, and project leadership skills.

Experience with strategy, multiplayer, or live-service games is preferred.
