Position: QA Engineer Platform Games Experience: 2-4 years Location: Netaji Subhash Place, Pitampura, Delhi Employment Type: Contract role Work Timings: 9AM - 6PM IST Overview

We re reimagining Atari s classic arcade catalog as short-session games for Reddit, MSN and emerging channels. We need a QA Engineer who can catch problems before players do, keep releases moving on tight timelines, and make sure every title meets platform certification standards before it goes live.

What You ll Do

Test each title through the full pipeline: functional, regression, compatibility and performance testing before every release

Look past surface bugs to judge the whole experience: gameplay feel, pacing, difficulty balance, onboarding and UX, not just whether the UI technically works

Write and maintain test plans and test cases for internal and outsourced titles

File clear, reproducible bug reports and track them through to a verified fix

Check builds against platform certification requirements (Reddit, MSN) before submission

Work closely with producers and developers to triage issues and keep releases on schedule

Test across multiple devices, browsers and aspect ratios, since these games need to hold up on any screen

Watch for performance issues like slow load times, dropped frames and memory spikes, and document them with clear repro steps

Run regression passes after patches and live-ops updates

Track quality metrics such as crash rate, open bug count and time-to-fix, and report weekly

Use AI tools to speed through repetitive work, like drafting test cases or doing a first pass on logs, so you can move on to the next title sooner

Qualifications

Required

3+ years in QA/testing for games (mobile, casual or browser titles)

Experience testing Unity and/or HTML5 games across browsers, devices and aspect ratios

An eye for UX as well as UI: you notice when something feels off even when nothing is technically broken

Good bug-reporting discipline: clear repro steps, sensible severity calls, thorough verification after fixes

Comfortable running QA on several titles at once within tight release windows

Some familiarity with platform certification or submission requirements (Reddit, MSN or similar)

Used to working with AI tools day to day, for things like generating test cases, scanning logs or drafting reports, to get through routine work faster.

Exposure to live-ops testing: post-launch patches, events, seasonal content

Basic scripting or test automation experience

Preferred

Passion for retro/arcade gaming and the Atari catalog

Experience with bug tracking or test management tools such as Jira or TestRail
