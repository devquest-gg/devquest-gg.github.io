ONLINE INFRASTRUCTURE

What We Do

We enable Epic’s online services teams to build, deploy, and manage services that are used by more than half a billion players around the world. Our mission is to provide world class tools and platforms to improve the experience of our developers and make it easier, faster, and safer to build, operate, and scale their applications. We operate at massive scale as one of the largest cloud computing users in the world.

What You ll Do

You ll own the backend systems behind Epic s CI/CD and build tooling and the cloud infrastructure they run on. These systems carry code from commit to production for teams across Epic, so they need to be fast, reliable and secure. Your primary focus is software supply chain security: securing the pipelines, runners and artifact stores that build and ship our software, working closely with our security and governance teams. This is a hands-on technical lead role. You ll spend most of your time building and running systems yourself, while helping set the team s technical direction, architecture and goals. Epic is an agentic-engineering-first company. You ll be expected to use Al coding tools and agents as a normal part of how you work, and help shape how we apply them to our own infrastructure.

In this role, you will

Design, build and operate the backend infrastructure for our CI/CD and artifact platforms on AWS and GCP

Lead software supply chain security work, including vulnerability scanning, artifact signing and provenance, and hardening of build runners and agents

Partner with security and governance teams on controls, audits and compliance requirements, and turn them into practical changes to tools and pipelines

Help set technical direction for the platform: weigh in on design, priorities and longer-term goals, and document the decisions

Guide and review the work of a team of contractors, setting clear tasks and standards and keeping the work moving

Automate wherever possible, with infrastructure as code, self-service tooling and workflows that reduce manual work

Improve the reliability, scale and observability of the build platform, troubleshoot hard production issues, and take part in incident response

Use AI coding tools and agentic workflows in your own work, and help apply them to how the team builds and runs its systems

What we re looking for

Strong experience running cloud infrastructure on AWS (experience with GCP or Azure is also welcome)

Experience deploying and maintaining environments with infrastructure as code (Terraform or similar) and configuration management tools

Hands-on experience running Kubernetes in production

Experience operating CI/CD and version control systems at scale, such as Lore, GitHub Actions, TeamCity, Codefresh, Jenkins, GitHub, GitLab or Perforce

Working knowledge of software supply chain security: vulnerability scanning, artifact and container registries, secrets management, and hardening build infrastructure

Strong scripting and programming skills in Python, Go, Bash or similar

Comfortable with Al coding tools and agentic programming, and interested in how far they can be pushed in infrastructure work

Experience leading technical work, such as making design decisions, mentoring programmers, or directing contractors or vendors

Excellent communicator, able to work across security, governance and programming teams

Experience with security or compliance frameworks in a large organization would be a plus
