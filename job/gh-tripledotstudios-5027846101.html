Job Summary

Wordscapes is one of the most-loved word puzzle games on mobile, and a long-running live game with a huge, loyal player base. We re looking for a Senior Game Designer who can do two things really well: craft levels players can t put down, and design features that keep them coming back for years.

You ll own work end to end, from the first idea and spec through ABT, tuning and live ops. Some weeks that means reshaping the early level ramp so new players stick around longer. Other weeks it means designing a new event, meta system or level mechanic and seeing it in front of millions of players. You ll sit on a small, senior, data-literate team where good ideas ship fast and every change is measured.

Responsibilities

Level design

Design, build and tune crossword levels and level ramps, balancing word choice, grid layout, letter sets and difficulty.

Shape progression and pacing across hundreds of levels, with deliberate spikes, breathers and rewards.

Use per-level funnels, churn points and completion data to find where players struggle, then fix it.

Design new level mechanics and goals that add variety and depth without breaking the core loop.

Partner with engineers on tooling, procedural generation, and general collaboration.

Feature design

Own features end to end: pitch, spec, UX flows, reward tuning, and success metrics.

Design retention-focused systems for new, engaged and returning lapsed players, from FTUE to long-term meta.

Write clear features specs with a hypothesis, treatments and KPIs, then read results with data partners and decide next steps.

Work closely with product, engineering, art, UX and QA to get features shipped on time and at quality.

Across both

Prototype ideas quickly, on paper, in whitebox, or with AI tools, and drop what doesn t test well.

Play the top casual and word games and bring back sharp, specific lessons.

Bring your years of experience and expertise to raise the bar for design docs and reviews on the team.

Requirements

5+ years of game design experience, with at least 3 years on mobile casual or puzzle games.

Hands-on level design: you ve built, tuned and shipped levels at scale in a level editor, and can show examples.

Feature design: you ve taken features from concept to live, and can walk us through what moved the metrics and what didn t.

Strong grasp of progression, pacing, difficulty curves, FTUE and retention levers.

Comfortable working from data: funnels, cohorts, retention curves and A/B test results.

Clear, structured writing. Your specs are easy for engineers, artists and QA to build from.

Experience working in a live game with regular releases and live ops.

This role is remote, with a hybrid schedule for those near our Dallas office: 4 days a week in office (Monday–Thursday) if you live in Dallas, or 3 days a week (Monday–Wednesday) if you live within a 3-hour radius.

Nice to have

Shipped word, crossword, puzzle or match-3 games.

Hands-on with Looker, BigQuery, Tableau or similar, enough to answer your own questions.

Designing ABTs on level content: difficulty, ramps, content swaps.

Spreadsheet fluency for ramp, economy and parameter tables.

Unity experience.

Procedural or AI-assisted content generation.

Prototypes or tools you ve built with AI.

Experience designing for returning and lapsed players, or for social and competitive features.

Don t tick every box? Apply anyway. We care more about how you think and what you ve shipped than a perfect match.
