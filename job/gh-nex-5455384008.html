Nex is on a mission to help families rediscover the joy of movement. Created by parents for parents, Nex combines technology and play to deliver fun, social, and interactive experiences powered by natural body motion, encouraging kids and adults to move more, play more, and have fun together. Nex Playground, the company’s award-winning active play system, is purpose-built to get families moving year-round, with safety and privacy as core considerations in its intentional design. It is certified kidSAFE+ COPPA compliant and built to support healthy, active play for all ages and abilities.

Nex Playground features a growing library of 50+ experiences, including motion and dance games, fitness and educational experiences, and Nex Originals. Content includes collaborations with partners like Hasbro, Sesame Workshop, and NBCUniversal. Nex has been recognized by Fast Company’s Most Innovative Companies, TIME’s Best Inventions, and Parents’ Best Entertainment System for Families, and has earned Red Dot, IDEA, and Core77 international design awards. We encourage you to explore Have Fun and Is Motion Gaming Back? , as they offer a deeper look into our culture, values, and explain how our approach to motion gaming differs from previous generations.

Nex employees may be expected to collaborate with colleagues across different time zones. Depending on the role and location, occasional travel may also be required for company events, external meetings, and other business-related activities.

Location: San Jose, California (Hybrid) Annual salary: USD 140,000 - USD 185,000 Type: Full Time Reports to: VP of Marketing

The Role

As our Sr. Marketing Manager, Accessories, you will own go-to-market for new Nex Playground accessories and merchandise.

That includes travel cases, skins, protectors, mounting solutions, extra cables, and Nex merch and apparel.

You ll lead the first phase of every launch: taking new products to market through our direct-to-consumer and e-commerce channels, including Amazon and TikTok Shop. Your job is to prove each product works: real consumer demand, product-market fit, and a healthy margin structure. Once a product is proven, you ll hand it off to our commercial team to scale into retail, with the story and data they need to start early retailer conversations.

This role is ideal for a marketer who loves launching products, reads the numbers closely, and can orchestrate many teams toward one clear outcome.

What You ll Do

Phase 1 Go-to-Market

Own end-to-end launch plans for new accessories and merch, from concept through launch and early sales.

Develop positioning, messaging, and launch assets that make each product easy to understand and want.

Provide input on pricing, bundles, and assortment in partnership with Product and Finance.

Orchestrate launches across our D2C store, Amazon, and TikTok Shop.

Prove Product-Market Fit

Define success metrics before launch, such as demand, conversion, attach rate, ratings and reviews, return rates, and contribution margin.

Design and run tests across pricing, bundles, offers, and creative to learn quickly.

Build clear recommendations to scale, iterate, or sunset each product based on results.

Cross-Functional Orchestration

Partner with our growth and performance marketing experts to plan and optimize paid, social, and marketplace programs.

Work with Creative, Content, and Social teams on assets for PDPs, TikTok, email, and the Nex website.

Coordinate with Product and Operations on launch timing, inventory, and forecasts.

Brief and manage vendors and partners for merch and apparel programs as needed.

Handoff to Scale

Package results, consumer insights, and the sell-in story so our retail management team can begin early retailer conversations.

Transition proven products to the commercial team with a repeatable launch playbook.

Consumer Insight

Bring the voice of the customer into every decision, using reviews, social listening, and community feedback.

Identify gaps and opportunities that shape the future accessories roadmap.

What You ll Bring

Experience

8+ years in product marketing or go-to-market roles for consumer products, ideally in accessories, consumer electronics, gaming, toys, or lifestyle brands.

A proven track record of launching products through D2C and Amazon.

TikTok Shop or social commerce experience is strongly preferred.

Experience with merchandise or apparel programs is a plus.

Skills Strengths

Strong commercial acumen across unit economics, pricing, margin, and demand forecasting.

Data-driven decision maker who is comfortable with marketplace, e-commerce, and marketing analytics.

Excellent writer and storyteller who can make simple products feel compelling.

Highly organized, with the ability to run several launches at once.

Collaboration Mindset

A natural orchestrator who aligns people without needing direct authority.

Test-and-learn mindset, with the judgment to move fast and the discipline to measure.

Comfortable in a small, fast-moving environment with evolving priorities.

Passion for building products and experiences that families love.

What Success Looks Like

A clear accessories roadmap that has cross-functional alignment of new accessories and merch that compliment our platform ecosystem strategy

A customer-led insights process that guides and supports key decisions on new products, driven by tangible market and customer research

Business plans and GTM plans that are fully documented and positioned for success with sign-off from the leadership team before new accessories move into production and marketing team action around the plan

Clear messaging, value proposition, and pricing recommendations for new products that support the business plan/thesis

Successful GTM orchestration across multiple internal teams, from the product teams to the marketing communications teams that assemble the creative and deliverables to drive new launches, meet the sales goals, and sustain the business plan

Initial learnings in market with a first launch phase of growth to validate the business contribution of the accessory and establish a growth path before broader commercial business development (retail development, international expansion, etc)

Product-driven culture that treasures talents and individual growth.

Front-row seat and hands-on experience with cutting edge technologies in the evolving gaming field
