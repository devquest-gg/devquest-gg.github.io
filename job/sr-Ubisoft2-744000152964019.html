Develop at Ubisoft is a mentorship program for students and recent graduates (with less than two years of experience) looking to grow their skills in Game Design, Audio, Programming, or Cybersecurity.&#xa0; This initiative supports individuals from genders historically underrepresented in gaming and STEM.&#xa0;&#xa0; &#xa0; Develop at Ubisoft is one of several talent programs we offer to engage and support different audiences across the industry.&#xa0;&#xa0; Please note: this program is a mentorship program and is not tied to any hiring process.&#xa0; From January to May 2027, selected mentees will work on a project based on a given theme. They will be mentored by volunteer Ubisoft developers, including from our mobile studios, who will provide feedback and guidance throughout the mentorship to help them develop their own personal project.&#xa0; In the Programming track , you will develop and submit a game using either C++ or C# with a freely available development tool, such as Unity for mobile.

Eligibility criteria:&#xa0; At least 18 years old&#xa0; Currently enrolled in studies, or graduated in Spring 2024 or later&#xa0; Fewer than 2 years of professional experience&#xa0; Legally entitled to work in the country of application&#xa0; Applications may be submitted to only one location&#xa0;&#xa0; Individual entries only – team entries will not be considered Additional criteria for Toronto applicants:&#xa0; Must be a student or recent graduate&#xa0; Must be a resident of Ontario&#xa0; Must have attended a recognized school, college or university in Ontario&#xa0; This program is designed to support early-career professionals from underrepresented genders – female and nonbinary – in the industry. By submitting an application, you confirm that you meet and accept the eligibility criteria outlined above.&#xa0; Apply with your resume, cover letter and, if available, a portfolio . Please also select your preferred track and location .&#xa0; Application period: October 1st –  October 31st&#xa0;&#xa0; &#xa0; We are looking for mentees in the locations where our participating studios are based:&#xa0; Canada (Montreal, Saguenay,Toronto); France (Bordeaux, Lyon - Ivory Tower, Montpellier, Paris); Germany (Berlin, Kolibri Games (mobile) - Berlin, Düsseldorf, Mainz); &#xa0; Italy (Milan) ; Singapore; Sweden (Malmö - Massive Entertainment);
