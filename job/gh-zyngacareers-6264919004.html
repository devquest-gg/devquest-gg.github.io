Join us and be part of the play! Position Overview

Zynga is seeking a skilled Senior Animator(Spine) to join a dynamic team for one of Zynga’s most established games. We are looking for driven individuals who want to contribute to the growth and development of our product in a highly collaborative environment. The ideal candidate must be able to work with talented contributors from different disciplines and bring a unique perspective to the table. Applicants must have a strong understanding of animation principles and be able to use a range of techniques to drive high-quality visuals with a limited toolset.

What You Will Do

Create high-quality 2D/2.5D animations for characters, props, UI, and VFX using Spine is a must.

Rig, animate, optimize, and integrate assets into Unity.

Collaborate with Art, Tech Art, Design, and Engineering teams to deliver engaging game experiences.

Develop particle systems, visual effects, and animation setups in Unity.

Interpret briefs, develop concepts, and present creative visual solutions.

Proactively identify design challenges and suggest innovative ideas.

Prepare and optimize assets using Photoshop for mobile game performance.

Troubleshoot integration issues and contribute to pipeline improvements.

Maintain high standards of quality, consistency, and delivery efficiency.

Contribute to Zynga’s collaborative and creativity-driven culture.

What You Bring

10+ years of animation experience in gaming or related industries, including at least 2 years of hands-on Spine 2D experience.

Strong expertise in Spine 2D and Unity, including 2D/2.5D rigging, animation, and game integration.

Excellent understanding of animation principles, Spine workflows, and optimization techniques.

Hands-on experience with Unity Animator Controllers, prefabs, particle systems, VFX, shaders, and lighting.

Experience working in mobile game production pipelines with a focus on performance and quality.

Working knowledge of Photoshop CC and version control tools such as Git.

Strong visual communication, problem-solving, and collaboration skills.

Passion for games and creating engaging player experiences.
