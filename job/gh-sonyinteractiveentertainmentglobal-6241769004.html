Technical Account Manager II

Location: United Kingdom

PlayStation has an exciting opportunity for a Technical Account Manager II supporting game development partners across the UK and Europe. You will help development teams deliver exceptional games on PlayStation platforms by connecting them with technical expertise, advocating for their needs, and coordinating support throughout the development lifecycle.

This role requires someone who can navigate ambiguity, align internal stakeholders, and turn complex technical and organisational challenges into clear, actionable plans.

Responsibilities

Own technical account relationships with assigned development partners, building a deep understanding of their development goals, production priorities, and support needs.

Build trusted relationships with technical leadership, production teams, and other key decision-makers.

Partner with Developer Technology Group (DTG) engineers, platform teams, and business account management teams to connect partners with the right expertise and support.

Navigate situations where requirements, ownership, or priorities are unclear, bringing structure to discussions and driving agreement on next steps.

Align internal stakeholders around partner needs, clarifying responsibilities, managing dependencies, and coordinating support across teams.

Meet regularly with partners to proactively identify project risks, technical challenges, and opportunities to improve development workflows.

Maintain ownership of account and title support, coordinating escalations and driving issues towards resolution.

Gather partner feedback and champion it within PlayStation to inform platform tools, documentation, services, and support priorities.

Share recommended development practices and facilitate knowledge exchange across development partners and technical teams.

Provide on-site support and training, and contribute to technical workshops, postmortems, and advisory panels.

Collaborate with teams around the world to deliver a consistent approach to partner support.

Travel regularly to partner sites across the UK and Europe, with occasional international travel as required.

Skills and Qualifications

Required

Engineering experience with hands-on exposure to game SDKs and the ability to understand and discuss technical documentation, tools, and integration requirements.

A solid understanding of game development pipelines and the challenges teams face across development, production, and release.

Demonstrated ability to work effectively through ambiguity, define problems, establish priorities, and drive progress when ownership or requirements are evolving.

Experience aligning stakeholders across technical and non-technical teams, balancing competing priorities, and influencing outcomes without direct authority.

Excellent written and verbal communication skills in English, with the ability to adapt communication for engineers, production teams, and senior stakeholders.

Strong relationship-building skills and a proactive, service-oriented approach to supporting development partners.

Familiarity with development tools and the ability to quickly understand complex technical projects and their support needs.

Ability to work independently, including at partner sites, exercising sound judgement and representing PlayStation professionally.

Ability to recognise when specialist expertise is needed and coordinate the right people and resources to resolve issues.

Willingness and ability to travel within the UK and Europe, with occasional international travel.

An undergraduate degree or equivalent industry experience.

Preferred

Previous experience in game studio operations, technical production, development management, or a related role coordinating development workflows and cross-functional teams.

Technical knowledge of PlayStation platforms, tools, and development processes.

Direct console game development experience, including contributing to the launch of a console title in a technical capacity.

Experience managing technical relationships with external development partners.

Experience working with globally distributed teams and coordinating across organisational boundaries.

Experience facilitating technical workshops, project reviews, or postmortems and translating their findings into practical improvements.
