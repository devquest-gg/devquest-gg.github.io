Cloud Imperium Games is a leading game development company known for its innovative and immersive gaming experiences. We are dedicated to creating high-quality games that captivate and engage players worldwide.

Founded in 2012 by renowned game developer Chris Roberts, Cloud Imperium Games (CIG) is one of the largest global independent studios, 1,000+ employees, dedicated to delivering next-generation AAA games. CIG prides itself in thinking outside the box, encouraging creativity and developing ground-breaking technology. CIG believes in raising the bar for the gaming industry and the community as a whole.

What will you do? The MTL Tools team designs, develops, and maintains internal tools primarily used to create, organize, profile, and optimize game content (environments, missions, performance, LOD, procedural scattering, data visualization, etc.), whether in the editor or directly in-game.

Design, create, document, maintain, and improve content development tools for Star Citizen Collaborate closely and effectively with artists, designers, other content developers, and software engineers on tool requirements and design Contribute ideas across all aspects of production and tool development Research and evaluate new third-party game development tools Provide support to tool users What do we expect from our Senior Tools Programmer? 5+ years of experience as a programmer, either in Tools or a related field Strong sense of user experience Ability to make sound technical decisions without constant supervision Deliver high-quality projects on time Proficiency in modern C++ Proficiency with C++ development tools on Windows Strong software engineering skills Knowledge of best practices in software design and debugging Ability to collaborate closely with colleagues from diverse disciplines Strong communication skills in both English and French Nice to have: Experience with Python Experience in automation Knowledge of support tools Knowledge of procedural generation Knowledge of visual node-based languages Cloud Imperium Games is committed to employing a diverse workforce.
