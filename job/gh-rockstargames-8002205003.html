Workplace Coordinator

Rockstar Games · Leeds

Mid Other Onsite Posted 2026-09-24 Full time

At Rockstar Games, we create world-class entertainment experiences.

Become part of a team working on some of the most rewarding, large-scale creative projects to be found in any entertainment medium - all within an inclusive, highly-motivated environment where you can learn and collaborate with some of the most talented people in the industry.

Rockstar Leeds is on the lookout for a passionate Workplace Coordinator. As part of the Workplace Services team you’ll play a key part in coordinating the day-to-day operations of the site and its facilities services. This is an exciting opportunity to contribute to a world-class working environment that supports creativity, collaboration, and innovation.

This is a full-time, permanent and in-office position based in Rockstar’s unique game development studio in the heart of Leeds, England.

WHAT WE DO

The Rockstar Workplace Services (WPS) team ensures that our workplace, facilities, and services work seamlessly to support the core business.

We create a safe, creative, and supportive environment for all Rockstar staff.

We manage and develop both hard and soft facilities services, including maintenance, security, cleaning, catering, and space strategy.

We ensure full compliance with all relevant legislation and guidance.

RESPONSIBILITIES

Serve as a key point of contact for WPS communications.

Log, prioritize, and delegate workplace issues to the appropriate team member or contractor, tracking them through to resolution.

Oversee daily operations of WPS contracts and services (e.g., cleaning, waste, drinks), ensuring consistently high standards.

Conduct regular site checks and audits to maintain service quality.

Maintain trackers and management systems, producing reports for WPS and other support teams as needed.

Support procurement and administration of WPS, including coordination of team and project purchase orders.

Produce and manage facilities documentation following company standards.

Perform ad-hoc duties as required to support the building and facilities.

REQUIREMENTS

3+ years experience in a workplace operations or administration role within a complex environment.

Experience managing service contracts.

Strong understanding of current safety legislation and standard processes.

Outstanding communication skills with the ability to work independently, prioritize, and seek guidance when necessary.

Strong organisational, planning and project management abilities, with a strategic approach to problem-solving.

Process-driven and outcome-focused, with a commitment to continuous improvement and accurate documentation.

Experience in overlapping support areas such as IT, administration, or operations.

Proficient in Microsoft Office (Outlook, Word, Excel).

PLUSES

Please note that these are desirable skills and are not required to apply for the position.

IWFM or IFMA qualification.

IOSH Managing Safely certification.

ADDITIONAL INFORMATION

Occasional weekend and early/evening out of core hours work will be required.
