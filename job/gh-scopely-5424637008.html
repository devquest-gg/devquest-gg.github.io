Finance Business Partner, Technology Vertical — MONOPOLY GO!

Scopely · Culver City

Mid Other Hybrid $113K–$168K Posted 2026-09-24 Full time
Apply on Scopely’s site →

Scopely is seeking a commercially minded Finance Business Partner to support the Technology Vertical Group behind MONOPOLY GO! , one of the fastest-growing mobile games in the world. This is a rare opportunity to take on a high-impact role within the finance organization of a dynamic, rapidly scaling global gaming company.

The role is based in Culver City, CA, on a hybrid basis .

MONOPOLY GO! is a global phenomenon and the biggest mobile game launch in history, captivating millions of players worldwide.

As the dedicated finance partner to our technology organization — engineering, platform, QA, and technical operations — you will be the single point of contact for the leaders who own that spend. You’ll build their budget with them, explain their variances before they’re asked about them, and translate engineering roadmaps and headcount plans into a financial plan the business can commit to. You will own OpEx reporting, accruals, headcount tracking, and workforce planning consolidation for the vertical, and you will be expected to automate the production side of that work so your time goes into partnership and analysis rather than report assembly.

To succeed in this position, you must be able to hold a credible conversation with an engineering leader about trade-offs, then go build the model that proves the point. This role offers a unique blend of senior-facing business partnership and deep analytics — a foundation for learning the business mechanics of a unit operating at the scale of a standalone company.

You’ll work directly with Finance Managers, the VP of Monopoly GO Finance, and the leadership of the Technology Vertical Group. We prioritize your growth; you will receive strong support from senior partners during your ramp-up, ensuring you have the mentorship and guidance needed to navigate Scopely’s global portfolio effectively.

What you ll do

Own a Vertical: You are the finance partner for technology — not one analyst among many on a shared queue. The vertical’s leaders will come to you first, and the numbers they take to senior leadership will be yours.

Build, Don t Just Report: You ll design and ship the reporting infrastructure — OpEx models, accrual engines, headcount and allocation pipelines — rather than rebuilding the same workbook every month.

AI-Powered by Default: This is an AI-first FP A team. You will be expected to use Claude, Claude Code, and agentic tooling at an expert level to automate recurring deliverables, and to set the standard for how the wider finance organization does the same.

Speak the Same Language: An engineering background means you can engage technology leaders on their terms — infrastructure, platform, tooling, and resourcing decisions — and earn the kind of trust a pure finance profile has to work much harder for.

High-Visibility Impact: You’ll work closely with Finance Managers and cross-functional partners across people and talent, business operations, customer support, chat app, technology, and quality assurance, preparing analysis that directly informs the game’s senior leadership.

Tech-Forward Finance: Be part of an AI-powered FP A team where automating your own reporting is the expectation, not a side project — so partnership and insight get the hours, not report production.

Top Talent Environment: Join a high-caliber team with backgrounds in consulting, banking, private equity, and high-growth tech from companies such as Amazon, Google, and Netflix.

Career Trajectory: High performers have a path to fast promotions and will gain experience in complex modeling, financial systems architecture, and automation across Scopely s game portfolio.

Learning Culture: Access to internal leadership development, mentorship, and cross-functional mobility across Scopely s global portfolio.

What we re looking for

Education: A bachelor’s degree in engineering (or a closely related technical discipline such as computer science). This role partners with a technology organization, and we want someone who has been through an engineering curriculum and can reason about technical work from the inside.

Business Partnership: Demonstrated ability to be the finance voice in a business leader’s room — gathering budget inputs, challenging assumptions constructively, explaining variances clearly, and being trusted by non-finance stakeholders as a partner rather than a reporting function.

Influence Without Authority: Comfort pushing back on spend requests, surfacing risk early, and holding a vertical accountable to its plan while keeping the relationship intact.

Financial Storytelling: Ability to turn a variance bridge or headcount model into a clear narrative and a recommendation for senior leadership, including VP-level audiences.

AI Automation (Required): Hands-on experience using AI tools — Claude, Claude Code, or comparable LLM and agentic tooling — to automate real financial reporting and analysis workflows. You should be able to describe processes you personally took from manual to automated, and how you validated that the output still tied out. This is a requirement of the role, not a nice-to-have.

Operational Analytical Balance: A solid foundation in financial planning and analysis with a track record of accuracy and attention to detail. You must be comfortable navigating ambiguity and moving quickly in a fast-paced environment.

Technical Skills: Strong Excel skills are required, including the ability to build and maintain revenue/cost models and manage large workforce-planning datasets. Experience with financial systems such as Oracle and Workday Adaptive Planning is a plus; SQL and data visualization tools (Looker, Tableau, Power BI) are a plus.

Proactive Problem Solving: A proven ability to manage high-volume operational tasks, such as PO and Workday approvals, while identifying opportunities to streamline workflows.

Process Improvement: Interest in refining processes, such as T E tracking, month-end accruals, or automating manual reporting tasks.

Integrity Independence: Ability to thrive in a fast-paced environment, exhibit the highest personal and professional integrity, and independently manage recurring monthly and quarterly deliverables.

Passion: A passion for games, consumer products, or technology-driven businesses is a strong plus.

About Scopely

Scopely is a leading video game and global interactive entertainment company, home to many of the world’s most beloved and enduring experiences, including two of the most successful mobile games of all-time “MONOPOLY GO!” and “Pokémon GO,” along with “Stumble Guys,” “Star Trek™ Fleet Command,” “MARVEL Strike Force,” “WWE Champions,” the Scrabble® franchise, “Yahtzee® With Buddies,” and many others. Across mobile, web, PC, and console, Scopely creates, develops, publishes, and live-operates one of the most diversified and award-winning portfolios in the games industry — bringing hundreds of millions of players together through a shared love of play.

Founded in 2011, Scopely is powered by its exceptional team — including thousands of world-class gamemakers around the globe, a distinctive tenet-driven culture, and its proprietary technology platform, Playgami. Together, these strengths have fueled Scopely’s position as the #1 mobile games company in the U.S. and #2 globally, generating more than $10 billion in lifetime revenue. Whether building global sensations like “MONOPOLY GO!” from the ground up, or expanding through strategic acquisitions, including the FoxNext, GSN, and Scopely Explore games businesses — Scopely consistently delivers experiences players love today and return to for years to come.

Recognized multiple times as one of the "100 Most Influential Companies in the World" by TIME magazine and one of Fast Company s "World s Most Innovative Companies" and “Best Workplaces for Innovators,” Scopely believes that video games can be a force for good — creating meaningful connections, vibrant communities, and making life better through play.
